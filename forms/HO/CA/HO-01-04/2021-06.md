# HO 01 04 California Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2021-06)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

This endorsement changes the insurance provided under your policy. It applies only to property and interests located in California.

The provisions of this endorsement apply to the extent they are relevant to the insurance provided. A provision that does not apply to the circumstances of a claim does not create coverage.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision otherwise applicable to the insurance, this endorsement controls. If no conflict exists, the provisions apply together.

We apply this endorsement in accordance with California law. A provision is enforced only to the extent permitted by California law.

We do not cover any loss for which coverage is excluded by a provision that remains applicable after this endorsement is applied. We do not broaden coverage unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

We do not reduce coverage unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise. A limitation stated in this endorsement applies only as written.

Any condition changed by this endorsement applies in place of the condition it changes. Any condition not changed by this endorsement remains applicable.

An insured must comply with all duties that remain applicable to a loss. Failure to comply with a duty may affect coverage only as permitted by California law.

An insured must provide information that we reasonably request to evaluate a claim. The information must be accurate and complete to the extent known by the insured.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. We may require reasonable documentation supporting a claimed loss.

An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We do not cover avoidable additional damage resulting from a failure to protect the property.

An insured must allow us a reasonable opportunity to inspect damaged property. An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably necessary for our inspection.

An insured must submit a claim in a manner that permits us to determine whether coverage applies. We may investigate a claim without waiving any provision of this endorsement.

Our investigation, payment, or negotiation does not waive a condition, exclusion, limitation, or defense. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

Any payment we make is subject to the applicable deductible. The "minimum Section I deductible" applies as required by the insurance.

An insured must provide a signed statement when we request one. The "sworn statement deadline" applies as required by the insurance.

We may request examinations under oath when permitted by the insurance and California law. An insured must answer questions truthfully and provide requested records.

We may settle a claim with an insured or another person entitled to payment. A settlement does not establish coverage for any other claim.

We do not cover loss caused by fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation by an insured in connection with a claim. This provision applies only as permitted by California law.

If a provision of this endorsement is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain applicable. The invalid or unenforceable provision is enforced to the fullest extent permitted by California law.

The duties and limitations in this endorsement apply to each insured seeking coverage. Knowledge or conduct of an insured is imputed only as permitted by California law.

This endorsement applies throughout the period during which the insurance is in force. It does not apply after the insurance has ended, except as required by California law.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** We apply the "Windstorm and Hail Deductible" to a "covered loss" caused directly by windstorm or hail. The applicable deductible is the amount shown in your policy for that deductible.

**T.2** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies only to property for which windstorm or hail is a covered cause of loss. We apply the deductible before determining the amount payable for the covered loss.

**T.3** We apply this provision to direct physical loss to covered property. We do not apply this provision to loss that is not a covered loss.

**T.4** "Windstorm" means wind, including wind-driven material, that causes direct physical loss to covered property. Windstorm does not include loss caused solely by flood, earth movement, or another excluded cause of loss.

**T.5** "Hail" means precipitation in the form of ice particles. Hail includes direct physical impact by hail upon covered property.

**T.6** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies when windstorm or hail causes a covered loss. It applies whether windstorm or hail acts alone or with another covered cause of loss.

**T.7** We determine whether windstorm or hail caused a covered loss from the facts of the loss. The presence of wind or hail near the insured location does not by itself establish covered damage.

**T.8** An "occurrence" means a weather event, or related weather conditions, causing direct physical loss. We may treat related weather conditions as the same occurrence when the damage results from a continuous weather pattern.

**T.9** We apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible separately to each occurrence. We do not combine separate occurrences merely because they affect the same property.

**T.10** We determine the applicable deductible from the policy provisions in effect when the occurrence begins. A change made after the occurrence begins does not alter that deductible.

**T.11** The deductible applies to the total covered loss arising from an occurrence. We do not apply the deductible separately to individual items of damaged property unless your policy states otherwise.

**T.12** "Damaged property" means covered property that sustains direct physical loss from windstorm or hail. Damaged property may include property requiring repair, replacement, or other covered treatment.

**T.13** We subtract the applicable deductible from the covered amount of loss. If the covered amount of loss does not exceed the deductible, we do not make a payment.

**T.14** The deductible is your responsibility. Payment of the deductible by another person does not change our rights or your duties under this policy.

**T.15** You must protect damaged property from further loss after an occurrence. Reasonable measures taken to protect damaged property do not eliminate the applicable deductible.

**T.16** An insured must allow us to inspect damaged property as often as reasonably necessary. You must preserve the damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**T.17** You must provide records, photographs, receipts, estimates, and other information that we reasonably request to evaluate the claim. Your failure to provide requested information may affect our ability to determine the covered loss.

**T.18** We may investigate the cause, extent, and value of a claimed loss. Our investigation does not waive any policy provision, condition, limitation, or exclusion.

**T.19** We may request that you separate damaged property from undamaged property when reasonably possible. You must not discard damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property.

**T.20** We determine the amount of covered loss after applying all applicable policy provisions. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible is applied to that determination.

**T.21** The deductible does not increase the limit of liability applicable to covered property. Our payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**T.22** We do not cover any portion of a windstorm or hail loss that is within the applicable deductible. This applies even if repair or replacement costs exceed the deductible.

**T.23** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail when the loss is excluded by this policy. The deductible does not create coverage for excluded loss.

**T.24** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, or improve property that has not sustained direct physical loss from windstorm or hail. The deductible does not apply to costs that are not covered.

**T.25** When a covered loss involves windstorm or hail and another cause of loss, we determine the covered portion of the loss under the policy. We then apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to the covered portion attributable to windstorm or hail.

**T.26** We do not duplicate payment for the same property damage. The deductible applies without regard to whether another source may pay for part of the loss.

**T.27** If another policy or agreement may provide payment for the same loss, you must notify us of that payment source. We may coordinate our payment with amounts available from that source.

**T.28** Your right to recover from a person responsible for the loss is transferred to us to the extent of our payment. You must do nothing after loss to impair that right.

**T.29** We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath when reasonably necessary. We may require the examination to address the cause, extent, value, and ownership of claimed property.

**T.30** You must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a claim. Cooperation includes providing information that is reasonably available to you.

**T.31** A claim for windstorm or hail damage must identify the property claimed to be damaged. You must describe the observed damage with reasonable particularity.

**T.32** You must notify us promptly of a loss that may involve windstorm or hail. Prompt notice permits us to inspect the property and evaluate the claim.

**T.33** Delay in reporting a loss may affect the availability and reliability of evidence. We may consider prejudice resulting from delay when permitted by applicable law.

**T.34** You must make reasonable efforts to mitigate further damage after a covered loss. We may pay reasonable expenses incurred solely to protect covered property, subject to applicable policy provisions.

**T.35** Expenses incurred to protect property are not payment for preexisting damage. Such expenses do not reduce or replace your responsibility for the deductible.

**T.36** We may pay you, a mortgagee, or another person having a legal interest in the covered property. Payment to a person with a legal interest does not alter the deductible.

**T.37** A mortgagee or other interested person may have rights under this policy. Those rights are subject to the applicable deductible and all other policy provisions.

**T.38** We may settle a covered loss by repair, replacement, payment, or another method permitted by this policy. The method of settlement does not change the deductible.

**T.39** If we elect to repair damaged property, you must provide reasonable access to the property. You must permit reasonable access for inspection, repair, and related work.

**T.40** We are not responsible for the quality, timeliness, or conduct of a contractor selected by you. You remain responsible for agreements that you make with contractors.

**T.41** If we recommend a contractor, you are not required to use that contractor. Your selection of a contractor does not alter coverage or the deductible.

**T.42** You must not incur repair costs beyond those reasonably necessary to protect property without our consent. Emergency measures reasonably necessary to prevent further damage may be taken without prior consent.

**T.43** We may request documentation supporting repair or replacement costs. Documentation may include invoices, estimates, contracts, and proof of payment.

**T.44** We may determine that damaged property can be repaired rather than replaced. A disagreement about repairability does not suspend the application of the deductible.

**T.45** "Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, considering depreciation and condition. Actual cash value is determined under the applicable policy provisions.

**T.46** We may calculate a covered loss on an actual cash value basis when the policy permits. The deductible applies to the covered loss as calculated under the policy.

**T.47** A request for replacement cost payment must comply with the policy requirements applicable to replacement cost. The deductible applies whether payment is made on an actual cash value or replacement cost basis.

**T.48** We do not cover increased costs resulting from delay caused by an insured. We do not cover increased costs resulting from your failure to protect damaged property.

**T.49** We do not cover loss resulting from faulty, inadequate, or defective maintenance that is not directly caused by windstorm or hail. This exclusion applies even if windstorm or hail reveals a preexisting condition.

**T.50** We do not cover wear, tear, deterioration, or decay. We do not cover the cost to correct conditions that existed before the occurrence.

**T.51** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion applies only as permitted by applicable law.

**T.52** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters property through an opening not created by windstorm or hail. We may cover direct physical loss when windstorm or hail creates an opening and water enters through that opening, subject to all policy provisions.

**T.53** We determine whether an opening was created by windstorm or hail from available evidence. An opening caused by deterioration, prior damage, construction defect, or maintenance failure is not an opening created by windstorm or hail.

**T.54** We do not cover damage caused by surface water, overflow, or water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies regardless of whether windstorm or hail occurs at the same time or contributes to the loss.

**T.55** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. This exclusion applies whether earth movement is caused by weather conditions or another event.

**T.56** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the insured location, unless the policy provides coverage for that loss. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not create coverage for power failure.

**T.57** We apply this provision as required by California bulletinas required by California bulletin (see California Bulletin CDI-2022-03 — Mandatory Earthquake Coverage Offer, B.2 Requirements). Any application of this provision remains subject to applicable law.

**T.58** If a policy provision conflicts with applicable law, we will apply the provision in a manner consistent with applicable law. Any remaining provision will continue to apply.

**T.59** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to a claim even if you do not agree with our estimate of the loss. You may provide information supporting a different estimate.

**T.60** A disagreement about the value of damaged property does not eliminate the deductible. A disagreement about the cause of loss does not create coverage.

**T.61** We may request that you provide a sworn statement regarding the loss. The statement must be based upon information known or reasonably available to you.

**T.62** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to a claim. Concealment or misrepresentation may affect coverage as permitted by applicable law.

**T.63** We may rely upon information supplied by an insured, a contractor, a public authority, or another source. Our reliance upon that information does not prevent further investigation.

**T.64** We may inspect property before or after repairs are completed. Inspection does not constitute acceptance of liability or agreement concerning the amount of loss.

**T.65** You must retain receipts and records for emergency work when reasonably possible. You must provide those materials to us upon request.

**T.66** We may request reasonable access to photographs, recordings, reports, and communications concerning the claimed loss. You must provide access to materials within your possession or control.

**T.67** We do not cover penalties, fines, or assessments imposed upon an insured, unless coverage is expressly provided. The deductible does not apply to amounts that are not covered.

**T.68** We do not cover the cost of complying with an ordinance or law, unless coverage is expressly provided. Any covered ordinance or law loss remains subject to the applicable deductible.

**T.69** We do not cover loss of use, delay, or interruption unless coverage is expressly provided. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies only as provided for the applicable coverage.

**T.70** We may require reasonable proof that claimed property was present at the insured location before the occurrence. Proof may include records, photographs, statements, or other reliable evidence.

**T.71** You must identify any prior damage to property for which you seek payment. We do not pay for damage that existed before the occurrence.

**T.72** We may distinguish between damage caused by windstorm or hail and damage caused by other conditions. We pay only for the portion of direct physical loss that is covered.

**T.73** We may determine that some claimed damage is cosmetic and does not impair the function of property. We do not cover cosmetic damage unless the policy provides coverage for that damage.

**T.74** The deductible applies regardless of whether damaged property is repaired, replaced, or left unrepaired. Your decision not to repair property does not increase the amount payable.

**T.75** Payment of a covered loss does not waive our right to apply the deductible to that loss. Payment also does not waive any other policy provision.

**T.76** This provision applies to all claims for covered loss caused directly by windstorm or hail. We will determine coverage, the amount of loss, and the applicable deductible under the policy provisions and applicable law.

## T.2 — Notice of Deductible Change

**T.1** A "deductible" is the portion of a covered loss that remains your responsibility before we make payment. A "Deductible Change Notice" is our written notice that changes a deductible applicable to your coverage.

**T.2** We will provide you a Deductible Change Notice before a changed deductible applies. The notice will state the deductible that is being changed.

**T.3** The Deductible Change Notice will identify the changed deductible in clear terms. The notice will state whether the deductible is increased, decreased, added, removed, or otherwise revised.

**T.4** The Deductible Change Notice will describe the coverage to which the changed deductible applies. We will identify any condition, cause of loss, property, or circumstance that affects application of the changed deductible.

**T.5** The Deductible Change Notice will state the date on which the changed deductible takes effect. The changed deductible will not apply before that date unless applicable law permits otherwise.

**T.6** We will send the Deductible Change Notice to you at the mailing address shown in our records. We may deliver the notice by another method permitted by applicable law.

**T.7** If you have agreed to receive notices electronically, we may provide the Deductible Change Notice electronically. Electronic delivery will be made in a manner permitted by applicable law.

**T.8** An insured must provide us with a current mailing address and electronic address, if applicable. An insured must promptly notify us of a change in contact information.

**T.9** A Deductible Change Notice is effective when we properly send or deliver it as permitted by applicable law. Actual receipt of the notice is not required when applicable law does not require actual receipt.

**T.10** We will state the information necessary for you to determine the deductible that applies after the effective date shown in the Deductible Change Notice. We will not rely on an oral statement to change a deductible.

**T.11** A changed deductible applies only to a covered loss that occurs on or after the effective date shown in the Deductible Change Notice. A changed deductible does not apply to a covered loss that occurred before that effective date.

**T.12** We will determine the applicable deductible by the event causing the covered loss. The date of discovery, reporting, adjustment, or payment does not determine whether a changed deductible applies.

**T.13** When a covered loss results from more than one event, we will apply the deductible that applies to the event causing the loss. We will apply the changed deductible only when the applicable event occurs on or after its effective date.

**T.14** When more than one deductible may apply to the same covered loss, the Deductible Change Notice will identify the circumstances in which the changed deductible applies. We will apply the deductible in accordance with the circumstances stated in the notice and applicable law.

**T.15** A Deductible Change Notice may apply to a deductible stated as a fixed amount, a percentage, or another method permitted by applicable law. The notice will describe the method used to determine the changed deductible.

**T.16** We may issue a Deductible Change Notice in connection with a change in your coverage, premium, risk characteristics, or other underwriting information. The notice need not state our underwriting reasons unless applicable law requires us to do so.

**T.17** A change in deductible does not by itself change the scope of coverage provided to you. A change in deductible affects only the portion of a covered loss for which you are responsible.

**T.18** Your payment of premium after the effective date of a Deductible Change Notice does not waive the changed deductible. Our acceptance of premium does not prevent us from applying the changed deductible when it applies.

**T.19** If you do not agree with a changed deductible, you may request information from us concerning the change. We will respond to a request in accordance with applicable law.

**T.20** You may request cancellation of your coverage if you do not wish to continue coverage subject to a changed deductible. Any cancellation will be subject to applicable law and the terms governing cancellation.

**T.21** We may withdraw or replace a Deductible Change Notice before its effective date. A replacement notice will identify the deductible that applies after the replacement notice becomes effective.

**T.22** We may correct a clerical, typographical, or administrative error in a Deductible Change Notice. We will not use a correction to apply a greater deductible retroactively unless applicable law permits us to do so.

**T.23** If a Deductible Change Notice contains conflicting information, we will resolve the conflict in accordance with applicable law. We will provide clarification when required by applicable law.

**T.24** A Deductible Change Notice does not extend coverage beyond the coverage otherwise provided to you. It does not create coverage for a loss that is not covered.

**T.25** We do not cover a loss solely because a Deductible Change Notice was delayed, misdirected, or not received, unless applicable law requires coverage. This provision does not limit any duty we have under applicable law.

**T.26** An insured must cooperate with us in providing information needed to determine the deductible that applies to a covered loss. An insured must not misrepresent facts concerning the date, cause, location, or circumstances of a loss.

**T.27** We may request information concerning a loss before determining whether a changed deductible applies. Our investigation of a loss does not waive any deductible that applies.

**T.28** We will apply the deductible in effect when the covered loss occurs, subject to the terms stated in the Deductible Change Notice. We will determine the amount of the deductible before calculating any payment for the covered loss.

**T.29** The Deductible Change Notice may state that a changed deductible applies only to specified property or specified causes of loss. A deductible not identified as changed remains applicable as otherwise provided.

**T.30** If a Deductible Change Notice changes more than one deductible, each changed deductible will be identified separately. We will apply each changed deductible only as stated in the notice.

**T.31** No representative has authority to change a deductible by an oral statement. A deductible may be changed only by a written notice issued by us or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

**T.32** You may request a copy of a Deductible Change Notice from us. We will provide a copy when required by applicable law.

**T.33** If this provision conflicts with applicable law, we will administer this provision to comply with applicable law. Any provision that cannot be enforced will not affect the enforceability of the remaining provisions.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** "Named Storm Period" means the time during which a "Named Storm" is active as determined by a Weather Authority. The Named Storm Period begins when the Named Storm is identified by a Weather Authority and ends when that authority determines that the Named Storm is no longer active.

**T.2** "Named Storm" means a storm that is assigned a name by a Weather Authority. A Named Storm includes its associated wind, rain, storm surge, flood, debris, and other weather conditions.

**T.3** "Weather Authority" means a governmental agency or other recognized organization that monitors, names, tracks, or reports weather events. We may rely upon information issued by a Weather Authority in determining whether a Named Storm Period applies.

**T.4** These provisions apply to a loss involving "Covered Property" when the loss occurs during a Named Storm Period. "Covered Property" means property for which coverage is otherwise provided.

**T.5** A Named Storm Period does not create coverage for any property, peril, loss, or expense that is not otherwise covered. A Named Storm Period does not increase any limit of liability that otherwise applies.

**T.6** We will determine whether a loss occurred during a Named Storm Period from the available facts and weather information. We may consider the location of the Covered Property, the reported time of loss, and weather conditions affecting that location.

**T.7** An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for us to determine whether a Named Storm Period applies. This information may include photographs, videos, repair records, weather reports, and statements concerning the loss.

**T.8** An insured must protect Covered Property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable measures taken to protect Covered Property do not establish that the loss was caused by a Named Storm.

**T.9** We will not determine that a loss occurred during a Named Storm Period solely because a Named Storm affected the general area. The facts must support that the Named Storm caused or contributed to the loss.

**T.10** When damage begins during a Named Storm Period and continues after the Named Storm Period ends, we will evaluate the damage based upon the causes of loss shown by the facts. We may distinguish damage caused by the Named Storm from damage caused by another event.

**T.11** When damage is discovered after a Named Storm Period ends, the discovery of damage does not by itself establish when the damage occurred. An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the time and cause of the loss.

**T.12** We may inspect Covered Property to determine the cause, extent, and timing of damage. An insured must allow us reasonable access to Covered Property for that purpose.

**T.13** We may obtain weather data, engineering information, repair estimates, and other relevant evidence in evaluating a loss. We may also consider information provided by an insured or another person with knowledge of the loss.

**T.14** A report, notice, or public statement concerning a Named Storm does not establish coverage for a claimed loss. Coverage depends upon the applicable facts, causes of loss, exclusions, conditions, and other provisions governing the claim.

**T.15** An insured must promptly report a loss that may involve a Named Storm Period. Delay in reporting a loss may affect our ability to investigate the claim.

**T.16** An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably possible until we have had an opportunity to inspect it. An insured may remove damaged property when necessary to protect persons or property from further harm.

**T.17** An insured must not discard, alter, or repair evidence of damage in a manner that prevents a reasonable investigation, unless necessary to protect persons or property. An insured must retain records of emergency repairs and expenses.

**T.18** Emergency repairs do not establish that a loss is covered or that a Named Storm caused the damage. We may require reasonable documentation of emergency repairs.

**T.19** We may determine that separate damage resulted from separate causes, even when the damage is discovered at the same time. We may allocate damage when the available evidence reasonably permits allocation.

**T.20** Damage caused by a Named Storm and damage caused by another cause may be evaluated separately. The existence of a Named Storm Period does not prevent us from applying any applicable exclusion or condition.

**T.21** We do not cover loss caused solely by wear, deterioration, neglect, faulty maintenance, or another cause that is not otherwise covered. The occurrence of a Named Storm Period does not change that result.

**T.22** We do not cover loss solely because Covered Property was exposed to weather conditions during a Named Storm Period. An insured must show a direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**T.23** "Direct Physical Loss" means accidental physical damage to Covered Property. Direct Physical Loss does not include a reduction in value without physical damage.

**T.24** A claim involving a Named Storm Period may include more than one cause of loss. We will evaluate each cause of loss under the applicable provisions governing that cause.

**T.25** If more than one Named Storm affects Covered Property, we will evaluate the facts to determine the cause of the claimed damage. The presence of more than one Named Storm does not by itself establish separate covered losses.

**T.26** The "Applicable Deductible" applies to covered loss occurring during a Named Storm Period. The Applicable Deductible is determined from the facts of the loss and the provisions governing the claim.

**T.27** A deductible applicable to a Named Storm Period does not apply to damage that is not caused by the Named Storm, unless another provision makes that deductible applicable. We may allocate covered damage when allocation is reasonably supported by the evidence.

**T.28** Payment for damage caused by a Named Storm does not establish coverage for later damage. Each claim must be evaluated from its own facts and circumstances.

**T.29** Repair, replacement, or cleaning of Covered Property does not establish the cause or timing of a loss. We may consider repair records in determining whether a Named Storm Period applies.

**T.30** An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests for information concerning prior damage, repairs, maintenance, and weather exposure. This duty applies when the information is relevant to our investigation of a claimed loss.

**T.31** Our investigation of a claim involving a Named Storm Period does not waive any condition, exclusion, limitation, or defense available to us. Our payment of any amount does not waive our right to investigate other claimed damage.

**T.32** A disagreement concerning whether a Named Storm Period applies does not relieve an insured of any duty concerning the claim. An insured must continue to protect Covered Property and cooperate with our investigation.

**T.33** These provisions apply only in determining the effect of a Named Storm Period on a claimed loss. They do not expand coverage beyond that otherwise available for Direct Physical Loss.

## T.4 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal

**T.1** This provision applies to cancellation and nonrenewal of this "policy". We will administer cancellation and nonrenewal in accordance with applicable law and the terms of this policy.

**T.2** "Cancellation" means termination of this policy before its expiration. We may cancel this policy only on a basis permitted by applicable law.

**T.3** "Nonrenewal" means our decision not to continue this policy after its expiration. Nonrenewal does not constitute cancellation.

**T.4** We will mail or deliver a "notice of cancellation" for "nonpayment of premium" at least ten days before cancellation takes effect. The notice will state the effective date of cancellation.

**T.5** We will mail or deliver a notice of cancellation for a reason other than nonpayment of premium at least twenty days before cancellation takes effect. The notice will state the reason for cancellation.

**T.6** A notice of cancellation will identify this policy. A notice of cancellation will be in writing.

**T.7** Cancellation will take effect at the time stated in the notice of cancellation. Coverage under this policy ends when cancellation takes effect.

**T.8** We may cancel this policy for nonpayment of premium. Nonpayment of premium includes failure to pay premium when due.

**T.9** We may cancel this policy for material misrepresentation. A material misrepresentation includes a statement or omission that affects our acceptance or continuation of the risk.

**T.10** We may cancel this policy for fraud. Fraud includes conduct by an insured that is fraudulent in connection with this policy.

**T.11** We may cancel this policy when the risk has changed substantially. The change must be relevant to the hazard insured under this policy.

**T.12** We may cancel this policy when an insured has violated a material condition of this policy. The violation must provide a basis for cancellation under applicable law.

**T.13** We may cancel this policy for any other reason permitted by applicable law. We will state that reason in the notice of cancellation.

**T.14** We will not cancel this policy for a reason prohibited by applicable law. A notice of cancellation does not create a basis for cancellation that does not otherwise exist.

**T.15** We will send a notice of cancellation to the "Named Insured" shown in the policy declarations. We may send the notice to the last mailing address known to us.

**T.16** You must notify us of a change in your mailing address. Your notice must be provided in a manner we authorize.

**T.17** Mailing a notice of cancellation to the last mailing address known to us constitutes delivery when permitted by applicable law. We may retain evidence of mailing in our records.

**T.18** Delivery of a notice of cancellation may be made personally when permitted by applicable law. Personal delivery does not alter the effective date stated in the notice.

**T.19** A notice of cancellation will identify the basis on which we are acting. We will provide any further information required by applicable law.

**T.20** Payment received by us after we mail or deliver a notice of cancellation does not automatically continue this policy. We may accept payment and reinstate coverage only as permitted by applicable law.

**T.21** If we reinstate this policy, we will provide notice of the reinstatement. Reinstatement may be subject to conditions we state in writing.

**T.22** Reinstatement does not provide coverage for loss occurring while this policy was canceled unless we state otherwise in writing. Any reinstatement is subject to the terms of this policy.

**T.23** If this policy is canceled, we will return any unearned premium due to you. Our return of premium does not affect the effective date of cancellation.

**T.24** We may calculate earned premium in accordance with applicable law. You remain responsible for earned premium through the effective date of cancellation.

**T.25** You may request cancellation of this policy by providing us written notice. Your request must identify the effective date you request for cancellation.

**T.26** We may require reasonable information to process your request for cancellation. Your request does not affect our right to collect earned premium.

**T.27** Cancellation requested by you is separate from cancellation initiated by us. We will process your request in accordance with applicable law.

**T.28** A request for cancellation does not terminate the rights of a mortgagee or other person entitled to notice. We will provide notice to such person when required by applicable law.

**T.29** We may decide not to renew this policy at its expiration. We will provide a "notice of nonrenewal" when nonrenewal notice is required.

**T.30** We will mail or deliver a notice of nonrenewal at least seventy-five days before this policy expires. The notice will state that this policy will not be renewed.

**T.31** A notice of nonrenewal will identify this policy. The notice will be in writing.

**T.32** We will send a notice of nonrenewal to the Named Insured shown in the policy declarations. We may send the notice to the last mailing address known to us.

**T.33** We will state the reason for nonrenewal when required by applicable law. We may provide information concerning the reason for nonrenewal in the notice.

**T.34** We may elect not to renew this policy for underwriting reasons permitted by applicable law. A nonrenewal decision may reflect information relevant to the insured risk.

**T.24** "Residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. Residence employee includes an employee who performs domestic services for an insured.

**T.26** "Spore" means a reproductive body produced by or arising from fungi. Spore includes any fragment or particulate matter capable of producing or releasing fungi.

**T.27** "Sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unexpected, and unintended from the standpoint of an insured. Sudden and accidental does not include a condition that develops gradually.

**T.28** "Temporary repair" means a reasonable measure taken to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Temporary repair does not include a permanent repair, replacement, or improvement.

**T.29** "Theft" means an unlawful taking of property by a person who lacks your permission to take the property. Theft includes attempted theft and resulting damage caused by the taking or attempted taking.

**T.30** "Vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary to sustain normal occupancy. A residence premises is not vacant solely because an insured is temporarily absent.

**T.31** "Vandalism" means willful and malicious damage to or destruction of property. Vandalism includes damage caused by malicious mischief.

**T.32** "Water" means liquid water in any state or form. Water includes rain, snow, sleet, hail, ice, steam, vapor, runoff, seepage, overflow, and surface water.

**T.33** "Water damage" means direct physical loss caused by water. Water damage includes accidental discharge, overflow, or leakage of water from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system.

**T.34** "Watercraft" means a craft designed to transport persons or property on water. Watercraft includes a vessel, boat, personal watercraft, and similar conveyance.

**T.35** "We," "us," and "our" mean the insurer providing coverage under this endorsement. Our duties apply only to the coverages provided by us.

**T.36** "You" and "your" mean the named insured and the spouse or domestic partner of the named insured if residing in the same household. Your duties apply to each insured seeking coverage.

**T.37** "Intentional loss" means any loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss. Intentional loss includes an act intended to cause damage to covered property.

**T.38** "Reasonable care" means the care that a prudent person would use under similar circumstances to protect property from loss. An insured must use reasonable care to preserve covered property after a loss.
