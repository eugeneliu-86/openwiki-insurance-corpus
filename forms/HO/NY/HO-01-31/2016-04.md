# HO 01 31 New York Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2016-04)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

**T.1** This endorsement applies to the policy where New York law governs. We will apply this endorsement as part of the policy.

**T.2** If this endorsement conflicts with a policy provision, this endorsement controls. The policy provision remains applicable when it does not conflict with this endorsement.

**T.3** We apply the terms of this endorsement according to their plain meaning. We give effect to applicable New York law when that law requires a different result.

**T.4** A term used in this endorsement has the meaning assigned by the policy unless this endorsement provides a different meaning. A term not assigned a meaning is interpreted according to its common and legal usage.

**T.5** The use of a heading, caption, or descriptive phrase does not expand or restrict coverage. The operative policy language determines the coverage provided.

**T.6** We apply conditions, exclusions, and limitations that remain applicable after this endorsement is applied. An insured must comply with applicable policy duties.

**T.7** This endorsement does not create coverage unless its language expressly provides that coverage. We do not cover loss that is otherwise excluded by the policy.

**T.8** This endorsement does not waive a policy condition unless its language expressly states that the condition is waived. A waiver by us must be in writing.

**T.9** An insured must provide information that we reasonably request to determine coverage or the amount of loss. We may rely on information provided by an insured unless we have reason to question its accuracy.

**T.10** An insured must preserve rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for covered loss. We may exercise rights of recovery to the extent permitted by law.

**T.11** The “base deductible minimum” applies only as provided by the policy and this endorsement. We apply the deductible that is required by applicable policy language.

**T.12** The “days to submit a signed, sworn proof of loss” apply as provided by the policy and this endorsement. An insured must submit a signed, sworn proof of loss when required.

**T.13** The “eighty percent condition” applies only where the policy makes that condition applicable. We determine compliance with that condition under the applicable policy language.

**T.14** We determine whether a loss is covered from the facts and circumstances of that loss. Coverage is not determined solely by an insured's description of the loss.

**T.15** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. We may require reasonable access to property and relevant records.

**T.16** We may inspect damaged property before it is repaired, replaced, or discarded when inspection is reasonably necessary. An insured must protect property from further damage after a loss.

**T.17** We may settle a claim only under the terms that apply to the loss. Our investigation or payment does not waive a policy provision.

**T.18** A coverage determination by us applies only to the claim under consideration. It does not establish coverage for a later loss or claim.

**T.19** If applicable law invalidates a provision of this endorsement, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. We will apply the valid provisions consistently with applicable law.

**T.20** An insured may not transfer rights or duties under the policy without our written consent, except as permitted by law. A permitted transfer does not enlarge coverage.

**T.21** We may enforce a policy condition or limitation without affecting our right to enforce another condition or limitation. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive that provision.

**T.22** This endorsement changes the policy only as expressly stated. All policy provisions not changed by this endorsement remain applicable.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** We apply the "windstorm and hail deductible" to covered direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail. This deductible applies only when a covered loss is subject to this provision.

**T.2** The selected windstorm and hail deductible must not be less than one percent. We will determine the deductible in accordance with the applicable loss amount.

**T.3** The selected windstorm and hail deductible must not exceed five percent. A deductible that exceeds this limitation will be adjusted to the maximum permitted deductible.

**T.4** We apply this deductible separately from any other deductible that may apply to the same loss. We will not apply more than one deductible to the same covered loss unless the policy expressly requires otherwise.

**T.5** "Windstorm" means wind, including wind-driven material, that directly causes physical loss to covered property. Windstorm does not include loss caused solely by rain, snow, sleet, or other precipitation without direct wind damage.

**T.6** "Hail" means precipitation in the form of ice that directly causes physical loss to covered property. Hail includes damage caused by hail striking covered property.

**T.7** "Covered property" means property for which coverage is provided under this policy. This deductible does not create coverage for property that is not covered property.

**T.8** "Covered loss" means direct physical loss for which coverage is provided under this policy. We apply the windstorm and hail deductible only after determining that a covered loss has occurred.

**T.9** The deductible applies whether windstorm, hail, or both contribute to a covered loss. The deductible applies regardless of the direction from which wind or hail affects covered property.

**T.10** We apply the deductible to loss caused by windstorm or hail whether the damage occurs suddenly or develops from direct damage caused by windstorm or hail. We do not apply this deductible to loss that is not caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.11** The deductible applies to direct physical loss caused by wind-driven rain only when windstorm first creates an opening in a roof or wall. We will determine whether windstorm created the opening from the facts of the loss.

**T.12** The deductible applies to direct physical loss caused by hail entering an opening created by hail. We will not treat an existing opening as damage caused by hail unless the facts establish that hail created the opening.

**T.13** We apply the deductible to covered damage to a roof caused by windstorm or hail. We also apply the deductible to covered resulting damage caused by the same windstorm or hail.

**T.14** We apply the deductible to covered damage to exterior building materials caused by windstorm or hail. Exterior building materials include materials that protect the structure from weather.

**T.15** We apply the deductible to covered damage to personal property caused by windstorm or hail. The deductible applies even if the personal property is located inside a covered structure.

**T.16** We apply the deductible to covered damage caused by wind-driven debris. We will apply this provision only when windstorm caused the debris to strike covered property.

**T.17** We apply the deductible to covered damage caused by falling objects when windstorm causes the object to fall. We do not apply this provision when the falling object is not caused by windstorm.

**T.18** We apply the deductible to covered damage caused by a tree or branch when windstorm causes the tree or branch to strike covered property. We will not apply the deductible to loss caused solely by the weight of a tree or branch.

**T.19** We apply the deductible to covered damage caused by hail accumulation only when hail directly damages covered property. We do not apply this deductible to loss caused solely by the weight of accumulated precipitation.

**T.20** The deductible applies to a covered loss caused by a named storm when windstorm or hail causes the loss. The deductible does not depend upon the name assigned to the storm.

**T.21** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by tropical storm conditions when windstorm or hail causes the loss. We do not require that a storm be classified in a particular manner for this deductible to apply.

**T.22** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by severe weather conditions when windstorm or hail causes the loss. We will determine the cause of loss from the available information.

**T.23** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by gusting wind. It also applies to covered loss caused by sustained wind.

**T.24** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by wind pressure. It also applies to covered loss caused by windborne objects.

**T.25** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by hail impact. It also applies to covered loss caused by repeated hail impact during the same occurrence.

**T.26** "Occurrence" means an event or a series of related events that causes covered loss. We will determine whether loss arises from the same occurrence based on the cause and circumstances of the loss.

**T.27** We apply a single windstorm and hail deductible to covered loss arising from the same occurrence. We will not separate related damage merely because the damage is discovered at different times.

**T.28** We may apply separate windstorm and hail deductibles when separate occurrences cause separate covered losses. The existence of similar weather conditions does not alone establish that losses arise from the same occurrence.

**T.29** We will determine the deductible amount after applying all applicable coverage limitations. The deductible amount will not be applied to an amount that is not covered.

**T.30** "Deductible amount" means the portion of covered loss for which you are responsible before we make payment. We will subtract the deductible amount from the covered loss in accordance with this provision.

**T.31** We do not pay the deductible amount. You remain responsible for the deductible amount whether repairs are completed or not completed.

**T.32** We will not reduce the deductible amount because a repair is delayed. We will not reduce the deductible amount because the cost of repair changes.

**T.33** We apply the deductible before making payment for a covered loss. We will not make payment for the portion of covered loss that does not exceed the deductible amount.

**T.34** We apply the deductible to the covered loss as adjusted by us. Our adjustment of the loss does not increase coverage or alter any policy limitation.

**T.35** We apply the deductible after determining the amount of covered direct physical loss. We do not apply the deductible to costs that are not covered by this policy.

**T.36** We may inspect damaged property to determine the cause and extent of loss. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property when reasonably requested.

**T.37** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a windstorm or hail loss. Reasonable protective measures do not waive the applicable deductible.

**T.38** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not dispose of damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**T.39** An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the cause of loss. This information may include photographs, repair estimates, invoices, and weather-related information.

**T.40** An insured must cooperate with our investigation of a claimed windstorm or hail loss. Cooperation includes providing access to covered property when reasonably requested.

**T.41** An insured must promptly notify us of a claimed windstorm or hail loss. Notice must include information reasonably available to you concerning the damaged property and the claimed cause of loss.

**T.42** We may use available weather information in determining whether windstorm or hail caused a claimed loss. Weather information does not prevent us from considering other relevant evidence.

**T.43** We may consider the condition of covered property before the claimed loss. Prior condition may be relevant to whether windstorm or hail caused the claimed damage.

**T.44** We may consider evidence of wear, deterioration, defect, or prior damage when adjusting a claimed loss. Such evidence does not by itself establish that windstorm or hail did not cause covered damage.

**T.45** We do not cover loss caused solely by wear, tear, deterioration, or maintenance failure. This deductible does not create coverage for excluded loss.

**T.46** We do not cover loss caused solely by faulty, inadequate, or defective design, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This deductible does not alter that exclusion.

**T.47** We do not cover loss caused solely by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. This deductible does not affect your duty to protect property from further damage.

**T.48** We do not cover loss caused solely by faulty materials. This deductible does not create coverage for damage arising from faulty materials.

**T.49** We do not cover loss caused solely by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We will apply the deductible only to covered windstorm or hail loss.

**T.50** We do not cover loss caused solely by surface water, flood, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. The presence of windstorm does not make excluded water loss covered.

**T.51** We do not cover loss caused solely by water below the surface of the ground. This deductible does not apply to excluded loss.

**T.52** We do not cover loss caused solely by seepage or leakage of water over time. We will apply this deductible only to covered direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.53** We do not cover loss caused solely by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. This deductible does not create coverage for excluded causes of loss.

**T.54** We do not cover loss caused solely by earth movement. Windstorm or hail damage that is otherwise covered remains subject to this deductible.

**T.55** We do not cover loss caused solely by ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, or demolition of property. This deductible does not alter any coverage that may otherwise apply to such loss.

**T.56** We will determine the amount of covered loss under the applicable loss settlement provisions. The windstorm and hail deductible applies to the covered amount determined under those provisions.

**T.57** "Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, subject to applicable policy terms. When actual cash value applies, we will apply the deductible to the covered loss amount.

**T.58** "Replacement cost" means the cost to replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, subject to applicable policy terms. When replacement cost applies, we will apply the deductible to the covered loss amount.

**T.59** We will not increase the amount payable because the windstorm and hail deductible applies. We will not reduce any deductible required by this provision unless we agree in writing.

**T.60** Payment by us for a covered windstorm or hail loss does not waive this deductible for any later loss. The deductible remains applicable to each covered loss to which this provision applies.

**T.61** We may request documentation supporting the claimed cost to repair or replace damaged property. An insured must provide documentation reasonably available to the insured.

**T.62** We may request that damaged property be preserved until our investigation is complete. An insured must comply with that request when reasonably possible.

**T.63** An insured must not make a voluntary payment for a claimed loss without our consent, except for reasonable measures to protect property from further damage. A voluntary payment does not change the deductible that applies.

**T.64** An insured must not assume an obligation or incur expense on our behalf without our consent. This duty does not prevent reasonable emergency measures to protect covered property.

**T.65** We may settle a covered windstorm or hail loss with you or with another person entitled to payment. Any settlement remains subject to the applicable deductible.

**T.66** We may make payment to a mortgagee or other person with a legal interest in covered property when required by applicable policy terms. Such payment does not eliminate your responsibility for the deductible amount.

**T.67** The deductible applies whether damage affects all or only part of covered property. The extent of damage does not alter the deductible that applies.

**T.68** The deductible applies whether covered property is occupied or unoccupied at the time of loss. Vacancy, occupancy, or use of covered property does not change this provision unless the policy provides otherwise.

**T.69** The deductible applies whether windstorm or hail damage is visible immediately or is discovered later. An insured must still comply with all duties after loss.

**T.70** We will apply this deductible as required by New York bulletinas required by New York bulletin (see New York Bulletin DFS-2016-02 — Nonrenewal and Cancellation Notice Requirements, B.2 Requirements). This provision will be administered in accordance with applicable law.

**T.71** If any portion of this provision is determined to be unenforceable, the remaining portions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. We will administer the remaining portions consistently with applicable law.

**T.72** We will not apply this deductible in a manner that is prohibited by law. Any required modification will apply only to the extent necessary to comply with law.

**T.73** An insured must provide truthful information concerning a claimed windstorm or hail loss. Misrepresentation, concealment, or fraud may affect coverage as provided by this policy.

**T.74** This provision applies to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail during the time this policy is in force. It does not apply to loss occurring before coverage takes effect or after coverage ends.

**T.75** All other terms and conditions of this policy remain unchanged. This provision controls when it conflicts with any other policy provision concerning the windstorm and hail deductible.

## T.2 — Notice of Deductible Change

**T.1** We may change the "deductible" that applies to your "policy" only as permitted by applicable law. Any change will apply only as stated in our notice to you.

**T.2** We will provide notice of a deductible change in writing. The notice will identify the deductible that will apply after the change.

**T.3** Our notice will state the coverage to which the changed deductible applies. Our notice will also state the circumstances under which the changed deductible applies.

**T.4** A deductible change may apply to a "loss" occurring after the effective date shown in our notice. A deductible change will not apply to a loss occurring before that effective date.

**T.5** We may provide notice of a deductible change with a renewal offer. Acceptance of the renewal offer constitutes acceptance of the changed deductible, subject to applicable law.

**T.6** We may provide notice of a deductible change with an offer to continue coverage. Your continued payment of premium after the change becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the changed deductible, subject to applicable law.

**T.7** We may provide notice of a deductible change following a change in the information used to underwrite your policy. The notice will describe the change that affects the deductible.

**T.8** We may provide notice of a deductible change following a change in the use, occupancy, condition, or protection of covered property. The changed deductible will apply only as stated in our notice.

**T.9** We may provide notice of a deductible change following a change in the location of covered property. The notice will identify the location to which the changed deductible applies.

**T.10** We may provide notice of a deductible change when we revise the deductible options available for your policy. We are not required to offer every deductible option that may otherwise be available for similar property.

**T.11** If we offer you a choice of deductibles, you must select an available deductible in the manner we specify. If you do not make a selection, we may apply the deductible stated in our notice, subject to applicable law.

**T.12** You must review each notice of deductible change upon receipt. You must notify us promptly if the notice does not accurately describe the property, coverage, or deductible to which it applies.

**T.13** You must provide us with accurate information concerning any matter that may affect the deductible applicable to your policy. We may revise a deductible when information we relied upon is incomplete, inaccurate, or no longer current.

**T.14** A deductible shown in our notice replaces the deductible previously applicable to the same coverage only when the notice states that it does so. Any deductible not changed by our notice remains applicable.

**T.15** A deductible change does not change the coverage provided by your policy except as specifically stated in our notice. A deductible change does not create coverage where coverage otherwise does not exist.

**T.16** A deductible change does not increase the limit of liability applicable to covered property or covered loss. The deductible applies in addition to all other policy terms and conditions that remain applicable.

**T.17** If more than one deductible could apply to the same loss, we will apply the deductible required by your policy and our notice. We will not apply a deductible that is inconsistent with applicable law.

**T.18** If our notice changes a deductible for a particular cause of loss, the changed deductible applies only to loss caused by that cause of loss. All other deductibles remain subject to the terms of your policy.

**T.19** If our notice changes a deductible for a particular property, the changed deductible applies only to that property. A deductible applicable to other property is not changed unless our notice so states.

**T.20** We may correct a clerical error in a notice of deductible change. We will provide corrected notice if the correction affects the deductible applicable to your policy.

**T.21** A notice of deductible change is effective only when issued by us or our authorized representative. Oral statements do not change a deductible.

**T.22** You must pay any premium required as a result of a deductible change when due. Failure to pay required premium may affect continuation of your policy as permitted by applicable law.

**T.23** We do not cover any portion of a loss that is subject to an applicable deductible. You remain responsible for the deductible applicable to each covered loss.

**T.24** Nothing in this provision limits any right you may have under applicable law concerning notice of a deductible change. We will administer deductible changes in accordance with applicable law.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** “Named Storm Period” means the time during which a “Named Storm” produces conditions that affect property at an insured location. A Named Storm is a weather system that has been named or designated by an official meteorological authority.

**T.2** A Named Storm Period begins when conditions associated with a Named Storm affect an insured location. A Named Storm Period ends when those conditions no longer affect that insured location.

**T.3** We will determine whether a Named Storm Period applies based on reliable meteorological information and the facts of the reported loss. Our determination may consider the location, movement, intensity, and effects of the Named Storm.

**T.4** A formal declaration by a governmental authority is not required for us to determine that a Named Storm Period applies. The absence of a formal declaration does not prevent us from making that determination.

**T.5** We may revise a Named Storm Period determination when additional reliable information becomes available. A revised determination will apply to the reported loss based on the facts established during our investigation.

**T.6** The existence of a Named Storm Period does not establish that a Named Storm caused a loss. We will determine the cause of loss from the facts applicable to the damaged property.

**T.7** The time at which damage is discovered does not by itself establish that a Named Storm caused the damage. We may consider evidence showing when the damage occurred and how it developed.

**T.8** A loss may be caused by a Named Storm even though another cause also contributed to the loss. We will determine coverage based on the applicable facts and the cause of loss.

**T.9** Damage that existed before a Named Storm Period began is not considered damage caused by the Named Storm solely because the damage is discovered during the Named Storm Period. An insured must provide available information concerning the condition of damaged property before the loss.

**T.10** Any deductible that applies to a covered loss during a Named Storm Period will be applied in accordance with its applicable terms. Our determination that a Named Storm Period exists does not by itself determine the deductible applicable to a loss.

**T.11** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage when storm conditions permit. An insured must use reasonable care when taking those steps.

**T.12** An insured must make reasonable emergency repairs necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must keep records of the materials, services, and expenses associated with those repairs.

**T.13** An insured must not discard damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property. An insured must preserve photographs, records, and other available evidence of the damage.

**T.14** An insured must notify us of a loss as soon as practicable after becoming aware of the loss. Notice must include available information concerning the damaged property and the storm conditions affecting it.

**T.15** An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation of a reported loss. An insured must provide requested information that is reasonably necessary to determine the cause and extent of damage.

**T.16** An insured must allow us reasonable access to damaged property for inspection, measurement, photography, and evaluation. An insured must take reasonable steps to make damaged property available for inspection when access is restricted by storm conditions.

**T.17** An insured must provide available records showing ownership, age, condition, repair, replacement, and maintenance of damaged property when we reasonably request them. An insured must preserve records that may assist in determining the cause or amount of loss.

**T.18** An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests for statements concerning the loss. An insured must provide information known to the insured regarding weather conditions, protective measures, and damage observed at the insured location.

**T.19** An insured must report any known condition that may have contributed to the loss. An insured must not misrepresent the cause, date, extent, or condition of damaged property.

**T.20** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized persons from entering damaged property when doing so can be accomplished safely. An insured must promptly report known additional damage that occurs after the reported loss.

**T.21** Compliance with an order or direction of a civil authority does not by itself establish that a Named Storm caused a loss. We may consider that order or direction as part of our investigation.

**T.22** Evacuation, restricted access, or interruption of utilities does not by itself establish that a Named Storm caused damage to property. We will evaluate the available evidence concerning the cause of the reported damage.

**T.23** Weather reports, storm warnings, and public communications may be considered in determining whether a Named Storm Period applies. Those materials do not by themselves establish coverage for a reported loss.

**T.24** Our request for information, inspection of property, or discussion of a reported loss does not waive any applicable policy provision. We may continue to investigate a loss after making an initial Named Storm Period determination.

**T.25** Payment for emergency measures does not establish that we will pay for all reported damage. We will determine the amount payable, if any, after completing our investigation.

**T.26** A repair, replacement, or cleanup undertaken by an insured does not establish the cause or extent of a loss. An insured must provide available documentation supporting the work performed and the expenses incurred.

**T.27** We may evaluate separate areas of damaged property independently when the evidence indicates that the areas sustained damage from different causes. We may also evaluate damage occurring at different times based on the applicable facts.

**T.28** We may rely on information from meteorological sources, contractors, engineers, inspectors, and other qualified persons when investigating a reported loss. We are not required to accept an opinion that is not supported by reliable information.

**T.29** An insured must assist us in obtaining information from persons who may have knowledge of the loss, when reasonably requested. An insured must provide available contact information for those persons.

**T.30** An insured must not interfere with reasonable efforts to inspect, document, protect, or evaluate damaged property. An insured must preserve access to the property when reasonably possible.

**T.31** This provision does not create coverage for loss that is not otherwise covered. This provision does not change any exclusion, limitation, condition, or deductible that otherwise applies.

**T.32** Duties applicable after a loss remain applicable during a Named Storm Period. An insured must comply with those duties to the extent reasonably possible under the circumstances.

**T.33** We will make Named Storm Period determinations in good faith based on the information available to us. We may request additional information when the available information is insufficient to make that determination.

## T.4 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal

**T.1** For purposes of this provision, "cancellation" means termination of this policy before its expiration. Cancellation is effective only when we give notice as required by this provision.

**T.2** "Nonrenewal" means our decision not to continue this policy at its expiration. Nonrenewal does not terminate coverage before the expiration of the policy period.

**T.3** A "notice" is a written communication that states our action and the effective date of that action. We may mail or deliver a notice as permitted by law.

**T.4** We may cancel this policy for nonpayment of premium by mailing or delivering notice to you at least fifteen days before the effective date of cancellation. The notice will state the amount due and the action required to avoid cancellation.

**T.5** We may cancel this policy for a reason other than nonpayment of premium by mailing or delivering notice to you at least twenty days before the effective date of cancellation. The notice will state the reason for cancellation.

**T.6** If we decide not to renew this policy, we will mail or deliver notice to you at least sixty days before the expiration of the policy period. The notice will state that we do not intend to renew the policy.

**T.7** We may cancel this policy if you do not pay premium when due. We may also cancel this policy if a premium payment is returned or otherwise unavailable for collection.

**T.8** We may cancel this policy if you obtained it through fraud or material misrepresentation. We may rely on fraud or material misrepresentation made by you or made on your behalf.

**T.9** We may cancel this policy if there has been a material change in the risk covered by this policy. A material change includes a change that increases the likelihood or severity of loss.

**T.10** We may cancel this policy if an insured has willfully or recklessly acted in a manner that substantially increases the hazard insured against. We may cancel only as permitted by applicable law.

**T.11** We may cancel this policy if the premises become vacant, unoccupied, or otherwise used in a manner that materially changes the risk. We may also cancel if the premises are no longer eligible for the coverage provided by this policy.

**T.12** We may cancel this policy if required by law. We may also cancel this policy when continuation of coverage would violate law or an order of a governmental authority.

**T.13** A notice of cancellation will identify this policy and the insured premises. The notice will state the effective date of cancellation.

**T.14** A notice of cancellation for nonpayment of premium will state the premium that remains unpaid. The notice may state the manner in which payment must be made.

**T.15** Payment received after we mail or deliver a notice of cancellation does not reinstate this policy unless we agree in writing. Acceptance of payment does not waive the cancellation unless we state otherwise in writing.

**T.16** We may accept a payment without changing the effective date stated in a notice of cancellation. We may return a payment that is not sufficient to prevent cancellation.

**T.17** You must provide us with your current mailing address. You must promptly notify us of any change in your mailing address.

**T.18** We may mail notice to the mailing address shown in our records. Notice mailed to that address is sufficient if mailing is authorized by law.

**T.19** We may deliver notice to you personally or to a person authorized to receive notice for you. Delivery to an authorized representative is delivery to you.

**T.20** Proof of mailing will be sufficient proof that notice was given when mailing is authorized by law. We are not required to obtain your signature to establish mailing.

**T.21** If more than one named insured is shown in the policy declarations, we may send notice to the mailing address shown for a named insured. Notice sent as permitted by law is effective for all insureds.

**T.22** An insured must cooperate with us in providing information needed to determine whether cancellation is permitted. An insured must not conceal or misrepresent facts relating to cancellation.

**T.23** Cancellation does not affect a claim that occurred before the effective date of cancellation. We will determine coverage for that claim under the policy terms that applied when the loss occurred.

**T.24** Cancellation does not create coverage for loss occurring after the effective date of cancellation. We do not cover loss occurring after cancellation becomes effective.

**T.25** If we cancel this policy, we will return any unearned premium as required by law. Return of unearned premium does not affect the effective date of cancellation.

**T.26** We may deduct from returned premium any amount lawfully owed to us. We will calculate any returned premium according to applicable law and the terms of this policy.

**T.27** You may cancel this policy by giving us written notice. Your cancellation will be effective as stated in your notice or as otherwise agreed by us.

**T.28** If you cancel this policy, we will return any unearned premium as required by law. We may retain premium earned through the effective date of cancellation.

**T.29** A request by you to cancel this policy does not affect our right to cancel the policy. Our cancellation rights remain subject to the notice requirements of this provision.

**T.30** We may withdraw a notice of cancellation before its effective date. Withdrawal must be made by written notice from us.

**T.31** If we withdraw a notice of cancellation, this policy will remain in effect unless it is otherwise cancelled. Withdrawal of a notice does not waive any future right of cancellation.

**T.32** We may issue a notice of cancellation after a prior notice has been withdrawn. Any later notice must comply with the requirements that apply to that cancellation.

**T.33** A notice of nonrenewal will identify this policy and the insured premises. The notice will state the date on which coverage will end.

**T.34** We may state in a notice of nonrenewal the reason we do not intend to renew this policy. A stated reason does not limit our rights under applicable law.

**T.35** We may elect to offer renewal on terms that differ from the expiring policy. An offer of renewal may include changes in premium, coverage, conditions, or deductibles.

**T.36** A renewal offer is not a cancellation or nonrenewal if we offer continuation of coverage in accordance with applicable law. You must accept the renewal offer and pay any required premium to continue coverage.

**T.37** If you do not accept a renewal offer, coverage will end at the expiration of the policy period. We are not required to continue coverage after expiration unless required by law.

**T.38** We may decline to renew this policy if the insured premises no longer meet our underwriting requirements. We may also decline to renew if information material to the risk is unavailable or unreliable.

## T.6 — Seacoast Territories

**T.1** "Seacoast Territory" means an area exposed to coastal conditions, including an area adjoining or materially affected by tidal waters, coastal inlets, bays, or similar bodies of water. We determine whether a location is in a Seacoast Territory from reliable geographic information available to us.

**T.2** This provision applies to the "residence premises" and any "insured location" situated in a Seacoast Territory. It also applies to covered property while that property is at an insured location in a Seacoast Territory.

**T.3** "Coastal waters" means tidal waters and bodies of water subject to tidal influence. Coastal waters include adjoining beaches, dunes, marshes, and other coastal landforms.

**T.4** "Windstorm" means wind, including wind associated with a weather disturbance. "Hail" means frozen precipitation falling from the atmosphere.

**T.5** "Hurricane" means a tropical weather system identified as a hurricane by an appropriate governmental weather authority. A hurricane includes wind, rain, and other conditions directly associated with that system.

**T.6** "Storm surge" means an abnormal rise of water caused by wind, atmospheric pressure, or both. Storm surge includes water driven onto land by a hurricane or other windstorm.

**T.7** "Tropical cyclone" means a weather system identified by an appropriate governmental weather authority as tropical in nature. A tropical cyclone includes a hurricane and any related weather condition.

**T.8** We may inspect the residence premises and any insured location in a Seacoast Territory. An inspection by us does not constitute a warranty that the property is safe, sound, or adequately protected.

**T.9** An insured must maintain the property in a condition that reasonably protects it from wind, rain, and coastal water intrusion. An insured must repair known damage that could materially increase the likelihood or extent of a loss.

**T.10** An insured must take reasonable measures to secure exterior doors, windows, roof coverings, and other exterior building components when severe weather threatens the property. Those measures must be appropriate to the property and the anticipated conditions.

**T.11** An insured must maintain drainage systems, roof drains, gutters, and downspouts so that water can flow away from the dwelling. An insured must remove debris that materially obstructs those systems.

**T.12** An insured must maintain any shutters, storm panels, impact protection, or similar protective devices in operable condition. An insured must use those devices when their use is reasonably necessary to protect covered property.

**T.13** An insured must secure outdoor furniture, equipment, building materials, and other personal property that may be displaced by wind or water. An insured must store such property in a manner that reduces the risk of damage to covered property.

**T.14** An insured must maintain trees, limbs, and vegetation so that they do not create a known material risk of damage to the dwelling or other covered structures. This duty applies when the condition is apparent to an insured.

**T.15** An insured must promptly report to us any material change in the condition, use, occupancy, or exposure of property in a Seacoast Territory. A material change includes a condition that increases the susceptibility of the property to wind, water, or erosion damage.

**T.16** An insured must permit us to obtain information concerning the location, elevation, construction, occupancy, and condition of property in a Seacoast Territory. An insured must provide information that is reasonably available to the insured.

**T.17** We may require reasonable evidence that protective measures have been completed or maintained. Such evidence may include photographs, records, reports, or other information relevant to the property.

**T.18** We may require an insured to make reasonable repairs or take reasonable protective action when we determine that a condition materially increases the risk of loss. An insured must comply with that requirement as a condition of continuing coverage for the affected property.

**T.19** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an insured to take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. This exclusion applies only to the extent the failure increases the loss.

**T.20** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, settling, erosion, subsidence, or collapse of land resulting from coastal action or tidal action. This exclusion applies whether the condition is gradual, sudden, natural, or caused by human activity.

**T.21** We do not cover loss caused by the action of waves, tidal water, storm surge, overflow of coastal waters, or waterborne material. This exclusion applies whether or not wind contributes to the loss.

**T.22** We do not cover loss caused by the failure, movement, or deterioration of seawalls, bulkheads, retaining walls, piers, docks, groins, jetties, or similar structures. This exclusion applies to damage to the structure itself and to resulting damage.

**T.23** We do not cover loss caused by corrosion, rust, rot, mold, decay, or deterioration resulting from salt air, salt water, humidity, or coastal conditions. This exclusion applies even if the condition contributes to other damage.

**T.24** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, inherent vice, latent defect, or inadequate maintenance. This exclusion applies to property exposed to wind, rain, salt air, or coastal waters.

**T.25** We do not cover loss to land, beaches, dunes, lawns, landscaping, trees, shrubs, or other natural vegetation caused by wind, water, wave action, or erosion. We also do not cover the cost to stabilize, restore, or replace such property.

**T.26** We do not cover the cost to comply with an ordinance, law, regulation, or governmental requirement relating to coastal construction, elevation, setback, erosion control, or shoreline protection. This exclusion applies unless coverage for that cost is expressly provided by this policy.

**T.27** We do not cover the cost to prevent, mitigate, or respond to erosion, flooding, wave action, storm surge, or tidal water unless the cost is incurred to protect covered property from further covered loss. Any such action must be reasonable under the circumstances.

**T.28** We may require an insured to preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, except when disposal is necessary to protect persons or property.

**T.29** An insured must cooperate with us in investigating a loss involving property in a Seacoast Territory. Cooperation includes providing access to the property and assisting us in identifying the cause and extent of damage.

**T.30** An insured must protect covered property from further damage following wind, rain, or coastal water conditions. Reasonable emergency repairs may be made when necessary to protect the property.

**T.31** An insured must keep records of emergency repairs and preserve receipts for reasonable expenses incurred. An insured must provide those records and receipts to us upon request.

**T.32** We may adjust a loss involving property in a Seacoast Territory based on the direct physical loss or damage caused by a covered peril. We do not pay for damage that is excluded or otherwise not covered under this policy.

**T.33** The duties and exclusions in this provision apply whether a loss occurs during a hurricane, tropical cyclone, windstorm, or other coastal weather event. They apply to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**T.56** An insured must notify us if any payment is available from another source for the same loss or expense. We may coordinate payment with another person or organization having responsibility for that loss or expense.

**T.57** We do not cover an expense incurred solely to improve property beyond its condition immediately before the loss. We may pay for necessary work that restores covered property in accordance with these provisions.

**T.58** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's intentional act when the act is committed with the intent to cause loss. This provision applies regardless of whether another insured seeks coverage for the same loss.

**T.59** We do not cover fraudulent claims or fraudulent statements made in connection with a claim. We may deny coverage to an insured responsible for the fraud.

**T.60** An insured must promptly notify us of any information suggesting that a claim may involve fraud or criminal conduct. We may refer that information to appropriate authorities.

**T.61** We may require an insured to provide a sworn proof of loss. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably requested by us concerning the loss.

**T.62** An insured must keep records of communications and expenses relating to a claim when reasonably possible. We may request copies of those records.

**T.63** We may waive a condition only by a written statement issued by us. Our investigation, inspection, negotiation, or payment does not waive a condition.

**T.64** An insured must comply with these provisions before bringing an action against us, unless compliance is excused by law. We may assert any defense available to us under applicable law.

**T.65** If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. We apply any unenforceable provision to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## T.9 — Definitions Amended

**T.1** The definitions in this section replace any conflicting definitions applicable to this policy. All other policy terms retain their ordinary meaning unless defined differently in this section.

**T.2** "Actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss, including appropriate deductions for depreciation and obsolescence. Actual cash value does not include any sentimental, historic, or special value.

**T.3** "Bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. Bodily injury includes required care, loss of services, and death resulting from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**T.4** "Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or activity engaged in for economic gain. Business includes any activity conducted on a regular or occasional basis for compensation.

**T.5** "Business day" means a day on which financial institutions generally conduct business in the jurisdiction where the property is located. Business day does not include a day on which those institutions are generally closed.

**T.6** "Business property" means property used in connection with a business. Business property includes property held for sale, lease, rental, or delivery.

**T.7** "Direct physical loss" means an accidental and distinct physical change to covered property. Direct physical loss includes physical destruction, damage, or disappearance of covered property.

**T.8** "Employee" means a person who performs duties for compensation under your direction and control. Employee includes a leased worker but does not include a temporary worker.

**T.9** "Family member" means a person related to you by blood, marriage, domestic partnership, adoption, or placement for adoption. Family member includes a ward or foster child in your care.

**T.10** "Fungi" means any type or form of fungus, including mold, mildew, and yeast. Fungi includes spores, mycotoxins, scents, and byproducts produced or released by fungi.

**T.11** "Insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives. Insured also means persons under your care who are residents of your household.

**T.12** "Insured location" means the residence premises. Insured location also includes property used by an insured in connection with the residence premises, subject to the policy terms.

**T.13** "Insured premises" means the dwelling, other structures, and land shown or described as the residence premises. Insured premises includes approaches and private ways used with the residence premises.

**T.14** "Landlord" means a person or organization that owns property occupied by a tenant under a rental agreement. Landlord includes a managing agent acting within the scope of assigned duties.

**T.15** "Loss" means accidental direct physical loss to property. Loss does not include diminution in value that is not caused by physical damage.

**T.16** "Motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle, trailer, or semi-trailer designed for travel on public roads. Motor vehicle includes machinery or equipment attached to or carried on such vehicle.

**T.17** "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. Occurrence includes resulting bodily injury or property damage during the policy period.

**T.18** "Other structure" means a structure separated from the dwelling by clear space or connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. Other structure includes a structure used solely as a private garage.

**T.19** "Personal property" means tangible property owned or used by an insured. Personal property includes property worn or carried by an insured.

**T.20** "Pollutants" means solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or chemical irritants or contaminants. Pollutants include smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, waste, and contaminated material.

**T.21** "Property damage" means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property. Property damage also means loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.

**T.22** "Residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are performed in connection with the maintenance or use of the residence premises. Residence employee includes a person who performs similar duties elsewhere for an insured.

**T.23** "Residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and that is shown or described in the policy. Residence premises includes the land and structures used principally as your private residence.

**T.24** "Residential property" means property used or intended for use as a private residence. Residential property does not include property used principally for business purposes.

**T.28** "Temporary worker" means a person furnished to you to substitute for a permanent employee or to meet seasonal or short-term workload conditions. Temporary worker does not include a leased worker.

**T.32** "Water" means liquid water in any form, including rain, snow, sleet, ice, condensation, and steam. Water includes water mixed with sewage or other substances.
