# HO 01 05 Colorado Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2022-10)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

**T.1** This "Endorsement" changes the terms governing insurance under the contract to which it is attached. We apply this Endorsement only to matters within its stated scope.

**T.2** A term changed by this Endorsement replaces any conflicting term otherwise applicable to the same matter. A term not changed by this Endorsement remains applicable.

**T.3** We do not broaden coverage unless this Endorsement expressly provides broader coverage. We do not reduce coverage unless this Endorsement expressly provides reduced coverage.

**T.4** An exclusion changed by this Endorsement applies as changed. An exclusion not changed by this Endorsement remains applicable.

**T.5** A condition changed by this Endorsement applies as changed. A condition not changed by this Endorsement remains applicable.

**T.6** A duty imposed on an insured by this Endorsement is in addition to any duty that remains applicable. An insured must comply with each applicable duty.

**T.7** A limitation changed by this Endorsement applies only to the coverage or circumstance addressed by that change. We do not apply a changed limitation beyond its stated scope.

**T.8** A deductible term changed by this Endorsement applies only as expressly stated. The "base deductible minimum" applies only to deductible terms changed by this Endorsement.

**T.9** A requirement concerning a claim applies only as expressly stated in this Endorsement. An insured must satisfy each applicable claim requirement.

**T.10** The requirement identified as "days to submit a signed, sworn proof of loss" governs the submission of a signed, sworn proof of loss when applicable. An insured must submit a signed, sworn proof of loss when required.

**T.11** The "eighty percent condition" applies only as provided by the terms governing that condition. An insured must satisfy the eighty percent condition when it applies.

**T.12** We construe a changed term according to its plain meaning within this Endorsement. We construe an unchanged term according to the meaning otherwise applicable to that term.

**T.13** If a changed term can reasonably be read with an unchanged term, we give effect to both terms. If the terms cannot reasonably be read together, the changed term controls.

**T.14** A provision of this Endorsement applies only to property, persons, locations, losses, or claims within its stated scope. We do not apply a provision to a matter outside that scope.

**T.15** The application of this Endorsement is subject to applicable Colorado law. If applicable Colorado law requires a different result, we apply that law.

**T.16** We do not waive a term by applying this Endorsement to a particular loss or claim. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

**T.17** An insured must provide information reasonably requested by us to determine whether this Endorsement applies. We may rely on information available to us in making that determination.

**T.18** We apply this Endorsement to a loss or claim only when its terms apply to that loss or claim. We do not apply a term by implication when the term does not state that it applies.

**T.19** A conflict between provisions within this Endorsement is resolved by giving effect to the provision that more specifically addresses the matter. We apply a general provision only when a more specific provision does not govern.

**T.20** If part of this Endorsement is unenforceable, we apply the remaining part to the extent permitted by law. The unenforceable part does not affect the enforceability of another part.

**T.21** An insured may not rely on a provision outside the scope of this Endorsement to change a term addressed by this Endorsement. We do not apply an inconsistent interpretation of a changed term.

**T.22** This Endorsement remains subject to its stated terms and applicable law. We apply it according to its scope and precedence provisions.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** The "windstorm and hail deductible" must not be less than one percent. We will apply the deductible only to a covered windstorm or hail loss.

**T.2** The windstorm and hail deductible must not exceed five percent. We will determine the applicable deductible from the terms governing the covered loss.

**T.3** We will administer the windstorm and hail deductible as required by Colorado bulletinas required by Colorado bulletin (see Colorado Bulletin DOI-2019-05 — Hail Deductibles, B.2 Requirements). An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine the applicable deductible.

**T.4** A "windstorm or hail loss" means direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail. The windstorm and hail deductible applies when windstorm or hail causes covered direct physical loss.

**T.5** We will subtract the applicable deductible from the amount of covered loss. We will not pay the portion of covered loss that is within the applicable deductible.

**T.6** The deductible applies whether windstorm or hail is the sole cause of loss or acts with another cause of loss. We will apply the deductible only when windstorm or hail caused covered direct physical loss.

**T.7** The deductible applies to covered property damaged by windstorm or hail. It does not apply to property for which windstorm or hail coverage is unavailable.

**T.8** We will determine the deductible before applying any payment limitation that applies to the covered loss. We will calculate payment after subtracting the applicable deductible.

**T.9** An insured must retain responsibility for the deductible amount. Payment by us does not relieve an insured of that responsibility.

**T.10** We will apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss before considering any voluntary payment by another person. A payment from another source does not reduce the deductible owed by an insured.

**T.11** The deductible applies to physical damage caused by windstorm. It also applies to physical damage caused by hail.

**T.12** We will apply the deductible to covered damage to structures. We will also apply the deductible to covered damage to personal property.

**T.13** The deductible applies to covered loss at the insured location. It also applies to covered loss away from the insured location when coverage applies.

**T.14** We will apply the deductible to loss involving more than one item of covered property. We will not apply a separate deductible merely because several items were damaged.

**T.15** When related damage results from the same windstorm or hail event, we will treat the related damage as part of the same loss. We will apply the deductible to that loss.

**T.16** We will evaluate the facts surrounding reported damage to determine whether the damage resulted from windstorm or hail. An insured must cooperate with that evaluation.

**T.17** An insured must protect damaged property from further loss when reasonably possible. Reasonable protective measures do not eliminate the applicable deductible.

**T.18** We will not cover the portion of a windstorm or hail loss that does not exceed the deductible. This exclusion applies even when the damage is covered.

**T.19** We will not cover a deductible amount as an additional payment for the same loss. The deductible remains the responsibility of an insured.

**T.20** The deductible applies to emergency measures taken because of covered windstorm or hail damage. We will determine coverage for those measures under the terms applicable to the loss.

**T.21** We will apply the deductible once to covered damage arising from the same occurrence. Damage discovered at different times does not create a separate deductible when it arose from the same occurrence.

**T.22** An "occurrence" means an event causing direct physical loss. We will determine whether damage arose from the same occurrence based on the facts of the loss.

**T.23** Windstorm and hail damage may arise from the same occurrence. We will not treat windstorm damage and hail damage as separate occurrences solely because both causes were present.

**T.24** Separate weather events may constitute separate occurrences. We will apply the deductible separately to each covered loss arising from a separate occurrence.

**T.25** An insured must promptly report a windstorm or hail loss. Delay in reporting does not change the deductible that otherwise applies.

**T.26** An insured must show us damaged property when reasonably requested. We may inspect the property to evaluate the cause and amount of loss.

**T.27** An insured must provide records, photographs, estimates, and other information reasonably needed to evaluate a claim. Such information does not alter the applicable deductible.

**T.28** We will determine whether windstorm or hail caused covered damage from the available evidence. We may consider weather information, inspections, and other reliable information.

**T.29** We will apply the deductible to covered damage even if other damage is not covered. We will not apply the deductible to damage for which no coverage exists.

**T.30** A "covered loss" means loss for which coverage applies under the policy terms. The deductible applies only to the covered portion of a windstorm or hail loss.

**T.31** We will separate covered damage from uncovered damage when the available information permits. The deductible applies after that determination.

**T.32** If covered and uncovered damage cannot reasonably be separated, we will determine the covered amount from the evidence available. The deductible applies to the covered amount we determine.

**T.33** We will not increase the deductible because an insured chooses a contractor, vendor, or repair method. Repair costs remain subject to the applicable deductible.

**T.34** We will not reduce the deductible because repairs cost more than expected. The deductible applies without regard to changes in repair pricing.

**T.35** The deductible applies before we issue payment for covered windstorm or hail damage. An insured must satisfy the deductible through the retained portion of the loss.

**T.36** We may issue payment directly to an insured or to another person entitled to payment. Any such payment remains subject to the deductible.

**T.37** A mortgagee or other interested person does not eliminate the deductible. We will determine payment interests after applying the deductible.

**T.38** The deductible applies to loss involving property owned by more than one insured. We will not divide the deductible among insureds for purposes of determining our payment.

**T.39** The deductible applies when an insured has an interest in damaged covered property. A change in ownership after loss does not change the deductible.

**T.40** We will apply the deductible to covered loss involving replacement, repair, or restoration of damaged property. The method used to value the loss does not change the deductible.

**T.41** We will apply the deductible before considering depreciation when the applicable valuation terms require depreciation. We will calculate any remaining payment under the applicable valuation terms.

**T.42** We will apply the deductible before considering any supplemental payment for the same covered loss. Supplemental payment remains subject to all applicable policy terms.

**T.43** We will not apply the deductible to amounts we do not owe under the policy. The deductible does not create coverage for excluded or otherwise uninsured loss.

**T.44** We will not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail when coverage is excluded by another applicable term. The deductible does not restore excluded coverage.

**T.45** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent material information concerning a windstorm or hail loss. A misrepresentation may affect coverage as permitted by applicable law.

**T.46** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve evidence of the cause and extent of damage. We may rely on available evidence when determining the deductible.

**T.47** We may request access to the insured location to inspect reported damage. An insured must provide reasonable access when requested.

**T.48** We may request records showing repairs, prior damage, or property condition. An insured must provide records within the insured's possession or control when reasonably requested.

**T.49** We will not require an insured to complete repairs before evaluating the deductible. We may require sufficient information to determine the covered amount of loss.

**T.50** Repair of damaged property does not waive our right to investigate the loss. We may apply the deductible after completing our investigation.

**T.51** We will evaluate reported damage without regard to whether an insured intends to repair or replace damaged property. The deductible applies to covered loss regardless of that decision.

**T.52** The deductible applies to a covered loss settled on an actual cash value basis. The deductible also applies to a covered loss settled on another basis authorized by the policy.

**T.53** We will determine the amount of covered loss using the information reasonably available. The deductible applies to the covered amount we determine.

**T.54** An insured may submit additional information concerning the cause or amount of loss. We will consider relevant information received before final adjustment of the claim.

**T.55** We will provide information concerning the deductible upon reasonable request. Our explanation does not alter the deductible that applies under the policy terms.

**T.56** A dispute concerning the amount of loss does not suspend the deductible. We will apply the deductible to any covered amount ultimately determined.

**T.57** We will apply the deductible if windstorm or hail damage is discovered after other damage. The timing of discovery does not control whether the deductible applies.

**T.58** We will consider the cause of damage rather than the appearance of damage alone. Visible damage does not establish that windstorm or hail caused the loss.

**T.59** We may distinguish damage caused by windstorm or hail from deterioration, wear, or other causes. The deductible applies only to covered windstorm or hail damage.

**T.60** We will not treat maintenance, repair, or replacement needs as covered windstorm or hail loss unless direct physical damage from windstorm or hail is established. The deductible does not apply to noncovered work.

**T.61** An insured must permit us to obtain information reasonably relevant to the reported loss. We may use that information to determine cause, extent, and coverage.

**T.62** An insured must submit to examination when reasonably requested and when permitted by the policy terms. The examination may address the reported windstorm or hail loss.

**T.63** We will apply the deductible without regard to a lien, assignment, or transfer affecting payment interests. Such interests do not increase our payment obligation.

**T.64** We will apply the deductible before determining any amount payable to an additional insured. The deductible applies to the covered loss as a whole.

**T.65** We may adjust a windstorm or hail claim with an insured or a person authorized to act for an insured. An authorized representative does not change the deductible.

**T.66** We will not waive the deductible unless we expressly agree in writing. Our investigation, inspection, or payment discussion is not a waiver.

**T.67** Acceptance of information from an insured does not waive any deductible requirement. We retain all rights available under the policy terms.

**T.68** We will determine the deductible from the terms applicable at the time of loss. A later change does not alter the deductible for a prior loss.

**T.69** An insured must notify us of any known facts that may affect the cause or extent of a windstorm or hail loss. Notice must be provided as soon as reasonably possible.

**T.70** We may request that damaged property be retained when reasonably necessary for inspection. An insured must comply when retention is reasonably possible.

**T.71** We will not deny application of the deductible because damage affects property in more than one location. We will determine whether the damage arose from the same occurrence.

**T.72** We will apply the deductible to covered windstorm or hail loss regardless of whether a public authority issues a weather-related notice. Such a notice does not determine coverage or the deductible.

**T.73** We will apply the deductible consistently with applicable law. If any provision conflicts with applicable law, we will administer that provision as required by applicable law.

**T.74** An insured must comply with the duties applicable to a property loss. Compliance with those duties does not remove or reduce the deductible.

**T.75** The windstorm and hail deductible applies only as stated in these provisions and the applicable policy terms. All other coverage conditions remain applicable to a windstorm or hail loss.

## T.2 — Notice of Deductible Change

**T.1** A "deductible" is the amount that applies to a covered loss before we make payment. A "deductible change" is a change to the deductible that applies under this policy.

**T.2** We will provide you with written notice at least thirty days before an increase to your "windstorm deductible" takes effect. The notice will identify the change that will apply.

**T.3** A "windstorm deductible" is a deductible that applies to loss caused by windstorm. The windstorm deductible may differ from another deductible that applies under this policy.

**T.4** We will send notice of a deductible change to the mailing address shown in our records. You must notify us of a change in your mailing address.

**T.5** We may provide notice by electronic means when you have agreed to receive communications by electronic means. Electronic notice will be sent to the electronic address shown in our records.

**T.6** Notice of a deductible change will state the deductible that will apply after the change takes effect. Notice may also describe the coverage to which the deductible applies.

**T.7** A deductible change applies only as stated in the notice. We will not apply a deductible change to a loss occurring before the deductible change takes effect.

**T.8** You must review each notice we send concerning a deductible change. You must contact us promptly if you believe the notice does not accurately reflect your policy.

**T.9** A deductible change does not change the coverage provided by this policy unless the notice expressly states otherwise. All other policy provisions remain unchanged.

**T.10** We may correct a clerical, typographical, or administrative error in a notice. A correction will not reduce coverage for a loss occurring before we provide the corrected notice.

**T.11** A notice may apply to a deductible for property coverage, liability coverage, or another coverage provided by this policy. The notice will identify the applicable coverage.

**T.12** We may send a separate notice for a deductible change that applies to a particular cause of loss. A separate notice does not affect a deductible that is not identified in that notice.

**T.13** If more than one deductible could apply to a covered loss, the deductible provisions of this policy determine the deductible that applies. A notice of deductible change does not alter those provisions unless it expressly states otherwise.

**T.14** You must retain notices concerning deductible changes with your policy records. A notice remains part of the information describing the deductible applicable to this policy.

**T.15** We may provide notice to the named insured shown in our records. Notice to that named insured is notice to all insureds under this policy.

**T.16** If the policy is transferred to another named insured, you must provide us the information needed to update our records. We will send future notices using the information then shown in our records.

**T.17** A deductible change may be withdrawn before it takes effect. We will provide notice if we withdraw a deductible change.

**T.18** A deductible change may be replaced by a later deductible change. The later notice will govern if it applies to the same deductible.

**T.19** We will apply a deductible change in accordance with the notice that applies to the loss. The occurrence of a loss does not prevent us from applying a deductible change that was effective before the loss.

**T.20** You may ask us for information about the deductible shown in a notice. Our response does not change the deductible unless we provide a written revision.

**T.21** A deductible change does not affect an obligation to report a loss promptly. You must comply with all duties that apply after a loss.

**T.22** We do not cover any portion of a loss that is subject to an applicable deductible. The deductible applies whether or not you receive payment for another portion of the loss.

**T.23** A notice of deductible change is not a waiver of any policy provision. We do not waive a policy provision unless we provide a written waiver.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** "Named Storm Period" means the time during which a "named storm" is subject to an official storm advisory, watch, warning, or similar declaration applicable to the "residence premises." The Named Storm Period includes any interruption in such declaration when the named storm remains capable of causing direct physical loss.

**T.2** A named storm is a weather system given a name by a governmental weather authority. The name assigned to the weather system governs whether this provision applies.

**T.3** We may determine whether a Named Storm Period applies based on information issued by a governmental weather authority. We may also rely on weather records, reports, and other reliable information available to us.

**T.4** The Named Storm Period applies to direct physical loss caused by wind, rain, hail, storm surge, flooding, or other weather conditions associated with a named storm. The Named Storm Period also applies to loss caused by debris, falling objects, or water entering through an opening created by the named storm.

**T.5** We do not cover loss caused by wear, deterioration, neglect, faulty maintenance, or faulty repair merely because the loss occurs during a Named Storm Period. You must establish that covered direct physical loss resulted from the named storm.

**T.6** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage during a Named Storm Period. Reasonable protective measures may include securing openings, moving property, and arranging for emergency repairs.

**T.7** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. You must not discard damaged property unless its condition creates an immediate hazard or disposal is required by a governmental authority.

**T.8** An insured must document the condition of damaged property as soon as reasonably possible. Documentation may include photographs, recordings, receipts, repair estimates, and written descriptions of damage.

**T.9** An insured must report a loss arising from a named storm promptly after the loss is discovered. Delay in reporting may impair our ability to inspect damaged property and determine the cause and extent of loss.

**T.10** An insured must cooperate with our investigation of a loss occurring during a Named Storm Period. Cooperation includes providing information, records, access to property, and authorization to obtain relevant weather or repair information.

**T.11** We may inspect the residence premises and damaged property after a loss is reported. An insured must permit reasonable access for our inspection, testing, photographing, and evaluation.

**T.12** We may request that an insured provide a signed statement concerning the loss. The statement must describe the property involved, the known cause of loss, the date of discovery, and the measures taken to protect the property.

**T.13** We may request records supporting a claim for loss during a Named Storm Period. Requested records may include ownership records, repair records, maintenance records, invoices, and communications concerning the damaged property.

**T.14** We do not cover an increase in loss caused by an insured's failure to use reasonable means to protect covered property after a named storm threatens or damages the residence premises. This exclusion does not apply when an insured is unable to take protective measures because of conditions created by the named storm.

**T.15** We do not cover loss caused by voluntary abandonment of covered property during a Named Storm Period. This exclusion does not apply to property left behind because evacuation or access restrictions make removal unsafe or impracticable.

**T.16** We do not cover loss caused by rain entering through an opening that existed before the named storm unless the opening was directly caused by the named storm. An insured must establish that the named storm caused the opening through which rain entered.

**T.17** We do not cover loss caused by surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or waterborne material, whether or not caused by a named storm. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**T.18** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including subsidence, settlement, erosion, or landslide, even when a named storm contributes to the conditions causing the earth movement. This exclusion applies whether the earth movement is naturally occurring or results from human activity.

**T.19** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a dam, levee, seawall, retaining wall, drainage system, or similar structure unless coverage is otherwise provided by this policy. The existence of a Named Storm Period does not create coverage for such loss.

**T.20** We may use information from weather services, public authorities, contractors, engineers, and other qualified sources to determine the cause of loss. Our use of such information does not waive any policy condition, limitation, or exclusion.

**T.21** An insured must not begin permanent repairs before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect the damaged property, unless immediate repairs are necessary to protect property from further damage. You may make reasonable emergency repairs and must retain records of the repairs.

**T.22** An insured must retain invoices, receipts, and other records for emergency repairs performed during a Named Storm Period. We may inspect repaired property and request information concerning the work performed.

**T.23** We will pay for covered loss subject to all policy terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions. The Named Storm Period does not expand the property covered or the causes of loss covered by this policy.

**T.24** We may deny coverage for any portion of a loss that an insured cannot reasonably substantiate. This provision does not relieve us of our obligation to evaluate available evidence concerning a covered loss.

**T.25** An insured must notify us if damaged property is repaired, removed, sold, or transferred before our inspection. You must provide available records showing the condition of the property before the repair, removal, sale, or transfer.

**T.26** We may request that an insured submit to an examination under oath concerning a loss arising during a Named Storm Period. An insured must answer material questions and provide requested records as required by this policy.

**T.27** We do not cover intentional loss caused by an insured, whether occurring before, during, or after a Named Storm Period. We do not cover concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud relating to a claim.

**T.28** An insured must provide notice of any claim, demand, or lawsuit arising from property damage associated with a named storm. You must forward any legal papers to us promptly.

**T.29** We may adjust a covered loss based on the condition of the property immediately before the named storm. Prior damage, unrepaired damage, and preexisting conditions are not part of the covered loss.

**T.30** We may separate covered damage from damage caused by excluded causes of loss. When the damage cannot be separated, we will apply the policy provisions governing concurrent causation.

**T.31** The Named Storm Period ends when the named storm no longer presents conditions capable of causing direct physical loss at the residence premises. Our determination of the Named Storm Period will be based on reliable available information.

**T.32** This provision applies to any claim involving a named storm affecting the residence premises. All other policy terms remain applicable.

## T.4 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal

**T.1** We may terminate this "policy" before its expiration only by "cancellation" as provided in this endorsement. Cancellation ends coverage on the effective date stated in the notice.

**T.2** We may decline to continue this policy at its expiration by "nonrenewal." Nonrenewal does not cancel coverage before the policy expires.

**T.3** A "notice" means a written communication that states the action we will take and the effective date of that action. We will mail or deliver notice to the named insured at the mailing address shown in our records.

**T.4** If we cancel this policy because of nonpayment of "premium," we will mail or deliver notice at least ten days before cancellation takes effect. The notice will state that cancellation is based on nonpayment of premium.

**T.5** If we cancel this policy for a reason other than nonpayment of premium, we will mail or deliver notice at least thirty days before cancellation takes effect. The notice will state the reason for cancellation.

**T.6** If we elect not to renew this policy, we will mail or deliver notice at least thirty days before the policy expires. The notice will state that coverage will not be renewed.

**T.7** We may cancel this policy if you do not pay premium when due. Payment is not made until we receive it in a form we accept.

**T.8** We may cancel this policy if you obtained coverage through material misrepresentation. A misrepresentation is material if it affects our acceptance of the risk or the premium charged.

**T.9** We may cancel this policy if you conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning the policy. This includes a material fact concerning the insured location, ownership, occupancy, or loss history.

**T.10** We may cancel this policy if there is a substantial change in the risk covered by this policy. A substantial change may arise from a condition, use, occupancy, or activity that materially increases the possibility of loss.

**T.11** We may cancel this policy if an insured commits fraud in connection with this policy. Fraud includes a knowingly false statement or intentional concealment made to obtain coverage or benefits.

**T.12** We may cancel this policy if you fail to comply with a material policy condition. We will identify the condition on which cancellation is based in the notice.

**T.13** We may cancel this policy if the insured location becomes vacant or unoccupied in a manner that materially changes the risk. The notice will describe the condition supporting cancellation.

**T.14** We may cancel this policy if the insured location is used for a purpose that is not disclosed to us and that materially changes the risk. This includes a use that creates a materially greater exposure to loss.

**T.15** We may cancel this policy if an insured intentionally causes or attempts to cause a covered loss. This provision applies whether the conduct is committed alone or with another person.

**T.16** We may cancel this policy if an insured refuses to permit an inspection reasonably necessary to evaluate the risk. We may request access to the insured location at a reasonable time.

**T.17** We may cancel this policy if you refuse to provide information reasonably necessary to determine eligibility for coverage. Our request may concern facts existing before or after the policy became effective.

**T.18** We may cancel this policy if a required underwriting condition is not satisfied. The condition must relate to the risk covered by this policy.

**T.19** We may cancel this policy if we discover information that would have caused us not to issue the policy had we known that information when coverage began. The information must be material to the risk.

**T.20** We may cancel this policy if the insured location is transferred without our consent when consent is required by this policy. A transfer includes a change in ownership or possessory interest.

**T.21** We may cancel this policy if the insured location is no longer primarily used as a residence. We may also cancel if the nature of residential occupancy materially changes the risk.

**T.22** We may cancel this policy if an insured fails to maintain a condition required for continued eligibility. The condition must be stated in the policy, an endorsement, or an underwriting requirement communicated to you.

**T.23** We may cancel this policy if the insured location contains a dangerous condition that materially increases the risk of loss. We may consider whether the condition has been corrected when we decide whether to cancel.

**T.24** We may cancel this policy if an insured fails to correct a condition that we reasonably identify as material to the risk. Our notice will state the condition that supports cancellation.

**T.25** We may cancel this policy if an insured engages in conduct that materially increases the hazard at the insured location. The conduct must be related to the risk covered by this policy.

**T.26** We may cancel this policy if a required protective safeguard is removed, disabled, or not maintained, and the resulting condition materially increases the risk. This provision applies only if the safeguard is required for eligibility or continued coverage.

**T.27** We may cancel this policy if an insured knowingly permits an undisclosed hazardous activity at the insured location. The activity must materially affect the risk we agreed to insure.

**T.28** We may cancel this policy if an insured fails to disclose a material change in ownership, occupancy, or use when disclosure is required. The failure to disclose must be material to the risk.

**T.29** We may cancel this policy if coverage was issued in error because material underwriting information was unavailable or incorrect. We will state the basis for cancellation in the notice.

**T.30** We may cancel this policy if you fail to pay an additional premium that is due because of a change in coverage, rating, or exposure. The additional premium must have been properly billed or otherwise communicated to you.

**T.31** We may cancel this policy if an insured fails to comply with a payment arrangement we have accepted. A payment arrangement does not change our right to cancel for nonpayment if its terms are not met.

**T.32** We may cancel this policy if a check, draft, electronic transfer, or other payment is not honored. We may treat the unpaid amount as premium that remains due.

**T.33** We may cancel this policy if an insured submits materially false information in an application, questionnaire, inspection response, or renewal submission. The false information must concern a matter material to the risk.

**T.34** We may cancel this policy if an insured fails to cooperate with an underwriting inquiry concerning a material risk condition. Cooperation includes providing reasonably requested information and access.

**T.35** We may cancel this policy if an insured prevents us from verifying material information used to issue or continue coverage. Verification may include inspection, records review, or communication with relevant parties.

**T.36** A cancellation notice will identify the policy and the insured location. It will state the effective date of cancellation and the reason for cancellation.

**T.37** A cancellation notice based on nonpayment of premium will state the amount due, if any, and the manner in which payment may be made. We may reinstate coverage only if we agree to do so.

**T.38** Mailing notice to the named insured at the last mailing address known to us is sufficient notice. Delivery of notice to the named insured is also sufficient notice.

**T.39** If the policy covers more than one named insured, we may mail or deliver notice to any named insured shown in our records. Notice to a named insured is notice to all insureds.

**T.40** We may mail or deliver notice electronically if you have agreed to receive notices electronically and applicable law permits electronic delivery. Electronic delivery is effective when made in accordance with that agreement and applicable law.

**T.41** Proof of mailing or delivery will be sufficient proof that notice was given. We are not required to prove actual receipt of a notice that is properly mailed or delivered.

**T.42** Cancellation is effective on the date and at the time stated in the notice. Coverage does not continue after cancellation takes effect unless we agree in writing.

**T.43** Cancellation does not affect our right to collect premium earned before cancellation takes effect. Cancellation also does not affect an insured's duties regarding a loss that occurred before cancellation takes effect.

## T.6 — Seacoast Territories

**T.1** “Seacoast Territory” means land adjoining or directly exposed to “Tidal Waters.” This provision applies only to property located within a Seacoast Territory.

**T.2** “Tidal Waters” means waters affected by the regular rise and fall of tides. Tidal Waters include adjoining bays, inlets, estuaries, and similar bodies of water.

**T.3** A property location is within a Seacoast Territory when Seacoast Conditions may directly affect the location. A property location remains within a Seacoast Territory despite the presence of natural or artificial barriers.

**T.4** “Seacoast Conditions” means weather, water, wind, salt air, wave action, tidal movement, or erosion arising from proximity to Tidal Waters. Seacoast Conditions include conditions occurring with a storm or other natural event.

**T.5** “Seacoast Event” means an occurrence involving Seacoast Conditions that causes direct physical loss to covered property. A Seacoast Event may involve wind, rain, tidal water, waves, or erosion.

**T.6** We cover direct physical loss caused by a Seacoast Event only when the loss is otherwise covered. The existence of a Seacoast Event does not create coverage for loss otherwise excluded.

**T.7** We do not cover loss caused by gradual erosion, settling, subsidence, or movement of land. This exclusion applies whether the condition results from Seacoast Conditions or another cause.

**T.8** We do not cover loss caused by repeated exposure to salt air, salt water, humidity, spray, corrosion, deterioration, or decay. This exclusion applies even when the damage is not visible.

**T.9** We do not cover loss caused by the action of waves, tidal water, storm surge, or overflow from Tidal Waters. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or another force.

**T.10** We do not cover loss caused by water entering through an opening unless the opening is created by a covered direct physical loss. The opening must be in a covered structure.

**T.11** An insured must use reasonable care to maintain the property in a condition that resists foreseeable Seacoast Conditions. An insured must promptly correct a condition that increases the likelihood of damage from Seacoast Conditions.

**T.12** An insured must secure exterior doors, windows, coverings, and other exterior openings when Seacoast Conditions threaten the property. An insured must maintain protective devices in operable condition.

**T.13** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage after a Seacoast Event. An insured must protect covered property from additional damage caused by rain, wind, water, or debris.

**T.14** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**T.15** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property and the surrounding location. An insured must provide access reasonably necessary for us to evaluate a claimed loss.

**T.16** An insured must cooperate with us in identifying the cause of loss. An insured must provide information reasonably available concerning Seacoast Conditions affecting the property.

**T.17** An insured must not make permanent repairs that prevent us from determining the cause or extent of loss unless immediate repair is necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must retain information concerning emergency protective measures.

**T.18** We do not cover increased loss caused by an insured’s failure to take reasonable protective action. This exclusion applies only to the increased portion of the loss.

**T.19** We do not cover loss caused by the failure, inadequacy, or deterioration of a seawall, bulkhead, revetment, groin, jetty, dock, pier, or similar structure. This exclusion applies whether the structure is located on or away from the property.

**T.20** We do not cover the cost to stabilize land, restore shoreline, replace beach material, or prevent erosion. This exclusion applies even when such work is intended to protect covered property.

**T.21** We do not cover loss resulting from the movement or collapse of land caused by erosion or the undermining of soil. This exclusion applies whether the movement is sudden or gradual.

**T.22** We may inspect property located within a Seacoast Territory. Our inspection does not constitute a representation that the property is safe or adequately protected from Seacoast Conditions.

**T.23** Our failure to inspect property does not waive any condition or exclusion that applies to a Seacoast Event or Seacoast Conditions. Our inspection does not impose a duty to identify or correct a hazardous condition.

**T.24** These provisions apply to each insured seeking coverage for loss involving property within a Seacoast Territory. An insured’s compliance with these provisions is a condition of coverage for loss subject to this section.

The term "property damage" means physical injury to tangible property. Property damage includes resulting loss of use of that property.

The term "occurrence" means an accident. Occurrence includes continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.

The term "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside. Residence premises includes the grounds and other structures used in connection with that dwelling.

The term "residence" means a dwelling used by you as a home. Residence includes a unit occupied by you within a building.

The term "premises" means the land and structures at the residence premises. Premises includes property used in connection with the residence premises.

The term "insured location" means the residence premises. Insured location also includes locations used by an insured as a residence.

The term "business" means trade, profession, occupation, or enterprise engaged in for economic gain. Business includes activities conducted through a corporation, partnership, or other organization.

The term "business pursuits" means activities regularly conducted for economic gain. Business pursuits do not include activities ordinarily incidental to nonbusiness pursuits.

The term "employee" means a person performing duties related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. Employee includes a person performing domestic services for an insured.

The term "residence employee" means an employee whose duties are related to the residence premises. Residence employee does not include a person performing duties in connection with an insured's business.

The term "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle. Motor vehicle includes a trailer designed for travel on public roads.

The term "recreational vehicle" means a motorized vehicle designed principally for recreational use. Recreational vehicle includes a vehicle designed for off-road use.

The term "watercraft" means a craft designed for travel on water. Watercraft includes a craft powered by a motor, sail, or other means of propulsion.

The term "aircraft" means a device designed for flight or navigation in the air. Aircraft includes a drone or other remotely operated aerial device.

The term "hovercraft" means a vehicle designed to travel over land or water on a cushion of air. Hovercraft includes equipment used to operate or control that vehicle.

The term "family" means persons related by blood, marriage, adoption, or legal guardianship. Family includes persons who reside in the same household.

The term "vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary for customary habitation or use. Property held for sale does not by itself make premises occupied.

The term "unoccupied" means not being used as a customary place of residence. Temporary absence does not by itself make premises unoccupied.

The term "property" means tangible property capable of physical possession. Property does not include land, water, or growing crops.

The term "personal property" means property owned or used by an insured that is not real property. Personal property includes property temporarily in an insured's care.

The term "loss" means accidental physical loss or accidental physical damage. Loss includes direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

The term "direct physical loss" means physical alteration of property or physical deprivation of property. Direct physical loss does not include diminished value without physical injury.

The term "covered property" means property for which coverage is provided. Covered property includes property only to the extent of the applicable coverage.

The term "pollutant" means an irritant or contaminant in solid, liquid, gaseous, or thermal form. Pollutant includes smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

The term "fungi" means any type or form of fungus. Fungi includes mold, mildew, spores, mycotoxins, and byproducts released by fungi.

The term "smoke" means visible or invisible products of combustion. Smoke does not include smoke arising from agricultural or industrial operations.

The term "ordinance or law" means a law, regulation, code, or rule affecting the use, repair, replacement, removal, or demolition of property. Ordinance or law includes an order issued under governmental authority.

The term "collapse" means abrupt falling down or caving in of a building or part of a building. Collapse requires that the building or affected part cannot be occupied for its intended purpose.

The term "theft" means an unlawful taking of property. Theft includes attempted theft and resulting damage to property.

The term "vandalism" means willful and malicious damage to property. Vandalism includes resulting physical damage caused during unlawful entry.

The term "insured's share" means the portion of property or loss attributable to an insured's financial interest. Insured's share includes an interest held jointly with another person.

The term "actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss. Actual cash value reflects applicable depreciation and physical condition.
