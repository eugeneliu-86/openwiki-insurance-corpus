# HO 01 32 North Carolina Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2018-05)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

"This endorsement" changes the coverage provisions to which it applies. We apply this endorsement only as stated in its provisions.

"We" and "us" refer to the insurer providing this coverage. "You" and "your" refer to the insured seeking coverage.

The terms used in this endorsement have the meanings assigned by its provisions. A term not changed by this endorsement retains its applicable meaning.

We apply the provisions of this endorsement to covered property, covered losses, duties, conditions, exclusions, and settlements addressed by this endorsement. We do not extend coverage beyond the coverage expressly provided.

A provision of this endorsement that changes another applicable provision controls over that provision. A provision not changed by this endorsement remains applicable.

We apply any limitation stated in this endorsement as part of the coverage to which it applies. We apply any exclusion stated in this endorsement regardless of any broader wording that would otherwise apply.

We apply any condition stated in this endorsement as a condition of coverage. An insured must satisfy each applicable duty imposed by this endorsement.

We determine coverage under the facts, circumstances, terms, limitations, exclusions, and conditions that apply to the loss. We do not cover loss unless coverage is available under the applicable provisions.

We apply this endorsement to a loss only when its provisions apply to the property and cause of loss involved. We do not cover property or loss outside that scope.

We apply the "AOP deductible floor" only as provided by the applicable coverage provisions. The "AOP deductible floor" does not create coverage.

We apply the "sworn proof deadline" as a duty applicable to a claim when required by the applicable provisions. An insured must comply with the "sworn proof deadline" when it applies.

We apply the "insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement" only when replacement cost settlement is available. The "insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement" does not create replacement cost coverage.

We apply a deductible only when a deductible applies to the loss. A deductible does not reduce a duty owed by an insured.

We apply settlement provisions only after determining that the loss is covered. We do not make a payment for an excluded loss.

We apply conditions affecting payment to each claim for which those conditions apply. An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine coverage.

We may investigate a reported loss. Our investigation does not waive any applicable condition, exclusion, limitation, deductible, or defense.

We may request records, documents, statements, or other information relevant to a claim. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable requests.

We apply duties after loss to an insured with knowledge of the loss. Failure to comply with an applicable duty may affect coverage as permitted by the applicable provisions.

We apply exclusions according to their terms. We do not cover loss excluded by an applicable exclusion.

We apply limitations according to their terms. A limitation does not create coverage for loss that is otherwise not covered.

We apply coverage only to the extent that all applicable requirements are met. We do not cover any portion of loss for which coverage is unavailable.

Any ambiguity in applying this endorsement is resolved according to applicable law. We apply the enforceable provisions of this endorsement to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** We apply the "Windstorm or Hail" deductible to a covered loss caused directly by windstorm or hail. We call this deductible the "Windstorm and Hail Deductible".

**T.2** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to a "covered loss" only when windstorm or hail is a direct cause of that loss. The deductible applies whether windstorm or hail acts alone or with another covered cause of loss.

**T.3** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible percentage may not be less than one percent. We will apply the deductible in accordance with the applicable policy terms.

**T.4** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible percentage may not exceed five percent. We will determine the applicable deductible from the coverage that applies to the loss.

**T.5** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to "covered property" for which this deductible applies. The deductible does not create coverage for property that is not covered property.

**T.6** We apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to an "occurrence" involving windstorm or hail. An occurrence includes loss arising from the same general weather conditions.

**T.7** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies before we determine the amount payable for a covered loss. We will not pay the portion of a covered loss that is subject to the deductible.

**T.8** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to loss to covered property caused directly by windstorm. The deductible also applies to loss to covered property caused directly by hail.

**T.9** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies when windstorm or hail causes direct physical loss. The deductible does not apply merely because windstorm or hail occurs near the covered property.

**T.10** We will investigate, evaluate, and adjust a covered loss as required by North Carolina bulletinas required by North Carolina bulletin (see North Carolina Bulletin NCDOI-2021-06 — Claims Handling Standards, B.2 Requirements). Our application of the deductible will be consistent with that adjustment.

**T.11** An insured must cooperate with us in determining whether windstorm or hail caused a claimed loss. An insured must provide information that we reasonably request concerning the cause and extent of loss.

**T.12** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a windstorm or hail loss. Reasonable protective measures do not waive the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.13** An insured must retain damaged property when reasonably necessary for our inspection. Disposal of damaged property may affect our ability to determine the cause and extent of loss.

**T.14** We may inspect covered property after a reported windstorm or hail loss. Our inspection does not constitute an agreement that coverage applies.

**T.15** We may request records, photographs, estimates, invoices, or other information relevant to a windstorm or hail claim. An insured must provide requested information within a reasonable time.

**T.16** We will determine the amount of covered loss after applying all applicable policy provisions. We will then apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to that covered loss.

**T.17** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies regardless of whether an insured repairs damaged property. The deductible also applies if an insured elects not to repair damaged property.

**T.18** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies regardless of whether a mortgagee, lienholder, or other person has an interest in covered property. The deductible does not reduce an insured's duties under this policy.

**T.19** We will not duplicate payment for the portion of loss subject to the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. Payment under another coverage does not eliminate the deductible unless that coverage expressly provides otherwise.

**T.20** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered loss involving exterior property. The deductible also applies to covered loss involving interior property when windstorm or hail is a direct cause of loss.

**T.21** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies when windstorm creates an opening through which rain enters covered property. We will determine coverage for resulting water damage under all applicable policy provisions.

**T.22** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies when hail damages a roof, wall, window, door, or other covered part of property. We will determine the extent of covered damage from the facts of the loss.

**T.23** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to loss caused by wind-driven debris when windstorm is a direct cause of that loss. We will not apply the deductible to damage that is not covered.

**T.24** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to loss caused by collapse only when collapse is covered and windstorm or hail is a direct cause of loss. All other policy conditions remain applicable.

**T.25** We do not cover loss that is excluded under this policy merely because windstorm or hail also occurs. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not restore coverage for excluded loss.

**T.26** We do not cover deterioration, wear, or maintenance-related damage under the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. We may consider those conditions in determining the extent of covered loss.

**T.27** We do not cover faulty repair, defective materials, or defective workmanship under the Windstorm and Hail Deductible unless coverage otherwise applies. The deductible does not change any exclusion applicable to that loss.

**T.28** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property after a loss. This exclusion applies in addition to the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.29** We do not cover loss to property that is not covered property. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not make such property covered.

**T.30** We may determine that damage resulted from causes other than windstorm or hail. In that event, we will apply the deductible and coverage terms that apply to the determined cause of loss.

**T.31** We may determine that covered damage resulted from windstorm or hail and another cause of loss. We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible when windstorm or hail directly causes covered damage.

**T.32** An insured must notify us promptly of a windstorm or hail loss. Prompt notice helps us inspect the claimed damage and evaluate coverage.

**T.33** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must also permit us to inspect the property and records relating to the loss.

**T.34** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer questions concerning the windstorm or hail loss truthfully.

**T.35** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we reasonably request it. The statement must identify the property claimed to be damaged and the nature of the claimed damage.

**T.36** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning a windstorm or hail loss. Concealment or misrepresentation may affect coverage.

**T.37** We may obtain information from weather sources, contractors, engineers, or other qualified persons. We may use that information to evaluate the cause and extent of claimed damage.

**T.38** We may inspect prior repairs, maintenance records, and physical conditions at the covered property. We may consider that information in determining whether claimed damage is covered.

**T.39** We may request reasonable access to covered property for inspection. An insured must not unreasonably prevent or delay our inspection.

**T.40** We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible only to covered loss caused directly by windstorm or hail. We will not apply that deductible to loss for which windstorm or hail is not a direct cause.

**T.41** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies without regard to the severity of weather conditions. The deductible applies based on the cause of covered loss.

**T.42** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies without regard to the source of a weather report. We will evaluate the facts available for the claimed loss.

**T.43** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to a covered loss at the location where the covered property is situated. Coverage remains subject to all applicable policy terms.

**T.44** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to a covered loss involving attached structures when those structures are covered property. The deductible does not determine whether an attached structure is covered.

**T.45** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to a covered loss involving detached structures when those structures are covered property. The deductible does not expand coverage for detached structures.

**T.46** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered personal property damaged directly by windstorm or hail. We will determine coverage for personal property under the applicable policy provisions.

**T.47** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to reasonable emergency measures when those measures are part of a covered loss. We will determine coverage for emergency measures under the applicable policy provisions.

**T.48** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to debris removal when debris removal is part of a covered loss. We will determine coverage for debris removal under the applicable policy provisions.

**T.49** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies before any payment is made for covered loss. We will calculate payment after determining the covered amount of loss.

**T.50** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible may reduce or eliminate payment for a covered loss. The deductible does not affect an insured's obligation to report the loss.

**T.51** We may issue payment jointly to an insured and a person having a legal interest in covered property. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies before such payment is issued.

**T.52** An insured must take reasonable steps to document windstorm or hail damage. Documentation may include photographs, repair proposals, receipts, and other relevant records.

**T.53** An insured must preserve evidence of the condition of covered property when reasonably possible. We may consider unavailable evidence in evaluating the claimed loss.

**T.54** An insured must provide records showing repairs or alterations relevant to claimed windstorm or hail damage when reasonably available. We may request records that help distinguish covered damage from other damage.

**T.55** We may request that an insured identify the date and circumstances of the claimed windstorm or hail loss. An insured must provide information known to that insured.

**T.56** We may request information concerning prior damage to covered property. An insured must provide information relevant to our evaluation of the claim.

**T.57** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not apply to liability claims. This provision applies only to covered property loss.

**T.58** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not apply to loss unless windstorm or hail directly causes covered damage. We will apply other deductibles as provided by the applicable policy terms.

**T.59** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not change the limit of liability applicable to covered property. The applicable limit of liability remains subject to all policy terms.

**T.60** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not create a duty for us to repair covered property. Our payment obligations remain subject to the applicable policy terms.

**T.61** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not require an insured to use a particular contractor. An insured remains responsible for complying with all applicable policy conditions.

**T.62** We may communicate with an insured concerning the application of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. Such communication does not waive any policy term or condition.

**T.63** We may reserve our rights while we investigate a windstorm or hail loss. A reservation of rights does not constitute a denial of coverage.

**T.64** We may request additional information when the information previously provided is incomplete or inconsistent. An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests for additional information.

**T.65** We will determine whether claimed damage is direct physical loss to covered property. We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible only after making that determination.

**T.66** We will determine whether windstorm or hail is a direct cause of claimed damage. We may rely on physical evidence, weather information, and other relevant information.

**T.67** We will determine the reasonable cost of covered repair or replacement in accordance with the applicable policy terms. We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to the covered loss determined under those terms.

**T.68** We will not increase payment because the Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies. We will pay only the covered amount remaining after application of the deductible.

**T.69** We will not reduce the Windstorm and Hail Deductible because an insured disputes the cause or extent of loss. We will consider information provided by an insured in evaluating the claim.

**T.70** An insured may provide estimates, reports, photographs, or other information supporting the claimed loss. We will consider relevant information in our evaluation.

**T.71** An insured must not make permanent repairs that prevent reasonable inspection of claimed damage unless necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must retain reasonable documentation of necessary repairs.

**T.72** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage. We will determine coverage for resulting damage under the applicable policy terms.

**T.73** We may deny coverage for a claimed loss when an insured fails to comply with a duty that materially affects our ability to evaluate the claim. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible remains applicable to covered loss.

**T.74** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered loss during the policy period. Coverage for that loss remains subject to all applicable policy terms.

**T.75** All other terms and conditions of this policy remain unchanged. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies as stated in these provisions.

## T.2 — Notice of Deductible Change

**T.1** This provision applies to an increase in the "windstorm deductible" applicable to your policy. We will provide notice of the increase as required by this provision.

**T.2** We will provide you with written notice of an increase in the "windstorm deductible" at least thirty days before the increase takes effect. The notice will identify the increase and its effective date.

**T.3** The "effective date" is the date upon which the increased windstorm deductible applies. An increased windstorm deductible does not apply before its effective date.

**T.4** We may deliver notice to you by mail or by another method permitted by applicable law. Notice sent to your mailing address shown in our records is notice to you.

**T.5** You must provide us with your current mailing address. You must promptly notify us of a change in your mailing address.

**T.6** Notice to a named insured is notice to all insureds. We may address notice to any named insured.

**T.7** The notice may be included with other policy communications. The notice need not be provided in a separate mailing.

**T.8** We will state the windstorm deductible that will apply after the effective date. The notice may describe the property or coverage to which the increase applies.

**T.9** An increase in the windstorm deductible applies only if we provide notice in accordance with this provision. We will not apply the increase before the effective date stated in the notice.

**T.10** This provision does not prevent us from reducing a windstorm deductible. A reduction does not require notice under this provision.

**T.11** This provision does not alter any deductible that applies before an increase becomes effective. The deductible otherwise applicable to a covered loss remains in effect until the increase takes effect.

**T.12** An increased windstorm deductible applies to a covered loss occurring on or after its effective date. The deductible applicable to a covered loss is determined by the date of loss.

**T.13** The notice may identify the policy to which the increase applies. A clerical error in identifying the policy does not invalidate notice if you can reasonably determine that the notice applies to your policy.

**T.14** We may correct a clerical error in a notice. A correction does not change the effective date unless we provide notice required by this provision.

**T.15** If notice is returned to us because you failed to provide a current mailing address, notice remains effective if sent to the mailing address shown in our records. You remain responsible for maintaining current address information.

**T.16** Delivery of notice does not constitute cancellation or nonrenewal of your policy. Your policy otherwise remains subject to its terms and conditions.

**T.17** This provision applies whether the increase results from an underwriting action or a change in our deductible requirements. The reason for the increase does not affect the notice requirement.

**T.18** We may include an explanation of the increase in the notice. The absence of an explanation does not affect the effectiveness of notice.

**T.19** The notice may be delivered electronically if you have agreed to electronic delivery or if electronic delivery is otherwise permitted by applicable law. Electronic notice is effective when delivered in accordance with the applicable delivery method.

**T.20** You may contact us regarding a notice of deductible change. A request for information does not postpone the effective date of an increase.

**T.21** Your failure to receive a notice does not invalidate notice properly sent to the mailing address shown in our records. This paragraph applies only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**T.22** We may provide notice through an authorized representative acting for us. Notice provided by an authorized representative is notice provided by us.

**T.23** A notice of deductible change applies only to the policy identified in the notice. It does not change coverage under another policy.

**T.24** This provision does not create coverage. All coverage remains subject to the policy terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions.

**T.25** This provision does not change the causes of loss for which a windstorm deductible applies. The policy provisions governing application of the windstorm deductible remain unchanged except as stated in the notice.

**T.26** We may withdraw an increase before its effective date. A withdrawal may be communicated by written notice.

**T.27** If we withdraw an increase, the windstorm deductible in effect immediately before the proposed increase remains applicable. A withdrawal does not reduce any deductible unless we state otherwise.

**T.28** We may replace a notice with a revised notice. A revised notice is subject to the notice requirement stated in this provision.

**T.29** You must pay any deductible applicable to a covered loss. Payment of a deductible does not waive any policy condition.

**T.30** A deductible change does not alter your duties after loss. You must comply with all policy duties that apply to a covered loss.

**T.31** We may rely upon the mailing address and other contact information shown in our records. You must notify us if that information is inaccurate.

**T.32** Any provision of your policy that conflicts with this provision is changed only to the extent necessary to comply with this provision. All other policy provisions remain unchanged.

**T.33** This provision applies to an increase in the windstorm deductible during the policy term. It also applies to an increase offered in connection with continuation of coverage.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** “Weather Authority” means a governmental agency that issues weather watches, warnings, advisories, or storm designations. “Named Storm” means a storm system identified by a Weather Authority.

**T.2** “Named Storm Period” means the time during which a Weather Authority has issued a watch or warning for a Named Storm affecting any part of North Carolina. The Named Storm Period ends when the applicable watch or warning is withdrawn or expires.

**T.3** “Loss” means direct physical loss to property. “Named Storm Deductible” means a deductible applicable to Loss caused by a Named Storm.

**T.4** A Named Storm Period applies only when the applicable watch or warning relates to the Named Storm. A Weather Authority's designation of a storm controls whether the storm is a Named Storm.

**T.5** We determine whether a Loss was caused by a Named Storm from the facts and circumstances of the Loss. We may consider weather records, reports, inspections, and other relevant evidence.

**T.6** Loss caused by wind, rain, storm surge, flooding, or debris may be caused by a Named Storm. The presence of any such condition does not by itself establish that a Named Storm caused the Loss.

**T.7** If a Named Storm Deductible applies to a covered Loss, we reduce the amount payable by that deductible. The Named Storm Deductible applies only to Loss caused by a Named Storm.

**T.8** A Loss may be caused by a Named Storm even if damage becomes apparent before or after the Named Storm Period. The timing of discovery does not alone determine whether the Named Storm Deductible applies.

**T.9** A Named Storm Period does not create coverage for Loss where coverage does not otherwise apply. The applicability of a Named Storm Deductible does not change the cause of Loss requirement.

**T.10** All covered Loss caused by the same Named Storm is treated as arising from the same occurrence. We apply the Named Storm Deductible to that Loss as provided by its terms.

**T.11** Damage unrelated to a Named Storm is not subject to the Named Storm Deductible solely because it occurs during a Named Storm Period. We determine the cause of damage separately from the timing of damage.

**T.12** Loss attributable to a different Named Storm is evaluated separately. The designation of a Named Storm does not establish that another storm caused the Loss.

**T.13** We do not cover Loss merely because a Named Storm occurs or a Named Storm Period applies. Coverage depends upon direct physical Loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**T.14** We do not cover Loss caused by a cause for which coverage does not apply merely because that cause occurs during a Named Storm Period. A Named Storm does not change any limitation applicable to the Loss.

**T.15** An insured must give us prompt notice of Loss that may have been caused by a Named Storm. The notice must identify the property involved and the known circumstances of the Loss.

**T.16** An insured must protect property from further damage after a Loss. An insured must make reasonable efforts to preserve damaged property for our inspection.

**T.17** An insured must retain records, receipts, photographs, and other documentation relating to repairs or replacement. An insured must provide those materials to us upon request.

**T.18** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property. An insured must provide reasonable access to the property and to relevant records.

**T.19** An insured must cooperate with our investigation of a Loss. An insured must provide information reasonably requested concerning the cause, date, and extent of damage.

**T.20** An insured must provide available weather reports, repair records, and other evidence bearing upon the cause of Loss. We may obtain information from a Weather Authority or other reliable source.

**T.21** We evaluate all available information when determining whether a Named Storm caused a Loss. We may rely upon evidence obtained after a Named Storm Period ends.

**T.22** If the available information does not establish that a Named Storm caused the Loss, the Named Storm Deductible does not apply. This determination does not create coverage for any Loss.

**T.23** The amount payable for covered Loss remains subject to the applicable deductible and all applicable conditions. Payment of a claim does not waive any right available to us.

**T.24** We apply the Named Storm Deductible in accordance with its terms. We do not apply that deductible to Loss for which it does not apply.

**T.25** Duties and conditions applicable to a Loss remain applicable during a Named Storm Period. An insured must comply with those duties and conditions.

**T.26** No action by us in investigating, inspecting, or adjusting a Loss constitutes a waiver of any policy provision. A waiver by us must be in writing.

**T.27** An insured must not impair our ability to determine the cause or extent of Loss. An insured must not dispose of damaged property unless necessary to protect property from further damage.

**T.28** This provision applies to Loss involving property in North Carolina. We apply this provision only as stated in this endorsement.

## T.4 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal

**T.1** We may cancel this "policy" only as permitted by applicable law. We will provide notice of cancellation as required by applicable law.

**T.2** We may elect not to renew this policy at the end of a "policy period." We will provide notice of "nonrenewal" as required by applicable law.

**T.3** A notice of cancellation or nonrenewal will be mailed or delivered to the "named insured" shown in the Declarations. Notice may be mailed or delivered to the address shown in our records.

**T.4** We will mail or deliver written notice of cancellation at least fifteen days before cancellation takes effect when cancellation is for nonpayment of "premium." The notice will state the effective date of cancellation.

**T.5** We will mail or deliver written notice of cancellation at least thirty days before cancellation takes effect when cancellation is for a reason other than nonpayment of premium. The notice will state the reason for cancellation.

**T.6** We will mail or deliver written notice of nonrenewal at least forty-five days before the end of the policy period. The notice will state the date on which coverage ends.

**T.7** A "cancellation" is the termination of this policy before the end of the policy period. Cancellation does not affect our right to collect premium owed for coverage provided before cancellation takes effect.

**T.8** Nonrenewal is our decision not to continue this policy beyond the end of the policy period. Nonrenewal does not terminate coverage before the end of the policy period.

**T.9** We may cancel this policy for nonpayment of premium. Premium is not paid when we do not receive the required payment when due.

**T.10** We may cancel this policy for a material misrepresentation or substantial change in the risk as permitted by applicable law. A material misrepresentation includes a statement that affects our acceptance or continuation of coverage.

**T.11** We may cancel this policy when an insured has committed fraud in connection with this policy. Fraud includes a material false statement or concealment made to obtain coverage or payment.

**T.12** We may cancel this policy when an insured has violated a material condition of this policy. The violation must relate to the insured property, the risk, or a claim under this policy.

**T.13** We may cancel this policy when the insured property presents a substantial increase in hazard. The increase in hazard may arise from a change in occupancy, condition, use, or maintenance.

**T.14** We may cancel this policy when cancellation is otherwise authorized by applicable law. Our notice will identify the basis for cancellation when required by applicable law.

**T.15** A notice of cancellation will state the date and time cancellation takes effect. Coverage ends at the stated date and time unless the notice is withdrawn or modified by us.

**T.16** A notice of nonrenewal will state the date and time this policy ends. Coverage ends at the end of the policy period unless we agree in writing to continue coverage.

**T.17** Mailing notice to the named insured at the address shown in our records is sufficient when mailing is permitted by applicable law. Proof of mailing will be sufficient proof of notice when permitted by applicable law.

**T.18** Delivery of notice to the named insured is sufficient when delivery is permitted by applicable law. We may use any method of delivery authorized by applicable law.

**T.19** You must notify us promptly of a change in your mailing address. We are not responsible for a failure to receive notice mailed to the address shown in our records.

**T.20** We may send notice to an authorized representative of the named insured when authorized by applicable law. Notice sent to an authorized representative does not limit our right to send notice directly to the named insured.

**T.21** Cancellation applies to all coverages provided by this policy unless our notice states otherwise. We may cancel a coverage only when permitted by applicable law.

**T.22** Nonrenewal applies to all coverages provided by this policy unless our notice states otherwise. We may offer renewal on terms that differ from the terms of this policy.

**T.23** We may condition renewal on payment of premium due for the renewal policy. We may condition renewal on information reasonably necessary to evaluate the risk.

**T.24** We may require information concerning the insured property before offering renewal. You must provide requested information that is material to the continuation of coverage.

**T.25** We may inspect the insured property in connection with cancellation or nonrenewal. An inspection does not obligate us to continue coverage or renew this policy.

**T.26** We may rely on information obtained through inspection, underwriting records, public records, or other reliable sources. We may act on that information as permitted by applicable law.

**T.27** A request by you to cancel this policy must be communicated to us or our authorized representative. We may require the request to be in writing.

**T.28** Cancellation requested by you takes effect on the date requested or on a later date agreed by us. We may not make cancellation requested by you effective before we receive the request unless permitted by applicable law.

**T.29** When you request cancellation, you remain responsible for premium owed through the effective date of cancellation. We will return unearned premium when required by applicable law.

**T.30** Return of unearned premium does not affect the effective date of cancellation. The effective date is determined by the cancellation notice or your cancellation request, as applicable.

**T.31** We may apply a return premium against amounts you owe us when permitted by applicable law. Any remaining return premium will be paid to the person entitled to receive it.

**T.32** Payment of premium after we issue a cancellation notice does not automatically reinstate this policy. We may reinstate this policy only by our written agreement.

**T.33** If we reinstate this policy, we may establish the terms of reinstatement as permitted by applicable law. Reinstatement does not affect a cancellation that remains in effect.

**T.34** We may withdraw a cancellation notice before the cancellation takes effect. Withdrawal of a cancellation notice must be communicated by us or our authorized representative.

**T.35** We may withdraw a nonrenewal notice before the end of the policy period. Withdrawal of a nonrenewal notice must be communicated by us or our authorized representative.

**T.36** Our acceptance of premium after cancellation does not waive cancellation unless we agree in writing. Our acceptance of premium after nonrenewal does not create a renewal policy unless we agree in writing.

**T.37** An insured must cooperate with our reasonable request for information relating to cancellation or nonrenewal. Failure to cooperate may affect our decision to continue coverage as permitted by applicable law.

**T.38** An insured must provide accurate information concerning the insured property and the risk. We may rely on information provided by an insured in making underwriting decisions.

**T.39** An insured must promptly notify us of a material change in ownership, occupancy, use, or condition of the insured property. Notice of a material change does not require us to continue coverage.

**T.40** An insured must promptly notify us of a material change in the mailing address of the named insured. We may rely on the mailing address shown in our records until we receive notice of a change.

**T.42** We may provide notice to a mortgagee, lienholder, or other interested person identified in our records. Failure to provide notice to an unidentified interested person does not affect cancellation or nonrenewal when permitted by applicable law.
