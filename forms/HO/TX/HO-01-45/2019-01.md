# HO 01 45 Texas Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2019-01)
> SUPERSEDED by HO 01 45 Texas Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2022-01) for policies effective on or after 2022-01-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.


## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

**T.1** This "endorsement" applies to insurance for property located in Texas. We apply this endorsement as part of the insurance contract.

**T.2** This endorsement changes the insurance contract only where its language expressly provides a change. We do not infer a change from silence.

**T.3** A provision of this endorsement prevails over an inconsistent provision applicable to the same matter. We apply the inconsistent provision only to the extent it does not conflict with this endorsement.

**T.4** A provision not changed by this endorsement remains applicable. We enforce that provision according to its terms.

**T.5** We construe this endorsement with all applicable provisions of the insurance contract. We give effect to language that can reasonably operate together.

**T.6** A provision of this endorsement that specifically addresses a matter governs that matter. A general provision does not limit that specific provision.

**T.7** This endorsement does not provide insurance beyond that expressly stated in its language. We do not provide insurance for a loss merely because this endorsement applies.

**T.8** This endorsement does not waive a condition, exclusion, limitation, or duty unless its language expressly provides a waiver. We retain all rights not changed by this endorsement.

**T.9** An "insured" must comply with a duty that remains applicable under the insurance contract. An insured must comply with a duty changed by this endorsement as changed.

**T.10** We may enforce a condition applicable to insurance provided under this endorsement. A failure to comply with an applicable condition may affect coverage as allowed by law.

**T.11** The "minimum Section I deductible" applies where applicable. This endorsement does not change its application unless expressly stated.

**T.12** The "sworn statement deadline" applies to a duty to provide a sworn statement. This endorsement does not change that duty unless expressly stated.

**T.13** The "coinsurance threshold" applies where coinsurance is applicable. This endorsement does not change the application of coinsurance unless expressly stated.

**T.14** We determine the applicability of this endorsement from its terms and the facts relating to the loss. An insured must provide information we reasonably request to make that determination.

**T.15** We do not cover a loss excluded by an applicable exclusion. This endorsement does not remove an exclusion unless its language expressly provides otherwise.

**T.16** We do not provide payment beyond an applicable limitation. This endorsement does not change a limitation unless its language expressly provides otherwise.

**T.17** A term used in this endorsement has the meaning given by the insurance contract unless this endorsement provides a different meaning. We apply that different meaning only within this endorsement.

**T.18** If a provision of this endorsement cannot be enforced, we apply the remaining provisions to the extent permitted by law. An unenforceable provision does not invalidate another provision that can be enforced.

**T.19** This endorsement applies while insurance under the insurance contract remains in effect. It does not create insurance after the insurance contract ends.

**T.20** We apply this endorsement to a loss only when its terms apply to that loss. An insured may not obtain insurance under this endorsement where its conditions are not satisfied.

**T.21** No conduct by us changes this endorsement unless we agree to that change in writing. An insured may not rely on an oral statement to alter this endorsement.

**T.22** This endorsement controls the scope of its changes to the insurance contract. All other applicable provisions remain subject to their terms.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** "Windstorm and Hail Deductible" means the amount you must pay for a covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. We will subtract this deductible from the amount otherwise payable for that covered loss.

**T.2** The minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage is one percent. An applicable deductible percentage must not be less than that percentage.

**T.3** The maximum windstorm and hail deductible percentage is ten percent. An applicable deductible percentage must not be greater than that percentage.

**T.4** "Applicable deductible percentage" means the percentage we apply when calculating the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. We will apply the applicable deductible percentage to the deductible basis.

**T.5** "Deductible basis" means the amount used to calculate the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. The deductible basis will be determined from the coverage applicable to the covered property.

**T.6** "Covered loss" means direct physical loss to covered property for which coverage applies. A covered loss is subject to all applicable terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions.

**T.7** "Windstorm" means wind, including wind-driven rain, where direct physical loss results from the wind. Windstorm does not include loss caused solely by water entering property without direct physical damage caused by wind.

**T.8** "Hail" means frozen precipitation that causes direct physical loss to covered property. Loss caused by hail is subject to the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.9** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered loss caused directly or indirectly by windstorm or hail. This deductible applies whether windstorm or hail acts alone or with another cause of loss.

**T.10** We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible when windstorm or hail contributes to direct physical loss. This applies even when another covered cause contributes to that loss.

**T.11** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to damage to a building caused by windstorm or hail. It also applies to damage to personal property caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.12** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to loss of use caused by a covered loss subject to this deductible. We will subtract the deductible before determining any loss payment.

**T.13** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to debris removal arising from a covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. The deductible applies before we determine any amount payable for debris removal.

**T.14** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to reasonable emergency measures taken to protect covered property from further direct physical loss. Those measures remain subject to all applicable conditions.

**T.15** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to loss occurring before and after those protective measures are taken.

**T.16** An insured must give us prompt notice of a covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. Notice must identify the damaged property and the known cause of loss.

**T.17** An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation of a covered loss. This duty includes providing information that we reasonably request concerning the loss.

**T.18** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged covered property when we reasonably request. An insured must preserve damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**T.19** An insured must provide records and documents that reasonably support a claim for a covered loss. We may examine those records and documents as reasonably necessary to determine the amount of loss.

**T.20** "Claim" means a request for payment under coverage provided by us. A claim for loss caused by windstorm or hail is subject to the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.21** We will determine the amount of covered loss before applying the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. We will not pay the amount of the deductible.

**T.22** If the amount of covered loss does not exceed the Windstorm and Hail Deductible, we will not make a loss payment. You remain responsible for that loss.

**T.23** If the amount of covered loss exceeds the Windstorm and Hail Deductible, we will reduce the amount otherwise payable by the deductible. Any remaining payment is subject to all applicable limitations.

**T.24** "Loss payment" means an amount we pay for covered loss after application of all applicable deductibles and limitations. A loss payment does not include the amount of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.25** We will not apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible more than required for covered loss arising from the same occurrence. Separate occurrences are subject to separate applicable deductibles.

**T.26** "Occurrence" means an event or related series of events causing direct physical loss. We will determine whether loss arises from the same occurrence based on the facts of the loss.

**T.27** Windstorm or hail occurring during an occurrence is treated as part of that occurrence. Damage discovered at different times may arise from the same occurrence.

**T.28** Damage occurring at different locations may arise from the same occurrence. We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible according to the occurrence causing the damage.

**T.29** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies regardless of whether covered property is repaired, replaced, or not repaired. Our application of the deductible does not depend on the method of loss settlement.

**T.30** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies before we determine any amount payable for repair. It also applies before we determine any amount payable for replacement.

**T.31** "Repair" means work performed to restore damaged covered property. Repair costs are subject to the Windstorm and Hail Deductible when caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.32** We do not cover the amount of a covered loss that is within the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. You are responsible for that amount.

**T.33** We do not cover any fee, charge, or expense incurred solely to avoid payment of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. Such fee, charge, or expense is not part of covered loss.

**T.34** We do not cover loss caused by failure to protect covered property after a windstorm or hail event when that failure increases the loss. This exclusion applies only to the increased loss.

**T.35** You may not transfer the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to us. The deductible remains your responsibility.

**T.36** You may not reduce or eliminate the Windstorm and Hail Deductible by assigning a claim. An assignment does not change our rights or your duties.

**T.37** A contractor, repairer, or other person acting for you has no authority to waive the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. Only we may determine the amount payable for a covered loss.

**T.38** A statement by an insured, contractor, repairer, or other person does not change the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. Any change must be made by us.

**T.39** We may request information concerning prior damage to covered property. Prior damage does not become covered merely because later windstorm or hail causes additional damage.

**T.40** We will determine the amount of loss caused by windstorm or hail based on the available evidence. We may distinguish covered loss from damage, deterioration, or loss not caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.41** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to the portion of loss caused by windstorm or hail. Loss caused solely by a cause not covered is not payable.

**T.42** We may require reasonable documentation of the condition of covered property before and after a claimed loss. Failure to provide requested documentation may affect our ability to determine the covered loss.

**T.43** An insured must submit to examination when reasonably requested by us. An insured must answer questions concerning the claimed loss truthfully.

**T.44** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when reasonably requested by us. The statement must identify the property claimed to be damaged and the cause of loss.

**T.45** We may pay an insured or a person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment does not waive the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.46** We may adjust a covered loss based on repair estimates, inspection findings, photographs, records, and other available evidence. Our adjustment remains subject to the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.47** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to supplemental payments for covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. A supplemental payment does not create additional coverage.

**T.48** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to any increase in the amount of covered loss discovered after a loss payment. We will determine the remaining amount payable after applying the deductible.

**T.49** We may recover any payment made in error that did not account for the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. An insured must cooperate with reasonable efforts to recover that payment.

**T.50** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning a claim. Concealment or misrepresentation may affect coverage for the claimed loss.

**T.51** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not create coverage for loss otherwise excluded or limited. All exclusions and limitations continue to apply.

**T.52** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not increase any limit applicable to covered loss. Any payment remains subject to the applicable limit.

**T.53** We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible consistently with the facts of the covered loss. Our determination of the deductible is subject to your duties under this endorsement.

**T.54** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail unless we state otherwise. No action by us waives this deductible unless we expressly agree in writing.

## T.2 — Notice of Deductible Change

**T.1** A "deductible change" is a change to the deductible that applies to covered property or covered loss. We may make a deductible change only as permitted by applicable law.

**T.2** A "windstorm deductible" is the deductible that applies to loss caused by windstorm. We will provide notice of an increase in the windstorm deductible at least thirty days before the increase takes effect.

**T.3** We will provide the notice in writing. We may deliver the notice by mail or by any other method permitted by applicable law.

**T.4** The notice will identify the deductible change. The notice will state the effective date of the deductible change.

**T.5** The notice will identify the property to which the deductible change applies. The notice will identify the coverage affected by the deductible change.

**T.6** We will send notice to you at your mailing address shown in our records. You must notify us of any change in your mailing address.

**T.7** We may send notice to an address provided by you for electronic delivery. You must maintain access to that address if you have agreed to electronic delivery.

**T.8** Notice is effective when we provide it in accordance with this endorsement and applicable law. Failure to receive notice does not invalidate notice properly provided by us.

**T.9** A deductible change does not apply before its stated effective date. A deductible change applies only to loss occurring on or after its effective date.

**T.10** We will not apply an increased windstorm deductible unless we have provided the notice required by this endorsement. The windstorm deductible in effect before the increase remains applicable until the increase becomes effective.

**T.11** The notice requirement applies to an increase in the windstorm deductible. This requirement does not limit any other notice obligation imposed by applicable law.

**T.12** We may revise a deductible that does not apply to windstorm loss as permitted by applicable law. We will provide notice of that revision when notice is required by applicable law.

**T.13** We may withdraw a proposed deductible change before its effective date. We may provide written notice of the withdrawal.

**T.14** We may replace a proposed deductible change with a different deductible change. We will provide notice of the replacement as required by applicable law.

**T.15** A deductible change applies only while this policy remains in effect. Cancellation or termination of this policy ends the applicability of a deductible change under this policy.

**T.16** Renewal of this policy does not prevent us from making a deductible change. We will provide notice of a deductible change associated with renewal as required by this endorsement or applicable law.

**T.17** Your payment of premium after a deductible change becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the deductible change to the extent permitted by applicable law. Your acceptance does not waive any right that cannot be waived under applicable law.

**T.18** A deductible change does not alter the coverage provided by this policy except as directly affected by the changed deductible. All other policy terms remain applicable.

**T.19** The deductible shown in the applicable policy records governs if the notice contains a clerical error. We may correct a clerical error by providing revised notice.

**T.20** A notice may describe the deductible change by coverage, peril, property, or location. The description will control only as applied to the property and coverage identified in the notice.

**T.21** We may include the notice with a billing statement, renewal offer, policy change notice, or other written communication. Inclusion with another communication does not change the notice requirement.

**T.22** We may provide notice to your authorized representative when permitted by applicable law. Notice to an authorized representative is notice to you when applicable law so provides.

**T.23** You must review each notice we provide concerning a deductible change. You must promptly notify us if the notice does not identify the property or coverage you believe is affected.

**T.24** You must provide information we reasonably request to determine the property or coverage affected by a deductible change. Your failure to provide requested information does not delay a deductible change otherwise effective under this endorsement.

**T.25** We may maintain records of the notice we provide. Our records may include the method, address, and date of delivery.

**T.26** A deductible change is not invalid solely because the notice is included in an electronic communication. Electronic notice must be provided in a manner permitted by applicable law.

**T.27** We may issue corrected notice if a prior notice is incomplete or inaccurate. Corrected notice does not make a deductible change effective before the date permitted by applicable law.

**T.28** A deductible change does not affect a loss that occurred before the deductible change became effective. The deductible applicable to that loss is determined by the policy terms in effect when the loss occurred.

**T.29** We may amend the form of notice we use. Any amendment to the form of notice must comply with applicable law.

**T.30** No oral statement changes a deductible or the effective date of a deductible change. A deductible change must be shown in written notice or policy records issued by us.

**T.31** If applicable law requires a more favorable notice standard, that standard applies. We will administer this endorsement in accordance with applicable law.

**T.32** If any provision of this endorsement is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. We will apply the enforceable provisions to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**T.33** This endorsement applies to a deductible change made by us during the policy period or at renewal. It does not create coverage that is not otherwise provided by this policy.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** A "Named Storm" is a storm for which a name has been assigned by a governmental weather authority. A "Named Storm Period" is the period described in this endorsement.

**T.2** The Named Storm Period begins when the Named Storm is designated. The Named Storm Period applies where covered property is located.

**T.3** The Named Storm Period continues for seventy-two hours after a Named Storm designation ends. The cessation of storm conditions does not, by itself, end the Named Storm Period.

**T.4** We determine whether a loss occurred during the Named Storm Period from the facts of the loss. We may consider weather information, governmental records, and other reliable evidence.

**T.5** The Named Storm Period applies to direct physical loss caused by a Named Storm. The Named Storm Period also applies to loss caused by wind, rain, water, or debris associated with a Named Storm.

**T.6** We apply the policy provisions applicable during a Named Storm Period to a loss occurring during that period. Those provisions apply even if the loss is discovered after the Named Storm Period has ended.

**T.7** The Named Storm Period does not create coverage. We provide coverage only if the loss is otherwise covered under the policy.

**T.8** We do not cover loss merely because a Named Storm Period applies. We do not cover loss that is excluded by the policy.

**T.9** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect covered property when required by the policy.

**T.10** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when practical. An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property and the location of the loss.

**T.11** An insured must provide records and information that we reasonably request to investigate the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of the claim.

**T.12** An insured must report a loss promptly. Notice of a loss must include information reasonably available about the Named Storm and the damaged property.

**T.13** We may inspect the property before, during, or after repairs are made. Repairs made to protect property do not waive our right to inspect the loss.

**T.14** We may obtain weather information and other records relating to the loss. We may use that information to determine whether the Named Storm Period applies.

**T.15** The application of the Named Storm Period is determined separately from the amount of loss. The application of the Named Storm Period does not determine the cause or extent of damage.

**T.16** Damage that existed before a Named Storm Period is not treated as damage caused by the Named Storm solely because it is observed during the Named Storm Period. An insured must provide available information distinguishing prior damage from claimed damage.

**T.17** Damage discovered after a Named Storm Period is not treated as damage caused by the Named Storm solely because it is discovered after that period. We determine the cause of loss from the available evidence.

**T.18** The Named Storm Period applies regardless of whether the Named Storm is identified in a claim report. We may apply the Named Storm Period when the facts establish that a Named Storm caused or contributed to the loss.

**T.19** The Named Storm Period applies regardless of whether other weather conditions are present. We determine coverage under all applicable policy provisions.

**T.20** We may request proof of loss and supporting documentation. An insured must provide requested information within the time required by the policy.

**T.21** Payment of a claim does not waive any policy provision. Our investigation of a claim does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**T.22** The Named Storm Period applies to all insureds seeking coverage for the same loss. An insured may not avoid the application of the Named Storm Period by characterizing the loss as a different event.

**T.23** All other policy terms remain applicable. If this endorsement conflicts with another policy provision, this endorsement applies.

## T.4 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal

**T.1** We may cancel this "policy" only as permitted by applicable law. A "cancellation" ends coverage on the effective date stated in our notice.

**T.2** "Nonrenewal" means our decision not to continue this policy upon its expiration. Nonrenewal does not constitute a cancellation.

**T.3** A "notice" under this provision must be in writing. We may deliver or mail a notice to you at your last known address.

**T.4** You may cancel this policy by providing us with written notice. Your notice must state the effective date you request for cancellation.

**T.5** We may cancel this policy for nonpayment of premium. We may also cancel this policy for any reason permitted by applicable law.

**T.6** We may cancel this policy when material information affecting the risk is false, misleading, or omitted. We may cancel this policy when an insured commits fraud in connection with this policy.

**T.7** We may cancel this policy when an insured materially violates a policy condition. We may cancel this policy when a change in the risk is material and permitted by applicable law.

**T.8** Our notice of cancellation will state the effective date of cancellation. Our notice will state the reason for cancellation when required by applicable law.

**T.9** We will provide notice of cancellation in the manner required by applicable law. Mailing or delivery of notice will be sufficient when permitted by applicable law.

**T.10** For cancellation based on "nonpayment of premium", we will provide written notice at least ten days before cancellation takes effect. The notice will identify the unpaid premium obligation.

**T.11** For cancellation for a reason other than nonpayment of premium, we will provide written notice at least thirty days before cancellation takes effect. The notice will state the applicable reason for cancellation.

**T.12** We will provide written notice of nonrenewal at least thirty days before this policy expires. The notice will state that coverage will not continue upon expiration.

**T.13** We may send a notice to the address shown in our records. You must promptly notify us of any change in your mailing address.

**T.14** A notice sent to your authorized representative may satisfy our notice obligation when permitted by applicable law. An authorized representative does not have authority to change the effective date stated in our notice unless we agree in writing.

**T.15** We may deliver notice electronically when you have consented to electronic delivery and applicable law permits electronic delivery. Electronic delivery must be made to an address designated by you for that purpose.

**T.16** Proof of mailing or delivery will be sufficient proof that notice was given when permitted by applicable law. Actual receipt of notice is not required when applicable law does not require actual receipt.

**T.17** Cancellation will take effect on the date stated in our notice. Coverage ends at the time cancellation takes effect.

**T.18** We may rescind this policy when permitted by applicable law. Rescission is distinct from cancellation and nonrenewal.

**T.19** Our failure to cancel this policy after learning of a circumstance does not waive our right to act upon a later circumstance. A waiver must be made by us in writing.

**T.20** Acceptance of premium after we issue a notice does not continue this policy unless we agree in writing. We may return premium that does not apply to coverage provided.

**T.21** Payment of an overdue premium does not automatically withdraw a notice of cancellation. Coverage will continue only if we confirm in writing that the notice has been withdrawn.

**T.22** We may reinstate this policy after cancellation only by written agreement. Reinstatement may be subject to terms we state in writing and that are permitted by applicable law.

**T.23** A cancellation does not affect our rights regarding premium earned before cancellation takes effect. A cancellation does not affect your duties regarding any loss occurring before cancellation takes effect.

**T.24** We will return any unearned premium due to you in accordance with applicable law. Return of unearned premium does not delay cancellation.

**T.25** You must pay premium that is earned before cancellation takes effect. We may pursue payment of earned premium as permitted by applicable law.

**T.26** A dispute concerning premium does not suspend a notice of cancellation. You must comply with this policy unless we agree otherwise in writing.

**T.27** A request for information from us does not withdraw a notice of cancellation. We may respond to your request without changing the stated effective date.

**T.28** A request for reconsideration does not extend coverage. We may reconsider a cancellation or nonrenewal decision without waiving any right.

**T.29** We may correct a clerical error in a notice. A corrected notice will control only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**T.30** If a notice contains an error that affects its validity, we will provide any further notice required by applicable law. We do not waive any lawful right by providing further notice.

**T.31** A cancellation notice applies to all coverage provided by this policy unless the notice states otherwise. We may cancel only a portion of coverage when permitted by applicable law.

**T.32** A nonrenewal notice applies to all coverage provided by this policy unless the notice states otherwise. We may offer continued coverage on terms that differ from the expiring policy.

**T.33** An offer of renewal is not binding until we accept the required premium and any other required information. We may withdraw an offer of renewal as permitted by applicable law.

**T.34** We may decline to renew this policy even if no claim has been reported. We will not decline to renew for a reason prohibited by applicable law.

**T.35** We may cancel this policy if continuation of coverage would violate applicable law. We may cancel this policy if an insured fails to provide information required by applicable law.

**T.36** You must cooperate with us in resolving any premium, address, or notice issue. Your failure to cooperate does not relieve us of any notice duty imposed by applicable law.

**T.37** We may communicate with you concerning cancellation or nonrenewal through an authorized representative. Communication through an authorized representative does not replace a notice required by applicable law.

**T.38** Notice to an insured other than you will be provided when required by applicable law. We may provide notice to any other person with an interest in this policy when permitted by applicable law.

**T.39** Cancellation or nonrenewal does not affect an insured's duty to protect property after a loss. Cancellation or nonrenewal does not affect our right to investigate a loss that occurred while coverage applied.

**T.40** Cancellation or nonrenewal does not affect any claim that arose before coverage ended. Our duties regarding a covered loss remain subject to this policy.

**T.41** We may cancel this policy when required information reveals that the risk is not eligible for coverage. We may take that action only as permitted by applicable law.

**T.42** We may cancel this policy when premium was calculated from materially inaccurate information. We may adjust premium as permitted by applicable law.

**T.43** We may cancel this policy when an insured fails to meet an underwriting requirement permitted by applicable law. Our notice will state the basis for cancellation when required.

**T.44** We may elect not to renew this policy when the risk no longer meets our underwriting requirements. Our election is subject to applicable law.

**T.45** We may elect not to renew this policy when continuation would expose us to a risk prohibited by applicable law. We may offer other coverage if we choose to do so.

**T.46** We may withdraw a cancellation notice before cancellation takes effect. Withdrawal of a notice must be made by us in writing.

**T.47** We may withdraw a nonrenewal notice before this policy expires. Withdrawal of a notice does not change any policy term unless we agree in writing.

**T.48** No employee, producer, or representative may alter a cancellation or nonrenewal notice unless authorized by us. Any alteration must be in writing.

**T.49** You must not rely on an oral statement concerning cancellation or nonrenewal. A change to a notice is effective only when confirmed by us in writing.

**T.50** We may retain records concerning mailing, delivery, cancellation, nonrenewal, premium, and related communications. Those records may be used to establish compliance with this provision.

**T.51** If applicable law requires a particular method of notice, we will use that method. If applicable law permits more than a single method, we may use any permitted method.

**T.52** If applicable law requires notice to contain specified information, our notice will contain that information. A notice may contain additional information that we consider appropriate.

**T.53** If applicable law prohibits cancellation or nonrenewal for a stated reason, we will not take that action for that reason. Nothing in this provision expands any right to cancel or nonrenew that applicable law restricts.

**T.54** If applicable law requires our notice to identify an insured right, our notice will identify that right. Your exercise of that right does not continue coverage unless applicable law requires otherwise.

**T.55** We may comply with a lawful order affecting cancellation or nonrenewal. Compliance with a lawful order does not waive any right under this policy.

**T.56** An insured must provide accurate information relevant to premium, eligibility, and notice delivery. An insured must promptly correct information that becomes inaccurate.

**T.57** An insured must preserve any notice received from us concerning cancellation or nonrenewal. An insured must promptly provide us with information requested concerning delivery of a notice.

**T.58** This provision applies to cancellation and nonrenewal to the extent permitted by applicable law. Any policy term inconsistent with applicable law will be applied only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## T.5 — Claims Handling

**T.1** A "claim" is a request for payment or other benefit arising from an alleged loss. You or your representative may give us notice of a claim.

**T.2** A "notice of claim" must provide information reasonably sufficient to identify you, the property involved, and the reported loss. An insured must provide available contact information and the location of the damaged property.

**T.3** You may authorize a "representative" to communicate with us concerning a claim. We may request reasonable confirmation of the representative's authority to act for you.

**T.4** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and payment of a claim. We may communicate directly with you concerning matters requiring your personal knowledge or consent.

**T.5** We will acknowledge receipt of a claim within fifteen days after we receive your notice of claim. After we receive all "requested items", we will notify you whether we accept or reject the claim within fifteen business days. We will pay an "accepted claim" within five business days after we notify you that we accept the claim.

**T.6** We will maintain a record of the date on which we receive your notice of claim. We may use that record to administer the claim.

**T.7** Our acknowledgment may be provided by mail, electronic communication, telephone, or another reasonable method. The acknowledgment may identify information that we need to evaluate the claim.

**T.8** We may request information, records, or documents reasonably related to the claim. Requested items may include information concerning the cause, extent, value, ownership, or repair of the reported damage.

**T.9** We will not request information already available to us unless we reasonably need it for claim handling. We may request clarification when information previously provided is incomplete, unclear, or inconsistent.

**T.10** Our requests for information will identify the matter to which the request relates. We may explain the relevance of requested items when appropriate to assist you in responding.

**T.11** An insured must provide truthful information concerning the claim. An insured must not knowingly provide false, misleading, or incomplete information concerning a material matter.

**T.12** An insured must permit us reasonable access to the damaged property when access is necessary to inspect the reported damage. We will make reasonable efforts to arrange access at a mutually suitable time.

**T.13** An insured must preserve available records, invoices, photographs, and other evidence relating to the claim. This duty applies when preservation is reasonably practicable.

**T.14** We may request a "proof of loss" when reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim. A proof of loss must state the information reasonably requested by us concerning the reported loss.

**T.15** An insured must return requested information within a reasonable time. If you need additional time to provide requested items, you must notify us promptly.
