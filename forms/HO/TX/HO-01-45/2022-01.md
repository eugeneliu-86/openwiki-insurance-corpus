# HO 01 45 Texas Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2022-01)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

**T.1** The terms stated in this section apply to you and us. They govern the matters addressed by these terms.

**T.2** If a term stated in this section conflicts with another applicable term, the term stated in this section governs. A term that does not conflict remains applicable.

**T.3** We will read applicable terms together when they can be given effect without conflict. We will give effect to the controlling term when a conflict exists.

**T.4** These terms do not provide coverage unless coverage is expressly provided. These terms do not enlarge coverage beyond that expressly provided.

**T.5** These terms do not reduce a right expressly provided unless they expressly state that result. Any restriction stated in these terms applies as written.

**T.6** An "insured" must comply with all applicable duties and conditions. Compliance with a duty or condition does not waive another applicable duty or condition.

**T.7** We may enforce a duty or condition applicable to you. Our failure to enforce a duty or condition does not waive our right to enforce it.

**T.8** A waiver by us is effective only when made by us in writing. A waiver applies only to the matter expressly waived.

**T.9** A change affecting our obligations is effective only when made by us in writing. No act or statement by an insured changes our obligations unless we agree in writing.

**T.10** You may not assign an interest in coverage without our written consent. Our consent does not waive an applicable term.

**T.11** Any term held invalid or unenforceable will be applied to the extent permitted by applicable law. The remaining terms remain applicable.

**T.12** We will apply these terms in accordance with applicable law. A requirement imposed by applicable law controls when it cannot be altered.

**T.13** You must provide information that we reasonably request to administer a matter addressed by these terms. Information provided by you must be accurate and complete.

**T.14** An insured must cooperate with us concerning a matter addressed by these terms. Cooperation includes permitting us to obtain information relevant to that matter.

**T.15** We may rely on information provided by you unless we have reason to know that the information is inaccurate. Our reliance does not limit an applicable duty of an insured.

**T.16** The "minimum Section I deductible" applies when applicable. Its application is subject to the controlling terms.

**T.17** The "sworn statement deadline" applies when applicable. An insured must comply with that requirement.

**T.18** The "coinsurance threshold" applies when applicable. Its application does not create coverage.

**T.19** A duty owed by an insured applies to every insured to whom that duty applies. Performance by an insured does not excuse nonperformance by another insured.

**T.20** We do not waive a right by investigating, communicating about, or evaluating a matter. Our actions concerning a matter do not constitute an admission of coverage.

**T.21** We may request documentation relevant to a matter addressed by these terms. An insured must preserve relevant documentation in that insured's possession or control.

**T.22** These terms apply only to matters within their stated scope. We will not apply these terms beyond that scope.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** We apply the "Windstorm and Hail Deductible" to a "Windstorm or Hail Loss" that is covered under this policy. A Windstorm or Hail Loss means direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.2** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible shown in the Declarations must not be less than one percent. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible shown in the Declarations must not exceed ten percent.

**T.3** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies separately from any other deductible that may apply to a covered loss. We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible before determining the amount we will pay.

**T.4** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered property damaged by windstorm or hail. It applies whether windstorm, hail, or both contribute to the loss.

**T.5** We determine the Windstorm and Hail Deductible from the amount shown in the Declarations. We calculate that deductible using the applicable limit of liability for the damaged property.

**T.6** The applicable limit of liability is the limit that applies when the Windstorm or Hail Loss occurs. A later change to that limit does not change the deductible applicable to the loss.

**T.7** We apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to loss under Coverage A. We also apply it to loss under any coverage for which the Declarations identify that deductible.

**T.8** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to loss caused by windstorm that occurs with rain. The presence of rain does not prevent application of that deductible.

**T.9** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to loss caused by hail that occurs with rain. The presence of rain does not prevent application of that deductible.

**T.10** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies when wind-driven material damages covered property. We apply that deductible if windstorm or hail directly causes the material to damage covered property.

**T.11** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies when a tree, branch, or other object damages covered property. We apply that deductible if windstorm or hail causes the object to fall or be driven against covered property.

**T.12** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail. It does not determine whether the cause of loss is otherwise covered.

**T.13** We do not cover any part of a Windstorm or Hail Loss that is subject to the Windstorm and Hail Deductible until the deductible has been applied. The deductible remains your responsibility.

**T.14** We will not pay the portion of a covered Windstorm or Hail Loss that is within the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. This applies regardless of the type of covered property damaged.

**T.15** We apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to the total covered Windstorm or Hail Loss. We then determine the amount payable under the applicable coverage.

**T.16** We may determine that more than a single cause contributed to a loss. If windstorm or hail is a covered cause of that loss, the Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to the covered portion of the loss.

**T.17** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies when windstorm or hail damages a dwelling. It also applies when windstorm or hail damages structures related to the dwelling.

**T.18** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies when windstorm or hail damages personal property. It applies whether the personal property is inside or outside the dwelling.

**T.19** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to damage to building materials. It also applies to damage to materials attached to a covered building.

**T.20** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to damage caused by wind-driven rain when windstorm creates an opening in a covered building. Coverage for that damage remains subject to all policy terms.

**T.21** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to damage caused by hail impact. Coverage for that damage remains subject to all policy terms.

**T.22** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to damage caused by wind pressure. Coverage for that damage remains subject to all policy terms.

**T.23** We determine whether windstorm or hail caused a loss based on the facts of the loss. Your report of the loss does not by itself determine the applicable deductible.

**T.24** An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation of a Windstorm or Hail Loss. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the cause and extent of the loss.

**T.25** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a Windstorm or Hail Loss. Reasonable measures to protect covered property do not waive the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.26** An insured must retain damaged property when reasonably necessary for our inspection. Disposal of damaged property without our consent may affect our ability to determine the covered loss.

**T.27** An insured must allow us to inspect covered property after a reported Windstorm or Hail Loss. Our inspection does not waive any policy condition, exclusion, or deductible.

**T.28** An insured must provide records that support the claimed loss when we reasonably request them. Such records may include repair estimates, receipts, photographs, and other relevant information.

**T.29** We may inspect the condition of covered property before determining the amount payable for a Windstorm or Hail Loss. We may also inspect repairs made after the loss.

**T.30** We may require an estimate for repair or replacement of damaged covered property. An estimate does not establish the amount of covered loss or the amount we will pay.

**T.31** We may adjust a Windstorm or Hail Loss based on the condition of covered property immediately before the loss. We may also consider the cause, extent, and location of the damage.

**T.32** Payment of a covered Windstorm or Hail Loss does not eliminate the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. The deductible applies to a later covered Windstorm or Hail Loss during the policy period.

**T.33** A change in weather conditions does not by itself create coverage. We apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible only when windstorm or hail causes covered direct physical loss.

**T.34** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail unless the loss is covered under the applicable coverage. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not create coverage for excluded loss.

**T.35** We do not cover the cost to repair damage that existed before the reported Windstorm or Hail Loss. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not affect our right to determine the prior condition of covered property.

**T.36** We do not cover loss attributable to wear, deterioration, or defective construction merely because windstorm or hail occurs. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to the covered portion of a loss, if any.

**T.37** We do not cover loss caused by neglect after a Windstorm or Hail Loss. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to any covered portion of the loss.

**T.38** We do not cover loss that is otherwise excluded under this policy. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not modify any exclusion.

**T.39** We determine the deductible applicable to a Windstorm or Hail Loss from the Declarations in effect when the loss occurs. No oral statement changes that deductible.

**T.40** Any endorsement that changes the Windstorm and Hail Deductible must be made part of this policy. We will apply the endorsement that is in effect when the loss occurs.

**T.41** If the Declarations contain a Windstorm and Hail Deductible, that deductible applies as stated in this section. Any other deductible applies only when this section does not apply.

**T.42** If more than a single covered property item is damaged by the same windstorm or hail occurrence, we determine the deductible under the applicable coverage. We do not apply a separate deductible merely because multiple items are damaged.

**T.43** If a covered loss involves windstorm or hail and another cause of loss, we determine coverage under all applicable policy provisions. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to the covered Windstorm or Hail Loss.

**T.44** If the cause of damage cannot be determined from available information, we may continue our investigation. We will apply the deductible that is supported by the facts of the loss.

**T.45** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies without regard to whether a public authority issues a weather notice. A weather notice does not control our coverage determination.

**T.46** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies without regard to whether a named weather event is assigned a name. The name of a weather event does not control our coverage determination.

**T.47** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered loss occurring at the residence premises. It applies only to property for which coverage is provided.

**T.48** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not apply to loss that is not caused by windstorm or hail. Any other applicable deductible may apply to that loss.

**T.49** We will pay no more than the amount permitted by the applicable limit of liability after application of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. All other policy limitations remain applicable.

**T.50** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies before we determine payment under any applicable loss settlement provision. It also applies before any applicable special limit is considered.

**T.51** Your payment of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible is not required before we adjust a covered loss. We may nevertheless withhold payment for the deductible portion of the loss.

**T.52** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible is part of the terms applicable to covered Windstorm or Hail Loss. It remains subject to all conditions of this policy.

**T.53** This section changes the policy only with respect to the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. All other policy provisions remain applicable.

## T.2 — Notice of Deductible Change

**T.1** This provision applies when we change a deductible applicable to your coverage. A "deductible change" includes an increase, decrease, addition, removal, or replacement of a deductible.

**T.2** A "deductible" is the amount that you must pay for a covered loss before we pay under this policy. We will identify the deductible affected by a deductible change.

**T.3** We will provide a "written notice" of a deductible change to you. The written notice will describe the change and its application to your coverage.

**T.4** We will provide you with written notice at least forty-five days before an increase to the "windstorm deductible" takes effect. The written notice will identify the windstorm deductible that will apply.

**T.5** The written notice will state the "effective date" of the deductible change. A deductible change applies as of its effective date.

**T.6** We may provide written notice by mail to your last known mailing address. We may provide written notice by electronic delivery when permitted by applicable law.

**T.7** An insured must maintain a current mailing address with us. An insured must provide us with a current electronic address when electronic delivery is requested.

**T.8** A written notice sent to the mailing address or electronic address provided to us is effective when sent. Receipt of the written notice is not required for the notice to be effective.

**T.9** We may rely upon our records when providing written notice. A change to your mailing address or electronic address does not apply until we receive it.

**T.10** You may request information concerning a deductible change from us. We will provide available information concerning the deductible change upon your request.

**T.11** A written notice may correct an error in an earlier notice. We will identify a correction in the written notice that contains it.

**T.12** A deductible change applies to a "covered loss" as determined by its effective date. The applicable deductible is determined by the date the covered loss occurs.

**T.13** The date you report a covered loss does not determine the applicable deductible. The date we pay a covered loss does not determine the applicable deductible.

**T.14** A deductible change does not alter coverage unless the written notice states that coverage is altered. We will not treat a deductible change as a waiver of any policy condition.

**T.15** An insured must comply with applicable duties after a covered loss. A deductible change does not excuse compliance with those duties.

**T.16** We may change a deductible as permitted by applicable law. We will provide notice of a deductible change as required by applicable law.

**T.17** An oral statement does not change a deductible. We are bound only by a deductible change stated in written notice or otherwise made in writing by us.

**T.18** A communication from an agent does not change a deductible unless we confirm the change in writing. You may rely upon a deductible change that we confirm in writing.

**T.19** A deductible change does not require your signature unless we state otherwise in written notice. Your failure to respond to written notice does not prevent a deductible change from taking effect.

**T.20** We may withdraw a deductible change before its effective date. We will provide written notice if we withdraw a deductible change.

**T.21** If a provision of this notice is invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect. We will apply the valid provisions to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**T.22** We will resolve an ambiguity in written notice by reference to the notice as a whole. A clerical error does not expand coverage or reduce a deductible unless we confirm the correction in writing.

**T.23** You must review written notice upon receipt. You must notify us if you believe the written notice does not accurately identify your coverage or deductible.

**T.24** We will retain records of deductible changes. Our records may be used to determine the deductible applicable to a covered loss.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** We apply this provision to a "Named Storm Period". A Named Storm Period applies when a Named Storm Designation is in effect.

**T.2** "Named Storm" means a storm identified by a name through a governmental weather authority. The name may be assigned before or after the storm affects covered property.

**T.3** "Named Storm Designation" means an official designation that a Named Storm exists or is expected to affect an area. We may rely on information issued by a governmental weather authority to determine whether a Named Storm Designation applies.

**T.4** The Named Storm Period begins when the Named Storm Designation begins. The Named Storm Period continues until seventy-two hours after the Named Storm Designation ends.

**T.5** We determine whether a loss occurred during a Named Storm Period from the available weather information. We may use information concerning the time and location of the loss.

**T.6** A Named Storm Period may apply to property at the residence premises. A Named Storm Period may also apply to property away from the residence premises when the Named Storm affects that location.

**T.7** The absence of damage at the residence premises does not establish that a Named Storm Period did not apply. The presence of damage at another location does not establish that a Named Storm Period applies to your loss.

**T.8** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the date, time, and location of a loss. An insured must provide available information that relates to weather conditions affecting the property.

**T.9** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**T.10** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. Reasonable protective measures do not establish that a loss was caused by a Named Storm.

**T.11** We may inspect the residence premises and damaged property. We may inspect property during or after a Named Storm Period.

**T.12** We may obtain weather reports, storm reports, and other relevant information. We may rely on information from a governmental weather authority or another reliable source.

**T.13** A Named Storm Period applies without regard to whether you received notice of the Named Storm Designation. A Named Storm Period applies without regard to whether you were present at the residence premises.

**T.14** A Named Storm Period applies without regard to whether evacuation, shelter, or emergency measures were ordered. Such measures do not determine the cause of loss.

**T.15** We do not cover loss merely because it occurs during a Named Storm Period. We cover loss during a Named Storm Period only when the loss is otherwise covered under this policy.

**T.16** We do not cover loss caused by a peril that is excluded under this policy. This provision does not make an excluded loss covered.

**T.17** We do not cover loss that is not direct physical loss to property covered under this policy. This provision does not expand the property covered under this policy.

**T.18** The cause of loss must be established from the facts of the loss. The Named Storm Period does not by itself establish the cause of loss.

**T.19** More than a single cause may contribute to a loss. We determine coverage in accordance with the policy provisions that apply to those causes.

**T.20** A change in weather conditions does not end a Named Storm Period. The Named Storm Period ends only as stated in this provision.

**T.21** A later Named Storm Designation may result in another Named Storm Period. Each Named Storm Period is determined separately.

**T.22** We may request records relating to repairs, estimates, photographs, and receipts. An insured must provide requested records that are reasonably available.

**T.23** An insured must promptly report a loss to us. Delay in reporting may affect our ability to determine the cause and extent of loss.

**T.24** An insured must submit to examination when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer questions concerning the loss truthfully.

**T.25** We may request that an insured provide a recorded statement. The statement must concern facts relevant to the loss.

**T.26** A dispute concerning the existence or duration of a Named Storm Period does not relieve an insured of policy duties. An insured must continue to comply with applicable policy conditions.

**T.27** Our determination concerning a Named Storm Period is based on the information available to us. We may revise that determination when additional reliable information becomes available.

**T.28** This provision applies to any loss for which a Named Storm Period is relevant. All other policy terms remain applicable.

## T.4 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal

**T.1** We may cancel this "policy" only as permitted by applicable law and this endorsement. A "cancellation" ends coverage before the policy would otherwise end.

**T.2** We will provide written "notice" of cancellation when cancellation is permitted. The notice will state the effective date of cancellation.

**T.3** We may deliver a notice of cancellation to you or mail it to your last known mailing address. We may also provide notice by any method permitted by applicable law.

**T.4** A notice of cancellation for "nonpayment of premium" must be mailed or delivered at least ten days before the effective date of cancellation. The notice will state that cancellation is based on nonpayment of premium.

**T.5** A notice of cancellation for a reason other than nonpayment of premium must be mailed or delivered at least thirty days before the effective date of cancellation. The notice will state the reason for cancellation.

**T.6** We may cancel this policy if you do not pay premium when due. Payment must be made in the manner we require.

**T.7** We may cancel this policy if you obtained coverage through a "material misrepresentation" or omission. The misrepresentation or omission must be relevant to our acceptance or continuation of the risk.

**T.8** We may cancel this policy if an insured commits "fraud" in connection with this policy. Fraud includes a deliberate false statement or deliberate concealment of a material fact.

**T.9** We may cancel this policy if there is an "increase in hazard" within your knowledge or control. The increase in hazard must materially affect the property or the risk of loss.

**T.10** We may cancel this policy if you fail to comply with a material policy condition. We will identify the applicable condition in the notice of cancellation.

**T.11** We may cancel this policy if the insured location is used in a manner materially different from the use disclosed to us. The changed use must be relevant to the coverage provided.

**T.12** We may cancel this policy if the insured location is vacant or unoccupied in a manner that materially affects the risk. We will state the applicable basis for cancellation in our notice.

**T.13** We may cancel this policy if the insured location becomes subject to conditions that materially increase the possibility of loss. Such conditions include circumstances affecting the construction, occupancy, maintenance, or protection of the property.

**T.14** We may cancel this policy if an insured refuses to permit an inspection that is reasonably necessary for underwriting or loss prevention. We will not require an inspection that is prohibited by applicable law.

**T.15** We may cancel this policy if an insured refuses to provide material information reasonably requested by us. The requested information must relate to the property, the risk, coverage, or premium.

**T.16** We may cancel this policy if an insured fails to cooperate with our investigation of information material to underwriting. Our request for cooperation must be reasonable.

**T.17** We may cancel this policy if continuation of coverage would violate applicable law. The notice will describe the legal basis for cancellation to the extent permitted by law.

**T.18** We may cancel this policy if the property cannot be insured because of a condition that materially affects insurability. We will state the condition that supports cancellation.

**T.19** A notice of cancellation will identify this policy and the insured location. The notice will state the effective date and reason for cancellation.

**T.20** A notice of cancellation will be effective even if you do not receive it, if we properly mail or deliver it as required by law. Proof of mailing or delivery will be sufficient evidence that notice was given.

**T.21** Your failure to receive a notice of cancellation does not invalidate cancellation when notice is properly provided. This provision applies only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**T.22** Cancellation will take effect at the time stated in our notice. Coverage will not apply to a loss occurring after cancellation takes effect.

**T.23** Cancellation does not affect a claim for loss that occurred before cancellation takes effect. All policy conditions remain applicable to that claim.

**T.24** Cancellation does not waive any right we have regarding a claim, premium, misrepresentation, fraud, or other policy matter. Your rights are also subject to the terms of this policy and applicable law.

**T.25** If we cancel this policy, we will return any "unearned premium" due to you. We may deduct any amount you owe us that applicable law permits us to deduct.

**T.26** Return of unearned premium does not affect the effective date of cancellation. Cancellation remains effective as stated in our notice.

**T.27** If you request cancellation, you must provide notice to us in a manner we accept. Your request must identify the policy and the requested cancellation date.

**T.28** A request by you to cancel this policy does not require our consent. We may require information reasonably necessary to process the request.

**T.29** If you request cancellation, coverage ends on the date stated in your request or on the date we receive your request, whichever is later. We may honor another effective date if permitted by applicable law.

**T.30** If you request cancellation, we will return any unearned premium due to you. The return premium will be calculated as required by applicable law and this policy.

**T.31** We may provide notice of cancellation to a "lienholder" shown in our records when required by law or by the lienholder's interest. Notice to a lienholder does not create coverage beyond the terms of this policy.

**T.32** A lienholder must provide us with a current mailing address if it seeks notice from us. We are not responsible for a notice that cannot be delivered because the lienholder did not provide current information.

**T.33** We may provide notice to an authorized representative acting for you. Notice to an authorized representative is not a substitute for notice to you when applicable law requires notice to you.

**T.34** An insured must promptly notify us of a change in mailing address. We may rely on the most recent mailing address shown in our records.

**T.35** An insured must promptly notify us of a change affecting ownership of the insured location. A change in ownership may affect eligibility, coverage, or premium.

**T.36** An insured must promptly notify us of a change affecting occupancy or use of the insured location. We may review the change to determine whether coverage may continue.

**T.37** We may rescind this policy if permitted by applicable law. Rescission is distinct from cancellation and may affect coverage from the inception of the policy.

**T.38** We will provide any notice required for rescission by applicable law. Our exercise of a right of rescission does not limit any other right available to us.

**T.39** We may elect not to cancel this policy despite the existence of a condition that could support cancellation. Our decision not to cancel does not waive our right to act upon a later occurrence or continuing condition.

**T.40** Acceptance of premium after we issue a notice of cancellation does not reinstate this policy unless we expressly agree in writing. Any reinstatement will be subject to terms we state in writing.

**T.41** We may reinstate this policy after cancellation if we agree to do so in writing. Reinstatement does not cover a loss occurring while the policy was cancelled unless we expressly state otherwise in writing.

**T.42** We may require payment of all premium due before we reinstate this policy. We may also require information relevant to the risk before reinstatement.

**T.43** We may amend the terms applicable to reinstatement if permitted by applicable law. An amendment will be effective only if we provide it in writing.

**T.44** We may decide not to continue this policy at its expiration. A decision not to continue coverage is a "nonrenewal".

**T.45** We will provide written notice of nonrenewal when required by applicable law. The notice will state that we do not intend to renew this policy.

**T.46** A notice of nonrenewal must be mailed or delivered at least forty-five days before the policy expires. The notice will state the reason for nonrenewal when required by applicable law.

**T.47** We may deliver a notice of nonrenewal to you or mail it to your last known mailing address. We may also provide notice by any method permitted by applicable law.

**T.48** A notice of nonrenewal will identify this policy and the insured location. The notice will state the date on which coverage will end.

**T.49** Nonrenewal does not affect coverage for a loss occurring before the policy expires. All policy conditions remain applicable to that loss.

**T.50** We may provide notice of nonrenewal to a lienholder when required by applicable law or by the lienholder's interest. Notice to a lienholder does not extend the policy expiration date.

**T.51** We may offer to renew this policy on terms different from the expiring policy if permitted by applicable law. A "renewal offer" may include revised coverage, conditions, deductibles, or premium.

**T.52** A renewal offer is not effective unless we provide it in writing. You must accept a renewal offer in the manner we require.

**T.53** We may decline to renew this policy because of changes in the property, occupancy, use, loss experience, underwriting information, or eligibility. We will provide notice as required by applicable law.

**T.54** We may decline to renew this policy if premium is not paid as required for renewal. Failure to pay renewal premium does not require us to continue coverage after expiration.

**T.55** We may withdraw a renewal offer if information material to the risk was incorrect, incomplete, or changed before renewal becomes effective. We will provide any notice required by applicable law.

**T.56** An insured must provide truthful and complete information in connection with a request for renewal. We may rely on information provided by an insured or an authorized representative.

**T.57** Cancellation, rescission, nonrenewal, and renewal are subject to applicable law. If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with applicable law, we will apply the provision in a manner that complies with applicable law.

## T.5 — Claims Handling

**T.1** You must give us prompt notice of a "claim." Notice must include information reasonably available to you concerning the loss.

**T.2** An "insured" must protect property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property.

**T.3** You must retain damaged "covered property" for our inspection when reasonably possible. You must allow us to inspect the property and examine the cause of loss.

**T.4** We may request a signed and sworn "proof of loss." The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary for us to evaluate the claim.

**T.5** After we receive notice of a claim, we will acknowledge receipt within fifteen days. Our acknowledgment may identify information we need to investigate the claim.

**T.6** We may request records, photographs, estimates, receipts, statements, or other information reasonably related to the claim. You must provide requested items that are reasonably available to you.

**T.7** We will notify you whether we accept or reject the claim within ten business days after we receive all requested items. Our notice may explain the basis for our decision.

**T.8** We will pay an "accepted claim" within five business days after we notify you that we accept it. Payment may be made to you, a mortgagee, or another person entitled to payment.

**T.9** You must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. Cooperation includes providing truthful information concerning the loss.

**T.10** You must show us damaged property when we reasonably request it. You must permit us to take samples, photographs, measurements, and recordings reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim.

**T.11** We may inspect repaired property when the inspection is reasonably necessary to verify claimed damage. You must preserve records relating to repairs and replacement property.

**T.12** You must provide us access to the residence premises when access is reasonably necessary to investigate a claim. You must use reasonable efforts to obtain access from persons who control property affected by the loss.

**T.13** We may contact persons who may have information concerning the claim. You must not prevent us from obtaining information that you are required to provide.

**T.14** We may request an examination under oath of an insured whose knowledge is material to the claim. An insured must submit to the examination and answer material questions truthfully.

**T.15** We may request that an examination under oath be recorded. An insured must sign the transcript or other record when it accurately states the examination testimony.

**T.16** You must provide records supporting the ownership, condition, value, and repair of claimed property. You must provide records concerning other insurance that may apply to the loss.

**T.17** We may request information concerning the occupancy, use, maintenance, or condition of property involved in the claim. You must provide information reasonably available to you.

**T.18** You must identify persons with knowledge of the loss when we reasonably request that information. You must provide contact information that is reasonably available to you.

**T.19** We may request estimates or invoices for repair or replacement of damaged property. You must not incur unnecessary expenses or increase the amount of loss.

**T.20** You must take reasonable steps to preserve evidence relating to the cause and extent of loss. You must not alter, discard, or destroy material evidence without first giving us a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.
