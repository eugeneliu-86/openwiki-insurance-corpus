# HO 01 45 Texas Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2022-01)

Texas. Attaches to HO-3 and amends it to comply with Texas law. Effective for policies with an effective date on or after 2022-01-01. Where this endorsement conflicts with the form to which it attaches, this endorsement governs.

## T.1 Separate Windstorm and Hail Deductible

A separate windstorm and hail deductible applies to loss caused by windstorm or hail, stated in the Declarations as a percentage of the Coverage A limit of liability. The percentage may not be less than one percent and may not exceed five percent, except in the seacoast territories described in T.6 where the maximum is ten percent. This provision implements Texas Department of Insurance Bulletin B-2021-08.

Where a loss is caused by windstorm or hail, only the windstorm and hail deductible is applied. The all-other-perils deductible shown in the Declarations is not also applied to that loss, and Section I — Conditions S.5 of the form is amended accordingly.

Where a single occurrence causes loss from both a windstorm or hail peril and another covered peril, and the damage attributable to each can be separately determined, each deductible applies to its respective portion of the loss. Where the damage cannot be separately determined, only the larger single deductible applies to the entire loss.

## T.2 Notice of Deductible Increase

We will give you written notice at least thirty days before the renewal effective date of any increase in the windstorm and hail deductible percentage. If we fail to give that notice, the increase does not take effect for that renewal term and the prior percentage continues to apply.

## T.3 Named Storm Period

Where the Declarations state that the windstorm and hail deductible applies on a per-named-storm basis, the named storm period begins when the National Hurricane Center names the storm and ends seventy-two hours after that designation is discontinued. All windstorm and hail loss occurring within that period is treated as a single occurrence for deductible purposes.

## T.4 Time to Bring Action

Section I — Conditions S.4 of the form is amended to extend the period in which an action may be brought against us from two years to two years and one day after the date of loss.

## T.5 Prompt Payment

We will acknowledge receipt of a claim within fifteen days, and will approve or deny the claim in writing within fifteen business days after receiving all items we reasonably requested. Where we approve a claim, payment will be made within five business days of that notice.

## T.6 Seacoast Territories

For the purposes of T.1, the seacoast territories are the first tier coastal counties and those portions of Harris County designated in the Texas Department of Insurance territory definitions in effect on the effective date of this endorsement.

## T.7 Windstorm and Hail Exclusion Option

Where the insured obtains windstorm and hail coverage through the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association, windstorm and hail may be excluded from this policy by attaching the applicable exclusion endorsement and obtaining the insured's signed acknowledgment at binding. Where windstorm and hail is so excluded, T.1 does not apply.

All other provisions of the policy apply.
