# HO 01 17 Louisiana Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2020-09)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

This endorsement modifies your policy as provided in this endorsement. We will apply this endorsement to property and loss subject to Louisiana law.

The term "policy" means the contract to which this endorsement is attached. The term "endorsement" means this amendatory provision and any amendment stated within it.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of the policy, this endorsement controls. A policy provision that does not conflict with this endorsement remains applicable.

We apply the terms of this endorsement only to coverage provided by the policy. We do not provide coverage that is not provided by the policy unless this endorsement expressly provides that coverage.

An insured must comply with all duties that remain applicable under the policy. An insured must also comply with any duty imposed by this endorsement.

We determine coverage under the policy as amended by this endorsement. We determine no coverage solely from a caption, descriptive statement, or administrative notation.

Words and phrases used in this endorsement have the meanings assigned by the policy unless this endorsement assigns a different meaning. A meaning assigned by this endorsement controls when applied to this endorsement.

The term "insured" has the meaning assigned by the policy. The term "covered property" has the meaning assigned by the policy.

We apply exclusions, conditions, and limitations of the policy unless this endorsement changes them. We do not cover loss excluded by the policy unless this endorsement expressly changes that exclusion.

A limitation stated in this endorsement applies in addition to any applicable limitation in the policy. We apply the limitation that most restricts coverage when applicable provisions address the same loss.

A deductible applicable under the policy remains applicable unless this endorsement changes its application. The "AOP deductible floor" applies as provided by the policy and this endorsement.

An insured must provide information, records, and cooperation required by the policy or this endorsement. We may request information reasonably necessary to determine our obligations under the policy as amended.

An insured must provide a sworn statement when required by the policy. The "sworn proof deadline" applies as provided by the policy and this endorsement.

We determine the amount of loss under the policy as amended by this endorsement. The "insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement" applies as provided by the policy and this endorsement.

We do not waive a policy condition by investigating a loss, requesting information, or discussing a claim. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

An insured must preserve rights of recovery as required by the policy. We retain any right of recovery available to us under the policy and applicable law.

If a provision of this endorsement is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain effective to the extent permitted by applicable law. We will interpret an unenforceable provision to preserve its lawful effect when permitted.

We apply this endorsement in a manner consistent with applicable law. A provision of this endorsement that is more favorable to an insured than applicable law does not reduce any right required by applicable law.

An insured may not assign rights under this endorsement except as permitted by the policy or applicable law. An assignment that is permitted under applicable law remains subject to the policy as amended.

We may enforce any condition, exclusion, limitation, or duty available under the policy as amended by this endorsement. Our failure to enforce a provision on any occasion does not prevent later enforcement.

This endorsement applies to a loss occurring while the policy is in effect. We do not cover loss occurring outside the applicable policy period.

All terms of the policy remain unchanged except as modified by this endorsement. This endorsement is part of the policy.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** The "windstorm and hail deductible" applies to covered direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail. We will reduce the amount otherwise payable for that loss by the applicable deductible.

**T.2** The minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage is two percent. We will not apply a windstorm and hail deductible that is less than that percentage.

**T.3** The maximum windstorm and hail deductible percentage is five percent. We will not apply a windstorm and hail deductible that exceeds that percentage.

**T.4** The applicable deductible is determined from the terms governing your policy at the time of loss. The deductible applies whether the loss is caused by windstorm, hail, or wind-driven rain entering through an opening caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.5** We apply the windstorm and hail deductible to a "covered loss" involving covered property. A covered loss is direct physical loss for which coverage is provided under your policy.

**T.6** We apply the deductible before determining the amount we will pay for covered loss. The deductible does not increase any limit of liability that applies to the loss.

**T.7** We will not pay for the portion of covered loss that is within the applicable deductible. You remain responsible for that portion of the loss.

**T.8** The deductible applies to loss caused directly by "windstorm." Windstorm includes wind, gusts, and wind-driven debris.

**T.9** The deductible applies to loss caused directly by "hail." Hail includes impact by hailstones and resulting direct physical damage.

**T.10** Windstorm or hail need not be the sole cause of covered loss for the deductible to apply. The deductible applies when windstorm or hail contributes directly to covered loss.

**T.11** We determine whether windstorm or hail caused covered loss from the facts available to us. We may consider weather reports, inspections, photographs, and other reliable information.

**T.12** The deductible applies to covered loss occurring during a storm or weather event involving windstorm or hail. The deductible also applies to later discovered damage caused by that event.

**T.13** The deductible applies to damage caused by wind-driven rain only when the rain enters through an opening created by windstorm or hail. We do not cover rain damage unless coverage otherwise applies under your policy.

**T.14** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail before, during, or after precipitation. The presence or absence of precipitation does not determine whether the deductible applies.

**T.15** The deductible applies separately from any deductible that may apply to a loss not caused by windstorm or hail. We will apply the deductible that governs the covered cause of loss.

**T.16** When a covered loss results from windstorm or hail and another covered cause of loss, we will apply the windstorm and hail deductible to the portion of loss caused by windstorm or hail. We will apply any other applicable deductible to the remaining covered loss.

**T.17** We will not combine deductibles to create payment for loss that is otherwise within an applicable deductible. We will determine payment under the terms that apply to each covered cause of loss.

**T.18** A deductible applies to the total amount of covered property damage arising from the same "occurrence." An occurrence is an event or a series of related events causing covered loss.

**T.19** Related damage caused by the same windstorm or hail event is treated as arising from the same occurrence. This applies even if damage is discovered at different times.

**T.20** Separate weather events may constitute separate occurrences. We will determine whether events are related from the facts and circumstances of the loss.

**T.21** The deductible applies to covered loss to the dwelling, other structures, and personal property when coverage applies. The deductible does not create coverage for property that is not covered.

**T.22** The deductible applies to covered loss involving property at the residence premises. It also applies to covered loss involving property when coverage is extended by your policy.

**T.23** The deductible applies to loss settled on a replacement cost basis. It also applies to loss settled on an actual cash value basis.

**T.24** The deductible applies before we determine payment for repair, replacement, or cash settlement. We may settle covered loss by any method permitted under your policy.

**T.25** The deductible applies to covered loss regardless of whether repairs are completed. Completion of repairs does not eliminate the deductible.

**T.26** The deductible applies to covered loss regardless of whether you make a claim for all damaged property. We may apply the deductible to the total covered loss arising from the occurrence.

**T.27** An insured must provide prompt notice of loss involving windstorm or hail. An insured must protect covered property from further damage after the loss.

**T.28** An insured must make reasonable temporary repairs necessary to protect covered property. We will consider reasonable costs incurred for that purpose as provided by your policy.

**T.29** An insured must retain damaged property when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property before it is repaired, replaced, or discarded.

**T.30** An insured must provide records, photographs, receipts, and other information reasonably requested by us. Those materials must relate to the cause, extent, and value of the claimed loss.

**T.31** An insured must cooperate with our investigation of a windstorm or hail claim. Cooperation includes providing access to covered property and relevant information.

**T.32** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning a claimed loss. We may deny coverage as permitted by your policy when material concealment or misrepresentation occurs.

**T.33** We may inspect the damaged property and surrounding area. Our inspection does not waive any policy term or condition.

**T.34** We may obtain weather information relevant to the claimed loss. We may use that information in determining whether windstorm or hail caused the damage.

**T.35** We may request an examination under oath when permitted by your policy. An insured must comply with that request as required by the policy.

**T.36** We may require a signed statement describing the loss. The statement must identify the damaged property and the known cause of damage.

**T.37** We will determine the amount of covered loss after applying all applicable policy terms. We will then subtract the applicable windstorm and hail deductible.

**T.38** We do not cover loss that is excluded under your policy. The application of a deductible does not affect an exclusion.

**T.39** We do not cover loss beyond the applicable limit of liability. The deductible does not increase that limit.

**T.40** We do not cover loss for which you have no insurable interest. The deductible applies only after we determine that coverage exists.

**T.41** We do not cover costs incurred solely to improve property beyond its condition immediately before the loss. The deductible applies to covered repair or replacement costs.

**T.42** We do not cover damage caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective maintenance when that damage is excluded under your policy. The deductible does not restore coverage for excluded damage.

**T.43** We do not cover wear, deterioration, or latent defect when that loss is excluded under your policy. Damage caused by windstorm or hail remains subject to the applicable deductible when covered.

**T.44** We do not cover loss caused by flood when flood coverage is not provided by your policy. The presence of windstorm or hail does not create coverage for excluded flood loss.

**T.45** We do not cover loss caused by surface water when surface water damage is excluded under your policy. We will determine the cause of damage from the available evidence.

**T.46** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement when earth movement is excluded under your policy. Windstorm or hail damage that is otherwise covered remains subject to the deductible.

**T.47** We do not cover loss caused by neglect when neglect is excluded under your policy. An insured must use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property after loss.

**T.48** The deductible applies to covered additional repair costs caused by windstorm or hail. Those costs must be necessary to repair covered direct physical damage.

**T.49** The deductible applies to covered debris removal arising from windstorm or hail damage. Debris removal remains subject to the terms that govern that coverage.

**T.50** The deductible applies to covered reasonable repairs made solely to protect property from further windstorm or hail damage. Those repairs must be necessary and reasonable.

**T.51** The deductible applies to covered loss of use resulting from windstorm or hail damage when such coverage is provided. We will determine payment under the loss of use terms of your policy.

**T.52** The deductible applies to covered loss involving detached structures. Coverage for detached structures remains subject to all applicable policy terms.

**T.53** The deductible applies to covered loss involving personal property. Coverage for personal property remains subject to all applicable policy terms.

**T.54** The deductible applies to covered loss involving building materials intended for use in construction or repair. The materials must otherwise qualify as covered property.

**T.55** The deductible applies to covered loss involving trees, shrubs, or other plants when coverage applies. We will not pay more than the coverage otherwise provides.

**T.56** The deductible applies to covered loss involving awnings, screens, storm shutters, and similar property when coverage applies. The deductible does not alter any special limitation applicable to that property.

**T.57** The deductible applies to covered damage caused by windborne objects. We will determine whether the object was propelled by windstorm from the evidence available.

**T.58** The deductible applies to covered damage caused by falling objects when windstorm or hail directly causes the object to fall. Other terms of your policy continue to apply.

**T.59** The deductible applies to covered damage caused by the weight of hail accumulation. We will determine whether the damage was caused directly by hail.

**T.60** The deductible applies to covered collapse loss when windstorm or hail is a covered cause of that loss. Coverage for collapse remains subject to all applicable policy terms.

**T.61** The deductible applies to covered water damage caused by windstorm or hail damage to a building. We will not apply the deductible to damage not caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.62** The deductible applies when windstorm damages a roof, wall, window, door, or other part of a building. It applies to resulting covered interior damage.

**T.63** The deductible applies when hail damages a roof, siding, window, skylight, or other covered property. It applies to resulting covered direct physical loss.

**T.64** The deductible applies regardless of the direction from which wind or hail strikes covered property. The deductible applies regardless of the location of the damage on the residence premises.

**T.65** The deductible applies regardless of whether visible exterior damage is present. We may determine covered loss from physical evidence and reliable information.

**T.66** The deductible applies regardless of whether damage is reported immediately after the weather event. Late reporting may affect our ability to determine the cause and extent of loss.

**T.67** An insured must take reasonable steps to document the condition of damaged property. An insured must preserve evidence relevant to the claim when reasonably possible.

**T.68** An insured must not authorize permanent repairs before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect the damage. Emergency measures necessary to protect property may be taken without delaying for inspection.

**T.69** An insured must provide invoices and proof of payment for claimed repair costs when reasonably requested by us. We may verify the reasonableness and necessity of those costs.

**T.70** An insured must notify us of known additional damage related to a windstorm or hail occurrence. We may inspect that damage before determining any additional payment.

**T.71** We will provide notice concerning this deductible as required by Louisiana bulletinas required by Louisiana bulletin (see Louisiana Bulletin LDI-2020-07 — Hurricane Deductible Notice and Named Storm Periods, B.2 Requirements). That notice does not alter the terms of this endorsement.

**T.72** The windstorm and hail deductible applies only to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. It does not apply to loss caused solely by a cause of loss to which the deductible does not apply.

**T.73** If the windstorm and hail deductible conflicts with another deductible provision, we will apply the deductible that is specifically applicable to windstorm or hail loss. All other policy terms remain unchanged.

**T.74** If any provision of this endorsement is determined to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. We will administer the remaining provisions to the fullest extent permitted by law.

**T.75** This endorsement changes your policy only as stated in this section. All other policy provisions remain applicable to covered loss.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** A "named storm designation" means a designation that identifies a storm by name. A "Named Storm Period" means the period during which this provision applies to a named storm.

**T.2** The "Named Storm Period" begins when the named storm designation takes effect. The "Named Storm Period" continues for seventy-two hours after the named storm designation ends.

**T.3** We determine whether a named storm designation applies from reliable weather information. Such information may include information issued by a governmental weather authority.

**T.4** We determine whether direct physical loss occurred during a "Named Storm Period" from the facts surrounding the loss. The date that an insured discovers damage does not alone determine when the loss occurred.

**T.5** Damage caused by wind, rain, storm surge, waves, tidal water, or debris may be considered in determining whether a loss is related to a named storm. We may consider the sequence of weather events affecting the property.

**T.6** A loss that begins during a "Named Storm Period" remains subject to this provision despite continued damage after that period ends. Damage that is unrelated to the named storm is evaluated separately.

**T.7** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss that may be related to a named storm. The notice must describe the damaged property and the known circumstances of the loss.

**T.8** An insured must protect covered property from further damage. Reasonable measures taken to protect covered property must be documented when practicable.

**T.9** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when practicable. An insured must allow us a reasonable opportunity to inspect the property before damaged property is removed or discarded.

**T.10** An insured must cooperate with us in investigating a loss that may be related to a named storm. This cooperation includes providing available weather information, photographs, receipts, and repair information.

**T.11** An insured must identify the location of damaged property. An insured must provide information concerning the condition of the property before and after the reported loss.

**T.12** We may inspect the property and obtain information needed to determine the cause and extent of loss. Our inspection does not waive any condition or exclusion.

**T.13** We may request records that show ownership, value, maintenance, repair, or condition of damaged property. An insured must provide requested records that are available to the insured.

**T.14** Emergency repairs do not establish coverage for a reported loss. Payment for emergency measures does not establish that we will pay for other damage.

**T.15** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to a reported loss. This duty applies to information concerning the cause, timing, condition, and extent of damage.

**T.16** We may consider weather records, inspection findings, witness information, and other reliable evidence in evaluating a reported loss. We may consider evidence showing that damage resulted from causes unrelated to the named storm.

**T.17** The presence of a named storm designation does not establish that all damage was caused by the named storm. An insured must establish that claimed damage resulted from a covered cause of loss.

**T.18** Damage that existed before the "Named Storm Period" is not treated as damage caused by the named storm merely because it is discovered during that period. We may consider evidence of prior damage or deterioration.

**T.19** Repairs or replacement completed after the "Named Storm Period" do not establish when the loss occurred. We may consider repair records in determining the cause and extent of damage.

**T.20** A payment made for a loss related to a named storm does not waive our right to investigate other claimed damage. A payment does not establish coverage for damage not included in that payment.

**T.21** This provision does not create coverage for a loss that is otherwise excluded. This provision does not change the requirement that direct physical loss be caused by a covered cause of loss.

**T.22** We may rely on information available when we evaluate a reported loss. We may revise our evaluation when reliable information becomes available.

**T.23** An insured must submit information requested by us concerning a reported loss. Failure to comply with this duty may affect coverage to the extent permitted by law.

**T.24** We will apply this provision according to the facts of the reported loss. Our determination is subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions that apply to the loss.

**T.28** If we accept payment after cancellation takes effect, acceptance does not create coverage unless we agree in writing. We may condition reinstatement on information we reasonably require.

**T.29** Reinstatement may be subject to terms we specify in writing. Coverage does not resume unless we confirm reinstatement in writing.

**T.30** A lapse in coverage does not alter the effective date stated in a cancellation notice. We have no duty to provide coverage during a lapse unless we agree in writing.

**T.31** You must cooperate with us concerning cancellation and premium adjustment. You must provide information we reasonably request to determine premium earned under this policy.

**T.32** We may correct a clerical error in a cancellation notice. A correction will be effective only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**T.33** If applicable law requires a different cancellation procedure, we will follow that procedure. Any policy provision inconsistent with applicable law is amended to conform to that law.

**T.34** We may elect not to renew this policy by giving written "nonrenewal" notice. Nonrenewal means that coverage will not continue after the expiration date of this policy.

**T.35** We will mail or deliver written notice of nonrenewal at least thirty days before the expiration date of this policy. The notice will state that we do not intend to renew this policy.

**T.36** A "notice of nonrenewal" will identify this policy and the named insured shown in our records. The notice will state the date on which this policy expires.

**T.37** We may mail notice of nonrenewal to the mailing address shown in our records. We may deliver notice of nonrenewal to you or to your authorized representative.

**T.38** Notice of nonrenewal mailed to the address shown in our records is sufficient notice. You must notify us promptly of a change in your mailing address.

**T.39** Nonrenewal does not cancel coverage before the expiration date of this policy. Coverage remains subject to all policy terms until expiration.

**T.40** We may elect not to renew this policy for any reason permitted by applicable law. We will comply with any notice requirement imposed by applicable law.

**T.41** We may elect not to renew this policy because of underwriting changes. We may elect not to renew this policy because the risk no longer meets our eligibility requirements.

**T.42** We may elect not to renew this policy because of loss history. We may elect not to renew this policy because of a change in the condition or use of the insured location.

**T.43** We may elect not to renew this policy because of nonpayment of premium. We may elect not to renew this policy because of fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation.

**T.44** Nonrenewal does not waive any premium due to us. Nonrenewal does not waive any right or defense we have under this policy.

**T.45** We may offer renewal on terms that differ from the terms of this policy. Your acceptance of a renewal offer is subject to payment of premium and satisfaction of our stated conditions.

**T.46** A renewal offer does not obligate us to provide coverage beyond the terms stated in that offer. We may withdraw a renewal offer as permitted by applicable law.

**T.47** A "material misrepresentation" is a statement or omission that would affect our decision to issue, continue, rate, or insure the risk. We will determine materiality based on the facts available to us.

**T.48** Your failure to receive a notice does not invalidate notice properly mailed or delivered in accordance with this policy and applicable law. We may rely on the mailing address shown in our records.

**T.56** No act by us waives cancellation or nonrenewal rights unless we agree in writing. No act by you changes the effective date of cancellation or nonrenewal unless we agree in writing.

**T.57** This provision applies to cancellation and nonrenewal of this policy. We will administer cancellation and nonrenewal in accordance with applicable law.

## T.5 — Claims Handling

**T.1** We will acknowledge receipt of a "claim" within fourteen days after we receive it. Our acknowledgment may be made by oral or written communication.

**T.2** We may request information reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim. An insured must provide requested information that is available to that insured.

**T.3** We may request a signed "proof of loss" when it is reasonably necessary to investigate or adjust a claim. An insured must provide a proof of loss that states the information we reasonably request.

**T.4** We will identify the information or materials we request with reasonable clarity. We may request additional information when information previously provided is incomplete, inconsistent, or insufficient.

**T.5** After we receive all requested items, we will accept or reject the claim within thirty business days. Our decision may be communicated by oral or written notice.

**T.6** We will pay an accepted claim within thirty business days. Payment may be made to you, a person legally entitled to receive payment, or another payee authorized by the policy.

**T.7** We may investigate the cause, extent, and value of claimed loss. An insured must cooperate with our investigation.

**T.8** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property that is reasonably available for inspection. An insured must preserve damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**T.9** We may photograph, measure, test, or otherwise examine property relevant to a claim. We will conduct an examination in a manner reasonably calculated to avoid unnecessary damage.

**T.10** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to protect covered property when that failure contributes to the loss.
