# HO 01 12 Illinois Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2015-02)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

This endorsement applies to matters expressly addressed in its provisions. We apply those provisions to you and us.

Where a provision of this endorsement conflicts with an otherwise applicable provision, this endorsement controls. We give effect to both provisions when they can be applied consistently.

A provision not expressly changed by this endorsement remains applicable. No change applies beyond its stated scope.

We construe this endorsement as part of the applicable insurance contract. Its provisions are subject to Illinois law.

If Illinois law requires a result different from a provision of this endorsement, we apply the required result. The remaining provisions continue to apply to the extent permitted by law.

We do not interpret this endorsement to provide coverage not expressly granted. We do not interpret this endorsement to remove an exclusion unless it expressly does so.

Terms used in this endorsement have the meanings assigned by applicable provisions, unless this endorsement provides a different meaning. A meaning provided by this endorsement controls for purposes of this endorsement.

Words and phrases must be read according to their plain meaning unless they are defined terms. A defined term retains its applicable meaning throughout this endorsement.

The use of a singular word includes its plural form when the context requires. The use of a plural word includes its singular form when the context requires.

A word denoting a particular gender includes any gender when the context requires. A word denoting a person includes an organization when the context permits.

The invalidity or unenforceability of a provision does not affect the validity or enforceability of any other provision. We apply the remaining provisions to the fullest extent permitted by law.

Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision later. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

No action or omission by an insured creates a waiver by us. No action or omission by us creates a waiver of a duty imposed on an insured.

Our rights and remedies under this endorsement are cumulative. The exercise of a right or remedy does not prevent us from exercising another applicable right or remedy.

An insured must satisfy any duty expressly imposed by this endorsement. Compliance with such duty is a condition of coverage when this endorsement so provides.

An insured must provide information required by this endorsement when we request it. We may rely on information provided by an insured in applying this endorsement.

Any provision concerning the "minimum Section I deductible" must be read consistently with this endorsement. We apply that provision only as permitted by its applicable terms.

Any provision concerning the "sworn statement deadline" must be read consistently with this endorsement. An insured must comply with any duty associated with that provision when it applies.

Any provision concerning the "coinsurance threshold" must be read consistently with this endorsement. We apply that provision only when its applicable conditions are satisfied.

This endorsement does not alter a right or duty except as expressly stated. A right or duty affected by this endorsement is governed by the terms of this endorsement.

This endorsement takes effect according to its terms. We apply it only to the extent it is applicable.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** An applicable "deductible" applies to each "covered loss" caused by "windstorm" or "hail". We will subtract the deductible from the amount otherwise payable for that covered loss.

**T.2** This deductible applies only to "direct physical loss" to covered property. The deductible does not create coverage for property or causes of loss that are not otherwise covered.

**T.3** We will not pay the portion of a covered loss that is within the applicable deductible. We will pay the amount of covered loss exceeding that deductible, subject to all applicable terms and limits.

**T.4** A separate deductible applies to each "occurrence" that causes covered loss. Damage arising from the same occurrence will be treated as part of that occurrence.

**T.5** This deductible applies when windstorm causes covered loss, whether or not hail also contributes to the loss. This deductible also applies when hail causes covered loss, whether or not windstorm also contributes to the loss.

**T.6** The application of this deductible depends on the cause of the direct physical loss. The presence of windstorm or hail near the residence premises does not alone establish that this deductible applies.

**T.7** We will determine whether windstorm or hail caused covered loss from the available facts. Our determination may include the nature, location, and pattern of damage.

**T.8** This deductible applies to covered property damaged by the same windstorm or hail occurrence. We will not apply separate deductibles merely because the damaged property consists of different items.

**T.9** This deductible applies to covered loss involving property owned by an insured. It also applies to covered loss involving property for which an insured has a covered interest.

**T.10** We will apply this deductible before determining the amount we owe for covered loss. We will not apply the deductible to an amount that is not covered.

**T.11** The deductible applies to the total covered loss arising from the applicable occurrence. We will not divide the loss into separate claims solely to avoid application of the deductible.

**T.12** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss that may involve windstorm or hail. Notice must identify the damaged property and provide available information concerning the occurrence.

**T.13** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable measures taken to protect the property do not eliminate the applicable deductible.

**T.14** An insured must keep reasonable records of repair, replacement, and protective measures related to the loss. An insured must provide those records to us when requested.

**T.15** An insured must allow us to inspect damaged property as reasonably necessary. An insured must preserve damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**T.16** An insured must provide available estimates, invoices, photographs, and other records supporting the claim. We may request information reasonably necessary to evaluate the cause and amount of loss.

**T.17** Repair or replacement of damaged property does not waive our right to apply this deductible. Payment for repair or replacement remains subject to the applicable deductible.

**T.18** Emergency measures taken to protect covered property do not change the cause of loss. We will determine application of this deductible from the facts of the occurrence and the resulting damage.

**T.19** Our inspection of property does not constitute an admission of coverage. Our inspection also does not waive application of this deductible.

**T.20** Our request for information does not waive any term applicable to the claim. Our investigation of a windstorm or hail loss does not prevent us from applying this deductible.

**T.21** We do not cover loss that is otherwise excluded merely because windstorm or hail is involved. This deductible does not affect the application of any exclusion.

**T.22** We will calculate the covered amount of loss before subtracting the deductible. We will then reduce the amount payable by the applicable deductible.

**T.23** The deductible does not increase any limit applicable to covered loss. Our payment remains subject to all applicable limits.

**T.24** The deductible applies regardless of whether covered property is repaired, replaced, or left unrepaired. The method used to settle the loss does not eliminate the deductible.

**T.25** The deductible applies regardless of whether an insured obtains estimates from contractors. Differences among estimates do not determine whether the deductible applies.

**T.26** Amounts recovered from persons responsible for covered loss do not change the initial application of the deductible. We may account for recoveries in settling the claim as permitted by applicable law.

**T.27** Salvage value does not eliminate the deductible. We may consider salvage in determining the amount of covered loss.

**T.28** The deductible applies to covered damage discovered after the windstorm or hail occurrence. An insured must still comply with duties applicable to the loss.

**T.29** Later deterioration does not establish that windstorm or hail caused covered loss. We will evaluate the evidence connecting the damage to the claimed occurrence.

**T.30** Prior damage does not become covered solely because later windstorm or hail occurs. We will apply this deductible only to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.31** Wear, deterioration, settling, and faulty maintenance do not become windstorm or hail loss merely because they are discovered after an occurrence. We will determine the portion of damage caused by a covered peril.

**T.32** When covered and uncovered causes contribute to damage, we will determine the amount of covered loss under the applicable terms. We will apply this deductible to the covered amount attributable to windstorm or hail.

**T.33** An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the cause of loss. Cooperation includes providing reasonably available information concerning weather conditions, damage, repairs, and prior condition.

**T.34** An insured must submit to an examination when reasonably requested in connection with a claim. An insured must provide truthful answers concerning the claimed windstorm or hail loss.

**T.35** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we reasonably request it. The statement must identify the property, describe the damage, and state the amount claimed.

**T.36** We may inspect records relevant to the claimed loss when reasonably necessary. An insured must make those records available to us.

**T.37** We may obtain weather information, expert opinions, and other evidence relevant to the claimed occurrence. Such information may be used to determine whether this deductible applies.

**T.38** Visible damage does not by itself establish the cause of loss. The absence of visible damage does not by itself establish that no covered loss occurred.

**T.39** A claim for windstorm or hail loss must be presented honestly and in good faith. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or exaggerate facts material to the claim.

**T.40** Fraud or intentional misrepresentation concerning a claim may affect coverage as permitted by applicable law. Application of this deductible does not limit our rights concerning fraud or misrepresentation.

**T.41** Payment of any portion of a claim does not waive our right to apply this deductible. Payment also does not waive our right to investigate additional claimed damage.

**T.42** Our delay in inspecting property does not waive this deductible. An insured must continue to protect property from further damage while the claim is being investigated.

**T.43** We may request that damaged property be retained for inspection when reasonably necessary. Disposal of damaged property without reasonable opportunity for inspection may affect our ability to evaluate the claim.

**T.44** This deductible applies whether damage occurs to the structure, personal property, or other covered property. We will apply the deductible to the covered loss arising from the applicable occurrence.

**T.45** This deductible applies whether the damaged property is occupied or unoccupied at the time of loss. The status of occupancy does not by itself determine whether windstorm or hail caused the damage.

**T.46** This deductible applies whether the occurrence affects only a portion of the residence premises or affects a broader area. The extent of area affected does not alter the deductible.

**T.47** An insured must not make permanent repairs that prevent reasonable inspection unless necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must preserve reasonable evidence of the damage when making necessary repairs.

**T.48** Temporary repairs do not establish the amount of covered loss. We may consider the necessity and reasonableness of temporary repairs in evaluating the claim.

**T.49** We may pay an insured, a person authorized to receive payment, or another person with a covered interest in the property. Payment does not alter the amount of the applicable deductible.

**T.50** A dispute concerning the amount of loss does not suspend application of this deductible. The deductible will apply to the covered amount ultimately determined.

**T.51** A dispute concerning the cause of loss does not eliminate the deductible. We will apply this deductible if windstorm or hail is determined to have caused covered loss.

**T.52** This deductible remains applicable after we make a payment for damage arising from the occurrence. Additional covered damage from that occurrence will be evaluated with the loss already considered.

**T.53** We provide this notice as required by Illinois bulletinas required by Illinois bulletin (see Illinois Bulletin IDOI-2017-10 — Water Backup Coverage Disclosure, B.2 Requirements). This notice does not alter our obligations under applicable law.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** "Named Storm Period" means the time identified by us in connection with a "Named Storm." We may identify a Named Storm Period by notice to you.

**T.2** "Named Storm" means a weather event that has been assigned an official name by a governmental weather authority. A Named Storm includes conditions associated with that weather event.

**T.3** Our notice may identify the Named Storm and the area to which the Named Storm Period applies. We may communicate that notice by any reasonable means.

**T.4** A Named Storm Period applies only as stated in our notice. We may revise a notice when information concerning the Named Storm changes.

**T.5** The Named Storm Period does not create coverage for property or loss that is not otherwise covered. The Named Storm Period does not expand the causes of loss for which coverage is available.

**T.6** An insured must use reasonable care to protect property from further damage during a Named Storm Period. An insured must take reasonable action to reduce the extent of a covered loss.

**T.7** "Emergency protective measures" means reasonable actions taken to protect property from imminent damage or to prevent further damage after a loss. Emergency protective measures must be appropriate to the conditions presented.

**T.8** An insured must retain records supporting Emergency Protective Measures. Those records must include available receipts, invoices, photographs, and other evidence of the work performed.

**T.9** An insured must not abandon damaged property to us. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**T.10** An insured must not discard, alter, or destroy damaged property before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. This duty does not prevent Emergency Protective Measures.

**T.11** "Claim" means a request made to us for payment or other benefits arising from a loss. An insured must give us prompt notice of a Claim involving a Named Storm.

**T.12** The notice of Claim must state the known location of the loss and the known nature of the damage. An insured must provide available contact information that permits us to communicate concerning the Claim.

**T.13** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of a Claim. An insured must provide information that we reasonably request concerning the loss.

**T.14** We may inspect property that is involved in a Claim. An insured must permit reasonable access to the property and must assist us in locating damaged property.

**T.15** We may request photographs, video, estimates, invoices, receipts, repair records, and other documents relating to a Claim. An insured must provide requested materials that are available to the insured.

**T.16** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must produce relevant records in the insured's possession or control.

**T.17** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when reasonably requested by us. The inventory must describe the property and identify available information concerning its value and condition.

**T.18** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to use reasonable care to protect property after the loss. This exclusion applies only to the extent that the failure causes additional damage.

**T.19** We do not cover a Claim that is supported by material concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud by an insured. This exclusion applies to conduct relating to the Claim or the loss.

**T.20** An insured must not begin repairs that prevent us from determining the cause or extent of damage, except for Emergency Protective Measures. An insured must preserve available evidence of the condition of property before repairs are made.

**T.21** If access to property is restricted by a public authority, an insured must notify us when practical. An insured must cooperate with reasonable efforts to arrange inspection after access becomes available.

**T.22** Our inspection of property, request for information, or communication concerning a Claim does not waive any condition or exclusion. Our investigation does not constitute acceptance of coverage.

**T.23** A payment or offer of payment by us does not waive any right available to us. Our payment does not establish coverage for any other loss or damage.

**T.24** An insured must comply with these provisions during a Named Storm Period. Failure to comply may affect coverage only to the extent that we are prejudiced by the failure.

## T.5 — Claims Handling

**T.1** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. Notice must identify the insured, the property involved, and the circumstances known to the insured.

**T.2** We may request information reasonably necessary to investigate a "claim". An insured must cooperate with our investigation and provide requested information that is available to the insured.

**T.3** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property when practicable.

**T.4** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when practicable. An insured must not discard damaged property unless its retention would create an unsafe condition or we authorize its disposal.

**T.5** We will acknowledge receipt of a claim within fifteen days days. After we receive all "requested items", we will accept or reject the claim within thirty business days business days. We will pay an "accepted claim" within thirty business days business days.

**T.6** Our acknowledgement of a claim does not constitute acceptance of coverage. We may investigate the facts, the cause of loss, and the extent of damage before making a coverage determination.

**T.7** We may inspect the property that is the subject of a claim. An insured must permit us reasonable access to the property for inspection, evaluation, and documentation.

**T.8** We may photograph, measure, test, sample, or otherwise examine property involved in a loss. We will conduct such activities in a manner reasonably calculated to avoid unnecessary damage.

**T.9** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the transcript of the examination if it is accurately recorded.

**T.10** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request. An insured must authorize us to obtain records that are relevant to the claim when the insured has authority to provide that authorization.

**T.11** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when we request it. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for the property claimed.

**T.12** An insured must provide receipts, bills, estimates, photographs, and other supporting documents that are reasonably available. We may consider the absence of reasonably available supporting documents in evaluating the claim.

**T.13** An insured must submit a signed, sworn "proof of loss" when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary for us to evaluate the claim.

**T.14** We may request a proof of loss even if we have inspected the property or obtained other information concerning the loss. Our request for a proof of loss does not waive any policy condition or coverage defense.

**T.15** We may communicate with an insured, an authorized representative, a public adjuster, a contractor, or another person acting for an insured. An insured must provide us with information sufficient to verify the authority of a person acting for the insured.

**T.16** We will communicate claim decisions in writing when required by law or when reasonably appropriate under the circumstances. Our written communication may identify the information on which we relied in reaching our decision.

**T.17** If we need additional information to evaluate a claim, we will request that information from an insured or the insured's authorized representative. An insured must provide the information or explain why the information is unavailable.

**T.18** We may ask an insured to clarify information that is incomplete, inconsistent, or unclear. An insured must respond truthfully to reasonable requests for clarification.

**T.19** We may obtain estimates, opinions, reports, or evaluations from persons we select. An insured may provide estimates, opinions, reports, or evaluations for our consideration.

**T.20** We may investigate a claim while reserving our rights under the policy. Our investigation does not waive any condition, exclusion, limitation, or defense available to us.

**T.21** We will evaluate a claim based on the information available to us. We may revise our evaluation if we receive material additional information.

**T.22** We may pay an amount that is not in dispute while continuing to investigate a disputed portion of a claim. Payment of an undisputed amount does not constitute acceptance of liability for any disputed amount.

## T.6 — Seacoast Territories

**T.1** As used in this provision, "Seacoast Territory" means an area we identify as subject to seacoast conditions. Seacoast conditions include conditions associated with proximity to coastal waters.

**T.2** The location of your residence premises determines whether it is within a Seacoast Territory. The location must be identified by its physical address.

**T.3** We may determine Seacoast Territory status from information available to us. Such information may include location records, maps, surveys, and other reliable sources.

**T.4** An insured must provide accurate information concerning the location of the residence premises. An insured must notify us of a change in that location information.

**T.5** A location remains subject to this provision while it is within a Seacoast Territory. A loss does not change the Seacoast Territory status of a location.

**T.6** A temporary absence from the residence premises does not change its Seacoast Territory status. The location of personal property away from the residence premises does not change that status.

**T.7** This provision applies to property at the residence premises located within a Seacoast Territory. It also applies to property customarily kept at that residence premises.

**T.8** We determine the applicability of this provision from the location of property when direct physical loss occurs. The location where an insured reports the loss does not control that determination.

**T.9** A Seacoast Territory designation applies regardless of the cause of loss. The designation does not establish that a particular cause of loss is covered.

**T.10** Any deductible applicable to a loss in a Seacoast Territory applies to covered loss at a location within that territory. We apply that deductible before determining the amount payable for the loss.

**T.11** A deductible applicable to a Seacoast Territory applies separately to each covered loss. Damage arising from the same occurrence is treated as part of that loss.

**T.12** We may inspect a residence premises to determine its location and Seacoast Territory status. An insured must cooperate with an inspection requested by us.

**T.13** An insured must provide records or information we reasonably request to verify the location of the residence premises. Such records or information must accurately identify the property involved.

**T.14** An insured must identify the location of damaged property when reporting a loss. An insured must preserve available evidence concerning that location.

**T.15** We do not cover any increase in loss caused by an insured's intentional misrepresentation of the location of the residence premises. We do not cover any increase in loss caused by an insured's intentional concealment of that location.

**T.16** We do not cover loss to property falsely represented as located outside a Seacoast Territory. This exclusion applies only to the extent the false representation affects our handling of the loss.

**T.17** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect damaged property from further loss. Those steps do not change the Seacoast Territory status of the property.

**T.18** Repairs, rebuilding, or replacement at the residence premises do not change its Seacoast Territory status. Improvements to the residence premises do not alter that status.

**T.19** A transfer of ownership does not change the Seacoast Territory status of the residence premises. A change in occupancy does not alter that status.

**T.20** We may rely on location information supplied by an insured unless we have reason to question its accuracy. Our reliance on that information does not waive any right to determine the actual location of the residence premises.

**T.21** Our prior handling of a loss does not establish Seacoast Territory status for another loss. Payment of a loss does not waive our right to apply this provision to later loss.

**T.22** A failure by us to identify a location as within a Seacoast Territory does not change the actual status of that location. An insured remains responsible for providing accurate location information.

**T.23** Our identification of a Seacoast Territory is not a representation concerning weather conditions at a particular location. It is not a warranty concerning the safety of the residence premises.

**T.24** Seacoast Territory status does not create coverage for a cause of loss that is otherwise not covered. It does not expand the property to which coverage applies.

**T.25** Seacoast Territory status does not reduce an insured's duties after a loss. An insured must comply with all duties applicable to the loss.

**T.26** We may request additional information when location information is incomplete, inconsistent, or unavailable. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable request.

**T.27** If damaged property was moved before the loss, an insured must identify its location immediately before the loss. We may consider reliable evidence of that location.

**T.28** If damaged property was moved after the loss, an insured must identify its location when the loss occurred. Movement after the loss does not alter our determination under this provision.

**T.29** A dispute concerning Seacoast Territory status does not relieve an insured of the duty to protect property from further loss. An insured must continue to cooperate with our investigation.

**T.30** We may use information obtained during our investigation to determine Seacoast Territory status. We may revise our determination when reliable information establishes that the prior determination was inaccurate.

**T.31** A revised determination applies to the loss under investigation. It does not itself establish coverage for that loss.

**T.32** An insured must not interfere with our efforts to inspect, locate, or evaluate damaged property. An insured must not intentionally alter location evidence relevant to a loss.

**T.33** This provision applies whenever a residence premises is within a Seacoast Territory. Its application is determined by the facts relating to the location involved.

## T.9 — Definitions Amended

“‘We,’ ‘us,’ and ‘our’ refer to the insurer. These terms include any authorized representative acting on our behalf.

“‘You’ and ‘your’ refer to the person or organization to whom this insurance applies. These terms include any person acting with that person’s or organization’s authority.

“‘Insured’ means you and any person entitled to protection under this insurance. An insured includes a person only while that person meets the applicable conditions of this insurance.

“‘Resident’ means a person who lives at the residence premises with the intent to remain there. Temporary absence does not alone end residency.

“‘Relative’ means a person related to you by blood, marriage, adoption, or guardianship who is a resident. A ward who is a resident is treated as a relative.

“‘Residence premises’ means the location where you reside and that is used principally for residential purposes. The residence premises includes land and structures that are part of that location.

“‘Residence’ means the place used by you as a home. A residence may include living space within a dwelling or other building.

“‘Dwelling’ means a building or part of a building used principally as a residence. A dwelling includes permanently installed fixtures and systems serving that building.

“‘Other structure’ means a structure that is separate from the dwelling. An other structure includes a structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

“‘Insured location’ means the residence premises and any location used by an insured in connection with the residence premises. An insured location includes land owned or rented by an insured for residential use.

“‘Business’ means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity conducted for financial gain. Business includes an activity regularly performed for compensation.

“‘Business property’ means property used in whole or in part for business. Business property includes records, equipment, supplies, and property held for sale.

“‘Motor vehicle’ means a land vehicle designed for travel on public roads. A motor vehicle includes a vehicle subject to motor vehicle registration requirements.

“‘Motorized land conveyance’ means a vehicle or device designed for movement on land and powered by a motor. A motorized land conveyance includes a vehicle not designed for public-road travel.

“‘Watercraft’ means a vessel designed for travel on water. Watercraft includes its furnishings, equipment, and attached machinery.

“‘Aircraft’ means a device designed for flight or navigation through the air. Aircraft includes its equipment and attached apparatus.

“‘Bodily injury’ means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. Bodily injury includes resulting death.

“‘Property damage’ means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use. Property damage also means loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.

“‘Personal injury’ means injury arising from an offense that causes harm other than bodily injury. Personal injury includes injury arising from false arrest, wrongful eviction, defamation, or invasion of privacy.

“‘Accident’ means an unexpected event that causes bodily injury or property damage. An accident does not include an event expected or intended by an insured.

“‘Occurrence’ means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same harmful conditions. An occurrence includes resulting bodily injury or property damage.

“‘Loss’ means accidental physical loss of or damage to covered property. Loss includes loss resulting directly from a covered cause.

“‘Direct physical loss’ means physical loss of or damage to tangible property caused without intervening independent cause. Direct physical loss requires actual alteration, destruction, or dispossession of property.

“‘Collapse’ means an abrupt falling down or caving in of a building or part of a building. Collapse does not include settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion unless the structure has fallen down or caved in.

“‘Fungus’ means any type or form of fungus, mold, or mildew. Fungus includes spores, mycotoxins, scents, and byproducts produced or released by fungus.

“‘Wet rot’ means decay of property caused by moisture. Wet rot includes deterioration resulting from persistent dampness.

“‘Pollutants’ means irritants or contaminants in solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, or biological form. Pollutants include smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

“‘Pollution’ means the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. Pollution includes the presence of pollutants on land, in water, or in the air.

“‘Owned’ means held by an insured through legal title, beneficial interest, or enforceable right of possession. Owned includes property acquired for an insured’s use.

“‘Care, custody, or control’ means possession, supervision, management, or responsibility for property. Property is in an insured’s care, custody, or control when the insured has the right or duty to direct its use or protection.

“‘Intentional loss’ means loss arising from an act committed by an insured with the intent to cause loss. Intentional loss includes an act performed with knowledge that loss is substantially certain to result.

“‘Vacant’ means containing no personal property necessary to normal occupancy. Property is vacant when it is not being used for its intended purpose.

“‘Unoccupied’ means not being lived in or used by an insured. Property may be unoccupied even though personal property remains at the location.

“‘Replacement cost’ means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement cost does not include deduction for depreciation.

“‘Actual cash value’ means the value of damaged property at the time of loss. Actual cash value includes consideration of depreciation and the property’s condition.

“‘Deductible’ means the amount of loss that you must bear before we pay for covered loss. A deductible applies separately to each loss unless applicable terms provide otherwise.

“‘Claim’ means a request for payment, protection, or performance under this insurance. A claim includes a demand arising from bodily injury, property damage, or other covered loss.

“‘Suit’ means a civil proceeding in which damages are alleged because of bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury. Suit includes an arbitration proceeding to which we consent.
