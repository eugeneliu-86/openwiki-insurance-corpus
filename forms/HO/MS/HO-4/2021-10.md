# HO-4 Homeowners 4 — Contents Broad Form (Edition 2021-10)




## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** In return for payment of the premium and subject to the terms of this "policy," we provide the insurance described in this agreement. You accept this policy by payment of the premium or by seeking coverage under this policy.

**AGR.2** We insure you for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a covered peril. Coverage applies only while this policy is in force.

**AGR.3** We will pay for a covered "loss" only when an insured has complied with the duties imposed by this policy. Our payment is subject to all applicable conditions, limitations, exclusions, and deductibles.

**AGR.4** This policy applies only to property in which you have an insurable interest at the time of loss. We will not pay more than your financial interest in the covered property.

**AGR.5** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will not pay for loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to preserve covered property at and after the time of loss.

**AGR.6** We may investigate any claim and examine the circumstances of a reported loss. You must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of any claim.

**AGR.7** We may repair, replace, or pay for covered property, as permitted by this policy. Our exercise of any option does not waive any condition or defense available to us.

**AGR.8** Payment for a covered loss does not transfer ownership of covered property to us unless we agree otherwise. We may take possession of damaged property after payment if permitted by law.

**AGR.9** We do not cover any loss that is excluded by this policy. An exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss, unless this policy provides otherwise.

**AGR.10** Coverage under this policy is personal to you and may not be assigned without our written consent. An assignment of a claim after loss is subject to applicable law.

**AGR.11** We may cancel or nonrenew this policy as permitted by applicable law. You may cancel this policy by providing notice to us.

**AGR.12** If any provision of this policy is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. A waiver by us of any provision applies only when made in writing.

**AGR.13** This policy contains the agreement between you and us concerning the insurance provided. No change to this policy is valid unless made by us in writing.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss, determined by applying depreciation for age, condition, and obsolescence to the cost of replacing the property. It includes the depreciated value of a damaged chair. It does not include the cost to replace that chair with a new chair without deduction for depreciation.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality without deduction for depreciation. It includes the reasonable cost to replace a damaged sofa with a comparable new sofa. It does not include the cost of improvements or additions that make the replacement property materially better than the damaged property.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, that results in bodily injury or property damage. It includes an accidental fall that damages another person's property. It does not include an event that is expected or intended from the standpoint of an insured.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling, unit, and related grounds shown in the declarations as the place where you reside. It includes a rented apartment that you occupy as your home and a storage area assigned for your use. It does not include a dwelling that you own but do not use as your residence.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or other persons under your care. It includes a resident spouse or resident child who meets these conditions. It does not include a tenant, boarder, or roommate who is not related to you and is not under your care.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations that are used in connection with the residence premises or are temporarily occupied by you. It includes a temporary lodging place while you are away from your residence premises. It does not include a location used principally for business activities.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain. It includes operating a service for compensation from a location away from the residence premises. It does not include an activity that is customarily undertaken without a profit motive and is not a regular occupation.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle or trailer designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration. It includes an automobile, motorcycle, or attached trailer. It does not include a vehicle designed solely for use within a residence or on grounds used in connection with a residence.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises or the personal affairs of an insured. It includes a person employed to perform household cleaning or yard maintenance. It does not include a person employed in connection with business activities of an insured.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting care, loss of services, and death. It includes a broken arm caused by an accidental fall. It does not include emotional distress that does not result from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. It includes damage to furniture caused by an accidental discharge of water. It does not include loss of use of data or other property that is not tangible property.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unexpected. It includes an unexpected discharge of water caused by the immediate rupture of a pipe. It does not include gradual seepage, leakage, wear, deterioration, or neglect.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means a condition in which a dwelling is not occupied and lacks sufficient personal property to show normal habitation. It includes an empty dwelling from which the occupants and their usual belongings have been removed. It does not include a dwelling that remains furnished and is temporarily unattended. In determining whether a dwelling is vacant, the claims manual's duration test applies the definitionthe claims manual's duration test applies the definition (see Property Claims Handling Manual, C3 Chapter 3 — Water Losses).

**DEF.14** "named storm" means a tropical cyclone that has been assigned a name by a governmental weather authority. It includes a hurricane bearing an assigned storm name. It does not include a thunderstorm or other weather event that has not been assigned a storm name.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.2** "Direct physical loss" means accidental physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. Loss of value without physical injury is not direct physical loss.

**B.3** "Other Structures" means structures that are separated from the dwelling by clear space. A structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, walkway, or similar connection is an Other Structure.

**B.4** An Other Structure must be located on the residence premises at the time of loss. Property located away from the residence premises is not covered under Coverage B.

**B.5** Coverage applies to an Other Structure that you own or for which you are legally responsible. Coverage does not create an insurable interest where none exists.

**B.6** Coverage applies to permanently installed equipment serving an Other Structure. Coverage also applies to materials and supplies located on the residence premises and intended for use in repairing or rebuilding an Other Structure.

**B.7** We cover fixtures that are attached to and form part of an Other Structure. We do not cover personal property that is not permanently attached to an Other Structure.

**B.8** We do not cover land, including land on which an Other Structure is located. We do not cover excavation, grading, filling, landscaping, or site preparation.

**B.9** We do not cover an Other Structure rented or held for rental to any person not residing on the residence premises. This exclusion does not apply to an Other Structure used solely as a private garage.

**B.10** We do not cover an Other Structure used for "business" purposes. Business means trade, occupation, profession, or activity engaged in for compensation or profit.

**B.11** We do not cover an Other Structure used for farming, ranching, or other agricultural operations. This exclusion applies whether or not the activity produces income.

**B.12** We do not cover an Other Structure used for unlawful activity. This exclusion applies to loss arising from the unlawful activity.

**B.13** We do not cover property owned by a tenant, boarder, or roomer. We do not cover property for which you have no legal responsibility.

**B.14** We do not cover fences, retaining walls, seawalls, docks, piers, bulkheads, or similar property that is not a building or structure with a roof. We do not cover property in or over water.

**B.15** We do not cover trees, shrubs, plants, lawns, or growing crops. We do not cover detached property that is not permanently affixed to the land.

**B.16** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, use, or demolition of property. We do not cover the increased cost of complying with an ordinance or law.

**B.17** We do not cover loss arising from an act committed by you with the intent to cause loss. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss is different from that intended.

**B.18** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An "insured" must protect damaged property from further damage.

**B.19** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or other action by a governmental authority. This exclusion does not apply to loss resulting from fire when the fire is otherwise covered.

**B.20** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard.

**B.21** We do not cover loss when an excluded cause of loss contributes to the loss directly or indirectly. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing to the loss.

**B.22** An insured must give us prompt notice of loss. The notice must identify the damaged property and describe the known circumstances of loss.

**B.23** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep a record of reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property.

**B.24** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property as often as reasonably necessary. An insured must preserve damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**B.25** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged property. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item claimed.

**B.26** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**B.27** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request.

**B.28** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property. We may deny payment for expenses that are not reasonable or necessary.

**B.29** We determine the "actual cash value" of damaged property by considering its condition, age, use, and depreciation. We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality.

**B.30** We will not pay more than the lesser of the amount necessary to repair or replace damaged property, the actual cash value of damaged property, or the applicable limit for Coverage B. We will not pay for loss that has been paid under any other coverage provided by us.

**B.31** If we pay for damaged property, we may take all or part of the property at the value we paid. An insured must transfer the property to us if we request the transfer.

**B.32** If an insured has a right to recover damages from another person, that right is transferred to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our right of recovery.

**B.33** We may adjust a loss with you or with any person having a legal interest in the damaged property. Payment by us does not waive any right or defense available to us under this section.

**C.64** If a part of an item is damaged and the part is unavailable, we may repair or replace the item with property of like kind and quality. We will not pay for an improvement to undamaged property.

**C.65** We may take all or part of damaged property at the agreed or appraised value. An insured must retain the property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**C.66** We may pay the cost to repair or replace damaged personal property. We may pay the actual cash value of the damaged property instead.

**C.67** If lost or stolen property is recovered, an insured must notify us promptly. We will determine whether the property will be returned to an insured or retained by us.

**C.68** If we pay for lost or stolen property and the property is later recovered, an insured may keep the property by returning the payment. An insured must return the property to us if we request it and retain ownership.

**C.69** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. Notice must identify the affected property and describe the known circumstances of the loss.

**C.70** An insured must notify the police when a loss involves theft, vandalism, or malicious mischief. An insured must cooperate with any investigation of the loss.

**C.71** An insured must protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and make damaged property available for inspection.

**C.72** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed, or stolen personal property. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item.

**C.73** An insured must provide bills, receipts, photographs, records, and other documents that support the claim. An insured must permit us to make copies of those materials.

**C.74** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine the location where the loss occurred.

**C.75** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require. An insured must provide a signed statement of the information requested by us.

**C.76** An insured must provide a signed proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the facts of the loss, the interest of all insureds and others in the property, and the amount claimed.

**C.77** We may investigate and adjust a claim with any person having an interest in the covered property. Payment to a person having a lawful interest in the property discharges us to the extent of the payment.

**C.78** We do not provide coverage for the benefit of a bailee or other person having custody of covered property. This provision does not affect the rights of an insured under this Coverage C.

**C.79** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may require an insured to remove damaged property from the residence premises when reasonably necessary.

**C.80** If we make payment for a loss, we are subrogated to the rights of an insured against any person responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after the loss to impair those rights.

**C.81** No action may be brought against us unless an insured has complied with all applicable provisions of this Coverage C. Our payment of a claim does not waive any provision of this policy.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** We provide the coverage described in this section for loss of use of the "residence premises" resulting from direct physical loss caused by a peril we cover.

**D.2** The limit of liability for this coverage is forty percent of the "Coverage A" limit. This limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss of use arising from any occurrence.

**D.3** Payments for "additional living expense", "fair rental value", and loss resulting from an order of "civil authority" are subject to the limit of liability for this coverage.

**D.4** Payment under one part of this coverage reduces the amount available under every other part of this coverage. We will not increase the limit of liability because more than one part of this coverage applies.

**D.5** If a covered loss makes the residence premises not fit to live in, we will pay the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living.

**D.6** We will pay only those additional living expenses that are reasonable and necessary because of the covered loss. Expenses that you would have incurred without the covered loss are not additional living expenses.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure for "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by a "covered peril." Direct physical loss means accidental physical loss of, or accidental physical damage to, covered property.

**P.2** Covered property means personal property for which we provide coverage. A covered peril means a cause of loss described in this section.

**P.3** We cover direct physical loss caused by fire. We also cover direct physical loss caused by lightning.

**P.4** We cover loss caused by fire that results from any source of ignition. We do not cover loss caused by intentional burning by an insured.

**P.5** We cover loss caused by lightning, including damage resulting from a fire started by lightning. We do not cover loss caused solely by electrical current unless the loss results from lightning.

**P.6** We cover direct physical loss caused by windstorm. We also cover direct physical loss caused by hail.

**P.7** We do not cover windstorm or hail damage to property while the property is outside a fully enclosed building. This exclusion does not apply to property being carried or worn by an insured.

**P.8** We do not cover loss to property caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless windstorm or hail first damages the building and creates an opening. The opening must permit the rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust to enter.

**P.9** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail to awnings, signs, outdoor antennas, or outdoor equipment. We do cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by windstorm or hail after the property enters a fully enclosed building.

**P.10** We cover direct physical loss caused by explosion. Explosion includes the sudden release of pressure resulting in an outward bursting force.

**P.11** We do not cover loss caused by explosion of a steam boiler, steam pipe, steam turbine, or steam engine that is owned by, rented to, or controlled by an insured. We do cover resulting fire or smoke damage.

**P.12** We cover direct physical loss caused by riot. We also cover direct physical loss caused by civil commotion.

**P.13** Riot and civil commotion include acts of persons participating in a public disturbance. We do not cover loss caused by governmental seizure or destruction of property.

**P.14** We cover direct physical loss caused by aircraft. Aircraft includes self-propelled vehicles designed for flight or travel through air.

**P.15** We cover loss caused by articles falling from aircraft. We do not cover loss caused by pressure waves resulting from aircraft travel.

**P.16** We cover direct physical loss caused by vehicles. Vehicles include self-propelled land conveyances.

**P.17** We cover loss caused by a vehicle owned or operated by an insured. We do not cover loss to a vehicle or to property contained in a vehicle.

**P.18** We cover direct physical loss caused by smoke. Smoke means the sudden and accidental discharge or release of smoke, soot, fumes, or vapor.

**P.19** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging. We do not cover loss caused by smoke from industrial operations.

**P.20** We cover direct physical loss caused by vandalism. We also cover direct physical loss caused by malicious mischief.

**P.21** We do not cover vandalism or malicious mischief if the dwelling is vacant. A dwelling is vacant when it contains no property necessary for normal habitation.

**P.22** We cover direct physical loss caused by theft. Theft includes attempted theft and loss of property from a known or unknown location.

**P.23** We do not cover theft committed by an insured. We do not cover theft committed by a person entrusted with the covered property.

**P.24** We do not cover theft from any part of the residence premises that is rented to others. We do not cover theft from a dwelling rented by an insured to others.

**P.25** We do not cover theft of watercraft, including trailers and equipment used with watercraft. We do not cover theft of a motor or other equipment designed for use with a vehicle.

**P.26** We do not cover theft of property while the property is at a residence owned, rented, or occupied by an insured unless an insured is temporarily living there. We do cover theft of property from a location where an insured is temporarily residing.

**P.27** We cover direct physical loss caused by falling objects. Falling objects include objects that fall from a building, tree, aircraft, or other source.

**P.28** We do not cover loss to property contained in a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless the falling object first damages the building. The damage must create an opening through which the rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust enters.

**P.29** We cover direct physical loss caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet. We cover resulting physical damage to covered property.

**P.30** We do not cover loss caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet on property outside a fully enclosed building. We do cover loss to covered property while it is being carried or worn by an insured.

**P.31** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam. The water or steam must escape from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system.

**P.32** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow from a household appliance. The appliance must be located within the residence premises or connected to a water supply source.

**P.33** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow from a sump, sump pump, drain, sewer, or watercourse. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains.

**P.37** We cover resulting damage to covered property caused by the sudden and accidental event. We do not cover loss to the system from which the water or steam escaped.

**P.38** We do not cover loss caused by deterioration, wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, or defect. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**P.39** We cover direct physical loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. The system must be located within the residence premises.
