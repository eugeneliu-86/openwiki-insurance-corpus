# HO-4 Homeowners 4 — Contents Broad Form (Edition 2021-10)




## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** In return for payment of the premium and subject to the terms of this "policy," we provide the insurance described in this agreement. You accept this policy by payment of the premium or by seeking coverage under this policy.

**AGR.2** We insure you for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a covered peril. Coverage applies only while this policy is in force.

**AGR.3** We will pay for a covered "loss" only when an insured has complied with the duties imposed by this policy. Our payment is subject to all applicable conditions, limitations, exclusions, and deductibles.

**AGR.4** This policy applies only to property in which you have an insurable interest at the time of loss. We will not pay more than your financial interest in the covered property.

**AGR.5** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will not pay for loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to preserve covered property at and after the time of loss.

**AGR.6** We may investigate any claim and examine the circumstances of a reported loss. You must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of any claim.

**AGR.7** We may repair, replace, or pay for covered property, as permitted by this policy. Our exercise of any option does not waive any condition or defense available to us.

**AGR.8** Payment for a covered loss does not transfer ownership of covered property to us unless we agree otherwise. We may take possession of damaged property after payment if permitted by law.

**AGR.9** We do not cover any loss that is excluded by this policy. An exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss, unless this policy provides otherwise.

**AGR.10** Coverage under this policy is personal to you and may not be assigned without our written consent. An assignment of a claim after loss is subject to applicable law.

**AGR.11** We may cancel or nonrenew this policy as permitted by applicable law. You may cancel this policy by providing notice to us.

**AGR.12** If any provision of this policy is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. A waiver by us of any provision applies only when made in writing.

**AGR.13** This policy contains the agreement between you and us concerning the insurance provided. No change to this policy is valid unless made by us in writing.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss, determined by applying depreciation for age, condition, and obsolescence to the cost of replacing the property. It includes the depreciated value of a damaged chair. It does not include the cost to replace that chair with a new chair without deduction for depreciation.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality without deduction for depreciation. It includes the reasonable cost to replace a damaged sofa with a comparable new sofa. It does not include the cost of improvements or additions that make the replacement property materially better than the damaged property.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, that results in bodily injury or property damage. It includes an accidental fall that damages another person's property. It does not include an event that is expected or intended from the standpoint of an insured.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling, unit, and related grounds shown in the declarations as the place where you reside. It includes a rented apartment that you occupy as your home and a storage area assigned for your use. It does not include a dwelling that you own but do not use as your residence.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or other persons under your care. It includes a resident spouse or resident child who meets these conditions. It does not include a tenant, boarder, or roommate who is not related to you and is not under your care.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations that are used in connection with the residence premises or are temporarily occupied by you. It includes a temporary lodging place while you are away from your residence premises. It does not include a location used principally for business activities.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain. It includes operating a service for compensation from a location away from the residence premises. It does not include an activity that is customarily undertaken without a profit motive and is not a regular occupation.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle or trailer designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration. It includes an automobile, motorcycle, or attached trailer. It does not include a vehicle designed solely for use within a residence or on grounds used in connection with a residence.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises or the personal affairs of an insured. It includes a person employed to perform household cleaning or yard maintenance. It does not include a person employed in connection with business activities of an insured.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting care, loss of services, and death. It includes a broken arm caused by an accidental fall. It does not include emotional distress that does not result from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. It includes damage to furniture caused by an accidental discharge of water. It does not include loss of use of data or other property that is not tangible property.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unexpected. It includes an unexpected discharge of water caused by the immediate rupture of a pipe. It does not include gradual seepage, leakage, wear, deterioration, or neglect.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means a condition in which a dwelling is not occupied and lacks sufficient personal property to show normal habitation. It includes an empty dwelling from which the occupants and their usual belongings have been removed. It does not include a dwelling that remains furnished and is temporarily unattended. In determining whether a dwelling is vacant, the claims manual's duration test applies the definitionthe claims manual's duration test applies the definition (see Property Claims Handling Manual, C3 Chapter 3 — Water Losses).

**DEF.14** "named storm" means a tropical cyclone that has been assigned a name by a governmental weather authority. It includes a hurricane bearing an assigned storm name. It does not include a thunderstorm or other weather event that has not been assigned a storm name.

## I.A — Coverage A — Not Provided

**A.1** We do not provide Coverage A under this policy. We do not cover the dwelling as Coverage A property. This provision applies regardless of whether the dwelling is occupied, vacant, or under construction.

**A.2** For purposes of this provision, "dwelling" means the building described in the policy declarations as the residence premises. We do not insure the dwelling under Coverage A. We do not provide an amount of insurance for the dwelling.

**A.3** We do not cover the dwelling or any part of the dwelling under Coverage A. We do not cover additions to the dwelling under Coverage A. This provision applies to additions that are attached to or detached from the dwelling.

**A.4** We do not cover structures that are used with the dwelling under Coverage A. We do not cover a structure because it is located on the residence premises. Coverage under another provision is not created by this provision.

**A.5** We do not cover building materials intended for construction, alteration, or repair of the dwelling under Coverage A. We do not cover labor or services performed in connection with that work under Coverage A. This provision applies while the materials or services are located at or away from the residence premises.

**A.6** We do not cover land under Coverage A. We do not cover soil, water, or other natural features of land under Coverage A. This provision applies whether the land is used with the dwelling or is otherwise associated with the residence premises.

**A.7** We do not cover foundations, retaining walls, or similar supporting structures under Coverage A. We do not cover those structures when they support the dwelling or another structure. This provision applies whether the structures are above or below ground.

**A.8** We do not cover underground pipes, wires, drains, or other underground equipment under Coverage A. We do not cover those items solely because they serve the dwelling. This provision applies to items located within or beyond the boundaries of the residence premises.

**A.9** We do not cover driveways, walkways, patios, or other paved surfaces under Coverage A. We do not cover those surfaces solely because they are used with the dwelling. This provision applies whether the surfaces are attached to or separate from the dwelling.

**A.10** We do not cover fences, gates, or similar boundary structures under Coverage A. We do not cover those structures solely because they enclose or protect the residence premises. This provision applies regardless of the material from which the structure is made.

**A.11** We do not cover trees, shrubs, plants, or lawns under Coverage A. We do not cover landscaping or other outdoor vegetation under Coverage A. This provision applies whether the vegetation is growing naturally or was planted by you.

**A.12** We do not cover swimming pools, spas, or similar outdoor equipment under Coverage A. We do not cover equipment or materials used to maintain those items under Coverage A. This provision applies whether the item is attached to or separate from the dwelling.

**A.13** We do not cover a detached garage, shed, or other detached structure under Coverage A. We do not cover a detached structure because it is located on the residence premises. This provision does not determine whether property in the structure is covered as personal property.

**A.14** We do not cover a building used for business under Coverage A. We do not cover a building used for farming, manufacturing, or another commercial purpose under Coverage A. This provision applies even if the building is located on the residence premises.

**A.15** We do not cover a building rented to others under Coverage A. We do not cover a building occupied by a tenant under Coverage A. This provision applies regardless of the terms of the rental arrangement.

**A.16** We do not cover a building that you own but do not occupy as a residence under Coverage A. We do not cover a building used as a seasonal, temporary, or occasional residence under Coverage A. This provision applies to the building itself and not to any personal property that may be insured elsewhere under this policy.

**A.17** We do not cover a dwelling in which you have no insurable interest under Coverage A. We do not cover a dwelling based solely on your possession, management, or use of the dwelling. This provision applies whether your interest is legal, equitable, or contractual.

**A.18** We do not cover a dwelling owned by a corporation, partnership, association, or other organization under Coverage A. We do not cover the organization's interest in the dwelling under Coverage A. This provision applies even if you occupy the dwelling.

**A.19** We do not cover a dwelling acquired after the policy begins under Coverage A. We do not cover an increase in the value of a dwelling under Coverage A. This provision applies unless coverage for the dwelling is expressly provided by a written change to this policy.

**A.20** We do not cover a dwelling that is sold, transferred, or conveyed under Coverage A. We do not cover the interest of a purchaser or transferee under Coverage A. This provision applies when your ownership or insurable interest ends.

**A.21** We do not cover a dwelling after it is condemned, demolished, or removed under Coverage A. We do not cover debris from the dwelling under Coverage A. This provision applies whether the condition results from damage, governmental action, or your decision.

**A.22** We do not cover a dwelling while it is being moved or transported under Coverage A. We do not cover the dwelling at a new location under Coverage A. This provision applies whether the move is temporary or permanent.

**A.23** We do not cover a dwelling while it is used for an unlawful purpose under Coverage A. We do not cover a dwelling used to manufacture, store, or distribute illegal substances under Coverage A. This provision applies to the dwelling itself and does not expand coverage for any other property.

**A.24** We do not cover a dwelling when it is used in a manner that materially changes its residential character under Coverage A. We do not cover the dwelling based solely on your continued ownership of it. This provision applies unless the changed use is accepted by us in writing.

**A.25** You must notify us promptly if the ownership, occupancy, use, or condition of the dwelling changes. You must provide information that we reasonably request about the dwelling. Your duties under this provision do not create Coverage A.

**A.26** You must not represent that Coverage A is provided by this policy. You must not assign, transfer, or pledge Coverage A because this policy does not provide it. Any representation or transaction that assumes Coverage A exists does not change this provision.

**A.27** We do not provide Coverage A for direct physical loss to the dwelling. We do not provide Coverage A for any resulting loss of use, rental value, or other interest in the dwelling. This provision applies except that it does not limit coverage for property or loss expressly covered elsewhere under this policy.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** “Coverage B” applies to “Other Structures” located on the “residence premises”. We cover those structures when they are set apart from the dwelling by clear space or connected to the dwelling by only a utility line, fence, or similar connection. We cover the structures only when you own them or are legally responsible for them.

**B.2** An Other Structure must be used in connection with the residence premises. We cover an Other Structure that serves a residential purpose or supports the use of the dwelling. We do not cover an Other Structure that is used principally for a purpose unrelated to the residence premises.

**B.3** We cover an Other Structure that is permanently installed at the residence premises. The structure must be anchored, attached, or otherwise intended to remain in place. We do not cover property that is designed to be moved from place to place.

**B.4** We cover an Other Structure while it remains at the residence premises. Coverage applies to the structure and to fixtures that are permanently attached to it. Coverage does not apply after the structure is removed from the residence premises, unless its removal is necessary to prevent covered damage.

**B.5** We cover building materials and supplies located at the residence premises when they are intended for use in constructing, altering, or repairing an Other Structure. The materials and supplies must be under your control. We do not cover materials and supplies intended for a business or for property not insured under this policy.

**B.6** We cover an Other Structure that is leased to another person for use as a private garage. The structure must remain part of the residence premises. We do not cover an Other Structure leased for any other use.

**B.7** We cover an Other Structure used for storage of household property. The storage must be incidental to the use of the residence premises. We do not cover an Other Structure used for commercial storage or for storage of property held for sale.

**B.8** We cover an Other Structure used as a private workshop. The workshop must be used for hobbies or personal activities. We do not cover an Other Structure used for manufacturing, repair services, or other business activities.

**B.9** We do not cover land, including land on which an Other Structure is located. We do not cover water or any substance in or on land. We do not cover landscaping, growing plants, or natural features of the land.

**B.10** We do not cover a structure used in whole or in part for business purposes. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted by you or by another person. This exclusion does not apply to a structure used only for incidental activities that do not involve customers, employees, inventory, or business equipment.

**B.11** We do not cover a structure used to conduct farming or other agricultural operations. This exclusion applies to the structure and to property permanently attached to it. This exclusion does not apply to ordinary household gardening that is not conducted for profit.

**B.12** We do not cover a structure rented or held for rental to any person for a purpose other than use as a private garage. This exclusion applies whether the rental is written or oral. This exclusion does not apply while you occupy the structure for your personal use.

**B.13** We do not cover a structure used to house animals. This exclusion applies to structures used for breeding, boarding, training, or selling animals. This exclusion does not apply to a structure used only for ordinary household pets.

**B.14** We do not cover a structure used to store or service watercraft, aircraft, or motor vehicles when the structure is used in connection with a business. This exclusion applies even if you also use the structure for personal purposes. This exclusion does not apply to a private garage used only for household transportation.

**B.15** We do not cover a structure that is vacant and unoccupied when the vacancy or unoccupancy contributes to the loss. This exclusion applies to damage caused by neglect, deterioration, or unauthorized entry. This exclusion does not apply when you take reasonable steps to protect the structure after learning that it is vacant or unoccupied.

**B.16** We do not cover a structure that is under construction when the loss results from construction activity. This exclusion applies to faulty workmanship, defective materials, and failure to protect the structure. This exclusion does not apply to ensuing loss caused by a covered peril when the ensuing loss is not otherwise excluded.

**B.17** We do not cover a structure that is being demolished. This exclusion applies from the time demolition begins and continues while the structure is being removed. This exclusion does not apply to covered damage that occurs before demolition begins.

**B.18** We do not cover a structure that is abandoned. A structure is abandoned when you have stopped using it and do not intend to resume its use. We do not cover loss that results from your failure to maintain or protect an abandoned structure.

**B.19** We cover an Other Structure that is damaged by a covered cause of loss. We cover the cost to repair or replace the damaged portion of the structure with material of like kind and quality. We do not cover an increase in the cost of repair or replacement caused by an ordinance or law unless coverage for that increase is otherwise provided by this policy.

**B.20** We cover reasonable measures taken to protect an Other Structure from further damage after a covered loss. You must provide receipts or other records showing the nature and cost of those measures. We do not cover measures that are unnecessary or that exceed what is reasonably required to protect the structure.

**B.21** You must give us prompt notice of a loss to an Other Structure. You must protect the structure from further damage and make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs. You must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and preserve damaged property for our inspection.

**B.22** You must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim involving an Other Structure. You must provide requested records and permit us to inspect the structure at reasonable times. You must not, except at your own cost, voluntarily assume an obligation or make a payment that may impair our rights.

**B.23** You must submit a signed, sworn proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must describe the damaged structure, the cause of loss, and the amount claimed. You must include available documents that support the claim.

**B.24** You must notify the police when a covered loss involves theft, vandalism, or another suspected violation of law. You must notify the appropriate authority when required by law. We may require evidence of that notice before we settle the claim.

**B.25** We do not cover loss caused by your failure to use reasonable means to protect an Other Structure from further damage. This exclusion applies after you know or reasonably should know that damage has occurred. This exclusion does not apply to additional damage caused by a covered cause of loss that you could not reasonably prevent.

**B.26** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an Other Structure. Neglect includes failure to use reasonable care to maintain, repair, or protect the structure. We do not cover damage that would not have occurred without the neglect.

**B.27** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet or dry rot, or fungus. We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown or latent defect unless a covered cause of loss results.

**B.28** We do not cover loss caused by insects, birds, rodents, or other animals. We do not cover damage caused by nesting, infestation, or discharge of waste by animals. We do not cover the cost of removing animals or repairing damage caused by their activity.

**B.29** We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, or ceilings. We do not cover loss caused by pressure or movement of soil. We do not cover the cost of stabilizing soil or correcting construction defects.

**B.30** We do not cover loss caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer or drain. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. We do not cover the cost of repairing or replacing a system or structure from which water escapes.

**B.31** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air-conditioning, or other household systems when you fail to maintain heat in the structure or fail to shut off and drain the system. We do not cover loss caused by freezing while the structure is vacant or unoccupied if you fail to take reasonable precautions. We do not cover the cost of thawing a frozen system.

**B.32** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water. We do not cover loss caused by spray from any of these, whether or not driven by wind. We do not cover loss caused by water or waterborne material carried by any of these.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover personal property owned or used by an "insured" while that property is anywhere in the world. We cover the property described in this Coverage C when a covered loss occurs. Coverage applies only to property for which you have an insurable interest.

**C.2** We cover personal property located at the "residence premises" when the property is in or on the described dwelling. We also cover personal property located in structures on the residence premises. Coverage applies to property belonging to you or used by you.

**C.3** We cover personal property while it is temporarily located away from the residence premises. We cover the property while you are traveling or while the property is being stored. Coverage applies subject to the provisions that limit or exclude coverage.

**C.4** We cover personal property owned or used by a resident of your household who is your relative. We cover that property while the resident is living with you. Coverage does not apply to property of a tenant who is not related to you.

**C.5** We cover personal property owned or used by a person under your care who is a resident of your household. We cover the property while the person is a resident of the residence premises. Coverage does not apply to property that is insured under another policy.

**C.6** We cover personal property belonging to guests or servants while the property is at the residence premises. We cover the property only when it is in your custody or in the custody of a resident of your household. Coverage does not apply to property belonging to a roomer or boarder.

**C.7** We cover property that you acquire after the policy period begins when the property is of a kind otherwise covered by this Coverage C. We cover the property while it is at the residence premises or temporarily away from that premises. Coverage remains subject to all applicable exclusions and conditions.

**C.8** We cover improvements and betterments made by you to a rented residence premises when you have an insurable interest in them. We cover the improvements and betterments as personal property. Coverage does not apply to property that another person is required to insure.

**C.9** We cover building additions, alterations, fixtures, and installations that you own or have installed at your expense in a rented residence premises. We cover the property when it is not otherwise insured. Coverage does not apply to land or to property that is permanently attached to a building you do not own when another insurance applies.

**C.10** We cover personal property while it is being moved from the residence premises to a new residence. We cover the property during the move and while it is at the new residence. Coverage does not apply to property abandoned or discarded during the move.

**C.11** We cover personal property while it is in a bank, safe-deposit facility, storage facility, or other place used for safekeeping. We cover the property when you retain an insurable interest in it. Coverage does not apply to property held for sale or delivery.

**C.12** We cover personal property while it is in the custody of a carrier or bailee. We cover the property when you have not transferred ownership of it. Coverage does not apply to property for which the carrier or bailee is legally responsible, unless that responsibility is denied.

**C.13** We cover personal property that is damaged by a "covered loss." We determine whether the loss is covered from the circumstances of the occurrence and the applicable provisions. Coverage does not apply to an excluded loss even when covered and excluded causes contribute to the loss.

**C.14** We cover personal property against direct physical loss to the property. We cover the property only when the loss is caused by a peril insured against. Coverage does not apply to loss that consists of or results from an excluded condition.

**C.15** We cover personal property when a covered peril causes the property to be lost or damaged. We also cover reasonable measures taken to protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss. Coverage does not apply to expenses incurred to protect property from an event that is not covered.

**C.16** We cover reasonable expenses you incur to remove covered property from danger after a covered loss. We cover those expenses when the property is endangered by a peril insured against. Coverage does not apply to expenses incurred to move property for convenience or renovation.

**C.17** We cover personal property that is stolen when the theft is caused by a peril insured against. We cover the property whether the theft occurs at the residence premises or away from that premises. Coverage does not apply to property voluntarily given to another person because of fraud, trick, or false pretenses.

**C.18** We cover personal property that is lost when the circumstances show that a covered peril caused the loss. We require reasonable evidence establishing the ownership, value, and cause of the loss. Coverage does not apply when the circumstances do not support a covered cause.

**C.19** We cover money, bullion, gold other than goldware, and silver other than silverware. The most we will pay for these items is two hundred fifty dollars. Except that this limit applies regardless of the number of items or persons owning them.

**C.20** We cover jewelry, watches, furs, and precious or semiprecious stones. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of these items is two thousand dollars. Subject to that limit, we cover the items when the theft is a covered loss.

**C.21** We cover firearms and related equipment. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of firearms is two thousand five hundred dollars. Except that the limit applies to firearms stolen from any location covered by this Coverage C.

**C.22** We cover watercraft, including their trailers, furnishings, equipment, and outboard motors. The most we will pay for watercraft, including trailers, is one thousand five hundred dollars. Subject to that limit, we cover the watercraft when a covered peril causes direct physical loss.

**C.23** We cover silverware, goldware, and pewterware. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of these items is two thousand five hundred dollars. Except that this limit applies to tableware, flatware, serving pieces, and similar property.

**C.24** We cover property used primarily for business purposes while the property is on the residence premises. The most we will pay for business property on the residence premises is three thousand dollars. Subject to that limit, we cover the property when a covered peril causes direct physical loss.

**C.25** We cover electronic apparatus and accessories while they are in or on a motor vehicle. The most we will pay for electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle is one thousand five hundred dollars. Except that this limit applies when the apparatus is powered by the vehicle or is designed for use in the vehicle.

**C.26** We cover business property while it is temporarily away from the residence premises. We cover the property only when it is in the custody of an insured. Coverage does not apply to property held for sale or delivery.

**C.27** We cover property used in a business conducted by an insured. We cover the property when it is of a type ordinarily used in that business. Coverage remains subject to the applicable limit for business property.

**C.28** We cover manuscripts, drawings, records, and other recorded material. We cover the cost of blank material and the reasonable expense of reproducing the recorded information. Coverage does not apply to the value of the information itself.

**C.29** We cover grave markers, mausoleums, and burial property owned by an insured. We cover the property when it is located away from the residence premises. Coverage does not apply to the cost of restoring the surrounding land.

**C.30** We cover property of an insured child while the child is attending school and living away from the residence premises. We cover the property when the child intends to return to the residence premises. Coverage does not apply to property at a separate residence maintained by the child.

**C.31** We cover personal property of an insured who is temporarily residing in a nursing facility or similar care facility. We cover the property while the insured maintains the residence premises as a residence. Coverage does not apply to property acquired for use at the care facility when another insurance applies.

**C.32** We cover personal property that an insured leases or rents for personal use. We cover the property when the insured is legally responsible for damage caused by a covered peril. Coverage does not apply to property rented for business use.

**C.33** We cover property that an insured is legally obligated to return to its owner. We cover the property when a covered peril causes direct physical loss while the property is in the insured's custody. Coverage does not apply to property held as security for a debt.

**C.34** We cover property belonging to an insured's guest when the property is damaged at the residence premises. We cover the property when the guest has entrusted it to an insured. Coverage does not apply to property that is separately insured for the same loss.

**C.35** We cover covered property that is damaged during removal from a building threatened by a covered peril. We cover the property when the removal is reasonably necessary to protect it. Coverage does not apply to damage caused by the removal itself when the removal was not reasonably necessary.

**C.36** We cover covered property that is damaged while being repaired, renovated, or restored. We cover the property when the damage is caused by a covered peril. Coverage does not apply to damage resulting from faulty workmanship, defective materials, or an inherent defect.

**C.37** We do not cover land, including land on which covered property is located. We do not cover water or any substance in land. This exclusion applies even when a covered peril causes damage to covered property on the land.

**C.38** We do not cover animals, birds, or fish. We do not cover property belonging to or used by those animals, birds, or fish. This exclusion does not apply to property that is otherwise covered and is damaged by a covered peril.

**C.39** We do not cover motor vehicles or recreational vehicles. We do not cover their parts, equipment, or accessories while they are installed in or attached to the vehicles. This exclusion does not apply to vehicles or equipment specifically covered by another provision of this Coverage C.

**C.40** We do not cover aircraft, hovercraft, or their parts and equipment. We do not cover property designed for use with those craft. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the craft is operational.

**C.41** We do not cover property rented or held for rental to others. We do not cover property used by others under a rental or lease agreement. This exclusion does not apply to property rented to an insured for personal use.

**C.42** We do not cover property used in any business when the property is not otherwise covered by this Coverage C. We do not cover property held for sale, delivery, or demonstration. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted at the residence premises or elsewhere.

**C.43** We do not cover property seized, confiscated, or destroyed by order of a governmental authority. We do not cover property subject to forfeiture. This exclusion does not apply to property destroyed to prevent the spread of fire when the destruction is otherwise covered.

**C.44** We do not cover property that is stolen from an unattended motor vehicle unless the property is secured in a locked compartment. We do not cover evidence of theft that is insufficient to establish a covered loss. This exclusion applies to property left in view through a window or other opening.

**C.45** We do not cover property damaged by wear and tear, marring, scratching, or deterioration. We do not cover damage caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical breakdown. This exclusion does not apply to ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.46** We do not cover property damaged by insects, birds, rodents, or other animals. We do not cover damage caused by nesting or infestation. This exclusion does not apply to ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.47** We do not cover property damaged by smog, smoke from agricultural operations, or industrial smoke. We do not cover property damaged by pollution or contamination. This exclusion does not apply to smoke damage caused by a hostile fire.

**C.48** We do not cover property damaged by dampness, dryness, wetness, or changes in temperature. We do not cover damage caused by condensation or humidity. This exclusion does not apply to ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.49** We do not cover property damaged by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion. We do not cover damage caused by inherent structural conditions. This exclusion does not apply to ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.50** We do not cover property damaged by rust, mold, fungus, or wet rot. We do not cover the cost of testing, monitoring, or removing those conditions. This exclusion does not apply to ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.51** We do not cover property damaged by power failure when the failure occurs away from the residence premises. We do not cover loss caused by interruption of utility service. This exclusion does not apply to ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.52** We do not cover property damaged by freezing when you have not taken reasonable measures to maintain heat in the building. We do not cover damage caused by freezing of a plumbing system when the system was not properly drained. This exclusion does not apply when the loss results from a sudden and accidental discharge of water.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure for "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by a "covered peril." Direct physical loss means accidental physical loss of, or accidental physical damage to, covered property.

**P.2** Covered property means personal property for which we provide coverage. A covered peril means a cause of loss described in this section.

**P.3** We cover direct physical loss caused by fire. We also cover direct physical loss caused by lightning.

**P.4** We cover loss caused by fire that results from any source of ignition. We do not cover loss caused by intentional burning by an insured.

**P.5** We cover loss caused by lightning, including damage resulting from a fire started by lightning. We do not cover loss caused solely by electrical current unless the loss results from lightning.

**P.6** We cover direct physical loss caused by windstorm. We also cover direct physical loss caused by hail.

**P.7** We do not cover windstorm or hail damage to property while the property is outside a fully enclosed building. This exclusion does not apply to property being carried or worn by an insured.

**P.8** We do not cover loss to property caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless windstorm or hail first damages the building and creates an opening. The opening must permit the rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust to enter.

**P.9** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail to awnings, signs, outdoor antennas, or outdoor equipment. We do cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by windstorm or hail after the property enters a fully enclosed building.

**P.10** We cover direct physical loss caused by explosion. Explosion includes the sudden release of pressure resulting in an outward bursting force.

**P.11** We do not cover loss caused by explosion of a steam boiler, steam pipe, steam turbine, or steam engine that is owned by, rented to, or controlled by an insured. We do cover resulting fire or smoke damage.

**P.12** We cover direct physical loss caused by riot. We also cover direct physical loss caused by civil commotion.

**P.13** Riot and civil commotion include acts of persons participating in a public disturbance. We do not cover loss caused by governmental seizure or destruction of property.

**P.14** We cover direct physical loss caused by aircraft. Aircraft includes self-propelled vehicles designed for flight or travel through air.

**P.15** We cover loss caused by articles falling from aircraft. We do not cover loss caused by pressure waves resulting from aircraft travel.

**P.16** We cover direct physical loss caused by vehicles. Vehicles include self-propelled land conveyances.

**P.17** We cover loss caused by a vehicle owned or operated by an insured. We do not cover loss to a vehicle or to property contained in a vehicle.

**P.18** We cover direct physical loss caused by smoke. Smoke means the sudden and accidental discharge or release of smoke, soot, fumes, or vapor.

**P.19** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging. We do not cover loss caused by smoke from industrial operations.

**P.20** We cover direct physical loss caused by vandalism. We also cover direct physical loss caused by malicious mischief.

**P.21** We do not cover vandalism or malicious mischief if the dwelling is vacant. A dwelling is vacant when it contains no property necessary for normal habitation.

**P.22** We cover direct physical loss caused by theft. Theft includes attempted theft and loss of property from a known or unknown location.

**P.23** We do not cover theft committed by an insured. We do not cover theft committed by a person entrusted with the covered property.

**P.24** We do not cover theft from any part of the residence premises that is rented to others. We do not cover theft from a dwelling rented by an insured to others.

**P.25** We do not cover theft of watercraft, including trailers and equipment used with watercraft. We do not cover theft of a motor or other equipment designed for use with a vehicle.

**P.26** We do not cover theft of property while the property is at a residence owned, rented, or occupied by an insured unless an insured is temporarily living there. We do cover theft of property from a location where an insured is temporarily residing.

**P.27** We cover direct physical loss caused by falling objects. Falling objects include objects that fall from a building, tree, aircraft, or other source.

**P.28** We do not cover loss to property contained in a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless the falling object first damages the building. The damage must create an opening through which the rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust enters.

**P.29** We cover direct physical loss caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet. We cover resulting physical damage to covered property.

**P.30** We do not cover loss caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet on property outside a fully enclosed building. We do cover loss to covered property while it is being carried or worn by an insured.

**P.31** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam. The water or steam must escape from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system.

**P.32** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow from a household appliance. The appliance must be located within the residence premises or connected to a water supply source.

**P.33** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow from a sump, sump pump, drain, sewer, or watercourse. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains.

**P.34** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building or other structure.

**P.35** We do not cover loss caused by water that continuously leaks or seeps from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. We do not cover loss caused by mold, wet rot, dry rot, or corrosion resulting from that leakage or seepage.

**P.36** We cover direct physical loss caused by sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or water heating system. The system must be located within the residence premises.

**P.37** We cover resulting damage to covered property caused by the sudden and accidental event. We do not cover loss to the system from which the water or steam escaped.

**P.38** We do not cover loss caused by deterioration, wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, or defect. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**P.39** We cover direct physical loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. The system must be located within the residence premises.

**P.40** We do not cover loss caused by freezing if an insured failed to use reasonable care to maintain heat in the building. We also do not cover loss caused by freezing if an insured failed to shut off the water supply and drain the system when heat was not maintained.

**P.41** We cover direct physical loss caused by sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current. The electrical current must damage covered property.

**P.42** We do not cover loss to a tube, transistor, electronic component, or similar component. We do cover resulting fire damage.

**P.43** We cover direct physical loss caused by volcanic eruption. Volcanic eruption includes airborne shock waves, ash, dust, and lava flow caused by volcanic activity.

**P.44** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, even if volcanic activity contributes to the earth movement. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire, explosion, or theft resulting from volcanic activity.
