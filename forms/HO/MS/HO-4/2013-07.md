# HO-4 Homeowners 4 — Contents Broad Form (Edition 2013-07)
> SUPERSEDED by HO-4 Homeowners 4 — Contents Broad Form (Edition 2021-10) for policies effective on or after 2021-10-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

We provide the insurance described in this "policy" in return for the premium and subject to all applicable provisions. This policy consists of the declarations, endorsements, and policy conditions.

We will insure you for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a covered peril. Coverage applies only when the loss occurs during the policy period.

We will pay only for a "covered loss" for which an "insured" is legally entitled to recover under this policy. Payment will be subject to the applicable limits, exclusions, conditions, and deductible.

An insured must have an insurable interest in the covered property at the time of loss. We will not pay more than an insured's financial interest in the covered property.

We may settle any covered loss with you or with any person having a lawful interest in the covered property. Our payment will not exceed the amount we owe under this policy.

We may inspect the covered property at reasonable times. An inspection does not constitute a warranty that the property is safe or complies with any law, ordinance, or regulation.

We may adjust a covered loss and require reasonable information necessary to determine coverage. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of any claim.

We may deny coverage when an insured fails to comply with a condition of this policy and the failure is material to the claim. We may also deny coverage when an insured has concealed or misrepresented a material fact.

This policy applies only to loss occurring within the coverage territory. Coverage is subject to all terms that apply to the particular property, peril, or loss.

The declarations identify the coverages that apply and the limits available for a covered loss. An endorsement may change the terms of this policy.

Words and phrases that have special meaning are shown in quotation marks when first used in this policy. Their meaning applies wherever they appear in this policy.

If the terms of this policy conflict with the declarations or an endorsement, the declarations or endorsement will control to the extent of the conflict. All remaining terms will continue to apply.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined after deduction for depreciation and obsolescence. A worn chair is valued according to its condition immediately before damage. It includes a reduction for age, wear, and condition. It does not include the cost of a new chair without deduction for depreciation.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. A damaged appliance may be replaced with an appliance of comparable function and quality. It includes reasonable material and labor costs necessary for repair or replacement. It does not include an upgrade or improvement beyond the property damaged.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An accidental fall causing injury is an occurrence. It includes resulting bodily injury or property damage that arises from the accident. It does not include an act expected or intended to cause injury or damage.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling unit used by you as a residence, together with the part of the premises used in connection with that dwelling unit. A rented apartment used as your home is included. A garage space assigned for your use in connection with the apartment is included. A dwelling unit you do not use as your residence is not included.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are under your care. A relative residing with you is included. A person temporarily visiting your household is not included merely because that person is present. A person performing work at the residence premises is not included solely because of that work.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and premises used by you in connection with the residence premises. A parking area or storage area provided for your use in connection with your residence is included. A premises where you temporarily reside may be included when used as your residence. Property used solely for business is not included.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for money or other compensation. Operating a repair service for compensation is business. It includes providing goods or services for compensation. It does not include an activity performed solely as a volunteer without compensation.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle, trailer, or semitrailer designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration. An automobile or motorcycle is a motor vehicle. It includes a vehicle used for transportation even when it is parked. It does not include a manually propelled bicycle.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. A housekeeper employed to clean your residence is a residence employee. It includes a person performing domestic services for you. It does not include an independent contractor hired to perform specialized repair work.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death. A broken arm caused by an accidental fall is bodily injury. It includes illness resulting from physical harm. It does not include emotional distress that is not caused by bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. Damage to furniture caused by water is property damage. It includes loss of use of tangible property that has been physically injured. It does not include loss involving property that has no tangible existence.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unexpected. A pipe that unexpectedly bursts is sudden and accidental. It includes damage directly resulting from an abrupt event. It does not include damage caused by gradual deterioration, wear, or neglect.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary for normal occupancy and without a resident living at the residence premises. A dwelling emptied of furnishings and no longer used as a home is vacant. A furnished dwelling that is temporarily unoccupied is not vacant. Property stored for ordinary residential use may show that the dwelling is not vacant.

**A.3** We do not cover loss to a "building" under Coverage A. This applies to a building used as a residence or for any other purpose.

**A.4** We do not cover loss to any part of a building under Coverage A. This includes property permanently attached to the building.

**A.5** We do not cover loss to a building foundation under Coverage A. This applies to footings, slabs, piers, supports, and structural elements.

**A.6** We do not cover loss to walls, floors, ceilings, roofs, doors, windows, or stairs under Coverage A. This applies whether such property is interior or exterior property.

**A.7** We do not cover loss to plumbing, heating, air conditioning, electrical, or mechanical systems under Coverage A. This applies when such systems serve a building or any part of a building.

**A.8** We do not cover loss to permanently installed fixtures under Coverage A. This includes fixtures attached to real property.

**A.9** We do not cover loss to cabinets, countertops, built-in appliances, or similar property under Coverage A. This applies when such property is attached to a building.

**A.10** We do not cover loss to materials intended for construction, repair, remodeling, or alteration of a building under Coverage A. This applies whether the materials are on or away from the premises.

**A.11** We do not cover loss to a structure being built under Coverage A. This applies before, during, or after construction.

**A.12** We do not cover loss to a structure being repaired, renovated, restored, or remodeled under Coverage A. This applies regardless of the extent of the work.

**A.13** We do not cover loss to additions, alterations, improvements, or betterments to a building under Coverage A. This applies even when you paid for the work.

**A.14** We do not cover loss to property installed by you in a building under Coverage A. This applies when the property becomes part of the building.

**A.15** We do not cover loss to property owned by a landlord, condominium association, cooperative association, or other person under Coverage A. Ownership of the property does not create Coverage A.

**A.16** We do not cover loss to property subject to a lease, rental agreement, occupancy agreement, or similar arrangement under Coverage A. Your responsibility for such property does not create Coverage A.

**A.17** We do not cover loss to "land" under Coverage A. This includes soil, fill, grading, landscaping, and property embedded in land.

**A.18** We do not cover loss to trees, shrubs, plants, lawns, or landscaping under Coverage A. This applies whether such property is growing, planted, or removed from the land.

**A.19** We do not cover loss to walkways, driveways, patios, decks, fences, retaining walls, or similar property under Coverage A. This applies whether such property is attached to a building or separate from it.

**A.20** We do not cover loss to wells, septic systems, drains, water lines, utility lines, or similar property under Coverage A. This applies whether such property is above or below ground.

**A.21** We do not cover loss to a pool, spa, hot tub, dock, pier, or recreational structure under Coverage A. This applies whether such property is permanently installed or movable.

**A.22** We do not cover loss to sheds, garages, workshops, storage buildings, or other detached structures under Coverage A. This applies whether such structures are used for personal, business, agricultural, or recreational purposes.

**A.23** We do not cover the cost to repair, rebuild, replace, restore, or stabilize a building under Coverage A. This applies following direct physical loss or threatened damage.

**A.24** We do not cover the cost of demolition, removal, cleanup, hauling, or disposal of building property under Coverage A. This applies even when such cost results from a covered cause of loss.

**A.25** We do not cover increased costs resulting from enforcement of any ordinance, law, code, regulation, or order under Coverage A. This includes costs to repair, rebuild, demolish, or remove building property.

**A.26** We do not cover costs incurred to comply with construction standards or governmental requirements under Coverage A. This applies whether compliance is required before or after a loss.

**A.27** We do not cover costs incurred to protect, preserve, secure, or safeguard a building under Coverage A. This includes costs incurred to prevent further damage.

**A.28** We do not cover costs incurred for temporary repairs to a building under Coverage A. This applies whether the repairs are necessary because of loss or threatened loss.

**A.29** We do not cover costs incurred to inspect, test, monitor, evaluate, or diagnose damage to a building under Coverage A. This includes costs for professional services.

**A.30** We do not cover fees, assessments, charges, penalties, or similar obligations relating to building property under Coverage A. This applies whether such obligations arise by contract, statute, ordinance, or agreement.

**A.31** We do not cover a claim by a mortgagee, lienholder, landlord, owner, or other person for loss to building property under Coverage A. No interest in building property creates Coverage A.

**A.32** We do not cover loss to building property merely because you are legally liable for that loss under Coverage A. Your contractual or legal responsibility does not create Coverage A.

**A.33** We do not cover diminished value, loss of market value, or loss of use of building property under Coverage A. The absence of Coverage A does not create coverage for such loss.

**A.34** We do not provide appraisal, valuation, replacement cost, or actual cash value settlement for building property under Coverage A. No valuation of building property creates Coverage A.

**A.35** No person may obtain payment for loss to building property under Coverage A. A claim, demand, judgment, agreement, or assignment does not create Coverage A.

**A.36** Coverage A is not available by endorsement, representation, waiver, or course of dealing unless we expressly provide it in writing. No action by us changes this provision unless we agree in writing.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover "other structures" at the "residence premises" for "direct physical loss" caused by a "covered peril". Other structures must be owned by you or held by you for your use.

**B.2** Other structures include structures set apart from the dwelling by "clear space". Other structures connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection are also covered.

**B.3** We do not cover any part of the "dwelling" under this coverage. We also do not cover property that is part of the dwelling because of its construction, use, or attachment.

**B.4** Coverage applies only to other structures located at an "insured location". A structure moved from an insured location is not covered unless it remains at an insured location.

**B.5** We cover permanently installed fixtures and equipment that service a covered other structure. We do not cover personal property located in or upon an other structure unless it is permanently attached to that structure.

**B.6** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We do not cover excavation, grading, filling, or similar work performed on land.

**B.7** We do not cover an other structure used in whole or in part for "business" purposes. This exclusion does not apply when the business use is limited to the storage of property owned by you.

**B.8** We do not cover an other structure held for "rental" to others. We do not cover loss arising from a rental use that materially changes the character of the structure.

**B.9** We do not cover an other structure used by persons who are not insureds as their residence. We do not cover a structure designed or altered primarily for residential occupancy by others.

**B.10** We cover materials and supplies located at the residence premises that are intended for use in constructing or repairing a covered other structure. Such materials and supplies must be owned by you or held by you for your use.

**B.11** We do not cover materials and supplies after they are removed from the residence premises. We do not cover materials and supplies intended for use in constructing or repairing property not covered under this provision.

**B.12** We cover direct physical loss to a covered other structure only while the structure has a roof and is enclosed against the weather. We do not cover loss caused by weather to property that is not so protected.

**B.13** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**B.14** An insured must make reasonable temporary repairs necessary to protect covered "property" from further damage. We will pay the reasonable cost of such repairs when the repairs are necessary because of a covered loss.

**B.15** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine records that relate to the loss.

**B.16** We may inspect the residence premises and covered property after a reported loss. Our inspection does not waive any policy provision or establish that we will provide coverage.

**B.17** We will determine the value of covered loss according to "actual cash value" when repair or replacement is not completed. Actual cash value includes a deduction for depreciation when appropriate.

**B.18** We will apply the applicable "deductible" to covered loss under this coverage. The deductible will not reduce an insured's duty to protect property from further damage.

**B.19** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion. We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective maintenance.

**B.20** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from such conditions.

**B.21** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law requiring the testing, monitoring, cleanup, removal, containment, treatment, detoxification, or neutralization of pollutants. We do not cover loss arising from the enforcement of ordinance or law concerning the use, construction, repair, or demolition of an other structure.

**B.22** We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We do not cover the cost to extract pollutants from land, water, or property.

**B.23** We do not cover loss caused by an "intentional loss" committed by or at the direction of an insured. Intentional loss includes an act intended to cause a loss or to obtain insurance benefits through a loss.

**B.24** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or other action by a governmental authority. We do cover loss caused by fire resulting from such action when the fire is otherwise covered.

**B.25** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes any nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination.

**B.26** We do not cover loss caused by war, undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. We do not cover loss caused by destruction, seizure, or use for a military purpose.

**B.27** An "insured" must give us prompt notice of a loss. An insured must provide information reasonably requested by us concerning the cause, extent, and value of the loss.

**B.28** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged property when requested by us. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for the property claimed.

**B.29** An insured must send us a signed statement of loss when requested by us. The statement must identify the interest of all insureds and any other persons having an interest in the property.

**B.30** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably requested by us.

**B.31** We may take all or part of the damaged property at the agreed or appraised value. We may repair, rebuild, or replace covered property with property of like kind and quality.

**B.32** If we make payment for a covered loss, we acquire any rights of recovery that an insured may have against another person responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**B.33** No person may bring legal action against us unless there has been full compliance with all duties imposed by this coverage. No legal action may be brought unless the amount owed has been determined by agreement or by a final judgment.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" while it is anywhere in the world. Covered property includes property worn or carried by an insured.

**C.2** We cover direct physical "loss" to covered property caused by a covered peril. Coverage applies only to property that is damaged, destroyed, or stolen.

**C.3** At your request, we may cover personal property owned by others while the property is on the "residence premises" occupied by an insured. We may also cover property of a guest or residence employee while the property is at a residence occupied by an insured.

**C.4** "Covered property" means personal property for which coverage is provided under this Coverage C. Covered property does not include property excluded by the provisions stated below.

**C.5** We cover personal property usual to the occupancy of a residence. We do not cover property held for sale, except property held for occasional personal sale.

**C.6** We do not cover property separately described and specifically insured by another source. We do not cover any amount recoverable from that other source.

**C.7** We do not cover animals, birds, fish, or other living creatures. We do not cover property consisting of growing plants, trees, shrubs, or lawns.

**C.8** We do not cover aircraft and parts of aircraft. This exclusion does not apply to model or hobby aircraft that are not designed to carry persons.

**C.9** We do not cover motor vehicles or other motorized land conveyances. This exclusion does not apply to vehicles used solely to service the residence premises and not subject to motor vehicle registration.

**C.10** We do not cover property rented or held for rental to others. This exclusion does not apply to property rented on an occasional basis for personal use.

**C.11** We do not cover property of roomers, boarders, or other tenants who are not insureds. We do not cover property owned by persons residing at the residence premises who are not insureds.

**C.12** We do not cover electronic data, including information, facts, concepts, or instructions stored or transmitted by electronic means. We do cover the physical medium on which electronic data is stored when the physical medium sustains covered direct physical loss.

**C.13** We do not cover credit cards, fund transfer cards, access devices, or similar instruments. We do not cover loss arising from unauthorized use of those items.

**C.14** For money, bank notes, bullion, gold other than goldware, silver other than silverware, platinum other than platinumware, and coins, the most we will pay is two hundred dollars. This limit applies regardless of the number of persons whose property is involved.

**C.15** For theft of jewelry, watches, precious and semiprecious stones, the most we will pay is one thousand five hundred dollars. This limit includes loss of articles containing those items.

**C.16** For theft of firearms and related equipment, the most we will pay is two thousand dollars. This limit applies to firearms whether operable or inoperable.

**C.17** For watercraft, including trailers, furnishings, equipment, and outboard engines or motors, the most we will pay is one thousand dollars. This limit applies to property whether attached to or detached from the watercraft.

**C.18** For theft of silverware, silver-plated ware, goldware, gold-plated ware, pewterware, and platinumware, the most we will pay is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit includes articles made wholly or partly of those materials.

**C.19** For business property on the residence premises, the most we will pay is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies to property used in connection with a trade, profession, occupation, or business.

**C.20** For electronic apparatus and accessories while in or upon a motor vehicle, the most we will pay is one thousand dollars. This limit applies when the apparatus can be operated from the electrical system of the motor vehicle.

**C.21** The special limits stated in this Coverage C apply to loss from any covered peril unless a provision states otherwise. A special limit does not increase the amount we will pay for covered property.

**C.22** We will pay no more than the applicable special limit for property described by that limit. The applicable special limit remains in effect even when the property belongs to more than one insured.

**C.23** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts committed by an insured. We do not cover loss caused by an insured acting with the intent to cause damage.

**C.24** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage when reasonably possible.

**C.25** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, latent defect, or faulty materials.

**C.26** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or decay. We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from agricultural smudging, or industrial operations.

**C.27** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover loss caused by ordinary settling of personal property.

**C.28** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals owned by an insured. We do not cover damage caused by nesting, infestation, or discharge from animals.

**C.29** We do not cover loss caused by any order of a governmental authority to seize, destroy, or dispose of property. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril resulting from that order.

**C.30** We do not cover loss caused by seizure or destruction of property by order of governmental authority. We do not cover loss resulting from confiscation or unlawful taking by a public authority.

**C.31** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, or revolution. We do not cover loss caused by seizure or destruction of property for military purposes.

**C.32** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies whether the event is controlled or uncontrolled.

**C.33** We do not cover loss arising from the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril when the loss is otherwise covered.

**C.34** We do not cover loss to property while it is used in connection with illegal activity by an insured. This exclusion applies when the illegal activity materially contributes to the loss.

**C.35** We do not cover loss to property while it is in the care, custody, or control of an insured for business purposes. This exclusion does not apply to personal property used incidentally in a residence-based activity.

**C.36** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. Notice must identify the damaged, destroyed, or stolen property to the extent reasonably known.

**C.37** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when a theft has occurred. An insured must cooperate with a reasonable investigation of the theft.

**C.38** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed, or stolen property. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item when reasonably available.

**C.39** An insured must attach bills, receipts, and related documents that support the inventory when reasonably available. An insured must provide those records to us for examination.

**C.40** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as reasonably required. An insured must not dispose of damaged property without our consent unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property.

**C.41** An insured must provide records and documents we reasonably request to evaluate a loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**C.42** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably requested by us. An insured must answer questions truthfully and provide information within the insured's knowledge.

**C.43** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when reasonably requested by us. The statement must state the facts of the loss and the insured's interest in the property.

**C.44** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss. An insured must assist us in obtaining information from persons who may know relevant facts.

**C.45** We may inspect damaged property before repair, replacement, or disposal. We may require reasonable evidence of ownership, value, and condition of the property.

**C.46** We may take possession of damaged property after payment for the loss. Our possession of damaged property does not mean that we accept ownership unless we state otherwise.

**C.47** We may repair, replace, or pay for covered property at our option. We may select the manner in which damaged property is repaired or replaced.

**C.48** We will not pay for loss to property that an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented. We will not pay for loss when an insured has made a material false statement concerning the loss.

**C.49** We will not pay more than the amount of direct physical loss to covered property. We will not pay for sentimental value or personal attachment to property.

**C.50** We will determine the value of covered property at the time of loss. We may consider age, condition, market value, and available evidence of value.

**C.51** We will pay for covered property only after an insured has complied with the duties stated in this Coverage C. Our payment does not waive any right we may have concerning the loss.

**C.52** We may require an insured to assign to us any rights of recovery against a person responsible for a covered loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**C.53** If an insured recovers payment from another person for a loss we have paid, the insured must hold the recovery for us to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate in transferring that recovery to us.

**C.54** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's voluntary parting with property when induced by trick, scheme, or false pretense. We do not cover loss resulting from dishonest acts by a person entrusted with the property.

**C.55** We do not cover loss resulting from unexplained disappearance of property. We do cover theft when an insured provides reasonable evidence that theft occurred.

**C.56** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, or maintenance. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril when the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**C.57** Coverage under this Coverage C applies only to property that qualifies as covered property at the time of loss. An insured must establish ownership or a lawful interest in the property for which payment is sought.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** The limit of liability for "Coverage D" is thirty percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". This limit is the most we will pay under this coverage.

**D.2** The Coverage D limit of liability applies to all loss of use payments arising from a covered loss. Payments made for additional living expense, fair rental value, or loss resulting from civil authority action reduce the amount available under this coverage.

**D.3** For purposes of this coverage, a "covered loss" is direct physical loss for which coverage applies. Coverage D applies only when a covered loss causes loss of use.

**D.4** If a covered loss makes that part of the "residence premises" where you reside unfit to live in, we cover the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you. The increased expenses must be necessary to maintain your normal standard of living.

**D.5** We cover only the portion of an expense that exceeds the expense you would have incurred if no covered loss had occurred. An expense is not an additional living expense to the extent it would have been incurred without the covered loss.

**D.6** Additional living expenses may include reasonable expenses for temporary housing, meals, and other necessary household services. The expenses must result directly from the loss of use of the residence premises.

**D.7** We will determine whether an expense is necessary by considering your normal standard of living immediately before the covered loss. We do not cover expenses that increase or improve that standard of living.

**D.8** An insured must use reasonable means to reduce additional living expenses. We do not cover increased expense caused by an insured's failure to act with reasonable dispatch.

**D.9** If a covered loss makes that part of the residence premises rented to others, or held for rental by you, unfit for its normal use, we cover the "fair rental value" of that part. Payment is subject to the terms of this coverage.

**D.10** Fair rental value means the rental income that you would have received from the use of the premises if no covered loss had occurred. We will deduct expenses that do not continue while the premises are unfit for use.

**D.11** We cover fair rental value only for the time during which the rented or held-for-rental part of the residence premises is unfit for its normal use. We do not cover loss of rental income after that part is again fit for normal use.

**D.12** We do not cover fair rental value resulting from your inability to obtain a tenant after the premises are fit for normal use. We do not cover loss caused solely by a change in rental demand or market conditions.

**D.13** If a civil authority prohibits you from using the residence premises because of direct damage to nearby premises from a covered loss, we cover the resulting additional living expense. We also cover resulting fair rental value, subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**D.14** Coverage for civil authority action applies only while the civil authority prohibits use of the residence premises. We do not cover loss resulting from advice, recommendation, or voluntary action that does not prohibit use.

**D.15** We do not cover loss resulting from civil authority action unless the action results from direct damage to nearby premises. The damage to the nearby premises must result from a covered loss.

**D.16** Payment for additional living expense and fair rental value is limited to the shortest time required to repair or replace the property that caused the loss of use. Payment also includes the time required for your household to settle elsewhere, if you permanently relocate.

**D.17** We do not cover additional living expense or fair rental value caused by delay that is within your control. We do not cover increased loss caused by your failure to make reasonable efforts to repair, replace, or relocate.

**D.18** We do not cover loss of use caused solely by a condition that existed before the covered loss. We do not cover loss resulting solely from ordinary maintenance, deterioration, or wear and tear.

**D.19** We do not cover expenses for repair, replacement, or improvement of property under Coverage D. Coverage D applies only to loss of use and the expenses described in this coverage.

**D.20** We do not cover expenses incurred for the benefit of persons who are not members of your household. We do not cover expenses that are not reasonably related to the loss of use of the residence premises.

**D.21** An insured must keep records of all additional living expenses and fair rental value claimed. The records must show the amount incurred or lost and the reason for the claim.

**D.22** An insured must provide receipts, invoices, rental records, and other supporting documents that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to examine those records as often as we reasonably require.

**D.23** An insured must promptly notify us of any change affecting the loss of use claim. This includes a change in occupancy, rental use, repair status, relocation, or civil authority action.

**D.24** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of additional living expense or fair rental value. An insured must provide information concerning expenses that would have continued if no covered loss had occurred.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure against "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by a named peril. Coverage applies only while the property is subject to this policy.

**P.2** A "named peril" is a cause of loss expressly described in this section. We do not cover direct physical loss caused by a peril that is not a named peril.

**P.3** Fire or lightning is a named peril. This peril includes direct physical loss caused by heat, flames, or electrical discharge from lightning.

**P.4** Windstorm or hail is a named peril. We do not cover loss to property inside a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless windstorm or hail first damages the building and creates an opening through which the material enters.

**P.5** Explosion is a named peril. Explosion includes direct physical loss caused by the sudden release of pressure.

**P.6** Riot or civil commotion is a named peril. This peril includes direct physical loss caused by persons participating in a public disturbance.

**P.7** Aircraft is a named peril. This peril includes direct physical loss caused by an aircraft, its parts, or property carried by an aircraft.

**P.8** Vehicles is a named peril. This peril includes direct physical loss caused by a vehicle operated by a person or by an object propelled from a vehicle.

**P.9** Smoke is a named peril when it is sudden and accidental. We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations.

**P.10** Vandalism or malicious mischief is a named peril. This peril includes willful damage to covered property by a person other than an insured.

**P.11** Theft is a named peril. Theft includes attempted theft and loss from property taken without your permission.

**P.12** We do not cover theft committed by an insured. We do not cover theft committed by a person entrusted with covered property.

**P.13** Falling objects is a named peril. We do not cover loss to property inside a building unless the falling object first damages the building and creates an opening through which the object enters.

**P.14** Weight of ice, snow, or sleet is a named peril. This peril includes direct physical loss caused by accumulation of ice, snow, or sleet.

**P.15** Accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam is a named peril. We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam.

**P.16** This peril applies to water or steam that escapes from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. We do not cover loss to the system or appliance from which the water or steam escaped.

**P.17** Sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system is a named peril. We do not cover loss caused by deterioration, corrosion, or maintenance failure.

**P.18** Freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system is a named peril. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat in the building or to shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**P.19** Sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current is a named peril. We do not cover loss to a tube, transistor, electronic component, or similar electrical component.

**P.20** Volcanic eruption is a named peril. This peril includes direct physical loss caused by airborne volcanic material, lava flow, or airborne shock waves.

**P.21** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, earth sinking, rising, shifting, or expanding.

**P.22** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by flood. Flood includes overflow of a body of water, surface water, waves, tidal water, or waterborne material.

**P.23** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks into a building or structure.

**P.24** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. We do not cover loss caused by overflow or discharge from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**P.25** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by weather conditions when weather conditions contribute with an excluded cause of loss. This exclusion does not remove coverage for direct physical loss caused solely by windstorm, hail, or another named peril.

**P.26** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by neglect. An insured must use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss.

**P.27** We do not cover direct physical loss caused intentionally by an insured. This exclusion applies even if another person sustains loss from the same act.

**P.28** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or action taken to hinder or defend against such action.

**P.29** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by governmental action. This exclusion does not apply to loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action.

**P.30** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination.

**P.31** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by enforcement of a law or ordinance. This exclusion applies to loss arising from construction, repair, demolition, remodeling, renovation, or use of property.

**P.32** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by power failure occurring away from the residence premises. This exclusion does not apply when the power failure directly results in a named peril.

**P.33** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. This exclusion applies whether the defect is in covered property or other property.

**P.34** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or latent defect. This exclusion applies when the property is damaged because of its own condition.

**P.35** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by rust, corrosion, smog, or dry rot. We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown.

**P.36** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion. This exclusion applies to foundations, walls, floors, ceilings, pavements, patios, and similar structural components.

**P.37** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, or gnawing.

**P.38** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies whether the condition is visible or concealed.

**P.39** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by contamination. Contamination includes the presence, release, discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, or escape of pollutants.

**P.40** We do not cover direct physical loss caused by the release of pollutants. "Pollutants" means solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or chemical irritants or contaminants.

**P.41** We do not cover loss consisting solely of loss of use, loss of market value, or delay. Coverage requires direct physical loss to covered property caused by a named peril.

**P.42** We do not cover loss arising from an act or decision of a person, group, organization, or governmental body. This exclusion applies whether the act or decision is negligent, intentional, or otherwise wrongful.

**P.43** We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain property in a condition that prevents damage. An insured must take reasonable measures to protect covered property from further loss.

**P.45** We determine coverage by the efficient cause of direct physical loss. We do not cover loss when an excluded cause is the efficient cause of loss.

**P.46** We do not cover loss caused by an excluded cause acting concurrently or in any sequence with a named peril. This exclusion applies regardless of the relative contribution of each cause.

**P.47** An insured must show that direct physical loss occurred to covered property. An insured must also show that a named peril caused the direct physical loss.

**P.48** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We do not cover avoidable damage resulting from failure to protect the property.

**P.49** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and records relating to the loss.

**P.50** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of a loss. An insured must provide information reasonably requested concerning the cause and extent of loss.

**P.51** We do not cover property that has not sustained direct physical loss. Damage to property must be actual and physical.

**P.52** We do not cover loss based solely on the threat of damage. We do not cover costs incurred solely to prevent a possible future loss.

**P.53** We do not cover loss caused by gradual discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of water, steam, or pollutants. This exclusion applies even when the resulting damage is discovered after it begins.

**P.54** We do not cover loss caused by repeated exposure to substantially the same harmful conditions. This exclusion applies whether the conditions arise from natural, mechanical, or human causes.

**P.55** The "roof surfacing age threshold" does not determine whether a named peril caused direct physical loss to covered property. Coverage depends upon the cause and nature of the loss.

**P.56** The "replacement cost threshold percentage" does not determine whether a named peril caused direct physical loss. Coverage under this section depends upon the applicable peril and exclusions.

**P.57** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, or improve property solely because it is obsolete, discontinued, or unavailable. We cover direct physical loss caused by a named peril, subject to this section.

**P.58** We do not cover loss caused by ordinary use of covered property. Ordinary use includes expected deterioration from handling, operation, storage, or exposure.

**P.59** We do not cover loss arising from inadequate maintenance or failure to make needed repairs. An insured must maintain covered property with reasonable care.

## I.S — Section I — Conditions

**S.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties stated in this section after a "loss" to "covered property". Compliance with these duties is a condition to recovery for a covered loss.

**S.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss and must identify the "claim" to the extent reasonably available. Notice must include information that permits us to contact the insured.

**S.3** The "deductible" applies to each covered loss unless a different deductible applies by law. The minimum Section I deductible is two hundred fifty dollars.

**S.4** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and must retain damaged property for our inspection when practicable.

**S.5** An insured must not make permanent repairs before we inspect the damaged property when inspection is reasonably necessary. An insured may make reasonable temporary repairs to protect property from further damage.

**S.6** An insured must give us reasonable access to the damaged property. An insured must permit us to inspect the property as often as reasonably required.

**S.7** An insured must show us the damaged property and any available evidence of the cause of loss. An insured must provide photographs, recordings, receipts, and other relevant records in the insured's possession.

**S.8** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property. The inventory must state the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item to the extent reasonably available.

**S.9** An insured must attach bills, receipts, and related documents that support the inventory when those materials are reasonably available. An insured must provide records that establish ownership, value, and age of damaged property.

**S.10** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably required by us. An insured must sign the examination record if it is accurately prepared.

**S.11** An insured must provide us with records and documents we request that are relevant to the claim. An insured must authorize us to obtain records from another person or organization when reasonably necessary to investigate the claim.

**S.12** Within sixty days after our request, an insured must send us a signed, sworn "proof of loss". The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary to establish the covered loss.

**S.13** The proof of loss must identify the interest of each insured and of any other person in the property. The proof of loss must state all known liens, encumbrances, and other interests affecting the property.

**S.14** An insured must state the time and cause of the loss in the proof of loss. An insured must state any changes in title, use, or occupancy that are material to the loss.

**S.15** An insured must state the actual cash value and amount of loss for damaged property in the proof of loss. An insured must state the amount claimed under this insurance.

**S.16** An insured must state all other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must promptly provide information concerning any payment made or expected from another source.

**S.17** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold a material fact relating to the insurance or the loss.

**S.18** An insured must preserve rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights without our written consent.

**S.19** If we make payment for a covered loss, we may require an assignment of rights of recovery to the extent of our payment. An insured must execute documents reasonably necessary to preserve and enforce those rights.

**S.20** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when property is stolen or when criminal conduct may have caused the loss. An insured must provide us with information concerning the report when reasonably available.

**S.21** An insured must notify us promptly if the insured learns of additional damage related to the claim. An insured must permit us to inspect the additional damage before permanent repairs are made when practicable.

**S.22** An insured must not abandon property to us unless we agree in writing. We may take possession of damaged property after a loss without accepting ownership of that property.

**S.23** We may examine, appraise, and test damaged property. An insured must cooperate with reasonable examinations, appraisals, and tests.

**S.24** We may request that damaged property be retained until our investigation is complete. An insured must comply with a reasonable request to retain property, except when retention would create a safety hazard.

**S.25** An insured must provide a release or authorization only to the extent reasonably necessary to investigate or settle the claim. We will not require an insured to release a claim unrelated to the covered loss.

**S.26** An insured must promptly notify us of a demand, notice, summons, complaint, or other legal process relating to the loss. An insured must send us copies of those materials when received.

**S.27** An insured must assist us in securing evidence, witnesses, and records relevant to the loss. An insured must attend hearings, examinations, or proceedings when reasonably required by us.

**S.28** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense other than reasonable emergency expenses without our consent. This duty does not prevent reasonable measures to protect property from further damage.

**S.29** An insured must provide information concerning prior damage to the property when reasonably requested by us. An insured must identify repairs, claims, and loss events that may affect the adjustment of the claim.

**S.30** An insured must provide information concerning the purchase, maintenance, repair, and use of damaged property when reasonably requested by us. An insured must provide available records supporting that information.

**S.31** An insured must promptly correct information provided to us if the insured learns that the information is incomplete or inaccurate. The correction must identify the information being corrected.

**S.34** The claim amount above which a loss is referred may apply to our handling of a claim. Our referral of a loss does not alter an insured's duties under this section.

## II.F — Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

**F.2** This coverage applies to a person other than an "insured". The accident must occur under the circumstances described in this Coverage.

**F.4** Medical expenses include reasonable charges for medical, surgical, dental, ambulance, hospital, professional nursing, prosthetic device, and funeral services. The charges must arise from the bodily injury caused by the accident.

**F.5** We cover bodily injury sustained by a person on an "insured location" with the permission of an insured. The person must not be an insured.

**F.8** We cover bodily injury sustained by a person away from an insured location if the bodily injury is caused by the activities of an insured. The activities need not occur on the insured location.

**F.9** We cover bodily injury sustained by a person away from an insured location if the bodily injury is caused by a "residence employee". The residence employee must be engaged in the employment of an insured.

**F.10** We cover bodily injury sustained by a person away from an insured location if the bodily injury is caused by an animal owned by or in the care of an insured. The animal need not be on the insured location when the accident occurs.

**F.11** An insured must provide us with information we need to determine whether medical expenses are covered. The information must be provided promptly after a request by us.

**F.12** An insured must authorize us to obtain medical records that are reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim. An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the claim.

**F.13** A person seeking payment must submit to physical examination by physicians selected by us when reasonably required. We will pay the reasonable cost of an examination we require.

**F.14** A person seeking payment must provide written proof of the medical expenses claimed. The proof must show that the expenses resulted from the bodily injury.

**F.15** We may pay the injured person, an insured, or a person legally authorized to receive payment for the injured person. Payment to a proper recipient discharges our obligation for that payment.

**F.16** We do not cover bodily injury to an insured. This exclusion applies even if the insured is otherwise entitled to recover damages from another insured.

**F.17** We do not cover bodily injury to a person regularly residing on any part of an insured location. This exclusion does not apply to a residence employee.

**F.18** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of a person's employment by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury sustained by a residence employee for which workers compensation benefits are not required.

**F.19** We do not cover bodily injury for which benefits are payable under workers compensation law, nonoccupational disability law, or occupational disease law. This exclusion applies whether benefits are claimed or not.

**F.20** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of the rendering or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies to services performed by an insured or by another person acting for an insured.

**F.21** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of a business conducted from an insured location or engaged in by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the business is owned by an insured or by another person.

**F.22** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle. This exclusion applies unless the bodily injury is otherwise covered by this Coverage.

**G.51** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this policy. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not relieve an insured of duties imposed by this policy.

**G.52** A person may not bring a legal action against us unless that person has complied with all policy provisions. A person may not bring a legal action against us unless the action is brought within the time permitted by applicable law.

**G.53** No person has a right under this policy to join us as a party to an action against an insured. No person may bring us into an action against an insured unless applicable law requires otherwise.

**G.54** We may investigate and settle a claim as we consider appropriate. Our investigation or settlement of a claim does not constitute an admission of liability.

**G.55** We may defend a suit seeking damages covered by this policy. We may discontinue a defense when the applicable limit of liability has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements.

**G.56** An insured must not incur expense for legal representation without our consent. We do not owe reimbursement for an unauthorized expense unless we are legally obligated to provide reimbursement.

**G.57** If more than one policy issued by us applies to the same loss, our total liability will not exceed the greatest amount payable under any one policy. This condition applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims, or covered properties.

**G.58** If other insurance applies to the same loss, we will pay our share as provided by applicable law and the terms of this policy. An insured must disclose other insurance that may apply to a loss.

**G.59** An insured must notify us of any other person or insurer that may be liable for a loss. An insured must provide information reasonably requested to identify available insurance or responsible parties.

**G.60** We do not cover a loss caused by an intentional act of an insured when the loss is expected or intended by that insured. This exclusion applies even if another insured did not participate in the intentional act.

**G.61** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising from an insured's criminal act when applicable law permits exclusion. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the insured is charged with or convicted of an offense.

**G.62** An insured must comply with applicable duties relating to preservation of evidence. An insured must not alter, destroy, conceal, or dispose of evidence relevant to a claim without our consent.

**G.63** An insured must promptly notify us if a claimant makes a demand for damages. A "claimant" includes a person asserting a right to payment, services, or other relief from an insured.

**G.64** We may require a release, assignment, or other document before making payment. Any document we require must be reasonably related to the claim or our rights under this policy.

**G.65** Our payment of a claim does not waive any right we have under this policy. A payment made while we investigate a claim does not establish coverage for any other claim or loss.

**G.66** The policy terms that are valid and enforceable will remain in effect if another term is held invalid or unenforceable. Any invalid or unenforceable term will be applied to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**G.67** Notices required of us may be mailed, delivered, or transmitted by another method permitted by applicable law. Notices required of an insured must be delivered by a method reasonably acceptable to us.

**G.68** An insured must provide truthful information in connection with an application, claim, examination, or proof of loss. We may rely upon information provided by an insured unless we have reason to know it is inaccurate.

**G.69** We do not provide coverage beyond that required by the express terms of this policy. No statement by an agent or representative changes this policy unless the change is made in writing by us.

**G.70** The rights and duties under this policy apply to us and to each insured. An insured's compliance with these conditions is a condition precedent to our obligation to provide coverage, except where prohibited by applicable law.
