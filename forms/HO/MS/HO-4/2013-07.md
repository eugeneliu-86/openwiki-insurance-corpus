# HO-4 Homeowners 4 — Contents Broad Form (Edition 2013-07)
> SUPERSEDED by HO-4 Homeowners 4 — Contents Broad Form (Edition 2021-10) for policies effective on or after 2021-10-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

We provide the insurance described in this "policy" in return for the premium and subject to all applicable provisions. This policy consists of the declarations, endorsements, and policy conditions.

We will insure you for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a covered peril. Coverage applies only when the loss occurs during the policy period.

We will pay only for a "covered loss" for which an "insured" is legally entitled to recover under this policy. Payment will be subject to the applicable limits, exclusions, conditions, and deductible.

An insured must have an insurable interest in the covered property at the time of loss. We will not pay more than an insured's financial interest in the covered property.

We may settle any covered loss with you or with any person having a lawful interest in the covered property. Our payment will not exceed the amount we owe under this policy.

We may inspect the covered property at reasonable times. An inspection does not constitute a warranty that the property is safe or complies with any law, ordinance, or regulation.

We may adjust a covered loss and require reasonable information necessary to determine coverage. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of any claim.

We may deny coverage when an insured fails to comply with a condition of this policy and the failure is material to the claim. We may also deny coverage when an insured has concealed or misrepresented a material fact.

This policy applies only to loss occurring within the coverage territory. Coverage is subject to all terms that apply to the particular property, peril, or loss.

The declarations identify the coverages that apply and the limits available for a covered loss. An endorsement may change the terms of this policy.

Words and phrases that have special meaning are shown in quotation marks when first used in this policy. Their meaning applies wherever they appear in this policy.

If the terms of this policy conflict with the declarations or an endorsement, the declarations or endorsement will control to the extent of the conflict. All remaining terms will continue to apply.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined after deduction for depreciation and obsolescence. A worn chair is valued according to its condition immediately before damage. It includes a reduction for age, wear, and condition. It does not include the cost of a new chair without deduction for depreciation.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. A damaged appliance may be replaced with an appliance of comparable function and quality. It includes reasonable material and labor costs necessary for repair or replacement. It does not include an upgrade or improvement beyond the property damaged.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An accidental fall causing injury is an occurrence. It includes resulting bodily injury or property damage that arises from the accident. It does not include an act expected or intended to cause injury or damage.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling unit used by you as a residence, together with the part of the premises used in connection with that dwelling unit. A rented apartment used as your home is included. A garage space assigned for your use in connection with the apartment is included. A dwelling unit you do not use as your residence is not included.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are under your care. A relative residing with you is included. A person temporarily visiting your household is not included merely because that person is present. A person performing work at the residence premises is not included solely because of that work.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and premises used by you in connection with the residence premises. A parking area or storage area provided for your use in connection with your residence is included. A premises where you temporarily reside may be included when used as your residence. Property used solely for business is not included.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for money or other compensation. Operating a repair service for compensation is business. It includes providing goods or services for compensation. It does not include an activity performed solely as a volunteer without compensation.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle, trailer, or semitrailer designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration. An automobile or motorcycle is a motor vehicle. It includes a vehicle used for transportation even when it is parked. It does not include a manually propelled bicycle.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. A housekeeper employed to clean your residence is a residence employee. It includes a person performing domestic services for you. It does not include an independent contractor hired to perform specialized repair work.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death. A broken arm caused by an accidental fall is bodily injury. It includes illness resulting from physical harm. It does not include emotional distress that is not caused by bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. Damage to furniture caused by water is property damage. It includes loss of use of tangible property that has been physically injured. It does not include loss involving property that has no tangible existence.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unexpected. A pipe that unexpectedly bursts is sudden and accidental. It includes damage directly resulting from an abrupt event. It does not include damage caused by gradual deterioration, wear, or neglect.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary for normal occupancy and without a resident living at the residence premises. A dwelling emptied of furnishings and no longer used as a home is vacant. A furnished dwelling that is temporarily unoccupied is not vacant. Property stored for ordinary residential use may show that the dwelling is not vacant.

## I.A — Coverage A — Not Provided

**A.2** We do not cover a building that you occupy as a residence under Coverage A. We do not provide insurance for the building structure under this coverage. This provision does not create coverage under another provision of this policy.

**A.3** We do not cover additions or extensions attached to a dwelling under Coverage A. We do not cover fixtures that are permanently installed in such additions or extensions. This provision applies whether the additions or extensions are completed or under construction.

**A.4** We do not cover a building used in whole or in part for residential purposes under Coverage A. We do not cover the building if you own only an interest in the land on which it stands. This provision applies to a building regardless of its occupancy.

**A.5** We do not cover detached structures under Coverage A. We do not cover a structure that is separated from a dwelling by a clear space or connected to a dwelling only by a fence, utility line or similar connection. This provision applies to structures used for any purpose.

**A.6** We do not cover a garage, shed, greenhouse or other structure under Coverage A. We do not cover a structure used for storage, parking, business or recreation. This provision applies whether the structure is permanent or temporary.

**A.7** We do not cover land under Coverage A. We do not cover soil, water, plants or natural growth as part of the land. This provision applies to land on which a covered person has an ownership or possessory interest.

**A.8** We do not cover foundations under Coverage A. We do not cover footings, retaining walls or similar supports associated with a building. This provision applies whether the supports are above or below the surface of the land.

**A.9** We do not cover underground structures under Coverage A. We do not cover underground tanks, pipes, drains or similar installations as part of a building. This provision applies whether the installation is located beneath the residence or elsewhere on the premises.

**A.10** We do not cover permanently installed equipment under Coverage A. We do not cover furnaces, air-conditioning equipment, water heaters or similar equipment as part of a dwelling. This provision applies whether the equipment serves the dwelling or another structure.

**A.11** We do not cover plumbing, wiring or similar building systems under Coverage A. We do not cover those systems when they are attached to, contained in or serving a building. This provision applies to the systems and their component parts.

**A.12** We do not cover materials intended for use in constructing or repairing a building under Coverage A. We do not cover such materials while they are stored at or near the premises. This provision applies whether the materials are owned by you or held for your use.

**A.13** We do not cover building materials that have been removed from a building under Coverage A. We do not cover materials awaiting installation, disposal or salvage. This provision applies whether the materials are located inside or outside a building.

**A.14** We do not cover improvements, alterations or additions to a building under Coverage A. We do not cover property that becomes part of a building through installation or attachment. This provision applies whether the work is performed by you or by another person for you.

**A.15** We do not cover property used exclusively for business as part of a building under Coverage A. We do not cover business fixtures, equipment or installations under this coverage. This provision applies whether the property is located in a residence or another building.

**A.16** We do not cover a residence that you do not own under Coverage A. We do not cover your interest as a tenant, occupant or holder of a contract to purchase a residence. This provision applies to the building and to any part of the building.

**A.17** We do not cover a residence that you own as a condominium unit under Coverage A. We do not cover portions of the building that are owned in common with other unit owners. This provision applies to the unit and to property serving the unit.

**A.18** We do not cover a residence that you own as a cooperative interest under Coverage A. We do not cover the building or any portion of the building owned by the cooperative association. This provision applies regardless of your right to occupy the residence.

**A.19** We do not cover a manufactured home, mobile home or similar dwelling under Coverage A. We do not cover additions, attachments or supports associated with such a dwelling. This provision applies whether the dwelling is located on land owned by you or by another person.

**A.20** We do not cover a building being constructed under Coverage A. We do not cover construction equipment, temporary supports or unfinished work associated with the building. This provision applies while construction is in progress and after construction has stopped.

**A.21** We do not cover a building being renovated, remodeled or repaired under Coverage A. We do not cover the portion of the building affected by the work. This provision applies whether the work is performed under a contract or without a contract.

**A.22** We do not cover a building that is vacant, unoccupied or uninhabitable under Coverage A. We do not cover the building because you intend to occupy it or return to it. This provision applies regardless of the reason for the vacancy, unoccupancy or uninhabitability.

**A.23** We do not cover property that is intended to become part of a building under Coverage A. We do not cover property awaiting delivery, installation or incorporation into a building. This provision applies while the property is at the residence, at a job site or in storage.

**A.24** We do not cover architectural, engineering or surveying services under Coverage A. We do not cover plans, specifications, permits or related records as part of a building. This provision applies whether the services or records relate to a covered person's residence or another structure.

**A.25** We do not cover the cost of complying with a building, zoning or land-use law under Coverage A. We do not cover increased costs that result from an order of a governmental authority. This provision applies to construction, repair, removal or replacement of a building.

**A.26** We do not cover loss of use of a dwelling under Coverage A. We do not cover rental value, additional living expense or any similar interest under this coverage. This provision applies whether the loss of use results from damage to property or from another cause.

**A.27** We do not cover an interest in real property under Coverage A. We do not cover the value of an ownership interest, leasehold interest or occupancy right. This provision applies regardless of the form of the interest or the document creating it.

**A.28** You must not represent that Coverage A insures a dwelling or other building. You must not assign, pledge or transfer Coverage A as security for an interest in real property. This provision does not enlarge, change or create coverage.

**A.29** You must promptly notify us if you acquire, construct or occupy a building that you expect to insure. Your notice does not provide insurance for the building under Coverage A. Coverage remains subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions of this policy.

**A.30** You must keep records that identify property for which you seek insurance under another coverage. You must provide those records to us when we reasonably request them in connection with a claim. This provision does not provide insurance for property excluded from Coverage A.

**A.31** We do not cover a claim made solely because a building is not insured under Coverage A. We do not cover the absence of insurance as physical loss or damage. This provision applies whether the claim is made by you or by another person.

**A.32** We do not cover an obligation to insure a building under Coverage A. We do not cover a contract, agreement or representation that requires insurance for a building. This provision applies whether the obligation is written or oral.

**A.33** We do not cover the value of a building under Coverage A. We do not cover appreciation, depreciation or market value associated with a building. This provision applies even if the building is located at premises where your personal property is insured.

**A.34** We do not cover a building because personal property is located in it. We do not cover the building merely because you occupy it or use it. This provision applies to the building and to property attached to it.

**A.35** You must not include the cost of a building in a claim under Coverage A. You must separately identify personal property for which you seek payment. We may require information reasonably necessary to determine whether claimed property is a building or personal property.

**A.36** We do not cover demolition, removal or disposal of a building under Coverage A. We do not cover debris from a building that is not insured under this coverage. This provision applies whether the demolition, removal or disposal is voluntary or required.

**A.37** We do not cover a building that becomes attached to land under Coverage A. We do not cover property that is converted from personal property into a building or fixture. This provision applies when the conversion occurs before or after a loss.

**A.38** Coverage A does not insure real property. We do not provide any limit, deductible or valuation for a dwelling or other building under Coverage A. The absence of Coverage A does not reduce or increase insurance provided for property that is otherwise covered under this policy.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover the "other structures" on the "residence premises" that are set apart from the dwelling by clear space or connected to the dwelling by only a fence, utility line, or similar connection. We cover these structures only when they are used in connection with the residence premises. A structure that is not used in connection with the residence premises is not covered under this Coverage B. Coverage applies only to structures that you own or for which you are legally responsible.

**B.2** A structure may be attached to the dwelling and remain covered under this Coverage B if it is connected only by a covered walkway, breezeway, or similar access. We cover the structure as an other structure when the connection does not make it part of the dwelling. We do not cover the portion of a structure that is used as living space. You must tell us if the use or construction of an other structure changes.

**B.3** We cover other structures used for private residential purposes. We cover a detached garage, shed, or similar structure when it is used for storage, parking, or household activities. We cover fixtures and permanently installed equipment that serve the covered structure. We do not cover property used for business purposes, except as provided by this policy.

**B.4** We cover an other structure that is under construction when it is intended to become a covered structure. Coverage applies to materials and supplies at the residence premises that are intended for that construction. Coverage ends for the materials and supplies when they are permanently installed or removed from the residence premises. You must protect the structure and its materials from further damage.

**B.5** We cover an other structure that is temporarily removed from the residence premises for repair, renovation, or rebuilding. Coverage continues while the structure is at a location where the work is being performed. We do not cover a structure that is permanently removed from the residence premises. You must notify us when the structure is permanently relocated.

**B.6** We do not cover land, including the land on which an other structure is located. We do not cover water, growing vegetation, or any natural feature of the land. We do not cover the cost of excavating, grading, filling, or replacing soil. We do cover covered property that is damaged by an insured cause of loss, subject to the terms that apply to this Coverage B.

**B.7** We do not cover an other structure used in whole or in part for business purposes. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted by you or by another person. This exclusion does not apply to an other structure used only for incidental business activity that does not involve customers, clients, or employees. You must tell us when an other structure is used for business purposes.

**B.8** We do not cover an other structure rented or held for rental to any person who is not a tenant of the dwelling. We do not cover an other structure rented for use as a residence. This exclusion does not apply to an other structure rented occasionally for private residential use when the rental is not a business activity. You must notify us before an other structure is rented for any other use.

**B.9** We do not cover an other structure used to store business property. We do not cover an other structure used to manufacture, process, repair, or sell property. We do not cover an other structure used to provide services to the public. This exclusion applies even if the business activity is conducted only occasionally.

**B.10** We do not cover an other structure used to store or maintain aircraft, watercraft, or motor vehicles, except when the property is ordinarily used for private residential purposes. We do not cover the cost to repair or replace a vehicle or watercraft stored in an other structure. We do not cover a structure used for racing, commercial transportation, or similar activities. You must maintain the structure in a condition that protects covered property from further damage.

**B.11** We do not cover an other structure used to contain or support water, except for a structure designed and used as a private swimming pool. We do not cover the water, soil, or landscaping associated with the structure. We do not cover a pool, pond, or similar structure used in connection with a business. You must take reasonable steps to prevent covered property from causing damage to other property.

**B.12** We do not cover an other structure used to transmit or distribute utility services to property away from the residence premises. We do not cover underground or overhead utility lines that are not part of the covered structure. We do not cover a structure owned by a utility provider. This exclusion does not apply to a structure and equipment that serve only the residence premises.

**B.13** We do not cover an other structure that is vacant, abandoned, or unoccupied and not maintained. We do not cover deterioration, neglect, or damage resulting from failure to protect the structure. We do not cover damage that occurs after you know the structure is unsafe and fail to take reasonable measures to secure it. You must promptly repair conditions that could cause additional damage.

**B.14** We do not cover an other structure damaged by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion, unless a covered cause of loss directly causes the damage. We do not cover damage caused by insects, vermin, birds, rodents, or animals owned or kept by you. We do cover ensuing damage when a covered cause of loss results.

**B.15** We do not cover an other structure damaged by rust, corrosion, mold, wet or dry rot, or contamination. We do not cover damage caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. We do not cover the cost to remove or replace defective materials. This exclusion does not apply to ensuing damage caused by a covered cause of loss.

**B.16** We do not cover an other structure damaged by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water. We do not cover damage caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer or drain. We do not cover damage caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or not.

**B.17** We do not cover an other structure damaged by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudslide, or sinking, rising, or shifting of earth. We do not cover damage caused by settling, cracking, or expansion of foundations, pavements, patios, or other structures. We do cover ensuing damage when a covered cause of loss directly causes the damage. You must take reasonable steps to protect the structure after earth movement occurs.

**B.18** We do not cover an other structure damaged by intentional loss or by an act committed by or at the direction of an insured. We do not cover damage caused by an insured who conceals or misrepresents a material fact concerning the property or loss. We do not cover loss resulting from an insured's failure to protect the property after a loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a claim.

**B.19** We cover reasonable repairs made to protect a covered other structure from further damage caused by a covered loss. We cover the reasonable cost of removing damaged property when that removal is necessary to make covered repairs. We do not cover repairs that increase the value, size, or quality of the structure beyond its condition before the loss. You must keep receipts and records of covered repair expenses.

**B.20** We settle a covered loss to an other structure according to the applicable valuation provision of this policy. We do not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace the damaged property. We do not pay for betterment, matching, or improvements that are not required to restore the damaged property. You must provide us with reasonable access to the property and with information that we request concerning the loss.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover personal property owned or used by you while it is anywhere in the world. We cover personal property owned by others while it is on the part of the residence premises occupied by you. We cover personal property at an insured location when it is intended for use by you or a resident of your household.

**C.2** In this Coverage, "personal property" means property that is movable and that is owned or used by an insured. Personal property includes property in your possession or control. Personal property does not include land, structures, or property permanently attached to real property.

**C.3** In this Coverage, "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the grounds and structures associated with that dwelling. The residence premises must be shown in the Declarations. The residence premises includes property used by you in connection with your household.

**C.4** In this Coverage, "insured location" means the residence premises and any location where an insured temporarily resides. It also means any location acquired by you for use as a residence during the policy period. An insured location includes a cemetery plot or burial vault owned by you.

**C.5** We cover personal property while it is at the residence premises. We cover personal property while it is temporarily away from the residence premises. Coverage applies while the property is being moved, stored, or used by an insured.

**C.6** We cover personal property owned or used by a student who is a resident of your household and is temporarily residing away from the residence premises. Coverage applies while the student is enrolled in school and resides away from the residence premises. Coverage ends when the student permanently resides at another location.

**C.7** We cover personal property of guests and domestic employees while the property is at the residence premises. We cover that property only to the extent that it is not otherwise insured. We do not cover property belonging to a roomer, boarder, or tenant who is not related to an insured.

**C.8** We cover improvements and betterments made by you to a residence that you occupy but do not own. Coverage applies to improvements and betterments that you made at your expense. We do not cover improvements and betterments that are insured by other insurance.

**C.9** We cover personal property while it is in a newly acquired principal residence occupied by you. Coverage applies when the newly acquired residence replaces the residence premises. Coverage ends when the applicable policy provisions provide that the newly acquired residence is no longer newly acquired.

**C.10** We cover personal property of an insured while it is being transported to or from an insured location. Coverage applies to property that is being moved by an insured or by a person acting for an insured. We do not cover loss caused by delay, loss of market, or loss caused by dishonest acts of a person entrusted with the property.

**C.11** We cover personal property owned by an insured and used for household purposes. Household purposes include personal, family, and domestic activities. Property used primarily for business purposes is subject to the limitations stated in this Coverage.

**C.12** We cover personal property that is in the care of an insured for repair, alteration, or servicing. Coverage applies only to the extent of your interest in the property. We do not cover loss caused by the faulty workmanship or faulty materials of the person performing the work.

**C.13** We cover personal property that is borrowed by an insured for personal use. Coverage applies only while the property is in the custody of an insured. We do not cover property borrowed for business purposes unless the property is covered as business property under this Coverage.

**C.14** We cover personal property that is leased by an insured for personal use. Coverage applies only to the interest of the insured in the property. We do not cover loss to property that is insured by the owner under insurance that applies to the same loss.

**C.15** We cover personal property belonging to an insured's guest while it is at the residence premises. Coverage applies only while the guest is temporarily present. We do not cover property that is kept at the residence premises for sale or delivery.

**C.16** We cover personal property that is temporarily located in a self-storage facility. Coverage applies while the property remains in the facility and is owned or used by an insured. We do not cover loss caused by vermin, deterioration, or inherent vice unless another covered loss ensues.

**C.17** We cover personal property in a bank, safe-deposit facility, or other secure location. Coverage applies to property owned by an insured and kept there for personal purposes. We do not cover property that is held as collateral for a debt unless you have an insurable interest in the property.

**C.18** We cover personal property while it is outside the residence premises. Coverage applies anywhere in the world, subject to the provisions of this Coverage. We do not cover property while it is in the custody of a carrier or bailee unless the property is covered by another provision of this Coverage.

**C.19** The most we will pay for money and precious metals is two hundred dollars. Except that, this limit applies to money and precious metals owned or used by all insureds. The limit applies regardless of the location of the property when the loss occurs.

**C.20** The most we will pay for loss by theft of jewelry, watches, and precious stones is one thousand five hundred dollars. Subject to this limit, we cover the property when it is owned or used by an insured. The limit applies to all property in the stated category arising from the same loss.

**C.21** The most we will pay for loss by theft of firearms is two thousand dollars. Except that, the limit applies to firearms and equipment used with firearms. Coverage remains subject to all other terms that apply to loss by theft.

**C.22** The most we will pay for watercraft, including their trailers, is one thousand dollars. Subject to this limit, we cover the watercraft and trailer while they are at the residence premises or temporarily elsewhere. We do not cover watercraft used for commercial purposes.

**C.23** The most we will pay for loss by theft of silverware is two thousand five hundred dollars. Except that, the limit applies to silverware, goldware, and pewterware. Coverage applies to property owned by an insured or in an insured's possession.

**C.24** The most we will pay for business property on the residence premises is two thousand five hundred dollars. Subject to this limit, we cover business property owned or used by an insured while it is on the residence premises. The limit applies to business property regardless of the number of insureds who have an interest in it.

**C.25** The most we will pay for electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle is one thousand dollars. Except that, the limit applies only while the apparatus is in or upon a motor vehicle. Coverage applies when the apparatus is owned or used by an insured.

**C.26** We cover business property while it is on the residence premises. We cover business property belonging to an insured or in the possession of an insured. We do not cover business property that is separately insured for the same loss.

**C.27** We cover business property while it is away from the residence premises. Coverage applies only when the property is owned or used by an insured. We do not cover business property held for delivery to a customer unless the property is otherwise covered by this Coverage.

**C.28** We cover samples, tools, and other property used by an insured in a business. Coverage applies while the property is at the residence premises. We do not cover loss to property used by a business operated by an insured if the property is not otherwise covered by this Coverage.

**C.29** We cover personal property that is used for educational, recreational, or household activities. Coverage applies while the property is in the possession of an insured. We do not cover property used in a business activity except as provided for business property.

**C.30** We cover manuscripts, drawings, records, and other similar property. Coverage applies to the value of the materials used to create the property. We do not cover the value of information contained in the property.

**C.31** We cover electronic data stored on personal property. Coverage applies to the cost of blank materials and reasonable expenses to reproduce the data. We do not cover the value of the data itself or the cost of recreating data that cannot be reproduced.

**C.32** We cover portable electronic equipment owned or used by an insured. Coverage applies while the equipment is at an insured location or temporarily away from an insured location. We do not cover loss caused by unauthorized use of the equipment.

**C.33** We cover photographic, recording, and media equipment owned or used by an insured. Coverage applies while the equipment is used for personal purposes. We do not cover the cost of reproducing photographs, recordings, or media unless that cost is part of a covered loss to the property.

**C.34** We cover clothing, luggage, and personal effects owned or used by an insured. Coverage applies while the property is at an insured location or temporarily away from an insured location. We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, or gradual deterioration.

**C.35** We cover furniture, household goods, and appliances owned or used by an insured. Coverage applies while the property is at the residence premises. We do not cover property that is permanently installed in a structure when the property is part of that structure.

**C.36** We cover food and other perishable property owned by an insured. Coverage applies when the property is damaged by a covered loss. We do not cover loss caused solely by spoilage, deterioration, or a change in temperature unless the change results from a covered loss.

**C.37** We cover plants and other living property owned by an insured. Coverage applies while the property is kept for personal use at the residence premises. We do not cover loss caused by disease, infestation, or neglect.

**C.38** We cover animals owned by an insured. Coverage applies while the animals are kept for personal use at an insured location. We do not cover loss caused by disease, injury, or death unless the cause is a covered loss.

**C.39** We cover grave markers, monuments, and similar property owned by an insured. Coverage applies while the property is at a cemetery or burial location. We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, or deterioration.

**C.40** We cover property that is damaged while being used by an insured. Coverage applies when the damage is caused by a covered loss. We do not cover damage caused by the ordinary use of the property or by a process performed on the property.

**C.41** We cover property that is damaged while being moved by an insured. Coverage applies when the damage results from a covered loss. We do not cover damage caused by improper packing, loading, or handling unless another covered loss ensues.

**C.42** We cover property that is damaged while being cleaned, repaired, or restored. Coverage applies when the damage results from a covered loss. We do not cover the cost of correcting faulty workmanship or faulty materials.

**C.43** We cover property that is damaged by water escaping from a plumbing system. Coverage applies when the water causes direct physical loss to covered property. We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage.

**C.44** We cover property that is damaged by smoke. Coverage applies when the smoke results from a sudden and accidental event. We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations.

**C.45** We cover property that is damaged by power failure. Coverage applies when the power failure results from a covered loss. We do not cover loss caused by a power failure that originates away from the residence premises.

**C.46** We cover property that is damaged by theft. Coverage applies when the theft is committed by a person who does not reside with an insured. We do not cover loss caused by a person entrusted with the property, except when the person is an employee of an insured.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure against "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by a named peril. Coverage applies only while the property is subject to this policy.

**P.2** A "named peril" is a cause of loss expressly described in this section. We do not cover direct physical loss caused by a peril that is not a named peril.

**P.3** Fire or lightning is a named peril. This peril includes direct physical loss caused by heat, flames, or electrical discharge from lightning.

**P.4** Windstorm or hail is a named peril. We do not cover loss to property inside a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless windstorm or hail first damages the building and creates an opening through which the material enters.

**P.5** Explosion is a named peril. Explosion includes direct physical loss caused by the sudden release of pressure.

## I.S — Section I — Conditions

**S.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties stated in this section after a "loss" to "covered property". Compliance with these duties is a condition to recovery for a covered loss.

**S.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss and must identify the "claim" to the extent reasonably available. Notice must include information that permits us to contact the insured.

**S.3** The "deductible" applies to each covered loss unless a different deductible applies by law. The minimum Section I deductible is two hundred fifty dollars.

**S.4** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and must retain damaged property for our inspection when practicable.

**S.5** An insured must not make permanent repairs before we inspect the damaged property when inspection is reasonably necessary. An insured may make reasonable temporary repairs to protect property from further damage.

**S.6** An insured must give us reasonable access to the damaged property. An insured must permit us to inspect the property as often as reasonably required.

**S.7** An insured must show us the damaged property and any available evidence of the cause of loss. An insured must provide photographs, recordings, receipts, and other relevant records in the insured's possession.

**S.8** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property. The inventory must state the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item to the extent reasonably available.

**S.9** An insured must attach bills, receipts, and related documents that support the inventory when those materials are reasonably available. An insured must provide records that establish ownership, value, and age of damaged property.

**S.10** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably required by us. An insured must sign the examination record if it is accurately prepared.

**S.11** An insured must provide us with records and documents we request that are relevant to the claim. An insured must authorize us to obtain records from another person or organization when reasonably necessary to investigate the claim.

**S.12** Within sixty days after our request, an insured must send us a signed, sworn "proof of loss". The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary to establish the covered loss.

**S.13** The proof of loss must identify the interest of each insured and of any other person in the property. The proof of loss must state all known liens, encumbrances, and other interests affecting the property.

**S.14** An insured must state the time and cause of the loss in the proof of loss. An insured must state any changes in title, use, or occupancy that are material to the loss.

**S.15** An insured must state the actual cash value and amount of loss for damaged property in the proof of loss. An insured must state the amount claimed under this insurance.

**S.16** An insured must state all other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must promptly provide information concerning any payment made or expected from another source.

**S.17** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold a material fact relating to the insurance or the loss.

**S.18** An insured must preserve rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights without our written consent.

**S.19** If we make payment for a covered loss, we may require an assignment of rights of recovery to the extent of our payment. An insured must execute documents reasonably necessary to preserve and enforce those rights.

**S.20** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when property is stolen or when criminal conduct may have caused the loss. An insured must provide us with information concerning the report when reasonably available.

**S.21** An insured must notify us promptly if the insured learns of additional damage related to the claim. An insured must permit us to inspect the additional damage before permanent repairs are made when practicable.

**S.22** An insured must not abandon property to us unless we agree in writing. We may take possession of damaged property after a loss without accepting ownership of that property.

**S.23** We may examine, appraise, and test damaged property. An insured must cooperate with reasonable examinations, appraisals, and tests.

**S.24** We may request that damaged property be retained until our investigation is complete. An insured must comply with a reasonable request to retain property, except when retention would create a safety hazard.

**S.25** An insured must provide a release or authorization only to the extent reasonably necessary to investigate or settle the claim. We will not require an insured to release a claim unrelated to the covered loss.

**S.26** An insured must promptly notify us of a demand, notice, summons, complaint, or other legal process relating to the loss. An insured must send us copies of those materials when received.

**S.27** An insured must assist us in securing evidence, witnesses, and records relevant to the loss. An insured must attend hearings, examinations, or proceedings when reasonably required by us.

**S.28** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense other than reasonable emergency expenses without our consent. This duty does not prevent reasonable measures to protect property from further damage.

**S.29** An insured must provide information concerning prior damage to the property when reasonably requested by us. An insured must identify repairs, claims, and loss events that may affect the adjustment of the claim.

**S.30** An insured must provide information concerning the purchase, maintenance, repair, and use of damaged property when reasonably requested by us. An insured must provide available records supporting that information.

**S.31** An insured must promptly correct information provided to us if the insured learns that the information is incomplete or inaccurate. The correction must identify the information being corrected.

**S.32** We may investigate any claim and may request additional information while the investigation continues. Our investigation of a claim does not waive any condition, limitation, or exclusion.

**S.33** The claim contact deadline applies to communications concerning a claim. An insured must provide current contact information and must promptly report a change in contact information.

**S.34** The claim amount above which a loss is referred may apply to our handling of a claim. Our referral of a loss does not alter an insured's duties under this section.

**S.35** If an insured and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either party may demand an "appraisal" in writing. The demand must identify the amount of loss in dispute.

**S.36** Each party must select a competent and impartial "appraiser" within twenty days after receipt of a written demand for appraisal. Each party must notify the other party of its selected appraiser.

**S.38** The appraisers must state separately the actual cash value and the amount of loss. If the appraisers submit a written agreement to us, the amount agreed upon is binding.

**S.39** If the appraisers fail to agree, they must submit their differences to the umpire. A written agreement signed by any combination of the appraisers and umpire is binding.

**S.40** Each party must pay its selected appraiser. Each party must bear equally the other expenses of appraisal and the expenses of the umpire.

**S.41** An appraisal determines only the amount of loss. Appraisal does not determine coverage, policy interpretation, compliance with conditions, or our right to deny a claim.

**S.51** An insured must not bring a legal action against us unless the insured has complied with all applicable terms of this insurance. An insured must bring the action within two years after the date of loss.

**S.54** We may deny a claim if an insured intentionally conceals or misrepresents a material fact relating to the insurance, the property, an insured's interest, or a claim. This condition applies before and after a loss.

**S.56** An insured must maintain an insurable interest in covered property at the time of loss. We will not pay more than the amount of that insurable interest.

**S.57** An insured must promptly notify us if ownership of covered property changes after a loss. A transfer of ownership does not transfer this insurance without our written consent.

**S.58** We may inspect property at reasonable times during the policy period. Our inspection does not constitute a warranty that property is safe, complies with law, or is free from defect.

**S.59** We may obtain reports, records, and information relating to the property or claim as permitted by law. An insured must reasonably cooperate with our efforts to obtain that information.

**S.60** An insured must comply with applicable law concerning damaged property, repair, removal, and disposal. Compliance with law does not create coverage where coverage does not otherwise apply.

**S.61** An insured must preserve salvage to the extent reasonably practicable. We may take salvage into account when determining the amount of loss.

**S.62** An insured must provide access to salvage when reasonably requested by us. An insured must not dispose of salvage before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**S.63** An insured must promptly notify us if any recovered property relates to a claim for which we made payment. We may require the insured to return payment for recovered property to the extent of its value.

**S.64** We may settle a claim with an insured or with another person entitled to payment. Our settlement does not create an obligation to settle another claim in the same manner.

**S.65** A waiver of a condition is valid only if made by us in writing. Our failure to enforce a condition does not waive our right to enforce that condition later.

**S.66** An insured must promptly provide notice of a change in mailing address or electronic contact information. Notice sent to the most recent contact information provided by an insured is effective when permitted by law.

**S.67** The hurricane deductible cap applies when required by law. Application of that cap does not alter any other deductible unless required by law.

**S.69** An insured must provide reasonable assistance if we seek to recover payment from a responsible person or organization. We will not require an insured to incur an unreimbursed expense in providing that assistance unless required by law.

**S.70** An insured must not settle a claim against a responsible person or organization without our written consent if the settlement impairs our recovery rights. This condition applies only to the extent of our payment.

**S.71** If an insured receives payment from a responsible person or organization after we make payment, the insured must hold the amount received in trust for us to the extent of our recovery rights. An insured must promptly notify us of the payment.

**S.72** We may require an insured to execute a receipt for payment. The receipt may identify the claim, the property, and the amount paid.

## II.E — Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** We provide "Coverage E" for damages for which an "insured" is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence." The occurrence must take place during the policy period.

**E.2** We will pay damages for bodily injury or property damage covered by this insurance. We will not pay damages that are excluded by this policy.

**E.3** We will provide a defense at our expense by counsel of our choice if a "suit" is brought against an insured for damages covered by this insurance. We may investigate and settle any "claim" or suit that we consider appropriate.

**E.4** Our duty to defend ends when the amount we pay for damages equals the applicable limit of liability. We have no duty to provide a defense for any claim or suit that is not covered by this insurance.

**E.5** "Insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives. Insured also means residents of your household who are under your care or the care of a resident relative.

**E.6** "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. Occurrence includes resulting bodily injury or property damage that is neither expected nor intended by an insured.

**E.7** "Claim" means a demand for damages because of bodily injury or property damage. Claim includes a demand made before a suit is filed.

**E.8** "Suit" means a civil proceeding alleging damages because of bodily injury or property damage. Suit includes arbitration or other dispute resolution proceedings to which we consent.

**E.9** "Insured location" means the residence premises. Insured location also includes premises used by you in connection with the residence premises.

**E.10** Insured location includes a premises not owned by an insured where an insured is temporarily residing. Insured location includes vacant land owned by or rented to an insured.

**E.11** Insured location includes land owned by or rented to an insured on which a dwelling is being constructed for use as a residence by an insured. Insured location includes individual or family cemetery plots or burial vaults of an insured.

**E.12** An insured must promptly notify us or our agent of an occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must include available information about the time, place, and circumstances of the occurrence.

**E.13** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, or other legal paper relating to a claim or suit. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit.

**E.14** An insured must assist us, upon our request, in enforcing any right of recovery against any person or organization that may be liable for the loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**E.15** No insured may voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense other than for reasonable emergency measures. We will not reimburse an insured for any payment, obligation, or expense made without our consent.

**E.16** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage that is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury resulting from the use of reasonable force by an insured to protect persons or property.

**E.19** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle." "Motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle, trailer, or semitrailer designed for travel on public roads.

**E.20** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an "aircraft." "Aircraft" means any device used or designed for flight or navigation in the air.

**E.21** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "watercraft." "Watercraft" means a vessel designed for use on water.

**E.22** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the transmission is alleged to be intentional, negligent, or otherwise.

**E.23** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of sexual molestation, corporal punishment, or physical or mental abuse. This exclusion applies whether the conduct is committed by an insured or by another person for whom an insured may be liable.

**E.24** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage for which an insured is obligated to pay because of an agreement or contract. This exclusion does not apply to liability assumed by an insured under a written contract relating directly to the ownership, maintenance, or use of an insured location.

**E.25** We do not cover bodily injury to an insured. We do not cover bodily injury to any person eligible to receive benefits voluntarily provided or required to be provided by an insured under workers compensation, disability benefits, or similar law.

**E.26** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care of an insured.

**E.27** We do not cover property damage to property owned by, rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care of any person residing in your household. This exclusion does not apply to property damage caused by fire, smoke, or explosion.

**E.28** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a "controlled substance." Controlled substance means a substance defined by law as a controlled substance, unless prescribed by a licensed medical professional for use by an insured.

We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of discharge of a nuclear weapon.

**II.C.8** We may inspect records relating to a loss assessment. An insured must make available records within the insured's possession or control.

**II.E.1** We pay for "property damage" to property of others caused by an insured. The damaged property must be in the care of a person other than an insured when the damage occurs.

**II.E.2** For this coverage, the most we will pay is one thousand dollars. Payment is subject to the terms of this Section.

**II.E.3** We determine the amount of loss based on the reasonable cost to repair or replace the damaged property. We may pay the owner of the property or an insured who has paid the owner.

**II.E.4** An insured must promptly notify us of property damage for which coverage may apply. An insured must provide the name and contact information of the property owner when known.

**II.E.8** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, or used by an insured.

**II.E.9** We do not cover property damage arising out of an insured's business activities. We do not cover property damage arising out of the rendering or failure to render professional services.

**II.E.10** We do not cover property damage intentionally caused by an insured. We do not cover property damage expected by an insured.

**II.E.11** We do not cover property damage to property for which an insured has assumed responsibility under a contract. We do not cover property damage to property entrusted to an insured for alteration, repair, or servicing.

**II.E.12** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a property damage claim. An insured must provide records and statements we reasonably request.

**II.E.13** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent. This duty does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable action to protect property from further damage.

**II.E.14** We may pursue recovery from a person responsible for property damage after making payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

## II.S — Section II — Conditions

**S.1** The limit of liability shown in the Declarations is the most we will pay for all damages resulting from any "occurrence." This limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims made, or persons injured.

**S.2** All bodily injury and property damage resulting from a continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions are considered to arise from the same occurrence. This applies even if the injury or damage is sustained by more than one person.

**S.3** This insurance applies separately to each insured. This provision does not increase our limit of liability for any occurrence.

**S.4** An insured must give us prompt notice of an occurrence that may result in a claim. The notice must identify the insured and provide available information concerning the occurrence.

**S.5** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, or other legal paper relating to a claim or "suit." An insured must not withhold any such material from us.

**S.6** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of any claim or suit. An insured must assist us in securing evidence and obtaining the attendance of witnesses when reasonably requested.

**S.7** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**S.8** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request. An insured must answer questions concerning the occurrence, the claim, and any injury or damage involved.

**S.9** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent. This duty does not apply to expenses incurred to provide necessary emergency aid for bodily injury at the time of an occurrence.

**S.10** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect persons and property from further injury or damage. An insured must preserve evidence relevant to the occurrence when reasonably possible.

**S.11** An insured must not alter, dispose of, or destroy damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. This duty applies when the property may be relevant to a claim.

**S.12** An insured must promptly notify us of any claim for damages because of bodily injury or "property damage." The notice must include available information about the claimant and the nature of the alleged injury or damage.

**S.13** An insured must notify us if a claimant threatens or begins a suit. An insured must provide us with information available concerning the allegations and requested relief.

**S.14** No insured may bring legal action against us unless the insured has complied with all applicable duties under this insurance. No insured may bring legal action against us concerning liability coverage unless the obligation to pay has been finally determined by judgment or written agreement signed by us.

**S.15** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this insurance. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not give any person a right of action against us unless that right exists without regard to such bankruptcy or insolvency.

**S.17** This insurance applies only to an occurrence that takes place during the policy period. The occurrence must arise from conditions existing during that policy period.

**S.18** We do not cover a claim if an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance relating to this insurance. This condition applies before or after a loss.

**S.19** We do not cover a claim if an insured has engaged in fraud relating to this insurance. Fraud includes fraudulent conduct concerning an occurrence, claim, or loss.

**S.20** A waiver or change of any provision of this insurance is effective only if made by us in writing. Our failure to enforce a provision does not constitute a waiver of that provision.

**S.21** An assignment of this insurance is not valid unless we give written consent. This condition does not prevent transfer of rights following the death of an insured as provided by applicable law.

**S.22** If a named insured dies, we provide coverage to the legal representative of the deceased insured. Coverage applies only with respect to duties and property interests that the deceased insured had at the time of death.

**S.23** If an insured dies, we provide coverage to a resident of the household who was an insured at the time of death. Coverage also applies to persons having temporary custody of covered property until a legal representative is appointed.

**S.24** If an insured dies, we provide coverage to any person having proper temporary custody of an insured location. This coverage applies only with respect to liability arising from maintenance or use of that location.

**S.25** If we make a payment under this insurance, we are subrogated to the rights of the person to whom payment was made. That person must do nothing after a loss to impair our rights of recovery.

**S.26** A person to whom we make payment must cooperate with us in enforcing our rights of recovery. That person must execute papers and take actions reasonably required to secure those rights.

**S.27** An insured must not abandon property to us. We may take possession of damaged property when necessary to adjust a claim.

**S.28** Our inspection of property or an insured location does not constitute a warranty that the property or location is safe. Our inspection does not create a duty to any person.

**S.29** Information provided by us concerning hazards, conditions, or recommendations does not constitute an undertaking to inspect, supervise, or control property. An insured remains responsible for the condition and maintenance of property under the insured's control.

**S.33** A claim made by one insured against another insured is subject to all terms of this insurance. The interests and duties of each insured remain subject to the applicable provisions.

**S.34** Knowledge possessed by one insured does not automatically establish knowledge by another insured. However, an insured with knowledge relevant to a claim must provide that information to us when reasonably requested.

**S.36** An insured must provide us with a signed statement when we reasonably request one. The statement must identify known facts concerning the occurrence, the claim, and the damages alleged.

**S.38** We may settle any claim or suit as we consider appropriate. Our duty to defend ends when our applicable limit of liability has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements.

**S.40** An insured must promptly inform us of any facts learned after reporting a claim that may affect coverage, liability, or damages. This duty continues while the claim or suit remains pending.

**S.42** An insured must not make statements or admissions that knowingly misrepresent material facts concerning a claim. An insured must correct a material misstatement promptly after discovering it.
