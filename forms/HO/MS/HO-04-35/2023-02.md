# HO 04 35 Loss Assessment Coverage (Edition 2023-02)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

**W.1** This endorsement modifies the insurance provided by your policy. It becomes part of your policy when attached to it.

**W.2** The provisions of this endorsement apply only while it is attached to your policy. All other provisions of your policy remain unchanged unless this endorsement states otherwise.

**W.3** We provide the coverage described in this endorsement subject to its terms and conditions. You must comply with all applicable policy conditions.

**W.4** The terms of this endorsement apply to you and to any insured seeking coverage under this endorsement. An insured must satisfy all duties that apply to a claim.

**W.5** The provisions of this endorsement control when they conflict with another provision of your policy. A provision of your policy that does not conflict with this endorsement continues to apply.

**W.6** Coverage under this endorsement is subject to all exclusions that apply to your policy unless this endorsement states otherwise. We do not cover loss excluded by your policy unless this endorsement specifically provides coverage for that loss.

**W.7** The words and phrases used in this endorsement have the meanings assigned to them by your policy unless this endorsement provides a different meaning. The meaning assigned by this endorsement applies wherever that word or phrase is used in this endorsement.

**W.8** An insured must give us the information we need to determine whether coverage applies. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

**W.9** Payment under this endorsement is subject to the applicable limits and conditions of your policy. Payment does not increase a limit that applies under your policy unless this endorsement states otherwise.

**W.10** We may rely on the statements, representations, and information provided by you or an insured. An insured must correct information that is material to a claim when the insured becomes aware that the information is incorrect.

**W.11** This endorsement does not create coverage for a loss that is not otherwise covered under its terms. It does not change any right or duty unless it expressly does so.

**W.12** An insured seeking coverage under this endorsement must meet the conditions that apply to that coverage. We may deny coverage when an insured fails to meet an applicable condition.

**W.13** This endorsement applies in accordance with the policy provisions that govern its attachment, cancellation, renewal, and termination. Its provisions cease to apply when it is no longer attached to your policy.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide coverage for a "Loss Assessment" charged against you by an "Association" in accordance with this coverage. A Loss Assessment must arise from a covered cause of loss or from covered liability.

**W.2** A Loss Assessment means your share of an assessment imposed by an Association against property owners or tenants. The assessment must be legally chargeable to you in your capacity as an owner or tenant.

**W.3** An Association means a corporation, association, cooperative, partnership, trust, or similar entity that administers property owned or controlled by its members collectively. An Association includes an entity having authority to assess its members for loss, expense, damage, or liability.

**W.4** We cover your share of an assessment resulting from "Direct Physical Loss" to "Collective Property." The Direct Physical Loss must be caused by a "Covered Cause of Loss."

**W.5** Collective Property means real property, personal property, or other property owned by all members of an Association collectively. Collective Property includes property for which an Association is responsible by agreement or governing instrument.

**W.6** Direct Physical Loss means accidental physical loss of or physical damage to tangible property. Direct Physical Loss does not include a loss that is merely economic, contractual, or consequential.

**W.7** Covered Cause of Loss means a cause of loss for which coverage would apply if the Collective Property were owned by you. Coverage applies only to the extent that the cause of loss is not excluded.

**W.8** We cover an assessment arising from a "Deductible" applicable to a "Master Policy" insuring Collective Property. The Direct Physical Loss giving rise to the Deductible must be caused by a Covered Cause of Loss.

**W.9** A Deductible means the portion of a covered loss that an insurer does not pay under a Master Policy. An assessment for a Deductible is covered only when the Deductible is charged to you by the Association.

**W.10** A Master Policy means insurance maintained by or for an Association for Collective Property. A Master Policy includes insurance maintained for the benefit of members of the Association.

**W.11** We cover your share of an assessment arising from "Property Damage" for which the Association is legally liable. The Property Damage must result from an "Occurrence" to which this coverage applies.

**W.12** Property Damage means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. Property Damage includes resulting loss of use of property that has not been physically injured.

**W.13** An Occurrence means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. The accident must result in Property Damage during the time this coverage applies.

**W.14** We cover an assessment arising from a judgment, settlement, or expense for Property Damage for which the Association is legally responsible. The assessment must be imposed upon you because of your ownership or tenancy interest.

**W.15** We cover only the portion of an assessment that is properly allocated to you. We do not cover any portion allocated to another member, owner, tenant, mortgagee, or other person.

**W.16** We do not cover an assessment that is not legally enforceable against you. We do not cover an assessment that you voluntarily assume without an enforceable obligation.

**W.17** We do not cover an assessment arising from loss to property that is not Collective Property. We do not cover an assessment for loss to property owned solely by you.

**W.18** We do not cover an assessment arising from ordinary maintenance, upkeep, repair, restoration, improvement, or replacement that is not made necessary by Direct Physical Loss. We do not cover an assessment for wear, deterioration, corrosion, decay, or latent defect.

**W.19** We do not cover an assessment arising from faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. This exclusion applies whether performed by you, an Association, or another person or organization.

**W.20** We do not cover an assessment arising from an increase in value, betterment, expansion, modernization, or enhancement of Collective Property. We do not cover an assessment for a cost that would have been incurred without Direct Physical Loss.

**W.21** We do not cover an assessment arising from fines, penalties, punitive damages, exemplary damages, sanctions, or assessments imposed for violation of law. We do not cover an assessment arising from an intentional violation of an Association rule or governing instrument.

**W.22** We do not cover an assessment arising from loss caused intentionally by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the Association also sustains loss from an accidental event.

**W.23** We do not cover an assessment arising from dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, or malicious acts by an insured. We do not cover an assessment arising from embezzlement, conversion, or misappropriation of Association funds.

**W.24** We do not cover an assessment for loss arising from an insured's business, trade, profession, occupation, or commercial activity. We do not cover an assessment arising from property used primarily for business purposes.

**W.25** We do not cover an assessment arising from contractual liability assumed by an Association unless the liability would exist without the contract. We do not cover an assessment arising solely from an indemnification agreement.

**W.26** We do not cover an assessment arising from a breach of contract, breach of fiduciary duty, misrepresentation, fraud, or failure to perform a contractual obligation. We do not cover an assessment based solely on an economic loss.

**W.27** We do not cover an assessment for loss of market value, loss of investment value, diminution in value, or inability to use or sell property. We do not cover an assessment for interest, financing charges, collection costs, or administrative expenses.

**W.28** We do not cover an assessment that is imposed before a covered loss occurs. We do not cover an assessment imposed after your ownership or tenancy interest has ended unless the assessment arises from a covered loss that occurred while your interest existed.

**W.29** We do not cover an assessment that results from a voluntary reserve, contingency fund, capital fund, or similar accumulation of money. We do not cover an assessment intended to replenish an Association fund that was not depleted by a covered loss.

**W.30** We do not cover an assessment arising from governmental action, ordinance, law, regulation, or enforcement activity unless the assessment results directly from a covered Direct Physical Loss. We do not cover any increased cost required solely because of a law or ordinance.

**W.31** We do not cover an assessment arising from condemnation, seizure, confiscation, nationalization, or destruction of property by order of a governmental authority. We do not cover an assessment arising from delay, loss of use, or loss of income resulting from such action.

**W.32** We do not cover an assessment arising from war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurpation of power, or military action. We do not cover an assessment arising from discharge of a nuclear weapon.

**W.33** We do not cover an assessment arising from nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. We do not cover an assessment arising from the hazardous properties of nuclear material.

**W.34** An insured must give us prompt notice of a Loss Assessment or circumstances that may result in a Loss Assessment. Notice must include information reasonably available to you concerning the Association, the loss, and the assessment.

**W.35** An insured must promptly send us copies of notices, demands, assessment statements, governing instruments, repair proposals, and other records relating to the Loss Assessment. An insured must preserve available evidence of the cause and amount of the loss.

**W.36** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a Loss Assessment. An insured must assist us in obtaining records and information from the Association and other persons having relevant information.

**W.37** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, incur expense, or settle a Loss Assessment without our consent. This condition does not apply to reasonable expenses incurred to protect property from further covered damage.

**W.38** An insured must permit us to inspect Collective Property, damaged property, relevant records, and available evidence. An insured must provide authorization for us to obtain pertinent records from an Association, contractor, insurer, or public authority.

**W.39** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably required by us. An insured must provide a signed statement of the facts surrounding the Loss Assessment when reasonably required by us.

**W.40** An insured must establish the legal basis for the assessment and the portion properly allocated to you. An insured must establish that the assessment arises from a Covered Cause of Loss or covered liability.

**W.41** We may investigate and settle any Loss Assessment as we consider appropriate. Our settlement of a Loss Assessment does not constitute an admission of liability or coverage for any other assessment.

**W.42** We may pay the Association, you, or another person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment to an Association satisfies our obligation for the amount paid.

**W.43** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for all covered Loss Assessments arising from the same occurrence. The applicable limit of liability is the most we will pay regardless of the number of insureds, claims, assessments, members, or Associations involved.

**W.44** A covered Loss Assessment is subject to any deductible that applies to this coverage. We will apply the deductible only to the covered portion of the Loss Assessment.

**W.45** We will reduce payment by amounts recovered or recoverable from another insurer, responsible person, organization, or source. We will not reduce payment by amounts that are not legally available to you or the Association.

**W.46** If we make payment, we are entitled to recover from any person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.47** An insured must transfer to us any rights of recovery against another person or organization to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing those rights.

**W.48** Coverage applies only when the assessment is charged against you while you have an ownership or tenancy interest in the property subject to the Association. Coverage does not apply to an assessment charged solely because of a former ownership or tenancy interest.

**W.49** All provisions of this coverage apply separately to each insured. This provision does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The amount we pay under "Loss Assessment Coverage" is subject to this provision. Payment is also subject to all applicable policy terms.

**W.2** The most we will pay is twenty-five thousand dollars. This amount applies to covered loss assessments.

**W.3** Any payment we make reduces the amount available for later payment under this coverage. Payment to any person is payment for the benefit of all persons entitled to payment for the same loss assessment.

**W.4** An "association" is an organization having authority to charge an assessment to you. The association must have a lawful basis for the assessment.

**W.5** A "property loss" is direct physical loss to property for which an assessment is charged. The property loss must be covered under the applicable policy terms.

**W.6** A "loss assessment" is an amount charged to you by an association because of a property loss. The amount charged must be attributable to your ownership interest or other legal responsibility.

**W.7** An "occurrence" is an event or related series of events causing property loss. We apply this provision to the occurrence giving rise to the assessment.

**W.8** An assessment remains subject to this provision even if the association issues separate notices to you. The form of the notice does not change the amount payable by us.

**W.9** An assessment remains subject to this provision even if the association allocates the property loss among its members. The allocation used by the association does not increase our payment obligation.

**W.10** We determine the amount payable from the assessment that is properly chargeable to you. We may consider the association's governing records and applicable law when making that determination.

**W.11** Payment is limited to the portion of the assessment arising from covered property loss. Amounts arising from matters not covered by this policy are not included in the amount payable.

**W.12** An assessment must be imposed by an association acting within its authority. An assessment that is invalid, rescinded, or unenforceable is not payable by us.

**W.13** A change in the assessment may change the amount payable by us. An insured must promptly provide us with written notice of the change.

**W.14** A reduction, credit, refund, or waiver applied to an assessment reduces the amount payable by us. An insured must report any such reduction, credit, refund, or waiver to us.

**W.15** An "insured" must establish that the assessment is properly chargeable to that insured. An insured must provide records supporting the amount claimed.

**W.16** An insured must cooperate with us in verifying the association's authority and the basis for the assessment. An insured must authorize us to obtain relevant records from the association when we request them.

**W.17** We may pay the amount payable to you, to the association, or to another person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment made in good faith discharges our obligation to the extent of that payment.

**W.18** We may make payment after receiving satisfactory evidence of the assessment and the covered property loss. Our payment does not constitute an admission concerning liability for any amount not paid.

**W.19** A payment by an association, another member, or any other source reduces the amount payable by us. An insured must preserve any right to reimbursement that may apply to the assessment.

**W.20** An insured must not impair our right to recover payment from a person responsible for the property loss. If an insured impairs that right, we may reduce payment to the extent we are prejudiced.

**W.21** An assessment that is combined with other charges must be separately identified. An insured must provide information sufficient to determine the portion attributable to covered property loss.

**W.22** Costs incurred by an association to investigate, administer, collect, or dispute an assessment are payable only to the extent they are part of the covered loss assessment. An insured must establish that such costs are properly included in the assessment.

**W.23** Interest, penalties, late charges, and similar additions are payable only to the extent they are part of the covered loss assessment. An insured must take reasonable action to avoid avoidable additions to an assessment.

**W.24** Our payment obligation is determined without regard to any amount an association seeks but does not lawfully charge to you. A demand for payment is not, by itself, proof of a covered loss assessment.

**W.25** A dispute between you and an association does not expand our payment obligation. An insured must notify us of a dispute that may affect the amount payable.

**W.26** We may rely on a final determination of an association or a court concerning the assessment. We may also rely on records that reasonably establish the amount properly chargeable to you.

**W.27** Our payment does not relieve you of duties imposed by the association or by law. Your compliance with those duties does not expand our payment obligation.

**W.28** This provision applies whether an assessment is charged directly to you or through an intermediary acting for the association. The method used to collect the assessment does not change the amount payable by us.

**W.29** This provision does not increase any other coverage available under this policy. Payment under this coverage is not available for the same amount under another coverage provided by us.

**W.30** The amount payable under this provision is determined at the time we make payment. An insured must promptly repay us for any payment received in excess of the amount properly payable.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** We will apply the "Deductible" to the amount otherwise payable for a "loss assessment" under "Loss Assessment Coverage." We will subtract the applicable deductible from the amount of the covered loss.

**W.2** The deductible applies only to a "covered loss." We will not make payment for any amount that does not exceed the applicable deductible.

**W.3** We will apply the deductible before determining the amount we will pay for a loss assessment. The deductible does not increase the amount otherwise payable.

**W.4** An "insured" must pay the deductible that applies to the covered loss. We will not pay the deductible on your behalf.

**W.5** The deductible applies regardless of whether the loss assessment is paid directly by you or by another person acting for you. Payment of an assessment does not eliminate the applicable deductible.

**W.6** We will apply the deductible to the loss assessment for which coverage is sought. We will not apply a deductible to an amount that is not covered.

**W.7** An insured must provide us with information necessary to determine the applicable deductible. This information includes the assessment, the cause of the assessment, and the amount for which payment is sought.

**W.8** We may request records from you concerning the loss assessment. An insured must cooperate with us in determining whether the deductible applies.

**W.9** We will determine the deductible based on the facts of the covered loss. A change in the amount of the assessment may change the amount payable after application of the deductible.

**W.10** The deductible applies before we consider any recovery that may be available from another source. A recovery received after our payment may affect the amount that remains payable.

**W.11** An insured must notify us of any recovery received for a covered loss. We may adjust payment to reflect the deductible and any applicable recovery.

**W.12** We will not apply the deductible to a loss assessment that is withdrawn before payment becomes due. We may require documentation showing that the assessment was withdrawn.

**W.13** If an assessment is revised, we will apply the deductible to the revised assessment. An insured must promptly provide us with the revised assessment.

**W.14** We will apply the deductible to the amount payable after determining whether the loss assessment is covered. The deductible does not apply to costs that are not payable under this coverage.

**W.15** An insured must not represent that we will pay the deductible. Only we may determine the amount payable after application of the deductible.

**W.16** We may pay a covered amount directly to you, to an association, or to another party entitled to payment. Any such payment remains subject to the applicable deductible.

**W.17** The deductible applies even if the assessment is imposed after the event causing the covered loss. The time at which an assessment is imposed does not change the applicable deductible.

**W.18** An insured must retain records supporting payment of the deductible. We may require those records before making payment for a covered loss.

**W.19** We will not waive the deductible unless we agree in writing. A waiver applies only to the covered loss identified in our written agreement.

**W.20** Our application of the deductible does not affect any duty imposed on an insured by this coverage. An insured must continue to comply with all applicable duties concerning the loss assessment.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover a "loss assessment" that is not imposed upon you as an owner of property. The assessment must arise from property for which you have an ownership interest.

**W.2** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from an assessment imposed for routine maintenance. Maintenance includes work performed to preserve, service, or maintain property.

**W.3** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. This exclusion applies whether the condition is sudden or gradual.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover an assessment attributable to flood or surface water.

**W.5** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mudslide, subsidence, sinkhole collapse, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.6** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from water damage caused by sewer backup or sump overflow. This exclusion applies to water that enters property through drains, sewers, sumps, or related equipment.

**W.7** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from water that backs up through a drain or sewer. A backwater valve does not create coverage for an otherwise excluded loss.

**W.8** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building or other structure.

**W.9** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from rain, snow, sleet, or ice that enters through an opening not caused by a covered direct physical loss. An opening caused by faulty construction, maintenance, or repair does not establish coverage.

**W.10** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of water, ice, snow, or sleet. This exclusion applies to damage to a structure, equipment, or personal property.

**W.11** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from power failure or interruption of utility service. This exclusion applies whether the failure occurs on or away from the insured property.

**W.12** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from the failure of power, heating, air conditioning, refrigeration, or protective systems. The cause of the system failure does not alter this exclusion.

**W.13** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies to property in any stage of construction or repair.

**W.14** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, pavements, patios, or ceilings. Resulting cracking or structural movement is also excluded.

**W.15** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from smog, smoke, vapor, fumes, or industrial pollutants. This exclusion does not depend upon the source of the contaminant.

**W.16** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, escape, or presence of pollutants. "Pollutants" include contaminants, irritants, waste, chemicals, and hazardous substances.

**W.17** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or other microbial matter. This exclusion applies to remediation, testing, monitoring, removal, or replacement.

**W.18** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from rust, corrosion, oxidation, or electrolysis. Damage caused by a chemical reaction is also excluded.

**W.19** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. This exclusion includes nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, contamination, and waste.

**W.20** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from animals owned or kept by an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of the animal's location when the injury or damage occurs.

**W.21** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from intentional loss. An insured must not cause or arrange for loss with the intent to cause loss.

**W.22** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from criminal, fraudulent, dishonest, or malicious acts. This exclusion applies whether committed by an insured, an association member, or another person.

**W.23** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, invasion, and military action.

**W.24** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from seizure, confiscation, destruction, or damage by order of a governmental authority. This exclusion does not apply where the authority acts to prevent the spread of fire.

**W.25** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies whether the event is controlled or uncontrolled.

**W.26** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from collapse unless the collapse results from a covered cause of loss. Cracking, bulging, settling, shrinking, or expansion is not collapse.

**W.27** We do not cover a loss assessment for repair, replacement, or improvement of property that has not sustained direct physical loss. Economic loss alone does not constitute direct physical loss.

**W.28** We do not cover a loss assessment imposed to correct a building code violation. This exclusion includes costs arising from ordinance, law, regulation, or governmental requirement.

**W.29** We do not cover a loss assessment imposed to upgrade property. Upgrades include changes intended to improve efficiency, appearance, safety, utility, or market value.

**W.30** We do not cover a loss assessment for deferred maintenance. A condition known to the association or its representatives does not become covered because an assessment is later imposed.

**W.31** We do not cover a loss assessment for ordinary operating expenses. Operating expenses include administrative, management, accounting, legal, and professional expenses not caused by a covered direct physical loss.

**W.32** We do not cover a loss assessment for reserve funding, replacement funding, or contingency funding. The purpose for which an association collects funds does not create coverage.

**W.33** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from a contract, agreement, warranty, guarantee, or voluntary undertaking. Liability assumed by an association does not create a covered loss assessment.

**W.34** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from a penalty, fine, forfeiture, interest charge, late charge, or similar charge. This exclusion applies even if the charge is included within an assessment.

**W.35** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from diminution in value. Loss of use, loss of market value, loss of investment value, and loss of anticipated income are excluded.

**W.36** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from defective title, lien, encumbrance, or boundary dispute. This exclusion includes costs to establish, defend, or transfer ownership rights.

**W.37** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from a dispute concerning governance, voting, elections, or association records. Legal expenses and administrative expenses associated with such a dispute are excluded.

**W.38** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from an association's failure to obtain, maintain, renew, or collect insurance. The absence, insufficiency, cancellation, or exhaustion of other insurance does not create coverage.

**W.39** We do not cover a loss assessment to the extent another insurance policy, bond, warranty, service contract, or indemnity agreement provides recovery. An insured must seek available recovery from the applicable source.

**W.40** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from a deductible applicable to property not subject to a covered direct physical loss. The application of a deductible does not by itself establish coverage.

**W.41** We do not cover a loss assessment merely because an association applies a "wind percentage deductible minimum." The application of that deductible term does not change an excluded cause of loss.

**W.42** We do not cover a loss assessment based solely upon a "Coverage B limit as a percentage of Coverage A." A stated limit relationship does not establish coverage for property or loss otherwise excluded.

**W.43** We do not cover a loss assessment based solely upon a "personal property limit percentage." The existence of that limit term does not enlarge the property subject to coverage.

**W.44** We do not cover a loss assessment based solely upon an "additional living expense limit." A limit applicable to living expenses does not create coverage for an assessment.

**W.45** We do not cover a loss assessment based solely upon a "clean-up allowance percentage." An allowance applicable to cleanup does not create coverage for an excluded condition.

**W.46** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from the failure to protect property from further damage. An insured must use reasonable means to protect property from further damage after a loss.

**W.47** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from neglect by an insured or an association after a loss. Neglect includes failure to preserve damaged property for our inspection.

**W.48** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from concealment, misrepresentation, fraud, or false statement by an insured. An insured must provide truthful information concerning the assessment and the claimed loss.

**W.49** We do not cover a loss assessment for which an insured fails to provide requested records, notices, or supporting information. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation of a claimed loss assessment.

**W.50** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from property used primarily for business, manufacturing, farming, or commercial purposes. Incidental personal use does not change the character of property used for such purposes.

**W.51** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from property held for sale. This exclusion applies to property offered, listed, marketed, or transferred in the ordinary course of business.

**W.52** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from loss to land. Land includes soil, fill, grading, landscaping, lawns, trees, shrubs, plants, and growing crops.

**W.53** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from erosion, shoreline recession, or movement of earth caused by water. The presence of a structure near the affected land does not create coverage.

**W.54** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from repeated seepage, leakage, or overflow of water or steam. This exclusion applies to water or steam from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems.

**W.55** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from failure of a dam, levee, dike, retaining wall, seawall, bulkhead, or similar structure. This exclusion applies whether the structure is natural or artificial.

**W.56** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from mechanical, electrical, electronic, or pressure-system breakdown. Damage limited to the failed component is excluded.

**W.57** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from latent defect or inherent vice. A condition that causes property to damage or destroy itself is excluded.

**W.58** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from loss caused by birds, insects, rodents, or animals to property kept outdoors. Exposure of property to weather does not create coverage for the resulting damage.

**W.59** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from voluntary parting with property by an insured or an association. This exclusion applies when possession is obtained by trick, false pretense, or fraudulent scheme.

**W.60** We do not cover any assessment that is excluded by these provisions, regardless of the method used to calculate or collect the assessment. An assessment remains excluded when it includes both covered and excluded charges.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with these conditions as a condition of payment under "loss assessment coverage". An insured must cause any person seeking payment through that insured to comply with these conditions.

**W.2** An insured must give us notice of a "loss" within sixty days after the insured knows of the loss. Notice must identify the "association" making or proposing the assessment, if known.

**W.3** An insured must provide information sufficient to identify the "assessment" for which payment is sought. An insured must identify the event, circumstance, or damage that gave rise to the assessment.

**W.4** An insured must promptly forward to us any notice, demand, assessment statement, invoice, correspondence, or legal paper relating to a loss. An insured must preserve the originals of those materials when reasonably possible.

**W.5** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a "claim". An insured must assist us in obtaining information from the association and from other persons having relevant knowledge.

**W.6** An insured must permit us to inspect property related to the loss when reasonably necessary. An insured must provide access to records and documents relating to the assessment.

**W.7** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from further damage after a loss. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair, protection, and mitigation expenses.

**W.8** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. This condition does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable measures to protect property from further damage.

**W.9** An insured must not abandon property to us. We may take possession of damaged property only as necessary to investigate or adjust the claim.

**W.10** An insured must provide a complete explanation of the basis on which the association imposed or proposes to impose the assessment. An insured must provide the governing documents, meeting records, and voting records relating to the assessment when available.

**W.11** An insured must provide information concerning any insurance, indemnity, reimbursement, reserve, or other source of recovery available for the loss. An insured must notify us of any payment received from another source.

**W.12** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the examination transcript if it accurately reflects the testimony given.

**W.13** An insured must provide records reasonably requested by us to verify ownership, responsibility, and the amount of the assessment. An insured must authorize the release of relevant records when the records are not otherwise available to us.

**W.14** An insured must allow us to communicate directly with the association concerning the assessment. An insured must direct the association to provide us with relevant information upon our request.

**W.15** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or omit a material fact relating to the loss or claim. We may deny coverage to the extent permitted by law when an insured breaches this condition.

**W.16** An insured must provide a signed proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the facts known to the insured concerning the assessment and the amount claimed.

**W.17** An insured must provide a sworn statement when we request it. The applicable "sworn statement deadline" governs the due date for that statement.

**W.18** An insured must notify us if the association revises, withdraws, reduces, or increases an assessment. An insured must provide the revised assessment materials promptly after receiving them.

**W.19** An insured must notify us if a dispute, appeal, arbitration, mediation, or legal proceeding concerns the assessment. An insured must not compromise that matter in a way that prejudices our rights.

**W.20** An insured must preserve evidence relating to the cause and amount of the loss. An insured must not alter, dispose of, or destroy relevant evidence unless necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.21** An insured must provide us with documents showing the association's authority to make the assessment. An insured must provide documents showing how the association allocated the assessment to the insured.

**W.22** An insured must provide information concerning the insured's interest in the property to which the assessment relates. An insured must notify us of any transfer, sale, assignment, or other change affecting that interest.

**W.23** An insured must disclose whether the assessment relates to property owned by the association, jointly owned property, or property separately owned by an insured. An insured must identify any person responsible for the damage or expense giving rise to the assessment.

**W.24** An insured must assist us in pursuing recovery from a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our rights of recovery.

**W.25** If we make a payment, an insured must transfer to us any rights of recovery that the insured has against another person or organization. An insured must execute documents and take actions reasonably necessary to secure those rights.

**W.26** An insured must hold any recovery received from another source for the benefit of the persons entitled to it. An insured must promptly notify us before distributing a recovery that may affect our payment.

**W.27** An insured must repay us to the extent that an insured receives payment for the same loss from another source. We may reduce payment by amounts recoverable from another source to the extent permitted by law.

**W.28** An insured must submit expenses for review before seeking reimbursement when reasonably possible. An insured must provide receipts, invoices, contracts, and other records supporting the expenses.

**W.29** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting persons who perform emergency, repair, or protective work. An insured must permit us to inspect completed work related to the loss.

**W.30** An insured must not agree to an assessment allocation that is materially different from the allocation authorized by the association's governing documents without our consent. An insured must provide us with notice of any proposed allocation change.

**W.31** An insured must provide information about any deductible applicable to other insurance covering the same loss. The applicable "base deductible minimum" applies in determining the amount payable by us.

**W.32** An insured must provide information concerning the value of property involved in the loss when that information is relevant to settlement. The applicable "insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement" applies when replacement cost settlement is requested.

**W.33** An insured must promptly notify us of a request by the association for funds, a special contribution, or other payment related to the assessment. An insured must not treat an anticipated assessment as a covered loss before the association has imposed liability on the insured.

**W.34** An insured must provide the association's written demand for payment when such a demand exists. An insured must provide evidence of payment if the insured has paid the assessment.

**W.35** An insured must notify us if the association receives insurance proceeds, a settlement, or reimbursement related to the same damage or expense. An insured must provide information concerning the association's use of those proceeds.

**W.36** An insured must not interfere with our investigation of the cause of loss, amount of loss, or liability for the assessment. An insured must provide truthful information in all communications concerning the claim.

**W.37** An insured must preserve all rights to challenge an improper assessment when doing so is reasonably necessary to protect our interests. An insured must consult with us before withdrawing a challenge that may affect coverage.

**W.38** An insured must notify us of any insolvency, dissolution, or inability of the association to collect from other persons responsible for the assessment. An insured must provide information concerning available association reserves and other assets.

**W.39** An insured must notify us of any lien, security interest, or other encumbrance affecting property related to the assessment. An insured must provide the names and addresses of persons claiming an interest in payment.

**W.40** An insured must keep records supporting the claim until we complete our adjustment. An insured must provide copies of those records upon our request.

**W.41** An insured must submit any disagreement about the amount of loss to the process required by law or by the applicable policy terms. An insured must cooperate in that process and provide information reasonably requested.

**W.42** An insured must not bring a legal action against us unless the insured has complied with all applicable policy conditions. The applicable "suit against us period" governs any legal action against us.

**W.43** An insured must notify us if the insured changes address, contact information, or representation while a claim remains pending. An insured must ensure that communications from us can be received and answered.

**W.44** An insured must notify us if a representative acts for the insured concerning the claim. An insured must provide written authority for that representative when we reasonably request it.

**W.45** An insured must provide information necessary for us to determine whether the assessment is covered. We may deny payment for prejudice resulting from an insured's material failure to comply with these conditions, to the extent permitted by law.

**W.46** An insured must comply with reasonable requests from us that are necessary to adjust the claim. Our request for information, inspection, examination, or cooperation does not waive any policy condition or defense.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** “Loss Assessment Coverage” applies only to a loss that results in an assessment against you. An insured must have an ownership interest in the property for which the assessment is made.

**W.2** An “association” means an organization that has authority to assess you for expenses arising from property or operations subject to its control. An insured must provide evidence of that authority upon our request.

**W.3** An “assessment” means a charge imposed upon you by an association because of a loss or expense. The assessment must be imposed in writing.

**W.4** An insured must report an assessment to us promptly after learning of it. The report must identify the association, the reason for the assessment, and the amount sought from you.

**W.5** An insured must provide us with all notices, demands, invoices, meeting records, and other documents relating to the assessment. An insured must also provide records showing the basis used by the association to allocate the assessment.

**W.6** The assessment must arise from a loss occurring while this coverage applies. An insured must establish the connection between the assessment and the loss.

**W.7** We cover an assessment for “property damage” only if the property damage results from a covered cause of loss. The damaged property must be owned by the association or be property for which the association has responsibility.

**W.8** We cover an assessment for “bodily injury” only if the bodily injury results from an occurrence for which coverage applies. The association must be legally obligated to pay damages because of that bodily injury.

**W.9** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of any matter that may result in an assessment. An insured must not interfere with our rights concerning that matter.

**W.10** An insured must notify us before consenting to an assessment, settlement, or compromise. Consent by an insured does not bind us unless we agree in writing.

**W.11** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage or expense after learning of a condition that may result in an assessment. We may require proof of those steps.

**W.12** An insured must preserve any right of recovery against a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must not waive that right without our written consent.

**W.13** We do not cover an assessment arising from your failure to pay association dues, fees, charges, fines, penalties, or similar obligations. We do not cover an assessment imposed because of your violation of an association rule, covenant, restriction, or agreement.

**W.14** We do not cover an assessment arising from a dispute concerning the ownership, use, occupancy, maintenance, repair, alteration, or improvement of property. This exclusion applies whether the dispute is asserted by an association, an owner, a tenant, or any other person.

**W.15** We do not cover an assessment for damage caused intentionally by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the assessment is imposed upon you without regard to your intent.

**W.16** We do not cover an assessment arising from dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, or malicious conduct by an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not the association also seeks recovery from another person.

**W.17** We do not cover an assessment arising from a business conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies to business activities conducted on or away from the residence premises.

**W.18** We do not cover an assessment for loss arising from the rendering or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies whether the services are performed by an insured or by a person acting for an insured.

**W.19** We do not cover an assessment for loss arising from the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle, watercraft, or aircraft. This exclusion applies unless coverage applies under this policy for that loss.

**W.20** We do not cover an assessment for loss arising from the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the pollutants originate on or away from the residence premises.

**W.21** We do not cover an assessment for loss arising from earth movement, flooding, surface water, overflow, or water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies regardless of any contributing cause or event.

**W.22** We do not cover an assessment for loss caused by war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or usurped power. We do not cover an assessment for loss caused by governmental or military action.

**W.23** We do not cover an assessment arising from ordinance, law, regulation, or order requiring demolition, repair, replacement, reconstruction, or upgrading of property. This exclusion applies even if the ordinance, law, regulation, or order is enforced because of covered property damage.

**W.24** We do not cover an assessment for the cost to correct faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, design, specification, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, grading, or compaction. This exclusion applies to property damage arising from those conditions.

**W.25** We do not cover an assessment arising from wear and tear, deterioration, rust, corrosion, decay, settling, bulging, shrinking, or expansion. This exclusion applies unless a covered cause of loss results from that condition.

**W.26** We do not cover an assessment arising from neglect by an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property after a loss. This exclusion applies only to the increased damage caused by that neglect.

**W.27** An insured must submit to examination under oath if we request it. An insured must sign the examination record when we require it.

**W.28** An insured must provide a signed statement describing the assessment and the facts supporting it. The statement must be provided within a reasonable time after our request.

**W.29** An insured must authorize us to obtain records from the association, a property manager, a contractor, a governmental authority, or another person having information about the assessment. An insured must assist us in obtaining those records.

**W.30** We may inspect property, records, repair work, and other evidence relating to the assessment. Our inspection does not constitute acceptance of liability or a waiver of any policy provision.

**W.31** We may participate in discussions, negotiations, mediation, arbitration, litigation, or other proceedings relating to the assessment. An insured must cooperate with our participation.

**W.32** An insured must not make voluntary payments for an assessment without our written consent. This duty does not prevent an insured from making payments required to protect property from further damage.

**W.33** Payment by us does not relieve an insured of any duty owed to the association. Payment by us does not alter the association's authority to collect amounts from you.

**W.34** If we make payment, we are entitled to recover from any person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must transfer recovery rights to us to the extent of our payment.

**W.35** An insured must hold any recovery received from a responsible person or organization for us to the extent of our payment. An insured must promptly deliver that recovery to us.

**W.36** An insured must notify us if the association withdraws, reduces, revises, or reallocates the assessment. An insured must provide written confirmation of that change.

**W.37** An insured must notify us if another insurer, indemnitor, association fund, or other source may pay for the assessment. An insured must provide information concerning available coverage or reimbursement.

**W.38** Coverage applies subject to all terms, conditions, exclusions, and duties of this policy. An insured's failure to comply with a duty may affect coverage to the extent permitted by law.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" to "covered property" as provided in this coverage. We will determine the amount of loss from the property affected by the covered cause of loss.

**W.2** We will pay no more than the amount for which you are legally responsible. We will not pay for a loss that is not covered under this coverage.

**W.3** We will consider the condition of damaged property immediately before the loss. We will consider material, workmanship, use, age, and any other factor affecting value.

**W.4** We may inspect damaged property and examine records relating to the loss. You must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.5** We will settle a covered "loss assessment" only after the assessment is validly made against you. The assessment must arise from property or liability for which coverage applies.

**W.6** We may require documentation from an association, corporation, or other entity that made the assessment. That documentation must identify the cause of loss, the amount assessed, and your obligation to pay.

**W.7** The settlement basis for a covered loss to "personal property" is replacement cost. We will apply that basis to the property involved in the covered loss.

**W.8** You must provide records, receipts, invoices, photographs, or other evidence supporting the amount claimed. We may use available market information to evaluate the evidence you provide.

**W.9** We may determine the amount of loss by agreement with you. If we do not agree, either party may seek appraisal as permitted by this coverage.

**W.10** We will not be bound by an estimate prepared for you unless we agree with the estimate. We may obtain estimates or opinions from persons we select.

**W.11** We will pay the amount of loss after we receive sufficient information to determine coverage and value. Payment does not waive any condition or exclusion that applies to the loss.

**W.12** You must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a claim. An insured must provide information that is material to the amount of loss.

**W.13** We may pay you, a person entitled to payment, or an entity that has a lawful interest in the payment. Payment to any such person does not increase the amount we owe.

**W.14** We may settle a covered loss by paying money, repairing property, replacing property, or arranging for repair or replacement. We will select the method of settlement unless we agree otherwise.

**W.15** If we elect to repair or replace property, you must permit reasonable access to the property. You must cooperate with reasonable efforts to protect the property from further damage.

**W.16** We will not pay for betterment, improvement, or increased value that is not required to restore damaged property. We may pay for work necessary to comply with applicable law when coverage applies to that work.

**W.17** We will not pay for property that has not been damaged by a covered cause of loss. We will not pay for loss resulting from delay, loss of use, or loss of market value unless coverage expressly applies.

**W.18** We will deduct the applicable deductible from the amount otherwise payable for a covered loss. We will apply the deductible in accordance with the terms that govern the loss.

**W.19** Payment for a covered loss will not exceed the applicable limit of liability. The applicable limit of liability is the most we will pay regardless of the number of persons seeking payment.

**W.20** We will apply the "loss assessment additional coverage limit" to a covered loss assessment. Payment under that limit is subject to all applicable terms of this coverage.

**W.21** We will settle a covered charge under "fire service charge coverage" according to the amount for which you are legally responsible. You must provide evidence of the charge and the circumstances that caused it.

**W.22** We will apply the "jewelry and watches limit" when a covered loss involves jewelry or watches. That limit applies whether the property is owned by you or by another person for whom coverage applies.

**W.23** We will apply the "on-premises business property cap" when covered property is business property located on the premises. We will determine whether property is business property from its ownership, use, and relation to business activity.

**W.24** We may require you to assign to us your rights of recovery against a person responsible for a covered loss. You must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.25** If you recover money or property from another source for a covered loss, you must hold the recovery for us to the extent of our payment. We will share a recovery with you according to the respective interests of the parties.

**W.26** We may take possession of damaged property after payment if we notify you of that decision. We are not required to take possession of damaged property.

**W.27** You must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property when coverage applies.

**W.28** We will not pay for expenses that are not reasonably related to protecting covered property from further damage. We will not pay for expenses incurred to improve property beyond its condition immediately before loss.

**W.29** You must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it.

**W.30** You must provide a signed statement of loss when we request it. The statement must include information reasonably necessary to determine the amount of loss.

**W.31** We may adjust a covered loss with any insured. Payment to an insured does not affect the rights of another person who has a lawful interest in the property.

**W.32** We will not pay more than the amount of the covered loss after applying all applicable limitations. No person may recover more than the actual financial interest that person has in damaged property.

**W.33** We may rely on records maintained by an association or other entity when evaluating a loss assessment. You must assist us in obtaining records that are not in your possession.

**W.34** We will not pay an assessment that is imposed for maintenance, wear, deterioration, or a condition that is not the result of a covered cause of loss. We will not pay an assessment for a charge that is voluntary or not legally enforceable.

**W.35** We may postpone payment while material facts concerning coverage, responsibility, or value remain unresolved. We will not unreasonably withhold payment of an amount that is not in dispute.

**W.36** You must notify us promptly of a covered loss. Delay in notice may affect coverage when the delay prejudices our ability to investigate or settle the loss.

**W.37** We will settle a covered loss without regard to sentimental, personal, or special value. We will determine value from the terms of this coverage and the evidence available to us.

**W.38** Our payment for a covered loss fulfills our obligations for that loss to the extent of the payment. We retain all rights available to us after payment.

## W.8 — Definitions

"Association" means a condominium association, cooperative association, homeowners association, or similar entity having authority to assess its members. An association includes its board, officers, managers, or authorized representatives when acting within the scope of their authority.

"Assessment" means a charge imposed by an association upon you as a member of that association. An assessment includes a charge made directly to you or allocated to you through a charge against property in which you have an ownership interest.

"Loss Assessment" means an assessment against you for a covered cause of loss, subject to the terms of this endorsement. A loss assessment does not include a charge arising from ordinary maintenance, repair, improvement, or operation of property.

"Covered Property" means property for which an assessment may be imposed upon you because of your ownership interest in property governed by an association. Covered property includes property owned collectively by association members.

"Common Property" means property owned by an association or owned jointly by members of an association. Common property includes property intended for the shared use or benefit of association members.

"Unit" means the portion of property designated for your exclusive ownership, occupancy, or use. A unit includes fixtures and improvements that are your responsibility under the governing agreement of the association.

"Member" means a person or entity having an ownership interest in property subject to the authority of an association. A member includes you when you are obligated to pay an assessment imposed by the association.

"Ownership Interest" means your legal or equitable interest in property subject to an association's governing authority. An ownership interest includes an interest held as a tenant in common, joint tenant, shareholder, beneficiary, or similar capacity.

"Governing Agreement" means a declaration, covenant, bylaw, proprietary lease, rule, regulation, or other written instrument that establishes an association's authority. A governing agreement includes amendments that are effective when an assessment is imposed.

"Master Policy" means insurance maintained by or for an association that applies to common property or association liability. A master policy includes a policy maintained for the benefit of association members.

"Deductible" means the portion of a covered loss that must be borne before insurance under a master policy applies. A deductible includes a retention or similar amount having the same effect.

"Property Damage" means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property. Property damage includes loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.

"Bodily Injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. Bodily injury includes resulting death.

"Personal Injury" means injury arising from false arrest, detention, imprisonment, malicious prosecution, wrongful eviction, wrongful entry, or invasion of private occupancy. Personal injury includes injury arising from oral or written publication that violates a person's privacy.

"Liability" means legal responsibility for damages because of bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury. Liability includes responsibility imposed by law, contract, or a governing agreement.

"Damages" means sums that an insured becomes legally obligated to pay because of a covered injury or loss. Damages include expenses assessed against you when those expenses are attributable to covered liability.

"Direct Physical Loss" means accidental physical loss of or physical damage to tangible property. Direct physical loss does not include loss consisting solely of reduced value, delayed use, or impaired marketability.

"Accidental" means abrupt or unexpected from the standpoint of an insured. An event is not accidental merely because the resulting damage or injury was unintended.

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence must result in bodily injury or property damage during the policy term.

"Suit" means a civil proceeding in which damages because of bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury are alleged. Suit includes arbitration or another dispute resolution proceeding when damages are claimed and you must participate.

"Claim" means a demand for money, services, or other relief because of an alleged covered loss or liability. A claim includes a written notice that asserts responsibility against you or an association.

"Insured" means you and any person qualifying as an insured under the policy to which this endorsement applies. Insured also means a person acting on behalf of an insured when authorized to do so.

"Resident" means a person who resides in your household. Residency depends upon the person's actual living arrangement and intent to remain, rather than a temporary absence.

"Your Household" means the household in which you reside. Your household includes persons who customarily live with you and share domestic arrangements with you.

"Insured Location" means property that qualifies as an insured location under the policy to which this endorsement applies. Insured location includes common property when you have an ownership interest in that property.

"Vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary to sustain normal occupancy. Property is not vacant solely because it is temporarily unoccupied.

"Neglect" means failure to use reasonable means to save and preserve property from further damage after a loss. Neglect includes failure to protect property when protection is reasonably possible.

"Intentional Loss" means a loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss. Intentional loss includes an act intended to cause damage or injury.

"Occurrence Date" means the date on which the event causing the assessment first takes place. When an occurrence involves repeated exposure to harmful conditions, the occurrence date is the date of the earliest related exposure.

"Assessment Date" means the date on which an association formally imposes an assessment upon you. An assessment is formally imposed when the association gives notice that payment is required.

"Notice" means written communication delivered, mailed, electronically transmitted, or otherwise provided in a manner authorized by a governing agreement or applicable law. Notice includes a communication issued by an association or its authorized representative.

"Emergency" means a sudden condition requiring immediate action to protect persons or property from imminent harm. An emergency does not include a condition that could reasonably be addressed through ordinary maintenance or planned repair.
