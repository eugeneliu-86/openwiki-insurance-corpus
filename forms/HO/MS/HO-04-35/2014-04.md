# HO 04 35 Loss Assessment Coverage (Edition 2014-04)
> SUPERSEDED by HO 04 35 Loss Assessment Coverage (Edition 2023-02) for policies effective on or after 2023-02-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" modifies insurance provided under your "policy". The provisions of this endorsement apply only while it is attached to your policy.

The insurance provided by this endorsement is subject to all policy provisions that do not conflict with this endorsement. If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a policy provision, this endorsement applies.

We provide "loss assessment coverage" as stated in this endorsement. This coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and definitions that apply to it.

An "insured" seeking coverage under this endorsement must comply with the duties imposed by the policy. Failure to comply with those duties may affect coverage.

We will determine coverage under this endorsement based on the facts and circumstances of each claimed loss. We will apply the policy provisions in effect at the time of loss.

This endorsement does not broaden coverage except as expressly provided. We do not cover loss excluded by the policy unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

Any payment we make under this endorsement is subject to the applicable policy conditions. Payment does not waive any right we have under the policy.

You must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of any claim. You must provide information and records we reasonably request.

The terms used in this endorsement have the meanings assigned to them in the policy unless this endorsement provides a different meaning. Words and phrases in quotation marks are defined terms.

All other policy provisions remain unchanged.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide "Loss Assessment Coverage" for an "insured" who is charged an "assessment" by an "association." This coverage applies only as stated in these provisions.

**W.2** "Insured" means you and any person who qualifies as an insured under this coverage. An insured must have an ownership or tenancy interest that is subject to the assessment.

**W.3** "Association" means a corporation, association, cooperative, condominium association, homeowners association, or similar entity that owns, controls, administers, or maintains property for the benefit of its members. The association must have authority to charge an assessment against you.

**W.4** "Assessment" means an amount charged against you by an association because of a loss, expense, obligation, or liability of the association. An assessment includes your allocated share of an amount properly chargeable by the association.

**W.5** "Covered loss" means direct physical loss to property owned by an association when the loss is caused by a covered cause of loss. The property must be owned, controlled, or maintained by the association at the time of loss.

**W.6** "Covered cause of loss" means a cause of loss for which this policy provides property coverage. A cause of loss is not a covered cause of loss when an exclusion or limitation applies.

**W.7** "Liability claim" means a claim or suit seeking damages because of bodily injury or property damage for which the association is legally responsible. The claim must arise from an occurrence to which liability coverage applies.

**W.8** We will pay your share of an assessment resulting from a covered loss to property owned by an association. The assessment must be charged against you during the policy period.

**W.9** We will pay your share of an assessment resulting from a liability claim against an association. The liability claim must arise from an occurrence during the policy period.

**W.10** We will pay only the amount of an assessment that is attributable to your ownership or tenancy interest. We will not pay an amount charged solely because you voluntarily assumed another person's obligation.

**W.11** An assessment must be validly authorized under the association's governing authority. An insured must provide us with evidence of the authority for the assessment when we request it.

**W.12** An assessment must be based on an actual loss, expense, obligation, or liability of the association. We will not pay an assessment based on a projected, anticipated, or speculative loss.

**W.13** We will pay an assessment only after the association has determined the amount properly chargeable to you. We may pay an assessment before you make payment if we determine that the assessment is valid and payable.

**W.14** We will pay your share of a deductible charged by an association when the deductible results from a covered loss. The association's property insurance must apply to the loss that gave rise to the deductible.

**W.15** We will pay an assessment for a covered loss even if the association has no insurance covering the loss. However, the loss must be caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.16** We will pay an assessment for a liability claim only when the association is legally liable for the bodily injury or property damage. We will not pay an assessment based solely on a contractual obligation of the association.

**W.17** The most we will pay for all covered assessments is the applicable limit of liability for this coverage. Payments under this coverage will not increase that limit.

**W.18** An insured must notify us promptly after learning of an assessment that may involve this coverage. The notice must state the nature of the assessment and the circumstances giving rise to it.

**W.19** An insured must send us any written notice, demand, invoice, claim, or suit relating to an assessment. The insured must send the material to us promptly after receiving it.

**W.20** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a covered assessment. The insured must assist us in obtaining information from the association when we request it.

**W.21** An insured must provide records showing the basis, amount, and allocation of the assessment. The records must identify the association property, loss, expense, obligation, or liability involved.

**W.22** An insured must provide the association's governing documents when we request them. The documents must show the association's authority to impose the assessment.

**W.23** An insured must permit us to inspect available records relating to the assessment. We may examine records of the association to the extent permitted by law and the association's governing authority.

**W.24** An insured must not make a voluntary payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. This condition does not apply to reasonable expenses necessary to protect property from further direct physical loss.

**W.25** We may investigate an assessment and negotiate with the association or another interested person. Our investigation or negotiation does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.26** We may settle a covered assessment as we consider appropriate. Our payment of a settlement does not require us to pay an assessment that is not covered.

**W.27** We may pay an assessment directly to the association, to you, or to another person entitled to payment. Payment to the association satisfies our obligation to the extent of that payment.

**W.28** If an assessment includes both covered and uncovered amounts, we will pay only the covered amount. We may allocate the assessment based on available evidence.

**W.29** We will not pay more than your share of the covered assessment. We will not pay an amount that exceeds the amount properly chargeable to you.

**W.30** We will reduce a covered payment by amounts recovered or recoverable from another source for the same assessment. This reduction applies whether the recovery is available to you or to the association.

**W.31** The applicable deductible applies to a covered assessment when required by this coverage. We will not pay the portion of an assessment that is within the applicable deductible.

**W.32** We will apply the deductible only once to an assessment arising from the same covered loss or liability claim. We may apply the deductible to the covered amount before making payment.

**W.33** We will not pay an assessment to the extent it duplicates payment for the same loss, expense, obligation, or liability. This applies even if the duplicate payment is sought under a different coverage.

**W.34** We do not cover an assessment arising from wear, deterioration, settling, shrinking, bulging, cracking, or faulty maintenance. This exclusion applies whether the condition affects association property or property of another person.

**W.35** We do not cover an assessment arising from defective design, faulty workmanship, inadequate construction, or defective materials. This exclusion applies regardless of the person responsible for the condition.

**W.36** We do not cover an assessment for routine maintenance, repair, replacement, improvement, or renovation that is not caused by a covered loss. We also do not cover an assessment for an expense that is merely anticipated by the association.

**W.37** We do not cover an assessment for a fine, penalty, punitive damage, or similar charge imposed because of a violation of law or governing authority. We also do not cover an assessment arising from an intentional violation by an insured.

**W.38** We do not cover an assessment arising from an act or omission intended by an insured to cause loss, expense, bodily injury, or property damage. This exclusion applies even if the resulting harm differs in kind or degree from that intended.

**W.39** We do not cover an assessment arising from a criminal act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not the insured is charged with or convicted of an offense.

**W.40** We do not cover an assessment for a loss to property that is not owned, controlled, administered, or maintained by an association. We also do not cover an assessment imposed by a person or entity that is not an association.

**W.41** We do not cover an assessment arising from an obligation that existed before your ownership or tenancy interest became subject to the association's authority. This exclusion does not apply to an assessment arising from a covered loss occurring after that interest became subject to the association.

**W.42** We do not cover an assessment based on an association's failure to collect amounts due from another member, owner, tenant, or occupant. We also do not cover an assessment based on insolvency or financial impairment of the association.

**W.43** We do not cover an assessment to the extent it arises from an uninsured or underinsured amount that the association elected not to insure. This exclusion applies when the association had authority to obtain insurance for the loss or liability.

**W.44** If we make payment under this coverage, we are subrogated to the rights of the person receiving payment against any person responsible for the assessment. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.45** An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing our rights of recovery. An insured must execute papers and take other actions reasonably required to secure those rights.

**W.46** If an insured recovers money for an assessment after we make payment, the insured must hold the recovery for us to the extent of our payment. The insured must promptly reimburse us from that recovery.

**W.47** Other insurance may apply to the same assessment. We will pay only our share of the covered amount after applying other insurance that is available to you or the association.

**W.48** Coverage applies only to an assessment imposed against an insured. No person or entity acquires a right under this coverage merely because it may benefit from our payment.

**W.49** Bankruptcy, insolvency, or financial impairment of an insured or association does not relieve us of our obligations under this coverage. It does not create an obligation for us beyond the coverage provided by these provisions.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We provide this Loss Assessment Coverage when a "loss assessment" is made against you because of direct physical loss to property insured under the policy. The loss assessment must result from a peril insured against under the policy. Coverage applies only when you have an insurable interest in the property for which the assessment is made.

**W.2** The most we will pay is ten thousand dollars. Subject to the terms of this coverage, our payment applies to your share of a covered loss assessment. The amount of insurance available under this coverage is not increased by the number of assessments made against you.

**W.3** The loss assessment must be imposed by a condominium association or other association of property owners. The association must have authority under its governing documents to impose the assessment. We do not cover an assessment imposed by a person or entity without that authority.

**W.4** We cover an assessment for damage to property owned by all members of the association when that property is insured under the policy. The property must be used for the benefit of the members of the association. We do not cover an assessment for property used exclusively by you or another unit owner.

**W.5** We cover an assessment for damage to property that is not owned by the association when the association is legally responsible for the damage. The legal responsibility must arise from ownership, maintenance, or use of the property. We do not cover an assessment based only on a voluntary agreement to pay for damage.

**W.6** The loss must occur during the policy period. The assessment must be made because of that loss. Provided that the loss is otherwise covered, we apply this coverage without regard to the date on which the association determines the amount of the assessment.

**W.7** We determine the amount payable under this coverage after applying the policy provisions that limit payment for the covered property. The amount payable is based on your proportionate share of the covered loss assessment. Subject to the association's governing documents, your proportionate share is determined by the method used to impose the assessment.

**W.8** We do not cover an assessment arising from an excluded loss. This exclusion applies even when the assessment also includes amounts arising from a covered loss. Unless the covered and excluded amounts are separately established, we do not cover the assessment.

**W.9** We do not cover an assessment for loss caused by earthquake, earth movement, or any other excluded earth event. This exclusion applies whether the earth event is the direct or indirect cause of the loss. Except that, this exclusion does not apply to the extent coverage for the assessment is provided by another applicable provision of the policy.

**W.10** We do not cover an assessment for loss caused by flood, surface water, or water under the ground. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the water enters the property suddenly or gradually. Unless the policy provides coverage for the particular water loss, the assessment is not covered.

**W.11** We do not cover an assessment for loss caused by neglect, lack of maintenance, or deterioration. This exclusion applies when those conditions contribute to the loss. Provided that a covered peril also contributes to the loss, we cover only the part of the assessment attributable to the covered peril.

**W.12** We do not cover an assessment for loss caused by an act or omission committed with the intent to cause harm. This exclusion applies to an act or omission by you or by any person acting with your consent. Subject to the applicable law, we determine whether the act or omission was intended to cause the loss.

**W.13** We do not cover an assessment for fines, penalties, or sanctions imposed by an association. This exclusion applies whether the fine, penalty, or sanction is described as an assessment. Except that, we cover an assessment for direct physical loss when the assessment is not imposed as a fine, penalty, or sanction.

**W.14** We do not cover an assessment for attorney fees, collection costs, or other expenses incurred solely to enforce payment of the assessment. We cover those expenses only when they are part of the covered loss. Subject to the terms of this coverage, the expenses must be reasonably related to the covered property damage.

**W.15** We do not cover an assessment for improvements, additions, or betterments to association property. This exclusion applies when the assessment is imposed to increase the value, capacity, or useful life of the property. Unless the improvement is required to repair covered physical damage, the assessment is not covered.

**W.16** We do not cover an assessment for a loss that is payable under a policy maintained by the association. This exclusion applies to the extent the association's policy provides payment for the loss. Provided that the association's insurance does not fully pay the covered loss, we consider the unpaid covered portion under this coverage.

**W.17** You must give us prompt notice of any demand for payment of a loss assessment. You must provide the assessment notice and any documents describing the basis for the assessment. Subject to our request for additional information, you must cooperate with our investigation of the loss.

**W.18** You must provide records showing your ownership or occupancy of the insured unit when we request them. You must provide records showing the amount assessed to you and the method used to calculate it. Unless you have a reasonable excuse, you must provide the records within a reasonable time after our request.

**W.19** You must authorize us to obtain information from the association concerning the loss assessment. You must permit us to inspect documents relevant to the cause and amount of the loss. Except that, we do not require you to disclose information protected by a recognized privilege unless the privilege is waived.

**W.20** You must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent when doing so may affect our investigation or recovery. This duty does not prevent you from making a payment required to protect your interest in the insured unit. Subject to our rights, we will not unreasonably withhold consent to a payment required by the association.

**W.21** We may inspect damaged association property when inspection is reasonably necessary to determine whether the assessment is covered. You must assist us in arranging access to the property. Provided that access is reasonably available, we may base our determination on the association's records and other reliable evidence.

**W.22** We may pay the covered loss assessment to you or to the association on your behalf. Payment to the association does not increase the amount available under this coverage. Unless the association releases you from the covered obligation, payment to the association does not eliminate your responsibility for any part of the assessment that is not covered.

**W.23** We may recover from a person or organization responsible for the loss to the extent of our payment. You must preserve our rights of recovery and must not impair them after the loss. Subject to our payment, we will not require you to pursue recovery from a responsible person or organization before we determine whether the assessment is covered.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the amount that applies to a covered "loss assessment" under this coverage. You must bear the deductible that applies to the covered loss assessment. We apply the deductible before we determine the amount payable under this coverage.

**W.2** We apply the deductible only to the portion of a loss assessment that results from covered loss. You must provide information that permits us to determine the portion of the assessment that is covered. We do not apply the deductible to an assessment that does not result from covered loss.

**W.3** An assessment must be made against you because of your ownership interest in the described premises. The assessment must arise from direct physical loss to property covered under this policy. We apply the deductible to the covered assessment after we determine that these requirements are satisfied.

**W.4** We apply the deductible to the covered amount of an assessment without regard to the number of items of property damaged. You must not combine unrelated assessments to avoid application of the deductible. We determine whether assessments arise from the same covered loss.

**W.5** When an assessment includes covered and uncovered amounts, we apply the deductible only to the covered amount. You must furnish records showing the basis for the assessment and the amounts included in it. We do not pay the uncovered portion of the assessment.

**W.6** The "association" must impose the assessment in accordance with its governing documents and applicable law. You must provide those documents and any notice of assessment that you receive. We may require additional information reasonably necessary to determine whether the assessment is covered.

**W.7** The assessment must be imposed because of loss to property in which the association and its members have an interest. You must show that the assessment is connected with the covered property or its appurtenant structures. We apply the deductible only after that connection is established.

**W.8** We determine the covered amount of an assessment based on the amount of direct physical loss caused by a covered peril. You must not include expenses that are unrelated to repairing or replacing property damaged by that peril. We may inspect the damaged property or the records supporting the assessment.

**W.9** You must notify us promptly when you receive an assessment for which coverage may be available. The notice must identify the association, the property involved, and the reason for the assessment. We are not relieved of our obligations solely because notice is provided after the assessment is imposed, unless our rights are prejudiced.

**W.10** You must submit a signed and sworn proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must describe the assessment, the covered property, and the circumstances giving rise to the assessment. We may require a revised proof of loss when the information provided is incomplete or inaccurate.

**W.11** You must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of the assessment. Your cooperation includes providing records, permitting inspection, and answering reasonable questions. We may suspend adjustment of the assessment while material information remains outstanding.

**W.12** We may pay the covered amount to you, the association, or another person entitled to receive payment. Payment to a person entitled to receive it discharges our obligation to the extent of that payment. You must assist us in identifying the proper payee.

**W.13** You must not voluntarily assume an obligation or make a payment that impairs our rights under this coverage. This requirement does not prevent you from taking reasonable action to protect property from further damage. You must preserve evidence of any payment made or obligation assumed.

**W.14** You must provide us with any demand, notice, decision, or legal paper concerning the assessment. You must send the material to us promptly after receiving it. We may participate in any proceeding that could affect our obligation under this coverage.

**W.15** We may defend or settle a claim against you that concerns the assessment when we have a right or duty to do so. You must not interfere with our investigation, defense, or settlement. We are not required to defend an association or another person solely because that person imposed the assessment.

**W.16** We determine whether an assessment results from a covered loss without regard to the label assigned to the assessment. You must provide the facts and documents necessary to establish the cause of the loss. We may consider the association's records, reports, contracts, and other reliable evidence.

**W.17** An assessment for maintenance, ordinary deterioration, or an excluded cause of loss is not a covered loss assessment. You must separate those amounts from amounts arising from covered loss. We do not pay amounts that cannot reasonably be allocated to covered loss.

**W.18** An assessment for property damage may include related charges only when those charges result directly from covered loss. You must identify the nature and basis of each related charge. We do not pay charges that arise solely from administrative action or from an increase in the cost of future services.

**W.19** We may require an appraisal or other method of determining the amount of covered loss when the amount is disputed. The appraisal or other method does not determine whether the assessment is covered. You must continue to comply with all duties that apply to a claim.

**W.20** You must preserve damaged property and records that may establish the cause or amount of the assessment. You may remove damaged property when necessary to prevent further damage or comply with law. You must permit us to inspect removed property when reasonably possible.

**W.21** We may inspect the premises and any property affected by the loss assessment. You must provide reasonable access for that purpose. Our inspection does not constitute an admission that the assessment is covered.

**W.22** We may request an examination under oath concerning the assessment and the facts supporting it. You must answer questions and produce documents within your possession, custody, or control. A person seeking payment under this coverage must comply with that request.

**W.23** We may require authorization to obtain records relevant to the assessment. You must provide the authorization when it is reasonably necessary to investigate coverage or amount. We use the records obtained for purposes connected with the claim.

**W.24** When more than one person has an ownership interest in the described premises, we determine the covered amount of the assessment based on the interests affected by the covered loss. Each person seeking payment must establish that person's interest in the property and the assessment. We do not pay the same covered loss more than once.

**W.25** When another insurance policy may cover the assessment, you must identify that insurance to us. You must cooperate in coordinating payment and in preserving any rights against another insurer or responsible person. We determine our payment after considering other available insurance and applicable law.

**W.26** Our payment of a covered loss assessment does not waive any right or defense available under this coverage. You must comply with all conditions that apply before and after payment. We may recover any payment made because of concealment, misrepresentation, fraud, or other conduct that defeats coverage.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover any "loss assessment" that is not imposed by an association having lawful authority to assess you. An assessment must arise from an obligation that is enforceable against you.

**W.2** We do not cover an assessment arising from your voluntary contribution to an association fund. A voluntary contribution does not become covered because it is requested by an association.

**W.3** We do not cover an assessment for a loss that occurred before you became an owner of the residence premises. This exclusion applies even if the assessment is imposed after you acquire ownership.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover an assessment caused by any other excluded cause of loss.

**W.5** We do not cover an assessment arising from earth movement. This exclusion applies whether the earth movement affects the residence premises or other property.

**W.6** We do not cover an assessment arising from settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, pavements, or other structural components. This exclusion applies regardless of the cause of the condition.

**W.7** We do not cover an assessment arising from wear and tear, deterioration, or latent defect. A condition that develops gradually is not a direct physical loss covered under this coverage.

**W.8** We do not cover an assessment arising from mechanical breakdown, rust, corrosion, or dry rot. This exclusion applies to property maintained by an association or by any other person.

**W.9** We do not cover an assessment arising from smog, smoke, agricultural smudging, or industrial operations. This exclusion applies whether the condition is sudden or gradual.

**W.10** We do not cover an assessment arising from discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. "Pollutants" include irritants, contaminants, and waste.

**W.11** We do not cover an assessment arising from enforcement of any ordinance or law. This exclusion includes costs to repair, replace, demolish, or alter property because of legal requirements.

**W.12** We do not cover an assessment for the increased cost of construction caused by an ordinance or law. This exclusion applies even if the property damage that led to the assessment would otherwise be covered.

**W.13** We do not cover an assessment arising from faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether performed by you, an association, or any other person.

**W.14** We do not cover an assessment arising from collapse unless the collapse results directly from a covered cause of loss. Damage that consists only of cracking, bulging, sagging, bending, settling, or expansion is not collapse.

**W.15** We do not cover an assessment arising from neglect by you, an association, or any other person to use reasonable means to save and preserve property from further damage. An insured must protect property from further loss after a covered loss.

**W.16** We do not cover an assessment arising from intentional loss. An insured must not intentionally cause or arrange for loss.

**W.17** We do not cover an assessment arising from criminal, dishonest, fraudulent, or illegal acts committed by you or at your direction. This exclusion applies whether the act is completed or attempted.

**W.18** We do not cover an assessment arising from war, undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military force. This exclusion applies whether the loss results directly or indirectly from such event.

**W.19** We do not cover an assessment arising from seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by governmental authority. This exclusion does not apply to loss caused by fire resulting from such action.

**W.20** We do not cover an assessment arising from nuclear hazard. "Nuclear hazard" includes nuclear reaction, radiation, and radioactive contamination.

**W.21** We do not cover an assessment arising from power failure or utility interruption occurring away from the residence premises. This exclusion applies unless the failure directly causes a covered cause of loss at the residence premises.

**W.22** We do not cover an assessment arising from failure of electronic data, software, or computerized equipment. This exclusion applies whether the failure results from error, malfunction, corruption, or unauthorized access.

**W.23** We do not cover an assessment arising from fungi, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or virus. This exclusion applies whether the condition is visible or concealed.

**W.24** We do not cover an assessment for testing, monitoring, cleaning, containing, treating, detoxifying, neutralizing, removing, or disposing of fungi, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or virus. This exclusion applies even if property damage is present.

**W.25** We do not cover an assessment arising from infestation by insects, birds, rodents, or other animals. Damage caused by domestic animals is also excluded.

**W.26** We do not cover an assessment arising from discharge or overflow of water or steam from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems when caused by freezing. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off the water supply when the residence premises is vacant.

**W.27** We do not cover an assessment arising from repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies to seepage or leakage occurring over time.

**W.28** We do not cover an assessment arising from water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, sidewalk, driveway, swimming pool, or other structure.

**W.29** We do not cover an assessment arising from backup of sewers, drains, sumps, sump pumps, or related equipment. This exclusion applies regardless of the source of the water or material.

**W.30** We do not cover an assessment arising from failure to maintain a roof, exterior wall, window, door, or other opening against entry of rain. Rain entering through an opening not caused by a covered cause of loss remains excluded.

**W.31** We do not cover an assessment arising from loss to property that is vacant or unoccupied when the vacancy or unoccupancy materially contributes to the loss. An insured must promptly report a change in occupancy that affects the risk.

**W.32** We do not cover an assessment arising from business activities conducted by you or an association. This exclusion applies to property used principally for business purposes.

**W.33** We do not cover an assessment arising from professional services. This exclusion applies whether the services are performed by you, an association, or another person.

**W.34** We do not cover an assessment arising from liability assumed by contract unless the liability would exist without the contract. An agreement to indemnify another person does not create covered property loss.

**W.35** We do not cover an assessment arising from a penalty, fine, punitive damage, or exemplary damage. This exclusion applies even if the charge is described as an assessment.

**W.36** We do not cover an assessment for loss of market value, loss of use, delay, inconvenience, or diminution in value. This exclusion applies even if physical damage occurs.

**W.37** We do not cover an assessment for defective title, lien, encumbrance, or boundary dispute. This exclusion applies whether the dispute concerns common property or property owned solely by you.

**W.38** We do not cover an assessment arising from faulty administration, accounting, budgeting, investment, borrowing, or management of association funds. Financial loss without direct physical loss is not covered.

**W.39** We do not cover an assessment to replenish reserves, operating funds, maintenance funds, or capital improvement funds. This exclusion applies regardless of the reason the funds were reduced.

**W.40** We do not cover an assessment for routine maintenance, upkeep, repair, renovation, modernization, or improvement. Work intended to prevent future damage is not covered.

**W.41** We do not cover an assessment for cosmetic alteration or betterment. Property may not be repaired or replaced with material of greater utility, quality, or value at our expense.

**W.42** We do not cover an assessment arising solely because the wind percentage deductible minimum applies. Application of a deductible does not create covered loss.

**W.43** We do not cover an assessment arising solely from the Coverage B limit as a percentage of Coverage A. A limit applicable to other property does not expand this coverage.

**W.44** We do not cover an assessment arising solely from the personal property limit percentage. A personal property limit does not determine whether an assessment is covered.

**W.45** We do not cover an assessment arising solely from the additional living expense limit. Loss of use coverage does not create coverage for an assessment.

**W.46** We do not cover an assessment arising solely from the clean-up allowance percentage. An allowance for clean-up does not create coverage for property damage or an assessment.

**W.47** We do not cover an assessment imposed because an association failed to maintain insurance. This exclusion applies whether the failure results from cancellation, nonrenewal, inadequate limits, or failure to collect premiums.

**W.48** We do not cover an assessment imposed to satisfy an association debt not resulting from direct physical loss to covered property. This exclusion includes loans, interest, financing charges, and collection expenses.

**W.49** We do not cover an assessment imposed for attorney fees, court costs, appraisal costs, or dispute resolution expenses. This exclusion applies unless such expense is part of a covered property loss.

**W.50** We do not cover an assessment arising from condemnation, eminent domain, or inverse condemnation. This exclusion applies whether property is wholly or partially taken.

**W.51** We do not cover an assessment arising from acts or decisions of a governmental body concerning planning, zoning, development, construction, repair, or occupancy. This exclusion applies even if the act or decision delays restoration.

**W.52** We do not cover an assessment arising from the failure of an association to enforce its governing documents. This exclusion applies to failure to collect assessments, enforce restrictions, or maintain property.

**W.53** We do not cover an assessment arising from misrepresentation, concealment, or fraud in obtaining insurance or presenting a claim. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation of a claim.

**W.54** We do not cover an assessment for loss that is paid or payable under another insurance policy, warranty, service contract, or indemnity agreement. An insured must provide information about other applicable coverage.

**W.55** We do not cover an assessment to the extent that another person is legally responsible for the loss and payment is available from that person. An insured must preserve our rights of recovery after loss.

**W.56** We do not cover an assessment imposed after you transfer ownership of the residence premises, unless you remain legally liable for the assessment. A transfer of ownership does not extend coverage for later obligations.

**W.57** We do not cover an assessment imposed against a person who is not an insured. Coverage does not apply merely because you voluntarily pay another person's assessment.

**W.58** We do not cover an assessment for loss occurring outside the policy territory. Property located outside the policy territory is not covered property for purposes of this coverage.

**W.59** We do not cover any assessment that is not supported by records establishing the cause of loss and the basis for the charge. An insured must provide available records reasonably requested by us.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties stated in this section after a "loss" that may result in an "assessment." An insured must cause each person seeking coverage to cooperate with us.

**W.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of any loss, assessment, claim, or circumstance that may result in a claim. Notice must identify the association, the affected property, and the known circumstances.

**W.3** An insured must report a loss within sixty days after the insured knows of the loss. An insured must provide any information then reasonably available.

**W.4** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, complaint, or other legal paper relating to a claim or assessment. An insured must not withhold any communication received from an association or its representative.

**W.5** An insured must notify us when an association gives notice that it may levy an assessment. An insured must provide the notice in the form received, including any attachments.

**W.6** An insured must identify the "association" that imposed or may impose the assessment. An insured must provide the association's current address and the name of its authorized representative.

**W.7** An insured must preserve all documents relating to the assessment. An insured must provide us with copies of meeting notices, minutes, resolutions, budgets, and correspondence when requested.

**W.8** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from further damage when the assessment arises from physical damage. An insured must keep a record of expenses incurred for that purpose.

**W.9** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable measures taken to protect property from further damage. An insured must permit us to inspect the circumstances before nonemergency work is performed when reasonably possible.

**W.10** An insured must cooperate with our investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must attend interviews, examinations, conferences, and proceedings when reasonably requested.

**W.11** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we request it. An insured must sign the transcript of the examination if it is accurately recorded.

**W.12** An insured must provide a "proof of loss" when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary to evaluate the claimed assessment.

**W.13** An insured must provide records showing the nature and basis of the assessment. An insured must provide records showing any payment made or required by the association.

**W.14** An insured must identify all persons having knowledge of the facts concerning the loss or assessment. An insured must provide the information reasonably available to locate those persons.

**W.15** An insured must authorize us to obtain pertinent records from an association, property manager, contractor, accountant, engineer, or other person having relevant information. An insured must execute authorizations reasonably necessary for that purpose.

**W.16** An insured must make available for inspection any damaged property, debris, repair work, or other physical evidence relating to the assessment. An insured must not alter or dispose of such evidence without our consent when it is reasonably possible to preserve it.

**W.17** An insured must provide photographs, recordings, reports, estimates, invoices, receipts, and other evidence supporting the claim when available. An insured must identify the source of each document supplied to us.

**W.18** An insured must provide us with the governing documents of the association when requested. Those documents include applicable declarations, bylaws, rules, policies, and amendments.

**W.19** An insured must provide us with the association's written explanation of the assessment when available. An insured must request that explanation from the association if we reasonably ask the insured to do so.

**W.20** An insured must disclose whether the assessment arises from a claim, a judgment, a settlement, repair work, maintenance, or another stated purpose. An insured must disclose all known facts affecting the validity or amount of the assessment.

**W.21** An insured must disclose any dispute concerning the assessment. An insured must promptly notify us of any appeal, reconsideration, arbitration, mediation, or other proceeding involving the assessment.

**W.22** An insured must not prejudice our right to investigate, settle, defend, or recover with respect to a claim. An insured must not make admissions concerning liability without our consent.

**W.23** An insured must permit us to communicate directly with the association concerning the assessment. An insured must direct the association to provide us with relevant information when we request it.

**W.24** An insured must provide us with any notice of a special meeting, vote, hearing, or proceeding concerning the assessment. An insured must provide the notice promptly after receiving it.

**W.25** An insured must take reasonable action to contest an invalid or improperly imposed assessment when requested by us. We will not require an insured to incur unreasonable expense in taking that action.

**W.26** An insured must preserve any right to reimbursement, contribution, indemnity, or recovery that may exist. An insured must not waive, release, or impair that right without our consent.

**W.27** An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our rights against another person or organization. If we make payment, an insured must cooperate in transferring and preserving those rights.

**W.28** An insured must provide information concerning "other insurance" that may apply to the assessment. An insured must provide copies of policies, certificates, declarations, and correspondence relating to other insurance when requested.

**W.29** An insured must promptly notify us of any payment received from another source for the same assessment. An insured must hold any such payment for proper allocation until the claim is resolved.

**W.30** An insured must disclose any reimbursement available from the association, a property manager, a contractor, a governmental body, or another source. An insured must assist us in obtaining information concerning that reimbursement.

**W.31** An insured must keep records supporting any claim for assessment coverage. Those records must be retained until we have completed our investigation and adjustment.

**W.32** An insured must submit invoices and receipts showing payment of the assessment when payment has been made. An insured must explain any difference between the amount assessed and the amount paid.

**W.33** An insured must provide a current statement of the assessment balance when an assessment remains unpaid. An insured must provide any revised statement received from the association.

**W.34** An insured must notify us if the association withdraws, modifies, reduces, reallocates, or rescinds an assessment. An insured must promptly provide documents showing that change.

**W.35** An insured must notify us if the association collects funds from another owner or source for the same loss. An insured must provide information necessary to determine the effect of those collections.

**W.36** An insured must not assign a claim or right of recovery without our written consent. An insured may transfer an interest in property without transferring rights under this coverage unless we agree otherwise in writing.

**W.37** An insured must provide a mailing address, electronic address, and telephone contact that will permit us to communicate concerning the claim. An insured must promptly notify us of a change in contact information.

**W.38** An insured must identify any attorney, public adjuster, consultant, or other representative acting for the insured concerning the claim. An insured must provide written authority for that representative when requested.

**W.39** An insured must ensure that a representative acting for the insured complies with duties imposed by this section. The act or omission of that representative may affect the claim to the same extent as the act or omission of the insured.

**W.40** An insured must submit requested information in a form reasonably acceptable to us. An insured must correct material errors or omissions in information previously provided.

**W.41** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to the claim, assessment, loss, or insurance. An insured must provide complete and accurate information to the best of the insured's knowledge.

**W.42** An insured must notify us if any information submitted in support of a claim becomes inaccurate. An insured must provide corrected information promptly after discovering the inaccuracy.

**W.43** An insured must assist us in determining whether the assessment is attributable to property or liability for which coverage is sought. An insured must provide information concerning the event that caused the assessment.

**W.44** An insured must provide information concerning any repair, replacement, restoration, or remediation related to the assessment. An insured must permit us to review contracts and work records relating to that work.

**W.45** An insured must identify the person or organization responsible for causing the underlying damage or liability, if known. An insured must provide information reasonably available concerning that person's insurance.

**W.46** An insured must notify us of any lawsuit or proceeding involving the underlying loss. An insured must provide copies of pleadings, orders, judgments, settlements, and releases when received.

**W.47** An insured must not settle a claim or assessment dispute in a manner that prejudices our interests. An insured must obtain our written consent before agreeing to a release that affects our rights.

**W.48** An insured must permit us to inspect records maintained by the association that are relevant to the assessment, if the insured has access to those records. An insured must use reasonable efforts to obtain access when we request it.

**W.49** An insured must provide the name and contact information of the association's property manager, if any. An insured must notify us if management of the association changes.

**W.50** An insured must provide us with any appraisal, engineering report, accounting, or expert report relating to the assessment. An insured must disclose the existence of such a report even if the insured does not possess a copy.

**W.51** An insured must participate in an appraisal process when we and the insured disagree on the amount of loss and appraisal is available. An insured must select a competent and impartial appraiser when required.

**W.52** An insured must pay the insured's chosen appraiser and share other appraisal expenses as required by the appraisal process. An insured must cooperate in selecting an umpire if the appraisers cannot agree.

**W.53** An insured must preserve any property, records, or electronic data that may be material to a dispute concerning the assessment. An insured must not destroy relevant evidence after receiving notice of a claim.

**W.54** An insured must notify us of any fraud, dishonesty, theft, or diversion of association funds that may affect the assessment. An insured must provide information reasonably available concerning the suspected conduct.

**W.55** An insured must apply the "all-other-perils deductible minimum" when determining the amount sought under this coverage. An insured must provide information necessary for us to apply that requirement.

**W.56** An insured must provide information relevant to the "coinsurance threshold" when we request it. An insured must cooperate in obtaining information from the association needed to evaluate that threshold.

**W.57** An insured must comply with the "sworn proof deadline" when we require a sworn proof of loss. An insured must request additional instructions promptly if the insured cannot provide requested information.

**W.58** An insured must commence any action against us within the "years within which an action against the insurer must be brought." An insured must satisfy all duties required before bringing such action.

**W.59** An insured must not bring an action against us unless there has been compliance with the applicable policy conditions. An insured must permit us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and adjust the claim.

**W.60** An insured must accept payment made by us as applying only to the covered interest for which payment is issued. An insured must reimburse us for any duplicate payment received for the same covered loss.

**W.61** An insured must hold in trust for us any recovery received after we have made payment for the same loss. An insured must promptly remit the portion of that recovery to which we are entitled.

**W.62** An insured must cooperate with us in allocating any recovery among covered and uncovered interests. An insured must provide documents supporting the allocation proposed by the association or another payor.

**W.63** An insured must notify us if ownership of the insured property changes while a claim remains pending. An insured must provide information concerning the successor's interest in the claim when requested.

**W.64** An insured must notify us if the insured no longer has an obligation to pay the assessment. An insured must promptly provide documents showing the termination or transfer of that obligation.

**W.65** An insured must perform duties under this section whether acting personally or through another person. We may deny coverage to the extent an insured's failure to comply with these duties prejudices our ability to investigate, adjust, defend, settle, or recover.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** An “assessment” is a charge imposed by an “association” against you because of your ownership interest in property subject to the association. An assessment must be legally enforceable against you.

**W.2** An association is an organization having authority to own, maintain, manage, or control property in which you have an ownership interest. The association must have authority to impose the assessment.

**W.3** A “covered loss” is direct physical loss to property caused by a peril for which coverage is provided. The loss must occur during the applicable coverage period.

**W.4** An insured must promptly notify us of an assessment that may result in a claim. Notice must include information sufficient to identify the association, the property involved, and the basis for the assessment.

**W.5** An insured must provide us with a copy of the assessment and all supporting records received from the association. An insured must provide additional information we reasonably request.

**W.6** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining records, statements, and other evidence relating to the assessment.

**W.7** An insured must preserve records and property that may establish the cause or amount of a covered loss. An insured must permit us to inspect those records and property as often as reasonably necessary.

**W.8** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses incurred to protect property from further loss. This duty does not prevent an insured from complying with a legally enforceable assessment.

**W.9** An insured must promptly advise us if the association withdraws, revises, or replaces an assessment. An insured must provide documentation of the revised obligation.

**W.10** An insured must notify us of any insurance, indemnity, reimbursement, or recovery available for the same assessment. An insured must preserve our rights to recover from any person or organization responsible for the loss.

**W.11** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require. An insured must sign and provide a statement describing the assessment, the property involved, and the facts supporting the claim.

**W.12** An insured must provide records showing the ownership interest that gave rise to the assessment. Those records must identify the property and the association imposing the assessment.

**W.13** An insured must provide records showing how the association calculated and allocated the assessment. The records must distinguish charges attributable to a covered loss from charges attributable to other causes.

**W.14** An insured must obtain from the association a written explanation of the event causing the assessment. The explanation must identify the damaged property, the nature of the loss, and the association's basis for imposing the charge.

**W.15** An insured must notify us of any dispute concerning the validity, amount, or allocation of an assessment. An insured must not settle that dispute in a manner that prejudices our rights.

**W.16** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from further damage after a covered loss. We may require evidence of the steps taken and the expenses incurred.

**W.17** We do not cover an assessment that is not imposed because of a covered loss. We do not cover any charge that is voluntary, optional, or otherwise not legally enforceable against you.

**W.18** We do not cover an assessment arising from wear, tear, deterioration, corrosion, settling, cracking, shrinking, expansion, or faulty maintenance. We do not cover an assessment arising from a condition that existed before the covered loss.

**W.19** We do not cover an assessment arising from design, construction, workmanship, material, or maintenance deficiencies. We do not cover the cost to correct or improve property because of those deficiencies.

**W.20** We do not cover an assessment imposed for ordinary operating expenses, administrative expenses, reserves, or contributions to a reserve fund. We do not cover an assessment imposed to increase the value, usefulness, or life of property.

**W.21** We do not cover an assessment arising from a fine, penalty, punitive charge, or interest. We do not cover a charge resulting from an insured's failure to comply with an obligation imposed by the association.

**W.22** We do not cover an assessment for loss caused intentionally by an insured. We do not cover an assessment arising from an insured's fraudulent, dishonest, or criminal act.

**W.23** We do not cover an assessment to the extent another source has paid or is obligated to pay for the same loss. We may reduce payment by amounts recovered or recoverable from that source.

**W.24** We do not cover an assessment based upon a liability that an insured assumed by contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have without that contract or agreement.

**W.25** Payment of a claim does not waive any right we have under this coverage. Our payment does not establish that future assessments are covered.

**W.26** An insured must reimburse us for payment we make that exceeds the amount properly payable under this coverage. We may recover that amount from any person who received the payment.

**W.27** An insured must comply with these special requirements as a condition of coverage. Failure to comply may affect our obligation to provide coverage when the failure prejudices us.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" in accordance with this provision. An "insured" must comply with all duties applicable to the loss before we make payment.

**W.2** We will determine the extent of direct physical loss to "covered property" from the information available to us. An insured must provide information that reasonably supports the claimed loss.

**W.3** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable efforts to preserve damaged property for our inspection.

**W.4** We may inspect covered property and examine records relating to the loss. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and records that are relevant to the loss.

**W.5** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must not dispose of damaged property without our consent when the property is material to the settlement of the loss.

**W.6** An insured must provide receipts, estimates, invoices, photographs, and other available evidence of the loss. We may require information concerning the ownership, condition, and value of covered property.

**W.7** The settlement basis for "personal property" is replacement cost. We will apply that basis after the covered loss has been established.

**W.8** We will determine the amount of covered loss from the evidence available to us. We may consider the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

**W.9** We may require an insured to identify the property that was damaged, destroyed, or stolen. An insured must identify any property that is subject to another person's interest.

**W.10** We may make payment to you or to a person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment to a person with a lawful interest in covered property does not increase our obligation.

**W.11** We may pay the portion of a covered loss that is not in dispute while we continue to investigate the remaining portion. Payment of an undisputed portion does not waive any right available to us.

**W.12** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a covered loss. An insured must provide requested information that is material to our determination of the loss.

**W.13** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath when reasonably necessary to determine a covered loss. An insured must provide requested records for examination when they are material to the loss.

**W.14** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment when we are legally entitled to do so.

**W.15** We may retain any salvage value that belongs to us after payment for covered property. An insured must cooperate in preserving our rights in salvage.

**W.16** We may settle a covered loss with an insured who has a legal interest in the damaged property. A settlement does not create coverage for property that is not covered property.

**W.17** An insured must provide any written assessment relating to a claimed loss. An insured must provide information showing the cause and purpose of the assessment.

**W.18** An insured must provide records showing that an assessment was made by an association or other entity with authority to make the assessment. We may require information concerning the insured's obligation to pay the assessment.

**W.19** We may require information concerning the property or occurrence that resulted in an assessment. An insured must provide available records showing the portion of the assessment attributable to the claimed loss.

**W.20** We will not make payment for an assessment until the insured's obligation to pay has been established. An insured must provide evidence that the assessment is enforceable against the insured.

**W.21** We may consider any recovery available from another source when determining payment for a covered loss. An insured must preserve rights of recovery against persons responsible for the loss.

**W.22** If we make payment for a covered loss, an insured must transfer to us any rights of recovery to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.23** We do not cover any portion of a loss that an insured intentionally causes or conceals. An insured must not knowingly misrepresent a material fact concerning a claimed loss.

**W.24** We do not cover a charge that is not attributable to a covered loss. We do not cover an assessment that an insured is not legally obligated to pay.

**W.25** We may rely on records maintained by an association, manager, contractor, or other person having knowledge of the loss. An insured must assist us in obtaining material information when reasonably requested.

**W.26** An insured must notify us of a change in circumstances that affects the claimed loss. An insured must provide corrected information if information previously provided was inaccurate or incomplete.

**W.27** We may obtain estimates or evaluations relating to damaged property. An insured may provide estimates or evaluations for our consideration.

**W.28** We may determine whether damaged property can be repaired, restored, or otherwise resolved. An insured must not interfere with our reasonable efforts to verify the condition of covered property.

**W.29** Our payment will not exceed the amount we are obligated to pay for the covered loss. Payment for a covered loss does not increase any applicable limit.

**W.30** We may apply any applicable limitation to the portion of loss for which payment is sought. An insured must identify any limitation known to apply to the claimed loss.

**W.31** The "special assessment limit", "fire department service charge limit", "jewelry theft sublimit", and "business property sublimit" identify limits that may apply to a covered loss. We will apply any such limit that applies to the loss.

**W.32** We may allocate payment among covered interests when more than one interest is affected by the loss. An insured must provide information necessary for us to determine the proper allocation.

**W.33** We may make payment directly to a person who performed work on covered property when you authorize that payment. Such payment reduces the amount otherwise payable for the covered loss.

**W.34** No payment is due until an insured has satisfied the duties that are material to the settlement of the loss. We may defer payment while material information remains unavailable.

**W.35** We may correct an error in a payment calculation. An insured must return any payment that exceeds the amount properly payable for the covered loss.

**W.36** Our investigation of a claimed loss does not waive any condition, limitation, or exclusion that applies. Our request for information does not constitute an admission of coverage.

**W.37** An insured must promptly notify us of any lawsuit or demand relating to a covered loss. An insured must provide us with papers received in connection with the loss.

**W.38** We will settle covered losses in good faith based on the information available to us. An insured must act in good faith in presenting and resolving a claimed loss.

## W.8 — Definitions

“Loss assessment” means a charge made against you as an owner or tenant of the “residence premises” by a “property owners association.” The charge must arise from a “direct loss” or from “director liability.”

A “charge” is an amount that a property owners association requires you to pay. A charge may be allocated to you individually or as part of an allocation to association members.

A “property owners association” is a corporation, association, or other organization of property owners. The organization must have authority to make a charge against its members.

“Residence premises” means the premises you own or occupy as your residence. Your interest in the residence premises may include an interest in property owned collectively by association members.

“Direct loss” means physical loss of or physical damage to property. The loss must result from a cause of loss for which coverage is provided.

“Property owned collectively” means property owned by all members of a property owners association. Such property may include property held for the common use or benefit of the members.

A loss assessment arising from direct loss must result from damage to property owned collectively. The property owners association must make the charge because of that damage.

“Director liability” means liability arising from an act or omission of a “director,” “officer,” or “trustee.” The act or omission must occur while that person acts in the applicable official capacity.

A “director” is a person elected or appointed to manage the affairs of a property owners association. A director acts in an official capacity when acting for the association.

An “officer” is a person authorized to perform executive or administrative duties for a property owners association. An officer acts in an official capacity when performing those duties.

A “trustee” is a person authorized to hold, administer, or manage property or affairs for a property owners association. A trustee acts in an official capacity when performing those responsibilities.

An “official capacity” is the capacity in which a director, officer, or trustee acts for a property owners association. It does not include personal activities unrelated to association affairs.

An “elected member” is a director, officer, or trustee chosen by the members of a property owners association. The selection must be made under the association’s governing authority.

“Association members” are the persons or entities having ownership, tenancy, membership, or voting interests in a property owners association. You may be an association member as an owner or tenant of the residence premises.

An “allocated charge” is the portion of a loss assessment assigned to you. Your allocated charge may be based on your ownership interest, tenancy interest, or other lawful method of allocation.

A “common interest” is your interest in property owned collectively by association members. Your common interest may exist separately from your ownership of property for your exclusive use.

“Common property” means property maintained, controlled, or owned for the benefit of association members generally. Common property may be subject to a loss assessment following direct loss.

A “shared expense” is an expense incurred by a property owners association for the benefit of its members. A shared expense becomes a loss assessment only when the association makes a charge against you.

An “association obligation” is a duty or liability of a property owners association. An association obligation may result in director liability when it arises from an act or omission in an official capacity.

A “member allocation” is the method by which a property owners association divides a charge among its members. The member allocation determines your share of a loss assessment.

A “required payment” is a payment the property owners association has authority to collect from you. A required payment may be a loss assessment when it meets the meaning of that term.

A “collective loss” is a direct loss involving property owned collectively by association members. A collective loss may result in a loss assessment when the association charges you for your share.

The singular includes the plural, and the plural includes the singular, when the context requires. Words describing any gender include all genders.
