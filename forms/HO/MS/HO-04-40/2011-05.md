# HO 04 40 Structures Rented to Others — Residence Premises (Edition 2011-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement attaches to and forms part of your contract of insurance. It applies only when issued by us for your contract of insurance.

The provisions of this endorsement apply to the insurance described in this endorsement. All provisions that are not modified by this endorsement remain applicable.

We provide the insurance stated in this endorsement for structures rented to others. Those structures must be located on your "residence premises."

This endorsement applies only to property that meets the requirements stated in this endorsement. Rental of property does not create coverage unless coverage is provided by this endorsement.

The terms of this endorsement control when they conflict with terms of your contract of insurance. A term that is not changed by this endorsement retains its meaning and effect.

This endorsement does not provide coverage beyond the coverage expressly stated. It does not change any applicable condition unless this endorsement expressly changes that condition.

An insured must comply with all applicable duties and conditions. Failure to comply with an applicable duty or condition may affect coverage.

An insured must provide complete and accurate information concerning the structures rented to others. We may rely on information provided to us in applying this endorsement.

An insured must notify us of a material change affecting the structures rented to others. We may apply the provisions of this endorsement based on the changed circumstances.

The use of a structure for rental must be consistent with the terms of this endorsement. A use that is not within those terms is not insured by this endorsement.

No act or omission by us waives any provision of this endorsement. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

The invalidity of a provision of this endorsement does not affect the remaining provisions. The remaining provisions remain enforceable to the extent permitted by law.

This endorsement applies separately to each insured where applicable. Our rights and duties under this endorsement remain unchanged except as expressly stated.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We cover a "covered rental structure" that is located on the "residence premises" and is rented or held for rental to another person. This coverage applies only to the interest you have in the covered rental structure.

**W.2** A covered rental structure is a structure that is separate from the dwelling and used for "rental use." Rental use means use by a "rental occupant" under a rental arrangement.

**W.3** A rental occupant is a person who occupies the covered rental structure with your permission. A rental occupant does not include a person occupying the structure without your knowledge or consent.

**W.4** A "rental agreement" is an agreement that permits a rental occupant to use the covered rental structure. The rental agreement may be written or oral.

**W.5** We cover direct physical loss to a covered rental structure caused by a "covered cause of loss." Direct physical loss means accidental physical damage to tangible property.

**W.6** A covered cause of loss is a cause of loss for which coverage is provided under this insurance. Coverage applies only when the covered cause of loss occurs during rental use.

**W.7** We cover permanently installed fixtures, appliances, equipment, and building materials that are part of a covered rental structure. We also cover additions and alterations that become part of that structure.

**W.8** We cover materials and supplies located on the residence premises and intended for use in repairing a covered rental structure. The materials and supplies must be owned by you.

**W.9** We cover debris resulting from a covered direct physical loss to a covered rental structure. The debris removal must be necessary to repair or replace covered property.

**W.10** We cover reasonable measures taken to protect a covered rental structure from further damage after a covered direct physical loss. Those measures must be necessary and reasonable under the circumstances.

**W.11** We cover damage to a covered rental structure caused by a rental occupant only when the damage results from a covered cause of loss. Intentional damage by a rental occupant is not a covered cause of loss.

**W.12** This coverage applies whether the covered rental structure is rented for residential purposes or held for rental for residential purposes. The structure must remain located on the residence premises.

**W.13** We do not cover a structure used principally for "business." Business means a trade, profession, occupation, or activity engaged in for financial gain.

**W.14** We do not cover property used to manufacture, process, store, distribute, or sell unlawful substances. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the property is also used for rental use.

**W.15** We do not cover property used for agricultural, commercial, industrial, or manufacturing purposes. This exclusion does not apply to ordinary residential activities of a rental occupant.

**W.16** We do not cover loss to personal property owned by a rental occupant. This coverage applies only to property in which you have an insurable interest.

**W.17** We do not cover loss to property owned by another person and entrusted to you for rental, storage, repair, or service. This exclusion does not apply to building property that becomes part of a covered rental structure.

**W.18** We do not cover loss arising from neglect by an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect the property from further damage.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting damage differs in kind or extent from that expected or intended.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by dishonest or criminal acts committed by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to an insured who did not participate in or know of the act.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by wear, deterioration, rust, corrosion, settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, latent defect, inherent vice, or defective materials. We cover ensuing damage caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, or maintenance. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, discharge, overflow, or leakage when an insured failed to use reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off the water supply. This exclusion applies only when reasonable care would have prevented the loss.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by seepage or leakage that occurs over time. We cover direct physical loss caused by a sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam when otherwise covered.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these sources. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or another cause.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sumps, or related equipment. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law requiring the testing, monitoring, cleanup, removal, containment, treatment, detoxification, or neutralization of pollutants. We cover direct physical loss otherwise covered before application of this exclusion.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by the release, discharge, migration, seepage, or escape of pollutants. Pollutants include irritants, contaminants, smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, chemicals, and waste.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, and radioactive contamination.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or action taken to hinder or defend against any of these events. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing to the loss.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by governmental seizure, destruction, or confiscation of property. We cover loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action when the fire is otherwise covered.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if the covered rental structure is "vacant." Vacant means substantially empty of personal property necessary for normal occupancy.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by a rental occupant or by a person lawfully on the residence premises with the rental occupant's permission. This exclusion applies to property in or attached to the covered rental structure.

**W.36** We do not cover the cost to repair or replace property solely because of diminished value, cosmetic damage, or loss of market value. We cover direct physical repair of covered damage when otherwise covered.

**W.37** We do not cover loss to land, including land on which a covered rental structure is located. We cover direct physical loss to covered building property.

**W.38** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss involving a covered rental structure. The notice must describe the property, the location, and the circumstances of the loss.

**W.39** An insured must protect the covered rental structure from further damage after a loss. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and provide that record to us upon request.

**W.40** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must allow us to inspect the property and examine records relating to the loss.

**W.41** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must provide truthful information relating to the claim.

**W.42** An insured must submit a signed statement describing the loss when we request it. The statement must identify the covered rental structure and your interest in that property.

**W.43** An insured must preserve evidence of the cause and extent of loss when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.44** We may repair, rebuild, or replace covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may also pay for covered loss without repairing or replacing the damaged property.

**W.45** We may require you to assign to us rights of recovery against a person responsible for covered loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.46** Payment for covered loss will be made to you and any other person having an insurable interest in the covered rental structure. We may adjust the loss with any person having a legal interest in the property.

**W.47** Coverage for a covered rental structure does not apply to the dwelling. Coverage for a covered rental structure does not apply to personal property unless that property is permanently attached to the structure.

**W.48** Coverage for a covered rental structure does not provide liability coverage for bodily injury or property damage. Coverage for a covered rental structure does not provide loss of use coverage.

**W.49** Coverage applies only while the covered rental structure is located on the residence premises. Coverage ends when the structure is removed from the residence premises.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The "applicable limit of liability" is the maximum amount we will pay for a "covered loss" under this coverage. It applies subject to the terms of this coverage.

**W.2** The most we will pay for "landlord's furnishings" is five thousand dollars. This limit applies to all covered loss involving that property.

**W.3** Payment for a covered loss reduces the applicable limit of liability. The remaining applicable limit is available only for covered loss occurring after that payment.

**W.4** Our total payment for covered loss will not exceed the applicable limit of liability. This is so regardless of the amount of damage or the amount claimed.

**W.5** The applicable limit of liability does not increase because more than an insured is entitled to payment. All insureds share the same applicable limit.

**W.6** The applicable limit of liability does not increase because additional claims arise from the same covered loss. Related claims are subject to the applicable limit.

**W.7** The applicable limit of liability does not increase because additional property is damaged. Damage to all covered property is subject to that limit.

**W.8** The applicable limit of liability does not increase because additional interests exist in covered property. Payment will be made only to the extent permitted by the applicable limit.

**W.9** The applicable limit of liability applies whether covered property is repaired, replaced, or not repaired. The method of restoring property does not increase that limit.

**W.10** The applicable limit of liability applies whether you retain or transfer your interest in damaged property. A transfer of interest does not create additional coverage.

**W.11** Expenses incurred by you do not increase the applicable limit of liability. We will pay such expenses only when this coverage provides for payment.

**W.12** Estimates, invoices, or valuations do not increase the applicable limit of liability. We may consider such information in determining the amount of covered loss.

**W.13** A settlement agreement does not increase the applicable limit of liability. Our payment under a settlement remains subject to that limit.

**W.14** A judgment against an insured does not increase the applicable limit of liability. Our obligation to pay remains limited by this coverage.

**W.15** Amounts paid by us to protect covered property are subject to the applicable limit of liability when this coverage applies to those amounts. Such payments do not create additional coverage.

**W.16** Amounts paid by us to investigate or adjust a covered loss do not increase the applicable limit of liability. Our handling of a claim does not waive that limit.

**W.17** An insured must not incur expense or assume obligation without our consent, except as this coverage permits. Unauthorized expense or obligation does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.18** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of covered loss. Cooperation does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.19** An insured must provide information that we reasonably request to evaluate a claim. The information provided does not change the applicable limit of liability.

**W.20** Payment to any insured does not increase the applicable limit of liability for another insured. We may pay any person legally entitled to receive payment.

**W.21** Payment made jointly to persons having an interest in covered property does not increase the applicable limit of liability. We may require proof of each interest before making payment.

**W.22** The insolvency or bankruptcy of an insured does not increase the applicable limit of liability. Our obligations remain subject to this coverage.

**W.23** No act by us, including inspection, investigation, negotiation, or payment, increases the applicable limit of liability. A waiver must be made by us in writing.

**W.24** Our obligation to pay ends when the applicable limit of liability has been exhausted. No further payment is available under this coverage after that limit is exhausted.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the portion of a covered loss that remains your responsibility. We will pay only the amount of a covered loss that exceeds the applicable deductible.

**W.2** The deductible applies to a loss involving property covered by this endorsement. The deductible does not increase the limit that applies to the covered property.

**W.3** An insured must bear the deductible before we make payment for a covered loss. An insured may not transfer responsibility for the deductible to us.

**W.4** We will apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss after we determine the loss is covered. We will not apply the deductible to amounts that are not covered.

**W.5** We will determine the applicable deductible from the facts of the covered loss. The deductible applicable to a loss remains your responsibility even if payment is made to another person.

**W.6** A "covered loss" is direct physical loss to covered property for which coverage applies. The deductible applies only when we owe payment for a covered loss.

**W.7** We will reduce the amount otherwise payable by the applicable deductible. We will not pay the portion of loss represented by the deductible.

**W.8** An insured must provide information needed for us to determine the applicable deductible. An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the loss.

**W.9** The deductible applies regardless of whether the covered loss affects property rented to others. The deductible also applies regardless of whether you occupy other property at the residence premises.

**W.10** We will apply the deductible before determining any payment owed for covered loss. We will not make payment when the covered loss does not exceed the applicable deductible.

**W.11** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by a peril for which coverage applies. The deductible does not create coverage for loss that is otherwise not covered.

**W.12** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. The deductible applies to covered loss despite those protective measures.

**W.13** We will determine covered loss in accordance with the terms that apply to the property. We will then apply the deductible to the amount we owe.

**W.14** The deductible applies to loss involving damaged property and property that requires repair or replacement. The deductible does not alter our right to repair or replace covered property.

**W.15** An insured must retain damaged property when reasonably possible for our inspection. The deductible applies whether damaged property is retained, repaired, or replaced.

**W.16** We will not pay the deductible as part of a loss settlement. We will not reimburse an insured for the deductible.

**W.17** The deductible applies when we make payment directly to an insured. The deductible also applies when we make payment to another person who is entitled to payment.

**W.18** An insured must not incur expenses for our account without our consent. The deductible applies to covered expenses included in a covered loss.

**W.19** We will apply the deductible to the amount payable after considering applicable coverage limitations. The deductible does not eliminate a limitation that otherwise applies.

**W.20** The deductible applies even if a covered loss results from an act or omission of another person. Our payment remains subject to the applicable deductible.

**W.21** An insured must preserve any rights of recovery against a person responsible for covered loss. The deductible applies without regard to any recovery that may be available.

**W.22** We will apply the deductible to a covered loss even if more than one insured has an interest in the damaged property. The deductible is not reduced because multiple insureds seek payment.

**W.23** The deductible applies to covered loss involving personal property and building property. The deductible does not change the property that is covered.

**W.24** An insured must submit a claim for covered loss in a timely manner. The deductible applies to a covered claim regardless of when payment is made.

**W.25** We will not waive the deductible unless we agree in writing. An insured may not rely on an oral statement as a waiver of the deductible.

**W.26** The deductible remains applicable when a covered loss is adjusted by appraisal, agreement, or other means. The method used to determine the amount of loss does not change the deductible.

**W.27** An insured must pay the deductible from the insured's own resources. We will not advance, finance, or absorb the deductible.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss that is otherwise excluded from coverage for "covered property". The rental of a structure to others does not eliminate an exclusion that applies to the loss.

**W.2** We do not cover loss to a "structure rented to others" that is not located on the "residence premises". A structure does not become covered solely because it is used in connection with the residence premises.

**W.3** We do not cover loss arising from a use of property that is unlawful. This exclusion applies whether the unlawful use is by you, an "insured", or another person.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. This exclusion applies regardless of the source of the water.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. We also do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of a law or ordinance regulating construction, repair, demolition, or occupancy. This exclusion applies even if the property has sustained a covered loss.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of power or other utility service. This exclusion applies whether the failure occurs on or away from the residence premises.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage when reasonably able to do so.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused intentionally by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss differs in kind or extent from that expected or intended.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or an act of a military force. This exclusion applies whether or not the event is declared or undeclared.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, and any consequence of those conditions.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by the seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by governmental authority. This exclusion does not create coverage for property that is otherwise excluded.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by the dishonest act of an insured or a person entrusted with the property. This exclusion applies whether the act is committed alone or with another person.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by a tenant, occupant, or guest. We also do not cover loss caused by the disappearance of property without evidence of a covered cause of loss.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief when the structure is vacant. Vacancy includes the absence of the customary furniture and personal property needed for normal occupancy.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We also do not cover loss caused by inherent vice or latent defect.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, smog, or dry rot. This exclusion applies whether the condition develops gradually or suddenly becomes apparent.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies to loss consisting of or resulting from those conditions.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We also do not cover cracking caused by those conditions.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. This exclusion includes damage caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, or contamination.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We also do not cover loss caused by discharge or dispersal of pollutants.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, or siting. This exclusion applies to work performed by you or by another person.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective design, specifications, workmanship, repair, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or construction. This exclusion applies whether the defect is in property or in work performed upon property.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by defective materials used in repair, construction, alteration, or maintenance. This exclusion applies even if the defective material is incorporated into otherwise sound property.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by collapse, unless the collapse results directly from a covered cause of loss. A condition showing evidence of cracking, bulging, sagging, bending, leaning, settling, shrinking, or expansion is not collapse.

**W.27** We do not cover loss to land, including land on which the structure rests. We also do not cover loss to soil, fill, grading, excavation, or landscaping.

**W.28** We do not cover loss to lawns, plants, shrubs, or trees growing for commercial purposes. We also do not cover loss to property held for sale.

**W.29** We do not cover loss to property of tenants, occupants, guests, or other persons. Such property does not become covered property merely because it is located in or near a rented structure.

**W.30** We do not cover loss to property owned by a person other than an insured and entrusted to you for storage, repair, servicing, or safekeeping. This exclusion applies whether or not you receive compensation for the custody of the property.

**W.31** We do not cover loss to money, bank notes, bullion, securities, accounts, bills, deeds, evidences of debt, or manuscripts. We also do not cover loss to tickets, stamps, or similar property having a stated value.

**W.32** We do not cover loss to electronic data, including data stored on computers, media, devices, or networks. This exclusion applies to loss caused by corruption, destruction, alteration, or unauthorized access.

**W.33** We do not cover loss to animals, birds, fish, or other living creatures. This exclusion applies whether the animal is owned by you, a tenant, or another person.

**W.34** We do not cover loss arising from business pursuits conducted at the rented structure. This exclusion does not depend on whether income is received from the business activity.

**W.35** We do not cover liability, medical payments, or damages claimed by a tenant, occupant, guest, or another person. This endorsement does not provide liability coverage.

**W.36** We do not cover loss consisting solely of diminished value, loss of market, loss of income, or delay. We also do not cover consequential loss that does not result in direct physical loss to covered property.

**W.37** We do not cover the cost to correct faulty work, faulty materials, or defective design. We also do not cover the cost to improve property beyond its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.38** We do not cover costs arising from contamination, monitoring, testing, removal, treatment, detoxification, or neutralization of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the pollutant is solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or chemical.

**W.39** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water, steam, or another substance from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance equipment. This exclusion applies to leakage that occurs over time.

**W.40** We do not cover loss caused by discharge from a plumbing system, heating system, air conditioning system, fire protective sprinkler system, or household appliance when the discharge results from freezing and an insured failed to use reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off the water supply. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect the property when it is unoccupied.

**W.41** We do not cover a loss for which an insured fails to comply with a duty imposed by this policy if the failure prejudices our ability to investigate or adjust the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

**W.42** The "other structures limit", "contents limit percentage", "ALE percentage", and "debris removal additional percentage" do not expand coverage for a loss that is excluded. They do not apply to property or expense that is not covered.

**W.43** The "minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage" does not create coverage for an excluded loss. We do not cover any portion of a loss that remains subject to an exclusion.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must ensure that all information provided to us is accurate and complete. An insured must promptly correct information that becomes inaccurate or incomplete.

**W.2** An insured must notify us of a material change in ownership, occupancy, use, or condition of the insured location. Notice must include information we reasonably request concerning the change.

**W.3** An insured must use reasonable care to protect the "insured location" from loss. An insured must maintain the insured location in a reasonably safe condition.

**W.4** An insured must maintain records that reasonably support a claim for loss. An insured must provide those records to us when we reasonably request them.

**W.5** An insured must report a "loss" to us. The report must be made within ninety days after the loss.

**W.6** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect damaged property from further loss. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair and protection expenses.

**W.7** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a "claim". An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the claim.

**W.8** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer our reasonable questions concerning the loss or claim.

**W.9** An insured must provide a signed proof of loss when we reasonably request it. The proof of loss must state the information we reasonably require to evaluate the loss.

**W.10** An insured must provide a sworn statement when we request it. The statement must be provided in accordance with the "sworn statement deadline".

**W.11** An insured must permit us to inspect property involved in a loss or claim. An insured must permit us to examine relevant records and documents.

**W.12** An insured must permit us to obtain copies of records that relate to a loss or claim. An insured must authorize persons or organizations holding those records to provide them to us when permitted by law.

**W.13** The applicable "deductible" will be subtracted from the amount otherwise payable for a covered loss. An insured must bear the portion of loss represented by the applicable deductible.

**W.14** The applicable deductible is subject to the "base deductible minimum". We will apply that requirement in determining the amount payable for a loss.

**W.15** When "replacement cost" applies, we will determine settlement in accordance with the applicable valuation provisions. An insured must provide information we reasonably require to determine the value of damaged property.

**W.16** Replacement cost settlement is subject to the "insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement". An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine whether that threshold is met.

**W.17** We may determine the "actual cash value" of damaged property when replacement cost settlement does not apply. Actual cash value may reflect depreciation and the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

**W.18** An insured must preserve rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for a loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.19** If we make a payment, an insured's rights of recovery are transferred to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing those rights.

**W.20** No person may bring a "suit" against us unless all applicable duties have been performed. A suit against us is subject to the "suit against us period".

**W.21** No person may bring a suit against us to recover under this insurance unless there has been full compliance with its terms. Any judgment against an insured does not bind us unless we had a reasonable opportunity to defend.

**W.22** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact or circumstance relating to this insurance. An insured must not make a false statement in connection with a loss or claim.

**W.23** We do not cover a loss arising from concealment, misrepresentation, fraud, or false swearing by an insured. This condition applies whether the conduct occurs before or after a loss.

**W.24** An insured must notify us if other insurance may apply to the same loss. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning that other insurance.

**W.25** If other insurance applies, we will determine our obligations in accordance with the applicable other insurance provisions. An insured must cooperate in obtaining payment from other insurers when appropriate.

**W.26** An insured must notify us of a change in the occupancy or rental use of the insured location. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the change.

**W.27** An insured must comply with applicable laws and regulations relating to the ownership, maintenance, and rental of the insured location. An insured must promptly notify us of an alleged violation that may affect a claim.

**W.28** An insured must retain rental agreements and other records relating to the rental of the insured location. An insured must provide those records to us when they are relevant to a loss or claim.

**W.29** An insured must promptly notify us of a claim alleging injury or damage arising from the acts or omissions of a tenant. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the tenant and the alleged occurrence.

**W.30** An insured must permit us reasonable access to the insured location when access is necessary to investigate a loss or claim. An insured must arrange access to areas under the control of a tenant when reasonably practicable.

**W.31** An insured must not abandon damaged property to us. We may take possession of damaged property only with the consent of the insured.

**W.32** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not dispose of damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.33** An insured must make reasonable temporary repairs necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must retain records supporting the cost of those repairs.

**W.34** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. This condition does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable action to protect persons or property from immediate harm.

**W.35** An insured must not waive a right of recovery without our written consent when that waiver may impair our rights. An insured must notify us of any requested waiver relating to a loss or claim.

**W.36** An insured must provide us with a current mailing address and other contact information. An insured must promptly notify us of a change in that information.

**W.37** An insured may not assign an interest under this insurance without our written consent. An assignment made without our consent does not bind us.

**W.38** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this insurance. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not give a claimant a right against us beyond the rights provided by this insurance.

**W.39** An insured must cooperate with us in seeking recovery from a responsible person or organization. An insured must attend proceedings and provide testimony when reasonably requested.

**W.40** If damaged property is recovered after we make payment, an insured must notify us promptly. We may adjust the amount payable to reflect the recovered property.

**W.41** An insured must cooperate in resolving a disagreement concerning the amount of loss. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for an appraisal or other authorized valuation process.

**W.42** An insured must preserve communications, records, and electronic information relevant to a claim. An insured must provide accessible copies of that information when we reasonably request them.

**W.43** A condition applicable to an insured applies separately to each insured seeking coverage. An act or omission of one insured may affect the rights of another insured when the terms of this insurance so provide.

**W.44** An insured must accept notices we send by an authorized method. Notice sent to the most recent contact information provided to us is sufficient notice to an insured.

**W.45** An insured must provide written notice of a request, demand, claim, or suit received by an insured. An insured must promptly send us copies of related papers and communications.

**W.46** A waiver or change of a provision is effective only when made by us in writing. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision later.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** For purposes of this endorsement, "rental occupant" means a person who occupies a covered structure under an agreement with you. A rental occupant does not include an insured.

**W.2** For purposes of this endorsement, "covered structure" means a structure at the residence premises that is rented to others. The structure must be used principally for residential purposes.

**W.3** An insured must maintain the covered structure in a safe and habitable condition. An insured must correct conditions that create an unreasonable risk of injury or property damage.

**W.4** An insured must comply with applicable laws, ordinances, regulations, and code requirements concerning the ownership, use, maintenance, and rental of the covered structure. Compliance with such requirements does not increase coverage under this policy.

**W.5** An insured must obtain and maintain required permits, licenses, and approvals for the rental use of the covered structure. An insured must make those records available to us upon request.

**W.6** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting rental occupants. An insured must not knowingly permit a rental occupant to use the covered structure for an unlawful purpose.

**W.7** An insured must maintain a written record of each rental arrangement. The record must identify the rental occupant and the terms under which the covered structure is occupied.

**W.8** An insured must retain records showing rental payments received for the covered structure. An insured must provide those records to us when we request them in connection with a claim.

**W.9** An insured must inspect the covered structure with reasonable frequency. An insured must investigate and address conditions disclosed by an inspection.

**W.10** An insured must maintain the roof, walls, floors, foundations, doors, windows, and other structural components of the covered structure. An insured must protect the covered structure from deterioration caused by neglect.

**W.11** An insured must maintain plumbing, heating, electrical, and ventilation systems in safe operating condition. An insured must arrange for repairs when an insured knows of a condition requiring repair.

**W.12** An insured must maintain smoke alarms, carbon monoxide alarms, and fire extinguishing equipment required by law. An insured must replace or repair such equipment when it does not operate as intended.

**W.13** An insured must maintain means of ingress and egress in a safe condition. An insured must keep walkways, stairs, railings, and handholds reasonably secure.

**W.14** An insured must take reasonable measures to prevent freezing, leakage, overflow, seepage, or discharge of water from plumbing or heating systems. An insured must take reasonable measures to prevent resulting damage when the covered structure is unoccupied.

**W.15** An insured must maintain utilities necessary to protect the covered structure from damage. An insured must promptly restore protective services after learning that they have been interrupted.

**W.16** An insured must secure the covered structure when it is not occupied. An insured must control access to keys, access devices, and security codes.

**W.17** An insured must change locks or access codes when reasonably necessary to protect the covered structure. An insured must take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized entry.

**W.18** An insured must not knowingly permit hazardous materials to be stored, used, or disposed of at the covered structure except as reasonably necessary for ordinary residential maintenance. An insured must take reasonable measures to address a release of hazardous materials.

**W.19** An insured must not knowingly permit the covered structure to be used for manufacturing, processing, cultivation, storage, or distribution of unlawful substances. An insured must report suspected unlawful activity to appropriate authorities when required by law.

**W.20** An insured must not knowingly permit the covered structure to be used in a manner that materially increases the chance of loss. An insured must notify us of a material change in the use or occupancy of the covered structure.

**W.21** An insured must notify us when the covered structure becomes vacant. An insured must take reasonable measures to protect a vacant covered structure from loss.

**W.22** An insured must notify us of a material structural alteration to the covered structure. An insured must provide information we request concerning the alteration.

**W.23** An insured must notify us of construction, renovation, demolition, or excavation at or adjoining the covered structure when the activity may affect the risk of loss. An insured must take reasonable measures to protect the covered structure during such activity.

**W.24** An insured must preserve evidence of a loss. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.25** In the event of a loss, an insured must take reasonable measures to protect the covered structure from further damage. An insured must keep a record of expenses incurred to protect the covered structure.

**W.26** An insured must promptly notify us of an occurrence, loss, or claim that may involve coverage under this endorsement. Notice must include information reasonably available to the insured concerning the circumstances involved.

**W.27** An insured must promptly provide us with notices, demands, legal papers, and other documents relating to a claim. An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except for reasonable emergency measures.

**W.28** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining records, information, statements, and testimony when requested.

**W.29** An insured must permit us to inspect the covered structure and related records when reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim or determine compliance with these requirements. An insured must provide reasonable access to the covered structure for that purpose.

**W.30** An insured must provide truthful and complete information to us. We do not cover a loss if an insured intentionally conceals or misrepresents a material fact relating to the insurance.

**W.31** An insured must notify us of a change in ownership of the covered structure. Coverage under this endorsement does not transfer by assignment unless we agree in writing.

**W.32** An insured must maintain any protective safeguards that are represented to us as being in place at the covered structure. An insured must notify us if a protective safeguard is removed, disabled, or materially impaired.

**W.33** An insured must use reasonable care in hiring persons to perform work at the covered structure. An insured must take reasonable measures to verify that work is performed in a safe manner.

**W.34** An insured must promptly investigate a complaint concerning unsafe conditions at the covered structure. An insured must take reasonable measures to correct a condition that may cause injury or damage.

**W.35** An insured must not retaliate against a rental occupant for reporting an unsafe condition or requesting a repair required by law. An insured must address such report or request in good faith.

**W.36** An insured must maintain records of repairs, maintenance, inspections, and complaints relating to the covered structure. An insured must provide those records to us when reasonably necessary.

**W.37** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's knowing violation of a law applicable to the rental use of the covered structure. This exclusion applies only to the extent the violation caused or contributed to the loss.

**W.38** Failure to comply with these special requirements may affect coverage only when the failure prejudices our rights or materially increases the risk of loss. We may deny coverage to the extent permitted by law.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle covered loss to "covered property" as provided in this Loss Settlement provision. The applicable coverage limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss arising from the same occurrence.

**W.2** We will determine the amount of loss after application of any deductible that applies to the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of loss.

**W.3** We will settle covered loss to a building or structure on the basis of the reasonable cost to repair or replace the damaged part with property of like kind and quality. We may consider available materials, labor, and construction methods in determining that cost.

**W.4** We will not pay for loss to a building or structure until the damaged property is repaired or replaced, unless you elect settlement on an actual cash value basis. Actual cash value includes a deduction for depreciation.

**W.5** If you elect actual cash value settlement, we will not later pay additional amounts unless the damaged property is repaired or replaced and you notify us of the completed repair or replacement. We will determine any additional payment under the applicable coverage limit.

**W.6** Repair or replacement may be made at the same location or at another location. We will not pay more than the amount we would have paid to repair or replace the damaged property at the "residence premises".

**W.7** We will settle covered loss to personal property on a replacement cost basis. We will apply the applicable coverage limit and deductible to that settlement.

**W.8** We will not pay more for personal property than the reasonable cost to repair or replace the property with property of like kind and quality. We may make payment based on the cost of available property that performs the same function.

**W.9** We may elect to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. If we make that election, we will provide written notice to you within a reasonable time after we receive your proof of loss.

**W.10** An insured must protect damaged property from further loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property from further covered loss, subject to the applicable coverage limit.

**W.11** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the property, obtain records, and examine evidence concerning the loss.

**W.12** We will not pay for any increase in cost resulting from a delay caused by an insured. We will not pay for any increase in cost resulting from an insured's failure to protect damaged property.

**W.13** We will not pay for loss to property that is repaired, replaced, or disposed of before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless the property was disposed of to protect persons or property from further damage. An insured must preserve reasonable evidence of the damaged property.

**W.14** We will determine depreciation based upon the age, condition, useful life, and expected remaining useful life of the damaged property. Depreciation may differ among separate items of property.

**W.15** We will not pay for betterment, remodeling, or improvement that is not necessary to repair or replace damaged property. We will pay only for work reasonably necessary to restore the damaged property after covered loss.

**W.16** We will not pay for undamaged property solely because materials used in the damaged property are unavailable, discontinued, or mismatched. We may pay for reasonable matching where required to complete repair of the damaged property.

**W.17** We will settle loss to property that is part of a set, pair, or collection based on the actual loss in value of the damaged property. We will not pay for loss in value to undamaged property solely because the damaged property is part of a set, pair, or collection.

**W.18** We will apply any special limit that applies to covered property before determining the amount payable. This includes any applicable "jewelry and watches limit" and "on-premises business property cap".

**W.19** We will not pay more than the amount required to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. The amount payable remains subject to all applicable limitations and exclusions.

**W.20** We will pay for covered loss involving property of others only to the extent that you are legally responsible for that property or have agreed to insure it. Payment to another person does not establish that person as an insured.

**W.21** We may make payment to you, to another person with a lawful interest in the property, or to both. Payment to any person with a lawful interest does not waive any right we may have concerning the loss.

**W.22** An insured must provide records and documents that support the claimed amount of loss when we reasonably request them. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**W.23** We will not increase the amount payable because a claim involves the "loss assessment additional coverage limit". We will determine any payment under that coverage in accordance with its applicable terms.

**W.24** We will not increase the amount payable under this provision because "fire service charge coverage" may apply to the same occurrence. Payment under that coverage is determined separately.

**W.25** We will reduce the amount payable by amounts recoverable from any person or organization responsible for the loss. Our payment does not affect any right of recovery that we may have.

**W.26** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment, but we are not required to do so.

**W.27** We will settle each covered loss separately when separate covered property is damaged by separate occurrences. We will apply the applicable deductible in accordance with the terms that govern the loss.

**W.28** Our payment of any amount under this provision does not waive any condition, limitation, exclusion, or right under this insurance. We may require reasonable information before making payment.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** The terms “we,” “us,” and “our” mean the insurer providing this coverage. The terms “you” and “your” mean the named insured shown in the Declarations and the named insured’s spouse, if a resident of the same household.

**W.2** “Declarations” means the declarations applicable to this coverage. Declarations include any schedule or endorsement made part of the policy.

**W.3** “Insured” means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are under your care. Insured also includes a person using covered property with your permission, but only with respect to that use.

**W.4** “Residence premises” means the dwelling where you reside and the grounds used in connection with that dwelling. Residence premises includes other structures and grounds at the location shown in the Declarations.

**W.5** “Dwelling” means the building, or portion of a building, used principally as a private residence by you. Dwelling includes permanently installed fixtures and equipment used to service that building.

**W.6** “Premises” means land and buildings at a described location. Premises includes approaches, driveways, walks, and areas used in connection with the land and buildings.

**W.7** “Insured location” means the residence premises. Insured location also includes a structure rented to others when that structure is located on the residence premises and is identified in the Declarations.

**W.8** “Structure rented to others” means a building or portion of a building that is rented, leased, or held for rental to a tenant. That structure must be located on the residence premises.

**W.9** “Other structure” means a structure separated from the dwelling by clear space. Other structure includes a structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

**W.10** “Tenant” means a person who occupies a structure rented to others under a rental agreement. Tenant includes a person occupying the structure with the permission of the person having rental rights.

**W.11** “Rental agreement” means an agreement granting a tenant the right to occupy a structure rented to others. A rental agreement may be written or oral.

**W.12** “Rental” means the granting of possession or use of property to another person in return for rent or another valuable consideration. Rental includes property held out for rental whether or not it is occupied.

**W.13** “Rent” means money or other valuable consideration paid for the use or occupancy of property. Rent does not include a refundable security deposit unless that deposit is retained as payment for occupancy.

**W.14** “Landlord” means an insured who owns, controls, or has the right to rent a structure rented to others. Landlord includes an insured acting through an authorized representative.

**W.15** “Business” means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for money or another economic gain. Business includes activities conducted by an insured or by a tenant at the residence premises.

**W.16** “Business pursuits” means continuous or customary business activities. Business pursuits does not include an occasional activity that is not customarily undertaken for economic gain.

**W.17** “Residence employee” means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. Residence employee includes a person performing household or domestic services.

**W.18** “Employee” means a person hired by an insured to perform services in exchange for compensation. Employee does not include an independent contractor unless the insured directs the manner and means of the work.

**W.19** “Bodily injury” means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. Bodily injury includes resulting care, loss of services, and death.

**W.20** “Property damage” means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. Property damage includes resulting loss of use of that property.

**W.21** “Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. Occurrence includes resulting bodily injury or property damage.

**W.22** “Loss” means accidental direct physical loss of or damage to covered property. Loss includes the cost to repair or replace property only when required by the applicable coverage terms.

**W.23** “Direct physical loss” means an accidental physical alteration of property or an accidental physical loss of property. Direct physical loss does not include a reduction in value without physical alteration or physical loss.

**W.24** “Actual cash value” means the value of property at the time of loss, after deduction for depreciation and obsolescence. Actual cash value reflects the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

**W.25** “Depreciation” means the decrease in value resulting from age, wear, use, deterioration, or obsolescence. Depreciation is determined from the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

**W.26** “Fair rental value” means the value of the portion of the residence premises rented to others, less charges and expenses that do not continue while that portion is unfit for use. Fair rental value is determined from the rental value immediately before the loss.

**W.27** “Reasonable repair expense” means the necessary and customary cost to protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss. Reasonable repair expense does not include the cost of improving property or correcting a condition that existed before the loss.

**W.28** “Vacant” means substantially empty of personal property and without a customary occupant. Property may be vacant whether or not utilities remain connected.

**W.29** “Unoccupied” means furnished property that is not being used as a place of customary residence. Property is unoccupied when no insured or tenant is residing there.

**W.30** “Abandonment” means the relinquishment of property or of an ownership interest in property without intent to reclaim it. Abandonment does not include temporary absence from the residence premises.

**W.31** “Reasonable” means customary, necessary, and appropriate under the circumstances known at the time of the act or expense. Reasonable does not include an expense that exceeds the cost of an available comparable alternative.

**W.32** “Covered property” means property to which this coverage applies. Covered property does not include property excluded by the applicable policy terms.
