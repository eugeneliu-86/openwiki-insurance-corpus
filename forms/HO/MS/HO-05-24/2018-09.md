# HO 05 24 Special Personal Property Coverage (Edition 2018-09)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This coverage form attaches to your policy. We provide the coverage described in this coverage form subject to its terms.

Throughout this coverage form, “you” and “your” refer to the person named in the policy and that person’s spouse if a resident of the same household. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to the insurer providing this insurance.

“Insured” means you and any person qualifying as an insured under the policy. The duties imposed upon an insured apply to each insured.

This coverage form changes the policy only as stated in this coverage form. All other policy provisions remain applicable unless changed by this coverage form.

We cover direct physical loss to “covered property” only when the loss is caused by a peril not otherwise excluded or limited by the policy or this coverage form. Coverage applies only while the property is within the scope of the applicable policy provisions.

“Covered property” means property for which coverage is provided under the policy, as changed by this coverage form. Property not eligible for coverage under the policy remains ineligible unless this coverage form provides otherwise.

We apply the exclusions, conditions, and definitions of the policy to this coverage form. We apply any provision of this coverage form that changes a policy provision in place of the changed provision.

An insured must comply with all duties that apply following a loss. Failure to comply with a duty may affect coverage where the failure prejudices us.

We do not cover loss that is excluded by the policy or this coverage form. We do not cover loss beyond any limitation that applies to the property or cause of loss.

The insurance provided by this coverage form is subject to the policy terms governing cancellation, nonrenewal, concealment, fraud, and misrepresentation. An insured must provide information that is true, complete, and accurate when requested by us.

If a provision of this coverage form conflicts with a provision of the policy, this coverage form applies to the extent of the conflict. If no conflict exists, the provisions apply together.

The terms of this coverage form are severable. If a term is unenforceable, the remaining terms remain in effect to the extent permitted by law.

This coverage form is part of your policy. Acceptance of the policy includes acceptance of this coverage form.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** For purposes of this coverage, an "insured" means you and a person who resides in your household and is related to you or is in your care. An insured must have an insurable interest in property at the time of loss.

**W.2** "Personal property" means tangible property owned or used by an insured. Personal property includes property that an insured acquires after this coverage begins.

**W.3** "Covered property" means personal property that is not otherwise excluded by this coverage. Covered property includes personal property while it is at or away from your residence premises.

**W.4** We cover covered property owned by an insured. We also cover covered property belonging to another person while it is in the care of an insured, subject to that person's insurable interest.

**W.5** "Direct physical loss" means accidental physical loss of or accidental physical damage to covered property. We cover direct physical loss to covered property unless an exclusion applies.

**W.6** We do not cover "loss of use" of covered property. We do not cover a reduction in value that is not caused by direct physical loss.

**W.7** "Residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the land used in connection with that dwelling. Coverage applies to covered property at the residence premises.

**W.8** Coverage also applies to covered property while it is temporarily away from the residence premises. The property must remain owned or used by an insured while away from the residence premises.

**W.9** Coverage applies while covered property is being transported. We cover direct physical loss occurring during loading, unloading, or ordinary movement of the property.

**W.10** Coverage applies to covered property that is moved from the residence premises because it is endangered by a covered cause of loss. The property remains subject to all terms of this coverage while it is moved.

**W.11** We cover reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property from further direct physical loss after a covered loss. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.12** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to protect property after a loss.

**W.13** We cover direct physical loss to a pair or set of covered property. We may elect to repair or replace any part needed to restore the pair or set to its value before the loss.

**W.14** We cover direct physical loss to an article that is part of a larger collection. We may pay the value of the damaged article without paying for any decrease in value of the collection.

**W.15** We may repair, replace, or restore covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may make payment instead of repairing or replacing covered property.

**W.16** We may take all or part of damaged covered property at its agreed or appraised value after payment for a covered loss. An insured must transfer any interest needed for us to take the property.

**W.17** We do not cover property that is separately described and specifically insured by other insurance. This exclusion applies only to the extent that other insurance provides coverage for the same loss.

**W.18** We do not cover land, including land on which the residence premises is located. We do not cover plants growing in or upon land.

**W.19** We do not cover a building or structure. We do not cover materials, supplies, or fixtures intended for use in constructing, altering, or repairing a building or structure.

**W.20** We do not cover property held for sale by an insured. We do not cover property held by an insured for delivery or transportation for another person.

**W.21** We do not cover an aircraft or aircraft equipment. We do not cover property designed for use in flight.

**W.22** We do not cover a hovercraft or hovercraft equipment. We do not cover property designed for use with a hovercraft.

**W.23** We do not cover a motorized land vehicle that is subject to registration for use on public roads. We do cover a motorized land vehicle that is not subject to registration and is used solely to service the residence premises.

**W.24** We do not cover property of a roomer, boarder, or tenant. We do not cover property of a person who regularly rents or occupies any part of the residence premises.

**W.25** "Business property" means property used primarily for trade, profession, or occupation. We do not cover business property while it is away from the residence premises unless it is property that an insured personally carries or uses.

**W.26** We do not cover property consisting of accounts, bills, evidences of debt, or commercial records. We do not cover property consisting of manuscripts, drawings, plans, or designs used for business purposes.

**W.27** We do not cover money, bullion, or precious metals held for investment or commercial purposes. We do not cover securities, tickets, stamps, or similar negotiable property held for business purposes.

**W.28** "Electronic data" means information, facts, concepts, or instructions stored, created, used, or transmitted by electronic or computerized means. We do not cover loss of electronic data except when the electronic data is an integral part of covered property that suffers direct physical loss.

**W.29** We do not cover contraband or property whose ownership, possession, transportation, or use is unlawful. We do not cover property seized or confiscated by a governmental authority.

**W.30** We do not cover animals, birds, fish, or insects. We do not cover loss caused by an animal owned or kept by an insured.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss. This exclusion applies even if another cause contributes to the loss.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, including seizure, destruction, or confiscation of property. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance, law, or regulation that requires demolition, repair, replacement, or removal of property. We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of an ordinance, law, or regulation.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mudslide, subsidence, sinkhole collapse, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, or sump systems. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a sump system.

**W.37** We do not cover loss caused by power failure or the failure of power, heating, cooling, or communication services. This exclusion applies whether the failure occurs on or away from the residence premises.

**W.38** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, or revolution. We do not cover loss caused by a warlike act by a military force or its agents.

**W.39** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies whether the loss is controlled or uncontrolled.

**W.40** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. An insured must act with reasonable care after a loss occurs.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or corrosion. We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, or other atmospheric conditions.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical breakdown. We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective maintenance.

**W.43** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, or remodeling. We do cover resulting direct physical loss to covered property when the resulting loss is not otherwise excluded.

**W.44** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of property. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.45** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.46** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire or lightning that results from mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.47** We do not cover loss caused by an artificially generated electrical current. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire that results from an artificially generated electrical current.

**W.48** We do not cover "mysterious disappearance" of covered property. Mysterious disappearance means loss of property when the cause and manner of disappearance cannot be established.

**W.49** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claimed loss. An insured must permit us to inspect covered property and relevant records when reasonably required.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The applicable "limit of liability" is the most we will pay for all "covered property" damaged or lost in any "occurrence". This limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims, or items of covered property involved.

**W.2** For theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones, the most we will pay is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies to all insureds and all property interests.

**W.3** Payment under this coverage does not increase the applicable limit of liability. The limit of liability remains unchanged after we make payment.

**W.4** The applicable limit of liability is not increased because property is owned by more than one insured. The limit also is not increased because more than one person has an interest in the property.

**W.5** We will apply the applicable limit of liability to the total amount of covered loss. We will not apply a separate limit of liability to each item of covered property unless we state otherwise.

**W.6** All loss arising from the same occurrence is subject to the applicable limit of liability. This is true even if the loss involves separate causes of damage.

**W.7** Loss arising from a continuing or repeated condition is considered part of the same occurrence. The applicable limit of liability applies to the total covered loss arising from that condition.

**W.8** The applicable limit of liability applies whether damage occurs at the insured location or away from the insured location. The location of covered property does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.9** The applicable limit of liability applies to property owned by you. It also applies to property owned by other insureds.

**W.10** The applicable limit of liability applies to property in your care, custody, or control only to the extent coverage applies. Such property does not receive an additional limit of liability.

**W.11** Payment for damage to covered property is subject to the applicable limit of liability. Payment for loss of covered property is subject to that same limit.

**W.12** We may pay the value of covered property, the cost to repair covered property, or the cost to replace covered property, as applicable. Any such payment is subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.13** The amount payable for a covered loss will not exceed the amount of your financial interest in the covered property. This condition does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.14** We will not pay more than the reasonable cost to repair or replace covered property. The applicable limit of liability remains the most we will pay.

**W.15** If covered property can be repaired, we may limit payment to the reasonable cost of repair. Payment for repair is subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.16** If covered property is replaced, we may require reasonable evidence of replacement. Payment for replacement is subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.17** If covered property is not repaired or replaced, we may determine payment based on its "actual cash value". Payment based on actual cash value is subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.18** We will not pay more than the amount of a covered loss. The applicable limit of liability does not create coverage for property or loss not otherwise covered.

**W.19** Payment made by us for a covered loss does not obligate us to make payment for another loss. Each loss remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.20** If other insurance applies to a covered loss, we will not pay more than our share of the loss. Our payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.21** If you recover payment from another source for a covered loss, we may reduce our payment by the amount of that recovery. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**W.22** If we make payment for covered property and later recover that property, we may take possession of the recovered property. The value of recovered property may be considered in determining the amount payable.

**W.23** You must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable measures taken to protect covered property do not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.24** You must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. Disposal of damaged property without our consent may affect the amount payable.

**W.25** You must provide records and information that reasonably support the amount of a covered loss. Our payment will be based on the information available to us.

**W.26** A dispute concerning the value of covered property does not increase the applicable limit of liability. Any resolution of that dispute remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.27** An error in describing covered property does not increase the applicable limit of liability. We will determine payment based on the property actually involved in the covered loss.

**W.28** A change in the condition, use, or location of covered property does not increase the applicable limit of liability. Coverage remains subject to all applicable policy terms.

**W.29** The applicable limit of liability applies before any payment is made. We will not increase that limit because of delay in adjustment or payment.

**W.30** The applicable limit of liability applies whether a covered loss is discovered immediately or later. The time at which a loss is discovered does not increase the limit.

**W.31** We may make payment to you, another insured, or a person authorized to receive payment. Payment to an authorized person does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.32** We may make partial payment for a covered loss while determining the remaining amount payable. All partial payments are included within the applicable limit of liability.

**W.33** The total amount we pay for covered loss will not exceed the applicable limit of liability. This provision applies to all payments made under this coverage.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the amount of a covered "loss" that you must bear. We will apply the deductible as provided in this condition.

**W.2** We will pay only the part of a covered loss that exceeds the applicable deductible. The deductible reduces the amount otherwise payable for that loss.

**W.3** An insured must bear the applicable deductible before we make payment for covered property. Payment of the deductible is not a condition that enlarges coverage.

**W.4** We will apply the deductible to direct physical loss to "covered property." We will not apply the deductible to property that is not covered.

**W.5** The applicable deductible is determined at the time of loss. A change made after the loss does not alter that deductible.

**W.6** We will apply a deductible separately to each covered occurrence. An "occurrence" includes related losses arising from the same cause or event.

**W.7** Losses arising from a common cause may be treated as a single occurrence. We will not apply more than one deductible to the same covered loss unless separate deductibles apply.

**W.8** When more than one deductible could apply to the same covered loss, we will apply the deductible that is applicable to that loss. We will not combine deductibles unless the coverage terms require that result.

**W.9** The deductible applies before we calculate the amount we owe for covered loss. We will determine the covered amount under the applicable coverage terms.

**W.10** The deductible does not reduce the amount of loss used to determine whether property is covered. It reduces only the amount we pay for covered loss.

**W.11** An insured must provide information we need to determine the applicable deductible. Failure to provide that information may affect our ability to adjust the claim.

**W.12** A "claim" includes a request for payment arising from covered loss. Submission of a claim does not waive the applicable deductible.

**W.13** Our investigation of a claim does not waive any deductible. Our inspection, estimate, or evaluation does not change the deductible.

**W.14** A partial payment does not satisfy the deductible unless we state otherwise in writing. We may apply the deductible before making any payment.

**W.15** A "payment" made by us is subject to the applicable deductible. We will not pay the portion of covered loss that is within the deductible.

**W.16** The deductible applies whether you elect "repair" or "replacement." The method used to settle the loss does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.17** A repair or replacement performed before we inspect the damaged property does not remove the deductible. An insured must preserve available evidence of loss.

**W.18** The deductible applies to covered loss whether the damaged property is restored, replaced, or not repaired. We will determine payment based on the applicable coverage terms.

**W.19** Any amount recovered from another party does not eliminate the deductible. A "recovery" may be considered in determining the net covered loss.

**W.20** We may seek reimbursement from a responsible party after payment of covered loss. An insured must not impair our rights of recovery.

**W.21** Proceeds from "salvage" may be considered in adjusting a covered loss. Salvage does not reduce your responsibility for the applicable deductible.

**W.22** The deductible applies to covered loss regardless of whether another person caused the loss. The existence of another responsible party does not alter the deductible.

**W.23** The deductible applies to covered property at each insured location. Loss at more than one location may be subject to separate deductibles when the losses arise from separate occurrences.

**W.24** We will determine whether losses are related from the facts available to us. Our determination will be made in accordance with the coverage terms.

**W.25** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the cause, extent, and timing of loss. Cooperation includes providing records reasonably needed to apply the deductible.

**W.26** The deductible does not create coverage for loss that is otherwise not covered. It does not limit any exclusion, condition, or other policy provision.

**W.27** All rights and duties under this condition apply to you and us. The deductible remains applicable unless we expressly agree otherwise in writing.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover "property" for "loss" caused by or resulting from an act committed by an insured with the intent to cause loss. We do not cover loss arising from a direction given by an insured to cause such loss.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, or use of property. We do not cover loss caused by the demolition, remodeling, renovation, or replacement of property required by an ordinance or law.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by seizure or destruction of property by order of a "governmental authority." We cover loss caused by fire resulting from an act of a governmental authority.

**W.4** We do not cover loss caused by "earth movement," including earthquake, landslide, mudslide, subsidence, sinkhole collapse, or earth sinking, rising, or shifting. We do not cover loss caused by earth movement whether naturally occurring or caused by human activity.

**W.5** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, or sump systems.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building or other structure.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by overflow from a body of water. We do not cover loss caused by spray from water described in this provision.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by "power failure" occurring away from the residence premises. We cover loss caused by fire resulting from power failure.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by the "neglect" of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. This exclusion applies only to the extent the neglect causes additional loss.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by "war," including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or seizure by a military force. We do not cover loss caused by an act or consequence of war.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by a "nuclear hazard." We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of nuclear material.

**W.12** We do not cover loss arising from a "criminal act" committed by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the insured acts alone or with another person.

**W.13** We do not cover loss arising from the theft, conversion, or concealment of property by an insured. We do not cover loss arising from the voluntary parting with property because of a fraudulent scheme, trick, or false pretense.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, or oxidation. We do not cover loss caused by dampness, dryness, or changes in temperature.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We do not cover loss caused by pollutants unless the loss results from a covered cause of loss.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of "contaminants." Contaminants include irritants, pollutants, and hazardous substances.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by latent defect or mechanical breakdown.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover loss caused by cracking of a structure or its components.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, or compaction. This exclusion applies whether the work is performed by you or another person.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of materials used in repair, construction, renovation, or remodeling. We do not cover loss caused by defective maintenance.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, gnawing, or discharge by animals.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do not cover loss caused by the presence, growth, proliferation, spread, or activity of mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage from a household appliance.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler systems, or household appliances while the residence premises is vacant or unoccupied. We do not cover loss if an insured fails to maintain heat or shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by the collapse of a building or other structure unless the collapse results directly from a covered cause of loss. We do not cover loss caused by the settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of a building or other structure.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if the residence premises has been vacant. We do not cover loss caused by theft from a building under construction.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by theft of property while it is away from the residence premises if the property is held for rental to others. We do not cover loss caused by theft of property held for sale.

**W.28** We do not cover loss to property used primarily for business purposes. We do not cover loss arising from a business conducted by an insured.

**W.29** We do not cover loss arising from professional services provided by an insured. We do not cover loss arising from advice, consultation, design, or other professional activity.

**W.30** We do not cover loss to electronic data, software, programs, or records caused by corruption, alteration, erasure, or failure of electronic media. We do not cover loss arising from the inability to access, use, or transmit electronic data.

**W.31** We do not cover loss arising from a virus, malicious code, or unauthorized instruction affecting electronic equipment. We do not cover loss arising from a failure of electronic equipment to perform its intended function.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by the operation, maintenance, use, or entrustment of an aircraft, drone, watercraft, or motor vehicle where liability or property coverage is excluded by these provisions. We do not cover loss to property while it is being used in connection with such equipment.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of controlled substances. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of prescription drugs by an insured.

**W.34** We do not cover loss arising from illegal trade, unlawful transportation, or unlawful possession of property. We do not cover loss arising from property obtained through theft, fraud, or similar unlawful conduct.

**W.35** We do not cover loss where payment would violate an applicable trade or economic sanction. We do not cover loss where payment would expose us to a prohibited transaction.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by an excluded cause of loss. Such loss is excluded regardless of any other cause or event that contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.37** We do not cover indirect or consequential loss unless expressly provided by this policy. We do not cover loss of use, delay, loss of market, or loss of value resulting from excluded loss.

**W.38** We do not cover expenses incurred to detect, access, repair, replace, or improve property unless caused by a covered direct physical loss. We do not cover expenses incurred solely to prevent an excluded loss.

**W.39** The "named storm deductible minimum" does not create coverage for property or loss excluded by these provisions. It does not alter an exclusion that otherwise applies.

**W.40** The "appurtenant structures percentage" does not create coverage for property or loss excluded by these provisions. It does not alter an exclusion that otherwise applies.

**W.41** The "Coverage C percentage" and the "Coverage D limit as a percentage of Coverage A" do not create coverage for property or loss excluded by these provisions. They do not alter an exclusion that otherwise applies.

**W.42** The "rubble removal percentage" does not create coverage for property or loss excluded by these provisions. It does not alter an exclusion that otherwise applies.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with the conditions stated in this section. Failure to comply may affect our obligation to pay for a claim.

**W.2** An insured must protect "covered property" from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable efforts to preserve the property in the condition existing after the loss.

**W.3** An insured must give us notice of a "loss" involving covered property. The notice must identify the property and describe the apparent damage.

**W.4** An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning a "claim". The information must be truthful, complete, and based on the insured's knowledge.

**W.5** An insured must report a loss to us within ninety days after the loss is known. Notice given by an insured may be given on behalf of another insured.

**W.6** An insured must notify us of an "occurrence" that may result in a claim. The notice must state the known facts concerning the occurrence.

**W.7** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when a loss involves theft, vandalism, or malicious damage. An insured must cooperate with any resulting investigation.

**W.8** An insured must take reasonable measures to protect covered property from weather, trespass, theft, or further physical damage. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to take those measures.

**W.9** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must not discard damaged property unless retention is impracticable.

**W.10** An insured must permit us to inspect covered property that is the subject of a claim. An insured must also permit us to inspect property that may establish the cause or extent of loss.

**W.11** An insured must show us the damaged property when we request it. An insured must identify property that was repaired, replaced, removed, or disposed of after a loss.

**W.12** An insured must provide records supporting ownership, value, condition, and use of covered property. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records.

**W.13** An insured must provide receipts, invoices, appraisals, photographs, manuals, and similar evidence that we reasonably request. An insured must explain any material inconsistency in the evidence provided.

**W.14** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed, or stolen property when we request it. The inventory must describe the property and state the insured's claimed value.

**W.15** An insured must identify any person who has knowledge of the loss. An insured must provide available contact information for that person when we request it.

**W.16** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must not interfere with our ability to determine coverage or evaluate damages.

**W.17** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we request it. An insured must answer questions concerning the loss and matters material to the claim.

**W.18** An insured must produce requested documents at an examination under oath. An insured must provide documents in the insured's possession, custody, or control.

**W.19** An insured must sign and provide a statement when we reasonably request one. The statement must be accurate and must identify the person making it.

**W.20** An insured must provide access to electronic records that are material to the claim. An insured may redact information that is not material if the redaction does not impair our review.

**W.21** An insured must preserve electronic information that may show ownership, value, location, condition, or use of covered property. An insured must not alter that information after a loss.

**W.22** An insured must notify us if covered property is subject to a lien, security interest, lease, or other interest held by another person. An insured must provide information identifying the holder of that interest.

**W.23** An insured must notify us of any other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must provide available information concerning the other insurer and the applicable insurance.

**W.24** An insured must not make a voluntary payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. This condition does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable measures to protect covered property from further damage.

**W.25** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment of a claim when permitted by law.

**W.26** An insured must not repair, replace, or alter damaged property in a manner that prevents us from determining the cause or extent of loss. This condition does not prevent reasonable emergency measures to protect property.

**W.27** An insured must provide us with access to the location where the loss occurred when we request it. An insured must make reasonable efforts to obtain access when the location is controlled by another person.

**W.28** An insured must permit us to inspect repairs or replacements for which payment is claimed. An insured must provide records showing the work performed and the cost incurred.

**W.29** An insured must notify us of any demand, notice, summons, complaint, or other legal paper relating to a claim. An insured must provide the document to us without alteration.

**W.30** An insured must cooperate with us in any proceeding involving recovery from a person responsible for a loss. An insured must preserve rights of recovery that may exist against that person.

**W.31** An insured must not waive, release, impair, or settle a right of recovery without our consent after a loss. We may require an insured to assign rights of recovery to us to the extent of our payment.

**W.32** An insured must comply with the "legal action deadline". No action may be maintained against us unless the insured has complied with the conditions applicable to the claim.

**W.33** An insured must submit a signed proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the facts known to the insured concerning the claim.

**W.34** An insured must comply with the "proof of loss period". A proof of loss must include information reasonably requested by us concerning the loss.

**W.35** An insured must state the interest of each insured and other person in covered property that is the subject of a claim. An insured must identify any person asserting an interest in that property.

**W.36** An insured must state all known insurance that may cover the same property or loss. An insured must provide available information concerning any payment received from another source.

**W.37** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to the insurance, covered property, a loss, or a claim. We do not cover a claim involving material concealment, material misrepresentation, fraud, or false statement by an insured.

**W.38** An insured must not submit information known by the insured to be false. We do not cover loss for which an insured presents false evidence concerning ownership, value, condition, or cause.

**W.39** An insured must assist us in determining the actual cash value of damaged property when that determination is necessary. An insured must provide available evidence of age, condition, quality, and market value.

**W.40** The "minimum Section I deductible" applies to a covered loss. An insured remains responsible for the deductible applicable to the claim.

**W.41** The "eighty percent condition" applies when its requirements are relevant to a claim. An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine whether that condition has been satisfied.

**W.42** We may adjust a claim with an insured who has an interest in the covered property. Payment to that insured discharges us to the extent of the payment.

**W.43** We may pay a person legally entitled to receive payment for covered property. Payment to that person does not create an obligation beyond the amount otherwise payable.

**W.44** We may require an appraisal when we and an insured disagree on the value of covered property or the amount of loss. Each party must cooperate in the appraisal process.

**W.45** An insured must select a competent and impartial appraiser when appraisal is required. An insured must bear the expense of the appraiser selected by that insured.

**W.46** An insured must provide the appraiser with information material to the valuation of covered property. An insured must not attempt to influence an appraiser through concealment, misrepresentation, or improper inducement.

**W.47** A valuation determination does not determine whether coverage applies. We retain all rights concerning coverage, cause of loss, exclusions, conditions, and amount payable.

**W.48** We may inspect covered property and examine records without waiving any right. Our investigation or payment of a claim does not waive a condition, exclusion, or defense.

**W.49** An insured must notify us of a change in facts that may affect a pending claim. An insured must correct information previously provided if the insured learns that the information was materially inaccurate.

**W.50** An insured must provide a current address and reliable contact information for claim communications. An insured must notify us when that information changes.

**W.51** An insured must preserve evidence concerning the cause of loss when reasonably able to do so. An insured must not permit destructive testing without our consent unless necessary to prevent further damage.

**W.52** An insured must make covered property available for testing when we reasonably request it. We may conduct testing necessary to determine the cause, extent, or value of loss.

**W.53** An insured must provide access to records maintained by a repairer, vendor, storage facility, or other person holding information material to the claim. An insured must authorize release of that information when authorized release is required.

**W.54** An insured must notify us if covered property is recovered after theft or disappearance. An insured must preserve the recovered property for our inspection when reasonably able to do so.

**W.55** An insured must notify us if property claimed as destroyed is later found. We may adjust the claim to reflect the recovered property and its condition.

**W.56** An insured must return any payment received in error. An insured must repay an amount that exceeds the amount properly payable for the claim.

**W.57** An insured must hold funds received for a claim for the benefit of persons having a legal interest in covered property when required by law. We may issue payment jointly to persons having such interests.

**W.58** An insured must not assign rights under this insurance without our written consent. An assignment of a claim payment does not bind us unless we agree to it in writing.

**W.59** An insured must provide written authorization for us to obtain records material to a claim when permitted by law. The authorization must be sufficient to identify the records requested.

**W.60** An insured must comply with lawful requests for information made by us through our representatives. A request by our representative is a request by us.

**W.61** An insured must not obstruct our inspection, examination, testing, or investigation. We do not cover loss to the extent an insured's obstruction materially prejudices our ability to adjust the claim.

**W.62** An insured must use reasonable care to prevent a loss involving covered property. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's intentional act when the act is intended to cause loss.

**W.63** An insured must notify us if a person claiming an interest in covered property disputes ownership or value. We may withhold payment until the dispute is resolved or payment can be made without prejudice.

**W.64** An insured must provide information concerning salvage when covered property has residual value. We may reduce payment by the value of salvage retained by an insured.

**W.65** An insured must comply with all duties that are material to our handling of a claim. We may deny coverage only to the extent permitted by law when an insured fails to comply with a condition.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** An insured must comply with the duties in this section as a condition of coverage for "covered property". These duties apply when an insured seeks payment for direct physical loss to covered property.

**W.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. The notice must state the known facts concerning the loss and the location of the property.

**W.3** An insured must protect covered property from further damage. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs solely to protect the property from further damage.

**W.4** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when practical. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.5** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses incurred to protect property from further damage. An insured must provide those records to us upon our request.

**W.6** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining information relevant to the loss.

**W.7** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and the place where the loss occurred.

**W.8** An insured must provide us with records, receipts, invoices, photographs, and other evidence that support the claim. An insured must authorize us to obtain relevant records from persons or organizations having custody of them.

**W.9** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed, or stolen property. The inventory must describe the property and state its actual cash value, amount of loss, and supporting information.

**W.10** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the examination record when it accurately states the testimony given.

**W.11** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we request it. The statement must include information reasonably necessary for us to evaluate the claim.

**W.12** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold a material fact concerning the insurance, covered property, or loss. This duty applies before and after a loss.

**W.13** An insured must preserve any evidence relating to the cause of loss. An insured must not alter, repair, or remove evidence before our inspection unless necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.14** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when property is stolen, vandalized, or maliciously damaged. An insured must cooperate with law enforcement authorities investigating the loss.

**W.15** An insured must take reasonable steps to recover stolen property. An insured must promptly notify us if stolen property is recovered.

**W.16** An insured must notify us of any claim, demand, suit, or legal proceeding arising from the loss. An insured must promptly send us any notice, summons, complaint, or other legal paper received.

**W.17** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses necessary to protect property from further damage. This provision does not prevent an insured from providing emergency assistance.

**W.18** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment for the property, but we are not required to do so.

**W.19** An insured must permit us to inspect repaired or replaced property when we reasonably request it. An insured must retain records supporting the cost of repair or replacement.

**W.20** An insured must provide us with evidence of ownership when reasonably available. Evidence of ownership may include receipts, records, photographs, appraisals, or other reliable information.

**W.21** An insured must provide us with information concerning any other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must provide us with information concerning any person or organization that may be responsible for the loss.

**W.22** An insured must transfer to us any rights of recovery against another person or organization to the extent we make payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.23** We do not cover loss when an insured intentionally causes or directs the loss. We do not cover loss when an insured materially impairs our ability to investigate, evaluate, or recover for the loss.

**W.24** We do not cover loss supported by knowingly false information. We do not cover loss for property that an insured falsely represents as owned, damaged, destroyed, or stolen.

**W.25** We may require an insured to provide access to the residence premises or any other location where covered property is kept. An insured must provide access that is reasonably necessary to inspect property or investigate the loss.

**W.26** An insured must preserve all available records concerning the purchase, maintenance, repair, and value of covered property. An insured must provide copies of those records when we reasonably request them.

**W.27** An insured must notify us when damaged property is subject to a lien, lease, security interest, or other interest held by another person or organization. An insured must provide information identifying that interest.

**W.28** An insured must use reasonable care to prevent loss to covered property. An insured must follow reasonable instructions we give to protect covered property after a loss.

**W.29** Our payment does not transfer ownership of covered property unless we take possession of the property. An insured must transfer title to property only when we request it after making payment for the property.

**W.30** Compliance with these requirements does not waive any right we have under this policy. Our investigation, inspection, payment, or settlement activity does not constitute a waiver of any policy provision.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We settle a "covered loss" to "personal property" in accordance with this provision. We will pay only for direct physical loss to property that is covered at the time of loss.

**W.2** The loss settlement basis for "personal property" is replacement cost. We apply that basis to property that is damaged or destroyed by a covered loss.

**W.3** We may pay the value of the damaged property, repair the damaged property, replace the damaged property, or provide property of like kind and quality. Our election will not increase the amount otherwise payable for the covered loss.

**W.4** We will determine the amount of loss after giving effect to the condition of the property immediately before the loss. We may consider age, use, wear, condition, quality, and available information concerning the property.

**W.5** We will not pay for any part of a loss that results from an improvement, betterment, or increase in value. We will not pay for property that has greater capacity, quality, utility, or value than the property that was lost or damaged.

**W.6** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must not voluntarily discard, dispose of, or abandon damaged property unless we agree.

**W.7** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, appraisals, manuals, and other information that reasonably establishes ownership, condition, age, and value. We may require an insured to provide a signed inventory of damaged or destroyed property.

**W.8** The inventory must identify the property, describe the property, and state the claimed amount of loss. The inventory must be supported by available documents and other reasonable evidence.

**W.9** We may inspect the property, examine the place where the loss occurred, and take samples or photographs. An insured must cooperate with our investigation and must permit reasonable access to the property.

**W.10** We will not pay more than an insured's financial interest in the damaged property. We will not pay for any loss for which an insured has no legal right to recover.

**W.11** We will apply the "deductible" to the covered loss as required by the policy. The deductible applies whether we repair, replace, or make payment for the loss.

**W.12** We may make payment to an insured, to a person having a legal interest in the property, or to both. Payment to a person having a legal interest does not create coverage that otherwise does not apply.

**W.13** We may pay for repair or replacement only after an insured has incurred the reasonable cost of repair or replacement, when that requirement applies to the settlement basis. We may require documentation of the incurred cost before making payment.

**W.14** We will not pay for repair or replacement that is not actually completed when completion is required for payment. We may pay the amount otherwise payable before completion, subject to the applicable settlement basis.

**W.15** We will not pay for costs that are unreasonable, unnecessary, or unrelated to the covered loss. We may use prevailing local costs in determining whether a claimed repair or replacement cost is reasonable.

**W.16** We may repair or replace damaged property with property that is available through customary sources. We are not required to use a particular supplier, contractor, retailer, or service provider selected by an insured.

**W.17** We will not pay for delay, inconvenience, loss of use, or loss of market value unless that loss is separately covered. We will not pay for emotional value, sentimental value, or personal attachment to property.

**W.18** We will not pay for the cost to replace property with property of a different style, design, color, texture, material, or finish unless reasonably necessary to complete the repair or replacement. Differences caused by discontinued, unavailable, or obsolete materials do not increase the amount payable.

**W.19** We may determine the amount of loss for property that is part of a set, pair, collection, or similar grouping by considering the value of the damaged property and the value of the remaining property. We will not pay for loss in value to undamaged property solely because the damaged property cannot be matched.

**W.20** We will not pay for property that an insured cannot reasonably identify as owned, possessed, or held for use at the time of loss. An insured must provide available evidence establishing the ownership or possession of claimed property.

**W.21** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath concerning the loss. An insured must provide requested records and must cooperate in producing information reasonably needed to determine the amount payable.

**W.22** We may require an insured to assign to us any rights of recovery against a person responsible for the loss, to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.23** We do not cover any amount that an insured recovers from another source for the same loss. We may reduce our payment by amounts received or reasonably expected to be received for the covered loss.

**W.24** We may postpone payment while we investigate material facts concerning coverage, ownership, value, or the cause of loss. We will not postpone payment longer than reasonably necessary to complete our investigation.

**W.25** If an insured and we disagree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal as permitted by the policy. Appraisal does not determine coverage, cause of loss, policy interpretation, or any issue other than the amount of loss.

**W.26** An insured must protect damaged property from further loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred solely to protect covered property from further damage when those expenses are covered.

**W.27** We do not cover expenses incurred for inspection, testing, monitoring, or remediation unless those expenses are necessary because of a covered loss. We do not cover costs incurred to discover damage that has not occurred.

**W.28** We may settle a claim based on the information reasonably available to us when an insured does not provide requested information. Our settlement based on available information does not waive an insured's duties.

**W.29** We will not pay for loss to property that is not covered property, even if that property is included in an inventory or estimate. Inclusion of property in a claim does not establish coverage or value.

**W.30** We may issue payment jointly when more than one person has an ownership interest, security interest, or other legal interest in the property. An insured must obtain any endorsement, release, or other documentation reasonably required to negotiate joint payment.

**W.31** We may recover any payment made in error or any payment exceeding the amount properly payable. An insured must return an overpayment upon our request.

**W.32** The "association assessment limit", "fire department call fee", and "on-premises business property cap" remain subject to their applicable terms. This provision does not expand those terms.

**W.33** We will determine whether claimed property is personal property based on its nature, use, ownership, and location at the time of loss. Property that becomes part of real property may be settled according to its character at the time of loss.

**W.34** We do not cover the cost to comply with laws, ordinances, or regulations unless that cost is covered. We do not cover costs caused solely by a requirement imposed after the loss.

**W.35** We may use estimates, invoices, market information, expert opinions, and other reliable evidence to determine the amount of loss. An insured may submit additional reliable information for our consideration.

**W.36** Our payment does not constitute an admission of liability for any uninsured loss or damage. Our payment does not waive any policy condition, exclusion, limitation, or defense.

**W.37** An insured must promptly notify us if damaged property is repaired, replaced, recovered, sold, transferred, or disposed of after the loss. An insured must notify us of any change that may affect the amount payable.

**W.38** We will settle covered loss in good faith based on the information available to us. Our obligation to pay ends when we have paid the amount properly due under this provision.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** "Insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or who are under your care.

**W.2** "You" means the person or persons named as the insured. You also include a spouse who is a resident of the same household.

**W.3** "We", "us", and "our" mean the insurer providing this coverage.

**W.4** "Residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and that is shown as your residence. It includes structures and grounds used in connection with that dwelling.

**W.5** "Insured location" means the residence premises and any location where an insured is temporarily residing. It also includes locations used by an insured in connection with the residence premises.

**W.6** "Personal property" means tangible property that you own or use. Personal property includes property for which you are legally responsible.

**W.7** "Covered property" means personal property to which special personal property coverage applies. Covered property does not include property that is otherwise excluded from coverage.

**W.8** "Special personal property coverage" means coverage for direct physical loss to covered property unless we exclude the loss. This coverage applies only while the property is within the coverage territory.

**W.9** "Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement cost does not include any reduction for depreciation.

**W.10** "Actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss after depreciation and physical condition are considered. Actual cash value may be less than replacement cost.

**W.11** "Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain. Business includes property held for sale or used in connection with such activity.

**W.12** "Business property" means personal property used primarily for business. Business property includes property owned by an insured and property in an insured's care, custody, or control.

**W.13** "Employee" means a person employed by an insured or by an insured's business. Employee does not include an independent contractor.

**W.14** "Residence employee" means an employee whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. A residence employee includes a person performing domestic services for an insured.

**W.15** "Aircraft" means a device designed for flight or navigation through the air. Aircraft includes attached equipment and parts.

**W.16** "Motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle, trailer, or semitrailer designed for travel on public roads. Motor vehicle includes attached equipment and parts.

**W.17** "Watercraft" means a vessel designed for travel on water. Watercraft includes attached equipment and parts.

**W.18** "Theft" means an unlawful taking of property. Theft includes an attempted unlawful taking of property.

**W.19** "Mysterious disappearance" means the disappearance of property when the cause of its disappearance cannot be determined. Mysterious disappearance does not establish theft.

**W.20** "Loss" means accidental direct physical loss of or damage to covered property. Loss includes damage resulting from a covered cause of loss.

**W.21** "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence must result in bodily injury or property damage.

**W.22** "Property damage" means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property. Property damage also means loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.

**W.23** "Bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. Bodily injury includes resulting care, loss of services, and death.

**W.24** "Fungi" means fungi, wet rot, dry rot, mildew, and spores. Fungi includes any byproduct or release produced by fungi.
