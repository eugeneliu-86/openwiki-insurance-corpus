# HO 17 32 Unit-Owners Rental to Others (Edition 2014-04)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" modifies insurance provided under your "policy."

The provisions of this endorsement apply to a "unit" that you rent to others. The unit must be part of the property insured under your policy.

We provide the coverage described in this endorsement subject to all policy provisions that are not changed by this endorsement.

The terms of this endorsement apply only while the unit is rented to others. Rental use includes occupancy by a tenant or other person who has your permission to occupy the unit.

You must maintain the unit in a condition that complies with applicable law. You must also take reasonable steps to protect the unit from further damage after a loss.

An "insured" must promptly notify us of any change in the rental use of the unit. An insured must provide information we request concerning the rental use.

We may inspect the unit and the property where the unit is located. Our inspection does not constitute a representation that the property is safe or complies with applicable law.

We do not cover loss that is excluded by your policy unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

Any coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to the applicable limits of liability in your policy. The limits of liability are not increased by this endorsement unless the policy expressly states otherwise.

If any provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of the policy, the provision of this endorsement applies.

The duties imposed by the policy apply to any loss involving the unit. An insured must comply with those duties as a condition of coverage.

This endorsement applies only to the named insured shown in the policy. No other person acquires rights under this endorsement solely because that person rents or occupies the unit.

All other provisions of the policy remain unchanged.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the coverage described in this section for a "rental unit" that you make available for rental occupancy. This coverage applies while the rental unit is occupied or held available for occupancy by a tenant.

**W.2** "Rental occupancy" means occupancy of the rental unit by a person who is not an insured and who occupies the unit under a rental agreement. Rental occupancy includes occupancy for residential purposes.

**W.3** "Rental agreement" means an agreement under which you permit a tenant to occupy the rental unit in exchange for rent or other consideration. The agreement may be written or oral.

**W.4** "Tenant" means a person who occupies the rental unit with your permission under a rental agreement. A tenant does not become an insured solely because of rental occupancy.

**W.5** We provide coverage for direct physical loss to "covered property" at the rental unit. The loss must arise from a covered cause of loss.

**W.6** "Covered property" means property that you own and that is used in connection with rental occupancy of the rental unit. Covered property includes property permanently installed in the rental unit when you are responsible for that property.

**W.7** Covered property includes appliances, furnishings, and equipment that you provide for a tenant's use. Covered property also includes improvements and betterments that you own.

**W.8** We cover building materials and supplies located at the rental unit when they are intended for repair, replacement, or maintenance of covered property. The materials and supplies must be owned by you.

**W.9** We cover property used to maintain the rental unit when that property is kept at the rental unit. Property must be used principally in connection with rental occupancy.

**W.10** We cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by a tenant only when the loss results from a covered cause of loss. Intentional damage by a tenant is not covered unless otherwise provided by this section.

**W.11** We cover reasonable expenses that you incur to protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss. The protective measures must be necessary and reasonable under the circumstances.

**W.12** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve damaged property for our inspection. An insured must not discard damaged property unless retention would create a hazard or would be prohibited by law.

**W.13** We cover reasonable expenses to remove covered property from the rental unit when removal is necessary to protect that property from a covered loss. We also cover reasonable expenses to return the property after the danger has passed.

**W.14** We cover direct physical loss to property of others while that property is in your care, custody, or control at the rental unit. Coverage applies only when you are legally responsible for the loss.

**W.15** We do not cover property of a tenant that is held for sale, rented to others, or used in a business. We also do not cover a tenant's personal property merely because it is located in the rental unit.

**W.16** We do not cover loss to property owned by a tenant unless you are legally liable for the loss. This exclusion does not apply to property of others that qualifies as covered property.

**W.17** We cover loss of rent when a covered loss makes the rental unit unfit for rental occupancy. The loss of rent must result directly from damage to property at the rental unit.

**W.18** "Loss of rent" means the rental value that you lose because a tenant cannot occupy the rental unit following a covered loss. Loss of rent does not include amounts that you would not have received without the covered loss.

**W.19** We determine loss of rent based on the fair rental value of the rental unit immediately before the covered loss. We may consider the terms of a rental agreement that was in effect at the time of loss.

**W.20** We do not cover loss of rent caused by cancellation, expiration, or nonrenewal of a rental agreement. We also do not cover loss of rent caused by a tenant's failure to pay rent when the rental unit remains fit for occupancy.

**W.21** We cover reasonable additional expenses that you incur to maintain rental occupancy after a covered loss. The expenses must be necessary to reduce a covered loss of rent.

**W.22** We do not cover an expense that would have been incurred without the covered loss. We do not cover an expense that increases the value or capacity of the rental unit.

**W.23** We cover the reasonable cost to repair damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may pay for repair, replacement, or the actual cash value of the damaged property.

**W.24** We may inspect the rental unit and the damaged property after a reported loss. An insured must allow us reasonable access to the rental unit for inspection and investigation.

**W.25** An insured must notify us promptly of a loss that may involve this coverage. Notice must include information reasonably available to the insured concerning the loss.

**W.26** An insured must protect the rental unit from further damage after a loss. An insured must keep a record of reasonable expenses incurred to protect the property.

**W.27** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged property when we request it. The inventory must identify the property and describe its condition, value, and extent of damage.

**W.28** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request to investigate a loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**W.29** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. Cooperation includes assisting us in obtaining information from a tenant when reasonably necessary.

**W.30** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath concerning a loss. We may require the examination to be recorded.

**W.31** We may require an insured to provide a signed statement describing the loss. The statement must be based on information known or reasonably available to the insured.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to maintain the rental unit in a reasonably safe condition. This exclusion applies only when the failure contributes to the loss.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by wear, tear, deterioration, rust, corrosion, or mechanical breakdown. We do not cover loss caused by latent defect or inherent vice.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss when the excluded condition results from that cause of loss.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not apply to reasonable emergency measures taken to protect covered property.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an insured. We do not cover loss caused by criminal conduct of an insured.

**W.37** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, seizure, or destruction of property by order of a governmental authority. This exclusion does not apply when property is destroyed to prevent the spread of fire.

**W.38** We do not cover loss arising from an ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, replacement, or use of property. We do not cover increased costs resulting from enforcement of an ordinance or law.

**W.39** We do not cover loss to property while it is vacant if vacancy increases the hazard of loss. Vacancy does not include temporary absence of a tenant who intends to return.

**W.40** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting and supervising persons who perform work at the rental unit. This duty applies to work performed for repair, maintenance, or improvement of the rental unit.

**W.41** We may pay a claim directly to an insured, to a person having an interest in covered property, or to both. Payment to a person having an interest in covered property does not create coverage for that person.

**W.42** We may settle a claim with a tenant or other person when that settlement protects our interests and does not increase our obligation. An insured must not voluntarily make payment or assume an obligation without our consent.

**W.43** If we make payment for a loss, we are subrogated to the rights of the insured to recover from a responsible person. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.44** An insured must assign to us any recovery rights that are necessary to enforce our subrogation rights. An insured must cooperate with us in any recovery action.

**W.45** Coverage applies only to a loss that occurs during the time this coverage is in effect. A loss must involve the rental unit and must arise from rental occupancy.

**W.46** Coverage does not apply to property that is owned by a person other than you unless that property is specifically included as covered property. Coverage does not apply to property used principally for a business conducted by an insured.

**W.47** We may require proof that the rental unit was rented or held available for rental occupancy at the time of loss. An insured must provide available rental agreements, rent records, and tenant communications that we reasonably request.

**W.48** The duties and conditions in this section apply to every insured seeking coverage for a loss. Failure to comply with a duty may affect coverage only if the failure prejudices our ability to investigate or settle the claim.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We provide a limit of liability for property described as "landlord's furnishings". This property must be owned by you or furnished by you for use in the residence premises. The property must be located at the residence premises or at a place where it is being used in connection with the residence premises.

**W.2** The most we will pay for loss to landlord's furnishings is five thousand dollars. Except that this limit applies regardless of the number of items damaged or the number of occurrences causing the loss. This limit is part of the Coverage C limit and does not increase that limit.

**W.3** We apply the limit of liability to the property that is damaged, destroyed, or lost. We determine the amount payable after applying any applicable deductible, exclusion, condition, or limitation. Except that we do not pay more than the amount of loss otherwise covered under this policy.

**W.4** We pay for covered loss to landlord's furnishings only when the loss results from a covered cause of loss. The cause of loss must occur while the property is at the residence premises or while it is being used in connection with the residence premises. Subject to the policy provisions that apply to the loss, we determine the amount payable under the applicable valuation condition.

**W.5** We do not cover landlord's furnishings that are not owned by you. We do not cover property belonging to a tenant or to any other person. Except that this exclusion does not apply to property for which you have an insurable interest as landlord.

**W.6** We do not cover property that you acquire or use solely for business purposes unless coverage is provided by this policy. We do not cover property used in a business conducted away from the residence premises unless the policy provides coverage for that use. Subject to these limitations, property used in connection with renting the residence premises remains subject to this limit.

**W.7** We do not cover landlord's furnishings that are permanently installed as part of the building when the building coverage applies to the property. We determine whether property is part of the building according to its nature, attachment, and intended use. Except that removable furnishings remain subject to this limit when they are not covered as building property.

**W.8** We determine the amount of loss to landlord's furnishings based on the applicable valuation condition. We consider the condition, age, quality, and usefulness of the property in determining the amount of loss. Subject to the policy provisions that apply to valuation, we do not pay more than the cost to repair or replace the damaged property as limited by this policy.

**W.9** We may pay the cost to repair or replace damaged landlord's furnishings. We may pay the value of the property immediately before the loss when repair or replacement is not made. Unless the policy provides otherwise, payment does not exceed the amount required to restore the property to substantially the condition it was in before the loss.

**W.10** We may make payment to you, to a person having a valid interest in the property, or to a person providing repair or replacement services. Payment to an authorized recipient discharges our obligation to the extent of that payment. Except that no payment transfers ownership of property to us unless we elect to take the property.

**W.11** We may take all or any part of the damaged property at the value agreed upon or determined under the policy. We may repair, rebuild, or replace the property with property of like kind and quality. Subject to our election, we may settle the loss by payment, repair, rebuilding, or replacement.

**W.12** We do not cover an increase in the cost of repair or replacement caused by an ordinance or law unless coverage is provided under this policy. We do not cover costs arising from a requirement to improve undamaged property. Except that covered costs remain subject to this limit when the applicable coverage permits payment for them.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, inherent vice, or latent defect. We do not cover loss caused by insects, vermin, or animals unless the policy provides coverage for the resulting loss. Subject to the applicable cause of loss provisions, we cover ensuing damage only when that damage is otherwise covered.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts or dishonest acts committed by you or at your direction. We do not cover loss resulting from concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact concerning the property or loss. Except that this exclusion applies only as permitted by the terms of this policy.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of the property after damage occurs. We do not cover avoidable damage resulting from your failure to protect the property from further loss. Unless taking protective action would be unsafe, you must make reasonable efforts to protect covered property after a loss.

**W.16** You must keep records that reasonably identify the landlord's furnishings and support your claim for loss. You must provide those records when we reasonably request them. Subject to your duties after loss, failure to provide available records may affect the amount we pay.

**W.17** You must promptly notify us of a loss to landlord's furnishings. You must identify the damaged property and describe the circumstances of the loss. Except that notice does not require a particular form unless we request a written statement.

**W.18** You must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of a claim. You must allow us to inspect the damaged property when reasonably requested. Unless we agree otherwise, you must preserve the damaged property for our inspection.

**W.19** We may require a sworn proof of loss describing the property, the cause of loss, and the amount claimed. You must sign and deliver the proof of loss when we require it. Subject to the policy conditions governing claims, the proof of loss does not change this limit of liability.

**W.20** We may require an examination under oath of you or any person claiming an interest in the property. You must answer questions and produce relevant documents when reasonably requested. Except that the examination must concern the loss, the property, or your claim under this policy.

**W.21** We settle covered loss with you unless another person has a valid interest in the property. We may include that person in the payment or issue payment jointly. Subject to proof of the person's interest, payment may be made as the interests appear.

**W.22** We may adjust a covered loss separately for each damaged item or as a combined loss when the items are affected by the same occurrence. We apply this limit to the total amount payable for the covered loss. Except that we do not apply the limit more than once to the same covered loss.

**W.23** We do not cover amounts that you recover from a tenant or another person to the extent that the recovery duplicates payment under this policy. We may seek recovery from a person responsible for the loss after we make payment. Subject to our rights of recovery, you must not impair our rights against a responsible person.

**W.24** We do not pay for loss that is payable under another insurance policy when that policy provides primary coverage for the property. We apply this limit after determining the amount payable under other applicable insurance. Unless the other insurance is exhausted or does not apply, we pay only the share required by this policy.

**W.25** We do not pay more than the amount of your insurable interest in landlord's furnishings. We determine your insurable interest at the time of loss. Subject to that interest, the amount payable remains limited by this provision and the other terms of this policy.

**W.26** We may settle a covered loss in accordance with the terms of this policy even when the damaged property is subject to a lease or rental agreement. We do not become responsible for obligations that you assumed under a lease or rental agreement. Except that we may consider those obligations when they establish your interest in the damaged property.

**W.27** We do not cover loss to property that you rent to others when the property is not landlord's furnishings. We do not cover the tenant's personal property or property held by you for sale. Subject to the policy provisions that apply to personal property, coverage under this limit is restricted to property furnished by you for the rented premises.

**W.28** We determine the amount payable for a covered loss after applying all applicable exclusions, conditions, and limitations. We do not pay for any part of a loss that is not covered. Unless this policy states otherwise, this limit applies to the amount remaining after those determinations.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the amount that applies to a covered loss before we pay for that loss. You must bear the deductible that applies to the loss. Except that the deductible applies only as provided by this policy.

**W.2** We determine the amount of covered loss before applying the deductible. We then subtract the applicable deductible from the covered loss. Subject to the terms of this policy, we pay the remaining amount.

**W.3** The deductible applies to loss to covered property caused by a covered peril. You must bear the deductible even if the covered loss involves property of different kinds. Except that the deductible does not apply to property or loss that this policy does not cover.

**W.4** We apply the deductible to loss resulting from an "occurrence". An occurrence includes a series of events that arise from the same cause. Unless this policy provides otherwise, we apply the deductible separately to each occurrence.

**W.5** You must identify the occurrence that caused the covered loss when you submit a claim. We may consider the cause and circumstances of the loss in determining whether losses arise from the same occurrence. Except that we do not combine unrelated losses merely because they are discovered at the same time.

**W.6** We apply the deductible after determining the amount of covered loss. We do not apply the deductible to amounts that are not part of the covered loss. Subject to the terms of this policy, amounts that are not covered do not reduce the deductible.

**W.7** You must provide information that permits us to determine the amount and cause of loss. We may use that information to determine the deductible that applies. Except that your failure to provide information does not create coverage for loss otherwise not covered.

**W.8** When covered loss involves damaged and undamaged property, we apply the deductible only to the covered loss. We do not apply the deductible to the value of property that is not damaged. Unless this policy provides otherwise, we determine covered loss without including excluded property.

**W.9** We apply the deductible to the total covered loss caused by the occurrence. The total covered loss includes covered damage to property for which you have an insurable interest. Except that we do not include a loss for which we have no obligation to pay.

**W.10** You must not divide a covered loss among separate claims to avoid application of the deductible. We may treat claims arising from the same occurrence as a single claim for purposes of applying the deductible. Subject to the terms of this policy, we determine whether claims arise from the same occurrence.

**W.11** We apply the deductible before determining the amount of any payment under this policy. The deductible does not increase the limit of insurance. Except that application of the deductible does not reduce a limit of insurance for property that is not involved in the covered loss.

**W.12** When more than one limit of insurance applies to a covered loss, we determine the covered loss before applying the deductible. We do not apply the deductible more than once solely because more than one limit applies. Unless this policy provides otherwise, the deductible applies to the covered loss as a whole.

**W.13** You must protect property from further damage after a loss. We may apply the deductible to covered damage that results from your failure to take reasonable protective measures. Except that we do not apply the deductible to damage that is not covered under this policy.

**W.14** We may pay part of a covered loss before the full amount of loss is determined. Any such payment is subject to application of the deductible. Provided that a payment does not waive our right to apply the deductible to the covered loss.

**W.15** You must cooperate with our investigation of the loss. Your cooperation must include providing records and permitting inspection of damaged property when reasonably requested. Except that inspection or investigation does not change the deductible that applies.

**W.16** We determine the amount of covered loss using the valuation provisions that apply to the damaged property. We apply the deductible to the amount determined under those provisions. Subject to those provisions, we do not apply the deductible to property values that are not part of the covered loss.

**W.17** We apply the deductible regardless of whether payment is made to you or to another person entitled to payment. A payment to another person does not transfer responsibility for the deductible from you. Unless this policy provides otherwise, you must bear the deductible.

**W.18** When you receive payment from another source for the same loss, we determine the covered loss under this policy before applying the deductible. We do not pay amounts that are not covered under this policy. Except that recovery from another source does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.19** We may settle covered loss in accordance with the applicable payment provisions of this policy. The deductible applies to the covered loss used to determine the settlement. Subject to those provisions, settlement of part of a claim does not prevent application of the deductible to the remaining covered loss.

**W.20** You must include the applicable deductible when determining the amount you seek from us. We will state the amount of any payment after applying the deductible. Provided that our payment does not exceed the amount of covered loss remaining after application of the deductible.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an "insured". We do not cover loss caused by an insured's direction that property be damaged.

**W.2** We do not cover loss arising from criminal conduct by an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not an insured is charged with or convicted of an offense.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by war, invasion, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or usurped power. We do not cover loss caused by governmental or military action related to those events.

**W.4** We do not cover loss arising from the water conditions addressed by this provision. Flood and surface water remains excluded.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, or earth sinking. We do not cover loss caused by the settling, cracking, bulging, or expansion of pavement, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by volcanic eruption, lava flow, or volcanic material. We do not cover loss caused by earth movement that follows a volcanic event.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sumps, or related equipment. We do not cover loss caused by water discharged from an overflow or malfunction of those systems.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by seepage, leakage, or the continuous presence of water or moisture. We do not cover loss caused by condensation, humidity, or vapor.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do not cover loss arising from the testing, monitoring, remediation, removal, or disposal of those conditions.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by pollution, including the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We do not cover loss arising from the cleanup, removal, containment, treatment, detoxification, or neutralization of pollutants.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. We do not cover loss caused by the hazardous properties of nuclear material.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of power, communication, water, or utility service away from the "residence premises". We do not cover loss caused by a utility interruption that affects property away from the residence premises.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, or compaction. We do not cover loss caused by defective materials used in those activities.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by latent defect or mechanical breakdown.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, smog, or smoke from agricultural operations. We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of personal property.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals owned by you. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, scratching, chewing, or waste from those animals.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by repeated leakage or seepage from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems. We do not cover loss caused by the failure to maintain those systems in sound condition.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems. We do not cover loss when an insured failed to use reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off water and drain the system.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. We do not cover additional damage resulting from that neglect.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, demolition, renovation, or occupancy of property. We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of an ordinance or law.

**W.21** We do not cover loss arising from the taking of property by governmental authority. We do not cover loss arising from the seizure, confiscation, destruction, or forfeiture of property by lawful authority.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by defective, inadequate, or failed maintenance of the "insured location". We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to protect property from foreseeable damage.

**W.23** We do not cover loss to property while it is being used for a business purpose not disclosed to us. We do not cover loss arising from commercial activity conducted at the insured location.

**W.24** We do not cover loss arising from a professional service performed by an insured. We do not cover loss arising from advice, instruction, supervision, inspection, or failure to provide a professional service.

**W.25** We do not cover loss arising from the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft. We do not cover loss arising from the operation of an aircraft by an insured.

**W.26** We do not cover loss arising from the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft subject to registration or licensing. We do not cover loss arising from a watercraft used to carry persons or property for a charge.

**W.27** We do not cover loss arising from the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle. We do not cover loss arising from a motor vehicle used in connection with a business.

**W.28** We do not cover loss arising from racing, speed contests, stunt activity, or organized vehicle events. We do not cover loss arising from preparation for those activities.

**W.29** We do not cover loss arising from the use of an unmanned aircraft system. We do not cover loss arising from the launch, operation, maintenance, or control of an unmanned aircraft system.

**W.30** We do not cover loss arising from bodily injury to an employee of an insured. We do not cover loss for which an insured is obligated under workers compensation, disability benefits, or similar law.

**W.31** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's liability under a contract or agreement. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's assumption of another person's liability.

**W.32** We do not cover loss arising from the sale, manufacture, distribution, furnishing, or serving of alcoholic beverages. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to prevent the consumption of alcoholic beverages.

**W.33** We do not cover loss arising from the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of a controlled substance. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to supervise conduct involving a controlled substance.

**W.34** We do not cover loss arising from physical or mental abuse, molestation, harassment, or corporal punishment. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to prevent, report, or respond to that conduct.

**W.35** We do not cover loss arising from discrimination, wrongful eviction, wrongful entry, or invasion of privacy. We do not cover loss arising from the refusal to rent, lease, sell, or make property available.

**W.36** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's ownership or operation of a premises not identified as an insured location. We do not cover loss arising from property rented to others when the rental arrangement is not disclosed to us.

**W.37** We do not cover loss arising from a vacancy, abandonment, or failure to secure property. We do not cover loss arising from damage occurring while property is vacant or abandoned.

**W.38** We do not cover loss arising from theft by an insured, tenant, guest, employee, or person lawfully on the insured location. We do not cover loss caused by conversion, concealment, or unlawful retention of property.

**W.39** We do not cover loss arising from a dishonest, fraudulent, or criminal act by an insured. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's misrepresentation of a material fact.

**W.40** We do not cover loss arising from failure to disclose a material change in occupancy, use, ownership, or condition of property. We do not cover loss arising from concealment of information material to the risk.

**W.41** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's refusal to provide requested records, information, or access.

**W.42** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's voluntary payment, assumption of obligation, or expense without our consent. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's settlement of a claim without our approval.

**W.43** We do not cover loss arising from the failure to preserve damaged property for our inspection. We do not cover loss arising from disposal, alteration, or repair of property that prevents a reasonable inspection.

**W.44** We do not cover loss arising from an appraisal, estimate, opinion, inspection, or recommendation by us. We do not cover loss arising from an alleged failure by us to identify a condition, defect, or hazard.

**W.45** We do not cover loss based solely on application of a hurricane deductible floor. We do not cover loss based solely on disagreement concerning the application of that term.

**W.46** We do not cover loss based solely on a Coverage B percentage. We do not cover loss based solely on the allocation of property to that coverage.

**W.47** We do not cover loss based solely on a Coverage C limit as a percentage of Coverage A. We do not cover loss based solely on a classification of personal property for that purpose.

**W.48** We do not cover loss based solely on a loss of use limit. We do not cover loss based solely on a dispute concerning the application of that limit.

**W.49** We do not cover loss based solely on a debris removal additional amount. We do not cover loss based solely on a request to apply that additional amount.

**W.50** We do not cover loss arising from erosion, deterioration, or collapse of land. We do not cover loss arising from the movement or failure of land supporting a structure.

**W.51** We do not cover loss arising from the discharge of water from a dam, levee, dike, seawall, bulkhead, or similar structure. We do not cover loss arising from the failure or overflow of those structures.

**W.52** We do not cover loss arising from acts or decisions of a governmental body or authority. We do not cover loss arising from delay, denial, suspension, or withdrawal of governmental approval.

**W.53** We do not cover loss arising from the transmission of a communicable disease. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to prevent exposure to a communicable disease.

**W.54** We do not cover loss arising from electronic data loss, corruption, destruction, alteration, or inability to access data. We do not cover loss arising from malicious code, computer virus, or unauthorized access to a computer system.

**W.55** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's publication, transmission, or display of material that violates a person's rights. We do not cover loss arising from electronic communication that is unlawful, defamatory, or invasive of privacy.

**W.56** We do not cover loss arising from emotional injury without bodily injury. We do not cover loss arising from economic loss that does not result from covered property damage.

**W.57** We do not cover loss arising from diminution in value, market conditions, delay, or loss of market. We do not cover loss arising from a failure to realize expected income, rent, profit, or return.

**W.58** We do not cover loss arising from a claim brought by an insured against another insured. We do not cover loss arising from property owned jointly by insureds when the claim is based on an ownership dispute.

**W.59** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to comply with a lawful duty imposed by a lease or rental agreement. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to obtain required permission for occupancy or use.

**W.60** We do not cover loss arising from a claim that is not caused by a covered occurrence. We do not cover loss that is excluded by any applicable exclusion, condition, or limitation.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties stated in this policy. Compliance with those duties is a condition to our payment of any claim.

**W.2** An insured must provide information that is true, complete, and accurate. An insured must correct information that is discovered to be incomplete or inaccurate.

**W.3** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining relevant records and information.

**W.4** An insured must give us prompt notice of any "loss". Notice must identify the insured, the affected property, and the circumstances known to the insured.

**W.5** An insured must report a loss within thirty days after it occurs. An insured must provide notice as soon as practicable if the loss is not known when it occurs.

**W.6** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when a loss may involve theft, vandalism, or another unlawful act. An insured must provide us with information concerning that notice upon request.

**W.7** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect "covered property" from further damage. An insured must keep a record of the expenses incurred to protect covered property.

**W.8** An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.9** An insured must preserve evidence relating to the cause and extent of a loss. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property as often as reasonably necessary.

**W.10** An insured must show us the damaged property upon our request. An insured must permit us to examine property before repair, replacement, disposal, or removal when reasonably possible.

**W.11** An insured must provide records and documents we request that relate to a loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**W.12** An insured must provide receipts, invoices, estimates, photographs, and other evidence supporting a claim when such evidence is available. An insured must explain the basis for any amount claimed.

**W.13** An insured must submit to an examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the transcript of the examination if it is accurately recorded.

**W.14** An insured must produce household members, tenants, employees, or other persons with relevant knowledge for examination when reasonably available. An insured must not interfere with our reasonable request to obtain information from those persons.

**W.15** An insured must provide us with a signed, sworn statement concerning the loss when we request it. The statement must include information we reasonably require to determine coverage and the amount of loss.

**W.16** An insured must comply with the "proof of loss period". An insured must provide a proof of loss that states the information we reasonably request.

**W.17** An insured must identify the interest of each person in property for which payment is claimed. An insured must identify any lien, mortgage, security interest, lease, or other encumbrance affecting that property.

**W.18** An insured must identify any other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must provide copies of other insurance policies, declarations, or correspondence upon request.

**W.19** An insured must notify us if another person may be responsible for the loss. An insured must preserve our rights against that person.

**W.20** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense, except for reasonable measures to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must obtain our consent before agreeing to a settlement relating to a claim.

**W.21** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or omit any material fact relating to insurance, a loss, or a claim. An insured must not engage in fraud in connection with this policy.

**W.22** We may inspect the property and the premises where a loss occurred. Our inspection does not constitute an admission of liability or a waiver of any policy provision.

**W.23** We may require an appraisal when we and an insured disagree on the amount of loss. An insured must cooperate in the appraisal process if appraisal is required.

**W.24** An insured must select a competent and impartial appraiser when appraisal is required. An insured must pay the expense of the appraiser selected by that insured.

**W.25** An insured must permit us to obtain relevant information from persons or organizations having custody of records concerning a loss. An insured must execute authorizations reasonably necessary for that purpose.

**W.26** An insured must notify us promptly of a change in ownership, occupancy, or use of the insured property that materially affects the risk. An insured must provide information concerning the change upon request.

**W.27** An insured must maintain the property in a condition that does not materially increase the hazard. An insured must notify us of a material increase in hazard known to the insured.

**W.28** An insured must use reasonable care to prevent accidental loss. An insured must maintain protective safeguards that are represented to us as being in use.

**W.29** An insured must provide access to the property when we reasonably need access to investigate a claim. An insured must arrange access with a tenant or other occupant when the insured has the ability to do so.

**W.30** An insured must forward to us promptly any notice, demand, summons, complaint, or legal paper relating to a claim. An insured must not alter, destroy, or withhold such material.

**W.31** An insured must notify us promptly of any potential claim for which coverage may be sought. An insured must provide the known facts concerning the occurrence or circumstances giving rise to the claim.

**W.32** An insured must not admit liability or make a statement assigning fault on our behalf. An insured may provide truthful factual information concerning an occurrence.

**W.33** An insured must assist us in securing evidence, witnesses, and records necessary to investigate or defend a claim. An insured must attend proceedings when reasonably requested by us.

**W.34** An insured must reimburse us for payments obtained through recovery from a responsible person to the extent permitted by law. An insured must hold any such recovery in trust for us when required.

**W.35** Our right of recovery is not impaired by an insured after a loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to prejudice our right of recovery.

**W.36** An insured may waive rights of recovery against another person before a loss only if the waiver is made in writing. A waiver made after a loss is not effective against us unless we agree in writing.

**W.37** Payment to an insured does not transfer ownership of damaged property to us unless we agree otherwise. We may take all or part of damaged property at an agreed value after payment of a loss.

**W.38** An insured must retain any salvage that remains after a loss unless we direct otherwise. An insured must account to us for proceeds received from salvage.

**W.39** We may settle a claim with any person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment made in good faith discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.40** We may pay a claim jointly to an insured and another person having a legal interest in the property. An insured must provide information needed to identify such persons.

**W.41** The "minimum Section I deductible" applies to a covered loss as provided by this policy. An insured must bear the portion of loss assigned by the deductible.

**W.42** The "eighty percent condition" applies when required by this policy. An insured must provide information concerning the value of the property and the amount of insurance in effect.

**W.43** An insured must notify us of any other claim or loss that may affect the amount payable for the current claim. An insured must provide documentation concerning payments made by another source.

**W.44** We may adjust a claim with an insured who has authority to act for all persons with an interest in the claim. An insured must notify us if that authority is disputed.

**W.45** An insured must provide a current mailing address and contact information during the handling of a claim. An insured must notify us promptly when that information changes.

**W.46** An insured must accept communications delivered by a method permitted by law. An insured must respond to reasonable requests for information and documents.

**W.47** An insured must preserve all rights and remedies available in connection with a loss. An insured must not release a responsible person from liability without our written consent.

**W.48** An insured must provide us with notice of any suit, arbitration, mediation, or other proceeding relating to a claim. An insured must provide updates concerning material developments in that proceeding.

**W.49** We may investigate any claim before determining whether coverage applies. Our investigation does not waive any right, defense, condition, limitation, or exclusion.

**W.50** We may request additional information when the information previously provided is insufficient to evaluate a claim. An insured must provide the additional information when reasonably available.

**W.51** An insured must permit us to verify the amount, cause, and circumstances of a loss. An insured must not obstruct a reasonable inspection, examination, or verification.

**W.52** An insured must notify us if property for which payment is claimed is recovered. An insured must return to us any payment for property recovered to the extent required by law.

**W.53** An insured must notify us of any change affecting the insured's interest in property after a loss. An insured must provide documents establishing that interest upon request.

**W.54** An insured must provide a written authorization when needed for us to obtain records relevant to a claim. The authorization must be valid and sufficient for the requested records.

**W.55** An insured must make reasonable efforts to mitigate loss. Reasonable mitigation does not require an insured to place any person in danger.

**W.56** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may require an insured to retain possession of property unless we agree otherwise.

**W.57** An insured must preserve damaged property that is material to determining the cause of loss. An insured must notify us before changing the condition of such property when practicable.

**W.58** An insured must disclose all known facts that may affect our evaluation of a claim. An insured must promptly disclose facts learned after a claim is reported.

**W.59** An insured must cooperate with us concerning any contribution or recovery from other insurers. An insured must provide information needed to determine the availability of other insurance.

**W.60** An insured must bring any action against us within the applicable "legal action deadline". An insured must comply with all policy conditions before bringing such action.

**W.61** No person may recover under this policy unless that person has complied with the applicable duties and conditions. Compliance by one insured does not excuse noncompliance by another insured when that noncompliance materially affects our rights.

**W.62** A waiver or change of any policy provision is effective only when made by us in writing. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision later.

**W.63** Our rights and duties under this policy apply separately to each insured when appropriate. Separate application does not increase the amount we will pay for a loss.

**W.64** An insured must provide information and documents in a form reasonably acceptable to us. We may rely on information provided by an insured unless we have reason to question its accuracy.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** This endorsement applies while any part of the "residence premises" is rented to others. An "insured" must comply with these special requirements.

**W.2** An insured must maintain the residence premises in a condition suitable for lawful residential use. An insured must correct conditions that create a known risk of bodily injury or property damage.

**W.3** An insured must use a written "rental agreement" for each rental arrangement. The rental agreement must identify the persons authorized to occupy the rented portion of the residence premises.

**W.4** An insured must obtain and retain current contact information for each "tenant". An insured must promptly provide that information to us when we request it in connection with a claim or loss.

**W.5** An insured must comply with applicable laws, ordinances, and regulations concerning the rental use of the residence premises. An insured must obtain and maintain permits, registrations, licenses, and approvals required for that use.

**W.6** An insured must not represent that the residence premises has features, services, or protections that it does not have. An insured must promptly correct material information given to a tenant concerning the condition or use of the residence premises.

**W.7** An insured must retain the right to enter the rented portion of the residence premises as permitted by applicable law. An insured must exercise that right when reasonably necessary to inspect, maintain, or protect the property.

**W.8** An insured must take reasonable steps to determine whether a prospective tenant is suitable for the intended occupancy. An insured must not knowingly permit occupancy by a person whose conduct presents a known risk of injury or property damage.

**W.9** An insured must maintain all means of ingress and egress under your control in a reasonably safe condition. An insured must remove or correct known hazards within a reasonable time after discovery.

**W.10** An insured must maintain utility services under your control when necessary to protect the residence premises from damage. An insured must promptly investigate an interruption of utility service that may cause property damage.

**W.11** An insured must not permit alterations, repairs, or improvements that violate applicable law or the governing requirements of a condominium association. An insured must obtain required approval before work begins.

**W.12** An insured must maintain locks, latches, and other security devices under your control in working condition. An insured must promptly change or resecure access devices when possession of the rented portion changes.

**W.13** An insured must control the distribution of keys, access cards, codes, and similar devices for the rented portion of the residence premises. An insured must promptly deactivate or replace an access device when unauthorized use is known or reasonably suspected.

**W.14** A rented portion of the residence premises is "vacant" when it is not occupied by a tenant and does not contain sufficient customary property to support its intended rental use. An insured must promptly notify us of a vacancy that materially increases the risk of loss.

**W.15** An insured must secure a vacant rented portion of the residence premises against unauthorized entry. An insured must regularly inspect a vacant rented portion and correct conditions that may cause damage.

**W.16** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect plumbing, heating, and air conditioning systems from damage during vacancy or unoccupied periods. An insured must maintain heat or shut off and drain water systems when necessary to prevent freezing or leakage.

**W.17** An insured must inspect the rented portion of the residence premises when circumstances indicate a condition may exist that could cause injury or property damage. An insured must document material conditions discovered during an inspection.

**W.18** An insured must promptly report to us any "occurrence" that may result in a claim under this policy. An insured must also promptly notify appropriate authorities when required by law.

**W.19** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.20** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of any claim. An insured must provide truthful information and must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact.

**W.21** An insured must retain rental records, maintenance records, inspection records, and communications concerning a reported loss. An insured must provide those records to us when reasonably requested.

**W.22** An insured must promptly notify us of a demand, notice, suit, or other communication alleging "bodily injury" or "property damage". An insured must send us copies of received legal papers without delay.

**W.23** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense relating to a claim without our consent, except for reasonable expenses incurred to protect persons or property from further harm. This requirement does not prevent an insured from obtaining emergency assistance.

**W.24** An insured must not knowingly permit the residence premises to be used for unlawful activity. We do not cover loss arising out of unlawful activity knowingly permitted by an insured.

**W.25** An insured must not knowingly permit the manufacture, cultivation, storage, sale, or distribution of controlled substances on the residence premises. We do not cover loss arising out of that activity when knowingly permitted by an insured.

**W.26** An insured must promptly address a known condition involving animals kept by a tenant that creates a risk of injury or property damage. An insured must comply with applicable law concerning animals on the residence premises.

**W.27** An insured must not store hazardous materials on the residence premises except for customary household use and lawful maintenance. An insured must promptly arrange for lawful handling of a known hazardous condition.

**W.28** An insured must promptly repair or isolate a known leak, overflow, seepage, or discharge of water from plumbing, appliances, or other equipment under your control. An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent recurring water damage.

**W.29** An insured must maintain smoke alarms, fire extinguishing equipment, and other safety devices under your control as required by applicable law. An insured must not disable or remove such equipment except for lawful inspection, repair, or replacement.

**W.30** An insured must maintain electrical wiring, heating equipment, and appliances under your control in a reasonably safe condition. An insured must promptly correct a known defect that may create a fire or shock hazard.

**W.31** An insured must not knowingly permit open flames, portable heating devices, or similar equipment to be used in a manner that creates a known fire hazard. An insured must enforce reasonable restrictions concerning such equipment when the equipment is used by a tenant.

**W.32** An insured must not dispose of property left by a tenant except as permitted by applicable law. An insured must preserve information concerning abandoned property when that information may be relevant to a claim.

**W.33** We do not cover property owned by, rented to, occupied by, or in the care of a tenant, except as otherwise provided by this policy. This exclusion applies whether the property is located within or outside the residence premises.

**W.34** We do not cover loss arising out of an intentional act committed by an insured or at the direction of an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting injury or damage differs in kind or degree from that expected or intended.

**W.35** An insured must not permit assignment, subletting, or transfer of possession without consent when consent is required by the rental agreement or applicable law. An insured must obtain current contact information for an approved substitute occupant.

**W.36** An insured must comply with applicable condominium association rules affecting the rented portion of the residence premises. An insured must promptly notify us of a known dispute that may result in a claim for bodily injury or property damage.

**W.37** An insured must notify us of material changes in the use, occupancy, or physical condition of the residence premises that may affect the risk of loss. An insured must provide information concerning such change when we request it.

**W.38** Failure by an insured to comply with these special requirements may affect coverage only to the extent permitted by applicable law. We retain all rights available under this policy and applicable law.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will determine the amount payable for a direct physical "loss" to "covered personal property" under this provision. We will apply the terms, conditions, and limitations that apply to the property involved in the loss.

**W.2** You must protect covered personal property from further damage after a loss. You must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.3** The loss settlement basis for your covered personal property is replacement cost. We will determine the amount payable from the information available to us.

**W.4** We may inspect the property, examine records, and obtain information necessary to evaluate a loss. An insured must cooperate with our investigation and adjustment of the loss.

**W.5** You must provide records, receipts, photographs, estimates, and other documentation that reasonably support the claimed loss. We may consider the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

**W.6** We will apply the applicable limit of liability to the property involved in the loss. Payment for a loss does not increase an applicable limit of liability.

**W.7** We will apply the "loss assessment additional coverage limit" independently from the settlement of covered personal property. A loss assessment claim must be supported by information showing the assessment and the cause of loss.

**W.8** We will apply "fire service charge coverage" under its applicable terms. Payment under that coverage does not alter the settlement of covered personal property.

**W.9** We will apply the "jewelry and watches limit" when the claimed property is subject to that limitation. You must identify the property and provide available information concerning its ownership and condition.

**W.10** We will apply the "on-premises business property cap" when property used in connection with a business is involved in a loss. The use of property will be determined from the facts existing at the time of loss.

**W.11** We may require you to furnish a detailed inventory of damaged or destroyed property. The inventory must identify the property, its condition, and available information concerning its value.

**W.12** We may use available market information, purchase records, repair estimates, and other relevant evidence to determine the amount of loss. We are not required to rely upon unsupported estimates or opinions.

**W.13** If property is damaged and can be repaired, we may consider the reasonable cost of repair in determining the amount payable. Repairs must restore the property to a condition reasonably comparable to its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.14** If property cannot be repaired, we may consider available evidence concerning comparable property. Comparable property need not be identical in every respect.

**W.15** We do not cover the cost to replace undamaged property solely to achieve a uniform appearance with damaged property. We may consider matching characteristics only to the extent required by the applicable settlement terms.

**W.16** If a "pair or set" is affected by a loss, we may determine the loss based on the effect of the damage upon the pair or set as a whole. We may require the remaining property to be retained for inspection.

**W.17** We may take all or part of damaged property at an agreed or appraised value. Our taking of property does not waive any condition applicable to the loss.

**W.18** You must not abandon property to us unless we agree in writing. We may require that property remain available while the claim is being adjusted.

**W.19** If lost property is recovered after payment, you must notify us promptly. We may require repayment of amounts attributable to the recovered property or may take possession of that property.

**W.20** We will not pay more than the amount of the covered loss. We will reduce payment by amounts recoverable from responsible parties to the extent permitted by law.

**W.21** If another source provides payment for the same loss, you must disclose that source to us. We will not make duplicate payment for the same covered damage.

**W.22** We may pay an insured, a person legally entitled to receive payment, or another party having a lawful interest in the property. Payment to a proper recipient discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.23** An insured must submit requested information in a form reasonably acceptable to us. We may request clarification when submitted information is incomplete, inconsistent, or insufficient.

**W.24** We may defer payment while information material to the amount of loss remains unavailable. Our request for information does not waive any policy condition.

**W.25** A disagreement concerning value does not relieve an insured of the duty to protect property and cooperate with us. We may continue our investigation while a valuation disagreement exists.

**W.26** Our investigation, inspection, or payment does not constitute an admission of liability. No action by us waives a condition unless we agree to the waiver in writing.

**W.27** You must preserve evidence of the cause and extent of loss when reasonably possible. You must permit us to inspect damaged property and relevant records.

**W.28** We may make payment for an undisputed portion of a covered loss while we continue to evaluate another portion. Such payment does not affect our rights concerning the remaining claim.

**W.29** We will settle covered losses in accordance with the information reasonably available to us. An insured must notify us if material information changes before settlement is completed.

**W.30** We do not cover a loss to the extent it is based upon false, concealed, or misrepresented material information. An insured must provide truthful information concerning the property and the loss.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** "You" and "your" mean the person or organization named as the unit-owner on the Declarations. These terms include that person's spouse if a resident of the same household.

**W.2** "Insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives. It also means persons under the care of you or those relatives.

**W.3** "Insured" includes a person using covered property with your permission. That person is an insured only while using the property within the scope of that permission.

**W.4** "Unit-owner" means the person or organization having an ownership interest in the condominium unit shown on the Declarations. A unit-owner may be an individual, trust, estate, partnership, corporation, or other legal entity.

**W.5** "Condominium unit" means the unit shown on the Declarations. It includes property that you are required to insure under the governing condominium agreement.

**W.6** "Residence premises" means the condominium unit where you reside. It includes the part of the premises used exclusively by you as a residence.

**W.7** "Rental unit" means the condominium unit, or part of that unit, rented to others. It includes property held for rental to others when that property is not occupied by you.

**W.8** "Tenant" means a person occupying a rental unit with your permission. A tenant includes that person's household members and guests.

**W.9** "Bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. It includes required care, loss of services, and death resulting from that bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**W.10** "Property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. Electronic data is not tangible property.

**W.11** "Electronic data" means information, facts, or computer programs stored, created, used, or transmitted on electronic media. It includes data that is lost, corrupted, inaccessible, or unable to be used.

**W.12** "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence must result in bodily injury or property damage.

**W.13** "Accident" means an unintended and unforeseen event. It includes an unintended and unforeseen result of an act.

**W.14** "Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain. Business includes the rental of property to others when that rental is conducted for compensation.

**W.15** "Business pursuits" means activities regularly undertaken for compensation or financial gain. Activities ordinarily incident to noncommercial ownership of property are not business pursuits.

**W.16** "Employee" means a person employed by you in connection with maintenance, repair, or management of the rental unit. Employee does not include an independent contractor.

**W.17** "Residence employee" means an employee whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. A residence employee includes a person performing domestic services for you.

**W.18** "Volunteer worker" means a person who performs duties related to the rental unit without compensation. A volunteer worker is not an employee.

**W.19** "Insured location" means the residence premises. It also means a rental unit and premises used in connection with that unit.

**W.20** "Premises" means land and structures at a location. Premises includes the portions of a condominium property assigned to your exclusive use.

**W.21** "Property" means tangible property. Property includes fixtures, appliances, and equipment attached to or used in connection with the condominium unit.

**W.22** "Personal property" means property owned or used by an insured. Personal property does not include land or property permanently attached to land.

**W.23** "Building property" means property forming part of the condominium unit. Building property includes fixtures, improvements, alterations, appliances, and items permanently installed in the unit.

**W.24** "Improvements and betterments" means additions, alterations, fixtures, or installations made or acquired by you. Such property must be part of the condominium unit.

**W.25** "Loss" means accidental direct physical loss of or damage to covered property. Loss includes resulting loss of use when covered by this policy.

**W.26** "Covered property" means property for which coverage is provided under this policy. Covered property is subject to the applicable terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions.

**W.27** "Vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary to normal occupancy. A rental unit is vacant when it is not occupied by a tenant or by you.

**W.28** "Unoccupied" means not being used as a customary place of residence. A rental unit may be unoccupied even though personal property remains in it.

**W.29** "Water" means liquid in any form, including rain, surface water, groundwater, overflow, runoff, and waterborne material. Water includes water that backs up, overflows, or is discharged from a drain, sewer, sump, or related system.

**W.30** "Fungi" means any type or form of fungus, mold, mildew, or mycotoxin. Fungi includes spores, scents, or byproducts produced or released by fungi.

**W.31** "Pollutants" means solid, liquid, gaseous, or thermal irritants or contaminants. Pollutants include smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

**W.32** "Waste" includes materials to be recycled, reconditioned, reclaimed, or disposed of. Waste includes discarded materials and substances intended for disposal.

**W.33** "Suit" means a civil proceeding in which damages because of bodily injury or property damage are alleged. Suit includes arbitration or other dispute resolution proceedings to which we consent.
