## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This form applies when attached to insurance issued by us to you. It becomes part of the
agreement between you and us when attached.

The provisions of this form apply only to loss involving an "Earthquake". We provide
coverage only as stated in this form.

"Earthquake" means earth movement caused by seismic forces. This term includes related
shaking, tremors, and shocks.

The insurance provided by this form is subject to all provisions stated in this form. A
provision of this form applies only to the extent that it concerns an Earthquake.

Words and phrases that are given a special meaning in this form have that meaning
wherever used in this form. The context determines whether a word or phrase has a
special meaning.

We apply this form to property and interests for which insurance is provided by us to
you. We apply this form only while the attachment remains applicable.

An insured seeking coverage under this form must comply with the duties imposed by this
form. Compliance with a duty does not expand coverage.

We determine coverage under this form from the facts of the loss. We apply the
provisions of this form to those facts.

We do not provide coverage beyond the coverage expressly granted by this form. No
conduct by us changes the terms of this form unless we agree in writing.

A statement made by an insured does not alter this form. A waiver by us does not alter
this form unless we state the waiver in writing.

The attachment of this form does not create coverage for a loss that is not covered by
this form. The attachment does not change a limitation stated in this form.

We apply exclusions stated in this form as written. An exclusion applies whether a loss
results directly or indirectly from the excluded cause, unless the exclusion provides
otherwise.

We apply conditions stated in this form as written. An insured must satisfy an
applicable condition before we owe payment under this form.

The terms of this form are severable to the extent permitted by law. The invalidity of a
provision does not affect the enforceability of another provision.

We may investigate a claimed Earthquake loss. Our investigation does not mean that we

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We cover "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by an "earthquake".
The loss must result from a sudden and accidental physical event.

**W.2** "Earthquake" means earth movement caused by natural or artificial forces.
Earthquake includes shaking, tremors, vibration, and seismic waves.

**W.3** Earthquake includes an aftershock that follows an earthquake. Earthquake also
includes related earth movement that results from the same seismic event.

**W.4** We cover direct physical loss caused by subsidence, settlement, sinking, rising,
shifting, cracking, or displacement of earth when the earth movement is caused by an
earthquake. The covered loss must be physical damage to covered property.

**W.5** We cover direct physical loss caused by the collapse of a building or other
structure when the collapse results from an earthquake. The collapse must involve damage
to covered property.

**W.6** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to a dwelling used as your
residence. Coverage applies while the dwelling is at the described location.

**W.7** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to structures separated
from the dwelling by clear space or connected to the dwelling by a fence, utility line,
or similar connection. The structures must be covered property.

**W.8** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to personal property. The
personal property must be owned or used by an insured.

**W.9** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to property of others
while that property is in your care. Coverage applies only when the property is covered
property.

**W.10** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to permanently installed
fixtures, equipment, and appliances. The damaged item must be part of covered property
at the time of loss.

**W.11** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to building materials and
supplies. The materials and supplies must be intended for use in construction,
alteration, or repair of covered property.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover “earthquake loss” caused by earth movement, whether the movement
is sudden, gradual, natural, or human made. This exclusion applies even when earth
movement causes fire, water escape, or another event that contributes to loss.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by “earth movement,” including shaking, tremors,
aftershocks, or shifting of earth. Earth movement includes movement occurring below, at,
or above the ground surface.

**W.3** We do not cover “direct physical loss” caused by settling, cracking, shrinking,
bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, ceilings, or pavements. This
exclusion applies whether the condition appears immediately or develops after earth
movement.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water
that enters through an opening created by flood or surface water.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by overflow of a body of water, whether driven by
wind, earth movement, or another force. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs
up, discharges, or overflows from a sewer, drain, sump, or related system.
