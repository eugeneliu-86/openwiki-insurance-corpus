# HO 04 54 Earthquake (Edition 2021-12)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This form applies when attached to insurance issued by us to you. It becomes part of the agreement between you and us when attached.

The provisions of this form apply only to loss involving an "Earthquake". We provide coverage only as stated in this form.

"Earthquake" means earth movement caused by seismic forces. This term includes related shaking, tremors, and shocks.

The insurance provided by this form is subject to all provisions stated in this form. A provision of this form applies only to the extent that it concerns an Earthquake.

Words and phrases that are given a special meaning in this form have that meaning wherever used in this form. The context determines whether a word or phrase has a special meaning.

We apply this form to property and interests for which insurance is provided by us to you. We apply this form only while the attachment remains applicable.

An insured seeking coverage under this form must comply with the duties imposed by this form. Compliance with a duty does not expand coverage.

We determine coverage under this form from the facts of the loss. We apply the provisions of this form to those facts.

We do not provide coverage beyond the coverage expressly granted by this form. No conduct by us changes the terms of this form unless we agree in writing.

A statement made by an insured does not alter this form. A waiver by us does not alter this form unless we state the waiver in writing.

The attachment of this form does not create coverage for a loss that is not covered by this form. The attachment does not change a limitation stated in this form.

We apply exclusions stated in this form as written. An exclusion applies whether a loss results directly or indirectly from the excluded cause, unless the exclusion provides otherwise.

We apply conditions stated in this form as written. An insured must satisfy an applicable condition before we owe payment under this form.

The terms of this form are severable to the extent permitted by law. The invalidity of a provision does not affect the enforceability of another provision.

We may investigate a claimed Earthquake loss. Our investigation does not mean that we accept coverage or waive a condition.

An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation of a claimed Earthquake loss. Cooperation includes providing information reasonably requested by us.

We may request records that relate to a claimed Earthquake loss. An insured must make requested records available to us when reasonably able to do so.

We may inspect damaged property that is the subject of a claimed Earthquake loss. An insured must preserve the property for our inspection when reasonably able to do so.

Payment by us under this form does not establish coverage for another loss. Our payment does not waive a provision of this form.

This form applies according to its terms. We and you are bound by the terms of this form.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We cover "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by an "earthquake". The loss must result from a sudden and accidental physical event.

**W.2** "Earthquake" means earth movement caused by natural or artificial forces. Earthquake includes shaking, tremors, vibration, and seismic waves.

**W.3** Earthquake includes an aftershock that follows an earthquake. Earthquake also includes related earth movement that results from the same seismic event.

**W.4** We cover direct physical loss caused by subsidence, settlement, sinking, rising, shifting, cracking, or displacement of earth when the earth movement is caused by an earthquake. The covered loss must be physical damage to covered property.

**W.5** We cover direct physical loss caused by the collapse of a building or other structure when the collapse results from an earthquake. The collapse must involve damage to covered property.

**W.6** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to a dwelling used as your residence. Coverage applies while the dwelling is at the described location.

**W.7** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to structures separated from the dwelling by clear space or connected to the dwelling by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. The structures must be covered property.

**W.8** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to personal property. The personal property must be owned or used by an insured.

**W.9** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to property of others while that property is in your care. Coverage applies only when the property is covered property.

**W.10** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to permanently installed fixtures, equipment, and appliances. The damaged item must be part of covered property at the time of loss.

**W.11** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to building materials and supplies. The materials and supplies must be intended for use in construction, alteration, or repair of covered property.

**W.12** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to improvements made by you. The improvements must be attached to or contained within covered property.

**W.13** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to attached equipment that supplies heat, water, electricity, ventilation, or other utility service to covered property. The damage must result directly from the earthquake.

**W.14** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to foundations that support covered property. Coverage applies only to damage resulting directly from the earthquake.

**W.15** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to retaining walls that are part of covered property. The retaining wall must be damaged by the earthquake.

**W.16** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to driveways, walks, patios, and similar property when that property is damaged by the earthquake. Coverage applies only when the property is covered property.

**W.17** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to fences, gates, and similar structures. The damaged property must be covered property.

**W.18** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to an underground pipe, flue, drain, or utility line located on the residence premises. The damaged item must be covered property.

**W.19** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to outdoor equipment that is permanently attached to covered property. The attachment must exist at the time of loss.

**W.20** We cover the reasonable cost to protect covered property from further direct physical loss caused by an earthquake. An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve covered property after a loss.

**W.21** We cover reasonable emergency repairs necessary to protect covered property from further damage following an earthquake. Emergency repairs do not increase the amount otherwise payable for the loss.

**W.22** We cover reasonable costs to remove debris of covered property damaged by an earthquake. The debris removal must result from a covered direct physical loss.

**W.23** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to trees, shrubs, and plants when they are covered property. Coverage applies only to physical damage caused by the earthquake.

**W.24** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to property stored within a covered structure. The property must otherwise qualify as covered property.

**W.25** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to property temporarily removed from the residence premises. The property must be covered property at the time of loss.

**W.26** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to property undergoing repair, alteration, or renovation. The property must remain covered property while the work is performed.

**W.27** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to a newly acquired structure when the structure is covered property. Coverage applies from the time you acquire an insurable interest in the structure.

**W.28** We cover direct physical loss caused by an earthquake to personal property kept at a temporary location. The personal property must be owned or used by an insured.

**W.29** We cover "ensuing loss" caused by fire, explosion, or theft when the ensuing loss results from an earthquake. The ensuing loss must be a direct physical loss to covered property.

**W.30** We cover direct physical loss caused by fire that follows an earthquake. Coverage applies even when the fire begins after the earth movement has ceased.

**W.31** We cover direct physical loss caused by explosion that follows an earthquake. The explosion must result from damage caused by the earthquake.

**W.32** We cover direct physical loss caused by smoke that follows an earthquake. The smoke damage must result from a covered fire or explosion.

**W.33** We cover direct physical loss caused by falling objects when the falling objects result from an earthquake. The falling objects must physically damage covered property.

**W.34** We cover direct physical loss caused by the weight of snow, ice, or sleet when the weight results from structural damage caused by an earthquake. The damage must be directly related to the earthquake.

**W.35** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam when the discharge or overflow results from earthquake damage. The damaged system must be located on the residence premises.

**W.36** We cover direct physical loss caused by the sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a system damaged by an earthquake. The system must be covered property.

**W.37** We cover direct physical loss caused by freezing when freezing results from an earthquake. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat or otherwise protect the property from freezing.

**W.38** We cover direct physical loss caused by electrical injury when the electrical injury results from an earthquake. The damaged electrical property must be covered property.

**W.39** We cover direct physical loss caused by a power surge resulting from an earthquake. The power surge must physically damage covered property.

**W.40** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or waterborne material. This exclusion applies whether the water results from an earthquake or any other cause.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by mudslide, mudflow, landslide, or earth movement that is not caused by an earthquake. This exclusion applies even if earth movement occurs with an earthquake.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or faulty maintenance. This exclusion applies even if an earthquake contributes to the loss.

**W.43** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, or compaction. This exclusion applies whether performed by you or another person.

**W.44** We do not cover loss consisting of settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion that is not direct physical loss caused by an earthquake. We cover resulting direct physical loss when it is otherwise covered under this endorsement.

**W.45** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not apply to loss occurring before the neglect.

**W.46** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. An insured must not cause or arrange for loss to covered property.

**W.47** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action that results in seizure, confiscation, destruction, or condemnation of property. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action when the fire is otherwise covered.

**W.48** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law that regulates the construction, repair, replacement, use, or removal of property. This exclusion applies to increased costs arising from compliance with ordinance or law.

**W.49** We do not cover loss to land, including land on which covered property is located. We do not cover the cost to restore, stabilize, compact, grade, or otherwise repair land.

**W.50** We do not cover loss to water, wells, springs, reservoirs, or underground water. This exclusion applies whether the loss results from an earthquake or related earth movement.

**W.51** We do not cover loss to property that is not covered property. Coverage applies only to direct physical loss within the scope of this endorsement.

**W.52** The "deductible" applicable to earthquake loss applies to each covered loss. The deductible applies before we determine the amount payable for covered direct physical loss.

**W.53** All direct physical loss arising from the same earthquake, aftershock, or related seismic event is treated as a single occurrence. This provision applies regardless of the number of damaged items or locations.

**W.54** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. An insured must describe the damaged property and the circumstances of the earthquake.

**W.55** An insured must protect covered property from further damage and keep an accurate record of repair expenses. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property as often as reasonably required.

**W.56** We will pay for covered direct physical loss after the applicable deductible and all applicable policy conditions are satisfied. Payment is limited to the amount of loss that is covered under this endorsement.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The "limit of liability" is the greatest amount we will pay for "earthquake loss" to "covered property." This limit applies to every payment we make for earthquake loss.

**W.2** The limit of liability applies to all claims made by an "insured" arising from the same earthquake loss. We will not increase the limit because more than one insured seeks payment.

**W.3** The limit of liability applies regardless of the quantity of covered property damaged or destroyed by earthquake loss. Damage to separate items of covered property does not create separate limits of liability.

**W.4** The limit of liability applies regardless of the quantity of claims arising from earthquake loss. The presentation of separate claims does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.5** The limit of liability applies regardless of the quantity of persons sustaining loss. Liability to more than one person does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.6** The limit of liability applies to direct physical loss to covered property caused by earthquake. It also applies to covered expense arising from that loss.

**W.7** The limit of liability includes amounts we pay to repair damaged covered property. It also includes amounts we pay to replace covered property when replacement is covered.

**W.8** The limit of liability includes amounts we pay for debris removal when that expense is covered. Debris removal does not provide an additional limit of liability.

**W.9** The limit of liability includes amounts we pay for reasonable repairs to protect covered property from further damage. Protective repairs do not increase the limit of liability.

**W.10** The limit of liability includes amounts we pay for loss assessment when that loss is covered. Payment of a loss assessment does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.11** The limit of liability includes amounts we pay for additional living expense when that expense is covered. Payment for additional living expense does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.12** The limit of liability includes amounts we pay for fair rental value when that loss is covered. Payment for fair rental value does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.13** The limit of liability includes amounts we pay for property removed from the "residence premises" to protect it from earthquake loss. Removal of property does not create an additional limit of liability.

**W.14** The limit of liability includes amounts we pay after an insured returns removed property to the residence premises. Return of property does not create an additional limit of liability.

**W.15** The limit of liability applies to loss occurring during repair, rebuilding, or replacement of covered property damaged by earthquake. Work performed after earthquake loss does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.16** The limit of liability applies whether covered property is repaired, rebuilt, replaced, or not repaired. An insured's selection of a method of settlement does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.17** The limit of liability applies whether covered property is owned solely by an insured or jointly with another person. The ownership interest of another person does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.18** The limit of liability applies whether covered property is subject to a mortgage, lien, lease, or other interest. Payment to a person having an interest in covered property does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.19** The limit of liability applies to loss involving personal property located at the residence premises. It also applies to covered personal property temporarily located elsewhere.

**W.20** The limit of liability applies to loss involving property of guests or other persons when that property is covered. The presence of property owned by another person does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.21** The limit of liability applies to loss involving property used for more than one purpose. A change in the use of covered property does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.22** The limit of liability applies to loss involving property that is damaged in different ways by earthquake. Separate types of damage do not create separate limits of liability.

**W.23** The limit of liability applies to loss involving damage that becomes apparent at different times. Later discovery of damage does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.24** The limit of liability applies to loss involving damage repaired at different times. The timing of repair or replacement does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.25** The limit of liability applies to loss involving more than one location at the residence premises. Damage affecting different locations does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.26** The limit of liability applies to loss involving more than one structure or item of personal property. Damage to separate structures or items does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.27** The limit of liability applies before we make any payment for earthquake loss. Payments we make reduce the remaining limit of liability.

**W.28** We will not make payment that exceeds the remaining limit of liability. The remaining limit of liability is reduced by amounts previously paid for earthquake loss.

**W.29** An insured must not incur expense or undertake repair solely because the limit of liability remains available. We will pay only amounts that are covered and do not exceed the remaining limit of liability.

**W.30** An insured must provide information we reasonably require to determine the amount of covered earthquake loss. We may withhold payment until we can determine the amount payable within the limit of liability.

**W.31** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after earthquake loss. The cost of protection is subject to the limit of liability when that cost is covered.

**W.32** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. Disposal of damaged property does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.33** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of earthquake loss. Cooperation does not change the limit of liability.

**W.34** A "deductible" applies to earthquake loss when applicable. Application of a deductible does not increase the limit of liability.

**W.35** We will apply the deductible to covered earthquake loss before determining the amount we will pay. The deductible remains the responsibility of the insured.

**W.36** Payment of the limit of liability ends our obligation to make further payment for earthquake loss. This applies even if covered loss remains unpaid.

**W.37** No act by us, including investigation, inspection, adjustment, or payment, increases the limit of liability. Our payment obligation remains subject to the limit of liability.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** This deductible applies to an "earthquake loss" for which coverage is provided. The deductible applies whether the loss affects real property, personal property, or both.

**W.2** An insured must bear the deductible amount before we pay for an earthquake loss. We will apply the deductible to the total amount of covered loss.

**W.3** The earthquake deductible percentage is fifteen percent. We calculate the deductible by applying that percentage to the Coverage A limit of liability.

**W.4** The deductible applies regardless of the number of insured locations affected by the earthquake loss. The deductible also applies regardless of the number of items of covered property damaged.

**W.5** We will determine the deductible before applying any payment limitation. We will not reduce the deductible because a loss is subject to a limitation.

**W.6** We will apply the deductible before determining the amount payable for covered property. The deductible is not increased because more than one type of covered property is damaged.

**W.7** The deductible applies to loss caused directly or indirectly by earthquake. It also applies to loss caused by earth movement resulting from earthquake.

**W.8** The deductible applies to all covered loss arising from the same earthquake. We will treat related damage arising from that earthquake as part of the earthquake loss.

**W.9** The deductible applies when an earthquake causes damage at more than one insured location. We will not apply a separate deductible to each insured location.

**W.10** The deductible applies when an earthquake damages property covered under different property coverages. We will not apply the deductible separately to each property coverage.

**W.11** We will subtract the deductible from the amount of covered loss after determining the value of the damaged property. We will make no payment for covered loss that does not exceed the deductible.

**W.12** If covered loss exceeds the deductible, we will pay the amount of covered loss that remains after the deductible is applied. Our payment remains subject to all applicable limits of liability.

**W.13** The deductible applies to loss involving a building and property contained in that building. The deductible also applies to loss involving property located away from the residence premises.

**W.14** The deductible applies to loss involving property owned by you and property owned by another person when both are covered. We will apply the deductible to the combined covered loss.

**W.15** The deductible applies to additional expense that is covered as part of an earthquake loss. We will subtract the deductible before paying covered additional expense.

**W.16** The deductible applies to loss involving repair, replacement, or restoration of covered property. The method used to settle the loss does not change the deductible.

**W.17** The deductible applies whether damaged property is repaired, replaced, or not repaired. An insured's decision concerning damaged property does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.18** The deductible applies whether an insured submits a claim for all or part of the covered loss. We will not reduce the deductible because an insured submits a partial claim.

**W.19** The deductible applies to covered loss discovered after the earthquake. The time when damage is discovered does not affect application of the deductible.

**W.20** The deductible applies when more damage becomes known after we have made payment. We will continue to apply the same deductible to the earthquake loss.

**W.21** The deductible applies before we determine any amount payable to a mortgagee or other person with an interest in covered property. No interest in covered property changes the deductible.

**W.22** The deductible applies before we make payment to an insured or any other person entitled to payment. We may make payment only for covered loss remaining after application of the deductible.

**W.23** An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine the covered loss. An insured must also cooperate with our investigation of the earthquake loss.

**W.24** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after an earthquake loss. Reasonable measures taken to protect covered property do not remove the deductible.

**W.25** The deductible applies even if damage results from more than one condition arising from earthquake. We will determine the covered amount of the earthquake loss before applying the deductible.

**W.26** The deductible applies whether the earthquake loss occurs suddenly or develops over time. The manner in which damage develops does not change the deductible.

**W.27** The deductible applies to covered property that is damaged, destroyed, or lost. We will apply the deductible to the amount otherwise payable for that property.

**W.28** The deductible applies without regard to any deductible that may apply to other covered loss. We will not combine this deductible with a deductible applicable to other loss.

**W.29** We will not refund any portion of the deductible after payment of an earthquake loss. The deductible remains the responsibility of an insured.

**W.30** The deductible does not increase our limit of liability. Payment of the deductible by an insured does not create coverage for property or loss not covered.

**W.31** All policy conditions remain applicable to an earthquake loss. An insured must comply with those conditions before we make payment.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover “earthquake loss” caused by earth movement, whether the movement is sudden, gradual, natural, or human made. This exclusion applies even when earth movement causes fire, water escape, or another event that contributes to loss.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by “earth movement,” including shaking, tremors, aftershocks, or shifting of earth. Earth movement includes movement occurring below, at, or above the ground surface.

**W.3** We do not cover “direct physical loss” caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, ceilings, or pavements. This exclusion applies whether the condition appears immediately or develops after earth movement.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water that enters through an opening created by flood or surface water.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by overflow of a body of water, whether driven by wind, earth movement, or another force. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up, discharges, or overflows from a sewer, drain, sump, or related system.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground, including water that presses on, flows through, or seeps through a building or structure. This exclusion applies to water affecting foundations, walls, floors, basements, or other parts of the residence premises.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by “land subsidence,” sinkhole activity, soil instability, or compaction. We do not cover loss caused by earth that slides, slips, sinks, settles, erodes, or collapses.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by landslide, mudslide, mudflow, debris flow, or earth flow. This exclusion applies whether water, gravity, excavation, vibration, or earth movement contributes to the loss.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by the collapse of land or earth into a cavity, void, mine, tunnel, trench, or excavation. We do not cover loss caused by damage arising from support that is weakened, removed, or disturbed.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by volcanic eruption, volcanic action, lava, ash, dust, cinders, or airborne volcanic material. We do not cover loss caused by tremors, shock waves, or earth movement associated with volcanic activity.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by a tsunami, tidal wave, seiche, or wave action. This exclusion applies whether the water movement follows an earthquake, landslide, volcanic event, or other occurrence.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by wind-driven water that enters a building through an opening not caused by a covered direct physical loss. We do not cover loss caused by rain, sleet, snow, or spray that enters through such an opening.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by wear, tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do not cover loss caused by latent defect, inherent vice, or any quality in property that causes it to damage or destroy itself.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, decay, or hidden or latent defect. We do not cover loss caused by dampness, dryness, wet rot, dry rot, or mold.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke, vapor, fumes, or emissions released over time. We do not cover loss caused by pollutants unless a covered direct physical loss causes the pollutant release.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by settling of a building, foundation, wall, floor, roof, or pavement. We do not cover loss caused by cracking or separation that results from ordinary settlement or construction defects.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We do not cover the cost to correct faulty, inadequate, or defective work or materials.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective materials used in repair, construction, renovation, or remodeling. This exclusion applies even when earth movement reveals or worsens the defective condition.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect property from further damage when it is safe to do so.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss committed by or at the direction of an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of whether another insured did not participate in the conduct.

**W.21** We do not cover loss arising from dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, or malicious acts by an insured. We do not cover loss arising from concealment, misrepresentation, or material omission by an insured.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by war, invasion, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, military force, or usurped power. We do not cover loss caused by discharge of a nuclear weapon or other hostile use of nuclear energy.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, or radioactive waste. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event that contributes to loss.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by order of a governmental authority. We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of an ordinance, law, or regulation affecting repair, rebuilding, demolition, or use of property.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of a building code, land use requirement, zoning requirement, or similar governmental requirement. We do not cover increased costs resulting from compliance with such requirements.

**W.26** We do not cover loss consisting of diminished value, loss of market value, stigma, or reduction in sale price. We do not cover loss based solely on the possibility of future damage.

**W.27** We do not cover loss consisting of delay, loss of use, loss of access, inconvenience, or interruption of occupancy unless expressly covered. We do not cover expenses that are not necessary because of a covered direct physical loss.

**W.28** We do not cover loss arising from failure of power, communication, water, gas, heating, cooling, or utility service away from the residence premises. We do not cover spoilage, interruption, or damage resulting from that failure.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by interruption of utility service occurring at a utility facility, transmission line, distribution line, or related equipment. This exclusion applies whether earth movement damages the utility property or prevents access to it.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by collapse of a well, septic system, drain field, retaining wall, bulkhead, pier, dock, or similar structure. We do not cover loss caused by failure of soil support beneath such property.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by cracking, settling, shifting, or movement of a swimming pool, spa, hot tub, patio, deck, walkway, driveway, fence, or similar property. We do not cover resulting damage to personal property located in or on such property.

**W.32** We do not cover loss to lawns, trees, shrubs, plants, gardens, or growing crops caused by earth movement. We do not cover loss caused by changes in soil condition, drainage, moisture, or elevation.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by excavation, drilling, blasting, mining, quarrying, or tunneling. This exclusion applies whether the activity occurs on or away from the residence premises.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by vibration, blasting, pile driving, drilling, or construction activity. We do not cover cracking, settling, or shifting attributed to those activities.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by movement of fill, spoil, refuse, or other material placed on land. We do not cover loss caused by inadequate compaction, grading, drainage, or slope stabilization.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by the failure, breach, overflow, or collapse of a dam, levee, dike, embankment, retaining structure, or water-control structure. This exclusion applies whether the failure follows earth movement or another event.

**W.37** We do not cover loss caused by erosion, scour, undermining, or washing away of land. We do not cover loss caused by loss of lateral or subjacent support.

**W.38** We do not cover loss caused by pressure of earth, water, ice, snow, or debris against a building or other structure. We do not cover loss caused by weight that damages foundations, walls, roofs, or support systems.

**W.39** We do not cover loss caused by ice damming, freezing, thawing, frost heave, or movement of frozen ground. We do not cover loss caused by the expansion or contraction of soil, rock, concrete, or other material.

**W.40** We do not cover loss caused by animals, birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic pets. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, burrowing, or waste.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or other microbial matter. This exclusion applies whether the condition results from earth movement, water intrusion, humidity, or another cause.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by contamination, including contamination of soil, water, air, or property. We do not cover costs to test for, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize contamination.

**W.43** We do not cover loss caused by release, discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, escape, or presence of pollutants. We do not cover loss caused by cleanup or remediation of pollutants.

**W.44** We do not cover loss to property while it is being repaired, renovated, remodeled, reconstructed, or altered when the loss arises from the work. We do not cover loss caused by materials, tools, equipment, or workmanship associated with that work.

**W.45** We do not cover loss to property that is vacant, abandoned, or unoccupied when vacancy, abandonment, or lack of occupancy contributes to loss. We do not cover deterioration or damage that occurs because property is not maintained.

**W.46** We do not cover loss to property used for business, professional, or commercial activity when the loss arises from that activity. We do not cover damage to business records, accounts, data, or electronic information.

**W.47** We do not cover loss to electronic data, software, programs, code, digital records, or media caused by earth movement. We do not cover costs to restore, recreate, replace, or reproduce electronic data.

**W.48** We do not cover loss consisting of loss of income, business interruption, loss of profits, or loss of opportunity. We do not cover consequential, indirect, exemplary, punitive, or special damages.

**W.49** We do not cover loss arising from delay in repair, rebuilding, replacement, or relocation. We do not cover additional expense resulting from a contractor shortage, labor dispute, material shortage, or governmental delay.

**W.50** We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain property in sound condition. An insured must make reasonable repairs needed to protect property from further damage.

**W.51** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage, leakage, discharge, or overflow of water or steam. We do not cover loss caused by continuous moisture, condensation, humidity, or vapor.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" subject to the terms of this endorsement and the policy. We will not pay for loss that is not covered.

**W.2** We will determine the amount of loss after application of all applicable exclusions, limitations, and conditions. We will apply the deductible to the adjusted amount of covered loss.

**W.3** We will settle loss to covered property at the location where the loss occurs. We will not increase payment because property is moved after the loss.

**W.4** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred solely to protect covered property from further covered damage.

**W.5** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when we request it. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.6** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, estimates, and other information we reasonably request to determine the amount of loss. We may examine any damaged property and any available records relating to the loss.

**W.7** The loss settlement basis for personal property is replacement cost. We will apply that basis only to covered personal property.

**W.8** We will determine the value of damaged property immediately before the loss. We may consider the property's age, condition, quality, use, and market value.

**W.9** We will not pay more than the amount reasonably necessary to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may consider available materials and labor in determining that amount.

**W.10** We may elect to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property. If we make that election, we will provide written notice to an insured.

**W.11** If we elect to repair or replace damaged property, we may select persons to perform the work. We will not be responsible for an insured's separate agreement with a contractor unless we approve that agreement.

**W.12** An insured must cooperate with us in arranging inspection, repair, rebuilding, or replacement. An insured must permit reasonable access to the damaged property.

**W.13** We will not pay for increased costs caused by delay attributable to an insured. We will not pay for increased costs caused by changes requested by an insured unless we agree to those changes.

**W.14** We will not pay for repair or replacement that improves property beyond its condition immediately before the loss. We may pay for necessary matching only when matching is required to complete a reasonable repair.

**W.15** We will not pay for undamaged property merely because damaged property cannot be matched in appearance. We may consider physical condition, availability, and reasonable repair methods in determining the amount of loss.

**W.16** We will settle loss to building property based on the damaged portion of the property. We will not pay for replacement of an undamaged portion unless replacement is necessary to repair covered damage.

**W.17** We will not pay for loss attributable to faulty, inadequate, or defective repair work performed after a loss. This provision does not reduce coverage for direct physical loss otherwise covered under this endorsement.

**W.18** An insured must submit a signed, sworn proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information we reasonably need to investigate and settle the claim.

**W.19** An insured must identify damaged property and describe the damage. An insured must distinguish damaged property from property that was not damaged.

**W.20** An insured must provide an inventory of damaged personal property when we request it. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item, when reasonably available.

**W.21** We may require an insured to provide available documentation supporting ownership, value, and condition of damaged property. We may consider the absence of documentation with all other available evidence.

**W.22** We may take statements from an insured under oath as permitted by the policy. An insured must answer material questions relating to the claim.

**W.23** We may inspect damaged property as often as reasonably necessary. We may take samples, photographs, measurements, and other records during an inspection.

**W.24** We will not pay for any part of a loss that an insured intentionally conceals or misrepresents. We may deny payment when a material misrepresentation affects our investigation or settlement of the claim.

**W.25** We will reduce payment by amounts received for the same covered loss from another source, to the extent permitted by law. We will not reduce payment for amounts that do not compensate for the same covered loss.

**W.26** We may pay an insured entitled to receive payment under the policy. Payment to an insured does not determine ownership of damaged property.

**W.27** We may include a mortgagee, lienholder, or other person with a legal interest in payment for building property. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning those interests.

**W.28** We may pay the cost of emergency measures that are reasonable and necessary to protect covered property from further covered damage. Emergency measures do not include permanent repairs unless we agree to them.

**W.29** We will not pay for costs incurred to comply with a law or ordinance unless that cost is covered by the policy or this endorsement. We will not pay for a cost that is unrelated to covered physical damage.

**W.30** We will not pay for the cost to replace property with property of superior kind or quality. If property of like kind and quality is unavailable, we may pay the amount reasonably necessary for comparable property.

**W.31** We will determine whether property can be repaired using reasonable methods. We may consider safety, structural integrity, functionality, and available repair materials.

**W.32** We may obtain estimates of repair or replacement cost. We may rely on estimates prepared by persons we select or approve.

**W.33** An insured may submit an estimate supporting a claimed amount of loss. We may request additional information when that estimate differs from our estimate.

**W.34** We will not be required to accept an estimate that includes work unrelated to covered damage. We may separate covered repair costs from costs for maintenance, improvement, or unrelated work.

**W.35** We will determine the amount of loss without regard to sentimental value. We will not pay for personal attachment to property.

**W.36** We will not pay for loss of use, inconvenience, or loss of enjoyment under this Loss Settlement provision. We will pay only amounts otherwise covered by the policy or this endorsement.

**W.37** We will not pay for the value of data, information, plans, or records apart from the material on which they are stored, unless otherwise covered. We may pay for the reasonable cost to restore covered records when coverage applies.

**W.38** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for any covered loss. The applicable limit includes all amounts payable for the same covered property and occurrence.

**W.39** The "association assessment limit" applies as provided by the policy and this endorsement. We will determine payment for an assessment based on the applicable coverage terms.

**W.40** The "fire department call fee" applies only when the policy provides coverage for that charge. We will not include that charge in the amount of loss unless coverage applies.

**W.41** The "precious stones theft limit" does not increase the amount we will pay for covered earthquake damage. We will apply any applicable limitation to the claimed property.

**W.42** The "special limit on business property on the residence premises" applies when property is subject to that limitation. We will determine payment for that property under the applicable coverage terms.

**W.43** We will not pay duplicate amounts for the same item of property. We may allocate payment among applicable coverages when more than one coverage applies.

**W.44** We may recover damaged property after we pay for its loss. An insured must cooperate in transferring any interest in recovered property to us when payment is made for its full value.

**W.45** If we recover property for which we have paid, we may return the property to an insured and adjust payment accordingly. An insured may retain recovered property only with our agreement.

**W.46** We may take possession of damaged property after payment if we pay for its full value. Our possession of damaged property does not waive any policy condition.

**W.47** An insured must notify us promptly if damaged property is recovered or repaired. We may revise payment to reflect recovery, repair, or other reduction in the amount of loss.

**W.48** We will not pay for expenses incurred to prepare a claim unless the expense is otherwise covered. An insured remains responsible for costs incurred in presenting a claim.

**W.49** We may request that an insured obtain repair estimates or replacement information. We will not require an insured to incur unreasonable expense to obtain information.

**W.50** We will consider reasonable repair methods that restore property to its preloss function. We are not required to select the most expensive repair method.

**W.51** We may pay for temporary repairs when they are reasonable and necessary to prevent further covered damage. Payment for temporary repairs will not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.52** We will not pay for costs caused by an insured's failure to protect property from further damage. This provision applies only to the increase in loss caused by that failure.

**W.53** We may defer settlement of an amount that cannot be determined until additional information is available. We will pay any undisputed amount of covered loss when required by law.

**W.54** We may reconsider an adjustment if material information becomes available after payment. Any additional payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability and policy terms.

**W.55** An insured must notify us of any person who may be responsible for the loss. An insured must preserve our rights of recovery against that person.

**W.56** We may pursue recovery to the extent we make payment for a covered loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.57** We will not settle a claim in a manner that impairs another insured's rights without considering that insured's interest. We may require agreement among persons claiming an interest in the same payment.

**W.58** We may make payment jointly to persons with conflicting interests in damaged property. Payment made jointly satisfies our obligation to the extent of that payment.

**W.59** We will settle loss in accordance with the facts existing at the time of loss. Later changes in market conditions do not increase the amount otherwise payable.

**W.60** We will not pay for a claimed loss unless an insured establishes that direct physical damage resulted from a covered cause of loss. An insured must establish the amount of the claimed covered loss.

**W.61** All payments under this Loss Settlement provision are subject to the policy terms and applicable law. Payment of a claim does not waive any right or defense available to us.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** Words and phrases defined in this endorsement have the meanings stated below. These meanings apply wherever the words or phrases are used in this endorsement.

**W.2** “Earthquake” means a sudden movement or trembling of the earth’s crust. Earthquake includes a seismic event that causes shaking, vibration, displacement, or rupture of land.

**W.3** “Seismic Event” means an occurrence originating below or at the surface of land that produces earth movement. A seismic event may produce shaking, tremors, fault movement, or related physical effects.

**W.4** “Earth Shaking” means movement of land that causes vibration of a building, structure, or personal property. Earth shaking includes vibration transmitted through soil, rock, or other materials.

**W.5** “Earth Tremor” means a perceptible vibration of land caused by a seismic event. An earth tremor may occur with or without visible damage to land or property.

**W.6** “Land Shock Wave” means a wave of energy moving through land as a result of a seismic event. Land shock wave includes movement transmitted beneath, across, or through the surface of land.

**W.7** “Aftershock” means earth shaking or an earth tremor following a seismic event. An aftershock is part of the earthquake occurrence from which it results.

**W.8** “Foreshock” means earth shaking or an earth tremor preceding a seismic event. A foreshock is part of the earthquake occurrence to which it is related.

**W.9** “Fault Movement” means displacement of land along a fracture, break, or zone of weakness within the earth’s crust. Fault movement includes slipping, shifting, or rupture of land.

**W.10** “Ground Rupture” means a break, crack, fissure, or displacement visible at the surface of land and caused by a seismic event. Ground rupture includes related separation or offset of land.

**W.11** “Land Displacement” means movement of soil, rock, or other earth materials from its prior location. Land displacement includes lateral, vertical, or rotational movement.

**W.12** “Land Subsidence” means the downward movement or settling of land. Land subsidence includes sinking, collapsing, or compaction of soil or rock.

**W.13** “Land Uplift” means the upward movement of land. Land uplift includes heaving, rising, or elevation of soil or rock.

**W.14** “Land Heave” means an upward or lateral movement of land that affects the support of property. Land heave includes movement caused by a seismic event.

**W.15** “Land Settlement” means the downward movement of land caused by compaction, shifting, or displacement. Land settlement includes uneven movement affecting a foundation, building, or other structure.

**W.16** “Landslide” means the downward movement of soil, rock, or other earth materials on a slope. Landslide includes a slide, slump, collapse, or fall of land.

**W.17** “Mudslide” means a landslide consisting substantially of mud, soil, silt, clay, or similar earth materials. Mudslide includes earth materials moved by gravity or water.

**W.18** “Mudflow” means a flowing mass of mud, soil, silt, clay, or similar earth materials. Mudflow includes a movement of earth materials carried by water.

**W.19** “Volcanic Eruption” means the emission, discharge, or movement of lava, ash, rock, gas, or other volcanic material from a volcano. Volcanic eruption includes related earth shaking, earth tremors, and land shock waves.

**W.20** “Volcanic Material” means lava, ash, cinders, dust, rock, gas, or other matter produced by volcanic activity. Volcanic material includes matter deposited, expelled, or carried from a volcano.

**W.21** “Lava” means molten rock expelled or flowing from a volcano. Lava includes molten rock that has cooled or hardened after movement from a volcano.

**W.22** “Volcanic Ash” means fine particles of rock, mineral, or other material emitted by volcanic activity. Volcanic ash includes dust, cinders, and similar particulate matter.

**W.23** “Surface Water” means water on the surface of land, including water that has flowed, accumulated, or spread over land. Surface water includes water from rain, runoff, melting snow, overflow, or a natural body of water.

**W.24** “Flood” means a general and temporary condition of partial or complete inundation of land. Flood includes inundation caused by surface water, overflow of water, or unusual accumulation of water.

**W.25** “Tidal Wave” means a large movement of water caused by an underwater disturbance or other natural force. Tidal wave includes water movement associated with a seismic event.

**W.26** “Tsunami” means a series of water movements caused by a seismic event, volcanic activity, or other disturbance affecting a body of water. Tsunami includes water that inundates land or property.

**W.27** “Earthquake Occurrence” means all earthquake-related conditions arising from the same seismic event. An earthquake occurrence includes associated earth shaking, earth tremors, land shock waves, foreshocks, and aftershocks.

**W.28** “Earthquake Loss” means direct physical loss to property caused by an earthquake occurrence. Earthquake loss includes physical damage resulting from earth shaking, earth tremors, land shock waves, or fault movement.

**W.29** “Direct Physical Loss” means accidental physical alteration, destruction, or damage to tangible property. Direct physical loss does not include an economic loss that is not caused by physical damage to property.

**W.30** “Physical Damage” means a distinct and observable change in the condition of property. Physical damage includes breakage, cracking, collapse, displacement, or other injury to property.

**W.31** “Property” means tangible property that is described in this endorsement or in the policy to which this endorsement is attached. Property includes a building, other structure, and personal property when applicable.

**W.32** “Building” means a structure used as a residence and permanently attached additions to that structure. Building includes materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises for use in construction, alteration, or repair.

**W.33** “Other Structure” means a structure separated from a building by clear space or connected to a building only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. Other structure includes a structure located at an insured location.

**W.34** “Personal Property” means movable tangible property owned or used by an insured. Personal property includes contents located in a building or other structure when applicable.

**W.35** “Residence Premises” means the dwelling where you reside and the land associated with that dwelling. Residence premises includes structures and locations used in connection with the dwelling.

**W.36** “Insured Location” means a location described as an insured location under the policy to which this endorsement is attached. Insured location includes the residence premises when applicable.

**W.37** “Foundation” means the supporting portion of a building or other structure that transfers weight to land. Foundation includes footings, piers, slabs, walls, and similar structural components that provide support.
