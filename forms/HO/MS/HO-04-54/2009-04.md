# HO 04 54 Earthquake (Edition 2009-04)
> SUPERSEDED by HO 04 54 Earthquake (Edition 2021-12) for policies effective on or after 2021-12-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" is attached to and made part of your insurance contract. It changes the insurance provided by that contract.

The provisions of this endorsement apply only while it is attached to your insurance contract. The terms of the insurance contract remain applicable except as changed by this endorsement.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of the insurance contract, the provision of this endorsement applies. No provision is changed unless this endorsement expressly changes it.

The term "earthquake" includes earth movement resulting from seismic activity. Earthquake may include related shaking, tremors, and aftershocks.

Earthquake loss may result from direct physical damage to covered property. Earthquake loss may also result from conditions caused by earthquake.

The application of this endorsement depends on the property, coverages, and interests insured under your insurance contract. We apply this endorsement in accordance with its terms and conditions.

You must read this endorsement together with the insurance contract. An insured must comply with all duties applicable to a loss.

Nothing in this endorsement creates coverage that is not expressly provided. Nothing in this endorsement extends coverage beyond the terms that apply to the insured property.

We determine coverage under this endorsement based on the facts of the loss. We may require information reasonably necessary to determine whether this endorsement applies.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation of a claimed loss. An insured must provide records and information that we reasonably request.

We may inspect damaged property as reasonably necessary. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably practicable.

An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. Those steps do not change the coverage provided by this endorsement.

This endorsement applies to loss occurring during the period in which it is attached to your insurance contract. It does not apply to loss occurring outside that period.

The attachment of this endorsement does not waive any right available to us. Our failure to exercise a right does not waive that right.

Terms that are not changed by this endorsement retain their meaning. Terms used in this endorsement have the meanings applicable to your insurance contract unless otherwise stated.

The term "insured" has the meaning applicable to your insurance contract. The term "covered property" has the meaning applicable to your insurance contract.

The term "loss" means accidental physical loss or damage as determined under the insurance contract and this endorsement. A loss must be subject to all applicable conditions and limitations.

We apply this endorsement separately to each insured interest when required by its terms. The rights and duties of an insured remain subject to the insurance contract.

This endorsement does not alter the ownership of insured property. It does not alter the responsibilities of an insured concerning that property.

The attachment of this endorsement is not a representation that earthquake loss will occur or will not occur. It is a statement of the terms that apply when earthquake loss is claimed.

You should retain this endorsement with your insurance contract. This endorsement remains part of your insurance contract while attached.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We cover "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by an "earthquake". Direct physical loss must result from physical damage to covered property.

**W.2** An earthquake means a shaking, trembling, or displacement of earth. An earthquake includes earth movement that results from natural geologic forces.

**W.3** An earthquake includes land shock waves and tremors. An earthquake includes earth movement occurring before, during, or after a volcanic eruption.

**W.4** We treat related earth shocks arising from the same earth movement as a single "earthquake loss". We determine whether earth shocks are related from the facts of the loss.

**W.5** Covered property means property in which you have an insurable interest and that is located at an "insured location". Covered property includes buildings, structures, and personal property that are subject to this coverage.

**W.6** An insured location means a location identified in our records as subject to this coverage. The insured location includes land only to the extent land is necessary to support covered property.

**W.7** We cover direct physical loss to a building caused by earthquake. Coverage includes damage to parts of the building that are permanently attached to it.

**W.8** We cover direct physical loss to structures that are separated from a building. Coverage applies when the structures are located at the insured location and are covered property.

**W.9** We cover direct physical loss to personal property caused by earthquake. Coverage applies while the personal property is at the insured location.

**W.10** We cover direct physical loss to permanently installed equipment caused by earthquake. Permanently installed equipment includes equipment that serves a covered building or structure.

**W.11** We cover direct physical loss to materials and supplies used to repair covered property. The materials and supplies must be at the insured location when damaged.

**W.12** We cover direct physical loss to fixtures caused by earthquake. Fixtures remain covered when they are attached to covered property.

**W.13** We cover direct physical loss to improvements made by you to covered property. The improvements must be your responsibility to repair or replace.

**W.14** We cover direct physical loss caused when earthquake damages a foundation supporting covered property. Coverage applies when the foundation damage results in physical damage to the supported property.

**W.15** We cover direct physical loss caused when earthquake damages a wall, roof, floor, or ceiling of covered property. Coverage includes resulting physical damage to interior property.

**W.16** We cover direct physical loss caused by the falling of covered property during an earthquake. The falling must result directly from earthquake damage.

**W.17** We cover direct physical loss caused by the collapse of covered property during an earthquake. Collapse must result directly from physical damage caused by earthquake.

**W.18** We cover direct physical loss caused by debris from covered property that is damaged by earthquake. Debris removal is covered when it is necessary to repair or replace damaged property.

**W.19** We cover reasonable costs to remove debris of damaged covered property. The debris removal must result from a covered earthquake loss.

**W.20** We cover reasonable costs for emergency measures necessary to protect damaged covered property from further damage. The emergency measures must be taken after an earthquake loss.

**W.21** An insured must use reasonable means to protect damaged property from further damage. An insured must keep a record of reasonable emergency measures taken after the loss.

**W.22** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property and the insured location.

**W.23** An insured must give us prompt notice of an earthquake loss. The notice must identify the insured location and describe the known damage.

**W.24** An insured must notify us when damaged property is removed from the insured location. The notice must state where the property may be inspected.

**W.25** An insured must cooperate with us in investigating an earthquake loss. Cooperation includes providing information reasonably requested by us about the loss.

**W.26** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when we request it. The inventory must identify the damaged property and its condition.

**W.27** An insured must provide records supporting the ownership, value, and condition of damaged property when reasonably available. Records may include receipts, photographs, estimates, and other relevant information.

**W.28** An insured must submit a signed "proof of loss" when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim.

**W.29** We may examine an insured under oath concerning an earthquake loss. An insured must sign a record of the examination when we request it.

**W.30** We may inspect, test, analyze, and photograph damaged property. An insured must provide reasonable access to the insured location and damaged property.

**W.31** We determine the amount of covered loss from the direct physical damage caused by earthquake. We may consider the condition of the property immediately before the earthquake loss.

**W.32** We determine whether damage was caused by earthquake from the available facts. We may consider reports, inspections, expert opinions, photographs, and other relevant evidence.

**W.33** We pay for the reasonable cost to repair damaged property with property of like kind and quality. A "repair" must restore the damaged property to a condition reasonably comparable to its condition immediately before the earthquake loss.

**W.34** We pay for the reasonable cost to replace damaged property when repair is not feasible. A "replacement" must use property of like kind and quality.

**W.35** We may pay the "actual cash value" of damaged property when repair or replacement has not been completed. Actual cash value reflects the value of damaged property immediately before the earthquake loss.

**W.36** We may pay the reasonable repair cost when damaged property can be repaired. A "reasonable repair cost" means the cost of necessary work performed in a customary manner.

**W.37** We do not cover costs to repair damage that was present before the earthquake loss. We do not cover costs to correct conditions that did not result from the earthquake loss.

**W.38** We do not cover loss caused solely by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of covered property. We do not cover loss caused solely by wear, deterioration, decay, or rot.

**W.39** We do not cover costs of ordinary maintenance. "Ordinary maintenance" means work necessary to preserve property in its existing condition without restoring earthquake damage.

**W.40** We do not cover costs to improve undamaged property. We do not cover costs to redesign property for reasons unrelated to repairing earthquake damage.

**W.41** We do not cover loss to land, including land on which covered property is located. We cover direct physical loss to covered property that results from movement of land.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by erosion, subsidence, or earth sinking unless it results directly from an earthquake loss. We determine the direct cause of loss from the facts of the loss.

**W.43** We do not cover loss caused solely by flood, surface water, mudflow, or water below the surface of the ground. We cover direct physical loss caused by earthquake when water damage results directly from physical damage to covered property.

**W.44** We do not cover loss caused by negligent, faulty, or defective design, construction, repair, or maintenance unless earthquake directly causes physical damage to covered property. We cover the resulting earthquake damage to covered property.

**W.45** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action unless the action is necessary to prevent the spread of fire following an earthquake loss. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from earthquake damage.

**W.46** We may settle an earthquake loss by paying for repair, replacement, or actual cash value. Our settlement method will reflect the damaged property and the information available to us.

**W.47** A "loss settlement" does not require us to replace property when repair will restore the property to a reasonably comparable condition. A loss settlement does not require replacement at the same location.

**W.48** We may take possession of damaged property after we settle an earthquake loss. "Salvage" means damaged property for which we have paid a covered loss.

**W.49** An insured must not abandon damaged property to us without our consent. An insured must preserve salvage when reasonably possible.

**W.50** We may require an insured to assign to us rights of recovery against a person responsible for an earthquake loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.51** We may recover from a person responsible for an earthquake loss to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing rights of recovery.

**W.52** We pay covered loss only to the extent of your insurable interest in the damaged property. An "insurable interest" means a financial interest in property that is damaged by an earthquake loss.

**W.53** We may make payment jointly to persons having an interest in damaged property. Payment does not determine ownership of the damaged property.

**W.54** We may adjust an earthquake loss with any insured having knowledge of the loss. Payment to that insured discharges our obligation for the payment made.

**W.55** We may request estimates for repair or replacement of damaged property. An insured must provide estimates reasonably available to the insured.

**W.56** Coverage applies only to direct physical loss caused by earthquake. We pay no more than the amount of covered loss determined under these provisions.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The "limit of liability" applicable to this endorsement is the most we will pay for "direct physical loss" caused by "earthquake". The applicable limit of liability applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims, or persons sustaining loss.

**W.2** The applicable limit of liability is not increased because an earthquake causes loss to more than one item of "covered property". The applicable limit of liability is not increased because loss occurs at more than one location.

**W.3** The applicable limit of liability applies to all loss arising from the same seismic disturbance. Earth movements that arise from the same seismic disturbance are treated as part of the same "occurrence".

**W.4** The applicable limit of liability applies whether the earthquake causes loss directly or through resulting collapse, fire, explosion, water damage, or another covered cause of loss. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for the combined loss.

**W.5** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for loss to property that is subject to more than one coverage under this policy. Payment under another coverage does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.6** The applicable limit of liability applies to the total loss sustained by all insureds. A payment to an insured reduces the amount available for any remaining loss.

**W.7** The applicable limit of liability applies to property owned by you and property owned by another person when both are included within covered property. Ownership by another person does not create an additional limit of liability.

**W.8** The applicable limit of liability applies to property having shared ownership. We will not pay more than the insured's financial interest in damaged property.

**W.9** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability because repair, replacement, or restoration costs exceed that limit. Increased construction costs do not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.10** The applicable limit of liability includes amounts payable for damage to covered property. Expenses payable as part of a covered loss do not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.11** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for reasonable emergency measures that are part of a covered loss. Emergency measures do not create separate coverage.

**W.12** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for removal of damaged property or debris when that expense is part of a covered loss. Debris removal does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.13** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for the cost to repair, rebuild, or replace covered property. The method used to repair, rebuild, or replace property does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.14** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for loss involving property that cannot be repaired. The unavailability of similar materials does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.15** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for loss involving property that has special architectural, artistic, historic, or personal value. Sentimental value does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.16** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for loss involving a pair, set, collection, or part of a larger unit. Loss to related property does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.17** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for loss involving attached property, detached property, or property temporarily away from the residence premises. The location of covered property at the time of loss does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.18** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for loss involving property in the care of an insured. The custody or control of property does not create additional coverage.

**W.19** We may take all or part of damaged property at the value we have paid for it. Our taking of damaged property does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.20** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless immediate disposal is necessary to protect persons or property. Disposal of damaged property does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.21** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. Our election to repair, rebuild, or replace does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.22** We may pay the "actual cash value" of damaged property instead of repairing or replacing it. Payment based on actual cash value does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.23** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability when "replacement cost" is available under this policy. Replacement cost does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.24** An insured must not incur costs that are unnecessary to protect, repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property. We do not cover unnecessary costs, even if the applicable limit of liability has not been exhausted.

**W.25** We will not pay for betterment except as required to complete covered repair or replacement. Betterment does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.26** We will not pay for loss to undamaged property solely to achieve uniformity of appearance. A difference in color, texture, design, or material does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.27** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability because local requirements affect repair or replacement. Compliance with laws, ordinances, or regulations does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.28** The applicable limit of liability applies before payment is reduced by any applicable "deductible". Application of a deductible does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.29** A deductible applies to a covered loss in accordance with this policy. An insured must satisfy the applicable deductible before we make payment for covered loss.

**W.30** The applicable limit of liability is not increased by "other insurance". We will not pay more than our share of covered loss when other insurance also applies.

**W.31** Payment by another insurer, person, organization, or governmental authority does not increase the applicable limit of liability. We may reduce our payment by amounts available from another source when permitted by law.

**W.32** The applicable limit of liability is not increased by the existence of a mortgage, lien, security interest, or other interest in covered property. Payment to a "lienholder" does not increase the amount payable for covered loss.

**W.33** The applicable limit of liability is not increased because an insured assigns an interest in damaged property after loss. An assignment does not bind us unless we agree to it in writing.

**W.34** The applicable limit of liability applies to a claim made by an insured or by a "legal representative" acting for an insured. The identity of the person making the claim does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.35** The applicable limit of liability is not increased by a demand, appraisal, mediation, arbitration, suit, or other proceeding. The costs of asserting or defending a claim do not increase the applicable limit of liability unless we agree otherwise in writing.

**W.36** Payment of part of a covered loss does not waive our right to apply the applicable limit of liability to all remaining loss. Our investigation, adjustment, or payment does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.37** Our payment of the applicable limit of liability exhausts our obligation for covered loss arising from the occurrence. A "settlement" does not create additional liability beyond the applicable limit of liability.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" applies to each "earthquake loss" for which we owe payment. We will pay only that part of an earthquake loss that exceeds the deductible.

**W.2** An "occurrence" means earthquake activity arising from the same earthquake. All earthquake loss arising from an occurrence is subject to a single deductible.

**W.3** The "applicable limit" means the limit of liability applicable to the dwelling. The deductible is five percent of the applicable limit.

**W.4** We will determine the applicable limit as of the time of the earthquake loss. A change made after the earthquake loss does not change the deductible.

**W.5** If no limit of liability applies to the dwelling, the applicable limit is the highest limit of liability applying to property damaged by the earthquake. We will use that applicable limit to determine the deductible.

**W.6** We will add all loss payable from an occurrence before applying the deductible. This includes loss involving more than one type of covered property.

**W.7** The deductible applies to the total earthquake loss, rather than separately to each item of covered property. The deductible is not increased because property is damaged at more than one location.

**W.8** Damage caused by the same occurrence remains subject to the same deductible even if it is reported in separate claims. We may combine information from related claims in determining the total earthquake loss.

**W.9** Damage caused by separate occurrences is subject to a separate deductible. We will determine whether damage arose from the same occurrence based on the facts available to us.

**W.10** Earth movement that continues from the same earthquake is part of the same occurrence. Later damage that arises from that earthquake is subject to the deductible already applicable to that occurrence.

**W.11** We will apply the deductible before determining the amount we will pay. We will not pay for earthquake loss that does not exceed the deductible.

**W.12** If earthquake loss exceeds the deductible, we will pay the amount of loss that remains after the deductible is applied. Our payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.13** The deductible does not reduce the applicable limit of liability. The deductible reduces only the amount otherwise payable for the earthquake loss.

**W.14** A payment for part of an earthquake loss reduces the remaining amount of the deductible not yet applied to that occurrence. We will apply later payments arising from that occurrence consistently with this provision.

**W.15** We may make payments for undisputed portions of an earthquake loss after applying the deductible. Such payment does not change the deductible applicable to the occurrence.

**W.16** An "insured" must provide information we reasonably need to identify damaged property and determine the occurrence causing the damage. An insured must distinguish, when reasonably possible, earthquake damage from damage caused by another event.

**W.17** An insured must provide records, estimates, receipts, and other supporting information that we reasonably request to determine the amount of earthquake loss. We may use that information to apply the deductible.

**W.18** An insured must promptly notify us of additional damage believed to arise from an occurrence for which a deductible has been applied. We may reopen our evaluation of that occurrence as necessary.

**W.19** We will determine the deductible from the applicable limit without regard to the number of claims submitted. Separate estimates or invoices do not create separate deductibles.

**W.20** We will not apply the deductible more than once to the same earthquake loss. We may apply the deductible to the combined amount of all loss payable from the occurrence.

**W.21** The deductible applies whether damaged property is repaired, replaced, or not repaired. The method used to settle the earthquake loss does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.22** The deductible applies before any payment is made for earthquake loss. No payment by us waives our right to apply the deductible to remaining loss from the occurrence.

**W.23** We may revise the amount payable if later information changes the amount of covered earthquake loss. Any revision will include application of the deductible required by this provision.

**W.24** A disagreement concerning the value of damaged property does not change the deductible. We will apply the deductible after determining the amount of earthquake loss payable by us.

**W.25** We will not duplicate payment for the portion of earthquake loss within the deductible. An insured remains responsible for that portion of the earthquake loss.

**W.26** The deductible applies to loss caused directly by earthquake. The deductible does not create coverage for loss that is otherwise not payable by us.

**W.27** We will apply this provision to each occurrence for which we owe payment. The deductible applies regardless of the order in which earthquake damage is discovered.

**W.28** An insured must cooperate with us in determining whether reported damage arose from the same occurrence as previously reported damage. Failure to provide requested information may delay our determination of the amount payable.

**W.29** We may inspect damaged property and review available information concerning the occurrence. Our inspection does not waive application of the deductible.

**W.30** We will calculate the amount payable after applying the deductible to the total earthquake loss. Our obligation to pay does not exceed the amount remaining after that calculation.

**W.31** This deductible provision applies whenever we pay for earthquake loss. It applies to all amounts payable by us for that occurrence.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by any peril that is excluded by this policy and is not changed by this endorsement. Such loss is excluded regardless of any other cause or event that contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by "earth movement" other than the earthquake loss for which coverage is provided by this endorsement. Earth movement includes, but is not limited to, volcanic eruption, landslide, mine subsidence, mudflow, earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. This exclusion applies whether or not an earthquake contributes to such loss.

**W.4** We do not cover loss caused by defective, inadequate, or faulty planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies to property on or away from the residence premises.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or smog, rust, or corrosion. We do not cover loss caused by dry rot or wet rot.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. "Pollutants" means any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or irritant contaminant, including smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, waste, and contaminated water.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by nesting or infestation, or discharge or release of waste products or secretions, by birds, rodents, insects, or other animals. We do not cover loss caused by vermin.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by birds, rodents, insects, or other animals owned or kept by you. This exclusion applies whether the animals are on the residence premises or elsewhere.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by neglect. An insured must use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. "Intentional loss" means any loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, including seizure, destruction, or confiscation of property. We do cover direct loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by war. "War" includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, military action, or action taken by a governmental authority to hinder or defend against any of these.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. "Nuclear hazard" includes nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination, whether controlled or uncontrolled.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by power failure or interruption of utility service occurring away from the residence premises. This exclusion applies even if an earthquake damages property used to provide utility service.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by failure of a utility service that is supplied through equipment located on the residence premises. We do cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by a covered peril resulting from that failure.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, remodeling, renovation, or demolition of property. We do not cover the increased cost of complying with any ordinance or law.

**W.17** We do not cover the value of land. Land includes the land on which a dwelling is located and any land supporting or surrounding covered property.

**W.18** We do not cover loss to "property not covered." Property not covered includes articles separately described and specifically insured in another insurance policy.

**W.19** We do not cover loss to currency, bullion, bank notes, securities, deeds, evidences of debt, letters of credit, notes other than bank notes, manuscripts, medals, stamps, or philatelic property. This exclusion applies whether the property is held for personal use or for any other purpose.

**W.20** We do not cover loss to electronic data, including programs, applications, software, and information stored or transmitted in electronic form. We do not cover the cost to restore, recreate, replace, or recover electronic data.

**W.21** We do not cover loss to property of roomers, boarders, or other tenants. This exclusion does not apply to property owned by an insured.

**W.22** We do not cover loss to property used primarily for business purposes. "Business" includes trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain.

**W.23** We do not cover loss to merchandise, samples, or property held for sale. We do not cover loss to property held as inventory.

**W.24** We do not cover loss to aircraft and their equipment. Aircraft includes drones and other devices designed for flight, whether or not occupied.

**W.25** We do not cover loss to watercraft and their equipment, except as otherwise provided for covered personal property. We do not cover loss to watercraft used to carry persons or property for a charge.

**W.26** We do not cover loss to motor vehicles, including their equipment and accessories, except as otherwise provided for covered personal property. A motor vehicle includes any vehicle designed for travel on public roads.

**W.27** We do not cover loss to property contained in or upon a motor vehicle when the loss arises from the operation, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of that vehicle. This exclusion applies whether the vehicle is owned by you or by another person.

**W.28** We do not cover loss to property while it is being transported for hire. We do not cover loss to property entrusted to a carrier for shipment.

**W.29** We do not cover loss to property at a location rented to others by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the rental arrangement is written, oral, temporary, or ongoing.

**W.30** We do not cover loss arising from conversion, theft by deception, or voluntary parting with property. This exclusion applies if you are induced to part with property by any fraudulent scheme, trick, device, or false pretense.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from seizure or destruction of property by any public authority. We do cover direct loss caused by fire resulting from such action.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by collapse, except as otherwise provided by this policy. Collapse does not include settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by pressure or weight of water, ice, or snow to a fence, pavement, patio, swimming pool, foundation, retaining wall, bulkhead, pier, wharf, or dock. This exclusion applies whether the property is attached to a dwelling or detached from it.

**W.34** Flood and surface water remains excluded.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps, leaks, or flows through a building, foundation, pavement, swimming pool, or other structure.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sumps, sump pumps, or related equipment. We do not cover loss caused by overflow or discharge from those systems or equipment.

**W.37** We do not cover loss caused by overflow from a body of water, whether natural or artificial. A body of water includes a lake, pond, reservoir, river, stream, canal, drainage course, or other watercourse.

**W.38** We do not cover loss caused by waterborne material carried or otherwise moved by water. This exclusion applies to mud, debris, silt, sediment, sand, and similar material.

**W.39** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or virus. This exclusion applies whether such condition is visible, hidden, or concealed within property.

**W.40** We do not cover loss caused by contamination. Contamination includes the introduction or presence of a foreign substance that causes property to be unsafe, impure, unusable, or unfit for its intended use.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical, electrical, or electronic failure. This exclusion includes failure caused by power surge, short circuit, arcing, or overload.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by interruption of heating, air conditioning, plumbing, electrical, or other building systems. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril resulting from the interruption.

**W.43** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system while the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied. An insured must maintain heat in the building or shut off the water supply and drain the system and appliances.

**W.44** We do not cover loss caused by leakage or seepage from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. This exclusion applies to continuous or repeated leakage or seepage.

**W.45** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. We do cover direct physical loss caused by sudden and accidental discharge or overflow when not otherwise excluded.

**W.46** We do not cover loss caused by failure to make necessary repairs. An insured must protect property from further damage after a loss.

**W.47** We do not cover loss caused by faulty repair, maintenance, alteration, or renovation. This exclusion applies to work performed by you or by any other person.

**W.48** We do not cover loss caused by soil movement, erosion, or subsidence that is not earthquake loss covered by this endorsement. This exclusion applies to naturally occurring and artificially caused conditions.

**W.49** We do not cover loss caused by excavation, drilling, blasting, mining, quarrying, or tunneling. This exclusion applies whether the activity occurs on the residence premises or elsewhere.

**W.50** We do not cover loss caused by vibration, settling, cracking, or collapse resulting from construction activity. We do not cover loss caused by movement of earth resulting from construction activity.

**W.58** An insured must comply with reasonable requests made by us to verify the amount of loss. An insured must permit reasonable review of records supporting the claimed damage and expense.

**W.59** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged covered property only with your consent or as otherwise permitted by law.

**W.60** An insured must perform the duties stated in these Special Requirements as a condition of coverage for an earthquake loss. We may deny coverage to the extent an insured's failure to perform a duty materially prejudices our ability to investigate or adjust the claim.

**W.61** We do not cover any increase in loss caused by an insured's unreasonable failure to protect covered property after an earthquake loss. This provision does not require an insured to endanger any person.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" to "covered property" on the basis stated in this section. A covered loss must result from direct physical loss caused by an earthquake.

**W.2** We may determine "actual cash value" by considering the property's condition, age, useful life, and depreciation. Actual cash value does not mean market value unless market value is the most appropriate measure of the loss.

**W.3** We may determine "replacement cost" by considering the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement cost does not include an amount attributable to betterment, increased value, or a change in design.

**W.4** We will not pay more than your insurable interest in covered property at the time of loss. We may require evidence of your interest before making payment.

**W.5** We will settle only the part of covered property that is physically damaged by a covered loss. We do not cover loss to property that has not sustained direct physical loss.

**W.6** We may inspect damaged property and examine the location where the loss occurred. You must permit us reasonable access to the property for that purpose.

**W.7** For personal property, the loss settlement basis is replacement cost. We will apply that basis only to personal property for which coverage applies.

**W.8** The "assessment coverage limit" does not increase the amount we will pay for direct physical loss to covered property. We will apply that limit only as applicable to a covered assessment.

**W.9** A "fire response charge" does not establish the value of damaged property. We will settle that charge only when it is covered and supported by satisfactory documentation.

**W.10** The special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones does not change the valuation of property damaged by a covered loss. We will apply that special limit when it applies to the property involved.

**W.11** The home business contents limit applies to covered property used in connection with a home business. That limit does not increase because the property is damaged by a covered loss.

**W.12** You must protect damaged property from further damage after a covered loss. We will consider reasonable measures taken to protect the property when settling the loss.

**W.13** You must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. We may deny payment for damage that cannot be verified because the property was discarded or altered without our consent.

**W.14** You must provide a description of damaged property that identifies the property and the nature of the damage. You must provide available records, photographs, receipts, and other evidence supporting the claimed loss.

**W.15** We may require you to identify property that was present at the location before the loss. We may also require information showing ownership, condition, and value.

**W.16** We may determine the cost of repair from estimates, invoices, material prices, labor charges, and other reliable information. We are not bound by an estimate obtained by you.

**W.17** We may determine the cost of replacement from the cost of property that performs the same intended function. We may use available property that is comparable in quality and utility.

**W.18** We will not pay for loss that is repaired or replaced with property that is substantially different in function or quality. We may pay the amount that would have been necessary to repair or replace with like kind and quality.

**W.19** We will not pay for any increase in cost caused by a delay that is not necessary to repair or replace covered property. We may consider delays caused by your failure to provide requested information or access.

**W.20** We may take all or part of damaged property as "salvage" after we pay for that property. Our right to salvage does not transfer your interest in property for which we have not paid.

**W.21** You must notify us if property for which we have paid is recovered. We may require you to return the payment for the recovered property or transfer the recovered property to us.

**W.22** We may make payment to you, to an insured, or to a person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment to a person having a valid interest in covered property may reduce the amount otherwise payable to you.

**W.23** We may make payment jointly to you and another person who has an interest in covered property. You must cooperate in obtaining any required endorsement or release.

**W.24** We may pay the reasonable cost of repair instead of replacement when repair restores the property to like kind and quality. We may select a reasonable method of repair.

**W.25** We may rely on "valuation evidence" obtained from contractors, suppliers, appraisers, or other qualified sources. We may consider conflicting valuation evidence in determining the amount of loss.

**W.26** We may require damaged property to be made available for examination by a qualified person selected by us. You must cooperate with that examination.

**W.27** We will not pay for the cost to replace "undamaged property" solely to obtain a uniform appearance. We may pay for direct physical loss to property that is actually damaged.

**W.28** We will not pay for the cost to correct defects, wear, deterioration, or inadequate maintenance that existed before the covered loss. We may pay for damage caused when a covered loss directly affects otherwise defective property.

**W.29** We will not pay for the cost of complying with an ordinance or law unless that cost is covered. We may separate the cost attributable to covered damage from the cost attributable to compliance.

**W.30** We will not pay for a loss that is based solely on reduced market value after repair or replacement. We will settle direct physical loss in accordance with the applicable valuation basis.

**W.31** We will not pay for loss caused by your failure to use reasonable means to preserve property after a covered loss. We may pay for the direct physical loss that existed before that failure.

**W.32** We may pay reasonable costs incurred to determine the extent of direct physical damage when those costs are necessary to settle a covered loss. We will not pay costs incurred solely to establish coverage where no covered loss exists.

**W.33** You must cooperate with us in separating covered damage from damage that is not covered. You must not conceal or misrepresent information material to the loss settlement.

**W.34** We may require a signed statement describing the loss and the property involved. The statement must be based on information known to you or reasonably available to you.

**W.35** We may require records showing the original cost, age, condition, repair history, and ownership of damaged property. The absence of records does not by itself establish the value of property.

**W.36** We may consider the cost of materials and labor reasonably necessary to restore damaged property. We may reject costs that are excessive, unrelated to the damage, or unsupported.

**W.37** We may settle a loss based on the cost to repair damaged property when repair is feasible. We may settle based on replacement when repair would not restore the property's intended function.

**W.38** We will not pay for costs incurred to improve property beyond its condition immediately before the covered loss. We may pay for reasonable work necessary to restore damaged property.

**W.39** We may reduce the amount of loss by the value of remaining usable property. We may consider whether materials, components, or portions of property can reasonably be retained.

**W.40** We may apply the applicable "deductible" to the amount of covered loss. The deductible applies before we determine the amount payable for that loss.

**W.41** We will not pay more than the amount of covered loss after application of the deductible and all applicable limits. A limit applicable to a class of property does not create additional coverage for another class of property.

**W.42** We may pay for repair or replacement performed by a contractor selected by you or by a contractor selected by us. We do not guarantee the work of a contractor who is not selected by us.

**W.43** You must promptly notify us if a contractor's estimate changes materially after work begins. We may review the change before making additional payment.

**W.44** We may withhold payment for work that has not been performed when the applicable settlement basis permits us to do so. We may require evidence that repair or replacement has been completed.

**W.45** We may make payment for completed work directly to a contractor when you authorize that payment. Such payment does not create a contractual relationship between us and the contractor.

**W.46** We may pay the value of damaged property rather than require repair or replacement. Payment does not require you to repair or replace the property.

**W.47** We will not pay for costs that you incur voluntarily without a reasonable relation to protecting, repairing, or replacing damaged property. We may pay reasonable emergency measures taken to prevent further damage.

**W.48** We may consider property available through customary sources when determining replacement cost. We are not required to use a source that is unavailable or unsuitable for the property involved.

**W.49** We may consider reasonable shipping, handling, delivery, and installation costs when they are necessary to repair or replace covered property. We will not pay charges that are unrelated to the covered loss.

**W.50** We may settle separately for different items of covered property. A dispute concerning one item does not prevent settlement of another item.

**W.51** You must identify any property that is subject to a lien, security interest, or other legal claim. We may protect the interest of a person entitled to payment from the loss proceeds.

**W.52** We may require an assignment of your rights against a person responsible for the loss to the extent we make payment. You must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.53** We may recover from a responsible person any amount we pay for a covered loss. Any recovery will be allocated in accordance with applicable law and your remaining interest in the loss.

**W.54** We may resolve a disagreement about the amount of loss through appraisal when permitted by the terms applicable to the loss. Appraisal determines the amount of loss and does not determine coverage.

**W.55** You must provide reasonably requested information to an appraiser or other person evaluating the amount of loss. You must not interfere with an inspection, valuation, or examination.

**W.56** We may reconsider a settlement if material information was unavailable when the settlement was made. We may adjust the settlement when the information establishes that the amount paid was incorrect.

**W.57** We will not pay an additional amount for property that was not identified until after settlement unless you provide satisfactory evidence of the property and its loss. We may require an opportunity to inspect any newly identified damaged property.

**W.58** We may settle a loss based on reasonable information available when you do not provide requested information. Our settlement does not waive our right to request additional information.

**W.59** We may require you to return any overpayment resulting from duplicate payment, inaccurate information, or recovery of property. You must cooperate in correcting an overpayment.

**W.60** We will determine the amount of loss without regard to sentimental value, personal preference, or special attachment to property. We will consider only the valuation basis applicable to the covered property.

**W.61** Our payment for a covered loss will not exceed the amount determined under these provisions and the applicable limits. Payment of a loss does not increase any limit available for another loss.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** "Earthquake" means a sudden movement or series of movements of the earth caused by natural geological forces. Earthquake includes land shock waves, tremors, and aftershocks.

**W.2** Earthquake includes shaking of the earth before, during, or after a volcanic eruption. Earthquake does not include volcanic action consisting solely of lava flow, ash, dust, or airborne shock waves.

**W.3** "Earth movement" means movement of earth, soil, rock, or other natural material at or below the surface of the ground. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mudslide, subsidence, sinkhole collapse, earth sinking, earth rising, and earth shifting.

**W.4** "Aftershock" means an earth tremor or land shock wave that follows an earthquake and arises from the same geological disturbance. An aftershock is part of the earthquake that caused it.

**W.5** "Land shock wave" means a wave of energy traveling through or across the earth. Land shock wave includes vibration transmitted through soil, rock, foundations, structures, or other property.

**W.6** "Tremor" means a shaking or vibrating movement of the earth. Tremor includes movement that is perceptible or imperceptible.

**W.7** "Volcanic eruption" means the release or discharge of volcanic material from a volcano or volcanic vent. Volcanic eruption includes associated earth movement.

**W.8** "Volcanic action" means direct loss caused by volcanic eruption, lava flow, ash, dust, or airborne shock waves. Volcanic action does not include earthquake unless earthquake coverage applies.

**W.9** "Landslide" means a downward or lateral movement of earth, soil, rock, or other material on a slope. Landslide includes movement caused by earthquake.

**W.10** "Mudslide" means a mass of mud, earth, rock, or other material moving downslope or across land. Mudslide includes material made fluid by water.

**W.11** "Subsidence" means the settling, sinking, or lowering of land. Subsidence includes movement caused by collapse of natural underground voids or movement of underlying earth.

**W.12** "Sinkhole collapse" means sudden settlement or collapse of earth into underground empty spaces created by the action of water on limestone or similar rock formations. Sinkhole collapse does not include settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, pavements, patios, or other property.

**W.13** "Earth sinking" means downward movement of land or other earth material. Earth sinking includes gradual or sudden movement.

**W.14** "Earth rising" means upward movement of land or other earth material. Earth rising includes heaving caused by geological forces.

**W.15** "Earth shifting" means horizontal, lateral, or angular movement of land or other earth material. Earth shifting includes displacement that damages a foundation or structure.

**W.16** "Direct physical loss" means accidental physical damage to tangible property. Direct physical loss includes loss of use only when caused by physical damage that we cover.

**W.17** "Property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of covered property. Property damage includes resulting loss of use of that property.

**W.18** "Covered property" means property for which coverage applies under this policy. Covered property does not include property excluded from coverage.

**W.19** "Damaged property" means covered property that sustains direct physical loss. Damaged property includes property requiring repair, replacement, stabilization, or removal because of such loss.

**W.20** "Structure" means a building or other construction permanently attached to land. Structure includes its foundation and attached fixtures.

**W.21** "Foundation" means the structural base that supports a building or other structure. Foundation includes footings, piers, slabs, foundation walls, and structural components attached to them.

**W.22** "Building" means a structure used principally for residential purposes. Building includes attached structures that are permanently connected to it.

**W.23** "Residence premises" means the residence described in the policy declarations and the land on which it is located. Residence premises includes structures and property used in connection with the residence.

**W.24** "Insured location" means a location where covered property is situated and for which this policy provides coverage. Insured location includes the residence premises.

**W.25** "Personal property" means movable property owned or used by an insured. Personal property includes property located at an insured location unless otherwise excluded.

**W.26** "Contents" means personal property located within a building or structure. Contents includes property attached to utility systems or equipment only while it remains movable property.

**W.27** "Additional living expense" means an increase in necessary living expenses incurred by an insured because a covered direct physical loss makes the residence premises unfit to live in. Additional living expense does not include expenses that would have been incurred without the loss.

**W.28** "Loss of use" means the inability to use covered property because of direct physical loss. Loss of use includes inability resulting from damage, destruction, or necessary repair.

**W.29** "Repair" means work reasonably necessary to restore damaged property to a condition substantially similar to its condition immediately before the loss. Repair includes stabilization required to perform restoration work.

**W.30** "Replacement" means substitution of property for damaged property. Replacement includes installation of replacement property when necessary to restore covered property.

**W.31** "Stabilization" means temporary or permanent work reasonably necessary to prevent further direct physical loss to damaged property. Stabilization includes shoring, bracing, securing, and protecting damaged property.

**W.32** "Collapse" means an abrupt falling down or caving in of a building or structure, or a substantial part of it. Collapse does not include settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion.

**W.33** "Crack" means a separation, fracture, or fissure in property. Crack includes a condition affecting a foundation, wall, floor, roof, pavement, or other structural component.

**W.34** "Settlement" means downward movement of a building, structure, foundation, or land. Settlement includes resulting cracking or separation.

**W.35** "Code" means a law, ordinance, regulation, or governmental requirement relating to the construction, repair, replacement, demolition, occupancy, or use of property. Code includes requirements imposed by a governmental authority.

**W.36** "Governmental authority" means a public agency, department, board, commission, or official having legal authority over property or its use. Governmental authority includes an entity acting under delegated public authority.

**W.37** "Occurrence" means an earthquake and all resulting earth movement arising from the same geological disturbance. Occurrence includes related aftershocks and land shock waves.
