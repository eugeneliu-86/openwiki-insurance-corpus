# HO 04 48 Other Structures — Increased Limits (Edition 2026-06)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement changes the insurance applicable to covered property described as "Other Structures." The changed insurance applies only when this endorsement is made part of your insurance.

The limit provided by this endorsement applies to covered direct physical loss to "Other Structures." The limit replaces the limit otherwise applicable to that property.

The increased limit applies subject to all applicable terms, conditions, exclusions, and duties. An insured must comply with those duties as a condition of coverage.

We do not cover loss that is not otherwise covered. We do not cover loss in excess of the limit applicable under this endorsement.

This endorsement does not change the property to which coverage applies. This endorsement does not create coverage for property that is not covered.

If there is a conflict between this endorsement and any other applicable provision, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. All other applicable provisions remain unchanged.

The insurance provided by this endorsement applies only to loss occurring while the endorsement is in effect. An insured must have an insurable interest in the damaged property at the time of loss.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide insurance under "Coverage B" for "other structures" at the "residence premises" that are owned by you or for which you are legally responsible. This coverage applies only to other structures that are used in connection with the residence premises.

**W.2** The "limit of liability" applicable to Coverage B is increased under this endorsement. The increased limit of liability applies to covered loss involving other structures.

**W.3** We cover "direct physical loss" to covered other structures caused by a covered peril. We pay only for loss that occurs during the applicable coverage period.

**W.4** "Covered property" includes an other structure permanently located at the residence premises. Covered property also includes materials and supplies located at the residence premises and intended for use in constructing, altering, or repairing an other structure.

**W.5** We cover an other structure located at an "insured location" when that structure is used solely in connection with the residence premises. The structure must be owned by you or be your responsibility under a written agreement.

**W.6** We do not cover an other structure used in whole or in part for "business" purposes. This exclusion does not apply when the structure is used solely to store property used by an insured for business purposes.

**W.7** We do not cover an other structure held for "rental" to a person who is not an insured. This exclusion does not apply when the rental use is incidental to the use of the residence premises as a private residence.

**W.8** We do not cover an other structure used for "farming" or agricultural operations. We also do not cover property used to support farming or agricultural operations.

**W.9** We do not cover property that is excluded from coverage by reason of its use, condition, or location. The increased limit of liability does not change any exclusion applicable to that property.

**W.10** We cover loss to an other structure caused by "collapse" only when collapse is covered under this endorsement. We do not cover loss consisting only of settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion.

**W.11** We cover reasonable repairs necessary to protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect the property from further damage.

**W.12** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine records relating to the loss.

**W.13** An insured must provide us with a prompt notice of loss. The notice must identify the property involved and describe the known circumstances of the loss.

**W.14** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when we request it. The inventory must identify the property, its condition, and any available supporting documentation.

**W.15** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request to investigate the loss.

**W.16** We may inspect the damaged property before repair, removal, or disposal. An insured must preserve evidence of the loss when reasonably possible.

**W.17** We may repair, rebuild, or replace covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may make payment for covered loss instead of repairing, rebuilding, or replacing the property.

**W.18** We do not pay for loss to property that an insured has intentionally concealed, misrepresented, or abandoned. We do not pay for loss resulting from a material misrepresentation made in connection with a claim.

**W.19** We pay only the amount of covered loss that is actually sustained. We do not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**W.20** The applicable limit of liability is the most we will pay for covered loss under Coverage B. The increased limit of liability does not create additional coverage for property that is not covered property.

**W.21** We do not combine the Coverage B limit of liability with any other limit of liability. Each applicable limit of liability applies separately to the coverage for which it is stated.

**W.22** We do not pay for loss that is payable under another source of insurance covering the same property. We pay our share of covered loss when other insurance applies.

**W.23** We may require an insured to assign to us rights of recovery against a person responsible for covered loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.24** We may recover from a person responsible for a loss after we make payment for that loss. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing our rights of recovery.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion applies whether the neglect occurs before or after we are notified of the loss.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or rust.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, or maintenance. We do cover ensuing direct physical loss caused by a covered peril unless that loss is otherwise excluded.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. We do not cover loss caused by wear, deterioration, corrosion, or decay of a plumbing system.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril when the loss is not otherwise excluded.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water acts on or flows, seeps, or leaks through any part of the structure.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies whether driven by wind or otherwise.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, overflow, or backup of water or sewage from a sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, or related equipment. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by power failure that occurs away from the residence premises. We do cover ensuing direct physical loss caused by a covered peril unless that loss is otherwise excluded.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance, law, or regulation requiring demolition, construction, repair, replacement, or removal of property. We do not cover loss resulting from enforcement of an ordinance, law, or regulation.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or warlike action. We do not cover loss caused by discharge of a nuclear weapon.

**W.37** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.38** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. Intentional loss means any loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss.

**W.39** We do not cover loss arising out of dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, or malicious acts by an insured. This exclusion applies whether an insured acts alone or in concert with another person.

**W.40** We do not cover loss to an other structure that is vacant and subject to an increased hazard. An insured must maintain the property in a condition that does not materially increase the risk of loss.

**W.41** We do not cover loss occurring after transfer of ownership of covered property unless an insured retains an insurable interest in the property. An insured must notify us of a material change in ownership or occupancy.

**W.42** We may adjust a covered loss with an insured who has an interest in the damaged property. Payment to that insured discharges our obligation to the extent of the payment.

**W.43** We may make payment jointly to persons having an interest in covered property. An insured must provide information concerning any person having an interest in the property.

**W.44** We may deny coverage when an insured fails to comply with a duty imposed by this endorsement and the failure prejudices our rights. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim.

**W.45** We do not cover a loss that is excluded regardless of the increased limit of liability. The increased limit of liability applies only after coverage for the loss has been established.

**W.46** The increased limit of liability applies to covered other structures without changing the character of Coverage B. All terms of this endorsement apply to the increased limit of liability.

**W.47** We determine the amount of covered loss based on the damaged property, the cause of loss, and the applicable valuation provisions. We may require reasonable documentation supporting the claimed amount of loss.

**W.48** An insured must allow us access to the residence premises and other structures when reasonably necessary to investigate a claim. An insured must provide reasonable assistance in identifying damaged property and determining the cause of loss.

**W.49** We provide no coverage beyond the increased limit of liability applicable to Coverage B. Our payment for covered loss is subject to all applicable duties, exclusions, and conditions of this endorsement.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We apply this provision to "Coverage B" for "Other Structures". The applicable limit of liability is subject to all terms that apply to Coverage B.

**W.2** The limit of liability for Coverage B is twenty percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". This limit applies to covered direct physical loss to Other Structures.

**W.3** The Coverage B limit of liability applies separately from the Coverage A limit of liability. Payment under Coverage B does not reduce the limit of liability for Coverage A.

**W.4** The Coverage B limit of liability is the most we will pay for all covered loss arising from any occurrence. This applies regardless of the number of insureds, claimants, damaged structures, or causes of loss.

**W.5** We do not increase the Coverage B limit of liability because covered property is owned by more than one insured. We do not increase that limit because more than one interest exists in covered property.

**W.6** We do not pay more than the Coverage B limit of liability for loss to any combination of Other Structures. This applies whether the loss is settled on a replacement cost basis or an actual cash value basis.

**W.7** The Coverage B limit of liability includes the cost to repair, replace, or rebuild covered property. It also includes debris removal and any other covered expense payable under Coverage B.

**W.8** We apply the Coverage B limit of liability before applying any deductible. Your deductible remains applicable to covered loss as otherwise provided.

**W.9** A payment for emergency measures is subject to the Coverage B limit of liability. Such payment does not create additional coverage or increase that limit.

**W.10** A payment for reasonable repairs is subject to the Coverage B limit of liability. Such payment does not preserve or restore any portion of that limit.

**W.11** We do not pay more than the Coverage B limit of liability for covered loss caused by a covered peril. The existence of concurrent or repeated damage does not increase that limit.

**W.12** We apply the Coverage B limit of liability to property that qualifies as an Other Structure at the time of loss. The use, occupancy, or location of the structure must otherwise satisfy the applicable coverage conditions.

**W.13** We do not cover loss to property that does not qualify for Coverage B. The Coverage B limit of liability does not extend coverage to excluded property.

**W.14** We do not cover loss that is excluded under the applicable coverage terms. The Coverage B limit of liability does not modify an exclusion, condition, or limitation.

**W.15** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of covered loss. An insured must provide information and records we reasonably request concerning the damaged property.

**W.16** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Expenses incurred to protect property are payable only to the extent coverage applies.

**W.17** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property as reasonably required. An insured must retain damaged property for our examination when reasonably requested.

**W.18** We may pay the value of covered loss, repair or replace damaged property, or take other action permitted by the applicable coverage terms. Any payment remains subject to the Coverage B limit of liability.

**W.19** Our payment does not transfer ownership of damaged property to us unless we agree otherwise. We may take possession of damaged property for inspection, preservation, or disposition.

**W.20** We do not pay for any loss that you intentionally conceal or misrepresent. The Coverage B limit of liability does not apply to fraudulent claims.

**W.21** An insured must not abandon property to us. We may require an insured to retain property until we complete our investigation.

**W.22** Any recovery from another party for covered loss must be held for us to the extent of our payment. Our rights of recovery do not increase the Coverage B limit of liability.

**W.23** The Coverage B limit of liability applies to loss occurring during the applicable policy term. Coverage applies only when the loss otherwise satisfies all applicable conditions.

**W.24** This provision governs the Coverage B limit of liability. All other applicable terms remain unchanged.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** "Deductible" means the portion of a "covered loss" that you must bear before we make payment. "Covered loss" means loss for which coverage is provided under this coverage.

**W.2** The applicable deductible applies to covered loss involving an "other structure". The increased limit does not create a separate deductible.

**W.3** We determine the amount of covered loss before applying the deductible. We then subtract the applicable deductible from the amount otherwise payable.

**W.4** An insured must bear the applicable deductible. We will not pay the portion of covered loss that is within the deductible.

**W.5** We do not cover covered loss that does not exceed the applicable deductible. No payment is owed for that loss.

**W.6** When covered loss exceeds the applicable deductible, we will pay the amount exceeding the deductible. Our payment remains subject to the applicable "limit of liability".

**W.7** The deductible does not increase the limit of liability. The deductible is not payable in addition to the limit of liability.

**W.8** An insured must provide information we reasonably need to determine the amount of covered loss. An insured must also provide information we reasonably need to apply the deductible.

**W.9** An insured must not divide a covered loss into separate claims to avoid or reduce the deductible. We apply the deductible to the loss as determined by us.

**W.10** Repair, replacement, or nonreplacement of damaged property does not eliminate the deductible. The deductible applies whether or not an insured completes repairs.

**W.11** We do not cover expenses incurred solely to satisfy the deductible. Such expenses do not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.12** Our investigation, adjustment, or discussion of a claim does not waive the deductible. A waiver is effective only when we state it in writing.

**W.13** An insured must not assign the right to avoid payment of the deductible. The deductible remains applicable after an assignment of an interest in damaged property.

**W.14** Payment of a claim does not relieve an insured of responsibility for the deductible. We may deduct the applicable deductible from any amount otherwise payable.

**W.15** An insured must cooperate with us in resolving any question concerning the deductible. Cooperation includes providing records concerning the damaged other structure and the loss.

**W.16** We apply the deductible without regard to any payment available from another source. A recovery available to an insured does not reduce the deductible unless we agree in writing.

**W.17** An insured must hold any recovery relating to covered loss for proper allocation. We may consider that recovery in determining the amount we owe.

**W.18** The deductible applies before we make payment for covered loss. The deductible does not create coverage for loss that is not covered.

**W.19** An insured must not abandon damaged property to us to avoid the deductible. We do not accept abandoned property merely because a deductible applies.

**W.20** The deductible remains applicable to any settlement of covered loss. No settlement changes the deductible unless we agree in writing.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss to an "other structure" unless the loss is covered by the policy to which this endorsement applies. This endorsement does not create coverage for property that is not an other structure.

**W.2** We do not cover loss arising from any cause of loss that is excluded by the policy. The "increased limit" does not delete, narrow, or modify an exclusion.

**W.3** We do not cover loss to property that is excluded from coverage under the policy. The increased limit applies only after all applicable coverage requirements have been met.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by flood or surface water.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through an other structure.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sumps, or related equipment. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the water originates on or away from the residence premises.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a body of water. This exclusion applies to tidal water, waves, spray, and water driven by wind.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mudslide, subsidence, sinkhole collapse, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion. This exclusion applies to foundations, walls, floors, roofs, and other structural components.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. This exclusion applies even when the condition causes damage to an other structure.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or decay. We do not cover loss caused by contamination or smog.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, discharge, secretion, or waste.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether the work is performed by you or by another person.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of an ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, demolition, or use of property. We do not cover the increased cost of complying with an ordinance or law.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action. Governmental action includes seizure, confiscation, destruction, or disposal of property by a governmental authority.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or warlike action by a military force.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, and radioactive contamination.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. An "insured" must not cause or arrange for loss to property with the intent to cause a loss.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. This exclusion applies only to loss caused by that neglect.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the residence premises. This exclusion applies unless the power failure results in a covered direct physical loss to property on the residence premises.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a utility service. Utility service includes water, communication, power, heating, cooling, or related service supplied from away from the residence premises.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. Pollutants include solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, or biological irritants and contaminants.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We do not cover loss caused by emissions from a manufacturing, processing, or commercial operation.

**W.24** We do not cover loss to property used primarily for business purposes. This exclusion applies to an other structure used for business storage, manufacturing, processing, repair, sale, or service.

**W.25** We do not cover loss to property rented or held for rental to others. This exclusion does not depend on whether rent is actually received.

**W.26** We do not cover loss to property used in connection with farming. Farming includes raising, harvesting, storing, processing, or caring for agricultural products or livestock.

**W.27** We do not cover loss to an other structure used to board, breed, train, or keep animals for commercial purposes. This exclusion applies whether the activity is conducted by you or another person.

**W.28** We do not cover loss to property while it is vacant if vacancy increases the hazard of loss. Vacancy includes the absence of customary care, maintenance, or protective measures.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of ice, snow, or sleet when the loss is otherwise excluded. The increased limit does not expand coverage for resulting damage.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by collapse unless collapse is covered by the policy. The increased limit does not make collapse a covered cause of loss.

**W.31** We do not cover loss consisting solely of cosmetic damage. Cosmetic damage includes damage that does not impair the function of the damaged property.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by defective materials or latent defect. This exclusion applies whether the defect is known or unknown to an insured.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by repeated leakage or seepage of water or steam. This exclusion applies when the leakage or seepage occurs over time from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance equipment.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by discharge from a sump, sump pump, or related system when the discharge is excluded by the policy. The increased limit does not alter any limitation applicable to that loss.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by acts or decisions of any person, group, organization, or governmental body if the loss is otherwise excluded. We do not cover loss caused by a failure to act when the failure is otherwise excluded.

**W.36** We do not cover any deductible applicable to a covered loss. The hurricane deductible floor remains applicable if it applies to the loss.

**W.37** We do not cover property otherwise subject to a special limit, restriction, condition, or exclusion. The Coverage C percentage does not increase coverage for an other structure.

**W.38** We do not cover loss for which the Coverage D limit as a percentage of Coverage A applies. The increased limit does not change that limitation.

**W.39** We do not cover debris removal that is otherwise excluded. The rubble removal percentage does not increase coverage for excluded debris removal.

**W.40** We do not cover loss for which another insurance policy, service agreement, warranty, or indemnity agreement provides no coverage because of an exclusion. This endorsement does not convert excluded loss into covered loss.

**W.41** We do not cover any expense, charge, assessment, fine, penalty, or fee unless the policy provides coverage for that item. The increased limit applies only to covered loss.

**W.42** We do not cover loss that is excluded regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss. All exclusions remain applicable notwithstanding the increased limit.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** The "increased limit" applies only to a "covered other structure" located on the "residence premises" and otherwise eligible for coverage. You must have an insurable interest in the covered other structure at the time of loss.

**W.2** You must provide complete and accurate information concerning the covered other structure, its construction, its use, and its value. We may rely on information you provide in determining whether the increased limit applies.

**W.3** You must notify us of a material change affecting the covered other structure. A material change includes a change in ownership, occupancy, use, condition, or exposure to loss.

**W.4** An insured must notify us if the covered other structure becomes vacant, unoccupied, condemned, or subject to a governmental order affecting its use. You must take reasonable steps to protect the covered other structure from further damage.

**W.5** An "insured" must report a "loss" to us within sixty days after the loss. Notice must identify the covered other structure, the location of the loss, and the known circumstances of the loss.

**W.6** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide information and assistance we reasonably request.

**W.7** You must protect damaged property from further loss. You must keep an accurate record of reasonable expenses incurred to protect damaged property.

**W.8** An insured must permit us to inspect the covered other structure and any damaged property. An insured must permit us to inspect records and other information reasonably related to the claim.

**W.9** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless preservation is not reasonably possible. An insured must preserve evidence of the cause and extent of loss when reasonably able to do so.

**W.10** An insured must provide photographs, videos, receipts, invoices, estimates, contracts, and other records that reasonably support the claim. You must provide records in your possession, custody, or control.

**W.11** An insured must submit a signed "proof of loss" within the "proof of loss period." The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary to determine the amount and extent of loss.

**W.12** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request. An insured must produce requested records for examination and copying.

**W.13** The "minimum Section I deductible" applies to a loss subject to the increased limit. You are responsible for the applicable deductible before we make payment for covered loss.

**W.14** The "eighty percent condition" applies when required by the applicable valuation method. An insured must provide information necessary to determine whether that condition has been met.

**W.15** The "legal action deadline" applies to any action brought against us concerning a loss. No action may be maintained unless all applicable policy duties have been met.

**W.16** You must use reasonable means to preserve the covered other structure in sound condition. You must make reasonable repairs necessary to prevent avoidable deterioration or damage.

**W.17** You must comply with applicable laws, ordinances, codes, permits, and governmental requirements affecting the covered other structure. Compliance with those requirements does not create coverage that is otherwise unavailable.

**W.18** You must obtain and maintain any permit required for construction, alteration, repair, or use of the covered other structure. You must promptly provide a copy of a permit when we reasonably request it.

**W.19** You must notify us of any substantial alteration to the covered other structure. An alteration includes a change affecting construction, materials, occupancy, use, or replacement value.

**W.20** You must notify us if the covered other structure is used for business, rental, agricultural, manufacturing, storage, or other purposes that increase the possibility or severity of loss. We may require information concerning that use.

**W.21** You must notify us if the covered other structure contains property owned by another person. The presence of property owned by another person does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.22** You must notify us if title to the covered other structure is transferred. The increased limit does not automatically continue for a person who acquires an interest in the covered other structure.

**W.23** You must notify us if a mortgage, lien, lease, easement, or other interest materially affects the covered other structure. We may request documents identifying the interest and the person holding it.

**W.24** You must not intentionally conceal or misrepresent a material fact or circumstance relating to this insurance, the covered other structure, or a claim. We do not cover loss when an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance relating to that loss.

**W.25** You must not intentionally cause or arrange a loss. We do not cover loss caused by an insured acting with intent to cause loss.

**W.26** You must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment, but we are not required to do so.

**W.27** You must not repair, replace, dispose of, or alter damaged property before our inspection if doing so would materially impair our ability to determine the cause or amount of loss. This duty does not prevent reasonable emergency measures necessary to protect property from further damage.

**W.28** You must give us access to the covered other structure when we reasonably request access for inspection, investigation, valuation, or adjustment. An insured must arrange access that is lawful and reasonably safe.

**W.29** You must provide a written statement describing the ownership, use, construction, and condition of the covered other structure when we reasonably request it. The statement must be accurate to the best of the insured's knowledge.

**W.30** You must provide records supporting the age, cost, maintenance, alteration, and repair of the covered other structure when reasonably available. We may consider the absence of reasonably available records in evaluating a claim.

**W.31** You must notify us of any claim, demand, suit, or governmental proceeding arising from damage to the covered other structure. An insured must promptly send us copies of papers received in connection with that matter.

**W.32** You must cooperate with us in preserving our rights against a person or organization that may be responsible for a loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to prejudice those rights.

**W.33** If we make payment for a loss, we may pursue recovery to the extent of our payment from a person or organization responsible for the loss. You must transfer to us any rights of recovery that are necessary to protect our interest.

**W.34** You must not waive, settle, release, or impair a claim against a person or organization responsible for a loss without our written consent. This condition does not apply when the waiver or release is required by law.

**W.35** You must permit us to inspect records concerning repairs, replacement, or rebuilding for which payment is sought. An insured must provide invoices, contracts, canceled payments, and other evidence of incurred costs when reasonably requested.

**W.36** You must notify us if the covered other structure is damaged by a prior loss that has not been repaired. We may consider unrepaired damage in determining the amount of covered loss.

**W.37** You must notify us if the covered other structure has sustained repeated water damage, fire damage, wind damage, collapse, or other material damage. You must provide information reasonably available concerning the cause and repair of that damage.

**W.38** You must maintain protective devices and safeguards that you represented were present at the covered other structure. If a protective device or safeguard is removed, disabled, or fails to operate, you must notify us when reasonably practical.

**W.39** You must use reasonable care to prevent freezing, leakage, seepage, overflow, and other water-related damage. You must take reasonable measures to maintain plumbing, heating, drainage, and related systems serving the covered other structure.

**W.40** A backwater valve is not required where no such device is required by applicable law or ordinance. If a backwater valve is installed, you must maintain it in working condition.

**W.41** You must use reasonable care to prevent fire damage, including maintaining electrical systems, heating equipment, fuel systems, and flammable materials in a reasonably safe condition. You must comply with applicable fire-safety requirements.

**W.42** You must maintain the roof, walls, foundation, doors, windows, and exterior openings of the covered other structure in a reasonably weather-resistant condition. You must correct conditions that materially increase the possibility of damage when reasonably able to do so.

**W.43** You must notify us if the covered other structure is moved from the residence premises. We do not cover loss to a covered other structure after it is moved unless coverage otherwise applies.

**W.44** You must notify us if the covered other structure is converted to living quarters or is used for sleeping purposes. We may request information concerning the conversion or use.

**W.45** You must notify us if the covered other structure is used to store hazardous, combustible, explosive, corrosive, or unlawful materials. We do not cover loss resulting from a materially increased hazard that you knowingly created or permitted.

**W.46** You must disclose any known defect, deterioration, structural weakness, or unsafe condition affecting the covered other structure. We may require reasonable evidence concerning the condition and repair of that condition.

**W.47** You must provide us with information concerning any insurance that may apply to the same loss. An insured must cooperate in determining the rights and obligations of all insurers.

**W.48** Payment under the increased limit does not transfer ownership of damaged property to us. Our payment does not waive any condition, exclusion, or limitation that applies to the claim.

**W.49** Our investigation of a loss, inspection of property, or request for information does not waive any policy condition or defense. We may reserve our rights while investigating or adjusting a claim.

**W.50** No insured may assign rights or duties under the increased limit without our written consent, except as permitted by law. An assignment made without our written consent does not bind us.

**W.51** A request for payment must be made in good faith. An insured must not submit a claim for property, damage, expense, or loss that the insured knows is not covered.

**W.52** All duties imposed on an insured apply to a person seeking payment under the increased limit. Failure to comply with a duty may affect coverage when the failure materially prejudices our ability to investigate, adjust, settle, or defend a claim.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** The "increased limit" applies only to an "other structure" that is located at an insured location. An insured must have an insurable interest in the other structure at the time of loss.

**W.2** An insured must accurately identify the other structure for which the increased limit is requested. An insured must provide information we request concerning the structure's location, construction, use, and condition.

**W.3** An insured must promptly notify us of a material change in the ownership, occupancy, use, or condition of the other structure. We may revise our underwriting determination based on information you provide.

**W.4** An insured must maintain the other structure in a condition that reasonably protects it from foreseeable damage. An insured must repair known deterioration, defects, and damage that could increase the likelihood or severity of loss.

**W.5** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect the other structure from freezing, water damage, wind damage, fire, and vandalism. An insured must use reasonable care in securing the structure when it is unattended.

**W.6** An insured must comply with applicable laws, ordinances, codes, and regulations affecting the other structure. We do not cover loss caused by your failure to comply with a requirement imposed by a governmental authority when that failure contributes to the loss.

**W.7** An insured must obtain and maintain permits required for construction, alteration, repair, or use of the other structure. An insured must provide permit information to us upon request.

**W.8** An insured must notify us before beginning a material alteration, addition, demolition, or reconstruction involving the other structure. We may require information concerning the planned work before continuing the increased limit.

**W.9** An insured must ensure that work performed on the other structure is completed in a workmanlike manner. An insured must take reasonable steps to verify that contractors performing work are properly qualified and authorized.

**W.10** An insured must keep the other structure reasonably protected during construction, renovation, repair, or remodeling. We do not cover loss caused by your failure to secure unfinished openings, exposed materials, or unsecured equipment when that failure contributes to the loss.

**W.11** An insured must maintain utilities serving the other structure in safe operating condition. An insured must promptly address a known defect in wiring, plumbing, heating, fuel supply, or other utility equipment.

**W.12** An insured must maintain reasonable access to the other structure for inspection, repair, and emergency response. An insured must not obstruct access needed to protect the structure from loss.

**W.13** An insured must notify us when the other structure is used for a purpose materially different from its stated use. We may determine that the increased limit does not apply to loss arising from an undisclosed use.

**W.14** An insured must not use the other structure for an unlawful purpose. We do not cover loss arising from an unlawful use of the other structure by an insured.

**W.15** An insured must not use the other structure primarily for business activities unless we agree to that use. An insured must provide information we request concerning any business activity conducted at or from the structure.

**W.16** An insured must promptly notify us if the other structure is rented, leased, or otherwise made available to others for occupancy or use. We may require additional underwriting information concerning that occupancy or use.

**W.17** An insured must promptly notify us if the other structure becomes vacant or unoccupied. An insured must inspect and secure the structure as reasonably necessary to prevent damage while it is vacant or unoccupied.

**W.18** An insured must maintain reasonable safeguards against unauthorized entry into the other structure. An insured must repair or replace damaged locks, doors, windows, and other protective devices when reasonably necessary.

**W.19** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent accumulation of combustible materials in or near the other structure. An insured must safely store fuels, chemicals, and other hazardous materials.

**W.20** An insured must maintain any fire protection equipment serving the other structure in operable condition. An insured must promptly correct a known condition that impairs available fire protection.

**W.21** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect the roof, walls, foundation, and exterior openings of the other structure from water intrusion. An insured must promptly investigate and address a known leak or condition that could permit water intrusion.

**W.22** An insured must maintain drainage around the other structure in a condition that reasonably directs water away from the structure. An insured must promptly correct a known drainage condition that increases the likelihood of water damage.

**W.23** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect pipes, plumbing fixtures, and water-connected equipment from freezing. An insured must maintain heat, drain water systems, or use another reasonable protective measure when freezing conditions are likely.

**W.24** An insured must notify us promptly of a "loss" involving the other structure. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for us to investigate the loss.

**W.25** An insured must protect the other structure from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to prevent additional damage.

**W.26** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard, alter, or dispose of damaged property before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, except when necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.27** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must provide records, photographs, receipts, estimates, contracts, and other information we reasonably request.

**W.28** An insured must permit us to inspect the other structure and damaged property as reasonably necessary. An insured must provide reasonable access to the insured location for that inspection.

**W.29** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we request it. An insured must sign and provide a recorded or written statement when we reasonably request it.

**W.30** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning the other structure, the increased limit, or a loss. We do not cover a loss when an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact relating to that loss.

**W.31** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting, supervising, and paying persons who perform work on the other structure. An insured must preserve contracts, invoices, and proof of payment relating to repairs or improvements when relevant to a claim.

**W.32** An insured must notify us of a dispute, demand, suit, or governmental action involving the other structure when it may affect a claim. An insured must promptly send us legal papers and notices relating to that matter.

**W.33** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume an obligation, or incur expense concerning a loss without our consent, except for reasonable emergency measures. This duty does not prevent you from taking reasonable action to protect persons or property from further damage.

**W.34** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve rights of recovery against a person responsible for loss to the other structure. An insured must not waive those rights after a loss without our consent.

**W.35** An insured must notify us if another policy, contract, warranty, or agreement may provide coverage for the loss. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning that other coverage.

**W.36** We do not cover loss arising from a condition that existed before the increased limit became applicable when an insured knew of that condition and failed to disclose it to us. This exclusion applies only when the undisclosed condition materially affected the risk of loss.

**W.37** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts of an insured when the act is committed with intent to cause loss. This exclusion applies regardless of any increased limit applicable to the other structure.

**W.38** The increased limit does not alter an insured's duties after loss or our rights to investigate, inspect, adjust, settle, or deny a claim. An insured must satisfy all applicable duties before we determine the amount payable for a covered loss.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" to property insured under this endorsement in accordance with these provisions. Settlement is subject to the applicable terms, conditions, and exclusions of this policy.

**W.2** We will determine the amount of loss after considering the property damaged or destroyed by a covered peril. We may inspect the property and require reasonable information concerning the loss.

**W.3** We will not pay more than your financial interest in the damaged property. We will not pay more than the amount for which you are legally responsible.

**W.4** We may consider the reasonable cost to repair, restore, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may consider the availability of materials, labor, and comparable property.

**W.5** We will not be obligated to pay for betterment, enlargement, or improvement unless required by the applicable loss settlement basis. A repair or replacement must reasonably restore the property that was damaged.

**W.6** We may elect to repair, restore, or replace damaged property instead of making a cash payment. If we elect that option, we will provide written notice of our election.

**W.7** We will settle covered loss to personal property on the following basis: replacement cost.

**W.8** We will settle loss to an "other structure" based upon the property damaged by the covered loss. We will not include the value of land in determining the amount of loss.

**W.9** When damaged property is part of a pair, set, or collection, we may determine the loss by the reasonable reduction in value of the pair, set, or collection. We may also elect to repair or replace the affected property.

**W.10** We will not pay for loss to undamaged property solely because it differs in appearance from repaired or replaced property. We may pay for work reasonably necessary to complete a covered repair.

**W.11** We will not pay for loss caused by delay, loss of use, or interruption of use unless that loss is otherwise covered. A payment under these provisions does not create coverage for an excluded loss.

**W.12** We may use materials, methods, and workmanship reasonably comparable to those customarily used for the damaged property. We are not required to use materials that are no longer reasonably available.

**W.13** You must protect damaged property from further loss after a covered loss. We will pay the reasonable cost you incur to protect covered property when that cost is necessary to prevent further covered damage.

**W.14** You must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. You must not dispose of damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.15** You must provide records, receipts, estimates, photographs, and other information we reasonably request to determine the amount of loss. You must cooperate with us in obtaining information relevant to settlement.

**W.16** We may obtain estimates, appraisals, or other evaluations of damaged property. We may consider information obtained from qualified contractors, suppliers, or valuation sources.

**W.17** We may take all or part of damaged property at its agreed or appraised value after payment for the loss. Our exercise of that right does not reduce your duty to preserve property for our inspection.

**W.18** If property for which we have paid is recovered, you must notify us promptly. We will determine the rights of all interested persons in the recovered property.

**W.19** We may make payment to you and any person or organization having a legal interest in the property. Payment made in good faith discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.20** We will not pay the same loss more than once. We may reduce our payment by amounts recoverable from another source for the same covered loss.

**W.21** The applicable limit for an other structure is the most we will pay for covered loss to that property. The applicable limit does not increase because repair or replacement costs exceed that limit.

**W.22** The "assessment coverage limit" does not govern settlement of loss to property under this endorsement. Any assessment claim will be considered under the coverage that applies to that claim.

**W.23** A "fire response charge" does not determine the amount of loss to damaged property. We will consider that charge only if coverage applies to it.

**W.24** The "special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones" applies only to property and loss for which that limitation applies. It does not establish the settlement basis for other covered property.

**W.25** The "home business contents limit" applies only when the damaged property is subject to that limitation. It does not alter the settlement of property that is not subject to that limitation.

**W.26** We may require that repairs or replacement be completed in a manner consistent with applicable laws and building requirements. We do not cover costs arising solely from a requirement that is not covered by this policy.

**W.27** Any disagreement concerning the amount of loss may be resolved in accordance with the policy provisions that apply to such disagreement. Our payment of an undisputed amount does not waive our rights concerning a disputed amount.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** The words "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the Declarations. The words "we", "us", and "our" refer to the company providing this insurance.

**W.2** The word "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives. It also includes persons under your care who are residents of your household.

**W.3** The term "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and that is shown in the Declarations. It includes the land and structures used principally as a private residence.

**W.4** The term "other structure" means a structure that is separated from the dwelling by clear space or is connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. An other structure includes its permanently installed fixtures.

**W.5** A structure is "separated" when it does not share a common wall, roof, foundation, or enclosed area with the dwelling. A covered walkway does not, by itself, make a structure part of the dwelling.

**W.6** The term "dwelling" means the building described as the residence premises. It includes structures attached to that building.

**W.7** The term "property" means tangible property. Property does not include an interest in land unless we state otherwise.

**W.8** The term "building" means a structure with walls and a roof. A building includes materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises for use in construction, alteration, or repair.

**W.9** The term "fixtures" means property permanently attached to a building or structure. Fixtures include equipment installed for the service of that building or structure.

**W.10** The term "land" means the ground forming part of the residence premises. Land includes soil, grading, landscaping, and naturally occurring vegetation.

**W.11** The term "private structure" means an other structure used primarily for your personal, family, or household purposes. A private structure may be used incidentally for a permitted business use.

**W.12** The term "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or activity engaged in for economic gain. Business includes activities conducted on a full-time, part-time, seasonal, or occasional basis.

**W.13** The term "business property" means property used principally in connection with a business. Business property includes property held for sale, lease, rental, or service.

**W.14** The term "rental" means the granting of possession or use of property to another person in exchange for compensation. Rental includes a lease, license, or similar arrangement.

**W.15** The term "compensation" means money, services, goods, or other consideration received or expected in exchange for property or activity. Compensation includes reimbursement beyond ordinary shared expenses.

**W.16** The term "repair" means work performed to restore property to its condition immediately before direct physical loss. Repair includes replacement of damaged materials with like kind and quality materials.

**W.17** The term "replacement" means substitution of damaged property with property serving the same general purpose. Replacement includes reconstruction of a damaged structure.

**W.18** The term "reconstruction" means rebuilding all or part of a damaged structure. Reconstruction includes work necessary to restore the structure after a covered loss.

**W.19** The term "alteration" means a change to the physical characteristics, design, layout, or use of property. Alteration includes an addition or improvement.

**W.20** The term "improvement" means a permanent change that increases the usefulness, value, efficiency, or desirability of property. Improvement does not include ordinary maintenance.

**W.21** The term "maintenance" means work performed to preserve property in its existing condition or to prevent ordinary deterioration. Maintenance includes routine cleaning and servicing.

**W.22** The term "direct physical loss" means accidental physical damage to tangible property. Direct physical loss includes loss of use only when that loss results from physical damage covered by this policy.

**W.23** The term "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence must result in property damage during the policy period.

**W.24** The term "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. Property damage includes resulting loss of use of that property.

**W.25** The term "vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary for normal use or occupancy. Property undergoing construction, renovation, or repair may be vacant.

**W.26** The term "unoccupied" means not being used as a regular place of residence or customary activity. Unoccupied does not necessarily mean vacant.

**W.27** The term "covered loss" means direct physical loss for which coverage applies under this policy. A covered loss is subject to all applicable terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits.

**W.28** The term "limit of liability" means the most we will pay for covered loss under an applicable coverage. A limit of liability does not increase because more than one insured has an interest in the property.

**W.29** The term "deductible" means the amount you must bear from a covered loss before we pay. The deductible applies as stated in the applicable coverage.

**W.30** The term "actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss, including deduction for depreciation. Actual cash value does not include sentimental or personal value.

**W.31** The term "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. Replacement cost is subject to applicable conditions and limits.

**W.32** The term "depreciation" means a decrease in value caused by age, wear, deterioration, or obsolescence. Depreciation may apply to materials, labor, or both where permitted by law.

**W.33** The term "Declarations" means the part of this policy that identifies the named insured, residence premises, coverages, limits, and other information applicable to this insurance. The Declarations include any applicable change or endorsement.
