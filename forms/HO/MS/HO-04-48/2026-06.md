# HO 04 48 Other Structures on the Residence Premises — Increased Limits (Edition 2026-06)

Multistate endorsement. Attaches to HO-3 (Edition 2018-09) and modifies Section I — Property
Coverages, Coverage B — Other Structures, paragraph B.2 only. Net-new; no earlier edition of
this endorsement exists.

## OS.1 Increased Limit

Paragraph B.2 of Coverage B is replaced by the following: The limit of liability for Coverage B
is the amount shown for this endorsement in the Declarations, which may not be less than ten
percent of the Coverage A limit. This amount is additional insurance.

## OS.2 What This Endorsement Does Not Change

This endorsement does not modify Coverage B paragraph B.1 or paragraph B.3, does not modify any
other Section I property coverage, and does not modify or remove any Section I exclusion or
condition. Other structures rented or held for rental remain subject to paragraph B.3.

## OS.3 Premium

The additional premium for the increased limit is shown in the Declarations.

All other provisions of the policy apply.
