# HO 04 96 No Section II — Liability Coverages (Edition 2011-05)

**W.2** The insurance applies only when this policy is in force. We provide coverage subject to all applicable provisions of this policy.

**W.3** You accept this policy by paying the required premium or by accepting its benefits. Your acceptance includes all provisions made part of this policy.

**W.4** We rely on information you provide in issuing this policy. You must ensure that information is accurate and complete.

**W.5** This policy includes the coverage terms that apply to you. No coverage applies unless it is provided by this policy.

**W.6** The terms of this policy may limit, restrict, or condition coverage. You must comply with all duties that apply to you.

**W.7** We may enforce any provision of this policy. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive our right to enforce it later.

**W.8** No person may change this policy except as we authorize in writing. A change is effective only when made part of this policy.

**W.9** Any provision added to this policy applies as written. A provision that changes this policy controls to the extent of any conflict.

**W.10** The provisions of this policy are to be read together. No provision may be interpreted to provide coverage that is not stated.

**W.11** Coverage applies only to the persons and interests described by this policy. We do not provide coverage merely because a person expects coverage.

**W.12** Your rights under this policy are subject to its conditions and exclusions. Our obligations arise only as provided by this policy.

**W.13** You may not transfer your rights or duties under this policy without our written consent. We may recognize a transfer when required by law.

**W.14** A request, notice, or statement made by you must be truthful. We may act upon information we reasonably believe to be accurate.

**W.15** Any notice we provide under this policy may be delivered by a method permitted by law. Notice is effective when delivered or otherwise provided as permitted by law.

**W.20** You must cooperate with us in the administration of this policy. Your duties apply whenever coverage may be available.

**W.21** This policy contains the complete terms of the insurance we provide. No coverage exists except as provided by this policy.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide "personal liability coverage" for damages for which an "insured" is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence". The occurrence must take place within the coverage territory.

**W.2** We will pay damages for covered bodily injury or property damage after the legal liability of an insured has been established. We may investigate and settle a "claim" as we consider appropriate.

**W.3** We will provide a defense at our expense when a "suit" seeks damages covered by personal liability coverage. We may select counsel and direct the defense of the suit.

**W.4** We may settle a claim or suit without obtaining consent from an insured. Our duty to defend ends when no covered damages remain payable.

**W.5** We provide "medical payments coverage" for reasonable medical expenses arising from an "accident" that causes bodily injury. The accident must result from a condition at an "insured location" or from the activities of an insured.

**W.6** Medical payments coverage applies regardless of fault. Payment under medical payments coverage does not establish the legal liability of an insured.

**W.7** Medical payments coverage applies only to an "injured person" who submits to examinations by physicians selected by us when reasonably requested. An injured person must provide records and information reasonably necessary to evaluate medical expenses.

**W.8** We will pay covered "medical expenses" that are necessary and reasonable for the bodily injury involved. Medical expenses include expenses for medical, surgical, dental, hospital, nursing, ambulance, professional nursing, prosthetic, and funeral services.

**W.9** Medical payments coverage applies to a person on an insured location with the permission of an insured. This coverage does not apply to a person who is regularly residing on an insured location unless that person is an insured.

**W.10** Medical payments coverage applies to a person away from an insured location if the bodily injury arises out of a condition on an insured location. This coverage also applies when bodily injury arises out of the activities of an insured.

**W.11** Personal liability coverage applies to the acts or omissions of an insured. This coverage also applies to the acts or omissions of a person for whose conduct an insured is legally responsible.

**W.12** We provide coverage for liability arising from the ownership, maintenance, or use of an insured location. We also provide coverage for liability arising from personal activities of an insured.

**W.13** Coverage applies to liability arising from the use of premises that an insured occupies as a residence. Coverage applies only while the premises qualify as an insured location.

**W.14** Coverage applies to liability arising from temporary occupancy of premises by an insured. The occupancy must be incidental to the use of the residence premises by an insured.

**W.15** We provide coverage for liability arising from the conduct of household activities. Household activities include activities customarily performed for the maintenance or use of an insured location.

**W.16** We provide coverage for liability arising from the acts of domestic employees of an insured. Coverage applies only when the domestic employee acts within the scope of duties for the insured.

**W.17** We provide coverage for liability arising from the acts of a person while performing duties on behalf of an insured. The person must be acting with the permission of an insured.

**W.18** We provide coverage for liability arising from "volunteer activities" performed by an insured. Volunteer activities must not be undertaken for compensation or as part of a business.

**W.19** We provide coverage for liability arising from the ownership or use of animals by an insured. This coverage applies only to the extent that no exclusion applies.

**W.20** We provide coverage for liability arising from the ownership or use of an "animal" kept by an insured. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and defense of any claim involving an animal.

**W.21** We provide coverage for liability arising from the maintenance of trees, shrubs, walks, driveways, and other appurtenant structures at an insured location. The maintenance must be performed for the benefit of an insured location.

**W.22** We provide coverage for liability arising from a condition of land owned or controlled by an insured. The land must be an insured location at the time of the occurrence.

**W.23** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of recreational equipment by an insured. Coverage applies only when the equipment is used in a manner customary for its intended purpose.

**W.24** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of tools and equipment maintained for personal use. This coverage does not apply when the tools or equipment are used in connection with a business.

**W.25** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of a "motor vehicle" when the motor vehicle is not subject to a motor vehicle exclusion. An insured must provide information concerning the ownership, operation, and use of the motor vehicle.

**W.26** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of a "watercraft" when the watercraft is not subject to a watercraft exclusion. An insured must preserve the watercraft and make it available for inspection when reasonably requested.

**W.27** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of an "aircraft" when the aircraft is not subject to an aircraft exclusion. An insured must promptly report any accident involving an aircraft.

**W.28** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of a rented or borrowed conveyance when no exclusion applies. The conveyance must be used with the permission of its owner or lawful custodian.

**W.29** We provide coverage for liability arising from the "entrustment" of property by an insured. Coverage applies only if the resulting bodily injury or property damage is otherwise covered.

**W.30** We provide coverage for liability arising from the supervision of a person by an insured. Coverage applies only if the supervision is not provided in connection with a business.

**W.31** We provide coverage for liability arising from the furnishing of food, beverages, or refreshments by an insured. This coverage does not apply to liability arising from the sale or distribution of alcoholic beverages when an insured is engaged in the business of selling or serving alcoholic beverages.

**W.32** We provide coverage for liability arising from the hosting of social gatherings by an insured. Coverage applies only to bodily injury or property damage otherwise covered by this section.

**W.33** We provide coverage for liability arising from an insured's use of another person's property. The property must be used with the permission of its owner or lawful custodian.

**W.34** We provide coverage for liability arising from accidental damage to property of others caused by an insured. This coverage applies only if the property damage is not otherwise excluded.

**W.35** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage that is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury or property damage resulting from the use of reasonable force by an insured to protect persons or property.

**W.36** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of or in connection with a "business" conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the business is operated for profit or otherwise.

**W.37** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the rendering or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies even if the professional services are performed without compensation.

**W.38** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of an insured's employment of another person. This exclusion does not apply to domestic employees when the bodily injury or property damage arises from household duties.

**W.39** We do not cover bodily injury to an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of the theory of liability asserted.

**W.40** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. This exclusion applies to property owned jointly or separately by any insured.

**W.41** We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care of an insured. This exclusion does not apply to property damage caused by fire, smoke, or explosion.

**W.42** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle when a motor vehicle exclusion applies. This exclusion applies whether the motor vehicle is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.43** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft when a watercraft exclusion applies. This exclusion applies to a watercraft operated by an insured or by another person.

**W.44** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft when an aircraft exclusion applies. This exclusion applies to an aircraft operated by an insured or by another person.

**W.45** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the transmission is alleged to be negligent, intentional, or otherwise.

**W.46** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of controlled substances. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of prescription drugs by an insured.

**W.47** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, or military action. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing to the loss.

**W.48** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the pollutants are produced, handled, stored, transported, or disposed of by an insured.

**W.49** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a criminal act or omission of an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not an insured is charged with or convicted of an offense.

**W.50** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of sexual molestation, corporal punishment, or physical or mental abuse. This exclusion applies to an insured and to a person for whose conduct an insured is legally responsible.

**W.51** We do not cover damages arising out of a "contract" or "agreement" assumed by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**W.52** We do not cover punitive or exemplary damages. We do not cover damages imposed as a penalty or fine.

**W.53** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of an insured's failure to disclose information concerning property. This exclusion applies to a representation, warranty, or statement made in connection with the sale, rental, or transfer of property.

**W.54** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership or operation of a home day care activity by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the activity is licensed, unlicensed, compensated, or uncompensated.

**W.55** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of property for agricultural purposes. This exclusion does not apply to incidental gardening for personal use.

**W.56** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising from an insured's participation in an organized athletic activity. This exclusion does not apply to activities undertaken solely for recreation and not as part of a business.

**W.57** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury to an insured. We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising from an excluded occurrence.

**W.58** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising out of a business conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted at an insured location or elsewhere.

**W.59** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle when a motor vehicle exclusion applies. This exclusion applies whether the motor vehicle is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.60** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft when a watercraft exclusion applies. This exclusion applies whether the watercraft is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.61** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft when an aircraft exclusion applies. This exclusion applies whether the aircraft is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.62** An insured must promptly notify us of an occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must include information reasonably available concerning the time, place, circumstances, and persons involved.

**W.63** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, complaint, or other legal paper relating to a claim or suit. An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent.

**W.64** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of a claim or suit. An insured must assist us in securing evidence and obtaining the attendance of witnesses.

**W.65** An insured must submit to an examination under oath when reasonably requested by us. An insured must provide records, documents, and authorizations reasonably necessary for our investigation.

**W.66** An insured must not interfere with our right to investigate, settle, defend, or appeal a claim or suit. An insured must not make statements that materially prejudice our interests.

**W.67** A person seeking payment under medical payments coverage must provide written proof of medical expenses when reasonably requested. The person must authorize the release of medical records relevant to the bodily injury.

**W.68** Payment of a claim does not constitute an admission of liability by an insured or by us. A settlement does not affect our right to deny coverage for a separate claim or occurrence.

**W.69** We may recover from a person responsible for a covered loss to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our recovery rights.

**W.70** If an insured has rights to recover damages from another person, those rights transfer to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate in the enforcement of those rights.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The "Limit of Liability" is the greatest amount we will pay for "damages" covered by this insurance. It applies regardless of the form of relief sought.

**W.2** The Limit of Liability applies to damages arising out of an "occurrence." An occurrence includes continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.

**W.3** The Limit of Liability applies to all damages for which an "insured" is legally responsible. It applies whether liability is alleged directly, vicariously, jointly, or otherwise.

**W.4** The Limit of Liability applies regardless of the number of "claimants" seeking damages. A claimant includes any person or organization seeking recovery.

**W.5** The Limit of Liability applies regardless of the number of "claims" made. A claim includes a demand for damages, whether written, oral, formal, or informal.

**W.6** The Limit of Liability applies regardless of the number of "suits" brought. A suit includes a civil proceeding in which damages are alleged.

**W.7** The Limit of Liability applies regardless of the number of insureds against whom damages are sought. Payment on behalf of any insured reduces the amount available for all insureds.

**W.8** The Limit of Liability applies regardless of the number of legal theories asserted. A change in the legal characterization of damages does not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.9** The Limit of Liability applies regardless of the number of injuries, losses, or alleged wrongful acts. Related injuries or losses do not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.10** The Limit of Liability applies regardless of whether damages are sought in a single suit or in separate suits. Separate proceedings do not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.11** The Limit of Liability applies regardless of whether damages are sought by persons acting in different capacities. A claimant’s status does not increase the amount available.

**W.12** The Limit of Liability applies regardless of whether damages are sought for bodily injury, property damage, personal injury, or another covered injury. The nature of the alleged injury does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.13** The Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from acts, omissions, conditions, events, or conduct that are related to an occurrence. Related allegations do not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.14** The Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from the same general harmful conditions. Continuous or repeated conditions are treated as part of the same occurrence when they are substantially related.

**W.15** The Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from an insured’s conduct and from conduct for which an insured is legally responsible. Vicarious liability does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.16** The Limit of Liability applies to damages claimed by an insured and damages claimed by a person who is not an insured. The identity of the claimant does not increase the amount available.

**W.17** The Limit of Liability applies to damages sought in a representative, derivative, or similar capacity. Such a capacity does not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.18** The Limit of Liability applies to damages sought through assignment, subrogation, contribution, indemnity, or similar rights. The source of a claimant’s asserted right does not increase the amount available.

**W.19** The Limit of Liability applies to damages sought under a judgment, settlement, arbitration award, mediation agreement, or similar resolution. The manner in which damages are resolved does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.20** The Limit of Liability is not increased because multiple insureds have coverage under this insurance. The obligations of insureds are subject to the same applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.21** The Limit of Liability is not increased because multiple locations, premises, residences, or properties are involved. The place of an occurrence does not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.22** The Limit of Liability is not increased because multiple vehicles, animals, watercraft, or other property are involved. The property involved in an occurrence does not increase the amount available.

**W.23** The Limit of Liability is not increased because damages arise from more than one act or omission. Related acts or omissions are subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.24** The Limit of Liability is not increased because damages arise over an extended course of conduct. Continuing conduct does not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.25** The Limit of Liability is not increased because an occurrence results in different kinds of damages. All covered damages arising from that occurrence are subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.26** The Limit of Liability is not increased because a claimant seeks damages in more than one capacity. Damages sought in different capacities are subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.27** The Limit of Liability is not increased because a claimant alleges different causes of action. All covered damages arising from the same occurrence are subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.28** The Limit of Liability is not increased because a claimant seeks damages under different legal remedies. The remedy requested does not alter the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.29** We do not cover damages that exceed the applicable Limit of Liability. An insured remains responsible for amounts not paid by us.

**W.30** We do not cover a payment that would increase the applicable Limit of Liability. No agreement, admission, judgment, or settlement may enlarge our obligation.

**W.31** We do not cover damages merely because they are alleged in a suit against an insured. Damages must otherwise be covered by this insurance.

**W.32** We do not cover damages merely because an insured is named as a party to a suit. The allegations must establish a covered basis for payment.

**W.33** We do not cover punitive, exemplary, multiplied, or similar damages unless they are otherwise covered by this insurance. Any covered payment remains subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.34** We do not cover fines, penalties, sanctions, or similar amounts unless they are otherwise covered by this insurance. Any covered payment remains subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.35** Payments we make for damages reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. Payments made on behalf of any insured reduce the amount available for all insureds.

**W.36** Payments we make under a settlement reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. A settlement payment reduces the amount available even if an insured does not admit liability.

**W.37** Payments we make to satisfy a judgment reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. A judgment payment reduces the amount available whether the judgment is final, appealed, or otherwise subject to further proceedings.

**W.38** Payments we make directly to a claimant reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. Payments we make through an insured, representative, or other person also reduce the amount available.

**W.39** Payments we make under an agreement resolving a covered claim reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. The title or form of the agreement does not affect that reduction.

**W.40** We may allocate a payment among claimants, insureds, claims, or suits. Our allocation does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.41** We may make payment to resolve a covered claim without obtaining the consent of every insured. Such payment reduces the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.42** After the applicable Limit of Liability is exhausted by payments for damages, we have no further obligation to pay damages. Exhaustion does not affect any duty that continues under this insurance.

**W.43** An insured must not make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense that would increase our obligation. An insured must obtain our consent before agreeing to a settlement involving covered damages.

**W.44** An insured must cooperate with us in evaluating claims and allocating payments. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary to determine the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.45** An insured must not represent that we will pay an amount greater than the applicable Limit of Liability. An insured must not impair our ability to apply the Limit of Liability.

**W.46** An insured must preserve any rights available to reduce or recover a payment we make. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing those rights.

**W.47** Our payment of defense costs does not reduce the applicable Limit of Liability unless those costs are damages. Defense costs that are damages are subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.48** Our investigation of a claim does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability. Our participation in settlement discussions does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.49** Our defense of an insured does not constitute an agreement to pay damages that are not covered. Our defense does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.50** Our payment of an amount that is not required does not waive the applicable Limit of Liability. No payment changes the Limit of Liability unless we agree in writing.

**W.51** Any recovery obtained by us for a payment we made may be applied as permitted by law. Such recovery does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability unless we agree in writing.

**W.52** The applicable Limit of Liability remains subject to all terms of this insurance. No insured or claimant acquires a right to payment beyond that Limit of Liability.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the amount you are responsible to pay for covered damages to which a deductible applies. We will reduce our payment by the applicable deductible.

**W.2** A deductible applies only to covered damages for which we are otherwise responsible under this insurance. The deductible does not create coverage.

**W.3** We will determine whether a deductible applies to a claim based on the facts of the loss and the applicable terms of this insurance. Our determination may be revised if material facts become known.

**W.4** The applicable deductible will be applied to the amount of covered damages. We will not apply the deductible to amounts that are not covered.

**W.5** An "insured" must pay the deductible when required by us. An insured must reimburse us if we pay an amount that is subject to the deductible.

**W.6** We may pay covered damages without collecting the deductible from you before making payment. Our payment does not waive your duty to reimburse us for the deductible.

**W.7** An insured must reimburse us promptly after we request payment of the deductible. An insured must make reimbursement in a manner acceptable to us.

**W.8** We may deduct the deductible from amounts otherwise payable to you or on your behalf. We may also seek reimbursement directly from an insured responsible for the deductible.

**W.9** Payment of a deductible does not limit an insured's duties under this insurance. An insured must continue to cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

**W.10** The deductible applies to covered damages arising from a "claim" to which the deductible applies. The deductible does not apply to damages merely because a claim is made.

**W.11** We will apply the deductible after determining the amount of covered damages. We will not include the deductible in the amount we pay on behalf of an insured.

**W.12** An insured's obligation to pay the deductible remains whether or not we make payment directly to a claimant. An insured's obligation also remains if we settle a claim without the insured's consent.

**W.13** A deductible does not affect our right to investigate a claim. A deductible does not affect our right to settle a claim as we consider appropriate.

**W.14** A deductible does not reduce our duty to defend an insured when a duty to defend exists. We will not treat the deductible as defense costs unless the deductible expressly applies to such costs.

**W.15** "Defense expense" paid by us is not part of the deductible unless the deductible expressly applies to defense expense. An insured must reimburse only amounts properly subject to the deductible.

**W.16** "Supplementary payment" made by us is not part of the deductible unless the deductible expressly applies to supplementary payment. We will identify the basis for any reimbursement we request.

**W.17** A deductible applies to covered damages included in a "settlement" or "judgment." The form of a settlement or judgment does not avoid application of the deductible.

**W.18** We may allocate a payment between covered damages and amounts not covered. The deductible applies only to the allocated covered damages.

**W.19** An insured must not represent that we will pay the deductible. An insured must not agree to pay a claimant amounts that improperly increase our responsibility.

**W.20** We do not cover the deductible. The deductible remains the responsibility of the insured from whom reimbursement is due.

**W.21** An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine the deductible. An insured must provide records supporting any position that a deductible does not apply.

**W.22** We may request that an insured identify payments already made toward the deductible. An insured must provide accurate information concerning those payments.

**W.23** A payment made by an insured may be applied to the deductible when the payment relates to covered damages. We may determine the proper application of a payment based on available information.

**W.24** A deductible does not alter our right to recover amounts from a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must preserve our rights of recovery.

**W.25** Amounts recovered by us from a responsible person or organization may affect the amount an insured must reimburse. We will apply recovered amounts in accordance with our rights and obligations.

**W.26** An insured must not impair our ability to recover amounts related to covered damages. An insured must cooperate with us in obtaining recovery related to the deductible.

**W.27** A deductible applies despite the insolvency, bankruptcy, or financial inability of an insured. Such condition does not relieve an insured of the duty to reimburse us.

**W.28** A deductible applies despite the insolvency, bankruptcy, or financial inability of a claimant. Such condition does not increase our obligation to pay.

**W.29** An insured must not assign responsibility for a deductible without our consent. An assignment does not relieve an insured of responsibility unless we agree otherwise.

**W.30** We may enforce our right to reimbursement against an insured after payment of covered damages. Our failure to seek immediate reimbursement does not waive that right.

**W.31** A deductible does not change the amount of damages a claimant may seek from an insured. A deductible affects only the amount we pay or the amount we may recover from an insured.

**W.32** An insured must notify us if the insured receives funds relating to damages for which we paid the deductible. An insured must preserve such funds while our reimbursement rights are determined.

**W.33** We may offset amounts owed to us for a deductible against funds payable by us to an insured when permitted by law. An insured remains responsible for any unpaid deductible.

**W.34** A deductible applies only after we determine that covered damages exist. We will not require reimbursement for amounts paid solely because of mistake if no covered damages were owed.

**W.35** An insured must return any deductible reimbursement that we determine was not properly due. We will return such reimbursement after making that determination.

**W.36** A dispute concerning a deductible does not excuse an insured from complying with other duties under this insurance. An insured must cooperate while the dispute is being resolved.

**W.37** We may request reimbursement from any insured whose acts, omissions, or liability resulted in our payment of covered damages. Our request does not determine the legal liability of any insured.

**W.38** A deductible may apply to covered damages arising from "bodily injury," "property damage," or "personal and advertising injury." The nature of the alleged injury does not by itself determine whether a deductible applies.

**W.39** A deductible may apply to covered damages arising from an "occurrence." We will determine the relationship between claimed damages and an occurrence from the available facts.

**W.40** An insured must assist us in identifying covered damages subject to the deductible. An insured must not conceal or misrepresent information concerning the deductible.

**W.41** We may obtain reimbursement of the deductible through lawful means. Our pursuit of reimbursement does not limit any other right available to us.

**W.42** The deductible applies as stated in this section and in the applicable terms of this insurance. If those terms conflict, we will apply the terms that specifically address the deductible.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of an act expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the bodily injury or property damage is of a different kind, quality, or degree than expected or intended.

**W.2** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of conduct intended to cause harm by or at the direction of an insured. This exclusion does not depend upon whether the resulting harm was foreseeable.

**W.3** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage for which an insured is obligated to pay damages because of rendering or failing to render professional services. Professional services include advice, consultation, design, inspection, supervision, or treatment requiring specialized knowledge or skill.

**W.4** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of business pursuits conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted from the residence premises or elsewhere.

**W.5** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a trade, occupation, profession, or commercial enterprise. We do not cover a claim merely because an insured receives compensation for the activity giving rise to the claim.

**W.6** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the sale, transfer, distribution, manufacture, delivery, or furnishing of alcoholic beverages. This exclusion applies if an insured caused or contributed to the intoxication of any person.

**W.7** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of furnishing alcoholic beverages to a person not legally permitted to consume them. We do not cover liability arising out of violating a law relating to the sale, gift, distribution, or use of alcoholic beverages.

**W.8** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of an aircraft. This exclusion applies whether the aircraft is owned by an insured or by any other person.

**W.9** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of an aircraft operated by or on behalf of an insured. We do not cover a claim alleging negligent supervision, entrustment, hiring, training, or monitoring related to that aircraft.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** We will provide the coverages stated in this policy subject to all applicable conditions. An "insured" must comply with these conditions.

**W.2** The terms "bodily injury", "property damage", and "occurrence" have the meanings assigned by this policy. We apply those meanings throughout these conditions.

**W.3** A "claim" means a demand for damages or other relief because of bodily injury or property damage. A "suit" means a civil proceeding in which damages because of bodily injury or property damage are alleged.

**W.4** An insured must give us written notice of an occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must be given as soon as practicable.

**W.5** Notice of an occurrence must state the identity of the insured and reasonably available information concerning the occurrence. Notice must also state the location, date, and circumstances of the occurrence.

**W.6** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, complaint, order, or other legal paper received in connection with a claim or suit. The insured must not alter, destroy, or withhold such material.

**W.7** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. Cooperation includes providing truthful information and reasonable assistance when requested.

**W.8** An insured must assist us in securing evidence, obtaining witnesses, and conducting examinations. An insured must attend hearings, trials, conferences, and other proceedings when reasonably requested.

**W.9** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent. This condition does not apply to reasonable expenses incurred to protect persons or property from further injury or damage.

**W.10** An insured must not admit liability for a claim or suit without our consent. An insured may provide factual information concerning an occurrence.

**W.11** We may investigate an occurrence, claim, or suit as we consider appropriate. Our investigation does not waive any right available to us under this policy.

**W.12** We may settle a claim or suit as we consider appropriate. Our settlement authority is subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.13** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably requested by us. The examination may concern any matter relevant to coverage, liability, damages, or recovery.

**W.14** An insured must provide records, documents, photographs, recordings, and other material reasonably requested by us. The insured must permit us to make copies of material provided.

**W.15** An insured must authorize us to obtain records that are reasonably necessary to investigate a claim or suit. An insured must use reasonable efforts to obtain records that the insured may lawfully obtain.

**W.16** An insured must identify persons who may have knowledge concerning an occurrence, claim, or suit. An insured must provide reasonably available contact information for those persons.

**W.17** An insured must preserve evidence relating to an occurrence, claim, or suit. An insured must not dispose of damaged property when we reasonably request an opportunity to inspect it.

**W.18** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further bodily injury or property damage after an occurrence. Expenses incurred solely to comply with this duty do not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.19** An insured must promptly notify us of any request for medical information, medical payment, or other benefit arising from an occurrence. An insured must provide reasonably available information concerning the request.

**W.20** An insured must notify us if another person or organization may be responsible for bodily injury or property damage. The notice must include reasonably available information concerning that person or organization.

**W.21** An insured must notify us if the insured receives payment from another person or organization for damages arising from an occurrence. An insured must preserve our rights concerning that payment.

**W.22** If we make payment under this policy, we are subrogated to the rights of the person receiving payment to the extent of our payment. That person must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.23** A person receiving payment from us must cooperate in any recovery effort that we undertake. We may require the person to execute documents reasonably necessary to protect our recovery rights.

**W.24** An insured must not waive rights of recovery against another person or organization after an occurrence without our written consent. A waiver made before an occurrence does not affect coverage if it is permitted by law.

**W.25** We do not cover loss for which an insured has impaired our right of recovery by a voluntary act after an occurrence. We may deny coverage only to the extent that our rights have been impaired.

**W.26** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this policy. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not make us liable beyond the applicable limit of liability.

**W.27** No person or organization has a right under this policy to join us as a party to a suit against an insured. No person or organization may bring us into a suit seeking damages from an insured unless permitted by law.

**W.29** A written agreement determining an insured's obligation must be signed by us, the insured, and the claimant. We are not bound by an agreement made without our written consent.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "claim" for which coverage is provided under this policy. Our settlement authority applies whether or not an insured agrees with the settlement.

**W.2** We will determine the value of a covered "loss" from the facts, applicable law, and the terms of this policy. We may consider information developed through investigation, negotiation, appraisal, or litigation.

**W.3** An "insured" must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide truthful information concerning the loss and the persons involved.

**W.4** We may inspect damaged property when inspection is reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim. An insured must preserve such property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.5** We may obtain statements from an insured, claimant, witness, or other person with relevant information. An insured must assist us in obtaining records, documents, and other evidence relating to the loss.

**W.6** We will evaluate alleged "bodily injury" based upon the nature and extent of the injury, available medical information, and applicable legal responsibility. We may request authorizations needed to obtain relevant information.

**W.7** We will evaluate alleged "property damage" based upon the damaged property, the cause of damage, repair information, and applicable legal responsibility. We may inspect the property, obtain estimates, or seek expert opinions.

**W.8** We will determine whether an "occurrence" has caused covered injury or damage. We may reserve our rights while we investigate facts affecting coverage.

**W.9** An insured must promptly notify us of a claim, demand, notice, or communication that may involve coverage. An insured must send us any legal papers received in connection with a claim or "suit."

**W.10** An insured must not admit liability, assume an obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except at the insured's own cost. This condition does not prohibit an insured from providing reasonable assistance to an injured person.

**W.11** We may settle a claim by payment, repair, replacement, release, compromise, or other lawful means. We may require a release, dismissal, satisfaction, or other documentation appropriate to the settlement.

**W.12** We may make payment to an insured, claimant, provider, repairer, or other person entitled to receive payment. Payment to a person legally authorized to receive payment discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.13** We may pay for repair or replacement of damaged property when that method reasonably resolves a covered claim. We may select or approve the person performing repair or replacement.

**W.14** We will not be responsible for improved condition, betterment, or value not caused by a covered loss. We may deduct the value of any salvage, recovery, or contribution available from another source.

**W.15** We may consider depreciation, wear, age, condition, prior damage, and market value when those factors affect the amount of covered property damage. We may obtain appraisals or valuations from qualified persons.

**W.16** We may settle a claim involving damaged property based upon the reasonable cost to repair the property. We may limit payment to the amount required to restore the property to its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.17** We may settle a claim involving property that cannot reasonably be repaired based upon its actual cash value or other applicable measure of damages. We will not pay more than the amount for which an insured is legally liable.

**W.18** We will not pay for loss resulting from fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation by an insured concerning a claim. An insured must not present false, altered, or misleading information to us.

**W.19** We may investigate whether another person or organization is responsible for all or part of a loss. An insured must preserve our rights to recover from that person or organization.

**W.20** If we make a payment, we may exercise any rights of recovery available to an insured against another person or organization. An insured must do nothing after a loss to prejudice those rights.

**W.21** We may pursue recovery in the name of an insured when permitted by law. Any recovery will be applied according to the respective interests of the persons entitled to it.

**W.22** We may settle a claim that includes covered and uncovered allegations. Our payment of any amount does not waive any coverage defense, exclusion, condition, or limitation.

**W.23** We may defend a suit under a reservation of rights when coverage is uncertain. A reservation of rights does not create coverage that is not otherwise provided.

**W.24** We may appoint counsel, experts, adjusters, investigators, or other representatives to assist in handling a claim. An insured must cooperate with those representatives as we reasonably request.

**W.25** We may require an insured to attend interviews, examinations, hearings, mediation, arbitration, or trial when reasonably necessary. An insured must provide reasonable assistance in presenting facts and evidence.

**W.26** We may obtain copies of records relating to the claim, including medical, employment, repair, financial, or legal records when relevant and permitted by law. An insured must execute authorizations reasonably necessary for that purpose.

**W.27** We may consult experts concerning causation, damages, valuation, repair methods, or legal responsibility. The cost of experts retained by us will be handled by us unless the cost is not covered under this policy.

**W.28** An insured may obtain independent advice or representation at the insured's expense. We are not responsible for fees or expenses incurred without our consent unless we are legally required to pay them.

**W.29** We will consider reasonable information submitted by an insured concerning liability, damages, or settlement. We are not required to accept an insured's evaluation of the claim.

**W.30** We may communicate directly with a claimant, claimant's representative, attorney, provider, repairer, or other person having relevant information. An insured must not interfere with our reasonable efforts to investigate or settle a claim.

**W.31** We may make a payment without obtaining an insured's consent when we determine that payment is appropriate. Such payment does not constitute an admission of liability by an insured.

**W.32** We may decline to make payment when the claimed injury, damage, expense, or obligation is not covered. We do not cover any amount for which coverage is excluded, limited, or unavailable under this policy.

**W.33** We will apply applicable policy conditions and limitations in determining the amount payable for a loss. No settlement practice alters the terms of this policy.

**W.34** We will apply the "assessment coverage limit" when it applies to a covered claim. We will not treat that limit as available for a loss to which it does not apply.

**W.35** We will evaluate a "fire response charge" based upon the facts of the claim and the applicable policy provisions. We will not pay a charge that is not covered under this policy.

**W.36** We will apply the "special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones" when a theft claim involves property subject to that limit. We will determine whether the claimed property is subject to that limit from the available facts.

**W.37** We will apply the "home business contents limit" when property associated with a home business is involved in a covered claim. We may request information concerning the ownership, use, and location of that property.

**W.38** We may require proof of ownership, value, condition, purchase, repair, or replacement when such proof is reasonably available. The absence of a particular record does not require us to deny a claim if other reliable evidence establishes the loss.

**W.39** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from further damage after a loss. We will consider only reasonable and necessary protective measures that are covered under this policy.

**W.40** We may inspect repairs or replacement property before making payment when inspection is reasonably necessary. An insured must provide access to the property for that purpose.

**W.41** We may issue payment jointly to an insured and another person or organization having a legal interest in the payment. An insured must assist us in identifying persons or organizations with such interests.

**W.42** We may withhold payment when competing claims, liens, assignments, or other legal interests create uncertainty concerning the proper recipient. We may seek judicial direction or take other lawful action to resolve that uncertainty.

**W.43** An insured must notify us of any bankruptcy, insolvency, receivership, or similar proceeding involving an insured or a claimant when the insured becomes aware of it. Such proceeding does not increase our obligations under this policy.

**W.44** We may settle a claim before or after a suit is filed. We may continue to investigate and evaluate the claim after making a settlement offer.

**W.45** We may withdraw, revise, or replace a settlement offer before it is accepted. An offer is not binding unless it is accepted in a manner authorized by us.

**W.46** An insured must not assign any interest in a claim without our written consent, except as permitted by law. We may recognize an assignment when we are legally required to do so.

**W.47** We may settle only the covered portion of a loss when a claim includes matters not covered under this policy. An insured remains responsible for any obligation that is not covered.

**W.48** We may allocate payments, expenses, and recoveries among covered and uncovered matters when appropriate. Our allocation will not create coverage for an uncovered matter.

**W.49** We may rely upon information provided by an insured unless we have reason to question its accuracy. An insured must correct information that the insured knows is incomplete or inaccurate.

**W.50** We may request that an insured identify all persons with knowledge of the loss. An insured must provide contact information reasonably available to the insured.

**W.51** We may request photographs, recordings, correspondence, invoices, receipts, estimates, reports, or other materials relating to the loss. An insured must preserve electronically stored information relevant to the claim when reasonably possible.

**W.52** We may consider applicable statutes, regulations, court decisions, and contractual obligations in evaluating legal responsibility. We do not assume obligations that are not imposed upon an insured by law or contract.

**W.53** We may pay a settlement directly to resolve a claim against an insured. We may condition payment upon execution of documents that protect our rights and fully resolve the covered claim.

**W.54** A settlement by us does not determine liability among insureds, claimants, or other persons unless the settlement expressly provides otherwise. We may seek contribution or reimbursement from a person or organization responsible for the loss.

**W.55** An insured must promptly inform us of any payment, settlement offer, judgment, recovery, or release relating to the loss. An insured must not accept payment that impairs our right of recovery.

**W.56** We may require an insured to reimburse us for amounts recovered from another person or organization when permitted by law. We will not seek reimbursement beyond the extent of our payment and legal right of recovery.

**W.57** We may close our claim file when no further action is reasonably required. Closing a claim file does not waive any policy provision or prevent us from reconsidering the matter if new information is received.

**W.58** We will provide information concerning our claim decision when required by law. Our decision will be based upon the information available to us and the terms of this policy.

**W.59** An insured must comply with all duties that apply to a claim or loss. Failure to comply with a duty may affect coverage when the failure causes prejudice to us.

**W.60** Our payment, investigation, negotiation, defense, or settlement of any claim does not waive any right available to us under this policy or law. We retain all rights not expressly waived in writing.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** "Bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. "Bodily injury" includes resulting care, loss of services, and death.

**W.2** "Property damage" means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property. "Property damage" also means loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.

**W.3** "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An "occurrence" must result in "bodily injury" or "property damage."

**W.4** "Accident" means an unexpected and unintended event from the standpoint of an insured. An accident may include an act or omission that produces unintended injury or damage.

**W.5** "Insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives. "Insured" also includes a person under your care or the care of a resident relative if that person is not a resident employee.

**W.6** "You" and "your" mean the person or persons named as the insured in the policy declarations. These terms also include a spouse if a spouse is a resident of the same household.

**W.7** "Resident" means a person who resides in your household with an intent to remain there. Temporary absence from your household does not by itself end residency.

**W.8** "Relative" means a person related to you by blood, marriage, or adoption. A relative includes a ward or foster child who is a resident of your household.

**W.9** "Household" means persons who reside together as a domestic unit. A household may include persons related by family relationship or persons who share a common residence and domestic arrangement.

**W.10** "Residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and which is shown as the insured residence in the policy declarations. "Residence premises" includes structures and grounds used in connection with that dwelling.

**W.11** "Insured location" means the "residence premises." "Insured location" also includes land owned by or rented to you where a dwelling is being constructed for your use as a residence.

**W.12** "Insured location" includes premises used by you in connection with the "residence premises." Such premises must be used primarily for residential purposes.

**W.13** "Insured location" includes premises not owned by you where you are temporarily residing. Such premises are an insured location only while used by you as a residence.

**W.14** "Insured location" includes vacant land owned by or rented to an insured. Vacant land does not include farmland or land used for business purposes.

**W.15** "Insured location" includes land owned by or rented to an insured on which a private garage is located. The garage must be used in connection with the "residence premises."

**W.16** "Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain. "Business" includes an activity conducted on a full-time, part-time, or seasonal basis.

**W.17** "Business" does not include activities ordinarily performed by minors without compensation other than reimbursement of expenses. "Business" does not include volunteer activities performed without economic gain.

**W.18** "Employee" means a person engaged in your service and subject to your direction and control. "Employee" includes leased workers while performing duties for you.

**W.19** "Employee" does not include a temporary worker. A temporary worker is a person furnished to you to substitute for a permanent employee or to meet short-term workload conditions.

**W.20** "Residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the "residence premises." A "residence employee" includes a person who performs domestic services for an insured.

**W.21** "Residence employee" does not include a person engaged by an insured in connection with an insured's "business." A person performing services principally for a business is not a residence employee.

**W.22** "Motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle, trailer, or semitrailer designed for travel on public roads. "Motor vehicle" includes attached machinery or equipment.

**W.23** "Motor vehicle" does not include a vehicle designed solely for use off public roads while it is not subject to motor vehicle registration. A vehicle that is subject to registration remains a motor vehicle whether or not it is registered.

**W.24** "Recreational vehicle" means a vehicle designed for recreational use and not principally designed for travel on public roads. A recreational vehicle includes a vehicle used for off-road travel.

**W.25** "Watercraft" means a craft designed for travel or operation on water. "Watercraft" includes a vessel, boat, personal watercraft, and related equipment.

**W.26** "Aircraft" means a device designed for flight in the air. "Aircraft" includes related equipment used in the operation or navigation of the device.

**W.27** "Hovercraft" means a craft designed to travel over land or water by means of a cushion of air. "Hovercraft" includes equipment attached to or used with the craft.

**W.28** "Trailer" means a vehicle designed to be pulled by a "motor vehicle." A trailer includes a semitrailer and a utility trailer.

**W.29** "Claim" means a demand for damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage." A claim may be made orally or in writing.

**W.30** "Suit" means a civil proceeding in which damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" are alleged. "Suit" includes an arbitration proceeding or other dispute resolution proceeding to which you submit with our consent.

**W.31** "Damages" means sums that an insured is legally obligated to pay because of covered injury or damage. Damages include prejudgment interest awarded against an insured where permitted by law.

**W.32** "Legal obligation" means responsibility imposed upon an insured by law. A legal obligation may arise from a judgment, settlement, or agreement approved by us.

**W.33** "Intentional act" means an act committed by an insured with knowledge that the act is substantially certain to cause injury or damage. An intentional act includes a deliberate failure to act.

**W.34** "Expected injury or damage" means injury or damage that an insured knew, intended, or reasonably anticipated would result from an act or omission. Expected injury or damage is determined from the standpoint of that insured.

**W.35** "Contract" means a written or oral agreement creating enforceable duties between parties. A contract includes a lease, rental agreement, or indemnity agreement.

**W.36** "Insured contract" means a contract under which an insured assumes the tort liability of another party to pay damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage." Tort liability means liability that would be imposed by law in the absence of a contract.

**W.37** "Premises" means land, buildings, structures, and areas used in connection with them. Premises includes approaches, driveways, walks, and other areas maintained for use with the property.

**W.38** "Tangible property" means property that has physical substance. Electronic data is not tangible property.

**W.39** "Electronic data" means information, facts, or programs stored, transmitted, or processed by computer equipment or similar devices. Electronic data includes software, code, and digital records.

**W.40** "Loss of use" means the inability to use property for its intended purpose. Loss of use includes diminished use of property caused by covered physical injury to that property.

**W.41** "Personal property" means property owned or used by a person other than real property. Personal property includes movable items and property customarily kept at a residence.

**W.42** "Real property" means land and property permanently attached to land. Real property includes buildings, fixtures, and permanently installed improvements.

**W.43** "Care" means custody, control, supervision, or responsibility for the well-being of a person. Care may be provided by an insured directly or through arrangements made by an insured.

**W.44** "Custody" means the physical possession or protective supervision of a person or property. Custody includes responsibility for safekeeping.

**W.45** "Control" means the authority or practical ability to direct the use, handling, or disposition of property. Control may exist whether or not the insured owns the property.

**W.46** "Domestic services" means services customarily performed in or about a residence. Domestic services include housekeeping, cooking, gardening, and care of household members.

**W.47** "Economic gain" means money, property, services, or another material benefit received in exchange for an activity. Economic gain does not include reimbursement of expenses incurred solely in performing the activity.

**W.48** "Temporary residence" means premises where an insured resides for a limited stay away from the "residence premises." A temporary residence does not become the "residence premises" solely because of the stay.

**W.49** "Vacant land" means land without a dwelling or other structure intended for occupancy. Land remains vacant when it contains incidental improvements not used for business purposes.

**W.50** "Domestic animal" means an animal ordinarily kept by people for companionship, protection, work, or household use. A domestic animal includes an animal owned, cared for, or harbored by an insured.

**W.51** "Harbor" means to shelter, keep, feed, possess, or exercise control over an animal. Harboring may occur whether or not the person owns the animal.

**W.52** "Negligence" means a failure to exercise the degree of care required by law under the circumstances. Negligence includes an act or omission that creates an unreasonable risk of harm.
