# HO 04 96 No Section II — Liability Coverages (Edition 2011-05)

**W.4** We rely on information you provide in issuing this policy. You must ensure that information is accurate and complete.

**W.13** You may not transfer your rights or duties under this policy without our written consent. We may recognize a transfer when required by law.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide "personal liability coverage" for damages for which an "insured" is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence". The occurrence must take place within the coverage territory.

**W.2** We will pay damages for covered bodily injury or property damage after the legal liability of an insured has been established. We may investigate and settle a "claim" as we consider appropriate.

**W.3** We will provide a defense at our expense when a "suit" seeks damages covered by personal liability coverage. We may select counsel and direct the defense of the suit.

**W.4** We may settle a claim or suit without obtaining consent from an insured. Our duty to defend ends when no covered damages remain payable.

**W.5** We provide "medical payments coverage" for reasonable medical expenses arising from an "accident" that causes bodily injury. The accident must result from a condition at an "insured location" or from the activities of an insured.

**W.6** Medical payments coverage applies regardless of fault. Payment under medical payments coverage does not establish the legal liability of an insured.

**W.7** Medical payments coverage applies only to an "injured person" who submits to examinations by physicians selected by us when reasonably requested. An injured person must provide records and information reasonably necessary to evaluate medical expenses.

**W.8** We will pay covered "medical expenses" that are necessary and reasonable for the bodily injury involved. Medical expenses include expenses for medical, surgical, dental, hospital, nursing, ambulance, professional nursing, prosthetic, and funeral services.

**W.9** Medical payments coverage applies to a person on an insured location with the permission of an insured. This coverage does not apply to a person who is regularly residing on an insured location unless that person is an insured.

**W.10** Medical payments coverage applies to a person away from an insured location if the bodily injury arises out of a condition on an insured location. This coverage also applies when bodily injury arises out of the activities of an insured.

**W.11** Personal liability coverage applies to the acts or omissions of an insured. This coverage also applies to the acts or omissions of a person for whose conduct an insured is legally responsible.

**W.12** We provide coverage for liability arising from the ownership, maintenance, or use of an insured location. We also provide coverage for liability arising from personal activities of an insured.

**W.13** Coverage applies to liability arising from the use of premises that an insured occupies as a residence. Coverage applies only while the premises qualify as an insured location.

**W.14** Coverage applies to liability arising from temporary occupancy of premises by an insured. The occupancy must be incidental to the use of the residence premises by an insured.

**W.15** We provide coverage for liability arising from the conduct of household activities. Household activities include activities customarily performed for the maintenance or use of an insured location.

**W.16** We provide coverage for liability arising from the acts of domestic employees of an insured. Coverage applies only when the domestic employee acts within the scope of duties for the insured.

**W.17** We provide coverage for liability arising from the acts of a person while performing duties on behalf of an insured. The person must be acting with the permission of an insured.

**W.18** We provide coverage for liability arising from "volunteer activities" performed by an insured. Volunteer activities must not be undertaken for compensation or as part of a business.

**W.19** We provide coverage for liability arising from the ownership or use of animals by an insured. This coverage applies only to the extent that no exclusion applies.

**W.20** We provide coverage for liability arising from the ownership or use of an "animal" kept by an insured. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and defense of any claim involving an animal.

**W.21** We provide coverage for liability arising from the maintenance of trees, shrubs, walks, driveways, and other appurtenant structures at an insured location. The maintenance must be performed for the benefit of an insured location.

**W.22** We provide coverage for liability arising from a condition of land owned or controlled by an insured. The land must be an insured location at the time of the occurrence.

**W.23** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of recreational equipment by an insured. Coverage applies only when the equipment is used in a manner customary for its intended purpose.

**W.24** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of tools and equipment maintained for personal use. This coverage does not apply when the tools or equipment are used in connection with a business.

**W.25** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of a "motor vehicle" when the motor vehicle is not subject to a motor vehicle exclusion. An insured must provide information concerning the ownership, operation, and use of the motor vehicle.

**W.26** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of a "watercraft" when the watercraft is not subject to a watercraft exclusion. An insured must preserve the watercraft and make it available for inspection when reasonably requested.

**W.27** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of an "aircraft" when the aircraft is not subject to an aircraft exclusion. An insured must promptly report any accident involving an aircraft.

**W.28** We provide coverage for liability arising from the use of a rented or borrowed conveyance when no exclusion applies. The conveyance must be used with the permission of its owner or lawful custodian.

**W.29** We provide coverage for liability arising from the "entrustment" of property by an insured. Coverage applies only if the resulting bodily injury or property damage is otherwise covered.

**W.30** We provide coverage for liability arising from the supervision of a person by an insured. Coverage applies only if the supervision is not provided in connection with a business.

**W.31** We provide coverage for liability arising from the furnishing of food, beverages, or refreshments by an insured. This coverage does not apply to liability arising from the sale or distribution of alcoholic beverages when an insured is engaged in the business of selling or serving alcoholic beverages.

**W.32** We provide coverage for liability arising from the hosting of social gatherings by an insured. Coverage applies only to bodily injury or property damage otherwise covered by this section.

**W.33** We provide coverage for liability arising from an insured's use of another person's property. The property must be used with the permission of its owner or lawful custodian.

**W.34** We provide coverage for liability arising from accidental damage to property of others caused by an insured. This coverage applies only if the property damage is not otherwise excluded.

**W.35** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage that is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury or property damage resulting from the use of reasonable force by an insured to protect persons or property.

**W.36** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of or in connection with a "business" conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the business is operated for profit or otherwise.

**W.37** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the rendering or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies even if the professional services are performed without compensation.

**W.38** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of an insured's employment of another person. This exclusion does not apply to domestic employees when the bodily injury or property damage arises from household duties.

**W.39** We do not cover bodily injury to an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of the theory of liability asserted.

**W.40** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. This exclusion applies to property owned jointly or separately by any insured.

**W.41** We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care of an insured. This exclusion does not apply to property damage caused by fire, smoke, or explosion.

**W.42** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle when a motor vehicle exclusion applies. This exclusion applies whether the motor vehicle is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.43** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft when a watercraft exclusion applies. This exclusion applies to a watercraft operated by an insured or by another person.

**W.44** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft when an aircraft exclusion applies. This exclusion applies to an aircraft operated by an insured or by another person.

**W.45** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the transmission is alleged to be negligent, intentional, or otherwise.

**W.46** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of controlled substances. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of prescription drugs by an insured.

**W.47** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, or military action. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing to the loss.

**W.48** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the pollutants are produced, handled, stored, transported, or disposed of by an insured.

**W.49** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a criminal act or omission of an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not an insured is charged with or convicted of an offense.

**W.50** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of sexual molestation, corporal punishment, or physical or mental abuse. This exclusion applies to an insured and to a person for whose conduct an insured is legally responsible.

**W.51** We do not cover damages arising out of a "contract" or "agreement" assumed by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**W.52** We do not cover punitive or exemplary damages. We do not cover damages imposed as a penalty or fine.

**W.53** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of an insured's failure to disclose information concerning property. This exclusion applies to a representation, warranty, or statement made in connection with the sale, rental, or transfer of property.

**W.54** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership or operation of a home day care activity by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the activity is licensed, unlicensed, compensated, or uncompensated.

**W.55** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of property for agricultural purposes. This exclusion does not apply to incidental gardening for personal use.

**W.56** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising from an insured's participation in an organized athletic activity. This exclusion does not apply to activities undertaken solely for recreation and not as part of a business.

**W.57** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury to an insured. We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising from an excluded occurrence.

**W.58** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising out of a business conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted at an insured location or elsewhere.

**W.59** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle when a motor vehicle exclusion applies. This exclusion applies whether the motor vehicle is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.60** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft when a watercraft exclusion applies. This exclusion applies whether the watercraft is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.61** We do not cover medical payments for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft when an aircraft exclusion applies. This exclusion applies whether the aircraft is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.62** An insured must promptly notify us of an occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must include information reasonably available concerning the time, place, circumstances, and persons involved.

**W.63** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, complaint, or other legal paper relating to a claim or suit. An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent.

**W.64** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of a claim or suit. An insured must assist us in securing evidence and obtaining the attendance of witnesses.

**W.65** An insured must submit to an examination under oath when reasonably requested by us. An insured must provide records, documents, and authorizations reasonably necessary for our investigation.

**W.66** An insured must not interfere with our right to investigate, settle, defend, or appeal a claim or suit. An insured must not make statements that materially prejudice our interests.

**W.67** A person seeking payment under medical payments coverage must provide written proof of medical expenses when reasonably requested. The person must authorize the release of medical records relevant to the bodily injury.

**W.68** Payment of a claim does not constitute an admission of liability by an insured or by us. A settlement does not affect our right to deny coverage for a separate claim or occurrence.

**W.69** We may recover from a person responsible for a covered loss to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our recovery rights.

**W.70** If an insured has rights to recover damages from another person, those rights transfer to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate in the enforcement of those rights.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The most we will pay for the total of all damages arising out of any "occurrence" is the applicable limit of liability shown in the Declarations. This limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims, claimants, or persons injured. Our obligation to pay damages is subject to all other terms of this policy.

**W.2** An "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. We determine whether an occurrence has taken place based on the facts and circumstances of the loss. We do not increase the applicable limit because an occurrence causes multiple injuries or damages.

**W.3** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages because of "bodily injury" to any person. It is also the most we will pay for damages because of bodily injury arising out of any occurrence. This limitation applies regardless of the number of insureds or claims.

**W.4** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages because of "property damage" arising out of any occurrence. Property damage includes physical injury to tangible property and loss of use of tangible property that is not physically injured. This limitation applies regardless of the number of persons or organizations asserting claims.

**W.5** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages because of bodily injury and property damage arising out of any occurrence. We apply this limitation to the total damages resulting from the occurrence. We do not apply a separate limit to each injured person or damaged item.

**W.6** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages because of "personal injury" arising out of any occurrence. Personal injury includes injury arising out of false arrest, detention, or imprisonment, and other covered offenses described by this policy. This limitation applies regardless of the number of persons claiming personal injury.

**W.7** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages because of personal injury and bodily injury arising out of any occurrence. We apply this limitation to all damages resulting from the occurrence. We do not increase the applicable limit because the occurrence involves different types of injury.

**W.8** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving premises insured under this policy. The same applicable limit applies to damages arising out of conditions, operations, or activities at those premises. This limitation applies regardless of the number of premises involved in the occurrence.

**W.9** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving an insured location. An insured location includes the residence premises and other locations that qualify under this policy. We determine whether a location is an insured location from the facts existing when the occurrence takes place.

**W.10** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving personal activities of an insured. Personal activities include activities that are not connected with a business or profession. This limitation applies even if the activities involve more than one insured.

**W.11** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving a vehicle or watercraft. Coverage applies only to the extent provided by this policy. We do not extend the applicable limit because the vehicle or watercraft is used by more than one person.

**W.12** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving an animal owned by, or in the care of, an insured. We apply this limitation to all damages caused by the animal. This limitation applies regardless of whether the animal causes bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury.

**W.13** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving a residence employee. A residence employee is a person whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. We apply this limitation to damages arising from acts or omissions of the residence employee.

**W.14** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving an insured's ownership, maintenance, or use of property. We apply this limitation to covered property and property that is not covered under this policy. We do not increase the applicable limit because an insured has more than one interest in the property.

**W.15** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving an insured's maintenance or use of an insured location. This limitation applies to conditions existing on the location and activities conducted there. We determine the applicable limit without regard to the legal theory asserted against an insured.

**W.16** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving an insured's negligence. We apply this limitation whether the alleged negligence is based on an act, an omission, or a condition. This limitation applies regardless of whether the claimant alleges direct or vicarious liability.

**W.17** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving an insured's contractual obligation. We apply this limitation only when the obligation is covered by this policy. We do not increase the applicable limit because the contract contains multiple duties or remedies.

**W.18** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving an insured's use of property belonging to others. We apply this limitation to covered liability for damage to that property. This limitation applies regardless of whether the property was borrowed, rented, occupied, or otherwise in the insured's care.

**W.19** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving property in the care, custody, or control of an insured. We apply this limitation only to the extent coverage is provided by this policy. We do not increase the applicable limit because the property is held for different purposes.

**W.20** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving construction, repair, alteration, or maintenance. We apply this limitation to covered operations performed by or for an insured. This limitation applies regardless of the location where the operations are performed.

**W.21** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving completed work of an insured. Completed work includes work that has been completed or abandoned. We apply this limitation whether the alleged injury or damage is discovered during or after the work.

**W.22** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving products supplied by an insured. We apply this limitation to covered products and materials. This limitation applies regardless of whether the product is supplied for payment or without charge.

**W.23** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving a condition that exists before the policy period. We apply this limitation only when the resulting injury or damage is covered by this policy. We do not increase the applicable limit because the condition continues after the policy period begins.

**W.24** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving a condition that continues after the policy period begins. We apply this limitation to the total covered damages resulting from the condition. This limitation applies regardless of when an insured or claimant discovers the condition.

**W.25** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving related acts, omissions, or conditions. We may treat related acts, omissions, or conditions as arising out of the same occurrence when the facts support that conclusion. We do not increase the applicable limit merely because the acts, omissions, or conditions occur at different locations.

**W.26** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages arising out of any occurrence involving separate acts, omissions, or conditions. We may treat separate acts, omissions, or conditions as separate occurrences when the facts support that conclusion. Our determination does not enlarge coverage otherwise excluded by this policy.

**W.27** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages awarded against all insureds for an occurrence. We apply this limitation before considering the portion of the damages for which each insured is legally responsible. We do not pay more because an insured is subject to joint or several liability.

**W.28** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages agreed to in a settlement for an occurrence. We do not pay a settlement that is not covered by this policy. We do not pay a settlement entered into without our consent when our consent is required by this policy.

**W.29** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages determined by judgment for an occurrence. A judgment does not establish coverage for an excluded claim. We are not responsible for amounts that exceed the applicable limit.

**W.30** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages established by arbitration for an occurrence. Arbitration does not change the terms, conditions, or exclusions of this policy. We do not pay amounts that are not covered damages.

**W.31** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from a claim against an insured. A claim includes a demand for money or services based on bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury. We apply this limitation whether the claim is made orally or in writing.

**W.32** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from a "suit" against an insured. A suit is a civil proceeding in which damages because of bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury are alleged. We apply this limitation whether the suit names one insured or multiple insureds.

**W.33** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from multiple claims arising out of an occurrence. We apply this limitation to claims made by related or unrelated claimants. This limitation applies whether the claims are presented together or separately.

**W.34** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from claims made at different times. We determine whether the claims arise out of the same occurrence from the facts of the loss. We do not increase the applicable limit because claims are delayed or presented in different proceedings.

**W.35** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that gives rise to claims under more than one coverage. We do not add the limits applicable to separate coverages to determine our maximum payment. We apply the limit that governs the covered damages involved.

**W.36** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that gives rise to claims under more than one policy provision. We do not combine limits merely because different allegations are made. We apply coverage according to the facts and the terms of this policy.

**W.37** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one insured. We treat all insureds as having the benefit of the same applicable limit for that occurrence. We do not provide a separate applicable limit to each insured.

**W.38** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one claimant. We apply the applicable limit to the total covered damages owed to all claimants. We do not guarantee payment of the entire amount claimed by any particular claimant.

**W.39** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one kind of injury or damage. We apply the applicable limit to the combined covered damages. We do not allocate a separate applicable limit to each kind of injury or damage.

**W.40** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one legal theory. We apply the applicable limit to the covered damages, regardless of the theory used to impose liability. We do not pay damages solely because a legal theory is asserted.

**W.41** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one cause. We determine the covered portion of the damages according to the terms of this policy. We do not increase the applicable limit because the damages have concurrent causes.

**W.42** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one location. We apply the applicable limit to the total covered damages arising out of the occurrence. We do not provide a separate applicable limit for each location.

**W.43** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one activity. We apply the applicable limit to the total covered damages arising out of the activities. We do not increase the applicable limit because the activities are performed at different times or places.

**W.44** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one item of property. We apply the applicable limit to the total covered property damage. We do not provide a separate applicable limit for each item of property.

**W.45** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one injured person. We apply the applicable limit to the total covered bodily injury and personal injury damages. We do not provide a separate applicable limit for each injured person.

**W.46** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one alleged act or omission. We apply the applicable limit to the covered damages arising from the alleged acts or omissions. We do not increase the applicable limit because the allegations are stated separately.

**W.47** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one demand for payment. We apply the applicable limit to all covered demands arising out of the occurrence. We do not increase the applicable limit because different demands seek different forms of relief.

**W.48** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one judgment. We apply the applicable limit to the total covered damages established by the judgments. We do not pay amounts that exceed the applicable limit, even when the judgments are entered in separate proceedings.

**W.49** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence that involves more than one settlement. We apply the applicable limit to the total covered amounts of the settlements. We do not pay a settlement that exceeds the remaining amount available under the applicable limit.

**W.50** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence after payments have been made for that occurrence. Each payment reduces the amount available for covered damages arising out of the occurrence. We do not restore an amount that has been paid.

**W.51** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence after our payments for defense or related expenses. We apply those payments as provided by this policy. We do not make payments beyond the applicable limit when the applicable limit has been exhausted.

**W.52** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for damages for which you are legally liable and that are covered by this policy. We pay covered damages only to the extent that you have complied with your duties under this policy. We do not pay amounts that are not covered damages or that exceed the applicable limit.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** We apply the "deductible" to a covered loss when this provision requires its application. The deductible is the amount that you must bear before we pay for covered loss. You must bear the deductible even when payment is made directly to another person.

**W.2** We determine the deductible from the circumstances of the loss. You must provide information that permits us to determine whether the deductible applies. We may request documents, statements, and other information reasonably related to that determination.

**W.3** We apply the deductible to covered loss caused by a covered peril. You must bear the deductible before we pay for covered property. The deductible does not increase the limit of insurance.

**W.4** We apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss that remains after application of all applicable provisions. You must bear any part of the loss that is not covered. We pay only the amount of covered loss that exceeds the deductible.

**W.5** We determine covered loss without including amounts that are not covered under this policy. You must identify the property and expenses for which payment is requested. We do not apply the deductible to excluded loss.

**W.6** We apply the deductible to the total covered loss arising from an "occurrence". You must cooperate in identifying all property and expenses arising from that occurrence. We treat loss arising from the same occurrence as a single covered loss for application of the deductible.

**W.7** We determine whether loss arises from the same occurrence based on the facts and circumstances. You must give us prompt notice of facts that may connect separate damage to a common cause. We may apply the deductible to connected loss when the facts support that treatment.

**W.8** We apply the deductible before calculating the amount payable under the applicable coverage. You must not subtract the deductible from the limit of insurance before determining covered loss. We do not reduce the limit of insurance by the deductible.

**W.9** We apply the deductible to covered loss after considering any applicable valuation provision. You must provide information needed to determine the value of damaged property. We do not use the deductible to change the method of valuation.

**W.10** We apply the deductible to covered loss whether payment is based on repair, replacement, or another covered measure of loss. You must bear the deductible regardless of the form of payment. We do not waive the deductible because payment is made in stages.

**W.11** We apply the deductible to covered loss involving damaged property and related covered expenses. You must identify each expense for which you seek payment. We may allocate the deductible in a manner that reflects the covered loss to which it applies.

**W.12** We apply the deductible before making payment for covered additional expenses. You must establish that an additional expense is covered before we include it in the amount of loss. We do not apply the deductible to an expense that is not covered.

**W.13** We apply the deductible to covered loss involving personal property. You must separate covered personal property from property that is not covered. We do not apply the deductible to property that is excluded from coverage.

**W.14** We apply the deductible to covered loss involving real property. You must identify the portion of the real property that was damaged. We may consider related damage together when it results from the same occurrence.

**W.15** We apply the deductible to covered loss involving property at the residence premises. You must establish that the damaged property was at the residence premises when the loss occurred. We do not apply the deductible to property for which no coverage exists.

**W.16** We apply the deductible to covered loss involving property away from the residence premises. You must provide the location and circumstances of the loss. We apply the deductible only to the covered portion of that loss.

**W.17** We apply the deductible to covered loss for which you make a "claim". You must make a claim in accordance with the conditions of this policy. We may use the information in the claim to determine whether the deductible applies.

**W.18** We determine the deductible when we adjust the claim. You must assist us in investigating the cause and amount of loss. We may revise the application of the deductible if material facts change.

**W.19** We apply the deductible to covered loss even when the amount of damage is disputed. You must identify the amount of loss that you contend is covered. We do not admit coverage merely by applying the deductible.

**W.20** We apply the deductible to the covered portion of a loss when the loss includes both covered and noncovered damage. You must distinguish covered damage from noncovered damage. We do not use the deductible to make noncovered damage payable.

**W.21** We apply the deductible after separating damage caused by covered and noncovered causes. You must provide information concerning each cause of damage. We pay only for the covered damage remaining after that separation and application of the deductible.

**W.22** We apply the deductible to covered loss caused by related damage when the facts show that the damage resulted from a common occurrence. You must notify us of related damage that you discover after reporting the claim. We may adjust the amount of covered loss as additional related damage is established.

**W.23** We apply the deductible to covered loss discovered at different times when the loss results from the same occurrence. You must report newly discovered damage promptly. We do not treat the timing of discovery alone as creating a separate deductible.

**W.24** We apply the deductible to covered loss discovered at the same time when the loss results from separate occurrences. You must provide facts concerning the timing and causes of the damage. We may apply a separate deductible when the facts establish separate occurrences.

**W.25** We apply the deductible to covered loss whether you repair the property or do not repair the property. You must permit us to inspect damaged property when reasonably requested. We determine the amount of covered loss without requiring you to complete repairs.

**W.26** We apply the deductible to covered loss whether payment is made to you or to a person having an interest in the property. You must identify all persons who may receive payment. We do not pay more than the covered loss after application of the deductible.

**W.27** We apply the deductible to covered loss when a person other than you submits information on your behalf. You must remain responsible for the accuracy of information submitted for the claim. We may communicate with that person to evaluate the application of the deductible.

**W.28** We apply the deductible to covered loss when damaged property is jointly owned. You must provide information concerning the ownership of the property. We apply the deductible to the covered loss and not separately to each owner's interest.

**W.29** We apply the deductible to covered loss involving property that is subject to a mortgage or other security interest. You must identify each interested person. We apply the deductible before determining the amount payable to any interested person.

**W.30** We apply the deductible to covered loss after considering salvage or other recoverable value that reduces the amount of loss. You must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. We do not treat recoverable value as covered loss.

**W.31** We apply the deductible to covered loss after considering payments received from sources that reduce the covered amount of loss. You must disclose payments or recoveries related to the loss. We do not pay again for an amount that has been recovered.

**W.32** We apply the deductible to covered loss after considering amounts that you are responsible for under a contract or agreement. You must provide any contract or agreement that affects the loss. We do not treat an obligation that is not covered as covered loss.

**W.33** We apply the deductible to covered loss after considering reasonable measures taken to protect property from further damage. You must take reasonable steps to protect property from further damage. We include only covered protective expenses in the amount to which the deductible applies.

**W.34** We apply the deductible to covered loss after considering reasonable temporary repairs. You must keep receipts and other records for temporary repairs. We do not include unnecessary or unrelated expenses in covered loss.

**W.35** We apply the deductible to covered loss after considering the condition of the property immediately before the occurrence. You must provide information concerning the prior condition of damaged property. We do not pay for deterioration or damage that existed before the occurrence.

**W.36** We apply the deductible to covered loss after considering the condition of property immediately after the occurrence. You must allow us a reasonable opportunity to inspect the property. We may consider evidence obtained during inspection in determining covered loss.

**W.37** We apply the deductible to covered loss established by records, photographs, estimates, and other reliable evidence. You must retain evidence that supports the amount of the claim. We may question evidence that is incomplete, inconsistent, or unreliable.

**W.38** We apply the deductible to covered loss even when the amount of loss is determined through appraisal or another authorized method. You must continue to comply with applicable duties while the amount of loss is being determined. We do not waive coverage defenses by participating in that determination.

**W.39** We apply the deductible to covered loss when payment is made under an agreed settlement. You must not settle with another person in a manner that impairs our rights. We may apply the deductible in calculating the settlement amount.

**W.40** We apply the deductible to covered loss after determining that the loss is otherwise covered. You must comply with all duties that apply to the claim. We do not treat compliance with this deductible provision as proof that other coverage requirements have been satisfied.

**W.41** We apply the deductible to covered loss regardless of whether you are responsible for causing the loss. You must not intentionally cause or increase the loss. We do not pay for loss caused intentionally by an insured.

**W.42** We apply the deductible to covered loss when more than one insured has an interest in the damaged property. You must coordinate information provided by insureds concerning the loss. We apply the deductible to the covered loss and not separately to each insured.

**W.43** We apply the deductible to the covered amount of loss that we determine after investigation and adjustment. You must cooperate with our investigation and provide requested information. We pay the covered amount remaining after application of the deductible.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** We will provide the coverages stated in this policy subject to all applicable conditions. An "insured" must comply with these conditions.

**W.4** An insured must give us written notice of an occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must be given as soon as practicable.

**W.5** Notice of an occurrence must state the identity of the insured and reasonably available information concerning the occurrence. Notice must also state the location, date, and circumstances of the occurrence.

**W.6** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, complaint, order, or other legal paper received in connection with a claim or suit. The insured must not alter, destroy, or withhold such material.

**W.7** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. Cooperation includes providing truthful information and reasonable assistance when requested.

**W.8** An insured must assist us in securing evidence, obtaining witnesses, and conducting examinations. An insured must attend hearings, trials, conferences, and other proceedings when reasonably requested.

**W.9** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent. This condition does not apply to reasonable expenses incurred to protect persons or property from further injury or damage.

**W.10** An insured must not admit liability for a claim or suit without our consent. An insured may provide factual information concerning an occurrence.

**W.11** We may investigate an occurrence, claim, or suit as we consider appropriate. Our investigation does not waive any right available to us under this policy.

**W.12** We may settle a claim or suit as we consider appropriate. Our settlement authority is subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.13** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably requested by us. The examination may concern any matter relevant to coverage, liability, damages, or recovery.

**W.14** An insured must provide records, documents, photographs, recordings, and other material reasonably requested by us. The insured must permit us to make copies of material provided.

**W.15** An insured must authorize us to obtain records that are reasonably necessary to investigate a claim or suit. An insured must use reasonable efforts to obtain records that the insured may lawfully obtain.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** "Bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. "Bodily injury" includes resulting care, loss of services, and death.

**W.2** "Property damage" means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property. "Property damage" also means loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.

**W.3** "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An "occurrence" must result in "bodily injury" or "property damage."

**W.4** "Accident" means an unexpected and unintended event from the standpoint of an insured. An accident may include an act or omission that produces unintended injury or damage.

**W.5** "Insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives. "Insured" also includes a person under your care or the care of a resident relative if that person is not a resident employee.

**W.6** "You" and "your" mean the person or persons named as the insured in the policy declarations. These terms also include a spouse if a spouse is a resident of the same household.

**W.7** "Resident" means a person who resides in your household with an intent to remain there. Temporary absence from your household does not by itself end residency.

**W.8** "Relative" means a person related to you by blood, marriage, or adoption. A relative includes a ward or foster child who is a resident of your household.

**W.9** "Household" means persons who reside together as a domestic unit. A household may include persons related by family relationship or persons who share a common residence and domestic arrangement.

**W.10** "Residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and which is shown as the insured residence in the policy declarations. "Residence premises" includes structures and grounds used in connection with that dwelling.

**W.11** "Insured location" means the "residence premises." "Insured location" also includes land owned by or rented to you where a dwelling is being constructed for your use as a residence.

**W.12** "Insured location" includes premises used by you in connection with the "residence premises." Such premises must be used primarily for residential purposes.

**W.13** "Insured location" includes premises not owned by you where you are temporarily residing. Such premises are an insured location only while used by you as a residence.

**W.14** "Insured location" includes vacant land owned by or rented to an insured. Vacant land does not include farmland or land used for business purposes.

**W.15** "Insured location" includes land owned by or rented to an insured on which a private garage is located. The garage must be used in connection with the "residence premises."

**W.16** "Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain. "Business" includes an activity conducted on a full-time, part-time, or seasonal basis.

**W.17** "Business" does not include activities ordinarily performed by minors without compensation other than reimbursement of expenses. "Business" does not include volunteer activities performed without economic gain.

**W.18** "Employee" means a person engaged in your service and subject to your direction and control. "Employee" includes leased workers while performing duties for you.

**W.19** "Employee" does not include a temporary worker. A temporary worker is a person furnished to you to substitute for a permanent employee or to meet short-term workload conditions.

**W.20** "Residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the "residence premises." A "residence employee" includes a person who performs domestic services for an insured.

**W.21** "Residence employee" does not include a person engaged by an insured in connection with an insured's "business." A person performing services principally for a business is not a residence employee.

**W.22** "Motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle, trailer, or semitrailer designed for travel on public roads. "Motor vehicle" includes attached machinery or equipment.

**W.23** "Motor vehicle" does not include a vehicle designed solely for use off public roads while it is not subject to motor vehicle registration. A vehicle that is subject to registration remains a motor vehicle whether or not it is registered.

**W.24** "Recreational vehicle" means a vehicle designed for recreational use and not principally designed for travel on public roads. A recreational vehicle includes a vehicle used for off-road travel.

**W.25** "Watercraft" means a craft designed for travel or operation on water. "Watercraft" includes a vessel, boat, personal watercraft, and related equipment.

**W.26** "Aircraft" means a device designed for flight in the air. "Aircraft" includes related equipment used in the operation or navigation of the device.

**W.27** "Hovercraft" means a craft designed to travel over land or water by means of a cushion of air. "Hovercraft" includes equipment attached to or used with the craft.

**W.28** "Trailer" means a vehicle designed to be pulled by a "motor vehicle." A trailer includes a semitrailer and a utility trailer.

**W.29** "Claim" means a demand for damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage." A claim may be made orally or in writing.

**W.30** "Suit" means a civil proceeding in which damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" are alleged. "Suit" includes an arbitration proceeding or other dispute resolution proceeding to which you submit with our consent.

**W.31** "Damages" means sums that an insured is legally obligated to pay because of covered injury or damage. Damages include prejudgment interest awarded against an insured where permitted by law.

**W.32** "Legal obligation" means responsibility imposed upon an insured by law. A legal obligation may arise from a judgment, settlement, or agreement approved by us.

**W.33** "Intentional act" means an act committed by an insured with knowledge that the act is substantially certain to cause injury or damage. An intentional act includes a deliberate failure to act.

**W.34** "Expected injury or damage" means injury or damage that an insured knew, intended, or reasonably anticipated would result from an act or omission. Expected injury or damage is determined from the standpoint of that insured.

**W.35** "Contract" means a written or oral agreement creating enforceable duties between parties. A contract includes a lease, rental agreement, or indemnity agreement.

**W.36** "Insured contract" means a contract under which an insured assumes the tort liability of another party to pay damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage." Tort liability means liability that would be imposed by law in the absence of a contract.

**W.37** "Premises" means land, buildings, structures, and areas used in connection with them. Premises includes approaches, driveways, walks, and other areas maintained for use with the property.

**W.38** "Tangible property" means property that has physical substance. Electronic data is not tangible property.

**W.39** "Electronic data" means information, facts, or programs stored, transmitted, or processed by computer equipment or similar devices. Electronic data includes software, code, and digital records.

**W.40** "Loss of use" means the inability to use property for its intended purpose. Loss of use includes diminished use of property caused by covered physical injury to that property.

**W.41** "Personal property" means property owned or used by a person other than real property. Personal property includes movable items and property customarily kept at a residence.

**W.42** "Real property" means land and property permanently attached to land. Real property includes buildings, fixtures, and permanently installed improvements.

**W.43** "Care" means custody, control, supervision, or responsibility for the well-being of a person. Care may be provided by an insured directly or through arrangements made by an insured.

**W.44** "Custody" means the physical possession or protective supervision of a person or property. Custody includes responsibility for safekeeping.

**W.45** "Control" means the authority or practical ability to direct the use, handling, or disposition of property. Control may exist whether or not the insured owns the property.

**W.46** "Domestic services" means services customarily performed in or about a residence. Domestic services include housekeeping, cooking, gardening, and care of household members.

**W.47** "Economic gain" means money, property, services, or another material benefit received in exchange for an activity. Economic gain does not include reimbursement of expenses incurred solely in performing the activity.

**W.48** "Temporary residence" means premises where an insured resides for a limited stay away from the "residence premises." A temporary residence does not become the "residence premises" solely because of the stay.

**W.49** "Vacant land" means land without a dwelling or other structure intended for occupancy. Land remains vacant when it contains incidental improvements not used for business purposes.

**W.50** "Domestic animal" means an animal ordinarily kept by people for companionship, protection, work, or household use. A domestic animal includes an animal owned, cared for, or harbored by an insured.

**W.51** "Harbor" means to shelter, keep, feed, possess, or exercise control over an animal. Harboring may occur whether or not the person owns the animal.

**W.52** "Negligence" means a failure to exercise the degree of care required by law under the circumstances. Negligence includes an act or omission that creates an unreasonable risk of harm.
