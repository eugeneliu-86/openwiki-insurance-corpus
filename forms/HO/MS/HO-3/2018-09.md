# HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2018-09)
> SUPERSEDED by HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2024-03) for policies effective on or after 2024-03-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** We provide the insurance described in this "policy" in return for payment of the required premium and compliance with all applicable policy terms. This policy consists of the provisions, endorsements, and "Declarations" that apply to you.

**AGR.2** The Declarations identify the named insured, the "residence premises", and the insurance that applies. The Declarations also state the applicable limits of liability and deductible.

**AGR.3** We insure the "covered property" against direct physical loss caused by a covered peril, subject to the applicable policy terms. Coverage applies only while this policy is in force.

**AGR.4** We will pay for covered loss only as provided by this policy. Our payment is subject to the applicable limit of liability and deductible.

**AGR.5** An "insured" must pay the required premium when due. Failure to pay a required premium may affect coverage as permitted by applicable law.

**AGR.6** An insured must comply with the duties imposed by this policy after a loss. We may require information, records, examinations, or other cooperation reasonably necessary to adjust a claim.

**AGR.7** We determine whether a loss is covered under the terms that apply when the loss occurs. We will adjust covered loss in accordance with this policy.

**AGR.8** We do not cover loss that is excluded by this policy. We do not cover loss beyond the coverage provided by the applicable policy terms.

**AGR.9** No action or inaction by us waives any policy term unless we agree to the waiver in writing. A waiver applicable to a particular matter does not waive any other matter.

**AGR.10** This policy is void as to an insured who has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact relating to this insurance or a loss. This policy is also void as otherwise provided by applicable law.

**AGR.11** Your rights and duties under this policy may not be assigned without our written consent. An assignment does not affect our rights under this policy.

**AGR.12** An insured must comply with all applicable policy terms before bringing legal action against us. Legal action against us must be brought in accordance with applicable law.

**AGR.13** Policy terms that conflict with applicable law are amended to conform to that law. All remaining policy terms continue to apply.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property, reduced by depreciation and physical condition.  
It includes the value of property immediately before the loss. It does not include the cost of new property without a reduction for depreciation.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without a deduction for depreciation.  
It includes reasonable costs necessary to restore damaged property. It does not include the cost to improve property beyond its condition immediately before the loss.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.  
It includes an unintended event that results in bodily injury or property damage. It does not include an intentional act expected or intended to cause injury or damage.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling used by you as a residence, together with the grounds and structures used in connection with that dwelling.  
It includes property customarily used as part of your home. It does not include property not used by you as a residence.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are in your care.  
It includes a resident relative living in your household. It does not include a guest who does not reside in your household.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and property used by you in connection with the residence premises.  
It includes premises temporarily occupied by you as a residence. It does not include property used solely for business purposes.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain.  
It includes the regular sale of goods or services for compensation. It does not include an activity performed solely as a volunteer without compensation.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a land vehicle, trailer, or similar conveyance designed for travel on public roads or propelled by mechanical power.  
It includes an automobile used for transportation. It does not include equipment designed solely for use in servicing the residence premises.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are connected with the maintenance or use of the residence premises.  
It includes a person performing domestic services for an insured. It does not include an independent contractor performing services without direction from an insured.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death.  
It includes physical injury caused by an accident. It does not include emotional distress that does not result from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.  
It includes loss of use of tangible property that has been physically injured. It does not include loss of electronic data.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unexpected.  
It includes an unforeseen pipe rupture that causes immediate water damage. It does not include seepage, leakage, or deterioration occurring gradually.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means property that lacks customary occupants and the personal property necessary for normal use.  
It includes a dwelling left without residents or customary furnishings. It does not include a furnished dwelling temporarily unoccupied by its residents.

**DEF.14** "named storm" means a tropical cyclone designated by a recognized weather authority.  
It includes wind and rain associated with a storm after that designation. It does not include a weather event that has not received such a designation.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the “dwelling” on the “residence premises” for direct physical loss. The dwelling includes structures attached to the dwelling.

**A.2** We cover materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises. The materials and supplies must be used to construct, alter, or repair the dwelling or structures attached to the dwelling.

**A.3** We cover fixtures, permanently installed equipment, and built-in appliances that are part of the dwelling. We cover such property while it remains attached to or installed in the dwelling.

**A.4** We cover property used to service the dwelling when that property is located on the residence premises. This coverage applies to property that is permanently connected to the dwelling.

**A.5** We do not cover land, including land on which the dwelling is located. We do not cover excavations, grading, filling, or landscaping unless the loss directly damages covered property.

**A.6** We do not cover detached structures under Coverage A. We cover a detached structure under Coverage A only when it is attached to the dwelling by a structure that is fully enclosed.

**A.7** We do not cover property held for sale, storage, or business purposes when that property is not part of the dwelling. We cover property used solely to maintain the residence premises.

**A.8** We cover direct physical loss caused by a peril unless an exclusion applies. We do not cover loss that is not accidental.

**A.9** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**A.10** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**A.11** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or smog. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**A.12** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by a covered peril that results from such event.

**A.13** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from damage by such animals.

**A.14** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**A.15** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of water, ice, or snow to foundations, retaining walls, bulkheads, piers, wharves, docks, fences, patios, swimming pools, or similar structures. We cover direct physical loss to the dwelling caused by a covered peril.

**A.16** We do not cover loss caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through sewers, drains, or sumps. We do not cover loss caused by water or waterborne material that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**A.17** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, pavement, driveway, sidewalk, or swimming pool.

**A.18** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must keep a record of repair expenses and make damaged property available for our inspection.

**A.19** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. An insured must provide information and records that we reasonably request to investigate the loss.

**A.20** We will settle a covered loss to the dwelling at “replacement cost” without deduction for depreciation when the limit of liability for Coverage A is at least eighty percent of the full replacement cost of the dwelling. The dwelling must be insured at that limit when the loss occurs.

**A.21** We will settle a covered loss at “actual cash value” when the dwelling is not insured as required for replacement cost settlement. Actual cash value includes a deduction for depreciation.

**A.22** We will not pay more than the cost to repair or replace damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may pay the value of the damaged property instead of repairing or replacing it.

**A.23** We will not pay replacement cost until the damaged property is repaired or replaced. We may settle the loss at actual cash value before repair or replacement is completed.

**A.24** An insured may later claim additional payment available under replacement cost settlement after repair or replacement is completed. An insured must provide records that establish the repair or replacement cost.

**A.25** We will not pay for increased costs caused by enforcement of an ordinance or law unless coverage for those costs applies. We will not pay for costs to comply with an ordinance or law that existed before the loss unless a covered peril caused direct physical loss.

**A.26** We will not pay for loss to undamaged property solely because matching materials are unavailable. We may repair or replace damaged property with materials that reasonably match the damaged property in appearance and function.

**A.27** We will not pay for improvements, betterments, or changes that are not necessary to repair covered property damaged by a covered loss. We will pay reasonable costs necessary to restore damaged covered property.

**A.28** We may inspect the dwelling and covered property after a loss. An insured must permit us reasonable access to the residence premises for inspection, evaluation, and adjustment of the loss.

**A.29** An insured must submit to an examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must produce records, documents, and other evidence reasonably related to the loss.

**A.30** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning the dwelling, the loss, or this coverage.

**A.31** We may require an appraisal when you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss. Each party will select a competent appraiser, and the appraisers will select an umpire.

**A.32** We will pay our appraisal expenses and the expenses of our appraiser. You will pay the expenses of your appraiser, and you and we will share the expenses of the umpire.

**A.33** We may elect to repair or replace damaged covered property. If we elect to repair or replace damaged covered property, we will provide notice of that election.

**A.34** We may take all or part of damaged property at the value agreed upon in settlement. An insured must transfer to us any interest in damaged property that we acquire.

**A.35** We will not pay for any loss for which an insured has received payment from another source. We will pay only the portion of a covered loss that remains unpaid.

**A.36** We may recover from a person or organization responsible for a covered loss after we make payment. An insured must preserve our recovery rights and must not impair those rights.

**A.37** Payment of a covered loss does not transfer ownership of the dwelling to us. Payment of a covered loss does not waive any policy condition or exclusion.

**A.38** The applicable limit of liability is the most we will pay for direct physical loss under Coverage A. Payment under this coverage reduces the available limit of liability for the remainder of the policy period.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover "other structures" on the "residence premises" that are set apart from the "dwelling" by clear space. We also cover other structures connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

**B.2** The limit of liability for "Coverage B" is ten percent of the limit of liability applicable to "Coverage A". This limit is an additional amount of insurance and does not reduce the limit applicable to Coverage A.

**B.3** Structures attached to the dwelling by more than a fence, utility line, or similar connection are part of the dwelling. We cover those structures as part of the dwelling rather than as other structures.

**B.4** Coverage applies only while the other structure is located on the residence premises. An insured must have an insurable interest in the other structure at the time of loss.

**B.5** We cover direct physical loss to other structures that is accidental and sudden. Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions applicable to this coverage.

**B.6** We do not cover land, including land on which the dwelling is located. We do not cover lawns, earth, or soil.

**B.7** We do not cover an other structure rented or held for rental to a person who is not a tenant of the dwelling. This exclusion does not apply when the structure is used solely as a private garage.

**B.8** We do not cover an other structure from which a "business" is conducted. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted by an insured or another person.

**B.9** We do not cover an other structure used to store "business property". We cover a structure containing business property solely owned by an insured or by a resident employee of an insured if no business is conducted from the structure.

**B.10** We do not cover an other structure that is used principally for business purposes. A private garage remains covered when its use is otherwise permitted by this coverage.

**B.11** We do not cover an other structure after an insured transfers all interest in that structure. Coverage ends when the insured no longer has an insurable interest in the structure.

**B.12** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect the other structure from further damage.

**B.13** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an insured that is intended to cause damage. This exclusion applies only to the insured whose conduct caused the loss.

**B.14** We do not cover loss caused by an act or decision, including the failure to act or decide, of an insured or another person. We do cover ensuing direct physical loss to other structures when the ensuing loss is otherwise covered.

**B.15** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We do cover ensuing direct physical loss when the ensuing loss is otherwise covered.

**B.16** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do cover resulting direct physical loss when the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**B.17** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do cover resulting direct physical loss when the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**B.18** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or decay. We do cover resulting direct physical loss when the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**B.19** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from the release of pollutants.

**B.20** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law requiring demolition, repair, reconstruction, remodeling, or replacement of an other structure. We do not cover increased costs imposed by ordinance or law.

**B.21** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, seizure, destruction, or confiscation of property. We cover loss caused by acts of civil authority taken to prevent the spread of fire when the loss is otherwise covered.

**B.22** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the residence premises. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from power failure.

**B.23** We may inspect an other structure at any reasonable time. An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged structure and any property related to the loss.

**B.24** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss involving an other structure. Notice must identify the damaged structure and the known circumstances of the loss.

**B.25** An insured must protect the damaged other structure from further loss. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair and protection expenses.

**B.26** An insured must show us the damaged other structure as often as we reasonably require. An insured must provide records and documents that relate to the loss when we request them.

**B.27** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of a loss. An insured must assist us in obtaining information reasonably related to the damaged other structure.

**B.28** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged other structures with property of like kind and quality. We may settle a covered loss by payment.

**B.29** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for a covered loss. Payments for damage to other structures are subject to that limit.

**B.30** We will determine the amount of loss after considering the condition of the damaged other structure immediately before the loss. We may consider depreciation, age, use, and condition when they are relevant to valuation.

**B.31** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath concerning a loss. An insured must provide requested information and documents relevant to the loss.

**B.32** We may inspect repaired or replaced property related to a covered loss. An insured must retain damaged property when reasonably necessary for our inspection.

**B.33** We will pay covered loss only after an insured has complied with the duties applicable to the loss. Our payment does not waive any right or defense available to us.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We provide "Coverage C" for "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" while it is at the "residence premises" or elsewhere. We may also cover property of others while that property is at the residence premises, if you request coverage for that property.

**C.2** The limit of liability for Coverage C is fifty percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". This limit is the most we will pay for covered loss under Coverage C, subject to applicable special limits.

**C.3** "Covered property" means personal property for which coverage is provided under this Coverage C. Covered property includes property normally used by an insured while away from the residence premises.

**C.4** We cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by a peril insured against under this Coverage C. Coverage applies only while the covered property is owned or used by an insured, unless we agree otherwise.

**C.5** The following special limits apply regardless of the amount of the Coverage C limit. These special limits are included within, and do not increase, the Coverage C limit.

**C.6** For money and precious metals, the most we will pay is two hundred fifty dollars. This limit applies to money, bank notes, bullion, coins, medals, scrip, and precious metals.

**C.7** For loss by theft of jewelry, watches, and precious stones, the most we will pay is one thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies whether the articles are worn, stored, loaned, or otherwise in your care.

**C.8** For loss by theft of firearms, the most we will pay is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit includes related equipment and accessories intended for use with firearms.

**C.9** For watercraft, including trailers, the most we will pay is one thousand five hundred dollars. This limit includes furnishings, equipment, and motors used with the watercraft.

**C.10** For loss by theft of silverware, goldware, or pewterware, the most we will pay is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies to flatware, hollowware, tea sets, trays, and similar articles.

**C.11** For "business property" at the residence premises, the most we will pay is three thousand dollars. This limit applies to property used in connection with a trade, profession, occupation, or other business activity.

**C.12** For "electronic apparatus" in or upon a "motor vehicle", the most we will pay is one thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies if the apparatus can be operated from the electrical power of the motor vehicle.

**C.13** We cover loss caused by fire or lightning. We also cover ensuing loss caused by smoke resulting from fire or lightning.

**C.14** We cover loss caused by windstorm or hail. We do not cover loss to property contained in a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless wind or hail first damages the building causing an opening.

**C.15** We cover loss caused by explosion. We do not cover loss caused by explosion of a steam boiler, steam pipe, steam turbine, or steam engine owned or leased by you.

**C.16** We cover loss caused by riot or civil commotion. We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, or destruction of property by order of governmental authority.

**C.17** We cover loss caused by aircraft. Aircraft includes self-propelled vehicles designed for flight or movement in the air.

**C.18** We cover loss caused by vehicles. We do not cover loss caused by a vehicle owned or operated by an insured while the vehicle is being used for racing, speed testing, or a similar activity.

**C.19** We cover loss caused by smoke. We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations.

**C.20** We cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief. We do not cover loss if the residence premises has been vacant for an extended duration before the loss.

**C.21** We cover loss caused by theft, including damage caused by attempted theft. We do not cover theft committed by an insured or by a person entrusted with the property.

**C.22** We cover loss caused by falling objects. We do not cover loss to personal property contained in a building unless the roof or an exterior wall is first damaged by a falling object.

**C.23** We cover loss caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet. We do not cover loss to property caused by pressure or weight of ice, snow, or sleet upon property that is not a building.

**C.24** We cover accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We do not cover loss caused by water or sewage that backs up through sewers, drains, or sumps.

**C.25** We cover sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a steam, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We do not cover loss caused by wear, deterioration, rust, corrosion, or mechanical breakdown.

**C.26** We cover loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat in the building or to shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**C.27** We cover sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current. We do not cover loss to a tube, transistor, semiconductor, or similar electronic component.

**C.28** We cover loss caused by volcanic eruption. We do not cover loss caused by earthquake, land shock waves, tremors, or volcanic tremors occurring before, during, or after volcanic eruption.

**C.29** We do not cover property separately described and specifically insured in this or any other insurance. This exclusion applies only to the extent that separate insurance provides coverage for the loss.

**C.30** We do not cover animals, birds, fish, insects, or other living creatures. We do not cover property held primarily for sale by an insured.

**C.31** We do not cover a motor vehicle, including its equipment and accessories, except a motor vehicle used solely to service the residence premises. We also cover a motor vehicle designed to assist a person with a physical disability.

**C.32** We do not cover aircraft and parts of aircraft. We do not cover equipment or devices designed for use with aircraft.

**C.33** We do not cover hovercraft or parts of hovercraft. We do not cover equipment or devices designed for use with hovercraft.

**C.34** We do not cover property of roomers, boarders, tenants, or other persons residing at the residence premises who are not related to an insured. We may cover such property if we agree in writing before loss.

**C.35** We do not cover property kept in an apartment regularly rented or held for rental to others by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to property of an insured while used by that insured.

**C.36** We do not cover business data, including records, files, plans, drawings, and electronic data. We cover only the value of the blank material upon which business data is recorded.

**C.37** We do not cover credit cards, debit cards, electronic fund transfer cards, access devices, or similar instruments. We do not cover loss resulting from unauthorized use of those instruments under Coverage C.

**C.38** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**C.39** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if another person has an interest in the covered property.

**C.40** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, including seizure, confiscation, destruction, or condemnation of property. We cover loss caused by acts of civil authority taken to prevent the spread of fire.

**C.41** We do not cover loss caused by war, undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military force. We do not cover loss caused by a nuclear hazard.

**C.42** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law requiring demolition, construction, repair, replacement, or removal of property. We do not cover loss arising from enforcement of building, zoning, or land use requirements.

**C.43** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, or earth sinking, rising, or shifting. We cover direct loss caused by fire, explosion, or breakage of glass resulting from earth movement.

**C.44** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**C.45** We do not cover loss caused by constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam occurring over time. We do not cover loss caused by condensation, humidity, moisture, or vapor.

**C.46** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We cover direct loss caused by fire or lightning resulting from mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria.

**C.47** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, decay, deterioration, hidden defect, or mechanical breakdown. We cover resulting loss caused by a peril insured against under this Coverage C.

**C.48** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, ceilings, pavements, patios, or fences. We cover direct loss caused by a peril insured against under this Coverage C.

**C.49** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We cover direct loss caused by fire resulting from damage caused by those causes.

**C.50** An insured must give us prompt notice of loss. An insured must describe the damaged property and provide available information concerning the cause of loss.

**C.51** An insured must protect covered property from further damage. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs solely to protect the property from further damage and keep an accurate record of repair expenses.

**C.52** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must provide records, documents, photographs, receipts, and other information that we reasonably request.

**C.53** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must submit to examination under oath if we reasonably request.

**C.54** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property. The inventory must identify the property, describe its condition, and state its actual cash value and amount of loss.

**C.55** We may inspect the covered property and examine records relating to the loss. We may require proof of ownership, possession, use, value, or condition of the property.

**C.56** We may repair, replace, or pay for covered property at our option. We may take possession of damaged property after payment for the loss.

**C.57** We will not pay for loss to covered property if an insured conceals or misrepresents a material fact relating to the insurance, the covered property, or the claim. This condition applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after loss.

**C.58** Payment for covered loss under Coverage C will be made to you unless another person is legally entitled to payment. Our payment will not exceed the applicable limit of liability or any applicable special limit.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** We cover "Loss of Use" resulting from a "covered loss" to property at the "residence premises" when that loss makes the residence premises not fit to live in. This coverage applies subject to its limit of liability.

**D.2** The limit of liability for this coverage is twenty percent of the limit applicable to your covered dwelling. Payments under this coverage reduce the amount remaining for covered loss of use.

**D.3** We pay the actual loss sustained by you, subject to the terms of this coverage. We do not pay more than the amount reasonably necessary to address the covered loss of use.

**D.4** We cover necessary increases in living expenses that you incur so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living. These expenses are "additional living expense."

**D.5** Additional living expense must result directly from a covered loss that makes the residence premises not fit to live in. The expense must be reasonable and actually incurred.

**D.6** We determine additional living expense by comparing your necessary living expenses before the covered loss with your necessary living expenses after the covered loss. We do not cover expenses that you would have incurred without the covered loss.

**D.7** We do not cover additional living expense that is not necessary to maintain your normal standard of living. We do not cover expenses that improve that standard of living.

**D.8** We cover loss of "fair rental value" when a part of the residence premises that you rent to others, or hold for rental, is not fit to live in because of a covered loss. Fair rental value is subject to the terms of this coverage.

**D.9** Fair rental value is the rental value of the part of the residence premises that is not fit to live in. We deduct expenses that do not continue while that part is not fit to live in.

**D.10** We do not cover fair rental value for a part of the residence premises that was not rented or held for rental before the covered loss. We do not cover anticipated rental income that is speculative or unsupported.

**D.11** Fair rental value must reflect the rental value that could reasonably have been obtained without the covered loss. You must provide records supporting the claimed rental value.

**D.12** We cover loss of use when a "civil authority" prohibits access to the residence premises because of direct damage to nearby property by a covered cause of loss. The prohibition must result from the covered cause of loss.

**D.13** Coverage for a civil authority prohibition applies only while the prohibition prevents access to the residence premises. Coverage ends when access is no longer prohibited.

**D.14** We do not cover loss of use when access is merely difficult, delayed, inconvenient, or limited. We do not cover loss of use when you may reasonably occupy the residence premises.

**D.15** We do not cover loss of use caused by a condition that existed before the covered loss. We do not cover loss of use caused by your voluntary decision not to occupy the residence premises.

**D.16** We do not cover additional living expense caused by construction delays that are not related to the covered loss. We do not cover expense resulting from your failure to act with reasonable promptness.

**D.17** We do not cover fair rental value resulting from a tenant's default, cancellation, or refusal to occupy the residence premises. We do not cover loss arising from a dispute concerning a lease or rental agreement.

**D.18** We do not cover business income, lost profits, or loss arising from interruption of business operations. This exclusion does not apply to fair rental value otherwise covered under this coverage.

**D.19** We do not cover penalties, fines, assessments, or charges imposed because of a delay in payment or performance. We do not cover charges arising from cancellation of services or agreements.

**D.20** We do not cover expenses for maintenance, improvements, remodeling, or betterment. We do not cover expenses that increase the value of property.

**D.21** We do not cover expenses incurred to comply with a law or ordinance unless the expense is otherwise covered as additional living expense. We do not cover costs to correct conditions unrelated to the covered loss.

## II.E — Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** We pay damages for which an "insured" is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence" to which this coverage applies. We will provide a defense at our expense against a "suit" seeking those damages.

**E.2** We may investigate and settle a "claim" or suit as we consider appropriate. Our duty to defend ends when the applicable limit of liability has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements.

**E.3** This coverage applies only to bodily injury or property damage occurring within the "coverage territory." The occurrence must arise from conditions existing during the policy period.

**E.4** "Bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. Bodily injury includes resulting care, loss of services, and death.

**E.5** "Property damage" means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property. Property damage also means loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.

**E.6** "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. The accident must result in bodily injury or property damage.

**E.7** "Suit" means a civil proceeding in which damages because of bodily injury or property damage are alleged. Suit includes arbitration or another dispute resolution proceeding to which we consent.

**E.8** "Coverage territory" means the territory in which this insurance applies under its terms. Coverage territory includes locations where an insured is temporarily present when the occurrence arises from personal activities.

**E.9** We will pay expenses we incur in the defense of a claim or suit. We will also pay reasonable expenses incurred by an insured at our request.

**E.10** We will pay costs taxed against an insured in a suit we defend. We will pay interest on a judgment as required by law.

**E.11** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage which is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury resulting from the use of reasonable force by an insured to protect persons or property.

**E.12** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of or in connection with a "business" conducted from or away from an insured location. This exclusion applies whether the business is operated by an insured or by another person.

**E.13** "Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain. Business does not include activities that are ordinarily considered personal or volunteer activities.

**E.14** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the rendering of or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the professional service is performed by an insured or by another person.

**E.15** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a "motor vehicle." This exclusion applies to ownership, maintenance, use, loading, unloading, entrustment, or supervision of a motor vehicle.

**E.16** "Motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle, trailer, or semitrailer designed for travel on public roads. Motor vehicle includes machinery or equipment subject to motor vehicle registration.

**E.17** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a "watercraft." This exclusion applies to ownership, maintenance, use, loading, unloading, entrustment, or supervision of a watercraft.

**E.18** "Watercraft" means a craft designed for travel on water. Watercraft includes related equipment used with the craft.

**E.19** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of an "aircraft." This exclusion applies to ownership, maintenance, use, loading, unloading, entrustment, or supervision of an aircraft.

**E.20** "Aircraft" means a device designed for flight or navigation in the air. Aircraft includes related equipment used with the device.

**E.21** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, or occupancy of premises owned by, rented to, or controlled by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to an "insured location."

**E.22** "Insured location" means the residence premises and premises used by an insured in connection with the residence premises. Insured location includes premises where an insured is temporarily residing.

**E.23** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the transmission was alleged to be negligent or intentional.

**E.24** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or usurpation of power. This exclusion includes consequences of those events.

**E.25** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the pollutants are actual, alleged, or threatened.
