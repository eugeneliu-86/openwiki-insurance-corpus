# HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2024-03)




## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** We will provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the "premium" and your compliance with the applicable terms of this policy. Coverage applies only as stated in this policy.

**AGR.2** This policy applies to an "insured" only while that person qualifies for coverage. You are responsible for ensuring that information furnished to us is accurate and complete.

**AGR.3** We will pay for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a "covered peril," subject to the applicable policy terms. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**AGR.4** We will pay damages for which an insured is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence." Our obligation is subject to the terms that apply to liability coverage.

**AGR.5** We will provide a defense for an insured against a "suit" seeking damages potentially covered by this policy. We may investigate or settle a "claim" as we consider appropriate.

**AGR.6** An insured must comply with the duties imposed by this policy after a loss, occurrence, claim, or suit. Failure to comply may affect coverage where the failure causes prejudice to us.

**AGR.7** The applicable "limit of liability" is the most we will pay for a covered loss or covered liability. Payment under this policy does not increase any applicable limit of liability.

**AGR.8** This policy applies only to loss or occurrence that takes place during the "policy period." Coverage is subject to the conditions existing when the loss or occurrence takes place.

**AGR.9** We will determine coverage based on the facts, the policy terms, and applicable law. No act by us in investigating, defending, or paying a claim waives any policy term.

**AGR.10** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to this insurance. We may deny coverage where concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud affects our rights.

**AGR.11** A change in risk, occupancy, ownership, or use may affect coverage. You must provide information we reasonably request to determine whether coverage remains available.

**AGR.12** This policy contains the agreement between you and us concerning the insurance provided. Its terms may be changed only by our authorized written action.

**AGR.13** If a policy term is determined to be invalid, the remaining terms remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. Our rights and your duties apply to each insured seeking coverage.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined with appropriate deduction for depreciation and obsolescence. It includes the value of used property in its condition immediately before damage. It does not include the cost to replace property with new property without deduction for depreciation. Market value may be considered when it reasonably reflects the value of the damaged property.



**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with material of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. It includes reasonable labor and material costs necessary to restore the property. It does not include any increase in value resulting from repair or replacement. It also does not include the value of land or the value of property that was not damaged.



**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. It includes accidental events that result in bodily injury or property damage. It does not include unrelated events merely because they arise during the same policy period. An event may be an occurrence even though its resulting injury or damage develops over time.



**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the property where you reside and that is described as the residence premises in this policy. It includes the dwelling and other structures used in connection with the property. It includes the part of the property used by you as a private residence. It does not include property where you do not reside unless it otherwise qualifies as an insured location.



**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are in your care. It includes a person using property covered by this policy with your permission when coverage applies to that use. It does not include a person who is merely visiting and is not a resident of your household. It also does not include a person acting outside the scope of permission you gave.



**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations qualifying for coverage under this policy. It includes land you own in connection with the residence premises and a location you use as a residence. It includes a location where an insured is temporarily residing. It does not include property used solely for business purposes unless coverage applies to that property.



**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain. It includes activities conducted from an insured location and activities conducted away from an insured location. It includes the rental of property when the rental activity is conducted for economic gain. It does not include volunteer activities for which no economic gain is received.



**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle or conveyance designed for travel on public roads. It includes attached machinery or equipment used with that vehicle or conveyance. It includes a trailer designed to be towed by a motor vehicle. It does not include a vehicle or conveyance designed solely for use on property where you reside and not subject to motor vehicle registration.



**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. It includes a person performing domestic services for an insured. It includes a person performing maintenance work for an insured at an insured location. It does not include a person performing services solely in connection with business activities.



**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. It includes resulting death. It includes physical injury arising from exposure to harmful conditions. It does not include purely emotional, reputational, or financial injury that is not caused by bodily harm, sickness, or disease.



**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. It includes loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured. It includes damage to personal property and real property. It does not include diminished value or other economic loss without physical injury to tangible property.



**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means an event that is abrupt, unintended, and unexpected from the standpoint of an insured. It includes damage resulting directly from an unforeseen discharge or release. It does not include damage caused by an event that was expected or intended by an insured. In this determination, the claims manual's duration test applies the definitionthe claims manual's duration test applies the definition (see Property Claims Handling Manual, C3 Chapter 3 — Water Losses).



**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of occupants and customary furnishings sufficient to show normal residential use. It includes a residence premises that is not being used as a home and lacks property ordinarily associated with occupancy. It does not include a furnished residence premises that is temporarily unoccupied. It also does not include property occupied by a tenant or other lawful resident.



**DEF.14** "named storm" means a storm that has been assigned a name by a governmental weather authority. It includes a tropical cyclone bearing an assigned name. It includes wind, rain, storm surge, and other conditions directly associated with that storm. It does not include an unnamed windstorm or other weather event.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the "dwelling" on the "residence premises" shown in the "Declarations". The dwelling includes structures attached to it.

**A.2** We cover materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises when they are used to construct, alter, or repair the dwelling. The materials and supplies must be intended for use in connection with the dwelling.

**A.3** We cover permanently installed fixtures, machinery, and equipment that service the dwelling. We also cover outdoor equipment permanently connected to the dwelling.

**A.4** We cover direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a risk of direct physical loss, subject to the terms of this policy. We cover loss only while the covered property is located at the residence premises.

**A.5** We do not cover land, including land on which the dwelling is located. We do not cover the value of land following loss to covered property.

**A.6** We do not cover a structure used primarily for business purposes. We do not cover property held for sale or used in a trade, profession, or occupation.

**A.7** We do not cover property separately insured under another policy. We do not cover loss to property for which other insurance provides coverage.

**A.8** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, demolition, or use of covered property. We do not cover the increased cost of complying with an ordinance or law.

**A.9** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**A.10** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, inherent vice, latent defect, or rust. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**A.11** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**A.12** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations, or discharge of pollutants. We cover smoke damage caused by sudden and accidental discharge from a household appliance or heating apparatus.

**A.13** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems while the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied. An insured must maintain heat in the dwelling or shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**A.14** We do not cover loss caused by theft from a dwelling under construction. We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if the dwelling has been vacant for an extended period.

**A.15** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**A.16** An insured must give us prompt notice of loss. An insured must describe the damaged property and the circumstances of the loss.

**A.17** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged covered property and to examine records relating to the loss. An insured must provide records, documents, and other information we reasonably request.

**A.18** An insured must show the damaged property when reasonably requested by us. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when practicable.

**A.19** We settle covered loss to the dwelling at "replacement cost" without deduction for depreciation when the Limit of Liability applicable to the dwelling equals or exceeds eighty percent of the full replacement cost of the dwelling immediately before the loss. Replacement cost does not include the value of land.

**A.20** If the condition for replacement cost settlement is not met, we settle the loss on an "actual cash value" basis until repair or replacement is completed. Actual cash value reflects depreciation and the condition of the damaged property at the time of loss.

**A.21** We pay no more than the amount actually and necessarily spent to repair or replace damaged covered property. We may pay the actual cash value of the loss before repair or replacement is completed.

**A.22** We settle covered loss to roof surfacing at replacement cost unless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attachedunless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attached (see HO 23 74 Actual Cash Value Loss Settlement — Roof Surfacing (Edition 2025-05), W.1 Coverage Provided). We determine the applicable settlement method at the time of loss.

**A.23** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may elect to pay for the loss instead of making repair or replacement.

**A.24** We do not pay for loss to undamaged parts of covered property solely because materials of like kind and quality are unavailable. We do not pay for matching of undamaged property.

**A.25** We do not cover diminution in value resulting from loss to covered property. We do not cover any reduction in market value arising from repair or replacement.

**A.26** Our payment for covered loss does not increase the Limit of Liability applicable to the dwelling. The applicable Limit of Liability is the most we will pay for all covered loss arising from the same occurrence.

**A.27** An insured must comply with the duties imposed by this policy before we are obligated to pay for loss. We may deny coverage when an insured materially prejudices our ability to investigate or adjust the loss.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover “other structures” located on the “residence premises.” Other structures must be separated from the dwelling by clear space or connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

**B.2** The limit of liability for “Coverage B” is ten percent of the limit of liability for “Coverage A.” This limit is additional insurance and does not reduce the limit of liability for Coverage A.

**B.3** We cover other structures that are owned by you or rented to you. The structure must be used in connection with the residence premises.

**B.4** We cover a detached garage used by you for private garage purposes. We do not cover a detached garage rented to a person who is not a tenant of the dwelling.

**B.5** We do not cover other structures used for “business” purposes. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted by an insured or by another person.

**B.6** We do not cover other structures used to store business property. We do not cover property used to manufacture, process, produce, or store goods for sale.

**B.7** We do not cover structures used for farming. This exclusion applies to structures used to keep livestock, poultry, or farming equipment.

**B.8** We do not cover structures from which a business is conducted. We do not cover structures containing property held for sale, display, repair, service, or delivery.

**B.9** We do not cover a structure that is occupied as a dwelling. This exclusion applies whether the occupancy is by you, a tenant, or another person.

**B.10** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We do not cover the cost to restore, grade, or stabilize land.

**B.11** We do not cover outdoor property that is not part of an other structure. This exclusion includes plants, trees, lawns, shrubs, and outdoor materials.

**B.12** We cover permanently installed equipment that serves an other structure. Covered equipment includes wiring, plumbing, heating equipment, and fixtures attached to the structure.

**B.13** We cover additions, alterations, and repairs that become part of an other structure. The work must be located on the residence premises.

**B.14** We do not cover property that is separately insured under another policy. We do not cover any amount recoverable under another insurance arrangement.

**B.15** We do not cover loss caused by an act or decision, including the failure to act or decide, by a person, group, organization, or governmental body. This exclusion applies whether the act, decision, or failure is negligent, intentional, or otherwise.

**B.16** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether the condition is performed by you or another person.

**B.17** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, decay, or latent defect.

**B.18** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover cracking, cracking apart, or structural movement unless caused by a covered peril.

**B.19** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations, or discharge of pollutants. We do not cover loss caused by release, migration, seepage, or escape of pollutants.

**B.20** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover damage caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, or gnawing.

**B.21** We do not cover loss caused by weather conditions that contribute in any way with an excluded cause of loss. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from weather conditions.

**B.22** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of ice or snow to property outside an other structure. We cover direct physical loss to an other structure when caused by a covered peril.

**B.23** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a structure.

**B.24** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or another cause.

**B.25** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**B.26** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**B.27** An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged other structure and the location of the loss. An insured must provide records, receipts, estimates, photographs, and other information we reasonably request.

**B.28** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss. An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable measures to protect covered property.

**B.29** We will pay for direct physical loss to covered other structures caused by a covered peril. We will not pay for loss excluded by this policy.

**B.30** We may repair, replace, or rebuild damaged property with material and workmanship of like kind and quality. We may make payment for the loss instead of completing repair, replacement, or rebuilding.

**B.31** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for covered loss. Payment for a loss will not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**B.32** Coverage under this provision applies only while the other structure is located on the residence premises. We do not cover an other structure after it is removed from the residence premises.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** "Coverage C" provides coverage for "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" while the property is anywhere in the world. The limit of liability for Coverage C is fifty percent of the limit of liability for Coverage A.

**C.2** We cover personal property that is usual or incidental to the occupancy of the "residence premises" by an insured. Coverage applies subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions and limits of this policy.

**C.3** "Covered property" means personal property for which coverage is provided under Coverage C. Covered property includes property acquired during the policy period if the property is otherwise eligible for coverage.

**C.4** At your request, we may cover property owned by a guest while that property is at the residence premises. Such coverage applies only while the property is in your care, custody or control.

**C.5** At your request, we may cover property owned by a residence employee while that property is at the residence premises. Such coverage does not increase the Coverage C limit of liability.

**C.6** We cover property of others only when the property is eligible for coverage under this policy. We may require evidence of ownership before we make payment.

**C.7** We cover "direct physical loss" to covered property only when caused by a peril covered by this policy. Direct physical loss must involve actual physical alteration, damage or destruction of covered property.

**C.8** We do not cover loss that is excluded by this policy. An exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event that contributes to the loss.

**C.9** A "special limit" is the greatest amount we will pay for covered property described by that limit. A special limit applies within, and does not increase, the Coverage C limit of liability.

**C.10** Special limits apply regardless of the ownership of covered property. Special limits apply whether covered property is owned individually, jointly, or held for the benefit of an insured.

**C.11** The most we will pay for loss to money, bank notes, bullion, coins, medals and precious metals is three hundred dollars. This special limit applies to property in any form and at any location.

**C.12** The most we will pay for loss by theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones is two thousand dollars. This special limit applies to articles, settings, mountings and related components.

**C.13** The most we will pay for loss by theft of firearms is three thousand dollars. This special limit includes firearms and related equipment.

**C.14** The most we will pay for loss to watercraft, including trailers, is one thousand five hundred dollars. This special limit includes furnishings, equipment and accessories used with the watercraft.

**C.15** The most we will pay for loss by theft of silverware is three thousand dollars. This special limit applies to silver-plated ware, gold-plated ware and pewterware.

**C.16** The most we will pay for loss to business property on the residence premises is three thousand dollars. This special limit applies to property used for business purposes.

**C.17** The most we will pay for loss to electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle is one thousand five hundred dollars. This special limit applies while the apparatus is in or upon a motor vehicle.

**C.18** A special limit applies even if the property is damaged, destroyed or stolen with other covered property. Payment under a special limit does not create additional coverage for related property.

**C.19** We may require an insured to identify covered property that is the subject of a claim. An insured must provide information sufficient for us to determine ownership, condition and value.

**C.20** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable efforts to preserve covered property for our inspection.

**C.21** An insured must retain damaged property when reasonably possible. An insured must not dispose of damaged property without our consent unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property.

**C.22** An insured must notify us promptly of a loss involving covered property. Notice must include available information concerning the cause, location and extent of the loss.

**C.23** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities promptly when theft, vandalism or other criminal conduct is suspected. An insured must cooperate with any lawful investigation concerning the loss.

**C.24** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed or stolen property when requested by us. The inventory must identify the property and include available evidence of its value and ownership.

**C.25** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, appraisals and other available documentation when requested by us. We may inspect originals or copies of such documentation.

**C.26** An insured must show us the covered property as often as reasonably required. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and the location where the loss occurred.

**C.27** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment and settlement of a claim. Cooperation includes providing truthful and complete information concerning the loss.

**C.28** We may examine an insured under oath concerning a claim. An insured must sign the examination record when requested by us.

**C.29** We may require a signed statement describing the loss. The statement must contain information reasonably necessary for us to evaluate coverage and damages.

**C.30** We may require proof that a claimed item was owned, used or possessed by an insured. Evidence of ownership may include purchase records, photographs, statements or other reliable information.

**C.31** We determine whether property is covered based on its character, use and location at the time of loss. A temporary change in location does not alter the character of property unless its use also changes.

**C.32** We may consider the condition of covered property immediately before the loss. We may consider wear, age, prior damage and market conditions when evaluating a claim.

**C.33** We do not cover loss to property after title has passed from an insured. We do not cover loss to property that an insured has abandoned.

**C.34** We do not cover loss to property that is unlawfully possessed by an insured. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's unlawful transfer, sale or disposal of property.

**C.35** We do not cover loss caused intentionally by an insured when the loss was expected or intended by that insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting damage differs in kind or degree from that intended.

**C.36** We do not cover loss caused by concealment, misrepresentation or fraud by an insured relating to coverage or a claim. This exclusion applies if the conduct is material to our investigation or settlement.

**C.37** We do not cover loss to property while it is being used for an unlawful purpose. We do not cover loss arising from unlawful conduct involving covered property.

**C.38** We do not cover loss to property that is separately described and specifically insured by another policy. This exclusion applies only to the extent that other insurance provides coverage for the same loss.

**C.39** We do not cover property that is owned by a person who is not an insured, except as otherwise provided by this section. A request for coverage does not establish ownership by an insured.

**C.40** We may pay the value of covered property that is damaged or destroyed. We may repair, replace or restore covered property when permitted by this policy.

**C.41** We may settle a claim with an insured or with the owner of covered property. Payment to a person lawfully entitled to receive payment discharges our obligation for that payment.

**C.42** We may take possession of damaged covered property after payment of a claim. An insured must transfer to us any interest in such property that is necessary to protect our rights.

**C.43** We may elect to repair or replace covered property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement property need not be identical if it performs substantially the same function.

**C.44** We are not required to replace property with property that has greater value, capacity or utility than the damaged property. Any betterment is at your expense unless we agree otherwise.

**C.45** An insured must not incur expense for repair or replacement without our consent, except for reasonable measures necessary to protect covered property from further damage. We may deny payment for expense that is unnecessary or unreasonable.

**C.46** An insured must preserve any right of recovery against a person or organization responsible for a loss. An insured must not impair our right to recover amounts paid under this policy.

**C.47** If we make payment for a loss, we are subrogated to the rights of an insured against a responsible person or organization. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing those rights.

**C.48** An insured may not assign a claim without our written consent. An assignment made without our consent does not bind us.

**C.49** Coverage for personal property applies only to a loss occurring during the policy period. The loss must be reported in accordance with the duties imposed by this policy.

**C.50** We may inspect covered property before or after a loss. An inspection does not constitute a representation that property is safe, sound or eligible for coverage.

**C.51** We may request information concerning the use of covered property. An insured must provide information concerning business, rental, personal or other use that may affect coverage.

**C.52** An insured must take reasonable care to maintain covered property. Failure to maintain property does not by itself establish that a loss is excluded.

**C.53** An insured must use reasonable means to prevent avoidable damage to covered property. Reasonable protective measures may include moving property away from an apparent source of danger.

**C.54** We do not cover the cost to upgrade, improve or redesign covered property. We do not cover increased value resulting solely from repair or replacement.

**C.55** We do not cover sentimental, emotional or personal value that exceeds the value otherwise payable under this policy. We do not cover loss of use, inconvenience or delay arising from damage to covered property.

**C.56** We do not cover a decrease in value that remains after covered property is repaired or replaced. We do not cover loss arising solely from a change in market value.

**C.57** If covered property is recovered after payment of a theft claim, we may require an insured to return the payment or transfer the recovered property to us. We will not require a return of payment that exceeds the value of the recovered property.

**C.58** An insured must notify us promptly if stolen property is recovered. An insured must preserve recovered property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**C.59** We may require an insured to submit to an examination concerning ownership or value of claimed property. An insured must provide records in the insured's possession, custody or control.

**C.60** We may deny coverage for prejudice caused by an insured's failure to comply with a duty under this section. A failure to comply must be material to our investigation, evaluation or settlement of the claim.

**C.61** Payment for covered property is subject to all applicable limits of liability. The Coverage C limit of liability is not increased by payment for property of others.

**C.62** Payment for a loss does not waive any policy provision. Our investigation, inspection, negotiation or partial payment does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**C.63** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation or incur expense for a loss without our consent. This duty does not prevent reasonable action necessary to protect persons or property from immediate harm.

**C.64** We may rely on information provided by an insured unless we have reason to question its accuracy. An insured must correct information that the insured knows is inaccurate or incomplete.

**C.65** We may request access to electronic records relating to ownership, purchase, repair or valuation of covered property. An insured must provide access that is reasonably available to the insured.

**C.66** We may require estimates for repair or replacement of damaged property. Estimates must be prepared by persons reasonably qualified to perform the proposed work.

**C.67** We may select persons to inspect, appraise, repair or replace covered property. An insured must provide reasonable access to the property for those purposes.

**C.68** A dispute concerning value does not create coverage for property that is not covered. A dispute concerning repair or replacement does not increase an applicable limit of liability.

**C.69** This coverage applies only to property that an insured has an insurable interest in at the time of loss. Our payment will not exceed an insured's financial interest in the covered property.

**C.70** We will adjust claims under Coverage C in accordance with this policy. An insured must comply with the duties and conditions applicable to a claim for personal property.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** The limit of liability for "Coverage D" is twenty percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". Payments under this coverage do not increase that limit.

**D.2** We cover the necessary increase in living expenses you incur when a "covered loss" makes the "residence premises" uninhabitable. The expenses must be necessary to maintain your normal standard of living.

**D.3** We will determine the necessary increase in living expenses by comparing expenses incurred after the covered loss with expenses that would have been incurred without the covered loss. Expenses that are not caused by the covered loss are not covered.

**D.4** We cover reasonable expenses for temporary lodging, meals, and related services when those expenses are necessary because the residence premises is uninhabitable. The expenses must result directly from the covered loss.

**D.5** We cover reasonable expenses to relocate household property when relocation is necessary because of the covered loss. We do not cover expenses for property that is not used by you or members of your household.

**D.6** We cover reasonable expenses for temporary storage of household property when storage is necessary because the residence premises is uninhabitable. Coverage applies only while the property cannot reasonably remain at the residence premises.

**D.7** If a part of the residence premises rented to others is made uninhabitable by a covered loss, we cover the "fair rental value" of that part. Fair rental value is reduced by expenses that do not continue while the rented part is uninhabitable.

**D.8** Fair rental value means the rental income that you would have received from the rented part of the residence premises. The rental income must be supported by a lease, rental agreement, rental history, or other reliable evidence.

**D.9** We cover fair rental value only when the rented part of the residence premises is unavailable for rental because of the covered loss. We do not cover lost rental income caused by a tenant's failure to occupy, pay rent, or comply with a rental agreement.

**D.10** We cover additional living expense and fair rental value when a "civil authority" prohibits access to the residence premises because of direct damage to nearby premises by a covered peril. The prohibition must result from the same covered peril that caused the direct damage.

**D.11** Coverage for a civil authority prohibition applies only while access to the residence premises is prohibited. We do not cover expenses or lost rental income after access is permitted.

**D.12** An insured must use reasonable means to protect property from further damage after a covered loss. An insured must also take reasonable steps to reduce additional living expense and loss of fair rental value.

**D.13** An insured must retain receipts, invoices, leases, rental agreements, and other records supporting a claim under Coverage D. An insured must provide those records to us upon request.

**D.14** An insured must notify us promptly of a change in the location where the household is temporarily living. An insured must also notify us promptly when the residence premises becomes fit to live in or available for rental.

**D.15** We do not cover expenses that are not reasonable or necessary. We do not cover expenses that exceed those required to maintain your normal standard of living.

**D.16** We do not cover expenses arising from your voluntary relocation when the residence premises remains fit to live in. We do not cover expenses arising from a personal preference for different accommodations.

**D.17** We do not cover expenses caused by an "excluded peril". This exclusion applies whether the excluded peril acts alone or with another cause of loss.

**D.18** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, rebuild, or restore the residence premises under Coverage D. We do not cover the cost to correct defects, improve property, or comply with ordinances.

**X.38** We do not cover loss caused by collapse, except as otherwise provided for covered collapse. We do not cover loss consisting only of settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion.

**X.39** We do not cover loss caused by pressure from roots, trees, shrubs, or vegetation. We also do not cover loss caused by the removal, trimming, cutting, or maintenance of vegetation.

**X.40** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural operations, industrial operations, or controlled burning. This exclusion does not depend upon where the smoke originates.

**X.41** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of toxic material. We also do not cover loss caused by the presence of toxic material in soil, water, air, or building materials.

**X.42** We do not cover loss caused by the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, possession, or disposal of controlled substances. This exclusion applies whether the controlled substance activity occurs on or away from the residence premises.

**X.43** We do not cover loss caused by the production, cultivation, processing, storage, or distribution of controlled substances. We also do not cover loss caused by property used to facilitate those activities.

**X.44** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by governmental authority. This exclusion applies whether the authority acts under actual or asserted legal power.

**X.53** We do not cover loss caused by theft, vandalism, or malicious mischief at a residence premises used primarily for business activities. This exclusion applies to business property and to property used in connection with business activities.

**X.56** We do not cover loss caused by the use of property for commercial storage, warehousing, manufacturing, processing, or assembly. This exclusion applies whether the activity is conducted by an insured or another person.

**X.62** We do not cover loss caused by intentional alteration of land, drainage, grading, or water flow. We also do not cover loss caused by the failure to maintain drainage systems, gutters, downspouts, or grading.

**X.63** We do not cover loss caused by the failure to preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property and records relating to the loss.

**X.64** We do not cover loss to property after transfer of ownership unless an insured retains an insurable interest in that property. An insured must notify us promptly of a transfer that affects the residence premises.

**X.65** We do not cover loss merely because the “minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage” applies. The application of that deductible does not alter any exclusion in this section.

## II.E — Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** We will pay, on behalf of an "insured", those "damages" for which an insured is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence" to which this coverage applies. Damages must arise from conduct by an insured or conduct for which an insured is legally responsible.

**E.2** We will provide a defense, at our expense, against a "claim" or "suit" seeking damages covered by this insurance. We may investigate and settle a claim or suit as we consider appropriate.

**E.3** Our duty to defend ends when the amount we pay for damages exhausts the limit of liability that applies. We have no duty to defend a claim or suit for damages not covered by this insurance.

**E.4** We will pay reasonable expenses incurred by an insured at our request in the investigation or defense of a claim or suit. We will also pay premiums on bonds required in a suit we defend, if available through a bond provider acceptable to us.

**E.5** This insurance applies to bodily injury or property damage arising from an occurrence at an "insured location" or arising from the personal activities of an insured. Coverage applies only to damages caused during the policy period.

**E.6** This insurance applies separately to each insured. This provision does not increase the limit of liability that applies.

**E.7** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage that is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury resulting from the use of reasonable force by an insured to protect persons or property.

**E.8** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a "business" conducted by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to activities ordinarily incident to nonbusiness pursuits.

**E.9** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle" by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to a motor vehicle used solely to service an insured location and not subject to motor vehicle registration.

**E.10** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an "aircraft" by an insured. We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "watercraft" by an insured.

**E.11** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the rendering or failure to render "professional services" by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the services are rendered for compensation or without compensation.

**E.12** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage for which an insured is obligated to pay damages because of liability assumed under a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**E.13** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of an obligation imposed by a workers compensation, disability benefits, or similar law. We do not cover bodily injury to a person eligible to receive benefits voluntarily provided by an insured under such a law.

**E.14** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, or used by an insured, except property damage caused by fire to property rented to an insured.

**E.15** We do not cover property damage to property in the care, custody, or control of an insured. We do not cover property damage to property over which an insured is exercising physical control.

**E.16** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of an "intentional loss" committed by or at the direction of an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of any claimed mental incapacity or impairment of the insured.

**E.17** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the manufacture, sale, distribution, possession, or use of a "controlled substance" by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of medication by a person acting under the direction of a licensed medical provider.

**E.18** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a "communicable disease" transmitted by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the transmission is alleged to be direct, indirect, actual, or alleged.

**E.19** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of "abuse", sexual molestation, corporal punishment, or physical or mental cruelty by an insured. We do not cover damages arising from an insured's failure to prevent, report, supervise, or respond to such conduct.

**E.20** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of "war", insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a "nuclear hazard".

**E.21** An insured must give us prompt notice of an occurrence that may result in a claim. An insured must provide the date, place, circumstances, names of injured persons, and names of available witnesses.

**E.22** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, or legal paper received in connection with a claim or suit. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit.

**E.23** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses incurred to protect persons or property from further injury or damage. An insured must assist us in securing and giving evidence and in obtaining the attendance of witnesses.

**E.24** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this coverage. An insured's rights of recovery against a person responsible for damages are transferred to us to the extent of our payment.

## II.F — Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

**F.1** We will pay "necessary medical expenses" incurred because of "bodily injury" caused by an "accident." Payment under this coverage is not dependent upon the legal liability of an "insured."

**F.2** Necessary medical expenses include reasonable charges for medical, surgical, dental, ambulance, hospital, professional nursing, prosthetic, and funeral services. The services must be necessary because of the bodily injury.

**F.3** Medical expenses must be incurred or medically ascertained within three years after the date of the accident. The bodily injury must arise from the same accident.

**F.4** We provide this coverage to a person who is on an "insured location" with the permission of an insured. The person must sustain bodily injury while on the insured location.

**F.5** We also provide this coverage to a person away from an insured location when the bodily injury arises out of a condition on the insured location or on ways immediately adjoining it. The condition must be attributable to an insured location.

**F.6** We also provide this coverage to a person away from an insured location when the bodily injury arises out of the activities of an insured. The activities must not be excluded by this coverage.

**F.7** We also provide this coverage to a person away from an insured location when the bodily injury is caused by a "residence employee" in the course of the residence employee's employment by an insured. The residence employee must be acting within the scope of assigned duties.

**F.8** We also provide this coverage to a person away from an insured location when the bodily injury is caused by an animal owned by or in the care of an insured. The animal must not be used in connection with a business.

**F.9** We may pay medical expenses directly to the person who incurred them or to a person or organization that provided services. Payment to any person or organization will satisfy our obligation to the extent of that payment.

**F.10** Payment of medical expenses is not an admission of liability by us or by an insured. Payment does not waive any right or defense available to us or to an insured.

**F.11** An insured must give us prompt notice of an accident that may result in a claim under this coverage. The notice must state the circumstances of the accident and the identity of the injured person, when known.

**F.12** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide information and records that we reasonably request.

**F.13** A person seeking payment must submit to physical examination by a physician selected by us when reasonably required. That person must authorize us to obtain medical reports and records relating to the bodily injury.

**F.14** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to you or a regular resident of your household. This exclusion does not apply to a residence employee.

**F.15** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to a person eligible to receive benefits voluntarily provided or required by law under a workers compensation law, nonoccupational disability law, or occupational disease law. This exclusion applies whether or not the benefits are claimed.

**F.16** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to a person, other than a residence employee, when the bodily injury arises out of premises owned by, rented to, or controlled by an insured and not an insured location. This exclusion applies whether the premises are occupied by an insured or by another person.

**F.17** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle" owned by, operated by, rented to, or loaned to an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of the location of the accident.

**F.18** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of "watercraft," "aircraft," or "hovercraft" owned by, operated by, rented to, or loaned to an insured. **G.57** An insured must not conceal information material to a claim or loss. An insured must correct material information previously given to us if the insured learns that information is inaccurate.

**G.63** Payment of a claim does not establish coverage for any other claim or loss. Each claim or loss is subject to the terms and conditions applicable to it.

**G.68** An insured must notify us of a material change in the use of the insured location. A material change includes use for business, rental, storage, construction, or other activity not previously disclosed to us.
