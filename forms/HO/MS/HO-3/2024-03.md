# HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2024-03)




## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** We will provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the "premium" and your compliance with the applicable terms of this policy. Coverage applies only as stated in this policy.

**AGR.2** This policy applies to an "insured" only while that person qualifies for coverage. You are responsible for ensuring that information furnished to us is accurate and complete.

**AGR.3** We will pay for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a "covered peril," subject to the applicable policy terms. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**AGR.4** We will pay damages for which an insured is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence." Our obligation is subject to the terms that apply to liability coverage.

**AGR.5** We will provide a defense for an insured against a "suit" seeking damages potentially covered by this policy. We may investigate or settle a "claim" as we consider appropriate.

**AGR.6** An insured must comply with the duties imposed by this policy after a loss, occurrence, claim, or suit. Failure to comply may affect coverage where the failure causes prejudice to us.

**AGR.7** The applicable "limit of liability" is the most we will pay for a covered loss or covered liability. Payment under this policy does not increase any applicable limit of liability.

**AGR.8** This policy applies only to loss or occurrence that takes place during the "policy period." Coverage is subject to the conditions existing when the loss or occurrence takes place.

**AGR.9** We will determine coverage based on the facts, the policy terms, and applicable law. No act by us in investigating, defending, or paying a claim waives any policy term.

**AGR.10** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to this insurance. We may deny coverage where concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud affects our rights.

**AGR.11** A change in risk, occupancy, ownership, or use may affect coverage. You must provide information we reasonably request to determine whether coverage remains available.

**AGR.12** This policy contains the agreement between you and us concerning the insurance provided. Its terms may be changed only by our authorized written action.

**AGR.13** If a policy term is determined to be invalid, the remaining terms remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. Our rights and your duties apply to each insured seeking coverage.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined with appropriate deduction for depreciation and obsolescence. It includes the value of used property in its condition immediately before damage. It does not include the cost to replace property with new property without deduction for depreciation. Market value may be considered when it reasonably reflects the value of the damaged property.



**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with material of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. It includes reasonable labor and material costs necessary to restore the property. It does not include any increase in value resulting from repair or replacement. It also does not include the value of land or the value of property that was not damaged.



**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. It includes accidental events that result in bodily injury or property damage. It does not include unrelated events merely because they arise during the same policy period. An event may be an occurrence even though its resulting injury or damage develops over time.



**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the property where you reside and that is described as the residence premises in this policy. It includes the dwelling and other structures used in connection with the property. It includes the part of the property used by you as a private residence. It does not include property where you do not reside unless it otherwise qualifies as an insured location.



**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are in your care. It includes a person using property covered by this policy with your permission when coverage applies to that use. It does not include a person who is merely visiting and is not a resident of your household. It also does not include a person acting outside the scope of permission you gave.



**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations qualifying for coverage under this policy. It includes land you own in connection with the residence premises and a location you use as a residence. It includes a location where an insured is temporarily residing. It does not include property used solely for business purposes unless coverage applies to that property.



**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain. It includes activities conducted from an insured location and activities conducted away from an insured location. It includes the rental of property when the rental activity is conducted for economic gain. It does not include volunteer activities for which no economic gain is received.



**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle or conveyance designed for travel on public roads. It includes attached machinery or equipment used with that vehicle or conveyance. It includes a trailer designed to be towed by a motor vehicle. It does not include a vehicle or conveyance designed solely for use on property where you reside and not subject to motor vehicle registration.



**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. It includes a person performing domestic services for an insured. It includes a person performing maintenance work for an insured at an insured location. It does not include a person performing services solely in connection with business activities.



**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. It includes resulting death. It includes physical injury arising from exposure to harmful conditions. It does not include purely emotional, reputational, or financial injury that is not caused by bodily harm, sickness, or disease.



**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. It includes loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured. It includes damage to personal property and real property. It does not include diminished value or other economic loss without physical injury to tangible property.



**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means an event that is abrupt, unintended, and unexpected from the standpoint of an insured. It includes damage resulting directly from an unforeseen discharge or release. It does not include damage caused by an event that was expected or intended by an insured. In this determination, the claims manual's duration test applies the definitionthe claims manual's duration test applies the definition (see Property Claims Handling Manual, C3 Chapter 3 — Water Losses).



**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of occupants and customary furnishings sufficient to show normal residential use. It includes a residence premises that is not being used as a home and lacks property ordinarily associated with occupancy. It does not include a furnished residence premises that is temporarily unoccupied. It also does not include property occupied by a tenant or other lawful resident.



**DEF.14** "named storm" means a storm that has been assigned a name by a governmental weather authority. It includes a tropical cyclone bearing an assigned name. It includes wind, rain, storm surge, and other conditions directly associated with that storm. It does not include an unnamed windstorm or other weather event.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We insure the “dwelling” described in the Declarations under Coverage A. The dwelling includes the residence premises and the structures attached to it. Coverage applies only when the property is used for residential purposes, subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions of this policy.

**A.2** We insure the dwelling against direct physical loss. We determine whether the loss is covered based on the cause of loss and the condition of the property when the loss occurs. Coverage does not apply to property that is not part of the dwelling, except as otherwise provided by this policy.

**A.3** The residence premises means the location where you reside and that is described in the Declarations. The residence premises includes the dwelling and the grounds immediately surrounding it. Coverage applies while the premises remains principally used as your private residence, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

**A.4** We insure building equipment and permanently installed fixtures that service the dwelling. This includes heating, cooling, plumbing, electrical, and permanently installed communications equipment. Coverage applies only to equipment located at or serving the dwelling, subject to applicable exclusions.

**A.5** We insure materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises when they are intended for construction, alteration, or repair of the dwelling. The materials and supplies must be owned by you or held by you for that work. Coverage applies only while the materials and supplies remain intended for use at the residence premises.

**A.6** We insure additions, alterations, fixtures, and improvements that you own or are legally responsible for as a tenant. The property must be contained within the dwelling or be attached to it. Coverage does not apply to property that you are not legally responsible to insure, except that coverage may apply when you have assumed that responsibility by written agreement.

**A.7** We insure permanently attached structures that are used with the dwelling. This includes attached garages, porches, decks, and similar structures. Coverage applies only while the structure remains attached to and serves the dwelling.

**A.8** We insure the dwelling when it is temporarily unoccupied. Unoccupancy does not by itself change the property insured under Coverage A. Coverage remains subject to all applicable conditions and exclusions, unless a limitation specifically applies to an unoccupied dwelling.

**A.9** We insure the dwelling when it is being repaired, renovated, or remodeled. The work must be performed at the residence premises and must not change the property into a structure principally used for a business purpose. Coverage applies to the existing dwelling and to covered materials and supplies, subject to all applicable exclusions.

**A.10** We settle covered damage to the dwelling on a replacement cost basis when the dwelling meets the insured-to-value threshold of eighty percent. Subject to the applicable loss settlement provisions, we determine the replacement cost without deduction for depreciation. We do not pay more than the cost to repair or replace the damaged property with property of like kind and quality.

**A.11** We pay the reasonable cost to repair or replace covered damaged property. The repair or replacement must be necessary to restore the property to substantially the condition it was in immediately before the loss. Payment is limited to the amount necessary to perform the covered work, subject to the policy limit and applicable loss settlement provisions.

**A.12** We may pay the value of the damaged property instead of repairing or replacing it. We may also repair or replace the damaged property with equivalent property. We will notify you of our choice within a reasonable time after receiving the information necessary to evaluate the loss.

**A.13** We settle covered damage to roof surfacing on the basis applicable to the dwelling, unless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attachedunless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attached (see HO 23 74 Actual Cash Value Loss Settlement — Roof Surfacing (Edition 2025-05), W.1 Coverage Provided). Subject to that endorsement, we do not apply a scheduled roof surfacing limitation. We determine the amount payable from the covered damage and the applicable loss settlement provisions.

**A.14** We do not cover land, including the land on which the dwelling stands. We do not cover water, trees, plants, or other natural features as part of the dwelling. Coverage may apply to covered property located on the land when that property is otherwise insured under Coverage A.

**A.15** We do not cover detached structures under Coverage A. A detached structure is insured only when coverage for that structure is provided elsewhere in this policy. This limitation does not remove coverage for a structure that is physically attached to the dwelling.

**A.16** We do not cover property used exclusively for a business conducted at the residence premises. We do not cover property that is held for sale or used to supply goods or services to others. This limitation applies to the property and not merely to the activity conducted there, subject to any coverage that otherwise applies under this policy.

**A.17** We do not cover property that is insured under a separate insurance policy. We do not cover any amount that another insurer is required to pay for the same loss. This limitation does not prevent us from paying covered loss when the other insurance does not fully satisfy the covered damage.

**A.18** You must protect the dwelling from further damage after a loss. You must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property from additional damage. Unless we approve otherwise, you must keep receipts and records of those repairs.

**A.19** You must give us prompt notice of a loss to the dwelling. Your notice must identify the property affected and describe the circumstances known to you. You must provide additional information when we reasonably request it to investigate or adjust the loss.

**A.20** You must preserve the damaged property for our inspection. You must not dispose of property that may be needed to determine the cause or amount of loss. You may remove property when necessary to protect it from further damage, provided that you keep the damaged property available for inspection.

**A.21** You must provide us with reasonable access to the dwelling after a loss. You must permit us to inspect the property and investigate the cause and extent of damage. We may enter the dwelling at reasonable times, subject to applicable law and your rights of occupancy.

**A.22** You must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of a loss. You must provide relevant records, receipts, estimates, photographs, and other information in your possession. You must not intentionally conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning the property or the loss.

**A.23** You must obtain our consent before beginning permanent repairs when the proposed work may affect the determination of coverage or the amount of loss. Emergency measures may be taken when reasonably necessary to protect the dwelling. Any permanent repair must be performed in a workmanlike manner.

**A.24** We may require you to submit to an examination under oath concerning a loss. You must answer questions separately from any other insured when we make that request. You must sign the transcript of the examination when we require a signed transcript.

**A.25** We may require a sworn proof of loss describing the property, the cause of loss, and the amount claimed. You must complete and return the proof of loss within the time required by this policy. We may request supporting documents when they are reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim.

**A.26** We may pay a covered claim to you and any mortgagee shown in the Declarations. We may also pay a person or entity that has a legal interest in the damaged dwelling. Payment to one person does not increase the amount we owe for the covered loss.

**A.27** We do not cover increased costs caused solely by an ordinance or law unless coverage for those costs is otherwise provided by this policy. We do not cover improvements that are not required to repair covered damage. This limitation does not prevent us from paying the reasonable cost of complying with applicable requirements when such payment is covered.

**A.28** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, construction, renovation, or maintenance. We do cover ensuing loss that is otherwise covered and that results from a covered cause of loss. This provision does not restore coverage for the faulty, inadequate, or defective property itself.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We insure property covered under "Coverage B" at the residence premises. The limit of insurance for Coverage B is ten percent of the limit of insurance for "Coverage A". Subject to that limit, we provide the coverages stated in this section.

**B.2** "Other structures" are structures set apart from the dwelling by clear space. A structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection is also an other structure. Unless the structure is connected in that manner, a structure attached to the dwelling is part of the dwelling.

**B.3** We cover other structures on the residence premises. The other structures must be owned by you or be held by you in a manner that gives you an insurable interest. Provided that the structure is located on the residence premises, coverage applies whether or not you occupy the structure.

**B.4** We cover a structure that is separated from the dwelling by clear space. A structure may be separated by landscaping, a walkway, or other property without losing its character as an other structure. Except that, a structure connected to the dwelling by a permanent foundation is not a separate other structure.

**B.5** We cover a structure that is connected to the dwelling only by a fence. We also cover a structure connected only by a utility line or similar connection. Unless the connection permits use of the structure as part of the dwelling, the structure remains an other structure.

**B.6** We cover an other structure used solely for private purposes. We cover the structure when it is used for storage, recreation, or another nonbusiness purpose. Subject to the exclusions in this section, incidental personal use does not change the coverage provided.

**B.7** We do not cover an other structure rented or held for rental to any person who is not a tenant of the dwelling. We do not cover an other structure rented for any purpose other than a private garage. Except that, coverage applies when the structure is rented to a tenant of the dwelling for that tenant's private use.

**B.8** We do not cover an other structure used in whole or in part for business. We do not cover an other structure used to conduct, store property for, or support a business. Unless the business use is only incidental to the private use of the structure, the exclusion applies to the entire structure.

**B.9** We do not cover an other structure used to store business property. We do not cover property of a business that is kept in an other structure. Provided that, personal property used for a private purpose remains subject to the applicable coverage for that property.

**B.10** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We do not cover soil, plants, trees, shrubs, or landscaping as an other structure. Except that, property attached to or forming part of a covered structure may be covered when damaged by a covered loss.

**B.11** We do not cover an other structure used to house farm animals or livestock. We do not cover an enclosure, shelter, or building used for that purpose. Subject to the other exclusions in this section, a structure used only for private domestic animals is not excluded by this provision.

**B.12** We do not cover an other structure used to manufacture, process, or distribute goods. We do not cover machinery, equipment, or supplies used for those activities. Unless the property is used solely for a private purpose, this exclusion applies regardless of whether income is received.

**B.13** We do not cover an other structure used to store flammable, explosive, or hazardous materials for a business. We do not cover containers, equipment, or fixtures used for that storage. Provided that, ordinary household quantities kept for private use are not excluded by this provision.

**B.14** We cover permanently installed fixtures and equipment that are part of a covered other structure. We cover those fixtures and equipment when they are damaged by a covered cause of loss. Except that, property used for a business is not covered under this provision.

**B.15** We cover an other structure while it is undergoing repair or alteration. We cover materials and supplies intended for that repair or alteration when they are at the residence premises. Unless the structure is used for a business, the temporary condition of the structure does not remove coverage.

**B.16** We cover an other structure that is temporarily moved from the residence premises for necessary repair. We cover the structure while it is at a location where the repair is being performed. Subject to the applicable exclusions, coverage ends when the structure is permanently relocated.

**B.17** We cover an other structure that is vacant. We cover the structure when it is unoccupied, provided it remains maintained and intended for a covered use. Except that, damage caused by neglect, deterioration, or lack of maintenance is not covered.

**B.18** We cover an other structure that is under construction for private use. We cover materials and supplies at the residence premises that will become part of the structure. Unless the construction is undertaken for a business purpose, the structure remains covered during construction.

**B.19** We cover an other structure owned jointly by you and another person. We cover your interest in the structure when you have an insurable interest in it. Provided that, we do not pay more than your interest in the damaged property.

**B.20** We cover an other structure that is subject to a lien, mortgage, or similar interest. We cover the structure without regard to the existence of that interest. Subject to the rights of any person legally entitled to payment, we pay the covered loss according to your interest.

**B.21** We do not cover an other structure that is not maintained in reasonably good condition. We do not cover damage resulting from wear and tear, deterioration, or defective maintenance. Unless a covered cause of loss independently causes the damage, the exclusion applies to the resulting loss as well.

**B.22** We do not cover an other structure damaged by insects, birds, rodents, or other animals. We do not cover damage caused by nesting, infestation, or animal occupancy. Except that, we cover ensuing damage caused by a covered cause of loss when that damage is otherwise covered.

**B.23** We do not cover an other structure damaged by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion. We do not cover damage caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical breakdown. Subject to the other exclusions in this section, ensuing damage caused by a covered cause of loss may be covered.

**B.24** We do not cover an other structure damaged by flood, surface water, or waterborne material. We do not cover damage caused by water that backs up through a drain or sump. Unless a covered cause of loss independently causes the damage, loss resulting from those causes is not covered.

**B.25** We do not cover an other structure damaged by earth movement. We do not cover damage caused by earthquake, landslide, mudslide, or subsidence. Provided that, ensuing damage caused by a covered cause of loss is not excluded solely because earth movement occurred.

**B.26** We do not cover an other structure damaged by governmental action. We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or demolition ordered by a governmental authority. Except that, we cover ensuing loss caused by a covered cause of loss when the loss is otherwise covered.

**B.27** We do not cover an other structure damaged by war or nuclear hazard. We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by those causes. Unless another covered cause of loss independently causes the damage, the exclusion applies to the resulting loss.

**B.28** We do not cover an other structure damaged intentionally by you or at your direction. We do not cover loss resulting from an act committed by an insured with the intent to cause damage. Subject to the rights of an insured who did not participate in the act, this exclusion applies to the loss.

**B.29** You must notify us promptly of a change in the use, ownership, or condition of an other structure. You must take reasonable steps to protect the structure from further damage after a loss. Provided that, you must not make permanent repairs or dispose of damaged property without giving us a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**B.30** You must provide us with records, receipts, and other information we reasonably request concerning an other structure and a covered loss. You must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of a claim. Unless we agree otherwise, you must preserve the damaged property for our inspection.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover personal property owned or used by an "insured". We cover that property while it is at the "residence premises" shown in the Declarations. We also cover the property while it is anywhere in the world, subject to the provisions that follow.

**C.2** "Coverage C" applies to personal property that is usual to a household. The property may be owned by you or by a member of your household who resides with you. We cover the property only to the extent that it is not excluded or limited by this policy.

**C.3** The limit of liability for Coverage C is fifty percent of the Coverage A limit. The Coverage C limit applies to the total amount of insurance available for covered personal property. Subject to the terms of this policy, the limit is not increased because covered property is located at more than one place.

**C.4** We cover personal property belonging to a guest or household employee while the property is at the residence premises. We cover the property only if the guest or household employee does not have insurance that applies to the property. Unless the property is otherwise excluded, our payment is limited by the applicable Coverage C limit.

**C.5** We cover personal property belonging to a student who is an insured while the student is temporarily residing away from the residence premises. The student must be related to an insured and must have been a resident of the insured's household before moving away. Coverage does not apply to property kept at a separate residence owned, rented or occupied by the student.

**C.6** We cover personal property newly acquired by an insured. The property must be intended for personal, family or household use. Subject to all policy conditions, coverage applies from the time the property becomes owned by an insured.

**C.7** We cover personal property that is being moved to a new residence occupied by an insured. Coverage applies while the property is in transit or at the new residence. Unless the property is otherwise covered, we do not cover property left at the former residence after the move is completed.

**C.8** We cover personal property in a bank, safe-deposit facility or other storage facility. The property must be owned or used by an insured. Coverage is subject to the applicable limit and to the exclusions that apply to personal property away from the residence premises.

**C.9** We cover personal property in a dwelling regularly occupied by an insured but not shown as the residence premises. The dwelling must be used by an insured for personal, family or household purposes. We do not cover property kept in such a dwelling if the dwelling is used primarily for business.

**C.10** We cover personal property in a condominium unit, cooperative unit or apartment occupied by an insured. The property must be intended for use in that residence. Subject to the policy provisions governing property away from the residence premises, coverage applies while the property is there.

**C.11** We cover personal property of an insured while the property is at a place temporarily occupied by an insured. The place must be used for personal, family or household purposes. Coverage does not apply to property at a place that an insured owns but does not occupy.

**C.12** We cover personal property while it is in the custody of a person to whom an insured has entrusted it. The custody must be for personal, family or household purposes. Unless the loss results from a covered cause of loss, we do not cover property that is being repaired, serviced or altered.

**C.13** We cover clothing, luggage, household goods and furnishings owned or used by an insured. We cover these items whether they are kept at the residence premises or temporarily away from it. The property must be used primarily for personal, family or household purposes.

**C.14** We cover appliances and household equipment owned by an insured. We cover the equipment while it is installed, stored or being used for household purposes. We do not cover equipment that is permanently installed in property not owned by an insured.

**C.15** We cover personal property used to furnish or maintain the residence premises. The property includes furniture, rugs, window coverings and similar household items. Subject to the applicable exclusions, coverage applies whether the property is in use or in storage.

**C.16** We cover personal property that is temporarily outside the residence premises for cleaning, repair, restoration or preservation. The work must be performed for an insured. Unless the loss is caused by a covered cause of loss, we do not cover damage resulting from the work.

**C.17** We cover personal property while it is being transported by an insured. The transportation must be for personal, family or household purposes. We do not cover property damaged because it was packed, loaded, carried or unloaded improperly.

**C.18** We cover personal property while it is at a school, workplace, place of worship or similar location. The property must be owned or used by an insured for personal purposes. Coverage is subject to the special limits and exclusions that apply to property away from the residence premises.

**C.19** We cover personal property used by an insured in a home office. The property must be used primarily for personal purposes. Property used primarily in a business is subject to the special limit and exclusions applicable to business property.

**C.20** We cover personal property owned by an insured that is used for entertainment, recreation or communication. The property includes equipment and accessories used with that property. We do not cover software, data or other information except as provided by this policy.

**C.21** We cover electronic apparatus owned by an insured. The apparatus must be designed for personal, family or household use. Subject to the applicable exclusions, coverage applies whether the apparatus is operating, stored or being transported.

**C.22** The most we will pay for money and precious metals is three hundred dollars. Except that this limitation applies to money and precious metals used for personal, family or household purposes, it does not apply to property that is not within this class. The limitation applies regardless of the number of items, owners or locations involved in the loss.

**C.23** We cover money and precious metals only when they are lost by a covered cause of loss. Money includes currency, coins, stored-value cards and similar items having monetary value. Unless otherwise covered, we do not cover accounts, funds, securities or records that represent money.

**C.24** The most we will pay for loss by theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones is two thousand dollars. Subject to this limitation, we cover jewelry, watches and precious stones owned or used by an insured. This limitation applies whether the property is worn, stored, displayed or temporarily entrusted to another person.

**C.25** We cover jewelry, watches and precious stones when they are lost by a covered cause of loss other than theft. The property must be owned or used by an insured. Unless the loss is caused by a covered cause of loss, we do not cover damage resulting from ordinary wear, marring or deterioration.

**C.26** The most we will pay for loss by theft of firearms is three thousand dollars. Except that this limitation applies only to firearms and property used with firearms, it does not limit other covered personal property. The limitation applies whether the firearms are kept at the residence premises or elsewhere.

**C.27** We cover firearms owned or used by an insured. We cover ammunition and equipment used with a covered firearm only when the ammunition or equipment is also covered personal property. We do not cover a firearm that is illegal for an insured to possess.

**C.28** The most we will pay for loss to watercraft, including trailers, is one thousand five hundred dollars. Subject to this limitation, we cover watercraft owned or used by an insured and equipment used with that watercraft. The limitation applies to watercraft and trailers whether they are on land, in water or being transported.

**C.29** We cover watercraft and their trailers when they are lost by a covered cause of loss. We cover oars, life preservers and similar equipment only when the equipment is owned or used by an insured. Unless the loss is caused by a covered cause of loss, we do not cover damage resulting from use, loading or unloading.

**C.30** The most we will pay for loss by theft of silverware, goldware and pewterware is three thousand dollars. Except that this limitation applies to items made of or plated with silver, gold or pewter, it does not apply to ordinary household utensils. The limitation applies to property owned or used by an insured.

**C.31** We cover silverware, goldware and pewterware when they are lost by a covered cause of loss other than theft. The property must be used primarily for personal, family or household purposes. We do not cover property used primarily as a business inventory.

**C.32** The most we will pay for business property on the residence premises is three thousand dollars. Subject to this limitation, we cover business property owned by an insured while it is on the residence premises. The limitation applies whether the business property is used by an insured or by another person.

**C.33** We cover business property while it is on the residence premises when the property is lost by a covered cause of loss. Business property includes property used in a trade, profession or occupation. Unless the property is covered under another provision, we do not cover business property located away from the residence premises.

**C.34** The most we will pay for electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle is one thousand five hundred dollars. Except that this limitation applies while the apparatus is in or upon a motor vehicle, it does not limit the apparatus while it is at the residence premises. The limitation applies whether the motor vehicle is owned, leased or used by an insured.

**C.35** We cover electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle when the apparatus is designed to be powered by the vehicle's electrical system. We also cover accessories used with the apparatus when they are personal property of an insured. Unless the apparatus is permanently installed in the motor vehicle, coverage applies only while the apparatus is in or upon the vehicle.

**C.36** We cover personal property that is separately described in the Declarations. The property must be owned by an insured or must be in an insured's care. Subject to the terms of the scheduled coverage, the property remains subject to this policy's exclusions.

**C.37** We cover personal property that an insured has purchased under a conditional sale agreement. The agreement must make an insured responsible for the property. We do not cover a loss that is solely the failure to make required payments under the agreement.

**C.38** We cover personal property that an insured has borrowed from another person. The property must be in an insured's possession for personal, family or household use. Unless the insured is legally responsible for the loss, we do not pay for damage to borrowed property.

**C.39** We cover personal property that an insured has rented for personal, family or household use. The rental agreement must make an insured responsible for the property. We do not cover a loss caused by failure to return the property as required by the rental agreement.

**C.40** We cover improvements and betterments made by an insured to property occupied by an insured. The improvements and betterments must be intended for personal, family or household use. Subject to the insured's legal interest in the property, payment is limited to the amount necessary to repair or replace the improvements and betterments.

**C.41** We cover grave markers, mausoleums and similar property owned by an insured. The property must be located on or away from the residence premises. We do not cover the cost of routine care, maintenance or preservation.

**C.42** We cover plants, shrubs and trees that are personal property of an insured. The property must be located on the residence premises and must be intended for personal use. Unless the loss is caused by a covered cause of loss, we do not cover damage caused by disease, insects or normal growth.

**C.43** We cover outdoor equipment owned or used by an insured. The equipment must be intended for personal, family or household purposes. We do not cover equipment that is permanently attached to land or a structure unless it is personal property under this policy.

**C.44** We cover tools and equipment owned by an insured and used for personal purposes. The property may be used to maintain the residence premises or personal property. Subject to the special limit applicable to business property, coverage does not apply to tools and equipment used primarily in a business.

**C.45** We cover bicycles, recreational equipment and similar personal property owned or used by an insured. The property must be used primarily for personal, family or household purposes. We do not cover a motor vehicle that is required to be registered for use on public roads.

**C.46** We cover parts and accessories for a motor vehicle when they are not installed in the vehicle. The parts and accessories must be owned by an insured and intended for personal use. Unless the loss is caused by a covered cause of loss, we do not cover damage resulting from installation or use.

**C.47** We cover animals owned by an insured only when this policy specifically provides coverage for the resulting property damage. Animals are not treated as personal property for purposes of valuing their lives. We do not cover veterinary expense, boarding expense or other expense resulting from illness or injury.

**C.48** We cover documents, manuscripts and other written material owned by an insured. We pay for the cost of blank paper and similar material when replacement is necessary because of a covered loss. Subject to the applicable valuation provision, we do not pay for the value of information recorded on the material.

**C.49** We cover photographs, recordings and media owned by an insured. We pay for the cost of the blank material when replacement is necessary because of a covered loss. Unless otherwise covered, we do not pay for the cost of recreating the subject matter or information contained on the media.

**C.50** We cover tickets, certificates and similar documents owned by an insured. We pay only for the amount that cannot be recovered from the issuer or another responsible person. We do not cover anticipated profit, expected appreciation or other speculative value.

**C.51** We cover property that is in the possession of an insured for safekeeping. The property must be intended to be returned to its owner. Unless the insured is legally responsible for the loss, we do not cover the property.

**C.52** We cover property that an insured has received as a gift. The property must have been accepted and retained by an insured. We do not cover a gift that is merely promised, ordered or awaiting delivery.

**C.53** We cover property that an insured is holding for sale only when the property is personal property of an insured. Property held as business inventory is subject to the limitation and exclusions applicable to business property. Unless the property is lost by a covered cause of loss, we do not cover a reduction in sale value.

**C.54** We cover personal property that is temporarily removed from the residence premises for protection from a covered peril. The removal must be reasonably necessary because of an imminent loss. Subject to the policy provisions applying away from the residence premises, coverage continues while the property is removed for that purpose.

**C.55** We cover personal property that is damaged while being moved to protect it from a covered peril. The movement must be reasonably necessary to prevent or reduce a covered loss. We do not cover damage caused by moving property when no such protection is reasonably necessary.

**C.56** We cover personal property that is damaged by smoke, soot or ash when the damage results from a covered cause of loss. The property must be owned or used by an insured. Unless the damage results from a sudden and accidental event, we do not cover damage caused by repeated or continuous exposure.

**C.57** We cover personal property damaged by water when the water results from a covered cause of loss. The property must be located where an insured has an insurable interest. We do not cover damage caused by flood, surface water or water that backs up through a sewer or drain unless coverage is otherwise provided.

**C.58** We cover personal property damaged by power interruption when the interruption results from a covered cause of loss. The interruption must occur after the covered cause of loss damages property covered by this policy. Unless the interruption results from damage to property at the residence premises, we do not cover resulting loss.

**C.59** We cover personal property damaged by a change in temperature when the change results from a covered cause of loss. The covered cause of loss must damage the equipment that supplies or controls the temperature. We do not cover loss caused by the failure to maintain or repair the equipment.

**C.60** We cover personal property damaged by falling objects when the damage results from a covered cause of loss. The falling object must cause direct physical loss to the property. We do not cover damage caused by an object that is part of the property or by settling, cracking or pressure.

**C.61** We cover personal property damaged by weight of ice, snow or sleet when the damage results from a covered cause of loss. The property must be located at the residence premises or at another covered location. We do not cover damage to property that is not reasonably protected from the weather.

**C.62** We cover personal property damaged by collapse when the damage results from a covered cause of loss. The collapse must be sudden and result in direct physical loss to the property. Unless the collapse is caused by a covered cause of loss, we do not cover damage caused by settling, cracking, shrinking or expansion.

**C.63** We do not cover loss to property caused by wear and tear, marring or deterioration. We also do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect or mechanical breakdown. This exclusion applies unless a covered cause of loss results in direct physical loss.

**C.64** We do not cover loss to property caused by insects, rodents, birds or vermin. We also do not cover damage caused by nesting or infestation. Subject to the policy provisions governing ensuing loss, this exclusion applies whether the damage is direct or indirect.

**C.65** We do not cover loss to property caused by theft from an unattended motor vehicle unless there is visible evidence of forcible entry. The motor vehicle must be locked at the time of the theft. This exclusion does not apply to property in the custody of a common carrier.

**C.66** We do not cover loss to property caused by seizure, confiscation or destruction by order of a governmental authority. We also do not cover property illegally acquired, possessed or transported. Unless the governmental action is taken to prevent the spread of fire, this exclusion applies regardless of the reason for the action.

**C.67** We do not cover loss to property caused by intentional acts of an insured. We also do not cover loss caused by a person acting at the direction of an insured. This exclusion applies whether the resulting damage is expected or unexpected.

**C.68** We do not cover loss to property caused by abandonment, neglect or voluntary parting with title. We also do not cover loss caused by a dishonest or fraudulent act of an insured. Subject to the rights of an innocent insured, this exclusion applies even if another cause contributes to the loss.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** “Coverage D” provides the coverage described in this section for loss of use of your “Residence Premises”. The limit for Coverage D is twenty percent of the Coverage A limit. Subject to the provisions that follow, we will pay only for loss of use resulting from a covered loss.

**D.2** We cover the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living. This coverage applies when a covered loss makes the Residence Premises uninhabitable. The increase in living expenses must be reasonable and necessary.

**D.3** We cover the necessary expenses you incur to obtain temporary living arrangements. This coverage includes reasonable expenses for lodging, meals, transportation, and other customary household needs. We cover only the amount by which those expenses exceed your normal living expenses.

**D.4** You must make reasonable efforts to maintain your normal standard of living while the Residence Premises is uninhabitable. You must keep records of the expenses for which you seek payment. We may require receipts or other evidence supporting the claimed expenses.

**D.5** We cover the necessary loss of rental income if a portion of the Residence Premises rented to others becomes uninhabitable because of a covered loss. This coverage applies to the rental income you would have received from the portion of the Residence Premises that cannot be occupied. Subject to the terms of the rental agreement, we will pay only the rental income that would reasonably have been collected.

**D.6** We cover the reasonable rental value of that portion of the Residence Premises which is rented to others or held for rental when it becomes uninhabitable because of a covered loss. This coverage applies whether or not a tenant remains obligated to pay rent. We will determine the rental value from the prevailing rental value of comparable premises.

**D.7** We cover the necessary increase in living expenses and the loss of rental income resulting from a covered loss to the Residence Premises. We do not cover expenses or loss of income that would have been incurred without the covered loss. You must provide information reasonably necessary to establish the amount of the expense or loss.

**D.8** We cover loss of use when a civil authority prohibits use of the Residence Premises because a covered loss has caused direct damage to a neighboring premises. This coverage applies when the prohibition is intended to prevent a dangerous condition resulting from that damage. The prohibited use must affect your use of the Residence Premises.

**D.9** We do not cover loss of use resulting from a civil authority prohibition that does not arise from direct damage to a neighboring premises. We do not cover loss of use resulting from a general evacuation or precautionary instruction. We do not cover loss of use when the Residence Premises remains available for your normal use.

**D.10** We cover loss of use only when the covered loss is the direct cause of the inability to use the Residence Premises. You must notify us promptly when the Residence Premises becomes uninhabitable or its use is prohibited. We may inspect the Residence Premises and the temporary living arrangements.

**D.11** We cover the necessary expenses you incur to reduce the amount of loss of use. You must take reasonable steps to avoid or reduce expenses for which you seek payment. We will not pay expenses that could reasonably have been avoided.

**D.12** You must cooperate with our investigation of the covered loss. You must provide information about your household, occupancy, rental arrangements, and living expenses when reasonably requested. We will use that information to determine the amount payable under this coverage.

**D.13** We cover the expenses resulting from the uninhabitability of the Residence Premises until you can reasonably resume normal use. The expenses must result from the covered loss and must be incurred by you. Subject to the remaining provisions of this section, we will not pay expenses incurred after normal use can reasonably be resumed.

**D.14** We cover the rental value of the Residence Premises when you rent it to others and a covered loss makes it uninhabitable. You must provide the rental agreement and other information reasonably necessary to establish the rental value. Unless the rental agreement provides otherwise, we will determine the rental value based on the rent payable for the affected premises.

**D.15** We do not cover loss of rental income caused by the cancellation of a rental agreement when the Residence Premises remains habitable. We do not cover loss of rental income caused by a tenant’s failure to pay rent unrelated to a covered loss. We do not cover expenses incurred to enforce a rental agreement.

**D.16** We cover the necessary expenses for moving and storing property when those expenses result directly from a covered loss. You must use reasonable care in selecting the moving and storage services. We do not cover expenses resulting from your voluntary relocation unrelated to the covered loss.

**D.17** We cover the necessary expenses for reconnecting or transferring household services when those expenses result directly from a covered loss. The services must be services customarily used by your household at the Residence Premises. We do not cover charges for improved or expanded services.

**D.18** We cover the necessary expenses for transportation when the expenses result from temporary living arrangements caused by a covered loss. The transportation must be reasonably necessary for work, education, medical care, or customary household activities. We do not cover transportation expenses resulting from your choice of temporary living arrangements when less costly arrangements were reasonably available.

**D.19** We cover the necessary expenses for meals when the expenses result from temporary living arrangements caused by a covered loss. We will consider your normal meal expenses in determining the amount of the increase. We do not cover charges for alcoholic beverages, entertainment, or other expenses unrelated to meals.

**D.20** We do not cover loss of use caused by your failure to comply with reasonable requirements for protecting the Residence Premises after a covered loss. We do not cover additional expenses caused by your refusal to cooperate with reasonable repairs. We do not cover expenses resulting from your delay in returning to the Residence Premises when it is reasonably fit for occupancy.

**D.21** You must notify us if the condition of the Residence Premises changes in a way that affects your need for temporary living arrangements. You must notify us if you obtain other accommodations without continuing to incur the claimed expenses. We may adjust payment to reflect the changed circumstances.

**D.22** We cover reasonable expenses incurred to secure the Residence Premises while it is uninhabitable because of a covered loss. The expenses must be necessary to protect the Residence Premises from further damage or unauthorized entry. We do not cover expenses for improvements, alterations, or optional security features.

**D.23** We do not cover expenses resulting from the voluntary sale, abandonment, or permanent relocation of the Residence Premises. We do not cover expenses resulting from a decision to occupy a residence that provides a higher standard of living than the Residence Premises. We do not cover the additional cost attributable solely to that decision.

**D.24** We will pay covered loss of use directly to you unless you direct us in writing to pay another person. You must assign to us any rights of recovery against a person responsible for the loss, to the extent of our payment. Payment under this coverage does not increase the limit for Coverage D.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure against risk of "direct physical loss" to "covered property" only when the loss is caused by a peril that is not excluded or limited by this policy. Loss must be accidental and must occur during the policy period.

**P.2** "Covered property" means property described as insured under this policy that is located at an "insured location." Property is covered only while it is owned by you or while you have a legal responsibility for it.

**P.3** An "insured location" means the residence premises shown in the policy declarations and land used in connection with that premises. An insured location also includes property temporarily occupied by an insured as a residence, subject to the terms of this policy.

**P.4** We insure the "dwelling" against direct physical loss if the dwelling is used principally as a private residence. The dwelling includes structures attached to the residence premises.

**P.5** We insure "other structures" against direct physical loss if those structures are separated from the dwelling by clear space or are connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. Other structures must be located at an insured location.

**P.6** We insure personal property against direct physical loss while it is at an insured location. Personal property away from an insured location is subject to the terms that apply to property customarily kept at the residence premises.

**P.7** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance, law, or governmental requirement regulating the construction, repair, replacement, removal, or demolition of property. This exclusion applies whether the loss results from a covered peril or from the condition of the property.

**P.8** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mudflow, subsidence, sinkhole collapse, rising, shifting, settling, and cracking of earth.

**P.9** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sump systems, or similar systems. This exclusion applies whether the water originates on or away from an insured location.

**P.10** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the water is driven by wind or another force.

**P.11** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, leaks through, or flows into a building, foundation, pavement, driveway, sidewalk, or other structure.

**P.12** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a sump system, drainage system, dewatering system, or similar equipment. This exclusion applies even if the equipment fails because of a power interruption or mechanical breakdown.

**P.13** We do not cover loss caused by neglect. An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage after a loss.

**P.14** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from an insured location. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from power failure occurring at an insured location.

**P.15** We do not cover loss caused by war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, invasion, military action, and similar acts.

**P.16** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, and any consequence of these conditions.

**P.17** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. Intentional loss means a loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with intent to cause loss.

**P.18** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by order of a governmental authority. We cover loss caused by fire, smoke, or explosion resulting from such action when the loss is otherwise covered.

**P.19** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, decay, deterioration, or contamination. We cover ensuing direct physical loss caused by a peril that is not otherwise excluded.

**P.20** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, scratching, mechanical breakdown, latent defect, or inherent vice. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**P.21** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by collapse if collapse is otherwise covered.

**P.22** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**P.23** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. Pollutants include irritants, contaminants, smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, chemicals, waste, and similar materials.

**P.24** We do not cover loss caused by acts or decisions of a governmental body or other person, group, organization, or entity. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a peril that is not otherwise excluded.

**P.25** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, domestic animals, or animals owned or kept by an insured. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire, smoke, explosion, or another covered peril resulting from such activity.

**P.26** We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by an insured. We do not cover loss caused by theft from a dwelling under construction unless the theft involves property that has been permanently installed.

**P.27** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if the dwelling has been vacant. A dwelling is vacant when it lacks the customary furnishings necessary for regular habitation.

**P.28** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems while the residence premises is unoccupied. An insured must maintain heat in the building or shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**P.29** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water, steam, or moisture from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. This exclusion applies when the seepage or leakage occurs over time.

**P.30** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. We do not cover loss to the system or appliance from which the water or steam escaped.

**P.31** We cover direct physical loss caused by sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. We do not cover loss caused by deterioration, wear, or maintenance failure.

**P.32** We cover direct physical loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. An insured must take reasonable care to protect the property from freezing conditions.

**P.33** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge of water or steam from a plumbing system located off the residence premises if the discharge damages covered property at an insured location. We do not cover repair of the off-premises system.

**P.34** We cover direct physical loss caused by falling objects. We do not cover loss to the falling object itself unless the object is covered property.

**P.35** We cover direct physical loss caused by weight of ice, snow, or sleet. We do not cover loss to a fence, pavement, patio, swimming pool, foundation, retaining wall, bulkhead, pier, wharf, or dock caused by such weight.

**P.36** We cover direct physical loss caused by sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current. We do not cover loss to a tube, transistor, electronic component, or similar electrical component unless fire results.

**P.37** We cover direct physical loss caused by fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, explosion, riot, civil commotion, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, and volcanic eruption. Coverage is subject to all exclusions and conditions in this policy.

**P.38** We do not cover loss to the interior of a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless the direct force of wind or hail first damages the building and creates an opening. We cover loss to the interior only through the opening created by that direct force.

**P.39** We do not cover loss caused by wind or hail to awnings, signs, outdoor radio equipment, outdoor television equipment, antennas, fences, patios, pavements, swimming pools, foundations, retaining walls, bulkheads, piers, wharves, or docks. We cover attached equipment when it is permanently installed as part of the dwelling.

**P.40** We do not cover cosmetic damage to roof surfacing caused by wind or hail. Cosmetic damage means damage that alters appearance without impairing the roof surfacing's ability to shed water.

**P.41** We cover direct physical loss to roof surfacing caused by wind or hail when the loss impairs the roof surfacing's ability to shed water. The roof schedule trigger age does not by itself establish that a loss is covered.

## II.E — Coverage E — Personal Liability

**F.14** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to you or a regular resident of your household. This exclusion does not apply to a residence employee.

**F.15** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to a person eligible to receive benefits voluntarily provided or required by law under a workers compensation law, nonoccupational disability law, or occupational disease law. **F.20** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury that is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury resulting from the use of reasonable force to protect persons or property.

## II.S — Section II — Conditions

**S.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties stated in this section. Compliance with these duties is a condition of coverage.

**S.7** An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning an occurrence, claim, or suit. An insured must provide truthful and complete information.

**S.8** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect persons and "property" from further harm after an occurrence. An insured must not take action that increases the possibility or extent of a loss.

**S.9** An insured must promptly report a "bodily injury" for which coverage may apply. An insured must provide information concerning the nature and extent of the bodily injury known to the insured.

**S.10** An insured must promptly report "property damage" for which coverage may apply. An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably possible.

**S.11** An insured must permit us to inspect property related to a claim when we reasonably request inspection. An insured must provide access to the property at a reasonable time.

**S.12** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer questions truthfully and provide records or documents we reasonably request.

**S.13** An insured must authorize us to obtain records that are relevant to a claim or suit when we reasonably request authorization. We may obtain information concerning the occurrence, claim, damages, or defense.

**S.14** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. This duty does not apply to reasonable emergency assistance provided at the time of an occurrence.

**S.15** An insured must not admit liability for an occurrence without our consent. An insured may provide truthful factual information to a person authorized to investigate the occurrence.

**S.16** An insured must not settle a claim or suit without our written consent. We are not bound by a settlement made without our consent.

**S.17** An insured must not interfere with our investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. An insured must not obstruct access to relevant evidence or witnesses.

**S.18** An insured must preserve any right of recovery against a person or organization that may be liable for a loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

**S.19** If we make a payment under this policy, we may recover that payment from a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in exercising that right.

**S.20** An insured must provide us with information concerning other insurance that may apply to a claim or suit. An insured must provide copies of other insurance documents we reasonably request.

**S.21** We do not cover a loss arising from an insured's intentional concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact relating to this insurance. We do not cover a loss arising from an insured's fraud in connection with a claim.

**S.22** We do not cover a loss if an insured has intentionally made a false statement concerning a material matter relating to coverage. This condition applies whether the statement is oral or written.

**S.23** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this policy. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not relieve an insured of duties imposed by this policy.

**S.24** A waiver or change of a provision of this policy is valid only if made by us in writing. Our investigation of a claim does not waive any policy provision or defense.

**S.25** An insured may not assign rights or duties under this policy without our written consent. This condition does not prevent transfer of rights that occurs by operation of law.

**S.26** If an insured dies, we provide coverage to the legal representative of the deceased insured for duties and obligations arising under this policy. Coverage applies only with respect to the interests of the deceased insured.

**S.27** If an insured dies, we provide coverage to a person having proper temporary custody of property covered by this policy. That coverage applies only until a legal representative is appointed.

**S.28** This insurance applies separately to each insured against whom a claim is made or a suit is brought. This condition does not increase our limit of liability.

**S.29** Knowledge possessed by an insured does not automatically constitute knowledge possessed by any other insured. Duties imposed upon an insured apply to the insured having the relevant knowledge or control.

**S.30** An insured must notify us of a change in contact information that materially affects our ability to communicate concerning a claim or suit. An insured must keep us informed of a current mailing address.

**S.31** An insured must notify us if property involved in a claim is transferred, disposed of, or materially altered. An insured must provide information concerning the transfer, disposition, or alteration.

**S.32** An insured must not abandon property to us unless we agree in writing. We may take possession of property only as permitted by law and this policy.

**S.33** An insured must retain records, photographs, communications, and other evidence relevant to a claim or suit. An insured must provide such materials to us when we reasonably request them.

**S.34** An insured must attend hearings, depositions, conferences, and other proceedings when we reasonably request attendance. We will cooperate with an insured in arranging reasonable participation.

**S.35** An insured must assist us in obtaining statements from persons having information relevant to a claim or suit. An insured must provide information concerning the location of such persons, if known.

**S.36** An insured must notify us if a claimant withdraws, changes, or expands a claim. An insured must promptly provide written material reflecting that action.

**S.38** An insured must not incur legal expenses or other claim expenses without our consent. We are not obligated to reimburse expenses incurred without our consent.

## G — Sections I and II — Conditions

**G.1** This "policy" is issued in reliance upon the statements made by you and by any insured. An insured must ensure that material information given to us is accurate and complete.

**G.2** An "insured" must comply with the duties imposed by this policy. Compliance by any insured does not excuse noncompliance by another insured whose conduct is material to a claim or loss.

**G.3** We do not provide coverage for a loss arising from concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact by an insured. This condition applies before or after a loss.

**G.4** An insured must promptly notify us of a material change in the ownership, occupancy, use, or condition of the insured location. We may revise the policy terms or cancel the policy as permitted by law.

**G.5** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. Cooperation includes providing information, records, authorizations, and access that we reasonably request.

**G.6** An insured must preserve evidence relating to a claimed loss when reasonably possible. An insured must not alter, discard, conceal, or destroy material evidence without our consent.

**G.7** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request. An insured must answer material questions truthfully and may be required to provide a recorded statement.

**G.8** An insured must provide books, records, receipts, photographs, communications, and other material information that we reasonably request. We may examine and copy those materials.

**G.9** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property and the location of a claimed loss. An insured must provide reasonable access for our representatives, contractors, and experts.

**G.10** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect "covered property" from further damage after a loss. We do not cover avoidable damage resulting from an insured's failure to protect covered property.

**G.11** An insured must promptly report a theft, vandalism, or other unlawful act to the appropriate law enforcement authority when applicable. An insured must provide us with information concerning that report when we request it.

**G.12** An insured must give us prompt notice of a "loss." The notice must state the identity of the insured, the location of the loss, and available information concerning the cause and extent of damage.

**G.13** An insured must notify us of any claim made against an insured that may be covered under this policy. Notice must include available information concerning the claimant, the alleged injury or damage, and the circumstances involved.

**G.14** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to obtain samples for testing, analysis, or examination when reasonably necessary.

**G.15** An insured must not voluntarily dispose of damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. This duty does not prevent necessary measures to protect property or persons from further harm.

**G.16** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when we reasonably request it. The inventory must describe the property and provide available information concerning its value, age, and condition.

**G.17** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we reasonably request it. The statement must identify the interest of each insured and any other person having an interest in the property.

**G.18** An insured must provide available records supporting the amount of a claimed loss. Such records may include receipts, invoices, appraisals, photographs, repair estimates, and purchase information.

**G.19** An insured must provide information concerning liens, encumbrances, ownership interests, and security interests affecting damaged property. We may require documentation establishing the interest of an insured in the property.

**G.20** An insured must not abandon property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment or settlement of a claim, subject to the rights of persons having a legal interest in that property.

**G.21** We may investigate a claim and settle it as we consider appropriate. Our investigation or settlement activity does not waive any policy condition or defense.

**G.22** We may require an appraisal when we and an insured disagree regarding the amount of a covered loss. An insured must cooperate in the appraisal process if appraisal is required.

**G.23** An insured must permit reasonable repair, replacement, or restoration measures necessary to determine the amount of a covered loss. We are not required to authorize work performed without our consent unless emergency conditions reasonably require it.

**G.24** An insured must notify us if lost or stolen property is recovered after a claim has been reported. We may require an insured to return payment for property that is recovered, subject to the property's condition and value.

**G.25** An insured must hold any recovered property for us when we request it. We may take possession of recovered property after payment for that property.

**G.26** If we make payment under this policy, we are entitled to the rights of recovery held by an insured against a person responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**G.27** An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing rights of recovery. An insured must execute documents and take actions reasonably necessary to secure those rights.

**G.28** We do not provide coverage for a loss to the extent an insured has impaired our right of recovery. This condition does not apply to a waiver of recovery rights made before a loss if the waiver is in a written agreement.

**G.29** No person may bring legal action against us unless that person has complied with all policy conditions. No person may bring legal action against us unless the action is commenced as permitted by applicable law.

**G.30** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this policy. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not give any person a right to bring legal action against us except as permitted by this policy and applicable law.

**G.31** An insured may not assign this policy without our written consent. An assignment of rights after a loss is subject to the duties and conditions of this policy.

**G.32** If a named insured dies, coverage may continue for the legal representative of the deceased insured with respect to the insured location and property. Coverage may also continue for a resident relative who was an insured at the time of death.

**G.33** If an insured becomes legally incapable of managing property, coverage may continue for that insured's legal representative. The legal representative must comply with the duties imposed upon an insured.

**G.34** We may cancel this policy for nonpayment of premium by mailing or delivering written notice to you. The notice will be mailed or delivered at least ten days days before cancellation takes effect.

**G.35** We may cancel this policy for a reason other than nonpayment of premium by mailing or delivering written notice to you. The notice will be mailed or delivered at least forty-five days days before cancellation takes effect.

**G.36** A notice of cancellation will state the effective date of cancellation. We will mail or deliver the notice to the mailing address known to us.

**G.37** We may elect not to renew this policy by mailing or delivering written notice to you. The notice will be mailed or delivered at least forty-five days days before the end of the policy term.

**G.38** A notice of nonrenewal will state that coverage will end at the conclusion of the current policy term. We will mail or deliver the notice to the mailing address known to us.

**G.39** Renewal of this policy is subject to our underwriting requirements and lawful rating practices. We may offer renewal on revised terms, conditions, or premium.

**G.40** An insured must notify us of a change in mailing address. Notice sent to the mailing address known to us is sufficient notice under this policy.

**G.41** A waiver or change of a policy provision is valid only if made by us in writing. Our failure to enforce a policy provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision later.

**G.42** No act by us creates coverage not provided by this policy. No statement by our representative changes this policy unless we confirm the change in writing.
