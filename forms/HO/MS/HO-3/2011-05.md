# HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2011-05)
> SUPERSEDED by HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2018-09) for policies effective on or after 2018-09-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

We provide the insurance described in this "Agreement" in return for the "premium" and your compliance with all applicable terms.

This Agreement applies to you and to each "insured" entitled to protection under its terms. We will perform our obligations only as stated in this Agreement.

We cover direct physical loss to covered "property" caused by a covered cause of loss. Coverage applies only when the loss occurs during the applicable coverage period.

We will pay covered "damages" for which an insured is legally responsible. Our obligation to pay damages is subject to all applicable limitations and exclusions.

We will provide a defense when this Agreement requires us to do so. Our duty to defend ends when our obligation to pay damages ends.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a "claim". An insured must provide information and documents that we reasonably request.

An insured must protect covered property from further loss after a loss occurs. We may inspect the property and examine the circumstances of the loss.

We may investigate any claim or loss. Our investigation does not waive any right available to us.

We may settle a claim as we consider appropriate. A settlement does not create an obligation to settle another claim.

We do not cover loss or damages that are excluded by this Agreement. We do not cover loss or damages beyond the applicable coverage provided by this Agreement.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined with appropriate deduction for depreciation and physical condition. It includes the value of property that has been used and has declined in condition. It does not include the cost to replace property with new property without deduction for depreciation. For example, the value of a worn appliance reflects its age and condition, not the price of a new appliance.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. It includes reasonable costs to restore property to substantially the same condition it had immediately before the loss. It does not include any increase in value resulting from better materials, design, or construction. For example, the cost to replace a damaged roof with comparable materials is included, but the added cost of an upgraded roof is not included.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. It includes an event that results in bodily injury or property damage that is neither expected nor intended by an insured. It does not include an intentional act committed with the expectation of causing injury or damage. For example, water escaping from a failed pipe may be an occurrence, but deliberate damage to property is not an occurrence.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the grounds used in connection with that dwelling. It includes private structures and portions of the premises used by you as a residence. It does not include premises used solely for business or property that is not used in connection with your residence. For example, an attached garage used for household purposes is included, but a separately operated commercial location is not included.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or other persons under your care. It includes a person who is temporarily away from your household if that person otherwise qualifies as a resident of your household. It does not include a person who merely visits your residence and is not a resident of your household. For example, a resident relative is included, but an unrelated overnight guest is not included.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and premises used by you in connection with the residence premises. It includes premises where you are temporarily residing and certain premises used by an insured for personal purposes. It does not include premises used solely for business or premises not used by an insured. For example, a private path serving your residence may be included, but a separate commercial office is not included.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain. It includes an activity conducted from your residence when that activity produces income or involves services for compensation. It does not include an activity performed solely as a volunteer or as an unpaid hobby. For example, operating a repair service for payment is business, but helping a neighbor without compensation is not business.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration. It includes a trailer designed for use with such a vehicle and a vehicle adapted for road travel. It does not include a device designed solely to assist a person with mobility and not designed for road travel. For example, a passenger automobile is a motor vehicle, but a nonmotorized wheelchair is not a motor vehicle.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. It includes a person who performs domestic services or similar personal services for an insured. It does not include a person whose duties are principally related to an insured's business. For example, a housekeeper performing household duties is a residence employee, but a worker serving a commercial operation is not a residence employee.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. It includes required care, loss of services, and death resulting from bodily harm, sickness, or disease. It does not include injury to tangible property. For example, a physical injury caused by a fall is bodily injury, but damage to clothing alone is not bodily injury.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. It includes loss of use of tangible property that is physically injured and loss of use of tangible property that is not physically injured. It does not include bodily injury or injury to a person. For example, damage to a wall caused by water is property damage, but a personal physical injury is not property damage.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unexpected. It includes an event that occurs without design or expectation by an insured. It does not include gradual deterioration, wear, corrosion, or a condition that is expected or intended. For example, an unexpected pipe break may be sudden and accidental, but ongoing seepage is not sudden and accidental.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means containing no substantial personal property necessary to normal use or occupancy. It includes premises that are unoccupied and substantially empty of property used for ordinary living. It does not include premises that contain substantial personal property necessary for normal use or occupancy. For example, an empty dwelling without furnishings may be vacant, but a furnished dwelling temporarily unoccupied is not vacant.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover "direct physical loss" to "covered property" under this coverage. Covered property means the "dwelling" and structures attached to the dwelling.

**A.2** The dwelling includes materials and supplies located on or next to the "residence premises" that are used to construct, alter, or repair the dwelling or attached structures. Materials and supplies must be intended for use in the covered property.

**A.3** The residence premises must be used as a private residence by an insured. Coverage applies to the dwelling at the insured location described for this coverage.

**A.4** We will settle covered loss to the dwelling at replacement cost without deduction for depreciation when the amount of insurance applicable to the dwelling equals or exceeds eighty percent of the full replacement cost of the dwelling immediately before the loss. Replacement cost is determined using materials and methods of like kind and quality.

**A.5** We will not pay more than the reasonable cost to repair or replace damaged covered property. We may repair or replace damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality.

**A.6** We may pay the "actual cash value" of the damaged covered property until actual repair or replacement is completed. Actual cash value means the value of the property at the time of loss, including deduction for depreciation.

**A.7** Repair or replacement must be completed as soon as reasonably possible after the loss. An insured must provide documentation supporting the cost of repair or replacement when we request it.

**A.8** We will not pay for increased costs caused by "ordinance or law" unless the increased costs result directly from a covered loss and are otherwise covered by this policy. Ordinance or law includes an ordinance, law, or regulation that regulates construction, repair, demolition, or occupancy.

**A.9** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of ordinance or law requiring the demolition, renovation, repair, or replacement of property that was not damaged by a covered loss. We also do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to comply with an ordinance or law.

**A.10** We do not cover loss caused by "earth movement." Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**A.11** We do not cover loss caused by "water damage." Water damage includes flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, and water below the surface of the ground.

**A.12** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, or sumps. We also do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**A.13** We do not cover loss caused by "power failure" occurring away from the residence premises. Power failure includes the interruption of electrical power, fuel, or utility service.

**A.14** We do not cover loss caused by "neglect" of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion applies whether the neglect occurs before or after a covered loss.

**A.15** We do not cover loss caused by an "intentional loss." Intentional loss means a loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss.

**A.16** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by "war." War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, and military action.

**A.17** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by "nuclear hazard." Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, and any consequence of those conditions.

**A.18** We do not cover loss consisting of wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril unless the resulting loss is otherwise excluded.

**A.19** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or other deterioration. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril when that loss is separate from the excluded condition.

**A.20** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril unless the loss is otherwise excluded.

**A.21** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals owned or kept by an insured. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire or explosion resulting from damage by those causes.

**A.22** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether the defective condition affects covered property or other property.

**A.23** We do not cover loss to land, including land on which the dwelling is located. We do not cover loss to lawns, trees, shrubs, plants, or growing crops except when such property is specifically included as covered property.

**A.24** We do not cover the cost to replace undamaged property solely because replacement of damaged property would result in a mismatch of color, texture, design, or appearance. We will pay only for direct physical loss to covered property that was damaged by a covered peril.

**A.25** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must keep a record of emergency measures and provide that record to us when we request it.

**A.26** We will pay reasonable expenses incurred for necessary emergency measures taken solely to protect covered property from further damage caused by a covered peril. We will not pay for emergency measures that are not reasonable or that do not relate to covered property.

**A.27** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged covered property as often as reasonably required. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**A.28** An insured must provide us with records, receipts, estimates, invoices, photographs, and other information needed to evaluate the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of the claim.

**A.29** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the examination transcript when it is accurately prepared.

**A.30** An insured must provide a signed statement describing the loss, the damaged property, and the basis for the amount claimed. The statement must identify any interest in the damaged property and any other insurance that may apply.

**A.31** We may pay the cost to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged covered property. Our election to repair or replace does not require us to pay for any betterment, improvement, or enhancement.

**A.32** We may take possession of damaged covered property after payment for the loss. An insured must not abandon covered property to us without our consent.

**A.33** If damaged covered property is repaired or replaced, the repair or replacement must be intended for the same use as the property before the loss. We will not pay for a change in use, location, design, or construction that is not required to restore covered property.

**A.34** If property is replaced, we may settle based on the cost to replace the damaged property with property of comparable utility. Comparable utility means reasonably similar function, capacity, and quality.

**A.35** We will not pay for any part of a loss that is covered by other insurance unless that insurance has been exhausted or does not apply. If other insurance applies, we will pay only our share of the covered loss.

**A.36** Any recovery from another person or organization responsible for the loss belongs to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

**A.37** Coverage under this provision applies only to direct physical loss occurring during the policy period. Coverage is subject to the applicable limit of liability for the dwelling.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover direct physical loss to "Coverage B—Other Structures" property described in this section. The property must be located on the "residence premises".

**B.2** Coverage B—Other Structures includes structures that are separated from the dwelling by clear space. A structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection is considered separated by clear space.

**B.3** The structures must be owned by you or be your responsibility under a written agreement. Coverage applies only to structures intended for your use or maintained for your use.

**B.4** A structure used for storage, recreation, shelter, or similar personal purposes may be covered. Its use must be consistent with the use of the residence premises.

**B.5** The limit of liability for Coverage B—Other Structures is ten percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A—Dwelling". This limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss to Coverage B—Other Structures.

**B.6** The applicable limit of liability applies to all covered structures collectively. Payment for loss to any covered structure reduces the amount available for other covered structures.

**B.7** We will not increase the applicable limit of liability because more than one covered structure is damaged. We will not apply a separate limit to each covered structure.

**B.8** We will determine the amount of loss according to the terms applicable to covered property. Payment will not exceed the amount of covered loss or the applicable limit of liability.

**B.9** We do not cover other structures used in whole or in part for "business" purposes. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted by an insured or by another person.

**B.10** We do not cover other structures rented or held for rental to any person not a tenant of the dwelling. This exclusion does not apply to a structure used solely as a private garage.

**B.11** We do not cover other structures used to store property primarily held for business purposes. This exclusion applies even if the structure is also used for personal purposes.

**B.12** We do not cover other structures used for manufacturing, processing, servicing, or commercial storage. This exclusion applies regardless of whether income is received from that use.

**B.13** We do not cover land, including land on which a covered structure is located. We do not cover the cost to grade, excavate, fill, or otherwise alter land.

**B.14** We do not cover outdoor property that is not part of a covered structure. This includes materials, equipment, and property kept outside a covered structure.

**B.15** We do not cover a structure that is not located on the residence premises. A structure remains subject to this requirement while it is being repaired, altered, or rebuilt.

**B.16** We do not cover loss to a structure caused by its intentional removal from the residence premises. We do not cover loss occurring after the structure has been removed.

**B.17** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered structures from further damage following a loss. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably practical.

**B.18** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged structures and records relating to the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim.

**B.19** An insured must provide information reasonably requested to establish ownership, use, and value of a damaged structure. An insured must provide supporting records when such records are available.

**B.20** We may pay you for covered loss to a structure or repair the structure with materials of like kind and quality. We may settle with any person having a legal interest in the damaged structure.

**B.21** Payment to an insured does not transfer ownership of damaged property to us unless we agree otherwise. We may require transfer of salvage rights as a condition of payment.

**B.22** We do not cover loss that is not a direct physical loss to a covered structure. We do not cover loss consisting solely of loss of use, loss of income, delay, or diminished value.

**B.23** We do not cover costs incurred to comply with ordinances, laws, or regulations unless those costs result directly from covered physical loss. This exclusion applies to costs relating to repair, replacement, demolition, or construction.

**B.24** All coverage provided by this section is subject to the applicable exclusions, conditions, and limits of liability. No provision of this section increases the applicable limit of liability.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover “personal property” owned or used by an “insured” while it is anywhere in the world. This coverage is subject to the applicable limit of liability and the provisions of this policy.

**C.2** At your request, we cover personal property owned by others while the property is on the part of a “residence premises” occupied by an insured. We cover that property only to the extent that it is not otherwise insured.

**C.3** At your request, we cover personal property owned by a guest or a residence employee while the property is at a residence occupied by an insured. This coverage applies only while the property is in the care of an insured.

**C.4** Personal property includes tangible property that is usual to the occupancy of a residence. Personal property does not include real property or property permanently attached to real property.

**C.5** We cover personal property that is customary to the maintenance, use, and enjoyment of the residence premises. We apply the limitations in this section to all covered personal property.

**C.6** The Coverage C limit of liability is fifty percent of the Coverage A limit of liability. This limit is the most we will pay for all loss covered under this coverage, subject to any applicable special limit.

**C.7** The Coverage C limit is not increased by the coverage provided for property owned by others. Payments for covered property owned by others reduce the amount available under Coverage C.

**C.8** We determine the amount of loss under this coverage in accordance with the valuation provisions of this policy. We do not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for the property involved.

**C.9** We cover clothing, furnishings, appliances, and other personal effects that are owned or used by an insured. We cover those items whether they are located at the residence premises or elsewhere, subject to this policy.

**C.10** We cover personal property temporarily removed from the residence premises. Coverage for removed property remains subject to all exclusions, conditions, and special limits.

**C.11** We cover personal property while it is being transported by an insured. We do not cover loss caused by a peril excluded by this policy.

**C.12** We cover personal property while it is stored away from the residence premises. The insured must protect the property from further damage after a loss.

**C.13** We cover personal property used by an insured in the ordinary conduct of household activities. Coverage does not depend on whether the property is used exclusively at the residence premises.

**C.14** We cover property jointly owned by an insured and another person. We pay only for the interest in the property that is owned by an insured.

**C.15** We cover property acquired by an insured during the policy period. Coverage for acquired property is subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**C.16** We cover property received by an insured as a gift, inheritance, or transfer of ownership. The insured must establish ownership and the value of the property when requested by us.

**C.17** We cover property leased to an insured when the insured is legally responsible for loss to that property. We do not cover a loss for which the insured has no legal responsibility.

**C.18** We cover property borrowed by an insured only when the insured has an insurable interest in that property. We do not cover property merely entrusted to an insured unless this policy provides coverage.

**C.19** We cover personal property that is customarily kept at the residence premises. We also cover personal property customarily kept at another location when it is owned or used by an insured.

**C.20** We do not cover property that is separately described and specifically insured under another policy for the same loss. This limitation applies only to the extent that other insurance applies.

**C.21** We do not cover a loss to property when payment would result in duplicate recovery. Any payment under this coverage is subject to the other insurance provisions of this policy.

**C.22** A special limit applies to money, bank notes, bullion, gold other than goldware, silver other than silverware, platinum, coins, medals, scrip, and stored value cards. The most we will pay for loss to this property is two hundred dollars.

**C.23** The special limit for money and similar property applies regardless of the cause of loss. The limit applies to all such property involved in the same loss.

**C.24** The special limit for money and similar property does not increase the Coverage C limit. Payment under that special limit reduces the amount available under Coverage C.

**C.25** A special limit applies to loss by theft of jewelry, watches, precious stones, and semiprecious stones. The most we will pay for loss by theft of this property is one thousand dollars.

**C.26** The special limit for jewelry and similar property applies whether the property is worn, carried, stored, or otherwise kept. The insured must establish the ownership of the property claimed.

**C.27** The special limit for jewelry and similar property applies to all covered theft loss arising from the same occurrence. We do not pay more than the applicable special limit for the property involved.

**C.28** A special limit applies to loss by theft of firearms and related equipment. The most we will pay for loss by theft of this property is two thousand dollars.

**C.29** Related equipment includes equipment and accessories used with firearms. The special limit applies whether the firearms and related equipment are owned together or separately.

**C.30** The special limit for firearms and related equipment applies only to theft loss. Other covered loss is subject to the Coverage C limit and all applicable policy provisions.

**C.31** A special limit applies to watercraft, including trailers, furnishings, equipment, and outboard engines or motors. The most we will pay for loss to this property is one thousand dollars.

**C.32** The special limit for watercraft applies to property used with or intended for use with a watercraft. The limit applies whether the property is attached to, carried in, or kept apart from the watercraft.

**C.33** Coverage for watercraft property remains subject to exclusions applicable to watercraft. We do not cover loss that is otherwise excluded by this policy.

**C.34** A special limit applies to loss by theft of silverware, silver-plated ware, goldware, gold-plated ware, platinumware, platinum-plated ware, and pewterware. The most we will pay for loss by theft of this property is two thousand five hundred dollars.

**C.35** The special limit for silverware and similar property applies to all articles composed of or plated with the described materials. The insured must provide reasonable evidence of ownership when requested by us.

**C.36** The special limit for silverware and similar property applies only to theft loss. Other covered loss is subject to the Coverage C limit and the valuation provisions of this policy.

**C.37** A special limit applies to “business property” while it is on the residence premises. The most we will pay for loss to this property is two thousand five hundred dollars.

**C.38** Business property includes property used primarily for a trade, profession, occupation, or business. Property used incidentally in connection with an insured’s employment is subject to this policy.

**C.39** The special limit for business property applies to property owned by an insured and property in the care of an insured. We do not cover business property to the extent that it is otherwise insured.

**C.40** Business property away from the residence premises is subject to the applicable limitations of this policy. We do not increase the Coverage C limit for business property located away from the residence premises.

**C.41** A special limit applies to electronic apparatus and accessories while they are in or upon a motor vehicle. The most we will pay for loss to this property is one thousand dollars.

**C.42** Electronic apparatus includes apparatus that is designed to be operated solely by power from a motor vehicle electrical system. The special limit also applies to accessories and antennas used with that apparatus.

**C.43** The special limit for electronic apparatus applies while the apparatus is in or upon a motor vehicle. It applies whether the apparatus is installed, portable, detached, or being transported in the motor vehicle.

**C.44** We cover personal property damaged by a covered peril. The insured must retain the damaged property for our inspection when we request it.

**C.45** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. The notice must identify the property involved and provide information reasonably available to the insured.

**C.46** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. The insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs solely to protect the property from further damage.

**C.47** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses incurred to protect property from further damage. We may inspect invoices, receipts, estimates, and other records relating to those expenses.

**C.48** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when requested by us. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item.

**C.49** An insured must attach bills, receipts, and related documents to the inventory when available. An insured must provide other evidence that supports the claimed ownership and value of the property.

**C.50** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine records relating to the loss.

**C.51** An insured must submit to examination under oath when requested by us. An insured must answer questions concerning the loss and the property involved.

**C.52** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**C.53** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to the claim.

**C.54** We may pay the insured for covered loss to personal property. We may also repair or replace covered property with property of like kind and quality.

**C.55** We may require an insured to assign to us the rights of recovery against any person responsible for a covered loss. The insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**C.56** We do not cover loss to personal property caused by a peril excluded under this policy. We do not cover loss to property for which coverage is otherwise limited or excluded by this policy.

**C.57** We do not cover loss that results from an intentional act of an insured when the act is intended to cause loss. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the property is owned by that insured or another insured.

**C.58** Any payment under Coverage C is subject to the applicable deductible. The deductible applies in accordance with the deductible provisions of this policy.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** The limit of liability for "Coverage D — Loss of Use" is twenty percent of the applicable limit for "Coverage A". This limit applies to all loss payable under this coverage.

**D.2** We will pay the loss described in this section when a covered loss makes the part of the "residence premises" where you reside not fit to live in. Payment is subject to the terms of this coverage.

**D.3** We will pay necessary increases in living expenses incurred by you to maintain your normal standard of living. We call these expenses "Additional Living Expense".

**D.4** "Additional Living Expense" must result directly from a covered loss. The expense must be reasonable and necessary.

**D.5** We will pay "Additional Living Expense" while the part of the "residence premises" where you reside is not fit to live in. Payment ends when that part of the "residence premises" is fit to live in.

**D.6** We will pay "Additional Living Expense" while repair or replacement is reasonably required following a covered loss. We will not pay expenses that continue after the property is fit to live in.

**D.7** We will pay "Additional Living Expense" if you permanently relocate because of a covered loss. Payment is limited to the expenses reasonably necessary for the relocation.

**D.8** An insured must take reasonable steps to reduce "Additional Living Expense". An insured must not incur expenses that are unnecessary or unreasonable.

**D.9** We will pay the fair rental value of the part of the "residence premises" rented to others or held for rental when a covered loss makes that part not fit to live in. We call this payment "Fair Rental Value".

**D.10** "Fair Rental Value" is based on the rental value of the part of the "residence premises" that is not fit to live in. We will reduce payment by expenses that do not continue while that part is not fit to live in.

**D.11** We will pay "Fair Rental Value" only for loss of rental income that results directly from a covered loss. We will not pay for rental income that you could have earned without the covered loss.

**D.12** We will pay "Fair Rental Value" while repair or replacement is reasonably required. Payment ends when the rented part of the "residence premises" is fit to live in.

**D.13** An insured must take reasonable steps to make the rented part of the "residence premises" available for repair or replacement. An insured must not delay repair or replacement in order to increase a loss payment.

**D.14** We will pay "Additional Living Expense" and "Fair Rental Value" when a civil authority prohibits use of the "residence premises". We call this coverage "Prohibited Use".

**D.15** "Prohibited Use" applies only when the civil authority prohibits use because of direct damage to neighboring premises. The direct damage to the neighboring premises must result from a covered cause of loss.

**D.16** We will pay "Prohibited Use" only while the civil authority prohibits use of the "residence premises". We will not pay after the prohibition ends.

**D.17** We will not pay under "Prohibited Use" for a prohibition caused by conditions at the "residence premises" that are unrelated to direct damage to neighboring premises. We will not pay for voluntary evacuation or voluntary relocation.

**D.18** We will not pay for expenses incurred to improve the "residence premises". We will not pay for expenses incurred to increase the value of the "residence premises".

**D.19** We will not pay for loss of use caused by delay that is within your control. We will not pay for loss of use caused by your failure to protect the "residence premises" from further damage.

**D.20** We will not pay for living expenses that you would have incurred without the covered loss. We will pay only the increase in living expenses caused by the covered loss.

**D.21** We will not pay for rental income that is speculative, contingent, or unsupported by available evidence. We will pay only the "Fair Rental Value" established by the rental use of the property.

**D.22** An insured must provide us with records supporting a claim for "Additional Living Expense". An insured must provide us with records supporting a claim for "Fair Rental Value".

**D.23** An insured must provide receipts, invoices, rental agreements, and other information reasonably requested by us. The information must identify the expense or loss claimed.

**D.24** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of loss. An insured must permit us to inspect the "residence premises" as reasonably necessary.

**D.25** Payment under this coverage does not increase any other limit of liability. Payment under another coverage does not increase the limit of liability stated in this section.

**D.26** We will not pay more than the actual covered loss. We will not pay duplicate amounts for the same expense or loss of rental income.

**D.27** We will apply all applicable terms, conditions, and exclusions to coverage under this section. Coverage applies only when the claimed loss is caused by a covered loss.

**D.28** We will determine payment based on the information available to us. An insured must promptly notify us of circumstances that may result in a claim under this coverage.

**D.29** We will not pay for loss of use caused solely by the unavailability of utilities, services, or access. This exclusion does not apply when coverage is provided under "Prohibited Use".

**D.30** We will not pay for costs incurred to comply with a law or ordinance unless the costs are otherwise covered. We will not pay for costs that are unrelated to making the "residence premises" fit to live in.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.3** "Covered property" means property for which this policy provides property insurance. Covered property includes only property that you own or for which an insured has an insurable interest.

## G — Sections I and II — Conditions

**G.1** We use the term "policy" to mean this contract of insurance, including its declarations, endorsements, and applicable provisions. The policy may be changed only as provided by its terms.

**G.3** We use the term "loss" to mean accidental direct physical loss or damage, or an accidental occurrence, for which coverage is sought. A loss must be reported and handled in accordance with this policy.

**G.5** You must promptly notify us of a material change in information that you provided to us. A material change includes a change in ownership, occupancy, use, or condition of insured property.

**G.11** We use the term "claim" to mean a request for payment, defense, or other benefit under this policy. A claim must be presented in good faith.

**G.13** You must provide records and documents that we reasonably request to verify a claim. You must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**G.14** You must submit to examination under oath if we reasonably request it. An insured must answer material questions truthfully and must sign the examination record if requested.

**G.15** You must provide a signed statement of loss if we request it. The statement must contain information reasonably necessary for us to determine the cause, extent, ownership, and value of the claimed loss.

**G.16** We may require an insured to submit to a medical examination when reasonably necessary to evaluate a bodily injury claim. We will select the examining physician and pay the reasonable cost of the examination.

**G.17** You must preserve evidence relating to a loss or claim. You must not alter, discard, repair, or dispose of relevant property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, except as necessary to protect persons or property.

**G.18** We may record statements, inspect locations, photograph property, and obtain information relevant to a claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining information from persons having knowledge of the loss or claim.

**G.19** You must notify us promptly if you become aware of facts that may give rise to a claim against an insured. Notice must be given even if no demand for payment has been made.

**G.20** You must promptly send us every notice, demand, summons, complaint, order, or other legal paper relating to a claim or suit. We use the term "suit" to mean a civil proceeding alleging damages because of bodily injury or property damage.

**G.21** You must not admit liability or settle a claim without our written consent. This condition does not prevent an insured from providing reasonable assistance to an injured person in an emergency.

**G.22** We may investigate and settle a claim as we consider appropriate. Our investigation or settlement does not constitute an admission of liability.

**G.23** We may pay the amount for which an insured is legally liable, subject to the applicable policy terms. Payment by us does not increase the limits or expand the coverage provided by this policy.

**G.24** We may defend a suit seeking damages covered by this policy. We may investigate or settle the suit as we consider appropriate.

**G.25** Our duty to defend ends when the applicable limit of liability has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements. We have no duty to provide a defense for a suit seeking damages not covered by this policy.
