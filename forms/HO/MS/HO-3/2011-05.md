# HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2011-05)
> SUPERSEDED by HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2018-09) for policies effective on or after 2018-09-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

We provide the insurance described in this "Agreement" in return for the "premium" and your compliance with all applicable terms.

This Agreement applies to you and to each "insured" entitled to protection under its terms. We will perform our obligations only as stated in this Agreement.

We cover direct physical loss to covered "property" caused by a covered cause of loss. Coverage applies only when the loss occurs during the applicable coverage period.

We will pay covered "damages" for which an insured is legally responsible. Our obligation to pay damages is subject to all applicable limitations and exclusions.

We will provide a defense when this Agreement requires us to do so. Our duty to defend ends when our obligation to pay damages ends.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a "claim". An insured must provide information and documents that we reasonably request.

An insured must protect covered property from further loss after a loss occurs. We may inspect the property and examine the circumstances of the loss.

We may investigate any claim or loss. Our investigation does not waive any right available to us.

We may settle a claim as we consider appropriate. A settlement does not create an obligation to settle another claim.

We do not cover loss or damages that are excluded by this Agreement. We do not cover loss or damages beyond the applicable coverage provided by this Agreement.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined with appropriate deduction for depreciation and physical condition. It includes the value of property that has been used and has declined in condition. It does not include the cost to replace property with new property without deduction for depreciation. For example, the value of a worn appliance reflects its age and condition, not the price of a new appliance.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. It includes reasonable costs to restore property to substantially the same condition it had immediately before the loss. It does not include any increase in value resulting from better materials, design, or construction. For example, the cost to replace a damaged roof with comparable materials is included, but the added cost of an upgraded roof is not included.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. It includes an event that results in bodily injury or property damage that is neither expected nor intended by an insured. It does not include an intentional act committed with the expectation of causing injury or damage. For example, water escaping from a failed pipe may be an occurrence, but deliberate damage to property is not an occurrence.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the grounds used in connection with that dwelling. It includes private structures and portions of the premises used by you as a residence. It does not include premises used solely for business or property that is not used in connection with your residence. For example, an attached garage used for household purposes is included, but a separately operated commercial location is not included.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or other persons under your care. It includes a person who is temporarily away from your household if that person otherwise qualifies as a resident of your household. It does not include a person who merely visits your residence and is not a resident of your household. For example, a resident relative is included, but an unrelated overnight guest is not included.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and premises used by you in connection with the residence premises. It includes premises where you are temporarily residing and certain premises used by an insured for personal purposes. It does not include premises used solely for business or premises not used by an insured. For example, a private path serving your residence may be included, but a separate commercial office is not included.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain. It includes an activity conducted from your residence when that activity produces income or involves services for compensation. It does not include an activity performed solely as a volunteer or as an unpaid hobby. For example, operating a repair service for payment is business, but helping a neighbor without compensation is not business.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration. It includes a trailer designed for use with such a vehicle and a vehicle adapted for road travel. It does not include a device designed solely to assist a person with mobility and not designed for road travel. For example, a passenger automobile is a motor vehicle, but a nonmotorized wheelchair is not a motor vehicle.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. It includes a person who performs domestic services or similar personal services for an insured. It does not include a person whose duties are principally related to an insured's business. For example, a housekeeper performing household duties is a residence employee, but a worker serving a commercial operation is not a residence employee.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. It includes required care, loss of services, and death resulting from bodily harm, sickness, or disease. It does not include injury to tangible property. For example, a physical injury caused by a fall is bodily injury, but damage to clothing alone is not bodily injury.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. It includes loss of use of tangible property that is physically injured and loss of use of tangible property that is not physically injured. It does not include bodily injury or injury to a person. For example, damage to a wall caused by water is property damage, but a personal physical injury is not property damage.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unexpected. It includes an event that occurs without design or expectation by an insured. It does not include gradual deterioration, wear, corrosion, or a condition that is expected or intended. For example, an unexpected pipe break may be sudden and accidental, but ongoing seepage is not sudden and accidental.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means containing no substantial personal property necessary to normal use or occupancy. It includes premises that are unoccupied and substantially empty of property used for ordinary living. It does not include premises that contain substantial personal property necessary for normal use or occupancy. For example, an empty dwelling without furnishings may be vacant, but a furnished dwelling temporarily unoccupied is not vacant.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the "dwelling" on the "residence premises" shown in the Declarations. The dwelling includes structures attached to it. It also includes fixtures and permanently installed equipment that service the dwelling.

**A.2** The dwelling includes materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises when those materials and supplies are intended for use in the construction, alteration, or repair of the dwelling. We cover those materials and supplies while they are awaiting use. We do not cover materials and supplies held for sale or intended for use at another location.

**A.3** The dwelling includes foundations, retaining walls, and other structures that support the dwelling. We cover those structures when they are located on the residence premises. We do not cover land, including land on which the dwelling or other structures are located.

**A.4** The dwelling includes permanently installed carpeting, wall-to-wall carpeting, and similar floor coverings. We cover those floor coverings when they are attached to the dwelling. We do not cover rugs or other floor coverings that are not permanently installed.

**A.5** The dwelling includes permanently installed appliances and equipment. We cover those appliances and equipment when they are used to service or maintain the dwelling. We do not cover personal property that is merely stored in or used within the dwelling.

**A.6** The amount of insurance provided for Coverage A must be at least eighty percent of the replacement cost of the dwelling; except that this requirement applies only when replacement cost settlement is requested and the dwelling is insured under the applicable coverage provisions. We determine replacement cost without deduction for depreciation when the requirements for replacement cost settlement have been satisfied. We may settle a covered loss on another basis when those requirements have not been satisfied.

**A.7** We cover the dwelling for direct physical loss to property described in this Coverage A. The loss must be caused by a covered peril. We do not cover loss that is excluded by this policy.

**A.8** We cover additions, alterations, and improvements that are permanently attached to or part of the dwelling. We cover those items whether they are made by you or acquired with the dwelling. We do not cover property that is not permanently attached or incorporated into the dwelling.

**A.9** We cover built-in cabinets, counters, shelves, and similar fixtures. We cover them when they are permanently attached to the dwelling. We do not cover movable furniture or equipment, even when it is used in connection with the dwelling.

**A.10** We cover heating, cooling, ventilating, plumbing, electrical, and security systems that are permanently installed in the dwelling. We cover their component parts and controls. We do not cover equipment that is not permanently installed.

**A.11** We cover outdoor equipment that is permanently attached to the dwelling. This includes equipment used for heating, cooling, lighting, communication, or security. We do not cover unattached equipment under this Coverage A.

**A.12** We cover garages, porches, decks, patios, and similar structures attached to the dwelling. We cover their permanently installed fixtures and equipment. We do not cover structures that are not attached to the dwelling under this Coverage A.

**A.13** We cover portions of the dwelling that extend below ground level. We cover those portions when they are part of the dwelling or its foundation. We do not cover excavation, fill, or land supporting the dwelling.

**A.14** We cover glass that is part of the dwelling. We cover permanently installed windows, skylights, doors, and similar glass. We do not cover glass that is personal property or that is not part of the dwelling.

**A.15** We cover permanently installed floor, wall, and ceiling coverings that are part of the dwelling. We cover their associated attachment materials when they are damaged by the same covered loss. We do not cover coverings that are removable and not permanently installed.

**A.16** We cover permanently installed outdoor structures that are attached to the dwelling. We cover their fixtures and equipment when they are part of the attached structure. We do not cover a detached structure under this Coverage A.

**A.17** We cover construction in progress at the dwelling when the construction is intended to become part of the dwelling. We cover materials incorporated into that construction and materials awaiting incorporation. We do not cover construction that is intended for another location.

**A.18** We cover property belonging to you that is used to service the dwelling when the property is permanently installed. We cover that property as part of the dwelling. We do not cover property belonging to a contractor or other person unless it has become part of the dwelling.

**A.19** We cover the dwelling when it is occupied as a private residence. We cover ordinary residential use of the dwelling. We do not cover a use that changes the character of the dwelling or increases the risk of loss beyond the use contemplated by this policy.

**A.20** We cover the dwelling while it is undergoing customary repair, maintenance, alteration, or renovation. We cover materials and fixtures intended to become part of the dwelling during that work. We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, design, specifications, workmanship, or construction, except to the extent a covered peril causes ensuing loss.

**A.21** We do not cover land, including land on which the dwelling is located. We do not cover water, including water contained in a body of water or resulting from a change in a body of water. We do not cover the cost of stabilizing or replacing land unless the cost is part of a covered repair to the dwelling.

**A.22** We do not cover paved surfaces, driveways, walkways, or other surfaces that are not part of the dwelling. We do not cover fences, landscaping, or outdoor property that is not attached to the dwelling under this Coverage A. We do not cover detached structures under this Coverage A.

**A.23** We do not cover animals, birds, or fish. We do not cover motor vehicles, aircraft, or watercraft. We do not cover property used in a business except as otherwise provided by this policy.

**A.24** We do not cover property rented to others when the property is not part of the dwelling. We do not cover property held for sale or delivery. We do not cover property that is intended to be installed at a location other than the residence premises.

**A.25** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, latent defect, or faulty maintenance. We do not cover loss that consists only of a defect or condition that was present before the loss.

**A.26** We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover loss caused by insects, vermin, birds, or rodents. We do not cover loss caused by animals owned or kept by you.

**A.27** We do not cover loss caused by pressure or weight of water or ice to a fence, pavement, patio, swimming pool, foundation, retaining wall, bulkhead, pier, wharf, or dock. We do not cover loss caused by freezing of a plumbing system when the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied and you have not maintained heat or shut off the water supply. We do not cover the cost to correct the condition that caused the loss.

**A.28** We cover reasonable and necessary repairs made solely to protect the dwelling from further damage caused by a covered loss. We cover those repairs when you give us prompt notice of the loss. We do not cover repairs that exceed what is reasonably necessary to protect the dwelling or that are unrelated to the covered loss.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** "Coverage B" applies to structures on the "residence premises" that are set apart from the dwelling by clear space. We cover those structures only when they are described in this Coverage B provision. We do not cover land, including land on which the structures are located.

**B.2** The limit of insurance for Coverage B is ten percent of the limit of insurance that applies to the dwelling, except that this limit applies only to covered property described in this Coverage B provision. The limit is subject to the terms that apply to Coverage B. The limit does not increase because the number of covered structures increases.

**B.3** We cover a structure that is separated from the dwelling by clear space. We also cover a structure connected to the dwelling by only a fence, utility line, or similar connection. We cover the structure only if it is located on the residence premises.

**B.4** We cover structures that are used in connection with the dwelling. We cover those structures whether they are used for storage, recreation, shelter, or another residential purpose. We do not cover a structure used for a business unless coverage is provided by this Coverage B provision.

**B.5** We do not cover a structure rented or held for rental to any person who is not a tenant of the dwelling. This exclusion applies whether the rental is for residential, commercial, storage, or recreational use. This exclusion does not apply to a private garage rented to you for use as a private garage.

**B.6** We do not cover a structure from which any business is conducted. This exclusion applies to a structure used in whole or in part for business activity. This exclusion does not apply to a structure used to store business property that we cover under the policy.

**B.7** We do not cover a structure used to store business property that is not covered under the policy. This exclusion applies even if the structure is otherwise used for a residential purpose. This exclusion does not apply to a structure that is used for incidental storage of property belonging to you.

**B.8** We cover a detached garage, carport, shed, or similar structure used for a private purpose. We cover the structure and fixtures that are permanently installed in it. We do not cover vehicles or watercraft merely because they are located in the structure.

**B.9** We cover a structure used as a private swimming pool, including equipment permanently installed for its operation. We cover the structure only when it is located on the residence premises. We do not cover water or landscaping surrounding the pool.

**B.10** We cover a fence, retaining wall, or similar structure located on the residence premises. We cover the structure only to the extent that it is a permanent improvement to the land. We do not cover a structure that is part of a public or common area.

**B.11** We cover a structure that is under construction on the residence premises. Coverage applies to the structure and materials intended for permanent installation in it. Coverage applies subject to the terms and exclusions that otherwise apply to Coverage B.

**B.12** We cover building materials located on the residence premises for use in constructing, altering, or repairing a covered structure. We cover the materials only while they remain at the residence premises. We do not cover materials intended for a structure that is not covered under Coverage B.

**B.13** We cover additions, alterations, fixtures, and improvements that are permanently attached to a covered structure. We cover them as part of the structure to which they are attached. We do not cover property that can be removed without damage to the structure or property.

**B.14** We cover outdoor equipment that is permanently attached to a covered structure. We cover the equipment only when it is intended to serve the covered structure. We do not cover equipment that is separately insured under another coverage.

**B.15** We cover a structure that is temporarily removed from the residence premises for repair, renovation, or restoration. Coverage applies while the structure is being moved or kept at another location. Coverage does not apply while the structure is being used at another location.

**B.16** We cover a structure that is being moved from the residence premises to another location. Coverage applies only while the structure is in transit. Coverage ends when the structure is installed or used at the other location.

**B.17** We cover a structure that is damaged by a covered cause of loss. We cover the direct physical loss to the structure. We do not cover loss that consists of defective design, workmanship, construction, or maintenance.

**B.18** We cover a covered structure when a covered cause of loss damages both the structure and the dwelling. We determine the applicable coverage for each damaged structure separately. We do not duplicate payment for the same property or loss.

**B.19** We cover reasonable repairs made to protect a covered structure from further damage after a covered loss. We cover the repairs only when they are necessary to protect covered property. You must keep records of the repairs and provide them to us when we request them.

**B.20** An insured must protect a covered structure from further damage after a covered loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs when required to protect the structure. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses.

**B.21** An insured must notify us promptly when a covered structure is damaged. The notice must identify the structure and describe the known circumstances of the loss. An insured must provide additional information when we reasonably request it.

**B.22** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or adjustment of a loss to a covered structure. An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged structure when reasonably necessary. An insured must not abandon the structure to us.

**B.23** We may pay the cost to repair or replace damaged portions of a covered structure. We may use materials of like kind and quality. We are not required to pay for undamaged portions of the structure.

**B.24** We may pay the value of a covered structure when repair or replacement is not reasonably possible. We determine that value under the applicable loss settlement provisions. We do not pay more than the applicable limit of insurance.

**B.25** We do not cover a structure that is vacant or unoccupied when the condition contributes to the loss. This exclusion applies only to the extent permitted by the terms governing the covered cause of loss. This exclusion does not apply when the vacancy or unoccupancy is caused by a covered loss.

**B.26** We do not cover a structure that is abandoned by you. This exclusion applies whether the structure remains on the residence premises or is moved elsewhere. This exclusion does not apply to temporary removal for repair or restoration.

**B.27** We do not cover a structure used to manufacture, process, store, or distribute controlled substances. This exclusion applies whether the activity is lawful or unlawful. This exclusion does not apply to ordinary household storage of legally possessed property.

**B.28** We do not cover a structure used to house animals for commercial purposes. This exclusion applies to boarding, breeding, training, or sale of animals. This exclusion does not apply to ordinary household use involving animals owned by you.

**B.29** We do not cover a structure used for a farming or agricultural operation. This exclusion applies to structures used for production, storage, processing, or sale of agricultural products. This exclusion does not apply to incidental household gardening.

**B.30** We do not cover a structure located on premises that you do not own or rent. This exclusion applies even if you use or maintain the structure. This exclusion does not apply to a covered structure temporarily located away from the residence premises for repair, renovation, or restoration.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover personal property owned or used by an "insured" while it is anywhere in the world. Personal property includes property that is not permanently attached to real property. This coverage applies subject to the limit of insurance, exclusions, and conditions that apply to Coverage C.

**C.2** The limit of insurance for "Coverage C" is fifty percent of the limit of insurance for the applicable dwelling coverage. This limit applies to the total amount of insurance available for covered personal property. The limit is subject to the special limits and other provisions that apply to personal property.

**C.3** We cover personal property owned by you or a resident of your household. We also cover personal property of others while it is on the part of the residence premises occupied by you. This coverage applies only to the extent that the property is used for personal, family, or household purposes.

**C.4** We cover personal property while it is located at the residence premises. We cover the property while it is temporarily located away from the residence premises. Coverage away from the residence premises is subject to the provisions that apply to property located away from the residence premises.

**C.5** We cover personal property of a guest or residence employee while the property is located at the residence premises. The property must be in your care, custody, or control. We do not cover property of a guest or residence employee if another insurance policy applies to the property.

**C.6** We cover personal property of a student who is an insured while the student is temporarily away from the residence premises. The student must be a resident of your household and must have been living at the residence premises before leaving to attend school. Coverage applies only while the student maintains a residence away from the residence premises.

**C.7** We cover personal property belonging to a student who is an insured while the student is at school. The property must be owned or used by the student for personal, family, or household purposes. Coverage does not apply to property rented to others or used for business.

**C.8** We cover personal property in a newly acquired principal residence for the period during which you are moving from the residence premises. The property must remain under your control during the move. Coverage ends when the move is completed or when the policy no longer provides coverage for the residence premises.

**C.9** We cover personal property in a building you occupy as a temporary residence. The building must be used by you for personal, family, or household purposes. We do not cover property located in a building that you own or rent for use by another person.

**C.10** We cover personal property while it is being moved to or from the residence premises. The property must be in your possession or under your control. We do not cover loss caused by the property being delivered to a purchaser or other transferee.

**C.11** We cover personal property while it is in a self-storage facility. The facility must be used by you for personal, family, or household storage. Coverage does not apply to property stored as part of a business or commercial activity.

**C.12** We cover personal property while it is in a building that you rent for your personal use. The building must be used as a temporary residence or for storage of covered property. We do not cover property in a building rented to others.

**C.13** We cover personal property that is attached to a motor vehicle when the property is otherwise covered. The attachment must not convert the property into a permanent part of the motor vehicle. We do not cover the motor vehicle itself under Coverage C.

**C.14** We cover personal property that is used with a motor vehicle. The property must be designed for personal use and must not be permanently installed in the vehicle. Coverage does not apply to property that is permanently installed in or specially adapted for the vehicle.

**C.15** We cover personal property that is used with a watercraft. The property must be designed for personal use and must not be permanently installed in the watercraft. We do not cover the watercraft under Coverage C except to the extent provided for covered watercraft.

**C.16** We cover personal property that is used with an aircraft. The property must be designed for personal use and must not be permanently installed in the aircraft. We do not cover an aircraft, its parts, or its equipment under Coverage C.

**C.17** We cover personal property that is used with a recreational vehicle. The property must be owned or used by an insured for personal, family, or household purposes. Coverage does not apply to the recreational vehicle itself.

**C.18** We cover personal property that is used for a hobby. The hobby must not be conducted as a business. We do not cover property held for sale or property used to produce income.

**C.19** We cover personal property used in an activity that produces income. The activity must be incidental to the use of the residence premises. Coverage is subject to the special limit that applies to business property.

**C.20** We cover personal property that is used in a business away from the residence premises. The property must be owned by you or a member of your household. Coverage is subject to the special limit that applies to business property.

**C.21** We cover business property on the residence premises. The most we will pay is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies except that business property is covered only when it is located on the residence premises and is otherwise covered.

**C.22** We cover business property away from the residence premises. The most we will pay for that property is subject to the applicable limit for business property away from the residence premises. This provision applies except that property used in a business conducted by an insured is covered only to the extent provided by this policy.

**C.23** We cover money and precious metals. The most we will pay is two hundred dollars. This limit applies subject to the terms that classify property as money or precious metals and except that covered loss must result from a covered cause of loss.

**C.24** We cover jewelry, watches, and precious stones. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft is one thousand dollars. This limit applies except that it does not increase the amount available for loss caused by a cause of loss other than theft.

**C.25** We cover firearms and related equipment. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft is two thousand dollars. This limit applies subject to the requirement that the property be owned or used by an insured.

**C.26** We cover watercraft, including their trailers, furnishings, and equipment. The most we will pay is one thousand dollars. This limit applies except that the watercraft and related property must be used for personal, family, or household purposes.

**C.27** We cover silverware, silver-plated ware, goldware, and gold-plated ware. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies subject to the requirement that the property be used for personal, family, or household purposes.

**C.28** We cover electronic apparatus while it is in a motor vehicle. The most we will pay is one thousand dollars. This limit applies except that the apparatus must be designed to be operated by power from the vehicle or to be used principally in the vehicle.

**C.29** We cover portable electronic equipment away from the residence premises. The equipment must be designed to be carried by an individual. We do not cover loss to electronic equipment that is permanently installed in a vehicle or watercraft.

**C.30** We cover photographic equipment and recording equipment. The equipment must be owned or used by an insured for personal purposes. We do not cover recordings, data, or media except as provided for under the applicable valuation provisions.

**C.31** We cover cameras, lenses, and related equipment. The equipment must be used for personal, family, or household purposes. Coverage does not apply to equipment held for sale or used in a business.

**C.32** We cover musical instruments. The instruments must be owned or used by an insured. We do not cover instruments used primarily in a business unless coverage is otherwise provided for business property.

**C.33** We cover manuscripts, drawings, and other documents. The documents must be owned or used by an insured for personal purposes. We do not cover the cost of reproducing information that is not otherwise covered property.

**C.34** We cover books and printed material. The books and printed material must be owned by an insured. We do not cover accounts, bills, money, or other documents that represent a financial interest.

**C.35** We cover works of art and similar property. The property must be owned or used by an insured for personal, family, or household purposes. We do not cover property held for sale or property used as business inventory.

**C.36** We cover collections of property. The collection must be owned or used by an insured for personal purposes. We do not cover a collection to the extent that it is maintained as business inventory.

**C.37** We cover furs and garments trimmed with fur. The property must be owned or used by an insured. Coverage is subject to the provisions that apply to personal property at the residence premises and away from the residence premises.

**C.38** We cover bicycles and their equipment. The bicycles must be owned or used by an insured for personal purposes. We do not cover a bicycle while it is used in a business or competition unless coverage is otherwise provided.

**C.39** We cover tools and equipment used for maintenance of the residence premises. The tools and equipment must be owned by an insured. We do not cover tools and equipment used primarily in a business.

**C.40** We cover lawn and garden equipment. The equipment must be used in connection with the residence premises. We do not cover motor vehicles that are required to be registered for use on public roads.

**C.41** We cover food and household supplies. The property must be owned by an insured and intended for personal, family, or household use. We do not cover property held for sale.

**C.42** We cover property used for furnishing the residence premises. The property must be owned by an insured. We do not cover property permanently attached to the residence premises if it is insured under another coverage.

**C.43** We cover clothing and personal effects. The property must be owned or used by an insured. We do not cover property that is rented to others or held for sale.

**C.44** We cover personal property that is temporarily in the possession of a person who is caring for an insured. The property must be used for personal, family, or household purposes. Coverage does not apply to property held by a person as part of a business.

**C.45** We cover personal property while it is in the possession of a moving company. The property must be in transit to or from the residence premises. We do not cover loss that is covered by a bill of lading, contract of carriage, or other agreement.

**C.46** We cover personal property while it is in the possession of a common carrier. The property must be in transit to or from the residence premises. Coverage applies subject to any right of recovery against the carrier.

**C.47** We cover personal property while it is being repaired. The property must be owned by an insured. We do not cover loss that is covered by a repairer's warranty, service agreement, or other contract.

**C.48** We cover personal property while it is being cleaned or serviced. The property must be temporarily away from the residence premises. We do not cover loss that results from the cleaning or servicing process itself unless a covered cause of loss occurs.

**C.49** We cover personal property while it is being used by a residence employee. The employee must use the property in connection with duties performed for you. We do not cover property owned by the residence employee.

**C.50** We cover personal property of a family member who is temporarily living away from the residence premises. The family member must remain a resident of your household. Coverage does not apply after the family member establishes a separate permanent household.

**C.51** We cover personal property of a person who is under the age of majority and is in your care. The property must be used for personal, family, or household purposes. We do not cover property owned by an organization or business.

**C.52** We cover personal property acquired during the policy period. The property must be acquired for personal, family, or household use. Coverage is subject to the applicable limit of insurance and all applicable exclusions.

**C.53** We cover personal property that is replaced after a covered loss. The replacement property must be used for the same general personal purpose. We do not cover an increase in value resulting solely from a change in the property purchased.

**C.54** We cover personal property that is damaged while being protected from further loss. The protective measures must be reasonably necessary because of a covered loss. Coverage does not apply to expenses or property that are otherwise excluded.

**C.55** We cover personal property that is removed from the residence premises to protect it from a covered loss. The removal must be reasonably necessary. Coverage continues subject to the applicable provisions for property away from the residence premises.

**C.56** We cover personal property that is damaged while being removed from danger. The removal must be caused by an imminent covered loss. We do not cover property damaged by an excluded cause of loss.

**C.57** We cover personal property that is lost as a result of a covered loss. The loss must be reasonably related to the covered event. We do not cover property that is voluntarily abandoned or intentionally discarded.

**C.58** We cover personal property that is stolen from an insured location. The property must be owned or used by an insured. Coverage does not apply to property that is voluntarily given to another person unless the property is taken by force or threat of force.

**C.59** We cover personal property when a covered cause of loss directly causes damage to it. The damage must occur during the policy period. We do not cover loss caused by an excluded cause of loss, whether the excluded cause acts alone or with another cause.

**C.60** We settle covered loss to personal property according to the valuation provisions that apply to the property. We may pay the cost to repair, replace, or otherwise restore the property when permitted by this policy. Payment is subject to the applicable limit of insurance, special limit, deductible, and other terms of this policy.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** We cover the reasonable increase in necessary living expenses incurred by you so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living when a covered loss makes the "residence premises" uninhabitable. We also cover the fair rental value of that part of the residence premises occupied by you before the loss. These coverages are subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions that apply to this coverage.

**D.2** The most we will pay for the total of "Additional Living Expense" and "Fair Rental Value" is twenty percent. Except that this limit does not increase because more than one insured or household member incurs an expense.

**D.3** "Additional Living Expense" means the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living. The increase must result from a covered loss to property described in the policy. You must incur the expense during the time reasonably required to make the residence premises fit to live in again.

**D.4** "Fair Rental Value" means the fair rental value of that part of the residence premises occupied by you before the loss, less expenses that do not continue while the premises are uninhabitable. We cover this amount when a covered loss makes the premises uninhabitable. Subject to the limit that applies to this coverage, we pay for the loss of rental value only while the premises remain unfit for occupancy.

**D.5** An insured must use that part of the residence premises that remains usable when doing so is reasonable. The insured must take reasonable steps to avoid or reduce an expense covered under this provision. We may reduce payment for an expense that could have been avoided by reasonable action.

**D.6** An insured must notify us promptly when a covered loss makes the residence premises uninhabitable. The notice must describe the condition of the premises and the expenses that the insured expects to incur. Unless we agree otherwise, the insured must provide supporting information when we request it.

**D.7** We cover necessary expenses incurred by you to obtain temporary housing when the residence premises cannot be occupied because of a covered loss. The temporary housing must be reasonably suitable for your household. Provided that the expense is not covered when you voluntarily choose to leave premises that remain suitable for occupancy.

**D.8** We cover necessary expenses incurred by you for meals when the covered loss causes a reasonable increase in the cost of maintaining your household. The covered expense is limited to the increase over the amount that you normally spend for meals. Except that we do not cover the cost of meals that you would have incurred if the covered loss had not occurred.

**D.9** We cover necessary expenses incurred by you for transportation when the covered loss causes a reasonable increase in transportation costs. The transportation must be reasonably related to obtaining temporary housing or maintaining customary household activities. Subject to this requirement, we may consider the cost of public transportation, use of a private automobile, or another reasonable means of transportation.

**D.10** We cover necessary expenses incurred by you for moving and storing property when the residence premises cannot be occupied because of a covered loss. The expense must be reasonably necessary to protect the property or to permit the household to occupy temporary housing. Unless we agree otherwise, we do not cover the cost of moving or storing property for convenience.

**D.11** We cover necessary expenses incurred by you to establish temporary household facilities when a covered loss makes the residence premises uninhabitable. The facilities must be reasonably necessary for your household to maintain its normal standard of living. Provided that we do not cover improvements or upgrades that are not reasonably necessary.

**D.12** We cover the fair rental value of any portion of the residence premises that was rented to others before the covered loss. The rented portion must be uninhabitable because of the covered loss. Except that we do not cover a reduction in rent caused solely by a tenant's failure to pay.

**D.13** We cover the fair rental value of any portion of the residence premises that was held for rental before the covered loss. The amount must be supported by rental records, a lease, or other information that reasonably establishes the rental value. Subject to the terms of the rental arrangement, we may pay only for the rental value that would have been received.

**D.14** An insured must continue to pay obligations that would have been payable if the residence premises had remained habitable. The insured must take reasonable steps to preserve the right to receive rental income. Unless the obligation is canceled or reduced, it is not an expense that reduces the amount otherwise payable under this coverage.

**D.15** We cover necessary expenses incurred by you when a civil authority prohibits use of the residence premises because a covered loss to property other than the residence premises makes the premises uninhabitable. The prohibition must directly result from the covered loss. Except that we do not cover an expense when the civil authority prohibits use for a reason unrelated to the covered loss.

**D.16** We cover necessary expenses incurred by you when access to the residence premises is prohibited by a civil authority because of a covered loss. The prohibition must prevent reasonable occupancy of the residence premises. Subject to this requirement, the covered expense may include the reasonable cost of temporary housing and related household needs.

**D.17** We do not cover expenses that result from the inability of a utility, service provider, or other facility to provide service when the residence premises remain habitable. We do not cover expenses caused solely by interruption of service away from the residence premises. We do not cover an expense unless the condition requiring the expense results from a covered loss.

**D.18** We do not cover expenses caused by a loss that is excluded from coverage. We do not cover an expense that results from an excluded cause of loss even when another condition contributes to the expense. Unless the excluded cause is removed from the sequence of events, the expense is not covered.

**D.19** We do not cover expenses caused by your failure to comply with an order of a governmental authority. We do not cover expenses incurred because you voluntarily vacate the residence premises when occupancy is permitted. Provided that we cover an expense when the governmental order or vacancy requirement directly results from a covered loss.

**D.20** An insured must keep receipts, records, leases, and other documents that support a claim for Loss of Use. The insured must provide those documents to us when we request them. Subject to our right to verify the claim, we may rely on reasonable evidence of the expense or lost rental value.

**D.21** An insured must cooperate with us in determining whether the residence premises are uninhabitable. The insured must permit us to inspect the premises at reasonable times. Unless circumstances make inspection impracticable, the insured must provide access before incurring a substantial expense.

**D.22** We may make payment to you, to a person who provided covered services, or to another person who has a lawful interest in the covered expense. Payment to a person who provided covered services does not increase the amount we will pay. Except that payment does not waive any condition that applies to your claim.

**D.23** We may advance payment for a covered expense when the circumstances reasonably require payment before the amount of the claim is finally determined. An advance does not constitute an agreement that all claimed expenses are covered. Subject to the final adjustment of the claim, an advance will be applied to the amount otherwise payable.

**D.24** We will determine the amount payable for covered living expenses and lost rental value based on the facts supporting the claim. We may consider the actual condition of the residence premises, the customary needs of your household, and the reasonable availability of temporary housing. Provided that we will not pay more than the applicable limit or for an expense that is not otherwise covered.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure against risk of "direct physical loss" to "covered property" unless the loss is excluded or limited by this policy. Loss must be accidental and fortuitous.

**P.2** We cover direct physical loss only when the loss occurs during the policy period. The property must be at the described location when the loss occurs, unless this policy provides otherwise.

**P.3** "Covered property" means property for which this policy provides property insurance. Covered property includes only property that you own or for which an insured has an insurable interest.

**P.4** Direct physical loss requires a distinct, demonstrable alteration of covered property. Mere loss of use, loss of market value, or loss of appearance without physical alteration is not direct physical loss.

**P.5** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance, law, or regulation requiring the testing, monitoring, cleaning up, removing, containing, treating, detoxifying, or neutralizing of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the enforcement follows a covered loss or an excluded loss.

**P.6** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, including seizure, confiscation, destruction, or condemnation of property. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action.

**P.7** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct committed by or at the direction of an insured. This exclusion applies even if another insured did not participate in or know of the conduct.

**P.8** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**P.9** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**P.10** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. This exclusion applies whether the nuclear hazard is controlled, uncontrolled, intended, accidental, or unavoidable.

**P.11** We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, escape, or presence of "pollutants." Pollutants means any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, chemical, or irritant contaminant.

**P.12** We cover direct physical loss caused by fire, smoke, or explosion that results from pollutants. We do not cover the cost to test for, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize pollutants.

**P.13** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, wet rot, dry rot, decay, deterioration, or latent defect. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril resulting from those conditions.

**P.14** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, scratching, mechanical breakdown, or inherent vice. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril resulting from those conditions.

**P.15** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, cracking, expansion, or movement of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril resulting from those conditions.

**P.16** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals owned or kept by an insured. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire or explosion resulting from damage by those animals.

**P.17** We do not cover loss caused by animals owned by an insured when the loss consists of damage caused by chewing, nesting, scratching, fouling, or infestation. This exclusion does not apply to direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**P.18** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, mildew, spores, or other microbial matter. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire or lightning that results in mold, fungus, mildew, spores, or other microbial matter, subject to all policy terms.

**P.19** We do not cover loss caused by the release of water from a plumbing system, heating system, air conditioning system, automatic fire protective sprinkler system, household appliance, or other source when the release occurs because of freezing. An insured must maintain heat in the building or shut off the water supply and drain the system and appliances when the building is unoccupied.

**P.20** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water, steam, or moisture. This exclusion applies whether the seepage or leakage is hidden or visible.

**P.21** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sumps, sump pumps, or related equipment. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the water originates on or away from the described location.

**P.22** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground, including water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, pavement, swimming pool, or other structure. This exclusion applies whether the water is naturally occurring or artificially maintained.

**P.23** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, spray from any of these, or waterborne material carried by any of these. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or another force.

## I.S — Section I — Conditions

**S.1** We insure only against covered loss occurring during the policy period. An insured must comply with the duties stated in these conditions.

**S.2** The "Section I deductible" applies to covered property loss unless another deductible applies. The applicable deductible will be deducted from the amount of covered loss.

**S.3** The Section I deductible applicable to a covered loss must not be less than five hundred dollars. We will apply the deductible shown in the policy or otherwise required by law.

**S.4** A deductible applies separately to each covered loss. We may apply a deductible to related damage arising from the same occurrence.

**S.5** The "hurricane deductible cap" applies when required by applicable law. We will determine the applicable deductible under the terms of this policy and applicable law.

**S.6** We will provide any required "advance notice of deductible change". A deductible change is effective only as permitted by this policy and applicable law.

**S.7** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. Notice must include sufficient information to identify the insured, the property, and the circumstances of the loss.

**S.8** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when property is stolen or when a crime may have occurred. An insured must cooperate with any investigation of the loss.

**S.9** An insured must protect covered property from further damage. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property from further damage.

**S.10** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**S.11** An insured must not dispose of damaged property without our consent, except when disposal is necessary to protect health or safety. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property before it is repaired or discarded when reasonably possible.

**S.12** An insured must exhibit the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must provide us with records and documents we reasonably request.

**S.13** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require. An insured must sign the examination record when it is accurately transcribed.

**S.14** An insured must produce relevant books, records, receipts, invoices, and estimates when we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to make copies of relevant records and documents.

**S.15** An insured must provide a signed, sworn proof of loss within sixty days after our request. The proof of loss must state the information we reasonably require to adjust the claim.

**S.16** The proof of loss must identify the time and cause of loss. The proof of loss must identify the interest of all insureds and others in the property.

**S.17** The proof of loss must identify other insurance that may cover the loss. The proof of loss must identify changes in title, use, occupancy, or possession of the property.

**S.18** The proof of loss must identify damaged property and the amount of loss. The proof of loss must include supporting records, estimates, and receipts when reasonably available.

**S.19** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. Cooperation includes providing truthful and complete information.
