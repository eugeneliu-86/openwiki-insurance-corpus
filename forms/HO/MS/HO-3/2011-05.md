# HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2011-05)

> SUPERSEDED by HO-3 edition 2018-09 for policies written on or after 2018-09-01.
> This edition remains in force for policies written under it and governs the adjustment of any
> loss occurring under such a policy, regardless of when that loss is reported.

Multistate. Effective for policies written on or after 2011-05-01.

## AGREEMENT

We will provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the premium and compliance with all applicable provisions.

## DEFINITIONS

**1. "Actual cash value"** means the cost to repair or replace the damaged property with material of like kind and quality, less a deduction for depreciation.

**2. "Replacement cost"** means the cost to repair or replace the damaged property with material of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.

**3. "Occurrence"** means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, which results in bodily injury or property damage during the policy period.

**4. "Residence premises"** means the one-family dwelling, other structures, and grounds at the location shown in the Declarations, where you reside.

---

## SECTION I — PROPERTY COVERAGES

### A. Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the dwelling on the residence premises shown in the Declarations, including structures attached to the dwelling.

**A.2** We cover materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises used to construct, alter, or repair the dwelling.

**A.3** Losses to the dwelling, including roof surfacing, are settled at replacement cost subject to the applicable deductible, provided the dwelling is insured to at least eighty percent of its replacement cost at the time of loss. No separate settlement basis applies to roof surfacing under this edition.

### B. Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover other structures on the residence premises set apart from the dwelling by clear space.

**B.2** The limit of liability for Coverage B is ten percent of the Coverage A limit, and this amount is additional insurance.

### C. Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world.

**C.2** Personal property is settled at actual cash value at the time of loss, unless a replacement cost personal property endorsement is attached.

**C.3** Special limits of liability apply as follows: two hundred dollars on money and precious metals; one thousand dollars on watercraft including trailers and equipment; one thousand dollars on theft of jewelry, watches, and precious stones; and two thousand dollars on theft of firearms.

### D. Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** If a covered loss makes the residence premises not fit to live in, we cover the reasonable increase in living expenses necessary to maintain your normal standard of living.

**D.2** The limit of liability for Coverage D is twenty percent of the Coverage A limit.

---

## SECTION I — EXCLUSIONS

### A. Water Damage

**A.1** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, storm surge, or overflow of a body of water, whether or not driven by wind.

**A.2** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by water below the surface of the ground, including water that exerts pressure on or seeps or leaks through a building, foundation, swimming pool, or other structure.

**A.3** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by water or waterborne material that backs up through sewers or drains, or that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. This exclusion does not apply if endorsement HO 04 90 Water Backup and Sump Discharge or Overflow is attached to this policy.

### B. Earth Movement

**B.1** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by earthquake, landslide, mudflow, sinkhole collapse, subsidence, or any other earth movement.

### C. Neglect and Deterioration

**C.1** We do not cover loss caused by neglect, meaning the failure of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss.

**C.2** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, rust, mold, wet or dry rot, or settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings.

### D. Intentional Loss

**D.1** We do not cover loss arising out of any act an insured commits or conspires to commit with the intent to cause a loss.

---

## SECTION I — CONDITIONS

**S.1** In case of a covered loss, an insured must give prompt notice to us or our agent and protect the property from further damage.

**S.2** An insured must submit a signed, sworn proof of loss within sixty days after our request.

**S.3** No action may be brought against us unless there has been full compliance with all Section I conditions and the action is started within two years after the date of loss.

**S.4** The deductible shown in the Declarations applies to each loss under Section I.

---

## SECTION II — LIABILITY COVERAGES

### E. Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** If a claim is made or suit is brought against an insured for damages because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence to which this coverage applies, we will pay up to our limit of liability for the damages for which an insured is legally liable, and provide a defense at our expense.

### F. Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

**F.1** We will pay the necessary medical expenses incurred or medically ascertained within three years from the date of an accident causing bodily injury to a person other than an insured.

---

## SECTION II — EXCLUSIONS

**L.1** Coverage E and Coverage F do not apply to bodily injury or property damage arising out of business activities of an insured.

**L.2** Coverage E and Coverage F do not apply to bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of a motor vehicle, watercraft over twenty-five horsepower, or aircraft.
