# HO 17 33 Unit-Owners Coverage A — Special Coverage (Edition 2014-04)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement is attached to and forms part of your "Policy." We amend the Policy only as stated in this endorsement.

The terms of the Policy apply to this endorsement unless they are changed by this endorsement. When this endorsement conflicts with the Policy, this endorsement controls.

We provide the coverage described in this endorsement subject to all applicable Policy provisions. You must comply with the duties and conditions that apply to the coverage provided by this endorsement.

This endorsement applies to property covered under the Policy. We cover that property only when a covered cause of loss results in direct physical loss to property.

We determine coverage under this endorsement according to the facts existing at the time of loss. We apply the applicable limitations, exclusions, conditions, and deductibles.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must provide records and information that we reasonably request.

We may inspect property at any time. An inspection does not constitute a warranty that property is safe, sound, or compliant with any law.

We do not cover loss that is excluded by the Policy or this endorsement. We do not cover loss beyond the coverage provided by this endorsement.

The coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to the limits shown in the Policy. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

Payment under this endorsement does not increase any applicable limit of liability. We may apply any deductible that applies to covered loss.

If any provision of this endorsement is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect. We will apply the remaining provisions to the extent permitted by law.

All other provisions of the Policy remain unchanged.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We cover direct physical loss to property described under "Coverage A". The loss must be caused by a risk of direct physical loss that is not excluded or limited by this coverage.

**W.2** Coverage applies to property at the "residence premises". Property must be owned by you or be property for which you are legally responsible.

**W.3** We cover the portion of the building that you own. We also cover fixtures, alterations, appliances, and improvements that are part of that portion.

**W.4** We cover property that is permanently attached to the building. We also cover materials and supplies located at the residence premises for use in construction, alteration, or repair.

**W.5** We cover direct physical loss only when the property is damaged by a covered cause of loss. Mere loss of value, wear, or change in appearance is not direct physical loss.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, or demolition. We do not cover the increased cost to repair or replace property because of such enforcement.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, rising, shifting, or settling of earth.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, pavement, or other structure.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or otherwise.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. We also do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters through a building opening. This exclusion applies unless the opening is first caused by a covered direct physical loss to the building.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by failure of power or other utility service. This exclusion applies whether the failure occurs on or away from the residence premises.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by neglect. An "insured" must use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, and action by a military force.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, and radioactive contamination.

**W.16** We do not cover loss arising out of an intentional act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting damage is different in kind or degree from that intended or expected.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. We do cover resulting direct physical loss if the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do cover resulting direct physical loss if the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or other decay. We do cover resulting direct physical loss if the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do cover abrupt collapse if it is otherwise covered.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire or explosion resulting from such damage.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. "Pollutants" include any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or irritant contaminant.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We do cover sudden and accidental smoke damage from a covered source on the residence premises.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of water or ice to a fence, pavement, patio, swimming pool, foundation, retaining wall, or bulkhead. We do cover loss to covered property resulting from collapse if the collapse is otherwise covered.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by theft in or to a dwelling under construction. We also do not cover theft of materials and supplies not yet attached to or installed in the building.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if the residence premises is vacant. A residence premises is vacant when it contains no substantial furnishings necessary for normal habitation.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies to seepage or leakage from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from discharge or overflow from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system when caused by freezing. We cover loss caused by freezing only if an insured used reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust entering through an opening. We cover such loss when the opening is first caused by a covered direct physical loss.

**W.30** We do not cover loss to awnings, outdoor antennas, outdoor equipment, or other property exposed to the weather when caused by wind, hail, ice, snow, or sleet. We cover direct physical loss to property enclosed within the building when the loss is otherwise covered.

**W.31** We do not cover loss to land. We also do not cover loss to lawns, plants, shrubs, trees, or growing crops.

**W.32** We do not cover loss resulting from acts or decisions of a governmental body. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss that results from such act or decision.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by theft by an insured. We also do not cover loss caused by theft by any person entrusted with the property.

**W.34** We do not cover loss resulting from business activity conducted by an insured at the residence premises. We cover property used for incidental office purposes when the property is otherwise covered.

**W.35** We do not cover loss to property while it is being manufactured, processed, tested, altered, repaired, or worked upon. We do cover resulting direct physical loss to other covered property if the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**W.36** We do not cover loss consisting of faulty workmanship or defective materials. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss that results from such workmanship or materials.

**W.37** We cover sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a steam, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or hot water heating system. We do not cover loss to the system from which the damage results unless the loss is caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.38** We cover accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. We do not cover the cost to repair the system or appliance from which the water or steam escaped.

**W.39** We cover direct physical loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**W.40** We cover direct physical loss caused by collapse of a building or any part of a building. Collapse means an abrupt falling down or caving in so that the building or part of the building cannot be occupied for its intended purpose.

**W.41** We do not cover a building or part of a building that is in danger of falling down or caving in. We do not cover a building or part of a building that is standing, even if it shows evidence of cracking, bulging, sagging, bending, leaning, settling, shrinkage, or expansion.

**W.42** We cover collapse caused by a covered cause of loss, hidden decay, hidden insect or vermin damage, weight of contents, weight of rain collecting on a roof, or use of defective material or methods in construction. The defective material or methods must be used during construction, remodeling, or renovation.

**W.43** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge of water or steam from a household appliance. We do not cover loss caused by continuous seepage or leakage.

**W.44** We cover direct physical loss caused by the sudden and accidental breaking, burning, or cracking of a water heater. We do not cover the cost to repair or replace the water heater itself unless it is damaged by a covered cause of loss.

**W.45** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. An insured must protect the property from further damage and keep an accurate record of repair expenses.

**W.46** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request and permit us to make copies.

**W.47** An insured must submit a signed, sworn statement of loss when we request it. The statement must describe the property, the cause of loss, the interests of all persons in the property, and the amount of loss claimed.

**W.48** We will adjust covered loss with you. We will not pay for loss that is not covered under this coverage.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The limit of liability for "Coverage A" is twenty-five thousand dollars. This limit is the most we will pay for direct physical loss to property covered under Coverage A.

**W.2** The Coverage A limit applies to all covered property at the "residence premises". We will not increase that limit because more than one insured has an interest in the property.

**W.3** We will not increase the Coverage A limit because more than one covered cause of loss contributes to a loss. We will not increase the limit because a loss affects more than one part of covered property.

**W.4** The Coverage A limit applies whether covered property is repaired, replaced, or not repaired or replaced. The amount we pay remains subject to all applicable policy terms.

**W.5** We will determine the amount of loss after applying the applicable deductible. The deductible does not reduce the Coverage A limit.

**W.6** We will not pay more than the amount of the covered loss. We will not pay for any loss that is excluded or otherwise not covered.

**W.7** The Coverage A limit is not an agreement to repair or replace covered property. Our payment obligation is limited by the actual terms that apply to the loss.

**W.8** A loss to property owned by an insured is subject to the Coverage A limit. A loss to property for which an insured is legally responsible is also subject to that limit when coverage applies.

**W.9** We will apply the Coverage A limit separately from any limit applicable to other coverage. Payment under other coverage does not increase the Coverage A limit.

**W.10** Payment under Coverage A does not reduce a limit applicable to other coverage unless the policy expressly provides otherwise. We will apply all applicable limits to the loss.

**W.11** We may pay for covered loss to an insured. We may also pay a person lawfully entitled to receive payment for covered property.

**W.12** Payment to any person does not create coverage for property otherwise not covered. Payment does not waive any policy condition, limitation, or exclusion.

**W.13** An insured must retain any interest required to recover under Coverage A. We will not pay an insured more than that insured's financial interest in covered property.

**W.14** We will not pay for a loss in excess of the value of the insured's interest in covered property. This provision applies even when another person has an interest in the same property.

**W.15** If covered property is subject to an encumbrance, our payment remains subject to the Coverage A limit. Payment does not alter the rights of an encumbrance holder.

**W.16** We may adjust a covered loss with an insured. Adjustment with an insured does not determine the rights of any person who is not an insured.

**W.17** We may require an insured to provide information necessary to determine the amount of loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of a loss.

**W.18** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable measures taken to protect covered property are subject to the Coverage A limit when covered.

**W.19** We will not pay for avoidable damage occurring after a loss when an insured fails to protect covered property. This provision does not require an insured to endanger any person.

**W.20** We may inspect covered property and examine the circumstances of a loss. Our inspection does not constitute a representation concerning the condition or safety of property.

**W.21** We may request records supporting the amount of a claimed loss. An insured must provide records that are reasonably available to the insured.

**W.22** We may require an insured to show us damaged property. An insured must preserve damaged property to the extent reasonably possible until we have had an opportunity to inspect it.

**W.23** We may pay the value of covered property that is lost or damaged. We may also repair, rebuild, or replace covered property with property of like kind and quality.

**W.24** Our election to repair, rebuild, or replace covered property remains subject to the Coverage A limit. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable efforts to complete that work.

**W.25** We will not be liable for loss resulting from delay caused by an insured's failure to cooperate. We will remain liable only for covered loss otherwise payable under the policy.

**W.26** Any recovered property remains subject to our rights after payment of a covered loss. An insured must notify us if property for which we paid is recovered.

**W.27** We may take possession of recovered property to the extent of our payment. We will account to an insured for any amount received from recovered property that exceeds our interest.

**W.28** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We are not required to accept abandoned property.

**W.29** We will not pay duplicate amounts for the same covered loss. Any payment we make will be reduced by amounts payable for that loss from another source when required by law or policy terms.

**W.30** Our payment for a covered loss is subject to any rights of recovery that apply to us. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.31** We may require an insured to assign rights of recovery to us to the extent of our payment. Such assignment does not increase the Coverage A limit.

**W.32** A dispute concerning the amount of loss does not increase the Coverage A limit. We retain all rights available under the policy and applicable law.

**W.33** The Coverage A limit applies regardless of the number of claims arising from a loss. We will pay no more than the applicable limit for covered loss.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** We will pay only that part of a covered "loss" that exceeds the applicable "deductible". The deductible applies to each covered loss unless this policy provides otherwise.

**W.2** The applicable deductible is the deductible shown in the "Declarations". We apply the deductible that applies at the time of loss.

**W.3** A deductible applies to covered direct physical loss to covered "property". The deductible applies before we determine the amount payable for that loss.

**W.4** All covered loss arising from the same "occurrence" is subject to a single deductible. Loss arising from separate occurrences is subject to separate deductibles.

**W.5** We determine whether loss arose from the same occurrence by considering the cause, time, and circumstances of the loss. Related damage does not create separate occurrences merely because it is discovered at different times.

**W.6** When more than a single deductible could apply to the same loss, we apply the highest applicable deductible. We do not apply deductibles cumulatively unless this policy expressly requires cumulative application.

**W.7** The deductible does not reduce the applicable "Limit of Liability". Our payment for covered loss remains subject to the applicable Limit of Liability after application of the deductible.

**W.8** We apply the deductible to the total covered loss before allocating payment among covered property interests. We do not apply the deductible separately to each item of damaged property.

**W.9** We apply the deductible after determining the amount of covered loss under the applicable valuation provisions. We do not apply the deductible to amounts that are not covered under this policy.

**W.10** A deductible applies regardless of the number of insureds claiming an interest in the damaged property. An insured may not combine deductibles to obtain a lower deductible.

**W.11** A deductible applies regardless of the number of persons who caused or contributed to the loss. A deductible also applies regardless of the number of claims presented for the same occurrence.

**W.12** Payment by us for a loss does not waive application of the deductible to that loss. Payment by us also does not waive our right to apply a deductible to later covered loss.

**W.13** An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for us to determine the applicable deductible. An insured must cooperate with us in identifying the occurrence that caused the loss.

**W.14** An insured must not increase a loss in an effort to satisfy a deductible. We will not pay for any increase in loss caused by an insured's failure to protect covered property from further damage.

**W.15** The deductible applies to loss whether or not an insured is legally responsible for the cause of loss. The deductible also applies when another person may be responsible for all or part of the loss.

**W.16** Any amount recovered from a responsible person is handled under our rights of recovery. Such recovery does not change the deductible applicable to the loss.

**W.17** We may pay a covered loss directly to an insured, a mortgagee, or another person entitled to payment. The manner of payment does not change the deductible that applies.

**W.18** A dispute concerning the value of loss does not suspend application of the deductible. We apply the deductible to the covered amount ultimately determined to be payable.

**W.19** A deductible applies to covered loss even if repair or replacement is not completed. The deductible applies to the covered amount otherwise payable under this policy.

**W.20** We do not apply a deductible to reduce amounts that we are required to pay apart from covered property loss. We apply a deductible only as provided by this policy.

**W.21** An insured may not transfer, assign, or divide a covered loss to avoid application of a deductible. Any such transfer, assignment, or division does not affect our application of the deductible.

**W.22** The deductible remains applicable if covered property is owned by more than one person. The deductible is not increased because more than one person has an interest in the property.

**W.23** The deductible remains applicable if damage affects more than one category of covered property. We apply the deductible to the combined covered loss arising from the occurrence.

**W.24** We apply the deductible without regard to the amount sought by an insured. We determine the deductible from the covered loss, not from the amount claimed.

**W.25** An insured must not incur repair costs solely to alter application of the deductible. Reasonable measures taken to protect covered property remain subject to the terms of this policy.

**W.26** The deductible applies to any settlement of a covered loss. A settlement does not alter the deductible unless we agree otherwise in writing.

**W.27** No act or omission by us changes the applicable deductible unless we agree to the change in writing. Any change must be authorized by us.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover "direct physical loss" unless the loss is caused by a covered peril. We do not cover loss consisting only of diminished value, loss of market, or loss of use.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, demolition, zoning, or land use. This exclusion applies whether the enforcement occurs before, during, or after a covered loss.

**W.3** We do not cover any increased cost caused by a requirement to test for, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize "pollutants." We do not cover loss arising from the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants.

**W.4** We do not cover loss caused by earthquake, land shock waves, tremors, volcanic eruption, landslide, mudslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, or earth movement. This exclusion applies whether the earth movement is caused by natural forces or human activity.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover resulting cracking, splitting, or separation unless a covered peril causes the loss.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or corrosion. We do not cover loss caused by rust, smog, wetness, dryness, or changes in temperature.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover damage caused by nesting, infestation, gnawing, scratching, chewing, or discharge by animals.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do not cover loss caused by any remediation, testing, monitoring, or treatment of these conditions.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by contamination. We do not cover loss caused by the presence, release, or dispersal of any substance that impairs property for its intended use.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by smoke, vapor, fumes, soot, acids, alkalis, chemicals, or waste unless directly caused by a covered peril. We do not cover damage caused by industrial, agricultural, or commercial operations.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by power failure, power surge, interruption of utility service, or failure of communication service. This exclusion applies when the failure occurs away from the residence premises.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by failure of a utility, appliance, heating system, cooling system, plumbing system, or protective device to operate. We do not cover loss caused by interruption of service to property.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sumps, or related equipment. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from any drainage, containment, or pumping system.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground, including water that exerts pressure on or seeps through a building, foundation, pavement, swimming pool, or other structure. Flood and surface water remains excluded.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, ice, or hail entering through an opening not caused by a covered peril. We do not cover loss caused by repeated leakage or seepage of water or steam.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems while the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied. An "insured" must use reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off water and drain the system.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, overflow, or leakage from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system that has been neglected. We do not cover loss caused by failure to repair a known condition.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether the work is performed by you or another person.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by defective materials used in repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, or maintenance. We do not cover the cost to correct faulty materials or faulty work.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by collapse, unless the collapse is directly caused by a covered peril. We do not cover loss caused by the settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of a building or part of a building.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by pressure or weight of water, ice, snow, or sleet to a fence, pavement, patio, retaining wall, foundation, or swimming pool. We do not cover damage caused by freezing or thawing beneath these structures.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by theft in or from a dwelling under construction. We do not cover loss caused by theft of materials or supplies intended for use in construction, renovation, or repair.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by an insured, tenant, resident, employee, or person entrusted with the property. We do not cover loss caused by voluntary parting with property by trick, scheme, false pretense, or fraud.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts or criminal acts committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss differs in kind or degree from that intended or expected.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. We do not cover further damage resulting from that neglect.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or action by a military force. We do not cover loss caused by destruction, seizure, or use of property for military purposes.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, nuclear radiation, radioactive contamination, or radioactive material. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, seizure, confiscation, destruction, or condemnation of property. We do not cover loss caused by delay, omission, or failure of a governmental authority to act.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by acts or decisions of a governmental body or other person concerning construction, repair, demolition, zoning, or land use. We do not cover loss caused by an order that prevents occupancy or use of property.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, electrical breakdown, short circuit, arcing, or failure of electronic components. We do not cover loss to a device, appliance, or system caused solely by internal failure.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by artificial electrical current, including arcing, unless fire results. If fire results, we cover only the direct loss caused by fire.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We do not cover loss caused by smoke that is not sudden and accidental.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by collapse of a dam, dike, levee, bulkhead, retaining wall, or similar structure. We do not cover loss caused by seepage, erosion, or deterioration of these structures.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by the failure, rupture, overflow, or discharge of a swimming pool, hot tub, spa, fountain, or similar water feature. We do not cover loss caused by leakage from related piping or equipment.

**W.35** We do not cover loss to property used primarily for business purposes. We do not cover loss arising from business activities conducted by you or by another person at the residence premises.

**W.36** We do not cover loss arising from professional, trade, service, rental, or commercial activity. We do not cover loss arising from the manufacture, sale, storage, or distribution of goods.

**W.37** We do not cover loss to property rented, held for rental, or occupied by persons other than you, except as otherwise specifically provided. We do not cover loss arising from a tenant's use, occupancy, or possession of property.

**W.38** We do not cover loss to property while it is being transported, shipped, mailed, or delivered by a carrier. We do not cover loss caused by handling, loading, unloading, or packing.

**W.39** We do not cover loss to property while it is in the care, custody, or control of a carrier, bailee, repairer, or processor. We do not cover loss caused by the acts or omissions of those persons.

**W.40** We do not cover loss to property used in connection with an aircraft, hovercraft, watercraft, or motorized vehicle. We do not cover loss caused by operation, maintenance, storage, or use of such property.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by animals owned, kept, boarded, bred, trained, or raised by you. We do not cover loss caused by livestock or animals maintained for commercial purposes.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by smoke, heat, or fumes from a fireplace, stove, furnace, grill, heater, or similar device that is not properly maintained. We do not cover loss caused by operation contrary to the manufacturer's instructions.

**W.43** We do not cover loss caused by repeated or continuous exposure to the same harmful condition. We do not cover loss caused by gradual damage that occurs over time.

**W.44** We do not cover loss caused by defective maintenance, deferred maintenance, or failure to make repairs. We do not cover the cost of restoring property to a condition better than its condition immediately before loss.

**W.45** We do not cover loss caused by loss of electronic data, corruption of electronic data, or inability to access electronic data. We do not cover loss caused by computer virus, malicious code, or unauthorized access to a computer system.

**W.46** We do not cover loss caused by interruption of internet, telephone, cable, satellite, or other communication service. We do not cover loss caused by failure of any device that receives, stores, transmits, or processes data.

**W.47** We do not cover loss arising from the failure of an alarm, detector, monitoring service, or protective safeguard. We do not cover loss caused by delayed notification of fire, theft, water, or other emergency.

**W.48** We do not cover a claimed increase based solely on the "Coverage B percentage." We do not cover a claimed increase based solely on the "Coverage C limit as a percentage of Coverage A."

**W.49** We do not cover an amount claimed solely because of the "loss of use limit." We do not cover an amount claimed solely because of the "debris removal additional amount."

**W.50** We do not cover an amount that is within the "hurricane deductible floor." We do not cover loss caused by wind-driven rain unless the rain enters through an opening caused by a covered peril.

**W.51** We do not cover loss caused by wind, hail, or wind-driven objects to property that is not fully enclosed. We do not cover loss caused by weather conditions when the property has been left exposed to those conditions.

**W.52** We do not cover loss caused by the weight of persons, animals, equipment, or personal property on a roof, deck, balcony, porch, or similar structure. We do not cover loss caused by overloading or improper use of such structure.

**W.53** We do not cover loss caused by vibration, movement, or shifting of property during repair, renovation, construction, or remodeling. We do not cover loss caused by excavation, drilling, blasting, or pile driving.

**W.54** We do not cover loss caused by leakage from a roof, wall, window, door, skylight, or flashing that has not been maintained. We do not cover damage caused by deterioration of caulking, sealant, grout, or weatherproofing material.

**W.55** We do not cover loss caused by odors, staining, discoloration, or cosmetic damage that does not impair the function of property. We do not cover loss caused by scratches, dents, chips, or scuffs unless directly caused by a covered peril.

**W.56** We do not cover loss caused by testing, inspection, appraisal, valuation, or examination of property. We do not cover loss caused by delay in repair, replacement, restoration, or settlement.

**W.57** We do not cover loss for which another person, organization, carrier, manufacturer, contractor, or governmental body is legally responsible. We do not cover loss to the extent that you recover or may recover from another source.

**W.58** We do not cover loss caused by failure to comply with a manufacturer's recall, repair notice, safety notice, or maintenance instruction. We do not cover the cost to replace property subject to recall or warranty.

**W.59** We do not cover loss consisting of fines, penalties, punitive damages, assessments, or legal expenses imposed upon you. We do not cover loss arising from a contract, agreement, or warranty that expands liability beyond that imposed by law.

**W.60** We do not cover any loss excluded by the terms of this coverage. We do not cover any expense, charge, or assessment that is not directly attributable to covered direct physical loss.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties and conditions stated in this insurance. Compliance by an insured does not relieve another insured of that insured's separate duties.

**W.2** This insurance is void as to an insured who has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance. This condition applies before or after a loss.

**W.3** An insured must not engage in fraud relating to this insurance or a claim. Fraud by an insured may affect the rights of that insured.

**W.4** An insured must keep us informed of a current mailing address and other contact information needed to administer a claim. Notice sent by us to the last address known to us is sufficient notice.

**W.5** An insured must give us notice of a "loss" within sixty days after the loss. The notice must identify the insured property involved and provide available information concerning the circumstances of the loss.

**W.6** An insured must use reasonable means to protect "covered property" from further damage following a loss. An insured must keep a record of reasonable measures taken to protect covered property.

**W.7** An insured must make reasonable repairs necessary to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.8** An insured must not discard damaged property unless disposal is necessary for safety or required by a public authority. An insured must provide available evidence of the condition of property that is discarded.

**W.9** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when a loss may involve theft, vandalism, malicious mischief, or another unlawful act. An insured must cooperate with any reasonable investigation concerning the loss.

**W.10** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when requested by us. The inventory must identify the property and state available information concerning its age, condition, and value.

**W.11** An insured must provide a signed, sworn "proof of loss" when requested by us. The "days to submit a signed, sworn proof of loss" applies to that duty.

**W.12** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as reasonably required. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine available records concerning the loss.

**W.13** An insured must provide records, receipts, invoices, photographs, estimates, and other documents reasonably requested by us. An insured must authorize us to obtain records that are material to a claim when authorization is reasonably necessary.

**W.14** An insured must submit to examination under oath when requested by us. An insured must sign the transcript of an examination under oath when it is accurately recorded.

**W.15** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a "claim." Cooperation includes providing truthful and complete information that is material to the claim.

**W.16** An insured must permit us reasonable access to the location where covered property is located. An insured must take reasonable steps to obtain access when the property is under the control of another person.

**W.17** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense that is not reasonably necessary to protect covered property. This condition does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable emergency measures.

**W.18** An insured must not abandon covered property to us unless we agree in writing. We may take possession of damaged property after payment without accepting abandoned property.

**W.19** An insured must provide information concerning any person with an interest in covered property. An insured must also provide available documents establishing the nature of that interest.

**W.20** An insured must notify us of any other insurance that may apply to a loss. An insured must provide available information concerning the insurer, coverage, and claim status of that other insurance.

**W.21** An insured must not seek duplicate recovery for the same loss. Amounts recovered from another source for the same loss will be considered in determining payment by us.

**W.22** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for a loss. An insured must not impair those rights after a loss without our written consent.

**W.23** If we make payment for a loss, we may exercise the rights of recovery held by an insured against a responsible person or organization. An insured must do whatever is reasonably necessary to secure and preserve those rights.

**W.24** An insured must assist us in pursuing recovery from a responsible person or organization when reasonably requested. We will not require an insured to pursue recovery at the insured's own expense.

**W.25** The applicable deductible will be applied to a covered loss. The "AOP deductible floor" applies when it is applicable to the loss.

**W.26** An insured must provide information reasonably necessary to determine the applicable deductible. This includes information concerning the cause, origin, and sequence of events producing the loss.

**W.27** We may determine the value of damaged property based on available evidence of its condition immediately before the loss. An insured must provide available evidence relevant to that determination.

**W.28** The "replacement cost threshold percentage" applies when it is applicable to the valuation of a loss. An insured must provide records reasonably necessary to determine whether that threshold applies.

**W.29** An insured seeking payment based on "replacement cost" must provide information showing that the damaged property has been repaired or replaced when we request that information. We may defer payment of amounts dependent on repair or replacement until the required information is provided.

**W.30** We may determine "actual cash value" by considering the property's condition, age, use, quality, and available market information. An insured may provide relevant evidence concerning actual cash value.

**W.31** We may consider "depreciation" when determining the value of damaged property. Depreciation will be based on the condition and expected useful life of the property.

**W.32** When a loss involves items that are part of a pair, set, or collection, we may consider the value of the item or items actually damaged. An insured must retain the undamaged item or items when reasonably requested by us.

**W.33** An insured must provide access to undamaged property when it is reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim involving a pair, set, or collection. An insured must not alter that property before we complete a requested inspection.

**W.34** If there is a disagreement concerning the amount of loss, either party may demand an "appraisal." The demand must be made in writing and must identify the matters in disagreement.

**W.35** Each party to an appraisal must select a competent and impartial appraiser. The appraisers must select an impartial umpire if they cannot agree on the amount of loss.

**W.36** An insured must cooperate in the appraisal process and provide information reasonably requested by the appraiser or umpire. An appraisal determines the amount of loss and does not determine coverage.

**W.37** Payment by us does not waive a condition, limitation, or defense available to us. Our investigation or negotiation of a claim does not waive any right unless we state otherwise in writing.

**W.38** An insured must not assign an interest in this insurance without our written consent. This condition does not prevent transfer of rights that arise after a loss.

**W.39** An insured must notify us of a change in ownership or occupancy that materially affects the insured property. An insured must provide available information concerning the change when we request it.

**W.40** An insured must notify us if covered property is transferred to another person after a loss. The notice must identify the property and describe the transferred interest.

**W.41** If an insured dies, the legal representative of the deceased insured may act with respect to covered property and a claim. A person having temporary custody of covered property must comply with applicable duties concerning that property.

**W.42** An insured must provide documents showing authority to act for an estate, trust, guardian, or other representative capacity when requested by us. We may rely on documents that reasonably appear valid.

**W.43** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this insurance. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not create an obligation beyond the coverage provided by this insurance.

**W.44** An insured must pay any premium due under this insurance when payment is required. Failure to pay premium may affect the continuation of this insurance as permitted by applicable law.

**W.45** An insured must notify us of a material error in information provided to us concerning the insured property or a claim. An insured must correct that information when the error becomes known.

**W.46** An insured must preserve electronic records, messages, photographs, and other digital information material to a claim when reasonably possible. An insured must provide reasonably accessible copies of that information when requested by us.

**W.47** An insured must not alter, fabricate, or destroy evidence material to a claim. An insured must identify evidence that cannot be preserved and explain the reason it cannot be preserved.

**W.48** An insured must provide reasonable assistance when we seek to determine the cause or origin of a loss. This assistance may include access to damaged property and available information concerning maintenance or repair.

**W.49** An insured must obtain reasonable estimates for repair or replacement when requested by us. An insured may select a contractor or repair service, subject to our right to review the proposed work and cost.

**W.50** An insured must notify us before permanent repairs are completed when reasonable circumstances allow. This condition does not prevent repairs necessary to protect persons or property from immediate harm.

**W.51** An insured must keep receipts and other records for temporary protective measures and emergency repairs. An insured must provide those records when requested by us.

**W.52** An insured must not increase a loss through neglect or intentional conduct. An insured must take reasonable measures to prevent avoidable damage after a loss.

**W.53** An insured must provide truthful information concerning prior damage, repairs, claims, and known conditions affecting covered property when requested by us. An insured must identify available records supporting that information.

**W.54** We may inspect covered property and review relevant records in connection with underwriting or a claim. An inspection by us does not constitute a warranty that property is safe, sound, or compliant with any law.

**W.55** An insured must comply with lawful requirements of a public authority that apply because of a loss. An insured must provide us with notices, orders, or other records issued by that authority when requested by us.

**W.56** An insured must notify us of a claim made by another person arising from a loss involving covered property. An insured must forward to us any demand, notice, summons, or legal paper relating to that claim.

**W.57** An insured must not admit liability or settle a claim made by another person without our written consent. This condition does not prevent an insured from providing truthful information to a public authority or emergency responder.

**W.58** An insured must cooperate with us in the defense of a claim made by another person when we provide a defense. An insured must attend proceedings and provide information when reasonably requested by us.

**W.59** No person or organization has a right to bring an action against us unless all applicable conditions have been met. The "time to bring action" applies to any action against us.

**W.60** An insured must bring any action against us in the manner required by applicable law. An insured must provide us with a copy of legal papers relating to that action.

**W.61** A waiver or change of a condition is effective only when made in writing by us. A waiver of a condition for a particular matter does not waive that condition for another matter.

**W.62** Our failure to enforce a condition does not waive our right to enforce that condition later. No course of conduct changes the terms of this insurance unless we agree in writing.

**W.63** If more than one insured has an interest in covered property, we may communicate with any insured concerning a claim. Payment to an insured or a person legally entitled to payment discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.64** An insured must provide information concerning competing claims to payment when known to the insured. We may withhold payment until competing interests are resolved or adequate direction is provided.

**W.65** An insured must not seek benefits under this insurance for a loss that is not covered. We may investigate a reported loss without admitting that coverage applies.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" to "covered property" in accordance with this section. We will not pay more than the amount of the covered loss.

**W.2** We will determine the amount of loss after application of all applicable terms of this policy. We may consider the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

**W.3** We will determine loss to property that is damaged but repairable by the reasonable cost to repair the damaged property. Repair must restore the property to a condition that is substantially similar to its condition before the loss.

**W.4** We may settle a covered loss by paying for repair, restoration, or replacement. We may also settle a covered loss by paying the value of the damaged property, when permitted by this policy.

**W.5** We will not pay for increased cost caused by delay, neglect, or failure to protect property from further damage. An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve damaged property for our inspection.

**W.6** An insured must provide us with records, estimates, receipts, and other information we reasonably request to determine the amount of loss. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property before it is repaired, replaced, or disposed of, unless emergency action is necessary.

**W.7** The loss settlement basis for "personal property" is replacement cost. We will apply that basis only to covered personal property.

**W.8** We will determine the amount of loss to personal property based on the property involved in the covered loss. We may consider the cost to repair or replace the property with property of like kind and quality.

**W.9** We may settle a covered loss to personal property by repair or replacement. If replacement is not reasonably available, we may settle based on the value of the property at the time of loss.

**W.10** We will not pay for loss to property that has not been damaged by a covered peril. We will not pay for loss resulting solely from deterioration, wear, or maintenance needs.

**W.11** We will not pay for any part of a loss that is betterment. Betterment includes an improvement that increases the usefulness, value, or expected life of damaged property beyond its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.12** We may use new, used, rebuilt, or reconditioned materials in making repairs or replacements. The materials used must be reasonably comparable in function and quality to the damaged property.

**W.13** We will not require an insured to replace damaged property. If an insured does not repair or replace damaged property, we will settle the loss on the basis otherwise applicable under this policy.

**W.14** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. We may require an insured to surrender damaged property to us when we pay for replacement.

**W.15** We may take all or part of damaged property at its agreed or appraised value. Our taking of damaged property will not increase the amount we otherwise owe for the covered loss.

**W.16** We may pay an insured, a mortgagee, or another person having a lawful interest in the property. Payment to a person having a lawful interest does not waive any policy condition.

**W.17** We will not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace damaged property. We will not pay for property that is not reasonably necessary to complete repair or replacement.

**W.18** We may settle loss to a part of a set, pair, or collection by paying the difference between its value before and after the loss. We may also pay the cost to repair or replace the affected item.

**W.19** We will not pay for diminution in value that remains after repair or replacement. We will pay only the amount determined under the applicable loss settlement basis.

**W.20** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of loss. An insured must provide access to the damaged property and to records relating to its ownership, value, and condition.

**W.21** We may require an insured to submit a signed statement of loss. The statement must identify the damaged property and describe the circumstances of the covered loss.

**W.22** We may require estimates from qualified persons selected by an insured or by us. We may evaluate the reasonableness of any estimate based on available materials, labor, and methods of repair.

**W.23** We will not pay for the cost to repair undamaged property solely to achieve matching in color, texture, design, or appearance. We may pay for matching when it is reasonably necessary to complete repair of damaged covered property.

**W.24** We will not pay for loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective repair work performed after the loss. An insured must ensure that repairs are performed in a competent manner.

**W.25** We may inspect repaired or replaced property before making payment for the completed work. An insured must make the property available for inspection upon our request.

**W.26** We may deduct the value of any salvage from the amount otherwise payable. We may recover salvage that becomes our property after payment of the covered loss.

**W.27** We will not pay for duplicate recovery of the same loss. Amounts recovered from another source for the same damaged property may reduce the amount we pay.

**W.28** We may make payment jointly to an insured and a person with an interest in the property. An insured must assist us in identifying persons who may have such an interest.

**W.29** We will settle loss to property subject to any applicable limitation that applies to that property. A limitation applicable to property remains applicable regardless of the method of settlement.

**W.30** The "association assessment limit" may apply to a covered loss involving an assessment. We will determine any payment for such loss in accordance with the applicable policy terms.

**W.31** A "fire department call fee" is not a measure of the value of damaged property. We will determine any payment involving that fee under the applicable policy terms.

**W.32** The "precious stones theft limit" may apply to covered loss involving precious stones. We will apply that limit before determining the amount payable for the loss.

**W.33** The "special limit on business property on the residence premises" may apply to covered business property. We will apply that limit to the amount otherwise payable for the covered loss.

**W.34** We may resolve a disagreement regarding the amount of loss by using available information concerning the property and the damage. Our payment of an undisputed amount does not prevent further consideration of a disputed amount.

**W.35** We will not pay for costs that are not directly related to a covered loss. We will not pay for costs incurred solely to prepare, present, or support a claim, unless otherwise provided by this policy.

**W.36** An insured must protect property from further damage after a covered loss. Reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property may be considered in determining the amount of loss.

**W.37** We may withhold payment for work that has not been completed when completion is required for the applicable settlement basis. We may release payment when an insured provides satisfactory evidence that the work has been completed.

**W.38** Our payment will not exceed the amount for which we are liable under this policy. Payment of a covered loss does not create an obligation to pay for any later loss.

## W.8 — Definitions

"We", "us", and "our" refer to the insurer providing this coverage. "You" and "your" refer to the person or organization identified as the insured.

"Insured" means you and any person qualifying for coverage under this policy. The term includes a person only while that person meets the applicable requirements for insured status.

"Residence premises" means the location where you reside and that is shown in the policy declarations. It includes the unit used by you as a private residence.

"Unit" means the portion of the condominium property designated for your ownership or exclusive use. It includes areas assigned to you for your exclusive use under the governing condominium documents.

"Condominium property" means the real property subject to condominium ownership. It includes common elements and limited common elements as established by the governing condominium documents.

"Common elements" means portions of condominium property owned in common by the unit owners. Common elements include property available for the shared use or benefit of unit owners.

"Limited common elements" means portions of condominium property reserved for the use of a particular unit or group of units. Such property is not exclusively owned by you unless the governing condominium documents provide otherwise.

"Real property" means land and property permanently attached to land. It includes materials, fixtures, and structures forming part of the property.

"Building" means a structure attached to the land and intended for permanent use or occupancy. Building includes installed fixtures and equipment serving the structure.

"Fixtures" means property that is permanently attached to real property. Fixtures become part of the real property to which they are attached.

"Improvements" means additions, alterations, decorations, or installations made by you to the unit. Improvements include property for which you are responsible under the governing condominium documents.

"Alterations" means changes that modify the construction, design, or finish of the unit. Alterations include work performed to improve the unit for your use.

"Additions" means property added to the unit after its original construction or installation. Additions include attached property that becomes part of the unit.

"Appliances" means devices installed for household use within the unit. Appliances include equipment used for heating, cooling, cooking, cleaning, or refrigeration.

"Personal property" means movable property owned or used by an insured. Personal property does not include real property or property permanently attached to real property.

"Property" means tangible property unless the context requires a different meaning. Property includes both real property and personal property.

"Covered property" means property for which coverage is provided under this policy. Property is covered only to the extent it is owned by an insured or is the insured's responsibility.

"Loss" means direct physical loss of or damage to covered property. Loss includes the resulting reduction in value only when caused by direct physical loss or damage.

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence must result in loss during the policy period.

"Accident" means an unforeseen event causing unintended loss. Accident does not include an act or condition expected or intended by an insured to cause loss.

"Windstorm" means wind, including wind-driven rain, that causes direct physical loss to covered property. Windstorm does not include water entering through an opening not caused by wind.

"Hail" means frozen precipitation that causes direct physical loss to covered property. Hail includes damage caused by the impact of hailstones.

"Weight of ice, snow, or sleet" means the pressure exerted by accumulated frozen precipitation. It includes the resulting strain upon covered property.

"Water" means liquid in any form, including water resulting from rain, melting ice, snow, or sleet. Water includes water containing substances carried or conveyed by it.

"Plumbing system" means pipes, fixtures, and equipment used to supply, distribute, heat, drain, or remove water. Plumbing system includes permanently installed connections serving the unit.

"Heating system" means equipment used to generate or distribute heat within the unit. Heating system includes permanently installed ducts, pipes, and controls.

"Air conditioning system" means equipment used to cool, dehumidify, or circulate air within the unit. Air conditioning system includes permanently installed components serving the unit.

"Electrical system" means wiring, panels, outlets, switches, and equipment used to distribute electrical power. Electrical system includes permanently installed components serving the unit.

"Mechanical breakdown" means a failure of moving or operating parts caused by defect, wear, or malfunction. Mechanical breakdown does not include loss caused solely by deterioration.

"Collapse" means an abrupt falling down or caving in of a building or part of a building. Collapse requires that the building or affected part cannot be occupied for its intended purpose.

"Vandalism" means willful and malicious damage to property. Vandalism includes damage caused by a person acting without your permission.

"Theft" means the unlawful taking of property belonging to an insured. Theft includes an attempted taking that causes direct physical loss to covered property.

"Vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary to normal occupancy. A unit is not vacant merely because an insured is temporarily absent.
