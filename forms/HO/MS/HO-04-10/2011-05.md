# HO 04 10 Additional Interests — Residence Premises (Edition 2011-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

**W.1** This endorsement is attached to and becomes part of your "policy". We provide coverage subject to the terms of this endorsement and the terms of your policy that remain applicable.

**W.2** This endorsement changes your policy only as expressly stated in this endorsement. We do not change any policy term by implication.

**W.3** The "additional interest" identified by this endorsement has an interest connected with the "residence premises". That interest is limited to the capacity in which the additional interest is identified.

**W.4** We recognize the additional interest only for the purposes expressly provided by this endorsement. Recognition of an additional interest does not make that interest an insured.

**W.5** An "insured" retains all rights, duties, and responsibilities that apply under the policy. The additional interest does not acquire an insured's rights, duties, or responsibilities unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

**W.6** This endorsement does not transfer ownership of the residence premises. It does not determine the validity, extent, or priority of any interest in the residence premises.

**W.7** We do not assume responsibility for determining whether the additional interest has a legal or equitable interest in the residence premises. We may rely on information provided to us concerning that interest.

**W.8** Information provided by an additional interest does not bind us unless we accept that information. Our acceptance of information does not waive any policy term or condition.

**W.9** An additional interest has no authority to change, cancel, assign, or reinstate your policy. We may make a policy change only as permitted by the policy.

**W.10** An additional interest has no authority to act for an insured. An insured has no authority to act for an additional interest solely because of this endorsement.

**W.11** We do not provide coverage to an additional interest merely because that interest is identified by this endorsement. Coverage applies only as expressly provided by the policy and this endorsement.

**W.12** We do not cover any loss arising solely from an additional interest's failure to protect its own interest. This endorsement does not impose a duty upon us to protect that interest.

**W.13** An insured must continue to comply with all applicable policy duties. The existence of an additional interest does not relieve an insured of any duty.

**W.14** We may enforce all applicable policy terms against an insured. The additional interest is subject to the terms of this endorsement to the extent those terms apply to that interest.

**W.15** If this endorsement conflicts with a policy term, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. All policy terms not changed by this endorsement remain in effect.

**W.16** This endorsement applies only while it remains attached to your policy. We may terminate its application only as permitted by the policy.

**W.17** The attachment of this endorsement does not create rights for a person or organization not identified as an additional interest. We do not recognize a claim based solely on an unlisted interest in the residence premises.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** An "additional interest" means a person or organization having a lawful financial interest in property at the "residence premises." The additional interest must be identified by you in a manner acceptable to us.

**W.2** An "insured" means a person qualifying as an insured under this coverage. An additional interest does not become an insured solely because of the interest described in this section.

**W.3** "Covered property" means property at the residence premises for which coverage is provided by us. Coverage for an additional interest applies only to the extent of that interest in covered property.

**W.4** A "loss" means accidental direct physical loss to covered property. We determine whether a loss is covered under the terms applicable to the property involved.

**W.5** We provide coverage for the additional interest's financial interest in a covered loss. Coverage applies only when you have an insurable interest in the covered property at the time of loss.

**W.6** The additional interest's recovery is subject to all terms applicable to your recovery for the same loss. The additional interest may not recover more than its lawful interest in the covered property.

**W.7** We may adjust a covered loss with you, the additional interest, or any person authorized to act for either. An adjustment with an authorized person is binding on the additional interest to the extent of that authority.

**W.8** Payment for a covered loss may be made jointly to you and the additional interest. We may make payment directly to the additional interest when its interest is established to our satisfaction.

**W.9** Payment to an additional interest does not increase the amount available for a covered loss. Any payment to the additional interest reduces the amount otherwise payable for that loss.

**W.10** We may require the additional interest to provide evidence of its interest in the covered property. The evidence must be satisfactory to us.

**W.11** An insured must cooperate with us in identifying the additional interest and the nature of its interest. An insured must provide records and information we reasonably request concerning that interest.

**W.12** An insured must promptly notify us of any transfer, release, satisfaction, or termination of the additional interest's interest. We may rely upon information available to us until we receive notice of a change.

**W.13** An additional interest must notify us of any known change affecting its interest in covered property. Notice by the additional interest does not relieve an insured of any duty imposed by this coverage.

**W.14** An additional interest must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require. The additional interest must produce relevant records and documents in its possession or control.

**W.15** An additional interest must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable protective measures do not establish coverage for an otherwise excluded loss.

**W.16** An additional interest must permit us to inspect covered property when reasonably necessary to investigate a claim. The additional interest must permit us to examine damaged property before its disposition when reasonably practicable.

**W.17** We may deny payment to an additional interest when the additional interest conceals or misrepresents a material fact concerning its interest or a loss. This provision applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after a loss.

**W.18** We may deny payment to an additional interest when the interest is not valid or enforceable at the time of loss. We may require documentation establishing the validity and priority of the interest.

**W.19** We do not cover an interest that is fraudulent, fictitious, or created for the purpose of obtaining payment under this coverage. We do not cover an interest transferred after a loss unless we consent in writing.

**W.20** We do not cover loss to property in which the additional interest has no lawful financial interest. We do not cover loss occurring after the additional interest's interest has ended.

**W.21** We do not cover any obligation of the additional interest arising from its ownership, possession, maintenance, operation, or use of property. This coverage applies only to the financial interest described in this section.

**W.22** We do not cover liability of an additional interest to an insured or to any other person. We do not cover damages, penalties, assessments, or costs imposed upon an additional interest.

**W.23** We do not cover any loss caused by the intentional act of the additional interest. We do not cover any loss caused by the direction of the additional interest.

**W.24** We do not cover loss arising from an agreement that changes the nature or extent of the additional interest after a loss. We do not cover loss arising from an agreement that materially impairs our rights.

**W.25** An additional interest must not impair our right to recover from a person responsible for a covered loss. An additional interest must preserve relevant evidence and rights of recovery when reasonably able to do so.

**W.26** If we make payment to an additional interest, we are subrogated to that interest's rights of recovery to the extent of our payment. The additional interest must execute documents and take actions reasonably necessary to secure those rights.

**W.27** An additional interest must hold in trust any recovery received from another source for a loss for which we made payment. The additional interest must promptly remit to us any amount that duplicates our payment.

**W.28** We may apply payment to the secured debt or other obligation represented by the additional interest. Application of payment does not determine the validity, amount, or enforceability of an obligation between you and the additional interest.

**W.29** We are not responsible for determining disputes between you and an additional interest concerning ownership, priority, indebtedness, or entitlement to payment. We may withhold payment until the dispute is resolved by agreement or other lawful means.

**W.30** An insured must notify us of a dispute concerning the additional interest when the dispute may affect a claim. The additional interest must notify us of such a dispute when it has knowledge of the dispute.

**W.31** We may pay the portion of a covered loss that is not subject to a dispute. Payment of an undisputed portion does not waive our rights concerning the remaining portion.

**W.32** We may require a release from an additional interest before making payment. A release may be limited to the loss and interest for which payment is made.

**W.33** An additional interest may assign its right to receive payment only with our written consent. Consent is not required for a transfer of the interest occurring by operation of law.

**W.34** We may recognize a successor to an additional interest upon receipt of satisfactory evidence of succession. The successor acquires no greater rights than the additional interest possessed.

**W.35** The additional interest must maintain records supporting the existence and amount of its interest. The additional interest must provide those records when we reasonably request them.

**W.36** An insured must not represent that this coverage protects the additional interest from loss beyond the interest described in this section. An insured must not make an agreement that purports to bind us without our written consent.

**W.37** An additional interest has no authority to cancel, modify, renew, reinstate, or otherwise alter this coverage. An additional interest has no authority to waive any duty imposed upon an insured.

**W.38** Notice given by us to an insured is notice to the additional interest when the notice concerns covered property in which the additional interest has an interest. We may also provide notice directly to the additional interest.

**W.39** Notice given by an additional interest to us is effective only when received by us. Notice concerning a claim must identify the covered property and the additional interest's asserted interest.

**W.40** We may investigate the financial condition of an additional interest when reasonably related to a claim or asserted interest. The additional interest must cooperate with a reasonable investigation.

**W.41** We may inspect documents evidencing the debt, lien, lease, contract, title, or other basis of the additional interest. The additional interest must provide accurate copies of requested documents.

**W.42** An additional interest must notify us if covered property is transferred, sold, abandoned, or otherwise disposed of. This duty applies when the additional interest has knowledge of the disposition.

**W.43** We do not cover an additional interest's loss of anticipated income, profit, use, market value, or business opportunity. We do not cover consequential loss sustained by an additional interest.

**W.44** We do not cover costs incurred by an additional interest to enforce, preserve, foreclose, repossess, or otherwise exercise its interest. We do not cover legal expenses of an additional interest.

**W.45** We do not cover loss resulting from a breach of contract by you or an additional interest. We do not cover loss resulting from a failure to pay a debt or perform an obligation.

**W.46** An additional interest must apply any payment received from us in a manner consistent with its lawful interest. The additional interest must account for the payment upon our reasonable request.

**W.47** Payment by us to an additional interest does not establish that the additional interest has a valid claim against you. Payment by us does not alter the rights or obligations between you and the additional interest.

**W.48** We may require you and the additional interest to execute a joint proof of loss when their interests cannot reasonably be determined separately. The proof of loss must state the basis for each asserted interest.

**W.49** An insured must provide access to information reasonably necessary to determine whether the additional interest is entitled to payment. An insured must authorize the release of relevant information when legally permitted.

**W.50** We may deny payment when an insured or additional interest fails to comply with a material duty under this section. We may deny payment only to the extent the failure prejudices our ability to determine or resolve the claim.

**W.51** The rights of an additional interest are subject to the acts, omissions, and concealment of an insured when those matters affect the covered property or loss. We may assert against the additional interest any defense available to us concerning the asserted claim.

**W.52** An additional interest may not bring an action against us unless the additional interest has complied with all applicable duties under this section. Any action must be based solely upon the additional interest's lawful interest in a covered loss.

**W.53** We may settle with an additional interest without obtaining the consent of an insured when the settlement does not increase our obligation. A settlement does not create a duty to resolve disputes among interested persons.

**W.54** This coverage ends as to an additional interest when its financial interest in the covered property ends. Coverage also ends when the additional interest knowingly provides false information concerning the interest or a loss.

**W.55** The additional interest's rights under this section are personal to that interest and apply only to covered property at the residence premises. No person or organization acquires rights under this section unless it qualifies as an additional interest.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The applicable "limit of liability" is the most we will pay for "loss" to "covered property" in which an "additional interest" has an interest. The limit of liability applies regardless of the extent of the additional interest's interest in the covered property.

**W.2** An additional interest does not have a separate limit of liability unless we expressly provide one. The presence of an additional interest does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.3** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for all loss arising from the same event. This applies whether payment is sought by you, an additional interest, or any other person claiming through either.

**W.4** Any payment we make to an additional interest reduces the amount available under the applicable limit of liability. Any payment we make to you for the same loss also reduces that amount.

**W.5** We may make payment jointly to you and an additional interest when their interests require joint payment. Joint payment does not increase our limit of liability.

**W.6** We may make payment separately to you or to an additional interest when separate payment is appropriate. Separate payments remain subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.7** We will not make duplicate payment for the same loss. This applies even if more than one person has an interest in the covered property.

**W.8** Payment to an additional interest will not exceed that additional interest's financial interest in the covered property. Payment will also remain subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.9** An additional interest may not recover more than you could recover for the same loss. The additional interest's rights are subject to all policy provisions that apply to you.

**W.10** We may satisfy our obligation by payment to you, to an additional interest, or jointly to both, as their interests appear. Our payment will discharge us to the extent of that payment.

**W.11** An additional interest's claim does not create coverage where coverage does not otherwise apply. We do not cover loss that is not covered under this policy.

**W.12** An additional interest's claim does not enlarge the property covered by this policy. We do not cover property merely because an additional interest has an interest in that property.

**W.13** An additional interest's claim does not enlarge the causes of loss covered by this policy. We do not cover loss caused by a peril that is excluded or otherwise not covered.

**W.14** We will determine the amount of loss before applying the applicable limit of liability. The amount payable will not exceed the lesser of the amount of loss, the applicable limit of liability, or the additional interest's financial interest.

**W.15** We may require reasonable evidence of an additional interest's financial interest before making payment. An insured must provide available information needed to verify that interest.

**W.16** We may require an additional interest to provide information concerning its claim. An additional interest seeking payment must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of the loss.

**W.17** An insured must not impair our rights concerning covered property after a loss. An additional interest must not impair our rights concerning covered property after a loss.

**W.18** We may preserve, protect, inspect, or take possession of damaged covered property to the extent permitted by law. Such action does not increase our limit of liability.

**W.19** We may pay the amount owed to an additional interest without obtaining payment instructions from you when the additional interest's right to payment is established. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**W.20** Payment to an additional interest does not make that additional interest an insured. An additional interest has only the rights expressly provided by this policy.

**W.21** An additional interest does not have a right to direct adjustment of a loss. We may adjust a loss with you, with an additional interest, or with both, as appropriate.

**W.22** We may settle a claim involving an additional interest in a manner that protects the respective interests of you and the additional interest. Any settlement remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.23** We are not obligated to pay an additional interest until the amount of covered loss is determined. We are not obligated to pay an additional interest whose claimed interest is not established.

**W.24** We may withhold payment while there is a reasonable dispute concerning ownership, the extent of an interest, or entitlement to payment. We may require appropriate documentation resolving that dispute.

**W.25** An insured must notify us of any known change affecting an additional interest's financial interest in covered property. A change in that interest does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.26** We may apply payment to the portion of covered property in which an additional interest has an interest. We are not required to apply payment to property in which the additional interest has no interest.

**W.27** We will not pay an additional interest for any amount that has already been paid for the same loss. We will not pay an additional interest for an amount that exceeds its financial interest.

**W.28** The applicable limit of liability is not increased by assignment, transfer, succession, or other change in an additional interest's rights. Any successor takes only the rights held by the additional interest.

**W.29** An additional interest may not assign its right to payment without our consent when consent is required by law or policy provisions. An assignment does not increase our limit of liability.

**W.30** We may require a release from an additional interest as a condition of payment. The release may be limited to the amount we pay.

**W.31** Payment to an additional interest does not waive any right we have against any person. An additional interest must preserve those rights to the extent its actions may affect our recovery.

**W.32** We may pursue recovery from a person responsible for a covered loss after making payment. Any recovery rights of an additional interest are subject to our rights to the extent of our payment.

**W.33** We do not cover an additional interest's costs, fees, charges, or expenses unless they are part of covered loss. Such amounts do not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.34** We do not cover loss to the extent an additional interest has no insurable interest in the covered property. We do not cover loss to the extent payment would be contrary to law.

**W.35** An insured must not make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense for which coverage is sought without our consent, except at the insured's own cost. Such action does not increase our limit of liability.

**W.36** An additional interest must promptly notify us of any claim it makes under this policy. Delay by an additional interest may affect payment to the extent the delay prejudices us.

**W.37** The applicable limit of liability applies after all other policy conditions affecting payment have been met. Satisfaction of a condition does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.38** Our total liability for a covered loss involving an additional interest will not exceed the applicable limit of liability. This provision applies notwithstanding any interest, claim, or payment arrangement involving the covered property.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "Deductible" is the amount you must bear for a covered loss before we make payment. The Deductible applies as stated in this section.

**W.2** The Deductible applies to loss involving the "residence premises" to which this insurance applies. The Deductible also applies to loss involving property covered for the benefit of an additional interest.

**W.3** "Covered property" means property for which this insurance provides coverage. The Deductible applies only when covered property sustains a covered loss.

**W.4** A "covered loss" is direct physical loss that is covered by this insurance. We do not cover the portion of a covered loss that is within the applicable Deductible.

**W.5** An "additional interest" is a person or organization shown as having an interest in covered property. An additional interest is subject to the Deductible applicable to the covered property.

**W.6** You must pay the applicable Deductible whether the loss is adjusted with you or with an additional interest. We may deduct the Deductible from any payment otherwise due.

**W.7** An "occurrence" means an accident, event, or continuing exposure to substantially the same harmful conditions that causes covered loss. We will apply the Deductible in accordance with the occurrence causing the loss.

**W.8** Loss caused by a single occurrence is subject to the applicable Deductible. We will not apply a separate Deductible to parts of the same covered loss merely because the loss affects more than one item of covered property.

**W.9** Loss caused by separate occurrences is subject to the Deductible applicable to each occurrence. This applies even when the occurrences are related in time, location, cause, or claim handling.

**W.10** The Deductible applies before we determine the amount payable for covered loss. We will not increase payment to reimburse you for the Deductible.

**W.11** The Deductible applies to loss caused by a covered peril. The application of the Deductible does not create coverage for loss that is otherwise excluded.

**W.12** The Deductible applies to property damage and to any resulting covered loss of use. We will not apply the Deductible to an expense unless that expense is part of covered loss.

**W.13** The Deductible applicable to a loss is determined by the coverage involved and the cause of loss. When more than one Deductible could apply, we will apply the Deductible that is applicable under the terms of this insurance.

**W.14** We may apply the Deductible to the total covered loss arising from an occurrence. We are not required to allocate the Deductible among damaged items.

**W.15** We may allocate the Deductible among covered property when allocation is necessary to determine payment. Any allocation will not increase the amount we are otherwise obligated to pay.

**W.16** The Deductible applies before payment is made to you, an additional interest, or any other person entitled to payment. Payment to an additional interest does not eliminate the Deductible.

**W.17** An additional interest has no greater right to recover for covered loss than you have under this insurance. The Deductible applies to the additional interest's interest in covered property.

**W.18** A dispute concerning ownership, possession, or priority of payment does not suspend the application of the Deductible. We may withhold payment until the applicable Deductible is determined.

**W.19** You must provide information reasonably necessary for us to determine the applicable Deductible. An insured must cooperate with us in identifying the occurrence and covered property involved.

**W.20** An insured must not incur expenses for the purpose of avoiding application of the Deductible. We will not pay expenses that are not covered merely because they exceed the Deductible.

**W.21** The Deductible applies to covered loss discovered after the event causing that loss. The time at which an insured discovers loss does not determine whether a separate Deductible applies.

**W.22** The Deductible applies to hidden damage when the damage results from covered loss. We will determine the applicable Deductible from the occurrence that caused the damage.

**W.23** The Deductible applies to covered loss that develops over time from an occurrence. We will not treat continuing damage as separate covered losses solely because the damage becomes apparent at different times.

**W.24** The Deductible applies to covered loss involving more than one category of covered property. We may determine payment separately for each category after applying the applicable Deductible.

**W.25** The Deductible applies regardless of whether an insured repairs, replaces, or does not repair damaged property. The manner in which an insured resolves the damage does not reduce the Deductible.

**W.26** The Deductible applies regardless of the actual cash value or replacement cost of damaged covered property. The valuation method used for payment does not change the Deductible.

**W.27** The Deductible applies before any reduction for depreciation, if depreciation applies to the loss. The Deductible remains your responsibility after any valuation adjustment.

**W.28** The Deductible applies to covered loss even when another person may be responsible for that loss. We may seek recovery from a responsible person after making payment.

**W.29** Recovery from a responsible person does not require us to refund the Deductible. We may reimburse the Deductible from a recovery when we determine that reimbursement is appropriate.

**W.30** The Deductible applies to covered loss even when another source of recovery may be available. We will determine our payment without regard to whether you pursue that other source.

**W.31** The Deductible does not reduce an insured's duties after a loss. An insured must comply with all duties that apply to a covered loss.

**W.32** The Deductible does not affect our right to inspect damaged covered property. An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably necessary for our investigation.

**W.33** The Deductible does not affect our right to examine records relating to a covered loss. An insured must provide records that reasonably support the amount and cause of loss.

**W.34** The Deductible applies when we make payment directly to a person or organization other than you. Our direct payment does not waive the Deductible.

**W.35** The Deductible applies when payment is made jointly to you and an additional interest. We may show the Deductible in the calculation supporting the joint payment.

**W.36** The Deductible applies when damaged covered property is subject to an interest held by more than one person or organization. We will not apply a separate Deductible solely because more than one interest exists.

**W.37** The Deductible applies to covered loss arising from negligence. The existence of negligence does not alter the Deductible.

**W.38** The Deductible applies to covered loss arising from intentional acts only to the extent the loss is otherwise covered. The application of the Deductible does not affect any exclusion or condition.

**W.39** The Deductible applies to covered loss occurring during a change in ownership or interest in covered property. No change in ownership or interest alters the Deductible unless we agree otherwise.

**W.40** The Deductible applies to covered loss while covered property is in the care, custody, or control of another person or organization. The location of covered property does not by itself eliminate the Deductible.

**W.41** We may state the Deductible in a payment explanation or settlement document. Any such statement is evidence of our application of the Deductible and does not waive any right under this insurance.

**W.42** An insured must notify us promptly if the insured believes that we applied an incorrect Deductible. We may revise our payment calculation if we determine that a different Deductible applies.

**W.43** The Deductible applies to every payment for covered loss unless we expressly agree otherwise. No action by us waives the Deductible unless we provide that waiver in writing.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss arising out of an interest that is not shown in our records as an "Additional Interest". We do not cover any obligation that exceeds the interest of that Additional Interest in covered property.

**W.2** We do not cover loss to property in which an Additional Interest has no lawful insurable interest. We do not cover loss occurring after that interest has ended, been transferred, or otherwise become unenforceable.

**W.3** We do not cover any debt, obligation, or claim owed by you to an Additional Interest unless the loss is otherwise covered. We do not cover collection costs, finance charges, penalties, or other amounts arising solely from that debt or obligation.

**W.4** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an Additional Interest. We do not cover loss caused by an Additional Interest acting with your knowledge, consent, or direction.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by fraud, concealment, misrepresentation, or material omission by an Additional Interest. We do not cover loss caused by any person acting for an Additional Interest in connection with a claim.

**W.6** We do not cover loss arising from any false statement about ownership, occupancy, use, value, condition, or location of covered property. We do not cover loss arising from a failure to disclose a material change affecting the risk.

**W.7** We do not cover loss occurring while the "residence premises" is used principally for a purpose not disclosed to us. We do not cover loss arising from a business, trade, profession, or occupation conducted at the residence premises.

**W.8** We do not cover loss arising from unlawful use of the residence premises. We do not cover loss arising from the manufacture, cultivation, storage, sale, or distribution of controlled substances.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by any act or decision of a governmental body relating to the regulation, zoning, demolition, repair, construction, or occupancy of property. We do not cover any increased cost arising from compliance with a law or ordinance.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by order of a governmental authority. We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of any law, ordinance, or regulation.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. We do not cover loss caused by a consequence of war.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, nuclear radiation, or radioactive contamination. We do not cover loss caused by the discharge or escape of radioactive material.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by contamination, including the presence, release, discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, or escape of pollutants. We do not cover testing, monitoring, cleanup, removal, containment, treatment, detoxification, or neutralization of pollutants.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke, vapor, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, or waste. We do not cover loss arising from pollutants that are manufactured, handled, stored, transported, or disposed of by you or an Additional Interest.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudflow, subsidence, sinkhole collapse, or earth sinking, rising, or shifting. We do not cover loss caused by volcanic activity except to the extent a covered peril otherwise applies.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up or overflows from a sewer, drain, sump, or related system. We do not cover loss caused by water discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a drain, plumbing system, heating system, air conditioning system, sprinkler system, or appliance to operate as intended. We do not cover loss caused by wear, deterioration, corrosion, or neglect affecting such property.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water, steam, or other fluid. We do not cover loss caused by condensation, humidity, or moisture damage that develops over time.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, pavements, patios, or other structural elements. We do not cover resulting cracking, rupture, or collapse unless directly caused by a covered peril.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or faulty maintenance. We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, decay, rot, or deterioration.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or other microbial matter. We do not cover loss arising from the investigation, remediation, removal, or disposal of such matter.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, gnawing, or discharge from animals.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by defective, inadequate, or faulty planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, grading, compaction, or materials. We do not cover loss caused by a failure to perform maintenance.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the residence premises. We do not cover loss caused by interruption of utility service unless directly resulting from physical loss by a covered peril at the residence premises.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of electronic equipment, software, computer systems, networks, or data processing systems. We do not cover loss caused by inability to access, use, transmit, receive, store, retrieve, process, or protect electronic data.

**W.27** We do not cover loss arising from the loss, corruption, alteration, destruction, theft, or unauthorized disclosure of electronic data. We do not cover loss arising from a cyber attack, malicious code, or unauthorized access to an electronic system.

**W.28** We do not cover loss arising from an act of terrorism. We do not cover loss arising from a threat, allegation, or response to an act of terrorism.

**W.29** We do not cover loss arising from the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of a controlled substance. We do not cover loss arising from property used to conceal, process, or distribute a controlled substance.

**W.30** We do not cover loss arising from criminal, dishonest, fraudulent, or malicious conduct by you, an Additional Interest, or any person acting on behalf of either. We do not cover property obtained through theft, fraud, or unlawful conversion.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a covered loss to property at the "residence premises" in accordance with the terms of this policy and this endorsement. We will not pay more than the amount of your insurable interest or the insurable interest of an "additional interest".

**W.2** We will determine the amount of loss from the direct physical loss to covered property. We will not include any decrease in value that results from a condition that did not arise from the covered loss.

**W.3** We will settle loss to covered property on the basis that applies to that property under this policy. This endorsement does not change the basis of settlement unless its terms expressly provide otherwise.

**W.4** We will not pay more for a covered loss than the applicable limit of liability. The applicable limit of liability is not increased because more than one insured or additional interest has an interest in the property.

**W.5** We will apply the deductible that applies to the covered loss. The deductible will reduce the amount payable for the loss before payment is made to any insured or additional interest.

**W.6** We will pay for covered property that is damaged only to the extent of the direct physical loss. We will not pay for undamaged property merely because it differs in appearance from repaired or replaced property.

**W.7** We may settle a covered loss by paying for repair, restoration, or replacement. Our election to settle by any of these methods will not create an obligation to pay for work that is not necessary to restore damaged covered property.

**W.8** We may pay the actual cash value of damaged covered property. "Actual cash value" means the value of the property immediately before the loss, taking into account its age, condition, quality, and useful life.

**W.9** We may consider reasonable evidence of value in determining actual cash value. Such evidence may include market information, purchase information, condition records, photographs, estimates, and appraisals.

**W.10** We will not include the value of land in determining the amount of loss to a building or other structure. We will include only the value of covered property that sustained direct physical loss.

**W.11** We may deduct depreciation when actual cash value is the applicable basis of settlement. Depreciation will reflect the condition and expected useful life of the damaged property immediately before the loss.

**W.12** We will not deduct depreciation from labor when the applicable basis of settlement requires replacement cost payment and the work has been completed. We may consider labor that is not necessary to repair or replace damaged covered property in determining the amount payable.

**W.13** "Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement cost does not include the cost to replace property with property that is better, larger, more useful, or more valuable than the damaged property.

**W.14** We may pay replacement cost after damaged covered property has been repaired or replaced. An insured must provide records that reasonably establish that the repair or replacement has been completed and that the costs were incurred.

**W.15** If an insured does not repair or replace damaged covered property, we will settle the loss on an actual cash value basis when that basis applies. We will not pay replacement cost merely because an insured intends to repair or replace property at a later time.

**W.16** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will pay the reasonable cost of necessary temporary measures taken solely to protect covered property from further damage caused by a covered peril.

**W.17** We will not pay for permanent repair work performed as a temporary protective measure unless that work is necessary to repair direct physical loss. We will not pay for improvements made while covered property is being repaired or replaced.

**W.18** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless retention is impracticable. An insured must permit us to inspect the property, examine records, and obtain information reasonably related to the loss.

**W.19** We may require an insured to provide photographs, inventories, receipts, contracts, estimates, and other records supporting the amount of loss. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable investigation and adjustment of the claim.

**W.20** We may require an insured to exhibit damaged property as often as reasonably necessary. An insured must not dispose of damaged property without our consent unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.21** We may take all or part of damaged property at its agreed or appraised value after payment of a covered loss. Our taking of damaged property does not increase the amount otherwise payable for the loss.

**W.22** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may exercise our rights concerning salvage without assuming ownership of property that we have not accepted.

**W.23** We will not pay for loss attributable to faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This provision applies only to the extent that the loss is not otherwise covered under this policy.

**W.24** We will not pay the cost to correct a preexisting defect, deterioration, wear, or maintenance condition. We will pay for resulting direct physical loss only when that loss is covered and no exclusion applies.

**W.25** We may require that repair or replacement be performed with materials of like kind and quality. We will not pay the increased cost resulting from an insured's preference for a different material, design, finish, or construction method.

**W.26** We will not pay for costs that are not necessary to repair or replace damaged covered property. We will not pay for work performed solely to achieve uniformity of color, texture, pattern, or appearance with undamaged property.

**W.27** We may consider reasonable local construction costs in determining the amount of loss. We are not required to pay charges that exceed customary costs for comparable repair or replacement work.

**W.28** We will not pay for the cost of complying with a law or ordinance unless that cost is covered under this policy. We will not pay for any cost imposed because of the use, occupancy, condition, or repair of property that was not damaged by a covered loss.

**W.29** We may pay the cost to repair damaged property rather than the cost to replace it when repair will restore the property to its pre-loss function and condition. We will not be required to replace property that can be reasonably repaired.

**W.30** We may settle a loss involving personal property by paying the cost to repair the property or its actual cash value. We will not pay more than the cost to replace the property with property of like kind and quality.

**W.31** We may consider obsolescence, condition, rarity, collectibility, and market demand in determining the actual cash value of personal property. Sentimental value does not increase the amount payable for a covered loss.

**W.32** We will not pay more than the amount required to replace an item with a comparable item that is reasonably available. We are not required to pay for property having special value unless that value is established by reliable evidence.

**W.33** We will apply any applicable special limit to the settlement of a covered loss. The "special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones" applies as provided by this policy.

**W.34** We will apply the applicable limit to loss involving property used in connection with a business. The "home business contents limit" applies as provided by this policy.

**W.35** We will apply the applicable limit to any covered assessment. The "assessment coverage limit" applies as provided by this policy.

**W.36** We will apply the applicable limit to a covered charge imposed for emergency response. The "fire response charge" is subject to the terms and conditions that apply to that coverage.

**W.37** We will not duplicate payment for the same loss under more than one coverage of this policy. If more than one coverage could apply, we will pay under the coverage that provides the broadest applicable protection, subject to its terms.

**W.38** We will not pay more than the amount of loss after applying all applicable deductibles, limitations, and conditions. Payment under this endorsement will reduce the amount available under the applicable coverage.

**W.39** We will pay an additional interest only to the extent of that additional interest's financial interest in covered property. Payment to an additional interest does not create coverage for property that is not covered property.

**W.40** We may issue payment jointly to an insured and an additional interest when both have an interest in the damaged property. A joint payment does not determine the ownership rights of any person.

**W.41** We may make payment to an additional interest named in our records when that additional interest has provided information reasonably necessary to establish its interest. We are not responsible for determining disputes among persons claiming an interest in the property.

**W.42** Payment to an additional interest will not exceed the amount that would be payable if no additional interest were named. An additional interest has no greater right to payment than the insured from whom that interest arises.

**W.43** An additional interest must cooperate with our investigation to the extent that its claim depends on information within its knowledge or control. We may deny payment to an additional interest that fails to provide material information reasonably requested by us.

**W.44** We may adjust a covered loss with an insured, an additional interest, or both. We may rely on information provided by either person unless we have reason to believe that the information is inaccurate or incomplete.

**W.45** We will not be bound by an estimate, invoice, appraisal, or statement obtained by an insured or additional interest. We may consider such material with all other relevant information in determining the amount of loss.

**W.46** If the amount of loss is disputed, we may obtain an estimate, appraisal, inspection, or other evaluation. Our investigation of the amount of loss does not waive any policy term, condition, limitation, or exclusion.

**W.47** An insured must submit a signed statement of loss when we reasonably request it. The statement must identify the damaged property, describe the loss, and state the interest of each person claiming payment.

**W.48** An insured must provide records supporting the ownership and value of claimed property when reasonably available. If records are unavailable, an insured must provide other reasonable evidence of ownership and value.

**W.49** We will not pay for loss that an insured intentionally conceals, misrepresents, or inflates. We will not pay for any portion of a claim that is supported by false or materially misleading information.

**W.50** We may reduce payment by amounts recovered from responsible persons or organizations to the extent permitted by law. Our recovery rights will apply only after an insured has been fully compensated for the covered loss.

**W.51** An insured must preserve our rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must not impair those rights after a covered loss without our consent.

**W.52** We may require an insured to assign to us any rights of recovery relating to amounts we have paid. Our exercise of recovery rights will not require an insured to pursue a claim at the insured's expense.

**W.53** We will not pay for loss that is covered by another source to the extent that payment is available from that source. We may make payment and seek recovery when necessary to protect our interests.

**W.54** We may make advance payment when information available to us establishes that part of a covered loss is payable. An advance payment does not constitute a final determination of the amount of loss or coverage.

**W.55** We may withhold payment for disputed portions of a claim while continuing to adjust undisputed portions. Our payment of an undisputed amount does not waive our right to contest any remaining amount.

**W.56** We will not pay for costs incurred before the loss unless those costs are directly related to covered property damaged by the loss. We will not pay for costs that arise from an insured's voluntary decision to alter, upgrade, or expand the property.

**W.57** We may require reasonable documentation of repair or replacement costs before making replacement cost payment. Documentation may include contracts, invoices, proof of payment, permits, and photographs of completed work.

**W.58** We may inspect repaired or replaced property before making payment based on replacement cost. An insured must provide reasonable access to the property for that purpose.

**W.59** We will not be liable for delays in repair or replacement caused by conditions beyond our control. Such conditions include shortages, labor conditions, governmental action, weather conditions, and delays caused by an insured or contractor.

**W.60** Our payment for a covered loss is not an admission of liability for any other loss. The settlement of any claim will not alter the terms, conditions, limitations, or exclusions of this policy.

## W.8 — Definitions

"This endorsement" means the additional interests endorsement attached to your policy. It applies only as provided by its terms.

"Additional interest" means a person or organization shown in the policy information as having an interest in property at the residence premises. An additional interest is not an insured solely because it is identified as an additional interest.

"Residence premises" means the premises where you reside and that are identified in the policy information. It includes the dwelling, other structures, and land used in connection with the premises, subject to the terms of your policy.

"Premises" means the location described as the residence premises. Premises includes the portion of land that is available for your use in connection with the dwelling.

"Dwelling" means the building used principally as a private residence by you. Dwelling includes structures attached to the building.

"Other structures" means structures separated from the dwelling by clear space or connected to the dwelling by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. Other structures do not include land.

"Land" means ground and soil at the residence premises. Land includes private ways and areas used in connection with the residence premises.

"Insured" means you and residents of your household who are related to you or are in your care. Insured also includes any person or organization to the extent provided by your policy.

"You" and "your" mean the named insured shown in the policy information and the spouse of that person if the spouse resides in the same household. These terms apply only while the policy remains in force.

"We," "us," and "our" mean the insurer providing the insurance. Our obligations are limited by the terms of your policy and this endorsement.

"Policy" means the homeowners insurance contract to which this endorsement is attached. Policy includes the declarations, endorsements, and conditions that apply to the insurance.

"Policy information" means the information made part of your policy that identifies the named insured, residence premises, and applicable interests. Policy information includes information shown in the declarations.

"Declarations" means the portion of the policy information that identifies the insurance provided. Declarations may identify an additional interest and the property in which that interest exists.

"Named insured" means the person or persons identified as named insured in the policy information. A named insured has the rights and duties assigned by the policy.

"Person" means an individual human being. Person does not include an organization.

"Organization" means a legal entity other than a person. Organization includes a corporation, partnership, association, trust, estate, governmental body, or similar entity.

"Property" means tangible property located at or associated with the residence premises. Property does not include an interest in land unless the policy provides otherwise.

"Covered property" means property for which insurance applies under your policy. Covered property remains subject to all policy terms.

"Personal property" means property owned or used by an insured. Personal property includes property in which an additional interest may have a lawful interest.

"Real property" means land, buildings, and structures attached to land. Real property includes fixtures that cannot be removed without material damage.

"Fixture" means property attached to real property in a manner that makes it part of the real property. Fixture includes property intended to remain permanently attached.

"Interest" means a lawful financial, possessory, security, contractual, or other recognized right in property. Interest does not itself establish ownership of property.

"Ownership interest" means a lawful right to possess, use, control, or transfer property as an owner. Ownership interest may be shared with another person or organization.

"Security interest" means an interest in property that secures payment or performance of an obligation. Security interest includes an interest held by a creditor under an enforceable agreement.

"Mortgagee" means a person or organization holding a mortgage interest in real property. Mortgagee includes a successor or assignee to that interest.

"Lienholder" means a person or organization holding a lien or security interest in property. Lienholder includes a successor or assignee to that interest.

"Lessor" means a person or organization that grants another person the right to possess or use property. Lessor does not include a person who merely provides services at the residence premises.

"Lessee" means a person or organization receiving the right to possess or use property from a lessor. Lessee may have an interest in property without owning that property.

"Vendor" means a person or organization that transfers or agrees to transfer property to another person or organization. Vendor includes a seller under an enforceable contract.

"Purchaser" means a person or organization that acquires or agrees to acquire property from a vendor. Purchaser may have an interest in property before legal title transfers.

"Trustee" means a person or organization holding property for the benefit of another person or organization. Trustee includes a successor acting in the same capacity.

"Beneficiary" means a person or organization entitled to receive benefits from property, a trust, or an agreement. Beneficiary does not include an additional interest unless identified as such in the policy information.

"Agent" means a person or organization authorized to act for another person or organization. Agent includes a representative acting within the scope of actual authority.

"Representative" means a person or organization acting for an insured or additional interest. Representative has no greater right than the person or organization represented.

"Legal representative" means a person or organization authorized by law to act for another person or organization. Legal representative includes a guardian, conservator, executor, administrator, or similar fiduciary.

"Successor" means a person or organization that lawfully succeeds to the rights or obligations of another person or organization. Successor must establish the claimed interest when requested by us.

"Assignee" means a person or organization to whom an interest has been transferred. Assignee has no greater right than the transferor had before the transfer.

"Transfer" means an act or agreement by which an interest in property is conveyed, assigned, or otherwise passed to another person or organization. Transfer includes a transfer by operation of law.

"Assignment" means a transfer of rights from one person or organization to another person or organization. Assignment does not alter our obligations unless we agree to the assignment.

"Loss" means accidental physical loss of or damage to covered property. Loss includes resulting loss only when covered by your policy.

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. Occurrence includes an event resulting in loss covered by your policy.

"Claim" means a request for payment, protection, or other performance under your policy. Claim may be made by an insured or by another person or organization with a lawful interest.

"Proof of loss" means a signed statement containing information reasonably necessary for us to evaluate a claim. Proof of loss must be provided when required by your policy.

"Notice" means information given in a manner permitted by your policy. Notice to us must be delivered to an address, representative, or electronic location we designate.

"Written" means expressed in a document, record, or communication capable of being retained and reproduced. Written includes an electronic communication when accepted by us.

"Knowledge" means actual awareness of a fact or circumstance. Knowledge of an additional interest is not knowledge by an insured unless the insured also has actual awareness.

"Concealment" means withholding or failing to disclose material information when there is a duty to provide that information. Concealment includes conduct intended to prevent discovery of material information.

"Misrepresentation" means a statement or omission that is false or misleading concerning a material fact. Misrepresentation includes a statement made by an insured or a person acting for an insured.

"Material" means having a natural tendency to affect our evaluation of a risk, claim, interest, or obligation. Material information includes information that would affect our handling of a claim.

"Vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary for customary occupancy. A dwelling under construction is not vacant solely because it has not yet been occupied.

"Unoccupied" means not being used as a residence by you or another person with your permission. Unoccupied does not necessarily mean vacant.
