# HO 04 42 Permitted Incidental Occupancies (Edition 2011-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement changes your "policy". Please read it carefully.

We attach this endorsement to your policy. It forms part of the agreement between you and us.

The provisions of this endorsement apply only to the insurance affected by this endorsement. All other provisions remain unchanged unless this endorsement expressly changes them.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy, this endorsement controls. We will apply the conflicting provisions accordingly.

The terms used in this endorsement have the meanings assigned to them in your policy. Terms that are not defined in this endorsement retain their policy meanings.

This endorsement applies to an insured location only as provided by its terms. Coverage applies subject to all applicable conditions and exclusions.

An insured must comply with the duties imposed by this endorsement. Failure to comply may affect coverage.

We do not cover loss for which coverage is excluded by this endorsement. We also do not cover loss that is not otherwise covered by your policy.

The attachment of this endorsement does not create coverage except as expressly provided. It does not broaden coverage beyond its stated terms.

This endorsement is effective only while it is attached to your policy. It remains subject to the terms of your policy except as changed by this endorsement.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the coverage stated in this endorsement for an "insured" arising out of an "incidental occupancy." This coverage applies only while the incidental occupancy is conducted at premises used in connection with your residence.

**W.2** "Incidental occupancy" means an occupancy conducted by an insured that is subordinate to the residential use of the premises. The occupancy must be lawful and must not materially change the character of the premises as a residence.

**W.3** This endorsement applies to "bodily injury," "property damage," or "personal injury" caused by an "occurrence" arising out of the incidental occupancy. The occurrence must take place during the policy period.

**W.4** We will pay damages for which an insured is legally liable because of bodily injury or property damage arising out of the incidental occupancy. Damages must result from an occurrence to which this coverage applies.

**W.5** We will pay damages for which an insured is legally liable because of personal injury arising out of the incidental occupancy. The personal injury must be caused by an offense committed during the policy period.

**W.6** We will provide a defense at our expense against a "suit" seeking damages to which this coverage applies. We may investigate and settle any claim or suit as we consider appropriate.

**W.7** Our duty to defend ends when the amount we pay for damages equals the limit of liability applicable to this coverage. Our duty to defend also ends when no coverage applies to the claim or suit.

**W.8** We do not cover liability arising out of an incidental occupancy that is conducted away from premises used in connection with your residence. This exclusion applies whether the occupancy is conducted by an insured or by another person.

**W.9** We do not cover liability arising out of the manufacture, sale, distribution, delivery, or furnishing of alcoholic beverages. This exclusion applies if the insured is in the business of manufacturing, distributing, selling, serving, or furnishing alcoholic beverages.

**W.10** We do not cover liability arising out of the rendering or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies to services requiring specialized knowledge, training, licensing, certification, or judgment.

**W.11** We do not cover liability arising out of medical, surgical, dental, nursing, therapeutic, counseling, or similar services. This exclusion applies regardless of whether a fee is charged for the services.

**W.12** We do not cover liability arising out of the operation of a health care facility, day care facility, nursing facility, or similar institution. This exclusion applies whether the facility is operated for profit or otherwise.

**W.13** We do not cover liability arising out of the care, custody, or control of property owned by others. This exclusion does not apply to property damage caused by fire, smoke, or explosion.

**W.14** We do not cover liability arising out of a product manufactured, sold, handled, distributed, or disposed of by an insured. This exclusion includes a product that is altered, repaired, installed, assembled, or delivered by an insured.

**W.15** We do not cover liability arising out of work performed by an insured if the claim alleges a defect, deficiency, inadequacy, or dangerous condition in that work. This exclusion applies after the work has been completed or abandoned.

**W.16** We do not cover liability arising out of an act or omission of an insured in the capacity of an employer. This exclusion applies to bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury sustained by an employee or applicant for employment.

**W.17** We do not cover liability arising out of an obligation imposed by workers compensation, unemployment compensation, disability benefits, or similar law. This exclusion applies whether benefits are payable by an insured or by another person.

**W.18** We do not cover liability arising out of an employment-related practice, policy, act, or omission. This exclusion includes refusal to employ, termination of employment, coercion, demotion, evaluation, reassignment, discipline, harassment, discrimination, or other employment-related conduct.

**W.19** We do not cover liability arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft. This exclusion applies to an aircraft owned, operated, rented, borrowed, or used by an insured.

**W.20** We do not cover liability arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft. This exclusion applies to a watercraft used in connection with the incidental occupancy.

**W.21** We do not cover liability arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle. This exclusion applies if the motor vehicle is used in connection with the incidental occupancy.

**W.22** We do not cover liability arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of machinery or equipment designed for use off public roads. This exclusion applies when the machinery or equipment is used in connection with the incidental occupancy.

**W.23** We do not cover liability arising out of pollution. This exclusion applies to the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, escape, or exposure to pollutants.

**W.24** We do not cover liability arising out of the actual, alleged, or threatened presence of fungi, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or virus. This exclusion applies whether the claim seeks damages because of bodily injury, property damage, personal injury, or loss of use.

**W.25** We do not cover liability arising out of a communicable disease transmitted by an insured. This exclusion applies whether transmission is actual, alleged, or threatened.

**W.26** We do not cover liability arising out of an act committed by an insured with the intent to cause bodily injury or property damage. This exclusion applies even if the bodily injury or property damage differs in kind or degree from that intended or expected.

**W.27** We do not cover liability arising out of a criminal act or omission committed by an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of whether an insured is charged with or convicted of a criminal offense.

**W.28** We do not cover liability arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or action taken by a governmental authority to hinder or defend against any of these. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.29** We do not cover punitive damages, exemplary damages, fines, penalties, or amounts imposed as a punishment. This exclusion applies to the extent such damages are not insurable under applicable law.

**W.30** An insured must promptly notify us of an occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must include information reasonably available to the insured concerning the time, place, circumstances, and persons involved.

**W.31** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, complaint, or legal process received in connection with a claim or suit. An insured must not assume any obligation or incur any expense without our consent, except at the insured's own cost.

**W.32** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. An insured must assist us in obtaining witnesses, evidence, records, and other information relevant to the claim or suit.

**W.33** An insured must permit us to inspect the premises where the incidental occupancy is conducted. An insured must permit us to examine records relevant to the occupancy when reasonably requested.

**W.34** An insured must preserve evidence relating to an occurrence, claim, or suit when reasonably possible. An insured must not alter, conceal, dispose of, or destroy relevant evidence without our consent.

**W.35** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume liability, or settle a claim without our consent. This duty does not prevent an insured from providing emergency assistance when reasonably necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.36** If we make a payment under this endorsement, we are entitled to recover that payment from a person responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.37** Our rights of recovery are transferred to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must execute and deliver instruments and take other action necessary to secure those rights.

**W.38** Coverage under this endorsement applies separately to each insured against whom a claim is made or suit is brought. This provision does not increase the amount we will pay for any occurrence.

**W.39** This endorsement does not provide coverage for an obligation assumed by an insured under a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**W.40** This endorsement does not provide coverage for damage to property owned by, rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care of an insured. This exclusion applies whether the property is used in connection with the incidental occupancy or otherwise.

**W.41** This endorsement does not provide coverage for loss of use of property that has not been physically injured, unless the loss of use results from an occurrence causing property damage to other property. The property damage must be covered under this endorsement.

**W.42** This endorsement does not provide coverage for a claim based upon a representation, warranty, guarantee, or promise concerning the quality, fitness, durability, performance, or safety of goods or work. This exclusion applies whether the representation is oral, written, express, or implied.

**W.43** This endorsement does not provide coverage for liability arising out of the ownership, possession, use, or operation of a firearm in connection with the incidental occupancy. This exclusion applies whether the firearm is owned by an insured or by another person.

**W.44** This endorsement does not provide coverage for liability arising out of the storage, handling, use, sale, or distribution of explosives. This exclusion includes blasting agents, fireworks, ammunition, and similar materials.

**W.45** An insured must comply with applicable laws, ordinances, regulations, permits, and licensing requirements relating to the incidental occupancy. Failure to comply does not create coverage where coverage otherwise does not apply.

**W.46** An insured must maintain the premises used for the incidental occupancy in a reasonably safe condition. An insured must correct known hazardous conditions within a reasonable time.

**W.47** We may inspect the premises and operations associated with the incidental occupancy. Our inspection does not constitute a representation that the premises or operations are safe, healthful, or in compliance with law.

**W.48** No person or organization has a right under this endorsement to join us as a party to an action against an insured. A person or organization may not bring us into an action to determine an insured's liability.

**W.49** The coverage provided by this endorsement applies only to liability arising out of the incidental occupancy. All other terms, conditions, exclusions, and provisions of this policy remain applicable.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** For purposes of this provision, "permitted incidental occupancy" means an occupancy conducted by you at the residence premises for which coverage applies. "Covered property" means property for which coverage applies because of its use in connection with that occupancy.

**W.2** The "applicable limit" is the most we will pay for covered property arising out of any loss. Payment of that limit does not increase any other limit that applies to the same loss.

**W.3** A "loss" means accidental direct physical loss to covered property. An "occurrence" means an event or a series of related events that results in loss.

**W.4** The applicable limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims, occurrences, persons, or items of covered property involved. The applicable limit applies regardless of the number of locations at which covered property is situated.

**W.5** We will not pay more than the applicable limit for all loss arising out of the same occurrence. All loss arising from a continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same harmful conditions is considered to arise from the same occurrence.

**W.6** We will not pay more than the applicable limit for covered property that is damaged, destroyed, or stolen. This limitation applies whether the property is owned by you, leased by you, or held by you for use in the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.7** We will determine the applicable limit without regard to the value of property that is not covered property. We will not include the value of property for which coverage does not apply in determining the amount we pay.

**W.8** We will pay only for covered property that is damaged by a covered cause of loss. We do not cover loss to property merely because it is present at or near the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.9** An insured must establish the existence, ownership, and value of covered property before we will make payment. An insured must also establish that the property was used in connection with the permitted incidental occupancy at the time of loss.

**W.10** We may require an insured to provide records, receipts, invoices, photographs, or other evidence supporting the claimed value of covered property. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable request for that information.

**W.11** We may inspect damaged property before it is repaired, replaced, disposed of, or removed. An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably possible until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.12** We do not cover any amount claimed for property that an insured cannot reasonably identify as covered property. We do not cover any amount based on speculation, unsupported valuation, or anticipated profit.

**W.13** We will not pay for loss that exceeds the value of the covered property immediately before the loss. We may consider the age, condition, quality, and use of the property in determining that value.

**W.14** We may pay the value of damaged property, repair the property, replace the property, or take any combination of those actions. Our election of a method of payment does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.15** If we repair or replace covered property, we may use property that is similar in material, quality, and function. We are not required to provide property that is identical in every respect to the damaged property.

**W.16** We do not cover any increase in value resulting from repair, replacement, alteration, or improvement of covered property. An insured is responsible for any amount that exceeds the cost of returning damaged property to substantially the same condition.

**W.17** We will reduce the amount we pay by the value of any remaining property that an insured retains. We may require transfer of the remaining property to us as a condition of payment.

**W.18** If covered property is recovered after we make payment for its loss, an insured must notify us promptly. We may require the recovered property to be returned to us or may adjust the payment to reflect its recovered value.

**W.19** We do not cover loss to property that is not owned by you unless that property qualifies as covered property. We do not cover loss to property held by you solely for storage, repair, processing, sale, or delivery unless coverage applies to that property.

**W.20** We do not cover property that is held for sale to customers. We do not cover stock, merchandise, samples, or materials intended for sale unless coverage expressly applies to them.

**W.21** We do not cover accounts, bills, currency, securities, records, electronic data, or evidence of debt. We do not cover the value of information contained in records or electronic data.

**W.22** We do not cover loss consisting solely of delay, loss of use, loss of market, interruption of operations, or reduction in income. We do not cover consequential loss arising from damage to covered property.

**W.23** We do not cover any cost arising from the enforcement of a law, ordinance, regulation, or order. We do not cover any cost required to test for, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize a pollutant.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts committed by an insured. We do not cover loss resulting from dishonest, criminal, or fraudulent conduct by an insured.

**W.25** Payment for covered property does not create coverage for property that is not covered property. Payment for a particular loss does not waive any condition, exclusion, or limitation that applies to another loss.

**W.26** We may adjust any claim with an insured who has a legal or equitable interest in covered property. Payment to that insured discharges our obligation for the amount paid.

**W.27** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We do not cover additional damage resulting from an insured's failure to take reasonable steps to protect the property.

**W.28** Reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property from further damage are subject to the applicable limit. Those expenses do not increase the amount otherwise available for loss to covered property.

**W.29** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of property for which we make payment, but we are not required to take possession of any property.

**W.30** We may settle a claim directly with a person or organization having a lawful interest in covered property. Such settlement does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.31** Any payment we make is subject to the applicable limit and all terms that apply to the loss. We will not pay amounts that an insured is not legally entitled to receive.

**W.32** The applicable limit is not increased by the presence of additional covered property, additional insureds, or additional claims. Our payment of the applicable limit ends our obligation to pay for that loss.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the portion of a covered loss that you must bear. We will pay only the amount of a covered loss that exceeds the applicable deductible.

**W.2** We will apply the deductible to the total covered loss before applying any payment provision. We will not apply the deductible to an amount that is not covered.

**W.3** An insured must pay the deductible whether or not a covered loss is repaired, replaced, or otherwise restored. Our payment obligation does not include the deductible.

**W.4** We will apply the deductible separately to each covered loss. Loss or damage arising from a common cause may be treated as a single covered loss.

**W.5** We will not reduce the deductible because more than one insured has an interest in the damaged property. The deductible applies to the covered loss as a whole.

**W.6** We will not reduce the deductible because more than one item of property is damaged. The deductible applies to all covered property involved in the same covered loss.

**W.7** We will not reduce the deductible because covered property is located at more than one premises. The deductible applies as required by the facts of the covered loss.

**W.8** We will apply the deductible before determining the amount payable for damaged property. We will not pay for loss below the applicable deductible.

**W.9** When a covered loss affects property subject to different coverage provisions, we will apply the deductible to the covered loss in a reasonable manner. Our allocation will reflect the property damaged and the coverage that applies.

**W.10** We will not apply the deductible to property for which no coverage applies. We will apply the deductible only to covered loss.

**W.11** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of the covered loss and the deductible that applies. Failure to provide requested information may delay payment.

**W.12** We may inspect damaged property before determining the covered loss. An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably necessary for our inspection.

**W.13** We may determine that separate damage results from separate causes. In that event, we may apply a deductible to each separate covered loss.

**W.14** We may determine that damage results from a continuing or repeated cause. In that event, we will apply the deductible in accordance with the circumstances of the covered loss.

**W.15** We will not apply more than the applicable deductible to the same covered loss. This provision does not increase the amount we otherwise would pay.

**W.16** Payment by us does not relieve an insured of responsibility for the deductible. An insured remains responsible for amounts within the deductible.

**W.17** We may pay a covered loss to an insured, a mortgagee, or another person entitled to payment. Such payment remains subject to the applicable deductible.

**W.18** We will calculate the deductible without regard to amounts recovered from another source unless otherwise required by law. Recovery by an insured does not alter our obligations under this provision.

**W.19** An insured must not incur expenses for our account within the deductible. We will not reimburse expenses that are part of the deductible.

**W.20** We will apply the deductible to covered loss resulting from repair work performed by an insured or another person. The method of repair does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.21** We may settle a covered loss without requiring repair or replacement. Any settlement remains subject to the deductible.

**W.22** The deductible applies whether damaged property is repaired, replaced, restored, or not repaired. We will determine payment based on the coverage that applies to the covered loss.

**W.23** We will not pay an insured for loss that consists solely of the deductible. We will pay only covered loss exceeding the deductible.

**W.24** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss. Expenses incurred for that purpose remain subject to the deductible when covered.

**W.25** We may require documentation supporting the amount of a covered loss. An insured must provide available records, receipts, estimates, and other requested information.

**W.26** We will determine the deductible before issuing payment for a covered loss. Our determination may be revised if material information becomes available.

**W.27** The deductible does not create coverage for loss otherwise excluded or not covered. All other terms, conditions, and exclusions remain applicable.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss arising out of an “incidental occupancy” that is not permitted by us. We do not cover loss arising out of an incidental occupancy conducted in a manner different from that represented to us.

**W.2** We do not cover loss arising out of an incidental occupancy conducted by a person who is not an “insured.” We do not cover loss arising out of an incidental occupancy conducted for the benefit of a person other than an insured.

**W.3** We do not cover loss arising out of an incidental occupancy conducted away from the “residence premises.” We do not cover loss arising out of property used for an incidental occupancy that is not located at the residence premises.

**W.4** We do not cover loss arising out of an incidental occupancy conducted at an “insured location” that is not used principally as a residence. We do not cover loss arising out of an incidental occupancy conducted at a location used principally for commercial purposes.

**W.5** We do not cover loss arising out of a “business” other than the permitted incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss arising out of a business conducted by an insured that is not described as part of the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.6** We do not cover loss arising out of a business conducted by an insured after the permitted incidental occupancy has ceased. We do not cover loss arising out of a business conducted before permission for the incidental occupancy applies.

**W.7** We do not cover loss arising out of an incidental occupancy involving unlawful activity. We do not cover loss arising out of an incidental occupancy conducted in violation of an applicable law, ordinance, regulation, license, permit, or order.

**W.8** We do not cover any part of a windstorm or hail loss that is subject to the "minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage". This exclusion applies regardless of the cause of the loss.

**W.9** We do not increase the "other structures limit" because of an incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss that exceeds the other structures limit otherwise applicable to covered property.

**W.10** We do not increase the "contents limit percentage" because property is used in connection with an incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss that exceeds the contents limit percentage otherwise applicable to covered property.

**W.11** We do not increase the "ALE percentage" because an incidental occupancy is interrupted. We do not cover loss of income, expense, or loss of use that exceeds the ALE percentage otherwise applicable to the loss.

**W.12** We do not increase the "debris removal additional percentage" because debris results from an incidental occupancy. We do not cover debris removal expense that exceeds the debris removal additional percentage otherwise applicable to the loss.

**W.13** We do not cover “business property” owned by a person who is not an insured. We do not cover business property held for sale, lease, rental, delivery, transportation, storage, or consignment.

**W.14** We do not cover loss to business property consisting of stock, merchandise, inventory, materials, supplies, or goods held for sale. We do not cover loss to property manufactured, processed, altered, repaired, serviced, or distributed in connection with an incidental occupancy.

**W.15** We do not cover loss to accounts, bills, currency, deeds, evidence of debt, manuscripts, securities, tickets, stamps, or records used in connection with an incidental occupancy. We do not cover the cost to research, replace, restore, reproduce, or reconstruct information contained in such property.

**W.16** We do not cover loss to data, software, electronically stored information, or media used in connection with an incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss caused by the inability to access, use, manipulate, process, transmit, or receive such property.

**W.17** We do not cover loss to property while it is in the care, custody, or control of an insured for a fee. We do not cover loss to property held by an insured for repair, service, alteration, processing, storage, delivery, transportation, or safekeeping.

**W.18** We do not cover loss to property rented to, leased to, borrowed by, or loaned to an insured for business purposes. We do not cover loss to property furnished for an insured's regular business use.

**W.19** We do not cover loss to property of customers, clients, patients, tenants, guests, or invitees. We do not cover loss to property entrusted to an insured in connection with an incidental occupancy.

**W.20** We do not cover loss to tools, equipment, machinery, appliances, or devices used principally for business purposes. We do not cover loss to property designed for industrial, manufacturing, commercial, or professional use.

**W.21** We do not cover loss to motorized land conveyances used in connection with an incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss arising out of the ownership, maintenance, operation, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of such conveyances.

**W.22** We do not cover loss arising out of the ownership, maintenance, operation, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of an aircraft. We do not cover loss arising out of the ownership, maintenance, operation, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of a watercraft used in connection with an incidental occupancy.

**W.23** We do not cover loss arising out of the ownership, maintenance, operation, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of a recreational motor vehicle used in connection with an incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss arising out of the ownership, maintenance, operation, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of a vehicle designed for use off public roads.

**W.24** We do not cover loss arising out of the rendering or failure to render professional services. We do not cover loss arising out of advice, consultation, design, inspection, certification, treatment, instruction, supervision, or professional judgment.

**W.25** We do not cover loss arising out of medical, surgical, dental, nursing, therapeutic, counseling, psychological, chiropractic, veterinary, or similar services. We do not cover loss arising out of the furnishing, dispensing, administering, prescribing, or distributing of medicine, drugs, devices, or treatment.

**W.26** We do not cover loss arising out of legal, accounting, financial, investment, insurance, real estate, appraisal, engineering, architectural, surveying, or similar services. We do not cover loss arising out of an error, omission, misrepresentation, or negligent act in the performance of such services.

**W.27** We do not cover loss arising out of the preparation, sale, service, furnishing, or distribution of food or beverages. We do not cover loss arising out of food contamination, spoilage, adulteration, allergic reaction, or failure to warn.

**W.28** We do not cover loss arising out of the sale, furnishing, serving, or giving of alcoholic beverages. We do not cover loss arising out of the intoxication of any person or the furnishing of alcoholic beverages to a person not legally permitted to receive them.

**W.29** We do not cover loss arising out of the sale, distribution, manufacture, cultivation, processing, or delivery of controlled substances. We do not cover loss arising out of paraphernalia, equipment, materials, or property used in connection with controlled substances.

**W.30** We do not cover loss arising out of the ownership, care, custody, control, breeding, boarding, training, grooming, sale, or transfer of animals. We do not cover loss arising out of injury caused by an animal used in connection with an incidental occupancy.

**W.31** We do not cover loss arising out of child care, adult care, health care, assisted living, home care, or custodial care provided for a fee. We do not cover loss arising out of supervision, transportation, or care of a person who is not a resident of the household.

**W.32** We do not cover loss arising out of the care, custody, control, supervision, or transportation of a person in connection with an incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss arising out of alleged abuse, molestation, harassment, neglect, deprivation, or exploitation.

**W.33** We do not cover loss arising out of the use, handling, storage, disposal, release, discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, or escape of pollutants. We do not cover loss arising out of cleanup, testing, monitoring, responding to, or assessing pollutants.

**W.34** We do not cover loss arising out of the use, handling, storage, disposal, release, discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, or escape of hazardous substances. We do not cover loss arising out of contamination of property, air, water, soil, or groundwater.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by “fungi,” wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, mold, mildew, or spores. We do not cover loss arising out of the presence, growth, spread, release, or removal of fungi, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, mold, mildew, or spores.

**W.36** We do not cover loss arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease. We do not cover loss arising out of an allegation that an insured failed to prevent, detect, report, contain, treat, or warn of a communicable disease.

**W.37** We do not cover loss arising out of the actual, alleged, or threatened release of confidential, personal, financial, medical, or proprietary information. We do not cover loss arising out of unauthorized access to, disclosure of, destruction of, alteration of, or use of information.

**W.38** We do not cover loss arising out of a computer system, network, electronic device, software, or data used in connection with an incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss caused by malicious code, unauthorized access, denial of service, electronic theft, or data corruption.

**W.39** We do not cover loss arising out of advertising, publishing, broadcasting, posting, displaying, or distributing material. We do not cover loss arising out of defamation, invasion of privacy, infringement, plagiarism, misappropriation, or violation of intellectual property rights.

**W.40** We do not cover loss arising out of an employment-related practice, policy, act, or omission. We do not cover loss arising out of hiring, firing, discipline, demotion, evaluation, compensation, discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or failure to employ.

**W.41** We do not cover loss arising out of a contract, agreement, warranty, representation, guarantee, or assumption of liability. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract, agreement, warranty, representation, guarantee, or assumption.

**W.42** We do not cover loss arising out of a failure of an insured's product, work, service, material, equipment, or operation to perform as represented. We do not cover loss arising out of delay, deficiency, defect, recall, withdrawal, replacement, repair, or correction.

**W.43** We do not cover loss arising out of “property damage” to an insured's product, work, service, material, equipment, or operation. We do not cover loss arising out of the cost to repair, replace, adjust, alter, improve, or remove such property.

**W.45** We do not cover loss arising out of an “occurrence” expected or intended by an insured. We do not cover “bodily injury” or property damage arising out of an act committed with the intent to cause injury or damage.

**W.46** We do not cover loss arising out of dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, malicious, or intentional conduct by an insured. We do not cover loss arising out of concealment, misrepresentation, forgery, theft, conversion, embezzlement, or unlawful taking.

**W.47** We do not cover loss arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurpation of power, military action, or civil commotion. We do not cover loss arising out of seizure, confiscation, destruction, or damage by order of a governmental authority.

**W.48** We do not cover loss arising out of nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, or nuclear hazard. We do not cover loss arising out of the handling, storage, use, transportation, or disposal of radioactive material.

**W.49** We do not cover loss arising out of earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudslide, subsidence, sinkhole, settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion. We do not cover loss arising out of earth movement whether caused by natural or human activity.

**W.50** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, spray, or waterborne material. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up, overflows, or otherwise escapes from a sewer, drain, sump, or related equipment.

**W.51** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage, leakage, overflow, discharge, or release of water or steam. We do not cover loss caused by wear, tear, deterioration, corrosion, rust, decay, mechanical breakdown, latent defect, or inherent vice.

**W.52** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations, or atmospheric contamination. We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, expansion, cracking, or movement of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings.

**W.53** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, domestic animals, or infestation. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, chewing, gnawing, scratching, contamination, discharge, or waste of animals.

**W.54** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must protect property from further damage after a loss.

**W.55** We do not cover loss for which an insured has concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

**W.56** We do not cover loss for which an insured has made a false statement, engaged in fraud, or committed a material misrepresentation. An insured must provide records, information, and access to property reasonably requested by us.

**W.57** We do not cover loss arising out of an insured's failure to maintain any license, permit, registration, certification, approval, or authorization required for an incidental occupancy. An insured must comply with applicable requirements governing the incidental occupancy.

**W.58** We do not cover loss arising out of structural alterations, additions, or renovations undertaken for an incidental occupancy without required approval. An insured must obtain required approval before beginning such work.

**W.59** We do not cover loss arising out of an incidental occupancy that creates a material increase in hazard. An insured must promptly notify us of a material change in the nature, location, ownership, operation, or use of the incidental occupancy.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** These Conditions apply to the "permitted incidental occupancy" shown in the policy. An insured must comply with these Conditions as a condition of coverage for that occupancy.

**W.2** An "insured" must maintain the permitted incidental occupancy in a lawful manner. An insured must obtain and maintain any license, permit, approval, or certification required for the occupancy operations.

**W.3** An insured must promptly notify us of a material change in the occupancy operations. Notice must describe the nature of the change and the effect the change may have on the hazard.

**W.4** An insured must notify us before using any additional area of the "residence premises" for occupancy operations. An insured must not represent that an unapproved area is part of the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.5** An insured must keep the occupancy operations separate from personal activities to the extent reasonably practicable. An insured must maintain reasonable controls over access to areas used for occupancy operations.

**W.6** An insured must maintain the premises in a reasonably safe condition. An insured must promptly correct a condition known to create an increased risk of loss.

**W.7** An insured must comply with applicable building, fire, health, safety, and zoning requirements. Compliance with such requirements does not create coverage where coverage does not otherwise apply.

**W.8** An insured must keep reasonable records relating to occupancy operations. Such records must be sufficient to identify income, expenses, property, customers, and transactions material to a claim.

**W.9** An insured must preserve records that may be material to a loss. An insured must make those records available to us upon our request.

**W.10** An insured must permit us to inspect the premises and occupancy operations at reasonable times. An insured must permit us to examine property that may be involved in a claim.

**W.11** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. Cooperation includes providing truthful information requested by us.

**W.12** An insured must promptly report to us any occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must include information reasonably available concerning the occurrence, persons involved, and property affected.

**W.13** An insured must notify the appropriate authorities when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect persons or property. An insured must preserve evidence of a reported incident when reasonably practicable.

**W.14** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will consider reasonable expenses incurred solely to protect covered property when adjusting the loss.

**W.15** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except for reasonable measures to protect persons or property. This Condition does not prohibit an insured from providing emergency assistance.

**W.16** An insured must not admit liability on our behalf. An insured may make factual statements concerning an occurrence but must not make commitments that bind us.

**W.17** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, complaint, or legal paper received in connection with a claim. An insured must not alter, destroy, or conceal any such item.

**W.18** An insured must assist us in securing evidence, witnesses, and information relevant to a claim. An insured must attend examinations, hearings, or proceedings when reasonably requested by us.

**W.19** An insured must submit to examination under oath when requested by us. An insured must answer questions truthfully and provide documents reasonably related to the claim.

**W.20** An insured must provide a signed, sworn proof of loss when requested by us. The "days to submit a signed, sworn proof of loss" applies to that duty.

**W.21** An insured must identify property involved in a loss and provide reasonable documentation of ownership, value, and condition. An insured must permit us to inspect the property before it is repaired, replaced, disposed of, or altered, when reasonably practicable.

**W.22** An insured must provide receipts, invoices, estimates, photographs, inventories, and other records reasonably requested by us. An insured must preserve damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.23** An insured must provide information concerning other insurance that may apply to a loss. An insured must promptly notify other insurers of a claim when requested by us.

**W.24** An insured must notify us of any claim, demand, proceeding, investigation, or complaint arising from occupancy operations. Notice must be given even if an insured believes the matter is groundless.

**W.25** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after a loss, but we are not required to do so.

**W.26** An insured must protect confidential information obtained through occupancy operations. An insured must notify us if unauthorized disclosure of such information may result in a claim.

**W.27** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting, supervising, and retaining persons who perform occupancy operations. An insured must not knowingly permit an unqualified person to perform work requiring a license or certification.

**W.28** An insured must maintain reasonable safeguards appropriate to the occupancy operations. An insured must promptly notify us if a required safeguard is removed, disabled, impaired, or no longer available.

**W.29** An insured must not knowingly increase the hazard arising from occupancy operations. An insured must notify us if an increased hazard cannot be promptly corrected.

**W.30** An insured must promptly notify us if occupancy operations cease, are suspended, or are materially reduced. An insured must also notify us if the premises are no longer used as described for the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.31** An insured must notify us of a transfer of ownership, possession, management, or control of the occupancy operations. Coverage for a transferred interest is subject to our written consent.

**W.32** An insured must notify us of a change in the legal status of an insured that may affect a claim or our rights. Such notice includes a dissolution, insolvency, death, incapacity, or appointment of a representative.

**W.33** An insured must not assign a claim or any interest in this coverage without our written consent. This Condition does not restrict an assignment that is valid by operation of law.

**W.34** An insured must preserve our rights of recovery against any person responsible for a loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.35** If we recover from a person responsible for a loss, an insured must cooperate in the recovery. Amounts recovered will be applied according to the respective interests of us and the insured.

**W.36** An insured must promptly disclose any information material to the underwriting or adjustment of this coverage. A material fact includes a fact that would affect our evaluation of the occupancy operations or a claim.

**W.37** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact or circumstance. This Condition applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after a loss.

**W.38** An insured must not engage in fraudulent conduct in connection with this coverage. Fraud includes knowingly presenting false information or supporting a claim with false documentation.

**W.39** We may deny coverage for a claim if an insured fails to comply with a duty imposed by these Conditions and the failure materially prejudices us. We may also deny coverage where an insured commits fraud or conceals or misrepresents a material fact.

**W.40** The "AOP deductible floor" applies in determining the amount payable for a covered loss. An insured remains responsible for the applicable deductible.

**W.41** The "replacement cost threshold percentage" applies when determining the basis upon which a covered property loss is settled. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for us to apply that condition.

**W.42** The "time to bring action" applies to any action against us arising from this coverage. An insured must comply with all applicable Conditions before bringing such action.

**W.43** No person or organization may bring an action against us unless all applicable duties have been performed. No action may be maintained against us to recover an amount not owed under this coverage.

**W.44** A waiver or change of any provision of these Conditions is effective only if made in writing by us. Our investigation of a claim does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.45** If any provision of these Conditions is determined to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. Any provision will be applied in a manner consistent with applicable law.

**W.46** An insured must comply with these Conditions separately with respect to each claim. The duty of an insured does not relieve another insured of that insured's separate duties.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** The "permitted incidental occupancy" must be conducted at the "residence premises". An insured must conduct the permitted incidental occupancy only as described in the schedule applicable to this policy.

**W.2** An insured must obtain and maintain any license, permit, registration, or approval required for the permitted incidental occupancy. An insured must comply with applicable laws, ordinances, regulations, and governmental requirements.

**W.3** An insured must maintain the residence premises in a condition that is reasonably safe for the permitted incidental occupancy. An insured must promptly correct a condition that creates a hazard arising out of the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.4** An insured must keep entrances, exits, walkways, and areas used in connection with the permitted incidental occupancy reasonably clear and safe. An insured must provide reasonable warnings of known hazards.

**W.5** An insured must use equipment, furnishings, supplies, and materials appropriate for the permitted incidental occupancy. An insured must maintain such property in safe operating condition.

**W.6** An insured must not use the residence premises for an activity that materially increases the hazard presented by the permitted incidental occupancy. An insured must notify us promptly of a material change in the nature of the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.7** An insured must not allow the permitted incidental occupancy to create a nuisance or unreasonable interference with neighboring property. An insured must take reasonable measures to control noise, odors, smoke, dust, vibration, and waste arising from the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.8** An insured must keep records reasonably necessary to identify property used in the permitted incidental occupancy. An insured must provide such records to us when reasonably requested in connection with a loss.

**W.9** An insured must separate property used for the permitted incidental occupancy from personal property not used in that occupancy when reasonably practicable. An insured must identify property held for sale, lease, repair, storage, or use in the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.10** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting, supervising, and controlling any person performing duties in connection with the permitted incidental occupancy. An insured must not delegate a duty imposed by this policy without retaining reasonable oversight.

**W.11** An insured must require a person working in connection with the permitted incidental occupancy to comply with applicable safety requirements. An insured must not knowingly permit a person to perform work for which that person lacks required qualifications.

**W.12** An insured must protect business records, customer property, and property in the care of an insured from reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. An insured must promptly report a theft, disappearance, or suspected criminal act to the appropriate authorities when required by law.

**W.13** An insured must use reasonable care in accepting, handling, storing, and returning property of others. An insured must document the receipt and return of such property when the nature of the property or transaction reasonably requires documentation.

**W.14** An insured must not represent that goods, products, or services have characteristics, uses, benefits, or approvals that they do not have. An insured must not make a warranty or guarantee beyond that authorized by the manufacturer or supplier.

**W.15** An insured must not provide professional advice, diagnosis, treatment, instruction, or service unless the permitted incidental occupancy requires it and the insured is qualified to provide it. An insured must maintain any professional credential required for such activity.

**W.16** An insured must obtain appropriate consent before performing a service that could reasonably result in bodily injury or property damage. An insured must retain consent records when such records are customary for the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.17** An insured must use reasonable care in preparing, storing, handling, and distributing any food, beverage, cosmetic, or similar product in connection with the permitted incidental occupancy. An insured must remove from use a product known to be contaminated, defective, or unsafe.

**W.18** An insured must not manufacture, distribute, sell, furnish, or serve a controlled substance except as permitted by applicable law. An insured must not knowingly permit illegal activity at the residence premises.

**W.19** An insured must store flammable, combustible, explosive, corrosive, toxic, or otherwise hazardous materials in accordance with applicable requirements. An insured must dispose of waste generated by the permitted incidental occupancy in a lawful and safe manner.

**W.20** An insured must promptly notify us of a loss, occurrence, claim, or suit arising out of the permitted incidental occupancy. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of any claim or suit.

**W.21** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense arising out of a claim without our consent, except at the insured's own cost. An insured may provide reasonable emergency assistance to protect persons or property.

**W.22** An insured must preserve property involved in a loss or occurrence when reasonably practicable. An insured must permit us to inspect the property, premises, records, and operations relevant to the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.23** An insured must notify us promptly if the permitted incidental occupancy ceases, relocates, or materially changes. An insured must notify us promptly if the residence premises are no longer used in connection with the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.24** We do not cover loss arising out of an activity not described as the permitted incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss arising out of an expansion, alteration, or substitution of that activity unless we agree in writing.

**W.25** We do not cover loss arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, operation, loading, or unloading of a motorized vehicle in connection with the permitted incidental occupancy, except to the extent coverage is otherwise provided by this policy. We do not cover loss arising out of delivery or transportation services performed for the permitted incidental occupancy.

**W.26** We do not cover loss arising out of a dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, malicious, or intentional act by an insured. This exclusion applies even if an insured asserts that the resulting loss was unintended or unexpected.

**W.27** We do not cover loss arising out of an employee benefit plan, employment practice, or alleged violation of a person's rights in the course of the permitted incidental occupancy. We do not cover loss arising out of a contractual obligation assumed by an insured unless that obligation would exist without the contract.

**W.28** An insured must comply with these special requirements as a condition of coverage for the permitted incidental occupancy. Failure to comply may affect coverage to the extent the failure prejudices our rights.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle covered direct physical loss to "covered property" according to the applicable valuation method. We will not pay more than the amount required to repair or replace the damaged property, subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.2** We will determine the value of damaged property at the time of loss. We may consider the property’s age, condition, quality, useful life, and value immediately before the loss.

**W.3** We will pay "actual cash value" when replacement cost loss settlement does not apply. Actual cash value includes a deduction for depreciation when appropriate.

**W.4** We will pay "replacement cost" only after the damaged property is repaired or replaced. Replacement cost means the cost to repair or replace with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.

**W.5** An insured must repair or replace damaged property as soon as reasonably possible after loss. We will not pay for increased costs caused by an unreasonable delay by an insured.

**W.6** We may pay the actual cash value of the loss before repair or replacement is completed. We will pay any additional amount due under replacement cost loss settlement after an insured provides evidence that repair or replacement has been completed.

**W.7** We may elect to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. If we make that election, we will provide notice to you within a reasonable time after we receive the information needed to evaluate the loss.

**W.8** We will not pay for loss to undamaged property solely because the damaged property cannot be matched in color, texture, size, material, or appearance. We will pay for the direct physical loss to the damaged property.

**W.9** We will not pay for any increase in value resulting from repair or replacement. We will not pay for improvements, betterments, or changes that exceed restoration of the property to its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.10** We will not pay for the cost of complying with any ordinance, law, or governmental requirement unless that cost is covered under this policy. An insured must provide us with information concerning any such requirement that affects the claimed loss.

**W.11** We will settle loss to personal property at actual cash value unless another valuation method applies. We may consider the cost of replacement, depreciation, condition, and ordinary use of the property.

**W.12** We will settle loss to property that is part of a set or pair by paying for the loss in value of the set or pair. We may repair or replace any part of the set or pair to restore its value.

**W.13** We will settle loss to an article having artistic, historic, collectible, or special value based on its actual cash value unless we agree otherwise. An insured must provide records, appraisals, photographs, or other evidence supporting the claimed value when we request them.

**W.14** We will apply the "jewelry and watches limit" to covered loss involving jewelry or watches. We will not pay more than the amount available under that limit for the applicable loss.

**W.15** We will apply the "on-premises business property cap" to covered business property at an insured location. We will not combine that cap with any other coverage amount unless this policy provides otherwise.

**W.16** We will settle a covered loss involving property used in an incidental occupancy under the valuation provisions that apply to the property. The use of property in an incidental occupancy does not change the applicable loss settlement method.

**W.17** We will determine the amount of loss without regard to any mortgage, lien, security interest, or other interest in the property. Payment will be made as the interests of the parties appear when required by this policy.

**W.18** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless we agree otherwise. An insured must protect property from further damage after a loss.

**W.19** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath, provide records, and produce damaged property for inspection. We may also require an insured to provide a signed statement of loss.

**W.20** We will reduce the amount of a covered loss by the applicable deductible. We will apply the deductible to the loss before applying any applicable limit of liability.

**W.21** We will not pay for any part of a loss that is not caused by a covered peril. We will not pay for costs that an insured does not incur, except where this policy expressly provides otherwise.

**W.22** We will not pay more than the lesser of the amount necessary to repair or replace the damaged property, the applicable limit of liability, or the amount actually spent to repair or replace the property. We may require evidence of the amounts incurred.

**W.23** We will settle a covered loss involving property that cannot be repaired by paying the amount due under the applicable valuation method. We may take possession of damaged property for which we make payment, subject to the interests of other parties.

**W.24** The "loss assessment additional coverage limit" applies to covered loss assessments in accordance with this policy. That limit does not increase the limit of liability otherwise applicable to damaged property.

**W.25** The "fire service charge coverage" applies to a covered fire department charge in accordance with this policy. Payment under that coverage does not establish the value of any other covered loss.

**W.26** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of loss. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and records that are reasonably related to the claim.

**W.27** We will pay the amount due for a covered loss after we reach agreement with you, receive an appraisal award, or receive a final judgment. Payment of a claim does not waive any policy condition or defense available to us.

## W.8 — Definitions

"We" and "us" mean the insurer providing this coverage. "You" and "your" mean the person or organization named as the insured.

"Insured" means a person or organization qualifying for coverage under this policy. An insured includes any person entitled to coverage under the terms of this policy.

"Residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the grounds used in connection with that dwelling. Residence premises includes structures and areas used primarily for residential purposes.

"Premises" means the residence premises and any part of the residence premises used for a permitted incidental occupancy. Premises includes areas under your care, custody, or control.

"Permitted incidental occupancy" means an occupancy conducted on the premises that is incidental to the residential use of the premises. A permitted incidental occupancy must not materially change the character of the premises as a residence.

"Occupancy" means the use of premises for a stated activity, trade, profession, service, or operation. Occupancy includes activities customarily associated with that use.

"Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or economic gain. Business includes an activity conducted regularly or with the expectation of compensation.

"Business pursuit" means a business activity that is continuous, customary, or regularly undertaken. A business pursuit includes activities necessary to the operation of the business.

"Commercial activity" means an activity involving the offering, sale, distribution, manufacture, repair, storage, or servicing of property or services. Commercial activity includes activities undertaken for compensation.

"Professional service" means a service requiring specialized knowledge, education, training, skill, or judgment. Professional service includes advice, consultation, instruction, treatment, or design.

"Customer" means a person or organization receiving, seeking, or being offered goods or services in connection with a permitted incidental occupancy. Customer includes a client, patient, student, guest, or purchaser.

"Client" means a person or organization for whom an insured performs a service. Client includes a person or organization receiving advice, consultation, representation, or professional assistance.

"Employee" means a person performing services for an insured in connection with a permitted incidental occupancy. Employee includes a leased worker or temporary worker while performing those services.

"Volunteer worker" means a person who performs duties related to a permitted incidental occupancy without compensation. Volunteer worker does not include a person performing services for compensation.

"Product" means tangible property manufactured, sold, handled, distributed, or disposed of by an insured in connection with a permitted incidental occupancy. Product includes containers, packaging, labels, and related materials furnished with that property.

"Completed work" means work that has been finished in connection with a permitted incidental occupancy. Completed work includes materials, parts, or equipment furnished in connection with that work.

"Operations" means activities arising from a permitted incidental occupancy. Operations include the ownership, maintenance, use, or occupancy of premises used for that occupancy.

"Personal property" means property owned by, used by, or in the care of an insured in connection with a permitted incidental occupancy. Personal property includes business property and property of others.

"Business property" means personal property used primarily in connection with a business or permitted incidental occupancy. Business property includes equipment, stock, supplies, records, and materials.

"Stock" means merchandise, goods, raw materials, or supplies held for sale, lease, delivery, manufacture, processing, repair, or service. Stock includes property held for use in business operations.

"Records" means written, printed, electronic, recorded, or stored information relating to a permitted incidental occupancy. Records include data, files, accounts, plans, drawings, and similar information.

"Advertising" means a notice, communication, publication, display, or announcement intended to attract customers or promote a permitted incidental occupancy. Advertising includes material distributed by electronic means.

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. Occurrence includes resulting bodily injury or property damage that is neither expected nor intended by an insured.
