# HO-6 Homeowners 6 — Unit-Owners Form (Edition 2023-02)




## AGR — Agreement

This "policy" is a contract between you and us. It states the terms upon which we provide the insurance described in this policy.

We agree to provide the coverage afforded by this policy in return for the required premium. Our agreement is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of this policy.

You accept this policy when you accept its coverage. You and any "insured" must comply with the duties imposed by this policy.

We provide coverage only for a "loss" that is covered under this policy. Coverage applies only while this policy remains in effect.

Our duty to pay for loss is limited by the applicable limits of this policy. We do not pay more than the amount for which we are liable under this policy.

We do not cover loss that is excluded by this policy. An exclusion applies whether the loss results directly or indirectly from an excluded cause.

The terms of this policy govern the rights and duties of you and us. No waiver by us changes any term of this policy unless we agree to the waiver in writing.

An insured must provide truthful information in connection with this policy. We may rely upon information provided by you or an insured in administering this policy.

A "claim" is subject to the duties and conditions that apply under this policy. Our performance of any duty does not waive any right available to us.

This policy may be changed only as permitted by its terms. Any change must be made by us and made part of this policy.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of covered property at the time of loss, determined by replacement cost less depreciation. Depreciation reflects age, condition, and expected useful life. It includes a reduction for wear affecting damaged property. It does not include the cost of improvements made after the loss.


**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace covered property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. The cost is based on materials and labor reasonably necessary to complete the repair or replacement. It includes customary charges associated with restoring damaged property. It does not include the cost of betterment or alterations unrelated to the loss.


**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An accident may result in bodily injury or property damage. It includes damage caused by an unintended release of water from a plumbing system. It does not include a deliberate act whose resulting injury or damage was expected or intended.


**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the unit where you reside and that is shown in the Declarations. It includes structures and portions of the property reserved for your exclusive use. It includes property used by you as a private residence. It does not include property used solely for rental or business purposes.


**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or persons in your care. It includes your spouse if your spouse is a resident of your household. It includes a person having custody of covered property after your death, but only with respect to that property. It does not include a person temporarily staying at the residence without becoming a member of your household.


**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations described in this definition. It includes land owned by or rented to you where a residence is being constructed for your use. It includes premises used by you in connection with the residence premises. It does not include premises used principally for business purposes.


**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain. It includes services performed for compensation and property rented to others for compensation. It includes an activity conducted from the residence premises for profit. It does not include a personal hobby not conducted for economic gain.


**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or off public roads and propelled by mechanical power. It includes an attached trailer or semitrailer. It includes a recreational vehicle capable of transporting persons or property. It does not include a vehicle operated exclusively upon rails or crawler treads.


**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of you whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. It includes a domestic worker performing household duties for you. It includes a person caring for your grounds or household property. It does not include an independent contractor retained to perform a project.


**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. It includes resulting care, loss of services, and death. It includes an injury caused by a fall at the residence premises. It does not include injury arising solely from emotional distress without bodily harm.


**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. It includes resulting loss of use of that property. It includes damage to a fixture caused by an accidental impact. It does not include loss involving property that has not sustained physical injury or loss of use.


**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unexpected, and unintended. It includes an unforeseen break in a plumbing pipe that immediately releases water. It includes damage directly caused by an abrupt discharge. It does not include seepage, leakage, or deterioration occurring gradually.


**DEF.13** "vacant" means the absence of customary furnishings and habitual occupancy at the residence premises. A unit without furniture and without customary use is vacant. Where vacancy is disputed, the claims manual's duration test applies the definitionthe claims manual's duration test applies the definition (see Property Claims Handling Manual, C3 Chapter 3 — Water Losses). It does not include a furnished unit temporarily unoccupied during customary travel or relocation.


**DEF.14** "named storm" means a weather system assigned a name by an appropriate governmental weather authority. It includes a storm identified by that assigned name in official weather reports. It includes wind, rain, and related conditions directly associated with that system. It does not include an unnamed thunderstorm or ordinary rainfall.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We provide the insurance described in this Coverage A — Dwelling provision for direct physical loss to property that is covered. The property must be at the "residence premises".

**A.2** "Coverage A" applies to the dwelling unit that you own and occupy as a residence. The dwelling unit must be shown as your residence premises.

**A.3** Unless another Coverage A limit is shown in the policy, the Coverage A limit is ten thousand dollars. This limit is the most we will pay for loss covered under Coverage A.

**A.4** We cover alterations, appliances, fixtures, and improvements that are part of the building and are contained within your dwelling unit. The property must be your responsibility under an agreement with the building owner or association.

**A.5** We cover property that you own and that is attached to the dwelling unit. The property must be used principally for residential purposes.

**A.6** We cover materials and supplies located at the residence premises that are intended for use in the construction, alteration, or repair of property covered under Coverage A. The materials and supplies must be your property or be your responsibility.

**A.7** We cover building property that you are required to insure under an agreement. The agreement must be in effect when the loss occurs.

**A.8** We cover property within areas reserved for your exclusive use. The property must be part of the residence premises and must be your responsibility.

**A.9** We cover loss caused by a covered peril. The loss must result in direct physical damage to covered property.

**A.10** We do not cover land, including land on which the residence premises is located. We also do not cover any value assigned to land.

**A.11** We do not cover foundations, patios, walkways, driveways, or other property that is not part of your dwelling unit. This exclusion applies whether the property is owned by you or by another person.

**A.12** We do not cover property that is owned by a building owner, association, or other person unless you are responsible for that property under an agreement. The agreement must require you to repair or replace the property.

**A.13** We do not cover property used primarily for business purposes. This exclusion does not apply to property used solely for activities ordinarily incidental to residential use.

**A.14** We do not cover loss arising from neglect by an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**A.15** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**A.16** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**A.17** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from such loss.

**A.18** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies whether the seepage or leakage is hidden or visible.

**A.19** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler systems while the residence premises is vacant or unoccupied. An insured must maintain heat in the building or shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**A.20** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril resulting from such occurrence.

**A.21** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss is greater than that intended or expected.

**A.22** We settle covered loss to property under Coverage A at replacement cost without deduction for depreciation when the Coverage A limit equals at least eighty percent of the replacement cost of the covered property. The property must be repaired or replaced before we pay replacement cost.

**A.23** If covered property is not repaired or replaced, we will pay no more than actual cash value. Actual cash value is determined at the time of loss.

**A.24** We will not pay more than the amount actually and necessarily spent to repair or replace covered property. Repair or replacement must be completed with materials of like kind and quality.

**A.25** We may pay the value of the lost or damaged property, repair the property, rebuild the property, or replace the property. We may select the method of settlement.

**A.26** An insured must provide us with access to the damaged property as often as reasonably required. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and obtain information concerning the loss.

**A.27** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must not voluntarily dispose of damaged property except when necessary to protect property from further damage.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover direct physical loss to "other structures" at the "residence premises" that are owned by an "insured". Coverage applies when the loss is caused by a "covered cause of loss".

**B.2** A structure must be set apart from the dwelling by clear space to be an other structure. A structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection is set apart by clear space.

**B.3** We do not treat a structure physically attached to the dwelling as an other structure. Coverage for an attached structure is not provided under this coverage.

**B.4** Coverage includes permanently installed fixtures that are part of a covered other structure. Coverage also includes materials intended for repair or alteration of a covered other structure while located at the residence premises.

**B.5** We do not cover "personal property" as an other structure. This exclusion applies even when personal property is located within a covered other structure.

**B.6** We do not cover land on which an other structure is located. We also do not cover landscaping, trees, shrubs, or plants.

**B.7** We do not cover a structure that is rented or held for rental. This exclusion does not apply to a private garage made available for rental.

**B.8** We do not cover a structure from which a "business" is conducted. This exclusion does not apply to a private garage used only for storage of business property owned by an insured.

**B.9** Coverage applies only to an other structure owned solely by an insured. We do not cover an other structure owned by a condominium association, another person, or another entity.

**B.10** An insured must maintain a covered other structure in a condition that reasonably protects it from preventable damage. An insured must take reasonable steps to secure a covered other structure when it is not occupied.

**B.11** An insured must notify us promptly of a loss involving an other structure. Notice must identify the damaged property and provide available information concerning the cause of loss.

**B.12** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect covered property when practicable.

**B.13** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when practicable. An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property and any records that reasonably relate to the loss.

**B.14** We may repair, replace, or pay for covered damaged property. We may select the materials, methods, and service providers used for repair or replacement.

**B.15** Our payment for loss is subject to the limit applicable to this coverage. The applicable limit is the most we will pay for loss arising from an "occurrence".

**B.16** If we pay for covered property, we may take title to that property. An insured must transfer any interest in the property to us when requested.

**B.17** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss. An insured must provide requested information that is material to the loss.

**B.18** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to this coverage or a loss. We may deny coverage when an insured commits fraud in connection with a loss.

**B.19** We do not cover loss arising from an intentional act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies when the insured acts with intent to cause loss.

**B.20** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not apply to damage that occurs before the insured has a reasonable opportunity to protect the property.

**B.21** We do not cover an other structure after an insured transfers ownership of that structure. Coverage ends when the insured no longer has an insurable interest in the structure.

**B.22** We do not cover loss to an other structure that is vacant and not maintained. An insured must maintain reasonable access to a covered other structure for inspection and necessary repairs.

**B.23** We need not accept abandoned property. An insured must remove property that we do not take after a loss.

**B.24** If damaged property is recovered after we make payment for the loss, an insured must notify us promptly. We may recover the property or adjust the payment to reflect its recovery.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" while it is anywhere in the world. Covered personal property is subject to the terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions of this policy.

**C.2** We cover personal property while it is at the "residence premises" or away from the residence premises. The residence premises means the unit and related property shown in the policy as your residence.

**C.3** At your request, we cover personal property owned by others while the property is at the residence premises. We may also cover property of a guest or residence employee while the property is in any residence occupied by an insured.

**C.4** Property owned by a guest or residence employee must be in the care of an insured when the loss occurs. We may require the owner of that property to submit to examination under oath and to provide records concerning the property.

**C.5** We cover property usually kept at an insured's residence other than the residence premises while the insured is temporarily living there. The property must be owned or used by an insured.

**C.6** We cover property of a student while the student is residing away from the residence premises. The student must be related to an insured or be under the care of an insured.

**C.7** We cover property acquired during the policy period if the property is owned or used by an insured. Coverage for acquired property is subject to the applicable limit of liability for Coverage C.

**C.8** We cover property intended for an insured's personal, family, or household use. We do not cover property held for sale by an insured in the regular course of business.

**C.9** We cover property used in connection with an insured's occupation only as provided by this Coverage C. Property used principally for business remains subject to the special limits stated in this section.

**C.10** We cover personal property damaged by a covered peril. The loss must be accidental and direct.

**C.11** We do not cover loss to property caused by intentional conduct of an insured. This exclusion applies even if another cause contributes to the loss.

**C.12** We do not cover property after it has been transferred to another person by fraud, deceit, or trickery. This exclusion applies whether the transfer is voluntary or involuntary.

**C.13** We do not cover property while it is in the care, custody, or control of a person engaged in transporting, storing, repairing, or servicing the property. This exclusion does not apply to loss caused by a covered peril while the property is at the residence premises.

**C.14** We do not cover property that is separately described and specifically insured under another insurance policy. This exclusion applies only to the extent that the other insurance provides coverage for the same loss.

**C.15** We do not cover "motor vehicles" or their equipment, except for motorized equipment used solely to service the residence premises. A motor vehicle means a land vehicle designed for travel on public roads.

**C.16** We do not cover aircraft or parts of aircraft. This exclusion does not apply to model or hobby aircraft that are not designed to carry persons.

**C.17** We do not cover "watercraft" or their furnishings, equipment, and outboard motors, except as provided by the special limit in this section. Watercraft means a vessel designed for travel on water.

**C.18** We do not cover property of roomers, boarders, or other tenants unless the property is owned or used by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to property of a guest while the property is at the residence premises.

**C.19** We do not cover property used for business purposes when the property is owned by a person who is not an insured. This exclusion applies whether the property is located at or away from the residence premises.

**C.20** We do not cover loss caused by confiscation, seizure, destruction, or detention of property by a governmental authority. This exclusion does not apply to property destroyed to prevent the spread of fire.

**C.21** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an insured to use reasonable means to preserve property from further damage after a loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**C.22** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must not discard property that may be material to our investigation of the loss.

**C.23** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request to establish ownership, value, and the extent of loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**C.24** An insured must submit a signed proof of loss if we request it. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary to determine the loss.

**C.25** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining information concerning the ownership and value of covered property.

**C.26** The special limits in this section apply to all covered property involved in the same loss. These limits apply regardless of the number of insureds, claims, or items of property involved.

**C.27** For money, bank notes, bullion, gold other than goldware, silver other than silverware, platinum, coins, medals, and precious metals, the most we will pay is two hundred fifty dollars. This limit applies to loss by any covered peril.

**C.28** For theft of jewelry, watches, precious and semiprecious stones, the most we will pay is two thousand dollars. This limit applies to articles worn, carried, stored, or otherwise possessed by an insured.

**C.29** For theft of firearms and related equipment, the most we will pay is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies whether the firearms are owned, borrowed, or used by an insured.

**C.30** For watercraft, including trailers and their furnishings, equipment, and outboard motors, the most we will pay is one thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies to loss by any covered peril.

**C.31** For theft of silverware, silver-plated ware, goldware, gold-plated ware, and pewterware, the most we will pay is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies to articles used for personal, family, or household purposes.

**C.32** For business property on the residence premises, the most we will pay is three thousand dollars. "Business property" means personal property used in connection with a trade, profession, or occupation.

**C.33** For electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle, the most we will pay is one thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies to apparatus that can be operated from the electrical system of the motor vehicle.

**C.34** The special limits apply before any applicable deductible. The special limits do not increase the Coverage C limit of liability.

**C.35** We will not pay more than the applicable special limit for property within a category described in this section. A special limit applies even if the property has greater market value, replacement cost, sentimental value, or appraised value.

**C.36** We may require an insured to provide evidence of ownership for property subject to a special limit. Such evidence may include receipts, photographs, appraisals, maintenance records, or other reliable documentation.

**C.37** We may investigate the circumstances of any loss involving property subject to a special limit. An insured must provide information that we reasonably request concerning the loss.

**C.38** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. This exclusion applies unless the loss results directly from a covered peril.

**C.39** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, malfunction, or defect. This exclusion does not apply to ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.40** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or contamination. This exclusion does not apply to direct loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.41** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from agricultural smudging, or industrial operations. This exclusion does not apply to sudden and accidental smoke damage from a covered source.

**C.42** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. This exclusion applies whether the condition develops gradually or suddenly.

**C.43** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. This exclusion does not apply to direct loss caused by fire or explosion.

**C.44** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further loss.

**C.45** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action affecting the use, repair, replacement, or restoration of property. This exclusion does not apply to direct loss caused by fire.

**C.46** We do not cover property that is illegal to possess or transport under applicable law. This exclusion applies whether the property is owned by an insured or by another person.

**C.47** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's business activities except as expressly provided by this Coverage C. This exclusion applies to property used primarily for commercial purposes.

**C.48** We do not cover loss to data, programs, software, or instructions stored on electronic media. This exclusion applies regardless of the cause of the loss.

**C.49** We do not cover loss resulting from the unauthorized use of credit cards, fund transfer cards, access devices, or stored value devices. This exclusion applies whether the unauthorized use occurs electronically or otherwise.

**C.50** We do not cover loss to property resulting from voluntary parting with the property because of any fraudulent scheme. This exclusion applies even if an insured was induced to part with the property by false pretenses.

**C.51** We may inspect covered property before or after a loss. Our inspection does not constitute an appraisal, warranty, or representation that property is safe or suitable.

**C.52** We may request that damaged property be made available for examination. An insured must allow us reasonable access to the property and to records concerning the property.

**C.53** We may require an insured to show the damaged property to us. An insured must not repair, replace, or dispose of damaged property before we inspect it, unless immediate action is necessary to protect the property from further damage.

**C.54** An insured must notify us promptly of a loss involving covered property. The notice must identify the property, describe the loss, and state the location where the loss occurred.

**C.55** An insured must notify the police when a theft or other criminal loss has occurred. An insured must cooperate with law enforcement authorities to the extent reasonably requested.

**C.56** Payment for covered property will not exceed the amount of loss sustained, subject to the applicable limit of liability and special limits. We may pay an insured, repair the property, replace the property, or take any other lawful action to settle the loss.

**C.57** If we pay for a loss, we may take title to damaged property or recoverable property to the extent permitted by law. An insured must cooperate with us in preserving our rights concerning that property.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** We provide "Coverage D" for "loss of use" resulting from a "covered loss" to property at the "residence premises". This coverage applies only as stated in this section.

**D.2** The limit of liability for "Coverage D" is fifty percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". This limit is the most we will pay for all loss of use arising from any covered loss.

**D.3** We will pay the "additional living expense" necessary to maintain your household's normal standard of living. The residence premises must be unfit for living because of a covered loss.

**D.4** Additional living expense means the increase in necessary living expenses you incur because you cannot live at the residence premises. We will pay only expenses that are reasonable and necessary.

**D.5** We will pay additional living expense while the part of the residence premises used by you is unfit for living. We will not pay additional living expense after that part becomes fit for living.

**D.6** An insured must take reasonable steps to minimize additional living expense. An insured must not incur expenses that are unnecessary or excessive.

**D.7** We do not cover additional living expense resulting from a loss that is not covered under this policy. We do not cover additional living expense resulting from conditions unrelated to a covered loss.

**D.8** We will pay the "fair rental value" of the part of the residence premises rented to others or held for rental. The residence premises must be unfit for living because of a covered loss.

**D.9** Fair rental value means the value of the rental portion of the residence premises less expenses that do not continue while that portion is unfit for living. We will pay fair rental value only for the loss of rental use actually sustained.

**D.10** We will pay fair rental value while the rental portion of the residence premises is unfit for living. We will not pay fair rental value after that portion becomes fit for living.

**D.11** We do not cover fair rental value for expenses that continue despite the covered loss. We do not cover fair rental value for a rental loss not caused by the covered loss.

**D.12** An insured must make reasonable efforts to rent property held for rental after it becomes fit for living. We will not pay fair rental value for loss caused by an insured's failure to make those efforts.

**D.13** We will pay additional living expense and fair rental value when a "civil authority" prohibits use of the residence premises. The prohibition must result directly from direct physical loss to property near the residence premises.

**D.14** The direct physical loss to nearby property must result from a cause of loss covered under this policy. The civil authority prohibition must prevent use of the residence premises.

**D.15** We will pay only the additional living expense or fair rental value sustained while the civil authority prohibition remains in effect. We will not pay after the prohibition ends.

**D.16** We do not cover loss of use resulting from a civil authority action that does not prohibit use of the residence premises. We do not cover loss of use resulting from a civil authority action unrelated to a covered cause of loss.

**D.17** Payment for loss of use ends when the residence premises are repaired or replaced and become fit for living. Payment also ends when you permanently relocate.

**D.18** An insured must permit repair or replacement to proceed with due diligence and dispatch. We will not pay loss of use caused by an insured's unreasonable delay.

**D.19** We will pay only loss of use that is sustained during the time reasonably required to repair or replace damaged property. We will not pay loss of use caused by delay unrelated to the covered loss.

**D.20** We do not cover loss of use after the residence premises become fit for living. We do not cover loss of use after a rental portion becomes fit for living.

**D.21** We do not cover expenses that would have been incurred without the covered loss. We do not cover expenses incurred for personal convenience rather than necessary living needs.

**D.22** We do not cover costs to improve the residence premises or to obtain accommodations exceeding your normal standard of living. We will pay only the reasonable increase in necessary living expenses.

**D.23** An insured must keep records supporting each claim for loss of use. Those records must show the expenses incurred and the reason each expense was necessary.

**D.24** An insured must provide us with receipts, invoices, rental records, and other information we reasonably request. An insured must cooperate with us in investigating and adjusting the claim.

**D.25** An insured must identify expenses that would have been incurred without the covered loss. We may deduct those expenses from any additional living expense payment.

**D.26** An insured must identify expenses that do not continue while rental property is unfit for living. We may deduct those expenses from any fair rental value payment.

**D.27** We may require reasonable proof that the residence premises were unfit for living. We may require reasonable proof that the condition resulted from a covered loss.

**D.28** We may require reasonable proof of the rental value claimed. We may consider lease agreements, rental records, and comparable rental information in determining fair rental value.

**D.29** Payment under this coverage does not increase any limit of liability applicable to this policy. All payments under this coverage are subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**D.30** We do not cover loss of use resulting from an insured's intentional act. We do not cover loss of use resulting from concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud by an insured.

**D.31** We do not cover loss of use resulting from neglect by an insured to use reasonable means to protect property after a covered loss. We do not cover loss of use resulting from an insured's failure to preserve property from further damage.

**D.32** An insured must notify us promptly of a claim for loss of use. An insured must provide information sufficient for us to determine the amount and duration of the claimed loss.

**D.33** We will pay loss of use only after an insured complies with the duties applicable to a claim. Our payment is subject to all terms, conditions, and exclusions of this policy.

## I.E — Additional Coverages

**E.1** We cover the reasonable expense of removing debris of "covered property" when the debris results from a covered "direct physical loss." The debris must result from a peril insured against under this policy.

**E.2** We will pay for debris removal only when the property damaged by the covered direct physical loss is covered under this policy. We do not cover the expense of removing debris of property that is not covered property.

**E.3** We will pay the expense of removing debris from the "residence premises" when the debris results from a covered direct physical loss. We do not cover expense caused by pollutants unless the pollutants result from a covered direct physical loss.

**E.4** We will pay the expense of removing fallen trees from the residence premises when the trees damage covered property. We do not cover the expense of removing trees that do not damage covered property.

**E.5** We will pay reasonable expense incurred to protect covered property from further debris-related damage after a covered direct physical loss. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**E.6** The amount available for debris removal includes the amount payable for the covered property that produced the debris. An insured must provide records supporting the expense claimed.

**E.7** When the amount payable for covered property and debris removal exhausts the applicable limit, we will provide an additional amount equal to five percent. This additional amount applies only to debris removal resulting from a covered direct physical loss.

**E.8** We cover your liability for a fire department service charge when the charge results from a fire occurring at the residence premises. The charge must be imposed by a public authority or fire protection organization.

**E.9** The most we will pay for a fire department service charge is seven hundred fifty dollars. This coverage does not reduce the limit applicable to the property loss.

**E.10** We do not cover a fire department service charge imposed because of your failure to maintain required fire protection equipment. We do not cover a charge arising from a fire that is not a covered direct physical loss.

**E.11** We cover loss resulting from the unauthorized use of a credit card issued to you or registered in your name. The unauthorized use must occur during the policy period.

**E.12** We cover loss resulting from the unauthorized use of a fund transfer card issued to you or registered in your name. The loss must arise from a transfer of funds without your authorization.

**E.13** We cover loss resulting from forgery or alteration of a check, draft, or similar written promise or order to pay money. The forgery or alteration must be committed by a person other than an insured.

**E.14** We cover loss resulting from the acceptance in good faith of counterfeit money. The counterfeit money must be accepted in the ordinary course of your personal activities.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We cover "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by a "named peril," subject to the provisions of this policy. Loss must result from a fortuitous event that causes physical damage.

**P.2** "Covered property" means property for which coverage applies under this policy. Property is covered only while it is at a location described in this policy or otherwise qualifies for coverage.

**P.3** A "named peril" means a cause of loss described in this section. We cover loss caused by a named peril only when the peril directly causes the loss.

**P.4** We do not cover loss caused by wear, tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or gradual damage. We do not cover any resulting loss unless that resulting loss is caused by a named peril.

**P.5** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover cracking caused by those conditions.

**P.6** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, renovation, construction, materials, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether the deficiency is performed by you or by another person.

**P.7** We do not cover loss caused by neglect by an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**P.8** We cover loss caused by fire or lightning. Fire includes hostile fire that escapes its intended location.

**P.9** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We cover sudden and accidental loss caused by smoke from a hostile fire.

**P.10** We cover loss caused by windstorm or hail. We do not cover loss to property inside a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless the direct force of wind or hail damages the building and creates an opening.

**P.11** We do not cover loss caused by wind, hail, rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust to awnings, fences, outdoor antennas, outdoor equipment, or property in the open. We cover loss to such property when caused by fire, lightning, explosion, riot, civil commotion, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, vandalism, malicious mischief, theft, or volcanic eruption.

**P.12** We cover loss caused by explosion. Explosion includes the bursting or rupture of an appliance, container, or system that releases pressure.

**P.13** We do not cover loss caused by explosion of steam boilers, steam pipes, steam engines, or steam turbines that are owned by, leased to, or operated by an insured. We cover resulting loss caused by fire or explosion.

**P.14** We cover loss caused by riot or civil commotion. Civil commotion includes a public disturbance involving acts of violence or disorder.

**P.15** We cover loss caused by aircraft. Aircraft includes self-propelled aircraft and objects falling from aircraft.

**P.16** We cover loss caused by vehicles. We cover loss caused by a vehicle operated by an insured only while the vehicle is used solely to service a residence.

**P.17** We do not cover loss caused by a vehicle to property owned by an insured when the vehicle is operated by an insured. We cover loss caused by fire, explosion, or theft resulting from that event.

**P.18** We cover loss caused by smoke. Smoke means a sudden and accidental discharge or release of smoke, soot, fumes, or vapors from within a heating, cooking, or power-generating device.

**P.19** We cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief. Vandalism includes willful and malicious damage to covered property.

**P.20** We do not cover vandalism or malicious mischief loss if the residence premises has been vacant. A residence premises is vacant when it lacks the customary furnishings necessary for normal habitation.

**P.21** We cover loss caused by theft. Theft includes attempted theft and loss of property from a known location when the circumstances indicate a theft occurred.

**P.22** We do not cover theft committed by an insured. We do not cover theft committed by a person regularly residing at the residence premises.

**P.23** We do not cover theft loss to property while it is at a residence owned by, rented to, or occupied by an insured, unless an insured is temporarily residing there. We do not cover theft loss to property in or upon a motor vehicle unless the property is secured within a fully enclosed and locked vehicle compartment.

**P.24** We do not cover theft loss to credit cards, fund transfer cards, access devices, or similar instruments under this peril. We do not cover theft loss to property held for rental or entrusted to an insured for business purposes.

**P.25** We cover loss caused by falling objects. Damage to the exterior of a building must occur before we cover loss to property inside the building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust.

**P.26** We do not cover loss caused by falling objects that are part of a building, structure, tree, or plant, unless the object is first severed from the property from which it falls. We cover resulting loss caused by a named peril.

**P.27** We cover loss caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet. This peril includes the weight of ice, snow, or sleet that accumulates on a building or other structure.

**P.28** We cover loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. We cover the direct physical loss caused by the discharge or overflow.

**P.29** We do not cover loss caused by constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. We do not cover loss caused by seepage or leakage that occurs over time.

**P.30** We do not cover loss to the system or appliance from which water or steam escaped. We cover damage caused by the accidental discharge or overflow to other covered property.

**P.31** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows or is otherwise discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.
