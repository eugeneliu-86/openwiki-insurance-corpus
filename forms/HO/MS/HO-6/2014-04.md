# HO-6 Homeowners 6 — Unit-Owners Form (Edition 2014-04)
> SUPERSEDED by HO-6 Homeowners 6 — Unit-Owners Form (Edition 2023-02) for policies effective on or after 2023-02-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

We provide the insurance described in this "policy" in return for the "premium" and your compliance with all applicable policy provisions. This policy consists of the terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions stated in it.

"You" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the policy declarations and the spouse of that person if a resident of the same household. "We," "us," and "our" refer to the insurer providing this policy.

Coverage applies only to an "insured" who meets the requirements of this policy. An insured must comply with all duties imposed by this policy before we have an obligation to provide coverage.

We insure against direct physical "loss" to covered property when the loss is caused by a covered peril. Coverage is subject to all applicable exclusions, limitations, deductibles, and conditions.

We provide coverage only for property that qualifies as "covered property" under this policy. The existence of property at the residence premises does not alone establish that the property is covered.

A "claim" must arise from an accidental loss that occurs during the policy period. We determine coverage based on the facts, circumstances, and policy provisions applicable to the claim.

An insured must give us truthful and complete information in connection with an application, renewal, claim, or other request for coverage. We may rely upon information provided by an insured in administering this policy.

An insured must protect covered property from further damage following a loss. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to take reasonable steps to protect covered property when those steps can be taken safely.

We may inspect the residence premises and covered property at a reasonable time. Our inspection does not constitute a representation that property is safe, complies with applicable requirements, or is insurable.

We may investigate a claim and request information reasonably necessary to determine coverage. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

We may pay for covered loss, repair covered property, replace covered property, or settle a claim as permitted by this policy. Our exercise of a policy right does not waive any other policy right.

We do not cover any loss excluded by this policy. An exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event that contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss when the policy so provides.

This policy is a contract between you and us. Its terms may be changed only as permitted by this policy and applicable law.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of covered property at the time of loss after adjustment for depreciation, age, condition, and obsolescence.  
It includes the value of a used appliance after its wear and condition are considered.  
It does not include the cost of a new appliance without adjustment for depreciation.  
It does not include sentimental value or personal attachment to the property.  
We determine this value from available information concerning the property and the loss.  

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with material of like kind and quality without deduction for depreciation.  
It includes the reasonable cost to replace a damaged cabinet with a comparable new cabinet.  
It does not include any amount attributable to betterment, enlargement, or improvement.  
It does not include the value of land.  
We determine this cost from the prices reasonably available at the time of repair or replacement.  

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.  
It includes an accidental fire that damages property or causes injury.  
It does not include an event caused deliberately by an insured.  
All injury or damage arising from substantially the same general harmful conditions is treated as a single occurrence.  
The accident must result in bodily injury or property damage.  

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the unit you own and use as your residence, together with structures and grounds used in connection with that unit.  
It includes a garage or storage area reserved for your exclusive use.  
It does not include a common area available for the use of other unit owners.  
It does not include premises used solely for business purposes.  
The unit must be used by you as a residence.  

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are under your care.  
It includes your spouse if your spouse resides in your household.  
It includes a person under your care who resides in your household.  
It does not include a visitor who does not reside in your household.  
It does not include a tenant who occupies part of the residence premises under a rental agreement.  

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and premises used by you in connection with the residence premises.  
It includes a parking area assigned for your exclusive use.  
It includes premises where you temporarily reside while the residence premises are being repaired.  
It does not include premises owned by you and used solely for business purposes.  
It does not include premises occupied by a person who is not an insured.  

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain.  
It includes an enterprise conducted from the residence premises for compensation.  
It includes services performed for compensation on a regular basis.  
It does not include an occasional activity performed without an intent to earn a profit.  
It does not include a personal hobby that is not conducted for economic gain.  

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration.  
It includes an automobile, motorcycle, or similar vehicle propelled by a motor.  
It includes a vehicle rented or borrowed by an insured.  
It does not include a mobility device designed solely to assist a person with a physical disability.  
It does not include a vehicle designed solely for use on rails.  

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises, including household or personal services.  
It includes a domestic worker who performs cleaning or cooking services for you.  
It includes a person who performs gardening or maintenance services under your direction.  
It does not include an independent contractor who controls the manner of performing the work.  
It does not include a person employed solely in connection with your business.  

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death.  
It includes physical injury caused by a fall on the residence premises.  
It includes sickness resulting from exposure to harmful conditions.  
It does not include emotional distress that does not result from bodily harm.  
It does not include an economic loss that is not caused by bodily harm, sickness, or disease.  

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.  
It includes damage to furniture caused by an accidental fire.  
It includes loss of use of tangible property resulting from physical injury to that property.  
It does not include loss caused solely by a decline in market value.  
It does not include injury to property that is not tangible.  

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unforeseen.  
It includes water damage caused when a pipe unexpectedly bursts.  
It includes damage caused by an unintended discharge of water.  
It does not include damage caused by gradual leakage, seepage, corrosion, or deterioration.  
The event or result must be both abrupt and unintended.  

**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of personal property and customary activities.  
It includes a unit that contains no furnishings and is not being used as a residence.  
It does not include a furnished unit temporarily unoccupied while you are away.  
It does not include a unit containing property that shows continued residential use.  
A unit may be vacant even though it remains available for occupancy.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** "Coverage A" applies to the building items described in this section that are part of the "residence premises." We cover direct physical loss to covered property, subject to the terms of this section.

**A.2** The "residence premises" means the "unit" where you reside and that is identified as your insured location. The unit includes the portion of the building reserved for your exclusive use.

**A.3** "Covered property" means alterations, appliances, fixtures, and improvements that are part of the building and are within the residence premises. Covered property includes items that you own or for which you are responsible under an agreement.

**A.4** Covered property includes building materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises when they are intended for use in construction, alteration, or repair of the unit. The materials and supplies must be owned by you or be your responsibility.

**A.5** Covered property includes interior walls, ceilings, floors, cabinets, built-in appliances, plumbing fixtures, heating equipment, electrical equipment, and similar items that are part of the unit. We cover these items only to the extent of your ownership interest or contractual responsibility.

**A.6** Covered property includes property that pertains exclusively to the residence premises and is not owned in common with other unit owners. We cover such property only while it remains part of the residence premises.

**A.7** Covered property includes a structure that you own solely and that is located on the residence premises. The structure must not be held for rental to a person who is not a tenant of the residence premises.

**A.8** We cover your interest in property owned in common with other unit owners only when you are legally responsible for repair or replacement of that property. Our payment will not exceed your ownership interest or legal responsibility.

**A.9** We do not cover land, including land on which the residence premises is located. We also do not cover property that is not part of a building or structure.

**A.10** "Direct physical loss" means accidental physical damage to covered property. The loss must result from a covered peril.

**A.11** A "covered peril" means a cause of loss that is not excluded by this section. We cover loss only when the covered peril causes direct physical loss to covered property.

**A.12** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of a loss. This exclusion applies whether the neglect occurs before or after we are notified of the loss.

**A.13** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must keep a record of expenses incurred to protect the property.

**A.14** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged covered property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably possible until we have completed our inspection.

**A.15** An insured must provide records, receipts, contracts, estimates, photographs, and other information that we reasonably request to evaluate the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation and adjustment of the loss.

**A.16** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses necessary to protect covered property from further damage. We will not be bound by a voluntary payment or obligation that we did not approve.

**A.17** An insured may make reasonable temporary repairs to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must retain damaged parts and receipts for temporary repairs when reasonably possible.

**A.18** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may make payment for the loss instead of repairing, rebuilding, or replacing the property.

**A.19** "Loss settlement" means the method we use to determine the amount payable for covered loss. We will apply the loss settlement provisions in effect when the direct physical loss occurs.

**A.20** To receive "replacement cost" settlement, the Coverage A limit must equal at least eighty percent of the full replacement cost of covered property at the time of loss. Replacement cost does not include the value of land.

**A.21** If the condition for replacement cost settlement is not met, we will settle the loss at "actual cash value." Actual cash value settlement is subject to the Coverage A limit.

**A.22** "Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, with appropriate deduction for depreciation and physical condition. We will determine actual cash value based on available information concerning the property and the loss.

**A.23** We will not pay more than actual cash value until repair or replacement is completed. After repair or replacement is completed, we will pay any additional amount due under replacement cost settlement, subject to the Coverage A limit.

**A.24** We will not pay more than the least of the amount actually spent to repair or replace covered property, the cost to repair or replace the property with property of like kind and quality, or the Coverage A limit. We will not pay for improvements that increase the value or usefulness of property beyond its condition immediately before the loss.

**A.25** We may pay for repair or replacement of damaged property with material that is reasonably comparable in appearance, function, and quality. We do not guarantee that repaired or replaced property will match undamaged property.

**A.26** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, or demolition of property. This exclusion applies even if the property was damaged by a covered peril.

**A.27** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, grading, compaction, or materials. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from such conditions unless otherwise excluded.

**A.28** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, latent defect, or inherent vice. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from these conditions unless otherwise excluded.

**A.29** We do not cover loss caused intentionally by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to an insured who did not participate in, cooperate in, or contribute to the intentional loss.

**A.30** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or other action by a governmental or public authority. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from such action if the fire is otherwise covered.

**A.31** If we make payment for a covered loss, we may recover from a person responsible for that loss. An insured must preserve our rights of "subrogation" and must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**A.32** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged covered property after payment if we choose to do so.

**A.33** The Coverage A limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss to covered property arising from an occurrence. Payment for a covered loss does not increase the Coverage A limit.

**A.34** The default Coverage A limit is five thousand dollars. We will not pay more than the applicable Coverage A limit.

**A.35** We will pay a covered loss only to an insured with an "insurable interest" in the covered property when the loss occurs. Insurable interest means a financial interest in the preservation of the property.

**A.36** We may adjust a covered loss with an insured having an ownership interest in the covered property. Payment to that insured will satisfy our obligation to all insureds to the extent of that payment.

**A.37** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we reasonably request it.

**A.38** An insured must promptly notify us of a loss involving covered property. An insured must provide information sufficient for us to identify the damaged property, determine the cause of loss, and evaluate the amount of loss.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover your interest in "other structures" located on the "residence premises." An other structure is a building or structure that is separate from the "dwelling."

**B.2** An other structure must be set apart from the dwelling by clear space. Clear space is an unobstructed area that separates the exterior portions of the structures.

**B.3** An other structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection is set apart by clear space. Such a connection does not make the other structure part of the dwelling.

**B.4** We do not cover a structure as an other structure when it is physically connected to the dwelling by more than a fence, utility line, or similar connection. Such a structure is considered part of the dwelling.

**B.5** An insured must have an insurable interest in an other structure at the time of loss. We cover no greater interest than the interest you have in the other structure.

**B.6** We cover your interest in an other structure that you own with another person. We do not cover the interest of a person who is not an insured.

**B.7** An other structure must be located on the residence premises at the time of loss. We do not cover an other structure after it has been permanently removed from the residence premises.

**B.8** We cover an eligible other structure while it is being constructed, altered, or repaired on the residence premises. The structure must otherwise satisfy the requirements of this coverage.

**B.9** A foundation, permanently installed equipment, and materials permanently attached to an other structure are part of that other structure. We cover those items only while they remain part of the other structure.

**B.10** We do not cover detached movable property located in, on, or near an other structure. Such property is not an other structure.

**B.11** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We also do not cover excavation, grading, or filling of land.

**B.12** We do not cover an other structure rented or held for rental to a person who is not a "tenant" of the dwelling. This exclusion applies whether the rental arrangement is written or oral.

**B.13** A tenant is a person who occupies the dwelling under a rental arrangement with you. Occupancy alone does not make a person a tenant.

**B.14** We do not cover an other structure rented or held for rental when the structure is used for a purpose other than a "private garage." A private garage is a structure used solely for the shelter or storage of vehicles.

**B.15** We cover an otherwise eligible private garage rented or held for rental only while it is used solely as a private garage. We do not cover a private garage used for any other purpose.

**B.16** We do not cover an other structure from which any "business" is conducted. This exclusion applies when the business is conducted by you, a tenant, or another person.

**B.17** We do not cover an other structure used in whole or in part for business activity. The presence of business equipment or business operations may establish business use.

**B.18** We do not cover an other structure used to store "business property." This exclusion applies whether the business property belongs to you or another person.

**B.19** We cover an otherwise eligible other structure containing business property solely owned by an insured or a tenant of the dwelling. The structure must contain no gaseous or liquid fuel, except as provided in this coverage.

**B.20** We cover fuel contained in a permanently installed fuel tank of a vehicle or craft parked or stored in an otherwise eligible other structure. We do not cover any other gaseous or liquid fuel kept in the structure.

**B.21** An insured must establish the ownership, location, and use of an other structure for which coverage is claimed. An insured must provide records or other information reasonably requested by us to establish that information.

**B.22** An insured must protect an other structure from further damage after a loss. Reasonable measures taken to protect the other structure do not expand the coverage provided by this section.

**B.23** We do not cover loss caused by a use of an other structure that makes the structure ineligible for coverage under this section. The exclusion applies while the ineligible use exists.

**B.24** Coverage for an other structure applies only while the structure meets the requirements of this section. No act or agreement concerning an other structure expands the coverage provided by us.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" while the property is anywhere in the world. Covered property includes property customarily kept at your "residence premises" and property temporarily away from the residence premises.

**C.2** At your request, we cover personal property owned by others while the property is on the residence premises occupied by an insured. We also cover personal property of a guest or residence employee while the property is at any residence occupied by an insured.

**C.3** Covered personal property must be subject to a direct physical loss caused by a peril insured against under this policy. We pay only for the part of the loss that is covered under this policy.

**C.4** We determine the value of covered property at the time of loss. We may consider the age, condition, quality, usefulness, and depreciation of the property.

**C.5** We may repair, replace, or pay for covered property at our option. We may require you to surrender damaged property to us when we pay for its full value.

**C.6** We do not cover property separately described and specifically insured in this or any other insurance. We do cover the excess over the amount recoverable from other insurance when this policy provides coverage for the loss.

**C.7** We do not cover property of roomers, boarders, or other tenants. We do cover property of a guest or residence employee as provided in this Coverage C.

**C.8** We do not cover property held as samples or for sale, delivery, or demonstration. This limitation does not apply to property used by an insured for personal purposes.

**C.9** We do not cover articles separately insured by a warranty, service agreement, or maintenance agreement for the loss involved. We do not reduce coverage solely because another person may be responsible for the loss.

**C.10** We do not cover property that is part of a building or structure, including materials and supplies intended for construction, alteration, or repair. We cover such property only when it qualifies as personal property and is not permanently attached.

**C.11** We do not cover animals, birds, fish, or other living creatures. We do cover property used to care for or maintain a covered animal when the property itself sustains a covered loss.

**C.12** We do not cover accounts, bills, currency, deeds, evidences of debt, manuscripts, securities, or similar valuable papers except as provided by the applicable special limit. We cover the physical medium of a record when the medium itself sustains covered direct physical loss.

**C.13** We do not cover electronic data solely because it is lost, corrupted, inaccessible, or unusable. We cover electronic data when loss to the data results directly from covered loss to the device or medium on which the data is stored.

**C.14** We do not cover property used primarily for business unless coverage is provided by this Coverage C. "Business property" means property used for trade, occupation, profession, or other activity conducted for economic gain.

**C.15** We do not cover property of an insured that is rented or held for rental to others. We cover property customarily used by an insured in connection with the maintenance of the residence premises.

**C.16** We do not cover motor vehicles or their equipment, except for equipment not required for the vehicle's operation. We cover a motor vehicle designed to assist the handicapped when the vehicle is not subject to motor vehicle registration.

**C.17** We do not cover aircraft and their parts. We cover model or hobby aircraft that are not designed to carry persons or cargo.

**C.18** We do not cover watercraft, including furnishings, equipment, and trailers, except as provided by the applicable special limit. "Watercraft" includes any vessel designed for travel or transportation on water.

**C.19** We do not cover property after it has been abandoned by you. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss.

**C.20** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not voluntarily dispose of damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**C.21** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, estimates, and other information reasonably requested by us. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim.

**C.22** The special limits stated in this Coverage C apply to covered property without regard to the number of items involved. The applicable special limit is the most we will pay for the covered property described in that provision.

**C.23** The most we will pay for loss to money, bank notes, bullion, gold other than goldware, silver other than silverware, platinum, coins, medals, scrip, stored value cards, and smart cards is two hundred dollars. This limit applies whether the loss is caused by theft or any other covered cause of loss.

**C.24** The most we will pay for loss by theft of jewelry, watches, and precious or semiprecious stones is one thousand five hundred dollars. This limit includes articles containing jewelry, watches, or precious or semiprecious stones.

**C.25** The most we will pay for loss by theft of firearms and related equipment is two thousand dollars. This limit applies to firearms whether they are owned for sporting, collecting, display, or personal protection purposes.

**C.26** The most we will pay for loss to watercraft, including trailers, furnishings, equipment, and outboard engines or motors, is one thousand dollars. This limit applies to watercraft whether on the residence premises or away from the residence premises.

**C.27** The most we will pay for loss by theft of silverware, silver-plated ware, goldware, gold-plated ware, and pewterware is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit includes flatware, hollowware, tea sets, trays, and trophies made of those materials.

**C.28** The most we will pay for loss to business property while on the residence premises is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies to property owned by an insured and property in the care, custody, or control of an insured.

**C.29** The most we will pay for loss to electronic apparatus while in or upon a motor vehicle is one thousand dollars. This limit applies when the apparatus is capable of being operated from the electrical system of the motor vehicle.

**C.30** The special limit for electronic apparatus applies to accessories and antennas used only with that apparatus. It also applies to tapes, wires, records, discs, and other media used with the apparatus.

**C.31** We do not cover loss by theft of property from a part of a residence premises rented to others unless an insured lives at the residence premises. We cover property stolen from a part of the residence premises occupied by an insured.

**C.32** We do not cover loss by theft committed by an insured. We do not cover loss by theft committed by a person regularly residing at the residence premises unless that person is legally entitled to possess the property.

**C.33** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts of an insured when the loss is expected or intended by that insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss differs in kind or degree from that expected or intended.

**C.34** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not relieve us of our obligation to pay for direct physical loss otherwise covered under this policy.

**C.35** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or smog. We do cover ensuing loss caused by a peril insured against when the ensuing loss is otherwise covered.

**C.36** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or contamination. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a peril insured against that results from those conditions when the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**C.37** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do cover direct physical loss to personal property when the loss is caused by a covered peril.

**C.38** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals owned or kept by an insured. We do cover loss caused by a covered peril that results from such damage when the resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**C.39** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. We do cover ensuing loss caused by a peril insured against when the ensuing loss is otherwise covered.

**C.40** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the residence premises. We cover loss resulting from a power failure when the failure results from direct physical damage by a covered peril to property on the residence premises.

**C.41** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants unless the loss is caused by a peril insured against. "Pollutants" include irritants, contaminants, smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

**C.42** We do not cover loss caused by war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. We do not cover loss caused by seizure or destruction of property by order of governmental authority.

**C.43** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies whether the loss is direct, indirect, immediate, delayed, or consequential.

**C.44** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law requiring the testing, monitoring, cleanup, removal, containment, treatment, detoxification, neutralization, or disposal of pollutants. We do cover direct physical loss to covered property when otherwise covered under this policy.

**C.45** We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain heat in a building containing covered property when the failure results in freezing, thawing, pressure, or overflow. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**C.46** An insured must give prompt notice to us or our agent after a loss. Notice must include information reasonably sufficient to identify the insured, the damaged property, the location of the loss, and the circumstances of the loss.

**C.47** An insured must notify the police when a loss involves theft, vandalism, malicious mischief, or other criminal conduct. An insured must provide us with information concerning any investigation by public authorities when reasonably requested.

**C.48** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged or stolen personal property. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item when that information is reasonably available.

**C.49** An insured must provide records and documents that support the inventory and amount of loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of records and documents reasonably related to the claim.

**C.50** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably requested by us. An insured must sign the transcript of the examination when it has been accurately prepared.

**C.51** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as reasonably required. An insured must permit us to inspect the residence premises where the loss occurred when reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim.

**C.52** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We pay reasonable expenses incurred for necessary temporary measures taken solely to protect covered property from further covered damage.

**C.53** We do not pay for permanent repairs, improvements, or alterations made before we inspect the damaged property unless immediate action is necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must keep records of temporary measures and related expenses.

**C.54** Payment for covered personal property does not transfer ownership of the property to us unless we agree otherwise. If we pay for lost or stolen property and the property is later recovered, you must notify us promptly.

**C.55** We may require you to return recovered property to us or repay the amount we paid for the recovered property. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred to recover covered property when those expenses are approved by us.

**C.56** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for covered loss under this Coverage C. Special limits apply within, and do not increase, the applicable limit of liability.

**C.57** If more than one coverage provision applies to the same property and loss, we will not pay more than the amount of the actual covered loss. Coverage applies only to the extent permitted by the terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions of this policy.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** "Coverage D" applies to covered "loss of use" arising from a loss covered under this policy. Coverage is subject to the limit of liability for this coverage.

**D.2** The limit of liability for "Coverage D" is fifty percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". This limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss under this coverage.

**D.3** We cover necessary increases in living expenses incurred by you when a covered loss makes the part of the "residence premises" used by you as a principal residence unfit to live in. The increased expenses must be necessary to maintain your household's normal standard of living.

**D.4** We cover only expenses that result directly from the covered loss. We do not cover expenses that you would have incurred in the absence of the covered loss.

**D.5** An insured must incur the expense before we will pay for it. An insured must provide records that support the expense and its necessity.

**D.6** We may require receipts, invoices, canceled checks, or other evidence of payment. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable request for information concerning a claimed expense.

**D.7** We will pay only for the portion of an expense that exceeds your normal living expense. We do not cover expenses that are not reasonably necessary for your household.

**D.8** We will pay covered increased living expenses while the covered loss makes the applicable part of the residence premises unfit to live in. Payment is subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**D.9** You may select reasonable accommodations and services needed because of the covered loss. We do not cover charges that are excessive when compared with reasonably available accommodations or services.

**D.10** We may consider the condition of the residence premises, the nature of the covered loss, and the living arrangements reasonably available to you. We may also consider expenses customarily incurred by your household before the loss.

**D.11** We cover loss of fair rental value when a covered loss makes the part of the residence premises rented to others unfit to live in. The rented part must have been held for rental at the time of loss.

**D.12** "Fair rental value" means the value of the rented part of the residence premises less expenses that do not continue while that part is unfit to live in. We will determine fair rental value from available rental information and supporting records.

**D.13** We will pay fair rental value only for loss that results directly from the covered loss. We do not cover loss of rental value caused by a condition that is not the result of a covered loss.

## I.S — Section I — Conditions

**S.1** We will adjust a covered loss with you or with a person authorized to act for you. An "insured" must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

**S.2** You must give us prompt notice of a "loss". Notice must identify the policy involved and provide information reasonably available to you.

**S.3** You must notify us of any occurrence that may result in a claim under this policy. An insured must provide notice even if the full extent of damage is not known.

**S.4** You must protect "covered property" from further damage after a loss. You must make reasonable and necessary repairs solely to protect the property from further damage.

**S.5** You must keep an accurate record of repair expenses incurred to protect covered property. You must retain receipts, invoices, estimates, and other available records supporting those expenses.

**S.6** You must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. You must not discard damaged property unless its retention is unsafe or prohibited by law.

**S.7** We may inspect damaged property as often as reasonably necessary. An insured must permit us reasonable access to the property and to relevant records.

**S.8** You must show us the damaged property when requested. You must identify property that was damaged, destroyed, lost, or stolen.

**S.9** You must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when requested. The inventory must describe the property and state its value, age, condition, and amount of loss.

**S.10** You must provide available documents supporting ownership, value, and condition of damaged property. Such documents may include receipts, photographs, appraisals, warranties, and records of purchase.

**S.11** You must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer questions concerning the claim truthfully and provide reasonably requested documents.

**S.12** The deductible applicable to a covered loss shall not be less than five hundred dollars. We will apply the deductible shown in the policy or otherwise required by applicable law.

**S.13** We will deduct the applicable deductible from the amount otherwise payable for a covered loss. A deductible does not reduce a limit of liability unless this policy states otherwise.

**S.14** The hurricane deductible cap applies when required by applicable law. We will administer a hurricane deductible in accordance with the policy and applicable law.

**S.15** Advance notice of deductible change will be provided when required by applicable law. A change in deductible will apply only as permitted by the policy and applicable law.

**S.16** You must provide records showing the amount and source of any payment received for the same loss. We may consider such payment in determining the amount payable under this policy.

**S.17** You must promptly notify us if you recover property for which we have made payment. We may require return of the property or repayment of the amount paid for it.

**S.18** You must notify us if damaged property is repaired, replaced, sold, or otherwise disposed of. You must preserve records of the transaction and provide them to us upon request.

**S.19** You must make reasonable efforts to prevent theft or further loss after a claim. You must notify appropriate authorities when property is stolen or when criminal conduct is reasonably suspected.

**S.20** You must provide us with information concerning any person who may be responsible for a loss. You must not impair our right to recover from that person.

**S.21** We may require you to assign to us your rights of recovery against a person responsible for a loss. An insured must execute documents reasonably necessary to preserve those rights.

**S.22** If we make payment for a loss, we are subrogated to your rights of recovery to the extent of our payment. You must not waive those rights after a loss without our written consent.

**S.23** Our subrogation rights do not reduce your right to recover an uninsured loss. We will not recover more than the amount we paid unless permitted by applicable law.

**S.24** You must notify us promptly of any demand, notice, summons, complaint, or other legal paper relating to a claim. You must send us copies of such material as soon as reasonably possible.

**S.25** We may investigate, negotiate, settle, or defend any claim as we consider appropriate. We are not obligated to provide a defense for a claim not covered by this policy.

**S.26** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except at the insured's own cost. This condition does not prevent reasonable emergency measures taken to protect covered property from further damage.

**S.27** You must assist us in obtaining statements, records, and other evidence relevant to a claim. You must attend meetings, hearings, examinations, and proceedings when reasonably requested.

**S.28** You must provide us with the names and contact information of persons having knowledge relevant to a claim when reasonably available. You must identify persons who may have inspected, repaired, maintained, or valued damaged property.

**S.29** You must authorize release of records reasonably necessary for us to investigate a claim. Such records may include records concerning ownership, repair, maintenance, value, or condition of property.

**S.30** An insured must provide a signed, sworn proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must be provided within sixty days after our request.

**S.31** The proof of loss must state the time and cause of loss to the extent known. It must identify all persons having an interest in the property involved.

**S.32** The proof of loss must state the actual cash value and amount of loss for each item claimed. It must include supporting information reasonably available to you.

**S.33** The proof of loss must state all encumbrances and other claims against the property involved. You must identify any person or organization having a financial interest in that property.

**S.34** The proof of loss must state any changes in title, use, occupancy, or possession that are relevant to the claim. You must disclose any other insurance that may apply to the loss.

**S.35** We may require additional information after receiving a proof of loss. An insured must supplement the proof of loss if material information becomes available.

**S.36** We may require you to provide records in electronic, paper, or other reasonably usable form. You must preserve originals when reasonably possible until we complete our investigation.

**S.37** We may examine books, records, receipts, correspondence, and other documents relating to the claim. An insured must make such material available for inspection and copying.

**S.38** You must permit us to inspect repairs or replacement property related to the claim. You must notify us before completing repairs when inspection is reasonably necessary.

**S.39** We may request estimates, bids, invoices, or other information concerning repair or replacement. You must provide information reasonably available to you concerning the scope and cost of work.

**S.40** You must notify us if a governmental authority requires repair, removal, demolition, or reconstruction because of the loss. You must provide any order, notice, or permit relating to that requirement.

**S.109** You must provide a forwarding address if you leave the insured location after a loss. You must make reasonable arrangements to receive communications concerning the claim.

**S.113** An insured must preserve all rights and remedies available under applicable law that may benefit us after a loss. You must execute reasonable documents necessary to protect those rights.

## II.E — Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** We will pay "damages" for which an "insured" is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence." The bodily injury or property damage must arise from conduct for which this coverage applies.

**E.2** “Bodily injury” means bodily harm, sickness, disease, or death. “Property damage” means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use, or loss of use of tangible property that is not physically injured.

**E.3** “Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially similar harmful conditions. “Damages” means compensatory sums that an insured is legally obligated to pay.

**E.4** We will provide a defense at our expense against a "suit" seeking damages covered by this insurance. We may investigate any "claim" and settle any claim or suit when we consider settlement appropriate.

**E.5** “Claim” means a demand for damages. “Suit” means a civil proceeding alleging damages because of bodily injury or property damage.

**E.6** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury resulting from the use of reasonable force to protect persons or property.

**E.7** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of or in connection with a "business" conducted by an insured. “Business” means a trade, profession, occupation, or activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain.

**E.8** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of business pursuits of an insured. This exclusion does not apply to activities ordinarily incident to nonbusiness pursuits.

**E.9** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of premises owned, rented, or controlled by an insured that are not an "insured location." “Insured location” means premises shown in the Declarations as the residence premises and premises used by an insured in connection with that residence.

**E.10** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle" by an insured. “Motor vehicle” means a land vehicle designed for travel on public roads and subject to motor vehicle registration.

**E.11** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle owned by or available for the regular use of an insured. This exclusion applies whether the motor vehicle is operated by an insured or by any other person.

**E.12** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "watercraft" by an insured. “Watercraft” means a craft designed for travel on water.

**E.13** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an "aircraft" by an insured. “Aircraft” means a device designed for flight or navigation in the air.

**E.14** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising from the entrustment of a motor vehicle, watercraft, or aircraft to any person by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the entrustment is alleged to be negligent or otherwise wrongful.

**E.15** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care, custody, or control of an insured.

**E.16** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage for which an insured is obligated to pay damages because of the assumption of liability in a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**E.17** We do not cover bodily injury to any person eligible to receive benefits required by workers compensation, disability benefits, occupational disease benefits, or similar law. We do not cover any obligation imposed upon an insured under such law.

**E.18** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurpation of power, or action taken by governmental authority to prevent or defend against such events. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing to the loss.

**E.19** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the actual, alleged, or threatened release, discharge, seepage, migration, dispersal, or escape of pollutants. “Pollutants” means irritants or contaminants, including smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

**E.20** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the transmission of a "communicable disease" by an insured. “Communicable disease” means a disease capable of being transmitted from a person, animal, substance, or object to another person.

**E.21** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of a controlled substance by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of a controlled substance under the direction of a licensed medical provider.

**E.22** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of abuse, molestation, harassment, or other sexual misconduct by an insured. This exclusion applies whether liability is asserted directly or indirectly against an insured.

**E.23** We do not cover punitive, exemplary, multiplied, or penal damages. We do not cover fines, penalties, or assessments imposed upon an insured by a governmental authority.

**E.24** An insured must give us prompt notice of an occurrence that may result in a claim. The notice must state the identity of the insured, the time and place of the occurrence, and the names of known injured persons and witnesses.

**E.25** An insured must promptly send us every notice, demand, summons, or legal paper relating to a claim or suit. An insured must not allow a default judgment to be entered without promptly notifying us.

**E.26** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of a claim or suit. An insured must assist us in securing evidence, obtaining witnesses, and enforcing any right of recovery.

**E.27** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses to protect persons or property from further injury or damage. We will not be bound by a voluntary payment, obligation, or expense made without our consent.

**E.28** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide records, documents, authorizations, and other information reasonably requested by us.

**E.29** This insurance applies separately to each insured against whom a claim is made or suit is brought. This provision does not increase the amount available for any occurrence.

**E.30** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this coverage. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not give any person a right of action against us except as provided by law.

**E.31** If other insurance applies to a loss, an insured must notify us of that insurance. We will pay our share of covered damages according to our rights and obligations under applicable law.

**E.32** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent any material fact relating to a claim or suit. We may deny coverage for a claim affected by fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation by an insured.

**E.33** An insured must transfer to us any right of recovery against another person responsible for damages we pay. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

## II.F — Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

**F.1** We will pay the necessary "medical expenses" incurred by a person because of "bodily injury" caused by an "occurrence." The bodily injury must be sustained by that person while this coverage applies.

**F.2** Medical expenses must be incurred or medically ascertained within three years years from the date of the accident causing bodily injury. The expenses must be necessary and reasonable.

**F.3** Medical expenses include reasonable charges for medical, surgical, dental, ambulance, hospital, professional nursing, prosthetic, and funeral services. Medical expenses also include reasonable charges for pharmaceuticals, eyeglasses, contact lenses, hearing aids, and the repair or replacement of damaged eyeglasses, contact lenses, or hearing aids.

**F.4** We will pay medical expenses without regard to fault. Payment under this coverage does not constitute an admission of liability by us or by an insured.

**F.5** This coverage applies to a person who is on an "insured location" with the permission of an "insured." The person must not be an insured.

**F.6** This coverage also applies to a person who is away from an insured location when the bodily injury arises out of a condition on an insured location. The condition may include a way immediately adjoining an insured location.

**F.7** This coverage also applies to a person who is away from an insured location when the bodily injury is caused by the activities of an insured. The activities must not be excluded by this policy.

**F.8** This coverage also applies to a person who is away from an insured location when the bodily injury is caused by a "residence employee." The residence employee must be acting within the course of employment by an insured.

**F.9** This coverage also applies to a person who is away from an insured location when the bodily injury is caused by an animal owned by, or in the care of, an insured. The animal need not be on an insured location when the bodily injury occurs.

**F.10** An insured must give us prompt notice of an accident or occurrence that may result in a claim under this coverage. The notice must include information sufficient to identify the injured person and the circumstances of the accident or occurrence.

**F.11** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide records, authorizations, and information that we reasonably request concerning the claimed medical expenses.

**F.12** The injured person, or a person acting for the injured person, must submit to physical examinations by physicians selected by us when we reasonably require. We will pay the reasonable cost of those examinations.

**F.13** The injured person, or a person acting for the injured person, must provide us with authorization to obtain medical records and other information relating to the bodily injury. The authorization must permit us to evaluate the necessity and reasonableness of the claimed medical expenses.

**F.14** We may require proof that medical expenses have been incurred or medically ascertained. We may also require proof that the expenses result from the bodily injury caused by the occurrence.

**F.15** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to an insured. This exclusion applies whether the insured is named in the policy or otherwise qualifies as an insured.

**F.16** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to a person who regularly resides on an insured location. This exclusion does not apply to a residence employee.

**F.17** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to a person eligible to receive benefits voluntarily provided or required to be provided by an insured under workers compensation law, occupational disease law, or a similar law. This exclusion applies whether or not the benefits are actually claimed.

**F.18** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury resulting from war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, and warlike action by a military force.

**F.19** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle." This exclusion applies whether the motor vehicle is owned, operated, rented, borrowed, or entrusted by an insured.

**F.20** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "watercraft." This exclusion applies unless the watercraft is of a type for which coverage is provided by this policy.

**F.21** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an "aircraft." This exclusion applies whether the aircraft is owned, operated, rented, borrowed, or entrusted by an insured.

**F.22** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of a "business" conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies to acts or omissions connected with the business.

**F.23** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the rendering or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies whether the services are performed by an insured or by another person for whom an insured may be liable.

**F.24** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies only when the insured knew or should have known of the communicable disease.

**F.25** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of sexual molestation, corporal punishment, or physical or mental abuse. This exclusion applies whether the conduct is alleged to have been intentional or negligent.

**F.26** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury resulting from the use of reasonable force by an insured to protect persons or property.

**F.27** Payment under this coverage is limited to medical expenses that are covered under these provisions. No person may recover more than the actual covered medical expenses incurred or medically ascertained.
