# HO-6 Homeowners 6 — Unit-Owners Form (Edition 2014-04)
> SUPERSEDED by HO-6 Homeowners 6 — Unit-Owners Form (Edition 2023-02) for policies effective on or after 2023-02-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

We provide the insurance described in this "policy" in return for the "premium" and your compliance with all applicable policy provisions. This policy consists of the terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions stated in it.

"You" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the policy declarations and the spouse of that person if a resident of the same household. "We," "us," and "our" refer to the insurer providing this policy.

Coverage applies only to an "insured" who meets the requirements of this policy. An insured must comply with all duties imposed by this policy before we have an obligation to provide coverage.

We insure against direct physical "loss" to covered property when the loss is caused by a covered peril. Coverage is subject to all applicable exclusions, limitations, deductibles, and conditions.

We provide coverage only for property that qualifies as "covered property" under this policy. The existence of property at the residence premises does not alone establish that the property is covered.

A "claim" must arise from an accidental loss that occurs during the policy period. We determine coverage based on the facts, circumstances, and policy provisions applicable to the claim.

An insured must give us truthful and complete information in connection with an application, renewal, claim, or other request for coverage. We may rely upon information provided by an insured in administering this policy.

An insured must protect covered property from further damage following a loss. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to take reasonable steps to protect covered property when those steps can be taken safely.

We may inspect the residence premises and covered property at a reasonable time. Our inspection does not constitute a representation that property is safe, complies with applicable requirements, or is insurable.

We may investigate a claim and request information reasonably necessary to determine coverage. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

We may pay for covered loss, repair covered property, replace covered property, or settle a claim as permitted by this policy. Our exercise of a policy right does not waive any other policy right.

We do not cover any loss excluded by this policy. An exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event that contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss when the policy so provides.

This policy is a contract between you and us. Its terms may be changed only as permitted by this policy and applicable law.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of covered property at the time of loss after adjustment for depreciation, age, condition, and obsolescence.  
It includes the value of a used appliance after its wear and condition are considered.  
It does not include the cost of a new appliance without adjustment for depreciation.  
It does not include sentimental value or personal attachment to the property.  
We determine this value from available information concerning the property and the loss.  

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with material of like kind and quality without deduction for depreciation.  
It includes the reasonable cost to replace a damaged cabinet with a comparable new cabinet.  
It does not include any amount attributable to betterment, enlargement, or improvement.  
It does not include the value of land.  
We determine this cost from the prices reasonably available at the time of repair or replacement.  

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.  
It includes an accidental fire that damages property or causes injury.  
It does not include an event caused deliberately by an insured.  
All injury or damage arising from substantially the same general harmful conditions is treated as a single occurrence.  
The accident must result in bodily injury or property damage.  

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the unit you own and use as your residence, together with structures and grounds used in connection with that unit.  
It includes a garage or storage area reserved for your exclusive use.  
It does not include a common area available for the use of other unit owners.  
It does not include premises used solely for business purposes.  
The unit must be used by you as a residence.  

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are under your care.  
It includes your spouse if your spouse resides in your household.  
It includes a person under your care who resides in your household.  
It does not include a visitor who does not reside in your household.  
It does not include a tenant who occupies part of the residence premises under a rental agreement.  

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and premises used by you in connection with the residence premises.  
It includes a parking area assigned for your exclusive use.  
It includes premises where you temporarily reside while the residence premises are being repaired.  
It does not include premises owned by you and used solely for business purposes.  
It does not include premises occupied by a person who is not an insured.  

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain.  
It includes an enterprise conducted from the residence premises for compensation.  
It includes services performed for compensation on a regular basis.  
It does not include an occasional activity performed without an intent to earn a profit.  
It does not include a personal hobby that is not conducted for economic gain.  

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration.  
It includes an automobile, motorcycle, or similar vehicle propelled by a motor.  
It includes a vehicle rented or borrowed by an insured.  
It does not include a mobility device designed solely to assist a person with a physical disability.  
It does not include a vehicle designed solely for use on rails.  

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises, including household or personal services.  
It includes a domestic worker who performs cleaning or cooking services for you.  
It includes a person who performs gardening or maintenance services under your direction.  
It does not include an independent contractor who controls the manner of performing the work.  
It does not include a person employed solely in connection with your business.  

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death.  
It includes physical injury caused by a fall on the residence premises.  
It includes sickness resulting from exposure to harmful conditions.  
It does not include emotional distress that does not result from bodily harm.  
It does not include an economic loss that is not caused by bodily harm, sickness, or disease.  

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.  
It includes damage to furniture caused by an accidental fire.  
It includes loss of use of tangible property resulting from physical injury to that property.  
It does not include loss caused solely by a decline in market value.  
It does not include injury to property that is not tangible.  

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unforeseen.  
It includes water damage caused when a pipe unexpectedly bursts.  
It includes damage caused by an unintended discharge of water.  
It does not include damage caused by gradual leakage, seepage, corrosion, or deterioration.  
The event or result must be both abrupt and unintended.  

**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of personal property and customary activities.  
It includes a unit that contains no furnishings and is not being used as a residence.  
It does not include a furnished unit temporarily unoccupied while you are away.  
It does not include a unit containing property that shows continued residential use.  
A unit may be vacant even though it remains available for occupancy.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We insure the part of the "dwelling" that is described in the Declarations as Coverage A. Coverage A applies to property used principally as a private residence.

**A.2** The most we will pay for direct physical loss to the dwelling is five thousand dollars. Subject to the terms of this policy, that limit applies to the covered property at the time of loss. The limit does not increase because the cost of repair or replacement exceeds the limit.

**A.3** We determine the applicable limit for a unit owner by applying the Coverage A limit shown in the Declarations. The limit is intended to apply to the portion of the dwelling that you own or are responsible to insure. The limit does not insure property that belongs solely to the condominium association.

**A.4** We use the "insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement" of eighty percent when determining whether replacement cost settlement applies. Except that, the threshold applies only when the damaged property is repaired or replaced as required by this policy. The threshold does not change the amount of insurance provided.

**A.5** We insure additions, alterations, fixtures, improvements and betterments within the residence premises when you own them or are required to insure them. Subject to the terms of this policy, these items are treated as part of the dwelling. Coverage does not extend to property that you do not own or have a responsibility to insure.

**A.6** We insure permanently installed fixtures within the dwelling. Fixtures include items attached to real property and intended to remain with the dwelling. We do not cover a fixture that is excluded under this policy.

**A.7** We insure wall, floor and ceiling coverings that you own or are required to insure. The coverings must be located within the dwelling. We do not cover coverings that are removable personal property unless coverage is otherwise provided.

**A.8** We insure built-in appliances and equipment that are attached to the dwelling. This includes equipment used to service the dwelling or its fixtures. We do not cover equipment that is used solely for a business or commercial purpose.

**A.9** We insure plumbing, heating, air-conditioning and electrical systems that form part of the dwelling. The systems must be located at the residence premises. We do not cover a system or component that is excluded by a peril exclusion or other limitation of this policy.

**A.10** We insure structures that are connected to the dwelling and used with it. The structures must be owned by you or be your responsibility under an agreement. We do not cover a structure used principally for a purpose unrelated to the residence premises.

**A.11** We insure materials and supplies located at the residence premises that are intended for repair or alteration of the dwelling. The materials and supplies must be owned by you or held for your benefit. We do not cover materials and supplies used in a business or held for sale.

**A.12** We insure property belonging to you that is installed in the dwelling as part of a permanent improvement. Subject to the terms of this policy, the property remains covered when it is temporarily removed for repair or replacement. We do not cover property removed permanently from the residence premises.

**A.13** We insure property that you acquire to replace covered property when the replacement is intended for the dwelling. The replacement property must be located at the residence premises or at a place where it is being prepared for installation. We do not cover the replacement property after it is used for a purpose other than the dwelling.

**A.14** We insure covered property against direct physical loss caused by a covered peril. The loss must occur during the policy period and at the residence premises. We do not cover loss that is not caused by a covered peril.

**A.15** We do not cover land, including land on which the dwelling is located. This exclusion applies whether the land is owned by you or held with others. It does not remove coverage for covered property attached to or installed in the dwelling.

**A.16** We do not cover water or steam that is not part of a covered system or appliance. This exclusion applies to water or steam outside the dwelling as well as inside it. It does not remove coverage for ensuing loss when coverage is otherwise provided.

**A.17** We do not cover the cost to correct faulty, inadequate or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction or maintenance. This exclusion applies to the defective condition itself. It does not remove coverage for ensuing loss when coverage is otherwise provided.

**A.18** We do not cover settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs or ceilings. This exclusion applies to loss caused by movement of the dwelling or its components. It does not remove coverage for ensuing loss when coverage is otherwise provided.

**A.19** We do not cover wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown or rust. This exclusion applies to the condition or defect that causes the loss. It does not remove coverage for ensuing loss when coverage is otherwise provided.

**A.20** We do not cover damage caused by insects, birds, rodents or other domestic or wild animals. This exclusion applies to damage caused by nesting, infestation, gnawing, chewing or waste. It does not remove coverage for ensuing loss when coverage is otherwise provided.

**A.21** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents or insects to insulation, wiring, plumbing or other components of the dwelling. This exclusion applies whether the animals are wild, domesticated or kept by you. It does not remove coverage for a covered loss that results from the animal damage.

**A.22** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents or insects when the loss results from neglect of the dwelling. This exclusion applies to damage that develops gradually or is not promptly repaired. It does not remove coverage for a sudden and accidental covered loss.

**A.23** We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, remodeling, renovation or demolition of the dwelling. This exclusion applies to the additional cost required solely because of the ordinance or law. It does not remove coverage for the covered portion of the physical loss.

**A.24** We do not cover the cost to excavate, test, monitor, contain, remove, abate or dispose of pollutants. This exclusion applies to pollutants located in, on or around the dwelling. It does not remove coverage for ensuing loss when coverage is otherwise provided.

**A.25** We do not cover loss caused by contamination, deterioration, corrosion, decay or pollution. This exclusion applies when the condition results from a gradual process. It does not remove coverage for ensuing loss when coverage is otherwise provided.

**A.26** We do not cover loss caused by power failure when the failure originates away from the residence premises. This exclusion applies even if the failure causes damage to covered property. It does not remove coverage for ensuing loss when coverage is otherwise provided.

**A.27** We do not cover loss caused by freezing when you fail to maintain heat in the dwelling or fail to shut off the water supply and drain the systems. This exclusion applies while the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied. It does not apply when you arrange for reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off and drain the systems.

**A.28** You must use reasonable means to protect the dwelling from further damage after a loss. You must keep a record of expenses incurred for necessary repairs. You must not make permanent repairs before we inspect the damaged property, except that you may make temporary repairs necessary to protect the dwelling.

**A.29** You must provide us with reasonable access to the dwelling and the damaged property. You must cooperate with our investigation, adjustment and settlement of the loss. You must preserve damaged property for our inspection unless preservation would increase the damage.

**A.30** You must promptly notify us of any change in ownership, occupancy or use of the dwelling. You must promptly notify us when the dwelling becomes vacant or unoccupied. You must provide information that we reasonably request concerning the change.

**A.31** You must comply with reasonable requests for estimates, specifications, invoices and other records concerning the repair or replacement of the dwelling. You must identify the property damaged and the cause of the loss to the best of your knowledge. You must permit us to take samples or make inspections when reasonably necessary to determine the cause or amount of loss.

**A.32** We settle covered loss to the dwelling according to the applicable valuation provision of this policy. Subject to the terms of this policy, we may pay the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We will not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace the damaged property.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover other structures on the "residence premises" that are set apart from the dwelling by clear space. A structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection is also covered. We cover such structures only when they are used for a purpose related to the residential use of the residence premises.

**B.2** We cover a structure that is not attached to the dwelling when the structure is located on the residence premises. The structure must be used by you or by members of your household. We do not cover a structure used for a purpose that is not related to the residential use of the residence premises.

**B.3** We cover structures that are customarily used with the dwelling. This includes a detached garage, storage building, or similar structure. We cover the structure only while it remains at the residence premises.

**B.4** We cover permanently installed fixtures attached to an other structure. We cover such fixtures as part of the structure to which they are attached. We do not cover personal property merely stored in or near the structure under this Coverage.

**B.5** We cover a structure that is under construction at the residence premises. The structure must be intended for use as an other structure when construction is completed. We do not cover land, excavation, or the cost of preparing land for construction.

**B.6** We cover materials and supplies intended for construction, alteration, or repair of an other structure. The materials and supplies must be located at the residence premises or at a location next to the residence premises. We cover them only while they are intended for use in the construction, alteration, or repair.

**B.7** We cover an other structure that is temporarily removed from the residence premises for repair or alteration. The structure must be intended to be returned to the residence premises. We do not cover the structure after it is permanently located at another premises.

**B.8** We cover an other structure occupied by you as a private garage. The garage may be used to store household property and vehicles used for personal purposes. We do not cover a garage used for a business purpose.

**B.9** We cover an other structure used for household storage. Household property stored in the structure is not covered under this Coverage solely because the property is located there. We cover the structure only if its principal use remains related to the residence premises.

**B.10** We cover an other structure used for recreation by you or members of your household. The structure must remain under your control and be used in connection with the residence premises. We do not cover a structure used primarily for commercial recreation.

**B.11** We cover an other structure rented to a person who rents the dwelling under a rental agreement. The structure must be used in connection with the person's residential occupancy. We do not cover a structure rented to any other person unless the rental is incidental to the residential use of the residence premises.

**B.12** We do not cover an other structure rented or held for rental to others when it is not used as a private garage. This exclusion applies whether the rental is written or oral. It does not apply while the structure is occupied by you or by a member of your household for residential purposes.

**B.13** We do not cover an other structure used in whole or in part for a business. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted by you or by another person. It does not apply to a structure used solely to store business property when the storage is incidental to the residential use of the residence premises.

**B.14** We do not cover an other structure used to conduct a business that is owned or operated by you. This exclusion applies to a business conducted by you, a member of your household, or any person acting on your behalf. It does not apply to customary incidental activities that do not change the principal residential use of the structure.

**B.15** We do not cover an other structure used to store business property. This exclusion applies to property owned by you or by another person. It does not apply when the property is stored only temporarily and the storage is incidental to the residential use of the structure.

**B.16** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We do not cover lawns, plants, trees, or other growing property under this Coverage. We do not cover the cost of stabilizing, excavating, filling, grading, or improving land.

**B.17** We do not cover a structure used in connection with farming or agricultural operations. This exclusion applies whether the operation is conducted by you or by another person. It does not apply to customary household gardening that is not conducted for profit.

**B.18** We do not cover a structure used for the sale, manufacture, storage, or distribution of goods. This exclusion applies whether the activity is conducted regularly or occasionally. It does not apply to household property temporarily stored for personal use.

**B.19** We do not cover a structure used to house animals for commercial purposes. This exclusion applies to a structure used for breeding, boarding, training, or selling animals. It does not apply to household pets kept for personal use.

**B.20** We do not cover a structure used to house livestock, poultry, or other animals raised for sale. This exclusion applies to the structure and to fixtures installed for that use. It does not apply to animals kept solely as household pets.

**B.21** We do not cover an other structure that is not maintained in a reasonably sound condition. You must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage when a structure is damaged. We may not cover loss that results from your failure to take those steps.

**B.22** You must protect an other structure from further damage after a loss. You must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs when those repairs can be made safely. You must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and preserve damaged property for our inspection.

**B.23** You must promptly notify us of loss to an other structure. You must identify the structure and describe the property damaged. You must provide information and documents that we reasonably require to investigate the loss.

**B.24** You must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim involving an other structure. You must provide access to the structure at reasonable times. You must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense other than for necessary repairs without our consent.

**B.25** You must provide us with records and receipts that support the amount and nature of a loss to an other structure. You must permit us to make copies of records that relate to the loss. You must sign and return documents that we reasonably require in connection with the claim.

**B.26** You must show us the damaged and undamaged portions of an other structure when we ask to inspect them. You must not dispose of damaged material until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. You may dispose of material that presents a continuing safety hazard after taking reasonable steps to document the damage.

**B.27** We cover direct physical loss to an other structure unless the loss is excluded or limited by this policy. We do not cover an increase in loss caused by an act, decision, or omission after the loss. We do not cover a loss that results from neglect to use all reasonable means to protect the structure.

**B.28** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, or latent defect. We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of an other structure. We do cover ensuing loss that is otherwise covered when it results from a covered cause of loss.

**B.29** We do not cover loss caused by insects, vermin, birds, rodents, or animals owned or kept by you. We do not cover loss caused by nesting or infestation. We do cover ensuing loss that is otherwise covered when it results from a covered cause of loss.

**B.30** We do not cover loss caused by "fungus", "wet rot", "dry rot", or "bacteria". We do not cover the cost of testing for, monitoring, containing, treating, removing, or disposing of those conditions. We do cover ensuing loss that is otherwise covered when it results from a covered cause of loss.

**B.31** We do not cover loss caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer or drain. We do not cover loss caused by water or waterborne material that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. We do cover ensuing loss that is otherwise covered when it results from a covered cause of loss.

**B.32** We do not cover loss to an other structure caused by movement of earth, including earthquake, landslide, mudslide, or earth sinking, rising, or shifting. We do not cover settling, cracking, or other damage caused by such movement. We do cover ensuing loss that is otherwise covered when it results from a covered cause of loss.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover your “personal property” owned or used by you while anywhere in the world. We cover the property for direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss. We apply the terms, conditions, and exclusions of this policy to covered personal property.

**C.2** We cover personal property owned by others while the property is on the part of the “residence premises” occupied by you. We treat that property as covered personal property when you have permission to possess it. We do not cover property owned by a tenant, boarder, or roomer unless you are related to that person.

**C.3** We cover personal property owned or used by an “insured” while the property is at your residence premises. We cover the property whether it is inside or outside a building. We do not cover property located away from the residence premises unless this policy provides coverage for that property.

**C.4** We cover personal property that you temporarily remove from the residence premises. We continue coverage while the property is in transit or at a location where you intend to reside temporarily. We do not cover property at a location you own, rent, or occupy as a permanent residence unless the property is at the residence premises.

**C.5** We cover personal property in a newly acquired principal residence. Coverage applies while you move the property to that residence. Coverage ends when the property is permanently located at the new residence or when this policy ends, whichever occurs first.

**C.6** We cover improvements and betterments made by you to the part of the residence premises occupied by you. We cover those improvements and betterments when you have an insurable interest in them. We do not cover improvements and betterments made for a tenant, boarder, or roomer.

**C.7** We cover property used to furnish or equip the residence premises. We cover furnishings, appliances, and household goods that are owned or used by you. We do not cover property that is permanently installed in the building when you do not own or have an insurable interest in the property.

**C.8** We cover personal property that is temporarily stored in a location away from the residence premises. We cover the property when the storage results from a move, repair, renovation, or similar circumstance. We do not cover property stored for sale, exhibition, or commercial distribution.

**C.9** We cover personal property belonging to a guest while the property is at the residence premises. We cover the property only when the guest is not paying you for the use of the residence premises. We do not cover property belonging to a tenant, boarder, or roomer.

**C.10** We cover personal property that is in your custody for repair, alteration, or cleaning. We cover the property while it is at the residence premises or while you are transporting it to or from the person performing the service. We do not cover property that you hold for sale or delivery.

**C.11** We cover money and precious metals owned by you. The most we will pay is two hundred dollars. Except that, this limit applies to loss caused by theft and to loss caused by any other covered cause of loss.

**C.12** We cover jewelry, watches, and precious stones owned by you. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft is one thousand five hundred dollars. Subject to that limit, we cover the property for a covered cause of loss other than theft.

**C.13** We cover firearms and related equipment owned by you. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft is two thousand dollars. Unless the loss is caused by theft, the limit does not restrict the amount payable under the Coverage C limit.

**C.14** We cover watercraft and their trailers owned by you. The most we will pay for watercraft, including trailers, is one thousand dollars. Provided that, the limit applies whether the property is at the residence premises or away from the residence premises.

**C.15** We cover silverware, silver-plated ware, goldware, and gold-plated ware owned by you. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft is two thousand five hundred dollars. Except that, the limit applies to flatware, hollowware, and similar property used for serving or preparing food.

**C.16** We cover business property located on the residence premises. The most we will pay for business property on the residence premises is two thousand five hundred dollars. Subject to that limit, we cover the property for a covered cause of loss.

**C.17** We cover electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle. The most we will pay for electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle is one thousand dollars. Unless the apparatus is designed solely for use in the vehicle, the limit applies only while the apparatus is in the vehicle.

**C.18** We cover electronic equipment that can be operated from a motor vehicle. We cover the equipment when it is designed to be used both inside and outside the vehicle. We do not cover the equipment while it is permanently installed in the vehicle.

**C.19** We cover property that is used for personal, family, or household purposes. We cover the property when its use is incidental to the occupancy of the residence premises. We do not cover property used primarily for business or professional purposes except as provided for business property.

**C.20** We cover personal property that is kept in a building on the residence premises. We cover the property when the building is used by you for storage or household purposes. We do not cover property in a building that is rented to others unless you retain possession and control of the property.

**C.21** We cover personal property in a self-storage facility. We cover the property while it is temporarily kept there because of a move, repair, or renovation. We do not cover property held in storage as part of a business enterprise.

**C.22** We cover personal property while it is being moved to or from the residence premises. We cover direct physical loss occurring during loading, transportation, or unloading. We do not cover loss caused by delay, loss of market, or damage caused by improper packing.

**C.23** We cover personal property while it is being repaired or restored. We cover the property while it is at the residence premises or at the premises of the person performing the work. We do not cover the cost of correcting faulty workmanship unless a covered cause of loss results in additional damage.

**C.24** We cover personal property that is temporarily outside the residence premises. We cover the property while it is being used or cared for by you. We do not cover property that is permanently kept at a location other than the residence premises.

**C.25** We cover personal property of a student who is an insured. We cover the property while it is at a residence occupied by the student. We do not cover property that is permanently moved to a residence not occupied by the student.

**C.26** We cover personal property that is used by an insured in a location away from the residence premises. We cover the property while the insured is temporarily present at that location. We do not cover property left at the location after the insured permanently leaves.

**C.27** We cover personal property for direct physical loss caused by fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, smoke, and other covered causes of loss. We determine whether a cause of loss is covered under the terms of this policy. We do not cover loss caused by a cause of loss that is excluded.

**C.28** We cover personal property when a covered cause of loss damages the property. We cover the resulting loss even when the property is outside the residence premises. We do not cover loss that consists only of wear and tear, deterioration, or inherent vice.

**C.29** We cover reasonable measures taken to protect covered personal property from further damage after a covered loss. We cover those measures when they are necessary because of the covered loss. We do not cover expenses incurred to protect property from a cause of loss that is not covered.

**C.30** We cover debris of covered personal property when the debris results from a covered loss. We cover the reasonable cost of removing the debris from the residence premises. We do not cover the cost of removing property that was not damaged by a covered cause of loss.

**C.31** We cover the cost to make reasonable temporary repairs to covered personal property. We cover the cost when the repairs protect the property from further damage. We do not cover permanent repairs unless the repairs are necessary to restore covered property.

**C.32** We cover covered personal property that is damaged while being removed from danger. We cover the property when the removal is necessary because of a covered cause of loss. We do not cover damage caused by an insured's unreasonable handling of the property.

**C.33** We do not cover land, including land on which the residence premises is located. We do not cover water or the cost of stabilizing or replacing land. We do not cover any improvement or alteration intended to control, contain, or divert water.

**C.34** We do not cover animals, birds, or fish. We do not cover loss to those animals, birds, or fish caused by a covered cause of loss. We do not cover the cost of treating, replacing, or caring for them.

**C.35** We do not cover motor vehicles or motorized land conveyances. We do not cover their equipment, parts, or accessories while installed in or attached to them. We cover vehicles and conveyances only to the extent specifically included as covered personal property.

**C.36** We do not cover aircraft, including parts, equipment, and accessories. We do not cover aircraft that are stored, displayed, or being repaired. We do not cover damage caused by the operation or use of aircraft.

**C.37** We do not cover property rented or held for rental to others. We do not cover property used by a tenant, boarder, or roomer in connection with an occupancy agreement. We do not cover property that you provide as part of a rental, boarding, or lodging arrangement.

**C.38** We do not cover property used in any business conducted by an insured. We do not cover property held as inventory, stock, or merchandise. We do not cover property used to provide services to others except as otherwise covered under this Coverage C.

**C.39** We do not cover property separately described and specifically insured by this policy or another insurance policy. We do not cover the property under this Coverage C when the other insurance applies. We cover resulting damage to other covered personal property when that damage is caused by a covered cause of loss.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** The limit for "Coverage D" is fifty percent of the limit for "Coverage A". Except that this limit applies to the total amount payable under all Coverage D provisions. We will not pay more than the applicable Coverage D limit for any loss.

**D.2** We cover the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living. Except that the increase must result from a covered loss that makes the residence premises unfit for occupancy. We will pay only for expenses that are reasonable and necessary.

**D.3** We cover the fair rental value of that part of the residence premises occupied by you or held for rental when a covered loss makes it unfit for occupancy. Subject to the applicable Coverage D limit, we will pay the fair rental value that you lose during the time required to restore the premises. We will not pay for any rental value that you would have received without the covered loss.

**D.4** We cover the necessary increase in living expenses and the loss of fair rental value when a civil authority prohibits access to the residence premises. Unless the prohibition results from a covered loss to property described in this policy, we will not provide this coverage. The prohibition must apply because of direct damage to property away from the residence premises.

**D.5** We will pay the additional living expense or fair rental value only for the shortest time required to repair or replace the damaged property. Except that this time ends when the residence premises could reasonably be made fit for occupancy, whether or not you resume occupancy. We will consider the condition of the premises and the availability of labor and materials.

**D.6** We will pay additional living expense when you temporarily occupy other premises. Subject to our approval, the other premises may be a hotel, furnished dwelling, or other suitable residence. We will not pay expenses that you would have incurred if the covered loss had not occurred.

**D.7** We will pay the reasonable cost of meals and other necessary household expenses that exceed your normal expenses. Except that you must provide records supporting the additional cost when we request them. We may compare the claimed expenses with your ordinary household expenses.

**D.8** We will pay the reasonable cost of transporting you and your household to temporary living quarters when the move is necessary because of a covered loss. Unless the move is necessary to avoid further damage or to permit repairs, we will not pay transportation expenses. We will not pay for commuting expenses that continue after you occupy the temporary quarters.

**D.9** We will pay the necessary expenses incurred to establish and maintain temporary household arrangements. Subject to the applicable Coverage D limit, these expenses may include utility connection charges, storage charges, and similar charges. The expenses must result from the covered loss.

**D.10** We will pay the loss of fair rental value when a rented part of the residence premises cannot be occupied because of a covered loss. Except that the rental arrangement must have existed before the loss or must have been entered into without knowledge of the loss. We will not pay amounts that you voluntarily waive under the rental arrangement.

**D.11** We will pay the reasonable cost of replacing essential household services that are unavailable at the residence premises because of a covered loss. Unless the service is necessary for ordinary occupancy, we will not pay the cost. We will not pay for improvements or upgrades.

**D.12** We will pay the reasonable cost of moving property to protect it from further damage when the move is necessary because of a covered loss. Subject to our right to inspect the property, you must keep records of the property moved and the expenses incurred. We will not pay for a move made solely for convenience.

**D.13** You must use the residence premises in a reasonably prudent manner after a loss. Except that you may take reasonable measures necessary to protect persons or property from further damage. We may reduce payment for expenses caused by your failure to take such measures.

**D.14** You must notify us promptly when the residence premises becomes unfit for occupancy. Unless circumstances beyond your control prevent prompt notice, the notice must include the cause of the condition and the location of the premises. We may inspect the premises after receiving notice.

**D.15** You must give us reasonable access to the residence premises and any temporary living quarters related to the claim. Subject to reasonable privacy and safety requirements, you must permit us to inspect the conditions supporting the claim. We may defer payment for expenses that cannot be verified because access is refused.

**D.16** You must keep receipts, rental records, leases, and other documents that support a claim under Coverage D. Except that we will not require a document that cannot reasonably be obtained. You must provide the documents to us when requested.

**D.17** You must cooperate with us in determining the amount and duration of the loss. Unless you have a reasonable explanation, you must answer questions concerning the occupancy, rental use, and expenses of the residence premises. Your failure to cooperate may affect the amount we pay.

**D.18** You must promptly notify us of any change in the condition or use of the residence premises that affects the claim. Subject to our right to verify the change, you must identify the date the change occurred and the expenses affected by it. We will not pay expenses incurred after the claim no longer qualifies for coverage.

**D.19** We do not cover expenses that are incurred because you choose to live in accommodations more expensive than reasonably necessary. Except that we will pay the reasonable cost of suitable accommodations when less expensive accommodations are unavailable. We may limit payment to the cost of comparable accommodations.

**D.20** We do not cover expenses for a loss of income, business interruption, or loss of use of property used for business. Unless the expense is an otherwise covered additional living expense of your household, it is not payable under Coverage D. We will not convert a business loss into a household living expense.

**D.21** We do not cover expenses caused by a condition that existed before the covered loss. Subject to the covered loss requirement, we will cover expenses caused by the direct effect of the covered loss on occupancy. We will not pay to correct a pre-existing condition.

**D.22** We do not cover expenses resulting from your failure to repair or replace damaged property within a reasonable time. Except that we will consider delays caused by conditions beyond your control. We will not pay for delays caused by elective changes, additions, or improvements.

**D.23** We do not cover expenses resulting from your voluntary decision to vacate the residence premises when it remains reasonably fit for occupancy. Unless a governmental authority requires the vacancy, we will require evidence that the premises could not reasonably be occupied. We will consider the availability of essential services and safe access.

**D.24** We do not cover rent that you do not collect because of a voluntary agreement with a tenant. Subject to the covered loss requirement, we will cover rent that cannot reasonably be collected because the rented premises is unfit for occupancy. You must provide the rental agreement and records of rent due when requested.

**D.25** We do not cover expenses for lodging or meals provided without charge by a relative, friend, or other person. Except that we may cover additional expenses actually incurred by that person for your benefit when those expenses are reasonable and necessary. You must provide evidence of the expenses.

**D.26** We do not cover expenses that another insurer, governmental program, landlord, or other source is obligated to pay. Unless the other source does not pay the full reasonable expense, we will not make payment under this coverage. We may require you to pursue available payment from that source.

**D.27** We do not cover expenses attributable to an increase in household size or a change in your personal circumstances. Subject to the covered loss requirement, we will cover the necessary increase caused by the temporary displacement. We will compare the claimed expenses with the expenses ordinarily incurred before the loss.

**D.28** We will make payment to you unless another person has a right to receive payment for the covered expense. Except that payment for lost fair rental value may be made jointly to you and the person entitled to the rent. Payment does not transfer your duties under this coverage.

**D.29** We may make advance payments for necessary living expenses when the facts support the claim. Unless we agree otherwise, an advance payment will be applied to the amount otherwise payable under Coverage D. An advance payment does not establish the cause of loss or the final amount payable.

**D.30** We will determine the amount payable from the reasonable and necessary expenses actually incurred and the fair rental value actually lost. Subject to the applicable Coverage D limit, we will pay the amount supported by the claim and not paid from another source. We will not pay duplicate amounts for the same expense or loss.

**E.87** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to replace keys, access cards, and similar devices damaged by a covered loss. The devices must provide access to the residence premises or covered property. Subject to, we do not pay for devices used solely for business access.

**E.88** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to remove or replace security devices damaged by a covered loss. The device must protect the residence premises or covered property. Unless, the device is replaced with an improvement or upgrade.

**E.89** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to clean personal property after a covered loss. The cleaning must be reasonably necessary to remove covered smoke, soot, or other covered residue. Provided that, we do not pay for cleaning that does not restore damaged property.

**E.90** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to board or shelter domestic animals after a covered loss. The residence premises must be unfit for the animals' normal care because of the loss. Except that, we do not pay for veterinary treatment or ordinary animal care.

**E.91** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to secure the residence premises after a covered loss. The security must be necessary because doors, windows, or other barriers were damaged. Subject to, we do not pay for security arranged solely for personal convenience.

**E.92** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to remove water from covered property after a covered loss. The removal must be necessary to prevent additional covered damage. Unless, the water results from flooding, surface water, or groundwater.

**E.93** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to dry covered property after accidental discharge of water or steam. The drying must be performed promptly and by reasonable methods. Provided that, we do not pay for drying property that was not damaged or threatened by the discharge.

**E.94** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to test covered property for contamination resulting from a covered loss. The testing must be reasonably necessary to determine whether the property can be safely restored. Except that, we do not pay for testing unrelated to the covered loss.

**E.95** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to dispose of covered property that cannot be safely restored after a covered loss. The property must be damaged beyond reasonable repair. Subject to, we do not pay for disposal when restoration is reasonably possible.

**E.96** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to transport covered property for repair after a covered loss. The transport must be reasonably necessary because repair cannot be performed at the residence premises. Unless, the transport is for convenience or an elective upgrade.

**E.98** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to inspect covered property after a covered loss. The inspection must be reasonably necessary to identify covered damage. Except that, we do not pay for inspections performed solely for maintenance or improvement.

**E.99** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to obtain estimates for repair or replacement of covered property. The estimates must be reasonably necessary to adjust a covered loss. Subject to, we do not pay for estimates prepared solely for litigation or improvement.

**E.100** We pay for reasonable costs you incur to protect covered property from weather after a covered loss. The protection must be reasonably necessary because the building or another protective barrier was damaged. Unless, the protection is installed to correct pre-existing deterioration.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure against risk of "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by a "peril" described in this section. The loss must be accidental and must occur during the "policy period".

**P.2** Direct physical loss means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of covered property resulting from a covered peril. Direct physical loss does not include a condition that exists only because of diminished value, market conditions, or a defect that has not caused physical injury.

**P.3** We cover direct physical loss only when the covered peril is the efficient cause of the loss. We do not cover loss when an excluded cause independently produces the loss.

**P.4** We cover loss caused by fire. We also cover loss caused by lightning.

**P.5** We cover loss caused by windstorm or hail. We do not cover loss to property inside a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless the direct force of wind or hail first damages the building and creates an opening through which the material enters.

**P.6** We cover loss caused by windstorm or hail to covered property outside a building when the property is ordinarily kept at the residence premises. We do not cover loss to property that is not reasonably secured against normal wind conditions.

**P.7** We cover loss caused by explosion. We do not cover loss caused by explosion arising from your intentional act or from an unlawful use of covered property.

**P.8** We cover loss caused by riot or civil commotion. We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, or destruction of property by governmental authority.

**P.9** We cover loss caused by aircraft. Aircraft includes self-propelled vehicles designed for flight or navigation through the air.

**P.10** We cover loss caused by vehicles. We do not cover loss caused by a vehicle that is owned or operated by an insured.

**P.11** We cover sudden and accidental loss caused by smoke. Smoke includes smoke from a sudden and accidental malfunction of a heating, cooking, or power-generating appliance.

**P.12** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We do not cover loss caused by smoke that is continuous, expected, or intentional.

**P.13** We cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief. We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief committed by an insured.

**P.14** We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by an insured. We cover loss caused by theft of covered property when the theft is not committed by an insured.

**P.15** We cover loss caused by theft occurring at the residence premises. We cover theft away from the residence premises only when the covered property is in your care or custody.

**P.16** We do not cover loss caused by theft from a part of the residence premises that you rent to others. We do not cover loss caused by theft from a location where covered property is held for rental to others.

**P.17** We cover loss caused by falling objects. The object must first strike the building or covered property before we cover resulting damage.

**P.18** We do not cover loss to property contained in a building caused by a falling object unless the roof or an exterior wall is first damaged by the falling object. We cover resulting damage to covered property inside the building when that condition is met.

**P.19** We cover loss caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet. We do not cover loss to property that is not part of a building structure.

**P.20** We cover sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We cover resulting damage to covered property caused by the discharge or overflow.

**P.21** We do not cover loss caused by constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. We do not cover loss caused by water or steam that leaks over time from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system.

**P.22** We do not cover loss caused by the failure to maintain a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain those systems in a condition that will prevent avoidable discharge or overflow.

**P.23** We cover the necessary access to a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system when access is required to repair covered damage. We do not cover the cost to repair the system from which the water or steam escaped unless the system itself was damaged by a covered peril.

**P.24** We cover sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a steam, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by that event.

**P.25** We do not cover loss caused by deterioration, corrosion, rust, wear, or mechanical breakdown. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by water, steam, or fire when that resulting loss is caused by a covered peril.

**P.26** We cover loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat in the building or to shut off the water supply and drain the system when heat is not maintained.

**P.27** We do not cover loss caused by freezing when an insured fails to take the protective action required by this section. We cover loss caused by freezing when the failure to take that action was beyond the control of an insured.

**P.28** We cover sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current. We do not cover loss to a tube, transistor, electronic component, or similar electrical component caused solely by electrical current.

**P.29** We cover resulting fire damage caused by artificially generated electrical current. We do not cover loss caused by electrical arcing, short circuit, or power surge unless the loss is otherwise caused by a covered peril.

**P.30** We cover loss caused by volcanic eruption. Volcanic eruption includes airborne shock waves, ash, dust, or lava flow directly resulting from volcanic activity.

**P.31** We do not cover loss caused by earthquake, land shock waves, tremors, or volcanic tremors. We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings.

**P.32** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of those sources. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**P.33** We do not cover loss caused by water that exerts pressure on, seeps, leaks, or flows through a building, foundation, pavement, or other structure. We do not cover loss caused by water that flows from a drain, sewer, sump, or related equipment.

**P.34** We do not cover loss caused by backup of a sewer or drain. We do not cover loss caused by overflow or discharge from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**P.35** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, mudflow, sinkhole collapse, and movement of earth beneath the surface.

**P.36** We do not cover loss caused by weather conditions when those conditions contribute in any way with an excluded cause of loss. We cover loss caused by weather conditions only when the weather condition itself is a covered peril and no exclusion applies.

**P.37** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. We do not cover loss caused by maintenance that is faulty, inadequate, or defective.

**P.38** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or mechanical failure.

**P.39** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, smog, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or contamination. We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion.

**P.40** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, discharge, or waste from any animal.

**P.41** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. Pollutants include solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or chemical irritants or contaminants.

**P.42** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss.

**P.43** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. Intentional loss means any loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with intent to cause a loss.

**P.44** We do not cover loss caused by illegal trade, transportation, or manufacture of controlled substances. We do not cover loss caused by the use of covered property in connection with unlawful activity.

**P.45** We do not cover loss caused by war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or action taken by a military force.

**P.46** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, and any consequence of those conditions.

**P.47** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, including seizure, confiscation, destruction, or condemnation of covered property. We cover loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action when fire is otherwise a covered peril.

**P.48** We do not cover loss caused by an act or decision of a governmental body, regulatory authority, or public official. We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of an ordinance, law, or code.

**P.49** We do not cover the cost to comply with an ordinance, law, or code regulating the construction, repair, replacement, use, or occupancy of covered property. We do not cover loss caused solely by a requirement that covered property be upgraded or altered.

**P.50** We do not cover loss caused by failure of power, heating, cooling, communication, or other utility service occurring away from the residence premises. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril at the residence premises.

**P.51** We do not cover loss caused by interruption of utility service when no covered property has sustained direct physical loss from a covered peril. We do not cover loss caused by spoilage, delay, loss of use, or loss of market.

**P.52** We do not cover loss caused by collapse except as provided by a covered peril described in this section. Collapse means an abrupt falling down or caving in of a building or part of a building.

**P.53** We do not cover loss caused by a building or part of a building that is in danger of falling down or caving in. We do not cover loss caused by cracking, bulging, sagging, bending, leaning, settling, shrinking, or expansion unless collapse directly results.

**P.54** We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by fire, explosion, or other covered peril that follows an excluded cause of loss. We do not cover the excluded cause of loss itself.

**P.55** We cover only the part of a loss that is directly caused by a covered peril. We do not cover loss that would have occurred in the absence of a covered peril.

**P.56** An insured must give prompt notice of a loss to us or our authorized representative. An insured must state the time, place, and circumstances of the loss to the extent known.

**P.57** An insured must protect covered property from further damage and must keep an accurate record of repair expenses. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property from further damage.

**P.58** An insured must exhibit damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must allow us to inspect the property and to examine records that relate to the loss.

**P.59** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**P.60** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request. An insured must answer material questions concerning the loss and must provide requested information under oath.

**P.61** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or omit a material fact relating to the insurance or a loss.

**P.62** We do not cover loss when an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact concerning the insurance, covered property, or a claim. This condition applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after a loss.

**P.63** We may investigate a loss and adjust a claim without waiving any right or defense available to us. Our investigation or payment does not constitute an admission that coverage applies.

**P.64** We may require an insured to provide a signed statement describing the loss and the claimed damage. The statement must identify covered property that was damaged, destroyed, or stolen.

**P.65** We may require reasonable proof of ownership, value, and loss. An insured must provide that proof when it is available.

**P.66** We may elect to repair, replace, or pay for covered property that is damaged or destroyed. We may take all or part of the damaged property at an agreed or appraised value.

**P.67** We will determine the value of damaged property according to the applicable valuation method. We may consider "ACV roof age" when determining the actual cash value of roof property.

**P.68** We do not cover any increase in loss caused by delay in reporting a claim or protecting covered property. An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent avoidable additional damage.

**P.69** We do not cover costs incurred before a loss unless those costs are necessary to protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss. We do not cover costs that are not reasonable or necessary.

**P.70** Coverage under this section applies only to covered property and only to direct physical loss caused by a covered peril. All exclusions, duties, and conditions stated in this section apply to each claim.

## I.X — Section I — Exclusions

## II.E — Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** We will pay "damages" for which an "insured" is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence." The bodily injury or property damage must arise from conduct for which this coverage applies.

**E.2** “Bodily injury” means bodily harm, sickness, disease, or death. “Property damage” means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use, or loss of use of tangible property that is not physically injured.

**E.3** “Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially similar harmful conditions. “Damages” means compensatory sums that an insured is legally obligated to pay.

**E.4** We will provide a defense at our expense against a "suit" seeking damages covered by this insurance. We may investigate any "claim" and settle any claim or suit when we consider settlement appropriate.

**E.5** “Claim” means a demand for damages. “Suit” means a civil proceeding alleging damages because of bodily injury or property damage.

**E.6** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury resulting from the use of reasonable force to protect persons or property.

**E.7** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of or in connection with a "business" conducted by an insured. “Business” means a trade, profession, occupation, or activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain.

**E.8** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of business pursuits of an insured. This exclusion does not apply to activities ordinarily incident to nonbusiness pursuits.

**E.9** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of premises owned, rented, or controlled by an insured that are not an "insured location." “Insured location” means premises shown in the Declarations as the residence premises and premises used by an insured in connection with that residence.

**E.10** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle" by an insured. “Motor vehicle” means a land vehicle designed for travel on public roads and subject to motor vehicle registration.

**E.11** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle owned by or available for the regular use of an insured. This exclusion applies whether the motor vehicle is operated by an insured or by any other person.

**E.12** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "watercraft" by an insured. “Watercraft” means a craft designed for travel on water.

**E.13** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an "aircraft" by an insured. “Aircraft” means a device designed for flight or navigation in the air.

**E.14** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising from the entrustment of a motor vehicle, watercraft, or aircraft to any person by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the entrustment is alleged to be negligent or otherwise wrongful.

**E.15** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care, custody, or control of an insured.

**E.16** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage for which an insured is obligated to pay damages because of the assumption of liability in a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**E.17** We do not cover bodily injury to any person eligible to receive benefits required by workers compensation, disability benefits, occupational disease benefits, or similar law. We do not cover any obligation imposed upon an insured under such law.

**E.18** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurpation of power, or action taken by governmental authority to prevent or defend against such events. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing to the loss.

**E.19** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the actual, alleged, or threatened release, discharge, seepage, migration, dispersal, or escape of pollutants. “Pollutants” means irritants or contaminants, including smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

**E.20** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the transmission of a "communicable disease" by an insured. “Communicable disease” means a disease capable of being transmitted from a person, animal, substance, or object to another person.

**E.21** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of a controlled substance by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of a controlled substance under the direction of a licensed medical provider.

**E.22** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of abuse, molestation, harassment, or other sexual misconduct by an insured. This exclusion applies whether liability is asserted directly or indirectly against an insured.

**E.23** We do not cover punitive, exemplary, multiplied, or penal damages. We do not cover fines, penalties, or assessments imposed upon an insured by a governmental authority.

**E.24** An insured must give us prompt notice of an occurrence that may result in a claim. The notice must state the identity of the insured, the time and place of the occurrence, and the names of known injured persons and witnesses.

**E.25** An insured must promptly send us every notice, demand, summons, or legal paper relating to a claim or suit. An insured must not allow a default judgment to be entered without promptly notifying us.

**E.26** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of a claim or suit. An insured must assist us in securing evidence, obtaining witnesses, and enforcing any right of recovery.

**E.27** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses to protect persons or property from further injury or damage. We will not be bound by a voluntary payment, obligation, or expense made without our consent.

**E.28** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide records, documents, authorizations, and other information reasonably requested by us.

**E.29** This insurance applies separately to each insured against whom a claim is made or suit is brought. This provision does not increase the amount available for any occurrence.

**E.30** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this coverage. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not give any person a right of action against us except as provided by law.

**E.32** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent any material fact relating to a claim or suit. We may deny coverage for a claim affected by fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation by an insured.

**E.33** An insured must transfer to us any right of recovery against another person responsible for damages we pay. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

## II.F — Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

**F.1** We will pay the necessary "medical expenses" incurred by a person because of "bodily injury" caused by an "occurrence." The bodily injury must be sustained by that person while this coverage applies.

**F.2** Medical expenses must be incurred or medically ascertained within three years years from the date of the accident causing bodily injury. The expenses must be necessary and reasonable.

**F.3** Medical expenses include reasonable charges for medical, surgical, dental, ambulance, hospital, professional nursing, prosthetic, and funeral services. Medical expenses also include reasonable charges for pharmaceuticals, eyeglasses, contact lenses, hearing aids, and the repair or replacement of damaged eyeglasses, contact lenses, or hearing aids.

**F.4** We will pay medical expenses without regard to fault. Payment under this coverage does not constitute an admission of liability by us or by an insured.

**F.5** This coverage applies to a person who is on an "insured location" with the permission of an "insured." The person must not be an insured.

**F.6** This coverage also applies to a person who is away from an insured location when the bodily injury arises out of a condition on an insured location. The condition may include a way immediately adjoining an insured location.

**F.7** This coverage also applies to a person who is away from an insured location when the bodily injury is caused by the activities of an insured. The activities must not be excluded by this policy.

**F.8** This coverage also applies to a person who is away from an insured location when the bodily injury is caused by a "residence employee." The residence employee must be acting within the course of employment by an insured.

**F.9** This coverage also applies to a person who is away from an insured location when the bodily injury is caused by an animal owned by, or in the care of, an insured. The animal need not be on an insured location when the bodily injury occurs.

**F.10** An insured must give us prompt notice of an accident or occurrence that may result in a claim under this coverage. The notice must include information sufficient to identify the injured person and the circumstances of the accident or occurrence.

**F.11** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide records, authorizations, and information that we reasonably request concerning the claimed medical expenses.

**F.12** The injured person, or a person acting for the injured person, must submit to physical examinations by physicians selected by us when we reasonably require. We will pay the reasonable cost of those examinations.

**F.13** The injured person, or a person acting for the injured person, must provide us with authorization to obtain medical records and other information relating to the bodily injury. The authorization must permit us to evaluate the necessity and reasonableness of the claimed medical expenses.

**F.14** We may require proof that medical expenses have been incurred or medically ascertained. We may also require proof that the expenses result from the bodily injury caused by the occurrence.

**F.15** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to an insured. This exclusion applies whether the insured is named in the policy or otherwise qualifies as an insured.

**F.16** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to a person who regularly resides on an insured location. This exclusion does not apply to a residence employee.

**F.17** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to a person eligible to receive benefits voluntarily provided or required to be provided by an insured under workers compensation law, occupational disease law, or a similar law. This exclusion applies whether or not the benefits are actually claimed.

**F.18** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury resulting from war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, and warlike action by a military force.

**F.19** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle." This exclusion applies whether the motor vehicle is owned, operated, rented, borrowed, or entrusted by an insured.

**F.20** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "watercraft." This exclusion applies unless the watercraft is of a type for which coverage is provided by this policy.

**F.21** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an "aircraft." This exclusion applies whether the aircraft is owned, operated, rented, borrowed, or entrusted by an insured.
