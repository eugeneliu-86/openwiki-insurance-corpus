# HO 04 90 Water Backup and Sump Discharge or Overflow (Edition 2026-01)

Multistate endorsement. Attaches to HO-3 and modifies Section I — Exclusions A.3. Replaces
edition 2010-10 of this endorsement for policies written on or after 2026-01-01.

## W.1 Coverage Provided

We cover direct physical loss to property described in Coverage A, Coverage B, and Coverage C
caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through sewers or drains, or that
overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment, whether or not the
backup, overflow, or discharge results from mechanical breakdown of that equipment.

## W.2 Sublimit

The most we will pay for all loss under this endorsement in any one policy period is ten
thousand dollars, unless a higher limit is shown for this endorsement in the Declarations. This
sublimit is part of, and not in addition to, the limits of liability that apply to Coverage A,
Coverage B, and Coverage C.

## W.3 Deductible

A separate deductible of one thousand dollars applies to each loss under this endorsement. The
Section I deductible shown in the Declarations does not apply to loss covered here.

## W.4 What Remains Excluded

This endorsement does not provide coverage for loss caused by flood, surface water, waves,
tidal water, storm surge, or overflow of a body of water, whether or not driven by wind.
Section I — Exclusions A.1 continues to apply in full.

This endorsement does not provide coverage for loss caused by water below the surface of the
ground, including water that exerts pressure on or seeps or leaks through a building,
foundation, or swimming pool. Section I — Exclusions A.2 continues to apply in full.

## W.5 Maintenance Condition

We do not cover loss under this endorsement if the backup, overflow, or discharge resulted from
an insured's failure to maintain the sewer line, drain, sump, or sump pump serving the
residence premises, where that failure was known to the insured before the loss and a
reasonable person would have remedied it.

## W.6 Backflow Prevention Requirement

Where the residence premises has a finished area below grade, coverage under this endorsement
applies only if a backwater valve or equivalent backflow prevention device was installed and
operable on the sewer line serving the premises at the time of loss. This requirement is new in
this edition and did not appear in edition 2010-10.

## W.7 Loss Settlement

Loss to property covered under Coverage A and Coverage B is settled on the basis stated in the
policy to which this endorsement attaches. Loss to property covered under Coverage C is settled
at actual cash value regardless of any replacement cost personal property endorsement, unless
the Declarations state otherwise for this endorsement.

All other provisions of the policy apply.
