# HO 04 90 Water Backup and Sump Discharge or Overflow (Edition 2010-10)
> SUPERSEDED by HO 04 90 Water Backup and Sump Discharge or Overflow (Edition 2027-01) for policies effective on or after 2027-01-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" modifies insurance provided under your "policy." Please read this endorsement carefully.

This endorsement is attached to and forms part of your policy. Its provisions apply with the provisions of your policy.

This endorsement is identified as "Water Backup and Sump Discharge or Overflow." Insurance provided by this endorsement is subject to its terms.

The terms and conditions of your policy continue to apply unless modified by this endorsement. A provision of this endorsement that changes a policy provision controls.

The "Declarations" are part of this endorsement. Information shown in the Declarations applies to insurance provided by this endorsement.

Coverage under this endorsement applies only as provided by its terms. We provide no coverage except as stated in this endorsement.

An insured must comply with the duties and conditions that apply to this endorsement. Failure to comply with an applicable duty or condition may affect coverage.

We may rely on information provided by you in connection with this endorsement. You must notify us of a material change affecting insurance provided by this endorsement.

This endorsement does not alter a policy provision unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise. All policy exclusions continue to apply unless changed by this endorsement.

The attachment of this endorsement does not expand coverage beyond its stated terms. We will determine coverage under this endorsement in accordance with its provisions and your policy.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We will pay for "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by a "water backup" or a "sump discharge or overflow." The loss must result from the water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.2** "Direct physical loss" means accidental physical injury to covered property. Direct physical loss includes resulting damage to covered property when caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.3** "Covered property" means property for which coverage applies under this policy. Covered property must be at an "insured location" when the loss occurs.

**W.4** "Insured location" means a location described as an insured location under this policy. Property must be located at the insured location to be covered under this endorsement.

**W.5** "Water backup" means water that backs up through a sewer. Water backup also means water that backs up through a drain.

**W.6** Water backup includes water that enters covered property after backing up through a sewer or drain. The backup must be the cause of the direct physical loss.

**W.7** "Sump discharge or overflow" means water that overflows from a sump. Sump discharge or overflow also means water that is discharged from a sump.

**W.8** Sump discharge or overflow includes water that overflows from a sump pump. It also includes water that is discharged from a sump pump.

**W.9** Sump discharge or overflow includes water that overflows from related equipment. It also includes water that is discharged from related equipment.

**W.10** "Related equipment" means equipment connected to a sump or sump pump. Related equipment includes equipment used to collect, contain, move, or discharge water.

**W.11** The water backup or sump discharge or overflow must be accidental. We will not treat an intended release of water as an accidental water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.12** The water backup or sump discharge or overflow must cause direct physical loss to covered property. We do not pay solely because water is present at an insured location.

**W.13** We will pay for damage caused by water that backs up through a sewer. We will pay for damage caused by water that backs up through a drain.

**W.14** We will pay for damage caused by water that overflows from a sump. We will pay for damage caused by water discharged from a sump.

**W.15** We will pay for damage caused by water that overflows from a sump pump. We will pay for damage caused by water discharged from a sump pump.

**W.16** We will pay for damage caused by water that overflows from related equipment. We will pay for damage caused by water discharged from related equipment.

**W.17** The source of the water may be within or outside the insured location. Coverage applies only when the water causes direct physical loss through water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.18** The sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, or related equipment need not be part of covered property. Covered property must sustain direct physical loss caused by the water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.19** Coverage applies when water enters a building through a sewer backup. Coverage also applies when water enters a building through a drain backup.

**W.20** Coverage applies when water escapes from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment by overflow. Coverage also applies when water escapes from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment by discharge.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by water from "precipitation." This exclusion applies whether precipitation enters the insured location directly or indirectly.

**W.22** "Precipitation" means rain, snow, sleet, hail, or similar moisture falling from the atmosphere. Precipitation includes water resulting from the melting of such moisture.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by water from precipitation that enters through an opening in a building. We do not cover loss caused by water from precipitation that accumulates outside a building.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by water from precipitation merely because that water later contacts a sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, or related equipment. The loss must be caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow and not by precipitation.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by water from precipitation that overflows gutters, downspouts, roof drains, or similar drainage equipment. This exclusion applies even if the water later enters covered property.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by water from precipitation that seeps, leaks, or flows through walls, floors, roofs, foundations, windows, doors, or other openings. This exclusion applies whether the opening is visible or concealed.

**W.27** We do not cover loss consisting only of repair, replacement, or maintenance of a sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, or related equipment. We cover resulting direct physical loss to covered property when caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.28** We do not cover loss consisting only of clearing, cleaning, repairing, or replacing an obstruction in a sewer or drain. We cover resulting direct physical loss to covered property when caused by water backup.

**W.29** We do not cover loss consisting only of correcting a defect in a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. We cover resulting direct physical loss to covered property when caused by sump discharge or overflow.

**W.30** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a water backup or sump discharge or overflow. An insured must make reasonable efforts to preserve damaged property for our inspection.

**W.31** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss under this endorsement. An insured must provide records and information that we reasonably request to evaluate the loss.

**W.32** An insured must show that covered property sustained direct physical loss. An insured must show that the direct physical loss was caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.33** We may inspect covered property and damaged property. We may investigate the cause and extent of the claimed loss.

**W.34** Payment for covered loss will not exceed the amount of direct physical loss to covered property. We will determine the amount payable in accordance with the terms that apply to the property loss.

**W.35** This endorsement provides coverage only for direct physical loss caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow. It does not provide coverage for loss caused by water from precipitation.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We provide coverage for direct physical loss to property caused by "water backup" or "sump discharge or overflow". The loss must occur at the residence premises shown in the Declarations. This coverage applies only when the loss results from the conditions described in this provision.

**W.2** The most we will pay for covered loss caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow is five thousand dollars. Except that this limit applies regardless of the number of items damaged in the loss. The limit includes the applicable amounts for property damage and related covered expenses.

**W.3** We apply this limit to the total amount of covered loss resulting from water backup or sump discharge or overflow. The limit applies to property insured under the policy. Payment under this coverage does not increase any other applicable limit.

**W.4** We determine the amount of covered loss under the policy provisions that apply to the damaged property. We do not pay more than the amount of the covered loss. We do not pay for loss that is not caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.5** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from additional damage after water backup or sump discharge or overflow occurs. We pay only for additional damage that results from a covered cause of loss. We do not pay for damage that results from an insured's failure to take reasonable steps to protect the property.

**W.6** An insured must promptly notify us of any loss caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow. An insured must provide us with information and records that we reasonably request concerning the loss. We may inspect the damaged property before making payment.

**W.7** We may pay the covered loss to you or to the person or organization having an insurable interest in the damaged property. Our payment will not exceed the amount of that interest. We may make payment jointly to the persons or organizations entitled to receive it.

**W.8** We may repair or replace damaged property instead of making a payment. We may use property of like kind and quality for the repair or replacement. We will not pay more than the amount otherwise payable for the covered loss.

**W.9** We do not cover the cost to remove, replace, repair or restore defective equipment or systems that cause water backup or sump discharge or overflow. We do not cover the cost of correcting a condition that permits the loss to occur. We do not cover damage that results from such defective equipment or systems except to the extent provided by this coverage.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by neglect, meaning your failure to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. We do not cover costs incurred to prevent a loss that has not occurred. We do not cover loss that results from a failure to maintain the property.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning or automatic fire protective sprinkler system or of a household appliance. We do not cover such loss unless you have used reasonable care to maintain heat in the building or have shut off the water supply and drained the system. We do not cover resulting damage that is excluded elsewhere under the policy.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by water or waterborne material that enters through an opening in a roof, wall, door, window or foundation. We do not cover loss caused by surface water or rising water. We do not cover loss that is not the direct result of water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. We do not cover loss that occurs gradually over time. We do not cover the cost to correct the source of the seepage or leakage.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus or wet rot except when resulting from a covered loss under this coverage. We do not cover the cost to remove or test for mold, fungus or wet rot except as otherwise provided for covered damage. We do not cover any increase in loss caused by an insured's failure to take reasonable steps to prevent its spread.

**W.15** We do not cover loss to land, including the ground, soil or earth. We do not cover the cost to stabilize, excavate, fill or replace land. We do not cover loss to landscaping except to the extent that the damaged property is otherwise covered.

**W.16** We do not cover loss to property belonging to a tenant, boarder or roomer unless the property is otherwise insured under the policy. We do not cover loss to property held for sale or delivery. We do not cover loss to property rented to others when the loss is the responsibility of the renter.

**W.17** An insured must protect the damaged property from further loss. An insured must keep records of expenses incurred for reasonable emergency measures. We will consider those expenses only when they are incurred to protect covered property from additional covered damage.

**W.18** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement and defense of any claim. An insured must attend examinations under oath and provide requested statements when reasonably required. An insured must give us all available information concerning the cause and extent of the loss.

**W.19** We may require a sworn proof of loss stating the interest of the insured and of all persons claiming an interest in the property. The proof of loss must describe the damaged property and the cause of loss. We may require supporting records concerning ownership, value and repair.

**W.20** We may investigate the cause of loss and inspect the premises where the loss occurred. We may take samples or conduct testing when reasonably necessary to determine the cause or extent of damage. We do not waive any policy provision by conducting an investigation or inspection.

**W.21** We do not cover loss that results from intentional acts committed by or at the direction of an insured. We do not cover loss caused by concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact. We do not cover loss when an insured makes fraudulent statements concerning the claim.

**W.22** We do not cover loss to property that is removed from the residence premises and not protected from damage. We do not cover loss that occurs while property is being altered, repaired or renovated, unless the loss is otherwise covered. We do not cover the cost of correcting faulty workmanship or defective materials.

**W.23** We may settle a claim with you or with another claimant whose interest is protected under the policy. Payment to a mortgagee or other claimant will be made according to that claimant's interest. Payment does not transfer to us any right to recover from a person responsible for the loss.

**W.24** You must preserve our right to recover from any person responsible for the loss. You must not impair that right after the loss. We retain our right to recover amounts we pay for covered loss when another person is legally responsible.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** We apply a deductible to covered loss caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow. You must satisfy the deductible before we pay for covered loss. The deductible applies only as provided by this coverage.

**W.2** We determine whether the reported loss resulted from water backup or sump discharge or overflow. We apply this provision when the loss is covered under this coverage. We do not apply this provision to loss that is not covered.

**W.3** The deductible applicable to covered water backup loss is five hundred dollars. Except that we apply the deductible only to covered loss subject to this coverage. We do not reduce the deductible because the covered loss involves more than one item of property.

**W.4** You must provide us with reasonable information about the property damaged by the covered loss. You must identify the location and nature of the damage. We may use the information you provide to determine the amount of covered loss.

**W.5** We apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss after we determine the amount of loss. The deductible does not increase the amount of covered loss. We do not pay the deductible as part of the amount of loss.

**W.6** You must cooperate with our investigation of the covered loss. Your cooperation must include permitting reasonable inspection of damaged property. We may require reasonable documentation supporting the amount of loss.

**W.7** We apply the deductible to covered property loss and covered additional living expense loss arising from the same covered cause of loss. The deductible applies before we determine the amount payable under this coverage. We do not apply the deductible to property or expenses that are not covered.

**W.8** You must take reasonable steps to protect the property from further damage. You must keep records of reasonable expenses incurred for those steps. We may consider those expenses in determining the amount of covered loss.

**W.9** We do not cover loss that results from your failure to take reasonable steps to protect the property from further damage. This exclusion applies to the portion of loss that could have been avoided by reasonable action. We may pay covered loss that was not caused by that failure.

**W.10** We determine the amount of covered loss without regard to the deductible. We then subtract the deductible from the amount otherwise payable. We do not subtract the deductible more than as permitted by this provision.

**W.11** You must promptly notify us of a loss to which this coverage may apply. You must describe the circumstances of the loss and the property affected. Prompt notice does not change the deductible.

**W.12** We may request access to the residence premises and to property affected by the loss. You must allow us to inspect the property at a reasonable time. We may request additional inspection when reasonably necessary to evaluate the loss.

**W.13** You must preserve damaged property for our inspection when it is reasonably possible to do so. You must not discard property that may be relevant to the amount or cause of loss. We do not waive any right by inspecting or accepting damaged property.

**W.14** We may repair or replace damaged property in accordance with the applicable coverage provisions. Any repair or replacement remains subject to the deductible. We do not pay more than the amount otherwise payable for covered loss.

**W.15** We determine the amount payable after applying all applicable conditions and exclusions. We apply the deductible to the amount that remains covered. We do not apply the deductible to amounts excluded from coverage.

**W.16** You must provide receipts, estimates, invoices, and other reasonable records supporting your claim. The records must identify the property, services, or expenses for which payment is requested. We may withhold payment for amounts that are not reasonably supported.

**W.17** We may settle the covered loss with you or with a person having an interest in the damaged property. Payment to a person having an interest in the property does not increase the amount payable. The deductible remains applicable to the covered loss.

**W.18** You must not voluntarily assume an obligation or incur an expense that is not reasonably necessary to protect covered property. You must obtain our consent when consent is reasonably required under the applicable coverage provisions. We do not pay an expense that is not covered.

**W.19** We do not cover damage caused by your failure to use reasonable means to prevent the recurrence of water backup or sump discharge or overflow. This exclusion applies when reasonable means were available to you. We may cover resulting loss that is otherwise covered and not excluded.

**W.20** We may require you to provide a signed proof of loss describing the property and the amount claimed. You must provide the proof of loss within the time required by the applicable coverage provisions. A proof of loss does not establish that the claimed amount is covered.

**W.21** We determine the amount of covered loss based on the condition of the property at the time of loss. We may consider the cost of reasonable emergency measures. We do not include expenses that are unrelated to the covered loss.

**W.22** You must cooperate with reasonable efforts to prevent additional water from entering the residence premises. You must promptly report conditions that may cause additional damage. We may inspect measures taken to protect the property.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by neglect, meaning your failure to use reasonable means to preserve property before or after a loss. This exclusion applies only to loss caused by the neglect. We may cover loss that is otherwise covered and independent of the neglect.

**W.24** We apply this deductible only after determining that the loss falls within the coverage provided for water backup or sump discharge or overflow. We do not apply this provision to a loss covered solely under another coverage. We may allocate a loss when covered and noncovered causes are involved.

**W.25** You must assist us in obtaining records and information reasonably related to the loss. You must answer reasonable questions about the cause, timing, and extent of the damage. We may use the information obtained to evaluate coverage and the amount payable.

**W.26** We may make payment for covered loss after completing our investigation and determining the amount payable. Payment is subject to your compliance with applicable conditions. Payment does not waive any exclusion or condition that applies to another loss.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** The "water backup coverage" does not extend to loss that is not caused by a covered backup, discharge, or overflow. We do not cover loss merely because water is present at the insured location.

**W.2** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water arising from a flood condition.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by water that seeps, leaks, migrates, or escapes through foundations, walls, floors, or other structural surfaces. This exclusion applies whether the water is below or above the ground.

**W.4** We do not cover loss caused by water pressure, hydrostatic pressure, or subsurface water. This exclusion applies even if the pressure causes water to enter a building.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by dampness, condensation, humidity, or atmospheric moisture. We also do not cover resulting mold, fungi, wet rot, dry rot, or microbial growth.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by the repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies when the seepage or leakage occurs from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler systems, or domestic appliances.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, rust, corrosion, or decay. We also do not cover loss caused by latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or inherent vice.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether the work is performed by you or by another person.

**W.9** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, or improve a sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, plumbing system, appliance, or other device that failed or malfunctioned. We also do not cover the cost to correct a condition that caused the backup, discharge, or overflow.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by a blocked, clogged, collapsed, broken, deteriorated, or improperly maintained sewer or drain. This exclusion applies to property outside the residence premises as well as property within a building.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by a power interruption, power surge, electrical failure, or loss of utility service. We also do not cover loss caused by the failure of a generator, battery, alarm, or backup power source.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a sump pump or related equipment to operate because of a mechanical defect or inadequate maintenance. We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an automatic control, float, switch, or alarm.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by intentional discharge of water by you, an "insured," or another person acting at your direction. This exclusion applies whether the discharge is negligent, deliberate, or malicious.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by water discharged during construction, repair, alteration, or maintenance of a building or plumbing system. We also do not cover loss caused by testing, draining, flushing, or servicing such property.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudslide, sinkhole collapse, subsidence, settling, or earth sinking. This exclusion applies whether the earth movement is natural or human made.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by volcanic eruption, volcanic activity, or volcanic material. We also do not cover loss caused by ash, dust, or particulate matter arising from volcanic activity.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by war, civil disturbance, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or governmental action. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause that contributes to the loss.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, or radioactive waste. We also do not cover loss caused by the discharge or escape of nuclear material.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss, fraud, concealment, or misrepresentation by an insured. This exclusion applies before or after a loss.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage when reasonably possible.

**W.21** We do not cover loss to property while it is vacant if the vacancy materially increases the hazard of water damage. An insured must maintain reasonable access to the property for inspection and emergency response.

**W.22** We do not cover loss to property used for business, manufacturing, farming, or other commercial activity, except as otherwise provided by the policy. This exclusion applies to business personal property and business income.

**W.23** We do not cover loss to property of roomers, boarders, tenants, or guests. We also do not cover loss to property owned by others that is in your care, custody, or control.

**W.24** We do not cover loss to land, including soil, lawns, trees, shrubs, plants, or landscaping. We also do not cover loss to outdoor property unless the loss results from a covered cause of loss.

**W.25** We do not cover loss to foundations, retaining walls, bulkheads, piers, pilings, docks, or wharves caused by water pressure or subsurface conditions. We also do not cover the cost to stabilize, shore up, or reinforce such property.

**W.26** We do not cover loss resulting from enforcement of an ordinance, law, regulation, or code that requires construction, demolition, repair, replacement, or removal. We also do not cover increased costs caused by compliance with such requirements.

**W.27** We do not cover loss resulting from the inability to use property that has not sustained direct physical loss. We also do not cover loss arising from delay, loss of market, loss of value, or loss of opportunity.

**W.28** We do not cover penalties, assessments, fines, sanctions, or other charges imposed by a governmental authority. We also do not cover costs arising from a contractual obligation assumed by you.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by pollutants, contaminants, chemicals, fumes, vapor, soot, or industrial waste. This exclusion applies to the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of such matter.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke, vapor, or emissions from industrial, agricultural, or commercial operations. We also do not cover cleanup, testing, monitoring, or disposal expenses associated with such matter.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by rodents, insects, birds, vermin, domestic animals, or other animals. We also do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, or waste from animals.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by theft, vandalism, malicious mischief, or criminal conduct. We also do not cover loss caused by acts or omissions of a person lawfully occupying the residence premises.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by defective or inadequate drainage systems located outside a building. We also do not cover the cost to install, enlarge, reroute, or improve drainage facilities.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by the overflow of a body of water, drainage channel, ditch, culvert, retention area, or irrigation system. This exclusion applies whether the water originates on or away from the residence premises.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by water entering through open windows, doors, vents, roofs, walls, or unfinished openings. We also do not cover loss caused by rain driven into a building by wind.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, expansion, contraction, or rupture of plumbing or related equipment. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat, water service, and protective systems when the residence premises are unoccupied.

**W.37** We do not cover loss that occurs after an insured has failed to comply with a duty imposed by this policy and the failure prejudices us. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim.

**W.38** The hurricane deductible floor does not create coverage for a loss otherwise excluded. It does not alter the exclusions stated in this section.

**W.39** The Coverage B percentage does not create coverage for property or loss otherwise excluded. It does not expand the property to which water backup coverage applies.

**W.40** The Coverage C limit as a percentage of Coverage A does not create coverage for property or loss otherwise excluded. It does not expand the causes of loss covered by this endorsement.

**W.41** The loss of use limit does not create coverage for loss otherwise excluded. We do not cover additional living expense or loss of rental value arising from an excluded loss.

**W.42** The debris removal additional amount does not create coverage for debris resulting from an excluded loss. We do not cover removal, transport, treatment, or disposal costs unless the underlying loss is covered.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties in this section after a "loss". We may deny coverage for prejudice resulting from material noncompliance.

**W.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss involving "water backup". Notice must identify the residence premises and describe the known circumstances.

**W.3** An insured must report the loss within thirty days. Notice to an authorized representative of ours is notice to us.

**W.4** An insured must protect "covered property" from further damage. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs when conditions require them.

**W.5** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard property that may assist in determining the cause or amount of loss.

**W.6** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve evidence concerning the source of water. An insured must permit us to inspect the premises and affected property.

**W.7** An insured must provide reasonable access to the premises. Access must include access to areas containing a drain, sewer line, water control device, or "sump".

**W.8** An insured must cooperate with us in investigating a loss. Cooperation includes providing information reasonably requested by us.

**W.9** An insured must identify each person with knowledge of the loss. An insured must provide contact information for those persons when available.

**W.10** An insured must provide records that support the ownership, value, age, condition, or use of damaged property. An insured must permit us to copy those records.

**W.11** An insured must provide receipts, invoices, estimates, photographs, and similar evidence reasonably available to support the claim. An insured must preserve electronic records in the form reasonably available.

**W.12** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as reasonably required. An insured must permit us to examine any relevant repair work.

**W.13** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably requested by us. An insured must answer questions concerning the loss truthfully and completely.

**W.14** An insured must produce requested documents during an examination under oath. An insured may have a representative present when permitted by applicable law.

**W.15** An insured must provide a signed statement describing the cause and circumstances of loss. The statement must identify known sources of water entering the premises.

**W.16** An insured must state whether any public authority, contractor, utility, neighbor, or other person may be responsible for the loss. An insured must provide available information concerning that responsibility.

**W.17** An insured must notify us of any demand, claim, notice, or proceeding arising from the same occurrence. An insured must send us copies of papers received in connection with that matter.

**W.18** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable measures to protect property from further damage. This duty does not prevent an insured from obtaining emergency services necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.19** An insured must keep a record of emergency measures and related expenses. The record must identify the work performed and the person performing it.

**W.20** An insured must provide a complete inventory of damaged personal property when requested by us. The inventory must describe the property and state its actual cash value and amount of loss.

**W.21** An insured must separate damaged property from undamaged property when reasonably possible. An insured must not commingle damaged property with replacement property before our inspection unless necessary to prevent further damage.

**W.22** An insured must preserve any failed appliance, pump, valve, pipe, or other component that may have contributed to the loss. An insured must not authorize disposal of that property without our consent when we have requested its preservation.

**W.23** An insured must provide maintenance records reasonably related to the claimed loss. An insured must provide repair records for water control systems when available.

**W.24** An insured must provide information concerning prior water intrusion, drainage conditions, or similar damage at the premises when requested by us. An insured must disclose known prior repairs addressing those conditions.

**W.25** An insured must notify us before permanent repairs are completed when reasonably possible. An insured must provide reasonable opportunity for us to inspect the damaged condition before it is altered.

**W.26** An insured must obtain reasonable estimates for repair or replacement when requested by us. An insured must provide the names and contact information of persons preparing those estimates.

**W.27** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to the insurance, the property, or the loss. We do not cover a loss when an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact.

**W.28** An insured must act in good faith in presenting a claim. An insured must not submit altered, fabricated, or misleading records.

**W.29** An insured must promptly notify us if additional damage is discovered. An insured must preserve evidence of the additional damage when reasonably possible.

**W.30** An insured must notify us if the source of water changes or recurs after notice of loss. An insured must take reasonable measures to limit resulting damage.

**W.31** An insured must make reasonable efforts to identify the source of a discharge or overflow. An insured must provide us with findings from inspections or investigations concerning that source.

**W.32** An insured must provide access to repair persons and other persons who inspected the premises for the loss. An insured must authorize those persons to provide relevant records to us when legally permitted.

**W.33** An insured must cooperate in obtaining information from public authorities concerning the loss. An insured must provide copies of notices, reports, or orders received from those authorities.

**W.34** An insured must preserve any available video, photographs, monitoring records, or electronic communications concerning the loss. An insured must provide copies in a reasonably usable form.

**W.35** An insured must notify us if any damaged property is subject to a lease, lien, security interest, or other interest held by another person. An insured must provide available documentation of that interest.

**W.36** An insured must assist us in protecting our rights against a person who may be liable for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to prejudice those rights.

**W.37** If we make payment, an insured must transfer to us any rights of recovery against another person to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate in any action we bring to enforce those rights.

**W.38** An insured must not abandon property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment or agreement with an insured.

**W.39** An insured must retain salvage when requested by us. We may require reasonable access to salvage for inspection, valuation, or disposition.

**W.40** An insured must permit us to inspect repairs, replacements, and temporary protective measures. An insured must provide related invoices and proof of payment when requested by us.

**W.41** An insured must provide a signed "proof of loss" when requested by us. The proof of loss must be submitted within the "proof of loss period".

**W.42** An insured must state in the proof of loss the time and cause of loss, the interest of all insureds and others in the property, and all applicable encumbrances. An insured must state the amount claimed and the supporting basis for that amount.

**W.43** An insured must include in the proof of loss any known information concerning other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must provide copies of applicable policies when reasonably requested by us.

**W.44** An insured must report any payment received or expected from another source for the same damage. We may reduce payment to prevent duplicate recovery.

**W.45** We will apply the "minimum Section I deductible" to a covered loss as provided by the insurance. An insured must pay the applicable deductible portion of the loss.

**W.46** We will apply the "eighty percent condition" when it applies to the covered property and loss. An insured must provide information necessary for us to determine its application.

**W.47** We may investigate a loss without waiving any right under the insurance. Our investigation does not constitute acceptance of coverage.

**W.48** We may request reasonable additional information after receiving notice of loss. An insured must provide the requested information without unreasonable delay.

**W.49** We may require an insured to submit to a medical examination when a bodily injury claim arising from the loss is presented and applicable law permits the examination. We will select the examining professional and pay the reasonable cost of the examination.

**W.50** An insured must promptly notify us of a change in address, telephone number, or electronic contact information during claim handling. We may rely on the most recent contact information provided by an insured.

**W.51** An insured must keep the premises reasonably available for inspection until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect. An insured must notify us before conditions at the premises materially change.

**W.52** An insured must provide us with information regarding repairs made before the loss that may relate to the claimed damage. An insured must identify the persons who performed those repairs when known.

**W.53** An insured must provide records showing ownership of damaged property when ownership is disputed or unclear. Such records may include purchase records, photographs, or other reliable evidence.

**W.54** An insured must permit us to obtain a recorded statement when reasonably requested. The statement must concern matters relevant to the loss or claim.

**W.55** An insured must notify us if a governmental authority restricts access to the premises or requires remediation. An insured must provide available written directives concerning those restrictions or requirements.

**W.56** An insured must take reasonable measures to prevent mold, deterioration, or other additional damage after a covered loss. We do not cover avoidable additional damage resulting from an insured's failure to take those measures.

**W.57** An insured must provide records supporting any claim for increased costs caused by a required repair method. An insured must identify the authority or condition requiring that method when known.

**W.58** An insured must promptly advise us of any disagreement concerning the scope, cause, or value of loss. An insured must provide the basis for the disagreement and supporting information reasonably available.

**W.59** An insured must not bring a legal action against us unless the insured has complied with all applicable duties under the insurance. An insured must commence any action within the applicable "legal action deadline".

**W.60** We may deny a claim when material information needed to determine coverage or amount of loss is not provided after reasonable request. We will consider information later provided, subject to our rights and applicable law.

**W.61** An insured must reimburse us for any payment obtained through fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation. Our right to reimbursement is in addition to any other remedy available to us.

**W.62** An insured must notify us if another insurer, governmental program, warranty provider, or service provider may owe payment for the loss. An insured must cooperate in coordinating applicable payments.

**W.63** An insured must execute documents reasonably necessary to confirm payment, transfer recovery rights, or resolve ownership of salvage. An insured must not unreasonably withhold execution of those documents.

**W.64** An insured must comply with lawful requests made by us to evaluate a claim. We may request that communications and records be provided in a reasonably understandable form.

**W.65** Compliance with these conditions is a condition precedent to coverage when permitted by applicable law. We will not deny coverage for immaterial noncompliance that does not prejudice our rights.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** This coverage applies only to direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a "water backup" or a "sump discharge or overflow." An insured must comply with the requirements stated in this section.

**W.2** "Water backup" means water or waterborne material that enters a structure through a sewer, drain, or similar system because of backup or overflow. Water backup includes water that reverses flow from a drainage system into covered property.

**W.3** "Sump discharge or overflow" means water released, discharged, or overflowed from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. An insured must take reasonable steps to keep such equipment capable of performing its intended function.

**W.4** "Finished areas below grade" means areas within a structure that are improved for occupancy and located below the surrounding ground level. Such areas include improvements, fixtures, and personal property located within those areas.

**W.5** An insured must maintain drainage systems serving covered property in a serviceable condition. An insured must remove accumulations that obstruct the normal flow of water through those systems.

**W.6** A "backwater valve" is not required where finished areas below grade are protected under this coverage. Any backwater valve installed at the residence premises must be maintained in serviceable condition.

**W.7** An insured must operate a backwater valve in accordance with its intended purpose. An insured must not disable, bypass, remove, or alter a backwater valve in a manner that impairs its operation.

**W.8** An insured must maintain any sump pump serving covered property in accordance with the equipment manufacturer's instructions. An insured must replace worn, damaged, or inoperative components when reasonably necessary.

**W.9** An insured must keep accessible any sump, drain opening, cleanout, or other component needed to inspect or service a drainage system. An insured must not permanently cover or conceal those components in a manner that prevents reasonable access.

**W.10** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent water from entering covered property after discovering a condition that may result in water backup or sump discharge or overflow. Those steps may include shutting off affected equipment, removing obstructions, or obtaining necessary service.

**W.11** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We may inspect the property and examine the measures taken to protect it.

**W.12** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged equipment, components, and materials before they are discarded or repaired.

**W.13** An insured must provide us with information reasonably requested concerning the condition, maintenance, repair, or replacement of a drainage system or sump equipment. The information must be accurate and complete to the best of the insured's knowledge.

**W.14** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation of a claim. An insured must provide access to covered property when we reasonably request access for inspection or evaluation.

**W.15** An insured must preserve records, invoices, service reports, photographs, and other documentation relevant to a claimed loss when reasonably possible. An insured must provide those materials to us upon request.

**W.16** An insured must notify us of a loss as soon as reasonably possible. The notice must describe the known circumstances of the loss and the property involved.

**W.17** An insured must take reasonable steps to identify the source of water entering covered property. An insured must not conceal, destroy, or materially alter evidence concerning that source.

**W.18** An insured must not make repairs that prevent us from determining the cause or extent of loss unless immediate repairs are necessary to protect covered property. An insured must preserve reasonable evidence of any necessary repair.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's intentional disabling or obstruction of a drainage system, sump pump, or backwater valve. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's intentional release of water into covered property.

**W.20** We do not cover loss resulting from an insured's concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact concerning a claimed loss. This provision applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after a loss.

**W.21** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take reasonable measures to protect covered property without assuming ownership or responsibility for its care.

**W.22** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense in connection with a loss without our consent, except for reasonable measures necessary to protect covered property from further damage. We will not be bound by an obligation assumed without our consent.

**W.23** An insured must submit to examination by us when reasonably required in connection with a claim. An insured must answer material questions truthfully and provide requested information within the insured's possession or control.

**W.24** An insured must promptly notify us if another person may be responsible for the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in preserving any rights of recovery against that person.

**W.25** Our inspection of covered property does not constitute a representation that the property, drainage system, sump equipment, or backwater valve is safe, serviceable, or adequate. Our inspection does not relieve an insured of any duty under this section.

**W.26** Our payment of a claim does not waive any requirement under this section. A waiver is effective only if made by us in writing.

**W.27** The duties of an insured apply to each person seeking coverage for a loss. The act or omission of an insured may affect coverage available to that insured.

**W.28** If an insured fails to comply with a requirement of this section, we may deny coverage to the extent the failure prejudices our ability to investigate, evaluate, or resolve the claim. We retain all rights available under this policy.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** This provision governs settlement of a "covered loss" to "covered property" under this endorsement. A "covered loss" is direct physical loss for which coverage is available under this endorsement.

**W.2** We settle a covered loss to "personal property" on the following basis: actual cash value. We determine the amount of loss from the condition of the property immediately before the loss and the extent of direct physical damage.

**W.3** We may inspect damaged property and require its preservation until we complete our evaluation. An insured must permit us reasonable access to the property and to records relevant to the loss.

**W.4** An insured must provide information that identifies the damaged property and describes its condition, ownership, use, and value. An insured must provide available receipts, photographs, estimates, records, and other supporting information we reasonably request.

**W.5** We will determine the loss settlement amount using the facts available to us at the time of adjustment. We may consider the age, condition, quality, utility, and remaining useful life of damaged property.

**W.6** We will not include the cost of repairing property that was not physically damaged by the covered loss. We will not include costs attributable to wear, deterioration, defect, maintenance, or prior damage.

**W.7** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss. Reasonable expenses incurred solely to protect covered property from further damage may be included in the settlement when they are necessary and supported by the loss information.

**W.8** We may require an insured to retain damaged property, remnants, and salvage for our inspection. An insured must not discard property that is material to our evaluation unless preservation is not reasonably possible.

**W.9** We may obtain estimates for repair, restoration, or replacement of damaged property. We are not bound by an estimate obtained by an insured.

**W.10** We may use available market information, repair information, and valuation information to determine the loss settlement amount. We may adjust an amount when the information submitted by an insured is incomplete, inaccurate, or inconsistent with the observed damage.

**W.11** We will not pay more than the amount of the direct physical loss to covered property. We will not duplicate payment for the same damage under this endorsement.

**W.12** We may make payment to an insured, to a person legally entitled to receive payment, or to a person with an interest in covered property. Payment to a person with an interest in covered property does not create coverage that otherwise does not apply.

**W.13** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a covered loss. An insured must answer reasonable questions and provide requested information concerning the loss.

**W.14** We may require an insured to show the damaged property or make it available for examination. We may photograph, test, measure, or otherwise examine property relevant to the loss.

**W.15** We may take possession of damaged property after payment if we obtain an interest in that property. Our possession of property does not waive any condition or limitation applicable to the covered loss.

**W.16** We will reduce the amount otherwise payable by amounts received from persons responsible for the damage. An insured must preserve our rights of recovery against any person responsible for the loss.

**W.17** We will not pay for any improvement, betterment, or increased value that is not required to restore damaged property after the covered loss. We will not pay for changes made solely for convenience, preference, or aesthetic reasons.

**W.18** When damaged property is part of a set, pair, or collection, we will consider the effect of the direct physical damage on the value of the property that was damaged. We will not pay for reduction in value to property that was not physically damaged unless coverage requires that payment.

**W.19** We may settle a covered loss by agreement with an insured after completing our evaluation. An agreement as to part of a loss does not require agreement as to any remaining part of the loss.

**W.20** Payment of a claim does not waive any term, condition, limitation, or exclusion of this endorsement. Our investigation, inspection, or request for information does not waive any right available to us.

**W.21** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold material information concerning a covered loss. We may deny payment to the extent an insured's material act or omission prejudices our ability to determine the loss.

**W.22** We may require proof that an insured owned or had an insurable interest in damaged property. We may require proof of the amount claimed and of the cause and extent of damage.

**W.23** We will apply any deductible that applies to the covered loss. We will apply the deductible only once to the same covered loss unless separate application is required by the terms of this endorsement.

**W.24** Payment under this provision is subject to the applicable coverage limit. We will not pay an amount that exceeds the amount available under this endorsement.

**W.25** We will not increase the amount payable because an insured elects not to repair, restore, or replace damaged property. The settlement amount remains subject to the basis stated in this provision.

**W.26** An insured must provide notice of any payment received from another source for the same damage. We may adjust our payment to prevent duplicate recovery.

**W.27** The "assessment coverage limit" does not increase the amount payable for a covered loss under this endorsement. We will determine settlement under this provision without combining that limit with the coverage available here.

**W.28** A "fire response charge" is not treated as repair, restoration, or replacement expense for covered property under this endorsement. We will not include that charge in the loss settlement amount.

**W.29** The "special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones" does not alter settlement of a covered loss under this endorsement. We will determine the loss settlement amount without treating that special limit as additional coverage.

**W.30** The "home business contents limit" remains applicable when it applies to covered property. We will not treat that limit as increasing the coverage available under this endorsement.

**W.31** We may defer payment of any disputed portion of a claim while we continue to investigate that portion. We may pay any undisputed portion when the information necessary to support payment is available.

**W.32** An insured must preserve records and evidence relevant to a covered loss until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect and evaluate them. Failure to preserve material evidence may affect the amount we are able to determine.

**W.33** We may rely on information obtained from an insured, a contractor, a supplier, a public authority, or another reliable source. We may request clarification when available information does not reasonably establish the amount of loss.

**W.34** We will settle each covered loss separately based on the property damaged and the circumstances of that loss. Damage from separate causes will not be combined solely to increase the amount payable.

**W.35** Our payment under this provision is final only as to the amount paid for the covered loss. All rights and duties under this endorsement remain applicable after payment.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** “Water Backup” means the backup of water or waterborne material through a “Sewer” or “Drain.” Water Backup includes backup caused by obstruction, surcharge, blockage, or failure of a Sewer or Drain.

**W.2** “Sewer” means a conduit, pipe, channel, or similar facility that carries wastewater, storm water, or waterborne material away from property. Sewer includes a public or private sanitary sewer, storm sewer, or combined sewer.

**W.3** “Drain” means a pipe, channel, fixture drain, or similar component designed to remove water or waterborne material from a building or premises. Drain includes a floor drain, foundation drain, roof drain, and plumbing drain.

**W.4** Water Backup does not include water that enters through an opening in a roof, wall, door, window, foundation, or other part of a building. Water Backup also does not include surface water that flows over land before entering property.

**W.5** “Sump Discharge” means the release of water from a “Sump,” “Sump Pump,” or “Related Equipment.” Sump Discharge includes water released because the equipment operates, fails to operate, malfunctions, or is unable to discharge water.

**W.6** “Sump” means a pit, basin, reservoir, well, or similar receptacle designed to collect water. A Sump may be located within or outside a building.

**W.7** “Sump Pump” means a pump designed to remove water collected in a Sump. Sump Pump includes a pump operated by electrical, mechanical, battery, or other power.

**W.8** “Related Equipment” means equipment, piping, valves, switches, alarms, discharge lines, or connections used with a Sump or Sump Pump. Related Equipment includes equipment intended to direct water away from property.

**W.9** “Sump Overflow” means the escape of water from a Sump because the Sump cannot contain water received by it. Sump Overflow includes water escaping over the edge of a Sump or through an opening in the Sump.

**W.10** Sump Discharge or Sump Overflow does not include water that is intentionally released from a Sump, Sump Pump, or Related Equipment. It also does not include water released during maintenance, testing, repair, alteration, or replacement of that equipment.

**W.11** “Water” means liquid in any form, including rainwater, groundwater, wastewater, storm water, and water mixed with sewage or other material. Water includes liquid that contains dissolved, suspended, or transported material.

**W.12** “Waterborne Material” means sewage, waste, debris, sediment, soil, organic matter, or other material carried by Water. Waterborne Material includes material deposited by Water Backup, Sump Discharge, or Sump Overflow.

**W.13** “Direct Physical Loss” means accidental physical injury to tangible property. Direct Physical Loss includes resulting loss of use of physically injured property when that loss of use is caused by the physical injury.

**W.14** Direct Physical Loss does not include loss caused solely by diminished value, loss of market value, inconvenience, or loss of enjoyment. Direct Physical Loss does not include the cost to correct a condition that has not caused physical injury to property.

**W.15** “Property Damage” means Direct Physical Loss to property. Property Damage includes contamination, staining, warping, deterioration, corrosion, decay, or other physical alteration caused by Water Backup, Sump Discharge, or Sump Overflow.

**W.16** “Building” means a structure and its permanently installed fixtures, machinery, and equipment. Building includes materials and supplies intended for use in construction, alteration, or repair of the structure.

**W.17** “Personal Property” means movable property owned or used by an insured. Personal Property includes contents of a Building that are not permanently installed.

**W.18** “Covered Property” means Building or Personal Property for which coverage is provided under this policy. Covered Property does not include property that is not insured under this policy.

**W.19** “Insured Location” means premises shown in the policy declarations and premises used by an insured in connection with those premises. Insured Location includes land and structures that are part of the premises.

**W.20** “Residence Premises” means the dwelling used by you as a residence and the land on which the dwelling is located. Residence Premises includes structures and property used in connection with the dwelling.

**W.21** “Plumbing System” means pipes, fixtures, drains, valves, traps, fittings, and related equipment used to distribute or remove Water within a Building. Plumbing System includes permanently installed water supply and waste removal components.

**W.22** Plumbing System does not include a Sewer or Drain located beyond the point at which the system connects to a public or private sewer facility. It does not include a Sump, Sump Pump, or Related Equipment unless that equipment is permanently installed as part of the Building.

**W.23** “Failure” means the inability of equipment to perform its intended function. Failure includes cessation of operation, intermittent operation, impaired operation, and operation that does not produce the intended result.

**W.24** “Malfunction” means defective, irregular, or improper operation of equipment. Malfunction includes operation caused by a mechanical defect, electrical defect, control defect, or installation defect.

**W.25** “Obstruction” means a blockage, restriction, accumulation, collapse, displacement, or other condition that impedes the flow of Water or Waterborne Material. Obstruction includes a condition within a Sewer, Drain, discharge line, or Related Equipment.

**W.26** “Surcharge” means a condition in which Water or Waterborne Material exceeds the carrying capacity of a Sewer or Drain. Surcharge includes pressure or flow that causes Water or Waterborne Material to reverse direction.

**W.27** “Public Sewer” means a Sewer owned, maintained, operated, or controlled by a governmental authority or utility. Public Sewer includes a sewer system made available for public use.

**W.28** “Private Sewer” means a Sewer not owned, maintained, operated, or controlled by a governmental authority or utility. Private Sewer includes a sewer serving an Insured Location or adjoining property.

**W.29** “Surface Water” means Water on the surface of the ground, including Water from rain, runoff, melting snow, overflow of a body of water, or drainage across land. Surface Water retains that character until it enters a Sewer, Drain, Sump, or Building.

**W.30** “Groundwater” means Water beneath the surface of the ground. Groundwater includes Water that seeps, flows, migrates, rises, or is forced through soil, rock, foundation material, or other subsurface material.

**W.31** “Sewage” means waste material carried by or contained in Water. Sewage includes human waste, household waste, industrial waste, and Waterborne Material associated with waste removal.

**W.32** “Accidental” means neither expected nor intended from the standpoint of an insured. Accidental does not include an act or omission committed with the purpose of causing Property Damage.

**W.33** Words and phrases used in the singular include the plural when required by the context. Words and phrases used in the plural include the singular when required by the context.
