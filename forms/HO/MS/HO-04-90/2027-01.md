# HO 04 90 Water Backup and Sump Discharge or Overflow (Edition 2027-01)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

**W.1** This "endorsement" modifies the insurance provided under your "policy". It is effective only when attached to your policy.

**W.2** This endorsement forms part of your policy. The provisions of this endorsement apply only to the extent stated in this endorsement.

**W.3** This endorsement addresses coverage for direct physical loss involving "water backup" and "sump discharge or overflow". Coverage is subject to the terms of this endorsement.

**W.4** If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy, this endorsement controls. Any provision of your policy not modified by this endorsement remains unchanged.

**W.5** The terms of your policy apply to this endorsement unless this endorsement provides otherwise. We apply those terms consistently with the provisions of this endorsement.

**W.6** This endorsement does not create insurance for a loss that is not covered under your policy or this endorsement. We provide coverage only as stated in this endorsement and your policy.

**W.7** An insured must comply with all duties applicable to a loss for which coverage is sought. Compliance with those duties is a condition of coverage.

**W.8** Your rights and duties under your policy apply to this endorsement. Our rights and duties under your policy also apply to this endorsement.

**W.9** This endorsement does not alter the identity of an insured under your policy. It applies only to an insured entitled to coverage under your policy.

**W.10** The application of this endorsement depends on the facts and circumstances of a claimed loss. We determine coverage in accordance with the applicable policy provisions and this endorsement.

**W.11** We do not cover loss except as provided by the terms of your policy as modified by this endorsement. No provision of this endorsement may be construed to provide broader coverage than its terms state.

**W.12** The attachment of this endorsement does not establish a separate contract of insurance. This endorsement and your policy are construed together.

**W.13** This endorsement remains subject to the conditions, exclusions, and limitations that apply to your policy unless modified by this endorsement. We will administer this endorsement as part of your policy.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** For purposes of this coverage, "Water Backup" means water that backs up through a sewer or drain. Water Backup includes waterborne material carried by that water.

**W.2** We cover direct physical loss to property insured by this policy that is caused by Water Backup. Coverage under this paragraph is subject to the limit insubject to the limit in (see W.2, Limit of Liability).

**W.3** For purposes of this coverage, "Sump Discharge or Overflow" means water that discharges or overflows from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. Coverage for Sump Discharge or Overflow is subject to the limit insubject to the limit in (see W.2, Limit of Liability).

**W.4** We cover direct physical loss to property insured by this policy that is caused by Sump Discharge or Overflow. The discharge or overflow must originate from equipment located on the residence premises.

**W.5** "Direct Physical Loss" means accidental physical damage to tangible property. Direct Physical Loss includes resulting damage caused by covered water.

**W.6** A "Sump System" means a sump, sump pump, pit, basin, discharge line, valve, alarm, and related equipment used to collect, contain, remove, or direct water. A Sump System includes permanently installed equipment and its connected components.

**W.7** Water Backup may occur through a sewer, drain, discharge line, or similar water-removal opening. The source of the backup need not be located on the residence premises.

**W.8** Sump Discharge or Overflow may result from mechanical failure, electrical interruption, obstruction, accidental activation, or inability of a Sump System to remove water. The cause of the discharge or overflow must be accidental.

**W.9** We cover loss caused by water that enters insured property after Water Backup. We also cover loss caused by water that enters insured property after Sump Discharge or Overflow.

**W.10** We cover damage to insured property caused by the force, weight, pressure, or movement of covered water. We cover damage caused by waterborne material that accompanies covered water.

**W.11** We cover reasonable and necessary measures taken to protect insured property from further direct physical loss after a covered Water Backup. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.12** We cover reasonable and necessary measures taken to protect insured property from further direct physical loss after a covered Sump Discharge or Overflow. An insured must permit us to inspect the affected location and damaged property.

**W.13** We cover loss caused by Water Backup that occurs suddenly or gradually. The loss must result in Direct Physical Loss during the policy period.

**W.14** We cover loss caused by Sump Discharge or Overflow that occurs suddenly or gradually. The loss must result in Direct Physical Loss during the policy period.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by flood. This exclusion applies whether flood contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by surface water that has not backed up through a sewer or drain. This exclusion applies even if surface water enters a sump, pit, basin, or similar opening.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground that seeps, leaks, flows, or presses through a building. This exclusion does not apply to loss otherwise caused by Water Backup.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a plumbing fixture, appliance, container, or vessel. This exclusion does not apply when the loss is caused by Water Backup or Sump Discharge or Overflow.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by a discharge from a roof drain, gutter, downspout, or exterior drainage system. This exclusion applies unless the discharge results directly from Water Backup.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an insured to maintain a Sump System. This exclusion applies when the failure to maintain materially contributes to the loss.

**W.21** An insured must use reasonable care to maintain a Sump System in serviceable condition. An insured must promptly repair known damage or malfunction affecting a Sump System.

**W.22** An insured must use reasonable care to keep accessible drains, discharge lines, and related openings free from known obstructions. This duty applies when access can be safely obtained.

**W.23** An insured must take reasonable steps to mitigate further damage after a Water Backup or Sump Discharge or Overflow. Reasonable steps may include removing standing water and protecting affected property.

**W.24** An insured must promptly notify us of a loss for which coverage is sought. Notice must include information reasonably available concerning the cause and extent of the loss.

**W.25** An insured must preserve evidence of the condition that caused or contributed to a claimed loss when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged equipment before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.26** An insured must provide records, photographs, invoices, receipts, and other information that we reasonably request to evaluate a claim. The requested information must relate to the claimed loss.

**W.27** We may inspect the residence premises, the Sump System, and damaged property after a reported loss. We may examine equipment, materials, and records relevant to the loss.

**W.28** We may require an insured to show the damaged property. We may require an insured to provide a statement concerning the circumstances of the loss.

**W.29** Coverage applies only when Water Backup or Sump Discharge or Overflow is the direct cause of Direct Physical Loss. Coverage does not apply to loss that would have occurred in the absence of the covered water event.

**W.30** Coverage applies to resulting damage caused by covered water. Coverage does not apply to repair or replacement of equipment solely because it failed, wore out, corroded, deteriorated, or became defective.

**W.31** We do not cover the cost to correct, redesign, upgrade, improve, or replace a sewer, drain, discharge line, or Sump System to prevent a future loss. This exclusion applies whether the work is performed before or after a covered loss.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property after a covered water event. This exclusion applies only to the additional loss caused by the neglect.

**W.33** We do not cover loss arising from business activity conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies only to property used primarily for business activity.

**W.34** We do not cover loss to property that is not insured by this policy. This coverage does not expand the classes of property insured by this policy.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance, law, regulation, or order requiring construction, repair, demolition, or removal. This exclusion applies even when the requirement follows a covered loss.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. This exclusion applies even if earth movement affects a sewer, drain, discharge line, or Sump System.

**W.37** We do not cover loss caused by intentional discharge, overflow, or diversion of water by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the act is performed directly or through another person.

**W.38** We do not cover loss caused by intentional damage to a sewer, drain, discharge line, or Sump System by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to an innocent insured who did not participate in or know of the act.

**W.39** We cover damage caused by Water Backup when the backup follows a blockage, collapse, break, or malfunction affecting a sewer or drain. The blockage, collapse, break, or malfunction need not occur on the residence premises.

**W.40** We cover damage caused by Sump Discharge or Overflow when a Sump System loses power or mechanical function. The loss of power or mechanical function must result in covered water entering insured property.

**W.41** We cover damage caused by water that backs up through an interior drain. We also cover damage caused by water that backs up through an exterior drain connected to a sewer or drainage system.

**W.42** We cover damage caused by water discharged from a Sump System into insured property. We also cover damage caused by water overflowing from a sump pit, basin, or related containment area.

**W.43** The existence of a Sump System does not by itself establish that a loss was caused by Sump Discharge or Overflow. An insured must establish that covered water caused Direct Physical Loss.

**W.44** The presence of water in insured property does not by itself establish that a loss was caused by Water Backup. An insured must establish that water backed up through a sewer or drain.

**W.45** We will determine the cause of loss from the facts available to us. Our determination may consider physical evidence, weather conditions, maintenance records, expert opinions, and information supplied by an insured.

**W.46** Payment for covered loss does not waive any condition, exclusion, or other provision of this policy. Our investigation of a loss does not confirm coverage.

**W.47** This coverage applies only to loss occurring during the policy period. The water event causing the loss must occur during the policy period.

**W.48** This coverage does not provide protection for loss that is otherwise excluded by this policy unless this coverage expressly provides otherwise. All policy provisions apply to this coverage unless modified by this section.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** This section applies to direct physical loss to covered property caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer or drain, or that discharges or overflows from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. It also applies to covered expenses arising from that loss.

**W.2** The most we will pay is ten thousand dollars. This amount applies subject to all terms and conditions of this policy.

**W.3** The amount stated in W.2 is the applicable limit for all covered loss subject to this section. It is not a separate limit for each item of covered property.

**W.4** Payments for direct physical loss reduce the amount available under the applicable limit. Payments for covered expenses also reduce the amount available under that limit.

**W.5** The applicable limit applies to all claims arising from the same water backup, sump discharge, or sump overflow. It applies regardless of the number of persons seeking payment.

**W.6** The applicable limit applies regardless of the number of insureds. The presence of more than one insured does not increase the amount available.

**W.7** The applicable limit applies regardless of the number of locations at which covered loss occurs. Loss affecting property at different locations does not increase the amount available.

**W.8** The applicable limit applies whether covered property is owned by you or by another person. The ownership interest in covered property does not increase the amount available.

**W.9** The applicable limit applies whether covered property is damaged, destroyed, or lost. The manner in which covered property is affected does not increase the amount available.

**W.10** The applicable limit applies to the cost to repair or replace covered property when payment for that cost is otherwise covered. The cost of repair or replacement does not increase the amount available.

**W.11** The applicable limit applies to the removal of damaged property when payment for that removal is otherwise covered. Removal expenses do not increase the amount available.

**W.12** The applicable limit applies to reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property from further damage when those expenses are otherwise covered. Such expenses do not increase the amount available.

**W.13** The applicable limit applies whether the covered loss is caused by a water backup, a sump discharge, a sump overflow, or any combination of those events. More than one cause does not increase the amount available.

**W.14** The applicable limit applies whether covered loss occurs at the same time or at different times. The timing of covered loss does not increase the amount available.

**W.15** Any deductible that applies to covered loss will be applied in accordance with this policy. Application of a deductible does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.16** Other insurance does not increase the applicable limit. We will apply this limit before determining any payment available under other insurance.

**W.17** Any recovery received from another person for covered loss will be applied in accordance with our rights under this policy. Such recovery does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.18** Payment under this section does not create coverage for loss that is not otherwise covered. Payment also does not enlarge the property or expenses for which coverage is provided.

**W.19** We do not cover any amount that exceeds the applicable limit. We do not have a duty to pay any additional amount after the applicable limit has been exhausted.

**W.20** A claim, suit, demand, or settlement does not increase the applicable limit. Expenses associated with a claim do not increase that limit unless coverage for those expenses is otherwise provided.

**W.21** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of covered loss. An insured must provide information and records we reasonably request to apply the applicable limit.

**W.22** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a water backup, sump discharge, or sump overflow. Reasonable protective measures do not increase the applicable limit.

**W.23** We may inspect damaged property and require reasonable documentation of covered loss. Our inspection or investigation does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.24** No action by us waives any provision of this section unless the waiver is in writing. A waiver of any provision does not increase the applicable limit.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "Deductible" applies to a "loss" covered by this endorsement. It applies when the loss is caused by "water backup".

**W.2** The Deductible that applies to a covered water backup loss is one thousand dollars. We will subtract the Deductible from the amount of covered loss.

**W.3** We will pay only that part of a covered loss that exceeds the Deductible. An insured must bear the part of the loss within the Deductible.

**W.4** We apply the Deductible to covered damage arising from the same "occurrence". We do not apply the Deductible separately to each item of damaged property arising from that occurrence.

**W.5** We apply the Deductible before determining the amount we will pay for covered loss. The Deductible does not increase the amount available for covered loss.

**W.6** We apply the Deductible whether or not an insured repairs damaged property. The Deductible applies to the covered loss as adjusted by us.

**W.7** We apply the Deductible to the amount of covered direct physical damage. We do not apply the Deductible to an amount that is not covered by this endorsement.

**W.8** An insured must provide information we reasonably require to determine the covered amount of loss. An insured must cooperate with us in determining the application of the Deductible.

**W.9** We may inspect damaged property to determine the covered amount of loss. Our inspection does not waive the Deductible.

**W.10** We may determine the covered amount of loss using available records, estimates, and other relevant information. An insured must make relevant records available to us upon request.

**W.11** A payment by us does not waive any right we have concerning the Deductible. A payment by us does not establish that a later loss is subject to the same adjustment.

**W.12** We apply the Deductible regardless of whether damage occurs at more than one location. We determine whether damage arises from the same occurrence based on the facts of the loss.

**W.13** The Deductible applies to covered damage to property owned by any insured. It also applies to covered damage to property for which an insured is legally responsible.

**W.14** We do not cover the part of a covered loss that is within the Deductible. This limitation applies before we make payment for the loss.

**W.15** An insured must not divide a loss into separate claims to avoid application of the Deductible. We may combine related damage arising from the same occurrence when applying the Deductible.

**W.16** An insured must not increase the amount of a claimed loss to offset the Deductible. We determine the covered amount of loss without regard to the Deductible.

**W.17** The Deductible applies whether a claim is presented by an insured or by another person on behalf of an insured. The Deductible applies to any payment we make for covered loss.

**W.18** We do not apply the Deductible to damage that is not covered by this endorsement. We do not pay for damage that is not covered by this endorsement.

**W.19** An insured must promptly report a covered loss to us. Delay in reporting does not eliminate the Deductible.

**W.20** An insured must protect damaged property from further damage when reasonably possible. The Deductible applies to covered loss resulting from water backup despite those protective efforts.

**W.21** We may adjust a covered loss with any insured having an interest in the damaged property. Such adjustment does not change the application of the Deductible.

**W.22** The Deductible applies if we settle a covered loss by payment, repair, replacement, or other lawful means. Our method of settlement does not affect the Deductible.

**W.23** An insured must submit information supporting the claimed amount of loss when requested by us. We may use that information to determine the covered amount of loss after application of the Deductible.

**W.24** We may reconsider a loss adjustment if additional relevant information becomes available. Any reconsideration remains subject to the Deductible.

**W.25** No insured may waive the Deductible on our behalf. Only we may agree in writing to modify the application of the Deductible.

**W.26** The Deductible applies separately to each covered occurrence. We determine the occurrence to which covered damage relates from the facts and circumstances of the loss.

**W.27** The Deductible remains applicable while this endorsement applies to the loss. Termination of this endorsement after a loss does not remove the Deductible from that loss.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover "direct physical loss" caused by "water backup" except as expressly provided by this endorsement. "Water backup" means water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer, drain, sump, or related equipment.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters a dwelling through an opening in a roof, wall, foundation, door, window, or other part of the building. This exclusion applies whether the opening existed before the water entered or resulted from deterioration, defect, or inadequate maintenance.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by seepage, leakage, or migration of water through foundations, walls, floors, slabs, roofs, or other building materials. This exclusion applies whether the water is visible or concealed.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water arising from those conditions.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or flows through a building or other structure.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a body of water. This exclusion applies whether the body of water is natural, artificial, or altered by human activity.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by runoff from precipitation, snowmelt, irrigation, or a similar source. This exclusion applies when the runoff enters the residence premises from outside the building.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters through a drain, pipe, conduit, opening, or channel located outside the building. This exclusion applies even if the water later enters a sewer, drain, sump, or related equipment.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by overflow from a drain, sewer, or plumbing system that is not connected to the insured location. This exclusion applies whether the overflow occurs on adjoining property or elsewhere.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by a discharge from a public sewer, public drain, or public water system unless the discharge is otherwise within the coverage provided by this endorsement. A failure by a public authority to maintain, repair, or operate its system does not create coverage.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by water that is intentionally released, diverted, retained, or discharged by any person or organization. This exclusion applies whether the release is authorized, accidental, negligent, or unlawful.

**W.14** We do not cover the cost to correct, improve, replace, or redesign a sewer, drain, sump, pump, pipe, valve, or related equipment because of wear, defect, inadequacy, or obsolescence. We also do not cover the cost to bring that property into compliance with a law, ordinance, code, or regulation.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, deterioration, decay, settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, expansion, or latent defect. This exclusion applies to the extent such condition causes or contributes to a discharge, overflow, or backup.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by neglect by an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property from further damage after a loss. We also do not cover loss caused by neglect to protect property from a known condition that is likely to cause water damage.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by repeated leakage, repeated seepage, or continuous discharge of water. This exclusion applies whether the condition is known or unknown to an insured.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, virus, or other microorganism. This exclusion applies even if such condition results from water backup, sump discharge, or sump overflow.

**W.19** We do not cover loss consisting of odor, staining, discoloration, contamination, or loss of use unless it results from direct physical loss otherwise covered by this endorsement. We do not cover the cost to test, monitor, assess, or remediate property for such condition unless required to repair covered direct physical loss.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including sinking, rising, shifting, erosion, landslide, mudflow, subsidence, or earth cracking. This exclusion applies whether earth movement is caused by water or any other source.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by collapse, unless the collapse is otherwise covered by this endorsement. Water backup, sump discharge, or sump overflow does not by itself make collapse covered.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by power failure, power surge, interruption of utility service, or failure of a utility connection. This exclusion applies even if the failure prevents operation of a sump pump or related equipment.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, electrical breakdown, or malfunction of a sump pump, alarm, switch, controller, battery, generator, or related equipment. We do not cover the failed equipment itself unless it is damaged by a covered cause of loss.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by inadequate capacity, improper installation, improper maintenance, or failure to operate a sump pump or related equipment. This exclusion applies whether the condition involves equipment, wiring, piping, drainage, or a backwater valve.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain a sewer line, drain line, plumbing system, sump pit, or related equipment in serviceable condition. We do not cover the cost to remove an obstruction, root intrusion, grease buildup, scale, sediment, or other material from that property.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, or expansion of water within a pipe, drain, sewer, sump, pump, tank, appliance, or related equipment. This exclusion applies unless the resulting discharge is otherwise covered by this endorsement.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by backup from a septic system, cesspool, leaching field, or similar private waste disposal system. This exclusion applies whether the system is located on or away from the insured location.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by overflow from a swimming pool, hot tub, spa, fountain, aquarium, waterbed, appliance, or container. This exclusion applies unless the overflow is directly caused by a covered water backup.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by water that escapes from a roof drain, gutter, downspout, rain leader, or exterior drainage system. This exclusion applies whether the system is clogged, disconnected, damaged, or inadequate.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by water that accumulates on roofs, balconies, decks, patios, driveways, walkways, or other exterior surfaces. This exclusion applies when the water enters a building through an exterior opening or building material.

**W.32** We do not cover loss to land, lawns, trees, shrubs, plants, landscaping, outdoor fixtures, walkways, driveways, retaining walls, fences, or docks. We do not cover the cost to restore grading, drainage, or soil conditions.

**W.33** We do not cover loss to property that is not "covered property." This exclusion applies even if the property is damaged by water that otherwise would be within the coverage provided by this endorsement.

**W.34** We do not cover loss to property owned by, rented to, occupied by, or in the care of a tenant, boarder, guest, or other person unless that property is covered property. We do not cover damage to property of a landlord arising from a tenant's act or omission.

**W.35** We do not cover loss to property held for sale, entrusted to an insured for sale, or used in a business activity. This exclusion applies to stock, samples, merchandise, tools, equipment, records, and business personal property.

**W.36** We do not cover loss to money, securities, accounts, bills, deeds, evidences of debt, manuscripts, or electronic data. We also do not cover the cost to recreate, replace, restore, or reproduce records or data.

**W.38** We do not cover loss to property while it is away from the residence premises unless the property is covered property and the loss otherwise satisfies the terms of this endorsement. Water backup at another location does not by itself establish coverage.

**W.39** We do not cover loss resulting from an act or decision of a governmental body, regulatory authority, or other organization. This exclusion applies whether the act or decision concerns drainage, sewer service, utility service, access, repair, demolition, or occupancy.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, civil disturbance, or hostile action. This exclusion applies regardless of any water-related condition that follows or contributes to the loss.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard, radioactive contamination, or radioactive waste. This exclusion applies whether the contamination is carried by water or any other medium.

**W.43** We do not cover loss caused by intentional, dishonest, criminal, or malicious acts of an insured. This exclusion applies whether the act is committed alone or in concert with another person.

**W.44** We do not cover loss caused by failure to disclose a known drainage, sewer, sump, plumbing, or water intrusion condition when required in connection with this insurance. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss.

**W.45** We do not cover loss if an insured conceals or misrepresents a material fact concerning the loss. An insured must provide truthful information and reasonably requested records relating to the claimed damage.

**W.46** We do not cover loss if an insured fails to permit us to inspect damaged property before it is repaired, removed, altered, or discarded, when inspection is reasonably necessary. An insured must preserve damaged property to the extent reasonably possible.

**W.47** We do not cover loss if an insured fails to take reasonable steps to stop an active discharge, overflow, or backup. An insured must protect covered property from further damage when reasonably able to do so.

**W.48** We do not cover charges for services that are not necessary to investigate, mitigate, repair, or restore covered direct physical loss. We do not cover charges that are excessive, unsupported, duplicated, or unrelated to covered property.

**W.49** We do not cover a loss merely because the wind percentage deductible minimum applies. The wind percentage deductible minimum does not expand coverage for water-related damage.

**W.50** We do not cover property merely because a Coverage B limit as a percentage of Coverage A applies. A Coverage B limit as a percentage of Coverage A does not expand coverage under this endorsement.

**W.51** We do not cover property merely because a personal property limit percentage applies. A personal property limit percentage does not expand coverage for water backup, sump discharge, or sump overflow.

**W.52** We do not cover expense merely because an additional living expense limit applies. An additional living expense limit does not create coverage for a loss otherwise excluded.

**W.53** We do not cover expense merely because a clean-up allowance percentage applies. A clean-up allowance percentage does not create coverage for a loss otherwise excluded.

**W.54** We do not cover consequential loss, economic loss, loss of market value, loss of rental value, or diminution in value. This exclusion applies unless the loss is direct physical loss otherwise covered by this endorsement.

**W.55** We do not cover delay, loss of use, inconvenience, interruption, or inability to occupy property unless expressly covered by this endorsement. We do not cover expenses incurred solely to avoid inconvenience or delay.

**W.56** We do not cover loss caused by inability to obtain materials, labor, services, permits, inspections, or access to property. This exclusion applies even if the inability follows a covered water-related loss.

**W.59** We do not cover any claim based upon a cause of loss excluded by this section, regardless of any other cause or event that contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss. This provision applies to the full extent permitted by law.

**W.60** We do not cover any loss that is excluded by this section, even if water backup, sump discharge, or sump overflow is present at the time of loss. Coverage applies only to direct physical loss that is not otherwise excluded.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An insured must give us notice of a "loss" within thirty days after the loss is discovered. The notice must identify the affected location and describe the known circumstances of the loss.

**W.2** An insured must protect "covered property" from further damage. An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve the property until we have had an opportunity to inspect it.

**W.3** An insured must use reasonable means to prevent further water intrusion, discharge, or overflow. Measures taken to protect property must not unnecessarily increase the extent of loss.

**W.4** An insured must retain records of any "mitigation expense" incurred after a loss. The records must identify the work performed and the person or organization performing the work.

**W.5** An insured must permit us to inspect the location of the loss and the affected property. An insured must provide access that is reasonably necessary for our inspection.

**W.6** An insured must preserve "damaged property" when reasonably possible. An insured must not dispose of damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.7** An insured must provide photographs, recordings, or other available evidence showing the condition of the property. The evidence must accurately depict the affected areas and any visible cause of loss.

**W.8** An insured must prepare and maintain "records" supporting the claim. The records must include available information concerning ownership, value, repair, and replacement.

**W.9** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of a "claim." Cooperation includes providing information that is material to the claim.

**W.10** An insured must identify any "representative" acting on the insured's behalf concerning a claim. A representative's actions and statements may be considered those of the insured.

**W.19** An insured must identify the source of any claimed "water backup." An insured must provide available information concerning the path by which water entered the insured location.

**W.20** An insured must identify the source of any claimed "sump discharge or overflow." An insured must preserve available evidence concerning the operation and condition of the sump system.

**W.21** An insured must show that claimed damage resulted in "direct physical loss" to covered property. An insured must distinguish covered damage from preexisting damage when reasonably possible.

**W.22** An insured must provide available information regarding the "cause of loss." An insured must not knowingly withhold information concerning any contributing cause of loss.

**W.23** An insured must disclose any preexisting "condition" that may have contributed to the claimed damage. An insured must provide available records concerning prior water intrusion, repair, or maintenance.

**W.24** An insured must not make a "misrepresentation" concerning a claim. We may deny coverage for a claim affected by a material misrepresentation.

**W.25** An insured must not engage in "fraud" in connection with a claim. We may deny coverage for a claim affected by fraud.

**W.26** An insured must disclose each "material fact" known to the insured concerning a claim. A fact is material if it could affect our investigation, evaluation, or payment of the claim.

**W.27** An insured must provide "cooperation" reasonably requested by us. An insured must assist us in obtaining information relevant to the claim.

**W.28** An insured must submit to an "examination" under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer material questions concerning the claim.

**W.29** An insured must provide a signed "statement" when we reasonably request it. The statement must be true to the best of the insured's knowledge.

**W.30** An insured must provide an "authorization" when reasonably necessary for us to obtain material records. The authorization must be limited to records relevant to the claim.

**W.31** An insured must provide available "financial record" information when the information is material to the claim. We may examine financial record information relevant to ownership, value, or claimed expense.

**W.32** An insured must provide a signed "proof of loss" when we request it. An insured must comply with the "proof of loss period."

**W.33** An insured must comply with the "legal action deadline." An insured must not bring an action against us unless the insured has complied with all material duties applicable to the claim.

**W.34** The "minimum Section I deductible" applies to a covered loss. We will apply the deductible in determining the amount payable for the loss.

**W.35** The "eighty percent condition" remains applicable to covered property. An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine whether that condition has been met.

**W.37** Our "payment" will not exceed the amount we are legally obligated to pay. Payment does not waive any right available to us under these conditions.

**W.38** We may determine "actual cash value" when that valuation method applies. Actual cash value may reflect the condition of property immediately before the loss.

**W.39** An insured must provide evidence of incurred cost when "replacement cost" is claimed. We may withhold any amount dependent on completed replacement until the required evidence is provided.

**W.40** We may consider "depreciation" when determining the value of damaged property. Depreciation must be based on the condition and expected useful life of the property.

**W.41** We may use available evidence to determine "valuation" of property. An insured must provide available information that may affect valuation.

**W.42** We may determine the "amount of loss" after evaluating the information available to us. Our determination may reflect covered damage, excluded damage, and applicable conditions.

**W.43** Either party may demand "appraisal" when there is disagreement regarding the amount of loss. Appraisal does not determine coverage, policy interpretation, or our liability.

**W.44** Each party selecting an "appraiser" must select a competent and impartial person. Each appraiser must state the amount of loss attributed to the property evaluated.

**W.45** The appraisers may select an "umpire" when necessary to resolve their disagreement. A determination agreed to by the appraisers or an umpire may establish the amount of loss.

**W.46** An "award" must state the amount of loss determined through appraisal. An award does not create coverage for damage that is not otherwise covered.

**W.47** An insured must preserve our rights of "subrogation" after a loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right to recover from another person or organization.

**W.48** An insured must hold any "recovery" received from another person or organization for the same loss. An insured must reimburse us to the extent that our payment is duplicated by that recovery.

**W.49** An insured must identify any known "responsible party" for the loss. An insured must provide available information concerning the responsible party's identity and involvement.

**W.50** An insured must provide any requested "transfer" of rights necessary to preserve our recovery rights. A transfer of rights must be limited to the extent of our payment.

**W.51** An insured must not make an "abandonment" of damaged property to us. We may elect to take property only as permitted by law.

**W.52** An insured must disclose any "property interest" held by another person or organization in damaged property. An insured must provide available documents supporting that interest.

**W.53** An insured must identify any "mortgagee" with an interest in damaged property. We may communicate with a mortgagee concerning payment or repair of the property.

**W.54** An insured must identify any "lienholder" with an interest in damaged property. We may protect a lienholder's interest to the extent required by law.

**W.55** An insured must disclose any "other insurance" that may apply to the loss. An insured must provide available information concerning the other insurance.

**W.58** An insured must not make an "assignment" of rights under a claim without our written consent, except as permitted by law. An assignment does not change an insured's duties under these conditions.

**W.59** "Bankruptcy" or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not relieve an insured of duties under these conditions.

**W.62** An insured must not engage in "concealment" of property, facts, or circumstances material to a claim. We may deny coverage for a claim affected by material concealment.

**W.63** An insured must satisfy each applicable duty of "compliance" before seeking payment, unless we waive that duty in writing. A failure to comply may affect coverage only to the extent permitted by law.

**W.64** If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue to apply to the extent permitted by law. This "severability" condition does not expand coverage.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** An "insured" must take reasonable steps to protect the "residence premises" from conditions that may cause "water backup" or "sump discharge or overflow". An insured must maintain the property in a condition that permits the prompt discovery of such conditions.

**W.2** An insured must maintain plumbing, drainage, and water-control equipment in serviceable condition. An insured must correct known defects that could contribute to water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.3** An insured must keep drains, gutters, downspouts, and drainage paths free from obstruction. An insured must direct surface water away from the residence premises where reasonably possible.

**W.4** An insured must maintain sump equipment and related components according to the manufacturer’s instructions. An insured must promptly repair or replace equipment that does not operate as intended.

**W.5** An insured must provide access to drainage, sump, and water-control equipment for inspection, maintenance, and repair. An insured must not disable, bypass, or materially alter such equipment in a manner that increases the chance of loss.

**W.6** A backwater valve is required for finished areas below grade. An insured must maintain any required backwater valve in operable condition.

**W.7** An insured must use reasonable care to prevent the entry of water through openings in the residence premises. An insured must secure openings that are known to permit unwanted water entry.

**W.8** An insured must protect "covered property" that is susceptible to damage from water backup or sump discharge or overflow. An insured must move covered property from an area of known exposure when reasonably possible.

**W.9** An insured must not store covered property directly in an area that is known to experience recurring water conditions unless reasonable protective measures are used. An insured must maintain those protective measures in serviceable condition.

**W.10** An insured must promptly investigate a known condition that may cause water backup or sump discharge or overflow. An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage.

**W.11** An insured must notify us promptly of a "loss" caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the cause, extent, and location of the loss.

**W.12** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard property that may assist in determining the cause or amount of loss unless necessary to protect health or safety.

**W.13** An insured must make reasonable efforts to identify the source of water affecting the residence premises. An insured must provide available records, reports, and repair information concerning that source when we request them.

**W.14** An insured must permit us to inspect the residence premises and damaged property. An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss.

**W.15** An insured must retain invoices, receipts, estimates, photographs, and other available evidence of damage and repair. An insured must provide those materials to us upon request.

**W.16** An insured must not make permanent repairs before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect the damage, unless immediate repair is necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must preserve evidence of the condition requiring immediate repair.

**W.17** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting persons who perform repair, maintenance, or remediation work. An insured must obtain and retain available documentation of work performed.

**W.18** An insured must promptly remove standing water when it can be done safely. An insured must take reasonable steps to dry affected areas and prevent further property damage.

**W.19** An insured must comply with applicable requirements concerning drainage, plumbing, and water-control systems. An insured must correct known conditions that are not in compliance when correction is reasonably possible.

**W.20** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold a material fact concerning water backup or sump discharge or overflow. This duty applies before and after a loss.

**W.21** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we request it. The statement must describe the damaged property and the circumstances of the loss.

**W.22** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide requested records and permit copies to be made.

**W.23** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may examine, preserve, or dispose of damaged property as necessary to investigate or settle a loss.

**W.24** An insured must take reasonable measures to protect unaffected property from damage during cleanup and repair. An insured must not increase a loss through unreasonable delay or neglect.

**W.25** An insured must notify us of any claim, demand, or proceeding arising from a loss for which coverage is sought. An insured must promptly send us any notice or legal paper received in connection with that matter.

**W.26** An insured must cooperate in the recovery of amounts paid by us from a person responsible for the loss. An insured must not impair our rights of recovery after a loss.

**W.27** Compliance with these requirements is a condition of coverage under this endorsement. We may deny coverage for a loss to the extent an insured’s failure to comply prejudices our ability to investigate, adjust, or settle that loss.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle direct physical loss to "covered property" only after an insured establishes that the property was damaged by a covered cause of loss. We may inspect the property and the place where the loss occurred before settlement.

**W.2** The settlement basis for personal property is actual cash value. We will apply that basis to property that is eligible for payment.

**W.3** We will determine the amount of loss from the condition of the property immediately before the loss. We may consider age, quality, condition, depreciation, obsolescence, and remaining useful life.

**W.4** We will not pay for loss to property that is not owned by you or an insured unless the property is eligible for coverage. An insured must identify the ownership interest in the damaged property.

**W.5** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not dispose of damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.6** We may require an insured to provide photographs, receipts, estimates, invoices, appraisals, or other records that support the amount claimed. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable requests for information.

**W.7** We may use repair cost, replacement cost, market value, actual cash value, or other reliable evidence to determine the amount of loss when applicable to the settlement basis. We may obtain our own estimates or evaluations.

**W.8** We will not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality when repair or replacement is applicable. We may consider reasonable differences in materials, construction, and availability.

**W.9** We may elect to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. Our election does not require us to restore the property to a condition better than its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.10** If we elect to repair or replace property, an insured must provide reasonable access to the property. An insured must cooperate with reasonable repair or replacement arrangements.

**W.11** We may make payment to you, to an insured, or to a person or organization having a legal interest in the property. Payment to a person or organization with a legal interest does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.12** We may pay the value of damaged property and retain any salvage. If we retain salvage, an insured must transfer possession as reasonably requested.

**W.13** We will deduct the applicable deductible from the amount of covered loss. We will not apply a deductible in a manner that increases our payment obligation.

**W.14** We will not pay for property that has not been damaged. We will not pay for improvement, betterment, maintenance, or correction of a condition that existed before the loss.

**W.15** We will not pay for repair or replacement that is not reasonably related to the covered loss. We may limit payment to the cost of reasonable and necessary repair or replacement.

**W.16** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect damaged property from further damage. We will consider reasonable expenses incurred solely to protect covered property from further covered damage.

**W.17** We will not pay for loss caused by an insured's failure to protect property after the loss when that failure increases the loss. This provision does not reduce payment for damage that occurred before the failure.

**W.18** We may require an insured to submit a signed statement of loss. The statement must identify the damaged property, the amount claimed, and the facts supporting the claim.

**W.19** We may require an insured to show us the damaged property and records relating to its value. An insured must permit us to make reasonable copies of those records.

**W.20** If damaged property can be repaired, we may settle based on the reasonable cost to repair it. We will not pay for repair that exceeds the value of the property as determined under the applicable settlement basis.

**W.21** If damaged property cannot reasonably be repaired, we may settle based on its value under the applicable settlement basis. We may consider whether comparable property is reasonably available.

**W.22** We may determine the amount of loss separately for distinct items of property. Damage to one item does not establish the value of another item.

**W.23** We will not pay for sentimental value, emotional value, or value arising solely from personal attachment. We will determine value using objective evidence.

**W.24** We will not pay for loss resulting from delay in repair, replacement, or restoration unless the loss is otherwise covered. We will not pay for loss of use unless that loss is expressly covered.

**W.25** We may settle a claim without obtaining a repair or replacement estimate from an insured. An insured may provide an estimate for our consideration.

**W.26** If an insured disputes our valuation, an insured must provide information supporting a different valuation. We may review the information and revise our valuation when warranted.

**W.27** A dispute concerning the amount of loss does not create coverage for property or damage that is not covered. We will apply the applicable settlement basis only to covered loss.

**W.28** We may make payment for a covered loss without admitting liability for any other claimed damage. Payment for a covered loss does not waive any condition, exclusion, or limitation.

**W.29** We do not cover loss that is intentionally concealed or misrepresented by an insured in connection with a claim. An insured must provide truthful information concerning the property and the loss.

**W.30** We do not cover any amount attributable to fraud by an insured in the presentation or settlement of a claim. We may recover payment obtained through fraud or material misrepresentation.

**W.31** We may require proof that replacement property was acquired or that repairs were completed when that proof is relevant to the applicable settlement basis. Acceptable proof may include receipts, invoices, contracts, or cancelled payments.

**W.32** We will not pay for replacement property that differs materially from the damaged property unless the difference is reasonably necessary. We may limit payment to the value of comparable property.

**W.33** We may account for discounts, rebates, credits, salvage value, or other reductions in the reasonable cost of repair or replacement. An insured must disclose such reductions when known.

**W.34** The "special assessment limit" does not change the settlement basis applicable to covered property. We will apply that limit only as applicable to the claim.

**W.35** The "fire department service charge limit" does not change the valuation of damaged property. We will apply that limit only as applicable to the claim.

**W.36** The "jewelry theft sublimit" does not change the settlement basis applicable to property subject to that sublimit. We will apply that sublimit only as applicable to the claim.

**W.37** The "business property sublimit" does not change the settlement basis applicable to covered property. We will apply that sublimit only as applicable to the claim.

**W.38** Our payment will not exceed the amount for which you are legally responsible or the amount of covered loss, whichever is less. All payments are subject to the applicable terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations.

## W.8 — Definitions

"Water" means liquid, whether clean or containing dissolved, suspended, or entrained matter. It includes liquid within a natural or constructed system.

"Waterborne material" means matter carried, deposited, or transported by water. It includes sediment, soil, debris, waste, and similar matter.

"Sewage" means waste matter carried in water from sanitary facilities, drains, or similar sources. It includes water containing human, animal, or household waste.

"Wastewater" means used water containing dissolved, suspended, or entrained matter. It includes sewage and water discharged from household, commercial, or sanitary uses.

"Groundwater" means water beneath the surface of the ground. It includes water within soil, rock, aquifers, and subsurface drainage areas.

"Surface water" means water on the surface of the ground. It includes water resulting from precipitation, melting snow, runoff, or the overflow of a natural or constructed watercourse.

"Sewer" means a conduit designed to carry sewage, wastewater, or surface water away from a structure or area. It includes a public, private, sanitary, storm, or combined sewer.

"Sanitary sewer" means a sewer designed principally to carry sewage or wastewater. It does not include a sewer designed principally to carry surface water.

"Storm sewer" means a sewer designed principally to carry surface water or groundwater. It includes a conduit serving a drainage area, street, roof, or similar surface.

"Combined sewer" means a sewer designed to carry both sewage and surface water. It includes a conduit that receives flow from sanitary and storm drainage systems.

"Drain" means a pipe, channel, opening, or conduit designed to remove water or wastewater. It includes a floor drain, fixture drain, foundation drain, and similar drainage opening.

"Drainage system" means interconnected drains, pipes, channels, pumps, and related components designed to collect, convey, or remove water. It includes components within or outside a structure.

"Plumbing system" means pipes, fixtures, valves, traps, and related components used to supply, distribute, or remove water. It includes components connected to a drainage system.

"Fixture" means a permanently installed plumbing device through which water is supplied, used, or drained. It includes a sink, toilet, bathtub, shower, and similar device.

"Appliance" means a device that uses, stores, heats, cools, pumps, or discharges water. It includes a washing machine, dishwasher, water heater, and similar device.

"Floor drain" means a drain installed at or near a floor surface to collect and remove water. It includes a drain connected to a plumbing or drainage system.

"Cleanout" means an access opening in a sewer, drain, or pipe. It is designed to permit inspection, maintenance, or removal of an obstruction.

"Septic system" means a system designed to receive, treat, store, or disperse sewage or wastewater. It includes related tanks, pipes, pumps, fields, and drainage components.

"Holding tank" means a container designed to receive and retain sewage, wastewater, or waterborne material. It includes a tank connected to a septic, plumbing, or drainage system.

"Sump" means a pit, basin, tank, or similar receptacle designed to collect water. It includes a receptacle that receives groundwater, surface water, drainage water, or wastewater.

"Sump basin" means the pit, tank, or receptacle portion of a sump. It includes an opening, liner, cover, inlet, outlet, and related component.

"Sump pump" means a pump designed to remove water from a sump. It includes a pump operated by electrical, mechanical, or other power.

"Discharge line" means a pipe, hose, conduit, or other passage through which water is carried away from a sump or pump. It includes connected fittings, valves, and outlets.

"Discharge" means the release or movement of water from a sump, pump, discharge line, or related component. It includes water released through an intended outlet or from an unintended opening.

"Overflow" means water that escapes from a sump, basin, tank, drain, or similar receptacle because its capacity is exceeded or its normal outlet is unable to remove water. It includes water escaping over an edge, through an opening, or from a cover.

"Sump discharge or overflow" means water released, expelled, or escaping from a sump, sump pump, sump basin, discharge line, or related component. It includes water resulting from an overflow or an unintended discharge.

"Backflow" means the reverse movement of water within a sewer, drain, pipe, or drainage system. It includes movement caused by pressure, blockage, surcharge, or failure of a component.

"Obstruction" means a condition that impedes the intended flow of water through a sewer, drain, pipe, or drainage system. It includes blockage caused by debris, sediment, roots, ice, damaged components, or waterborne material.

"Failure" means the inability of a pump, valve, pipe, drain, sewer, or related component to perform its intended operation. It includes mechanical malfunction, electrical malfunction, breakage, collapse, disconnection, and deterioration.

"Power interruption" means the loss, reduction, or disruption of electrical power. It includes an interruption affecting a sump pump or related component.

"Source" means the location, system, component, or condition from which water originates before entering a structure or causing damage. It includes a sewer, drain, sump, plumbing system, drainage system, or natural water source.

"Water backup" means water or waterborne material that enters a structure through a sewer, drain, plumbing system, drainage system, or similar opening because water is unable to pass through, reverses within, or escapes from that system. It includes water resulting from backflow, obstruction, failure, or a condition affecting the source.
