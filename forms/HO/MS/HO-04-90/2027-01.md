## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

**W.1** This "endorsement" modifies the insurance provided under your "policy". It is
effective only when attached to your policy.

**W.2** This endorsement forms part of your policy. The provisions of this endorsement
apply only to the extent stated in this endorsement.

**W.3** This endorsement addresses coverage for direct physical loss involving "water
backup" and "sump discharge or overflow". Coverage is subject to the terms of this
endorsement.

**W.4** If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy,
this endorsement controls. Any provision of your policy not modified by this endorsement
remains unchanged.

**W.5** The terms of your policy apply to this endorsement unless this endorsement
provides otherwise. We apply those terms consistently with the provisions of this
endorsement.

**W.6** This endorsement does not create insurance for a loss that is not covered under
your policy or this endorsement. We provide coverage only as stated in this endorsement
and your policy.

**W.7** An insured must comply with all duties applicable to a loss for which coverage
is sought. Compliance with those duties is a condition of coverage.

**W.8** Your rights and duties under your policy apply to this endorsement. Our rights
and duties under your policy also apply to this endorsement.

**W.9** This endorsement does not alter the identity of an insured under your policy. It
applies only to an insured entitled to coverage under your policy.

**W.10** The application of this endorsement depends on the facts and circumstances of a
claimed loss. We determine coverage in accordance with the applicable policy provisions
and this endorsement.

**W.11** We do not cover loss except as provided by the terms of your policy as modified
by this endorsement. No provision of this endorsement may be construed to provide
broader coverage than its terms state.

**W.12** The attachment of this endorsement does not establish a separate contract of
insurance. This endorsement and your policy are construed together.

**W.13** This endorsement remains subject to the conditions, exclusions, and limitations
that apply to your policy unless modified by this endorsement. We will administer this
endorsement as part of your policy.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** For purposes of this coverage, "Water Backup" means water that backs up through
a sewer or drain. Water Backup includes waterborne material carried by that water.

**W.2** We cover direct physical loss to property insured by this policy that is caused
by Water Backup. Coverage under this paragraph is subject to the limit insubject to the
limit in (see W.2, Limit of Liability).

**W.3** For purposes of this coverage, "Sump Discharge or Overflow" means water that
discharges or overflows from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. Coverage for Sump
Discharge or Overflow is subject to the limit insubject to the limit in (see W.2, Limit
of Liability).

**W.4** We cover direct physical loss to property insured by this policy that is caused
by Sump Discharge or Overflow. The discharge or overflow must originate from equipment
located on the residence premises.

**W.5** "Direct Physical Loss" means accidental physical damage to tangible property.
Direct Physical Loss includes resulting damage caused by covered water.

**W.6** A "Sump System" means a sump, sump pump, pit, basin, discharge line, valve,
alarm, and related equipment used to collect, contain, remove, or direct water. A Sump
System includes permanently installed equipment and its connected components.

**W.7** Water Backup may occur through a sewer, drain, discharge line, or similar
water-removal opening. The source of the backup need not be located on the residence
premises.

**W.8** Sump Discharge or Overflow may result from mechanical failure, electrical
interruption, obstruction, accidental activation, or inability of a Sump System to
remove water. The cause of the discharge or overflow must be accidental.

**W.9** We cover loss caused by water that enters insured property after Water Backup.
We also cover loss caused by water that enters insured property after Sump Discharge or
Overflow.

**W.10** We cover damage to insured property caused by the force, weight, pressure, or
movement of covered water. We cover damage caused by waterborne material that
accompanies covered water.

**W.11** We cover reasonable and necessary measures taken to protect insured property
from further direct physical loss after a covered Water Backup. An insured must retain
damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.12** We cover reasonable and necessary measures taken to protect insured property
from further direct physical loss after a covered Sump Discharge or Overflow. An insured
must permit us to inspect the affected location and damaged property.

**W.13** We cover loss caused by Water Backup that occurs suddenly or gradually. The
loss must result in Direct Physical Loss during the policy period.

**W.14** We cover loss caused by Sump Discharge or Overflow that occurs suddenly or
gradually. The loss must result in Direct Physical Loss during the policy period.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by flood. This exclusion applies whether flood
contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We provide coverage for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by
water backup or sump discharge or overflow, subject to the terms of this provision. The
most we will pay for any "loss" caused by water backup or sump discharge or overflow is
ten thousand dollars. Except that, this limit applies regardless of the number of
persons insured, claims made, or items of covered property involved in the loss.

**W.2** The limit of liability applies to property covered under this endorsement. It
applies separately from any limit that may apply to other causes of loss. Subject to the
terms of this policy, this limit is the most we will pay for covered loss caused by
water backup or sump discharge or overflow.

**W.3** We determine the amount of covered loss in accordance with the applicable
valuation provisions of this policy. We do not increase the limit of liability because
the cost to repair or replace covered property exceeds that limit. Unless otherwise
provided by this policy, payment for covered property does not exceed the amount
necessary to repair or replace the damaged property.

**W.4** We cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by water that backs up
through sewers or drains. We also cover direct physical loss caused by water that
overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. Except that,
coverage applies only when the loss results from a covered cause under this endorsement.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or
waterborne material. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the
ground that exerts pressure on or flows, seeps, or leaks through a building or other
structure. Subject to the terms of this policy, this exclusion applies whether the water
is driven by wind or not.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of a plumbing system, heating system,
air-conditioning system, or household appliance. We do not cover loss caused by failure
to maintain heat in the building when you occupy the building. Unless the failure

**W.2** The deductible applies only when the loss is covered under this endorsement. We
do not apply the deductible to property, causes of loss, or expenses that are not
covered under this endorsement. We determine coverage without regard to the amount of
the deductible.

**W.3** The water backup endorsement deductible is one thousand dollars. Subject to the

**W.6** An insured must give us prompt notice of a covered water backup loss. An insured

repairs that are not reasonably necessary. We do not cover costs attributable solely to

amount after those costs are removed.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by neglect to use reasonable means to prevent
further damage after a water backup. We do not cover expenses that result from an

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover "direct physical loss" caused by "water backup" except as
expressly provided by this endorsement. "Water backup" means water or waterborne
material that backs up through a sewer, drain, sump, or related equipment.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters a dwelling through an opening
in a roof, wall, foundation, door, window, or other part of the building. This exclusion
applies whether the opening existed before the water entered or resulted from
deterioration, defect, or inadequate maintenance.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by seepage, leakage, or migration of water through
foundations, walls, floors, slabs, roofs, or other building materials. This exclusion
applies whether the water is visible or concealed.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water
arising from those conditions.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This
exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or flows through
a building or other structure.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a body of water. This
exclusion applies whether the body of water is natural, artificial, or altered by human
activity.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by runoff from precipitation, snowmelt, irrigation,
or a similar source. This exclusion applies when the runoff enters the residence
premises from outside the building.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters through a drain, pipe, conduit,
opening, or channel located outside the building. This exclusion applies even if the
water later enters a sewer, drain, sump, or related equipment.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by overflow from a drain, sewer, or plumbing system
that is not connected to the insured location. This exclusion applies whether the
overflow occurs on adjoining property or elsewhere.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by a discharge from a public sewer, public drain,
or public water system unless the discharge is otherwise within the coverage provided by
this endorsement. A failure by a public authority to maintain, repair, or operate its
system does not create coverage.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by water that is intentionally released, diverted,
retained, or discharged by any person or organization. This exclusion applies whether
the release is authorized, accidental, negligent, or unlawful.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by water used in construction, repair, remodeling,
testing, cleaning, or maintenance. This exclusion applies when the water is not
discharged from a covered sewer, drain, sump, or related equipment.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by defective design, specifications, workmanship,
repair, construction, renovation, grading, compaction, or drainage. This exclusion
applies even if the defect contributes to a water backup or discharge.

**W.14** We do not cover the cost to correct, improve, replace, or redesign a sewer,
drain, sump, pump, pipe, valve, or related equipment because of wear, defect,
inadequacy, or obsolescence. We also do not cover the cost to bring that property into
compliance with a law, ordinance, code, or regulation.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, deterioration, decay, settling,
cracking, shrinking, bulging, expansion, or latent defect. This exclusion applies to the
extent such condition causes or contributes to a discharge, overflow, or backup.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by neglect by an insured to use reasonable means to
save and preserve property from further damage after a loss. We also do not cover loss
caused by neglect to protect property from a known condition that is likely to cause
water damage.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by repeated leakage, repeated seepage, or
continuous discharge of water. This exclusion applies whether the condition is known or
unknown to an insured.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, virus,
or other microorganism. This exclusion applies even if such condition results from water
backup, sump discharge, or sump overflow.

**W.19** We do not cover loss consisting of odor, staining, discoloration,
contamination, or loss of use unless it results from direct physical loss otherwise
covered by this endorsement. We do not cover the cost to test, monitor, assess, or
remediate property for such condition unless required to repair covered direct physical
loss.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by pollutants, contaminants, debris, sediment,
chemicals, or waste material carried by water. This exclusion applies except for
physical damage otherwise covered by this endorsement.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including sinking, rising,
shifting, erosion, landslide, mudflow, subsidence, or earth cracking. This exclusion

## W.8 — Definitions

"Water" means liquid, whether clean or containing dissolved, suspended, or entrained
matter. It includes liquid within a natural or constructed system.

"Waterborne material" means matter carried, deposited, or transported by water. It
includes sediment, soil, debris, waste, and similar matter.

"Sewage" means waste matter carried in water from sanitary facilities, drains, or
similar sources. It includes water containing human, animal, or household waste.

"Wastewater" means used water containing dissolved, suspended, or entrained matter. It
includes sewage and water discharged from household, commercial, or sanitary uses.

"Groundwater" means water beneath the surface of the ground. It includes water within
soil, rock, aquifers, and subsurface drainage areas.

"Surface water" means water on the surface of the ground. It includes water resulting
from precipitation, melting snow, runoff, or the overflow of a natural or constructed
watercourse.

"Sewer" means a conduit designed to carry sewage, wastewater, or surface water away from
a structure or area. It includes a public, private, sanitary, storm, or combined sewer.

"Sanitary sewer" means a sewer designed principally to carry sewage or wastewater. It
does not include a sewer designed principally to carry surface water.

"Storm sewer" means a sewer designed principally to carry surface water or groundwater.
It includes a conduit serving a drainage area, street, roof, or similar surface.

"Combined sewer" means a sewer designed to carry both sewage and surface water. It
includes a conduit that receives flow from sanitary and storm drainage systems.

"Drain" means a pipe, channel, opening, or conduit designed to remove water or

"Drainage system" means interconnected drains, pipes, channels, pumps, and related
components designed to collect, convey, or remove water. It includes components within
or outside a structure.

"Plumbing system" means pipes, fixtures, valves, traps, and related components used to

"Fixture" means a permanently installed plumbing device through which water is supplied,

"Appliance" means a device that uses, stores, heats, cools, pumps, or discharges water.
It includes a washing machine, dishwasher, water heater, and similar device.

"Floor drain" means a drain installed at or near a floor surface to collect and remove
water. It includes a drain connected to a plumbing or drainage system.

"Cleanout" means an access opening in a sewer, drain, or pipe. It is designed to permit
inspection, maintenance, or removal of an obstruction.

"Septic system" means a system designed to receive, treat, store, or disperse sewage or
wastewater. It includes related tanks, pipes, pumps, fields, and drainage components.

"Holding tank" means a container designed to receive and retain sewage, wastewater, or
waterborne material. It includes a tank connected to a septic, plumbing, or drainage
system.

"Sump" means a pit, basin, tank, or similar receptacle designed to collect water. It
includes a receptacle that receives groundwater, surface water, drainage water, or
wastewater.

"Sump basin" means the pit, tank, or receptacle portion of a sump. It includes an
opening, liner, cover, inlet, outlet, and related component.

"Sump pump" means a pump designed to remove water from a sump. It includes a pump
operated by electrical, mechanical, or other power.

"Discharge line" means a pipe, hose, conduit, or other passage through which water is
carried away from a sump or pump. It includes connected fittings, valves, and outlets.

"Discharge" means the release or movement of water from a sump, pump, discharge line, or
related component. It includes water released through an intended outlet or from an
unintended opening.

"Overflow" means water that escapes from a sump, basin, tank, drain, or similar
receptacle because its capacity is exceeded or its normal outlet is unable to remove
water. It includes water escaping over an edge, through an opening, or from a cover.

"Sump discharge or overflow" means water released, expelled, or escaping from a sump,
sump pump, sump basin, discharge line, or related component. It includes water resulting

"Backflow" means the reverse movement of water within a sewer, drain, pipe, or drainage
system. It includes movement caused by pressure, blockage, surcharge, or failure of a
component.

"Obstruction" means a condition that impedes the intended flow of water through a sewer,
drain, pipe, or drainage system. It includes blockage caused by debris, sediment, roots,

"Failure" means the inability of a pump, valve, pipe, drain, sewer, or related component

malfunction, breakage, collapse, disconnection, and deterioration.

"Power interruption" means the loss, reduction, or disruption of electrical power. It
includes an interruption affecting a sump pump or related component.

"Source" means the location, system, component, or condition from which water originates

plumbing system, drainage system, or natural water source.

"Water backup" means water or waterborne material that enters a structure through a
sewer, drain, plumbing system, drainage system, or similar opening because water is
unable to pass through, reverses within, or escapes from that system. It includes water
resulting from backflow, obstruction, failure, or a condition affecting the source.
