# HO 04 61 Scheduled Personal Property (Edition 2020-11)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This “endorsement” modifies insurance provided under your “policy.” The terms of your policy apply unless this endorsement changes them.

This endorsement is attached to and made part of your policy. Its provisions apply only to property described in the “Schedule.”

The “Schedule” is the record that identifies the property to which this endorsement applies. Property not identified in the Schedule is not scheduled personal property under this endorsement.

“Scheduled personal property” means personal property described in the Schedule. The description may include information that identifies the property or distinguishes it from other property.

The insurance provided by this endorsement applies to scheduled personal property while it is owned by an “insured.” An insured must have an insurable interest in the scheduled personal property at the time of loss.

The term “insured” has the meaning assigned to it in your policy. The use of that term in this endorsement does not expand who is an insured under your policy.

The term “loss” means accidental direct physical loss or damage to scheduled personal property. Loss includes damage that impairs the property’s condition, usefulness, or value.

The terms used in the Schedule are incorporated into this endorsement. A description in the Schedule applies only to the scheduled personal property associated with that description.

Information shown in the Schedule is material to the insurance provided by this endorsement. You must ensure that information you provide concerning scheduled personal property is accurate and complete.

You must promptly notify us of a change in ownership, location, condition, or use of scheduled personal property when that change may affect the insurance provided by this endorsement. We may revise the applicable insurance after receiving notice of the change.

The presence of scheduled personal property in the Schedule does not create coverage for property that is not owned by an insured. It also does not create coverage for property acquired after the Schedule is issued unless we agree to add that property.

We do not cover loss to property that is not scheduled personal property under this endorsement. We do not cover loss that is excluded by the terms of your policy unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

This endorsement does not alter the duties imposed upon an insured after loss. An insured must comply with those duties as they apply to scheduled personal property.

You must protect scheduled personal property from further loss after a loss occurs. You must allow us to inspect the property and examine relevant records when reasonably required.

You must retain damaged scheduled personal property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless we agree otherwise. You must not voluntarily dispose of property that may be needed to determine the cause or amount of loss.

If the Schedule contains inconsistent descriptions of scheduled personal property, we will apply the description that most specifically identifies the property. If no description sufficiently identifies the property, coverage applies only as supported by reliable evidence of the property’s identity.

An error in describing scheduled personal property does not create coverage for different property. An insured must cooperate with us in correcting an error that affects the identification of scheduled personal property.

No change to this endorsement is valid unless made by us in writing. No representative may change the terms of this endorsement unless authorized by us to do so in writing.

The insurance provided by this endorsement is subject to all applicable policy conditions. When the provisions of this endorsement conflict with the provisions of your policy, this endorsement controls.

This endorsement applies only during the time your policy is in effect. Termination of your policy terminates the insurance provided by this endorsement.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We cover "scheduled personal property" described in the "Schedule" for direct physical loss, subject to these provisions. The Schedule identifies the property to which this coverage applies.

**W.2** Scheduled personal property includes only property specifically described in the Schedule. Property not described in the Schedule is not scheduled personal property.

**W.3** An "insured" may recover under this coverage only for property in which an insured has an ownership interest or for which an insured is legally responsible. An insured must have an insurable interest in the property at the time of loss.

**W.4** "Direct physical loss" means accidental loss of or damage to scheduled personal property. Loss includes disappearance only when the circumstances establish that the property was stolen or accidentally lost.

**W.5** We cover scheduled personal property while it is at your residence or away from your residence. Coverage applies wherever the property is located, unless a provision of this coverage states otherwise.

**W.6** We cover scheduled personal property while it is being worn, used, stored, transported, displayed, or temporarily entrusted to another person. Coverage does not transfer ownership of the property to us.

**W.7** We cover loss caused by theft of scheduled personal property. Theft includes loss caused by unlawful taking with intent to deprive an insured of the property.

**W.8** We cover loss caused by mysterious disappearance of scheduled personal property when an insured cannot reasonably explain the disappearance. An insured must provide all available information concerning the disappearance.

**W.9** We cover loss caused by breakage of scheduled personal property. Breakage coverage applies to fragile property unless an exclusion applies.

**W.10** We cover loss caused by accidental damage to scheduled personal property. Accidental damage includes damage resulting from handling, dropping, impact, or other unintended contact.

**W.11** We cover loss caused by fire, lightning, smoke, explosion, windstorm, hail, riot, civil commotion, aircraft, vehicles, vandalism, malicious mischief, and falling objects. Coverage applies whether the loss occurs at or away from your residence.

**W.12** We cover loss caused by water that accidentally escapes or overflows from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect the property from further damage after the loss.

**W.13** We cover loss caused by sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a system or appliance. We do not cover damage caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage.

**W.14** We cover loss caused by power surge, electrical arcing, or artificially generated electrical current. We do not cover damage caused solely by electrical or mechanical breakdown.

**W.15** We cover loss caused by collapse of a building or other structure when the scheduled personal property is damaged by the collapse. Coverage applies only to the direct physical loss to the scheduled personal property.

**W.16** We cover loss caused by transportation of scheduled personal property by an insured. We also cover loss while the property is in the custody of a common carrier.

**W.17** We cover loss caused by a collision, overturn, derailment, stranding, sinking, or grounding of a vehicle or conveyance transporting scheduled personal property. An insured must preserve any rights of recovery against a carrier or other responsible person.

**W.18** We cover loss caused by theft from a vehicle, subject to the duties of an insured following loss. An insured must report the theft promptly to the appropriate law enforcement authority.

**W.19** We cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief. We do not cover loss caused by intentional damage committed by an insured.

**W.20** We cover loss caused by a sudden and accidental event occurring during repair, cleaning, restoration, or maintenance of scheduled personal property. An insured must use reasonable care in selecting any person who performs such work.

**W.21** We cover loss to scheduled personal property temporarily loaned to another person with your permission. Coverage applies only while you retain an ownership interest in the property.

**W.22** We cover loss to scheduled personal property temporarily held by a dealer, repairer, appraiser, conservator, or similar custodian. An insured must retain documentation showing the identity of the property and the purpose of the custody.

**W.23** We cover loss to scheduled personal property while it is exhibited, stored, or transported in connection with a lawful personal activity. We do not cover property while it is used principally for a business unless the Schedule identifies that use.

**W.24** We cover loss to scheduled personal property used as a gift, heirloom, collection, adornment, furnishing, hobby item, or personal article. The character or sentimental value of property does not establish its value for loss settlement.

**W.25** The description in the Schedule controls the identity of the scheduled personal property. An insured must notify us promptly if a description becomes materially inaccurate.

**W.26** The Schedule may identify a specific item, a collection, a pair, a set, or a group of articles. Coverage applies only to the property included within the scheduled description.

**W.27** When the Schedule describes property by maker, model, serial identifier, inscription, gem characteristics, artistic attributes, or other identifying information, that information forms part of the scheduled description. An insured must cooperate with us in verifying the identity of property claimed as lost or damaged.

**W.28** When the Schedule describes a collection, pair, set, or group, we may settle the loss based on the value of the damaged or missing property or the reduction in value of the collection, pair, set, or group. We will not pay for any reduction in value that is speculative or unsupported by competent evidence.

**W.29** We will pay no more for loss to scheduled personal property than the applicable limit shown in the Schedule. The applicable limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss involving the described property.

**W.30** If more than one scheduled description could apply to the same property, we will apply the description providing the greatest available coverage for that property. We will not combine limits applicable to separate descriptions for the same loss.

**W.31** If the Schedule identifies property as a pair or set, we may repair or replace any part of the pair or set that sustains covered loss. We may also pay the difference between the value of the pair or set before the loss and its value after the loss.

**W.32** If the Schedule identifies property as a collection, we may repair or replace the damaged article or pay for its loss. We will consider the effect of the loss on the value of the collection when supported by reliable evidence.

**W.33** We may repair, restore, replace, or pay for covered loss to scheduled personal property. Our choice of method will not exceed the applicable limit shown in the Schedule.

**W.34** We may use property of like kind and quality when replacing scheduled personal property. Replacement property need not be identical if identical property is not reasonably available.

**W.35** We may require an insured to transfer to us title to property for which we have paid the applicable limit. We may take possession of damaged property after payment for the loss.

**W.36** We may require an insured to surrender damaged or recovered property when we replace it or pay for its full value. An insured must not abandon property to us unless we agree in writing.

**W.37** If lost property is recovered after we pay for the loss, an insured must notify us promptly. We will determine whether the property will be retained by us or returned to an insured subject to repayment of the amount we paid.

**W.38** We will pay the reasonable cost to protect scheduled personal property from further covered loss after a covered event. An insured must take reasonable measures to preserve the property and prevent additional damage.

**W.39** We will pay reasonable costs incurred to remove scheduled personal property from immediate danger of covered loss. We will not pay costs that improve, alter, or increase the value of the property.

**W.40** We will pay reasonable costs to transport scheduled personal property for repair or restoration following covered loss. We will not pay transportation costs unrelated to the covered loss.

**W.41** An insured must give us prompt notice of loss. Notice must include sufficient information to identify the scheduled personal property and the circumstances of the loss.

**W.42** An insured must notify the appropriate law enforcement authority when a theft, disappearance, robbery, burglary, or vandalism has occurred. An insured must provide us with available reports and records relating to the event.

**W.43** An insured must protect the scheduled personal property from further loss. An insured must keep a record of repair expenses and retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.44** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must allow us to inspect the property, examine relevant records, and obtain information concerning the loss.

**W.45** An insured must provide records, photographs, appraisals, purchase information, authenticity documentation, repair estimates, and other evidence reasonably requested by us. An insured must cooperate in establishing the identity, ownership, condition, and value of the property.

**W.46** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign and provide a recorded statement, affidavit, or other proof concerning the loss when reasonably requested by us.

**W.47** An insured must provide a signed proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary for us to investigate and settle the claim.

**W.48** An insured must preserve any rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our right to recover amounts we have paid.

**W.49** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, scratching, denting, deterioration, fading, corrosion, rust, or gradual damage. We do cover direct physical loss resulting from a covered cause of loss that follows such condition.

**W.50** We do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, electrical breakdown, or faulty material. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered event that is separate from the excluded condition.

**W.51** We do not cover loss caused by insects, rodents, vermin, birds, animals, mold, mildew, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered event that follows such damage.

**W.52** We do not cover loss caused by dampness, dryness of atmosphere, temperature change, smog, contamination, or pollution. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire, explosion, or another covered event resulting from those conditions.

**W.53** We do not cover loss caused by delay, loss of use, loss of market, loss of value, or consequential loss. We do not cover sentimental, historic, artistic, or personal value that exceeds the value established under these provisions.

**W.54** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, renovation, construction, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered event that is separate from the defective work.

**W.55** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve scheduled personal property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not apply to an insured's inability to protect property because of a covered event.

**W.56** We do not cover loss arising out of any intentional act committed by an insured with the intent to cause loss. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss differs in kind or degree from that intended.

**W.57** We do not cover loss arising out of fraudulent, dishonest, or criminal acts committed by an insured. We do not cover loss resulting from concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact relating to the coverage or claim.

**W.58** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by war, undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or a military force. Such loss is excluded regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.59** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by order of a governmental or public authority. We do cover loss caused by fire resulting from such action when the fire would otherwise be covered.

**W.60** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, or radioactive waste. Such loss is excluded regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.61** We do not cover loss to property while it is unlawfully possessed by an insured. We do not cover loss to property that an insured acquires through fraud, theft, or other unlawful means.

**W.62** We do not cover loss to property while it is used in connection with a business, profession, trade, occupation, or commercial enterprise unless the Schedule identifies that use. Personal incidental use does not by itself establish business use.

**W.63** We do not cover loss to property held for sale, rental, consignment, exhibition for sale, or delivery in connection with a business unless the Schedule identifies that use. This exclusion does not apply to property temporarily entrusted to a dealer solely for appraisal, repair, restoration, or safekeeping.

**W.64** We do not cover loss to property while it is used as stock, merchandise, inventory, samples, or materials for sale. We do not cover the value added by an insured's labor, processing, or business activity unless shown in the Schedule.

**W.65** We do not cover loss to property for which another insurance policy provides coverage, except as provided by applicable law. If other insurance applies, we will pay our share of the covered loss.

**W.66** We will not pay more than the amount of covered loss after applying all applicable insurance. An insured must disclose other insurance that may apply to the same property or loss.

**W.67** Coverage applies separately to each scheduled description. A loss affecting more than one scheduled description will be adjusted according to the applicable limits and terms for each description.

**W.68** A change in ownership of scheduled personal property does not extend coverage to the new owner unless the property remains an insured's property. An insured must notify us promptly when an ownership change materially affects the Schedule.

**W.69** Coverage for scheduled personal property ends when an insured no longer has an insurable interest in the property. Coverage also ends when the property is permanently transferred to another person, unless we agree otherwise.

**W.70** An insured may request that scheduled personal property be added, removed, or changed. A change is effective only when we agree to it and record it in the Schedule.

**W.71** An insured must provide accurate information when requesting a change to the Schedule. We may require an appraisal, receipt, photograph, description, or other evidence before agreeing to a requested change.

**W.72** We may inspect scheduled personal property and records relating to it. An inspection does not constitute a warranty, representation, or guarantee that the property is safe or adequately protected.

**W.73** We may obtain an appraisal or expert evaluation of scheduled personal property after loss. An insured must cooperate with the appraisal process and provide reasonable access to the property and relevant records.

**W.74** If an insured and we disagree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal. Each party will select a competent appraiser, and the appraisers will select an umpire if they cannot agree on the amount of loss.

**W.75** An award agreed to by the appraisers or determined by an appraiser and the umpire will establish the amount of loss. Appraisal does not determine coverage, liability, or the application of any exclusion.

**W.76** We may make payment to an insured, to a person legally entitled to receive payment, or to another person having an interest in the scheduled personal property. Payment to any such person does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.77** Payment for covered loss does not transfer an insured's duties under these provisions. An insured must continue to cooperate with us after payment when reasonably necessary to protect our rights.

**W.78** This coverage applies only to direct physical loss occurring while the scheduled personal property is covered by us. All rights and duties under this coverage are subject to the terms stated in these provisions.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** For purposes of this section, "covered property" means property for which coverage applies. The "applicable limit" is the maximum amount we will pay for loss to particular covered property.

**W.2** Our liability for loss to covered property is limited to the applicable limit. We do not cover any portion of loss that exceeds the applicable limit.

**W.3** The applicable limit applies regardless of the number of causes of loss involved. The applicable limit also applies regardless of the number of claims arising from the loss.

**W.4** Payment for direct physical loss to covered property applies against the applicable limit. Payment does not create additional insurance for the covered property.

**W.5** The cost to repair covered property does not increase the applicable limit. The cost to replace covered property does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.6** A change in market value does not increase the applicable limit. A change in the availability of covered property does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.7** The amount you paid to acquire covered property does not increase the applicable limit. The amount required to obtain similar property does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.8** We may determine the value of covered property by considering its condition immediately before the loss. Our valuation does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.9** "Actual cash value" means the value of covered property at the time of loss, after appropriate deduction for depreciation and physical condition. Actual cash value does not exceed the applicable limit.

**W.10** "Replacement cost" means the reasonable cost to repair or replace covered property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement cost does not exceed the applicable limit.

**W.11** If covered property can be repaired, we may base payment on the reasonable cost of repair. Our payment remains subject to the applicable limit.

**W.12** If covered property cannot be repaired, we may base payment on its actual cash value or replacement cost, as applicable. Our payment remains subject to the applicable limit.

**W.13** If covered property is part of a set, our payment is limited to the loss in value of the damaged or lost property. We do not cover any decrease in value of undamaged property that exceeds the applicable limit.

**W.14** If covered property consists of matched articles, our payment is limited to the loss affecting the covered property. We do not cover the cost to replace undamaged articles solely to obtain a match.

**W.15** If covered property has artistic, historic, collectible, or sentimental value, our payment remains subject to the applicable limit. We do not cover sentimental value.

**W.16** If covered property has been altered, repaired, restored, or improved, our payment remains subject to the applicable limit. We do not cover any value attributable to work that is not part of covered property.

**W.17** If covered property is unavailable in the marketplace, our payment remains subject to the applicable limit. We do not cover an amount resulting solely from scarcity or special demand.

**W.18** If covered property is no longer manufactured, our payment remains subject to the applicable limit. We may consider reasonably available property of like kind and quality in determining payment.

**W.19** We may pay for loss in money, repair covered property, replace covered property, or take any combination of those actions. Our chosen method of settlement does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.20** We may retain any damaged covered property after payment for the loss. The value of retained property does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.21** If covered property is recovered after we make payment, you must promptly notify us. We may require you to return our payment or transfer recovered property to us, subject to your interest in that property.

**W.22** If you recover money or property from another source for the same loss, you must hold that recovery for us to the extent of our payment. Any recovery does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.23** Payment by another insurer, responsible party, or other source does not increase the applicable limit. We will not pay more than the amount of covered loss remaining after applicable recoveries.

**W.24** The existence of legal liability of another person does not increase the applicable limit. A judgment, settlement, or agreement involving another person does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.25** We do not cover expenses incurred to prepare, present, document, or support a claim unless we agree otherwise. Any payment we make for an agreed expense is subject to the applicable limit.

**W.26** We do not cover fees charged by a public adjuster, consultant, appraiser, attorney, or other representative retained by you. Those fees do not increase the applicable limit.

**W.27** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of loss. An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, valuations, and other information reasonably requested by us.

**W.28** An insured must protect covered property from further loss after a loss occurs. Reasonable protective measures do not increase the applicable limit.

**W.29** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged covered property before it is repaired, replaced, discarded, or otherwise disposed of, when reasonably practicable. Failure to preserve covered property may affect our ability to determine the amount of loss.

**W.30** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may elect to take possession of covered property after payment, but we are not required to do so.

**W.31** We may require that damaged covered property be made available to us for examination. Our examination does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.32** We may require an examination under oath of an insured concerning the loss. Information obtained through that examination does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.33** A dispute concerning the amount of loss does not increase the applicable limit. An appraisal, valuation, or other dispute-resolution process does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.34** Our payment for a partial loss is limited to the amount of covered loss. We do not cover any amount for undamaged portions of covered property except as expressly provided by this insurance.

**W.35** Our payment for loss caused by theft, disappearance, or damage is subject to the applicable limit. The manner in which the loss occurred does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.36** Loss occurring while covered property is in the care, custody, or control of another person remains subject to the applicable limit. We do not cover an amount exceeding that limit because another person had possession of the property.

**W.37** The applicable limit applies whether covered property is located at your residence premises or elsewhere. A change in location does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.38** No act, omission, representation, adjustment, investigation, payment, or settlement by us increases the applicable limit. Our liability ends when we have paid the applicable limit for the covered loss.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "Deductible" applicable to loss involving "scheduled personal property" is two hundred fifty dollars. We will subtract the Deductible from the amount otherwise payable for covered loss.

**W.2** The Deductible applies to each covered loss involving scheduled personal property. The Deductible applies whether the loss affects all or part of the scheduled personal property.

**W.3** We will apply the Deductible after determining the amount of covered loss. We will not apply the Deductible to an amount that is not otherwise payable.

**W.4** The Deductible does not increase the amount of insurance applicable to scheduled personal property. The Deductible does not reduce your duty to protect property from further damage.

**W.5** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of covered loss. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for us to apply the Deductible.

**W.6** We will apply the Deductible to the total amount of covered loss involving scheduled personal property arising from the same event. We will not apply the Deductible separately to property merely because the property is described separately.

**W.7** When covered loss affects more than one item of scheduled personal property, we will determine the covered amount for the affected property before subtracting the Deductible. We may allocate payment among affected items after subtracting the Deductible.

**W.8** We will not pay the portion of covered loss that is within the Deductible. You remain responsible for that portion of covered loss.

**W.9** The Deductible applies to payment for repair of damaged scheduled personal property. The Deductible also applies to payment for replacement of scheduled personal property when replacement is covered.

**W.10** The Deductible applies if we elect to repair, replace, or pay for covered loss. Our election of a method of settlement does not eliminate the Deductible.

**W.11** We will apply the Deductible before making payment to you or to another person entitled to payment. Payment to another person does not change the Deductible.

**W.12** The Deductible applies to covered loss caused by a single event even if damage becomes apparent at different times. The time at which damage is discovered does not alone determine whether more than one Deductible applies.

**W.13** We will consider the facts and circumstances of the loss in determining whether damage arises from the same event. An insured must provide available information concerning the cause and circumstances of loss.

**W.14** The Deductible applies to covered loss involving property in your care, custody, or control when that property qualifies as scheduled personal property. The Deductible does not create coverage for property that is not covered.

**W.15** We will determine the amount of covered loss without regard to any amount you may recover from another source. We will apply the Deductible to our payment obligation.

**W.16** The Deductible applies before we make payment for reasonable expenses that are included in covered loss. We will not pay expenses that are not covered merely because the Deductible has been satisfied.

**W.17** If damaged scheduled personal property has value after loss, we may consider that value in determining the amount of covered loss. The Deductible applies to the resulting amount otherwise payable.

**W.18** An insured must not abandon property to us unless we agree in writing. Our possession of damaged property does not waive the Deductible.

**W.19** We may inspect damaged scheduled personal property before determining payment. Inspection does not waive any right concerning the Deductible.

**W.20** We may request records, photographs, receipts, appraisals, or other information relevant to the loss. An insured must provide such information when reasonably available.

**W.21** The Deductible applies even if repair or replacement is not completed. We will determine payment based on the applicable terms of coverage and this provision.

**W.22** The Deductible applies to covered loss for which payment is made in currency or by repair or replacement. The form of payment does not change the Deductible.

**W.23** We will not reduce the Deductible because an insured elects not to repair or replace damaged property. We will not increase the Deductible because an insured elects to repair or replace damaged property.

**W.24** The Deductible applies to the amount of covered loss after any applicable adjustment for property that is recovered. Recovery of property does not itself eliminate the Deductible.

**W.25** If we recover property after making payment, an insured must cooperate with us in resolving the effect of that recovery. We may adjust payment to reflect the recovered property and the Deductible.

**W.26** The Deductible applies regardless of whether a claim is presented by you or by another insured. Presentation of separate claims does not by itself require separate application of the Deductible.

**W.27** An insured must promptly notify us of loss involving scheduled personal property. Delay in notice does not change the Deductible.

**W.28** An insured must protect damaged scheduled personal property from further damage when reasonably possible. The cost of protecting property is subject to the Deductible when it is part of covered loss.

**W.29** We may make payment for covered loss without admitting liability for any other loss. Such payment does not waive the Deductible as to any other covered loss.

**W.30** A disagreement concerning the amount of covered loss does not suspend application of the Deductible. We will apply the Deductible to the amount ultimately determined to be payable.

**W.31** The Deductible remains applicable unless we agree otherwise in writing. No act by us waives the Deductible unless the waiver is in writing.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss to "scheduled personal property" caused by or resulting from any peril excluded by this section. We do not cover loss that is excluded regardless of any other cause or event that contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an "insured". We do not cover loss caused by an insured acting with the intent to cause damage to property.

**W.3** We do not cover loss arising out of criminal conduct by an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not an insured is charged with or convicted of an offense.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, or sumps.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies to water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, pavement, swimming pool, or other structure.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by overflow of a body of water. We do not cover loss caused by spray from a body of water driven by wind.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, mudflow, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by volcanic eruption. We do not cover loss caused by earth movement that results from volcanic eruption.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover loss caused by cracking that results from such movement.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, or deterioration. We do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or mechanical failure.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, smog, or wet or dry rot. We do not cover loss caused by decay or other gradual deterioration.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We do not cover loss caused by pollutants released or dispersed by any source.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, scratching, chewing, or gnawing.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, mildew, or microbes. We do not cover loss caused by dampness or humidity.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by contamination. Contamination includes the presence, growth, release, or dispersal of harmful substances.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage when reasonably possible.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by intentional abandonment of property. We do not cover property that an insured discards, gives away, or relinquishes without our consent.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by governmental authority. We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of any ordinance, law, or regulation.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, and warlike action by a military force.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies whether the nuclear reaction is controlled or uncontrolled.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. Pollutants include solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, and chemical irritants or contaminants.

**W.48** We may pay for covered loss by repairing, restoring, replacing, or paying for the damaged or lost scheduled property. We may select the method of settlement that is appropriate under the circumstances.

**W.49** If we replace scheduled property, we may select property that is comparable in kind, quality, condition, utility, and value. We are not required to replace property with property having sentimental, historic, artistic, or collector value beyond that supported by reliable evidence.

**W.50** If we pay for a covered loss, we may take all or part of the damaged property at an agreed or appraised value. An insured must transfer to us any interest in property for which we make payment when we request the transfer.

**W.51** You must notify us promptly if recovered property is found after we make payment for its loss. You must allow us to decide whether you may retain the recovered property or must return payment to us.

**W.52** If recovered property has been damaged, we will determine the amount payable based on the covered loss remaining after recovery. An insured must cooperate with our inspection and evaluation of recovered property.

**W.53** An insured must keep records of communications concerning a loss with carriers, custodians, repairers, appraisers, law enforcement authorities, and other persons or organizations. You must provide those records to us when we reasonably request them.

**W.54** You must not incur expenses for appraisal, authentication, restoration, repair, storage, transport, or protection of scheduled property without our consent, except for reasonable expenses necessary to protect property from further loss. We will consider those necessary expenses in adjusting the claim.

**W.55** We do not cover expenses incurred to establish the marketability, provenance, authenticity, or ownership of scheduled property unless we agree to those expenses in writing. This exclusion does not apply to expenses we request in writing.

**W.56** You must notify us promptly if an insured dies, becomes legally incapacitated, or loses the legal authority to possess scheduled property. We may require information concerning the person authorized to act for the insured or the insured's estate.

**W.57** You must notify us promptly if scheduled property becomes part of an estate, trust, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, or other legal entity. We may require evidence of ownership and authority to insure the property.

**W.58** We may cancel or nonrenew coverage for scheduled property as permitted by applicable law. You must pay any premium due for coverage provided before cancellation or nonrenewal takes effect.

**W.59** Any waiver or change of a provision in this endorsement must be made by us in writing. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision later.

**W.60** If any provision of this endorsement is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. The invalid or unenforceable provision will be applied to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**W.61** An insured must comply with these Special Requirements as a condition of coverage. We do not cover a loss to the extent that your failure to comply with a requirement materially prejudices our ability to investigate, evaluate, or settle the loss.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will adjust each "covered loss" separately. We will determine the amount of loss from the facts available to us at the time of adjustment.

**W.2** "Covered property" means property for which coverage applies under this endorsement. We will not pay for loss caused by a peril, condition, or act that is not covered.

**W.3** "Schedule" means the description of covered property and related information shown for that property. The Schedule may identify property by description, category, location, value, or other identifying information.

**W.4** The loss settlement basis for covered property is replacement cost. We will apply that basis subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.5** We will determine whether the property was damaged, destroyed, lost, or stolen. We may require information reasonably necessary to make that determination.

**W.6** An insured must protect covered property from further loss after an occurrence. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property when practicable.

**W.7** We will pay only for direct physical loss to covered property. We will not pay for deterioration, neglect, or any increase in loss resulting from an insured's failure to protect the property.

**W.8** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must not discard, sell, give away, or otherwise dispose of damaged property without our consent unless necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.9** We may inspect covered property before or after repair, replacement, restoration, or disposal. We may examine available records, photographs, receipts, appraisals, and other evidence relating to ownership, condition, value, or loss.

**W.10** An insured must provide records and documents that reasonably support the claim. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**W.11** We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath. An insured must answer our reasonable questions concerning the loss, the covered property, and the claim.

**W.12** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of any claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining relevant information from persons having knowledge of the loss.

**W.13** We may require an insured to provide a signed statement describing the loss. The statement must identify the property involved, the circumstances of loss, and the extent of damage known to the insured.

**W.14** We may determine the value of covered property from purchase records, receipts, appraisals, market information, expert opinion, and other reliable evidence. We are not required to accept a value stated by an insured if available evidence does not support that value.

**W.15** "Actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss, considering depreciation and condition. We may use actual cash value when it is relevant to determine the amount of loss.

**W.16** "Depreciation" means a reduction in value resulting from age, wear, use, condition, obsolescence, or other applicable factors. We will determine depreciation from the evidence applicable to the damaged property.

**W.17** "Replacement cost" means the cost to replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation where that basis applies. Replacement cost does not include any cost that is not necessary to repair or replace covered property.

**W.18** We may consider reasonable repair cost when damaged property can be repaired. We will not pay more than the amount necessary to restore the damaged property to its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.19** We may consider the cost to replace property when repair is not practical or would not restore the property to its condition immediately before the loss. Replacement property need not be identical if identical property is not reasonably available.

**W.20** We may settle a covered loss by paying money, repairing the property, replacing the property, or arranging for repair or replacement. We may select the method of settlement consistent with the applicable loss settlement basis.

**W.21** If we elect to repair or replace covered property, we may select the persons or entities that perform the work or provide the property. An insured must cooperate in making the covered property available for that purpose.

**W.22** We may pay an insured, a person legally entitled to receive payment, or another person having a lawful interest in the covered property. Payment to any such person does not increase our limit of liability.

**W.23** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for covered property. We will not pay more than the amount of the covered loss.

**W.24** The value assigned to covered property in the Schedule does not establish that the property existed at the time of loss. An insured must establish ownership, possession, or another insurable interest in the property.

**W.25** The value assigned to covered property in the Schedule does not establish the condition of that property immediately before loss. We may consider evidence of condition in determining the amount of loss.

**W.26** We will not pay for any part of a loss that is not supported by reasonable evidence. We may consider the absence of records, documentation, or other available evidence in evaluating a claim.

**W.27** We will not pay for any improvement, betterment, enlargement, or alteration that is not necessary to repair or replace damaged covered property. We may pay for a reasonably available substitute when original materials or property are unavailable.

**W.28** We will not pay for loss of use, delay, inconvenience, or loss of sentimental value. We will not pay for any value arising solely from personal attachment to covered property.

**W.29** We will not pay for a reduction in value caused solely by the repair of covered property when the repair restores the property to its condition immediately before loss. We may consider diminished value only when required by applicable law.

**W.30** We may require an appraisal when we and an insured fail to agree on the amount of loss. Each party will select a competent appraiser and will bear the expense of that appraiser.

**W.31** The appraisers will select a competent and impartial umpire. If the appraisers cannot agree on an umpire, either party may seek selection of an umpire through a court of competent jurisdiction.

**W.32** The appraisers will state separately the amount of loss and the value of the covered property. An agreement by the appraisers or an agreement by an appraiser and the umpire will establish the amount of loss.

**W.33** Appraisal does not determine whether coverage applies. Appraisal does not waive any coverage condition, exclusion, limit of liability, or defense available to us.

**W.34** If an insured recovers property after we have paid for its loss, an insured must notify us promptly. We may require the insured to return our payment or permit us to take possession of the recovered property.

**W.35** If we pay for lost or stolen covered property and later recover that property, we may retain the property. If we return recovered property to an insured, we may adjust the payment to reflect the property's condition and value.

**W.36** We may take possession of damaged covered property for salvage after payment for that property. An insured must transfer to us any interest in salvage that is necessary to protect our rights.

**W.37** "Salvage" means damaged, recovered, or remaining property having value after a covered loss. Our possession of salvage does not increase our obligation to pay for the loss.

**W.38** We will reduce payment by amounts received or recoverable from another source for the same covered loss, to the extent permitted by law. An insured must not impair our right to recover from a person responsible for the loss.

**W.39** If we make payment, we may require an insured to assign to us rights of recovery against a person responsible for the loss. An insured must cooperate in preserving and enforcing those rights.

**W.40** We will not pay for duplicate recovery of the same loss. Payment under this endorsement will be coordinated with other applicable insurance and lawful sources of recovery.

**W.41** The "loss assessment additional coverage limit" does not increase the limit of liability applicable to covered property. A loss assessment does not change the loss settlement basis for covered property.

**W.42** The "fire service charge coverage" does not increase the amount we will pay for a covered loss to property. A fire service charge does not alter our valuation of covered property.

**W.43** The "jewelry and watches limit" applies as provided for covered jewelry and watches. The jewelry and watches limit does not establish the value of any item of jewelry or watch.

**W.44** The "on-premises business property cap" does not increase the limit of liability applicable to covered property. The on-premises business property cap does not establish ownership, condition, or value of property.

**W.45** We may postpone payment while an insured fails to comply with a duty that materially affects our ability to investigate, value, or settle the claim. We will not deny coverage solely because of a technical failure that does not materially affect our rights.

## W.8 — Definitions

"We," "us," and "our" mean the insurer providing this insurance. "You" and "your" mean the named insured shown in the Declarations and the spouse of that named insured if a resident of the same household.

"Insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives. Insured also means persons under the care of a person described in this definition.

"Policy" means the contract of insurance, including its Declarations, endorsements, and attached forms. The policy includes any schedule that identifies property insured under this coverage.

"Scheduled personal property" means personal property specifically described in the schedule. Scheduled personal property includes property insured for a stated limit and subject to any stated conditions.

"Property" means tangible personal property. Property does not include land, water, air, or interests in real property.

"Personal property" means property owned or used by an insured. Personal property includes property held for personal, family, or household use.

"Schedule" means the part of the policy that identifies scheduled personal property. The schedule may describe property by category, item, ownership interest, location, or other identifying information.

"Item" means a particular article of scheduled personal property. An item may consist of related articles that are described together in the schedule.

"Description" means the identifying information assigned to scheduled personal property in the schedule. A description may include characteristics, marks, materials, quality, origin, or other information used to identify property.

"Limit" means the maximum amount we will pay for covered loss to scheduled personal property. A limit applies subject to all policy terms and conditions.

"Loss" means accidental direct physical loss of or damage to property. Loss includes disappearance of property when the facts support that the property has been stolen.

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence must result in loss during the policy period.

"Theft" means an unlawful taking of property with intent to deprive the insured of it. Theft includes attempted theft resulting in direct physical damage to property.

"Mysterious disappearance" means the disappearance of property under unknown, uncertain, or unexplained circumstances. Mysterious disappearance does not establish that theft occurred.

"Damage" means physical injury to property that impairs its value, usefulness, or condition. Damage includes breakage, destruction, or loss of a material part of property.

"Direct physical loss" means loss that results from a physical event affecting property. Direct physical loss does not include loss based solely on diminished value, market conditions, or loss of use.

"Actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss, considering depreciation and physical condition. Actual cash value does not exceed the amount necessary to repair or replace the property with property of like kind and quality.

"Replacement cost" means the amount necessary to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement cost does not include any increase in value resulting from repair or replacement.

"Pair or set" means articles that are substantially alike, complementary, or customarily used together. A pair or set may be scheduled as a single item or as separate items.

"Fine arts" means paintings, drawings, etchings, sculptures, antiques, and similar articles of artistic, historical, or collectible value. Fine arts includes frames, mounts, and customary accessories attached to the article.

"Jewelry" means articles customarily worn as personal adornment. Jewelry includes watches, precious stones, precious metals, and articles containing such materials.

"Furs" means garments, accessories, and articles composed in whole or in part of animal fur. Furs includes articles lined, trimmed, or ornamented with fur.

"Silverware" means articles made wholly or partly of silver, gold, pewter, or platinum. Silverware includes flatware, hollowware, tea sets, trays, and similar articles.

"Firearms" means guns, rifles, pistols, and similar devices designed to discharge a projectile. Firearms includes accessories and attachments described with the firearm in the schedule.

"Musical instruments" means instruments designed to produce musical sound. Musical instruments includes cases, bows, reeds, and customary accessories described with the instrument in the schedule.

"Photographic equipment" means cameras, lenses, lighting equipment, and related equipment used to create or process images. Photographic equipment includes cases and customary accessories described with the equipment in the schedule.

"Collection" means articles assembled because of common characteristics, use, origin, subject matter, or value. A collection may be scheduled as a group or by individual item.

"Appraisal" means a written estimate of the value, authenticity, condition, or characteristics of property. An appraisal may be prepared by a qualified person selected by you, us, or both.

"Repair" means restoration of damaged property to substantially the condition existing immediately before loss. Repair includes necessary labor, materials, and services.

"Replace" means to furnish property of like kind and quality that performs substantially the same function as the property lost or damaged. Replacement may be made with property that is new, used, rebuilt, or reconditioned.

"Residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the land immediately surrounding that dwelling. Residence premises includes structures and portions of structures used by you in connection with the dwelling.

"Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain. Business includes activities conducted on a full-time, part-time, or occasional basis.

"Custody" means possession or control of property by an insured or by another person acting with an insured's permission. Custody does not transfer ownership of property.

"Concealment" means withholding or failing to disclose a material fact. Concealment includes an omission made with intent to mislead us.

"Misrepresentation" means a false statement of material fact made by or for an insured. Misrepresentation includes a statement made in connection with insurance, property, or loss.

"Material fact" means a fact that could affect our decision to provide coverage, determine a premium, investigate a loss, or settle a claim. A fact is material whether or not it concerns the amount of loss.
