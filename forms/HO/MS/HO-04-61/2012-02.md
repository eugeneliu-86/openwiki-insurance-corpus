# HO 04 61 Scheduled Personal Property (Edition 2012-02)
> SUPERSEDED by HO 04 61 Scheduled Personal Property (Edition 2020-11) for policies effective on or after 2020-11-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" is attached to and forms a part of your "policy". The terms of your policy apply unless this endorsement changes them.

The "Schedule" attached to this endorsement is incorporated into this endorsement. The Schedule identifies the "scheduled personal property" to which this endorsement applies.

We provide coverage for scheduled personal property only as described in the Schedule. Coverage applies subject to the terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions of your policy and this endorsement.

The description in the Schedule governs the identity of scheduled personal property. A description may include information that distinguishes the property from other property.

The Schedule may show an applicable "limit of liability" for scheduled personal property. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability shown in the Schedule.

Scheduled personal property must be shown in the Schedule for this endorsement to apply. Property not shown in the Schedule is not scheduled personal property.

Coverage for scheduled personal property does not extend to other property merely because the other property is similar in kind, quality, use, or value. The Schedule does not create coverage for property that is not described in it.

We apply the Schedule only to the property described in it. We do not extend coverage beyond the property and coverage shown in the Schedule.

The terms of this endorsement apply only to scheduled personal property. The terms of your policy continue to apply to property that is not scheduled personal property.

If the Schedule and this endorsement differ concerning the description of scheduled personal property, the Schedule controls for that description. If the Schedule and this endorsement differ concerning an applicable limit of liability, the Schedule controls for that limit.

The Schedule is part of this endorsement when attached to it. A Schedule that is not attached to this endorsement does not identify scheduled personal property under this endorsement.

Changes to the Schedule must be made by us. A change is effective only when made part of this endorsement.

We do not recognize a change based solely on an oral statement, representation, or understanding. A change must be shown in the Schedule or in an endorsement made by us.

This endorsement does not change the ownership of scheduled personal property. Coverage applies to your interest in scheduled personal property as provided by your policy and this endorsement.

This endorsement does not waive any condition of your policy. You must continue to comply with all conditions that apply to scheduled personal property.

We determine whether property is scheduled personal property from the Schedule and the facts of the loss. We may require information reasonably necessary to identify the property.

We do not cover loss to property that cannot be identified as scheduled personal property. You must provide available information that supports identification of the property.

The attachment of this endorsement does not provide coverage for a loss that is otherwise excluded. All exclusions that apply to scheduled personal property remain in effect unless changed by this endorsement.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** "Scheduled Personal Property" means personal property identified for coverage by description and an applicable limit. Scheduled Personal Property includes only property for which coverage is in force.

**W.2** "Covered property" means Scheduled Personal Property owned by an insured. Covered property also includes an interest of an insured in Scheduled Personal Property.

**W.3** "Direct physical loss" means accidental loss of or damage to tangible Covered property. Direct physical loss must result from a covered cause of loss.

**W.4** We cover direct physical loss to Covered property. Coverage applies while the property is anywhere in the world.

**W.5** Coverage applies to Covered property while it is in your possession. Coverage also applies while the property is temporarily in the possession of another person.

**W.6** Coverage applies while Covered property is being worn or used by an insured. Coverage does not depend on whether the property is located at your residence premises.

**W.7** We cover Covered property while it is being transported. Transportation includes handling that is customary and necessary to move the property.

**W.8** We cover Covered property while it is stored. Storage must be incidental to the ownership, use, maintenance, or repair of the property.

**W.9** We cover direct physical loss caused by theft. Theft includes loss resulting from a wrongful taking of Covered property.

**W.10** We cover direct physical loss caused by disappearance. An insured must provide information available to you concerning the circumstances of the disappearance.

**W.11** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental breakage. Accidental breakage includes loss caused by impact or dropping.

**W.12** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental damage. Accidental damage includes tearing, cracking, crushing, or similar physical injury.

**W.13** We cover direct physical loss caused by fire or lightning. Coverage applies whether the fire originates on or away from the residence premises.

**W.14** We cover direct physical loss caused by smoke. Smoke must be sudden and accidental.

**W.15** We cover direct physical loss caused by windstorm. Coverage applies to Covered property whether it is inside or outside a building.

**W.16** We cover direct physical loss caused by hail. Coverage applies to resulting breakage or other accidental physical damage.

**W.17** We cover direct physical loss caused by explosion. Coverage applies to direct damage resulting from the explosion.

**W.18** We cover direct physical loss caused by riot or civil commotion. Coverage applies to loss caused by acts occurring during the disturbance.

**W.19** We cover direct physical loss caused by aircraft. Coverage applies to loss caused by objects falling from aircraft.

**W.20** We cover direct physical loss caused by vehicles. Coverage applies to loss caused by vehicles operated by an insured or by another person.

**W.21** We cover direct physical loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief. Coverage applies to intentional damage caused by a person other than an insured.

**W.22** We cover direct physical loss caused by falling objects. Coverage applies to the portion of Covered property directly damaged by the falling object.

**W.23** We cover direct physical loss caused by the weight of snow, ice, or sleet. Coverage applies to resulting crushing, breakage, or other physical damage.

**W.24** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam. The discharge or overflow must originate within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system.

**W.25** We cover direct physical loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler systems. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat or otherwise protect the systems from freezing.

**W.26** We cover direct physical loss caused by sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. Coverage applies to direct damage to Covered property.

**W.27** We cover direct physical loss caused by sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current. Coverage applies to resulting physical damage to Covered property.

**W.28** We cover direct physical loss caused by water damage. Water damage includes direct physical damage caused by water that is released accidentally.

**W.29** We cover direct physical loss caused by collapse. Collapse means an abrupt falling down or caving in of Covered property or a part of Covered property.

**W.30** We cover direct physical loss caused by a sudden accidental event. The event must cause physical alteration, destruction, or disappearance of Covered property.

**W.31** The applicable limit is the most we will pay for direct physical loss to an item of Covered property. The applicable limit applies regardless of the number of persons having an interest in the property.

**W.32** The applicable limit applies to loss of the described item and its permanently attached parts. The applicable limit also applies to accessories customarily used with that item.

**W.33** We will not pay more than the applicable limit for loss to an item of Covered property. Payment for any loss does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.34** If Covered property is repaired, we will pay the reasonable cost of repair. Our payment will not exceed the applicable limit.

**W.35** If Covered property is replaced, we will pay the reasonable cost to replace it with property of like kind and quality. Our payment will not exceed the applicable limit.

**W.36** "Replacement cost" means the reasonable cost to repair or replace property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement cost does not include the value of sentimental, artistic, historic, or collector interest.

**W.37** We may pay the actual cash value of Covered property until repair or replacement is completed. "Actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss, considering its age, condition, and remaining useful life.

**W.38** We will make additional payment for replacement cost after you repair or replace the damaged property. An insured must provide records supporting the repair or replacement cost.

**W.39** We may elect to repair, replace, or restore Covered property. If we make that election, we may select persons reasonably qualified to perform the work.

**W.40** We may take all or part of damaged Covered property at the value agreed upon when we pay for the loss. An insured must make the property available to us if we request it.

**W.41** We will pay for reasonable costs incurred to protect Covered property from further damage after a covered loss. An insured must preserve the damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.42** An insured must use reasonable means to protect Covered property from further loss. This duty does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.43** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. Notice must identify the affected Covered property and describe the known circumstances of the loss.

**W.44** An insured must notify the appropriate law enforcement authority when Covered property is stolen, lost, or damaged by a criminal act. An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests for information concerning the report.

**W.45** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed, or stolen Covered property. The inventory must show descriptions, quantities, dates of purchase, values, and amounts of loss to the extent known.

**W.46** An insured must provide records and documents that support the ownership, value, and condition of Covered property. Records may include receipts, appraisals, photographs, certificates, correspondence, or similar evidence.

**W.47** An insured must show us the damaged Covered property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and the location where the loss occurred.

**W.48** An insured must submit to examination under oath if we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the examination record.

**W.49** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss if we request it. The statement must set forth the information reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim.

**W.50** We may require an appraisal when you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss. Each party will select a competent appraiser and will bear the expense of its selected appraiser.

**W.51** The appraisers will select a competent and impartial umpire. An agreement by the appraisers or an agreement by an appraiser and the umpire will establish the amount of loss.

**W.52** We will pay our share of the umpire expense and other appraisal expenses. An insured will pay your share of those expenses.

**W.53** "Pair or set" means property that is part of a matched, complementary, or related group. Loss to part of a pair or set will be adjusted based on the direct physical loss to the damaged or missing property.

**W.54** We may repair or replace the damaged part of a pair or set. We may also pay the difference between the actual cash value of the property before and after the loss.

**W.55** We may pay the loss in money. We may also arrange for repair, replacement, or restoration of Covered property.

**W.56** Payment will be made to you and any person shown to have a legal interest in Covered property. Payment to a person with a legal interest will not exceed that person's interest in the property.

**W.57** We will adjust each loss separately. Loss affecting more than one item of Covered property will be evaluated according to the applicable limit for each affected item.

**W.58** We will determine the amount of loss using the condition of Covered property immediately before the loss. We will consider any available evidence concerning the property's ownership, age, quality, and value.

**W.59** We will not reduce payment solely because Covered property was custom made or unique. We may consider evidence concerning comparable property, materials, workmanship, and market availability.

**W.60** We may obtain information concerning ownership or value from persons having knowledge of the property. An insured must authorize the release of information reasonably necessary to investigate the loss.

**W.61** We may require proof that an insured had an insurable interest in Covered property at the time of loss. Payment will not exceed the value of that interest.

**W.62** Coverage for Covered property applies without regard to where the loss occurs. An insured must comply with applicable duties after loss regardless of the location of the property.

**W.63** Coverage applies to Covered property while it is temporarily entrusted to a repairer, cleaner, jeweler, conservator, or similar custodian. An insured must retain available records concerning the transfer of possession.

**W.64** Coverage applies to Covered property while it is displayed, exhibited, or loaned. An insured must provide information available to you concerning the person or location having possession of the property.

**W.65** Coverage applies to Covered property while it is being packed, unpacked, installed, maintained, or repaired. The property must remain identifiable as Covered property.

**W.66** Coverage applies to parts and accessories that are included within the description of Covered property. Parts and accessories not included within that description are not Covered property.

**W.67** Coverage applies to property acquired as a replacement for Covered property when the replacement is identified for coverage. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary to identify the replacement property.

**W.68** Coverage applies to a change in the physical location of Covered property. A change in location does not alter the applicable limit.

**W.69** Coverage applies to property jointly owned by an insured and another person if the property is Covered property. We will pay only for the insured's interest unless all interested persons establish their respective interests.

**W.70** Coverage applies to property held by an insured under a lease or similar agreement if the property is identified as Covered property. We will pay only for the insured's financial interest in that property.

**W.71** An insured must not voluntarily abandon Covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment for the loss.

**W.72** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. Cooperation includes providing truthful and complete information reasonably requested by us.

**W.73** We may inspect Covered property before or after a loss. Our inspection does not constitute a representation that the property is safe, secure, or adequately protected.

**W.74** We may request an appraisal, receipt, invoice, photograph, certificate, or other evidence of value. An insured must provide available evidence within a reasonable time after our request.

**W.75** We may require an insured to identify any other person having possession, ownership, or a legal interest in Covered property. An insured must provide contact information available to you for that person.

**W.76** Coverage applies to loss that occurs during the policy term while the property is Covered property. The loss must be discovered by an insured within a reasonable time after it occurs.

**W.77** The obligations imposed by this coverage apply to each insured seeking payment. A breach of a duty by an insured may affect coverage only as permitted by applicable law.

**W.78** We will provide coverage subject to the terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions applicable to this coverage. We will pay covered loss in accordance with the applicable limit and the terms of this coverage.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The limit of liability for "scheduled property" is the applicable limit shown for that property under this policy.  
Subject to the terms of this policy, that limit applies to covered loss or damage to the scheduled property.  
We will not pay more than the applicable limit for the covered property.

**W.2** We determine our liability for covered loss or damage based on the property identified in the policy.  
Except that coverage applies only to property that meets the description of scheduled property, we do not insure unidentified property under that limit.  
You must promptly notify us of any change that materially affects the identification of the property.

**W.3** The limit of liability applies separately to each item of scheduled property.  
Subject to the applicable terms of coverage, an item remains subject to its own limit when covered loss or damage affects that item.  
We do not combine limits for separate items unless this policy provides otherwise.

**W.4** We may pay the lesser of the amount required to repair or replace covered property and the applicable limit of liability.  
Except that this provision applies only to covered loss or damage, we do not pay amounts attributable to an excluded cause of loss.  
We may make payment to you or to a person or organization that has an interest in the property.

**W.5** The applicable limit of liability does not increase because the cost of repair or replacement exceeds that limit.  
Subject to the terms governing valuation, the limit remains applicable regardless of the method used to determine the amount of loss.  
You remain responsible for any amount that exceeds the applicable limit.

**W.6** We apply the limit of liability to the covered property that is damaged, destroyed, or lost.  
Unless this policy provides coverage for related property, we do not use the limit for one item to cover another item.  
We determine the amount payable after applying all applicable conditions and exclusions.

**W.7** A covered loss may involve more than one part of scheduled property.  
Subject to the applicable limit for each affected item, we determine our liability for each item separately.  
We do not pay duplicate amounts because the same loss affects related parts of the property.

**W.8** We may require reasonable evidence of the identity, ownership, and value of scheduled property.  
Except that our request must relate to the claimed loss, you must provide the evidence in your possession or control.  
Our receipt of evidence does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.9** We determine the amount of covered loss after considering the condition of the property immediately before the loss.  
Subject to the applicable valuation provision, we do not determine the amount of loss solely from the cost of acquiring new property.  
We may consider age, condition, use, and quality when those factors are relevant to valuation.

**W.10** We may settle a covered loss by paying the amount of the loss or by repairing or replacing the property.  
Provided that the settlement does not exceed the applicable limit, the method of settlement is at our option.  
You must cooperate with the method of settlement we select.

**W.11** We may replace damaged or destroyed property with property of like kind and quality.  
Subject to availability and the applicable limit, the replacement property need not be identical to the property that was lost.  
We will not replace property that is not covered under this policy.

**W.12** We may repair damaged scheduled property instead of paying the cost of replacement.  
Except that the repair must reasonably restore the property to its condition before the loss, we are not required to use the original manufacturer or supplier.  
The repaired property remains subject to the terms of this policy.

**W.13** We may use salvaged, refurbished, or comparable materials when they are suitable for the repair.  
Unless the nature of the property requires different materials, we are not required to restore concealed or nonfunctional features that were not damaged.  
Our repair obligation remains limited by the applicable limit of liability.

**W.14** We may pay the reasonable cost of necessary services performed to prevent further covered damage.  
Subject to the applicable limit and the terms governing additional expenses, payment for those services does not create a separate limit.  
You must take reasonable steps to protect the property from further damage.

**W.15** We may pay for covered property that cannot be located after a covered event.  
Except that the property must be reasonably identified and its loss must be supported by available evidence, an unsupported claim is not payable.  
Payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.16** We do not pay more than the amount of your interest in the covered property.  
Subject to any applicable mortgage or other secured interest, payment is limited to the interest established by the evidence of loss.  
You must disclose any known interest held by another person or organization.

**W.17** We may pay a person or organization with a valid interest in covered property.  
Unless the payment fully satisfies the covered claim, payment to an interested person does not release you from your duties under this policy.  
We may require evidence of the recipient's right to payment.

**W.18** We apply any payment for covered property to the loss for which the payment is made.  
Subject to our rights under this policy, a payment reduces the amount remaining available for that covered loss.  
You must not seek duplicate payment for the same loss from us.

**W.19** We may make partial payment when the full amount of covered loss has not been established.  
Except that partial payment does not waive any applicable condition or exclusion, the remaining claim must be supported by evidence of loss.  
The total payment for the covered loss will not exceed the applicable limit of liability.

**W.20** We may withhold payment for property whose ownership or right to payment is disputed.  
Subject to resolution of the dispute and receipt of satisfactory evidence, we are not required to pay the disputed amount.  
We may pay any undisputed amount that is otherwise owed under this policy.

**W.21** We do not pay for an increase in the value of property resulting from repair or replacement.  
Unless the increase is inseparable from work reasonably required to restore covered property, the increase is not part of the covered loss.  
You must provide information reasonably necessary to distinguish restoration from improvement.

**W.22** We do not pay for the cost of correcting a defect that caused or contributed to the loss.  
Except that we may pay for resulting damage that is otherwise covered, the defect itself is not covered property damage.  
The applicable limit governs any payment for the resulting covered damage.

**W.23** We determine whether damaged property can be repaired before determining the amount payable for replacement.  
Subject to the nature and extent of the covered damage, we may use a repair estimate as evidence of the amount of loss.  
You must allow reasonable access for inspection and evaluation.

**W.24** We may inspect scheduled property before agreeing to the amount of a covered loss.  
Provided that the inspection is reasonably related to the claim, you must permit us to examine the property and relevant records.  
An inspection does not constitute an admission that the loss is covered.

**W.25** We may require an appraisal or other evaluation when the amount of loss cannot be agreed upon.  
Unless this policy requires a different procedure, the evaluation determines the amount of loss but does not determine whether the loss is covered.  
Coverage remains subject to all applicable terms, conditions, and exclusions.

**W.26** We apply the applicable limit of liability after determining that the claimed loss is covered.  
Subject to the terms of this policy, the limit does not expand coverage for property, causes of loss, or expenses that are otherwise excluded.  
Our payment for covered scheduled property is limited to the amount determined under these provisions.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The deductible applying to scheduled personal property is zero dollars, except that this deductible applies only to covered loss to scheduled personal property. We apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss remaining after all applicable conditions are satisfied.

**W.2** We determine the amount of covered loss before applying the deductible. We do not reduce the value of covered property by the deductible before determining the amount of loss. The deductible reduces the amount we otherwise owe for covered loss.

**W.3** The deductible applies to loss to property scheduled under this coverage. We do not apply the deductible to property that is not scheduled under this coverage. The deductible applies only when the loss is covered.

**W.4** You must provide us with information sufficient to identify the scheduled personal property involved in a loss. You must identify the property and describe the circumstances of the loss. We may require supporting information that is reasonably available to you.

**W.5** An insured must protect the scheduled personal property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property from further damage. An insured must keep an accurate record of those repairs and expenses.

**W.6** You must promptly notify us of a loss involving scheduled personal property. You must include the known circumstances of the loss in the notice. You must provide additional information when we reasonably request it.

**W.7** We determine the amount of loss based on the information available to us. We may inspect the damaged property before making payment. You must permit us to inspect the property at a reasonable time.

**W.8** An insured must preserve the damaged property for our inspection. An insured must not dispose of the property without our consent. We may waive this requirement by written notice.

**W.9** You must cooperate with us in the investigation of a loss. You must answer questions truthfully and completely. You must attend an examination under oath when we reasonably request one.

**W.10** We may require a signed proof of loss for a loss involving scheduled personal property. You must state the known facts concerning the loss in the proof of loss. You must sign the proof of loss and provide it to us within the time we require.

**W.11** We may require records supporting the ownership or value of scheduled personal property. You must provide records that are in your possession or control. We will consider other reasonably available evidence when those records are not available.

**W.12** You must provide us with receipts, appraisals, photographs, or other evidence that supports your claim. We may use that evidence to determine the amount of covered loss. We do not waive any policy condition by requesting or reviewing evidence.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by a concealment of material facts. We do not cover loss caused by a material misrepresentation made by an insured. These limitations apply to the extent permitted by the policy.

**W.14** We do not cover an amount that exceeds the applicable limit of insurance. We determine the amount payable before applying any deductible. The deductible does not increase the limit of insurance.

**W.15** We do not cover an amount that is not supported by evidence of covered loss. We may determine that evidence supports only part of a claimed loss. We apply the deductible to the supported amount of covered loss.

**W.16** An insured must separate covered property from property that is not involved in the loss. An insured must identify damage that existed before the loss. We do not cover damage that did not result from the covered loss.

**W.17** We do not cover wear and tear, deterioration, or an inherent defect. We do not cover loss that results solely from a condition excluded by the policy. We may cover ensuing loss when the policy provides coverage for that loss.

**W.18** You must take reasonable steps to prevent theft, damage, or destruction of scheduled personal property. You must comply with reasonable safeguards that apply to the property. We do not cover loss resulting from your failure to take those steps when that failure causes the loss.

**W.19** An insured must notify the police when a loss involves theft or another criminal act. An insured must provide us with the police report or other evidence of the report when we request it. This duty applies unless reporting is not reasonably possible.

**W.20** We may require an appraisal of damaged scheduled personal property. You must cooperate with a reasonable appraisal request. An appraisal does not determine whether the loss is covered.

**W.21** We may pay the amount of covered loss in accordance with the applicable valuation provision. We may use the least costly method that restores the property to its condition immediately before the loss when that method is permitted. We do not owe more than the amount required under the applicable valuation provision.

**W.22** You must not abandon scheduled personal property to us. We may take possession of property that we pay for when permitted by the policy. You must transfer your interest in that property when we reasonably request the transfer.

**W.23** We may repair or replace damaged scheduled personal property instead of paying the amount of loss. You must permit us reasonable access to the property for that purpose. We will notify you before undertaking a repair or replacement.

**W.24** We may pay the amount of loss directly to you or to a person having an interest in the property. Payment to a person having an interest in the property does not increase our obligation. You must cooperate in identifying each person entitled to payment.

**W.25** You must notify us of any change in ownership, possession, or location of scheduled personal property when the change affects the risk. You must provide the information we reasonably need to evaluate the change. We may apply the policy provisions that govern the changed risk.

**W.26** We do not cover loss to property that is sold, transferred, or otherwise disposed of before the loss. We do not cover an interest that you no longer have in the property. This limitation does not apply when the policy continues coverage after the change.

**W.27** An insured must use reasonable care to maintain scheduled personal property. An insured must comply with applicable laws and regulations concerning the property. We do not cover loss caused by a failure to comply with those duties.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts of an insured. We do not cover loss caused by an act performed with the intent to cause the loss. This exclusion applies even if another person contributed to the loss.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by dishonest or fraudulent conduct of an insured. We do not cover a claim that contains a material false statement. These exclusions apply to the extent the conduct affects the claim.

**W.30** You must promptly inform us of any other insurance that may cover the loss. You must provide the name of the other insurer and the applicable policy information. We determine our obligation in accordance with the insurance provisions that apply to the loss.

**W.31** We may recover from a person responsible for a covered loss after we pay the claim. You must preserve our rights of recovery. You must not impair those rights by agreement, release, or other action.

**W.32** You must assist us in enforcing rights of recovery against a person responsible for a loss. You must sign documents and provide information that we reasonably request. We pay the reasonable expenses you incur at our request.

**W.33** We may require an insured to testify about the loss. An insured must provide testimony that is truthful and complete. The testimony may concern the property, the loss, or the amount claimed.

**W.34** We do not cover loss that is excluded by the policy. We do not cover an amount that results from an excluded cause of loss. We may cover a separate loss when that loss is otherwise covered.

**W.35** You must distinguish damage caused by the covered loss from damage caused by an excluded cause. You must provide evidence supporting that distinction. We determine the covered amount from the evidence provided and from our investigation.

**W.36** We do not cover the cost of correcting a defect that caused the loss. We do not cover the cost of improving scheduled personal property beyond its condition immediately before the loss. We may cover resulting damage when the policy provides coverage for it.

**W.37** An insured must take reasonable measures to secure scheduled personal property after a loss. An insured must notify us before making permanent repairs when notice is reasonably possible. We may inspect repairs that are made without our prior inspection.

**W.38** We may settle a covered loss when we receive satisfactory evidence of the amount of loss. You must accept payment made in accordance with the policy. Payment does not waive any right or defense concerning an amount not included in the payment.

**W.39** You must promptly notify us if you discover additional damage after submitting a claim. You must provide evidence that the additional damage resulted from the covered loss. We may inspect the additional damage before considering payment.

**W.40** We do not cover duplicate recovery for the same loss. We may deduct amounts paid or payable from another source when the policy permits that deduction. You must refund any payment that exceeds the amount owed under the policy.

**W.41** An insured must comply with all applicable duties after a loss involving scheduled personal property. A failure to comply may affect the amount payable under the policy. We apply that consequence only to the extent the failure prejudices our investigation or determination of the loss.

**W.42** We determine the amount payable after applying the coverage provisions, valuation provisions, applicable limits, and the deductible. You must cooperate with our determination of the amount payable. We owe no amount for a loss that is not covered under the policy.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** In this section, "covered property" means property for which coverage is provided under this endorsement. "Loss" means accidental direct physical loss to covered property.

**W.2** We do not cover loss that is excluded by the terms, conditions, limitations, or exclusions applicable to covered property. This endorsement does not make excluded property covered.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, earth sinking, rising, shifting, and expanding soil.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, or sumps.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail to the extent the loss is subject to the minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage. The applicable deductible does not create coverage for property or causes of loss otherwise excluded.

**W.6** We do not cover loss to structures that are subject to the other structures limit when that property is not covered property. The other structures limit does not increase the coverage provided for scheduled property.

**W.7** We do not cover loss exceeding coverage otherwise available under the contents limit percentage. The contents limit percentage does not expand coverage for property that is excluded.

**W.8** We do not cover expenses that exceed coverage otherwise available under the ALE percentage. The ALE percentage does not provide coverage for loss to property.

**W.9** We do not cover debris removal costs that exceed coverage otherwise available under the debris removal additional percentage. The debris removal additional percentage does not provide coverage for debris resulting from an excluded loss.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, demolition, zoning, or land use. This exclusion applies whether enforcement occurs before, during, or after a loss.

**W.11** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, remove, or alter property because it fails to comply with an ordinance or law. We also do not cover the reduced value of property resulting from an ordinance or law.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of a loss. This exclusion does not require an insured to endanger any person.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with all conditions applicable to this coverage. Compliance by an insured is a condition precedent to our obligation to pay for a "loss".

**W.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss and, in all events, must report the loss within ninety days. Notice must state the identity of the insured, the location of the loss, and available information concerning the property involved.

**W.3** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when the loss may involve theft, disappearance, vandalism, or other unlawful conduct. An insured must provide us with information concerning any report made to those authorities.

**W.4** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect "covered property" from further damage after a loss. An insured must keep a record of expenses incurred to protect covered property and must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.5** An insured must not abandon covered property to us unless we agree in writing. We may take possession of damaged property after a loss without waiving any coverage condition or admitting liability.

**W.6** An insured must permit us to inspect covered property and the location of the loss as often as reasonably required. An insured must provide reasonable access to information, records, and persons having knowledge of the loss.

**W.7** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of any "claim". An insured must not interfere with our efforts to determine the cause, extent, or value of a claim.

**W.8** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed, or missing covered property when we request it. The inventory must identify the property, describe its condition, and state its claimed value and the amount of loss.

**W.9** An insured must provide available bills, receipts, appraisals, photographs, purchase records, and other documents supporting ownership or value of covered property. An insured must authorize us to obtain relevant records from persons or organizations having such records.

**W.10** An insured must show us the damaged covered property when reasonably requested. An insured must not repair, alter, discard, sell, or otherwise dispose of damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, except as necessary to protect property from further damage.

**W.11** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the transcript of the examination when it is accurately prepared.

**W.12** An insured must provide a signed, sworn "proof of loss" when we request it. The proof of loss must state the facts concerning the loss, the interest of all persons in the property, and the amount claimed.

**W.13** An insured must comply with the "proof of loss period" applicable to a proof of loss. A failure to provide a requested proof of loss may affect coverage to the extent we are prejudiced by that failure.

**W.14** An insured must provide information concerning any other insurance that may apply to covered property or the loss. An insured must provide copies of policies, declarations, endorsements, and correspondence relating to that insurance when we request them.

**W.15** An insured must notify us of any person or organization asserting an interest in covered property. An insured must not settle, release, or compromise that person's interest without our written consent when the action may affect our rights.

**W.16** An insured must promptly forward to us any notice, demand, summons, complaint, or other legal paper received concerning a claim. An insured must not admit liability, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses necessary to protect covered property.

**W.17** An insured must preserve all rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for a loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right to recover from that person or organization.

**W.18** If we make payment under this coverage, an insured must transfer to us any rights of recovery against another person or organization to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing those rights.

**W.19** An insured must execute documents and take actions reasonably necessary to secure our rights of recovery. Our exercise of those rights does not reduce any remaining duty of an insured under this coverage.

**W.20** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to this insurance, covered property, a loss, or a claim. We do not provide coverage for an insured who has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact.

**W.21** An insured must maintain records sufficient to establish ownership of scheduled property when reasonably possible. The absence of a record does not by itself establish ownership, value, or the extent of a loss.

**W.22** An insured must notify us promptly if the ownership, possession, or principal location of covered property changes. An insured must provide information concerning the change when we request it.

**W.23** An insured must notify us promptly if covered property becomes subject to a lien, security interest, court order, seizure, or other claim of right. We may investigate the effect of that interest on coverage and payment.

**W.24** An insured must use reasonable care to preserve covered property from foreseeable loss or damage. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's intentional failure to use reasonable care after becoming aware of circumstances likely to cause damage.

**W.25** An insured must promptly notify us when recovered property is found after a claim for that property has been made. An insured must cooperate with us in determining whether the recovered property will be retained, returned, repaired, or sold.

**W.26** If covered property is recovered after we have made payment for its loss, an insured must hold the property for us when we request it. An insured must reimburse us for any payment that exceeds the covered loss after accounting for the recovered property.

**W.27** An insured must return any payment made in error when we request its return. Our request for repayment does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.28** An insured must notify us if any information provided in connection with a claim becomes inaccurate or incomplete. An insured must correct that information promptly after discovering the inaccuracy or incompleteness.

**W.29** An insured must provide us with access to electronic records, communications, images, and stored information relevant to a claim when reasonably requested. An insured must preserve such information when it may be material to our investigation.

**W.30** An insured must cooperate in identifying the cause and circumstances of a loss. An insured must provide the names and contact information of persons known to have relevant information when reasonably requested.

**W.31** An insured must permit us to obtain statements from persons having information concerning a claim. An insured must not direct a person to withhold information from us.

**W.32** An insured must submit any disputed amount of loss to appraisal when appraisal is available and properly demanded. An insured must cooperate in selecting or participating with any appraiser or other person required for that process.

**W.16** An insured must preserve evidence relating to the cause and extent of a loss when reasonably possible. An insured must not alter, destroy, or discard relevant evidence without our consent, except when necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.17** We may require an insured to provide proof of ownership before we pay for a loss. We may require documentation showing the acquisition, possession, and value of scheduled property.

**W.18** We may require an insured to provide a signed statement describing the circumstances of a loss. The statement must identify all persons with knowledge of the loss and all other insurance that may apply.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by any dishonest or criminal act committed by an insured, whether acting alone or in collusion with another person. This exclusion applies whether or not the property is recovered.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by voluntary parting with scheduled property if induced by any fraudulent scheme, trick, device, or false pretense. This exclusion applies even if an insured intended to transfer possession but not title.

**W.21** We do not cover unexplained loss of scheduled property when the facts do not establish that a covered cause of loss occurred. We do not cover inventory shortage or unexplained disappearance discovered only through an accounting or inventory process.

**W.22** "Mysterious disappearance" means disappearance of scheduled property under circumstances that cannot be explained by a known cause of loss. We do not cover mysterious disappearance unless the schedule specifically provides otherwise.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical breakdown. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss unless otherwise excluded.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by insects, vermin, rodents, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or contamination. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss that results from such condition unless otherwise excluded.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by processing, servicing, repairing, restoring, or renovating scheduled property. We do cover loss caused by fire or explosion resulting from such activity unless otherwise excluded.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by intentional alteration, alteration of identifying marks, or removal of serial numbers from scheduled property. This exclusion does not apply when removal is required as part of a documented repair or restoration.

**W.27** An insured must notify us before making a material alteration to scheduled property that affects its identity, condition, authenticity, or value. We may require revised documentation after the alteration.

**W.28** "Restoration" means work intended to repair, conserve, stabilize, or return scheduled property to a prior condition. An insured must obtain our consent before authorizing restoration of property involved in a loss, except for reasonable emergency measures.

**W.29** We may select the person or organization that will repair, restore, or replace scheduled property after a loss. We will consider an insured's reasonable preference when practicable.

**W.30** We may pay for loss in money, repair, restoration, replacement, or any combination of these methods. Our election of a method of payment does not transfer ownership of scheduled property to us.

**W.31** "Replacement cost" means the cost to replace scheduled property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. Replacement cost does not include any increase in value attributable to sentimental, historic, or collectible qualities.

**W.32** "Actual cash value" means replacement cost less a deduction for depreciation and condition. We may use actual cash value when replacement is not reasonably available or when an insured does not replace the property.

**W.33** "Market value" means the value that scheduled property would reasonably bring in an open and competitive market. Market value may be determined from sales of comparable property, recognized market data, or other reliable evidence.

**W.34** An "agreed value" is the value shown for scheduled property in the schedule. The agreed value is not proof that the property was owned by an insured at the time of loss.

**W.35** We may require transfer to us of damaged scheduled property for which we make payment. We may require an insured to execute documents reasonably necessary to transfer title or salvage rights.

**W.36** An insured must not incur expense for repair, restoration, or replacement without our consent, except for reasonable expense necessary to protect scheduled property from further loss. We will not pay for expense that increases value beyond the condition immediately before loss.

**W.37** If scheduled property is part of a "pair or set", we may repair or replace the affected item or pay for the reduction in value of the pair or set. Pair or set means property that has greater value because its components belong together.

**W.38** We do not cover loss to property while it is entrusted to a carrier, bailee, repairer, cleaner, appraiser, auctioneer, consignee, or similar custodian, to the extent that person is legally responsible. We may pursue recovery rights against any person responsible for the loss.

**W.39** An insured must preserve our rights against persons responsible for loss. An insured must not waive those rights after a loss without our written consent.

**W.40** If an insured recovers scheduled property after we have paid for the loss, an insured must promptly notify us. We may require return of our payment or transfer of the recovered property to us, subject to our interest in the property.

**W.41** We may settle a claim with any insured who has an interest in scheduled property. Payment to that insured discharges our obligation to the extent of the payment.

**W.42** Fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation by an insured concerning scheduled property, a loss, or a claim voids coverage for the affected property. This provision applies whether the conduct occurs before or after a loss.

**W.43** An insured must comply with these special requirements as a condition of coverage. Failure to comply may affect our obligation to provide coverage to the extent permitted by law.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "loss" to "covered personal property" on this basis: replacement cost. Settlement is subject to the "applicable limit" for the property involved.

**W.2** We will consider the property description, ownership information, and available evidence when settling a loss. We may request information that reasonably identifies the property and its condition.

**W.3** An insured must provide information supporting the amount of a "claim". An insured must provide available records of purchase, ownership, maintenance, repair, and valuation.

**W.4** An insured must identify the property for which payment is requested. An insured must distinguish property damaged by the loss from property that was damaged before the loss.

**W.5** We may inspect "damaged property" before settlement. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property and any remaining parts of that property.

**W.6** An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably possible. An insured must not dispose of damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.7** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from further damage after a loss. An insured must keep a record of expenses incurred to protect the property.

**W.8** We will consider reasonable evidence of the property’s condition immediately before the loss. Such evidence may include photographs, receipts, records, descriptions, and testimony.

**W.9** An insured must provide available records showing the property’s age, use, condition, and features. An insured must provide available records showing repairs, alterations, or improvements affecting value.

**W.10** We may consider the quality, workmanship, materials, and useful condition of the property. We may consider evidence of wear, prior damage, and prior repair.

**W.11** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a claim. An insured must answer reasonable questions concerning ownership, possession, use, and damage.

**W.12** An insured must provide a signed statement when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide information that is known to the insured and relevant to the loss.

**W.13** An insured must provide a complete description of the claimed damage. An insured must identify any part of the property that remains usable after the loss.

**W.14** An insured must identify any person or organization that may have an interest in the property. An insured must provide available information concerning the nature of that interest.

**W.15** We may request that damaged property be separated from undamaged property. An insured must preserve identifying marks, labels, serial information, and other available evidence of identity.

**W.16** We may require reasonable documentation of ownership. An insured must provide available bills of sale, receipts, appraisals, photographs, or similar records supporting ownership.

**W.17** An insured must notify us before making a "repair" that prevents our inspection of the damage. An insured must preserve removed parts when reasonably possible.

**W.18** We may make payment after receiving information reasonably needed to settle the claim. An insured must provide requested information that is available to the insured before requesting payment.

**W.19** We may make payment to you and an "interested party" shown by the available evidence. Payment to an interested party does not increase our obligation.

**W.20** We may require evidence that an insured has authority to receive payment for the property. An insured must provide available evidence of that authority.

**W.21** Payment for a loss does not transfer ownership of the property to us unless we agree otherwise. An insured must protect our rights concerning property for which we make payment.

**W.22** We may take possession of "salvage" for property for which we make payment. An insured must make salvage available to us when we request it.

**W.23** We do not cover property that an insured voluntarily abandons to us. An insured must not abandon property to us without our written agreement.

**W.24** We will settle loss to a "pair or set" based on the loss to the property that was directly damaged. We do not cover loss caused solely by the reduced usefulness of an undamaged companion item.

**W.25** We do not cover the cost of "matching" undamaged property with damaged property. We may consider matching when it is necessary to restore damaged property to a reasonably comparable condition.

**W.26** We do not cover loss of use of covered personal property under this provision. We do not cover inconvenience resulting from repair, replacement, or settlement of property.

**W.27** We do not cover "diminution in value" that remains after damaged property has been repaired or replaced. We do not cover a decrease in value based solely on an insured’s personal preference.

**W.28** We do not cover changes in "market value" that occur after the loss. We may consider market information that reasonably reflects the property’s condition immediately before the loss.

**W.29** We do not cover the cost to correct a defect, fault, or condition that existed before the loss. We may pay for direct physical damage caused by a covered loss, subject to the applicable limit.

**W.30** We may consider the availability and cost of reasonably comparable "replacement property". An insured must provide available information concerning any proposed replacement property.

**W.31** We may consider whether proposed materials and workmanship are reasonably comparable to the damaged property. We are not required to pay for better quality, greater capacity, or additional features.

**W.32** We are not required to select a contractor, vendor, repair facility, or supplier for you. An insured must remain responsible for agreements made with a contractor, vendor, repair facility, or supplier.

**W.33** Payment of an undisputed amount does not waive our right to investigate the remaining claim. An insured must continue to cooperate with our reasonable investigation after receiving payment.

**W.34** We may request information from persons who have knowledge of the loss or the property. An insured must authorize the release of available information that is relevant to the claim.

**W.35** We may consider estimates, appraisals, invoices, and other valuation evidence. We are not required to accept an estimate, appraisal, invoice, or valuation that is unsupported by available facts.

**W.36** We do not cover a claim involving "fraud" by an insured relating to the loss. An insured must not conceal, fabricate, alter, or destroy material evidence relating to a claim.

**W.37** We do not cover a claim involving material "misrepresentation" by an insured concerning the loss or the property. An insured must provide truthful information in connection with a claim.

**W.38** We may reduce payment by amounts already paid for the same loss by another source. An insured must notify us of payments received or expected for the same damaged property.

**W.39** We will not make duplicate payment for the same loss. An insured must preserve our right to recover payment from a person or organization responsible for the damage.

**W.40** An insured must notify us of any claim against another person or organization arising from the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in preserving evidence relevant to that claim.

**W.41** We may settle a loss based on information available to us when an insured does not provide requested information. An insured must provide additional available information when it becomes known after settlement.

**W.42** We may revise settlement when material information was unavailable when payment was made. An insured must promptly notify us of material information affecting the claim.

**W.43** We may issue payment jointly when the available evidence shows that more than one person has an interest in the property. An insured must assist us in identifying persons with an interest in the property.

**W.44** We may withhold payment for property that is subject to unresolved ownership concerns. An insured must provide available evidence resolving those concerns.

**W.45** We may consider the condition of property after repair when evaluating a claim for additional payment. An insured must make repaired property available for inspection when reasonably requested.

**W.46** An insured must retain receipts and other available records for claimed expenses. We will consider reasonable expenses that are directly related to protecting damaged property from further damage.

**W.47** We do not cover expenses that are unrelated to the direct physical damage to covered personal property. An insured must identify the purpose of each claimed expense.

**W.48** We may settle a claim by payment, repair, replacement, or another method agreed by us and you. An insured must cooperate with the selected settlement method.

**W.49** We may require that payment be applied to the claimed damaged property. An insured must provide available evidence showing that settlement proceeds were applied to that property when we reasonably request it.

**W.50** Settlement under this coverage does not alter the "loss assessment additional coverage limit". The loss assessment additional coverage limit remains subject to its applicable terms.

**W.51** Settlement under this coverage does not alter "fire service charge coverage". Fire service charge coverage remains subject to its applicable terms.

**W.52** Settlement under this coverage does not alter the "jewelry and watches limit". The jewelry and watches limit remains subject to its applicable terms.

**W.53** Settlement under this coverage does not alter the "on-premises business property cap". The on-premises business property cap remains subject to its applicable terms.

**W.54** We may require an insured to identify property used for business, professional, or commercial purposes. An insured must provide available information concerning the use of that property.

**W.55** We may consider whether property was permanently attached, temporarily attached, or freely movable when evaluating damage. An insured must provide available information concerning the location of the property at the time of loss.

**W.56** We may consider evidence of prior valuations obtained by an insured. An insured must provide available prior valuations that are relevant to the claimed property.

**W.57** We will not pay more than the amount necessary to settle the covered loss, subject to the applicable limit. An insured must not include unrelated property or expenses in a claim.

**W.58** Our payment does not create coverage for property that is not covered personal property. An insured must identify the basis for claiming that property is covered personal property.

**W.59** We may require a release of our payment obligation for the amount paid. An insured must not execute a release that impairs our rights without our written consent.

**W.60** We will perform our obligations under this provision after an insured has performed the duties stated in this provision. An insured must comply with those duties as a condition of settlement.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** “Scheduled Personal Property” means personal property described in the Schedule and for which coverage is shown. Such property is covered subject to the terms of this endorsement.

**W.2** “Schedule” means the part of this endorsement that identifies Scheduled Personal Property and applicable coverage. The Schedule may describe property by category, item, or other identifying information.

**W.3** “Covered Property” means Scheduled Personal Property for which a limit of liability is shown in the Schedule. Covered Property does not include property not described in the Schedule.

**W.4** “Insured” means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or other persons under your care. An insured includes a person only while that person qualifies under this definition.

**W.5** “You” and “your” mean the named insured shown in the policy declarations. These terms also include a spouse if a spouse resides in the same household as the named insured.

**W.6** “Residence Premises” means the dwelling where you reside and which is shown in the policy declarations. The term includes structures and land used in connection with that dwelling.

**W.7** “Personal Property” means tangible property that is owned or used by an insured. Personal Property includes property for which an insured has a legal interest.

**W.8** “Property” means tangible personal property unless the context requires a different meaning. Property does not include land or interests in land.

**W.9** “Article” means an item of Personal Property that is separately identifiable by its nature, description, markings, or other characteristics. An Article may consist of related components that ordinarily function together.

**W.10** “Set” means Articles that are customarily used, displayed, worn, or maintained together. A Set may have greater value because its Articles are associated with each other.

**W.11** “Pair” means related Articles that are customarily used or valued together. A Pair includes matching Articles intended to function together.

**W.12** “Collection” means Articles assembled, maintained, or valued as a group because of their relationship, rarity, origin, condition, or other common characteristic. A Collection may include Articles acquired at different times.

**W.13** “Appraisal” means a written valuation of Property prepared by a qualified appraiser. An Appraisal may describe the Property, its condition, and the basis for the valuation.

**W.14** “Appraised Value” means the value assigned to Property in an Appraisal. Appraised Value does not establish the amount we will pay for a loss.

**W.15** “Actual Cash Value” means the amount required to repair or replace damaged Property, less a deduction for physical deterioration and depreciation. Actual Cash Value is determined as of the time of loss.

**W.16** “Replacement Cost” means the cost to replace damaged Property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement Cost does not include any amount for betterment or increased value.

**W.17** “Market Value” means the price that Property would reasonably bring in a customary market for that Property. Market Value may reflect condition, rarity, provenance, and current demand.

**W.18** “Agreed Value” means the value stated in the Schedule for Covered Property. Agreed Value applies only when the Schedule states that the property is insured on that basis.

**W.19** “Loss” means accidental direct physical loss of or damage to Covered Property. Loss includes disappearance only when disappearance results from a covered cause of loss.

**W.20** “Damage” means physical injury to Property that impairs its condition, usefulness, or value. Damage includes breakage, cracking, tearing, bending, staining, or other physical alteration.

**W.21** “Mysterious Disappearance” means the disappearance of Property when the cause of the disappearance cannot be determined. Mysterious Disappearance does not include a loss established to result from an excluded cause of loss.

**W.22** “Theft” means unlawful taking of Property with intent to deprive an insured of the Property. Theft includes attempted theft that causes direct physical damage to Covered Property.

**W.23** “Robbery” means Theft involving the taking of Property from a person by force or threat of force. Robbery includes a taking that occurs while an insured is compelled to surrender Property.

**W.24** “Burglary” means Theft involving unlawful entry into a place where Covered Property is kept. Burglary includes unlawful exit from that place after a theft.

**W.25** “Fine Arts” means paintings, prints, drawings, etchings, photographs, sculptures, tapestries, art glass, and other works of artistic merit. Fine Arts include frames, mats, and customary accessories that are part of the work.

**W.26** “Antiques” means Property of age, rarity, historical interest, or recognized collectible value. Antiques include furnishings, decorative objects, and similar Property customarily traded as antiques.

**W.27** “Collectibles” means Property acquired, maintained, or valued because of rarity, condition, historical significance, artistic merit, or association with a particular subject. Collectibles include memorabilia and similar property held as part of a Collection.

**W.28** “Jewelry” means articles customarily worn as personal adornment and made of precious metal, gemstones, or other valuable materials. Jewelry includes watches, necklaces, rings, bracelets, brooches, earrings, and similar articles.

**W.29** “Furs” means garments, garments’ components, and accessories made principally of animal fur or trimmed with animal fur. Furs include items customarily worn or used for personal adornment.

**W.30** “Silverware” means articles made principally of silver, gold, platinum, pewter, or similar precious metal. Silverware includes flatware, hollowware, tea services, trays, and decorative articles.

**W.31** “Camera Equipment” means cameras, lenses, cases, lighting equipment, tripods, and related accessories used for photography. Camera Equipment includes equipment designed for recording still images or moving images.

**W.32** “Musical Instrument” means an instrument designed to produce musical sound. Musical Instrument includes its case, bow, mouthpiece, and customary accessories.

**W.33** “Golf Equipment” means clubs, bags, carts, footwear, apparel, and accessories designed principally for the playing of golf. Golf Equipment includes related equipment customarily used on a golf course.

**W.34** “Sporting Equipment” means equipment designed principally for recreational athletic activity. Sporting Equipment includes customary protective equipment and accessories used with that equipment.

**W.35** “Stamp” means a postage stamp, revenue stamp, commemorative stamp, or similar philatelic item. Stamp includes an album, mounting, or protective holder only when scheduled with the Stamp.

**W.36** “Coin” means a coin, medal, token, bank note, or similar numismatic item. Coin includes a protective holder only when the holder is scheduled with the Coin.

**W.37** “Manuscript” means a handwritten, typed, printed, or otherwise recorded original document of collectible, historical, literary, artistic, or research value. Manuscript includes related pages and customary binding.

**W.38** “Book” means a bound volume, folio, atlas, or similar printed or written work. Book includes a rare or collectible edition and its customary protective covering.

**W.39** “Firearm” means a weapon designed to discharge a projectile by the action of an explosive, compressed gas, or similar force. Firearm includes its customary case and accessories when scheduled with the Firearm.

**W.40** “Electronic Equipment” means portable equipment designed to receive, transmit, record, process, reproduce, or display electronic data, sound, or images. Electronic Equipment includes customary chargers, cases, and accessories.

**W.41** “Computer Equipment” means portable or stationary equipment designed to process, store, or communicate electronic data. Computer Equipment includes peripheral devices and customary accessories used with the equipment.

**W.42** “Bicycle” means a pedal-driven vehicle designed for use by a rider. Bicycle includes permanently attached equipment and customary accessories.

**W.43** “Hearing Aid” means an electronic device designed to assist hearing. Hearing Aid includes customary batteries, molds, and accessories.

**W.44** “Prosthetic Device” means an artificial device designed to replace or support a body part or bodily function. Prosthetic Device includes customary attachments and accessories.

**W.45** “Authorized Representative” means a person authorized by an insured to act concerning Covered Property. An Authorized Representative does not have authority to alter this endorsement unless we agree in writing.

**W.46** “Bailee” means a person or organization having temporary possession of Property belonging to an insured. A Bailee includes a person or organization receiving Property for storage, repair, cleaning, appraisal, transportation, or similar service.

**W.47** “Custody” means physical possession or control of Property. Custody includes possession by an insured or by a person to whom an insured entrusts the Property.

**W.48** “Entrustment” means voluntary delivery of Property to another person or organization. Entrustment includes delivery for examination, repair, storage, display, transportation, sale, or other purpose.

**W.49** “Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. Occurrence includes an event that results in direct physical loss of or damage to Covered Property.

**W.50** “Cause of Loss” means a peril that directly causes Loss. A Cause of Loss may be sudden or may result from conditions that develop over time.

**W.51** “Business” means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or other economic gain. Business includes an activity conducted by an insured or by another person on behalf of an insured.

**W.52** “Vacant” means substantially empty of Personal Property necessary to support customary occupancy. Property is not Vacant solely because an insured is temporarily absent.
