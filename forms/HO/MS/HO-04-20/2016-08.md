# HO 04 20 Specified Additional Amount of Insurance for Coverage A (Edition 2016-08)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement changes the "policy". We provide the insurance stated in this endorsement subject to its terms.

The insurance provided by this endorsement applies to "Coverage A". This endorsement applies only when attached to the policy.

The "specified additional amount of insurance" provided by this endorsement is in addition to the insurance otherwise available for Coverage A. We will apply that additional amount only as provided by this endorsement.

An "insured" may recover under this endorsement only for a loss that is covered under the policy. All policy conditions apply to this endorsement.

We will determine coverage under this endorsement after applying all applicable policy provisions. This endorsement does not create coverage for loss otherwise excluded under the policy.

The additional insurance provided by this endorsement does not alter the property to which Coverage A applies. It does not alter the causes of loss covered under the policy.

We will pay under this endorsement only after all applicable policy requirements have been met. An insured must comply with all duties that apply to a covered loss.

If any provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of the policy, this endorsement controls. All other policy provisions remain unchanged.

This endorsement is part of the policy. The policy and this endorsement constitute the agreement between you and us.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** This endorsement changes the insurance provided under "Coverage A". We increase the "Limit of Liability" for Coverage A by the "Specified Additional Amount".

**W.2** The Specified Additional Amount applies only to a covered loss involving property insured under Coverage A. We do not apply the Specified Additional Amount to property not insured under Coverage A.

**W.3** The Specified Additional Amount is available only after the Coverage A Limit of Liability otherwise applicable to the loss has been exhausted. The Specified Additional Amount does not replace that Limit of Liability.

**W.4** We apply the Specified Additional Amount only when the amount of covered loss exceeds the Coverage A Limit of Liability. We do not apply the Specified Additional Amount to reduce any deductible.

**W.5** The Specified Additional Amount applies to direct physical loss to property insured under Coverage A. It applies only when the cause of loss is covered.

**W.6** We determine the amount of covered loss under the terms applicable to Coverage A. The Specified Additional Amount does not expand the causes of loss that we cover.

**W.7** We do not cover loss that is excluded under the policy. The Specified Additional Amount does not restore coverage for an excluded loss.

**W.8** We do not cover loss that is subject to a limitation under the policy. The Specified Additional Amount does not eliminate or modify that limitation.

**W.9** We apply all conditions applicable to Coverage A when determining whether a loss is covered. An insured must comply with those conditions.

**W.10** An insured must protect the property from further damage after a loss. We do not pay for avoidable additional damage caused by failure to protect the property.

**W.11** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. Notice must include information sufficient for us to identify the property and evaluate the loss.

**W.12** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine the loss.

**W.13** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request to evaluate the loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**W.14** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. We may request reasonable information concerning ownership, value, repair, replacement, and loss.

**W.15** We determine the amount payable for covered loss in accordance with the valuation provisions applicable to Coverage A. The Specified Additional Amount does not change the method used to value covered property.

**W.16** We do not pay more than the amount of covered loss. The Specified Additional Amount does not create a right to payment when no covered loss has occurred.

**W.17** We do not pay more than the amount required to repair or replace covered property, subject to the applicable valuation provisions. The Specified Additional Amount does not increase payment for undamaged property.

**W.18** We may pay the value of damaged property, repair the property, replace the property, or take other action permitted under the policy. The Specified Additional Amount applies to the payment otherwise determined for covered loss.

**W.19** We may investigate and settle any claim or loss as we consider appropriate. Our investigation or settlement does not waive any policy condition, exclusion, or limitation.

**W.20** The Specified Additional Amount applies separately to each covered occurrence. We do not combine the Specified Additional Amount with any other additional amount unless the policy expressly provides otherwise.

**W.21** The Specified Additional Amount does not apply to liability coverage. We do not use the Specified Additional Amount to increase any liability limit.

**W.22** The Specified Additional Amount does not apply to medical payments coverage. We do not use the Specified Additional Amount to increase any payment available for medical expenses.

**W.23** The Specified Additional Amount does not apply to additional living expense coverage. We do not use the Specified Additional Amount to increase payment for loss of use.

**W.24** The Specified Additional Amount does not apply to personal property coverage. We do not use the Specified Additional Amount to increase a limit applicable to personal property.

**W.25** The Specified Additional Amount does not apply to property insured under any coverage other than Coverage A. We apply it only to covered loss involving property insured under Coverage A.

**W.26** We do not apply the Specified Additional Amount to a deductible. Any deductible applicable to the loss remains applicable.

**W.27** We do not apply the Specified Additional Amount to taxes, fees, penalties, or charges that are not part of covered loss. We determine covered loss without regard to amounts that are not covered.

**W.28** We do not apply the Specified Additional Amount to costs resulting from delay, unless those costs are covered under Coverage A. The existence of the Specified Additional Amount does not create coverage for delay.

**W.29** We do not apply the Specified Additional Amount to loss caused by neglect after a loss. An insured must use reasonable means to preserve covered property.

**W.30** We do not apply the Specified Additional Amount to intentional loss caused by an insured. The applicable intentional loss provisions remain in effect.

**W.31** We do not apply the Specified Additional Amount to loss resulting from fraud or concealment. An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to insurance or a loss.

**W.32** The Specified Additional Amount is subject to all duties after loss. Failure to perform a required duty may affect coverage.

**W.33** We may require an insured to submit a signed statement of loss when permitted under the policy. The statement must provide the information reasonably required to evaluate the claim.

**W.34** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath when permitted under the policy. An insured must answer questions concerning the loss and the property involved.

**W.35** We may require proof of ownership for property claimed as damaged or destroyed. An insured must provide available evidence of ownership and value.

**W.36** We may require evidence supporting the cost to repair or replace damaged property. An insured must provide estimates, invoices, receipts, or other available documentation.

**W.37** We do not pay for repair or replacement work that is not necessary because of covered loss. We determine necessity based on the damaged condition of the property.

**W.38** We do not pay for betterment except as provided under the valuation provisions applicable to Coverage A. The Specified Additional Amount does not change those valuation provisions.

**W.39** We do not pay for increased value unrelated to covered repair or replacement. The Specified Additional Amount applies only to covered loss.

**W.40** We may exercise our rights of recovery against a person responsible for covered loss. An insured must preserve those rights after a loss.

**W.41** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except at the insured's own cost. This duty applies to expenses claimed under Coverage A.

**W.42** We may take possession of damaged property when permitted under the policy. Our possession of damaged property does not transfer ownership unless we agree otherwise.

**W.43** We may pay a claim jointly to you and another person having an interest in the property. Payment to a person entitled to payment discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.44** The Specified Additional Amount does not alter the interests of mortgagees or other persons having an interest in the property. Their rights remain subject to the terms applicable to Coverage A.

**W.45** We do not cover loss occurring after coverage has ended. The Specified Additional Amount applies only while this endorsement is in effect.

**W.46** The Specified Additional Amount applies only to property located at an insured location. We do not apply it to property at a location not insured under Coverage A.

**W.47** The Specified Additional Amount does not create coverage for property that is not described, used, or occupied as required for Coverage A. All eligibility requirements applicable to Coverage A remain applicable.

**W.48** If another provision conflicts with this endorsement, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict. All other provisions remain unchanged.

**W.49** The increased Coverage A Limit of Liability includes the Specified Additional Amount. We do not pay more than the increased Coverage A Limit of Liability for covered loss.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We provide this provision for the "dwelling" insured under Coverage A. The dwelling must be located at the "residence premises" shown in the Declarations. This provision applies only while the residence premises qualifies for Coverage A.

**W.2** The most we will pay for a covered loss to the dwelling is the Coverage A "Limit of Liability" increased by twenty-five percent. Except that this increase applies only when the loss is covered under Coverage A. We will not pay more than the amount permitted by this provision.

**W.3** We determine the amount payable for a covered loss according to the provisions that apply to Coverage A. The amount payable includes amounts that are otherwise recoverable under the policy. Subject to the terms of this provision, the increased Limit of Liability replaces the Coverage A Limit of Liability for the covered loss.

**W.4** You must maintain Coverage A on the dwelling as required by the policy. You must promptly notify us of any change that materially affects the value, use, or condition of the dwelling. Except that we may apply the increased Limit of Liability only if the applicable policy conditions have been satisfied.

**W.5** We will pay for direct physical loss to the dwelling when the loss is caused by a covered peril. We will determine the amount of loss without including property that is not insured under Coverage A. Provided that the loss is otherwise covered, this provision applies to the dwelling and structures insured with it.

**W.6** We will not pay for loss that is excluded by the policy. We do not cover loss caused by an excluded peril merely because the loss occurs during a covered event. Unless the policy provides otherwise, the increased Limit of Liability does not change any exclusion.

**W.7** We will not pay for property that is not part of the dwelling. We do not cover personal property, land, or property insured under a coverage other than Coverage A under this provision. Subject to the policy, a covered loss to such property is subject to the limit that applies to that property.

**W.8** We may require you to provide records that support the amount of loss. You must cooperate with our investigation and settlement of the loss. Except that we are not required to make payment for an amount that you cannot support under the policy.

**W.9** We may inspect the damaged dwelling and any property involved in the loss. You must preserve the damaged property and make it available for inspection. Provided that preservation is reasonably possible, you must protect the dwelling from further damage.

**W.10** You must take reasonable steps to protect the dwelling from further loss after a covered loss occurs. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred for those steps when the policy provides coverage for them. Unless we agree otherwise, you must not make permanent repairs before we inspect the damage.

**W.11** You must promptly report the loss to us. Your report must state the known facts concerning the date, place, and circumstances of the loss. Except that failure to provide information that is not reasonably available does not by itself prevent us from investigating the loss.

**W.12** You must provide a signed proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must include information required by the policy and must be made part of our claim file. Subject to the policy conditions, we may rely on the proof of loss and other information in determining the amount payable.

**W.13** We may settle the loss by payment, repair, or replacement as permitted by the policy. We may select the method of settlement that applies to the covered damage. Except that the method of settlement does not extend coverage to property or loss that is excluded.

**W.14** We may pay the amount of loss to you or to another person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment to a mortgagee or other person with a valid interest does not increase the amount we will pay. Provided that the applicable interest is shown in our records or otherwise established, payment discharges our obligation to that extent.

**W.15** We will determine the amount of loss by considering the cost to repair or replace the damaged portion of the dwelling. We may consider the condition, age, and utility of the damaged property. Unless the policy provides replacement cost coverage, we may apply applicable depreciation.

**W.16** We will not pay for an improvement that is not necessary to repair or replace covered damaged property. We do not cover an increase in cost caused solely by a voluntary change in design or materials. Subject to applicable law, we may limit payment to the cost of property of like kind and quality.

**W.17** We may use materials and methods that are reasonably comparable to those existing before the loss. We may repair or replace only the part of the dwelling damaged by a covered loss. Except that this does not prevent payment for additional work required by an applicable building ordinance or law when coverage applies.

**W.18** You must comply with applicable building ordinances or laws that govern repair or replacement of the damaged dwelling. You must give us reasonable notice of requirements that increase the cost of repair or replacement. Provided that coverage applies, we will consider covered ordinance or law costs in determining the amount payable.

**W.19** We do not cover the cost of correcting faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, workmanship, or maintenance. We do not cover the cost of correcting a condition that existed before the covered loss unless the policy provides coverage for that cost. Except that resulting damage may be covered when caused by a covered peril and not otherwise excluded.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of the dwelling after a loss. We do not cover damage that results from your failure to use reasonable means to protect the dwelling. Unless the failure was beyond your control, we may reduce the amount payable for damage that could reasonably have been prevented.

**W.21** We will consider the amount of insurance applicable to the dwelling when determining payment for a covered loss. We will not use this provision to increase the limit applicable to another coverage. Subject to the policy, the increased Limit of Liability applies only to the dwelling insured under Coverage A.

**W.22** We will not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace the covered damaged property. We will not pay for a financial gain resulting from a covered loss. Except that payment may include reasonable costs required to comply with covered construction requirements.

**W.23** You must use the payment for the repair, replacement, or restoration of the damaged dwelling when the policy requires that use. You must notify us if the payment will be used for another purpose. Provided that the policy permits another use, our payment remains subject to all applicable terms and conditions.

**W.24** We may withhold payment for an amount that is reasonably disputed. We will pay an amount that is not disputed when required by the policy or applicable law. Unless the dispute is resolved, payment of an undisputed amount does not waive our rights concerning the remaining amount.

**W.25** We may revise the amount of loss when additional covered damage is discovered. You must promptly notify us of the additional damage and provide supporting information. Subject to the policy conditions, a revision does not create coverage for damage that was not caused by the covered loss.

**W.26** We will not pay for damage that occurs after the covered loss unless the later damage is caused by a covered peril. We do not cover deterioration, wear and tear, or neglect that occurs after the loss. Except that reasonable temporary measures taken to protect the dwelling do not constitute neglect.

**W.27** We may require you to obtain estimates, invoices, contracts, or other evidence of repair or replacement costs. You must provide the evidence within a reasonable time after our request. Provided that the evidence is reasonably available, we may consider its absence in determining the amount payable.

**W.28** We will apply any deductible that is applicable to the covered loss. We will apply the deductible as required by the policy and applicable law. Subject to the policy, this provision does not eliminate or reduce any deductible.

**W.29** We will pay only for the portion of the loss that is covered under the policy. We will allocate an amount of loss when covered and uncovered causes contribute to the damage, as permitted by the policy. Unless applicable law requires otherwise, we will not pay for an amount allocated to an excluded cause.

**W.30** Our payment under this provision is subject to your compliance with the policy conditions. We retain all rights and defenses available under the policy concerning the loss. Except that payment or investigation of a claim does not by itself waive any exclusion, condition, or other provision.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the amount that applies to a covered loss before we pay for that loss. We determine the amount of covered loss under the applicable coverage provisions. You are responsible for the deductible that applies to the covered loss.

**W.2** We apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss after we determine the amount of that loss. The deductible does not increase the amount of covered loss. We do not pay the deductible on your behalf.

**W.3** A "loss" means accidental physical loss to property that is covered under this policy. We determine whether the loss is covered before applying the deductible. We apply the deductible only to the covered portion of the loss.

**W.4** An "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. We determine whether related damage resulted from the same occurrence. We apply the deductible according to that determination.

**W.5** We apply the deductible to each occurrence that results in covered loss. Related loss caused by the same occurrence is treated as part of that occurrence. You must provide information that reasonably assists us in identifying the occurrence.

**W.6** We determine the amount of covered loss using the applicable valuation provisions of this policy. We do not apply the deductible to property that is not covered. You must identify the property damaged by the occurrence.

**W.7** "Covered property" means property for which this policy provides coverage. We apply the deductible to covered property damaged by a covered occurrence. We do not apply the deductible to property that is not damaged by the occurrence.

**W.8** "Covered loss" means loss to covered property for which we are legally obligated to pay. We apply the deductible to the covered loss before determining the amount we will pay. You must support the amount of covered loss with records and other information we reasonably request.

**W.9** You must promptly notify us of any occurrence that may result in covered loss. Your notice must describe the property damaged and the circumstances of the occurrence. We may require a signed proof of loss before making payment.

**W.10** You must take reasonable steps to protect property from further damage after an occurrence. We pay only for covered loss that results from the occurrence and not from your failure to protect the property. You must keep damaged property available for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.11** We may inspect damaged property and examine available records relating to the occurrence. You must cooperate with our investigation and settlement of the covered loss. Your cooperation does not change the deductible that applies to the covered loss.

**W.12** We determine the amount of covered loss without regard to the deductible. We then subtract the applicable deductible from that amount. We pay the remaining amount, subject to all other applicable policy provisions.

**W.13** We do not pay for the deductible as part of the cost to repair or replace covered property. The deductible remains your responsibility even if another person agrees to reimburse you. We may seek recovery from a responsible person to the extent permitted by law.

**W.14** You must not abandon damaged property to us without our consent. We may take possession of damaged property after paying the covered loss if permitted by law. The deductible remains applicable to the covered loss associated with that property.

**W.15** We may settle a covered loss by paying the cost to repair, replace, or otherwise restore covered property. We apply the deductible before making payment under the settlement. You must comply with the applicable conditions for payment.

**W.16** We may make payment to you or to another person with a legally recognized interest in the covered property. Payment to a person with such an interest does not eliminate the deductible. You remain responsible for any deductible that applies to the covered loss.

**W.17** We may pay for covered loss in accordance with an agreed estimate, invoice, or other reliable valuation. We apply the deductible to the covered loss supported by that valuation. You must promptly provide revised information if the amount of covered loss changes.

**W.18** We determine whether damage to property resulted from a covered occurrence. Damage that does not result from a covered occurrence is not included in the covered loss. The deductible applies only after that determination is made.

**W.19** We do not pay for expenses that are not incurred to repair, replace, or otherwise address covered loss, unless this policy provides coverage for those expenses. We apply the deductible to covered expenses when they are part of the covered loss. You must retain documents supporting covered expenses.

**W.20** You must use reasonable care to prevent avoidable damage during repair or replacement of covered property. We may consider avoidable damage when determining the covered loss. The deductible applies to the covered loss that remains after that determination.

**W.21** We may require access to the location of the occurrence to inspect the covered property. You must allow reasonable access for inspection, investigation, and adjustment of the covered loss. We do not waive the deductible by inspecting or adjusting the loss.

**W.22** We may request information concerning ownership, condition, value, and repair of damaged property. You must provide information that is material to the amount of covered loss. We determine the deductible after considering the information reasonably available to us.

**W.23** A claim for covered loss must identify the property damaged and the cause of the damage. We may combine claims that arise from the same occurrence when determining the deductible. You must not separate a claim in a manner that changes the deductible that applies.

**W.24** We apply the deductible before making payment for covered loss under a claim. Payment of part of a claim does not waive the deductible for the remaining covered loss from the same occurrence. We may adjust the payment when additional covered loss is established.

**W.25** You must notify us if damaged property is repaired, replaced, sold, discarded, or otherwise disposed of before our inspection. We may limit payment when disposal prevents us from determining the amount or cause of covered loss. The deductible continues to apply to any covered loss that we determine.

**W.26** We may resolve a dispute concerning the amount of covered loss under the dispute procedures that apply to the claim. A dispute concerning value does not eliminate the deductible. We apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss determined under the applicable procedure.

**W.27** We pay covered loss only after applying the deductible and determining that all applicable conditions have been satisfied. You must comply with your duties relating to notice, protection of property, proof of loss, and cooperation. We do not pay an amount that is not part of the covered loss.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** Flood and surface water remains excluded. No coverage is created by the specified additional amount of insurance for "Coverage A".

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by "earth movement". This exclusion applies whether the earth movement is natural or results from human activity.

**W.3** We do not cover loss arising out of "intentional loss". An insured must not cause or arrange for loss to covered property.

**W.4** We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of any "ordinance or law". This exclusion includes loss resulting from the demolition, repair, replacement, or use of property.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by "war". This exclusion applies whether war is declared or undeclared.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by "nuclear hazard". This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing to the loss.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by "neglect" of an insured to use reasonable means to preserve property from further damage. An insured must protect covered property from further loss after a loss occurs.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by "power failure" occurring away from the residence premises. This exclusion applies even if the power failure affects property on the residence premises.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by "governmental action". This exclusion does not apply when governmental action is taken to prevent the spread of fire.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, or compaction. This exclusion applies to property whether performed by an insured or another person.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by latent defect or mechanical breakdown.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, smog, or dry rot. We do not cover loss caused by contamination.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. This exclusion applies whether the condition occurs gradually or suddenly.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, or discharge by animals.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies to pollutants whether introduced intentionally or accidentally.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We do not cover loss caused by smoke resulting from a source that is not sudden or accidental.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain property in a condition reasonably suited to prevent damage. An insured must make reasonable repairs needed to protect property from loss.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies when the seepage or leakage occurs over time.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow of water from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems when an insured fails to use reasonable care. An insured must maintain heat in the building or shut off the water supply and drain the system and appliances.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies whether the condition results from a covered or excluded cause of loss.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by collapse except as expressly covered. We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion.

**W.23** We do not cover loss to property that is vacant when vacancy increases the hazard of loss. An insured must take reasonable care to secure vacant property.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by theft from a dwelling under construction. We do not cover loss caused by theft of materials and supplies intended for construction.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by weather conditions when weather contributes with an excluded cause of loss. This exclusion applies regardless of the sequence of contributing events.

**W.26** The "minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage" continues to apply to loss subject to that deductible. The specified additional amount of insurance for Coverage A does not remove that deductible.

**W.27** The "other structures limit" is not increased by the specified additional amount of insurance for Coverage A. We do not cover loss exceeding the applicable other structures limit.

**W.28** The "contents limit percentage" is not increased by the specified additional amount of insurance for Coverage A. We do not cover loss to personal property exceeding the applicable contents limit percentage.

**W.29** The "ALE percentage" is not increased by the specified additional amount of insurance for Coverage A. We do not cover additional living expense exceeding the applicable ALE percentage.

**W.30** The "debris removal additional percentage" is not increased by the specified additional amount of insurance for Coverage A. We do not cover debris removal expense exceeding the applicable debris removal additional percentage.

**W.31** We do not cover any deductible applicable to a covered loss. An insured remains responsible for the applicable deductible.

**W.32** We do not cover loss to property for any amount that another person or organization is legally responsible to pay. An insured must preserve our rights of recovery against that person or organization.

**W.33** We do not cover loss for which an insured has made a fraudulent statement or engaged in fraudulent conduct. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a claim.

**W.34** We do not cover loss arising from concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact. This exclusion applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after a loss.

**W.35** We do not cover loss arising from business activities conducted by an insured unless the loss is otherwise covered. This exclusion applies to property used principally for business purposes.

**W.36** We do not cover loss arising from professional services. This exclusion includes loss caused by an insured's failure to render professional services.

**W.37** We do not cover loss arising from contractual liability voluntarily assumed by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract.

**W.38** We do not cover loss arising from unlawful activity by an insured. An insured must comply with applicable law concerning the use and occupancy of property.

**W.39** We do not cover loss to property seized, confiscated, or destroyed by lawful authority. This exclusion applies whether the property is seized before or after a loss.

**W.40** We do not cover loss caused by deterioration resulting from ordinary use of property. We do not cover the cost to repair a defect that existed before the loss.

**W.41** We do not cover loss consisting solely of diminished value. We do not cover loss consisting solely of a decrease in market value.

**W.42** We do not cover loss that is not direct physical loss to covered property. We do not cover economic loss that does not result from covered direct physical loss.

**W.43** We do not cover any loss excluded by these provisions. The specified additional amount of insurance for Coverage A does not modify an exclusion stated in these provisions.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** This endorsement modifies the insurance provided for "Coverage A". The additional insurance applies only as stated in this endorsement.

**W.2** The "specified additional amount" is available only for a covered loss to property insured under Coverage A. We will apply all other applicable policy terms to that loss.

**W.3** An insured must have an insurable interest in the damaged property at the time of loss. We will not pay an insured more than that insured's interest in the property.

**W.4** An insured must give us notice of a "loss" as required by this policy. Notice must identify the property involved and describe the known circumstances of the loss.

**W.5** An insured must report a loss to us within sixty days after the loss. Notice may be given by you or by a person acting on your behalf.

**W.6** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property.

**W.7** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses incurred to protect covered property. An insured must make those records available to us upon request.

**W.8** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.9** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and the location where the loss occurred.

**W.10** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request to investigate a claim. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**W.11** An insured must submit to an examination under oath when we reasonably require. An insured must answer questions concerning the loss truthfully and completely.

**W.12** An insured must produce other persons for examination when those persons have relevant knowledge of the loss and are under your control. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable efforts to obtain relevant information.

**W.13** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we request it. The statement must state the facts known to the insured concerning the loss.

**W.14** An insured must provide a "proof of loss" when required by us. The proof of loss must include information reasonably necessary to establish the claim.

**W.15** The proof of loss period applies as provided by this policy. An insured must comply with any applicable proof of loss requirement.

**W.16** An insured must identify all persons having an interest in property for which a claim is made. An insured must disclose any lien, mortgage, or other encumbrance known to the insured.

**W.17** An insured must identify any other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must provide us information concerning the insurer and the applicable insurance.

**W.18** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning the insurance, the property, or the loss. We do not cover a loss when an insured has concealed or misrepresented a material fact.

**W.19** An insured must not intentionally cause a loss. We do not cover a loss caused intentionally by an insured.

**W.20** An insured must not make a claim for property that was not damaged by a covered loss. We do not cover any amount based on false, fraudulent, or inflated information.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We settle a "covered loss" to "covered property" in accordance with this condition. The amount payable remains subject to the "applicable limit" and any applicable deductible.

**W.2** Loss settlement applies only to direct physical loss that is covered under this endorsement. We do not pay more than the amount required to repair or replace damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality.

**W.3** We determine the amount of loss from the condition of the covered property immediately before the loss. We may consider the age, condition, quality, and useful life of the damaged property.

**W.4** We may pay the cost to repair damaged covered property when repair restores the property to its pre-loss condition. We may pay the cost to replace damaged covered property when repair is not practicable.

**W.5** We will not pay for any increase in value resulting from repair or replacement. We will not pay for any improvement that exceeds the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

**W.6** You must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. You must make the damaged property available to us upon our request.

**W.7** We will settle a covered loss to "personal property" on the following basis: replacement cost. This basis applies only to personal property that is covered under this endorsement.

**W.8** You must provide records, receipts, photographs, and other evidence that reasonably establishes ownership and value of damaged personal property. You must cooperate with us in identifying the property involved in the loss.

**W.9** We may require you to submit damaged property for examination. We may require you to provide samples, specifications, or other information needed to evaluate the loss.

**W.10** We may determine whether damaged property can be repaired, restored, or replaced. Our determination may be based on available materials, customary methods, and reasonably comparable property.

**W.11** When damaged property is part of a pair, set, or collection, we may determine the loss based on the value of the damaged item or items. We will not pay for any reduction in value of undamaged property unless that reduction results directly from covered physical damage.

**W.12** When replacement property is unavailable, we may use reasonably comparable property to determine the amount of loss. Comparable property must serve substantially the same function as the damaged property.

**W.13** We may repair or replace only the portion of property that sustained covered physical damage. We are not required to replace undamaged property solely to obtain a uniform appearance.

**W.14** We may select the person or organization that performs repair or replacement work. If we make that selection, the work must be performed in a competent manner.

**W.15** You must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. You must keep a record of expenses incurred to protect the property.

**W.16** We may pay reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property from further damage when those expenses result from a covered loss. Payment of those expenses does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.17** You must permit us to inspect the location where the loss occurred. You must permit us to inspect any property, records, or evidence relevant to the loss.

**W.18** You must provide a signed statement of loss when we request it. The statement must identify the damaged property and describe the circumstances of the loss.

**W.19** You must provide documents that support the amount claimed. Those documents may include invoices, estimates, appraisals, receipts, and records of prior condition.

**W.20** We may obtain estimates for repair or replacement from persons or organizations of our choice. We may use those estimates in determining the amount payable.

**W.21** We may require an examination under oath of any person seeking payment for a covered loss. That person must answer questions concerning the loss and the claimed property.

**W.22** You must preserve any right of recovery against a person or organization responsible for the loss. You must do nothing after the loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.23** We may take possession of damaged property after payment for the loss. We are not required to take possession of any damaged property.

**W.24** You must not abandon covered property to us. Abandonment does not transfer ownership of covered property to us.

**W.25** Any salvage value of damaged property may be considered in determining the amount payable. We may require that salvage be retained, transferred, or made available to us.

**W.26** We may reduce the amount payable by any amount recovered for the same loss from another source. We will apply recoveries in a manner that prevents duplicate payment.

**W.27** The applicable deductible applies to the covered loss before payment is made. We will not pay the deductible amount as part of loss settlement.

**W.28** Payment for repair or replacement may be made to you, to a person performing the work, or to both. Payment to a person performing the work does not relieve you of your duties under this endorsement.

**W.29** We may make payment after we receive sufficient information to determine the covered loss. We may withhold payment for any portion of the loss that cannot be reasonably verified.

**W.30** We may require reasonable proof that repair or replacement has been completed when payment depends on that completion. You must provide that proof upon our request.

**W.31** We do not pay for loss that is not covered under this endorsement. We do not pay for costs that do not result directly from covered physical damage.

**W.32** The "association assessment limit" does not alter the loss settlement of covered property. An assessment is not physical damage to covered property merely because it results from a loss.

**W.33** A "fire department call fee" is not a measure of the amount payable for direct physical loss. Payment of that fee does not change the loss settlement of covered property.

**W.34** The "precious stones theft limit" does not determine the condition or value of other covered property. Loss settlement for property subject to that limit remains subject to the applicable limit.

**W.35** The "special limit on business property on the residence premises" applies independently of the method used to determine loss. That limit does not increase the amount payable for other covered property.

**W.36** We may require additional information when the claimed amount is not supported by the information available to us. You must provide that information if it is reasonably available to you.

**W.37** We may resolve a disagreement concerning the amount of loss by appraisal when appraisal is available. An appraisal determines the amount of loss and does not determine whether coverage applies.

**W.38** Our payment does not waive any right or defense available to us under this endorsement. Acceptance of payment does not waive any duty you owe after a covered loss.

## W.8 — Definitions

"We" and "us" mean the insurer providing this coverage. "You" and "your" mean the insured named in the policy and any other insured entitled to coverage.

"Covered property" means property for which coverage is provided under Coverage A. Covered property includes property that is part of the dwelling described in the policy.

"Coverage A" means coverage for the dwelling and property attached to the dwelling. Coverage A applies only to property at the residence premises.

"Dwelling" means the building used principally as a private residence. Dwelling includes structures attached to the building.

"Residence premises" means the location where the dwelling is situated. Residence premises includes land used in connection with the dwelling.

"Loss" means accidental physical loss of or damage to covered property. Loss includes direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

"Covered peril" means a cause of loss for which coverage is provided. A covered peril does not include a cause of loss excluded by the policy.

"Repair" means restoration of damaged property to substantially the same condition as existed immediately before the loss. Repair includes work necessary to correct physical damage caused by a covered peril.

"Replacement" means substitution of damaged property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement includes construction necessary to replace damaged portions of the dwelling.

"Rebuilding" means construction of a dwelling to replace a dwelling damaged by a covered peril. Rebuilding includes necessary labor and materials.

"Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, including a deduction for physical deterioration and depreciation. Actual cash value does not include the value of land.

"Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property without deduction for depreciation. Replacement cost is determined using materials of like kind and quality.

"Depreciation" means reduction in the value of property resulting from age, wear, deterioration, obsolescence, or other physical condition. Depreciation applies to property damaged by a covered peril.

"Building materials" means materials intended to become a permanent part of the dwelling. Building materials include fixtures, equipment, and supplies used in repair or rebuilding.

"Labor" means work performed in connection with repair, replacement, or rebuilding. Labor includes work necessary to restore covered property after a loss.

"Additional amount of insurance" means the additional limit of liability available under this endorsement for covered loss to property insured under Coverage A. Additional amount of insurance applies only as provided by this endorsement.

"Specified additional amount of insurance" means the additional amount of insurance that applies to a covered loss under Coverage A. Specified additional amount of insurance is not separate coverage for property not insured under Coverage A.

"Limit of liability" means the most we will pay for covered loss. The applicable limit of liability is subject to all policy terms and conditions.

"Policy limit" means the limit of liability applicable to Coverage A before application of the specified additional amount of insurance. Policy limit does not include any amount available under this endorsement.

"Insured value" means the value assigned to covered property for purposes of Coverage A. Insured value may differ from actual cash value or replacement cost.

"Covered loss" means loss caused by a covered peril and not otherwise excluded. Covered loss must occur during the policy period.

"Property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. Property damage does not include diminution in value without physical loss or damage.

"Loss settlement" means determination of the amount we will pay for covered loss. Loss settlement is subject to the applicable terms of the policy and this endorsement.
