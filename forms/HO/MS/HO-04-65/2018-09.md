# HO 04 65 Coverage C — Increased Special Limits of Liability (Edition 2018-09)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

**W.1** This endorsement applies when attached. The insurance we provide is subject to the terms of this endorsement.

**W.2** The terms of this endorsement apply to the matters addressed by this endorsement. We will determine coverage under those terms.

**W.3** You and any insured must comply with the applicable terms of this endorsement. Our obligations apply only as stated in those terms.

**W.4** This endorsement does not create coverage except as expressly provided. We do not provide coverage by implication.

**W.5** Any coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to all applicable limitations. We will not pay more than the applicable limit.

**W.6** Any limit that applies under this endorsement is part of the insurance we provide. The applicable limit is not available for any matter not covered by this endorsement.

**W.7** We apply the terms of this endorsement according to their plain meaning. We read those terms in the context of the insurance provided.

**W.8** A term that specifically addresses a matter governs that matter. A general term does not expand coverage beyond the terms that expressly apply.

**W.9** We do not waive any term of this endorsement unless we expressly agree in writing. Our conduct does not constitute a waiver.

**W.10** An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine our obligations. The information must be accurate and complete.

**W.11** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation of any claim. We may require documentation that supports a claim.

**W.12** We may determine whether claimed property and claimed loss are subject to this endorsement. Our determination is based on the information available to us.

**W.13** We do not cover loss that is not within the scope of this endorsement. We do not pay for property that is not subject to the insurance we provide.

**W.14** The rights provided by this endorsement belong only to an insured. An insured may not transfer those rights without our written consent.

**W.15** If a provision of this endorsement is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to apply. We will apply the remaining provisions to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** This endorsement provides increased "Coverage C" special limits for "covered property" to which an increased special limit applies. The increased special limits apply only as stated in this endorsement.

**W.2** We apply an increased special limit only to property within a category for which this endorsement provides that limit. An increased special limit does not create coverage for property that is not covered property.

**W.3** We determine whether a "loss" is covered without regard to an increased special limit. An increased special limit applies only after coverage for the loss has been established.

**W.4** An increased special limit replaces the special limit that would otherwise apply to the applicable category of covered property. No other limit is changed unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

**W.5** We do not cover property merely because an increased special limit applies to its category. The property must satisfy all requirements applicable to covered property.

**W.6** We do not cover a loss that is excluded from coverage. An increased special limit does not change an exclusion applicable to the loss.

**W.7** We apply the increased special limit to the category of property involved in the loss. We do not combine increased special limits applicable to different categories of property.

**W.8** We apply the applicable increased special limit regardless of whether the covered property is owned by you or by another "insured". Ownership does not increase the limit available for the loss.

**W.9** We apply an increased special limit to covered property only while the property remains within the category to which that limit applies. A change in the property's use, character, or classification may affect the applicable limit.

**W.10** We determine the applicable category of covered property from the property's nature and use at the time of loss. A description assigned by you does not control that determination.

**W.11** We do not apply an increased special limit to property that is principally used for a purpose outside the category to which the limit applies. Incidental use does not change the property's principal character.

**W.12** We apply an increased special limit only to the extent the property is covered under Coverage C. The increased special limit does not provide coverage under another coverage.

**W.13** We do not apply an increased special limit to property for which a separate limitation applies under this endorsement. The more specific limitation applies when it governs the property involved in the loss.

**W.14** We apply an increased special limit to direct physical loss to covered property. We do not apply that limit to consequential, indirect, or incidental loss.

**W.15** We determine the amount of loss in accordance with the valuation provisions applicable to covered property. An increased special limit does not change the method used to determine the amount of loss.

**W.16** We do not pay more than the applicable increased special limit for covered property in the applicable category. This restriction applies regardless of the number of items involved in the loss.

**W.17** We do not pay more than the applicable increased special limit when property of the same category is damaged, destroyed, or stolen. The manner in which the loss occurs does not increase that limit.

**W.18** We apply the increased special limit to all covered property in the applicable category involved in the loss. We do not apply the limit separately to each item of property.

**W.19** We do not apply an increased special limit separately to property based upon separate ownership interests. The applicable limit remains subject to the terms of this endorsement.

**W.20** We apply an increased special limit only to the amount of covered loss that remains after all applicable conditions have been satisfied. An insured must comply with applicable duties following a loss.

**W.21** An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for us to identify the property and determine its applicable category. An insured must provide records, receipts, descriptions, or other evidence that we reasonably request.

**W.22** An insured must identify property for which an increased special limit is claimed. An insured must distinguish that property from other property involved in the loss.

**W.23** An insured must cooperate with us in determining whether the property is covered property. An insured must permit us to inspect the property when inspection is reasonably possible.

**W.24** An insured must preserve damaged property for our examination when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.25** We may require an insured to provide evidence of ownership, possession, use, value, and condition of property for which an increased special limit is claimed. We may consider all available evidence in determining the amount we will pay.

**W.26** We do not apply an increased special limit based solely upon an insured's statement of value. We may require documentation supporting the claimed value of the property.

**W.27** We apply the increased special limit only to property that existed before the loss. We do not apply that limit to anticipated property, expected property, or property acquired after the loss.

**W.28** We do not apply an increased special limit to an item that cannot be identified as covered property. An insured must provide information sufficient to establish the item's identity and applicable category.

**W.29** We apply an increased special limit without changing the property interest required for coverage. An insured must have the interest in the property required for a covered loss.

**W.30** We do not apply an increased special limit to property held, stored, transported, or used in a manner that prevents coverage from applying. The increased special limit does not alter any restriction based on possession, location, or use.

**W.31** We apply an increased special limit to property at the "residence premises" only when the property is otherwise covered there. The increased special limit does not expand the locations at which coverage applies.

**W.32** We apply an increased special limit to property away from the residence premises only when the property is otherwise covered away from that location. The increased special limit does not change any limitation based on location.

**W.33** We do not apply an increased special limit to property used in a "business" unless that property is otherwise covered. The increased special limit does not change a restriction applicable to business property.

**W.34** We apply an increased special limit to property used for personal purposes only when the property otherwise qualifies as covered property. The increased special limit does not change the purpose for which property is covered.

**W.35** We do not apply an increased special limit to property that is subject to a condition limiting coverage because of its use. The condition remains applicable to the property and the loss.

**W.36** We apply an increased special limit to "theft" only when theft is a covered cause of loss. The increased special limit does not make theft covered when theft is otherwise not covered.

**W.37** We do not apply an increased special limit to unexplained disappearance unless that loss is otherwise covered. The increased special limit does not change the proof required for a theft loss.

**W.38** We apply an increased special limit to damaged property only when the damage results from a covered cause of loss. The increased special limit does not change the causes of loss for which coverage is provided.

**W.39** We do not apply an increased special limit to loss caused by wear, deterioration, defect, or inherent vice when that loss is otherwise not covered. The increased special limit does not change the nature of covered loss.

**W.40** We apply an increased special limit after determining the value of the covered property involved in the loss. We do not pay for value attributable to property that is not covered.

**W.41** We do not apply an increased special limit to costs that are not part of a covered loss. The increased special limit does not create coverage for fees, penalties, or expenses that are otherwise not covered.

**W.42** We apply all applicable deductibles to a covered loss as otherwise required. An increased special limit does not eliminate or reduce an applicable deductible.

**W.43** We do not pay an increased special limit in addition to another payment for the same covered property. We do not duplicate payment for the same loss.

**W.44** We apply an increased special limit subject to all rights of recovery available to us. An insured must preserve our rights against a person responsible for the loss.

**W.45** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense for a loss without our consent, except at the insured's own cost. This duty applies to property for which an increased special limit is claimed.

**W.46** We may examine an insured under oath concerning a loss involving property subject to an increased special limit. An insured must provide requested information concerning ownership, use, value, and circumstances of loss.

**W.47** We may require an insured to submit a signed statement describing the loss and the property involved. An insured must provide that statement when we reasonably request it.

**W.48** We may require an insured to provide records showing acquisition, maintenance, repair, appraisal, or disposition of property for which an increased special limit is claimed. The absence of records may affect our ability to determine the covered amount of loss.

**W.49** We determine the applicability of an increased special limit from the facts existing at the time of loss. A later change in ownership, use, value, or location does not alter that determination.

**W.50** We do not apply an increased special limit when the property is intentionally misdescribed to obtain a greater payment. Misrepresentation concerning the property or loss may affect coverage.

**W.51** An insured must promptly notify us of a loss involving property for which an increased special limit is claimed. Prompt notice must provide sufficient information for us to investigate the loss.

**W.52** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss when reasonably possible. Reasonable protective measures do not increase the applicable special limit.

**W.53** We may pay the covered amount of loss by repair, replacement, or payment, as permitted by the applicable coverage terms. An increased special limit does not require us to use a particular method of payment.

**W.54** We apply an increased special limit only to the extent of our obligation for a covered loss. Our payment remains subject to all applicable conditions, limitations, and exclusions.

**W.55** This endorsement changes only the Coverage C special limits expressly increased by this endorsement. All other coverage terms remain unchanged.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We provide the increased special limits of liability described in this section for covered personal property. We apply these limits when the property is insured under Coverage C. An insured must establish that the property was in your possession or under your control when the loss occurred.

**W.2** The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones is five thousand dollars. We apply this limit to the property described in this sentence, except that it does not increase the amount payable for any property that is not covered under Coverage C. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of firearms is six thousand five hundred dollars. We apply this limit to firearms covered under Coverage C, subject to all applicable conditions and exclusions. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of silverware is ten thousand dollars. We apply this limit to silverware covered under Coverage C, provided that the loss results from a covered cause of loss.

**W.3** We determine the amount payable for covered property after applying the applicable limit of liability. We do not combine limits that apply to different classes of property. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary to identify the property and establish the amount of the loss.

**W.4** We apply the applicable special limit to all property of the described class involved in the same occurrence. We apply the limit regardless of the number of persons who own, possess, or claim an interest in the property. We do not increase the applicable limit because ownership or possession is divided among persons.

**W.5** We do not cover loss that is excluded under the policy. We do not cover property unless the property is insured under Coverage C. An insured must comply with all duties that apply after a loss.

**W.6** We determine whether property falls within a class described in this section by considering the nature and use of the property. We do not change the classification of property merely because the property is kept with property of another class. We apply the classification that most accurately describes the property at the time of loss.

**W.7** We include property temporarily removed from your residence when the property remains insured under Coverage C. We apply the applicable special limit while the property is away from your residence, subject to any applicable territorial limitation. An insured must provide the location of the property when reasonably requested after a loss.

**W.8** We do not cover a loss that results from an excluded cause of loss. We do not cover increased value arising solely from sentimental, historical, or personal significance. An insured must support any claimed value with reliable information when requested.

**W.9** We apply the applicable special limit before we make payment for covered property. We do not pay more than the amount of covered loss determined under the policy. An insured must not abandon property to us without our consent.

**W.10** We may pay the amount necessary to repair or replace covered property when the policy permits payment on that basis. We apply the applicable special limit to the amount of covered loss determined under that basis, unless the policy requires another method of settlement. An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the repair or replacement.

**W.11** We may settle a covered loss with you or with another person who has a valid interest in the property. We do not make payment to a person whose interest has not been established. An insured must identify all persons who claim an interest in the property.

**W.12** We may require an examination under oath concerning the loss. An insured must answer questions completely and sign the transcript when requested. We do not waive any policy condition by investigating, evaluating, or discussing a claim.

**W.13** We do not cover property that you intentionally conceal or misrepresent in connection with a claim. We do not cover a loss when an insured makes a material misrepresentation concerning the property or the circumstances of the loss. An insured must give us truthful and complete information.

**W.14** We may inspect damaged property before it is repaired, replaced, or discarded. An insured must protect the property from further damage and preserve evidence of the loss. We do not pay for damage caused by failure to protect the property after the loss.

**W.15** We may request receipts, appraisals, photographs, ownership records, or other information relating to the property. An insured must provide the requested information within a reasonable time. We do not treat the absence of a particular record as proof of ownership or value.

**W.16** We determine the amount of covered loss from the condition of the property immediately before the loss. We do not include costs that are unrelated to restoring or replacing covered property. An insured must distinguish covered damage from prior damage, wear, deterioration, or other unrepaired condition.

**W.17** We do not cover ordinary wear and tear, deterioration, inherent vice, or latent defect. We do not cover loss caused by neglect or failure to use reasonable means to protect property. An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage after a covered loss.

**W.18** We may use the applicable special limit when a claim includes property of the described class and property of another class. We apply the limit only to the property to which the limit applies. We do not apply the limit to property that is not within the described class.

**W.19** We may require proof of ownership, possession, or legal responsibility for the property. An insured must provide that proof when reasonably available. We do not make payment for property that an insured cannot reasonably identify or substantiate.

**W.20** We do not cover loss resulting from voluntary parting with property when the applicable policy provisions exclude that loss. We do not cover property obtained through fraudulent, dishonest, or illegal conduct. An insured must promptly notify law enforcement when required by the policy or applicable law.

**W.21** We may consider the age, condition, quality, and available market information for covered property. We do not determine the amount payable solely from a claimed purchase price. An insured must provide information concerning acquisition, ownership, and condition when reasonably requested.

**W.22** We apply the applicable special limit to covered property regardless of whether the property is scheduled or unscheduled under the policy. We do not create separate limits merely because property is described in separate records. An insured must maintain records sufficient to support the claim when reasonably possible.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts of an insured. We do not cover a loss when an insured conceals or misrepresents a material fact concerning the loss. An insured must notify us promptly when covered property is lost, stolen, or damaged.

**W.24** We may make payment after completing our investigation and receiving information reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim. We do not waive our rights by making an advance or partial payment. An insured must return any payment that was made because of fraud or material misrepresentation.

**W.25** We apply this section only to covered loss involving property insured under Coverage C. We do not extend coverage to property that is excluded by the policy or otherwise not insured. An insured must comply with the policy provisions that govern the claim.

**W.26** We pay no more than the amount of covered loss determined under the policy and this section. We do not pay duplicate amounts for the same property or occurrence. An insured must assign to us any rights of recovery against a responsible person when required by the policy.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** We apply the "deductible" to a covered loss under this Coverage. The deductible is the amount you must bear before we pay for a covered loss. We determine the amount of a covered loss under the applicable provisions of this policy.

**W.2** The deductible applies to each covered loss. It applies regardless of the number of items damaged, destroyed, or lost in that covered loss. We do not apply the deductible more than once to the same covered loss.

**W.3** A covered loss means direct physical loss to property covered under this Coverage. The loss must result from a peril insured against under this policy. We do not cover loss that is not otherwise covered under this policy.

**W.4** You must promptly notify us of a loss for which you seek payment. You must provide all information and documentation that we reasonably request. We apply the deductible after considering the information available to us.

**W.5** You must protect the property from further damage after a loss. You must make reasonable and necessary repairs to prevent further damage. We do not cover damage resulting from your failure to protect the property.

**W.6** You must keep damaged property available for our inspection. You must not dispose of damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. We may waive this requirement in writing.

**W.7** We may pay the amount of a covered loss after applying the deductible. Our payment is subject to the applicable limit of insurance. We do not pay more than the amount of covered loss remaining after application of the deductible.

**W.8** We determine the amount of loss in accordance with the loss settlement provisions that apply to the damaged property. We consider the applicable valuation method when determining the amount of loss. The deductible applies to the amount determined under those provisions.

**W.9** We may settle a covered loss by payment, repair, or replacement. We may select the method of settlement that applies under this policy. The deductible applies regardless of the method of settlement.

**W.10** You must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a covered loss. You must provide records, receipts, and other evidence that support your claim. We may withhold payment to the extent that your failure to cooperate prevents us from determining the amount of loss.

**W.11** A "claim" means your request for payment for a covered loss. You must submit a claim in accordance with the duties after loss. We apply the deductible in determining the amount payable for the claim.

**W.12** We may investigate whether a claim involves a covered loss. We may inspect the property and question you under oath when permitted by this policy. Our investigation does not waive any provision of this policy.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by an excluded peril. We do not apply the deductible to create coverage for an excluded loss. An exclusion remains applicable whether or not the loss exceeds the deductible.

**W.14** We do not cover loss that is excluded by a condition of this policy. We do not apply the deductible to amounts that are not payable under this policy. Your compliance with the deductible provision does not affect any other condition of coverage.

**W.15** You must give us notice of any occurrence that may result in a claim. You must give notice as soon as practicable under the circumstances. We may require additional information concerning the occurrence and resulting loss.

**W.16** An "occurrence" means an event that causes a covered loss. We determine whether related damage arose from the same occurrence based on the facts and circumstances. We apply the deductible in accordance with that determination.

**W.17** We consider the cause, timing, and location of damage when evaluating whether losses are related. We may treat damage as part of the same covered loss when it results from a single occurrence. We may treat separate damage as separate covered losses when the facts support that treatment.

**W.18** You must identify the property for which you seek payment. You must provide a description of the damage and the circumstances that caused it. We may request additional proof concerning ownership, value, or condition.

**W.19** We may require a signed proof of loss. The proof of loss must state the information required by this policy and must be supported by records when requested. We may not make payment until the required proof of loss is received.

**W.20** We may inspect the residence premises and any property involved in a claim. You must permit reasonable access for inspection, appraisal, repair, or replacement. We must give reasonable notice of an inspection unless an emergency requires immediate access.

**W.21** We may use an appraisal or other method provided by this policy to determine the amount of loss. An appraisal determines the amount of loss but does not determine whether the loss is covered. We apply the deductible after the amount of covered loss is determined.

**W.22** You must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense for which we may be responsible without our consent. You must preserve our rights against any person who may be responsible for the loss. We may reduce payment to the extent your conduct impairs those rights.

**W.23** We may pay you and any person having an interest in the damaged property. We may require evidence of that interest before making payment. Payment remains subject to the deductible and all applicable policy provisions.

**W.24** We may pay a mortgagee or other interested party when required by this policy. Payment to an interested party does not increase the amount payable for the covered loss. The deductible applies to the total covered loss before payment is allocated among interested parties.

**W.25** You must use reasonable means to preserve property after a loss. You must separate damaged property from undamaged property when reasonably possible. We do not cover damage caused by failure to take reasonable protective measures.

**W.26** We do not cover increased loss caused by delay in making reasonable repairs. We do not cover loss resulting from neglect of damaged property. These limitations apply whether or not the original damage was covered.

**W.27** We may repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may use materials from available sources. The deductible applies to the covered loss for which repair or replacement is provided.

**W.28** You must assist us in securing and preserving evidence concerning a claim. You must attend examinations under oath when reasonably requested. You must provide access to persons and records relevant to the claim.

**W.29** We may communicate with persons who have knowledge of the loss. We may obtain statements, records, and other information relevant to coverage or valuation. You must cooperate with these efforts.

**W.30** We may deny a claim when the loss is not covered under this policy. We may deny a claim when you fail to comply with a material duty after loss. We may deny a claim to the extent that your conduct prejudices our investigation or adjustment.

**W.31** We do not cover concealed or deliberately misrepresented material facts concerning a claim. We do not cover loss resulting from fraud or concealment by an insured. These provisions apply to the claim even if other damage is covered.

**W.32** You must retain records that reasonably establish the condition and value of property claimed to be damaged. You must provide those records when we request them. We may consider the absence of records when determining the amount of loss.

**W.33** We may make an advance payment when we determine that an advance is appropriate. An advance payment does not establish coverage for any additional loss. We may apply the deductible in calculating the final amount payable.

**W.34** We may recover payments made for loss that is not covered. You must return any payment that was made because of material misrepresentation or concealment. Our recovery rights are not limited by the deductible.

**W.35** We may pursue recovery from a person responsible for a covered loss after payment. You must transfer to us your rights of recovery to the extent of our payment. You must not impair those rights after a loss.

**W.36** We apply this deductible provision together with the other provisions that determine whether payment is owed. You must satisfy all applicable duties before we are required to make payment. Payment is limited to the amount of covered loss remaining after all applicable provisions are applied.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss to "covered property" when the loss is excluded. A change to a "special limit of liability" does not create coverage for excluded loss.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by an excluded event. We do not cover loss resulting from an excluded condition.

**W.3** We do not cover loss that arises from an excluded act or omission. We do not cover loss that results from an excluded use of property.

**W.4** A change to the special limit of liability does not affect an exclusion applicable to covered property. Flood and surface water remains excluded.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement when earth movement is excluded. We do not cover ensuing loss unless that loss is otherwise covered.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion when that loss is excluded. We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear when wear and tear is excluded.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by deterioration when deterioration is excluded. We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown when mechanical breakdown is excluded.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical defect when the loss is excluded. We do not cover loss caused by corrosion, rust, or decay when the loss is excluded.

**W.12** The "ALE percentage" does not create coverage for an excluded cause of loss. The ALE percentage does not change any condition applicable to a loss.

**W.13** The "debris removal additional percentage" does not create coverage for excluded debris. The debris removal additional percentage does not change any exclusion applicable to a loss.

**W.14** The "minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage" remains applicable when a windstorm or hail deductible applies. The minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage does not remove an exclusion.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by neglect when neglect is excluded. An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further loss.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct when intentional conduct is excluded. We do not cover loss caused by an act committed with intent to cause loss.

**W.7** We will consider the property’s condition immediately before the loss. We will also consider the property’s age, quality, usefulness, and condition after the loss.

**W.8** We may consider available information concerning the original purchase of the property. We may consider information concerning comparable property available in the relevant market.

**W.9** An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property. If disposal is necessary, an insured must preserve reasonable evidence of the damage.

**W.10** We may request that an insured submit a signed statement of loss. The statement must identify the property, describe the loss, and state the amount claimed.

**W.11** We may request records showing ownership of the property. Such records may include purchase records, warranties, appraisals, inventories, or financial records.

**W.12** We may request records showing the condition of the property before the loss. An insured must provide records that are reasonably available.

**W.13** We may request information concerning repairs, replacement property, or estimates. An insured must provide estimates or invoices that are reasonably available.

**W.14** We may determine the reasonable cost to repair damaged property. We may determine the reasonable cost to replace destroyed or stolen property with property of like kind and quality.

**W.15** We are not required to pay for improvements, enhancements, or additions that exceed the property’s condition immediately before the loss. We are not required to pay for costs unrelated to the covered loss.

**W.16** We may pay for repair of damaged property when repair will restore the property to its condition immediately before the loss. We may elect to replace damaged or destroyed property instead of making payment.

**W.17** If we elect to repair or replace property, we may select the persons or organizations that perform the work. We will have no obligation for work that an insured directs without our agreement.

**W.18** An insured must allow reasonable access to the damaged property. An insured must take reasonable steps to make the property available for inspection, repair, or replacement.

**W.19** We may pay the amount we determine is owed to an insured. We may pay a person or organization that has a lawful interest in the property.

**W.20** Payment of a claim does not transfer ownership of damaged property to us unless we agree otherwise. We may take possession of property for which we make payment if permitted by law.

**W.21** We may require an insured to assign to us any rights of recovery against a person responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.22** We will not pay more than the amount of the covered loss. We will not pay for property that is not covered property.

**W.23** We will not pay for loss caused by an excluded peril. We will not pay for loss that is otherwise excluded or limited by the policy.

**W.24** We will apply any applicable deductible to the covered loss. The deductible will not reduce an amount that is not covered.

**W.25** We may apply any applicable special limit of liability to the property involved in the loss. A special limit of liability applies whether the property is damaged, destroyed, or stolen.

**W.26** The "association assessment limit" does not determine the value of personal property. It does not alter the basis used to settle a loss involving personal property.

**W.27** The "fire department call fee" does not determine the value of damaged or destroyed property. It does not establish a measure of loss for personal property.

**W.28** The "on-premises business property cap" does not determine the value of property after a covered loss. It does not change our right to investigate the claim.

**W.29** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath when permitted by the policy. An insured must answer questions concerning the loss and the property involved.

**W.30** We may request that an insured produce records for examination. We may make copies of records that relate to the claim.

**W.31** An insured must identify all persons with an interest in the property. An insured must provide information concerning any lien, security interest, or other lawful claim to the property.

**W.32** We may consider payments available from another source when permitted by the policy. We will not make duplicate payment for the same covered loss.

**W.33** We may obtain estimates, opinions, or appraisals concerning the property. We may use information from qualified persons to evaluate the amount of loss.

**W.34** An insured may provide information supporting a different valuation. We will consider relevant information that an insured provides.

**W.35** We may require additional information when the information already provided is insufficient to evaluate the claim. An insured must provide the requested information if it is reasonably available.

**W.36** We may defer payment while material information concerning the claim remains unavailable. We will not deny a claim solely because a requested record is unavailable if other reliable evidence establishes the loss.

**W.37** We may pay an undisputed amount of a covered loss while we continue to evaluate a disputed amount. Payment of an undisputed amount does not waive any policy right.

**W.38** We may resolve a disagreement concerning the amount of loss by any method available under the policy or applicable law. An insured must comply with the requirements of any such method.

**W.39** We will not be responsible for delay caused by an insured’s failure to provide requested information. We will not be responsible for increased costs caused by unreasonable delay by an insured.

**W.40** An insured must use reasonable means to obtain repair or replacement services. An insured must not incur unreasonable expenses in connection with a covered loss.

**W.41** We may consider reasonable charges necessary to protect covered property from further loss. We will not pay expenses that are not related to protecting covered property.

**W.42** We may require an insured to retain receipts for claimed expenses. An insured must provide receipts or other reasonable documentation when requested.

**W.43** We will settle a covered loss based on the property and circumstances involved. We will not settle a claim based on sentimental value or personal attachment.

**W.44** We may rely on information provided by an insured unless we have reason to question its accuracy. An insured must correct information that the insured knows is inaccurate.

**W.45** We may revise our evaluation when additional relevant information becomes available. A revised evaluation will reflect the information reasonably available to us.

**W.46** Our payment will not exceed the limit of liability that applies to the covered property. Any applicable special limit of liability remains part of the loss settlement determination.

**W.47** An insured must comply with duties that apply after a loss. Failure to comply with a duty may affect our obligation to pay for a covered loss.

**W.48** We will determine our payment after applying the policy terms that govern the claim. Our determination will be subject to all applicable conditions, exclusions, and limits.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** We use the term "endorsement" to mean this change to your policy. This endorsement modifies coverage only as stated in its provisions.

**W.2** "Coverage C" means the coverage for personal property owned or used by an insured. Coverage C includes property for which an insured is responsible, subject to the terms that apply.

**W.3** "Personal property" means movable property that is owned, used, worn, or carried by an insured. Personal property does not include real property or property permanently attached to land.

**W.4** "Special limit of liability" means the most we will pay for covered property within a described category. A special limit of liability applies regardless of the number of items involved in a loss.

**W.5** "Increased special limit" means a special limit of liability changed by this endorsement. The increased special limit applies only to the property category identified by the endorsement.

**W.6** "Insured" means you and residents of your household who qualify as insureds under your policy. An insured includes a person acting within the scope of that person's authority for an insured.

**W.7** "You" means the person named as an insured in the policy declarations. You include a spouse if that spouse resides in the same household.

**W.8** "We", "us", and "our" mean the insurer providing this coverage. We perform our obligations under this endorsement according to its terms.

**W.9** "Covered property" means property to which Coverage C applies. Covered property remains subject to all applicable exclusions and conditions.

**W.10** "Loss" means accidental direct physical loss to covered property. Loss includes damage resulting from a covered cause of loss.

**W.11** "Occurrence" means an accident or exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence may result in loss to more than one item of property.

**W.12** "Property category" means a class of personal property subject to a special limit of liability. Property is assigned to a category according to its nature, use, and characteristics.

**W.13** "Money" means currency, coins, and bank notes used as a medium of exchange. Money includes domestic and foreign currency.

**W.14** "Bank note" means paper currency issued or authorized for circulation by a governmental authority. A bank note remains money whether held for personal or business use.

**W.15** "Coin" means a piece of metal authorized for use as currency. Coin includes currency held for its face value rather than its collectible value.

**W.16** "Bullion" means gold, silver, platinum, or another precious metal in bar, ingot, or similar form. Bullion includes property held principally for its metal value.

**W.17** "Securities" means negotiable instruments and other documents representing money, ownership, debt, or contractual rights. Securities include instruments capable of transfer by delivery, endorsement, or assignment.

**W.18** "Negotiable instrument" means a signed writing that promises or orders payment of money. A negotiable instrument includes a check, draft, note, or similar document.

**W.19** "Check" means a written order directing a financial institution to pay money. A check includes a cashier's check and traveler's check.

**W.20** "Stamp" means a postage stamp, revenue stamp, or similar evidence of payment or value. Stamp includes unused stamp property held for collection or exchange.

**W.21** "Gift card" means a card, certificate, code, or other device redeemable for goods, services, or money. A gift card includes stored-value property issued by a merchant or financial institution.

**W.22** "Smart card" means a card or similar device containing stored information or value. A smart card includes a device that permits access to funds or services.

**W.23** "Debit card" means a card or access device used to obtain funds from an account. A debit card includes property used to authorize an electronic transfer of funds.

**W.24** "Electronic fund transfer card" means a card or device used to initiate an electronic transfer of funds. Electronic fund transfer card includes an access device associated with a deposit account.

**W.25** "Watercraft" means a vessel designed for travel on water. Watercraft includes its motor, trailer, equipment, and accessories when they are used with the vessel.

**W.26** "Vessel" means a craft capable of being used as a means of transportation on water. A vessel includes a boat, sailboat, and similar craft.

**W.27** "Outboard motor" means a motor designed to be attached externally to a watercraft. An outboard motor includes its controls and integral components.

**W.28** "Trailer" means a vehicle designed to be drawn by another vehicle. Trailer includes a trailer used to transport watercraft or recreational property.

**W.29** "Recreational vehicle" means a motorized or nonmotorized vehicle designed principally for recreation. Recreational vehicle includes its equipment and accessories.

**W.30** "Aircraft" means a device used or designed for flight. Aircraft includes its parts, equipment, and accessories.

**W.31** "Hovercraft" means a device supported by a cushion of air and designed to travel over land or water. Hovercraft includes its equipment and accessories.

**W.32** "Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or profit. Business includes occasional activity conducted for compensation or profit.

**W.33** "Business property" means personal property used primarily for business purposes. Business property includes property held for sale, lease, rental, or use in a business.

**W.34** "Property held for sale" means personal property offered, displayed, stored, or intended for transfer to another person for compensation. Property held for sale includes inventory and merchandise.

**W.35** "Inventory" means personal property held for sale, lease, rental, or distribution in a business. Inventory includes materials and supplies intended for use in producing property for sale.

**W.36** "Merchandise" means personal property acquired or held for sale in the ordinary course of business. Merchandise includes goods displayed or stored for prospective customers.

**W.37** "Sample" means personal property used to demonstrate, promote, or solicit sales of goods or services. Sample includes property furnished without charge for promotional purposes.

**W.38** "Electronic apparatus" means equipment that receives, transmits, records, reproduces, processes, or displays electronic data, sound, or images. Electronic apparatus includes its integral components.

**W.39** "Electronic data" means information, facts, concepts, or instructions converted into a form usable by electronic equipment. Electronic data includes software and digitally stored information.

**W.40** "Software" means programs, applications, instructions, and related data that direct the operation of electronic equipment. Software includes operating systems and application programs.

**W.41** "Media" means material on which electronic data is recorded or stored. Media includes discs, drives, memory devices, and similar property.

**W.42** "Portable electronic equipment" means electronic apparatus designed to be carried by a person. Portable electronic equipment includes equipment capable of operation away from the residence premises.

**W.43** "Sound receiving equipment" means electronic apparatus used to receive, play, record, or reproduce sound. Sound receiving equipment includes speakers, headphones, and related components.

**W.44** "Visual receiving equipment" means electronic apparatus used to receive, display, record, or reproduce images. Visual receiving equipment includes screens, monitors, and related components.

**W.45** "Camera" means equipment used to capture still images or moving images. Camera includes lenses, mounts, and integral recording components.

**W.46** "Photographic equipment" means property used to capture, process, display, or store photographic images. Photographic equipment includes cameras, lenses, lighting equipment, and accessories.

**W.47** "Theft" means an unlawful taking of property by a person who lacks permission to take it. Theft includes attempted theft resulting in direct physical loss to covered property.

**W.48** "Mysterious disappearance" means the loss of property when the cause or manner of disappearance cannot be determined. Mysterious disappearance does not establish that theft occurred.

**W.49** "Residence premises" means the residence location identified in your policy declarations. Residence premises includes the part of that location used by you as a residence.

**W.50** "Residence location" means the place where you reside and that qualifies for coverage under your policy. Residence location includes land and structures associated with that place.

**W.51** "Actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss, adjusted for depreciation and physical condition. Actual cash value does not exceed the amount required to repair or replace the property.

**W.52** "Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement cost remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.
