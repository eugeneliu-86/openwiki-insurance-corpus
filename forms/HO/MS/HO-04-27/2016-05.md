# HO 04 27 Limited Water Damage Coverage (Edition 2016-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

**W.1** This endorsement is attached to and forms part of your policy. It changes certain provisions of the insurance provided by your policy.

**W.2** The provisions of this endorsement apply to the coverage described in this endorsement. All other policy provisions continue to apply unless changed by this endorsement.

**W.3** We provide the coverage stated in this endorsement subject to its terms. You must comply with all duties that apply to the insurance.

**W.4** This endorsement applies only while it is attached to your policy. Its provisions are subject to the applicable policy conditions.

**W.5** The insurance provided by this endorsement is limited in scope. We do not cover loss except as provided by this endorsement.

**W.6** Coverage under this endorsement is subject to the applicable exclusions. Any exclusion that applies to the loss remains in effect unless changed by this endorsement.

**W.7** The terms of this endorsement apply to property covered by your policy. They do not create coverage for property not otherwise eligible for coverage.

**W.8** We will determine coverage under this endorsement according to its terms and the applicable policy provisions. An insured must provide information we reasonably require to determine coverage.

**W.9** This endorsement does not change any duty imposed upon an insured by the policy. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation of any loss.

**W.10** The terms of this endorsement control only when they conflict with the terms of your policy. All terms that do not conflict remain unchanged.

**W.11** Coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to the conditions applicable to the loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage when reasonably possible.

**W.12** We may inspect property or examine information relating to a claimed loss as permitted by the policy. An insured must allow such inspection or examination when required.

**W.13** This endorsement does not alter the policy except as stated in this endorsement. The policy remains in effect subject to the changes made by this endorsement.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We cover direct physical loss to "Covered Property" caused by "Water Damage", subject to the provisions of this endorsement. "Water Damage" means accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system.

**W.2** We cover direct physical loss caused by the accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a household appliance. The discharge or overflow must be sudden and unintended.

**W.3** We cover direct physical loss caused by water or steam escaping from a plumbing system located within the residence premises. The plumbing system must be connected for its intended use at the time of loss.

**W.4** We cover direct physical loss caused by water or steam escaping from a heating system located within the residence premises. The heating system must contain water or steam as part of its normal operation.

**W.5** We cover direct physical loss caused by water or steam escaping from an air conditioning system located within the residence premises. The air conditioning system must be installed for the service of the residence premises.

**W.6** We cover direct physical loss caused by water or steam escaping from an automatic fire protective sprinkler system. The sprinkler system must be maintained for fire protection purposes.

**W.7** We cover direct physical loss caused by the breaking, cracking, burning, bulging, or freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We also cover the cost to repair the portion of the system from which the water or steam escaped.

**W.8** We cover direct physical loss caused by the breaking, cracking, burning, bulging, or freezing of a household appliance. We also cover the cost to repair the portion of the appliance from which the water escaped.

**W.9** We do not cover loss to a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system caused by wear and tear, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do not cover loss to a household appliance caused by wear and tear, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies whether the seepage or leakage occurs from a plumbing system, household appliance, roof, wall, window, foundation, or other opening.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sump systems, or related equipment. This exclusion applies regardless of the source of the water.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, sidewalk, driveway, swimming pool, or other structure.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or otherwise.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, or hail entering a building through an opening that was not created by a covered cause of loss. We do not cover loss caused by water entering through a roof, wall, window, door, or foundation unless the opening resulted from a covered cause of loss.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by condensation, humidity, or vapor. We do not cover loss caused by the presence, growth, proliferation, spread, or any activity of fungi, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a sump system, drain system, gutter system, downspout, or similar water diversion system. We do not cover loss caused by the inability of such system to handle water.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by water that escapes from a swimming pool, hot tub, spa, fountain, or similar structure. We do not cover loss caused by water that escapes from equipment used with any such structure.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by water or steam escaping from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system that is not located within the residence premises. We do not cover loss caused by water or steam escaping from a household appliance that is not located within the residence premises.

**W.19** We cover reasonable measures taken by an insured to protect covered property from further direct physical loss after a covered loss occurs. An insured must use reasonable care to preserve damaged property for our inspection.

**W.20** An insured must take reasonable steps to stop or reduce the escape of water or steam when it is safe to do so. An insured must protect covered property from further damage after the source of water or steam has been controlled.

**W.21** An insured must provide us with prompt notice of loss. An insured must show us the damaged property and provide records or other information we reasonably request.

**W.22** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless removal is necessary to protect persons or property. An insured must preserve evidence of the cause of loss when reasonably possible.

**W.23** We will pay only for direct physical loss that is caused by Water Damage covered under this endorsement. We will not pay for loss caused by an excluded event, condition, or source of water.

**W.24** Coverage under this endorsement applies only to covered property for which you have a covered interest at the time of loss. We will not pay more than the amount of your financial interest in the damaged property.

**W.25** The duties and exclusions in this endorsement apply to every insured seeking coverage for water damage. Compliance with these duties is a condition of coverage when the failure to comply prejudices us.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We determine our liability for covered direct physical loss under the limits stated in this section. We do not pay more than the applicable stated limit.

**W.2** The most we will pay for direct physical loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains or overflows from a sump is two thousand five hundred dollars. We apply that amount to all covered loss caused by such water.

**W.3** The most we will pay for loss caused by "fungi", wet or dry rot, or bacteria is five thousand dollars. We apply that amount to all covered loss caused by fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.4** We apply each stated limit regardless of the number of claimants, locations, items of property, or causes of loss. We do not increase a stated limit because loss affects more than one location.

**W.5** We do not increase a stated limit because more than one "insured" has an interest in the damaged property. We may make payment jointly to insureds having an interest in the property.

**W.6** We do not combine stated limits to increase the amount payable for the same loss. We apply the limit that is applicable to the covered cause of loss.

**W.7** We treat related damage arising from the same source of water as part of the same loss. We do not apply a separate stated limit to damage that is a continuation of that loss.

**W.8** We apply a stated limit without regard to the number of occurrences alleged. We do not increase a stated limit because damage develops at different times.

**W.9** We pay only the portion of loss that is covered. We may allocate loss between covered and noncovered causes when the available information permits allocation.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by an excluded cause of loss. We do not pay for damage that is not the direct result of a covered cause of loss.

**W.11** We pay only for reasonable and necessary repair or replacement of damaged property. We do not pay for repair or replacement that improves property beyond its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.12** We include reasonable emergency measures within the applicable stated limit. We do not pay for measures that are not necessary to protect property from further covered damage.

**W.13** We include reasonable costs to remove damaged property within the applicable stated limit. We do not pay costs to remove property that was not damaged by a covered cause of loss.

**W.14** We do not pay for loss that occurred before the applicable coverage became effective. We do not pay for loss that occurs after the applicable coverage ends.

**W.15** We do not pay for expected or intended loss. We do not pay for loss that is speculative, remote, or unsupported by reliable information.

**W.16** We do not pay for the cost of correcting a condition that existed before the covered loss. We do not pay for maintenance, upkeep, or preventive measures.

**W.17** We may require reasonable records, estimates, invoices, photographs, or other information to determine the amount of a "claim". We may determine the amount of loss from the information available to us.

**W.18** An insured must protect damaged property from further damage. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property and relevant records when reasonably requested.

**W.19** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must provide truthful information concerning the loss.

**W.20** We may take possession of damaged property after payment for that property. We may dispose of property for which we have paid when permitted by law.

**W.21** We may recover from a person responsible for covered loss to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.22** We do not pay for loss that has been paid by another source. We reduce our payment by amounts that an insured receives for the same covered loss.

**W.23** We may pay an insured, a person having a legal interest in damaged property, or another person authorized to receive payment. Payment does not determine ownership of damaged property.

**W.24** We may settle a claim without admitting liability. Payment of a claim does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.25** We do not increase a stated limit because an insured incurs expenses in preparing or presenting a claim. We do not pay expenses that are not part of covered direct physical loss.

**W.26** We apply any applicable deductible in determining the amount payable for covered loss. We do not return a deductible because loss is paid under a stated limit.

**W.27** We do not provide additional coverage by making payment for a loss. We do not extend coverage beyond the terms that apply to the loss.

**W.28** When we have paid the applicable stated limit, we have no further obligation for that loss. Our payment of a stated limit exhausts our liability for covered loss subject to that limit.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** For purposes of this coverage, a "covered loss" means direct physical loss for which coverage applies. The "deductible" is the portion of a covered loss that you are responsible for before we make payment.

**W.2** We will apply the deductible to the amount otherwise payable for a covered loss. We will pay only the amount remaining after application of the deductible.

**W.3** The deductible applies whether a covered loss is repaired, replaced, or settled by payment. The deductible also applies when damaged property is not repaired or replaced.

**W.4** We do not make payment for any covered loss when the amount otherwise payable does not exceed the deductible. Your responsibility for the deductible remains unchanged by our decision not to make payment.

**W.5** We apply the deductible to the covered loss as a whole. The manner in which you present a claim does not change application of the deductible.

**W.6** We apply the deductible before making payment for covered damage to property. We also apply the deductible before making payment for covered loss of use.

**W.7** The deductible applies to covered expenses that are included in the amount otherwise payable for a covered loss. The deductible does not create coverage for expenses that are not covered.

**W.8** The deductible applies regardless of whether you repair damaged property. The deductible applies regardless of whether you retain or dispose of damaged property.

**W.9** The deductible applies to a covered loss even when salvage has value. Any salvage value does not reduce your responsibility for the deductible.

**W.10** The deductible applies before we consider any recovery available from another person or organization. A recovery received after payment does not eliminate the deductible applied to the covered loss.

**W.11** The deductible applies even if another person or organization may be responsible for the covered loss. Your right to seek recovery from that person or organization does not change application of the deductible.

**W.12** An insured must protect property from further damage after a covered loss. Compliance with that duty does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.13** We may treat related damage as part of the same covered loss when the damage results from the same event or condition. Damage that is unrelated to that event or condition may be treated separately.

**W.14** The cause of damage does not by itself determine application of the deductible. We will apply the deductible based on the facts of the covered loss.

**W.15** Discovery of damage at different times does not by itself create separate covered losses. We will determine whether damage is part of a covered loss from the circumstances of the damage.

**W.16** A disagreement about the value of damaged property does not affect application of the deductible. We apply the deductible after determining the amount otherwise payable.

**W.17** Allocation of a covered loss among damaged property does not change the deductible. We apply the deductible to the covered loss and not to an allocation of that loss.

**W.18** The status or identity of insureds does not change application of the deductible. We apply the deductible without regard to which insured seeks payment.

**W.19** The deductible applies when payment is made directly to you. The deductible also applies when payment is made to another person or organization on your behalf.

**W.20** We do not apply the deductible in a manner that increases the amount otherwise payable for a covered loss. The deductible does not increase coverage for any loss.

**W.21** We do not apply the deductible to property that is not part of a covered loss. The deductible does not make otherwise excluded damage covered.

**W.22** Payment of the deductible is your responsibility. An insured must not represent that we will pay the deductible.

**W.23** An insured must provide information we reasonably need to determine the amount of a covered loss. An insured must provide records and documents that support the amount claimed.

**W.24** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the cause and extent of a covered loss. That cooperation includes permitting inspection of damaged property when reasonably requested.

**W.25** We may apply the deductible based on information available to us when we determine the amount otherwise payable. We may revise that determination when additional relevant information becomes available.

**W.26** Our payment of any amount does not waive application of the deductible to the covered loss. Our investigation of a claim does not waive any right concerning the deductible.

**W.27** The deductible does not increase the amount we otherwise owe for a covered loss. We will not pay any portion of the deductible on your behalf.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss except to the extent that this endorsement expressly provides coverage for "Water Damage". This endorsement does not broaden coverage for any other cause of loss.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by a condition that existed before the loss for which coverage is sought. We do not cover loss that results from a condition known to an insured before the loss begins.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by seepage, leakage, or overflow that is not within the coverage provided by this endorsement. We do not cover loss caused by water that enters a building through an opening not caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by either of these sources of water.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building or foundation.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up from a drain, sewer, sump, or related system unless the loss is within the express coverage granted by this endorsement. We do not cover loss caused by a failure to maintain such a system.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by water discharged from a sump because of mechanical breakdown, power interruption, or improper operation. We do not cover loss caused by water discharged from equipment designed to remove water from a structure.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by groundwater, subsurface water, or waterborne material. We do not cover loss caused by dampness or moisture arising from those conditions.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, or ice entering a structure through an opening. We do not cover loss caused by runoff entering a structure at or below ground level.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by erosion, settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover resulting water damage unless it is directly caused by a covered water event.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. This exclusion applies regardless of whether water contributes to the earth movement.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by landslide, mudflow, subsidence, sinkhole collapse, or earth sinking. We do not cover loss caused by material carried by any of those events.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by the weight, pressure, or movement of water against a building, foundation, retaining wall, pavement, or other structure. We do not cover resulting damage to personal property.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by wear, tear, deterioration, rust, corrosion, decay, or mechanical breakdown. We do not cover loss caused by an appliance, fixture, or system that fails because of such conditions.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by latent defect, inherent vice, or defective materials. We do not cover loss caused by faulty design, workmanship, repair, renovation, or construction.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by neglect. An insured must use reasonable means to protect property from further damage after a loss.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an insured. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's direction, authorization, or concealment of conduct causing the loss.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by illegal activity. We do not cover property used for an illegal purpose when the use contributes to the loss.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We do not cover costs to test for, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize pollutants.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or other microbial matter. We do not cover remediation, testing, removal, or replacement arising from those conditions.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke, vapor, fumes, or industrial contamination. We do not cover loss caused by chemicals carried or deposited by water.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, gnawing, or discharge from animals.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems unless an insured used reasonable care to maintain heat in the structure. An insured must take reasonable precautions to prevent freezing when the structure is vacant or unattended.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the insured location. We do not cover loss caused by failure of a utility service that contributes to water discharge or overflow.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance, law, regulation, or enforcement action requiring repair, replacement, demolition, or construction. We do not cover increased costs arising from compliance with such requirements.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action. We do not cover seizure, destruction, confiscation, or disposition of property by a governmental authority.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military force. We do not cover loss caused by an act or consequence of any of those events.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. We do not cover loss caused by radioactive contamination or radioactive material.

**W.29** We do not cover loss to property that is not owned or used by an insured as required for coverage. We do not cover loss to property held for sale, storage, or commercial use except as expressly provided by this endorsement.

**W.30** We do not cover loss to property of tenants, roomers, boarders, or other occupants unless the property qualifies for coverage. We do not cover property belonging to a guest or visitor unless coverage expressly applies.

**W.31** We do not cover loss to property while it is outside the insured location unless coverage expressly applies. We do not cover loss to property in transit.

**W.32** We do not cover loss to land, including land on which a building is located. We do not cover loss to landscaping, trees, shrubs, lawns, plants, or growing crops.

**W.33** We do not cover loss to foundations, piers, retaining walls, bulkheads, docks, wharves, or pavements caused by water pressure or subsurface water. We do not cover loss to exterior structures caused by settling or erosion.

**W.34** We do not cover loss to property that is permanently installed below ground level when the loss results from water below the surface of the ground. We do not cover loss to personal property stored in an area subject to recurring water intrusion.

**W.35** We do not cover loss consisting solely of odor, discoloration, staining, or loss of use. We do not cover loss consisting solely of reduced value or diminished marketability.

**W.36** We do not cover costs to repair the source of leakage, seepage, overflow, or discharge unless coverage expressly applies. We do not cover costs to replace an appliance, fixture, pipe, drain, sewer line, sump, or pump solely because it failed.

**W.37** We do not cover costs to maintain, inspect, service, clean, repair, or replace property to prevent a future loss. We do not cover preventative work undertaken without direct physical loss covered by this endorsement.

**W.38** We do not cover expenses caused by delay, loss of market, loss of use, or interruption of business. We do not cover loss of income arising from property not eligible for coverage.

**W.39** We do not cover loss arising from misrepresentation, concealment, fraud, or material omission by an insured. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss.

**W.40** We do not cover loss for which an insured has made a false statement relating to the loss. An insured must provide truthful information requested by us concerning the loss.

**W.41** We do not cover loss for which an insured fails to preserve damaged property when preservation is reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect property affected by a claimed loss.

**W.42** We do not cover loss when an insured disposes of damaged property before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must retain records and evidence reasonably necessary to support a claim.

**W.43** We do not cover duplicate payment for the same loss. We do not cover any portion of loss recoverable from another source to the extent recovery is available.

**W.44** We do not cover loss to the extent an insured has waived rights of recovery against a responsible party after a loss. An insured must preserve our rights of recovery to the extent permitted by law.

**W.45** We do not cover loss caused by a failure to comply with duties that apply after a loss. An insured must promptly give notice of a loss and protect property from further damage.

**W.46** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. We do not cover loss caused by a condition that develops gradually.

**W.47** We do not cover loss caused by condensation, humidity, or changes in temperature. We do not cover loss caused by inadequate ventilation or inadequate maintenance.

**W.48** We do not cover loss caused by overflow from a body of water, watercourse, drainageway, or containment area. We do not cover loss caused by water released from a dam, levee, dike, or similar structure.

**W.49** We do not cover loss caused by a failure of grading, drainage, drainage piping, gutters, downspouts, or exterior water-control devices. We do not cover loss caused by improper direction of water away from a structure.

**W.50** We do not cover loss caused by defective caulking, sealing, flashing, siding, roofing, windows, doors, or exterior finishes. We do not cover loss caused by an opening that existed before the event causing water intrusion.

**W.51** The "wind percentage deductible minimum" does not create or expand coverage for water damage. We do not cover loss that is otherwise excluded because that deductible applies.

**W.52** The "Coverage B limit as a percentage of Coverage A" does not create coverage for property otherwise excluded. We do not cover loss to structures or property not eligible for coverage.

**W.53** The "personal property limit percentage" does not create coverage for excluded property. We do not cover loss to personal property unless the property and cause of loss qualify for coverage.

**W.54** The "additional living expense limit" does not create coverage for loss otherwise excluded. We do not cover expenses that do not result from a covered direct physical loss.

**W.55** The "clean-up allowance percentage" does not create coverage for pollutants, mold, fungus, or other excluded material. We do not cover clean-up costs except to the extent coverage is expressly provided.

**W.56** We do not cover any loss that is excluded even though another cause, event, or condition contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss. We do not cover resulting loss unless it is separately covered by this endorsement.

**W.57** We do not cover loss that begins before the effective date of this endorsement. We do not cover loss that occurs after coverage under this endorsement ends.

**W.58** We do not cover loss arising from a claim made by an insured against another insured. We do not cover loss arising from conduct of a person acting with an insured's knowledge or consent.

**W.59** We do not cover loss caused by property being vacant, unoccupied, or abandoned when that condition contributes to the loss. An insured must take reasonable care to secure and maintain property during such condition.

**W.60** We do not cover any loss excluded by the terms that remain unchanged by this endorsement. Coverage applies only as expressly provided by this endorsement.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** We provide coverage for direct physical loss caused by "water damage" only when the loss involves property eligible for coverage. An "insured" must comply with all duties stated in these Conditions.

**W.2** An insured must protect "covered property" from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable efforts to preserve the condition of covered property until we inspect it.

**W.3** An insured must give us prompt notice of an "occurrence" that may result in a loss. Notice must identify the location, circumstances, and apparent cause of the occurrence.

**W.4** An insured must notify us of any "claim" made by another person arising from an occurrence. An insured must promptly send us all communications relating to that claim.

**W.5** An insured must report the loss to us within thirty days after the loss occurs. Notice must include information available to the insured concerning the damaged property and the cause of loss.

**W.6** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect damaged property from further injury or deterioration. We will consider reasonable emergency measures when adjusting the loss.

**W.7** An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must preserve samples, photographs, and other evidence that may assist in determining the cause or extent of loss.

**W.8** An insured must make damaged property available for our inspection. We may inspect the property as often as reasonably necessary to investigate the loss.

**W.9** An insured must show us all records and documents that relate to ownership, value, maintenance, repair, or use of the damaged property. We may make copies of those records and documents.

**W.10** An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the circumstances of the loss. The information must be complete and accurate to the best of the insured's knowledge.

**W.11** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss. An insured must assist us in obtaining information from persons with knowledge of the occurrence.

**W.12** An insured must permit us to examine the insured under oath when reasonably requested. An insured must sign a transcript of that examination when the transcript accurately states the testimony given.

**W.13** An insured must provide records of repairs, maintenance, replacements, and improvements relating to the damaged property when those records are available. We may consider the absence of records when evaluating the claimed loss.

**W.14** An insured must submit a signed, sworn "proof of loss" when we request it. The proof of loss must state the facts known to the insured concerning the loss and the amount claimed.

**W.15** An insured must comply with the applicable "days to submit a signed, sworn proof of loss" requirement. A proof of loss must be supported by information sufficient for us to evaluate the claim.

**W.16** An insured must identify all persons having an interest in the damaged property. An insured must provide available information concerning the nature of each interest.

**W.17** An insured must identify any other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must provide available information concerning the insurer and the applicable coverage.

**W.18** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense except for reasonable emergency measures to protect property from further damage. We will not be bound by a voluntary payment or obligation made without our consent.

**W.19** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or omit a material fact relating to the loss. This duty applies before and after a claim is presented to us.

**W.20** An insured must not abandon damaged property to us. We may take possession of damaged property when necessary to investigate, adjust, or settle a loss.

**W.21** An insured must preserve any right of recovery against a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.22** If we make payment for a loss, an insured must transfer to us rights of recovery against a responsible person or organization to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing those rights.

**W.23** An insured must submit to our reasonable requests for access to the location of loss. An insured must arrange access when the property is under the control of another person.

**W.24** An insured must provide photographs, recordings, invoices, estimates, receipts, and other available evidence supporting the claimed loss. We may require information concerning the source and authenticity of any evidence provided.

**W.25** An insured must preserve removed materials when their condition may assist in determining the cause of loss. An insured must not authorize destructive testing without giving us a reasonable opportunity to participate.

**W.26** An insured must notify us before beginning nonemergency repair work when practicable. An insured must retain documentation of work performed and expenses incurred.

**W.27** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting persons who perform repair or cleanup work. We do not assume responsibility for the quality, cost, or completion of work performed by a person selected by an insured.

**W.28** An insured must permit us to obtain statements from persons who may have knowledge of the occurrence. An insured must provide available contact information for those persons.

**W.29** An insured must provide available records concerning prior damage, prior repairs, or prior claims involving the damaged property. We may consider those records when determining the cause and extent of the current loss.

**W.30** An insured must separate property damaged by the occurrence from property not damaged by the occurrence when reasonably practicable. An insured must identify property claimed as damaged and property claimed as undamaged.

**W.31** An insured must allow us to inspect repair work while it is in progress when inspection is reasonably necessary. An insured must provide notice before concealed work prevents meaningful inspection.

**W.32** An insured must provide information concerning any lease, occupancy arrangement, or other agreement affecting the damaged property. An insured must identify persons responsible for maintenance or repair of the property.

**W.33** An insured must provide available information concerning the source of water, the path of water movement, and the duration of water presence. An insured must preserve evidence relating to those circumstances when reasonably practicable.

**W.34** An insured must take reasonable steps to mitigate damage from water, including removal of water and drying of affected property when appropriate. An insured must retain records supporting mitigation efforts and related expenses.

**W.35** An insured must not make a false statement concerning the cause, condition, ownership, value, or repair of damaged property. We may rely on information provided by an insured in investigating and adjusting a loss.

**W.36** The "AOP deductible floor" applies to a covered loss as required by its terms. An insured must provide information necessary to determine the deductible applicable to the loss.

**W.37** The "replacement cost threshold percentage" applies when replacement cost is determined. An insured must provide information necessary to determine the applicable basis of loss settlement.

**W.38** An insured must keep us informed of a change in address, contact information, or location of damaged property while a claim remains unresolved. Notice of a change must be provided promptly.

**W.39** An insured must notify us if damaged property is repaired, replaced, sold, transferred, or disposed of while we are investigating the loss. An insured must provide available records concerning that action.

**W.40** An insured must provide us with information concerning salvage value when requested. We may take possession of salvage after payment of a covered loss.

**W.41** An insured must not interfere with our inspection, investigation, adjustment, or settlement of a loss. An insured must provide reasonable access to information and property needed for those activities.

**W.42** An insured must comply with the applicable "time to bring action" requirement before bringing an action against us. No action may be brought against us unless an insured has complied with these Conditions.

**W.43** An insured must provide written authorization when reasonably necessary for us to obtain records relating to the loss. The authorization must be limited to information relevant to our investigation or adjustment.

**W.44** An insured must advise us of any dispute concerning ownership, value, cause of loss, or amount of loss. An insured must cooperate in any reasonable effort to resolve that dispute.

**W.45** An insured must not assign a claim or any interest in a claim without our written consent. An assignment made without our consent does not bind us.

**W.46** Compliance with these Conditions is required before we have a duty to make payment for a covered loss. We may enforce any right available to us when an insured fails to comply with these Conditions.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** An "insured" must take reasonable care to protect "covered property" from "water damage." An insured must maintain the property in a condition that reasonably prevents accidental discharge, leakage, overflow, or seepage of water.

**W.2** An insured must maintain each "plumbing system" in good working condition. An insured must repair known defects, leaks, and deteriorated components within a reasonable time.

**W.3** An insured must inspect exposed plumbing, supply lines, drain lines, valves, and connections when conditions reasonably permit inspection. An insured must correct conditions that could result in water damage.

**W.4** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent freezing of plumbing, heating equipment, and appliances containing water. An insured must maintain heat or otherwise protect water-bearing equipment when freezing conditions are reasonably likely.

**W.5** An insured must shut off the water supply when a known leak cannot be promptly repaired. An insured must drain or otherwise protect affected plumbing when shutting off the supply is reasonably necessary to prevent further damage.

**W.6** An insured must maintain appliances, fixtures, and equipment that use or contain water in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions. An insured must replace hoses, seals, connections, and other components that show deterioration or leakage.

**W.7** An insured must keep drains, gutters, downspouts, and similar water-control devices reasonably clear and functional. An insured must correct known obstructions that could cause water to overflow or accumulate.

**W.8** An insured must maintain caulking, flashing, seals, and other exterior water-resisting materials in reasonably sound condition. An insured must repair known openings that permit water to enter covered property.

**W.9** An insured must use reasonable care when installing, moving, disconnecting, or reconnecting an appliance or plumbing component. An insured must secure water-supply connections before restoring water service.

**W.10** An insured must promptly investigate visible signs of leakage, staining, dampness, corrosion, or mold. An insured must take reasonable steps to stop the source of water and protect covered property from further damage.

**W.11** An insured must take reasonable emergency measures after a loss to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep a record of the measures taken and the expenses incurred.

**W.12** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.13** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss involving water damage. The notice must identify the location of the loss and the apparent source of the water.

**W.14** An insured must cooperate with us in investigating a claim. An insured must provide access to the damaged property when we reasonably request access.

**W.15** An insured must provide records, photographs, receipts, estimates, invoices, and other information reasonably requested by us. An insured must preserve available evidence concerning the cause and extent of the loss.

**W.16** An insured must submit to an examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer material questions truthfully and provide requested information concerning the loss.

**W.17** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning a loss. An insured must not present altered, fabricated, or misleading documents in support of a claim.

**W.18** An insured must not make voluntary payments, assume obligations, or incur expenses without our consent, except for reasonable emergency measures. An insured must permit us to inspect any proposed repair before work begins when reasonably possible.

**W.19** An insured must use reasonable efforts to obtain repair estimates that describe the damaged property and proposed work. An insured must provide those estimates to us when requested.

**W.20** An insured must not increase the cost of repair by delaying reasonable protective measures. An insured must mitigate loss after discovering water damage.

**W.21** We do not cover water damage caused by an insured's failure to maintain a plumbing system, appliance, fixture, drain, gutter, or water-control device after the insured knew of a condition likely to cause damage.

**W.22** We do not cover water damage caused by repeated leakage, seepage, or overflow that an insured knew of or should reasonably have discovered. This exclusion applies whether the damage is visible or concealed.

**W.23** We do not cover deterioration, corrosion, rust, wear, decay, or failure of the part from which water escapes. We do not cover the cost to repair or replace that part solely because it failed.

**W.24** We do not cover water damage caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective maintenance, repair, installation, remodeling, or construction performed by or for an insured. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to correct known defective work.

**W.25** We do not cover water damage that existed before the reported loss. We do not cover damage that occurred before an insured acquired an interest in the covered property.

**W.26** We do not cover water damage intentionally caused by an insured. We do not cover loss resulting from an insured's intentional failure to stop a known source of water.

**W.27** We do not cover damage caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of a loss. This exclusion applies to additional damage resulting from that neglect.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow from a drain, sewer, sump, or related equipment when an insured has failed to maintain that equipment after knowledge of a condition requiring repair. We do not cover resulting damage caused by an insured's failure to take reasonable protective action.

**W.29** We do not cover mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or other microbial matter resulting from an insured's failure to promptly address known moisture or water intrusion. We do not cover the cost to test for such matter unless it is necessary to evaluate covered water damage.

**W.30** We do not cover contamination, pollution, or the discharge of waste resulting from an insured's failure to maintain plumbing, drains, or waste-removal equipment. We do not cover cleanup costs that are not reasonably necessary to repair covered water damage.

**W.31** An insured must permit us to inspect the cause of a loss before permanent repairs are completed when reasonably possible. An insured must retain removed components that may assist in determining the cause of the loss.

**W.32** An insured must notify us if a repair contractor identifies conditions unrelated to the reported water damage. An insured must not include unrelated maintenance, improvement, or remodeling costs in a claim.

**W.33** An insured must provide available information concerning prior leaks, repairs, water intrusion, and related damage affecting the covered property. An insured must disclose known conditions that may have contributed to the loss.

**W.34** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property when it is unoccupied. An insured must arrange for reasonable observation of conditions that could cause water damage.

**W.35** An insured must restore protective measures after emergency repairs are completed. An insured must continue reasonable efforts to prevent additional water damage until permanent repairs are made.

**W.36** Duties imposed by this section apply to each insured seeking payment for a loss. The act or omission of an insured may affect payment for damage caused or increased by that act or omission.

**W.37** We do not cover any increase in loss resulting from an insured's failure to comply with a duty imposed by this section. We do not deny payment for damage that would have occurred despite the failure.

**W.38** An insured must comply with these special requirements as a condition of payment for water damage. Compliance must be determined from the facts and circumstances of the loss.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a covered loss to “covered property” under this endorsement as provided in this Loss Settlement provision. Payment is subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions that apply to the loss.

**W.2** An insured must establish that direct physical loss was caused by a covered cause of loss. An insured must also establish the amount of loss claimed.

**W.3** We may inspect damaged property before it is repaired, replaced, discarded, or otherwise disposed of. An insured must retain the property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.4** We may require an insured to provide records, receipts, photographs, estimates, invoices, or other information relevant to the loss. An insured must cooperate with our investigation and settlement of the claim.

**W.5** We will not pay for loss that is excluded, limited, or otherwise not covered under this endorsement. We will apply any applicable deductible to the covered loss.

**W.6** We will determine the amount of covered loss after considering the damaged property, its condition immediately before the loss, and the cost to repair or replace the property. We may consider any applicable policy limitation in making that determination.

**W.7** The loss settlement basis for “personal property” is actual cash value. We will not pay more than the amount necessary to settle the covered loss under that basis.

**W.8** We may settle a covered loss by paying money, repairing the property, replacing the property, or arranging for repair or replacement. Our election of a settlement method does not increase the coverage provided.

**W.9** If we elect to repair or replace damaged property, we may select the persons or entities that will perform the work. An insured must provide reasonable access to the property for that work.

**W.10** We will not pay for repair or replacement that improves property beyond its condition immediately before the loss. We may pay for reasonable repair or replacement using materials of like kind and quality.

**W.11** We may pay the amount of loss to an insured, to an insured and another person having an interest in the property, or directly to a person providing repair or replacement services. Payment to a person having an interest in the property does not create coverage for property not otherwise covered.

**W.12** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will consider reasonable expenses incurred for that purpose only when they are covered by this endorsement.

**W.13** We do not cover loss resulting from an insured's voluntary disposal of damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. This exclusion does not apply when immediate disposal is necessary to protect persons or property from further harm.

**W.14** We may require an insured to submit a signed statement describing the damaged property and the circumstances of loss. An insured must provide that statement when we request it.

**W.15** We may require an insured to identify property that was repaired, replaced, or removed following the loss. An insured must preserve available documentation supporting those actions.

**W.16** We will not pay for undamaged property solely because materials, appearance, design, or color do not match damaged property. We may settle covered damage without replacing property that sustained no direct physical loss.

**W.17** We will not pay for delay, inconvenience, loss of use, or interruption of an insured's activities unless such loss is specifically covered. A settlement for covered property does not include indirect or consequential loss.

**W.18** If damaged property can be repaired, we may base settlement on the reasonable cost of repair. The repair must restore the damaged property without creating an improvement beyond its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.19** If damaged property cannot reasonably be repaired, we may base settlement on the applicable value of the property immediately before the loss. We may require an insured to surrender the damaged property when we pay for its replacement or value.

**W.20** We may take all or part of damaged property when we settle the loss. Any property we take becomes our property to the extent of our payment.

**W.21** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We are not required to accept abandoned property.

**W.22** We will apply the assessment coverage limit when determining the amount payable for a covered assessment. That limit does not increase any other coverage.

**W.23** We will consider a fire response charge only when it results from a covered loss and is otherwise covered. Payment for a fire response charge is subject to all applicable policy terms.

**W.24** We will apply the special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones when it applies to a covered loss. That special limit does not expand coverage for property not otherwise covered.

**W.25** We will apply the home business contents limit when covered property is subject to that limit. That limit does not create coverage for business property that is not otherwise covered.

**W.26** We may require an insured to provide proof of ownership for claimed property. Proof of ownership may include receipts, records, photographs, or other reliable evidence.

**W.27** We may consider depreciation, condition, age, obsolescence, and market value when those factors are relevant to the applicable settlement basis. We may also consider the property's use and expected remaining usefulness.

**W.28** We do not cover the cost to comply with laws, ordinances, or regulations unless that cost is specifically covered. This exclusion applies even when repair or replacement is required by a governmental authority.

**W.29** We do not cover expenses incurred to determine whether property is damaged unless those expenses are necessary to repair covered damage. An insured must obtain our approval before incurring unusual or extensive expenses.

**W.30** We may require estimates from qualified persons or entities before settling a loss. An insured must permit reasonable evaluation of the damaged property.

**W.31** A disagreement concerning the amount of loss does not create coverage. We will pay only for direct physical loss that is covered under this endorsement.

**W.32** We may adjust a settlement when information obtained after payment shows that the amount paid was not correct. An insured must return any overpayment received.

**W.33** We may recover payment from a person responsible for a covered loss to the extent permitted by law. An insured must preserve our rights of recovery after a loss.

**W.34** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense that interferes with our rights of recovery. This duty does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable action to protect property from further damage.

**W.35** We will not pay more than an insured's financial interest in covered property. Payment does not transfer an insured's interest in property unless we take the property as part of settlement.

**W.36** We may settle different items of damaged property separately. A settlement for one item does not require us to settle another item in the same manner.

**W.37** An insured must notify us promptly of additional damage discovered after a settlement. We may inspect that damage before making any additional payment.

**W.38** Our payment of a claim does not waive any term, condition, exclusion, or limitation of this endorsement. Any waiver must be made by us in writing.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** In this endorsement, "we", "us", and "our" refer to the insurer providing this coverage. "You" and "your" refer to the insured.

**W.2** "Water Damage" means direct physical loss to covered property caused by water or steam as described in this endorsement. Water Damage includes loss resulting from accidental discharge, overflow, or leakage.

**W.3** "Accidental Discharge" means the unintended release of water or steam from a contained system, appliance, fixture, or equipment. The release must be abrupt and unintended.

**W.4** "Overflow" means water or steam escaping from its intended container, system, fixture, appliance, or equipment because the container or system cannot retain it. Overflow includes water escaping from a drain, sewer, or sump.

**W.5** "Leakage" means the escape of water or steam through an unintended opening, joint, connection, seal, valve, fitting, or component. Leakage may occur within concealed or accessible portions of a covered location.

**W.6** "Seepage" means the gradual passage of water or steam through a material, opening, joint, crack, foundation, wall, floor, roof, or other surface. Seepage includes moisture that migrates into property over time.

**W.7** "Continuous or Repeated Seepage or Leakage" means seepage or leakage that occurs without interruption or recurs from the same source. The condition may result from a defect, deterioration, corrosion, wear, or failure to maintain property.

**W.8** "Plumbing System" means pipes, drains, valves, fittings, fixtures, traps, and connected equipment used to supply, distribute, heat, remove, or dispose of water or steam. A Plumbing System includes permanently installed components within the residence premises.

**W.9** "Heating System" means equipment and connected components used to generate, distribute, or control heat within the residence premises. A Heating System includes equipment using water or steam as a heating medium.

**W.10** "Air Conditioning System" means equipment and connected components used to cool, dehumidify, circulate, or control air within the residence premises. An Air Conditioning System includes condensate lines, drains, and collection components.

**W.11** "Automatic Fire Protective Sprinkler System" means permanently installed equipment designed to discharge water in response to fire or heat. The system includes connected pipes, valves, heads, and water-delivery components.

**W.12** "Household Appliance" means equipment used for domestic purposes and normally located within the residence premises. A Household Appliance includes equipment connected to a water supply, drain, or internal water reservoir.

**W.13** "Water Backup" means water or waterborne material that backs up through or overflows from a sewer, drain, sump, septic system, or similar discharge system. Water Backup includes water that cannot flow through the system as intended.

**W.14** "Sewer" means a pipe, conduit, or system used to carry sewage, waste water, storm water, or other liquid away from property. A Sewer may be publicly maintained or privately maintained.

**W.15** "Drain" means a pipe, channel, fixture, or opening designed to remove water or liquid from property. A Drain includes connected components that direct water toward a sewer, septic system, sump, or disposal system.

**W.16** "Sump" means a pit, basin, reservoir, or collection area designed to receive or collect water. A Sump includes connected pumping equipment and discharge components.

**W.17** "Sump Pump" means a pump designed to remove water from a Sump or similar collection area. A Sump Pump includes its motor, switch, float, discharge line, and related connections.

**W.18** "Surface Water" means water on the surface of the ground, including water from rain, melting snow, runoff, overflow, or drainage. Surface Water does not become part of a Plumbing System merely because it enters a drain, sewer, or sump.

**W.19** "Ground Water" means water below the surface of the ground, including water in soil, rock, underground streams, or subsurface formations. Ground Water includes water that exerts pressure on or seeps through a foundation, wall, floor, or other structure.

**W.20** "Flood" means a general and temporary condition of partial or complete inundation of normally dry land by water. Flood includes water from the overflow of inland or tidal waters, the unusual accumulation or runoff of surface water, or mudflow.

**W.21** "Mudflow" means a river of liquid and flowing mud on the surfaces of normally dry land areas. Mudflow is caused by flooding or similar conditions affecting the ground.

**W.22** "Waterborne Material" means sewage, waste, debris, contaminants, or other material carried or transported by water. Waterborne Material includes material discharged from a sewer, drain, sump, septic system, or similar system.

**W.23** "Direct Physical Loss" means accidental physical injury to tangible property. Direct Physical Loss includes loss of use only when the property has sustained physical injury.

**W.24** "Covered Property" means property for which coverage is provided under this endorsement. Covered Property must be damaged by a cause of loss to which this endorsement applies.

**W.25** "Residence Premises" means the dwelling, other structures, and premises described in the policy as the insured location. Residence Premises includes property used in connection with that location.

**W.26** "Concealed" means hidden from ordinary view by walls, ceilings, floors, cabinets, insulation, coverings, or similar construction. Property is not Concealed merely because it is difficult to reach or inspect.

**W.27** "Known" means discovered by you or by another insured. A condition is Known when its existence, nature, or effects are reasonably apparent.

**W.28** "Repair" means work necessary to restore damaged property to substantially the condition existing immediately before the direct physical loss. Repair includes replacement of damaged material when restoration is not practical.

**W.29** "Mitigation" means reasonable action taken to protect covered property from further damage after a direct physical loss. Mitigation includes removing water, drying affected property, and protecting exposed property from additional damage.

**W.30** "Emergency Services" means immediate measures necessary to address Water Damage and prevent further direct physical loss. Emergency Services include reasonable efforts to stop the release of water or steam and to protect covered property.

**W.31** "Mold" means fungi, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or other microbial matter. Mold includes spores, odors, toxins, and byproducts produced or released by microbial matter.

**W.32** "Contaminant" means any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, chemical, or other irritant or pollutant. Contaminant includes sewage, waste, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and Waterborne Material.
