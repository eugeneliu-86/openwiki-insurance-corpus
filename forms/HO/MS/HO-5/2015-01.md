# HO-5 Homeowners 5 — Comprehensive Form (Edition 2015-01)
> SUPERSEDED by HO-5 Homeowners 5 — Comprehensive Form (Edition 2022-06) for policies effective on or after 2022-06-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

We provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the premium and subject to all applicable provisions of this policy. This insurance applies only to the extent that its terms, conditions, and exclusions permit coverage.

We insure you for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a peril that is not excluded or otherwise limited by this policy. We will pay only for loss that occurs during the "policy period."

We insure an "insured" for damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" for which an insured is legally liable, subject to the terms of this policy. We will provide a defense when this policy requires us to do so.

We provide medical payments coverage for bodily injury when this policy applies. Payment under that coverage does not establish that an insured is legally liable.

Coverage under this policy is subject to all duties imposed upon an insured. An insured must comply with those duties as a condition of coverage.

We do not cover loss, damage, injury, or liability to the extent that an exclusion applies. We do not cover any amount that exceeds the applicable limit of liability.

We may investigate any "claim" or "occurrence" that may be covered under this policy. We may settle a claim when we consider settlement appropriate.

Our payment for covered loss will not exceed the amount we are obligated to pay under this policy. Payment will be made subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

Coverage applies only to an insured who has an interest in the property, loss, damage, injury, or liability for which coverage is sought. An insured may not transfer rights or duties under this policy without our written consent.

We will not be liable for any loss that is not covered by this policy. We will not be liable for any loss caused by an act or condition that is excluded from coverage.

The terms of this policy determine the rights and duties of you and us. No representation, waiver, or change is valid unless made by us in writing.

If a provision of this policy conflicts with another provision, the provision that is more specific to the coverage or circumstance will apply. All provisions will be applied according to their plain meaning.

This policy applies only within the scope of coverage provided by its terms. Your acceptance of this policy confirms your agreement to those terms.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, reduced by depreciation and obsolescence.  
It includes the value of property in its used condition.  
It does not include sentimental value or the cost of replacing property with property of greater value.  
For example, actual cash value may include a deduction for wear affecting damaged carpeting but does not include the cost of upgrading that carpeting.  

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.  
It includes the reasonable cost of materials and labor necessary to restore the damaged property.  
It does not include the cost of improving, enlarging, or changing the property.  
For example, replacement cost may include the cost to install comparable roofing but does not include the cost of adding a different roofing system.  

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.  
It includes an event that results in bodily injury or property damage during the policy period.  
It does not include an expected or intended act merely because injury or damage results from that act.  
For example, an unintended discharge of water may be an occurrence, but deliberate damage to property is not an occurrence.  

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the land and structures used in connection with that dwelling.  
It includes structures and grounds used principally for residential purposes.  
It does not include land or structures used exclusively for business purposes.  
For example, a detached garage used with the dwelling is part of the residence premises, but a separately operated commercial building is not.  

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are in your care.  
It includes a person qualifying as an insured while acting within the scope of household residency and care.  
It does not include a person who does not reside in your household merely because that person visits the residence premises.  
For example, a resident relative may be an insured, but a nonresident guest ordinarily is not an insured.  

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations used in connection with the residence premises.  
It includes a temporary residence occupied by an insured and land owned by an insured where a residence is being constructed.  
It does not include a location used exclusively for business purposes or a location not connected with the residence premises.  
For example, a temporary vacation residence may be an insured location, but a separately leased commercial office is not an insured location.  

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain.  
It includes an activity conducted from the residence premises when it is regularly undertaken for compensation.  
It does not include an activity that is ordinarily noncommercial and is not undertaken for compensation.  
For example, operating a retail enterprise is business, but an unpaid personal hobby is not business.  

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or otherwise subject to motor vehicle registration.  
It includes vehicles designed for off-road recreational use when operated on public roads.  
It does not include a device designed solely to assist the mobility of a person with a physical disability.  
For example, an automobile is a motor vehicle, but a manually propelled wheelchair is not a motor vehicle.  

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises.  
It includes a person performing household or domestic services for an insured.  
It does not include an independent contractor or a person performing services in connection with business.  
For example, a household caretaker may be a residence employee, but a contractor hired to operate a commercial enterprise is not a residence employee.  

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person.  
It includes resulting death.  
It does not include emotional distress or mental anguish that does not arise from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.  
For example, a broken arm is bodily injury, but disappointment without physical harm is not bodily injury.  

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.  
It includes loss of use resulting from physical injury to tangible property.  
It does not include loss of use of property that has not suffered physical injury unless otherwise provided by this policy.  
For example, damage to a wall is property damage, but a reduction in market value without physical injury is not property damage.  

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt and unintended, arising from an unexpected event.  
It includes an unforeseen discharge, release, or escape that occurs abruptly.  
It does not include seepage, leakage, deterioration, or wear that develops gradually.  
For example, an unexpected pipe burst may be sudden and accidental, but persistent moisture from a slow leak is not sudden and accidental.  

**DEF.13** "vacant" means a dwelling that lacks sufficient personal property to permit normal habitation.  
It includes a dwelling from which furnishings and other property necessary for customary residential use have been removed.  
It does not include a dwelling that remains furnished for normal use during a temporary absence.  
For example, an unfurnished dwelling held without customary household property may be vacant, but a furnished dwelling left while you travel is not vacant.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the "dwelling" used principally as a private residence. The dwelling includes structures attached to it and fixtures that are permanently installed. We also cover materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises when they are intended for use in constructing, altering, or repairing the dwelling.

**A.2** We cover additions, alterations, and improvements that you own or that you are legally responsible for. We cover these items when they are located on the residence premises. We do not cover property that is not intended to remain part of the dwelling.

**A.3** We cover permanently installed appliances and equipment that serve the dwelling. This includes equipment used for heating, cooling, plumbing, electrical service, or sanitation. We do not cover equipment used solely for a business conducted on the residence premises.

**A.4** We cover outdoor equipment that is permanently attached to the dwelling. This includes antennas, satellite dishes, and related wiring. We do not cover the equipment when it is used primarily for commercial communication or commercial broadcasting.

**A.5** We cover building glass that is part of the dwelling. We cover the cost to remove and replace the glass when necessary to repair covered damage. We do not cover damage caused solely by wear, deterioration, or faulty installation.

**A.6** We cover wall-to-wall carpeting that is permanently installed in the dwelling. We cover similar floor coverings that are permanently attached to the dwelling. We do not cover rugs or floor coverings that are not permanently installed.

**A.7** We cover structures connected to the dwelling by a common wall, foundation, roof, or utility service. We cover the connected structure as part of the dwelling when it is used for residential purposes. We do not cover a structure used primarily for a business unless the coverage is otherwise provided by this policy.

**A.8** We cover a detached structure when it is connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. We cover the detached structure under Coverage A when it is used as part of the dwelling. We do not cover a detached structure that is separately insured under this policy.

**A.9** We cover construction in progress at the residence premises. We cover building materials that you own or that you are legally responsible for when they are intended for the construction. We do not cover materials that are intended for a separate property or project.

**A.10** We cover property that is part of the dwelling and that is temporarily removed for repair, renovation, or cleaning. We cover the property while it is at the residence premises or at a location where the work is being performed. We do not cover property that is permanently removed from the dwelling.

**A.11** We cover land improvements that are permanently attached to the dwelling. This includes walkways, patios, and similar improvements that are part of the dwelling or serve as an integral part of it. We do not cover landscaping, soil, or growing vegetation under Coverage A.

**A.12** We cover foundations, footings, retaining walls, and underground piping that are part of the dwelling. We cover these items when they support or provide service to the dwelling. We do not cover underground property that is not connected with the dwelling.

**A.13** We cover fuel tanks and related equipment that are permanently installed to serve the dwelling. We cover the equipment when it is located on the residence premises. We do not cover fuel or other contents of the tanks.

**A.14** We cover permanently installed fire protection and security systems that serve the dwelling. We cover related wiring, controls, and fixtures that you own or are legally responsible for. We do not cover monitoring services or charges imposed by a service provider.

**A.15** We cover renewable energy equipment that is permanently installed on the dwelling. We cover the equipment when it is used to provide service to the dwelling. We do not cover energy that is generated for sale or commercial distribution.

**A.16** We cover a dwelling that you acquire during the policy term when you notify us of the acquisition. The acquired dwelling is covered when it becomes your residence premises. We do not cover property that you acquire primarily for sale or investment.

**A.17** We cover a dwelling that you are constructing for your occupancy. We cover the dwelling while construction continues at the residence premises. We do not cover a dwelling constructed for another person or entity.

**A.18** We cover alterations and repairs that you make to the dwelling. We cover damage to the work and to materials used in the work when caused by a covered loss. We do not cover the cost to correct defective workmanship or defective materials.

**A.19** We cover the reasonable cost to protect the dwelling from further damage after a covered loss. We cover temporary repairs that are necessary to prevent additional covered damage. We do not cover permanent repairs that are not otherwise covered under this policy.

**A.20** We cover debris removal when covered damage to the dwelling produces debris. We cover reasonable expenses that you incur to remove the debris from the residence premises. We do not cover removal of property that was not damaged by a covered cause of loss.

**A.21** We cover reasonable expenses required to comply with an ordinance or law when covered damage requires repair or replacement of the dwelling. We cover the increased cost of construction or demolition when the expense is required by the enforcing authority. We do not cover an expense arising solely from an ordinance or law that is enforced because of an excluded condition.

**A.22** We cover the increased cost to repair or replace undamaged portions of the dwelling when an ordinance or law requires their removal. We cover the undamaged portions when their removal is required because of covered damage. We do not cover undamaged portions that are not required to be removed.

**A.23** We cover the reasonable cost to replace damaged parts of the dwelling with property of like kind and quality. We may use materials and methods that are customary for the locality. We do not pay for improvements or enhancements that are not required to restore the damaged dwelling.

**A.24** We settle covered damage to the dwelling at replacement cost when the property satisfies an insured-to-value threshold of eighty percent. Except that, when the required valuation is not maintained, we settle the covered loss under the applicable loss settlement basis. We do not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace the damaged property.

**A.25** We may settle covered damage by repairing, replacing, or paying the value of the damaged property. We may select materials and contractors that are reasonably suitable for the repair. We do not have to replace undamaged property to create a uniform appearance.

**A.26** We cover damage to the dwelling caused by a covered cause of loss. We cover the resulting physical loss when the damage is accidental and direct. We do not cover loss that consists of or results from an excluded cause of loss.

**A.27** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. This exclusion applies whether the movement is natural or caused by human activity. We do not apply this exclusion to ensuing loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**A.28** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through a sewer or drain. This exclusion applies to water that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. We do not apply this exclusion to ensuing loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**A.29** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or not. We do not apply this exclusion to ensuing loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**A.30** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies when the seepage or leakage occurs over a period of time. We do not apply this exclusion to sudden and accidental discharge that causes covered damage.

**A.31** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air-conditioning, or household appliances. This exclusion applies when you fail to maintain heat in the dwelling. We do not apply this exclusion when you shut off the water supply and drain the systems.

**A.32** We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. This exclusion applies to the affected property and to property damaged by the excluded condition. We do not apply this exclusion to ensuing loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**A.33** We do not cover loss caused by insects, birds, rodents, or other animals. This exclusion applies to nesting, infestation, and damage caused by their waste or secretions. We do not apply this exclusion to ensuing loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**A.34** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or latent defect. This exclusion applies to a quality or condition that causes the property to fail or require repair. We do not apply this exclusion to ensuing loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**A.35** We cover the dwelling only while you comply with the duties that apply after a loss. You must protect the dwelling from further damage, keep records of expenses, and cooperate with our investigation. We may reduce payment for damage that results from your failure to perform these duties.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We provide "Coverage B" for direct physical loss to property described as "other structures" when that property is located on the "residence premises". We cover the structures under the terms of this policy. The property must be used in connection with the residence.

**B.2** Other structures must be separated from the dwelling by clear space. A structure connected to the dwelling by only a fence, utility line, or similar connection is considered separated. We cover the structure when the connection does not make it part of the dwelling.

**B.3** The "Coverage B" limit is ten percent of the "Coverage A" limit. except that the Coverage B limit does not increase because the cost to repair or replace an other structure increases. This limit applies regardless of the number of covered other structures involved in a loss.

**B.4** We cover an other structure that is permanently installed on the residence premises. The structure must be designed for use with the residence. We do not require the structure to be occupied when the loss occurs.

**B.5** We cover detached garages, storage buildings, sheds, and similar structures. The structure must be located on the residence premises. We cover fixtures and permanently installed equipment that are part of the structure.

**B.6** We cover a structure that is connected to the dwelling by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. The structure is covered as an other structure when it remains separated from the dwelling by clear space. We do not cover a structure that has become part of the dwelling.

**B.7** We cover an other structure used for household purposes. The structure may be used for storage, recreation, maintenance, or another use related to the residence. We do not cover a structure used primarily for a commercial purpose.

**B.8** We cover an other structure while it is being repaired, renovated, or maintained. Materials and equipment intended to become part of the structure are covered while located on the residence premises. The work must be performed in a manner consistent with the structure's intended use.

**B.9** We cover permanently attached fixtures serving an other structure. We cover plumbing, wiring, heating, and similar installations that are part of the structure. We do not cover items that can be removed without material damage to the structure.

**B.10** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We also do not cover the cost of improving, stabilizing, or replacing land. This exclusion applies even when damage to the land results from a covered loss to an other structure.

**B.11** We do not cover an other structure used in whole or in part for business purposes. This exclusion applies to a structure used for the conduct, storage, or administration of a business. We cover incidental personal use that does not change the primary use of the structure.

**B.12** We do not cover an other structure rented or held for rental to a person who is not a tenant of the dwelling. This exclusion applies whether the rental is written, oral, temporary, or continuing. We cover a private garage rented to a person who is not a tenant of the dwelling when the garage is used solely for private purposes.

**B.13** We do not cover an other structure used to store business property. This exclusion applies when the structure is used primarily for that storage. We cover property used for a business when the storage is incidental to the personal use of the structure.

**B.14** We do not cover an other structure used to house animals raised for commercial purposes. We cover a structure used for household pets or personal domestic animals. The use must not be connected with a business or farming operation.

**B.15** We cover an other structure that is vacant or unoccupied. The structure must remain reasonably maintained and secured. We do not cover loss caused by neglect of the structure.

**B.16** We cover an other structure that is temporarily removed from its usual location for repair or maintenance. The structure must remain on the residence premises or at a location where the repair or maintenance is being performed. We do not cover loss that occurs while the structure is being transported.

**B.17** We cover debris from a covered loss to an other structure. We cover the reasonable expense to remove the debris when the expense is incurred by you. We do not cover the cost to remove property that is not covered under this policy.

**B.18** We cover reasonable repairs made to protect an other structure from further damage after a covered loss. The repairs must be necessary and related to the covered loss. You must keep records of the repairs and provide them to us when we request them.

**B.19** An insured must protect an other structure from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs when required to prevent further damage. We do not pay for repairs that are unnecessary or unrelated to the covered loss.

**B.20** An insured must promptly notify us of a loss to an other structure. The notice must describe the property involved and the circumstances of the loss. An insured must provide additional information when we reasonably request it.

**B.21** An insured must preserve the damaged property for our inspection. An insured must not dispose of damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. We may take possession of damaged property after a covered loss.

**B.22** An insured must provide records and documents that relate to a loss to an other structure. The records must support the amount and cause of the claimed loss. We may examine an insured under oath when reasonably necessary to investigate the loss.

**B.23** We may pay the cost to repair or replace an other structure with property of like kind and quality. We may pay the value of the damaged property when repair or replacement is not completed. Payment is subject to the applicable limit and the terms of this policy.

**B.24** We may settle a covered loss to an other structure by paying the amount necessary to repair the damage. We may use materials of comparable quality and may select a qualified contractor. We do not pay for an improvement or betterment beyond what is necessary to restore the damaged structure.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. We cover the property only to the extent that it is described in this Coverage C provision. Property that is not personal property is not covered under this provision.

**C.2** The limit of liability for Coverage C is fifty percent of the Coverage A limit. This limit applies to the total amount of insurance available for covered personal property. The limit is subject to all other provisions that apply to Coverage C.

**C.3** We cover personal property that is located at the residence premises. We cover the property while it is temporarily elsewhere. We do not cover property that is permanently located at another residence unless this provision provides coverage for it.

**C.4** We cover personal property owned by an insured. We cover property used by an insured if the property is in the care of an insured. We do not cover property owned by or rented to a tenant unless the property is otherwise covered by this provision.

**C.5** We cover personal property of a guest or residence employee while the property is at the residence premises. We cover the property only when an insured is legally responsible for the property. We do not cover property that is insured under another policy issued to its owner.

**C.6** We cover personal property of a student who is an insured and who is temporarily residing away from the residence premises. We cover the property while it is in the student's possession. We do not cover property that is kept at a separate residence occupied by the student and maintained as a permanent household.

**C.7** We cover personal property that is newly acquired by an insured. We cover the property when it is intended for personal, family, or household use. We do not cover property acquired primarily for business, professional, or commercial use.

**C.8** We cover personal property while it is being moved to a new residence. We cover the property while it is in transit or temporarily stored in connection with the move. We do not cover loss caused by an intentional act to damage the property during the move.

**C.9** We cover personal property in a building that is newly occupied by an insured. We cover the property when the building is intended to become the residence premises. We do not cover property located in a building that an insured does not intend to occupy as a residence.

**C.10** We cover personal property in a self-storage facility. We cover the property while it is temporarily stored away from the residence premises. We do not cover property held for sale, delivery, or exhibition.

**C.11** We cover clothing, luggage, and personal effects. We cover these items when they are owned or used by an insured. We do not cover animals, birds, or fish under this provision.

**C.12** We cover household furniture and furnishings. We cover these items when they are used to furnish the residence premises. We do not cover fixtures or building materials that are permanently attached to the residence premises.

**C.13** We cover appliances and equipment used for personal, family, or household purposes. We cover the property whether it is located inside or outside a building at the residence premises. We do not cover equipment used primarily in a business.

**C.14** We cover lawn and garden equipment. We cover the equipment while it is used in connection with the residence premises. We do not cover equipment held for sale or used in a commercial landscaping operation.

**C.15** We cover tools and instruments used for personal activities. We cover the property when it is used by an insured for a hobby or other nonbusiness purpose. We do not cover tools and instruments used primarily to earn income.

**C.16** We cover personal property used for entertainment. We cover audio, visual, and electronic equipment used for personal purposes. We do not cover electronic data except to the extent that the data is incorporated in covered media.

**C.17** We cover records, manuscripts, and other documents. We cover the cost of blank materials and the reasonable expense of reproducing the property. We do not cover the value of information that cannot be reproduced.

**C.18** We cover books and printed material. We cover the property when it is owned for personal, family, or household use. We do not cover property held as inventory for sale.

**C.19** We cover money and precious metals. The most we will pay for loss of money and precious metals is three hundred dollars. Except that this special limit applies regardless of the location of the property, it does not increase the Coverage C limit.

**C.20** We cover jewelry, watches, and precious stones. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of jewelry, watches, and precious stones is two thousand five hundred dollars. Subject to all other applicable provisions, this special limit applies to the total loss of such property caused by theft.

**C.21** We cover firearms and related equipment. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of firearms is three thousand dollars. Except that this special limit applies to firearms wherever located, it does not apply to loss caused by a peril other than theft.

**C.22** We cover watercraft and their trailers. The most we will pay for loss of watercraft, including trailers, is one thousand dollars. Subject to the applicable valuation provision, this special limit applies to the combined loss of the watercraft and its trailer.

**C.23** We cover silverware, goldware, and pewterware. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of silverware, goldware, and pewterware is five thousand dollars. Unless the property is lost by theft, this special limit does not apply.

**C.24** We cover business property at the residence premises. The most we will pay for business property at the residence premises is two thousand five hundred dollars. Except that the special limit applies to property used in a business, it does not apply to property used solely for personal, family, or household purposes.

**C.25** We cover electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle. The most we will pay for electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle is one thousand dollars. Subject to the applicable provisions for motor vehicles, this special limit applies when the apparatus is powered by the vehicle's electrical system or is designed for use in the vehicle.

**C.26** We cover personal property that is separately described and specifically insured under another policy. We cover the property only to the extent that this provision applies after other insurance. We do not cover an amount that another insurer is obligated to pay.

**C.27** We cover personal property that is in the possession of an insured. We cover the property when an insured is exercising control over it for personal, family, or household purposes. We do not cover property that an insured possesses unlawfully.

**C.28** We cover personal property that is being repaired, renovated, or cleaned. We cover the property while it is temporarily in the custody of a person performing the work. We do not cover loss caused by faulty workmanship or defective materials.

**C.29** We cover personal property that is being transported by a common carrier. We cover the property while it is in transit to or from the residence premises. We do not cover loss that is paid by the carrier.

**C.30** We cover personal property that is outside the residence premises. We cover the property when it is temporarily away for personal, family, or household use. We do not cover property that is permanently located at a place other than the residence premises unless this provision otherwise provides coverage.

**C.31** We cover personal property in a hotel, motel, or similar temporary lodging. We cover the property while an insured is temporarily residing there. We do not cover property in a lodging location that an insured occupies as a permanent residence.

**C.32** We cover personal property in a college, school, or similar residence. We cover the property when the occupant is an insured. We do not cover property used in a business conducted from that location.

**C.33** We cover personal property in a bank vault or safe-deposit facility. We cover the property when an insured owns or uses it. We do not cover property that is not owned or used by an insured.

**C.34** We cover personal property in a garage, shed, or other structure at the residence premises. We cover the property when it is used in connection with the residence premises. We do not cover a structure itself under this Coverage C provision.

**C.35** We cover personal property in the open. We cover the property when it is at the residence premises. We do not cover loss caused by weather conditions when the property was left in the open in disregard of reasonable protection.

**C.36** We cover personal property that is temporarily attached to a motor vehicle. We cover the property when it is designed for personal, family, or household use. We do not cover the motor vehicle or its permanently installed equipment under this provision.

**C.37** We cover personal property used with a watercraft. We cover the property when it is owned or used by an insured. We do not cover the watercraft itself except as provided for watercraft under this provision.

**C.38** We cover personal property used with a recreational vehicle. We cover the property when it is separate from the recreational vehicle. We do not cover the recreational vehicle itself under this Coverage C provision.

**C.39** We cover personal property used in a hobby. We cover the property when the hobby is conducted for personal enjoyment. We do not cover property used to produce goods for sale as a business activity.

**C.40** We cover personal property used in a charitable or volunteer activity. We cover the property when an insured is using it without compensation. We do not cover property for which an insured is legally responsible solely because of a business activity.

**C.41** We cover personal property that is gifted to an insured. We cover the property when an insured has accepted the gift. We do not cover property that remains in the possession and control of the donor.

**C.42** We cover personal property that is inherited by an insured. We cover the property when an insured has an ownership interest in it. We do not cover property that is held solely for distribution to another person.

**C.43** We cover personal property that is jointly owned by an insured and another person. We cover the insured's interest in the property. We do not cover an interest that belongs solely to another person.

**C.44** We cover personal property that is leased by an insured for personal use. We cover the property when an insured is legally responsible for direct physical loss to it. We do not cover amounts that an insured is required to pay solely because of a contractual penalty.

**C.45** We cover personal property that is borrowed by an insured. We cover the property when an insured is legally responsible for its loss. We do not cover property borrowed for business, professional, or commercial use.

**C.46** We cover personal property that is entrusted to an insured. We cover the property when the entrustment is for personal, family, or household purposes. We do not cover property entrusted to an insured for sale or commercial service.

**C.47** We cover personal property that is intended for use by a child of an insured. We cover the property when the child is a member of the insured's household. We do not cover property that is maintained solely at another household.

**C.48** We cover personal property that is intended for use by a domestic worker. We cover the property when it is located at the residence premises. We do not cover property belonging to the domestic worker when an insured is not legally responsible for it.

**C.49** We cover personal property that is used by an insured during travel. We cover the property while it is in the insured's possession or in temporary storage. We do not cover property that is permanently stored at a travel destination.

**C.50** We cover personal property that is shipped by an insured. We cover the property when it is shipped for personal, family, or household purposes. We do not cover the property when it is shipped as merchandise or commercial inventory.

**C.51** We cover personal property that is displayed at a private event. We cover the property when the event is not conducted as a business. We do not cover property displayed for sale or promotional purposes.

**C.52** We cover personal property used for a private ceremony or gathering. We cover the property when an insured owns or uses it. We do not cover property rented to others as part of a business.

**C.53** We cover personal property that is temporarily loaned to another person. We cover the property when the loan is for personal, family, or household use. We do not cover loss for which the borrower is legally responsible and able to pay.

**C.54** We cover personal property that is temporarily left in the care of a repair facility. We cover the property while the facility is performing authorized work. We do not cover loss caused by an intentional act of the person having custody of the property.

**C.55** We cover personal property that is temporarily located in a vacant residence. We cover the property when an insured is moving into or out of the residence. We do not cover property left there after the residence has ceased to be used for the insured's personal purposes.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** We provide "Coverage D" for the loss described in this section when a covered loss makes the "Residence Premises" uninhabitable. The most we will pay under Coverage D is twenty percent of the "Coverage A" limit. Subject to the terms of this policy, we pay only for loss or expense that results directly from the covered loss.

**D.2** "Additional Living Expense" means the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you to maintain your household's normal standard of living. You must incur the expense because the Residence Premises is uninhabitable. You must provide records that support the amount of Additional Living Expense claimed.

**D.3** We determine whether the Residence Premises is uninhabitable by considering the part of the premises affected by the covered loss. We may consider whether the affected part can reasonably be used for its intended purpose. We do not require you to occupy premises that present an unreasonable risk to your health or safety.

You must continue to use the habitable part of the Residence Premises when reasonably possible. **D.7** We pay Additional Living Expense for a household member who cannot reasonably remain at the Residence Premises because of the covered loss. You must use lodging and services that are reasonably suitable for the household member's needs. We do not pay for a preference for more expensive lodging or services.

**D.9** We pay reasonable transportation expense when the covered loss requires you to travel farther to obtain customary household services. You must use reasonable transportation in light of the circumstances. We do not pay transportation expense that arises from your personal preference.

**D.10** We pay reasonable storage expense for personal property removed from the Residence Premises because of the covered loss. **D.11** We pay reasonable moving expense when the covered loss requires you to move personal property from the Residence Premises. You must take reasonable steps to prevent damage during the move. We do not pay moving expense caused by a voluntary change in your residence.

**D.12** We pay reasonable expenses required to establish temporary living arrangements after a covered loss. You must notify us of the arrangements and provide supporting records. We do not pay expenses that are not necessary to maintain your household's normal standard of living.

**D.26** You must take reasonable steps to avoid or reduce a loss of use. You must promptly report conditions that increase the loss of use. We do not pay an increase in loss of use caused by your unreasonable failure to act.

**D.27** We do not cover expenses arising from your decision to improve, enlarge, or remodel the Residence Premises. We do not cover expenses arising from a change in your household's preferences. We do not cover expenses that are not caused by the covered loss.

**D.30** You must submit a claim for Coverage D with a description of the loss of use and the expenses incurred. You must cooperate with our investigation and settlement of the claim. We pay only the covered loss of use that is established by the information and records available to us.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure against risk of direct physical loss to property described in this policy, unless the loss is excluded or limited by this policy. Loss must be caused by a fortuitous event that occurs during the policy period.

**P.2** We cover direct physical loss only when the property is physically altered, damaged, or destroyed. We do not cover diminution in value, loss of use, loss of market, or loss of opportunity unless expressly covered.

**P.3** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or faulty material.

**P.4** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do cover ensuing direct physical loss caused by a covered peril if the ensuing loss is not otherwise excluded.

**P.5** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, or electrolysis. We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from agricultural smudging, or atmospheric contamination.

**P.6** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril if the mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria did not cause the covered peril.

**P.7** We do not cover loss caused by contamination, including the presence of pollutants. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril if the contamination did not cause the covered peril.

**P.8** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire, explosion, or vandalism resulting from such activity, unless otherwise excluded.

**P.9** We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, discharge, or release by animals or insects. We do not cover the cost to remove animals, insects, nests, or related materials.

**P.10** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies whether the seepage or leakage occurs from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler equipment, or a household appliance.

**P.11** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated leakage from within a plumbing system. We do cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam if the discharge or overflow is sudden and accidental.

**P.12** We do not cover loss caused by neglect. An insured must use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss.

**P.13** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an insured. This exclusion applies even if another insured did not participate in, know of, or consent to the conduct.

**P.14** We do not cover loss caused by an act or decision of a governmental body. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril resulting from an act or decision of a governmental body.

**P.15** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We do cover ensuing direct physical loss caused by a covered peril if the ensuing loss is not otherwise excluded.

**P.16** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, mudflow, earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**P.17** Earth movement includes movement caused by natural or human forces. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire, explosion, or theft resulting from earth movement, unless otherwise excluded.

**P.18** We do not cover loss caused by water damage arising from flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or otherwise.

**P.19** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sumps, sump pumps, or related equipment. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows or is discharged from such equipment.

**P.20** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, sidewalk, driveway, swimming pool, or other structure.

**P.21** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. Pollutants include solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, chemical, and radioactive irritants or contaminants.

**P.22** We do not cover loss arising out of war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, and military action by a military force.

**P.23** We do not cover loss arising out of seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by order of a governmental authority. We do cover loss caused by fire resulting from such action, unless otherwise excluded.

**P.24** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, and any consequence of these.

**P.25** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the insured location. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril resulting from the power failure, unless otherwise excluded.

**P.26** We do not cover loss caused by failure of a utility service, communication service, or internet service. This exclusion applies whether the failure results from interruption, fluctuation, surge, or outage.

**P.27** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, demolition, remodeling, or use of property. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril before application of the ordinance or law.

**P.28** We do not cover the increased cost to repair or replace property because of an ordinance or law. We do not cover loss of value resulting from an ordinance or law.

**P.29** We do not cover loss caused by collapse, except as provided in this policy. Collapse means an abrupt falling down or caving in of a building or part of a building.

**P.30** A building or part of a building that is in danger of falling down or caving in is not in a state of collapse. A building or part of a building that remains standing is not in a state of collapse.

**P.31** We cover direct physical loss caused by collapse when collapse is caused by hidden decay. We cover direct physical loss caused by collapse when collapse is caused by hidden insect or vermin damage.

**P.32** We cover direct physical loss caused by collapse when collapse is caused by weight of contents, equipment, animals, or people. We cover direct physical loss caused by collapse when collapse is caused by weight of rain collecting on a roof.

**P.33** We cover direct physical loss caused by collapse when collapse is caused by use of defective material or methods in construction, remodeling, or renovation. Coverage applies only if collapse occurs during the course of the construction, remodeling, or renovation.

## II.E — Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** We will pay damages for which an “insured” is legally liable because of “bodily injury” or “property damage” caused by an “occurrence” to which this coverage applies. The occurrence must take place during the policy period.

**E.2** “Bodily injury” means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death. Bodily injury includes required care, loss of services, and resulting damages.

**E.3** “Property damage” means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use. Property damage also includes loss of use of tangible property that is not physically injured.

**E.4** “Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence must result in bodily injury or property damage.

**S.16** An insured must notify us promptly if the insured becomes aware of facts that materially change the nature of a claim or suit. This includes information concerning additional claimants, injuries, damage, or allegations of wrongdoing.

**S.17** We may investigate an occurrence and settle a claim as we consider appropriate. Our right to investigate or settle does not create an obligation to investigate or settle every claim.

**S.18** We may defend an insured against a suit seeking damages for "bodily injury" or "property damage" to which this insurance applies. We may make any investigation and settlement we consider appropriate.

**S.19** Our duty to defend ends when we have used the applicable limit of liability in the payment of judgments or settlements. The exhaustion of a limit does not affect duties that arose before the limit was exhausted unless this policy provides otherwise.

**S.20** An insured must not bring a legal action against us unless the insured has complied with all policy conditions. An insured may not bring an action against us concerning a claim until the amount of the insured's obligation has been determined by judgment after trial or by a written agreement signed by the insured, the claimant, and us.

**S.21** No person or organization has a right under this policy to join us as a party to an action against an insured. No person or organization may otherwise bring us into an action against an insured except as permitted by applicable law.

**S.22** The applicable limit of liability is the most we will pay for damages resulting from an occurrence. The limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claimants, claims made, or persons injured.

**S.23** Payments made under this policy do not increase the applicable limit of liability. The applicable limit is not reduced by expenses we incur in investigating or defending a claim unless this policy expressly provides otherwise.

**S.24** This insurance applies separately to each insured against whom a claim is made or a suit is brought. This provision does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**S.25** If other valid and collectible insurance applies to a loss covered by this policy, we will pay our share of the loss. Our share is determined by the applicable terms of this policy and the other insurance.

**S.26** This insurance is excess over other insurance that is available to an insured for a loss arising from the use of property not owned by an insured. This insurance is also excess when another insurance contract is written specifically to cover the same loss.

**S.27** We will not pay more than the amount of loss that remains after the application of other insurance. We may contribute with other insurers according to the applicable terms of their policies and this policy.

**S.28** If we make a payment under this policy, we are subrogated to the rights of the person receiving the payment against any person or organization responsible for the loss. That person must do everything necessary to preserve those rights and must do nothing after a loss to impair them.

**S.29** An insured may waive rights of recovery against another person or organization before an occurrence. An insured may not waive rights of recovery after an occurrence without our written consent.

**S.30** Any recovery obtained by an insured after we make a payment for a loss must be held for us to the extent of our payment. An insured must reimburse us from that recovery after deducting reasonable expenses incurred to obtain it.

**S.31** We may recover payments made by us from any person or organization legally responsible for the loss. Our recovery rights do not apply against an insured under this policy unless the loss resulted from an intentional act of that insured.

**S.32** An assignment of this policy is not valid unless we give our written consent. An assignment of a claim after a loss does not affect our rights or defenses under this policy.

**S.33** A waiver or change of a provision of this policy is valid only if made in writing by us. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision later.

**S.34** A change in this policy must be made by a written endorsement issued by us. A representative may not change this policy or waive a provision unless authorized by us in writing.

**S.35** We may inspect the "residence premises" and other property described in this policy at reasonable times. Our inspection does not constitute a warranty that the property is safe, complies with law, or is suitable for any purpose.

**S.36** We may make recommendations based on an inspection. An insured is not relieved of responsibility for maintaining property in a safe condition by reason of an inspection or recommendation.

**S.37** This policy does not give any person or organization a right to recover under this policy unless that person or organization is an insured or is otherwise entitled to payment under its terms. A person or organization that receives services from an insured does not become an insured solely because of that relationship.

**S.38** Notice to us must be given through a method that reasonably identifies the policy and the insured providing notice. Notice is effective when received by us or by our authorized representative.

**S.39** An insured must provide truthful and complete information in connection with an application, claim, occurrence, or suit. An insured must correct information previously provided if the insured learns that the information is materially inaccurate or incomplete.

**S.40** We may request information from an insured concerning the ownership or control of property involved in an occurrence. An insured must provide available information concerning persons or organizations that may be responsible for the loss.

**S.41** If an insured dies, coverage will apply to the legal representative of that insured for duties and liabilities arising before the insured's death. Coverage will also apply to a person having proper temporary custody of property of the deceased insured, but only with respect to that property.

**S.42** If this policy is broadened without additional premium during the policy term, the broadened coverage will apply to this policy. This condition applies only if the broadened coverage is available generally to policies of this type.

**S.43** The terms of this policy may be enforced only to the extent permitted by applicable law. If any provision of this policy is determined to be invalid, the remaining provisions will remain in effect.

## G — Sections I and II — Conditions

**G.1** This policy applies only to an "insured" who has an insurable interest in covered property at the time of loss. We will not pay more than your interest in the covered property.

**G.2** The "policy period" begins and ends as shown in the policy declarations. Coverage applies only to loss occurring during the policy period.

**G.3** The "policy territory" is the territory described in the policy declarations. We do not cover loss occurring outside the policy territory.

**G.4** An insured must maintain the information shown in the policy declarations accurately. An insured must notify us promptly of a material change affecting the risk.

**G.5** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide information and records we reasonably request.

**G.6** An insured must permit us to inspect the insured location and covered property at reasonable times. Our inspection does not constitute a warranty that property is safe or complies with law.

**G.7** An insured must preserve all rights of recovery against a person who may be responsible for a loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**G.8** If we make a payment under this policy, we may require an assignment of rights of recovery to the extent of our payment. An insured must execute documents reasonably necessary to preserve those rights.

**G.9** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except at the insured's own cost. This condition does not apply to reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property from further loss.

**G.10** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We do not cover additional loss resulting from an insured's failure to protect the property.

**G.11** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and emergency measures following a loss. An insured must provide those records to us upon request.

**G.12** An insured must promptly report to us a loss that may be covered under this policy. An insured must also notify the appropriate authorities when the loss involves theft, vandalism, or another unlawful act.

**G.13** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to take samples, photographs, measurements, and other evidence reasonably necessary to evaluate the loss.

**G.14** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged or stolen personal property. The inventory must identify the property, describe its condition, and state its value and amount of loss.

**G.15** An insured must attach available bills, receipts, appraisals, photographs, and related documents to the inventory. An insured must provide a copy of the inventory to us upon request.

**G.16** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require. An insured must sign and provide a transcript of the examination when it is prepared.

**G.17** An insured must provide records and documents we reasonably request and permit us to make copies. An insured must obtain records from other persons or organizations when those records are reasonably available to the insured.

**G.18** An insured must provide a signed, sworn statement of loss when we request it. The statement must include information reasonably necessary to establish the cause, amount, and ownership of the loss.

**G.19** An insured must identify all persons having an interest in damaged property. An insured must state any encumbrance, lien, or other claim against that property.

**G.20** An insured must notify us promptly when property that was lost or stolen is recovered. We may return recovered property to an insured or retain it as permitted by law.

**G.21** An insured must submit to a medical examination when we reasonably require and when permitted by law. We will select the examining physician and pay the examination expense.

**G.22** An insured seeking coverage for bodily injury must authorize us to obtain medical records that are reasonably related to the claimed injury. The authorization must be valid and enforceable to the extent permitted by law.

**G.23** An insured must forward to us promptly every notice, demand, summons, complaint, or legal paper relating to an occurrence or claim. An insured must not admit liability or settle a claim without our consent.

**G.24** An insured must assist us in securing evidence, attending hearings, and obtaining witnesses when we reasonably request. We may require an insured's attendance at a proceeding relating to a claim.

**G.25** No action may be brought against us unless there has been compliance with all policy provisions. No action may be brought against us unless it is commenced within the time permitted by law.

**G.26** If an insured and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal. Each party will select a competent appraiser and will notify the other of the appraiser selected.

**G.27** The appraisers will select a competent and impartial umpire. If the appraisers cannot agree on an umpire, either may request that a court having jurisdiction appoint an umpire.

**G.28** Each party will pay its selected appraiser and bear its own expenses. The parties will share the expense of the umpire and other appraisal expenses equally.

**G.29** An agreement by any appraisers or an agreement by an appraiser and the umpire will set the amount of loss. An appraisal decision does not determine coverage or waive a policy condition.

**G.30** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may take this action after giving an insured notice of our intention.

**G.31** We may require an insured to transfer damaged property to us when we pay for its replacement or repair. We will pay the applicable value of property that we retain.

**G.32** If there is other insurance covering a loss, we will pay our share as determined by this policy and applicable law. An insured must disclose all other insurance that may apply to the loss.

**G.33** If more than one policy issued by us applies to the same loss, we will not pay more than the amount payable under the policy providing the broadest coverage. This condition does not increase any limit of liability.

**G.34** A person or organization holding an interest in covered property must provide information we reasonably request concerning that interest. Payment to that person or organization does not increase our limit of liability.

**G.35** If an insured dies, coverage will continue for a legal representative of the deceased insured with respect to the premises and property of the deceased insured. Coverage will also continue for a member of the insured's household who was an insured at the time of death.

**G.36** A legal representative must comply with all policy duties that apply to an insured. We may deal with a legal representative regarding any claim arising after the death of an insured.

**G.37** An assignment of this policy is not valid unless we give written consent. An assignment of a claim after loss is subject to applicable law.

**G.38** A waiver or change of a policy provision must be in writing and signed by us. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive that provision.

**G.39** A statement made by an agent or other person does not change this policy unless the change is made by written endorsement. Knowledge held by an agent or other person does not waive a policy provision.

**G.40** If we adopt a revision that broadens coverage without additional premium, the broadened coverage will apply to this policy when permitted by law. The revision must apply generally to policies of this type issued by us.

**G.41** We do not cover a loss if an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance relating to this insurance. We do not cover a loss if an insured has engaged in fraud or made a false statement relating to this insurance.

**G.42** Concealment, misrepresentation, fraud, or a false statement by an insured may void coverage for all insureds to the extent permitted by law. We may deny coverage when the conduct is material to the risk or claim.

**G.43** We may cancel this policy as permitted by law. We will deliver or mail notice of cancellation to the named insured at the mailing address shown in the policy declarations.

**G.44** We may cancel this policy for nonpayment of premium. Notice of cancellation for nonpayment of premium will be mailed or delivered at least ten days before cancellation takes effect.

**G.45** We may cancel this policy for a reason other than nonpayment of premium as permitted by law. Notice of cancellation for a reason other than nonpayment of premium will be mailed or delivered at least thirty days before cancellation takes effect.

**G.46** A cancellation notice will state the effective date of cancellation and the reason for cancellation when required by law. Cancellation is effective even if the named insured does not receive the notice, if we have complied with applicable law.

**G.47** If this policy is cancelled, we will refund any unearned premium as required by law. Payment or tender of unearned premium is not a condition of cancellation.

**G.48** We may elect not to renew this policy as permitted by law. We will deliver or mail notice of nonrenewal to the named insured at the mailing address shown in the policy declarations.

**G.49** Notice of nonrenewal will state the effective date of nonrenewal and the reason for nonrenewal when required by law. Notice of nonrenewal will be mailed or delivered at least thirty days before nonrenewal takes effect.

**G.50** An insured must pay premium when due. We may cancel this policy if premium is not paid as required.

**G.51** A premium payment accepted by us after cancellation does not reinstate this policy unless we agree in writing. Any reinstatement is subject to the terms we specify in writing.

**G.52** We may adjust premium when information used to determine premium is incomplete, inaccurate, or changes during the policy period. An insured must pay any additional premium that is due.

**G.53** An insured must notify us promptly of a change in occupancy, ownership, or use of the insured location. We may revise coverage, premium, or policy terms because of that change.

**G.59** If a provision of this policy conflicts with applicable law, the provision is amended to conform to that law. The remainder of this policy remains in effect.

**G.61** An insured must provide a current mailing address and other contact information upon our request. Notice sent to the most recent mailing address provided to us is sufficient when permitted by law.

**G.66** An insured must reimburse us for payment made in error to the extent permitted by law. We may recover an overpayment by lawful means.
