# HO-5 Homeowners 5 — Comprehensive Form (Edition 2015-01)
> SUPERSEDED by HO-5 Homeowners 5 — Comprehensive Form (Edition 2022-06) for policies effective on or after 2022-06-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

We provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the premium and subject to all applicable provisions of this policy. This insurance applies only to the extent that its terms, conditions, and exclusions permit coverage.

We insure you for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a peril that is not excluded or otherwise limited by this policy. We will pay only for loss that occurs during the "policy period."

We insure an "insured" for damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" for which an insured is legally liable, subject to the terms of this policy. We will provide a defense when this policy requires us to do so.

We provide medical payments coverage for bodily injury when this policy applies. Payment under that coverage does not establish that an insured is legally liable.

Coverage under this policy is subject to all duties imposed upon an insured. An insured must comply with those duties as a condition of coverage.

We do not cover loss, damage, injury, or liability to the extent that an exclusion applies. We do not cover any amount that exceeds the applicable limit of liability.

We may investigate any "claim" or "occurrence" that may be covered under this policy. We may settle a claim when we consider settlement appropriate.

Our payment for covered loss will not exceed the amount we are obligated to pay under this policy. Payment will be made subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

Coverage applies only to an insured who has an interest in the property, loss, damage, injury, or liability for which coverage is sought. An insured may not transfer rights or duties under this policy without our written consent.

We will not be liable for any loss that is not covered by this policy. We will not be liable for any loss caused by an act or condition that is excluded from coverage.

The terms of this policy determine the rights and duties of you and us. No representation, waiver, or change is valid unless made by us in writing.

If a provision of this policy conflicts with another provision, the provision that is more specific to the coverage or circumstance will apply. All provisions will be applied according to their plain meaning.

This policy applies only within the scope of coverage provided by its terms. Your acceptance of this policy confirms your agreement to those terms.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, reduced by depreciation and obsolescence.  
It includes the value of property in its used condition.  
It does not include sentimental value or the cost of replacing property with property of greater value.  
For example, actual cash value may include a deduction for wear affecting damaged carpeting but does not include the cost of upgrading that carpeting.  

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.  
It includes the reasonable cost of materials and labor necessary to restore the damaged property.  
It does not include the cost of improving, enlarging, or changing the property.  
For example, replacement cost may include the cost to install comparable roofing but does not include the cost of adding a different roofing system.  

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.  
It includes an event that results in bodily injury or property damage during the policy period.  
It does not include an expected or intended act merely because injury or damage results from that act.  
For example, an unintended discharge of water may be an occurrence, but deliberate damage to property is not an occurrence.  

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the land and structures used in connection with that dwelling.  
It includes structures and grounds used principally for residential purposes.  
It does not include land or structures used exclusively for business purposes.  
For example, a detached garage used with the dwelling is part of the residence premises, but a separately operated commercial building is not.  

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or are in your care.  
It includes a person qualifying as an insured while acting within the scope of household residency and care.  
It does not include a person who does not reside in your household merely because that person visits the residence premises.  
For example, a resident relative may be an insured, but a nonresident guest ordinarily is not an insured.  

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations used in connection with the residence premises.  
It includes a temporary residence occupied by an insured and land owned by an insured where a residence is being constructed.  
It does not include a location used exclusively for business purposes or a location not connected with the residence premises.  
For example, a temporary vacation residence may be an insured location, but a separately leased commercial office is not an insured location.  

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain.  
It includes an activity conducted from the residence premises when it is regularly undertaken for compensation.  
It does not include an activity that is ordinarily noncommercial and is not undertaken for compensation.  
For example, operating a retail enterprise is business, but an unpaid personal hobby is not business.  

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or otherwise subject to motor vehicle registration.  
It includes vehicles designed for off-road recreational use when operated on public roads.  
It does not include a device designed solely to assist the mobility of a person with a physical disability.  
For example, an automobile is a motor vehicle, but a manually propelled wheelchair is not a motor vehicle.  

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises.  
It includes a person performing household or domestic services for an insured.  
It does not include an independent contractor or a person performing services in connection with business.  
For example, a household caretaker may be a residence employee, but a contractor hired to operate a commercial enterprise is not a residence employee.  

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person.  
It includes resulting death.  
It does not include emotional distress or mental anguish that does not arise from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.  
For example, a broken arm is bodily injury, but disappointment without physical harm is not bodily injury.  

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.  
It includes loss of use resulting from physical injury to tangible property.  
It does not include loss of use of property that has not suffered physical injury unless otherwise provided by this policy.  
For example, damage to a wall is property damage, but a reduction in market value without physical injury is not property damage.  

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt and unintended, arising from an unexpected event.  
It includes an unforeseen discharge, release, or escape that occurs abruptly.  
It does not include seepage, leakage, deterioration, or wear that develops gradually.  
For example, an unexpected pipe burst may be sudden and accidental, but persistent moisture from a slow leak is not sudden and accidental.  

**DEF.13** "vacant" means a dwelling that lacks sufficient personal property to permit normal habitation.  
It includes a dwelling from which furnishings and other property necessary for customary residential use have been removed.  
It does not include a dwelling that remains furnished for normal use during a temporary absence.  
For example, an unfurnished dwelling held without customary household property may be vacant, but a furnished dwelling left while you travel is not vacant.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the "dwelling" on the "residence premises" shown in the Declarations. The dwelling includes structures attached to the dwelling and property used to service the dwelling.

**A.2** We cover materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises and used to construct, alter, or repair the dwelling or other structures on the residence premises. Such materials and supplies are covered only while they are intended for that use.

**A.3** We cover structures connected to the dwelling by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. We do not cover land, including land on which the dwelling is located.

**A.4** We cover "direct physical loss" to covered property unless the loss is excluded or limited by this policy. Covered property includes the dwelling and property described in these provisions.

**A.5** We settle covered loss to the dwelling at "replacement cost" without deduction for depreciation, subject to the terms of this policy. Replacement cost means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality.

**A.6** We will pay replacement cost without deduction for depreciation if the amount of insurance that applies to the dwelling is not less than eighty percent of the full replacement cost of the dwelling immediately before the loss.

**A.7** If the condition for replacement cost settlement is not satisfied, we will pay no more than the "actual cash value" of the damaged property until repair or replacement is completed. Actual cash value means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, reflecting depreciation.

**A.8** An "insured" must give us prompt notice of a loss involving covered property. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**A.9** An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect covered property from further damage. We will cover reasonable costs incurred for those repairs when the loss is covered.

**A.10** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine records that relate to the loss.

**A.11** An insured must provide us with a signed, sworn statement of loss when we request it. The statement must identify the damaged property and state the circumstances of the loss.

**A.12** We may elect to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. Our election may include arranging for repair or replacement through persons selected by us.

**A.13** We will not pay more than the amount reasonably necessary to repair or replace damaged property. We will not pay for any increase in value resulting from repair or replacement.

**A.14** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, marring, or scratching.

**A.15** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, smog, or smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings.

**A.16** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, or discharge of waste by those animals.

**A.17** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not apply to reasonable measures taken to protect covered property.

**A.18** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. Intentional loss means any loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with intent to cause a loss.

**A.19** We do not cover loss caused by "earth movement." Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, earth sinking, rising, or shifting, and volcanic eruption.

**A.20** We do not cover loss caused by "water damage." Water damage includes flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, and water below the surface of the ground.

**A.21** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**A.22** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of "pollutants." Pollutants include substances that are solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or otherwise contaminating or irritating.

**A.23** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, replacement, demolition, or zoning. We do not cover the cost associated with an increased cost of construction resulting from enforcement of an ordinance or law.

**A.24** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the residence premises. This exclusion applies whether the failure affects utility service, communication service, or another service.

**A.25** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, including seizure, destruction, or confiscation of property. We do cover loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action.

**A.26** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, or revolution. We do not cover loss caused by a warlike act by a military force or agent.

**A.27** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, and any consequence of those conditions.

**A.28** We do not cover loss caused by "fungus," wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. Fungus includes mold, mildew, and any mycotoxins, spores, scents, or byproducts produced or released by fungus.

**A.29** We do not cover loss caused by defective, inadequate, or faulty planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, grading, compaction, or materials. We do not cover loss resulting from maintenance that is defective, inadequate, or faulty.

**A.30** We do not cover loss caused by collapse, except as provided for a covered collapse. "Collapse" means an abrupt falling down or caving in of the dwelling or part of the dwelling.

**A.31** We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered collapse when the collapse is caused by hidden decay, hidden insect damage, or the weight of contents, equipment, animals, rain, snow, ice, or sleet. We do not cover a building that is standing, even if it shows evidence of cracking, bulging, sagging, bending, leaning, settling, or expansion.

**A.32** We do not cover loss to property that is vacant when the loss occurs, if the vacancy increases the hazard of loss. A dwelling is vacant when it lacks the customary contents needed for normal habitation.

**A.33** We do not cover theft from a dwelling under construction. We do not cover materials and supplies stolen from a location away from the residence premises.

**A.34** We do not cover property used primarily for business purposes, except property used solely to maintain the dwelling. "Business" includes trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation.

**A.35** We do not cover loss to property owned by a tenant, boarder, roomer, or guest. We do cover property owned by you when that property otherwise qualifies as covered property.

**A.36** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request.

**A.37** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath while not in the presence of another insured. An insured must provide a signed record of that examination when we request it.

**A.38** We will adjust covered loss with you. Payment of covered loss does not assign to us any interest in the dwelling unless we agree otherwise in writing.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover direct physical loss to "other structures" that are located on the "residence premises" and are owned by an "insured." Coverage applies subject to the provisions of this Coverage B.

**B.2** Other structures include structures that are set apart from the "dwelling" by clear space. A structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection is considered set apart by clear space.

**B.3** We cover a structure only when it is appurtenant to the residence premises. A structure that is not appurtenant to the residence premises is not covered under this Coverage B.

**B.4** Coverage applies to permanently installed equipment that serves a covered other structure. Such equipment must be located in or on the covered other structure.

**B.5** The limit of liability for Coverage B is ten percent of the limit that applies to the dwelling. This limit is the most we will pay for covered loss under this Coverage B.

**B.6** Payment under this Coverage B does not increase the limit that applies to the dwelling. Payment for loss to the dwelling does not increase the limit that applies to other structures.

**B.7** The applicable limit is not increased by payment for a prior covered loss. We will not pay more than the applicable limit for all covered loss arising from the same occurrence.

**B.8** We determine whether property is an other structure by its physical characteristics, location, and use. The name given to the property by an insured does not determine coverage.

**B.9** We cover a structure that serves both the dwelling and the residence premises only to the extent it satisfies the requirements of this Coverage B. We may determine the covered portion of the structure from the facts of the loss.

**B.10** Coverage for an other structure applies only while the structure is located on the residence premises. We do not cover a structure after it has been removed from the residence premises.

**B.11** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We also do not cover the value of land disturbed, altered, or damaged in connection with loss to an other structure.

**B.12** We do not cover an other structure rented or held for rental to a person who is not a tenant of the dwelling. This exclusion applies whether or not rental income is received.

**B.13** The rental exclusion does not apply to an other structure used solely as a "private garage." The private garage must not be used for dwelling purposes.

**B.14** We do not cover an other structure from which a "business" is conducted. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted by an insured or by another person.

**B.15** The business exclusion does not apply to an other structure used solely as a private garage. This exception applies only while the structure remains solely a private garage.

**B.16** We do not cover an other structure used to store "business property." This exclusion applies to business property owned by an insured or by another person.

**B.17** The business property storage exclusion does not apply to business property solely owned by an insured or a tenant of the dwelling. The property must be stored in an other structure used solely as a private garage.

**B.18** We do not cover property used principally for agricultural, commercial, or manufacturing activity. This exclusion applies even if the activity is incidental to use of the residence premises.

**B.19** We do not cover an other structure used principally for lodging or dwelling purposes. This exclusion does not affect coverage for a private garage that is not used for lodging or dwelling purposes.

**B.20** An insured must identify each other structure for which a claim is made. The identification must describe the structure, its location, ownership, and use at the time of loss.

**B.21** An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the construction, use, occupancy, rental, or business use of an other structure. The information must be complete and accurate.

**B.22** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable measures taken to protect covered property must not create a greater hazard.

**B.23** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard, destroy, alter, or repair damaged property before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, except when necessary to protect property from further damage.

**B.24** An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged other structure and the residence premises. An insured must also permit us to inspect records reasonably related to the claim.

**B.25** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, estimates, contracts, rental agreements, and other documents that reasonably support the claim. Copies are acceptable when original documents are unavailable.

**B.26** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. Cooperation includes providing requested information and assisting in determining the cause and extent of loss.

**B.27** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable measures necessary to protect property from further damage. We will not be bound by an obligation assumed without our consent.

**B.28** We may inspect an other structure before or after a loss. An inspection by us does not constitute a warranty that the structure is safe, sound, or compliant with any law.

**B.29** We may require an insured to show the damaged property and the undamaged property of like kind and quality. We may also require reasonable access to the location where the loss occurred.

**B.30** We may request that an insured submit a signed statement describing the loss, the interest of each insured in the property, and the use of the other structure. The statement must be based on the knowledge reasonably available to the insured.

**B.31** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's intentional concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact relating to an other structure or a claim. This exclusion applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after a loss.

**B.32** Coverage under this Coverage B applies only to direct physical loss to covered other structures. We do not cover loss that is otherwise excluded by the provisions of this policy.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** The limit of liability for "Coverage C" is fifty percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". This limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss under this coverage.

**C.2** We cover "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" while it is anywhere in the world. Coverage applies to direct physical loss to covered personal property unless an exclusion applies.

**C.3** At your request, we cover personal property owned by others while the property is on the "residence premises" occupied by an insured. We may also cover property of a "guest" while the property is in the residence occupied by an insured.

**C.4** "Direct physical loss" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property caused by a "peril". We cover direct physical loss only when the loss results from a covered peril.

**C.5** We cover personal property that is temporarily removed from the residence premises when it remains owned or used by an insured. Coverage for property away from the residence premises remains subject to the applicable limitations and exclusions.

**C.6** We cover "business property" owned by an insured when the property is used in an occupation conducted by an insured. Coverage for business property is subject to the special limit stated in this coverage.

**C.7** We do not cover a "motor vehicle" that is subject to registration for use on public roads. We cover a motor vehicle used solely to service the residence premises or designed to assist a person with a physical disability.

**C.8** We do not cover a "watercraft" except as provided by this coverage. Coverage for watercraft remains subject to the applicable special limit.

**C.9** We do not cover an "aircraft" or its equipment. This exclusion does not apply to model or hobby aircraft that are not designed to carry people.

**C.10** We do not cover a "hovercraft" or its equipment. This exclusion applies whether the hovercraft is owned, rented, borrowed, or used by an insured.

**C.11** The most we will pay for loss to "money" and "precious metals" is three hundred dollars. This limit applies to the total loss of such property arising from the same occurrence.

**C.12** The most we will pay for loss by "theft" of "jewelry", watches, and precious stones is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies whether the property is worn, stored, or otherwise possessed by an insured.

**C.13** The most we will pay for loss by theft of "firearms" is three thousand dollars. This limit includes related equipment and accessories.

**C.14** The most we will pay for loss to watercraft including "trailers" is one thousand dollars. This limit includes equipment, furnishings, and outboard motors used with the watercraft.

**C.15** The most we will pay for loss by theft of "silverware" is five thousand dollars. Silverware includes silver-plated ware, goldware, gold-plated ware, and pewterware.

**C.16** The most we will pay for loss to business property on the residence premises is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies to property owned by an insured and property in the care of an insured.

**C.17** The most we will pay for loss to "electronic apparatus" in a motor vehicle is one thousand dollars. This limit includes accessories and antennas used solely with the electronic apparatus.

**C.18** The special limits stated in this coverage apply regardless of the number of items involved in a loss. A special limit does not increase the limit of liability for Coverage C.

**C.19** We will pay no more than the applicable limit of liability for covered property. Payment for loss to any item does not increase any remaining limit.

**C.20** We determine the value of covered personal property at the time of loss. We may consider age, condition, quality, usefulness, and depreciation in determining the amount of loss.

**C.21** We may repair, replace, or pay for covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may take all or part of damaged property at the value we pay.

**C.22** For a "pair or set", we may repair or replace any part that is lost or damaged, restore the pair or set to its value before loss, or pay the difference in value. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**C.23** For a "collection", we may pay the value of the part that is lost or damaged. We do not cover any reduction in value to the remaining property caused by the loss of a part.

**C.24** We do not cover loss to "property held for sale" by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to property held for sale that is not related to a business conducted by an insured.

**C.25** We do not cover loss to "data" stored or contained in electronic equipment unless the equipment itself is physically damaged by a covered peril. We cover the cost to restore data only when restoration is necessary to repair covered electronic equipment.

**C.26** We do not cover loss to "animals", birds, fish, or insects. We cover direct physical loss caused by an animal only when the damaged property is otherwise covered.

**C.27** We do not cover loss caused by insects, rodents, vermin, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, or discharge from animals.

**C.28** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.29** We do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or any quality in property that causes the property to damage or destroy itself. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.30** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, grading, compaction, or materials. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.31** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, smog, or contamination. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.32** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.33** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of water, ice, or snow. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.34** We do not cover loss arising out of an intentional act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss differs in kind or degree from the loss intended or expected.

**C.35** We do not cover loss caused by "earth movement", including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, earth sinking, rising, shifting, or expanding. This exclusion applies whether earth movement is caused by natural or human forces.

**C.36** We do not cover loss caused by "water" that floods, overflows, backs up, seeps, leaks, or otherwise enters property from below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or another cause.

**C.37** We do not cover loss caused by failure of power or other utility service when the failure occurs away from the residence premises. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril resulting from that failure.

**C.38** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, demolition, or use of property. We do not cover the increased cost of complying with an ordinance or law.

**C.39** We do not cover loss caused by war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military force. This exclusion applies whether the loss is direct or indirect.

**C.40** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies whether the cause is controlled or uncontrolled.

**C.41** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by governmental authority. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from such action.

**C.42** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**C.43** We do not cover loss by theft from a dwelling under construction or from property at that location. This exclusion applies until the dwelling is occupied as a residence.

**C.44** We do not cover loss to property rented or held for rental to others. This exclusion does not apply to property of an insured that is rented on an occasional basis for personal use.

**C.45** We do not cover loss to property of roomers, boarders, or other tenants who are not insureds. We may cover property of a guest as provided by this coverage.

**C.46** In the event of loss, an insured must give us prompt notice. The notice must state the identity of the insured, the location of the loss, and the circumstances of the loss.

**C.47** An insured must notify the appropriate authorities when property is stolen. An insured must cooperate with us in any investigation of the theft.

**C.48** An insured must protect covered property from further damage. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property and keep an accurate record of repair expenses.

**C.49** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed, or stolen personal property. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item.

**C.50** An insured must provide records, receipts, bills, photographs, and other documents that support the claimed loss when reasonably available. An insured must permit us to make copies of those materials.

**C.51** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and take samples for examination or testing.

**C.52** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require. An insured must sign the examination transcript when it is prepared accurately.

**C.53** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we reasonably require. The statement must identify the interest of all insureds and others in the property involved.

**C.54** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of any claim. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold a material fact concerning the loss.

**C.55** We may require an insured to assign to us any rights of recovery against a person responsible for a covered loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**C.56** We may settle a covered loss with an insured or with a person having a lawful interest in the property. Payment to that person satisfies our obligation to the extent of the payment.

**C.57** We will pay for covered loss only after an insured has complied with the duties stated in this coverage. Our payment is subject to the applicable limits of liability, exclusions, and conditions.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** The limit of liability for "Coverage D" is twenty percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". This limit is the most we will pay for all loss payable under this coverage.

**D.2** The Coverage D limit applies collectively to all coverages provided by this section. Payment under any provision of this section reduces the amount available under this coverage.

**D.3** We provide loss of use coverage when a "covered loss" causes a loss described in these provisions. The loss must result directly from the covered loss.

**D.4** If a covered loss makes that part of the "residence premises" where you reside unfit to live in, we cover the resulting additional living expense. The residence premises must be used by you as your principal place of residence when the covered loss occurs.

**D.5** We cover any necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you. The increase must be necessary for your household to maintain its normal standard of living.

**D.6** Additional living expense includes reasonable expenses for temporary housing, meals, and other necessary services. The expense must result from your inability to reside in the damaged part of the residence premises.

**D.7** We determine the necessary increase in living expenses by comparing your actual living expenses with the expenses that would have been incurred without the covered loss. We consider only expenses that are reasonable and necessary.

**D.8** We pay additional living expense for the shortest time required to repair or replace the residence premises. If you permanently relocate, we pay for the shortest time required for your household to settle elsewhere.

**D.9** Additional living expense ends when the residence premises are fit for you to live in. Payment also ends when your household has settled elsewhere after a permanent relocation.

**D.10** If a covered loss makes that part of the residence premises rented to others unfit to live in, we cover the resulting loss of "Fair Rental Value". We also provide this coverage when the damaged part of the residence premises is held for rental.

**D.11** Fair Rental Value means the rental value of the part of the residence premises that is unfit to live in. We reduce that value by expenses that do not continue while the premises are unfit to live in.

**D.12** We determine fair rental value based on the rental value that you could reasonably expect to receive. The rental value must relate to the damaged part of the residence premises.

**D.13** We pay fair rental value for the shortest time required to repair or replace the part of the residence premises that is unfit to live in. The loss of rental value must result directly from the covered loss.

## I.S — Section I — Conditions

**S.14** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when practical. An insured must allow us to inspect the property as often as reasonably required.

## II.E — Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** We will pay damages for which an “insured” is legally liable because of “bodily injury” or “property damage” caused by an “occurrence” to which this coverage applies. The occurrence must take place during the policy period.

**E.2** “Bodily injury” means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death. Bodily injury includes required care, loss of services, and resulting damages.

**E.3** “Property damage” means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use. Property damage also includes loss of use of tangible property that is not physically injured.

**E.4** “Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence must result in bodily injury or property damage.

**E.5** We will provide a defense at our expense against a “suit” seeking damages covered by this insurance. We may investigate and settle a claim or suit as we consider appropriate.

**E.6** “Suit” means a civil proceeding in which damages because of bodily injury or property damage are alleged. Suit includes arbitration or another dispute-resolution proceeding to which we consent.

**E.7** Our duty to defend ends when we have used the applicable limit of liability to pay judgments or settlements. We have no duty to provide a defense for a claim or suit that is not covered by this insurance.

**E.8** We will pay reasonable expenses incurred by an insured at our request in assisting us with the investigation or defense of a claim or suit. We will pay expenses we incur in defending a claim or suit.

**E.9** Our liability is limited to the applicable limit of liability for damages arising from an occurrence. The applicable limit is the most we will pay for all damages resulting from that occurrence.

**E.10** An insured must give us prompt notice of an occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must include information sufficient to identify the insured and the circumstances of the occurrence.

**E.11** An insured must notify us promptly of a claim or suit. An insured must promptly send us copies of notices, demands, summonses, and legal papers received in connection with the claim or suit.

**E.12** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. An insured must assist us in securing evidence, obtaining witnesses, and protecting our rights.

**E.13** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses incurred at our request. This condition does not apply to emergency medical assistance provided to protect a person from bodily injury.

**E.14** No insured may bring legal action against us unless the insured has complied with all applicable duties under this coverage. No person may join us as a party to an action against an insured until the insured’s obligation to pay damages has been determined by judgment or written agreement signed by us.

**E.15** This insurance applies separately to each insured. This provision does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**E.16** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage that is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury resulting from the use of reasonable force by an insured to protect persons or property.

**E.17** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of or in connection with a business conducted from an insured location or engaged in by an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not the business is owned or operated by an insured.

**E.18** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the rendering or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the services are performed for compensation.

**E.19** We do not cover liability assumed by an insured under a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**E.20** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or a similar act of hostility. This exclusion applies whether or not an insured is directly involved in the act or condition.

**E.21** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle owned or operated by an insured. This exclusion includes liability arising from entrustment of a motor vehicle to another person.

**E.22** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft owned or operated by an insured. This exclusion includes liability arising from entrustment of a watercraft to another person.

**E.23** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft owned or operated by an insured. This exclusion includes liability arising from entrustment of an aircraft to another person.

**E.24** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a hovercraft owned or operated by an insured. This exclusion includes liability arising from entrustment of a hovercraft to another person.

**E.25** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the transmission is alleged to be intentional, negligent, or otherwise wrongful.

**E.26** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, cultivation, or distribution of a controlled substance by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of prescription medication by a person following lawful directions.

## II.F — Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

**F.1** Under "Coverage F", we will pay necessary "medical expenses" incurred because of "bodily injury" caused by an "accident." Necessary medical expenses must be incurred within three years from the date of the accident.

**F.2** Medical expenses include reasonable charges for medical, surgical, dental, ambulance, hospital, professional nursing, prosthetic, and funeral services. The services must be necessary because of the bodily injury.

**F.3** We will pay medical expenses without regard to whether an "insured" is legally liable for the bodily injury. Payment by us does not constitute an admission of liability by any insured.

**F.4** We will pay medical expenses to a person who sustains bodily injury while on an "insured location" with permission. The person's presence must be related to the person's permitted use of the insured location.

**F.5** We will also pay medical expenses to a person who sustains bodily injury away from an insured location when the bodily injury results from any circumstance stated in this coverage. The bodily injury must result directly from that circumstance.

**F.6** The bodily injury may arise out of a condition on the insured location or on ways immediately adjoining it. The condition must exist at the time of the accident.

**F.7** The bodily injury may be caused by the activities of an insured. The activities may occur away from the insured location.

**F.8** The bodily injury may be caused by a "residence employee" in the course of the residence employee's employment by an insured. The residence employee must be acting within the scope of those duties.

**F.9** The bodily injury may be caused by an animal owned by or in the care of an insured. The animal need not be on the insured location when the accident occurs.

**F.10** We do not cover bodily injury to you or to a "regular resident" of your household. This exclusion does not apply to a residence employee.

**F.11** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury to a person eligible to receive benefits provided voluntarily or required by a "workers compensation law." This exclusion applies whether the benefits are sought or received.

**F.12** We will pay only charges for services actually rendered because of bodily injury. We will not pay charges that duplicate amounts paid or payable for the same services.

**F.13** The applicable "limit of liability" is the most we will pay for medical expenses arising from bodily injury to any person. Payments under this coverage do not increase that limit.

**F.14** An insured must promptly notify us of an accident that may result in a claim under this coverage. The notice must state the time, place, and circumstances of the accident, to the extent known.

**F.15** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or payment of a claim. An insured must provide information and records that we reasonably request.

**F.16** A person seeking payment must submit to physical examination by a physician selected by us when we reasonably require. We will pay the cost of the examination.

**F.17** A person seeking payment must authorize us to obtain medical records and other information relevant to the bodily injury and medical expenses. The authorization must permit us to verify the necessity and reasonableness of the charges.

**F.18** We may pay medical expenses directly to a person, a medical provider, or another person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment by us does not waive any right we may have under this policy.

**G.37** An assignment of this policy is not valid unless we give written consent. An assignment of a claim after loss is subject to applicable law.

**G.38** A waiver or change of a policy provision must be in writing and signed by us. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive that provision.

**G.39** A statement made by an agent or other person does not change this policy unless the change is made by written endorsement. Knowledge held by an agent or other person does not waive a policy provision.

**G.40** If we adopt a revision that broadens coverage without additional premium, the broadened coverage will apply to this policy when permitted by law. The revision must apply generally to policies of this type issued by us.

**G.41** We do not cover a loss if an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance relating to this insurance. We do not cover a loss if an insured has engaged in fraud or made a false statement relating to this insurance.

**G.42** Concealment, misrepresentation, fraud, or a false statement by an insured may void coverage for all insureds to the extent permitted by law. We may deny coverage when the conduct is material to the risk or claim.

**G.43** We may cancel this policy as permitted by law. We will deliver or mail notice of cancellation to the named insured at the mailing address shown in the policy declarations.

**G.44** We may cancel this policy for nonpayment of premium. Notice of cancellation for nonpayment of premium will be mailed or delivered at least ten days before cancellation takes effect.

**G.45** We may cancel this policy for a reason other than nonpayment of premium as permitted by law. Notice of cancellation for a reason other than nonpayment of premium will be mailed or delivered at least thirty days before cancellation takes effect.

**G.46** A cancellation notice will state the effective date of cancellation and the reason for cancellation when required by law. Cancellation is effective even if the named insured does not receive the notice, if we have complied with applicable law.

**G.47** If this policy is cancelled, we will refund any unearned premium as required by law. Payment or tender of unearned premium is not a condition of cancellation.

**G.48** We may elect not to renew this policy as permitted by law. We will deliver or mail notice of nonrenewal to the named insured at the mailing address shown in the policy declarations.

**G.49** Notice of nonrenewal will state the effective date of nonrenewal and the reason for nonrenewal when required by law. Notice of nonrenewal will be mailed or delivered at least thirty days before nonrenewal takes effect.

**G.50** An insured must pay premium when due. We may cancel this policy if premium is not paid as required.

**G.51** A premium payment accepted by us after cancellation does not reinstate this policy unless we agree in writing. Any reinstatement is subject to the terms we specify in writing.

**G.52** We may adjust premium when information used to determine premium is incomplete, inaccurate, or changes during the policy period. An insured must pay any additional premium that is due.

**G.53** An insured must notify us promptly of a change in occupancy, ownership, or use of the insured location. We may revise coverage, premium, or policy terms because of that change.

**G.54** An insured must notify us promptly if the insured location becomes vacant or unoccupied. We may take action permitted by this policy or applicable law because of vacancy or unoccupancy.

**G.55** An insured must comply with applicable laws and ordinances relating to the maintenance and use of covered property. Compliance with law does not create coverage otherwise excluded by this policy.

**G.56** We may deny coverage to the extent an insured's failure to comply with a policy duty prejudices our rights. We will not deny coverage where denial is prohibited by law.

**G.57** A bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this policy. A bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not give a claimant a right of action against us except as permitted by law.

**G.58** Our rights and duties under this policy may be transferred only as permitted by law. A transfer required by law will not enlarge coverage or increase a limit of liability.

**G.59** If a provision of this policy conflicts with applicable law, the provision is amended to conform to that law. The remainder of this policy remains in effect.

**G.60** The terms of this policy include the policy declarations and any written endorsement issued by us. An endorsement controls if it conflicts with another policy provision.

**G.61** An insured must provide a current mailing address and other contact information upon our request. Notice sent to the most recent mailing address provided to us is sufficient when permitted by law.

**G.62** We may communicate with an insured electronically when an insured has consented to electronic delivery or when permitted by law. An insured must promptly notify us of a change in electronic contact information.

**G.63** An insured may request a copy of policy records that we maintain relating to this insurance. We may charge a reasonable fee for records when permitted by law.

**G.64** A request for coverage does not obligate us to provide coverage unless coverage is afforded by this policy. Our investigation of a claim does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**G.65** We may settle a claim with an insured, claimant, mortgagee, or other person having an interest in the claim. A settlement does not admit liability or establish coverage for another claim.

**G.66** An insured must reimburse us for payment made in error to the extent permitted by law. We may recover an overpayment by lawful means.

**G.67** The duties and conditions in this section apply separately to each insured. A breach by an insured affects coverage only as permitted by this policy and applicable law.
