# HO-5 Homeowners 5 — Comprehensive Form (Edition 2022-06)




## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** We will provide the insurance described in this "policy" in return for the "premium" and subject to all applicable provisions. This policy applies only while it is in force.

**AGR.2** We insure you and any other "insured" for covered loss, damage, expense, or liability arising from an "occurrence" to the extent provided by this policy. Coverage applies only when the applicable conditions are met.

**AGR.3** We will pay for "direct physical loss" to "covered property" when that loss is covered by this policy. We do not cover loss that is excluded or limited by this policy.

**AGR.4** We will pay damages for which an insured is legally liable when coverage applies. We may investigate and settle any "claim" or suit as we consider appropriate.

**AGR.5** We will provide a defense for an insured against a covered claim or suit. Our duty to defend ends when the applicable "limit of liability" has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements.

**AGR.6** Coverage for property applies only to the extent of your financial interest in that property. Coverage relating to your "residence premises" applies only while the property meets the requirements of this policy.

**AGR.7** An insured must comply with all duties imposed by this policy after a loss, occurrence, claim, or suit. Failure to comply with those duties may affect coverage when the failure prejudices us.

**AGR.8** We will reduce covered payments by the applicable "deductible." We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**AGR.9** We do not cover loss, damage, expense, or liability arising from concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud by an insured concerning this policy or a claim. This provision applies whether the conduct occurs before or after a loss.

**AGR.10** This policy contains the agreement between you and us concerning the insurance provided. A waiver or change is effective only when made by us in writing.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined by applying depreciation for age, condition, and obsolescence to replacement cost.  
It includes the reduced value of property that has been used or has deteriorated.  
It does not include sentimental value or the cost of improving property beyond its condition immediately before the loss.  
For example, the value of a worn appliance includes depreciation and does not include the cost of a newer model with added features.  

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the reasonable cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.  
It includes necessary labor and materials used to restore the damaged property.  
It does not include the cost of improvements, additions, or changes that increase the value or utility of the property.  
For example, the cost to replace damaged flooring with comparable flooring is included, while the added cost to install a premium material is not included.  

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, which results in bodily injury or property damage.  
All bodily injury or property damage resulting from the same general harmful conditions is considered a single occurrence.  
It includes accidental damage caused by an unexpected event.  
For example, damage caused by an accidental overflow is included, while unrelated accidents are not included in the same occurrence.  

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the land and structures used in connection with that dwelling.  
It includes structures attached to the dwelling and grounds used for residential purposes.  
It does not include land or structures that are not used in connection with your residence.  
For example, an attached garage is included, while a separate property used solely for rental purposes is not included.  

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or persons in your care.  
It includes a resident relative who lives with you as part of your household.  
It does not include a tenant, boarder, or guest who is not a resident of your household or in your care.  
For example, a resident child in your care is included, while a visiting friend is not included.  

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations used by an insured in connection with the residence premises.  
It includes land owned by an insured that is used for nonbusiness purposes and a temporary residence occupied by an insured.  
It does not include premises used primarily for business or property owned by another person that is not used by an insured.  
For example, a temporary vacation residence occupied by you is included, while an office leased for commercial activity is not included.  

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain.  
It includes services performed regularly for payment and property used principally for commercial purposes.  
It does not include personal activities that are not undertaken for compensation or financial gain.  
For example, operating a paid repair service is included, while selling personal belongings on an occasional basis is not included.  

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or for use away from public roads.  
It includes an automobile, truck, motorcycle, and similar motorized land conveyance.  
It does not include a vehicle in dead storage on an insured location or a device moved solely by human power.  
For example, a car is included, while a bicycle is not included.  

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises.  
It includes a person performing domestic services, groundskeeping, or similar household work for an insured.  
It does not include an independent contractor who controls the manner and means of performing the work.  
For example, a housekeeper employed by you is included, while a separately operated landscaping business is not included.  

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death.  
It includes physical injury caused by an accident or harmful condition.  
It does not include emotional distress, humiliation, or other mental injury that does not result from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.  
For example, a broken arm is included, while disappointment arising from damaged property is not included.  

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.  
It includes resulting loss of use of property that has not been physically injured.  
It does not include financial loss, loss of market value, or injury to property that is not tangible.  
For example, damage to furniture is included, while lost investment income is not included.  

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unforeseen, and unintended.  
It includes an event that happens unexpectedly and without design or intention.  
It does not include deterioration, gradual leakage, wear, or damage that develops over time.  
For example, an unexpected pipe burst is included, while seepage from a long-standing leak is not included.  

**DEF.13** "vacant" means a dwelling or structure that lacks the substantial furnishings, occupancy, or customary activity necessary for its intended use.  
It includes a property that has been abandoned or left without meaningful residential use.  
It does not include a furnished residence that is temporarily unoccupied but remains available for customary use.  
For this purpose, the claims manual's duration test applies the definitionthe claims manual's duration test applies the definition (see Property Claims Handling Manual, C3 Chapter 3 — Water Losses).  

**DEF.14** "named storm" means a tropical cyclone or similar weather system that has been assigned an official name by a governmental weather authority.  
It includes wind, rain, and other weather conditions directly associated with that identified system.  
It does not include an unnamed thunderstorm, ordinary rainstorm, or weather event not associated with an officially named system.  
For example, damage caused by an officially named hurricane is included, while damage caused by an unrelated local storm is not included.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the "dwelling" on the "residence premises" shown in the Declarations. The dwelling includes structures attached to it. This coverage applies while the premises is used for residential purposes.

**A.2** We cover building materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises when intended for use in constructing, altering, or repairing the dwelling. We cover those materials only to the extent they are used for the dwelling. Materials intended for another structure are not covered under this provision.

**A.3** We cover permanently installed fixtures, machinery, and equipment that service the dwelling. This includes equipment used to heat, cool, ventilate, or provide utility service to the dwelling. We cover such property whether it is attached to the dwelling or located within it.

**A.4** We cover permanently installed floor coverings, wall coverings, and window coverings that are part of the dwelling. We cover these items when they are attached to or installed in the dwelling. We do not cover personal property merely because it is located within the dwelling.

**A.5** We cover additions, alterations, and improvements that you make to the dwelling. We cover them when they are owned by you or when you are required by a written agreement to insure them. This coverage does not increase the applicable limit of insurance.

**A.6** We cover property owned by you and used to maintain the dwelling. This includes equipment and materials used for ordinary maintenance and repair. We cover the property while it is at the residence premises or temporarily away from it for that purpose.

**A.7** We cover a building component that is removed from the dwelling while it is being repaired or replaced. We cover the component while it is at the residence premises or at a location where the repair or replacement is being performed. We do not cover a component that is permanently abandoned.

**A.8** We cover outdoor equipment that is permanently connected to the dwelling. This includes equipment used for electrical, plumbing, heating, cooling, or communications service. We cover the equipment only while it serves the dwelling.

**A.9** We cover a structure connected to the dwelling by only a utility line, fence, or similar connection when the structure is used as part of the dwelling. We cover the structure as a portion of the dwelling. We do not cover a detached structure solely because it is near the dwelling.

**A.10** We cover the dwelling while it is being renovated, repaired, or rebuilt. We cover the dwelling when the work is performed by you or by a contractor acting for you. Coverage continues subject to the duties that apply to the condition of the premises.

**A.11** We cover direct physical loss to the dwelling. We cover the loss when it is caused by a peril not otherwise excluded. We do not cover loss that is excluded by the policy, even if another cause contributes to the loss.

**A.12** We cover damage to the dwelling caused by a covered loss to another covered property. We cover the damage when it results directly from efforts to protect the dwelling or prevent further damage. We do not cover the cost of correcting a condition that did not result from the loss.

**A.13** We cover the dwelling when a covered loss makes a portion of it unsafe. We cover reasonable measures taken to protect the dwelling from further covered loss. We do not cover improvements made solely to increase the value, usefulness, or appearance of the dwelling.

**A.14** We cover debris from covered property when the debris results from a covered loss to the dwelling. We cover reasonable expenses to remove the debris from the residence premises. We do not cover debris removal when there has been no covered loss to the dwelling.

**A.15** We cover the cost to tear out and replace portions of the dwelling necessary to repair covered damage. We cover the cost when access to the damaged portion is reasonably necessary. We do not cover the cost to repair an undamaged portion that is removed only for convenience.

**A.16** We cover reasonable expenses to make temporary repairs to the dwelling after a covered loss. We cover those expenses when the repairs are intended to prevent further damage. You must preserve the damaged property for our inspection unless we agree otherwise.

**A.17** We cover the dwelling while it is vacant or unoccupied, subject to the policy conditions that apply to the premises. We cover a covered loss that occurs during that condition unless the loss is otherwise excluded. You must notify us promptly when the condition of the premises materially changes.

**A.18** We cover the dwelling when you temporarily occupy another location during covered repairs. We cover the dwelling while it remains your residence premises. You must take reasonable steps to protect the dwelling from further damage.

**A.19** We cover the dwelling when it is leased to others for residential use. We cover the dwelling only while the use is consistent with the use described in the Declarations. We do not cover loss caused by an unauthorized business or other prohibited use.

**A.20** We cover the dwelling when a portion of it is used for an incidental office or professional activity. We cover the dwelling when the activity does not materially increase the risk of loss. We do not cover loss arising from a business use that is not incidental to the residential use.

**A.21** We cover the dwelling when a portion of it is used to provide care or services to others. We cover the dwelling when the use is permitted by law and does not materially increase the risk of loss. We do not cover loss arising from an unreported change in occupancy or use.

**A.22** We cover a detached portion of the dwelling when it is physically connected to the dwelling by a covered structure. We cover the portion as part of the dwelling when it is used for residential purposes. We do not cover property that is separately insured under another coverage.

**A.23** We cover exterior fixtures that are permanently attached to the dwelling. This includes fixtures used for lighting, security, access, or ordinary residential use. We cover the fixtures only to the extent they are part of the dwelling.

**A.24** We cover exterior surfaces of the dwelling, including siding, trim, doors, windows, and permanently attached glass. We cover direct physical loss to those surfaces when caused by a covered peril. We do not cover damage caused solely by wear and tear, marring, or deterioration.

**A.25** We cover the roof of the dwelling for direct physical loss caused by a covered peril, unless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attachedunless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attached (see HO 23 74 Actual Cash Value Loss Settlement — Roof Surfacing (Edition 2025-05), W.1 Coverage Provided). Except that, we do not cover roof damage caused solely by wear and tear, marring, or deterioration. We cover resulting damage to the dwelling when the resulting damage is caused by a covered peril.

**A.26** We cover water or steam systems that are permanently installed in the dwelling. We cover those systems when they are used to supply, circulate, or discharge water or steam. We do not cover a system that is not part of the dwelling.

**A.27** We cover electrical systems and permanently installed equipment that serve the dwelling. We cover the systems and equipment when they are located within the dwelling or attached to it. We do not cover a system used primarily to serve property that is not covered.

**A.28** We cover fuel tanks, tanks, and related equipment that are permanently installed as part of the dwelling. We cover the equipment when it serves the dwelling. We do not cover fuel or other contents of a tank unless the contents are part of the damaged property covered by this provision.

**A.29** We cover permanent landscaping features that are attached to the dwelling. We cover those features when they are part of the building or necessary for its ordinary residential use. We do not cover plants, trees, shrubs, or other growing property under this Coverage.

**A.30** We cover a swimming pool that is permanently attached to the dwelling. We cover the pool and its permanently installed equipment when they are part of the dwelling. We do not cover water in the pool.

**A.31** We cover permanent construction features that provide access to the dwelling. This includes ramps, railings, steps, and similar features attached to the dwelling. We cover them when they are used for ordinary access to the dwelling.

**A.32** We cover a building feature that is damaged by the action of a covered peril on the dwelling. We cover the feature when the damage results directly from that action. We do not cover a feature that is not part of the dwelling.

**A.33** We cover the dwelling when it is damaged by a covered peril that begins outside the residence premises. We cover the resulting direct physical loss when the peril reaches the dwelling. We do not cover the outside source of the peril unless that source is itself covered property.

**A.34** We cover the dwelling when it is damaged by a covered peril that begins within the residence premises. We cover the resulting direct physical loss to the dwelling. We do not cover the cost to locate or correct an excluded condition unless covered damage results.

**A.35** We cover the dwelling when a covered loss requires compliance with an ordinance or law. We cover the increased cost of repairing the damaged portion only when the increased cost is otherwise covered. You must comply with our reasonable requests for information concerning the required work.

**A.36** We will settle covered damage to the dwelling on a "replacement cost" basis when the amount of insurance on the dwelling is at least eighty percent of its replacement cost. Subject to, we will not pay more than the applicable limit of insurance. We may settle the loss on another basis when the required repairs are not completed as required by the policy.

**A.37** We determine the cost to repair or replace the damaged portion of the dwelling with property of like kind and quality. We consider labor, materials, and reasonable charges necessary to complete the covered work. We do not include the cost of betterment or improvement.

**A.38** You must notify us promptly of a loss to the dwelling. You must protect the dwelling from further damage and make reasonable temporary repairs when necessary. You must provide us with records, estimates, and other information that we reasonably request.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We provide "Coverage B" for "Other Structures" on the "residence premises". The Coverage B limit is ten percent of the Coverage A limit. Subject to the provisions that follow, we apply this limit to covered loss involving Other Structures.

**B.2** "Other Structures" means structures set apart from the dwelling by clear space. A structure connected to the dwelling by only a fence, utility line, or similar connection is an Other Structure. Unless otherwise stated, we consider a structure connected to the dwelling by a roof to be part of the dwelling.

**B.3** An Other Structure must be located on the residence premises. It must be used in connection with the residence premises. Subject to the policy provisions that apply to the residence premises, we cover an Other Structure that you own or maintain.

**B.4** We cover an Other Structure used for a private garage. We cover an Other Structure used for storage of household property. We cover an Other Structure used for a private swimming pool, provided that the structure otherwise qualifies as an Other Structure.

**B.5** We cover an Other Structure used for a private greenhouse. We cover an Other Structure used for a private workshop. Unless the structure is used for business purposes, we apply Coverage B to the structure as an Other Structure.

**B.6** We cover an Other Structure that is rented to others for use as a private garage. We cover an Other Structure that is rented to others for use as a private storage facility. Subject to the terms that apply to rental use, we cover the structure while it remains an Other Structure.

**B.7** We cover an Other Structure that is under construction. The construction must be on the residence premises. Provided that the structure remains an Other Structure, we cover materials and supplies intended for its construction.

**B.8** We cover an Other Structure that is temporarily removed from the residence premises for repair or restoration. The structure must be intended to be returned to the residence premises. Unless it is permanently relocated, we continue to treat it as an Other Structure.

**B.9** We do not cover land, including land on which an Other Structure is located. We do not cover water or growing vegetation. We do not cover the cost of preparing or improving land.

**B.10** We do not cover an Other Structure rented to others for any purpose other than a private garage or private storage facility. We do not cover an Other Structure used for any business purpose. This exclusion applies unless the structure qualifies for coverage under another provision of this Coverage B.

**B.11** We do not cover an Other Structure used in whole or in part for the manufacture, sale, or distribution of goods. We do not cover an Other Structure used for professional, occupational, or commercial purposes. Subject to any applicable exception, this exclusion applies regardless of whether the use is continuous or occasional.

**B.12** We do not cover an Other Structure used to store property held for sale. We do not cover an Other Structure used to store property belonging to others for compensation. Unless the use is incidental to a covered private use, this exclusion applies to the entire structure.

**B.13** We do not cover an Other Structure used to house animals, except when the animals are household pets. We do not cover an Other Structure used for raising or boarding animals. Provided that the structure is not used for those purposes, ordinary storage of pet supplies does not change its classification.

**B.14** We do not cover an Other Structure used for farming, ranching, or agricultural purposes. We do not cover an Other Structure used for cultivation, harvesting, or processing of crops. This exclusion applies unless the use is solely for your personal, noncommercial needs.

**B.15** We do not cover an Other Structure used as a dwelling for any person. We do not cover an Other Structure used as a place of lodging. Unless the structure is occupied only as an incidental part of a covered private use, this exclusion applies.

**B.16** We do not cover an Other Structure that is vacant and unoccupied when loss occurs if the vacancy or unoccupancy contributes to the loss. We do not cover loss caused by neglect of a vacant or unoccupied structure. Subject to the terms of the policy, we cover a covered cause of loss that is unrelated to the vacancy or unoccupancy.

**B.17** We do not cover an Other Structure that is under construction when construction is abandoned. We do not cover materials or supplies left at a construction site after construction is abandoned. Unless construction is resumed and the structure is again maintained, this exclusion applies.

**B.18** We do not cover a structure used to contain or control water, except a private swimming pool. We do not cover a dock, wharf, or pier. Subject to the terms applying to watercraft-related property, this exclusion applies whether the structure is attached to land or extends over water.

**B.19** We do not cover a structure used to contain animals, except a structure used for household pets. We do not cover a structure used for commercial animal activity. Unless the structure is used solely for a covered private purpose, this exclusion applies.

**B.20** We do not cover a fence, retaining wall, or similar boundary or landscaping structure when it is not part of a covered building. We do not cover paving, patios, or other surfaces attached to the ground. Provided that an item is a covered building, we determine its status under the provisions for Other Structures.

**B.21** We do not cover a structure that you do not own unless you have a duty to insure it. We do not cover property held solely for the benefit of another person. Subject to your insurable interest, we cover a structure that you are legally responsible to insure.

**B.22** We cover a covered loss to an Other Structure when the loss is caused by a covered peril. We determine the amount of loss under the applicable valuation provisions. Unless the loss is excluded, we pay no more than the applicable Coverage B limit.

**B.23** You must protect an Other Structure from further damage after a loss. You must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the structure. Provided that the repairs are made solely to prevent further damage, we consider their reasonable cost in determining the covered loss.

**B.24** You must give us prompt notice of a loss involving an Other Structure. You must identify the structure and describe the cause of loss. Subject to our rights to inspect the damage, you must preserve the damaged property for examination.

**B.25** You must provide records and documents that relate to the ownership, use, condition, and value of an Other Structure. You must cooperate with our investigation of the loss. Unless we agree otherwise, you must not dispose of damaged property before we inspect it.

**B.26** You must take reasonable steps to secure an Other Structure after a loss. You must prevent unauthorized entry when the structure has been damaged. Provided that the steps are reasonable and necessary, we consider their cost in adjusting the covered loss.

**B.27** You must notify us if the use of an Other Structure changes. You must notify us if an Other Structure becomes vacant, unoccupied, or under construction. Subject to the terms of the policy, a change in use may affect whether the structure is covered.

**B.28** You must maintain an Other Structure in reasonably good condition. You must correct a known condition that could cause further damage. Unless a condition is sudden and accidental, we do not cover loss caused by lack of maintenance.

**B.29** We do not cover wear and tear, marring, or deterioration of an Other Structure. We do not cover inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical breakdown. Subject to the exclusions that apply, we cover ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**B.30** We do not cover damage caused by insects, vermin, birds, rodents, or other animals. We do not cover damage caused by nesting or infestation. Unless a covered peril causes ensuing loss, this exclusion applies to the affected Other Structure.

**B.31** We do not cover damage caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of an Other Structure. We do not cover damage caused by soil conditions or pressure of the ground. Subject to the applicable exclusions, we cover ensuing loss that is otherwise covered.

**B.32** We do not cover an Other Structure used in violation of law or regulation when the violation contributes to the loss. We do not cover the cost of complying with a law or regulation. Provided that the loss is otherwise covered, we cover the resulting physical damage to the Other Structure.

**B.33** We do not cover the cost to repair or replace an Other Structure because of a construction, design, or workmanship defect. We do not cover the cost to correct faulty, inadequate, or defective maintenance. Unless a covered peril causes ensuing loss, these costs are not covered.

**B.34** We do not cover an Other Structure that has been abandoned or is intended for demolition. We do not cover loss caused by demolition or removal of the structure. Subject to the terms of the policy, we cover a covered loss that occurs before abandonment or intended demolition.

**B.35** We apply the Coverage B limit separately from the amount payable for covered property under Coverage A. We do not increase the Coverage B limit because more than one Other Structure is damaged in the same occurrence. Provided that the damaged property qualifies as an Other Structure, the applicable limit applies to the covered loss.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover personal property owned or used by you while it is anywhere in the world. We cover the property described in this Coverage C only if it is personal property. We do not cover property that is excluded or limited by this policy.

**C.2** The limit of liability for Coverage C is fifty percent of the limit of liability for Coverage A. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we will pay for covered loss to personal property. The limit of liability is not increased because covered property is located at more than one premises.

**C.3** We cover personal property owned by you or used by you while at the residence premises. We cover the property whether it is inside or outside a building. This coverage applies subject to all applicable conditions, exclusions, and limitations.

**C.4** We cover personal property owned by you or used by you while temporarily away from the residence premises. We cover the property while it is in your possession or in the possession of a member of your household. We do not cover property that is permanently located at another residence occupied by you.

**C.5** We cover personal property owned by a guest or residence employee while the property is at the residence premises. We cover the property only if it is not otherwise insured. We do not cover property belonging to a roomer, boarder, or tenant who is not a member of your household.

**C.6** We cover personal property owned or used by a resident employee while the property is at the residence premises. We cover the property for covered causes of loss. We do not cover property owned by a resident employee if it is used in a business.

**C.7** We cover personal property that you acquire after the policy period begins. We cover the property when it becomes your property and is located at the residence premises. We do not cover property acquired primarily for sale or delivery to another person.

**C.8** We cover personal property in your newly acquired principal residence. We cover the property while you occupy the residence as your principal residence. We do not cover property located at premises that you do not occupy as your residence.

**C.9** We cover household and personal effects used by you or a member of your household. We cover furniture, furnishings, clothing, and similar personal property. We do not cover any property specifically excluded by this policy.

**C.10** We cover appliances and equipment used for personal, family, or household purposes. We cover the property whether it is permanently installed or movable. We do not cover a structure or other property that is insured under another coverage.

**C.11** We cover property of students who are related to you and who reside at the residence premises while attending school. We cover the property while the student maintains a residence away from the residence premises. We do not cover property of a student who is not a resident of your household.

**C.12** We cover personal property in a self-storage facility. We cover the property while it remains owned or used by you. We do not cover property in a facility used for commercial storage of property belonging to others.

**C.13** We cover personal property in a bank, safe-deposit facility, or other secure location. We cover the property for covered causes of loss. We do not cover property that is not owned or used by you.

**C.14** We cover personal property in a vehicle. We cover the property while it is in the vehicle or being loaded into or unloaded from the vehicle. We do not cover a vehicle itself under this Coverage C.

**C.15** We cover personal property in a watercraft. We cover the property while it is on the watercraft or being loaded into or unloaded from the watercraft. We do not cover the watercraft under this Coverage C.

**C.16** We cover personal property in an aircraft. We cover the property while it is on the aircraft or being loaded into or unloaded from the aircraft. We do not cover the aircraft under this Coverage C.

**C.17** We cover personal property in a detached structure at the residence premises. We cover the property when it is used for personal, family, or household purposes. We do not cover property used in a business unless this policy provides coverage for that use.

**C.18** We cover personal property in a structure rented to you for residential purposes. We cover the property while you occupy the structure. We do not cover property belonging to the owner of the structure.

**C.19** We cover personal property that is removed from the residence premises to prevent loss from a covered cause of loss. We cover the property while it is being protected or stored. We do not cover loss that occurs because the property was voluntarily abandoned.

**C.20** We cover personal property that is damaged while being moved to a new residence. We cover the property while it is in your possession or in the possession of a mover. We do not cover loss caused by your failure to protect the property.

**C.21** We cover personal property that is damaged while being transported by a carrier. We cover the property while it is being transported to or from the residence premises. We do not cover loss caused by delay, loss of market, or inherent defect.

**C.22** We cover personal property that is damaged while being repaired or serviced. We cover the property when the damage results from a covered cause of loss. We do not cover the cost of repairing or replacing defective workmanship unless covered damage also occurs.

**C.23** We cover personal property that is damaged while being cleaned. We cover the property when the damage results from a covered cause of loss. We do not cover the cost of cleaning property that is not damaged.

**C.24** We cover personal property that is damaged while being altered. We cover the property when the damage results from a covered cause of loss. We do not cover the cost of correcting an alteration that does not result in covered damage.

**C.25** We cover personal property that is damaged by a covered cause of loss while outside the residence premises. We cover the property subject to the applicable limit of liability. We do not cover property located at a premises that is rented to others for occupancy.

**C.26** We cover money and precious metals owned by you. The most we will pay for loss to money and precious metals is three hundred dollars. Except that the limit applies to the property described in this provision regardless of where the property is located, the applicable Coverage C limit does not apply separately.

**C.27** We cover jewelry, watches, and precious stones owned by you. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of jewelry, watches, and precious stones is three thousand dollars. Subject to that limitation, we cover the property for any covered cause of loss.

**C.28** We cover firearms and related equipment owned by you. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of firearms is three thousand five hundred dollars. Except that the limitation applies only to theft, we cover the property for other covered causes of loss.

**C.29** We cover watercraft, including their trailers, owned by you. The most we will pay for loss to watercraft, including trailers, is two thousand dollars. Subject to that limitation, we cover the property for covered causes of loss.

**C.30** We cover silverware, silver-plated ware, goldware, and gold-plated ware owned by you. The most we will pay for loss caused by theft of silverware is five thousand dollars. Except that the limitation applies to theft, we cover the property for other covered causes of loss.

**C.31** We cover business property located on the residence premises. The most we will pay for loss to business property on the residence premises is five thousand dollars. Subject to that limitation, we cover the property for covered causes of loss.

**C.32** We cover electronic apparatus located in a motor vehicle. The most we will pay for loss to electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle is two thousand dollars. Except that the limitation applies to property located in a motor vehicle, we cover the property for covered causes of loss.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** "Coverage D" applies when a covered loss makes the residence premises unfit for its intended use. The most we will pay under Coverage D is twenty percent of the Coverage A limit. Except that, payment is subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions that apply to this Coverage.

You must cooperate with us in determining whether the residence premises is fit for use. You must promptly tell us about any condition that affects the use of the residence premises.

**D.5** An insured must maintain the household's customary standard of living as nearly as practicable. You must incur the expense before we will consider it for payment. Except that, we will not pay an expense that is not reasonable, necessary, or related to the covered loss.

**D.6** An insured must keep records of expenses for which payment is requested. You must provide receipts, invoices, rental agreements, or other information that we reasonably request. Except that, we may consider other reliable evidence when ordinary records are not available because of the covered loss.

**D.7** We cover Additional Living Expense when the residence premises cannot be safely occupied because of a covered loss. The expense may include the reasonable cost of temporary lodging, meals, transportation, and other necessary household services. Except that, we do not pay more than the amount reasonably required to maintain the household's normal standard of living.

**D.8** We cover Fair Rental Value when a portion of the residence premises is rented to others and cannot be occupied because of a covered loss. The covered rental value is reduced by expenses that do not continue while the portion is unoccupied. Except that, we will not pay rental value that you could have avoided by reasonable action.

**D.9** An insured must make reasonable efforts to find suitable temporary housing when the residence premises is unfit for occupancy. You must consider available housing that reasonably accommodates the household's needs. Except that, you are not required to accept housing that presents an unreasonable health, safety, or accessibility concern.

**D.10** We cover the reasonable cost of moving household property to temporary housing when the move is necessary because of a covered loss. We also cover the reasonable cost of storing that property when storage is necessary. Except that, we do not cover the cost of moving or storing property for your convenience or preference.

**D.11** We cover the reasonable cost of returning household property to the residence premises after it becomes fit for occupancy. An insured must arrange the return within a reasonable time after the residence premises becomes suitable for occupancy. Except that, we do not cover return costs caused by a delay that is not related to the covered loss.

**D.12** We cover the reasonable cost of temporary care for domestic animals when that care is necessary because temporary housing does not permit the animals. An insured must use reasonable efforts to obtain temporary housing that permits the animals. Except that, we do not cover costs resulting from a choice to use housing that excludes the animals when suitable housing that permits them is reasonably available.

**D.13** We cover the reasonable cost of temporary storage for household property when storage is necessary to protect the property from damage during repairs. An insured must take reasonable measures to protect stored property. Except that, we do not cover loss to property caused by neglect of those measures.

**D.14** We cover Additional Living Expense when a "Civil Authority" prohibits use of the residence premises because of a covered loss. The prohibition must directly result from the covered loss. Except that, an order issued solely because of a general public concern does not create coverage under this provision.

**D.15** An insured must provide us with a copy of any order issued by a Civil Authority that affects use of the residence premises. You must identify the covered loss that resulted in the order. Except that, we may accept other reliable evidence when the order is not available to you.

**D.16** We do not cover Additional Living Expense caused solely by your failure to maintain the residence premises in a condition suitable for occupancy. We do not cover expense caused solely by a voluntary decision to leave the residence premises. Except that, this exclusion does not apply when the decision is reasonably necessary to protect persons or property from a covered loss.

**D.17** We do not cover Fair Rental Value for a portion of the residence premises that was vacant or unoccupied before the covered loss. We do not cover rental value that would not have been received from the tenant. Except that, this exclusion does not apply when the vacancy or nonoccupancy results from repairs for a covered loss.

**D.18** We do not cover loss of rental income caused by a tenant's failure to pay rent when the portion of the residence premises remains fit for occupancy. We do not cover a reduction in rent that is unrelated to a covered loss. Except that, we may cover Fair Rental Value when the tenant cannot safely occupy the portion because of a covered loss.

**D.19** An insured must notify us promptly when the residence premises becomes fit for occupancy. You must provide access for inspection when we reasonably request it. Except that, we will not require access that would unreasonably interfere with necessary repairs or personal safety.

**D.20** We may inspect the residence premises and temporary housing to determine the amount and necessity of a claimed expense. You must cooperate with our inspection and provide information relevant to the claim. Except that, an inspection does not waive any policy condition or establish that a loss is covered.

**D.21** We cover Additional Living Expense and Fair Rental Value only to the extent that the expense or loss is caused by the covered loss. We will separate covered expense from expense that arises from another cause. Except that, we will not pay an expense that cannot reasonably be distinguished from an excluded expense.

**D.22** We do not cover increased living expenses or lost rental value resulting from an ordinance, law, regulation, or public authority requirement that does not arise from a covered loss. We do not cover costs incurred to bring the residence premises into compliance with such a requirement. Except that, this exclusion does not apply to an expense otherwise covered that is increased solely because compliance is required as part of covered repairs.

**D.23** An insured must promptly advise us of any other insurance, benefit, reimbursement, or payment that may apply to the same Additional Living Expense or Fair Rental Value. You must assist us in recovering amounts for which another person or organization may be responsible. Except that, this duty does not require you to incur an expense or surrender a legal right that is not reasonably related to the claim.

**D.24** We will pay covered Additional Living Expense and Fair Rental Value as the loss is incurred and supported. We may make payments directly to you, the provider of services, or another person entitled to payment. Except that, payment to another person does not increase the amount available under Coverage D.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure against risk of "direct physical loss" to "covered property" unless the loss is excluded or limited by this form. Loss must involve an actual alteration of covered property.

**P.2** We cover direct physical loss caused by a peril that is not excluded. Coverage applies only while covered property is at an insured location.

**P.3** We cover loss caused by a sudden and accidental event that results in direct physical loss. We do not cover deterioration, wear, or other gradual damage merely because it becomes apparent after a sudden event.

**P.4** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance, law, regulation, or governmental requirement regulating construction, repair, replacement, demolition, zoning, or occupancy. This exclusion applies whether the enforcement is direct or results from damage to covered property.

**P.5** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudslide, sinkhole collapse, subsidence, settling, expansion, contraction, rising, shifting, or erosion of earth. This exclusion applies whether earth movement is caused by natural forces or human activity.

**P.6** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, leaks through, or flows through any part of a building or structure.

**P.7** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these sources. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or otherwise.

**P.8** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up or overflows from a sewer, drain, sump, or related system. This exclusion applies whether the discharge originates on or away from an insured location.

**P.9** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters a building through a foundation, wall, floor, roof, window, door, or other opening. We do cover direct physical loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, or hail entering through an opening created by a covered peril.

**P.10** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage, leakage, overflow, or discharge of water, steam, vapor, or sewage. This exclusion applies whether the condition is hidden or visible.

**P.11** We do not cover loss caused by backup, overflow, or discharge from a plumbing system that is not located on an insured location. We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain a plumbing system in a condition that prevents foreseeable leakage.

**P.12** We do not cover loss caused by power failure or interruption occurring away from an insured location. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that results from power failure occurring on an insured location.

**P.13** We do not cover loss caused by failure of a utility service, communication service, heating service, cooling service, or protective service. This exclusion applies even if the failure results from damage to equipment serving an insured location.

**P.14** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage when reasonably possible.

## II.E — Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** We will pay damages for which an "insured" is legally liable because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence." The bodily injury or property damage must arise from a covered occurrence.

**E.2** We will provide a defense at our expense by counsel of our choice against a "suit" seeking damages covered under this Coverage E. We may investigate and settle any claim or suit as we consider appropriate.

**E.3** Our duty to defend ends when we have paid the applicable limit of liability for damages. We have no duty to defend any suit seeking damages not covered under this Coverage E.

**E.4** We will pay reasonable expenses incurred by an insured at our request in the investigation or defense of a claim or suit. We will not pay expenses incurred by an insured without our consent.

**E.5** We will pay reasonable costs taxed against an insured in a suit we defend. We will pay interest on a judgment as required by law while we have a duty to defend the suit.

**E.6** We will pay premiums on bonds required in a suit we defend. We are not obligated to furnish any bond.

**E.7** Coverage applies only to bodily injury or property damage that occurs during the policy period. Coverage applies only when the occurrence takes place within the coverage territory.

**E.8** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury resulting from the use of reasonable force by an insured to protect persons or property.

**E.9** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of or in connection with a "business" conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted for profit or not.

**E.10** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the rendering of or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the professional services are performed by an insured or by another person for whom an insured is legally responsible.

**E.11** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a business pursuit of an insured. This exclusion does not apply to activities ordinarily incident to nonbusiness pursuits.

**E.12** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle" owned or operated by an insured. This exclusion applies to any person vicariously liable for the acts or omissions of an insured.

**E.13** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle owned by or rented to an insured. This exclusion applies while the motor vehicle is used in connection with a business.

**E.14** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an "aircraft." This exclusion applies whether the aircraft is owned, operated, rented, borrowed, or entrusted by an insured.

**E.15** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "watercraft." This exclusion applies when the watercraft is used to carry persons or property for a charge.

**E.16** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the entrustment of a motor vehicle, aircraft, or watercraft to any person by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the insured alleges negligent supervision, hiring, training, or control.

**E.17** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the transmission was actual, alleged, or threatened.

**E.18** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of sexual molestation, corporal punishment, or physical or mental abuse. This exclusion applies whether the conduct was committed by an insured or by another person for whom an insured is legally responsible.

**E.19** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of a controlled substance. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of prescription drugs by a person following the lawful instructions of a licensed medical provider.

**E.20** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care, custody, or control of an insured.

**E.21** We do not cover bodily injury to an insured. We do not cover bodily injury to any person eligible to receive benefits voluntarily provided by an insured under workers compensation or similar law.

**E.22** We do not cover any obligation of an insured under workers compensation, disability benefits, unemployment compensation, or similar law. We do not cover any obligation imposed by law because of bodily injury to an employee of an insured arising out of employment.

**E.23** We do not cover property damage caused by an insured to property owned by, rented to, occupied by, or in the care of another insured. This exclusion applies regardless of the legal theory asserted.

**E.24** We do not cover liability assumed by an insured under a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**E.33** Coverage under this Coverage E applies separately to each insured. This provision does not increase the applicable limit of liability for any occurrence.

## II.F — Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

**F.1** We will pay the necessary medical expenses incurred by a person who sustains "bodily injury" caused by an "occurrence." The bodily injury must be caused by an accident.

**F.2** We will pay necessary medical expenses only if they are incurred within three years from the date of the accident. The expenses must be medically necessary and reasonable in amount.

**F.3** Medical expenses include reasonable charges for medical, surgical, dental, hospital, nursing, ambulance, professional nursing, prosthetic, and funeral services. Medical expenses also include reasonable charges for x-ray and other diagnostic services.

**F.4** We will pay medical expenses regardless of fault. Payment under this coverage does not constitute an admission of liability by an insured.

**F.5** We will pay medical expenses for a person who is on an "insured location" with the permission of an insured. The person must sustain bodily injury arising out of the condition of the insured location or the activities of an insured.

**F.6** We will pay medical expenses for a person who is away from an insured location if the bodily injury arises out of a condition on the insured location. The condition must be one for which an insured is legally responsible.

**F.7** We will pay medical expenses for a person who is away from an insured location if the bodily injury is caused by the activities of an insured. The activities must not be excluded by this policy.

**F.8** We will pay medical expenses for a "residence employee" who sustains bodily injury arising out of and in the course of the residence employee's employment by an insured. This coverage applies only when the employment is not excluded by law.

**F.9** An insured must provide us with prompt notice of an accident that may give rise to a claim under this coverage. Notice must include sufficient information to identify the injured person and the circumstances of the accident.

**F.10** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide records, authorizations, and other information that we reasonably request.

**F.11** A person seeking payment under this coverage must submit to physical examination by a physician selected by us when we reasonably require. We will pay the reasonable expense of an examination we request.

**F.12** A person seeking payment under this coverage must authorize us to obtain medical records and other records relevant to the claim. The authorization must permit us to evaluate the claimed medical expenses.

**F.13** Payment of medical expenses to or for an injured person reduces the amount available under this coverage for that bodily injury. We may pay the injured person, a person providing medical services, or another person legally entitled to receive payment.

**F.14** We do not cover bodily injury to you or to any "insured." We also do not cover bodily injury to a person who is regularly residing in your household.

**F.15** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of or in the course of employment by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to bodily injury sustained by a residence employee when coverage is provided under this section.

**F.16** We do not cover bodily injury for which benefits are payable or required to be provided under workers compensation, disability benefits, occupational disease, or similar law. We do not cover bodily injury for which an insured has an obligation under such law.

**F.17** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of a "business" conducted from an insured location or engaged in by an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not the business is operated for profit.

**F.18** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of the rendering of or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies even if the services are performed by an insured who is not engaged in a business.

**F.19** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle. This exclusion applies to a motor vehicle owned or operated by, or rented or loaned to, an insured.

**F.20** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft. This exclusion applies whether the aircraft is operated by an insured or by another person.

**F.21** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft subject to registration. This exclusion applies while the watercraft is away from an insured location.

**F.22** We do not cover bodily injury that is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting bodily injury differs in kind or degree from that expected or intended.

**F.23** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the transmission is alleged to be negligent, intentional, or otherwise.

**F.24** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of sexual molestation, corporal punishment, or physical or mental abuse. This exclusion applies whether committed by an insured or by another person for whom an insured may be legally responsible.

**F.25** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or an act of a military force. This exclusion applies regardless of the cause of the bodily injury.

**F.26** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of a "nuclear hazard." This exclusion applies whether the nuclear hazard is accidental or intentional.

**F.27** We do not cover medical expenses for bodily injury arising from an illegal act committed by or at the direction of an insured. This exclusion does not apply to an act committed in lawful self-defense.

## II.E2 — Section II — Additional Coverages

**II.1** We will pay expenses incurred by an "insured" in the defense of a "claim" or "suit" alleging "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of an "occurrence." We will pay these expenses even if the allegations are groundless, false, or fraudulent.

**II.4** We will pay premiums on bonds required in a suit that we defend. We are not obligated to furnish or apply for a bond.

**II.8** We will pay reasonable expenses incurred by an insured for "first aid" to others for bodily injury caused by an occurrence. The first aid must be provided at the time of the occurrence.

**II.9** An insured must give us prompt notice of bodily injury for which first aid expenses are claimed. An insured must provide records and other information supporting the expenses.

**II.10** We do not cover first aid expenses for bodily injury to an insured. We do not cover expenses for services provided by an insured.

**II.11** We do not cover expenses that are not reasonably related to immediate first aid. We do not cover expenses incurred after the injured person has received professional medical care, unless we request the expense.

**II.12** We will pay for property damage to property of others caused by an insured. This coverage applies whether or not an insured is legally liable for the property damage.

**II.13** Property damage must arise from an occurrence. The damaged property must belong to a person other than an insured.

**II.14** We will determine the amount payable based on the reasonable cost to repair or replace the damaged property. We may elect to repair or replace the property instead of making payment.

**II.15** An insured must protect damaged property from further damage when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the property before it is repaired, replaced, or disposed of.

**II.16** The most we will pay for property damage under this coverage is two thousand five hundred dollars. This limit applies regardless of the number of persons seeking payment.

**II.17** An insured must give us prompt notice of property damage for which coverage is sought. The notice must identify the damaged property and the circumstances of the occurrence.

**II.18** An insured must provide a signed statement describing the property damage when we request it. An insured must assist us in obtaining statements, records, and other evidence.

**II.19** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. We do not cover property damage to property owned jointly by an insured and another person.

**II.20** We do not cover property damage to property owned by or rented to a tenant of an insured. We do not cover property damage to property in the care, custody, or control of an insured.

**II.21** We do not cover property damage arising out of an insured's "business" pursuits. This exclusion does not apply when the property damage arises from activities ordinarily incidental to nonbusiness pursuits.

**II.22** We do not cover property damage caused intentionally by an insured. We do not cover property damage resulting from an insured's criminal act.

**II.24** The association must have authority to make the loss assessment. You must be legally obligated to pay your share of the assessment.

**II.30** We do not cover a loss assessment arising from property owned by an insured. We do not cover an assessment for damage to property in the care, custody, or control of an insured.
