# HO-5 Homeowners 5 — Comprehensive Form (Edition 2022-06)




## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** We will provide the insurance described in this "policy" in return for the "premium" and subject to all applicable provisions. This policy applies only while it is in force.

**AGR.2** We insure you and any other "insured" for covered loss, damage, expense, or liability arising from an "occurrence" to the extent provided by this policy. Coverage applies only when the applicable conditions are met.

**AGR.3** We will pay for "direct physical loss" to "covered property" when that loss is covered by this policy. We do not cover loss that is excluded or limited by this policy.

**AGR.4** We will pay damages for which an insured is legally liable when coverage applies. We may investigate and settle any "claim" or suit as we consider appropriate.

**AGR.5** We will provide a defense for an insured against a covered claim or suit. Our duty to defend ends when the applicable "limit of liability" has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements.

**AGR.6** Coverage for property applies only to the extent of your financial interest in that property. Coverage relating to your "residence premises" applies only while the property meets the requirements of this policy.

**AGR.7** An insured must comply with all duties imposed by this policy after a loss, occurrence, claim, or suit. Failure to comply with those duties may affect coverage when the failure prejudices us.

**AGR.8** We will reduce covered payments by the applicable "deductible." We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**AGR.9** We do not cover loss, damage, expense, or liability arising from concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud by an insured concerning this policy or a claim. This provision applies whether the conduct occurs before or after a loss.

**AGR.10** This policy contains the agreement between you and us concerning the insurance provided. A waiver or change is effective only when made by us in writing.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined by applying depreciation for age, condition, and obsolescence to replacement cost.  
It includes the reduced value of property that has been used or has deteriorated.  
It does not include sentimental value or the cost of improving property beyond its condition immediately before the loss.  
For example, the value of a worn appliance includes depreciation and does not include the cost of a newer model with added features.  

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the reasonable cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.  
It includes necessary labor and materials used to restore the damaged property.  
It does not include the cost of improvements, additions, or changes that increase the value or utility of the property.  
For example, the cost to replace damaged flooring with comparable flooring is included, while the added cost to install a premium material is not included.  

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, which results in bodily injury or property damage.  
All bodily injury or property damage resulting from the same general harmful conditions is considered a single occurrence.  
It includes accidental damage caused by an unexpected event.  
For example, damage caused by an accidental overflow is included, while unrelated accidents are not included in the same occurrence.  

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the land and structures used in connection with that dwelling.  
It includes structures attached to the dwelling and grounds used for residential purposes.  
It does not include land or structures that are not used in connection with your residence.  
For example, an attached garage is included, while a separate property used solely for rental purposes is not included.  

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or persons in your care.  
It includes a resident relative who lives with you as part of your household.  
It does not include a tenant, boarder, or guest who is not a resident of your household or in your care.  
For example, a resident child in your care is included, while a visiting friend is not included.  

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations used by an insured in connection with the residence premises.  
It includes land owned by an insured that is used for nonbusiness purposes and a temporary residence occupied by an insured.  
It does not include premises used primarily for business or property owned by another person that is not used by an insured.  
For example, a temporary vacation residence occupied by you is included, while an office leased for commercial activity is not included.  

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain.  
It includes services performed regularly for payment and property used principally for commercial purposes.  
It does not include personal activities that are not undertaken for compensation or financial gain.  
For example, operating a paid repair service is included, while selling personal belongings on an occasional basis is not included.  

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or for use away from public roads.  
It includes an automobile, truck, motorcycle, and similar motorized land conveyance.  
It does not include a vehicle in dead storage on an insured location or a device moved solely by human power.  
For example, a car is included, while a bicycle is not included.  

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises.  
It includes a person performing domestic services, groundskeeping, or similar household work for an insured.  
It does not include an independent contractor who controls the manner and means of performing the work.  
For example, a housekeeper employed by you is included, while a separately operated landscaping business is not included.  

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death.  
It includes physical injury caused by an accident or harmful condition.  
It does not include emotional distress, humiliation, or other mental injury that does not result from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.  
For example, a broken arm is included, while disappointment arising from damaged property is not included.  

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.  
It includes resulting loss of use of property that has not been physically injured.  
It does not include financial loss, loss of market value, or injury to property that is not tangible.  
For example, damage to furniture is included, while lost investment income is not included.  

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unforeseen, and unintended.  
It includes an event that happens unexpectedly and without design or intention.  
It does not include deterioration, gradual leakage, wear, or damage that develops over time.  
For example, an unexpected pipe burst is included, while seepage from a long-standing leak is not included.  

**DEF.13** "vacant" means a dwelling or structure that lacks the substantial furnishings, occupancy, or customary activity necessary for its intended use.  
It includes a property that has been abandoned or left without meaningful residential use.  
It does not include a furnished residence that is temporarily unoccupied but remains available for customary use.  
For this purpose, the claims manual's duration test applies the definitionthe claims manual's duration test applies the definition (see Property Claims Handling Manual, C3 Chapter 3 — Water Losses).  

**DEF.14** "named storm" means a tropical cyclone or similar weather system that has been assigned an official name by a governmental weather authority.  
It includes wind, rain, and other weather conditions directly associated with that identified system.  
It does not include an unnamed thunderstorm, ordinary rainstorm, or weather event not associated with an officially named system.  
For example, damage caused by an officially named hurricane is included, while damage caused by an unrelated local storm is not included.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the “dwelling” described in the Declarations and used principally as a private residence on the “residence premises.” Coverage under this provision is subject to the applicable limit of insurance and the terms of this policy.

**A.2** We cover structures attached to the dwelling by a foundation, wall, roof, or other permanent connection. We treat attached structures as part of the dwelling.

**A.3** We cover materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises when they are intended for use in construction, alteration, or repair of the dwelling. The materials and supplies must be your property or property for which you are legally responsible.

**A.4** We cover building equipment permanently installed in the dwelling. We also cover fixtures that are part of the dwelling.

**A.5** We cover property used to service the dwelling when the property is located on the residence premises. This includes equipment used for heating, cooling, plumbing, electrical service, and similar dwelling functions.

**A.6** We do not cover land, including land on which the dwelling is located. We also do not cover the value of land resulting from a “loss.”

**A.7** The limit of insurance shown for Coverage A is the most we will pay for covered loss to the dwelling. This limit does not increase because more than one item of covered property is damaged in the same loss.

**A.8** Payment for covered loss to the dwelling reduces the applicable limit of insurance. We will restore the available limit only as provided by this policy.

**A.9** We will settle covered loss to the dwelling on a “replacement cost” basis, subject to the terms of this provision. Replacement cost means the reasonable cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality.

**A.10** We will settle covered loss to the dwelling at replacement cost when the amount of insurance applicable to the dwelling is at least eighty percent of the replacement cost of the dwelling at the time of loss. We may use available information to determine replacement cost.

**A.11** When the condition for replacement cost settlement is not met, we will settle the loss on an “actual cash value” basis. Actual cash value reflects depreciation and the condition of the damaged property immediately before the loss.

**A.12** We settle covered loss to roof surfacing at replacement cost, unless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attachedunless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attached (see HO 23 74 Actual Cash Value Loss Settlement — Roof Surfacing (Edition 2025-05), W.1 Coverage Provided). Any applicable roof settlement terms control the settlement of loss to roof surfacing.

**A.13** We will determine actual cash value from the cost to repair or replace the damaged property, less depreciation. We may consider the age, condition, useful life, and obsolescence of the property.

**A.14** We will not pay for depreciation that does not result from the covered loss. We will not pay for deterioration that existed before the loss.

**A.15** We will not pay more than the reasonable cost to repair damaged property or replace it with property of like kind and quality. We may elect to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property.

**A.16** We may use new materials where reasonably necessary to complete repair or replacement. We do not guarantee that repaired or replaced property will match undamaged property in color, texture, appearance, or design.

**A.17** We will not pay for any decrease in market value resulting from repair or replacement. We will not pay for loss of value because repaired property differs in appearance from undamaged property.

**A.18** We will pay no more than the amount you actually and necessarily spend to repair or replace damaged property when payment is based on replacement cost. Until repair or replacement is completed, we may pay actual cash value.

**A.19** If you repair or replace damaged property, the repair or replacement must be intended for substantially the same use as before the loss. We may settle on an actual cash value basis when the property is not repaired or replaced.

**A.20** We do not require you to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property. Your decision not to repair, rebuild, or replace does not increase the amount we will pay.

**A.21** We do not cover the increased cost to comply with an ordinance, law, or regulation governing construction, repair, or demolition of property under Coverage A. We also do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of an ordinance, law, or regulation.

**A.22** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, or alter any part of the dwelling that was not damaged by a covered loss. This includes costs incurred solely to obtain a uniform appearance.

**A.23** We do not cover the cost to excavate, grade, fill, or stabilize land except when the work is necessary to repair covered physical damage to the dwelling. Any payment remains subject to the Coverage A limit of insurance.

**A.24** We do not cover retaining walls, bulkheads, docks, piers, wharves, or similar structures unless they are attached to the dwelling. We also do not cover foundations for property that is not part of the dwelling.

**A.25** We do not cover outdoor property that is not permanently attached to the dwelling. This includes fences, paving, driveways, walks, and patios.

**A.26** We do not cover personal property under Coverage A. Personal property is covered only as provided by the applicable terms of this policy.

**A.27** We do not cover property owned by a tenant, boarder, roomer, or guest unless the property is part of the dwelling. We do not cover property owned by another person solely because it is located on the residence premises.

**A.28** We cover a portion of the dwelling rented to others when the rented portion remains part of the dwelling. Rental use does not increase the Coverage A limit of insurance.

**A.29** An “insured” must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property.

**A.30** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. Notice must identify the damaged property and provide available information concerning the cause and circumstances of the loss.

**A.31** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property as reasonably required.

**A.32** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property without preserving reasonable evidence of the damage.

**A.33** An insured must provide records, receipts, estimates, photographs, and other information reasonably requested to support the claim. An insured must identify any person or entity having an interest in the damaged property.

**A.34** An insured must submit a signed proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary to determine the amount of loss.

**A.35** We may inspect the dwelling and any damaged property before determining the amount of payment. Our inspection does not waive any policy term or defense.

**A.36** We may make payment to you and to any person or entity having an interest in the covered property. Payment to an interested person or entity does not increase the amount we will pay.

**A.37** We may take possession of damaged property after payment for the loss. We do not acquire title to the dwelling or other property solely by making a payment.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover "other structures" that are located on the "residence premises" and are separated from the dwelling by clear space. This coverage applies to structures that are not attached to the dwelling.

**B.2** A structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection remains an other structure. A structure attached to the dwelling is not covered as other structures.

**B.3** We cover other structures that are owned by you or in which you have an insurable interest. We pay only for your interest in covered property.

**B.4** We do not cover a structure used in whole or in part for "business" purposes. This exclusion does not apply to a structure used solely as a private garage.

**B.5** We do not cover a structure rented or held for rental to a person who is not a tenant of the dwelling. This exclusion does not apply to a private garage.

**B.6** The "limit of liability" for this coverage is ten percent of the limit of liability for the dwelling. This limit applies to all covered loss under this coverage.

**B.7** The limit of liability under this coverage is additional insurance. Payment under this coverage does not reduce the limit of liability for the dwelling.

**B.8** We cover "direct physical loss" to other structures that are covered under this provision. Coverage applies only while the property remains an other structure.

**B.9** Coverage for an other structure includes permanently attached fixtures and equipment. Coverage does not include property that is not permanently attached to the structure.

**B.10** We do not cover "personal property" located in or on an other structure under this coverage. Personal property does not become part of an other structure merely because it is stored there.

**B.11** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We do not cover the value of land caused by damage to an other structure.

**B.12** We do not cover an other structure in which you have no insurable interest. We will not pay more than your insurable interest in the damaged property.

**B.13** An other structure under construction is covered when it is intended to become an other structure located on the residence premises. Coverage ends when the structure no longer qualifies as an other structure.

**B.14** An other structure remains covered while it is being repaired, altered, or rebuilt. The repair, alteration, or rebuilding must relate to covered property.

**B.15** An other structure remains covered while temporarily unoccupied. Vacancy or unoccupancy does not increase the limit of liability or broaden coverage.

**B.16** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**B.17** An insured must make reasonable repairs necessary to protect covered property from further damage. We will consider reasonable repair expenses in adjusting a covered loss.

**B.18** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss involving an other structure. The notice must identify the damaged property and describe the known circumstances of the loss.

**B.19** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged property when we request it. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss claimed.

**B.20** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request to investigate a loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of records and documents that relate to the loss.

**B.21** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine the cause and extent of damage.

**B.22** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer questions truthfully and provide requested information concerning the loss.

**B.23** If you and we disagree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal. The appraisal must be conducted in accordance with the applicable policy conditions.

**B.24** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment of a covered loss.

**B.25** We will pay a covered loss to you unless another person is legally entitled to payment. Payment does not transfer ownership of property to us unless we agree otherwise.

**B.26** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may select the persons who perform repair, rebuilding, or replacement work.

**B.27** When we settle loss on an "actual cash value" basis, we determine value at the time of loss, subject to applicable depreciation. When we settle loss on a "replacement cost" basis, we will not pay more than the cost to repair or replace the damaged property with like kind and quality.

**B.28** An insured must not intentionally conceal or misrepresent any material fact relating to coverage or a loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss.

**B.29** An insured must comply with all duties that apply after a loss before bringing legal action against us. Legal action must be commenced within the time allowed by applicable law.

**B.30** An insured must disclose other insurance that may apply to a covered loss. We will not pay more than the amount of loss that remains after other applicable insurance is considered.

**B.31** An insured must notify us if damaged property is recovered after payment of a loss. We may require repayment of amounts attributable to recovered property.

**B.32** An insured must notify us of a change in ownership or use of an other structure that materially affects the risk. A change in ownership or use does not increase or broaden coverage.

**B.33** Coverage under this provision does not benefit a person or organization having custody of covered property. This provision does not create rights in a person or organization that is not an insured.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" while it is anywhere in the world. Covered property must be subject to a direct physical loss unless an exclusion applies.

**C.2** We cover personal property usual to the occupancy of the "residence premises." We cover property of others while it is in the care of an insured, subject to the conditions of this Coverage C.

**C.3** We cover property of a guest or residence employee while that property is at the residence premises. We cover that property only when you request coverage and the property is not otherwise insured.

**C.4** We cover property of a student who is an insured while the student is residing at a location away from the residence premises. The student must have been a resident of your household before moving to that location.

**C.5** We cover personal property of an insured who is temporarily residing away from the residence premises. The property remains subject to all applicable exclusions and special limits.

**C.6** We cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by a risk of direct physical loss unless the loss is excluded. Loss includes damage, destruction, or disappearance of covered property.

**C.7** We cover covered property while it is being moved, stored, repaired, or transported. Coverage applies only while the property remains owned or used by an insured.

**C.8** We cover property acquired by an insured after the policy begins if the property meets the definition of personal property. Coverage for acquired property is subject to the Coverage C limit and applicable special limits.

**C.9** The limit shown for "Coverage C" is fifty percent of the limit shown for "Coverage A." The Coverage C limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss under this coverage.

**C.10** The Coverage C limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, locations, items of property, or claims involved. Payment for loss under this coverage reduces the amount available for other covered loss under this coverage.

**C.11** We apply a "special limit" to loss of money, bank notes, bullion, precious metals, and stored value cards. The most we will pay for that loss is three hundred dollars.

**C.12** We apply a special limit to loss by "theft" of jewelry, watches, and precious or semiprecious stones. The most we will pay for that theft loss is four thousand five hundred dollars.

**C.13** We apply a special limit to loss by theft of firearms and related equipment. The most we will pay for that theft loss is three thousand five hundred dollars.

**C.14** We apply a special limit to loss of watercraft, including its trailer and accessories. The most we will pay for that loss is two thousand dollars.

**C.15** We apply a special limit to loss by theft of silverware, goldware, pewterware, platinumware, and articles plated with precious metal. The most we will pay for that theft loss is five thousand dollars.

**C.16** We apply a special limit to loss of "business property" while it is on the residence premises. The most we will pay for that loss is five thousand dollars.

**C.17** We apply a special limit to loss of electronic apparatus while it is in or upon a motor vehicle. The most we will pay for that loss is two thousand dollars.

**C.18** Special limits apply to the described property whether the property is owned solely by an insured or jointly with another person. A special limit applies without regard to the value assigned to the property by an insured.

**C.19** We do not cover property separately described and specifically insured under another policy. This exclusion applies only to the extent that other insurance provides coverage for the loss.

**C.20** We do not cover animals, birds, fish, or other living creatures. We do cover property used to care for an animal if that property is otherwise covered.

**C.21** We do not cover motor vehicles or equipment designed for use with a motor vehicle. We cover a motorized wheelchair, a power-driven mobility device, and equipment used solely to service the residence premises.

**C.22** We do not cover aircraft or parts of aircraft. We cover model or hobby aircraft that are not designed to carry a person or cargo.

**C.23** We do not cover property of roomers, boarders, tenants, or other residents who are not insureds. We cover property of an insured that is rented to another person only while the property remains personal property.

**C.24** We do not cover property held for sale by an insured. We cover property that is temporarily held for sale as part of a household activity if no other exclusion applies.

**C.25** We do not cover property used primarily for business purposes, except as otherwise provided by this coverage. This exclusion applies whether the business is conducted for profit or not.

**C.26** We do not cover property used in connection with a business conducted from a location away from the residence premises. We cover property that is personal in nature and only incidentally used in a business.

**C.27** We do not cover data, programs, or instructions stored in electronic media, except for the physical loss of the media itself. We cover the physical media when the media is otherwise covered property.

**C.28** We do not cover property that is contraband or property whose possession is unlawful. We do not cover property obtained through fraud or other dishonest means.

**C.29** We do not cover loss caused by an intentional act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss differs in kind or degree from that intended or expected.

**C.30** We do not cover loss arising out of any act committed by an insured with the intent to cause loss. We do not cover loss arising out of an act directed at a person, property, or interest.

**C.31** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not require an insured to endanger a person.

**C.32** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We cover resulting loss caused by a covered risk if the resulting loss is not otherwise excluded.

**C.33** We do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or any quality in property that causes it to damage or destroy itself. We cover resulting loss to other covered property when caused by a covered risk.

**C.34** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or decay. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered risk that results from such condition when the resulting loss is not otherwise excluded.

**C.35** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We cover direct physical loss to covered personal property caused by a covered risk.

**C.36** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire or another covered risk that results from such cause.

**C.37** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire or explosion resulting from pollutants when the resulting loss is not otherwise excluded.

**C.38** We do not cover loss caused by governmental seizure, confiscation, destruction, or forfeiture of property. We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of any law or ordinance regulating property.

**C.39** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, or revolution. We do not cover loss caused by a military force or an authority using military personnel.

**C.40** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes any nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination.

**C.41** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of power, communication, water, or other utility service when the failure occurs away from the residence premises. We cover resulting loss caused by a covered risk on the residence premises.

**C.42** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, or compaction. We cover resulting loss caused by a covered risk if the resulting loss is not otherwise excluded.

**C.43** We do not cover loss caused by the collapse of personal property unless the collapse is caused by a covered risk. We cover loss to personal property resulting from the collapse of a building or part of a building when not otherwise excluded.

**C.44** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, mudflow, or earth sinking, rising, or shifting. We cover direct loss caused by fire, explosion, or breakage of glass that results from earth movement.

**C.45** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. We do not cover water that backs up through sewers, drains, or sumps.

**C.46** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground, including water that exerts pressure on or seeps through a building. This exclusion applies whether the water is natural or otherwise caused.

**C.47** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler systems, or household appliances while the residence premises is vacant. We cover loss if an insured used reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off the water supply and drained the system.

**C.48** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies when the seepage or leakage occurs over an extended duration.

**C.49** We do not cover loss caused by theft from a dwelling under construction or of materials intended for use in construction. We do not cover theft from a dwelling that is vacant.

**C.50** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if the residence premises has been vacant. We cover resulting loss caused by a covered risk when the resulting loss is not otherwise excluded.

**C.51** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred solely to protect covered property from further covered damage.

**C.52** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not dispose of damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**C.53** An insured must provide us with records, receipts, photographs, inventories, and other information that we reasonably request to verify ownership, value, and loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a claim.

**C.54** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed, or stolen property when we request it. The inventory must identify the property and state the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss.

**C.55** An insured must show the damaged property to us as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to examine books, records, and documents that relate to the claimed loss.

**C.56** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the examination transcript if it is accurately recorded.

**C.57** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we request it. The statement must include information reasonably necessary for us to determine the amount and cause of loss.

**C.58** We may inspect the property and examine the facts relating to a claim. Our inspection does not constitute acceptance of coverage or an agreement as to value.

**C.59** We may require an appraisal when an insured and we fail to agree on the amount of loss. Each party must select a competent appraiser and cooperate in completing the appraisal.

**C.60** We will adjust covered loss with you unless another person is legally entitled to payment. We may make payment to an insured, a person having an insurable interest, or both.

**C.61** We will not pay more than the applicable limit for covered property. We will not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace the damaged property, subject to the applicable loss settlement provisions.

**C.62** We may repair, replace, or restore covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may make payment instead of repairing or replacing property.

**C.63** We may take all or part of damaged property at its agreed or appraised value after payment for the loss. Property that we take becomes our property.

**C.64** We may require an insured to assign to us rights of recovery against a person responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**C.65** We will not pay for loss to property if another policy provides coverage for the same loss, except as allowed by applicable law. We will pay our share of covered loss in accordance with the other insurance provisions.

**C.66** Loss to property owned jointly by an insured and another person is covered only to the extent of the insured's interest. We will not pay for the interest of a person who is not an insured.

**C.67** Property permanently removed from the residence premises remains covered if it otherwise qualifies as personal property. Coverage remains subject to all conditions, exclusions, and special limits.

**C.68** Property temporarily removed from the residence premises remains covered while it is in the custody or control of an insured. Coverage does not extend to property that is no longer owned or used by an insured.

**C.69** We cover personal property kept in a storage facility if it otherwise qualifies as covered property. Coverage remains subject to the applicable Coverage C limit.

**C.70** We cover personal property while it is entrusted to a repairer, cleaner, or other custodian. We do not cover loss caused by the dishonesty of a person to whom the property was voluntarily entrusted.

**C.71** We cover personal property while it is being shipped or delivered for personal use. We do not cover loss for which a carrier, bailee, or other person is legally responsible to the extent of that responsibility.

## I.D — Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** We cover "loss of use" described in this section when "direct physical loss" to property by a "peril insured against" makes that property unfit for its intended use. The property must be part of the "residence premises" where you reside.

**D.2** The limit of liability for "Coverage D" is twenty percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". This limit is the most we will pay for all loss of use covered under this section.

**D.3** We cover "Additional Living Expense" when a covered loss makes that part of the residence premises where you reside unfit to live in. We will pay the necessary increase in living expenses you incur so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living.

**D.4** Additional Living Expense must result directly from the covered loss. We will pay only expenses that are reasonable and necessary because you cannot live in the affected part of the residence premises.

**D.5** We determine Additional Living Expense by comparing your actual living expenses with the living expenses you would have incurred if no covered loss had occurred. We do not cover ordinary living expenses that you would have incurred regardless of the covered loss.

**D.6** We may pay Additional Living Expense for reasonable temporary accommodations, meals, transportation, storage, and other necessary expenses. The expense must be incurred by you or by a member of your household.

**D.7** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, alter, or improve damaged property under this coverage. We do not cover an expense that is payable as damages by another party.

**D.8** Payment for Additional Living Expense ends when the affected part of the residence premises is fit to live in. Payment also ends when you permanently relocate your household.

**D.9** We cover "Fair Rental Value" when a covered loss makes that part of the residence premises rented to others, or held for rental, unfit for its intended use. We will pay the fair rental value of that part of the residence premises less any charges and expenses that do not continue while it is unfit for use.

**D.10** Fair Rental Value must result from the covered loss. We will pay only for the period during which the rented part of the residence premises is not fit for its intended use.

**D.11** We do not cover lost rent resulting from the cancellation of a rental agreement that is not caused by the covered loss. We do not cover lost rent caused solely by a tenant's failure to pay rent.

**D.12** We do not cover an increase in Fair Rental Value resulting from improvements, additions, or changes made after the covered loss. We do not cover income from a business conducted on the residence premises.

**D.13** We cover Additional Living Expense and Fair Rental Value when a "Civil Authority" prohibits use of the residence premises because of direct physical loss to neighboring premises by a peril insured against. The prohibition must prevent you from using the residence premises for its intended purpose.

**D.14** Coverage for a prohibition by Civil Authority applies only while the prohibition remains in effect. We do not cover loss of use after access to the residence premises is restored unless direct physical loss otherwise makes the residence premises unfit for its intended use.

**D.15** We do not cover loss of use caused solely by loss of power, water, heat, communications, or other utility service away from the residence premises. This exclusion does not apply when the interruption results from direct physical loss to utility property on the residence premises by a peril insured against.

**D.16** We do not cover loss of use caused by your voluntary decision to leave the residence premises when it remains fit for its intended use. We do not cover expenses caused by your preference for accommodations that exceed your normal standard of living.

**D.17** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss that may result in a claim under this coverage. An insured must describe the condition that makes the residence premises unfit for its intended use.

**D.18** An insured must keep records supporting each Additional Living Expense and each claim for Fair Rental Value. An insured must provide us with receipts, invoices, rental records, and other documents we reasonably request.

**D.19** An insured must take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary increases in Additional Living Expense and Fair Rental Value. An insured must use reasonable efforts to make the residence premises fit for its intended use when repairs can be made.

**D.20** An insured must cooperate with us in investigating a claim under this coverage. An insured must allow us to inspect the residence premises and relevant records when reasonably necessary.

**D.21** We do not cover any expense or loss of rental value that has been paid or is payable from another source. We will not pay more than the actual covered loss.

**D.22** Payment under this coverage does not increase any other limit of liability in this policy. Payments for Additional Living Expense, Fair Rental Value, and loss caused by a prohibition by Civil Authority are subject to the Coverage D limit of liability.

**D.23** An insured must not abandon property to us. We may require reasonable information concerning available accommodations, repairs, rental arrangements, and other matters relevant to a claim under this coverage.

## I.S — Section I — Conditions

**S.1** The deductible applicable to a covered loss will be shown in the policy declarations. The deductible applies to each loss unless this policy provides otherwise.

**S.2** The deductible applicable to a covered loss may not be less than one thousand dollars. We will subtract the applicable deductible from the amount of covered loss.

**S.3** We will apply only the deductible that applies to the cause of loss involved. If more than one deductible could apply, we will apply the deductible that results in the greater reduction of our payment.

**S.4** The "maximum windstorm and hail deductible percentage" applies as stated in the policy declarations. We will administer that deductible according to its stated terms.

**S.5** The "deductible increase notice period" applies to a change in deductible that we make as permitted by law. A change will be effective only when we provide any notice required by law.

**S.6** An insured must promptly give us notice of a loss. Notice must identify the insured, the affected property, the location of the loss, and the circumstances known to the insured.

**S.7** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities when property is stolen, vandalized, or damaged by a criminal act. An insured must cooperate with any resulting investigation.

**S.8** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable expenses incurred for that purpose will be considered in determining a covered loss.

**S.9** An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect property from further damage. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses.

**S.10** An insured must not discard damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. This duty does not prevent emergency measures needed to protect persons or property.

**S.11** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine the cause and extent of loss.

**S.12** An insured must provide records, documents, and other information that we reasonably request to investigate a loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of those materials.

**S.13** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged or stolen property. The inventory must describe the property and state its actual cash value, replacement cost, amount of loss, and supporting information available to the insured.

**S.14** An insured must attach bills, receipts, and related documents to the inventory when available. An insured must explain the absence of requested supporting documents that are unavailable.

**S.15** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer material questions concerning the loss.

**S.16** An insured must produce other insureds, household members, employees, or representatives for examination when we reasonably request their testimony concerning the loss. This duty applies only to persons whose information is material to the loss.

**S.17** An insured must sign and submit a sworn statement when we reasonably request it. The statement must be true, complete, and based on information reasonably available to the insured.

**S.18** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. Cooperation includes providing access to relevant property, records, and persons.

**S.19** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses to protect covered property from further damage. We will not be bound by an unauthorized payment or obligation.

**S.20** An insured must promptly forward to us any notice, demand, summons, pleading, or legal paper relating to a claim or suit. An insured must not admit liability or settle a claim without our consent.

**S.21** An insured must preserve rights of recovery against any person or organization responsible for a loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**S.22** An insured must provide a signed, sworn proof of loss within sixty days after our request. The proof of loss must state the information we reasonably require to determine the amount and cause of loss.

**S.23** The proof of loss must identify the time and cause of loss. It must identify all persons having an interest in the property and all encumbrances on the property.

**S.24** The proof of loss must state the actual cash value and amount of loss for each item of property claimed. It must state the amount of any applicable deductible.

**S.25** The proof of loss must state all other insurance that may apply to the loss. It must identify any changes in title, use, occupancy, or substantial risk known to the insured.

**S.26** The proof of loss must state all receipts for expenses incurred in protecting property from further damage. It must include records supporting any claim for additional living expense or fair rental value.

**S.27** Our request for a proof of loss does not waive any policy condition, exclusion, limitation, or defense. Our investigation of a loss does not constitute acceptance of coverage.

**S.28** The "receipt acknowledgement period" applies to our acknowledgement of communications concerning a claim. Our acknowledgement does not constitute acceptance of coverage or liability.

**S.29** We may inspect damaged property before determining coverage or the amount of loss. Our inspection does not obligate us to repair, replace, or pay for any property.

**S.30** We may take samples, photographs, measurements, recordings, and statements during our investigation. An insured must reasonably assist us in obtaining information material to the loss.

**S.31** We may request that damaged property be retained for examination. An insured must preserve the property in its post-loss condition to the extent reasonably possible.

**S.32** We may obtain records from persons or organizations having information relevant to a claim when authorized by an insured or permitted by law. An insured must execute reasonable authorizations needed to obtain those records.

**S.33** We may require an insured to submit to a medical examination when bodily injury is claimed under this policy. The examination must be conducted by a physician selected by us and at our expense.

**S.34** We may require an insured to provide a recorded statement concerning a loss. The statement may be taken by us or by a person acting on our behalf.

**S.35** We may investigate a claim without prejudice to our rights. No action we take in investigating a claim will waive any right unless we expressly waive that right in writing.

**S.36** We will adjust each covered loss based on the information available to us. We may revise our adjustment if additional material information becomes available.

**S.37** We will determine the amount of loss based on the terms of this policy and the facts of the loss. We may consider repair estimates, replacement estimates, inventories, receipts, expert reports, and other relevant evidence.

**S.38** We may pay the actual cash value of damaged property until repair or replacement is completed when this policy permits payment on that basis. We will not pay more than the amount required under the applicable loss settlement terms.

**S.39** We may elect to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with like kind and quality. Our election may be made through persons selected by us.

**S.40** If we elect to repair, rebuild, or replace property, an insured must provide reasonable access to the property. An insured must cooperate with persons acting on our behalf to complete the work.

## II.S — Section II — Conditions

**S.1** The applicable "limit of liability" is the most we will pay for damages arising out of an "occurrence." It applies regardless of the number of "insureds," claims, persons injured, or persons making claims.

**S.2** This "policy" applies separately to each insured. This condition does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**S.3** If a "claim" is made or a "suit" is brought against an insured, the insured must notify us promptly. The notice must identify the insured and provide information reasonably available about the claim or suit.

**S.4** An insured must give us prompt notice of an occurrence or "offense" that may result in a claim. The notice must state the time, place, and circumstances of the occurrence or offense.

**S.5** An insured must provide the names and addresses of injured persons and available witnesses. An insured must also provide information concerning the nature and location of any injury or damage.

**S.6** An insured must preserve evidence relating to an occurrence, offense, claim, or suit. An insured must not alter, conceal, discard, or destroy relevant evidence without our consent.

**S.7** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. An insured must assist us in enforcing any right against a person or organization that may be liable.

**S.8** An insured must attend hearings, depositions, and other proceedings when we reasonably request. An insured must give truthful testimony and provide information reasonably available to the insured.

**S.9** An insured must promptly send us copies of notices, demands, summonses, pleadings, and other legal papers received in connection with a claim or suit. Delivery of those papers to us does not waive any policy condition.

**S.10** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. This condition does not apply to expenses reasonably incurred to protect persons or property from immediate harm.

**S.11** An insured must not interfere with our investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. An insured must not take action that prejudices our rights.

**S.12** At our request, an insured must provide signed statements describing the occurrence, offense, claim, or suit. The statements must be complete and accurate to the best of the insured's knowledge.

**S.13** At our request, an insured must submit to examination under oath. We may require the examination to address matters relevant to our obligations under this policy.

**S.14** At our request, an insured must authorize us to obtain records relating to a claim or suit. We will seek only records that are reasonably relevant to our investigation, settlement, or defense.

**S.15** We may investigate an occurrence, offense, claim, or suit. Our investigation does not obligate us to pay a claim that is not covered.

**S.16** We may settle a claim or suit when we consider settlement appropriate. Our settlement authority is subject to the applicable limit of liability and the terms of this policy.

**S.17** No insured may bring a legal action against us unless the insured has fully complied with the terms of this policy. A legal action against us must also be brought in accordance with applicable law.

**S.18** No person or organization may join us as a party to a suit against an insured. No person or organization may bring us into a suit against an insured.

**S.19** No person or organization may bring a legal action against us to determine an insured's liability for damages. This condition does not prevent a legal action after the insured's obligation has been determined by judgment or written agreement.

**S.20** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this policy. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not give a claimant rights against us that are not provided by this policy.

**S.21** If other valid and collectible insurance applies to a loss, we will pay our share of the covered damages. Our share is determined in accordance with the terms of this policy and the other insurance.

**S.22** We will not pay more than our share of covered damages when other insurance applies. This condition applies whether the other insurance is primary, excess, contingent, or otherwise available.

**S.23** This policy applies only to an occurrence or offense that takes place while this policy is in effect. Coverage ends when this policy is no longer in effect.

**S.24** We do not provide coverage for an insured who has concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance. This condition applies when the concealment or misrepresentation relates to this policy, an occurrence, a claim, or a suit.

**S.25** We do not provide coverage for an insured who has engaged in fraud relating to this policy, an occurrence, a claim, or a suit. Fraud by an insured does not affect coverage for another insured unless applicable law requires otherwise.

**S.26** If you die, we insure your legal representative while acting within the scope of that representative's duties. We also insure a person having temporary custody of your property, but only with respect to liability arising out of that custody.

**S.27** A person insured because of your death must comply with the duties imposed by this policy. That person's rights and duties are subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**S.28** If an insured has rights to recover damages from another person or organization, those rights are transferred to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**S.29** An insured must cooperate with us in the exercise of transferred rights of recovery. An insured must execute documents and take actions reasonably necessary to protect those rights.

**S.30** Amounts recovered by us through the exercise of rights of recovery will be applied to reimburse payments made under this policy. We will distribute any remaining amounts as required by applicable law.

**S.31** Assignment of this policy will not be valid without our written consent. This condition does not apply to transfer of rights after the death of an insured as provided by this policy.

**S.32** A waiver or change of a policy provision must be made by us in writing. Our failure to enforce a policy provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision later.

**S.33** A representative or agent may not change this policy unless we authorize the change in writing. A representation by a representative or agent does not alter this policy unless we confirm it in writing.

**S.34** If we adopt a change that broadens coverage without additional premium, the broadened coverage applies while this policy is in effect. The change applies only if it is adopted for the form of this policy.

**S.35** We may inspect property and operations that may give rise to liability under this policy. Our inspection does not constitute a warranty that property or operations are safe or comply with applicable law.

**S.36** We may make surveys and reports concerning property or operations. Our surveys and reports are for our use and do not create a duty to any person or organization.

**S.37** You must notify us promptly of a change in your mailing address or other contact information. We may rely on the contact information last provided to us.

**S.38** Notices required by this policy must be provided in a manner permitted by applicable law. A notice is effective when received by us, unless applicable law provides otherwise.

**S.39** This policy conforms to applicable law. If a policy provision conflicts with applicable law, that provision is amended to conform to the law.

**S.40** If a policy provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by applicable law. The invalidity of a provision does not expand coverage.

**S.41** The terms of this policy state the agreement between you and us. No oral statement may modify the terms of this policy.

**S.42** Our rights and duties under this policy are subject to applicable law. An insured's rights and duties under this policy are also subject to applicable law.

**S.43** An insured must comply with all conditions that apply to the insured. Failure to comply with a condition may affect coverage to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## G — Sections I and II — Conditions

**G.1** This "policy" may be changed only by a written endorsement issued by us. No representative may change this policy or waive any provision unless we issue the change in writing.

**G.2** We do not provide coverage if an "insured" has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance relating to this insurance. This condition applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after a loss.

**G.3** An insured must have an insurable interest in covered property at the time of loss. We will not pay an insured more than that insured's financial interest in the property.

**G.4** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. The notice must state the identity of the insured, the location of the loss, and the circumstances known to the insured.

**G.5** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property from further damage when the expenses result from a covered loss.

**G.6** An insured must make reasonable efforts to preserve evidence of a loss. An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property as often as reasonably necessary.

**G.7** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property. The inventory must identify the property, describe its condition, and state its value and amount of loss.

**G.8** An insured must provide records and documents we request and permit us to make copies. An insured must provide reasonable access to information relevant to the loss.

**G.9** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining statements, records, and other information concerning the claim.

**G.10** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the examination transcript if it is accurately recorded.

**G.11** An insured must provide a signed, sworn proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary for us to evaluate the claim.

**G.12** If you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal. Each party must select a competent and impartial appraiser and must cooperate in the appraisal process.

**G.13** We will pay the amount agreed upon by you and us, the amount determined by appraisal, or the amount established by final judgment. Payment is subject to all applicable provisions of this policy.

**G.14** An insured may not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment, but we are not required to do so.

**G.15** If we pay for property that is later recovered, an insured must notify us promptly. We may return the recovered property to the insured and adjust the payment to reflect its recovered value.

**G.16** We may repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may use available materials, methods, and labor reasonably suited to restore the property.

**G.17** Loss to a pair, set, or collection will be adjusted according to the reduction in value of the complete pair, set, or collection. We may repair or replace the affected property or pay the applicable loss.

**G.18** If an insured has rights to recover damages from another person, those rights are transferred to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**G.19** An insured must preserve and pursue rights of recovery when we request it. We will not require an insured to transfer rights against a person who is also an insured.

**G.20** No action may be brought against us unless an insured has complied with all applicable policy conditions. No action may be brought until the amount of the insured's obligation has been determined by agreement with us or final judgment.

**G.21** Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this policy. Bankruptcy or insolvency does not give a claimant any greater rights than an insured has under this policy.

**G.22** An insured may not assign this policy without our written consent. An assignment of rights after a covered loss is subject to the rights and obligations existing at the time of the loss.

**G.23** We may cancel this policy for nonpayment of premium by mailing or delivering written notice at least ten days before cancellation takes effect. The notice will state the effective date of cancellation.

**G.24** We may cancel this policy for reasons other than nonpayment of premium by mailing or delivering written notice at least forty-five days before cancellation takes effect. The notice will state the reason for cancellation when required by applicable law.

**G.25** We will mail or deliver a cancellation notice to the address shown in our records. Proof of mailing will be sufficient proof of notice.

**G.26** We may elect not to renew this policy by mailing or delivering written notice at least sixty days before the end of the policy term. The notice will state the effective date of nonrenewal.

**G.27** If this policy is canceled, we will refund any unearned premium due to you. The refund may be made after cancellation takes effect.

**G.28** You may cancel this policy by giving us written notice. Cancellation requested by you will take effect as stated in your notice or upon our receipt of the notice if no effective date is stated.

**G.29** We may adjust premium based on information relevant to the risk insured under this policy. You must provide information reasonably necessary for us to determine the proper premium.

**G.30** We do not cover any loss arising from fraudulent conduct by an insured in connection with a claim. Fraudulent conduct includes presenting false information or withholding material information with intent to deceive.

**G.31** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, or legal paper relating to a claim or suit. An insured must not admit liability or assume an obligation without our consent.

**G.32** An insured must notify us promptly of an "occurrence" that may result in a claim. Notice must include information reasonably available to the insured about the occurrence and involved persons.

**G.33** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. This condition does not apply to reasonable expenses incurred to protect persons or property from further harm.

**G.34** If other insurance applies to a loss, we will pay our share of the loss. Our share is determined according to the applicable terms of this policy and the other insurance.

**G.35** This policy applies separately to each insured. This provision does not increase the limit available for any occurrence or loss.

**G.37** If a provision of this policy is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. The invalid or unenforceable provision will be applied to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**G.38** If an insured dies, we will provide coverage to the legal representative of the deceased insured. Coverage also applies to a person having proper temporary custody of covered property until a legal representative is appointed.

**G.39** A legal representative must comply with the duties and conditions that apply to an insured. We may require evidence of authority before making payment to a legal representative.
