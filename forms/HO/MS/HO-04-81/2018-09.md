# HO 04 81 Limited Fungi, Wet or Dry Rot, or Bacteria Coverage (Edition 2018-09)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This “endorsement” modifies the insurance provided by your “policy”. The provisions of this endorsement apply only to the extent they change that insurance.

This endorsement addresses loss involving “fungi”, “wet or dry rot”, or “bacteria”. These terms apply as used in this endorsement and in the provisions it modifies.

We provide the coverage described in this endorsement subject to all applicable policy conditions. You must comply with all duties that apply to a loss.

The terms, limitations, exclusions, and conditions of your policy continue to apply unless this endorsement changes them. We do not cover loss except as provided by the insurance as modified by this endorsement.

An insured must read this endorsement together with the policy. An insured must give effect to this endorsement when determining whether coverage applies.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of the policy, this endorsement controls. If this endorsement does not change a policy provision, that provision remains in effect.

This endorsement does not expand coverage beyond its express terms. We do not cover any loss that is otherwise excluded or limited unless this endorsement expressly provides coverage for that loss.

The coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to all applicable definitions. An insured must satisfy any condition precedent to coverage.

We may investigate any claimed loss involving fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim.

An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We do not cover avoidable additional loss resulting from failure to perform that duty.

An insured must provide information, records, and evidence reasonably requested by us. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property when reasonably necessary.

Payment under this endorsement is subject to the applicable policy provisions governing loss settlement and payment. We will not pay more than the amount otherwise payable under the policy as modified by this endorsement.

Any recovery available to us under applicable policy provisions remains available after payment under this endorsement. An insured must preserve our rights of recovery.

This endorsement applies to the insurance provided during the policy term. It does not create coverage for loss that occurred before coverage became effective or after coverage ended.

All other policy provisions remain unchanged.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** For purposes of this coverage, "fungi" means fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. This term includes any form, type, or substance produced by fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.2** We cover direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by fungi only as provided in this coverage. "Covered property" means property to which this policy applies.

**W.3** We cover direct physical loss caused by fungi when the fungi results from a "covered cause of loss." A "covered cause of loss" means a cause of loss for which coverage is provided by this policy.

**W.4** The covered cause of loss must cause direct physical loss to covered property. The fungi must result from that direct physical loss.

**W.5** The covered cause of loss must occur before the fungi causes direct physical loss to covered property. The fungi loss must be a result of the covered cause of loss.

**W.6** We cover loss caused by fungi only when the requirements of this coverage are met. We do not cover loss caused by fungi that does not result from a covered cause of loss.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by fungi arising from constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies whether the seepage or leakage occurs from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler system, or household appliance.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by fungi arising from constant or repeated discharge or overflow of water or steam. This exclusion applies whether the discharge or overflow occurs from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler system, or household appliance.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by fungi arising from flood. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.10** We cover the reasonable and necessary cost to remove fungi from covered property when the direct physical loss is covered under this coverage. The removal must be necessary because of fungi resulting from a covered cause of loss.

**W.11** We cover the reasonable and necessary cost to tear out and replace any part of a building as needed to gain access to fungi. The fungi must be located on covered property and its removal must be covered under this coverage.

**W.12** We cover the reasonable and necessary cost to tear out and replace any part of a building as needed to repair covered property. The repair must be necessary because of direct physical loss caused by fungi that is covered under this coverage.

**W.13** We cover the reasonable and necessary cost of testing air or property for the presence of fungi. The testing must be necessary after removal, repair, replacement, restoration, or remediation of covered property.

**W.14** We cover testing only when there is a reason to believe that fungi remain after covered removal, repair, replacement, restoration, or remediation. The testing must relate to the covered property affected by the fungi loss.

**W.15** "Remediation" means measures taken to remove, contain, treat, clean, dispose of, or otherwise address fungi. Remediation includes reasonable measures necessary to restore covered property after covered fungi damage.

**W.16** We cover remediation only when it is reasonable and necessary to address fungi for which coverage is provided. We do not cover remediation that is not related to covered direct physical loss.

**W.17** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must also preserve property affected by fungi for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.18** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss involving fungi. The notice must identify the affected property and the known circumstances of the loss.

**W.19** An insured must permit us to inspect the property affected by fungi. An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the cause, extent, and necessary remediation of the loss.

**W.20** An insured must retain records supporting claimed remediation, repair, replacement, testing, and removal costs. The records must be made available to us upon request.

**W.21** An insured must not discard property affected by fungi before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property. An insured must preserve available evidence of the cause of loss.

**W.22** We do not cover any cost to monitor property for fungi when no covered direct physical loss has occurred. We do not cover preventive maintenance, routine cleaning, or periodic inspection.

**W.23** We do not cover the cost to identify, assess, investigate, abate, mitigate, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, neutralize, monitor, clean up, or dispose of fungi except as provided in this coverage. This exclusion applies to any expense that is not necessary because of covered direct physical loss.

**W.24** We do not cover loss resulting from a condition that existed before the covered cause of loss. This exclusion applies to preexisting fungi, wet or dry rot, bacteria, deterioration, or decay.

**W.25** We do not cover damage caused by wear and tear, deterioration, or neglect. We also do not cover loss caused by inadequate maintenance of covered property.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether the work is performed by you or another person.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We also do not cover loss caused by cracking of those items.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, or mechanical breakdown. We also do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical defect.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the pollutants are solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or otherwise.

**W.30** We do not cover bodily injury, sickness, disease, death, or emotional distress arising from fungi. We do not cover any claim for diminished value, loss of market value, stigma, or loss of use except as expressly covered by this policy.

**W.31** We do not cover any fine, penalty, assessment, or charge imposed by a governmental authority because of fungi. We do not cover the cost to comply with any law, ordinance, regulation, or order except as otherwise provided by this policy.

**W.32** We do not cover the cost of testing persons for exposure to fungi. We do not cover medical examination, medical monitoring, treatment, or related expenses.

**W.33** We do not cover the cost to replace undamaged property solely because fungi are present or suspected. We cover replacement only when necessary to repair or restore covered property damaged by fungi.

**W.34** We do not cover the cost to improve, upgrade, redesign, or alter covered property. We cover only reasonable and necessary work to repair, replace, restore, or remediate covered property after covered loss.

**W.35** We do not cover any increase in cost resulting from delay by an insured. This exclusion applies when the delay prevents prompt inspection, repair, removal, remediation, or testing.

**W.36** We may investigate a fungi loss and may require reasonable documentation of the cause and extent of the loss. Our investigation does not waive any policy condition, exclusion, or limitation.

**W.37** We may select, approve, or require reasonable testing, inspection, repair, removal, or remediation measures. Any such action by us does not create a duty to perform work beyond the coverage provided.

**W.38** We may pay the reasonable value of covered repair, replacement, removal, remediation, or testing. We may settle covered loss by repair, replacement, restoration, payment, or any lawful combination of these methods.

**W.39** Payment for covered fungi loss is subject to all applicable policy terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations. Coverage under this provision does not broaden coverage for any other cause of loss.

**W.40** Covered fungi loss includes direct physical loss to covered property when caused by fungi that results from a covered cause of loss. Covered fungi loss also includes covered removal, repair, replacement, remediation, and testing costs.

**W.41** The cost of removing fungi from property that is not covered property is not covered. The cost of repairing, replacing, restoring, or remediating property that is not covered property is not covered.

**W.42** We do not cover a loss when an insured intentionally conceals or misrepresents a material fact relating to the loss. This exclusion applies to facts concerning the cause, extent, value, condition, repair, removal, remediation, or testing of property.

**W.43** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide requested records, invoices, estimates, photographs, reports, and other information relevant to the fungi loss.

**W.44** An insured must permit reasonable access to covered property for inspection, testing, appraisal, repair, removal, remediation, and investigation. An insured must take reasonable steps to make affected areas available for that purpose.

**W.45** We do not cover any expense incurred without our consent when our consent is required by this policy. This provision does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable emergency measures to protect covered property from further damage.

**W.46** Reasonable emergency measures must be related to a covered fungi loss or to a covered cause of loss. We do not cover emergency measures that are unrelated to protecting covered property from further covered damage.

**W.47** Payment for covered direct physical loss to covered property is available up to the aggregate inup to the aggregate in (see W.2, Limit of Liability). This payment is subject to the terms of this coverage.

**W.48** Payment for covered removal and remediation expenses is available up to the aggregate inup to the aggregate in (see W.2, Limit of Liability). These expenses must be reasonable and necessary.

**W.49** Payment for covered tear out, repair, replacement, and restoration expenses is available up to the aggregate inup to the aggregate in (see W.2, Limit of Liability). The work must be necessary to address covered fungi damage.

**W.50** Payment for covered testing expenses is available up to the aggregate inup to the aggregate in (see W.2, Limit of Liability). The testing must be necessary after covered removal, repair, replacement, restoration, or remediation.

**W.51** Amounts paid for direct physical loss and related covered expenses reduce the coverage available for further fungi loss. No payment under this coverage restores coverage previously used.

**W.52** We do not cover any loss caused by fungi after the available coverage for fungi loss has been exhausted. This exclusion applies to all covered property and all covered expenses.

**W.53** This coverage applies only to loss that occurs during the policy period. The direct physical loss and the fungi loss must occur while this policy is in effect.

**W.54** This coverage does not apply to any claim arising from fungi unless the claim involves covered direct physical loss to covered property. All other policy provisions remain applicable.

**W.55** We will provide coverage for fungi only as expressly stated in this provision. No coverage applies by implication, inference, or construction beyond the coverage provided here.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The "fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit" applies to covered loss caused by "fungi", wet or dry rot, or bacteria. This limit applies regardless of the cause of the loss.

**W.2** The most we will pay for all covered loss subject to the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is ten thousand dollars. This is the most we will pay for all such loss during the policy term.

**W.3** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to loss involving property covered under this policy. It also applies to related costs that are covered under this policy.

**W.4** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies whether the loss results from a sudden event or a gradual condition. The manner in which the loss develops does not increase the limit.

**W.5** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies whether the loss affects personal property, real property, or both. Damage to different types of property does not create separate limits.

**W.6** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to all insureds. Payments made for the benefit of any insured reduce the amount available to all insureds.

**W.7** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to all covered locations. Loss at different locations does not create separate limits.

**W.8** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to all claims arising from covered loss. Separate claims do not increase the limit.

**W.9** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to loss reported at different times. The timing of notice does not increase the limit.

**W.10** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to loss discovered at different times. The timing of discovery does not increase the limit.

**W.11** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to loss occurring in different areas of covered property. Damage in separate areas does not create separate limits.

**W.12** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to loss involving different materials. The presence of different materials does not increase the limit.

**W.13** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to loss involving more than a single source. Multiple sources do not increase the limit.

**W.14** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to loss involving more than a single cause. Multiple causes do not increase the limit.

**W.15** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies when fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria is present with another cause of loss. The presence of another cause of loss does not increase the limit.

**W.16** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to direct physical loss. It also applies to covered ensuing loss caused by fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.17** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to covered costs to remove fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. It also applies to covered costs to treat property affected by fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.18** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to covered costs to test for fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. It also applies to covered costs to confirm the presence of fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.19** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to covered costs to clean property. It also applies to covered costs to repair property.

**W.20** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to covered costs to replace property. It also applies to covered costs to restore property.

**W.21** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to covered costs incurred to gain access to damaged property. It also applies to covered costs incurred to protect damaged property from further covered loss.

**W.22** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to covered costs incurred after a covered loss. Such costs reduce the amount available under the limit.

**W.23** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by the amount of insurance applicable to covered property. A higher amount of insurance does not increase this limit.

**W.24** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by the value of damaged property. The value of property does not increase this limit.

**W.25** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by repair costs. Repair costs in excess of the limit remain your responsibility.

**W.26** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by replacement costs. Replacement costs in excess of the limit remain your responsibility.

**W.27** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by costs to remove damaged property. Removal costs in excess of the limit remain your responsibility.

**W.28** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by costs to dispose of damaged property. Disposal costs in excess of the limit remain your responsibility.

**W.29** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by costs to inspect property. Inspection costs in excess of the limit remain your responsibility.

**W.30** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by costs to investigate a loss. Investigation costs in excess of the limit remain your responsibility.

**W.31** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by costs incurred to comply with a requirement of a governmental authority. Such costs are subject to the limit when covered.

**W.32** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is not increased by costs incurred to prevent additional damage. Such costs are subject to the limit when covered.

**W.33** Payments under the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit reduce the amount remaining under that limit. No payment restores an amount previously paid.

**W.34** Payments made to settle a covered claim reduce the amount remaining under the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit. Payments made to satisfy a covered judgment also reduce the amount remaining under that limit.

**W.35** Payments made directly to you reduce the amount remaining under the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit. Payments made to another person on your behalf also reduce the amount remaining under that limit.

**W.36** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies without regard to the number of insureds seeking coverage. The participation of additional insureds does not increase the limit.

**W.37** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies without regard to the number of damaged items. Damage to additional items does not increase the limit.

**W.38** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies without regard to the number of affected rooms, buildings, or structures. Damage in additional areas does not increase the limit.

**W.39** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies without regard to the number of reports, estimates, inspections, or repairs. Additional reports, estimates, inspections, or repairs do not increase the limit.

**W.40** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies without regard to whether damage is visible. Concealed damage does not increase the limit.

**W.41** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies without regard to whether damage is discovered after repairs begin. Later discovery of damage does not increase the limit.

**W.42** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies without regard to whether fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria spreads. Spread does not increase the limit.

**W.43** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies without regard to whether damaged property is repaired or replaced. Your decision to repair or replace does not increase the limit.

**W.44** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies to covered loss arising from the same event. It also applies to covered loss arising from separate events.

**W.45** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit applies after any applicable deductible. A deductible does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.46** You must provide notice of a covered loss as required by this policy. Notice of a loss does not increase the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit.

**W.47** An insured must protect property from further damage after a covered loss. Reasonable protective measures do not increase the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit.

**W.48** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. The inspection of damaged property does not increase the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit.

**W.49** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of a covered loss. Cooperation does not increase the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit.

**W.50** We do not cover loss in excess of the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit. This applies even if the covered loss exceeds that limit.

**W.51** We do not cover costs in excess of the fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit. This applies even if those costs are necessary to repair, replace, restore, clean, remove, test, or treat property.

**W.52** The fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria aggregate limit is the maximum amount available for covered loss subject to that limit. All payments for such covered loss are subject to that maximum.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the portion of a "covered loss" that you must bear. We subtract the deductible before determining the amount we will pay.

**W.2** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by "fungi", "wet or dry rot", or "bacteria". The deductible also applies to covered loss resulting from the presence, growth, spread, or removal of fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.3** We apply the deductible to the total covered loss arising from the same cause or related causes. We do not apply the deductible to an amount that is not covered.

**W.4** We determine the deductible after applying all applicable limitations of coverage. We do not increase a limitation of coverage by the amount of the deductible.

**W.5** We do not cover the portion of a covered loss that is within the deductible. You remain responsible for that portion of the loss.

**W.6** The deductible applies whether the covered loss involves damage to property, remediation, removal, testing, cleaning, repair, or replacement. The deductible applies to all covered expenses arising from the covered loss.

**W.7** The deductible applies when fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria directly causes covered loss. The deductible also applies when a covered loss results from efforts to prevent further damage from fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.8** We apply the deductible before making payment for covered property. We do not make payment for expenses that do not exceed the deductible.

**W.9** An insured must pay the deductible when we request payment as a condition of repairing or replacing covered property. An insured must cooperate with us in allocating the deductible among covered expenses.

**W.10** We may deduct the deductible from any payment otherwise due for a covered loss. We may also require you to pay the deductible directly to a person performing covered repair, remediation, or removal work.

**W.11** The deductible applies regardless of whether you arrange for repair, remediation, cleaning, testing, removal, or replacement. The deductible applies regardless of whether we arrange for those services.

**W.12** We do not waive the deductible because a covered loss affects more than one item of covered property. We do not waive the deductible because a covered loss requires more than one type of covered service.

**W.13** The deductible applies to covered loss occurring at the insured location. The deductible also applies to covered loss involving personal property away from the insured location when that loss is covered.

**W.14** We determine whether expenses are subject to the deductible based on the cause of the covered loss. We may allocate expenses between covered and excluded causes when the information available permits allocation.

**W.15** An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine the amount of the deductible. An insured must provide records, invoices, estimates, and receipts relating to the covered loss.

**W.16** The deductible applies to reasonable and necessary expenses that are otherwise covered. We do not apply the deductible to expenses that we do not cover.

**W.17** We may inspect damaged property and affected areas before determining payment. An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably possible until we have completed our inspection.

**W.18** The deductible applies to covered loss discovered after fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria has caused damage. The time at which you discover the damage does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.19** We do not cover a charge imposed solely because a contractor, remediator, or service provider requires a deposit. You remain responsible for any portion of that charge within the deductible.

**W.20** The deductible applies to covered emergency measures. We do not pay for emergency measures to the extent their cost is within the deductible.

**W.21** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. Compliance with that duty does not eliminate or reduce the deductible.

**W.22** The deductible applies to covered loss involving concealed damage. The deductible also applies to covered loss involving damage that is visible when discovered.

**W.23** We may determine the deductible from the facts of the covered loss and the applicable coverage. Our determination is subject to the terms and conditions of this policy.

**W.24** The deductible applies before we calculate any payment for repair or replacement. The deductible applies whether repair or replacement is completed.

**W.25** We do not cover the deductible as part of any claim for loss of use, additional expense, or other consequential loss. You may not transfer responsibility for the deductible to us.

**W.26** The deductible applies to covered loss sustained by an insured. The deductible also applies to covered loss sustained by a resident relative when that loss is covered.

**W.27** An insured must not incur unreasonable expenses in an effort to avoid the deductible. We pay only those expenses that are covered and that remain payable after application of the deductible.

**W.28** The deductible applies when covered loss is caused by the accidental discharge, overflow, or leakage of water that results in fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. The deductible applies only to the extent the resulting loss is covered.

**W.29** We may apply the deductible to a settlement payment. Acceptance of a settlement payment does not change your responsibility for the deductible.

**W.30** The deductible applies to covered loss for which another person may be legally responsible. Our right to recover from that person does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.31** An insured must assist us in preserving our recovery rights after a covered loss. An insured must not impair those rights by releasing a responsible person without our written consent.

**W.32** We do not reimburse you for the deductible from any recovery we obtain unless we agree otherwise in writing. Any recovery we obtain remains subject to our rights under this policy.

**W.33** The deductible applies to covered loss involving property that is repaired, replaced, cleaned, treated, removed, or discarded. The manner in which affected property is handled does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.34** An insured must retain documentation supporting expenses claimed as covered. We may decline payment for claimed expenses when you do not provide information reasonably necessary to determine coverage.

**W.35** The deductible applies separately from any amount you may owe a contractor, remediator, or other service provider. A dispute between you and that person does not alter our application of the deductible.

**W.36** We do not pay the deductible to an insured, mortgagee, lienholder, contractor, remediator, or other person. We pay only the covered amount that remains after application of the deductible.

**W.37** The deductible applies to covered loss arising from testing for fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria when testing is covered. The deductible also applies to covered loss arising from monitoring or verification when those services are covered.

**W.38** An insured must allow us to inspect invoices and other records relating to remediation or repair. An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests concerning the scope and cost of covered work.

**W.39** We apply the deductible without regard to whether covered work is performed by a licensed professional or by another person. We do not pay for work that is not otherwise covered.

**W.40** The deductible applies when the covered loss affects structural property, personal property, or both. We apply the deductible to the covered loss as a whole.

**W.41** We do not reduce the deductible because you have incurred expenses before reporting the covered loss. Prompt notice of loss does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.42** An insured must give us prompt notice of a covered loss involving fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. An insured must protect covered property from further damage and keep an accurate record of repair expenses.

**W.43** The deductible remains your responsibility even if we make payment directly to another person. Payment by us does not create coverage for the portion of loss within the deductible.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss caused by "fungi", "wet or dry rot", or "bacteria". This exclusion applies whether the loss is direct or indirect.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by the presence, growth, proliferation, spread, migration, release, discharge, or dispersal of fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.3** We do not cover property damage arising from the testing for fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies whether testing is performed before, during, or after physical damage to property.

**W.4** We do not cover loss arising from the inspection, monitoring, detection, sampling, analysis, treatment, containment, removal, disposal, or replacement of fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. We do not cover the cost to determine the existence, extent, location, source, or condition of fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by the failure to maintain property in a condition that prevents moisture accumulation. This includes failure to repair leaks, drains, plumbing, ventilation systems, roofing, siding, foundations, or other building components.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water, steam, or vapors. This exclusion applies whether the seepage or leakage is hidden or visible.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by condensation, humidity, dampness, or inadequate ventilation. This exclusion applies even if those conditions contribute to fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to protect property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect property from further damage.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether performed by you or by others.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by materials used in repair, construction, renovation, or maintenance that are unsuitable for their intended purpose. This exclusion applies even if those materials retain moisture or promote microbial growth.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by corrosion, decay, deterioration, wear and tear, or mechanical breakdown. This exclusion applies even if the condition results in fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover resulting fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria unless otherwise covered under this endorsement.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. This exclusion applies even if their activity creates conditions favorable to fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies to pollutants that create, carry, or contain fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by contamination of property. Contamination includes any condition that makes property unsafe, unusable, unmarketable, or unsuitable for its intended use.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by odors, spores, allergens, irritants, toxins, or byproducts arising from fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies whether the condition is airborne, waterborne, or present on property.

**W.17** We do not cover loss for bodily injury, sickness, disease, emotional distress, or other personal injury arising from fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies whether injury is alleged to result from exposure, ingestion, inhalation, contact, or absorption.

**W.4** We may inspect the location where the loss occurred and any property involved in the loss. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable requests for access and information.

**W.5** We determine whether claimed damage resulted from a covered cause of loss. We may distinguish between damage caused by a covered cause of loss and damage caused by deterioration, wear, contamination, or another cause.

**W.6** We settle loss only for property in which an insured has a financial interest. We do not pay more than an insured's financial interest in the damaged property.

**W.7** We settle covered loss to "personal property" on the following basis: replacement cost. This basis applies to property that is otherwise eligible for payment under this coverage.

**W.8** We may require an insured to identify each damaged item and describe the nature and extent of the damage. An insured must separate damaged property from undamaged property when reasonably possible.

**W.9** We may require documentation showing ownership of the damaged property. Documentation may include purchase records, maintenance records, appraisals, photographs, or other reliable evidence.

**W.10** We may determine the condition of property immediately before the loss from available evidence. We may consider prior damage, prior repairs, maintenance, and ordinary use.

**W.11** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable protective measures do not enlarge the property covered or the amount payable.

**W.12** We may inspect completed repair, cleaning, remediation, or replacement work. An insured must make the affected property available for that inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.13** We may obtain samples, test results, reports, or opinions concerning the presence or effect of fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. An insured must provide copies of such materials when they are in the insured's possession or control.

**W.14** We may rely on information from qualified persons in evaluating the cause and extent of damage. An insured may provide information from qualified persons in support of the claim.

**W.15** We determine the amount payable from the covered physical damage sustained by the property. We do not include amounts attributable solely to undamaged property.

**W.16** We do not cover the cost to correct a condition that did not result from a covered loss. We do not cover the cost to improve property beyond its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.17** We do not cover loss consisting solely of diminished value, loss of use, inconvenience, or sentimental value. We do not cover an increase in market value resulting from repair, replacement, cleaning, or remediation.

**W.18** We may pay the amount of covered loss in money, repair the damaged property, restore the damaged property, or replace the damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may make payment to an insured or to another person with a lawful interest in the property.

**W.19** If we elect to repair, restore, or replace damaged property, an insured must permit us reasonable access to perform that work. An insured must cooperate in arranging access to the damaged property.

**W.20** If an insured elects to repair, restore, or replace damaged property, an insured must provide information reasonably necessary to evaluate the claimed cost. We may review estimates, invoices, work descriptions, and related records.

**W.21** We may consider reasonable and necessary costs incurred to investigate covered damage. Such costs must relate directly to the covered loss.

**W.22** We may consider reasonable and necessary costs incurred to remove covered damaged property. An insured must preserve records supporting such costs.

**W.23** We do not cover charges that are unrelated to the evaluation, repair, restoration, removal, or replacement of covered damaged property. We do not cover charges incurred for personal preference or optional enhancement.

**W.24** An insured must notify us promptly if damaged property is repaired, restored, replaced, or discarded before our inspection. Failure to provide notice may affect our ability to determine the amount of covered loss.

**W.25** An insured must submit any requested statement concerning the loss in a form reasonably acceptable to us. The statement must identify the property claimed and the basis for the amount claimed.

**W.26** We may request that an insured submit to examination under oath concerning the loss. An insured must answer questions truthfully and provide requested information within the insured's knowledge.

**W.27** We may require an insured to provide records relating to the purchase, maintenance, repair, or replacement of damaged property. An insured must authorize the release of records when reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim.

**W.28** We may reduce the amount payable by amounts previously paid for the same covered damage. We do not make duplicate payment for the same property damage.

**W.29** We do not cover property damage that an insured intentionally conceals, misrepresents, or falsely describes. An insured must not knowingly present false information concerning the loss.

**W.30** We may adjust a claim based on information obtained after an initial payment. An insured must return any payment that exceeds the amount properly payable for the covered loss.

**W.31** We may issue payment jointly to an insured and a person or organization with a lawful interest in the property. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary to identify that interest.

**W.32** Payment for covered loss does not transfer ownership of damaged property to us unless we agree otherwise. We may take possession of property for inspection, testing, salvage, repair, restoration, or replacement.

**W.33** An insured must preserve salvage when reasonably possible. We may consider the value of salvage in determining the amount payable.

**W.34** We do not cover costs arising from an insured's voluntary decision to replace undamaged property. We do not cover costs arising from an insured's decision to perform work beyond that necessary to address covered damage.

**W.35** We may determine whether property can be repaired, restored, cleaned, or replaced. Our determination may be based on the nature and extent of the covered damage and the available evidence.

**W.36** An insured must provide us with any estimate obtained for repair, restoration, cleaning, or replacement of the damaged property. We may review the scope and reasonableness of the proposed work.

**W.37** We may request that an insured obtain additional information concerning the source, duration, or spread of fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests for that information.

**W.38** We do not cover costs attributable to a condition that existed before the covered loss unless that condition was directly damaged by the covered cause of loss. We do not cover costs attributable to a condition that remains after covered damaged property has been repaired, restored, or removed.

**W.39** The "assessment coverage limit" does not alter the basis on which we settle property damage under this provision. We apply that coverage according to its applicable terms.

**W.40** A "fire response charge" does not constitute property damage for purposes of this Loss Settlement provision. We determine any payment for that charge separately from the settlement of damaged property.

**W.41** The "special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones" does not apply to determine the basis for settling damage caused by fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. We determine the amount payable for damaged property under this provision.

**W.42** The "home business contents limit" applies only to the extent that the damaged property is subject to that limit. We do not treat property as home business contents unless it otherwise qualifies as such.

**W.43** We may withhold payment until an insured has complied with duties that are material to our determination of the claim. An insured must cooperate with us in resolving information reasonably necessary to settle the loss.

**W.44** We may make payment for undisputed covered damage while we continue to evaluate other claimed damage. Payment for undisputed damage does not waive our right to evaluate the remaining claim.

**W.45** We may reopen our evaluation when additional evidence concerning the covered loss becomes available. An insured must promptly provide newly discovered information that may affect settlement.

**W.46** We do not cover costs incurred to determine whether property is damaged when no covered direct physical damage is found. We do not cover costs that are not reasonably related to investigating a claimed covered loss.

**W.47** An insured must keep records of expenses incurred because of the claimed property damage. An insured must provide those records upon our request.

**W.48** We may settle a claim with an insured, a mortgagee, a lienholder, or another person having a lawful interest in the property. Such settlement does not increase the amount otherwise payable for the covered loss.

**W.49** We may require reasonable proof that repair, restoration, cleaning, or replacement has been completed when that information is relevant to the claim. An insured must provide that proof when requested.

**W.50** Our payment for covered loss is subject to all applicable limitations and conditions of this coverage. We do not pay for any loss that is not covered under this coverage.

## W.8 — Definitions

“Fungi” means any type or form of fungus, including mold or mildew. It includes mycotoxins, spores, scents, and byproducts produced or released by fungi.

Fungi include material that is living, dormant, or dead. Fungi include material that is active or inactive.

Fungi include material that is visible or concealed. Fungi include material that is detectable or not detectable.

Fungi include mold. Fungi include mildew.

Fungi include any growth of fungus. Fungi include any accumulation of fungal material.

Fungi include spores produced or released by fungi. Fungi include spores whether airborne, settled, or contained within material.

Fungi include mycotoxins produced or released by fungi. Fungi include mycotoxins whether present with or apart from fungi.

Fungi include scents produced or released by fungi. Fungi include odors associated with fungi.

Fungi include byproducts produced or released by fungi. Fungi include substances resulting from fungal growth or activity.

Fungi include material generated during fungal decay. Fungi include material generated during fungal decomposition.

Fungi include material resulting from fungal contamination. Fungi include material resulting from fungal colonization.

Fungi include fungal material on surfaces. Fungi include fungal material within property.

Fungi include fungal material in air. Fungi include fungal material in water or other substances.

Fungi include fungal material in building materials. Fungi include fungal material in personal property.

Fungi include fungal material regardless of its source. Fungi include fungal material regardless of the manner in which it is introduced.

Fungi include fungal material regardless of the condition of the property affected. Fungi include fungal material regardless of whether it causes physical change.

Fungi include fungal material regardless of whether it is identified by species. Fungi include fungal material regardless of whether its origin is known.

Fungi include mold and mildew whether or not they are accompanied by spores. Fungi include mold and mildew whether or not they produce a scent.

“Wet Or Dry Rot” means decay of wood caused by fungi. It includes decay caused by fungal growth within or upon wood.

Wet Or Dry Rot includes deterioration of wood caused by fungi. It includes weakening, softening, or disintegration of wood caused by fungi.

Wet Or Dry Rot includes decay of structural wood. It includes decay of nonstructural wood.

Wet Or Dry Rot includes decay of finished wood. It includes decay of unfinished wood.

Wet Or Dry Rot includes decay of wood that is visible. It includes decay of wood that is concealed.

Wet Or Dry Rot includes decay regardless of the source of moisture associated with the fungi. It includes decay regardless of whether the fungi remain present.

Wet Or Dry Rot includes fungal decay that affects a portion of wood. It includes fungal decay that affects wood in its entirety.

Wet Or Dry Rot does not require that the wood be identified by type. It does not require that the fungi causing the decay be identified by species.

“Bacteria” means any bacteria. It includes byproducts produced or released by bacteria.

Bacteria include material that is living, dormant, or dead. Bacteria include material that is active or inactive.

Bacteria include material that is visible or concealed. Bacteria include material that is detectable or not detectable.

Bacteria include bacterial growth. Bacteria include bacterial colonies and accumulations.

Bacteria include byproducts resulting from bacterial growth or activity. Bacteria include substances produced or released by bacteria.

Bacteria include material associated with bacterial contamination. Bacteria include material associated with bacterial colonization.

Bacteria include bacteria on surfaces. Bacteria include bacteria within property.

Bacteria include bacteria in air. Bacteria include bacteria in water or other substances.

Bacteria include bacteria regardless of their source. Bacteria include bacteria regardless of the manner in which they are introduced.

Bacteria include bacteria regardless of whether they are identified by species. Bacteria include bacteria regardless of whether their origin is known.
