# HO 04 81 Limited Fungi, Wet or Dry Rot, or Bacteria Coverage (Edition 2018-09)

Multistate endorsement. Attaches to HO-3 and provides limited coverage for a cause otherwise excluded by Section I — Exclusions C.2.

## M.1 Coverage Provided

We cover direct physical loss to property covered under Coverage A, Coverage B, and Coverage C caused by fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria, but only where that fungi, rot, or bacteria results from a peril insured against under Section I that occurred during the policy period.

To the extent of the coverage provided here, Section I — Exclusions C.2 does not apply.

## M.2 Trigger Condition

Coverage applies only where the water or moisture that produced the fungi, rot, or bacteria came from an event that was itself covered — for example, a sudden and accidental discharge from a plumbing system as described in Section I — Perils Insured Against P.2, or water backup covered by an attached water backup endorsement.

Coverage does not apply where the water or moisture came from flood, surface water, or subsurface water, because Section I — Exclusions A.1 and A.2 continue to apply in full and remove the underlying loss from coverage entirely.

Coverage does not apply where the water or moisture came from continuous or repeated seepage or leakage over a period of weeks, months, or years, because Section I — Exclusions C.3 removes that underlying loss from coverage.

## M.3 Limit

The most we will pay under this endorsement for all loss in any one policy period is ten thousand dollars, unless a higher limit is shown for this endorsement in the Declarations. This limit is part of, and not in addition to, the limits of liability that apply to Coverage A, Coverage B, and Coverage C.

The limit applies in the aggregate for the policy period regardless of the number of occurrences, the number of claims, or the number of locations involved.

## M.4 What the Limit Includes

The limit in M.3 includes the cost to remove the fungi, rot, or bacteria, the cost to tear out and replace any part of the building necessary to gain access to it, the cost of testing performed after removal to confirm its absence, and any increase in Coverage D loss of use attributable to the fungi, rot, or bacteria.

## M.5 Duty to Mitigate

We do not cover loss under this endorsement to the extent it results from an insured's failure to take reasonable steps to dry, clean, or otherwise mitigate water intrusion after the insured knew or reasonably should have known of it. This is a separate condition from the general neglect exclusion in Section I — Exclusions C.1 and applies whether or not that exclusion also applies.

## M.6 Liability Coverage Not Affected

This endorsement provides Section I property coverage only. It does not provide, extend, or modify any Section II liability coverage for bodily injury or property damage arising out of fungi, rot, or bacteria.

All other provisions of the policy apply.
