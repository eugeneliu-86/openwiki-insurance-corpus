# HO 04 81 Limited Fungi, Wet or Dry Rot, or Bacteria Coverage (Edition 2026-09)

Applies to policies written or renewed on or after 2026-10-01.

Multistate endorsement to HO-3; it controls where it conflicts with the policy. This edition
replaces edition 2018-09 for those policies; policies written under 2018-09 remain governed by
it.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** For purposes of this coverage, "fungi" means fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria,
including any form, type, or substance produced by them.

**W.2** We cover direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by fungi only when the fungi
results from a "covered cause of loss" that occurred before the fungi caused the loss.

**W.3** We cover the reasonable and necessary cost to remove fungi, to tear out and replace any
part of a building to gain access to it, and to test for it after covered remediation.

**W.4** We do not cover loss caused by fungi arising from constant or repeated seepage or
leakage of water or steam, or from flood.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.5** The most we will pay for "fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria" is twenty-five thousand
dollars. This limit applies to the total of all covered loss involving fungi, wet or dry rot,
or bacteria, regardless of the number of claims, persons insured, or items of property
involved. This limit is not an additional amount of insurance.

## W.3 — What This Edition Changes

**W.6** The only change from edition 2018-09 is the limit in W.5, raised from ten thousand
dollars to twenty-five thousand dollars. All other provisions are unchanged.
