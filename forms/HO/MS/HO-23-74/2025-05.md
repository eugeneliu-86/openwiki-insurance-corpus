# HO 23 74 Actual Cash Value Loss Settlement — Roof Surfacing (Edition 2025-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" is attached to your "policy". It forms part of your policy when it is attached to your policy.

This endorsement applies to loss settlement for "roof surfacing". Roof surfacing includes the materials forming the outermost weather-resistant surface of a roof.

We will apply this endorsement to a covered loss involving roof surfacing. We will determine the amount of loss under this endorsement in accordance with its terms.

Actual cash value is the value of damaged roof surfacing at the time of loss. Actual cash value reflects applicable depreciation and the condition of the damaged roof surfacing.

We will determine depreciation based on relevant characteristics of the damaged roof surfacing. Those characteristics may include age, condition, expected useful life, and degree of wear.

We will not include depreciation for a condition that did not exist before the loss. We will apply depreciation only to the extent it is supported by the condition of the damaged roof surfacing.

The amount payable for roof surfacing may be less than the cost to repair or replace the damaged roof surfacing. Payment under this endorsement does not require us to pay replacement cost for roof surfacing.

You must provide information we reasonably need to determine the actual cash value of damaged roof surfacing. You must cooperate with us in our investigation and adjustment of the loss.

You must protect damaged roof surfacing from further damage after a loss. You must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property from further damage.

We may inspect damaged roof surfacing before repair, replacement, or disposal. You must retain damaged roof surfacing when we reasonably request that you do so.

We may require photographs, estimates, invoices, receipts, reports, or other records relating to the loss. You must provide requested records that are available to you.

We may determine the amount of loss based on available information. Our determination may include information obtained from inspections, records, experts, contractors, or other sources.

We do not cover any increase in loss caused by your failure to protect the property from further damage. We do not cover costs that are not caused by a covered loss.

This endorsement does not create coverage for a loss that is not otherwise covered. This endorsement does not change any applicable exclusion, condition, limit, or deductible.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy, this endorsement controls with respect to roof surfacing. All other provisions of your policy remain applicable.

The terms of this endorsement apply to each insured seeking payment for roof surfacing. An insured must comply with all duties that apply to a loss.

Our payment for roof surfacing is subject to all applicable policy terms. Payment does not waive any right or defense available to us.

We may adjust a loss involving roof surfacing separately from other damaged property. A separate adjustment does not change the coverage applicable to other damaged property.

No action by us in investigating, inspecting, estimating, or paying a loss changes the terms of this endorsement. Any waiver by us must be made in writing.

This endorsement applies only to loss involving roof surfacing. It does not apply to property that is not roof surfacing.

You and we are bound by the terms of this endorsement. Acceptance of payment under this endorsement does not change its terms.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the loss settlement described in this endorsement for "Roof Surfacing" damaged by a "Covered Loss". This endorsement applies only to property for which coverage is provided under your policy.

**W.2** "Roof Surfacing" means the exterior material applied to a roof to shed water and protect the structure beneath it. Roof Surfacing includes associated materials that are necessary to install the damaged roof surfacing in a workmanlike manner.

**W.3** "Actual Cash Value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss after deduction for depreciation, condition, age, and obsolescence. We determine Actual Cash Value using available information concerning the damaged property and the cost to repair or replace it.

**W.4** When the Roof Age is twelve years or greater, we will settle a Covered Loss to Roof Surfacing on an Actual Cash Value basis. We will apply this loss settlement whether or not you repair or replace the damaged Roof Surfacing.

**W.5** "Roof Age" means the age of the damaged Roof Surfacing at the time of loss. We may determine Roof Age from installation records, permits, invoices, inspections, photographs, statements, or other reliable evidence.

**W.6** We will determine Roof Age based on the portion of Roof Surfacing that sustained direct physical damage. Replacement of a limited area does not establish the age of Roof Surfacing outside that area unless the evidence shows that the roof surfacing was replaced as part of the same installation.

**W.7** We will pay the Actual Cash Value of damaged Roof Surfacing that is necessary to repair the direct physical loss. We will not reduce payment solely because undamaged Roof Surfacing remains in serviceable condition.

**W.8** We will consider the material type, condition, maintenance, wear, weathering, useful life, and expected performance of the damaged Roof Surfacing. We may also consider evidence of prior repairs, prior damage, and known defects.

**W.9** "Depreciation" means a reduction in value resulting from age, wear, deterioration, condition, obsolescence, or expected useful life. We will apply Depreciation only to the extent it reasonably reflects the condition of the damaged Roof Surfacing immediately before the loss.

**W.10** We will calculate Depreciation using information available at the time we adjust the loss. We may revise the calculation if reliable information later establishes a different condition, age, or value for the damaged Roof Surfacing.

**W.11** We will determine the reasonable cost to repair damaged Roof Surfacing using materials of like kind and quality. We may use available market pricing, contractor estimates, supplier information, inspections, and other relevant evidence.

**W.12** "Like Kind and Quality" means materials that are reasonably comparable in type, function, durability, and performance. Like Kind and Quality does not require identical color, pattern, texture, manufacturer, or product line.

**W.13** We will pay for labor reasonably necessary to remove damaged Roof Surfacing and install replacement roof surfacing. We will include labor that is reasonably necessary to complete repairs in a workmanlike manner.

**W.14** We will pay for materials reasonably necessary to repair the damaged Roof Surfacing. We will not require you to obtain a particular supplier or contractor as a condition of payment.

**W.15** We may inspect the damaged Roof Surfacing before repairs begin. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.16** An insured must provide reasonable access to the insured location for inspection, measurement, photographing, testing, and adjustment of the loss. An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests for information concerning the damaged Roof Surfacing.

**W.17** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a Covered Loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property when those expenses are covered under your policy.

**W.18** We will determine the amount of loss without requiring that Roof Surfacing be repaired or replaced. If you repair or replace Roof Surfacing, the cost you incur may be considered in evaluating the reasonable cost of repair or replacement.

**W.19** We will not pay more than the Actual Cash Value of the damaged Roof Surfacing, subject to the applicable limit of insurance and deductible. All policy conditions continue to apply to a loss settled under this endorsement.

**W.20** We will apply the deductible in the manner required by your policy. The deductible applies to the covered loss before payment is made under this endorsement.

**W.21** We will settle damage to Roof Surfacing separately from damage to other covered property when separate evaluation is reasonably possible. This separate evaluation does not increase the applicable limit of insurance.

**W.22** We will include reasonable charges for disposal of damaged Roof Surfacing when disposal is necessary to complete covered repairs. Payment for disposal remains subject to the terms, limits, and conditions of your policy.

**W.23** We will include reasonable charges for materials and labor necessary to detach and reset undamaged property when detachment and resetting are necessary to repair damaged Roof Surfacing. We will determine whether such work is necessary based on the facts of the loss.

**W.24** We will pay for matching materials only to the extent matching is required to repair the direct physical damage using Like Kind and Quality materials. A difference in appearance alone does not establish that replacement of undamaged Roof Surfacing is necessary.

**W.25** We will consider whether available replacement materials are reasonably comparable to the damaged Roof Surfacing. We may consider variation caused by normal weathering, fading, prior repairs, and manufacturing changes.

**W.26** We will not require replacement materials to be identical to the damaged Roof Surfacing when Like Kind and Quality materials are available. We will evaluate available materials based on reasonable similarity in function and performance.

**W.27** We will include reasonable costs required to comply with applicable building requirements when those costs are covered under your policy. You must establish that the building requirement applies to the covered repair.

**W.28** We may rely on written direction from an authority having jurisdiction when evaluating building requirements. We may also consider plans, permits, inspection records, and applicable code provisions.

**W.29** We will pay for reasonable emergency measures that are necessary to protect damaged Roof Surfacing from further covered damage. Emergency measures do not increase coverage beyond the applicable policy limit.

**W.30** We may make payment to you, to a mortgagee, or to another person entitled to payment under your policy. Payment to any person does not alter the amount we owe for the covered loss.

**W.31** We may pay the reasonable cost of repair directly to a contractor when you authorize that payment. Direct payment does not create a contract between us and the contractor.

**W.32** We may obtain estimates or opinions from contractors, roof consultants, engineers, material suppliers, or other qualified persons. We may use those estimates or opinions in determining the amount of loss.

**W.33** You may provide estimates, invoices, photographs, reports, and other information supporting your claim. We will consider relevant information you provide in adjusting the loss.

**W.34** We will settle the covered loss based on the condition of the damaged Roof Surfacing immediately before the loss. We will not reduce payment for damage caused by the covered event merely because the Roof Surfacing had prior wear or weathering.

**W.35** The amount payable for covered Roof Surfacing damage is never less than the floor innever less than the floor in (see W.4, What Remains Excluded). That floor applies after we determine the covered amount of loss.

**W.36** The amount payable for covered Roof Surfacing damage is never less than the floor innever less than the floor in (see W.4, What Remains Excluded). We will apply that floor in determining the final covered payment.

**W.37** The amount payable for covered Roof Surfacing damage is never less than the floor innever less than the floor in (see W.4, What Remains Excluded). That floor does not increase the applicable limit of insurance.

**W.38** We will not reduce Actual Cash Value by applying Depreciation more than once to the same element of damaged Roof Surfacing. We may apply appropriate Depreciation to separate materials or labor when their condition and useful life differ.

**W.39** We will not apply Depreciation to labor unless the law applicable to the loss permits that treatment. When labor Depreciation is permitted, we will apply it reasonably and consistently with the condition of the damaged property.

**W.40** We will consider the condition of flashing, underlayment, fasteners, vents, and related components when those components are damaged by a Covered Loss. We will pay for those components to the extent they are covered property and are necessary to complete covered repairs.

**W.41** We will determine whether damage to Roof Surfacing resulted from the Covered Loss based on the available evidence. We may consider the location, pattern, character, and extent of the damage.

**W.42** We may distinguish covered damage from wear, deterioration, defective installation, mechanical damage, or other conditions not caused by the Covered Loss. Our determination will be based on the facts and evidence available for the claim.

**W.43** We will pay for temporary protective materials when reasonably necessary to prevent further covered damage. Temporary protective materials do not require us to pay for permanent replacement before the amount of loss is determined.

**W.44** We may require reasonable documentation of repair or replacement costs when those costs are material to adjustment of the claim. An insured must provide documentation that is available to the insured or reasonably obtainable by the insured.

**W.45** We will not deny payment solely because an insured does not repair or replace damaged Roof Surfacing. The amount payable remains subject to Actual Cash Value loss settlement and all other policy terms.

**W.46** We will evaluate the reasonable cost to repair damaged Roof Surfacing without requiring use of the least expensive material or method. We may use a reasonable repair method that restores the damaged property to Like Kind and Quality.

**W.47** We will consider reasonable local labor and material costs in determining the amount of loss. We may consider market conditions affecting the availability of qualified labor or suitable materials.

**W.48** We will pay for reasonable overhead and profit when those costs are necessary to complete covered repairs. We will determine whether overhead and profit are necessary based on the scope and complexity of the covered work.

**W.49** We will not require that all work be performed by the same contractor. We may evaluate reasonable costs for separate portions of covered work performed by different qualified contractors.

**W.50** We will consider evidence that the damaged Roof Surfacing was subject to a manufacturer warranty, contractor warranty, or service agreement. The existence of a warranty does not reduce payment unless recovery under that warranty is available for the same loss.

**W.51** We may seek recovery from a person responsible for causing a covered loss to the extent permitted by your policy. An insured must protect our rights of recovery after payment of a covered loss.

**W.52** We will make payment after we determine the covered amount of loss and receive the information reasonably necessary to adjust the claim. We may issue supplemental payment when additional covered damage is established.

**W.53** We will consider supplemental information concerning hidden damage discovered during covered repairs. An insured must provide reasonable notice of the discovered damage before it is repaired when inspection is reasonably possible.

**W.54** We will pay for hidden damage only when the damage results from the Covered Loss and is otherwise covered under your policy. The discovery of hidden damage does not establish that all related conditions are covered.

**W.55** We may revise the loss settlement when an error, omission, or newly available evidence affects the covered amount. Any revision will remain subject to the terms and conditions of your policy.

**W.56** We will not treat a voluntary upgrade, redesign, expansion, or improvement as necessary repair of damaged Roof Surfacing. We will pay the reasonable cost of work necessary to repair the covered damage.

**W.57** We will pay for reasonable materials needed to connect repaired Roof Surfacing to undamaged adjoining materials when necessary to prevent water intrusion. We will determine necessity based on the repair method and condition of the property.

**W.58** We will evaluate the claim using information reasonably available from the insured location and the surrounding area. We may use aerial imagery, weather information, inspection findings, and other reliable sources.

**W.59** An insured must promptly notify us of a loss involving Roof Surfacing and must comply with all duties after loss. Compliance with those duties allows us to investigate the loss and determine the covered amount.

**W.60** This endorsement changes the loss settlement applicable to Roof Surfacing as stated in these provisions. All other policy provisions apply unless changed by this endorsement.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We will not pay more than the applicable "Limit of Liability" for loss to "Roof Surfacing." This limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims, or locations involved in the loss.

**W.2** The Limit of Liability is the most we will pay for all covered loss arising from the same occurrence. Payment for a covered loss does not increase the Limit of Liability.

**W.3** The Limit of Liability applies to the Roof Surfacing as a whole. We will not apply a separate limit to materials, labor, debris handling, or related services unless a separate limit expressly applies.

**W.4** We will determine the amount payable for covered loss to Roof Surfacing on an "Actual Cash Value" basis. Actual Cash Value means the value of the damaged property at the time of loss, reflecting appropriate depreciation.

**W.5** We will determine Actual Cash Value without regard to the cost of upgrading the damaged Roof Surfacing. We will not include the cost of materials or methods that are superior to those used in the damaged property.

**W.6** We will consider the age, condition, quality, and expected useful life of the damaged Roof Surfacing. We may also consider evidence of wear, deterioration, maintenance, and prior damage.

**W.7** We will not pay the cost to replace undamaged Roof Surfacing merely to achieve a uniform appearance. This limitation applies even if replacement materials differ in color, texture, design, or finish.

**W.8** We will pay for direct physical loss to damaged Roof Surfacing only. We will not pay for loss limited to cosmetic variation, weathering, fading, or ordinary discoloration.

**W.9** We will not pay more than the Actual Cash Value of the damaged Roof Surfacing. This applies whether you repair, replace, or do not repair the damaged property.

**W.10** We will not pay replacement cost for Roof Surfacing. The amount payable remains subject to the applicable Limit of Liability and Actual Cash Value determination.

**W.11** We will not pay for betterment resulting from repair or replacement. Betterment includes increased value, increased useful life, enhanced performance, or improved appearance.

**W.12** We will not pay for materials that exceed the kind and quality of the damaged Roof Surfacing. We may pay the Actual Cash Value of materials reasonably comparable to the damaged property.

**W.13** We will not pay for voluntary improvements made while repairing covered damage. This includes changes in design, construction, color, texture, or roof configuration.

**W.14** We will not pay for costs caused by delay, inconvenience, or loss of use of Roof Surfacing. Such costs do not increase the amount payable for direct physical loss.

**W.15** We will not pay for expenses incurred to investigate a loss unless we request those expenses in writing. Expenses you incur without our request do not increase our Limit of Liability.

**W.16** We may inspect the damaged Roof Surfacing before determining the amount payable. An insured must preserve the damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.17** An insured must provide records, photographs, estimates, invoices, and other information we reasonably request to evaluate the loss. Failure to provide requested information may affect our ability to determine the amount payable.

**W.18** We may use available market information to determine Actual Cash Value. Such information may include material pricing, labor pricing, condition assessments, and customary construction practices.

**W.19** We may account for physical depreciation affecting the damaged Roof Surfacing. Depreciation may differ among materials or portions of the damaged property.

**W.20** We will not reduce payment for depreciation attributable solely to covered sudden damage. We may reduce payment for preexisting wear, deterioration, or damage unrelated to the covered loss.

**W.21** We will not pay for damage that existed before the covered loss. We will pay only for the portion of loss directly caused by the covered occurrence.

**W.22** If covered damage combines with preexisting damage, we will determine the amount attributable to covered damage. We will not pay for the cost to correct preexisting damage.

**W.23** We will not pay for defective design, defective construction, faulty workmanship, or improper installation. We will, however, determine the amount payable for direct physical loss caused by a covered occurrence, subject to all applicable limitations.

**W.24** We will not pay for maintenance, upkeep, cleaning, or routine servicing. These expenses do not become covered merely because they are performed with repairs to damaged Roof Surfacing.

**W.25** We will not pay for loss caused by neglect after a covered occurrence. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect damaged Roof Surfacing from further damage.

**W.26** We will pay reasonable costs incurred to protect covered damaged Roof Surfacing from further damage when those costs are necessary and properly documented. Such payment is included within the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.27** We will not pay for permanent repairs made before we inspect the loss if those repairs prevent us from determining the cause or extent of damage. This does not prohibit reasonable temporary measures necessary to protect the property.

**W.28** We may determine the amount payable based on repair of the damaged portion of Roof Surfacing. We are not required to pay for replacement of larger areas unless replacement is necessary to repair direct physical damage.

**W.29** We may determine the amount payable based on replacement when repair is not reasonably practicable. Any replacement valuation remains limited to Actual Cash Value.

**W.30** We will not pay for materials, labor, or services that are not reasonably necessary to repair covered damage. We may limit payment to the reasonable cost of necessary work.

**W.31** We may consider whether repair is feasible using materials of like kind and quality. Differences in availability or market preference do not by themselves require payment for superior materials.

**W.32** We will not pay for costs arising solely from the inability to obtain an exact match for undamaged Roof Surfacing. We may pay for reasonably comparable materials available for repair.

**W.33** We will not pay for removal or replacement of undamaged property unless necessary to access covered damaged Roof Surfacing. Payment for necessary access remains subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.34** We will not pay for costs to alter structural components unless direct physical loss to those components is covered. Work performed solely to accommodate replacement Roof Surfacing is not payable unless necessary to repair covered damage.

**W.35** We will not pay for costs arising from changes in building requirements unless those costs result directly from covered physical damage and are otherwise payable. Such costs remain included within the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.36** We will not pay for permits, engineering, design, testing, or inspection costs unless they are reasonably necessary to repair covered damage. Any payment for those costs is included within the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.37** We will not pay for contractor overhead or profit unless reasonably necessary for repair of covered damage. We may evaluate the complexity and scope of the covered repair in making that determination.

**W.38** We may determine the amount payable using reasonable local pricing for materials and labor. We are not required to pay charges that exceed customary and reasonable pricing.

**W.39** We will not pay for charges resulting from a contractor's scheduling practices, business preferences, or financing arrangements. Such charges do not increase the amount payable for covered loss.

**W.40** We will not pay for charges based solely on emergency conditions unless emergency work was reasonably necessary to protect the damaged property. Payment for necessary emergency work remains subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.41** We may make payment to you, to a person or organization having an interest in the property, or to both. Payment does not create an obligation to make payment to any person not entitled to receive it.

**W.42** We may issue payment based on the information available when we adjust the loss. We may revise the amount payable if material information later establishes that the prior determination was incorrect.

**W.43** We may recover any payment made in excess of the amount properly payable. An insured must return any excess payment upon our request.

**W.44** Payment for a covered loss does not constitute an admission that other claimed damage is covered. We retain all rights to determine coverage and the amount payable for each claimed loss.

**W.45** We may require reasonable evidence of the condition of Roof Surfacing before the loss. Such evidence may include maintenance records, photographs, inspection records, and repair records.

**W.46** An insured must not intentionally conceal, discard, alter, or misrepresent material evidence concerning the loss. We may rely on available evidence when determining the amount payable.

**W.47** We may separate covered damage from damage resulting from causes not covered. We will not pay for the portion of loss attributable to causes not covered.

**W.48** We will not pay for repeated seepage, leakage, deterioration, or damage occurring over time. We will determine whether claimed damage resulted from a covered occurrence based on the facts of the loss.

**W.49** We will not pay for loss limited to granule loss, staining, marring, or surface wear that does not impair the function of Roof Surfacing. This limitation does not affect payment for direct physical damage otherwise covered.

**W.50** We will not pay for loss arising from improper prior repair of Roof Surfacing. We will pay only for direct physical loss caused by a covered occurrence.

**W.51** We will not pay for costs to correct conditions that existed before the loss. This includes inadequate fastening, inadequate flashing, improper ventilation, and inadequate drainage.

**W.52** We may deduct the value of salvage from the amount otherwise payable when salvage has value. An insured must not dispose of salvage without our consent when we request that it be retained.

**W.53** We may take all or part of the damaged Roof Surfacing at the value we determine. Our election to take salvage does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.54** We will not pay more than the financial interest an insured has in the damaged Roof Surfacing. This limitation applies even if the Actual Cash Value exceeds that financial interest.

**W.55** We will not pay more than the amount of direct physical loss sustained. We will not pay for speculative, contingent, or unsubstantiated costs.

**W.56** We may require an insured to provide a signed statement of the claimed loss. The statement must identify the damaged Roof Surfacing and the basis for the amount claimed.

**W.57** An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation and adjustment of the loss. Cooperation includes allowing reasonable inspection and providing requested information.

**W.58** We may obtain estimates, opinions, or reports from qualified persons to evaluate the claimed loss. We may consider those materials with information provided by an insured.

**W.59** We are not bound by an estimate obtained by an insured. We may determine the amount payable based on our evaluation of the covered damage and reasonable repair methods.

**W.60** We will not pay for costs included in an estimate that are unrelated to direct physical loss to Roof Surfacing. We may remove unrelated charges from the amount claimed.

**W.61** We will not pay for costs that duplicate amounts paid or payable from another source for the same damage. This limitation applies only to the extent payment would duplicate recovery for the same loss.

**W.62** We may reduce the amount payable by amounts recovered for the same damage from another source. We will not seek recovery beyond the amount necessary to prevent duplicate payment.

**W.63** We may settle a covered loss without requiring repair or replacement. Our settlement does not require you to use payment for repair or replacement.

**W.64** We will not pay additional amounts because you elect to delay repair or replacement. The amount payable is determined from the condition and value of Roof Surfacing at the time of loss.

**W.65** We will not pay for loss arising from an increase in construction costs after the time of loss. Changes in market conditions do not increase Actual Cash Value.

**W.66** We will not pay for costs associated with personal preference in selecting materials, contractors, or repair methods. We may pay the reasonable Actual Cash Value of necessary covered repairs.

**W.67** We may determine the amount payable without requiring that all damaged Roof Surfacing be removed. We may rely on inspection, testing, photographs, measurements, and other available evidence.

**W.68** We will not pay for exploratory work that is not reasonably necessary to determine or repair covered damage. We may pay for reasonable exploratory work when it is necessary to identify direct physical loss.

**W.69** We will not pay for costs to repair damage caused during voluntary repair work. This limitation does not apply to further damage directly caused by a covered occurrence.

**W.70** We may require that repairs be completed in a reasonable manner before considering supplemental payment. Supplemental payment remains subject to the applicable Limit of Liability and Actual Cash Value.

**W.71** We will not pay supplemental amounts based on invoices that exceed reasonable charges for covered work. An insured must provide sufficient documentation supporting any supplemental claim.

**W.72** The Limit of Liability applies whether loss is adjusted as repair, replacement, or a cash settlement. No adjustment method increases the amount we will pay beyond that limit.

**W.73** All payments for covered loss to Roof Surfacing are subject to the applicable Limit of Liability. Our total liability for that loss will not exceed the amount properly payable under this provision.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** A "Deductible" is the part of a "covered loss" that you must bear before we make payment. Covered loss means direct physical loss to covered property that we cover.

**W.2** We will reduce the amount otherwise payable for a covered loss by the applicable deductible. You are responsible for the deductible whether or not you repair or replace damaged property.

**W.3** "Composition shingle" means roof surfacing composed of manufactured shingle material. For composition shingle subject to an age-based roof-surfacing adjustment, the payable percentage is twenty percent.

**W.4** We apply any roof-surfacing payment adjustment before applying the deductible. We apply the deductible to the resulting amount of covered loss.

**W.5** "Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, subject to applicable depreciation and condition. The deductible applies to a covered loss settled on an actual cash value basis.

**W.6** If more than one deductible could apply to the same covered loss, we apply the deductible that applies under the circumstances of that loss. We will not apply deductibles in a manner that duplicates the same deductible obligation for the same covered loss.

**W.7** We apply the deductible separately to each occurrence. "Occurrence" means an accident, incident, or event that causes covered loss.

**W.8** All covered loss arising from the same occurrence is subject to the applicable deductible. This includes covered loss to roof surfacing and other covered property damaged by that occurrence.

**W.9** We determine the amount of covered loss before subtracting the deductible. We do not include the deductible in any payment made to you or for your benefit.

**W.10** If the amount of covered loss does not exceed the applicable deductible, we will make no payment for that loss. Your obligation to protect property from further damage continues despite the absence of payment.

**W.11** The deductible applies whether covered property is repaired, replaced, or not repaired. It also applies when payment is based on the value of damaged property.

**W.12** We apply the deductible only to covered loss. We do not apply the deductible to an amount that is not payable because it is excluded, limited, or otherwise not covered.

**W.13** You must cooperate with us in determining the amount of covered loss. You must provide information reasonably needed to determine the deductible that applies.

**W.14** You must identify the damaged property for which you seek payment. You must allow us to inspect that property when reasonably necessary to adjust the loss.

**W.15** We may determine the deductible based on the cause of loss and the property damaged. Our determination will be based on the facts of the occurrence and the coverage that applies.

**W.16** A deductible does not increase the amount of insurance available for covered loss. It reduces the amount that we otherwise would pay.

**W.17** Payment of a deductible by you does not create coverage for loss that is not covered. It does not change any condition applicable to a covered loss.

**W.18** The deductible applies to the total amount of covered loss before we issue payment. We may pay the amount remaining after application of the deductible directly to you or to another person entitled to payment.

**W.19** If you and we disagree about the amount of covered loss, the deductible will apply to the amount ultimately determined to be payable. We may withhold payment until the amount payable is determined.

**W.20** You may not avoid application of the deductible by presenting separate claims for damage arising from the same occurrence. We will treat related damage from the same occurrence as a single covered loss for deductible purposes.

**W.21** Damage discovered at different times may be treated as arising from the same occurrence when the facts establish a common cause. The deductible will apply according to that occurrence.

**W.22** We apply the deductible without regard to the identity of a person who caused the covered loss. We apply it whether the loss results from an accident, an event, or the acts of another person.

**W.23** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by wind, hail, falling objects, or other covered causes of loss. It applies only to the extent that the loss is covered.

**W.24** The deductible applies to covered loss involving roof surfacing. Roof surfacing means the exterior material forming the weather-resistant surface of a roof.

**W.25** We determine the condition of roof surfacing from the information available to us. You must provide records or other information reasonably requested to evaluate the damaged roof surfacing.

**W.26** We may consider inspection findings, photographs, repair records, and other relevant information in determining covered loss. Such information may be used to determine the amount remaining after the deductible is applied.

**W.27** You must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a covered loss. Costs that we pay for reasonable protective measures remain subject to the deductible when applicable.

**W.28** We will not pay for any part of a covered loss that is your responsibility under the deductible. This applies even if a mortgagee, lienholder, or other interested person has an interest in the covered property.

**W.29** A payment to a mortgagee, lienholder, or other interested person is subject to the deductible. Such payment does not relieve you of your deductible obligation.

**W.30** We may apply the deductible when calculating a payment for damaged property that is not replaced. We may also apply it when damaged property is repaired or replaced.

**W.31** The deductible applies before we determine any amount payable for loss of use or other covered consequential loss when the deductible applies to that loss. We will not apply a deductible to a payment unless that payment is part of a covered loss subject to a deductible.

**W.32** We may adjust a covered loss with you, your representative, or another person authorized to act for you. Any agreement concerning the deductible is subject to the terms of this policy.

**W.33** You must not incur expenses solely to avoid or reduce application of the deductible. We will not pay expenses that are not reasonable or necessary to protect covered property or adjust covered loss.

**W.34** If we recover money from a person responsible for a covered loss, we may account for the deductible in distributing the recovery. Your rights of recovery remain subject to our rights after payment of a covered loss.

**W.35** You must preserve our rights of recovery against any person responsible for covered loss. You must not impair those rights after a loss.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** "Covered Property" means property for which coverage is afforded under this endorsement. We will settle a covered loss to Covered Property as provided in this section.

**W.4** "Replacement Cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with material of like kind and quality. Replacement Cost includes reasonable costs necessary to restore the property to its pre-loss condition.

**W.5** "Depreciation" means a reduction in value resulting from age, wear, deterioration, obsolescence, condition, and remaining useful life. We will determine Depreciation from information reasonably available to us.

**W.7** Actual Cash Value for Roof Surfacing will be determined before application of any deductible. We will then apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss as otherwise provided by this endorsement.

**W.8** We may use repair, replacement, market, condition, material, labor, and other relevant information to determine Actual Cash Value. We may consider the age and condition of undamaged Roof Surfacing that is physically connected to damaged Roof Surfacing.

**W.9** We will not reduce payment for a condition that did not exist before the covered loss. We may reduce payment for preexisting wear, deterioration, damage, or defect.

**W.10** We will pay no more than the Actual Cash Value of the damaged Roof Surfacing immediately before the covered loss. We will not pay for betterment resulting from repair or replacement.

**W.11** If Roof Surfacing can be repaired, we may settle the loss based on the reasonable cost of repair, subject to Actual Cash Value. Repair may include material reasonably necessary to return the damaged area to its pre-loss condition.

**W.12** The loss settlement basis for personal property is actual cash value. We will determine the amount payable after application of all applicable limitations and deductibles.

**W.13** We may elect to repair damaged Roof Surfacing, replace damaged Roof Surfacing, or pay for the covered loss. Our election will not increase the amount otherwise payable under this section.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** "Actual Cash Value" means the value of covered property at the time of loss after deduction for depreciation. We determine depreciation from the condition, age, useful life, and remaining serviceability of the property.

**W.2** "Roof Surfacing" means the exterior material applied to a roof to shed water and protect the roof assembly from weather. Roof Surfacing includes materials that form the exposed weather barrier of the roof.

**W.3** "Roof Assembly" means the components of a roof that together provide weather protection and structural support. A Roof Assembly includes Roof Surfacing and may include other roof components.

**W.4** "Roof Covering" means material installed over a roof deck or other roof substrate to resist the entry of water. Roof Covering includes material commonly identified as roofing material.

**W.5** "Roof Deck" means the structural surface to which roofing materials are attached. A Roof Deck may consist of wood, metal, concrete, or another suitable construction material.

**W.6** "Roof Substrate" means material located beneath Roof Surfacing and above the Roof Deck. Roof Substrate may provide a base for the attachment or support of Roof Surfacing.

**W.7** "Underlayment" means material installed beneath Roof Surfacing to provide supplemental resistance to water intrusion. Underlayment does not include Roof Surfacing.

**W.8** "Flashing" means material installed at roof intersections, edges, penetrations, or transitions to direct water away from the roof assembly. Flashing may be composed of metal, membrane, or another weather-resistant material.

**W.9** "Roof Penetration" means an opening through a Roof Assembly for a vent, pipe, conduit, equipment support, or similar item. A Roof Penetration includes the surrounding flashing and sealing materials.

**W.10** "Roof Opening" means an opening in a Roof Assembly that allows water, wind, debris, or other elements to enter the building. A Roof Opening may result from direct physical loss to Roof Surfacing or another roof component.

**W.11** "Roof Edge" means the perimeter area of a Roof Assembly where Roof Surfacing terminates. Roof Edge includes an eave, rake, or other exposed perimeter transition.

**W.12** "Eave" means the lower edge of a sloped roof that projects beyond an exterior wall. An Eave may include a gutter, fascia, soffit, or drip edge.

**W.13** "Rake" means the inclined edge of a sloped roof extending from an eave toward a ridge. A Rake may include trim, flashing, or edge protection.

**W.14** "Ridge" means the upper horizontal intersection of opposing roof slopes. A Ridge may be covered by material designed to protect the intersection from weather.

**W.15** "Valley" means the interior angle formed by intersecting roof slopes that directs water toward a roof drainage path. A Valley may contain flashing or specially designed roofing material.

**W.16** "Hip" means the exterior angle formed by intersecting roof slopes. A Hip may be protected by cap material, flashing, or other roofing material.

**W.17** "Slope" means the inclined surface of a roof that directs water toward a drainage point or roof edge. Slope includes a substantially level roof surface designed to drain water.

**W.18** "Low-Slope Roof" means a roof having a generally shallow inclination. A Low-Slope Roof commonly uses membrane, built-up, modified bitumen, metal, or similar Roof Surfacing.

**W.19** "Steep-Slope Roof" means a roof having an inclination that permits the use of overlapping roofing units or panels. A Steep-Slope Roof commonly uses shingles, shakes, slate, tile, metal panels, or similar Roof Surfacing.

**W.20** "Shingle" means an individual overlapping roofing unit installed as Roof Surfacing. A Shingle may be composed of asphalt, composite material, wood, metal, or another roofing material.

**W.21** "Shake" means a split or sawn roofing unit installed in overlapping courses. A Shake may be composed of wood, composite material, metal, or another roofing material.

**W.22** "Tile" means an individual roofing unit commonly composed of clay, concrete, composite material, or another durable material. Tile is installed to form Roof Surfacing through overlapping or interlocking placement.

**W.23** "Slate" means a natural stone or manufactured roofing unit installed as Roof Surfacing. Slate may be attached by fasteners, hooks, or another suitable method.

**W.24** "Metal Roof Panel" means a formed metal unit installed as Roof Surfacing. A Metal Roof Panel may be fastened, interlocked, seamed, or otherwise secured to the roof structure.

**W.25** "Membrane Roofing" means flexible roofing material installed in sheets or layers to form a weather-resistant roof surface. Membrane Roofing may be adhered, mechanically attached, ballasted, or otherwise secured.

**W.26** "Built-Up Roofing" means Roof Surfacing composed of alternating layers of roofing material and protective surfacing. Built-Up Roofing may include aggregate, coating, cap sheet, or similar material.

**W.27** "Modified Bitumen Roofing" means asphalt-based membrane roofing modified with polymers or similar materials. Modified Bitumen Roofing may be installed in sheets or layers.

**W.28** "Roof Coating" means a liquid-applied material intended to protect, seal, restore, or extend the service life of Roof Surfacing. Roof Coating does not include structural repair material.

**W.29** "Protective Surfacing" means material applied over Roof Surfacing to protect against weather, ultraviolet exposure, foot traffic, or similar conditions. Protective Surfacing may include granules, aggregate, coating, or another protective material.

**W.30** "Granular Loss" means the displacement or wearing away of granules from asphalt-based Roof Surfacing. Granular Loss may occur from age, weathering, installation conditions, mechanical action, or direct physical loss.

**W.31** "Weathering" means gradual deterioration caused by exposure to sunlight, temperature changes, moisture, wind, airborne contaminants, or ordinary environmental conditions. Weathering includes fading, discoloration, surface erosion, and loss of flexibility.

**W.32** "Deterioration" means a decline in the physical condition or serviceability of property resulting from wear, age, decay, corrosion, or similar causes. Deterioration does not require sudden or accidental damage.

**W.33** "Wear and Tear" means damage or reduction in condition resulting from ordinary use, friction, contact, maintenance activities, or normal exposure. Wear and Tear develops gradually through use or time.

**W.34** "Useful Life" means the expected period during which property remains capable of performing its intended function under ordinary conditions. Useful Life may be affected by installation, maintenance, climate, material quality, and prior damage.

**W.35** "Serviceability" means the ability of property to perform its intended function. Serviceability of Roof Surfacing includes its ability to shed water under ordinary weather conditions.

**W.36** "Condition" means the physical state of property immediately before a loss. Condition includes visible wear, prior repairs, deterioration, and existing damage.

**W.37** "Prior Damage" means physical damage that existed before the reported loss. Prior Damage includes damage that has not been repaired or has been repaired inadequately.

**W.38** "Pre-Existing Condition" means a condition of property that existed before the reported loss, whether known or unknown to you. A Pre-Existing Condition may affect the condition or value of covered property.

**W.39** "Direct Physical Loss" means accidental physical damage to tangible property. Direct Physical Loss requires a distinct alteration of the property caused by a covered peril.

**W.40** "Covered Peril" means a cause of loss for which coverage is provided under this policy. A Covered Peril does not include a cause of loss subject to an exclusion or limitation.

**W.41** "Loss" means direct physical loss or damage resulting from a Covered Peril. Loss includes damage to Roof Surfacing only to the extent coverage applies.

**W.42** "Replacement Cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with material of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. Replacement Cost is determined subject to the terms of this policy.

**W.43** "Depreciation" means a reduction in value attributable to age, wear, deterioration, condition, obsolescence, or diminished useful life. Depreciation may vary among components of the same Roof Assembly.

**W.44** "Obsolescence" means a reduction in value resulting from a condition, design, material, feature, or construction method that is no longer current, commonly used, or readily available. Obsolescence may be functional, economic, or technological.

**W.45** "Like Kind and Quality" means material and workmanship reasonably comparable in function, utility, appearance, and performance to the damaged property. Like Kind and Quality does not require identical material, color, texture, design, or manufacturer.

**W.46** "Matching" means the degree of similarity in color, texture, pattern, design, size, material, or appearance between repaired or replaced property and undamaged property. Matching does not determine whether undamaged property has sustained Direct Physical Loss.

**W.47** "Repair" means work performed to restore damaged property to serviceable condition. Repair may include labor, material, equipment, and associated work reasonably necessary to complete the restoration.

**W.48** "Replacement" means removal and installation of property in place of damaged property. Replacement includes reasonable labor and material necessary to install the replacement property.

**W.49** "Roofing Contractor" means a person or organization engaged in the business of installing, repairing, replacing, or inspecting roofing materials. A Roofing Contractor may provide an estimate, inspection report, invoice, or other documentation concerning a loss.

**W.50** "Estimate" means a written evaluation of the anticipated cost of Repair or Replacement. An Estimate may include labor, material, equipment, overhead, profit, taxes, and other applicable charges.

**W.51** "Invoice" means a written request for payment identifying work performed, material supplied, or charges incurred. An Invoice may support a claim for covered loss when it reasonably identifies the work and charges related to the loss.

**W.52** "Proof of Loss" means a statement or documentation provided by you to establish the facts, amount, and circumstances of a claimed loss. Proof of Loss may include records, photographs, estimates, invoices, receipts, or other supporting information.
