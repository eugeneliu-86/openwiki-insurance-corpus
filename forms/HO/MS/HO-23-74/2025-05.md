## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" is attached to your "policy". It forms part of your policy when it is
attached to your policy.

This endorsement applies to loss settlement for "roof surfacing". Roof surfacing
includes the materials forming the outermost weather-resistant surface of a roof.

We will apply this endorsement to a covered loss involving roof surfacing. We will
determine the amount of loss under this endorsement in accordance with its terms.

Actual cash value is the value of damaged roof surfacing at the time of loss. Actual
cash value reflects applicable depreciation and the condition of the damaged roof
surfacing.

We will determine depreciation based on relevant characteristics of the damaged roof
surfacing. Those characteristics may include age, condition, expected useful life, and
degree of wear.

We will not include depreciation for a condition that did not exist before the loss. We
will apply depreciation only to the extent it is supported by the condition of the
damaged roof surfacing.

The amount payable for roof surfacing may be less than the cost to repair or replace the
damaged roof surfacing. Payment under this endorsement does not require us to pay
replacement cost for roof surfacing.

You must provide information we reasonably need to determine the actual cash value of
damaged roof surfacing. You must cooperate with us in our investigation and adjustment
of the loss.

You must protect damaged roof surfacing from further damage after a loss. You must make
reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property from further damage.

We may inspect damaged roof surfacing before repair, replacement, or disposal. You must
retain damaged roof surfacing when we reasonably request that you do so.

We may require photographs, estimates, invoices, receipts, reports, or other records
relating to the loss. You must provide requested records that are available to you.

We may determine the amount of loss based on available information. Our determination
may include information obtained from inspections, records, experts, contractors, or
other sources.

We do not cover any increase in loss caused by your failure to protect the property from
further damage. We do not cover costs that are not caused by a covered loss.

This endorsement does not create coverage for a loss that is not otherwise covered. This
endorsement does not change any applicable exclusion, condition, limit, or deductible.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy, this
endorsement controls with respect to roof surfacing. All other provisions of your policy
remain applicable.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the loss settlement described in this endorsement for "Roof
Surfacing" damaged by a "Covered Loss". This endorsement applies only to property for
which coverage is provided under your policy.

**W.2** "Roof Surfacing" means the exterior material applied to a roof to shed water and
protect the structure beneath it. Roof Surfacing includes associated materials that are
necessary to install the damaged roof surfacing in a workmanlike manner.

**W.3** "Actual Cash Value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss
after deduction for depreciation, condition, age, and obsolescence. We determine Actual
Cash Value using available information concerning the damaged property and the cost to
repair or replace it.

**W.4** When the Roof Age is twelve years or greater, we will settle a Covered Loss to
Roof Surfacing on an Actual Cash Value basis. We will apply this loss settlement whether
or not you repair or replace the damaged Roof Surfacing.

**W.5** "Roof Age" means the age of the damaged Roof Surfacing at the time of loss. We
may determine Roof Age from installation records, permits, invoices, inspections,
photographs, statements, or other reliable evidence.

**W.6** We will determine Roof Age based on the portion of Roof Surfacing that sustained
direct physical damage. Replacement of a limited area does not establish the age of Roof
Surfacing outside that area unless the evidence shows that the roof surfacing was
replaced as part of the same installation.

**W.7** We will pay the Actual Cash Value of damaged Roof Surfacing that is necessary to
repair the direct physical loss. We will not reduce payment solely because undamaged
Roof Surfacing remains in serviceable condition.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We limit our payment for direct physical loss to roof surfacing to the amount
determined under this provision. We determine that amount using the "actual cash value"
of the damaged roof surfacing. We apply this limit only to roof surfacing that is
damaged by a covered peril.

**W.2** "Roof surfacing" means the material forming the weather-resistant exterior
surface of a roof. Roof surfacing includes associated materials that are installed as
part of that surface. Roof surfacing does not include structural framing or interior
building materials.

**W.3** We determine the actual cash value of damaged roof surfacing at the time of
loss. We consider the cost to repair or replace the damaged roof surfacing with material
of like kind and quality. We also consider depreciation and other factors that reduce
the value of the damaged roof surfacing.

**W.4** We do not pay more than the actual cash value of the damaged roof surfacing.
This limitation applies whether the damaged roof surfacing is repaired, replaced, or not
repaired or replaced. The limitation applies regardless of the method used to determine
the amount of loss.

**W.5** We determine the amount of loss based on the portion of roof surfacing that is
damaged by a covered peril. We do not include the cost of replacing undamaged roof
surfacing solely to achieve a uniform appearance. We do not include the cost of
correcting conditions that existed before the loss.

**W.6** We may use reasonable methods to determine the age, condition, quality, and
remaining useful life of the damaged roof surfacing. These methods may include
inspection, photographs, invoices, maintenance records, and information from
manufacturers or contractors. We may also consider comparable roof surfacing in the
local market.

**W.7** You must provide information that we reasonably request to determine the
actual cash value of the damaged roof surfacing. You must permit us to inspect the
damaged property as often as we reasonably require. You must not discard damaged roof
surfacing before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, except that you may
take reasonable measures to protect the property from further damage.

**W.8** We may inspect the roof surfacing before determining the amount of loss. We may
request samples or other evidence of the materials used in the roof surfacing. We do not
waive any provision of this policy by inspecting the property or by discussing the loss
with you.

**W.9** We determine depreciation based on the age, condition, and expected service life
of the damaged roof surfacing. We may consider deterioration, weathering, wear and tear,
neglect, and prior damage. We do not apply depreciation to a condition that did not
affect the value of the damaged roof surfacing.

**W.10** We do not cover loss resulting solely from wear and tear, marring,
deterioration, or inherent defect. We do not cover the cost to replace roof surfacing

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** We apply the "deductible" to a covered "loss" under this provision. You must
bear the deductible before we pay for the loss. We determine the amount payable after
applying the deductible.

**W.2** The deductible applies to covered damage to "roof surfacing". We apply the
deductible to the amount of covered damage otherwise payable. We do not apply the
deductible to excluded damage.

**W.3** For composition shingle roof surfacing within the applicable age category, the
payable percentage is twenty percent. Except that this payable percentage applies only
when the covered loss involves composition shingle roof surfacing within that category.
We determine the amount payable before applying the deductible.

**W.4** We determine the amount of covered damage from the repair or replacement
required because of the covered loss. You must provide information reasonably necessary
to determine that amount. We may inspect the damaged property before determining the
amount payable.

**W.5** We apply the deductible to the covered damage that results from the same cause.
We do not reduce the deductible because separate items are damaged. We consider damage
together when it results from the same occurrence.

**W.6** You must notify us promptly of any covered loss. You must protect the property
from further damage after the loss. We do not cover damage resulting from your failure
to protect the property.

**W.7** You must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property
from further damage. You must keep records of those repairs and preserve the damaged
materials when reasonably possible. We may include covered temporary repair expenses in
the amount of the loss.

**W.8** We do not cover the cost of correcting faulty, inadequate, or defective
maintenance. We do not cover deterioration that is not caused by a covered loss. We may
cover resulting damage when the policy provides coverage for that damage.

**W.9** We determine the amount of covered damage without including improvements or
betterments. You must bear the cost of improvements that exceed what is reasonably
necessary to repair or replace the damaged property. We do not apply the deductible to
amounts that are not covered.

property to its condition before the loss. You must obtain our consent before making
repairs that materially affect the amount of the loss. We may determine that replacement

**W.33** "Wear and Tear" means damage or reduction in condition resulting from ordinary
use, friction, contact, maintenance activities, or normal exposure. Wear and Tear
develops gradually through use or time.

**W.34** "Useful Life" means the expected period during which property remains capable
of performing its intended function under ordinary conditions. Useful Life may be
affected by installation, maintenance, climate, material quality, and prior damage.

**W.35** "Serviceability" means the ability of property to perform its intended
function. Serviceability of Roof Surfacing includes its ability to shed water under
ordinary weather conditions.

**W.36** "Condition" means the physical state of property immediately before a loss.
Condition includes visible wear, prior repairs, deterioration, and existing damage.

**W.37** "Prior Damage" means physical damage that existed before the reported loss.
Prior Damage includes damage that has not been repaired or has been repaired
inadequately.

**W.38** "Pre-Existing Condition" means a condition of property that existed before the
reported loss, whether known or unknown to you. A Pre-Existing Condition may affect the
condition or value of covered property.

**W.39** "Direct Physical Loss" means accidental physical damage to tangible property.
Direct Physical Loss requires a distinct alteration of the property caused by a covered
peril.

**W.40** "Covered Peril" means a cause of loss for which coverage is provided under this
policy. A Covered Peril does not include a cause of loss subject to an exclusion or
limitation.

**W.41** "Loss" means direct physical loss or damage resulting from a Covered Peril.
Loss includes damage to Roof Surfacing only to the extent coverage applies.

**W.42** "Replacement Cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with
material of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. Replacement Cost
is determined subject to the terms of this policy.

**W.43** "Depreciation" means a reduction in value attributable to age, wear,
deterioration, condition, obsolescence, or diminished useful life. Depreciation may vary
among components of the same Roof Assembly.

**W.44** "Obsolescence" means a reduction in value resulting from a condition, design,

**W.45** "Like Kind and Quality" means material and workmanship reasonably comparable in

**W.46** "Matching" means the degree of similarity in color, texture, pattern, design,
size, material, or appearance between repaired or replaced property and undamaged

Physical Loss.

**W.47** "Repair" means work performed to restore damaged property to serviceable
condition. Repair may include labor, material, equipment, and associated work reasonably
necessary to complete the restoration.

**W.48** "Replacement" means removal and installation of property in place of damaged
property. Replacement includes reasonable labor and material necessary to install the
replacement property.

**W.49** "Roofing Contractor" means a person or organization engaged in the business of
installing, repairing, replacing, or inspecting roofing materials. A Roofing Contractor
may provide an estimate, inspection report, invoice, or other documentation concerning a
loss.

**W.50** "Estimate" means a written evaluation of the anticipated cost of Repair or
Replacement. An Estimate may include labor, material, equipment, overhead, profit,
taxes, and other applicable charges.

**W.51** "Invoice" means a written request for payment identifying work performed,
material supplied, or charges incurred. An Invoice may support a claim for covered loss
when it reasonably identifies the work and charges related to the loss.

**W.52** "Proof of Loss" means a statement or documentation provided by you to establish
the facts, amount, and circumstances of a claimed loss. Proof of Loss may include
records, photographs, estimates, invoices, receipts, or other supporting information.
