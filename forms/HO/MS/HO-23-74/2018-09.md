# HO 23 74 Actual Cash Value Loss Settlement — Roof Surfacing (Edition 2018-09)

Multistate endorsement. Attaches to HO-3 and modifies Section I A.4.

## R.1 Scope

This endorsement changes the loss settlement basis for roof surfacing only. Roof surfacing means shingles, tiles, shakes, metal panels, membrane, and the underlayment and flashing directly beneath them. It does not include the roof deck, trusses, rafters, sheathing, or any interior finish.

All components of the dwelling other than roof surfacing continue to be settled at replacement cost under HO-3 Section I A.3.

## R.2 Settlement Basis

Loss to roof surfacing caused by windstorm or hail is settled at actual cash value, determined by applying the depreciation schedule in R.3 to the replacement cost of the roof surfacing at the time of loss, less the applicable deductible.

Loss to roof surfacing caused by any other covered peril is settled at replacement cost, and this endorsement does not apply to that loss.

## R.3 Depreciation Schedule

The depreciation applied is based on the age of the roof surfacing at the date of loss and on the surfacing material, as follows.

For composition shingle, the payable percentage of replacement cost is one hundred percent for roofs under five years of age, eighty percent from five to nine years, sixty percent from ten to fourteen years, forty percent from fifteen to nineteen years, and twenty-five percent for roofs twenty years of age or older.

For architectural shingle, metal, and tile, the payable percentage is one hundred percent for roofs under ten years of age, eighty percent from ten to nineteen years, sixty percent from twenty to twenty-nine years, and forty percent for roofs thirty years of age or older.

For wood shake, the payable percentage is one hundred percent for roofs under five years of age, seventy percent from five to nine years, and forty percent for roofs ten years of age or older.

## R.4 Minimum Payment

In no event will the payable amount for roof surfacing be less than twenty-five percent of the replacement cost of that surfacing, before application of the deductible.

## R.5 Age Determination

The age of the roof surfacing is determined from the documented date of original installation or of the most recent full replacement, whichever is later. Where the insured cannot document that date, the age of the roof surfacing is presumed to be the age of the dwelling.

Partial repair of a roof does not reset its age. Replacement of a roof slope in full resets the age for that slope only.

## R.6 Interaction With Ordinance or Law

Where an ordinance or law requires replacement of undamaged roof surfacing in order to repair the damaged portion, the cost of that additional replacement is subject to HO-3 Section I — Exclusions D.1 and is not payable under this endorsement unless an ordinance or law endorsement is attached.

All other provisions of the policy apply.
