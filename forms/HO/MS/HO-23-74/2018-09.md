# HO 23 74 Actual Cash Value Loss Settlement — Roof Surfacing (Edition 2018-09)
> SUPERSEDED by HO 23 74 Actual Cash Value Loss Settlement — Roof Surfacing (Edition 2025-05) for policies effective on or after 2025-05-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement is attached to and forms part of your policy. It changes the insurance provided by your policy only as stated in this endorsement.

This endorsement applies to a covered loss involving "roof surfacing". The term "roof surfacing" means material that forms the exposed exterior surface of a roof.

The loss settlement basis for covered roof surfacing is "actual cash value". We determine actual cash value after deduction for depreciation.

This endorsement applies when covered damage requires repair or replacement of roof surfacing. Coverage applies only to the extent provided by your policy and this endorsement.

This endorsement does not provide coverage for any property that is not otherwise covered by your policy. It does not broaden any cause of loss covered by your policy.

An insured must comply with all applicable duties relating to a covered loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss.

We will determine the amount payable for covered roof surfacing under the terms of this endorsement. Any payment remains subject to all applicable policy conditions.

Depreciation may reflect the age, condition, and expected useful life of damaged roof surfacing. Depreciation may also reflect wear, deterioration, and obsolescence.

We may inspect damaged property before determining the amount payable. An insured must permit us to inspect the property when reasonably required.

An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for us to evaluate a claim involving roof surfacing. This information may include records, estimates, invoices, photographs, and other relevant evidence.

We may consider the condition of roof surfacing immediately before the covered loss. We may also consider repairs, maintenance, and prior damage affecting that property.

This endorsement does not require us to pay for improvement, betterment, or increased value. Payment is limited to the applicable actual cash value of covered roof surfacing.

Any policy provision that is inconsistent with this endorsement is amended to conform to this endorsement. All other policy provisions remain unchanged.

The application of this endorsement depends on the facts of each covered loss. We will apply its provisions according to the information available to us.

This endorsement applies only while it forms part of your policy.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the loss settlement described in this section for direct physical loss to “roof surfacing” caused by a covered peril. “Roof surfacing” means the exterior material applied to a roof to shed water and protect the structure beneath it.

**W.2** The actual cash value loss settlement described in this section applies when the roof surfacing has reached fifteen years of age at the time of loss. We determine the age of roof surfacing from reliable evidence available to us.

**W.3** “Actual cash value” means the amount required to repair or replace damaged property with material of like kind and quality, less a deduction for depreciation. Depreciation reflects the age, condition, useful life, wear, and obsolescence of the damaged property.

**W.4** We will pay actual cash value for covered loss to roof surfacing subject to the applicable limit of liability. We will not pay replacement cost for roof surfacing to which this section applies.

**W.5** We determine actual cash value as of the time of loss. We may consider the condition of the roof surfacing immediately before the loss.

**W.6** We will determine the amount of depreciation applicable to damaged roof surfacing. Depreciation may differ among materials, components, and areas of the roof.

**W.7** We may apply depreciation to labor when labor is necessary to repair or replace roof surfacing. We will apply depreciation reasonably and consistently with the condition of the damaged property.

**W.8** We will not reduce payment for depreciation attributable solely to a covered loss. We may reduce payment for deterioration, wear, or other condition that existed before the covered loss.

**W.9** We will pay for repair of damaged roof surfacing when repair will restore the damaged property to like kind and quality. We are not required to pay for replacement when repair is reasonably available.

**W.10** We will pay for replacement of damaged roof surfacing when replacement is necessary because repair is not reasonably feasible. Replacement payment remains subject to actual cash value loss settlement.

**W.11** We may select the method used to determine the reasonable cost of repair or replacement. We may use estimates, invoices, material pricing, labor pricing, inspections, and other reliable information.

**W.12** We may inspect the damaged roof surfacing before determining payment. An insured must allow us reasonable access to the property for inspection.

**W.13** An insured must protect damaged property from further loss. We will pay for reasonable and necessary measures taken solely to protect covered property from further covered damage.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by an insured’s failure to protect damaged property after the loss. We do not cover any increase in loss resulting from that failure.

**W.15** An insured must retain damaged roof surfacing when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.16** We may require an insured to provide photographs, samples, invoices, records, or other information relevant to the loss. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable request for that information.

**W.17** We may require an insured to provide evidence establishing the age and condition of roof surfacing. Such evidence may include installation records, maintenance records, inspection reports, or other reliable documentation.

**W.18** If reliable evidence does not establish the age of roof surfacing, we may determine age from available information. Available information may include the condition, construction, materials, and history of the roof surfacing.

**W.19** We will determine whether damaged roof surfacing can be repaired without impairing its function. We may consider appearance when appearance is materially affected by a covered loss.

**W.20** We will not pay for repair or replacement of undamaged roof surfacing unless required to complete repair of covered damaged property. Any payment for such work remains subject to actual cash value loss settlement.

**W.21** We will not pay for any improvement, betterment, or upgrade. We will pay only the actual cash value of property of like kind and quality.

**W.22** We will not pay for replacement with materials of greater quality, greater durability, or greater value than the damaged roof surfacing. If an insured chooses such materials, the insured must pay the additional cost.

**W.23** We will not pay for changes in design, construction, or layout that are not necessary to repair covered damage. We will not pay for work performed solely to improve the property.

**W.24** We will not pay for costs arising from defective design, defective construction, defective workmanship, or defective materials. We will pay for ensuing covered direct physical loss to roof surfacing unless otherwise excluded.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, decay, or inherent defect. We do not cover loss caused by maintenance failure.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective maintenance. We do not cover the cost to correct conditions that existed before the covered loss.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage. We do not cover loss caused by water entering through a condition that existed before the covered loss.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungi, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do not cover any cost to test for, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize those conditions.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not apply to reasonable emergency measures taken to protect covered property.

**W.30** We do not cover loss to roof surfacing caused by earth movement, flood, or surface water. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law that requires demolition, repair, replacement, reconstruction, remodeling, or remediation. We do not cover increased costs resulting from enforcement of ordinance or law.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion. We do not cover loss caused by corrosion, rust, or mechanical breakdown.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by birds, rodents, insects, or vermin. We do not cover damage caused by animals owned or kept by an insured.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations, or discharge of pollutants. We do not cover costs arising from the release, dispersal, seepage, migration, or escape of pollutants.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts committed by an insured. We do not cover loss caused by criminal acts committed by an insured.

**W.36** We will pay only for direct physical loss caused by a covered peril. We will not pay for economic loss that does not result from direct physical loss to roof surfacing.

**W.37** We will not pay for loss resulting from delay, loss of use, loss of market, or loss of value. We will not pay for inconvenience or loss of enjoyment.

**W.38** We will not pay for costs to match color, texture, style, or appearance of undamaged property unless necessary to repair covered damaged roof surfacing. Any such payment remains subject to actual cash value loss settlement.

**W.39** We may determine that roof surfacing has sustained separate areas of damage. We may apply actual cash value separately to each damaged area.

**W.40** We may determine that different roof materials have different useful lives and depreciation characteristics. We may apply different depreciation to different materials or components.

**W.41** We will not pay for loss attributable to preexisting damage. We will pay only for the portion of damage directly caused by a covered peril.

**W.42** If covered and uncovered causes contribute to loss, we will determine the portion of loss caused by the covered peril. We will not pay for the portion caused by excluded or otherwise uncovered causes.

**W.43** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide documents and records relevant to the claimed loss.

**W.44** An insured must provide a signed statement describing the loss when we reasonably request it. The statement must identify damaged property and the circumstances of the loss.

**W.45** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent any material fact or circumstance relating to the loss. We may deny coverage for a claim affected by material concealment or misrepresentation.

**W.46** We may pay an insured, a contractor, or another person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment to any person does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.47** We may adjust the loss with an insured or an authorized representative. We are not required to recognize an assignment of benefits unless we agree in writing.

**W.48** We may require that damaged property be repaired or replaced before payment is made to a contractor. We may issue payment jointly to an insured and a person having a financial interest in the property.

**W.49** We may recover from a responsible person any payment we make for a covered loss. An insured must transfer to us any rights of recovery against that person to the extent of our payment.

**W.50** An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our right of recovery. Any recovery obtained by an insured for covered loss must be held for us to the extent of our payment.

**W.51** We may settle a covered loss by paying the actual cash value of damaged roof surfacing. We may elect to repair or replace damaged roof surfacing with material of like kind and quality.

**W.52** If we elect to repair or replace damaged roof surfacing, we may select persons to perform the work. We will not be responsible for work performed by a person selected by an insured unless we agree otherwise.

**W.53** Payment under this section does not increase any applicable limit of liability. All payments for the same covered loss remain subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.54** This section applies only to roof surfacing for which a covered loss occurs during the policy period. Coverage remains subject to all applicable policy terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions.

**W.55** If any provision of this section conflicts with another applicable provision, this section governs loss settlement for roof surfacing to which it applies. All other applicable provisions remain in effect.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We determine the amount payable for covered damage to "roof surfacing" under the actual cash value basis stated in this provision. We apply that basis when the loss involves roof surfacing, regardless of whether other covered property is damaged. We do not increase the amount payable because the damaged roof surfacing could be repaired or replaced with materials of greater quality.

**W.2** "Actual Cash Value" means the amount required to repair or replace damaged roof surfacing, less applicable depreciation. We determine depreciation based on the age, condition, and remaining useful life of the damaged roof surfacing. We do not treat the cost of undamaged property as part of Actual Cash Value.

**W.3** Our payment for covered damage to roof surfacing is subject to the applicable "Limit of Liability." The Limit of Liability applies to the covered roof surfacing that is damaged by the same loss. We do not pay more than the applicable Limit of Liability for that covered roof surfacing.

**W.4** We determine the applicable Limit of Liability when we adjust the loss. We use the Limit of Liability that applies to the covered property at the time of loss. We do not apply a limit that became effective after the loss.

**W.5** We calculate the amount of covered damage before applying the applicable deductible. We then apply the deductible in accordance with the terms that govern the loss. We do not reduce the applicable Limit of Liability by the deductible.

**W.6** We pay only for direct physical loss to covered roof surfacing. We determine the amount of direct physical loss from the condition of the roof surfacing immediately before the loss and immediately after the loss. We do not pay for a condition that does not result from direct physical loss.

**W.7** We do not cover wear and tear affecting roof surfacing. We do not cover deterioration, inherent vice, or latent defect affecting roof surfacing. We do not include those conditions in the amount of covered damage.

**W.8** We consider the age and condition of roof surfacing when we determine Actual Cash Value. We may use available information concerning the materials, installation, maintenance, and repair of the roof surfacing. We do not assume that roof surfacing has the same remaining useful life as the building.

**W.9** We determine depreciation for the damaged portion of the roof surfacing. We may consider whether the damaged portion can be repaired without replacing adjoining undamaged materials. We do not depreciate undamaged roof surfacing that is not included in the covered damage.

**W.10** We pay the reasonable cost to repair covered damage when repair is permitted under applicable law and can be completed without replacing undamaged roof surfacing. We reduce that cost by applicable depreciation to determine Actual Cash Value. We do not pay for work that is not reasonably related to repairing the covered damage.

**W.11** We pay the reasonable cost to replace damaged roof surfacing when repair is not reasonably possible. We reduce that cost by applicable depreciation to determine Actual Cash Value. We do not pay the cost of replacing roof surfacing that was not damaged by the covered loss.

**W.12** We determine whether repair or replacement is reasonably possible from the physical condition of the roof surfacing. We may consider the availability of matching materials and the customary practices of competent contractors. We do not treat a preference for replacement as proof that repair is not reasonably possible.

**W.13** We do not cover the cost of replacing undamaged roof surfacing solely to create a uniform appearance. We do not cover the cost of replacing undamaged roof surfacing solely because matching materials are unavailable. We do cover covered damage to undamaged property that is independently caused by the same covered loss.

**W.14** We determine the amount payable without regard to an increase in value resulting from the repair or replacement. We do not pay for improvements, upgrades, or betterments to roof surfacing. We pay only for the covered interest that existed immediately before the loss.

**W.15** We may inspect the damaged roof surfacing before determining the amount payable. You must permit us to inspect the roof surfacing and related portions of the building. You must preserve evidence of the loss unless preservation would increase the damage.

**W.16** You must provide information reasonably necessary to determine the condition and value of the damaged roof surfacing. The information may include invoices, maintenance records, repair records, photographs, and estimates. We do not require information that is not reasonably related to the loss.

**W.17** You must take reasonable steps to protect the roof surfacing from further damage after a loss. You must keep receipts and other records for reasonable temporary measures taken to protect the property. We do not pay for measures that are unreasonable or unrelated to protecting covered property.

**W.18** We include reasonable temporary measures in the amount payable when those measures are necessary to protect covered property from further damage. We apply Actual Cash Value to covered temporary measures when they form part of the covered loss. We do not pay for permanent repairs made without our consent when those repairs prevent us from inspecting the damage.

**W.19** We determine the amount payable from competent and reliable information concerning the damaged roof surfacing. We may compare estimates, invoices, and other evidence that addresses the same covered damage. We do not base payment solely on an estimate that includes work unrelated to the covered loss.

**W.20** We may consider the cost of labor and materials reasonably necessary to repair or replace the damaged roof surfacing. We may consider the customary cost for comparable work in the area where the loss occurred. We do not include charges that are not reasonably necessary for the covered repair or replacement.

**W.21** We do not cover costs caused solely by a change in building codes or ordinances. We do not cover costs caused solely by a requirement to upgrade undamaged roof surfacing. We apply this limitation even when the upgraded work is performed as part of repairing covered damage.

**W.22** We do not cover costs caused solely by defective, faulty, or inadequate design, specifications, workmanship, construction, or materials. We do not include the cost to correct those conditions in Actual Cash Value. We do cover ensuing covered damage when the applicable coverage otherwise applies.

**W.23** We do not cover damage caused solely by the failure to maintain roof surfacing. We do not cover the cost to correct maintenance that should have been performed before the loss. We do include covered damage that is not solely the result of a maintenance condition.

**W.24** We determine the value of damaged roof surfacing without including the value of salvage that you retain. We may deduct the reasonable value of salvage when you retain damaged materials. We do not deduct salvage that has no reasonable value.

**W.25** You must allow us to take possession of damaged property when we reasonably require it for inspection or salvage. You must not abandon damaged property to us without our consent. We do not accept responsibility for property you retain or remove without our consent.

**W.26** We do not pay for the cost to remove undamaged roof surfacing when removal is not reasonably necessary to repair covered damage. We do not pay for the cost to remove materials that are unrelated to the covered loss. We pay reasonable removal costs when removal is necessary to perform covered repairs.

**W.27** We do not pay for consequential loss resulting from the condition of roof surfacing. We do not pay for loss of use, delay, loss of market value, or other indirect loss unless coverage for that loss otherwise applies. We determine the amount payable for covered direct physical loss separately from any consequential loss.

**W.28** We apply the applicable Limit of Liability to the covered roof surfacing that is the subject of the claim. We do not combine that Limit of Liability with a limit applicable solely to other covered property. We do not use the value of other property to increase the amount payable for roof surfacing.

**W.29** We determine the amount payable for each covered loss separately when roof surfacing sustains damage in separate losses. We apply the applicable deductible and Limit of Liability to each loss as required by the terms governing that loss. We do not combine unrelated losses merely because they affect the same roof surfacing.

**W.30** We may allocate repair or replacement costs when a portion of the claimed work relates to covered damage and another portion relates to an uncovered condition. We pay the portion reasonably attributable to covered damage, subject to the applicable Limit of Liability. We do not pay an undivided charge when the covered and uncovered work can reasonably be separated.

**W.31** We may require a detailed estimate that identifies labor, materials, removal, disposal, and other charges. You must cooperate with our reasonable request for a detailed estimate. We do not delay determination of the covered amount for information that is not material to the claim.

**W.32** We determine Actual Cash Value as of the time of loss. We do not use a later increase or decrease in repair costs to change the condition of the damaged roof surfacing at the time of loss. We may use later cost information to evaluate the reasonableness of an estimate.

**W.33** We do not pay for damage that existed before the covered loss. We do not pay for the cost to restore roof surfacing to a condition better than its condition immediately before the covered loss. We do pay for new covered damage that can be distinguished from preexisting damage.

**W.34** You must give us reasonable access to the building and roof surfacing for inspection of the claimed damage. You must cooperate with reasonable requests to identify the location, extent, and cause of the loss. We do not require access that would be unsafe or unrelated to the claim.

**W.35** We may base our determination on the condition of the roof surfacing before the claimed loss. We may consider photographs, inspection findings, repair records, and other reliable evidence of that condition. We do not presume that all visible damage resulted from the claimed loss.

**W.36** We do not pay more than the amount permitted under the applicable Limit of Liability after applying the Actual Cash Value basis. We do not pay for amounts that exceed the reasonable cost of covered repair or replacement. We do not pay for amounts that are attributable to uncovered damage, excluded conditions, or improvements.

**W.37** We pay the amount of covered loss determined under this provision, subject to all applicable conditions and exclusions. You must comply with your duties that apply to the claim and the damaged property. We do not waive any condition or exclusion by investigating, estimating, or making payment.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "Deductible" is the amount you must bear for a covered loss before we pay for that loss. You must satisfy the Deductible for each covered loss unless this provision provides otherwise. The Deductible applies to the amount of loss that is otherwise payable under this coverage.

**W.2** You must bear the Deductible before we pay any amount for covered damage to roof surfacing. We apply the Deductible after determining the amount of covered damage and before determining the amount we will pay. You may not recover the Deductible from us as part of the covered loss.

**W.3** For composition shingle roof surfacing that has reached the applicable age, the payable percentage is twenty-five percent. Except that this percentage applies only to the covered amount attributable to that roof surfacing. We determine the covered amount before applying the Deductible.

**W.4** We apply the payable percentage to the cost to repair or replace the damaged composition shingle roof surfacing. Subject to the terms that apply to the loss, the resulting amount is the maximum amount considered for payment for that roof surfacing. We do not apply the payable percentage to covered property that is not composition shingle roof surfacing.

**W.5** You must provide us with information reasonably necessary to identify the type, condition, and age of the damaged roof surfacing. Except that we may use available information to determine those facts when you do not provide information that is reasonably available to you. We may require reasonable documentation supporting the information you provide.

**W.6** We determine the amount of covered loss based on the condition of the damaged property immediately before the loss. Subject to the applicable settlement basis, we may consider wear, deterioration, and other conditions that affect the value of the damaged roof surfacing. We do not cover damage that is not caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.7** We do not cover the cost to replace undamaged roof surfacing solely to create a uniform appearance. Except that we cover such additional cost when replacement of undamaged property is required by an applicable building ordinance or law and that coverage applies to the loss. We determine any covered additional cost separately from the amount attributable to the damaged roof surfacing.

**W.8** You must protect the property from further damage after a loss. Unless we approve otherwise, you must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property from further damage. We do not pay for damage resulting from your failure to protect the property.

**W.9** You must give us prompt notice of the loss and cooperate with our investigation. Except that cooperation includes providing access to the property and permitting us to inspect the damaged roof surfacing. We may require a sworn proof of loss when reasonably necessary to determine the amount of loss.

**W.10** We may inspect the roof surfacing before determining the amount of covered loss. Subject to reasonable notice, you must permit us and persons acting on our behalf to inspect the property. Our inspection does not waive any policy condition or establish that the loss is covered.

**W.11** We determine the amount of covered loss without regard to any amount that exceeds the value of the damaged property under the applicable settlement basis. Except that we may consider reasonable costs necessary to repair or replace covered damaged property. We do not pay for costs attributable solely to improvements or betterments.

**W.12** You must submit estimates and other documents that reasonably support the amount you claim. Unless we agree otherwise, the estimates must separately identify costs for damaged roof surfacing and other damaged property. We may use our own estimate when the information provided does not reasonably establish the amount of loss.

**W.13** We pay only for covered damage that is supported by the facts and circumstances of the loss. Subject to the applicable coverage provisions, we may allocate a repair or replacement cost between covered damage and excluded damage. We do not pay the portion attributable to an excluded cause or condition.

**W.14** When covered damage affects roof surfacing with different characteristics, we determine the amount payable for each affected type of roof surfacing separately. Except that we may use a reasonable allocation when the repair or replacement cost cannot be separated from the available evidence. The Deductible is then applied to the covered loss as required by this provision.

**W.15** We may settle the covered loss by payment or by arranging for repair or replacement of the damaged property. Provided that any repair or replacement arranged by us will be performed using materials and methods reasonably suitable for the property. The amount of any payment remains subject to the Deductible and other applicable conditions.

**W.16** You must not begin permanent repair or replacement of the damaged roof surfacing without giving us a reasonable opportunity to inspect the damage. Except that you may make temporary repairs when reasonably necessary to protect the property. We do not pay for permanent work that prevents us from reasonably determining the amount of covered damage.

**W.17** We may pay the undisputed amount of a covered loss while the remaining amount is being determined. Subject to your compliance with the applicable duties after loss, payment of an undisputed amount does not prevent us from evaluating the remaining amount. Payment does not increase the amount otherwise payable for the covered loss.

**W.18** If the amount of covered damage is less than the Deductible, we do not make payment for that loss. Except that this provision does not prevent payment for another covered loss to which a separate Deductible applies. You must bear the Deductible even when the amount payable for the loss is reduced by another applicable provision.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss to "roof surfacing" unless the loss is caused by a peril we cover. We do not cover any part of a loss that is excluded by this policy.

**W.2** We do not cover loss that results from wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, latent defect, or faulty materials.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether the condition is performed by you or by another person.

**W.4** The minimum payable percentage of roof surfacing replacement cost is twenty-five percent. Flood and surface water remains excluded.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, swimming pool, or other structure.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, or sumps. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a sump or related equipment.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. "Pollutants" include solid, liquid, gaseous, or thermal irritants or contaminants.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by neglect by an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must protect property from further damage.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. Intentional loss means a loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by an act or decision of a governmental body. This exclusion applies whether the governmental body acts lawfully or unlawfully.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law requiring the construction, demolition, remodeling, renovation, or repair of property. We do not cover increased costs caused by enforcement of an ordinance or law.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by power failure or interruption occurring away from the "residence premises." We do not cover resulting loss unless a covered peril ensues.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or action taken to hinder or defend against any of these.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a person, organization, governmental body, or other entity to provide services. Services include water, electricity, gas, fuel, sewer, drainage, or communication services.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or other decay. We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavement, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover damage caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, or gnawing.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies when the seepage or leakage occurs from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by condensation, humidity, or changes in temperature. We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight unless a covered peril directly causes the loss.

**W.21** We do not cover loss to roof surfacing caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless a covered peril first damages the roof or exterior wall. We do not cover loss caused by water entering through an opening that was not created by a covered peril.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by wind-driven rain unless the rain enters through an opening created by direct action of wind or hail. We do not cover deterioration or preexisting damage to roof surfacing.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by defective installation of roof surfacing. We do not cover loss caused by improper fastening, sealing, flashing, underlayment, drainage, or ventilation.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by inadequate maintenance of roof surfacing. An insured must maintain roof surfacing in a condition reasonably suited to resist ordinary weather conditions.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by failure to repair known damage. We do not cover additional damage caused by delay in making reasonable repairs after discovery of a condition.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by collapse unless the collapse results directly from a peril we cover. We do not cover loss consisting only of cracking, bulging, sagging, bending, settling, or expansion.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by faulty repair of roof surfacing. We do not cover the cost to correct defective work, even if resulting damage is otherwise covered.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by defective materials incorporated into roof surfacing. We do not cover the cost to replace materials because of a manufacturer’s defect, recall, or warranty obligation.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by corrosion, electrolysis, or chemical action. We do not cover loss caused by exposure to vapors, fumes, acids, or other contaminants.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by animals owned or kept by you. We do not cover loss caused by animals used for business, agricultural, breeding, racing, or commercial purposes.

**W.31** We do not cover loss arising from business conducted on or away from the residence premises. This exclusion applies to property used principally for business purposes.

**W.32** We do not cover loss arising from professional services. Professional services include design, engineering, architecture, inspection, supervision, or construction management.

**W.33** We do not cover loss arising from criminal, fraudulent, dishonest, or illegal acts by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the act is committed alone or in concert with another person.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if the residence premises has been vacant. We do not cover ensuing damage caused by vandalism or malicious mischief during vacancy.

**W.31** Payment for a covered loss may be made to an insured and any person having an insurable interest in the property. We may include a mortgagee, lienholder, or other interested party in payment where appropriate.

**W.32** An insured must establish the existence and amount of a covered loss. An insured must establish that the damaged property was owned by, or legally the responsibility of, an insured.

**W.33** We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath as permitted by the policy. An insured must provide requested information that is material to the claim.

**W.34** We may require an insured to provide a signed statement of loss. The statement must identify the damaged property and describe the claimed loss.

**W.35** We may adjust a claim based upon available information when an insured cannot provide complete records. We may consider credible evidence of ownership, value, and condition.

**W.36** We will not pay for property that is missing when there is insufficient evidence that a covered loss caused its disappearance. We may require evidence supporting the claimed cause of loss.

**W.37** We will not pay for loss caused by an insured's intentional concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact. This condition applies to information presented in connection with a claim.

**W.38** The "association assessment limit" applies in accordance with the policy terms applicable to that coverage. This provision does not change that limit.

**W.39** The "fire department call fee" applies only as provided by the policy. This provision does not expand coverage for that fee.

**W.40** The "precious stones theft limit" applies to covered theft loss involving precious stones. This provision does not alter that limit.

**W.41** The "special limit on business property on the residence premises" applies to covered business property at the residence premises. This provision does not increase that limit.

**W.42** We will not pay for costs incurred to comply with a voluntary upgrade, improvement, or redesign. We will not pay for costs that exceed those necessary to repair covered damage.

**W.43** We may settle a covered loss with an insured, a contractor, or another person authorized by an insured. Payment to an authorized person discharges our obligation to the extent of that payment.

**W.44** We may require reasonable proof that a person claiming payment is authorized to receive it. We may withhold payment until authority is established.

**W.45** An insured must promptly notify us of a loss. Delay in reporting a loss must not prejudice our ability to investigate, inspect, or determine coverage.

**W.46** An insured must show the damaged property if we request an inspection. An insured must permit us to inspect the property before permanent repairs conceal the damage when reasonably possible.

**W.47** We will not pay for additional loss caused by an insured's failure to protect property from further damage. This limitation applies only to the additional damage resulting from that failure.

**W.48** We may use estimating methods, pricing information, and expert opinions to determine the amount of loss. We may revise an estimate when additional relevant information becomes available.

**W.49** We will consider local labor and material costs when determining the amount of covered repair or replacement. We may use reasonable market pricing for work necessary to repair covered damage.

**W.50** We will not pay for contractor overhead or profit unless reasonably necessary to repair covered damage. We may determine whether such costs are reasonably necessary based on the scope of repairs.

**W.51** We will not pay for fees charged solely to prepare, present, or negotiate a claim unless otherwise covered. We will not pay for legal, consulting, or public adjusting fees under this provision.

**W.52** We may require an insured to assign to us any recovery rights against a person responsible for a covered loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.53** If an insured recovers payment from another source for the same loss, we may reduce our payment by the amount recovered. We will not seek reimbursement beyond the amount we paid for the covered loss.

**W.54** We may issue payment based upon the information available at the time of settlement. A later adjustment may be made if additional covered damage is discovered and properly supported.

**W.55** We will not pay for loss to property that has not been damaged by a covered loss. We will not pay for preventive replacement of property that remains undamaged.

**W.56** We will not pay for any portion of loss attributable to excluded causes of loss. If covered and excluded causes contribute to loss, we will determine payment under the applicable policy terms.

**W.57** We may obtain expert evaluations concerning the cause, scope, repairability, or value of damaged property. We may rely upon those evaluations in settling the claim.

**W.58** An insured must preserve records that reasonably support the claim. An insured must provide copies of those records when we reasonably request them.

**W.59** We will settle a covered loss in accordance with this provision and the applicable policy terms. No payment will exceed the amount available for the covered property.

**W.60** This provision applies only to loss occurring during the policy period. Coverage remains subject to all applicable policy conditions, exclusions, and limitations.

## W.8 — Definitions

“Roof Surfacing” means the exterior material forming the weather-exposed surface of a roof. Roof Surfacing includes material applied over a roof deck or other supporting roof structure.

Roof Surfacing includes shingles, shakes, tiles, slate, metal panels, membrane material, and similar weather-exposed roofing material. Roof Surfacing does not include material located beneath the weather-exposed roofing material unless that material itself forms the exterior roof surface.

“Actual Cash Value” means the value of damaged Roof Surfacing at the time of loss. We determine Actual Cash Value by reducing Replacement Cost for Depreciation.

“Replacement Cost” means the reasonable cost to repair or replace damaged Roof Surfacing with material of like kind and quality. Replacement Cost includes reasonable labor necessary to perform the repair or replacement.

“Depreciation” means a reduction in value resulting from age, wear, deterioration, condition, obsolescence, or other factors affecting the remaining usefulness of Roof Surfacing. Depreciation reflects the condition of the damaged Roof Surfacing immediately before the loss.

“Physical Condition” means the observable state of Roof Surfacing before the loss. Physical Condition includes evidence of wear, deterioration, damage, repair, alteration, or maintenance.

“Useful Life” means the period during which Roof Surfacing is reasonably expected to perform its intended function. Useful Life may be affected by material type, installation, maintenance, weather exposure, and Physical Condition.

“Weather Exposure” means the effect of sunlight, precipitation, wind, temperature variation, moisture, ice, and similar environmental conditions on Roof Surfacing. Weather Exposure may affect the rate at which Roof Surfacing deteriorates.

“Roof Covering” means material installed to protect a roof from weather. Roof Covering includes Roof Surfacing and may include material beneath Roof Surfacing.

“Roof System” means the assembly of components that support, protect, or cover a roof. Roof System may include a roof deck, underlayment, flashing, ventilation components, fasteners, and Roof Surfacing.

“Roof Deck” means the structural surface to which roofing material is attached. A Roof Deck may be constructed of wood, metal, concrete, or similar structural material.

“Underlayment” means material installed beneath Roof Surfacing to provide moisture protection or a separation layer. Underlayment is not Roof Surfacing unless it forms the weather-exposed exterior roof surface.

“Flashing” means material installed at roof joints, openings, edges, or transitions to direct water away from vulnerable areas. Flashing may be composed of metal, membrane material, or similar material.

“Fastener” means a nail, screw, clip, adhesive, or similar item used to secure roofing material. Fasteners are part of the Roof System when they support or secure Roof Surfacing.

“Roof Accessory” means a component attached to or installed through a roof. Roof Accessories include vents, skylights, antennas, solar equipment, and similar components.

“Attached” means physically connected to a structure or component by fastening, adhesion, integration, or similar means. Material remains Attached even though it may be removable for repair or replacement.

“Direct Physical Loss” means accidental physical damage to tangible property. Direct Physical Loss does not include an economic loss that occurs without physical damage to property.

“Physical Damage” means a distinct and observable alteration in the condition of property. Physical Damage may include breakage, puncture, tearing, displacement, crushing, or loss of material.

“Covered Cause of Loss” means a cause of loss for which coverage applies. A Covered Cause of Loss must directly cause the Physical Damage for which payment is sought.

“Date of Loss” means the date on which Direct Physical Loss occurs. When damage results from continuing conditions, the Date of Loss is the date on which the Direct Physical Loss first becomes apparent.

“Repair” means work performed to restore damaged Roof Surfacing to a condition that is reasonably comparable to its condition immediately before the loss. Repair may include removal, preparation, installation, sealing, and related work.

“Replacement” means removal of damaged Roof Surfacing and installation of new roofing material in its place. Replacement may be necessary when Repair cannot reasonably restore the damaged Roof Surfacing.

“Like Kind and Quality” means material that is reasonably comparable in type, function, performance, and utility to the damaged material. Like Kind and Quality does not require identical material when identical material is unavailable.

“Reasonably Comparable” means sufficiently similar to perform the intended roofing function and provide comparable protection from weather. Reasonably Comparable material may differ in appearance, manufacturer, composition, or method of installation.

“Matching” means similarity in color, texture, pattern, size, shape, or appearance. Matching is considered only to the extent it affects the determination of Like Kind and Quality.

“Uniform Appearance” means a consistent visual appearance across Roof Surfacing. Uniform Appearance does not by itself establish Physical Damage or determine Actual Cash Value.

“Discontinued Material” means roofing material no longer manufactured or ordinarily available through customary sources. Discontinued Material may be replaced with Reasonably Comparable material.

“Availability” means the ability to obtain material through customary suppliers within a reasonable market area. Availability may be affected by manufacturing practices, supply conditions, or material demand.

“Market Area” means the geographic area in which labor and roofing material are customarily obtained for repair or replacement. We may consider prevailing conditions within the Market Area when determining Replacement Cost.

“Labor” means work performed in connection with the Repair or Replacement of Roof Surfacing. Labor includes work necessary to remove, prepare, install, secure, seal, and finish roofing material.

“Material Cost” means the reasonable cost of roofing material used in a Repair or Replacement. Material Cost does not include the value of undamaged material retained by you.

“Removal” means taking damaged Roof Surfacing from the Roof System. Removal includes reasonable work necessary to access damaged Roof Surfacing.

“Disposal” means the handling and removal of debris resulting from covered Physical Damage. Disposal includes reasonable transportation and deposit of debris at an authorized location.

“Building Ordinance” means a law, regulation, or code that regulates construction, Repair, Replacement, demolition, or use of property. Building Ordinance includes requirements adopted or enforced by a governmental authority.

“Betterment” means an improvement that increases the value, usefulness, efficiency, or expected service life of property beyond its condition immediately before the loss. Betterment may result from the use of material that is not Like Kind and Quality.

“Maintenance” means care, upkeep, servicing, cleaning, or repair intended to preserve property in serviceable condition. Maintenance includes work performed to address ordinary wear or deterioration.

“Wear and Tear” means gradual damage resulting from ordinary use, age, friction, or exposure. Wear and Tear may occur without a sudden or accidental event.

“Deterioration” means gradual impairment of property resulting from age, use, weather exposure, moisture, or similar conditions. Deterioration includes loss of strength, flexibility, water resistance, or protective quality.

“Defect” means a fault, weakness, imperfection, or deficiency in material, design, manufacture, installation, or workmanship. A Defect may exist whether or not it is visible.

“Latent Defect” means a Defect that is not discoverable by reasonable observation. A Latent Defect may become apparent after installation or use.

“Inherent Vice” means a quality within property that causes it to damage or destroy itself. Inherent Vice includes an internal condition that results in gradual impairment without an external cause.

“Mechanical Breakdown” means failure of moving or operating mechanical equipment. Mechanical Breakdown does not include damage to Roof Surfacing caused by a Covered Cause of Loss.

“Settling” means the downward movement of soil, foundations, structural supports, or other supporting material. Settling may affect the Roof System through shifting, cracking, separation, or distortion.

“Expansion” means an increase in size resulting from heat, moisture, pressure, or similar conditions. “Contraction” means a decrease in size resulting from cooling, drying, or similar conditions.

“Faulty Workmanship” means deficient work in the construction, installation, repair, renovation, or maintenance of property. Faulty Workmanship includes work that does not conform to customary roofing practices.

“Faulty Material” means material that is defective, unsuitable, improperly manufactured, or otherwise inadequate for its intended use. Faulty Material includes material that fails under ordinary conditions of use.

“Neglect” means failure to use reasonable means to protect property from further damage after a loss. Neglect includes unreasonable delay in making temporary repairs necessary to protect damaged property.

“Temporary Repair” means reasonable work performed to protect damaged property from further damage until permanent Repair or Replacement can be completed. Temporary Repair includes covering exposed areas and securing displaced roofing material.

“Estimate” means a written calculation of anticipated Repair or Replacement costs. An Estimate may include Material Cost, Labor, Removal, Disposal, and related work.

“Inspection” means an examination of property to determine its Physical Condition, the nature and extent of Physical Damage, and the work reasonably necessary to Repair or Replace damaged Roof Surfacing. We may rely on an Inspection when determining Actual Cash Value.
