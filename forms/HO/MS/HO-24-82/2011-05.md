# HO 24 82 Personal Injury Coverage (Edition 2011-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement modifies insurance provided under your policy. The provisions of this endorsement apply only while this endorsement is attached to and made part of your policy.

We provide the coverage described in this endorsement subject to all applicable policy provisions. The terms and conditions of your policy remain unchanged except as modified by this endorsement.

The insurance provided by this endorsement applies to an "insured." The term "insured" has the meaning assigned to it in your policy.

This endorsement provides coverage for "personal injury." "Personal injury" means injury arising out of an offense described in this endorsement.

We will pay damages for "personal injury" for which an insured is legally responsible. The damages must arise from an offense committed during the policy period.

The offense must arise out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of an insured location, or from your personal activities. The offense must not be otherwise excluded by your policy or this endorsement.

We will provide a defense for an insured against a suit seeking damages covered by this endorsement. We may investigate and settle any claim or suit as we consider appropriate.

Our duty to defend ends when we have paid the applicable limit of liability for damages arising from a covered occurrence. We have no duty to provide a defense for a claim or suit not covered by this endorsement.

Coverage applies only to damages because of personal injury. Coverage does not apply to obligations assumed by an insured unless the insured would have been liable in the absence of that assumption.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. An insured must promptly notify us of a claim or suit that may involve this endorsement.

An insured must provide us with records, information, and assistance we reasonably request. An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except at the insured's own cost.

We do not cover personal injury arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with knowledge that the act would violate the rights of another person. We do not cover personal injury arising out of publication by an insured with knowledge of its falsity.

We do not cover personal injury arising out of oral or written publication of material whose first publication took place before the beginning of the policy period. We do not cover personal injury arising out of a criminal act committed by or at the direction of an insured.

We do not cover personal injury arising out of a business conducted from an insured location or engaged in by an insured. This exclusion does not apply when coverage is otherwise provided by your policy for a permitted incidental activity.

We do not cover personal injury arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, or revolution. We do not cover personal injury arising out of an act of terrorism.

All provisions of this endorsement are subject to the applicable limits of liability. The limits of liability are not increased by the attachment of this endorsement.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide "Personal Injury Coverage" for an "insured" who becomes legally liable for "damages" because of personal injury to which this coverage applies. The personal injury must arise from an offense committed during the policy period.

**W.2** "Personal injury" means injury arising out of false arrest, detention, or imprisonment; malicious prosecution; or wrongful eviction from, wrongful entry into, or invasion of the right of private occupancy of a room, dwelling, or premises that a person occupies. Personal injury also includes injury arising out of oral or written publication that slanders, libels, or defames a person or organization, or violates a person's right of privacy.

**W.3** We will pay damages for personal injury for which an insured is legally responsible. We will also provide a defense for an insured against a "claim" or "suit" seeking those damages.

**W.4** We may investigate any claim and settle any claim or suit that we consider appropriate. Our duty to defend ends when our obligation to pay damages ends.

**W.5** Our duty to defend applies even if the allegations of a claim or suit are groundless, false, or fraudulent. We have no duty to defend a claim or suit for damages that are not covered by this coverage.

**W.6** We will pay only those damages that arise from personal injury caused by an offense of an insured. The offense must arise from the insured's ownership, maintenance, or use of a covered residence or from personal activities of an insured.

**W.7** Coverage applies to personal injury arising from an offense committed by an insured. Coverage does not depend on whether the injured person makes a demand before bringing a suit.

**W.8** We will pay damages that an insured is legally obligated to pay because of personal injury. We will not pay damages that an insured assumes solely by contract unless the insured would have been legally liable in the absence of that contract.

**W.9** We do not cover personal injury that is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to personal injury resulting from the use of reasonable force by an insured to protect persons or property.

**W.10** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an offense committed by an insured with knowledge that the act would violate the rights of another person or organization. We do not cover personal injury arising from conduct that an insured knew was false.

**W.11** We do not cover personal injury arising out of oral or written publication of material by or at the direction of an insured with knowledge that the material was false. This exclusion applies whether the publication is made directly or through another person or organization.

**W.12** We do not cover personal injury arising out of oral or written publication of material whose first publication was made before the beginning of the policy period. This exclusion applies to later publication of the same or substantially similar material.

**W.13** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a criminal act committed by or at the direction of an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of whether an insured is charged with or convicted of a criminal offense.

**W.14** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the rendering of or failure to render professional services. Professional services include services requiring specialized knowledge, training, licensing, or certification.

**W.15** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's business pursuits. This exclusion does not apply to activities that are ordinarily incidental to nonbusiness pursuits.

**W.16** We do not cover personal injury arising out of business activities conducted from an insured location. This exclusion applies whether the business activity is conducted by an insured or by another person acting on behalf of an insured.

**W.17** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft. This exclusion applies to an aircraft owned, operated, rented, borrowed, or used by an insured.

**W.18** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft. This exclusion applies while the watercraft is being used in a manner that creates legal liability for an insured.

**W.19** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle. This exclusion applies to personal injury arising from an insured's operation or entrustment of a motor vehicle.

**W.20** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a recreational motor vehicle. This exclusion applies whether the recreational motor vehicle is operated on public or private property.

**W.21** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a trailer. This exclusion applies when the trailer is connected to, being towed by, or detached from a motor vehicle.

**W.22** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the disease is transmitted directly or indirectly.

**W.23** We do not cover personal injury arising out of sexual molestation, corporal punishment, or physical or mental abuse. This exclusion applies to an insured who commits, directs, permits, or fails to prevent such conduct.

**W.24** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of a controlled substance. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of a prescription drug by a person following lawful medical direction.

**W.25** We do not cover personal injury arising out of war, declared or undeclared, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. This exclusion applies regardless of the source or sequence of events contributing to the injury.

**W.26** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an act of terrorism. This exclusion applies whether the act is committed by an individual, group, organization, or governmental authority.

**W.27** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. "Pollutants" means irritants, contaminants, smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

**W.28** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a pollutant that is brought to or kept at an insured location in connection with an insured's business. This exclusion applies whether the pollutant is handled by an insured or by another person.

**W.29** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a pollutant that is used by an insured in connection with a business activity. This exclusion applies to the transportation, storage, disposal, or treatment of a pollutant.

**W.30** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the failure to warn of or prevent the release of pollutants. This exclusion applies even if the alleged injury is based on negligence or another theory of liability.

**W.31** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, operation, use, or entrustment of an insured location that is not used as a residence by an insured. This exclusion applies to personal injury resulting from the condition of that location.

**W.32** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a room, dwelling, or premises that an insured rents to another person. This exclusion does not apply to personal injury arising from a part of an insured location that is rented on an occasional basis for residential use.

**W.33** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the wrongful eviction from, wrongful entry into, or invasion of the right of private occupancy of a room, dwelling, or premises occupied by an insured. This exclusion applies when the injured person is a tenant, occupant, or other person claiming occupancy rights.

**W.34** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's role as an officer, director, trustee, manager, or employee of an organization. This exclusion applies to acts performed in that capacity.

**W.35** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's duties for a corporation, partnership, joint venture, limited liability company, association, or similar organization. This exclusion applies whether the organization is operated for profit or otherwise.

**W.36** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's participation in a joint venture or partnership that is not identified in this policy. This exclusion applies to acts of the joint venture or partnership and to acts performed on its behalf.

**W.37** We do not cover personal injury sustained by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the injury is alleged by the insured directly or through another person acting on the insured's behalf.

**W.38** We do not cover personal injury sustained by a person who is eligible to receive benefits voluntarily provided by an insured under workers compensation, disability benefits, occupational disease law, or similar law. This exclusion applies to obligations imposed by law or assumed by an insured.

**W.39** We do not cover personal injury arising out of employment-related practices, policies, acts, or omissions. This exclusion applies to hiring, employment, termination, coercion, demotion, evaluation, reassignment, discipline, defamation, harassment, humiliation, discrimination, or other employment-related conduct.

**W.40** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a refusal to employ a person. This exclusion applies whether the alleged injury occurs before, during, or after an employment relationship.

**W.41** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a violation of a person's right of privacy when the alleged violation results from the distribution, collection, recording, or disclosure of information in connection with an insured's business. This exclusion applies to information in any form.

**W.42** We do not cover personal injury arising out of material published by an insured in the course of advertising, broadcasting, publishing, or telecasting. This exclusion applies whether the activity is conducted for compensation or without compensation.

**W.43** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's failure to maintain the confidentiality of information. This exclusion applies to information obtained through personal, business, professional, or electronic activities.

**W.44** We do not cover personal injury arising out of access to, disclosure of, or failure to protect electronic data. "Electronic data" means information, facts, concepts, computer programs, software, or other data stored, transmitted, or processed by electronic means.

**W.45** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's use of an electronic communication system for an unlawful purpose. This exclusion applies to communications made through a computer, telephone, mobile device, network, or similar device.

**W.46** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the knowing violation of a statute, ordinance, regulation, or rule that restricts the collection, communication, recording, printing, dissemination, disposal, or use of material or information. This exclusion applies whether the material or information concerns a person or organization.

**W.47** We do not cover fines, penalties, punitive damages, exemplary damages, or damages imposed for conduct that is uninsurable under applicable law. We will pay damages only to the extent they are insurable under applicable law.

**W.48** We do not cover personal injury for which an insured is obligated to pay damages because of the assumption of liability in a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**W.49** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of property that an insured sells, gives away, abandons, or otherwise transfers to another person or organization. This exclusion applies after the insured no longer owns or controls the property.

**W.50** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a product manufactured, sold, handled, distributed, or disposed of by an insured. This exclusion applies to representations, warranties, instructions, warnings, or failures to warn relating to that product.

**W.51** We do not cover personal injury arising out of work performed by an insured or on an insured's behalf. This exclusion applies to completed work and to materials, parts, or equipment furnished in connection with the work.

**W.52** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a defect, deficiency, inadequacy, or dangerous condition in work performed by or for an insured. This exclusion applies after the work has been completed or abandoned.

**W.53** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the operation of a day care service by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the service is conducted for compensation or without compensation.

**W.54** We do not cover personal injury arising out of the care, custody, or control of another person's property by an insured. This exclusion applies while the property is entrusted to, possessed by, or controlled by an insured.

**W.55** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a dispute concerning title to, possession of, or boundaries of real property. This exclusion applies to alleged trespass, nuisance, encroachment, or interference with property rights.

**W.56** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a condemnation, inverse condemnation, or similar taking of property by a governmental authority. This exclusion applies whether the taking is alleged to be temporary or permanent.

**W.57** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's violation of a law concerning land use, zoning, subdivision, building, housing, or occupancy. This exclusion applies whether the violation is alleged to be intentional or negligent.

**W.58** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's failure to obtain a permit, license, approval, inspection, or certificate. This exclusion applies to an act or omission by an insured or by a person acting on behalf of an insured.

**W.59** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's ownership or operation of a lodging, boarding, or rental activity. This exclusion applies to a claim made by a guest, tenant, occupant, invitee, or other person.

**W.60** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's participation in an athletic, recreational, or amusement activity when the personal injury results from conduct occurring in connection with that activity. This exclusion applies whether the activity is organized or informal.

**W.61** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's ownership, care, custody, or control of an animal used in connection with a business activity. This exclusion applies whether the animal is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.62** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's failure to supervise a person for whom the insured is legally responsible. This exclusion applies when the alleged injury arises from an act or omission of that person.

**W.63** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's failure to provide security or protection services. This exclusion applies whether the alleged duty arises by contract, statute, ordinance, common law, or another legal theory.

**W.64** We do not cover personal injury arising out of a labor dispute, strike, lockout, picketing, boycott, or similar activity. This exclusion applies whether the activity is peaceful or unlawful.

**W.65** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's collection of a debt or enforcement of a security interest. This exclusion applies to alleged harassment, intimidation, wrongful repossession, or invasion of privacy.

**W.66** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's performance of law enforcement, security, investigative, or detention services. This exclusion applies whether those services are performed for compensation or without compensation.

**W.67** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's exercise of authority as a public official, governmental employee, or member of a governmental body. This exclusion applies to acts performed under color of law.

**W.68** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's operation of a school, educational program, camp, or similar activity. This exclusion applies to claims by students, participants, parents, guardians, employees, or volunteers.

**W.69** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an insured's operation of a health care, counseling, social service, or therapeutic activity. This exclusion applies whether the services are rendered professionally or informally.

**W.70** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. An insured must provide information, records, and assistance that we reasonably request.

**W.71** An insured must promptly notify us of an offense that may result in a claim under this coverage. The notice must include information available to the insured concerning the circumstances of the offense and the persons involved.

**W.72** An insured must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, legal paper, or other document received in connection with a claim or suit. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold material information concerning the claim or suit.

**W.73** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except at the insured's own cost. This duty does not prevent an insured from obtaining immediate assistance to protect a person from further injury.

**W.74** An insured must assist us in securing and giving evidence, obtaining the attendance of witnesses, and conducting a defense. An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require.

**W.75** We may exercise the rights of recovery that an insured has against a person or organization responsible for a covered personal injury. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.76** If we make a payment under this coverage, an insured's rights of recovery against another person or organization transfer to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing those rights.

**W.77** Coverage applies separately to each insured against whom a claim is made or a suit is brought. This provision does not increase our obligation to pay damages or defend a claim or suit.

**W.78** The terms, conditions, exclusions, and duties of this coverage apply to every claim and suit seeking damages because of personal injury. We will apply this coverage only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The "Limit of Liability" is the maximum amount we will pay for covered damages under this Personal Injury Coverage. This limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claimants, claims, or suits.

**W.2** A "claim" is a demand for damages alleging covered personal injury. "Damages" are sums an insured is legally obligated to pay because of covered personal injury.

**W.3** An "occurrence" is an event, act, omission, or related series of events, acts, or omissions that results in covered personal injury. Related events, acts, or omissions will be treated as arising from the same occurrence when they have a common factual basis.

**W.4** A "suit" is a civil proceeding in which damages because of covered personal injury are alleged. A suit includes arbitration or another dispute-resolution proceeding to which we agree.

**W.5** The applicable Limit of Liability is the most we will pay for all damages resulting from any occurrence. This applies even when the occurrence results in injury to more than one person.

**W.6** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to all covered damages arising from the same occurrence. It does not apply separately to each injured person, claimant, insured, claim, or suit.

**W.7** All damages arising from the same occurrence are subject to the same applicable Limit of Liability. This is true whether the damages are claimed at the same time or at different times.

**W.8** All damages arising from related acts or omissions are subject to the same applicable Limit of Liability. This is true even if the acts or omissions involve different insureds.

**W.9** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages alleged in a suit, settlement, judgment, or other resolution of a claim. It applies whether liability is established by agreement, adjudication, or otherwise.

**W.10** We will not pay more than the applicable Limit of Liability because more than one insured is legally responsible for the same damages. The limit is not increased by the separate liability of any insured.

**W.11** We will not pay more than the applicable Limit of Liability because a claimant asserts more than one legal theory. Different allegations arising from the same occurrence do not create separate limits.

**W.12** We will not pay more than the applicable Limit of Liability because a claimant seeks damages in more than one suit. Separate suits arising from the same occurrence do not create separate limits.

**W.13** We will not pay more than the applicable Limit of Liability because damages are sought in more than one jurisdiction. A change in venue, forum, or governing law does not increase the limit.

**W.14** We will not pay more than the applicable Limit of Liability because an insured has additional insurance. Other insurance does not increase the amount available under this Personal Injury Coverage.

**W.15** We will not pay more than the applicable Limit of Liability because a claim includes alleged conduct occurring over time. Continuing, repeated, or progressively harmful conduct will be treated in accordance with its related occurrence.

**W.16** We will not pay more than the applicable Limit of Liability for damages that arise from a common source of alleged personal injury. The fact that damages develop, worsen, or are discovered at different times does not increase the limit.

**W.17** We may investigate, settle, or defend any claim or suit as we consider appropriate. Our exercise of that right does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.18** We may settle a claim or suit for an amount within the applicable Limit of Liability. An insured must not interfere with a settlement we make within that limit.

**W.19** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. An insured must not make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent.

**W.20** An insured must promptly provide us with information that may affect the application of the Limit of Liability. This includes information concerning related claims, suits, injuries, acts, omissions, or occurrences.

**W.21** An insured must notify us when the insured becomes aware of another claim or suit arising from the same occurrence. This duty applies even if the claim or suit does not seek payment from us.

**W.22** An insured must preserve records and other information relevant to the amount of damages claimed. An insured must allow us a reasonable opportunity to inspect and copy that information.

**W.23** We may allocate payments among claimants, claims, suits, damages, or insureds. Our allocation will not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.24** We may make payment to resolve any covered claim or suit. Payment to resolve a covered claim or suit does not require payment to any other claimant.

**W.25** We may make payment directly to a claimant, an insured, or another person legally entitled to receive payment. The recipient of payment does not affect the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.26** We may pay damages by settlement, judgment, compromise, or other lawful means. The manner in which we pay damages does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.27** Amounts we pay for covered damages reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. Amounts paid on behalf of any insured reduce that limit.

**W.28** Amounts paid to settle a covered claim reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. This is true even if the settlement does not resolve all claims arising from the occurrence.

**W.29** Amounts paid to satisfy a judgment reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. This is true even if the judgment is subject to appeal or further proceedings.

**W.30** Amounts paid under an agreement to which we consent reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. An insured must obtain our consent before entering such an agreement.

**W.31** We will not make payment for damages in excess of the applicable Limit of Liability. An insured remains responsible for any amount exceeding that limit.

**W.32** We will not be obligated to contribute toward damages after the applicable Limit of Liability has been exhausted by payment. This applies whether other insurance is available or unavailable.

**W.33** Our duty to defend ends when the applicable Limit of Liability has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements. We have no duty to continue a defense after that exhaustion.

**W.34** We may withdraw from the defense of a claim or suit after our duty to defend ends. An insured must take reasonable steps to avoid prejudice to the defense following our withdrawal.

**W.35** Expenses we incur in investigating or defending a covered claim or suit do not reduce the applicable Limit of Liability. Such expenses do not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.36** We may continue to incur defense expenses after a claim is made. Our payment of defense expenses does not constitute an admission of coverage or liability.

**W.37** Interest, costs, and expenses that we are obligated to pay in addition to damages do not increase the applicable Limit of Liability. Our obligation for such amounts is subject to the terms of this coverage.

**W.38** We may seek reimbursement from an insured for amounts paid because of an insured's unauthorized obligation or payment. Any reimbursement we receive does not restore the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.39** We may seek recovery from any person or organization responsible for damages we have paid. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.40** Amounts recovered by us from a responsible person or organization do not increase the applicable Limit of Liability. We may apply recovered amounts as permitted by law.

**W.41** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to the number of locations involved in an occurrence. The involvement of more than one location does not create separate limits.

**W.42** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to the number of vehicles, watercraft, animals, products, or other property involved in an occurrence. The involvement of additional property does not create separate limits.

**W.43** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to the number of persons alleged to have caused personal injury. The participation of additional persons does not create separate limits.

**W.44** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to the number of persons seeking damages. The assignment, transfer, or succession of a claim does not create separate limits.

**W.45** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to the number of legal proceedings arising from an occurrence. Consolidation or separation of proceedings does not affect that limit.

**W.46** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to punitive, exemplary, multiplied, or similar damages to the extent such damages are covered. The characterization of damages does not increase the limit.

**W.47** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages awarded for bodily injury, property damage, emotional distress, economic loss, or other alleged harm when covered personal injury is alleged. The categories of damages claimed do not create separate limits.

**W.48** We may rely on information reasonably available to us when determining the amount remaining under the applicable Limit of Liability. An insured must promptly correct material information provided to us.

**W.49** A payment we make does not waive our right to assert that other damages are not covered. A payment we make does not waive our right to apply the Limit of Liability.

**W.50** Our payment of damages does not create a duty to pay damages arising from a different occurrence. Our payment of damages does not create a separate Limit of Liability.

**W.51** An insured must not represent that we will pay an amount greater than the applicable Limit of Liability. An insured must not represent that we will continue a defense after our duty to defend ends.

**W.52** The Limit of Liability applies to our total obligation for covered damages arising from an occurrence. No act, omission, payment, settlement, defense, or agreement by us changes that limit.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the portion of covered damages for which you are responsible. We will apply the deductible as provided in this provision.

**W.2** "Covered damages" means damages for which coverage applies. Covered damages do not include damages for which coverage does not apply.

**W.3** We will reduce covered damages by the applicable deductible. We will pay only the covered damages that remain after that reduction.

**W.4** The deductible applies to covered damages arising out of "personal injury." The deductible does not create coverage for personal injury that is not covered.

**W.5** We apply the deductible separately to covered damages arising out of each occurrence. Covered damages arising out of the same occurrence are subject to the same applicable deductible.

**W.6** We will determine whether personal injury arises out of the same occurrence based on the facts giving rise to the claimed damages. The number of persons asserting claims does not by itself determine the occurrence.

**W.7** Separate occurrences remain separate occurrences even if they involve related acts, omissions, persons, property, or conditions. We will not combine separate occurrences to reduce the deductible.

**W.8** The deductible applies whether a claim is made by an insured or by any other person or organization. The deductible also applies whether damages are sought directly or indirectly.

**W.9** The deductible applies to damages claimed by any person or organization because of personal injury. The deductible is not increased solely because damages are claimed under different legal theories.

**W.10** The deductible applies before we make payment for covered damages. Our payment of any covered damages does not waive the deductible.

**W.11** We may apply the deductible in calculating a settlement, judgment, award, or other payment. We may make that calculation before or after resolving the claim.

**W.12** The deductible does not reduce our duty to investigate a claim. The deductible does not limit our right to investigate, negotiate, settle, or defend a claim.

**W.13** Expenses we incur to investigate, negotiate, settle, or defend a claim are not part of the deductible unless they are included in covered damages. Expenses incurred by an insured are not included in the deductible unless we agree otherwise.

**W.14** We may pay covered damages to an insured, claimant, or other person or organization entitled to payment. Any such payment remains subject to the applicable deductible.

**W.15** If we pay covered damages without collecting the deductible, an insured must reimburse us for the deductible. An insured must make reimbursement promptly after we request it.

**W.16** Our request for reimbursement may be made to any insured responsible for the occurrence. Payment by any insured satisfies the reimbursement obligation to the extent of that payment.

**W.17** An insured must not direct a claimant, attorney, or other person or organization to collect the deductible from us. An insured must not represent that we will pay the deductible.

**W.18** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the covered damages to which the deductible applies. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning any payment, settlement, judgment, award, or recovery.

**W.19** An insured must notify us promptly if an insured receives money, property, services, or other consideration relating to covered damages. An insured must preserve records relating to that receipt.

**W.20** We may credit against covered damages any amount recovered for the same personal injury. We will not apply a credit that would duplicate a reduction already made for the deductible.

**W.21** Money returned by a claimant or other person or organization because of a settlement, judgment, award, or payment reduces the covered damages to which it relates. We may recalculate the deductible after that reduction.

**W.22** A settlement between an insured and a claimant does not bind us concerning application of the deductible unless we agree to that settlement. An insured must not agree to waive, reduce, or assume the deductible on our behalf.

**W.23** An agreement between an insured and another person or organization does not change the deductible. We are not bound by an allocation of damages made without our consent.

**W.24** We may allocate a payment between covered damages and damages that are not covered. We will make that allocation reasonably based on the available information.

**W.25** The deductible applies only to covered damages. We do not apply the deductible to damages that are not covered.

**W.26** The deductible does not increase any limit of liability. Payment of the deductible by an insured does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.27** The deductible does not reduce an insured's responsibility for damages that exceed the amount we are obligated to pay. An insured remains responsible for damages that are not paid by us.

**W.28** The deductible applies regardless of an insured's financial condition. Bankruptcy, insolvency, or inability to pay does not eliminate or reduce the deductible.

**W.29** The deductible applies regardless of whether an insured disputes liability. A dispute concerning liability does not delay our right to apply the deductible.

**W.30** The deductible applies regardless of whether damages are alleged, claimed, settled, awarded, or paid in a different form. The character of the remedy does not by itself alter the deductible.

**W.31** We may withhold the deductible from money otherwise payable to an insured when permitted by law. Any withholding does not limit our right to seek reimbursement from an insured.

**W.32** We may seek reimbursement of the deductible from an insured after making payment to a claimant. Our payment to a claimant does not relieve an insured of the reimbursement duty.

**W.15** An insured must assist us in obtaining statements from persons with relevant knowledge. The insured must not direct, influence, or attempt to influence another person's statement.

**W.16** An insured must notify us if the insured receives information indicating that a claim may involve additional persons, publications, communications, or events. The insured must provide the information in the form in which it was received.

**W.17** An insured must notify us of any change in the insured's address, telephone number, or electronic mail address while a claim or suit remains pending. The insured must also notify us of any change in the location of requested records.

**W.18** An insured must promptly notify us if the insured becomes aware of a related claim or suit. A related matter includes a claim or suit arising from the same alleged conduct or communication.

**W.19** An insured must provide us with copies of correspondence relating to a claim or suit. This includes correspondence with an attorney, claimant, court, governmental body, or other person acting on behalf of a claimant.

**W.20** An insured must notify us before responding to a subpoena, investigative demand, or similar request relating to a claim or suit. The insured must cooperate with us in seeking appropriate protection for material subject to the request.

**W.21** An insured must not disclose our communications concerning the handling, defense, or settlement of a claim or suit unless disclosure is required by law. The insured must notify us before making a required disclosure when practicable.

**W.22** An insured must protect "confidential information" relating to a claimant or other person involved in a claim or suit. The insured must use reasonable care when transmitting confidential information to us or to a person acting for us.

**W.23** An insured must notify us of any request to correct, retract, remove, or cease a statement, communication, or publication. The insured must preserve the request and any response to it.

**W.24** An insured must not alter, remove, or republish material that is alleged to have caused personal injury without our consent. The insured may take reasonable action necessary to prevent further harm or comply with law.

**W.25** An insured must notify us if the insured issues a correction, retraction, apology, or other response concerning an alleged personal injury. The insured must provide us with a copy of the response.

**W.26** An insured must give us access to relevant accounts, profiles, pages, postings, and messages within the insured's control. The insured must not delete or modify relevant material after receiving our request to preserve it.

**W.27** An insured must notify us if access to relevant electronic data is controlled by another person or entity. The insured must reasonably cooperate in obtaining available access or copies.

**W.28** An insured must identify each attorney representing the insured in connection with a claim or suit. The insured must authorize reasonable communication between us and that attorney concerning the defense of the matter.

**W.29** An insured must notify us before retaining counsel in connection with a claim or suit, unless immediate action is necessary to protect the insured's interests. The insured must provide us with information concerning any counsel retained.

**W.30** We may select counsel to defend an insured when we provide a defense. An insured must cooperate with counsel selected by us.

**W.31** An insured must not interfere with counsel's professional judgment in the defense of a claim or suit. The insured must provide counsel with truthful and complete information concerning the matter.

**W.32** An insured must notify us of any actual or potential conflict affecting the defense of a claim or suit. We may take action we consider appropriate to address the conflict.

**W.33** An insured must appear at hearings, conferences, depositions, examinations, and trial proceedings when reasonably requested. The insured must make reasonable efforts to avoid conduct that delays the defense of a claim or suit.

**W.34** An insured must notify us of any proposed "settlement" received from a claimant or another person acting for a claimant. The insured must not reject or accept a proposed settlement without our consent.

**W.35** We may investigate and settle a claim as we consider appropriate. An insured must not obstruct our investigation or settlement efforts.

**W.36** An insured must provide information concerning insurance that may apply to the claim or suit. This includes information concerning any "other insurance" available to an insured.

**W.37** An insured must notify us if another insurer, self-insurer, indemnitor, or other person may be responsible for a claim or suit. The insured must provide available information concerning that responsibility.

**W.38** An insured must preserve our rights against a person or entity that may be responsible for "damages". The insured must not waive those rights without our written consent.

**W.39** If we make payment for damages, an insured must cooperate with us in enforcing any right of recovery. An insured must execute documents reasonably necessary to preserve or transfer that right.

**W.40** An insured must notify us of any payment, offer, reimbursement, or indemnity received in connection with a claim or suit. The insured must provide records supporting the payment, offer, reimbursement, or indemnity.

**W.41** An insured must notify us if the insured receives a "judgment" or other determination in a claim or suit. The insured must promptly provide us with a copy of the judgment or determination.

**W.42** An insured must not satisfy a judgment or agree to an appeal without our consent. We may decide whether to pursue an "appeal" when we have the right to do so.

**W.43** An insured must cooperate with us in seeking relief from a judgment, order, or other determination when we request. The insured must execute documents reasonably necessary for that purpose.

**W.44** An insured must notify us of any bankruptcy, insolvency, receivership, or similar proceeding involving the insured or a person seeking damages. The insured must provide copies of relevant notices and filings.

**W.45** An insured must notify us if the insured becomes aware of threatened criminal, administrative, disciplinary, or regulatory action arising from alleged conduct related to a claim or suit. The insured must provide us with available information concerning the action.

**W.46** An insured must not make a false statement to us, a claimant, a court, or another person concerning a claim or suit. An insured must correct a material statement that the insured later learns is inaccurate.

**W.47** An insured must not conceal a material fact relating to a claim or suit. An insured must disclose material facts known to the insured when we reasonably request them.

**W.48** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further publication, communication, or conduct that may increase alleged personal injury. The insured must notify us of the steps taken when we request.

**W.49** An insured must not retaliate against a claimant, witness, or other person because of a claim or suit. The insured must notify us of any allegation of retaliation relating to the matter.

**W.50** An insured must maintain records sufficient to identify relevant communications, publications, and recipients. The insured must provide those records to us when reasonably requested.

**W.51** An insured must notify us if any relevant record is unavailable, lost, destroyed, or controlled by another person. The insured must describe the circumstances known to the insured concerning the unavailable record.

**W.52** An insured must use reasonable care to maintain the security of records provided to us. The insured must notify us if records containing confidential information are disclosed to an unauthorized person.

**W.53** An insured must notify us of any request for access to, or disclosure of, material relating to a claim or suit. The insured must cooperate with us in responding to the request.

**W.54** An insured must not assign any right or interest under this coverage without our written consent. An attempted assignment without our consent does not bind us.

**W.55** An insured must notify us if the insured changes legal representation, contact information, or authority to act for another insured in connection with a claim or suit. The insured must provide documents supporting the change when reasonably requested.

**W.56** An insured must cooperate with us in determining whether coverage applies to a claim or suit. Our investigation of a claim or suit does not waive any right available to us.

**W.57** We may provide a defense under a "reservation of rights". An insured must cooperate with us while we evaluate coverage and defend the claim or suit.

**W.58** An insured must notify us of facts that may affect the availability of coverage. The insured must provide notice even if the insured believes that no coverage applies.

**W.59** An insured must notify us if a claimant seeks relief other than damages. The insured must provide us with copies of papers describing the requested relief.

**W.60** An insured must assist us in obtaining releases, dismissals, and other documents necessary to resolve a claim or suit. The insured must not sign such a document without our consent.

**W.61** Compliance with these requirements is a condition to coverage under this endorsement. An insured's failure to comply may affect our obligations when the failure causes prejudice to us.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" in accordance with the terms of this coverage. A covered loss must result from an offense to which this coverage applies.

**W.2** We will pay only those damages for which an insured is legally responsible. Damages must arise from "personal injury" caused by an insured.

**W.3** We may investigate any occurrence that may result in a covered loss. We may settle any claim or suit when we consider settlement appropriate.

**W.4** Our duty to settle ends when the applicable limit of liability has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements. Expenses we incur in investigating or defending a claim do not reduce that limit unless this coverage provides otherwise.

**W.5** We will not pay more than the amount for which an insured is legally liable. We will not pay an amount that exceeds the value of the covered loss.

**W.6** We will determine the amount of a covered loss from the available facts, applicable law, and the terms of this coverage. We may consider a judgment, settlement demand, release, or other evidence of damages.

**W.7** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide information and documents we reasonably request.

**W.8** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. We will not pay for a voluntary payment, assumed obligation, or expense that we did not authorize.

**W.9** An insured must promptly notify us of a claim, demand, or suit. Notice must include information known to the insured concerning the alleged offense and the injured person.

**W.10** An insured must immediately send us copies of legal papers received in connection with a claim or suit. An insured must preserve evidence relating to the alleged offense when reasonably possible.

**W.11** We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath. An insured must answer material questions truthfully and must provide requested records.

**W.12** We may require an insured to assist in securing the attendance of witnesses. An insured must cooperate in obtaining statements, affidavits, and other evidence.

**W.13** We may settle with any claimant without obtaining the consent of an insured. A settlement by us does not constitute an admission of liability by us or an insured.

**W.14** We may defend a claim or suit in the name of an insured. We may appoint counsel to represent an insured when we undertake that defense.

**W.15** An insured must not interfere with our selection or direction of counsel. Counsel selected by us may take actions reasonably necessary to protect the interests of an insured.

**W.16** We will pay reasonable defense expenses that we incur. We will not pay defense expenses incurred by an insured without our prior consent.

**W.17** We may appeal a judgment or seek review of a ruling. An insured must cooperate with us in an appeal or other proceeding arising from a covered loss.

**W.18** We may pay the amount of a judgment into court when permitted by law. Payment into court does not create a duty to continue defending an insured after our obligations have ended.

**W.19** We will apply payments to damages covered by this coverage. We may allocate a payment among covered and uncovered allegations when the facts support allocation.

**W.20** We will not pay damages that are not legally recoverable from an insured. We will not pay damages imposed solely because an insured failed to obtain insurance.

**W.21** We do not cover a loss arising from an agreement by an insured to assume the liability of another person. This exclusion does not apply to liability an insured would have in the absence of that agreement.

**W.22** We do not cover a fine, penalty, sanction, or punitive amount to the extent such payment is prohibited by law. We will pay defense expenses for allegations seeking such amounts until our duty to defend ends.

**W.23** We do not cover restitution, disgorgement, or the return of money or property wrongfully obtained by an insured. We may defend allegations seeking those remedies when defense is otherwise owed.

**W.24** We do not cover damages arising from an intentional misrepresentation by an insured. We may defend a claim alleging both intentional and unintentional conduct until covered allegations are resolved.

**W.25** We do not cover damages arising from a criminal act of an insured when a final adjudication establishes that act. This exclusion applies only to the insured whose conduct is established.

**W.26** We do not cover a loss caused by an insured acting outside the scope of authority granted by law. We will determine the scope of authority from the applicable facts and law.

**W.27** We do not cover a claim made by an insured against another insured. This exclusion does not apply when the claim is made by a person who is not an insured.

**W.28** We will not duplicate payment for the same covered loss. We may reduce our payment by amounts received from another source for the same damages.

**W.29** If other insurance applies to a covered loss, we will share the loss according to the terms of that other insurance and this coverage. An insured must disclose other insurance that may apply.

**W.30** We may recover from a responsible person any payment we make. An insured must preserve our right of recovery and must not impair that right after a covered loss.

**W.31** An insured must transfer to us any right of recovery that exists against a responsible person to the extent of our payment. An insured must execute documents reasonably necessary to protect that right.

**W.32** We may require an insured to reimburse us from a recovery obtained from a responsible person. Reimbursement will not exceed the amount we paid for the covered loss.

**W.33** We will pay only after the legal obligation of an insured has been established by judgment or by a settlement we approve. We may settle a claim without a judgment.

**W.34** A claimant may not bring a direct action against us unless permitted by law. A claimant does not acquire greater rights against us than an insured has under this coverage.

**W.35** We may require a release from a claimant before making payment. A release may include terms necessary to protect us and an insured from further liability.

**W.36** We may issue payment jointly to an insured and a claimant when appropriate. We may issue payment to a representative legally authorized to receive it.

**W.37** We may withhold payment when ownership of the right to payment is disputed. We may seek a court determination concerning entitlement to payment.

**W.38** We will not pay an amount attributable to fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation by an insured. An insured must provide complete and accurate information concerning a covered loss.

**W.39** We may deny payment when an insured fails to comply with a duty imposed by this coverage and that failure prejudices us. We will determine prejudice from the facts of the claim.

**W.40** We may require proof of the damages claimed. Proof may include records, correspondence, pleadings, invoices, receipts, or other reliable evidence.

**W.41** We will evaluate nonmonetary relief only to the extent it results in covered damages. We will not pay the cost to comply with injunctive, declaratory, or equitable relief.

**W.42** We will not pay the cost to correct, replace, withdraw, or alter a product, publication, statement, or work. We may pay covered damages resulting from personal injury if otherwise covered.

**W.43** We will not pay a loss arising from a dispute concerning the ownership, use, or transfer of property unless the loss otherwise constitutes covered personal injury. The existence of a property dispute does not by itself establish a covered loss.

**W.44** We will not pay an amount arising solely from an insured's breach of contract. We may pay damages arising from personal injury that are independent of the breach of contract.

**W.45** The "special assessment limit" does not increase the amount available for a covered loss under this coverage. We will apply that limit only when its requirements are met.

**W.46** The "fire department service charge limit" does not apply to damages payable for personal injury. A charge assessed for fire department services is not a covered loss unless otherwise provided by this coverage.

**W.47** The "jewelry theft sublimit" does not apply to the settlement of a personal injury claim. Loss of jewelry does not establish personal injury by itself.

**W.48** The "business property sublimit" does not apply to damages payable for personal injury. Property used in a business does not create a covered loss unless the alleged offense is otherwise covered.

**W.49** We will determine whether a claim involves covered personal injury from the allegations, known facts, and applicable law. The characterization of a claim by a claimant does not control our determination.

**W.50** We may investigate facts that relate to coverage, liability, damages, or defenses. Our investigation does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.51** We may reserve our rights while we investigate or defend a claim. A reservation of rights does not create coverage that is not otherwise provided.

**W.52** We may seek contribution from another insurer that has a duty to pay the same covered loss. An insured must cooperate with our efforts to obtain contribution.

**W.53** We will not pay interest that accrues before a judgment unless we are legally obligated to pay it. We will pay postjudgment interest only as required by law and only on the portion of the judgment we owe.

**W.54** We may pay taxable costs awarded against an insured when they arise from a covered claim or suit. We will not pay costs attributable solely to matters that are not covered.

**W.55** We may settle a claim for less than the amount demanded by a claimant. An insured must not make statements that impair our ability to negotiate or settle.

**W.56** We may refuse to settle a claim when we reasonably determine that no covered liability exists. Our refusal to settle does not waive our right to investigate, defend, or deny the claim.

**W.57** We will not pay damages arising after an insured has failed to comply with a final court order directed to that insured, when the damages result from that failure. We may defend a claim alleging such damages until our duty to defend ends.

**W.58** We will not pay a loss to the extent payment would violate an applicable law, regulation, or governmental order. We may take any action necessary to comply with such requirement.

**W.59** Payment by us will be made in the lawful currency of the place where payment is required. We may make payment by check, electronic transfer, or other commercially reasonable method.

**W.60** Our payment of a covered loss does not waive any provision of this coverage. No act by us will waive a provision unless we expressly agree in writing.

## W.8 — Definitions

“Personal Injury” means injury arising out of false arrest, detention, or imprisonment. Personal Injury includes injury arising out of malicious prosecution.

“Wrongful Eviction” means the wrongful eviction from, wrongful entry into, or invasion of the right of private occupancy of a premises. The premises must be occupied by a person who is not an insured.

“Oral Publication” means the communication of spoken words to another person. Oral Publication includes a communication made in person or by means of a recording or transmission.

“Written Publication” means the communication of written, printed, pictured, or electronically displayed matter to another person. Written Publication includes matter transmitted by electronic means.

“Libel” means a false Written Publication that tends to injure another person's reputation. Libel includes a publication that exposes another person to public contempt, ridicule, or disgrace.

“Slander” means a false Oral Publication that tends to injure another person's reputation. Slander includes a spoken communication that exposes another person to public contempt, ridicule, or disgrace.

“Defamation” means Libel or Slander. Defamation includes an implied assertion that would reasonably be understood as injuring another person's reputation.

“Privacy Right” means a right of a person to be free from an unauthorized publication concerning that person. A Privacy Right includes a right protected by law against unauthorized disclosure of private facts.

“Privacy Violation” means an Oral Publication or Written Publication that violates a person's Privacy Right. Privacy Violation includes an unauthorized use of a person's name, likeness, or identity.

“False Arrest” means the unlawful restraint of a person without lawful authority. False Arrest includes restraint by an insured acting under an asserted authority that does not exist.

“Detention” means the restraint of a person's freedom of movement. Detention includes a restraint that is not accompanied by physical force.

“Imprisonment” means the confinement of a person against that person's will. Imprisonment includes confinement in a place from which the person is not free to leave.

“Malicious Prosecution” means the institution or continuation of a legal proceeding without probable cause and with malice. Malicious Prosecution includes a proceeding brought against a person who is not an insured.

“Premises” means land, buildings, structures, and the parts of those buildings or structures used in connection with land. Premises includes approaches, private ways, and areas used for access or occupancy.

“Private Occupancy” means possession or use of Premises by a person under a right of occupancy. Private Occupancy includes occupancy as an owner, tenant, guest, or other lawful occupant.

“Right of Private Occupancy” means the right to possess, use, or enjoy Premises without wrongful interference. Right of Private Occupancy includes the right to remain in Premises under a lawful occupancy arrangement.

“Wrongful Entry” means entry into Premises without the consent of a person entitled to control access to the Premises. Wrongful Entry includes remaining in Premises after consent to remain has been withdrawn.

“Invasion” means an interference with a person's Right of Private Occupancy. Invasion includes conduct that substantially impairs a person's use or enjoyment of Premises.

“Publication” means an Oral Publication or Written Publication. Publication occurs when the communication is made available to a person other than the person who is the subject of the communication.

“Communication” means the conveyance of information, statements, images, sounds, or other matter. Communication includes conduct intended to convey a message.

“Electronic Means” means equipment, systems, networks, or devices used to create, store, transmit, receive, display, or reproduce information. Electronic Means includes electronic mail, messaging services, websites, and social media services.

“Identity” means information that identifies or is capable of identifying a particular person. Identity includes a person's name, image, voice, signature, or personal characteristics.

“Likeness” means a representation of a person's appearance, voice, manner, or other identifying characteristics. Likeness includes a photograph, image, recording, or depiction.

“Private Facts” means information concerning a person's personal life that is not generally known to the public. Private Facts includes information concerning a person's family, health, finances, residence, or personal affairs.

“Unauthorized” means without permission, consent, legal authority, or other lawful right. Unauthorized includes conduct exceeding the scope of permission or consent that was given.

“Consent” means a voluntary agreement by a person with authority to give permission. Consent may be express or implied from conduct or circumstances.

“Lawful Authority” means authority granted by law, court order, governmental action, or other legally effective source. Lawful Authority includes authority that is valid at the time of the act or omission.

“Legal Proceeding” means a civil, criminal, administrative, regulatory, or other proceeding authorized by law. Legal Proceeding includes an investigation or accusation that may result in a formal proceeding.

“Probable Cause” means facts and circumstances sufficient to cause a reasonable person to believe that a claim, charge, or proceeding is justified. Probable Cause is determined from the facts known at the time the proceeding is instituted or continued.

“Malice” means an intent to cause harm or an intentional disregard of another person's rights. Malice includes conduct undertaken for an improper purpose.

“Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. Occurrence includes conduct that results in Personal Injury when that conduct is not expected or intended by an insured.

“Suit” means a civil proceeding in which damages because of Personal Injury are alleged. Suit includes an arbitration proceeding or other dispute resolution proceeding to which damages because of Personal Injury are submitted.

“Damages” means sums that an insured is legally obligated to pay because of Personal Injury. Damages includes prejudgment interest assessed against an insured on that obligation.

“Claim” means a demand for Damages because of Personal Injury. Claim includes a written or oral assertion that an insured is responsible for Personal Injury.

“Insured” means a person qualifying as an insured under this coverage. Insured includes a person for whom we provide coverage under this form.

“You” and “your” mean the person named as an insured and any other person qualifying as an insured. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean the insurer providing this coverage.

“Policy Period” means the period during which this coverage is in effect. Policy Period begins when coverage takes effect and ends when coverage terminates.

“Bodily Injury” means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. Bodily Injury includes resulting care, loss of services, or death.
