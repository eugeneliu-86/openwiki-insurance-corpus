# HO 04 92 Water Backup — Tenants (Edition 2019-03)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

**W.1** This endorsement is attached to, forms part of, and modifies the policy. The insurance provided by this endorsement is subject to the terms of the policy except as modified by this endorsement.

**W.2** We provide the coverage described in this endorsement only when the endorsement applies to your insurance. No provision of this endorsement broadens coverage except as expressly stated.

**W.3** The term "Water Backup" means water or waterborne material that backs up through sewers, drains, or sumps. "Water Backup" also includes overflow or discharge from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**W.4** The term "insured" has the meaning assigned to it in the policy. An insured must comply with all duties and conditions that apply to the insurance.

**W.5** The term "you" refers to the named insured shown in the policy. The terms "we", "us", and "our" refer to the insurer providing this insurance.

**W.6** Words and phrases that are not defined by this endorsement have the meanings assigned to them in the policy. Words and phrases used in the singular include the plural when the context requires.

**W.7** This endorsement applies only to loss that is otherwise covered by the policy and is caused by a covered "Water Backup". All exclusions, limitations, conditions, and duties continue to apply unless changed by this endorsement.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by any condition that is excluded by the policy unless this endorsement expressly provides coverage for that condition. We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an insured to preserve property from further damage when reasonable means are available.

**W.9** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further loss after a "Water Backup". An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably practicable.

**W.10** An insured must provide us with information, records, and access that we reasonably request to investigate a claimed loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the adjustment of any claim under this endorsement.

**W.11** Coverage under this endorsement applies only to property for which coverage is provided by the policy. This endorsement does not create coverage for property that is not covered by the policy.

**W.12** If the terms of this endorsement conflict with the terms of the policy, the terms of this endorsement apply. All terms that do not conflict remain in effect.

**W.13** This endorsement applies only while it is in effect as part of the policy. The attachment of this endorsement does not change any insurance except as stated in this endorsement.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** In this section, "water backup" means water that backs up through a sewer or drain, or water that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**W.2** We cover direct physical loss to your covered personal property caused by "water backup." The water backup must result in physical damage to the property.

**W.3** Coverage provided by this section is subject to the limit insubject to the limit in (see W.2, Limit of Liability). The applicable limit is the most we will pay for loss covered under this section.

**W.4** Payment for loss under this section will reduce the amount available for other loss covered under this section. We will not pay more than the applicable limit regardless of the number of insureds, damaged items, or claims.

**W.5** Coverage applies when water enters or affects covered personal property as a result of water backing up through a sewer or drain. The backup must be a cause of the direct physical loss.

**W.6** Coverage also applies when water overflows from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. Coverage also applies when water is discharged from that equipment.

**W.7** Water may be covered under this section whether the water backup results from a blockage, obstruction, or mechanical breakdown. The cause of the water backup must otherwise be within the coverage provided by this section.

**W.8** A sewer includes a sanitary sewer, storm sewer, or similar system intended to carry water or waste away from property. A drain includes a pipe, channel, or opening intended to receive or carry water away.

**W.9** A sump includes a pit, basin, or similar receptacle designed to collect water. A sump pump includes equipment designed to remove water from a sump.

**W.10** Related equipment includes components that are connected to and used with a sump or sump pump. Such components may include pipes, hoses, valves, switches, and electrical connections.

**W.11** The presence of water near a sewer, drain, sump, or sump pump does not by itself establish that a water backup occurred. An insured must show that the claimed loss was caused by water backup.

**W.12** We cover only the direct physical loss caused by water backup. We do not cover loss consisting solely of wear, deterioration, corrosion, or mechanical failure of equipment.

**W.13** We do not cover the cost to repair or replace a sump, sump pump, or related equipment solely because that equipment failed to operate. We will consider resulting direct physical loss caused by water backup under this section.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused solely by water that leaks, seeps, or drips from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or fire protective sprinkler systems. This limitation does not apply when the direct physical loss is caused by water backup.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused solely by surface water entering the property without backing up through a sewer or drain. This section applies only to water backup as defined in this section.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused solely by groundwater, subsurface water, or water below the surface of the ground. This limitation applies unless the water causes covered direct physical loss through water backup.

**W.17** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered personal property from further damage after a water backup. Reasonable protective measures do not increase the applicable limit.

**W.18** An insured must retain damaged property, when reasonably possible, for our inspection. An insured must not discard damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.19** An insured must give us prompt notice of a water backup that may result in a claim. The notice must identify the property involved and the known circumstances of the loss.

**W.20** An insured must show us the damaged property and permit us to inspect the location where the water backup occurred. An insured must provide records or other information reasonably needed to evaluate the claim.

**W.21** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a claim. This duty includes providing information concerning the source, timing, and extent of the water backup.

**W.22** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve evidence concerning the cause of the water backup. Such evidence may include damaged components, photographs, receipts, repair records, and reports.

**W.23** An insured must keep a record of expenses incurred to protect property from further damage. We may require documentation supporting any claimed expense.

**W.24** An insured must not make voluntary payments, assume obligations, or incur expenses other than reasonable emergency measures without our consent. This duty does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable action to protect covered personal property from further damage.

**W.25** We may inspect the damaged property and any sump, sump pump, drain, sewer connection, or related equipment involved in the claimed loss. Our inspection does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.26** We may require an insured to provide a statement concerning the water backup. We may require that statement to describe the condition of the property before and after the loss.

**W.27** We will determine the amount of covered loss based on the direct physical damage caused by water backup. We may consider the condition, age, use, and value of the damaged property.

**W.28** Repair, replacement, or restoration of damaged property must be supported by reasonable evidence of the covered loss. We may pay the amount necessary to repair or replace damaged property, subject to the applicable limit.

**W.29** We do not cover increased costs that result solely from replacing damaged property with property of greater value, capacity, or utility. We will pay only for the covered direct physical loss.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by an insured intentionally causing a water backup. This limitation applies whether the intentional act is performed directly or through another person.

**W.31** Coverage under this section applies only to property in which you have an insurable interest at the time of loss. We will not pay more than your financial interest in the damaged property.

**W.32** An insured must permit us to examine records relating to ownership, value, repair, or replacement of damaged property. The records must be provided within a reasonable time after our request.

**W.33** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably required by us. The examination may concern the claimed water backup, the damaged property, and the amount of loss.

**W.34** Payment under this section does not waive any policy condition, limitation, or exclusion that applies to the claim. Our investigation or inspection does not constitute an admission of coverage.

**W.35** We may pursue recovery from a person or organization responsible for covered loss after we make payment. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.36** This coverage applies only to direct physical loss that begins during the policy period. The water backup must occur during the policy period for coverage to apply.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We provide this coverage for direct physical loss to Covered Property caused by "water backup" or "sump overflow". The property must be insured under this policy and located at the residence premises. Coverage applies subject to all terms, conditions, and exclusions of this policy.

**W.2** The most we will pay for loss caused by "water backup" or "sump overflow" is two thousand five hundred dollars. Subject to this limit, we will pay only for covered loss to Covered Property. This limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims, or items of Covered Property involved.

**W.3** We determine the amount of loss based on the cost to repair or replace the damaged Covered Property. The damaged property must be reasonably repaired or replaced with property of like kind and quality. We do not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace the damaged property.

**W.4** We cover reasonable expenses you incur to remove water from the residence premises when the expense is necessary to prevent further covered loss. The expense must be incurred while the water is causing covered damage or while the water remains at the residence premises. We do not pay expenses incurred solely to maintain, inspect, or improve a plumbing system.

**W.5** We cover damage to Covered Property that results directly from water entering through a drain, sewer, sump, or related system. The water must enter or rise through the system and cause the damage at the residence premises. We do not cover damage that results only from humidity, condensation, or seepage through a foundation.

**W.6** We cover damage caused by water that escapes from a sump pump or related equipment. The equipment must be located at the residence premises. Coverage applies only when the escaping water causes direct physical loss to Covered Property.

**W.7** We cover necessary access to property when access is required to repair the source of covered water damage. The access must be reasonably necessary and must relate to covered damage. We do not pay the cost to repair or replace the equipment or system from which the water escaped.

**W.8** We cover reasonable costs to remove and replace portions of the residence premises that obstruct access to the source of covered water damage. The work must be limited to what is reasonably necessary to obtain access. We do not cover improvements or alterations made during the access work.

**W.9** We cover damage to personal property caused directly by covered water. The personal property must be located at the residence premises when the damage occurs. We do not cover property that is not Covered Property under this policy.

**W.10** You must take reasonable steps to protect Covered Property from further damage after covered water is discovered. You must preserve the damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. We may reduce the amount we pay for damage resulting from your failure to take reasonable protective measures.

**W.11** You must promptly notify us or your insurance representative of covered water damage. Your notice must describe the location and apparent cause of the damage. You must provide additional information when we reasonably request it.

**W.12** You must provide us with records and documents that relate to the claimed damage. The records must support the amount and nature of the loss. We may not require records that are not reasonably available to you.

**W.13** We may inspect the damaged property before repairs begin. You must permit us to inspect the property at a reasonable time and place. You may make reasonable temporary repairs before our inspection when delay would cause further damage.

**W.14** You must keep receipts for reasonable temporary repairs and necessary water removal expenses. The receipts must identify the work performed and the amount charged. We may use the receipts to determine the amount of covered loss.

**W.15** We may require a signed statement describing the circumstances of the loss. The statement must be provided within a reasonable time after our request. The statement does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.16** We may require you to show us the damaged Covered Property. The property must be made available at a reasonable time and place. We may inspect, photograph, or document the property to evaluate the claim.

**W.17** We may settle the loss by paying the cost to repair or replace the damaged Covered Property. The repair or replacement must be completed with materials of like kind and quality. We may use a qualified contractor or other method reasonably suited to determine the cost.

**W.18** We may pay the amount of covered loss after applying any applicable deductible. The deductible applies as provided by this policy. We do not pay an amount that is less than the applicable deductible.

**W.19** We apply this coverage to the covered water damage that results from an occurrence. The occurrence must take place while this policy applies to the residence premises. Damage that does not result from the same occurrence is evaluated separately.

**W.20** We cover ensuing loss when covered water causes another covered peril to damage Covered Property. The ensuing loss must be separate from the excluded cause of loss. We do not cover the excluded cause of loss itself.

**W.21** We do not cover damage caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage. This exclusion applies whether the water is visible or concealed. It does not eliminate coverage for a separate covered loss that results from the seepage or leakage.

**W.22** We do not cover damage caused by freezing, thawing, or expansion of water in a plumbing system. This exclusion applies to the system and to property damaged solely by that cause. Coverage may apply when a separate covered cause directly damages Covered Property.

**W.23** We do not cover damage caused by defective, inadequate, or faulty design, construction, repair, or maintenance. This exclusion applies to the equipment or system that causes the water loss. We may cover ensuing loss that is otherwise covered under this policy.

**W.24** We do not cover the cost to replace or repair a sump pump, drain, sewer, pipe, or related equipment that causes covered water damage. This exclusion applies even when the equipment is damaged at the same time as Covered Property. We may cover resulting damage to other Covered Property when the loss is otherwise covered.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by neglect to use reasonable means to save and preserve Covered Property after a loss. This exclusion applies when reasonable protective measures were available to you. We may cover the portion of loss that would have occurred without the neglect.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by power failure when the failure results from a cause outside the residence premises. This exclusion applies to damage caused by the failure of a sump pump or related equipment. We may cover ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, or water that enters through an opening in the foundation. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the water combines with water from another source. We may cover damage caused by a separate covered peril.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including water released or displaced by earth movement. This exclusion applies whether the movement is natural or caused by human activity. We may cover ensuing loss that is otherwise covered.

**W.29** We do not cover loss to land, soil, or other property that is not Covered Property. This exclusion applies even when the property is located at the residence premises. We may cover resulting damage to Covered Property when the loss is otherwise covered.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. This exclusion applies to the property that is damaged and to the system causing the damage. We may cover ensuing loss caused by a covered peril.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by neglect, deterioration, or failure to maintain a sump pump or related system. This exclusion applies to the cost of correcting the condition that caused the loss. We may cover direct physical loss to Covered Property when the damage is otherwise covered.

**W.32** We pay covered loss under this coverage subject to the terms of this policy. You must comply with all duties that apply to a claim for covered loss. We may deny coverage to the extent your failure to comply with those duties prejudices our investigation or adjustment of the loss.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** We apply a deductible to covered "water backup loss." You must bear the applicable deductible before we pay for that loss. Subject to the terms of this provision, the deductible applies to the amount of covered loss remaining after adjustment.

**W.2** We determine the amount of covered water backup loss before applying the deductible. You must provide information that reasonably supports the amount of the loss. Unless we agree otherwise, we determine the loss from the available records, evidence, and inspection.

**W.3** The most we will pay is two hundred fifty dollars. Except that the deductible applies only to covered loss resulting from water backup. We do not reduce the limit of insurance by the deductible.

**W.4** You must promptly notify us of a water backup loss. You must protect covered property from further damage after the loss. Subject to reasonable safety concerns, you must preserve the damaged property for our inspection.

**W.5** We apply the deductible to covered personal property loss. You must separate damaged personal property from undamaged personal property when reasonably possible. Provided that the property is covered, we may inspect it before determining the amount of loss.

**W.6** We apply the deductible to covered improvements and betterments that you own or are responsible for. You must identify the improvements and betterments affected by the loss. Except that we do not determine the amount of covered improvements and betterments from damage to property for which you have no responsibility.

**W.7** We apply the deductible only after determining that the loss is covered. You must assist us in determining whether the loss resulted from water backup. Unless the available evidence establishes covered water backup loss, we do not apply this deductible to an unrelated loss.

**W.8** We may adjust covered water backup loss with you or with a person acting on your behalf. You must cooperate with reasonable requests for information concerning the loss. Subject to your rights under the policy, an adjustment does not waive any policy condition.

**W.9** You must submit a claim for covered water backup loss in accordance with the policy. We may require a description of the damaged property and the circumstances of the loss. Provided that the information is reasonably available, you must identify the date and source of the water backup.

**W.10** We may inspect the residence premises in connection with a covered water backup loss. You must provide reasonable access for inspection and adjustment. Except that we will not require access that would unreasonably endanger you or another person.

**W.11** We determine the amount of covered loss without regard to amounts that are not recoverable under the policy. You must not include unsupported amounts in a claim. Subject to the policy terms, we may consider depreciation, repair estimates, receipts, and other relevant evidence.

**W.12** We apply the deductible to the covered amount after any applicable valuation provision is applied. You must provide records reasonably necessary to establish the applicable valuation. Unless the policy provides otherwise, the deductible does not increase the amount payable for damaged property.

**W.13** You must take reasonable steps to prevent additional damage after water backup occurs. We may reduce payment for additional damage that results from your failure to take those steps. Subject to reasonable safety concerns, you must arrange for necessary temporary protection.

**W.14** We may pay for reasonable measures to protect covered property from further damage when those measures are otherwise covered. You must keep receipts and other records for those measures. Provided that the measures relate to covered water backup loss, we include them in determining the covered amount.

**W.15** We apply the deductible to the covered amount after considering payments received from responsible persons. You must disclose payments or recoveries that relate to the loss. Unless the policy provides otherwise, such payments do not eliminate your duty to bear the deductible.

**W.16** We may pursue recovery from a person responsible for covered water backup loss. You must preserve our rights of recovery to the extent required by the policy. Subject to those rights, you must not release a responsible person from liability without our written consent.

**W.17** We may make payment to you or to another person with an interest in covered property. You must identify any person whose interest may affect payment. Except that payment to another person does not change your responsibility for the deductible.

**W.18** We apply the deductible before determining the amount payable to any interested person. You must provide information concerning liens, leases, or other interests affecting covered property. Unless we agree otherwise, those interests do not create additional insurance.

**W.19** We may settle covered water backup loss by repairing, replacing, or paying for damaged property. You must cooperate with the selected method of settlement. Subject to the policy terms, the deductible applies regardless of the method of settlement.

**W.20** We may use materials or services of comparable kind and quality in settling covered water backup loss. You must allow reasonable access for authorized repairs. Provided that the selected materials or services are reasonably suitable, disagreement with the selection does not eliminate the deductible.

**W.21** You must keep damaged property available for inspection when we reasonably request it. We may inspect the property before making payment. Except that we may rely on photographs, records, or other evidence when inspection is not reasonably possible.

**W.22** We may request a sworn proof of loss for covered water backup loss. You must complete and return the proof of loss within the time required by the policy. Unless we waive the requirement in writing, failure to provide the proof may affect payment.

**W.23** You must answer reasonable questions concerning the cause and amount of covered water backup loss. We may require an examination under oath when permitted by the policy. Subject to applicable law, the examination does not increase the amount payable.

**W.24** We determine whether separate damage resulted from the same water backup event. You must identify the property and damage associated with the claim. Except that we do not apply this deductible to damage that is not covered water backup loss.

**W.25** We may combine related damage when the available evidence shows that the damage resulted from a common water backup. You must provide information that distinguishes separate causes of damage. Unless the evidence establishes a different cause, we may rely on the facts reasonably available to us.

**W.26** We do not cover amounts that exceed the applicable limit of insurance after application of the deductible. You must bear amounts that are not payable under the policy. Subject to the policy terms, the deductible does not increase any limit of insurance.

**W.27** We may make payment when we have completed the adjustment of covered water backup loss. You must comply with applicable policy conditions before payment is due. Provided that those conditions are satisfied, we pay the amount owed after application of the deductible.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** Flood and surface water remains excluded. This exclusion applies regardless of the source of the loss.

**W.2** We do not cover under this endorsement loss unless it is caused by a "Water Backup". Water that enters a structure without backing up through a sewer, drain, sump, or related system is not a Water Backup.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a foundation, wall, floor, or paved surface.

**W.4** We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows a body of water or watercourse. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or arises from natural conditions.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by rain entering through an opening in a roof, wall, window, door, or other part of a structure. This exclusion applies even if the opening was created by wind, wear, deterioration, or defective construction.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by the discharge or overflow of water from a plumbing fixture. A discharge or overflow is not covered merely because the water later enters a drain or sump.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by water escaping from an appliance, container, pipe, hose, or plumbing system. This exclusion applies unless the loss results directly from a Water Backup.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by water entering through a roof drain, gutter, downspout, or similar exterior drainage system. Water collected or conveyed by an exterior drainage system is not a Water Backup.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by water entering from an exterior storm drain. This exclusion applies when the water does not back up through a covered sewer, drain, or sump.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a sump, pump, drain, or related equipment to operate because of wear, deterioration, corrosion, or improper maintenance. The failure of such equipment does not by itself establish a Water Backup.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by an interruption of electrical power. This exclusion applies whether the interruption occurs on or away from the residence premises.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown of a pump, motor, switch, valve, or related equipment. Mechanical breakdown does not include water damage otherwise caused by a Water Backup.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or latent defect. These conditions remain excluded even when they contribute to a Water Backup.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, decay, or hidden defect. This exclusion applies to property that fails to contain, convey, or remove water.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies whether performed by you or by another person.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, ceilings, or pavements. Damage resulting from these conditions is not a Water Backup loss.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by neglect by an "insured" to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect property from further damage when reasonably possible.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused intentionally by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting damage is greater than expected or intended.

**W.19** We do not cover loss arising from a criminal, fraudulent, dishonest, or illegal act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the act is committed alone or with another person.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, demolition, or use of property. This exclusion includes costs incurred to comply with an ordinance or law.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or other action by a governmental authority. We will cover loss caused by fire resulting from such action if the fire would otherwise be covered.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, earth sinking, rising, or shifting. This exclusion applies whether the earth movement is caused by natural or human forces.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies even if the condition results from moisture associated with a Water Backup.

**W.24** We do not cover the testing, monitoring, cleaning, containing, treating, detoxifying, neutralizing, removing, disposing of, or replacing property because of mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. Such expense is excluded whether incurred by you or another person.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies to cleanup, removal, testing, monitoring, and remediation costs.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by contamination of property. Contamination includes the presence of pollutants, harmful substances, or unsafe conditions requiring cleanup or disposal.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by failure of a utility service to supply water, power, communications, or other service. This exclusion applies when the utility failure prevents a sump, pump, or drainage system from operating.

**W.28** We do not cover loss to property used primarily for business purposes. This exclusion applies to business property even when it is located at the residence premises.

**W.29** We do not cover loss to property owned by another person and held by you for sale, storage, repair, service, or safekeeping. This exclusion does not apply to property otherwise insured under this endorsement.

**W.30** We do not cover loss arising from contractual liability assumed by you. This exclusion does not apply to liability that you would have without the contract.

**W.31** We do not cover loss consisting of a decrease in market value. We also do not cover loss resulting from delay, loss of use, loss of income, or loss of opportunity.

**W.32** We do not cover costs to replace undamaged property solely to achieve uniformity, matching, or improved appearance. We do not cover costs that exceed the repair or replacement of property physically damaged by a covered Water Backup.

**W.33** We do not cover costs arising from the correction of preexisting defects. This exclusion applies even if the correction is performed while repairing covered damage.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies whether the seepage or leakage occurs from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by the failure to maintain drains, sewers, sumps, pumps, or related equipment in serviceable condition. An insured must permit reasonable inspection of damaged property when requested by us.

**W.36** We do not cover loss when an insured conceals or misrepresents a material fact concerning the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim.

**W.37** We do not cover avoidable damage occurring after an insured becomes aware of a Water Backup. An insured must make reasonable temporary repairs necessary to protect property from further damage.

**W.38** We do not cover expenses incurred without our consent when consent is required by this endorsement. An insured must retain damaged property for examination when reasonably possible.

**W.39** The "wind percentage deductible minimum" does not alter an exclusion under this endorsement. It does not create coverage for an excluded cause of loss.

**W.40** The "Coverage B limit as a percentage of Coverage A" does not alter an exclusion under this endorsement. It does not create coverage for property or loss otherwise excluded.

**W.41** The "personal property limit percentage" does not alter an exclusion under this endorsement. The "additional living expense limit" does not create coverage for an excluded loss.

**W.42** The "clean-up allowance percentage" does not alter an exclusion under this endorsement. It does not create coverage for contamination, pollutants, mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An “insured” must comply with the duties stated in these Conditions as they apply to a “water backup loss”. Compliance by any insured does not relieve any other insured of a duty that applies to that insured.

**W.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of a water backup loss. An insured must give us notice of the loss within ninety days after the loss is discovered.

**W.3** Notice must identify the insured, the location where the loss occurred, and the known circumstances of the loss. Notice must include information sufficient to permit us to contact the insured.

**W.4** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair and mitigation expenses.

**W.5** An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect health or safety. If property must be discarded, an insured must preserve photographs, records, or other evidence of its condition when reasonably possible.

**W.6** An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property as often as reasonably necessary. An insured must permit us to examine the location of the loss and any property related to the claim.

**W.7** An insured must provide us with records, documents, receipts, estimates, photographs, and other information we reasonably request. An insured must authorize us to obtain records that are material to the claim when we reasonably request that authorization.

**W.8** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must not knowingly conceal, misrepresent, or withhold a material fact concerning the claim.

**W.9** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the examination record if we request a signature.

**W.10** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when we reasonably request it. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for the property claimed.

**W.11** An insured must provide bills, receipts, invoices, and related records that support the inventory. An insured must identify property that is not owned by an insured but is included in the claim.

**W.12** An insured must provide a “sworn proof of loss” when we reasonably request it. The sworn proof of loss must state the information we reasonably require concerning the claim.

**W.13** An insured must comply with the “sworn proof deadline” when we request a sworn proof of loss. Failure to provide a sworn proof of loss may affect our ability to determine the claim.

**W.14** An insured must show us the damaged property, records, and other evidence supporting the claim. An insured must permit us to make copies of records that are material to the claim.

**W.15** An insured must preserve rights of recovery against a person or organization that may be responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.16** If we pay a claim, an insured must transfer to us any right of recovery against a person or organization to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing that right.

**W.17** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense, except for reasonable emergency measures to protect property from further damage. We will not be bound by a voluntary payment or obligation unless we approve it.

**W.18** An insured must notify us promptly if another insurer may provide coverage for the loss. An insured must provide us with information concerning any other insurance known to the insured.

**W.19** If other insurance applies to the same loss, an insured must preserve all rights available under that other insurance. We will determine our payment in accordance with the applicable terms of the other insurance and this coverage.

**W.20** An insured must not abandon property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment, but we are not required to do so.

**W.21** An insured must permit us to inspect repaired or replaced property when reasonably necessary to verify the claim. An insured must retain records supporting repair or replacement costs.

**W.22** An insured must promptly notify us of any material change in the facts or circumstances previously reported concerning the claim. A material change includes newly discovered damage, property, records, or information concerning the cause of loss.

**W.23** An insured must provide us with a complete and accurate mailing address, electronic address, and telephone contact information. An insured must promptly notify us of a change in contact information.

**W.24** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting persons who perform emergency services, mitigation, repair, or restoration work. An insured must provide us with available agreements and invoices for that work.

**W.25** An insured must keep damaged property separate from undamaged property when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to identify the property for purposes of inspection and valuation.

**W.26** An insured must make reasonable efforts to prevent mold, deterioration, contamination, and additional damage after a water backup loss. An insured must provide us with information concerning measures taken to prevent further damage.

**W.27** An insured must disclose whether the property was subject to repair, renovation, vacancy, or maintenance activity at the time of loss. An insured must provide records concerning such activity when those records are material to the claim.

**W.28** An insured must disclose any prior damage to the claimed property that is known to the insured. An insured must identify repairs or replacements completed before the reported loss.

**W.29** An insured must notify us if a governmental authority, landlord, property manager, or other person requires access to the location of the loss. An insured must provide us with copies of notices or orders related to the loss when available.

**W.30** An insured must permit us to communicate with persons having relevant information concerning the loss. An insured must not instruct a person to withhold material information from us.

**W.31** An insured must promptly forward to us any demand, notice, correspondence, or legal paper relating to the loss. An insured must not admit liability or make a statement binding us without our written consent.

**W.32** An insured must assist us in identifying the source of water, the path of water, and the property affected by the loss. An insured must provide information concerning maintenance, repairs, or conditions that may be material to our investigation.

**W.33** An insured must provide access to utility, plumbing, drainage, and related records when those records are material to the claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining information from persons who possess relevant records.

**W.34** An insured must submit any dispute concerning the amount of loss to appraisal if we and an insured agree to appraisal. Each party must select a competent and impartial appraiser.

**W.35** The appraisers must select an umpire if they cannot agree on the amount of loss. An award agreed to by the appraisers or by an appraiser and the umpire will determine the amount of loss.

**W.36** Each party will pay the expense of its appraiser and will share the expense of the umpire and appraisal. Appraisal does not determine coverage, policy interpretation, or any legal issue.

**W.37** We may require an insured to submit a claim to appraisal before we determine the amount payable for disputed damage. An insured must cooperate in the appraisal process after appraisal is agreed upon.

**W.38** We will apply the applicable deductible to a covered water backup loss. The applicable deductible will be determined without regard to the "all-other-perils deductible minimum".

**W.39** We will not apply a "coinsurance threshold" to determine payment for a water backup loss. An insured must nevertheless provide information necessary to determine the value of claimed property.

**W.40** Payment of a claim does not waive any condition, exclusion, limitation, or right available to us. Our investigation, inspection, appraisal, or payment does not constitute an admission of coverage.

**W.41** No person may bring an action against us unless an insured has complied with all applicable Conditions and the amount of loss has been determined. An action must be brought within the "years within which an action against the insurer must be brought".

**W.42** An insured must bring any action against us in a court having jurisdiction over the action. An insured must provide us with notice of the action as required by applicable law.

**W.43** An insured must return any payment that exceeds the amount properly payable under this coverage. We may recover an overpayment by lawful means.

**W.44** If an insured receives payment from another source for the same loss, an insured must notify us promptly. We may reduce our payment by the amount that duplicates payment for the same covered damage.

**W.45** An insured must reimburse us for amounts we pay that are later recovered from a person or organization responsible for the loss. Reimbursement will not exceed the amount recovered for the same loss.

**W.46** An insured must perform all duties under these Conditions in good faith. A failure to perform a duty may affect coverage only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** An insured must use reasonable care to protect the residence premises from a water backup. An insured must maintain the premises in a condition that permits prompt discovery of a water backup.

**W.2** An insured must keep drains, plumbing fixtures, and related connections free from conditions that obstruct ordinary flow. An insured must promptly correct a condition that is known to increase the likelihood of a water backup.

**W.3** An insured must provide us with prompt notice of a water backup that may result in a claim. Notice must include information reasonably available to you concerning the location, cause, and extent of damage.

**W.4** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a water backup. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.5** An insured must permit access to the residence premises when we reasonably request access to inspect damage or investigate a claim. An insured must cooperate with us in determining the cause and extent of a water backup.

**W.6** A backwater valve is not required where you maintain a finished area below grade. An insured must keep a required backwater valve in operating condition.

**W.7** An insured must not intentionally obstruct, disable, or alter a device designed to prevent water from entering the residence premises. An insured must promptly report a known malfunction of such a device to the person responsible for its maintenance.

**W.8** An insured must use reasonable care when storing personal property in an area susceptible to a water backup. An insured must elevate or otherwise protect personal property when conditions reasonably require protection.

**W.9** An insured must promptly remove standing water when removal can be performed safely. An insured must take reasonable steps to dry affected areas and prevent further damage.

**W.10** An insured must preserve records, receipts, photographs, and other evidence supporting a claim when reasonably available. An insured must provide those materials to us upon request.

**W.11** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require it. An insured must provide truthful information concerning the claimed loss.

**W.12** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged covered property when we request an inventory. The inventory must identify the property, its condition, and the claimed amount of loss.

**W.13** An insured must provide records that support the ownership, value, and condition of covered property when we reasonably request them. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records.

**W.14** An insured must not dispose of damaged covered property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect health or safety. An insured must preserve evidence of property disposed of under that circumstance.

**W.15** An insured must not make voluntary payments, assume obligations, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses necessary to protect covered property from further damage. This provision does not prevent an insured from taking emergency action reasonably necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.16** An insured must promptly report to the appropriate authority a water backup caused by suspected vandalism or other unlawful conduct. An insured must provide us with information concerning that report when we request it.

**W.17** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting persons who perform maintenance or repair work affecting plumbing, drains, or related systems. An insured must retain documentation for work that may relate to the claimed loss when reasonably available.

**W.18** An insured must promptly notify the person responsible for maintenance of the residence premises of a known condition that may cause a water backup. Notice to that person does not replace the duty to notify us of a claim.

**W.19** An insured must permit us to inspect the residence premises and covered property as often as reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim. An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning the claim.

**W.20** An insured must cooperate with our efforts to determine whether a water backup resulted from a condition at the residence premises. An insured must provide information reasonably available concerning prior repairs, maintenance, and similar conditions.

**W.21** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent contamination, mold, or deterioration after a water backup. An insured must comply with reasonable directions we give concerning preservation of covered property.

**W.22** An insured must not resume use of damaged plumbing, drains, or related systems when use may cause further damage. An insured must obtain appropriate repair service when such service is reasonably necessary.

**W.23** An insured must provide access to damaged covered property for inspection, testing, repair, or salvage when we reasonably request access. An insured must cooperate with persons acting on our behalf.

**W.24** An insured must promptly notify us if another person may be responsible for the water backup. An insured must preserve any rights of recovery against that person to the extent reasonably possible.

**W.25** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we request it. The statement must include information reasonably available to you concerning the damaged covered property and the circumstances of the loss.

**W.26** An insured must comply with these special requirements before seeking payment for a claim, unless compliance is not reasonably possible. We may require reasonable additional information needed to evaluate the claim.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We settle direct physical "loss" to your "covered property" caused by a "water backup loss". We determine the amount of loss under this provision.

**W.2** We settle loss to your "personal property" using actual cash value. The applicable basis governs our valuation of covered property.

**W.3** An "insured" must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a loss. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the loss.

**W.4** An insured must show us the damaged property when it is available for inspection. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and the location where the loss occurred.

**W.5** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable expenses incurred solely to protect covered property from further damage are considered in the settlement of the loss when covered.

**W.6** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must not discard damaged property without our consent unless retention is not reasonably possible.

**W.7** An insured must provide records establishing ownership of the property for which payment is sought. Such records may include receipts, invoices, photographs, leases, or other reliable evidence.

**W.8** An insured must provide information concerning the age, condition, use, and value of damaged property before the loss. We may consider available evidence concerning those matters in determining the amount of loss.

**W.9** An insured must separate damaged property from property that was not damaged when reasonably possible. We may require an insured to identify property claimed as damaged.

**W.10** We may consider the condition of property immediately before the loss. We may also consider any remaining value of damaged property after the loss.

**W.11** We may take possession of damaged property for inspection, evaluation, or disposition after payment. Our exercise of that right does not constitute acceptance of property that is not covered.

**W.12** We may obtain estimates, invoices, reports, or other information concerning the amount of loss. An insured must provide available estimates or records relating to the damaged property.

**W.13** We may investigate the cause of loss and the extent of damage. An insured must assist us in obtaining information reasonably needed for that investigation.

**W.14** We determine the amount of loss without regard to sentimental, personal, or emotional value. We do not include value that cannot be supported by reliable evidence.

**W.15** We do not pay for loss to property that an insured cannot establish was damaged by a covered water backup loss. We do not pay for loss that is excluded or otherwise not covered.

**W.16** An insured must identify any property belonging to another person for which payment is sought. We may require evidence of the insured's responsibility for that property.

**W.17** We may pay an insured or any person legally entitled to receive payment for covered property. Payment to a person legally entitled to receive payment discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.18** We may settle a loss by payment, repair, restoration, or replacement, as permitted by applicable law. Our election does not increase the amount otherwise payable for the loss.

**W.20** The "special assessment limit", "fire department service charge limit", "jewelry theft sublimit", and "business property sublimit" remain applicable to losses within their respective coverage. Loss settlement does not alter the application of those limits.

**W.21** We apply any applicable "deductible" to the amount otherwise payable for covered loss. The deductible does not reduce our duty to investigate a reported loss.

**W.22** We do not pay more than the applicable limit of liability for a covered loss. Payments made under this endorsement reduce the amount remaining available under the applicable limit.

**W.23** We do not pay for the same loss more than once. Payment under any other insurance or source of recovery may affect the amount we pay.

**W.24** An insured must notify us of any other insurance that may apply to the loss. We may obtain information concerning payment made or available under that insurance.

**W.25** An insured must notify us of any recovery received from a person responsible for the loss. We may consider that recovery in determining the amount payable.

**W.26** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We are not required to take possession of damaged property.

**W.27** We may require an insured to submit a signed statement concerning the loss. The statement must contain information reasonably requested by us.

**W.28** An insured must provide records supporting expenses claimed in connection with covered loss. We may require documentation showing that the expenses were incurred.

**W.29** We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath concerning the loss when permitted by applicable law. An insured must produce requested records in connection with that examination.

**W.30** We may require an "appraisal" when the amount of loss remains disputed. Appraisal does not determine whether coverage applies.

**W.31** An insured must participate in appraisal when required under this provision. An insured must cooperate in selecting an appraiser and providing relevant information.

**W.37** We may recover amounts we paid from any person responsible for the loss. Any recovery is handled in accordance with applicable law and the terms of this policy.

## W.8 — Definitions

"Water Backup" means water or waterborne material that enters a covered location through a "sewer", "drain", or "sump system". The entry may result from backup, overflow, discharge, or failure of the equipment described in this definition.

"Water" includes liquid in any state that is capable of flowing or causing direct physical loss. Waterborne material includes matter carried by water.

"Sewer" means a conduit designed to carry waste water, storm water, or other liquid away from property. A sewer may be publicly or privately owned.

"Drain" means a pipe, channel, fixture, or similar means of carrying water away from property. A drain includes a component connected to a plumbing system.

"Sump system" means a sump, sump pump, pit, basin, tank, pipe, valve, switch, alarm, or related equipment used to collect, control, or remove water. A sump system includes equipment located within or outside a building.

"Backup" means the reverse flow of water or waterborne material through a sewer, drain, or sump system. Backup includes a condition caused by blockage, overload, malfunction, or failure.

"Overflow" means water or waterborne material escaping from a sewer, drain, or sump system because the system cannot contain or carry it. Overflow includes water that escapes from an opening, connection, fixture, or receptacle.

"Discharge" means the release of water or waterborne material from a sump system. Discharge includes release caused by mechanical, electrical, or operational malfunction.

"Failure" means the inability of a sump system or related equipment to perform its intended function. Failure includes interruption of power, loss of pumping capacity, defect, wear, deterioration, or improper installation.

"Malfunction" means an unintended condition affecting the operation of a sewer, drain, or sump system. Malfunction includes a condition that prevents normal flow, collection, pumping, or discharge of water.

"Blockage" means an obstruction that restricts or prevents the intended flow of water or waterborne material. Blockage includes material within a sewer, drain, pipe, trap, valve, or related component.

"Plumbing system" means pipes, fixtures, appliances, valves, drains, and related equipment used to supply, distribute, collect, or remove water. A plumbing system includes equipment permanently connected to the building.

"Related equipment" means equipment that is connected to, serves, controls, monitors, or supports a sewer, drain, or sump system. Related equipment includes wiring, switches, alarms, check valves, and discharge pipes.

"Check valve" means a valve intended to permit flow in a particular direction and restrict reverse flow. A check valve includes a backwater valve.

"Backwater valve" means a device intended to prevent water or waterborne material from flowing backward through a sewer or drain. A backwater valve may be manually operated or automatically operated.

"Power interruption" means a loss, reduction, or disruption of electrical power necessary for a sump system to operate. Power interruption includes interruption originating on or away from the insured location.

"Mechanical breakdown" means physical failure of moving parts or operating components of a sump system. Mechanical breakdown includes failure resulting from wear, deterioration, or defect.

"Direct physical loss" means accidental physical loss of or physical damage to tangible property. Direct physical loss does not include loss of use unless the property has sustained physical damage.

"Property damage" means direct physical loss of or physical damage to property. Property damage includes resulting loss caused by water or waterborne material.

"Covered property" means property for which coverage applies under your policy. Covered property does not include property for which coverage is excluded or limited.

"Insured location" means a location shown in your policy as an insured location. Insured location includes property used in connection with that location when coverage applies.

"Residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and that is shown in your policy. Residence premises includes structures and property used in connection with the dwelling when coverage applies.

"Building" means a structure, including its permanently installed fixtures and equipment. Building includes enclosed areas forming part of the structure.

"Premises" means the land and buildings at an insured location. Premises includes areas used for access to or from the insured location.

"Surface water" means water on the surface of the ground, including water that flows, spreads, pools, or accumulates on the ground. Surface water does not become water from a sewer, drain, or sump system merely because it later enters that system.

"Ground water" means water below the surface of the ground. Ground water includes water that seeps, flows, or rises through soil or rock.

"Flood" means a general and temporary condition of partial or complete inundation of land by water. Flood includes water arising from overflow of inland or tidal waters, unusual accumulation of surface water, or mudflow.

"Storm water" means rainwater, melting snow, or other precipitation that flows or accumulates outside a building. Storm water may enter a sewer, drain, or sump system.

"Waste water" means water containing sewage, waste, contaminants, or other matter carried from a building or premises. Waste water includes water conveyed through a sanitary sewer.

"Sewage" means waste matter carried in water through a sewer or drain. Sewage includes waste water from plumbing fixtures.

"Waterborne material" means debris, sediment, sewage, waste, or other matter transported by water. Waterborne material includes material deposited after water recedes.

"Accidental" means sudden, unintended, and unexpected from the standpoint of an insured. Accidental does not include an act or condition that an insured intended to cause.
