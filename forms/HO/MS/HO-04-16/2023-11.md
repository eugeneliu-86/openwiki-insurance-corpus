## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement forms part of the insurance agreement between you and us. It changes
the insurance provided for loss involving the enforcement of an ordinance or law.

We apply this endorsement only when an ordinance or law affects property because of a
covered loss. All provisions of this endorsement are subject to its terms and
conditions.

“Ordinance or law” means a law, ordinance, regulation, code, or governmental requirement
that regulates the demolition, repair, reconstruction, replacement, or use of property.
It includes a requirement imposed by a governmental authority having jurisdiction.

“Enforcement” means the application of an ordinance or law by a governmental authority.
Enforcement may include an order, notice, permit requirement, inspection requirement, or
similar governmental action.

“Covered property” means property to which insurance applies under this endorsement.
Property is covered only to the extent that it is affected by a covered loss.

“Covered loss” means direct physical loss to covered property for which insurance
applies. A covered loss does not include loss that is excluded or otherwise not covered.

We do not cover loss caused solely by the enforcement of an ordinance or law. We cover
only the loss that results when enforcement applies because of a covered loss.

An ordinance or law may affect the damaged portion of covered property. It may also
affect an undamaged portion of covered property when enforcement requires related work.

We will determine coverage under this endorsement based on the requirements that apply
at the time of loss. We may consider applicable governmental orders, permit
requirements, and construction standards.

An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve covered property after a covered loss.
An insured must protect covered property from further damage when reasonably possible.

An insured must notify us of an ordinance or law that may affect a covered loss. An
insured must provide us with information concerning governmental requirements that
relate to the loss.

An insured must cooperate with us in obtaining records, notices, orders, permits, and
similar information relevant to the loss. We may inspect covered property and review
plans, estimates, and governmental communications.

We may require evidence that enforcement of an ordinance or law applies to covered
property. We may require evidence that the required work results from a covered loss.

We will not pay for work that is not required by an applicable ordinance or law. We will
not pay for costs that are unrelated to a covered loss.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with another provision of the insurance
agreement, this endorsement controls with respect to Ordinance or Law Coverage. All
other provisions remain unchanged.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide "ordinance or law coverage" for a covered loss to covered property
when the loss results in the enforcement of an applicable ordinance or law. This
coverage applies only as stated in this endorsement.

**W.2** "Applicable ordinance or law" means a building, zoning, land use, or similar
governmental requirement that regulates repair, construction, demolition, occupancy, or
use of property. The requirement must be in force and enforced by a governmental
authority having jurisdiction.

**W.3** We cover loss caused by the enforcement of an applicable ordinance or law only
when the enforcement results from direct physical loss to property caused by a peril
covered by the policy. The direct physical loss must occur during the policy period.

**W.4** "Covered building" means a building or structure insured under the policy for
which a covered loss has occurred. A covered building includes permanently attached
structures when they are insured under the policy.

**W.5** We cover the loss in value of the "undamaged portion" of a covered building when
the applicable ordinance or law requires that portion to be demolished. The requirement
must result directly from a covered loss to the covered building.

**W.6** "Undamaged portion" means the part of a covered building that did not sustain
direct physical loss from the covered cause of loss. It does not include property that
was damaged before the covered loss.

**W.7** We cover reasonable "demolition cost" when demolition of damaged property or the
undamaged portion of a covered building is required by an applicable ordinance or law.
Demolition cost includes necessary debris removal resulting from the required
demolition.

**W.9** For a claim involving a residence premises, the most we will pay is stated inthe

**W.10** For a claim involving a unit used as a residence, the most we will pay is

**W.11** We cover the "increased cost of construction" required to repair or replace
covered property damaged by a covered loss. The increased cost must result directly from
compliance with an applicable ordinance or law.

**W.12** "Increased cost of construction" means the additional cost to repair or replace
covered property so that the repaired or replaced property complies with an applicable
ordinance or law. It does not include the cost of improvements that are not required by
the applicable ordinance or law.

**W.18** We do not cover the cost of correcting a condition that existed before the

repair, replacement, or demolition.

**W.19** We do not cover loss resulting from a delay in repair, replacement, demolition,

variance, easement, dedication, or similar land use approval.

**W.23** We do not cover the cost of bringing property into compliance with
accessibility, energy efficiency, environmental, historic preservation, or similar
requirements unless the requirement results directly from a covered loss. The
requirement must apply to repair, replacement, or demolition of covered property.

**W.24** We do not cover the cost of any betterment that exceeds the work required by
the applicable ordinance or law. A betterment includes a change in design, materials,
capacity, layout, utility, or function that is not required for compliance.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We provide "ordinance or law coverage" subject to the terms of this provision.
The most we will pay is twenty-five percent; except that this limit applies only to
covered loss involving enforcement of an ordinance or law. We determine the amount
payable under this provision after applying all other applicable terms.

**W.2** This coverage applies to "Coverage A" property when a covered loss causes an
ordinance or law to apply to that property. We consider the property insured under
Coverage A only to the extent provided by this policy. We do not expand coverage for
property that is not otherwise insured.

**W.3** We cover the increased cost to repair or replace damaged property when an
ordinance or law requires that cost. The requirement must result from a covered loss. We
do not cover an increased cost that is unrelated to the covered loss.

**W.4** We cover the cost to demolish and clear the undamaged portion of property when

**W.5** We cover the cost to repair, rebuild, or replace covered property as required by

**W.6** We apply the limit of liability for ordinance or law coverage to the total of
covered costs payable under this provision. The limit applies regardless of the number

because more than one requirement applies.

## W.8 — Definitions

"Ordinance or law" means a statute, ordinance, regulation, code, rule, order, or other
legal requirement. It includes a requirement imposed by a governmental authority having
jurisdiction over a building.

"Governmental authority" means a public body, agency, board, department, or officer
authorized to enforce an ordinance or law. It includes an authority acting through a
person authorized to issue or enforce a requirement.

"Enforcement" means the application of an ordinance or law by a governmental authority.
Enforcement includes a requirement that results from repair, rebuilding, demolition,
removal, or continued use of property.

"Building code" means an ordinance or law regulating the design, construction, repair,
alteration, occupancy, or use of a building. A building code includes a requirement
concerning materials, methods, systems, or safety features.

"Covered building" means a building to which this coverage applies. It includes the
building property for which we provide coverage for direct physical loss.

"Building" means a structure described as covered property. It includes permanently
installed fixtures and equipment serving the structure.

"Structure" means an assembly of materials forming a building or other construction. It
does not include land or property that is not attached to the structure.

"Premises" means the location where a covered building is situated. It includes the land
immediately surrounding the covered building when that land is used in connection with
the building.

"Direct physical loss" means accidental physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of
property. It does not include an economic loss that is not caused by physical injury,
destruction, or loss.

"Covered loss" means a direct physical loss for which coverage is provided. A covered
loss does not include a loss excluded from coverage.

"Damaged part" means the portion of a covered building that sustains direct physical
loss. It includes property that must be repaired or replaced because of that loss.

"Undamaged part" means the portion of a covered building that does not sustain direct
physical loss. It includes property that is required to be altered, removed, or replaced
because of enforcement of an ordinance or law.

"Repair" means work performed to restore damaged property to its condition immediately
before direct physical loss. Repair includes replacement of damaged materials when
restoration cannot reasonably be completed otherwise.
© 2023 Sample Mutual Insurance Company. Includes copyrighted material of Insurance Services Office, Inc., with its permission.
HO-04-16 · Edition 2023-11 · Page 35 of 36

"Reconstruction" means rebuilding a covered building after direct physical loss.
Reconstruction includes work necessary to restore the building for occupancy or use.

"Demolition" means the tearing down, dismantling, or destruction of a building or part

"Removal" means the taking away of debris, building materials, or other property from
the premises. Removal includes handling and disposal required by a governmental

"Increased cost" means an additional expense resulting from enforcement of an ordinance
or law. It does not include an expense that would have been incurred without the
enforcement.

"Cost to comply" means the expense required to meet an ordinance or law. It includes the
cost of labor, materials, and services necessary to perform required work.

"Required work" means repair, reconstruction, demolition, removal, alteration, or other
work mandated by a governmental authority. Required work must arise from enforcement of
an ordinance or law.

"Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace property with material of like
kind and quality. Replacement cost includes reasonable labor and installation expenses.

"Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss. Actual cash
value reflects depreciation and the condition of the property.

"Depreciation" means a reduction in value caused by age, wear, deterioration, or
obsolescence. Depreciation does not include an increase in value caused by a required
improvement.

"Improvement" means a change that increases the value, usefulness, efficiency, safety,
or capacity of property. An improvement includes work required solely to meet an
ordinance or law.

"Alteration" means a change to the design, arrangement, construction, or use of a
building. Alteration includes installation of property required by an ordinance or law.

"Upgrade" means an alteration that brings property into compliance with an ordinance or
law. An upgrade includes a change required because existing property does not meet
current requirements.

"Existing building" means a building that was constructed before enforcement of an
applicable ordinance or law. It includes additions and alterations that existed before
the covered loss.

"Replacement building" means a building constructed to replace a covered building
following direct physical loss. It includes a building rebuilt at the premises or at
another location.

authority. Code-required work must be applicable to the covered building and the covered

"Applicable ordinance or law" means an ordinance or law that governs the covered
building, the premises, or the required work. It must be in effect when the covered loss
occurs or when enforcement begins.

"Loss of use" means the inability to occupy or use property because of direct physical
loss or required work. It includes a reduction in the normal use of the property.

"Occupancy" means the use of a building by persons for residential or other permitted
purposes. Occupancy includes the right to enter and remain in the building.

"Vacant" means substantially empty of the customary contents necessary for normal
occupancy. A building is not vacant solely because occupants are temporarily absent.

"Debris" means remaining materials from damaged, demolished, or removed property. Debris
includes materials that cannot reasonably be used in repair or reconstruction.

"Property" means tangible property that is capable of being damaged, repaired, removed,
or replaced. Property does not include an ordinance or law, a permit, or a governmental
authorization.

"Permit" means written authorization issued by a governmental authority for required
work. A permit includes an approval, certificate, or inspection required to perform that
work.

"Inspection" means an examination by a governmental authority to determine compliance
with an ordinance or law. Inspection includes a reinspection required before occupancy
or use.

"Notice" means a written communication from a governmental authority concerning
enforcement of an ordinance or law. Notice includes an order, citation, directive, or
similar requirement.

"Order" means a command issued by a governmental authority that requires action
concerning a covered building. An order includes a requirement to repair, alter, remove,
demolish, or refrain from using property.
