# HO 04 16 Ordinance or Law Coverage (Edition 2018-09)
> SUPERSEDED by HO 04 16 Ordinance or Law Coverage (Edition 2023-11) for policies effective on or after 2023-11-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

**W.1** This endorsement modifies the insurance provided by this policy. The provisions of this endorsement apply only to loss arising from the enforcement of an "ordinance or law".

**W.2** An ordinance or law may regulate the construction, repair, renovation, remodeling, occupancy, demolition, or removal of property. An ordinance or law may also require property to be brought into compliance with applicable building requirements.

**W.3** We provide the coverage described in this endorsement subject to all policy provisions that are not changed by this endorsement. If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a policy provision, this endorsement applies.

**W.4** This endorsement applies only when an insured suffers a direct physical loss to covered property. The loss must be caused by a peril insured against under this policy.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused solely by the enforcement of an ordinance or law when no direct physical loss to covered property has occurred. We do not cover costs incurred solely to comply with an ordinance or law in the absence of such loss.

**W.6** An insured must comply with all applicable ordinances or laws affecting the damaged property. An insured must also take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage.

**W.7** We will determine the amount of loss in accordance with the applicable loss settlement provisions of this policy. The application of an ordinance or law does not increase the amount payable for property not covered by this policy.

**W.8** Coverage under this endorsement applies only to the extent that the ordinance or law is in force and applicable to the damaged property. An insured must establish that the ordinance or law requires the claimed cost or expense.

**W.9** We do not cover any cost that results from an insured's voluntary decision to improve, alter, enlarge, or relocate property. We do not cover any cost that is not required by an applicable ordinance or law.

**W.10** We do not cover the cost of correcting conditions that existed before the direct physical loss. This exclusion applies whether or not those conditions are discovered during repair or reconstruction.

**W.11** We do not cover loss arising from delay in repairing, rebuilding, or replacing damaged property. We do not cover increased costs resulting from an insured's failure to proceed with reasonable diligence.

**W.12** An insured must provide us with information reasonably necessary to determine the applicability of an ordinance or law. Such information may include governmental orders, permits, notices, plans, specifications, and cost estimates.

**W.13** An insured must notify us promptly of any requirement imposed by a governmental authority that may affect the amount of loss. An insured must not incur covered costs without our consent unless immediate action is necessary to protect property from further damage.

**W.14** We may inspect the damaged property and review the applicable ordinance or law. We may require reasonable documentation of the cost to repair, rebuild, demolish, or remove damaged property.

**W.15** We do not cover fines, penalties, assessments, or charges imposed upon an insured for violation of an ordinance or law. We do not cover costs arising from an insured's failure to comply with an ordinance or law before the loss.

**W.16** We do not cover costs associated with the enforcement of an ordinance or law that applies to undamaged property. This exclusion applies unless coverage for such costs is specifically provided by this endorsement.

**W.17** Payment under this endorsement does not require us to repair, rebuild, replace, demolish, or remove property. An insured remains responsible for deciding whether to undertake any repair, reconstruction, demolition, or removal.

**W.18** We may pay for covered loss as the work progresses or after the work is completed, subject to the applicable policy conditions. An insured must provide evidence of incurred covered costs when we request it.

**W.19** Any payment under this endorsement is subject to the applicable deductibles, conditions, exclusions, and limits of insurance. This endorsement does not broaden coverage for any loss otherwise excluded by this policy.

**W.20** The duties and conditions applicable to an insured under this policy also apply to this endorsement. No insured may recover more than the amount of covered loss sustained.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide "ordinance or law coverage" for loss arising from the enforcement of an ordinance or law described in this section. This coverage applies only when the enforcement results from a "covered direct physical loss" to a covered building.

**W.2** "Covered direct physical loss" means direct physical loss caused by a peril for which we provide coverage under this insurance. The ordinance or law enforcement must result from that loss.

**W.3** We will pay for the loss to the undamaged part of a covered building when the enforcement of an ordinance or law requires demolition of that part. The required demolition must result from a covered direct physical loss.

**W.4** We will pay the cost to demolish and clear the site of the damaged and undamaged parts of a covered building when demolition is required by an ordinance or law. The demolition requirement must result from a covered direct physical loss.

**W.5** We will pay the increased cost to repair, rebuild, or construct a covered building when the increased cost is required by an ordinance or law. The ordinance or law must be in force and applicable to the covered building at the time of the covered direct physical loss.

**W.6** Increased cost means the necessary cost of construction that exceeds the cost to repair or replace the damaged building without the ordinance or law requirement. We will determine that cost by comparing compliant construction with otherwise comparable construction.

**W.7** We will pay only for an ordinance or law requirement that applies to the part of the building affected by the covered direct physical loss. We will not extend coverage merely because an ordinance or law could apply to another part of the building.

**W.8** We will pay for required construction changes only when the responsible governmental authority enforces the ordinance or law. An insured must provide evidence of the enforcement when we request it.

**W.9** We will pay for required demolition only when demolition is ordered or otherwise required by the responsible governmental authority. An insured must not voluntarily demolish property before we have an opportunity to inspect it, unless immediate action is necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.10** We will pay covered demolition expense only for demolition that is necessary to comply with the enforced ordinance or law. We do not cover demolition that is performed for convenience, modernization, or a change in use.

**W.11** We will pay covered debris removal expense when it is necessary to complete demolition required by an enforced ordinance or law. We do not cover expense to remove property that was not damaged or required to be demolished.

**W.12** We will pay increased construction cost only for work necessary to comply with the enforced ordinance or law. We do not cover cost attributable to optional design, upgraded materials, or enhanced finishes.

**W.13** We will pay for construction required to restore the covered building to a condition permitted by the enforced ordinance or law. We do not cover cost to construct improvements unrelated to compliance with that ordinance or law.

**W.14** We will pay for required changes to foundations, framing, walls, roofs, wiring, plumbing, heating, cooling, and permanent fixtures when those changes are necessary to comply with the enforced ordinance or law. The changes must be directly related to repair or reconstruction of the covered building.

**W.15** We will pay for required accessibility features when they are necessary to comply with an enforced ordinance or law. We do not cover accessibility features that are not required for the covered repair or reconstruction.

**W.16** We will pay for required fire protection, safety, or structural features when they are necessary to comply with an enforced ordinance or law. The required features must relate to the covered building.

**W.17** We will pay for required utility, sanitation, drainage, or energy-conservation features when they are necessary to comply with an enforced ordinance or law. We do not cover work undertaken solely to reduce future operating expense.

**W.18** We will pay for permits, plan review, inspection, and similar governmental charges when they are necessary for covered demolition or covered construction. The charges must arise directly from compliance with the enforced ordinance or law.

**W.19** We will pay for reasonable architectural, engineering, or design services when those services are necessary to complete covered construction. The services must be required because of the enforced ordinance or law.

**W.20** We do not cover a loss caused solely by the enforcement of an ordinance or law when no covered direct physical loss has occurred. An ordinance or law requirement does not itself constitute direct physical loss.

**W.21** We do not cover loss resulting from an ordinance or law that regulates land, zoning, lot size, building location, occupancy, or use of land. This exclusion applies even when the requirement affects a covered building.

**W.22** We do not cover loss resulting from an ordinance or law that requires testing, monitoring, cleaning, detoxifying, removal, containment, treatment, or disposal of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the requirement follows a covered direct physical loss or another event.

**W.23** "Pollutants" means any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or other irritant or contaminant, including smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, waste, and contaminated material. Waste includes material to be recycled, reclaimed, reconditioned, or disposed of.

**W.24** We do not cover loss resulting from an ordinance or law that requires you to repair, replace, or restore property because of its age, condition, obsolescence, or prior damage. This exclusion applies unless the requirement results directly from a covered direct physical loss.

**W.25** We do not cover cost to correct a condition that existed before the covered direct physical loss. This exclusion applies even when the condition is discovered during covered repair, demolition, or construction.

**W.26** We do not cover cost to correct defective design, faulty workmanship, inadequate maintenance, deterioration, or wear and tear. We do not cover increased construction cost caused by those conditions.

**W.27** We do not cover cost to replace undamaged property merely to achieve uniformity, matching, or aesthetic consistency. We will pay only for work required by the enforced ordinance or law.

**W.28** We do not cover cost caused by a voluntary decision to alter the size, layout, appearance, occupancy, or use of a building. Such a decision does not become covered because construction occurs after a covered direct physical loss.

**W.29** We do not cover cost to construct a building that exceeds the dimensions, capacity, quality, or character of the covered building before the covered direct physical loss. We will pay only the cost required to comply with the enforced ordinance or law.

**W.30** We do not cover cost to construct on land other than the location of the covered building. We may pay covered increased construction cost at another location only when construction at the original location is prohibited by the enforced ordinance or law.

**W.31** When construction at the original location is prohibited, we will pay only the amount we would have paid for covered construction at that location. We do not cover any increased cost caused by selecting another location.

**W.32** We do not cover loss resulting from an ordinance or law that you knew was applicable before the covered direct physical loss and failed to disclose when required by us. An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the applicability of an ordinance or law.

**W.33** An insured must promptly notify us when an ordinance or law may affect demolition, repair, rebuilding, or construction. Notice must include available information concerning the governmental authority and the stated requirement.

**W.34** An insured must preserve notices, orders, citations, permits, plans, and correspondence relating to the ordinance or law. An insured must provide those materials to us when we request them.

**W.35** An insured must allow us to inspect the damaged building before demolition, repair, or reconstruction begins when reasonably possible. An insured must also allow us to inspect the work while it is in progress.

**W.36** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect the property from further damage. Reasonable protective measures do not waive our right to inspect the property or determine covered loss.

**W.37** An insured must obtain required permits and approvals for covered demolition or construction. We do not cover fines, penalties, assessments, or charges imposed because an insured failed to obtain a required permit or approval.

**W.38** An insured must submit records supporting the cost of covered demolition, debris removal, and construction. Supporting records may include contracts, invoices, receipts, plans, permits, and inspection reports.

**W.39** An insured must not incur increased construction cost without reasonable efforts to determine whether the cost is required by the enforced ordinance or law. We may request clarification from the governmental authority or other reliable evidence of the requirement.

**W.40** We will determine covered increased construction cost based on reasonable and necessary compliance work. We may consider customary local construction methods, materials, labor charges, and governmental requirements.

**W.41** We will not pay more for a construction method or material than is reasonably necessary to comply with the enforced ordinance or law. We may pay based on a less costly method or material when that method or material provides compliant construction.

**W.42** We will pay covered loss after you incur the cost, unless we agree otherwise. An insured must provide evidence that the covered demolition or construction has been performed.

**W.43** We may make payment directly to a contractor, supplier, governmental authority, or other person entitled to payment for covered work. Payment to that person does not increase the amount we owe for covered loss.

**W.44** We may withhold payment for increased construction cost until the covered building is repaired, rebuilt, or constructed. An insured must provide evidence that the completed work complies with the enforced ordinance or law.

**W.45** We will not pay for increased construction cost that is not incurred. We will not pay for estimated future compliance work that has not been required or performed.

**W.46** We will not pay for a cost that is recoverable from another source, except to the extent permitted by this insurance. An insured must preserve our rights against persons or organizations responsible for the loss.

**W.47** We may require an insured to assign to us rights of recovery for amounts we pay under this coverage. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.48** We will adjust covered demolition loss separately from covered increased construction cost when necessary. The method of adjustment does not alter the applicable limit of liability.

**W.49** We will determine the amount of loss to the undamaged part of a building based on the value of the property required to be demolished. We will not include value attributable to land.

**W.50** We will determine demolition expense based on the reasonable cost of required demolition and site clearing. We do not cover charges unrelated to required demolition.

**W.51** We will determine increased construction cost based on the difference between compliant repair or reconstruction and otherwise comparable repair or reconstruction. We will not include costs unrelated to the enforced ordinance or law.

**W.52** We do not cover the cost of repairing or replacing property that is not a covered building. We do not cover personal property under this ordinance or law coverage.

**W.53** We do not cover business income, loss of rent, loss of use, loss of market value, delay, or loss of opportunity under this ordinance or law coverage. These items are not demolition cost or increased construction cost.

**W.54** We do not cover a decrease in property value caused by an ordinance or law. We do not cover a decrease in value caused by demolition, reconstruction, or a change in lawful use.

**W.55** We do not cover fines, penalties, punitive charges, or sanctions imposed by a governmental authority. We do not cover costs arising from a violation of law by an insured.

**W.56** We do not cover the cost of complying with an ordinance or law that applies because of an insured's intentional act. This exclusion applies regardless of any concurrent governmental enforcement.

**W.57** We do not cover the cost of complying with an ordinance or law that applies because of neglect after a covered direct physical loss. An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent avoidable increase in loss.

**W.58** We do not cover loss caused by delay when an insured unreasonably delays demolition, repair, rebuilding, or construction. We will consider delays beyond an insured's reasonable control.

**W.59** We do not cover cost caused by a contractor's error, omission, defective work, or failure to perform. This exclusion applies after we pay for covered construction work.

**W.60** We do not cover the cost to repair or replace property damaged by demolition or construction work unless that damage results from a covered direct physical loss. Damage caused solely by the work is not covered under this section.

**W.61** We do not cover cost resulting from contamination, corrosion, rust, decay, settling, cracking, bulging, or expansion unless covered under this insurance apart from ordinance or law coverage. An ordinance or law requirement does not create coverage for an otherwise excluded condition.

**W.62** We do not cover cost resulting from earth movement, flood, surface water, water below the surface of the ground, or another excluded cause of loss. This exclusion applies even when an ordinance or law requires demolition or construction after that cause of loss.

**W.63** We do not cover cost resulting from war, nuclear hazard, intentional loss, or another excluded cause of loss. This exclusion applies even when governmental enforcement follows the excluded cause of loss.

**W.64** We will pay only when the ordinance or law is enforced against the covered building. We do not cover a general recommendation, advisory notice, or anticipated future requirement.

**W.65** We will pay only when the ordinance or law requirement is legally applicable to the covered work. An insured must provide available evidence of legal applicability when we request it.

**W.66** We may rely on written direction from the responsible governmental authority in determining whether demolition or construction is required. We may also consider applicable codes, permits, plans, inspection records, and professional opinions.

**W.67** A disagreement between an insured and a governmental authority concerning an ordinance or law does not require us to contest the authority's position. We may determine covered loss based on the requirement that is enforced.

**W.68** An insured must cooperate with us in obtaining information needed to determine the scope of required demolition or construction. An insured must authorize release of relevant records when permitted by law.

**W.69** An insured must notify us before materially changing the scope of covered construction. We do not cover increased cost caused by a material change that is not required by the enforced ordinance or law.

**W.70** We may inspect completed work to determine whether the work corresponds to the covered requirement. Our inspection does not constitute a warranty of the work or a representation that the work complies with law.

**W.71** We may obtain estimates, opinions, or reports concerning the reasonable cost of covered demolition or construction. An insured must cooperate in providing access to the property and relevant records.

**W.72** We will not require an insured to rebuild when demolition or rebuilding is not required by an enforced ordinance or law. We will not pay increased construction cost unless the cost is required and incurred.

**W.73** We will not pay for replacement property that is not reasonably comparable to the covered building. We will not pay for features that exceed those required for compliance with the enforced ordinance or law.

**W.74** We will apply this coverage only to loss that occurs during the period of this insurance. We do not cover loss arising from an ordinance or law requirement triggered by damage occurring before that period.

**W.75** We will apply all applicable exclusions, conditions, and limits of liability to this ordinance or law coverage. This coverage does not broaden coverage for a cause of loss that is otherwise excluded.

**W.76** If an insured is entitled to payment under another coverage for the same demolition or construction cost, we will not pay that cost more than once. We will determine the amount payable without duplication.

**W.77** If any provision of this section conflicts with a requirement of law that cannot be waived, we will apply the provision to the extent permitted by law. The remaining provisions will continue to apply.

**W.78** We will provide payment under this ordinance or law coverage only for covered loss established in accordance with this insurance. An insured must comply with the duties stated in this section as a condition to payment.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We provide Ordinance or Law coverage for the increased costs described in this provision when a covered loss causes enforcement of an ordinance or law. We apply this coverage only to property insured under Coverage A. Except that, this coverage does not change the property covered under the policy.

**W.2** The most we will pay for Ordinance or Law coverage is ten percent of Coverage A. Subject to the applicable provisions of this coverage, our payment is limited to the amount necessary to comply with the enforced ordinance or law. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**W.3** We determine the limit of liability for Ordinance or Law coverage when we adjust the covered loss. The limit applies to the total of all amounts payable under this coverage. Except that, the limit does not increase the amount payable for the direct physical loss.

**W.4** We cover the increased cost to repair or replace the damaged portion of a building when an ordinance or law requires the use of materials or construction methods not required for the undamaged property. The increased cost must result from enforcement of the ordinance or law. Unless the ordinance or law applies to the damaged portion, we do not cover the increased cost under this provision.

**W.5** We cover the cost to demolish and clear the undamaged portion of a building when an ordinance or law requires that portion to be demolished. The demolition requirement must result from damage to another portion of the same building. Except that, we do not cover voluntary demolition that is not required by an ordinance or law.

**W.6** We cover the increased cost to repair, rebuild, or reconstruct the undamaged portion of a building when an ordinance or law requires the undamaged portion to be demolished. The requirement must apply to the building because of the covered loss. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the increased cost attributable to the ordinance or law.

**W.7** We cover the increased cost of construction required by an ordinance or law that regulates the repair or replacement of damaged property. The regulation must be in effect at the time of the covered loss. Unless the regulation is enforced because of the covered loss, we do not cover the increased cost.

**W.8** We cover the cost of making a damaged building comply with an ordinance or law when the building is repaired or replaced. The compliance work must be required by an ordinance or law. Except that, we do not cover improvements made solely for your convenience.

**W.9** We cover the cost of making undamaged portions of a building comply with an ordinance or law when compliance is required as a condition of repairing the damaged portions. The condition must be imposed by a governmental authority having jurisdiction. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the cost required for compliance.

**W.10** We cover the increased cost caused by an ordinance or law that requires accessibility features in a repaired or replaced building. The accessibility features must be required for the building because of the covered loss. Unless the requirement is imposed by an ordinance or law, we do not cover the cost under this provision.

**W.11** We cover the increased cost caused by an ordinance or law that requires energy efficiency features in a repaired or replaced building. The features must be required in connection with the repair or replacement of covered property. Except that, we do not cover elective features that are not required for compliance.

**W.12** We cover the increased cost caused by an ordinance or law that requires changes to plumbing, electrical, heating, ventilation, or air-conditioning systems. The changes must be required in connection with the repair or replacement of covered property. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the increased cost of required changes.

**W.13** We cover the increased cost caused by an ordinance or law that requires changes to structural components of a building. The changes must be required because of the covered loss. Unless the changes are required for compliance, we do not cover the cost of the changes.

**W.14** We cover the increased cost caused by an ordinance or law that requires a building to be relocated on its premises. The relocation must be required in connection with the repair or replacement of covered property. Except that, we do not cover relocation undertaken solely to obtain a better location.

**W.15** We cover the increased cost caused by an ordinance or law that requires a building to be rebuilt at a different location. The requirement must be imposed by a governmental authority having jurisdiction. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the increased cost resulting from that requirement.

**W.16** We cover the increased cost of complying with an ordinance or law that requires a building to be elevated, reinforced, or otherwise modified. The modification must be required for the repaired or replaced building. Unless the modification is required for compliance, we do not cover its cost.

**W.17** We cover the increased cost of complying with an ordinance or law that requires a damaged building to be replaced rather than repaired. The requirement must apply because the building is damaged by a covered loss. Except that, we do not cover the cost of replacement that exceeds the cost required by the ordinance or law.

**W.18** We cover the cost of demolishing an undamaged portion of a building when an ordinance or law requires demolition because the damaged portion cannot be repaired without that demolition. The demolition must be required by a governmental authority having jurisdiction. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the cost required for demolition and removal.

**W.19** We cover the cost of removing debris from the undamaged portion of a building when demolition is required by an ordinance or law. The debris removal must result from demolition covered under this provision. Unless the demolition is required by an ordinance or law, we do not cover the related debris removal under this provision.

**W.20** We cover the increased cost of debris removal when an ordinance or law requires special handling, treatment, or disposal of debris from covered property. The special handling, treatment, or disposal must be required by an ordinance or law. Except that, we do not cover costs arising solely from your preferred method of disposal.

**W.21** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires materials with particular fire-resistance characteristics. The requirement must apply to the repair or replacement. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the increased cost of the required materials.

**W.22** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires fire-protection equipment or systems. The equipment or systems must be required for compliance with the ordinance or law. Unless the requirement applies to the repair or replacement, we do not cover the cost.

**W.23** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires storm-resistant construction. The requirement must apply because of the covered loss. Except that, we do not cover construction selected voluntarily in excess of the requirement.

**W.24** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires flood-resistant construction. The requirement must apply to the property because of the covered loss. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the increased cost required for compliance.

**W.25** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires specialized foundations or anchoring. The requirement must apply to the repaired or replaced property. Unless the requirement results from enforcement of an ordinance or law, we do not cover the increased cost.

**W.26** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires changes to entrances, exits, or egress routes. The changes must be required for compliance with the ordinance or law. Except that, we do not cover changes made solely to improve convenience or appearance.

**W.27** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires changes to sanitation facilities. The changes must be required in connection with the covered loss. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the increased cost of required changes.

**W.28** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires environmental safeguards. The safeguards must be required for the repaired or replaced property. Unless the safeguards are required by an ordinance or law, we do not cover their cost.

**W.29** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires the use of specified construction documents, inspections, or approvals. The documents, inspections, or approvals must be required for compliance. Except that, we do not cover ordinary construction administration costs that would have been incurred without the ordinance or law.

**W.30** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires testing or certification of materials or systems. The testing or certification must be required for compliance. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the increased cost attributable to that requirement.

**W.31** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires temporary protection during construction. The protection must be required because of the repair or replacement. Unless the protection is required by an ordinance or law, we do not cover its cost under this coverage.

**W.32** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires a particular construction sequence. The sequence must be required for compliance with the ordinance or law. Except that, we do not cover delays or inefficiencies arising from your chosen construction sequence.

**W.33** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires a particular method of connection to public utilities. The connection requirement must apply to the repaired or replaced property. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the increased cost caused by the requirement.

**W.34** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires a particular method of water, sewer, or drainage control. The method must be required for compliance with the ordinance or law. Unless the requirement applies to the covered loss, we do not cover the increased cost.

**W.35** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires removal of hazardous materials encountered during construction. The removal must be required in connection with the covered loss. Except that, we do not cover the cost of hazardous materials that were not encountered as a result of the covered loss.

**W.36** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires preservation or replacement of a designated architectural feature. The requirement must apply to the building because of the covered loss. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay only the increased cost required for compliance.

**W.37** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires a change in the height, size, or configuration of the building. The change must be required for compliance. Unless the change is required by an ordinance or law, we do not cover the cost of the change.

**W.38** We cover the increased cost of repairing or replacing covered property when an ordinance or law requires a building to satisfy current construction standards. The standards must be enforced because of the covered loss. Except that, we do not cover costs resulting from a standard that is not applicable to the repair or replacement.

**W.39** We cover the cost of complying with an ordinance or law only when you repair, rebuild, or replace the damaged property. You must notify us of the intended repair, rebuilding, or replacement when requested. Subject to the applicable limit of liability, we pay no cost that is not incurred for compliance with an ordinance or law.

**W.40** We determine the amount payable under this coverage after applying all applicable policy provisions. You must provide us with the ordinance or law, notices, estimates, invoices, and other information we reasonably request. Except that, we do not pay an amount that cannot be supported by records showing that the cost was incurred for compliance.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The deductible applies to covered loss under this policy. Except that, the deductible applies only to loss for which coverage is otherwise provided. The deductible does not create coverage.

**W.2** We subtract the deductible from the amount of covered loss. Subject to, we determine the amount of covered loss before applying the deductible. We do not subtract the deductible from amounts that are not part of covered loss.

**W.3** You must bear the deductible as your share of covered loss. Except that, you bear the deductible only to the extent that covered loss remains after application of all applicable limitations. We pay the remaining covered loss, subject to the terms of this policy.

**W.4** The deductible applies to property covered under this policy. Unless, the property is not insured for the loss, the deductible does not apply to that property. We do not pay for property that is excluded or otherwise not covered.

**W.5** We apply the deductible to covered loss caused by a covered peril. Provided that, the peril must cause direct physical loss to covered property. We do not apply the deductible to an amount that does not result from covered loss.

**W.6** The deductible applies regardless of whether covered loss affects one item or several items of covered property. Except that, the deductible does not apply to property that is not involved in the covered loss. We determine the covered loss before applying the deductible.

**W.7** We apply the deductible after determining whether the loss is covered. Subject to, we do not apply the deductible to loss that is excluded by this policy. A deductible does not remove an exclusion.

**W.8** The deductible applies to covered loss resulting from a covered event. Except that, the event must be the cause of the covered loss. We do not apply the deductible to damage that is not caused by a covered event.

**W.9** We apply the deductible to the covered loss that remains after all applicable exclusions are applied. Unless, the loss is not covered, the deductible does not determine whether coverage exists. We determine coverage without increasing or decreasing it because of the deductible.

**W.10** You must pay or absorb the deductible without reimbursement from us. Except that, we may pay an amount that includes the deductible when required to settle a covered claim. You remain responsible for the deductible in determining the amount we owe.

**W.11** The deductible applies to covered loss under the applicable coverage. Subject to, the applicable coverage must insure the property and peril involved in the loss. We do not transfer a deductible from an uncovered loss to a covered loss.

**W.12** We determine the covered loss using the applicable loss settlement provisions. Except that, the deductible is applied after the covered loss is valued under those provisions. We do not use the deductible to determine the condition or value of damaged property.

**W.13** The deductible applies to covered loss whether the property is damaged or destroyed. Unless, the property is not covered for the loss, the deductible applies without regard to the extent of damage. We pay no more than the amount of covered loss remaining after the deductible.

**W.14** We apply the deductible to covered loss that results from a covered cause of loss. Provided that, the cause of loss must be covered at the time the loss occurs. We do not apply the deductible to a cause of loss that is excluded.

**W.15** The deductible applies to covered loss at the location where the covered property is situated. Except that, the location must be insured under this policy. We do not apply the deductible to property at an uninsured location.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" under this endorsement in accordance with the applicable loss settlement provisions. A covered loss must result from the enforcement of an "ordinance or law".

**W.2** We will determine the amount of loss from the reasonable cost to repair, rebuild, replace, or remove the affected property. The work must be necessary because of the covered loss.

**W.3** We will not include costs that are not required by the applicable ordinance or law. An insured must establish that the requirement applies to the damaged property.

**W.4** We will settle loss to a "building" based on the cost of work using materials of like kind and quality. We may consider materials that are reasonably available for the required work.

**W.5** We will not pay for betterment that is not required by an ordinance or law. We will not pay for a change in design, layout, or use that is not required for repair or reconstruction.

**W.6** We will determine the cost of demolition from the reasonable charges necessary to remove the affected portions of the building. The demolition must be required because of the covered loss.

**W.7** For personal property subject to this endorsement, the loss settlement basis is replacement cost.

**W.8** We will determine increased construction cost from the reasonable cost of complying with an ordinance or law. The compliance cost must arise from repair, rebuilding, or replacement following a covered loss.

**W.9** We will not pay for costs attributable to an ordinance or law that was in effect before the covered loss unless the ordinance or law is enforced because of that loss. An insured must provide information supporting the enforcement requirement.

**W.10** We will not pay for loss caused by the enforcement of an ordinance or law when no covered loss has occurred. The ordinance or law must apply because of physical damage caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.11** We will settle the loss based on the property that was damaged by the covered loss. We will not include property that was not affected unless its removal or alteration is required by an ordinance or law.

**W.12** We will determine whether an undamaged portion of a building must be removed from the enforcement action taken by the public authority. An insured must provide notices, orders, and similar records received from that authority.

**W.13** We will not pay for the cost to correct a preexisting condition unless correction is required because of the covered loss. We will not pay for maintenance, wear, deterioration, or defective workmanship.

**W.14** We will not pay for a delay expense unless it is otherwise covered under this endorsement. Delays caused by an insured's failure to proceed with reasonable diligence are not covered.

**W.15** An insured must protect damaged property from further loss after a covered loss. We will consider reasonable emergency measures necessary to preserve property for repair or reconstruction.

**W.16** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. We may inspect the property, the site, and the proposed work before determining the amount of loss.

**W.17** An insured must provide estimates, invoices, contracts, permits, and other records that support the claimed loss. The records must distinguish covered work from work that is not covered.

**W.18** We may obtain estimates from contractors or other qualified persons. We may rely on reasonable estimates in determining the amount we will pay.

**W.19** We will not pay for charges that exceed the reasonable cost of the required work. We may limit payment to the amount necessary to repair, rebuild, replace, or remove the affected property.

**W.20** We will not pay for loss involving a building that is vacant or unoccupied when the vacancy or unoccupancy affects the claimed cost. An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the condition and use of the property.

**W.21** We will not pay for costs incurred to comply with voluntary standards, guidelines, or recommendations. The cost must be required by an ordinance or law that is applicable to the covered loss.

**W.22** We will not pay for expenses associated with contamination unless those expenses are required by an ordinance or law because of a covered loss. We will not pay for testing, monitoring, or remediation that is not necessary for the required work.

**W.23** We will not pay for costs associated with the repair, replacement, or removal of property that is not insured. An insured must show that the property involved is covered property.

**W.24** We will settle a loss involving common property only to the extent that you are legally responsible for that loss. An insured must provide records establishing the applicable responsibility.

**W.25** We will not pay for a charge imposed solely because property is located within a particular jurisdiction. The charge must be directly related to the required repair, reconstruction, replacement, or removal.

**W.26** We will not pay for a fee, penalty, or assessment that is not necessary to comply with an ordinance or law. We will consider permit and inspection charges when they are required for covered work.

**W.27** We will not pay for costs arising from a change in zoning that prevents reconstruction at the location of the covered loss. We will pay only for costs otherwise covered under this endorsement.

**W.28** We will determine replacement cost without deduction for depreciation when replacement cost settlement applies. We will not pay more than the reasonable cost of the covered repair or replacement.

**W.29** We will determine actual cash value by considering depreciation and the property's condition immediately before the covered loss. We will apply actual cash value when replacement cost settlement does not apply.

**W.30** We will not pay for the cost of replacing property with property of greater capacity or utility unless required by an ordinance or law. We will not pay for an upgrade selected by an insured for personal preference.

**W.31** An insured must use reasonable means to obtain required permits and approvals. We may require evidence that the permit or approval relates to the covered loss.

**W.32** We will not pay for work performed without any required permit when the absence of the permit prevents us from determining the covered cost. An insured must cooperate in obtaining records relating to the work.

**W.33** We will not pay for a loss that is based on speculative construction costs. The claimed cost must be supported by completed work, binding obligations, or reliable estimates.

**W.34** We will not pay for costs resulting from the insured's decision to abandon repair or reconstruction. We may settle the covered loss on the basis otherwise applicable to the property.

**W.35** We will not pay for costs incurred to comply with an ordinance or law after property has been sold, transferred, or otherwise disposed of by an insured. The claimed cost must arise while an insured has an insurable interest in the property.

**W.36** We will not pay for any amount that another party is legally obligated to pay. An insured must preserve our rights of recovery against a responsible party.

**W.37** Payment of a covered loss does not increase or alter the "assessment coverage limit". We will apply that limit as provided by the applicable coverage.

**W.38** We do not cover a "fire response charge" under this endorsement. That charge is not included in the amount of loss settled under this endorsement.

**W.39** Loss settlement under this endorsement does not alter the "special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones". We will apply that special limit to property subject to it.

**W.40** Loss settlement under this endorsement does not alter the "home business contents limit". We will apply that limit to property subject to it.

**W.41** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require it. An insured must provide truthful information concerning the loss and the claimed cost.

**W.42** An insured must not knowingly conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to the loss. We may deny coverage when an insured has committed fraud in connection with the loss.

**W.43** We will pay the amount of covered loss after we reach agreement with you, obtain a final judgment, or receive an appraisal award. Payment is subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions of this endorsement.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** "Ordinance or law" means a law, regulation, code, rule, or order of a governmental authority that regulates the construction, repair, demolition, renovation, remodeling, or use of property. Ordinance or law includes a requirement that is legally enforceable.

**W.2** Ordinance or law includes a building code, zoning requirement, land use requirement, or safety requirement. It includes a requirement concerning access, occupancy, energy efficiency, environmental conditions, or structural standards.

**W.3** "Governmental authority" means a public body or official having legal authority to adopt, administer, interpret, or enforce an ordinance or law. A governmental authority includes an agency, department, board, commission, or court acting within its authority.

**W.4** "Building code" means an ordinance or law that establishes standards for the design, construction, alteration, repair, maintenance, occupancy, or use of a building. Building code includes an adopted code and an amendment to an adopted code.

**W.5** "Code requirement" means a provision of an ordinance or law that applies to a covered building. A code requirement may apply because of a loss, repair, demolition, reconstruction, alteration, change in occupancy, or change in use.

**W.6** "Covered building" means a building to which this endorsement applies and for which coverage is provided under this policy. Covered building includes a structure only to the extent it is included in the property for which coverage applies.

**W.7** "Damaged portion" means the part of a covered building that sustains direct physical loss from a covered cause of loss. Damaged portion includes property that must be removed because of that direct physical loss.

**W.8** "Undamaged portion" means the part of a covered building that does not sustain direct physical loss from a covered cause of loss. Undamaged portion does not become a damaged portion solely because an ordinance or law requires its repair, alteration, or removal.

**W.9** "Direct physical loss" means accidental physical injury to tangible property or accidental physical loss of tangible property. Direct physical loss does not include a reduction in value that is not caused by physical injury or physical loss.

**W.10** "Loss" means direct physical loss to covered property caused by a covered cause of loss. Loss includes resulting damage only when that damage is otherwise covered under this policy.

**W.11** "Covered cause of loss" means a peril for which coverage is provided under this policy. A covered cause of loss does not include a peril that is excluded or limited by this policy.

**W.12** "Demolition" means the tearing down, removal, or disposal of a building or part of a building. Demolition includes the removal of debris resulting from the tearing down of property.

**W.13** "Required demolition" means demolition that a governmental authority requires because of an ordinance or law. Required demolition includes demolition of an undamaged portion when the requirement applies to that portion.

**W.14** "Repair" means the work necessary to restore damaged property to a safe and usable condition. Repair includes materials and labor that are reasonably necessary to perform that work.

**W.15** "Replacement" means the construction or acquisition of property to take the place of property that has been damaged or destroyed. Replacement does not include betterment except as required by an applicable ordinance or law.

**W.16** "Reconstruction" means rebuilding a covered building or part of a covered building after a loss. Reconstruction includes work required to comply with an applicable ordinance or law.

**W.17** "Alteration" means a change to the design, arrangement, materials, systems, or use of a building. Alteration includes work that is required to bring property into compliance with an ordinance or law.

**W.18** "Increased cost of construction" means the additional cost to repair, replace, reconstruct, or alter a covered building because of an applicable ordinance or law. Increased cost of construction does not include costs that would have been incurred without the ordinance or law.

**W.19** Increased cost of construction includes the cost of required materials, labor, design, permits, inspections, and testing. It includes reasonable costs directly related to compliance with a code requirement.

**W.20** Increased cost of construction does not include the cost of improving property beyond the requirements of an applicable ordinance or law. It does not include costs resulting from a change in plans that is not required by an ordinance or law.

**W.21** "Compliance" means performance of work required by an applicable ordinance or law. Compliance includes obtaining approvals, permits, and inspections required for the work.

**W.22** "Applicable ordinance or law" means an ordinance or law that is in force and applies to the covered building, the loss, or the work performed after the loss. An ordinance or law is applicable only when it is enforced by a governmental authority having jurisdiction.

**W.23** "Enforced" means that a governmental authority requires compliance with an ordinance or law. Enforced includes a written order, permit condition, inspection requirement, or other official direction requiring compliance.

**W.24** "Order" means a written directive issued by a governmental authority that requires demolition, repair, reconstruction, alteration, removal, or other action. Order includes a directive issued in connection with a permit or inspection.

**W.25** "Permit" means written authorization issued by a governmental authority for construction, repair, demolition, reconstruction, alteration, or occupancy. Permit includes a condition imposed as part of that authorization.

**W.26** "Reasonable cost" means an amount that is customary and appropriate for the work required at the location of the covered building. Reasonable cost includes charges that are necessary to complete the required work.

**W.27** "Necessary cost" means an expense that is directly required to complete covered demolition, repair, replacement, reconstruction, or compliance work. Necessary cost does not include an expense that is avoidable or unrelated to the required work.

**W.28** "Debris" means material remaining after property is damaged, destroyed, demolished, repaired, or reconstructed. Debris includes material that must be removed to allow required work to proceed.

**W.29** "Removal" means the taking away, hauling, handling, or disposal of property or debris. Removal includes work required to prepare a covered building for repair, reconstruction, or demolition.

**W.30** "Building system" means equipment or components that provide electrical, plumbing, heating, cooling, ventilation, fire protection, communication, or similar services to a building. Building system includes permanently installed equipment serving the building.

**W.31** "Structural component" means a part of a building that supports, stabilizes, encloses, or protects the building. Structural component includes foundations, framing, walls, floors, roofs, and permanently attached supports.

**W.32** "Improvement" means an addition, upgrade, enhancement, or change that increases the quality, capacity, utility, or value of property. Improvement does not include work required solely to comply with an applicable ordinance or law.

**W.33** "Betterment" means an improvement that makes property more valuable, more useful, or of better quality than it was immediately before the loss. Betterment does not include a change required by an applicable ordinance or law.

**W.34** "Change in use" means a change in the purpose, occupancy, operation, or manner in which a building is used. Change in use includes a change that causes a different ordinance or law to apply.

**W.35** "Change in occupancy" means a change in the persons, activities, or operations for which a building or part of a building is occupied. Change in occupancy includes a change that requires compliance with an ordinance or law.

**W.36** "Functional replacement" means replacement property that performs the same general function as property damaged by a covered cause of loss. Functional replacement may differ in design, materials, size, or appearance when required by an applicable ordinance or law.

**W.37** "Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, including appropriate deductions for depreciation and physical condition. Actual cash value does not include costs attributable solely to an ordinance or law.
