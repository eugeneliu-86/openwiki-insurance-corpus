# HO 04 16 Ordinance or Law Coverage (Edition 2018-09)

Multistate endorsement. Attaches to HO-3 and modifies Section I — Exclusions D.1.

## O.1 Coverage Provided

We cover the increased cost you incur to repair, rebuild, or demolish the damaged dwelling or other structure because of an ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, or demolition, where that ordinance or law is in force at the time of loss and the loss itself is covered under Section I.

Section I — Exclusions D.1 does not apply to loss covered under this endorsement.

## O.2 Limit

The most we will pay under this endorsement is ten percent of the Coverage A limit of liability shown in the Declarations, unless a higher percentage is shown for this endorsement. This amount is additional insurance and does not reduce the Coverage A or Coverage B limits.

## O.3 Undamaged Portions

We cover the cost to demolish and clear the site of undamaged portions of the dwelling where an ordinance or law requires their removal in order to repair or rebuild the damaged portion.

Where an ordinance or law requires replacement of undamaged roof surfacing in order to repair the damaged portion, that additional replacement is covered under this endorsement. This is the coverage contemplated by HO 23 74 R.6, which excludes that cost from the roof schedule endorsement itself.

## O.4 What Is Not Covered

We do not cover the cost to comply with an ordinance or law requiring the testing for, monitoring of, cleanup of, removal of, or neutralizing of pollutants, contaminants, or hazardous substances, including asbestos and lead, except where that requirement is triggered solely by the covered loss and applies only to the damaged portion.

We do not cover loss in value to the undamaged portion of a structure caused by the enforcement of an ordinance or law.

We do not cover the cost to comply with an ordinance or law where the insured was required to comply before the loss occurred and had not done so.

## O.5 Time Limit

We cover the increased cost only if the repair, rebuilding, or demolition is completed as soon as reasonably possible after the loss, and in no event more than two years after the date of loss unless we agree in writing to extend that period.

## O.6 Loss Settlement

Coverage under this endorsement applies only after the loss to the damaged property itself has been settled under the applicable Section I loss settlement provisions, and only to the increased cost actually incurred.

All other provisions of the policy apply.
