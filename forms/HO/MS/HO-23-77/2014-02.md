# HO 23 77 Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible (Edition 2014-02)
> SUPERSEDED by HO 23 77 Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible (Edition 2022-07) for policies effective on or after 2022-07-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "Endorsement" changes the insurance provided under your "Policy." It applies only when attached to your Policy.

The terms of your Policy apply unless this Endorsement changes them. We will apply this Endorsement as part of your Policy.

If a provision of this Endorsement conflicts with a provision of your Policy, this Endorsement controls. All provisions that do not conflict remain unchanged.

"Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible" means the deductible that applies to a covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. This deductible is part of the amount you are responsible for bearing before we pay for covered loss.

"Covered loss" means direct physical loss for which coverage is provided under your Policy. Coverage remains subject to all applicable Policy terms and conditions.

We will apply the Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible only to a covered loss to which it applies. We will determine the amount of the deductible under the terms of this Endorsement.

The applicable deductible will be applied before we determine the amount we will pay for covered loss. We will not pay that part of covered loss that is subject to the applicable deductible.

This Endorsement does not provide coverage for a loss that is not otherwise covered under your Policy. It does not change any exclusion unless this Endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

An insured must comply with all duties that apply after a loss. Failure to comply with those duties may affect coverage.

You must protect property from further damage after a loss. We do not cover avoidable additional damage caused by your failure to protect the property.

You must provide us with information we reasonably request to investigate a claim. You must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim.

We may inspect damaged property and examine records relating to a claimed loss. You must preserve property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

We may determine whether a loss was caused by windstorm or hail. Our determination will be based on the facts, the damage, and the applicable Policy terms.

The application of this Endorsement does not waive any right we have under your Policy. Your compliance with this Endorsement does not waive any right you have under your Policy.

This Endorsement is not intended to create coverage beyond that provided by your Policy. We will apply its terms according to their plain meaning.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the "Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible" subject to the terms of this endorsement. This deductible applies only to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail.

**W.2** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to property covered under this policy. We apply it whether windstorm, hail, or both contribute to the loss.

**W.3** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies when windstorm or hail is a direct cause of loss. It also applies when windstorm or hail combines with another covered cause of loss.

**W.4** We determine whether the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies from the facts of the loss. The cause of loss must be established from available information.

**W.5** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies separately to each covered loss. We do not combine separate losses to reduce the deductible.

**W.6** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible before determining the amount payable for covered loss. We do not pay the portion of covered loss that is within the deductible.

**W.7** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to loss to covered property. It does not create coverage for property that is not covered.

**W.8** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to loss that is otherwise covered. It does not change any exclusion, limitation, or condition that applies to the loss.

**W.9** We determine the deductible from the applicable limit of liability. The applicable limit of liability is the limit that applies to the damaged property.

**W.10** When more than one limit of liability could apply, we use the limit that applies to the covered property sustaining loss. We do not use a limit that does not apply to that property.

**W.11** We apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss after all applicable coverage terms are applied. We do not apply the deductible to an amount that is not covered.

**W.12** The deductible applies regardless of the extent of damage caused by windstorm or hail. The deductible may equal or exceed the amount of covered loss.

**W.13** When the deductible equals or exceeds the covered loss, we do not make payment for that loss. Your duties under this policy continue to apply.

**W.14** When covered loss exceeds the deductible, we pay the covered amount that remains after application of the deductible. Our payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.15** We do not pay any amount for loss that is excluded. The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible does not affect an exclusion.

**W.16** We do not pay any amount beyond the applicable limit of liability. The deductible does not increase a limit of liability.

**W.17** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to loss caused by wind. It applies whether the wind is accompanied by rain or other weather conditions.

**W.18** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to loss caused by hail. It applies whether hail causes damage directly or through impact with covered property.

**W.19** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible when windstorm causes an opening in covered property. We apply it to covered loss resulting from that opening.

**W.20** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible when hail damages covered property. We apply it to covered loss resulting from that damage.

**W.21** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to direct physical loss to covered property. We do not apply it to a claim without direct physical loss unless this policy provides otherwise.

**W.22** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to loss occurring during a windstorm event. It applies even if the damage becomes apparent after the event ends.

**W.23** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to loss occurring during a hail event. It applies even if the damage becomes apparent after the event ends.

**W.24** We determine the cause of loss from the condition of the property and other relevant evidence. You must cooperate with our investigation of the loss.

**W.25** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable measures to protect property do not change the applicable deductible.

**W.26** An insured must retain damaged property when reasonably necessary for our inspection. Disposal of property must not prevent us from determining the cause or amount of loss.

**W.27** An insured must provide records and information that reasonably relate to the loss. We may require information concerning the damaged property and the circumstances of damage.

**W.28** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as reasonably required. We may inspect the property before repair, replacement, or removal.

**W.29** Repair or replacement of damaged property does not determine whether the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies. We determine applicability from the cause of loss.

**W.30** The cost of temporary repairs is subject to the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible when the cost is part of covered loss. We do not pay for temporary repairs that are not covered.

**W.31** The cost to protect property from further damage is subject to the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible when covered. We do not pay for protection costs that are not covered.

**W.32** We may adjust a loss involving windstorm or hail with other covered causes of loss. The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to the portion of loss to which it applies.

**W.33** When damage results from windstorm or hail and another cause of loss, we determine the covered amount under the policy terms. We apply the deductible to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail.

**W.34** We do not apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to loss that is not caused by windstorm or hail. Another applicable deductible may apply to that loss.

**W.35** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible does not eliminate any other deductible that applies to a separate loss. We apply each deductible according to the cause of loss and applicable coverage.

**W.36** We do not apply more than one deductible to the same covered loss unless this policy requires otherwise. We apply the deductible that applies to the covered cause of loss.

**W.37** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to covered property at the location where the windstorm or hail loss occurs. We determine the applicable limit of liability for that location.

**W.38** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to covered property whether the property is damaged by wind pressure, airborne material, or hail impact. Coverage remains subject to all policy terms.

**W.39** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to damage caused by debris when windstorm is a direct cause of the debris impact. We do not provide coverage for debris damage that is otherwise excluded.

**W.40** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to damage caused by water entering through an opening created by windstorm or hail. Coverage for the water damage remains subject to all policy terms.

**W.41** We do not cover damage caused by water merely because windstorm or hail occurs at the same time. The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible does not create coverage for water damage.

**W.42** We determine the amount of covered loss under the valuation provisions that apply to the property. We then apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible.

**W.43** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies before we make payment for covered loss. It applies before any payment is issued to you or another person entitled to payment.

**W.44** Payment of a claim does not waive the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible for another loss. We determine the applicable deductible for each loss.

**W.45** Our investigation or adjustment of a loss does not waive any policy provision. Our inspection of property does not establish that coverage applies.

**W.46** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning a windstorm or hail loss. We may apply the policy terms based on the information available to us.

**W.47** We may request proof of loss for a claim involving windstorm or hail. An insured must provide requested proof of loss as required by this policy.

**W.48** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to covered loss regardless of whether the loss is reported promptly or later discovered. Your duty to give notice of loss remains unchanged.

**W.49** We do not provide payment for a loss until all applicable policy conditions are met. Application of the deductible does not excuse compliance with those conditions.

**W.50** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to property loss and related covered expense. It does not expand the types of loss or expense that are covered.

**W.51** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to the covered amount of a claim. We do not apply it to an amount payable solely under a provision to which the deductible does not apply.

**W.52** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible remains subject to the terms of this endorsement. If a term of this endorsement conflicts with another policy term, this endorsement applies.

**W.53** We do not cover loss beyond that provided by this policy. The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible does not alter the scope of covered property.

**W.54** An insured must comply with all duties that apply after a loss. Those duties apply to a loss involving windstorm or hail.

**W.55** We will pay covered loss caused by windstorm or hail only after application of the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible. Our payment is subject to all applicable policy terms.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We limit the amount we will pay for a covered "loss" to the applicable "limit of liability" shown in your policy. The limit applies subject to all terms and conditions of this policy. We will not pay more than the amount of covered loss that you sustain.

**W.2** The applicable "limit of insurance" is the most we will pay for covered property under the coverage to which that limit applies. The limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims, or persons with an interest in the property. Payment remains subject to the applicable deductible.

**W.3** Our limit of liability applies to the property and coverage identified in the policy. The limit does not increase because more than one person owns, occupies, or has an interest in the property. We will pay only for property that is covered under the applicable coverage.

**W.4** We determine the amount of covered loss before applying any applicable deductible. We then apply the deductible to the amount otherwise payable under this policy. The amount payable will not exceed the applicable limit of liability.

**W.5** We do not cover loss that exceeds the applicable limit of liability. The limit applies even if the amount of damage is greater than the limit. We do not divide the limit among items of property unless this policy requires that division.

**W.6** We apply the applicable limit of liability to covered property damaged by a covered peril. The limit does not apply to property that is not covered. We do not use the limit to create coverage for a peril or property excluded by this policy.

**W.7** We may pay for covered damage by repair, replacement, or payment of money, as permitted by this policy. Our choice of method does not increase the applicable limit of liability. We will not pay more than the amount required to repair or replace the covered property, subject to the applicable limit.

**W.8** We may make payment to you or to another person with a valid interest in the covered property. Payment to a person with an interest in the property does not increase our total liability. We may allocate payment according to the interests shown by the applicable evidence of ownership.

**W.9** Your interest in covered property does not establish the amount of our liability. We determine our liability from the covered loss and the applicable limit of liability. We do not pay for an interest that is not covered by this policy.

**W.10** The applicable limit of liability applies to covered property at the location described in the policy. Property moved from that location remains subject to the terms applicable to the coverage. We do not extend the limit to property that is not insured under the policy.

**W.11** We determine the amount of covered loss using the valuation provisions that apply to the damaged property. The valuation does not change the applicable limit of liability. We will not pay an amount that exceeds the applicable limit after applying all required adjustments.

**W.12** We may consider the condition, age, quality, and use of the property when determining the amount of covered loss. Those considerations do not reduce coverage below the amount otherwise payable under the applicable terms of this policy. They also do not increase our liability beyond the applicable limit.

**W.13** We do not cover the cost of correcting a defect, condition, or maintenance issue that is not covered under this policy. We may cover ensuing damage when coverage otherwise applies. Payment for ensuing damage remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.14** We do not cover an amount that represents an improvement or betterment beyond the condition of the damaged property before the loss. We may pay the reasonable cost to restore covered property as provided by this policy. That payment remains subject to the applicable limit.

**W.15** We determine our liability from the facts existing at the time of the covered loss. Later changes in the value of the property do not increase the applicable limit of liability. Later damage is covered only when this policy provides coverage for that damage.

**W.16** You must provide information reasonably necessary to determine the amount of covered loss. You must identify the damaged property and provide available records supporting your claim. We may withhold payment for an amount that cannot be established under the terms of this policy.

**W.17** You must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We may pay reasonable expenses incurred for that protection when this policy provides coverage for those expenses. Payment of such expenses does not increase the applicable limit unless this policy expressly provides otherwise.

**W.18** We do not cover damage resulting from your failure to protect covered property from further damage. We may cover damage that could not reasonably have been prevented. Any payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.19** We may inspect damaged property and require reasonable access to the location of the loss. Inspection does not constitute an admission of coverage or establish the amount of our liability. We retain all rights under this policy while evaluating the loss.

**W.20** We may require a description of the property, the cause of loss, and the amount claimed. You must cooperate with our investigation of the loss. Failure to provide requested information may prevent us from determining the amount payable.

**W.21** We do not cover duplicate payment for the same damage. If another payment compensates you for covered damage, we may reduce our payment to prevent recovery exceeding the covered loss. The reduction does not exceed the amount otherwise payable under this policy.

**W.22** We may recover payment made for property or damage that is not covered. We may also correct a payment made because of an error in determining coverage or the amount of loss. Recovery of an incorrect payment does not enlarge the applicable limit of liability.

**W.23** We may pay for covered damage to property that is jointly owned. Payment to an owner does not waive our rights against any other person. The total payment for the covered property remains subject to the applicable limit.

**W.24** We do not cover the value of land, water, or any other property that is not insured under the applicable coverage. We may cover reasonable costs for covered property when those costs are included within the coverage. Those costs remain subject to the applicable limit unless this policy provides otherwise.

**W.25** We do not cover a reduction in market value that is not attributable to covered physical damage. We may cover direct physical damage to covered property when caused by a covered peril. Payment for that damage remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.26** We determine the amount payable without regard to sentimental value, personal value, or value based on use that is not recognized by this policy. We may consider the applicable valuation method when determining the covered loss. We will not pay more than the amount allowed by that method and the applicable limit.

**W.27** We do not cover costs incurred solely because a law, ordinance, or regulation requires an upgrade unless this policy provides coverage for those costs. We may pay covered repair costs that are otherwise required by this policy. The applicable limit continues to apply to those costs.

**W.28** We do not cover the cost of repairing property that was not damaged by a covered peril. We may cover reasonable access, removal, or related costs when coverage applies to those costs. Payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.29** We may settle a covered loss with you, the owner of the property, or another person entitled to receive payment. A settlement does not establish coverage for unrelated damage. The settlement amount will not exceed the applicable limit of liability.

**W.30** We may require a signed agreement or other documentation before making payment when reasonably necessary to establish entitlement to payment. You must provide that documentation within a reasonable time. The documentation requirement does not increase our liability.

**W.31** We do not cover amounts claimed solely because the cost of labor, materials, or services has increased after the covered loss. We may consider reasonable costs applicable to the covered repair when determining the amount of loss. Payment remains subject to the applicable limit and all other policy terms.

**W.32** We may make payment in installments when the nature of the repair or replacement reasonably requires payment at different stages. Each payment reduces the amount remaining available for the covered loss. The total payment will not exceed the applicable limit of liability.

**W.33** We do not cover interest, penalties, or fees imposed on you by another person unless this policy provides coverage for those amounts. We may pay covered expenses that are necessary to address covered damage. Such payment remains subject to the applicable limit.

**W.34** You must use payment for the covered property or covered expense for which it is made. Your use of payment does not alter the amount of our liability. We are not responsible for costs resulting from your choice of contractor, supplier, or repair method.

**W.35** We may base payment on a reasonable repair or replacement estimate. An estimate does not guarantee that every claimed item is covered. We may revise the payment when additional information establishes a different covered loss, subject to the applicable limit.

**W.36** We do not cover amounts that result from fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation. We may deny the affected claim or recover payment made on that claim. Our rights under this provision apply in addition to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.37** We will pay the amount of covered loss that remains after applying the applicable deductible and all other required adjustments. Our payment is subject to the applicable limit of liability and every applicable condition of this policy. We have no liability for any amount that is not covered under this policy.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** We apply the deductible shown in the Declarations to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. You must satisfy the applicable deductible before we pay for covered loss. This deductible applies regardless of the number or type of covered property damaged.

**W.2** You must determine the amount of covered loss before applying the deductible. We apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. We do not apply this deductible to loss caused solely by a peril other than windstorm or hail.

**W.3** The minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage is one percent. Except that this minimum applies only when the deductible percentage shown in the Declarations is less than the minimum. The maximum windstorm and hail deductible percentage is five percent. Subject to the terms of this provision, the deductible percentage shown in the Declarations may not exceed the maximum. The applicable deductible remains the deductible shown in the Declarations.

**W.4** The deductible is calculated by applying the applicable percentage to the limit of insurance for the covered dwelling. You must use the limit of insurance that applies when the loss occurs. The calculation does not change because the amount of damage is less than the applicable limit of insurance.

**W.5** We apply the deductible separately to each covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. You must not combine losses that arise from unrelated causes to reduce the deductible. We may treat related damage as one loss when it results from the same windstorm or hail event.

**W.6** You must give us prompt notice of loss caused by windstorm or hail. You must protect the property from further damage after the loss. We do not cover additional loss that results from your failure to take reasonable steps to protect the property.

**W.7** You must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect covered property from further damage. You must keep receipts and other records of those repairs. We may inspect the damaged property before repairs are completed, except that you may make temporary repairs when immediate action is reasonably necessary.

**W.8** We determine the amount of covered loss under the applicable policy provisions. We do not include uncovered property or uncovered causes of loss in that determination. You must provide information and documentation that reasonably supports the amount of loss.

**W.9** We apply the deductible after determining the amount of covered loss. We do not pay the deductible amount. You remain responsible for that amount even when the amount of covered loss exceeds the applicable limit of insurance.

**W.10** When windstorm or hail damages more than one covered item, we determine the covered loss for the damaged property before applying the deductible. You must not apply the deductible separately to each damaged item. We may require an itemized statement of damaged property and related repair costs.

**W.11** When windstorm or hail damage occurs with damage caused by another covered peril, we determine the portion of loss caused by windstorm or hail. We apply this deductible to that portion of covered loss. You must provide information that assists us in distinguishing the causes of damage.

**W.12** We do not cover loss that is excluded under the policy, even if windstorm or hail also contributes to the loss. We apply the deductible only after excluded loss has been removed from the calculation. You must separate covered damage from damage that is not covered.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by neglect to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. You must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage after windstorm or hail occurs. We may reduce payment for loss that results from your failure to do so.

**W.14** You must retain damaged property for our inspection when we reasonably request it. We may inspect the property and take samples when necessary to determine the cause or amount of loss. You must not dispose of damaged property before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, except that unsafe property may be removed when necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.15** You must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of the claim. You must provide records, documents, and statements that we reasonably request. We may not make payment for an amount of loss that cannot be reasonably verified because of your failure to cooperate.

**W.16** We may require a sworn proof of loss for a claim involving windstorm or hail. You must state the amount claimed and the facts supporting the claim. We do not waive our rights by investigating, inspecting, or discussing the loss with you.

**W.17** You must promptly inform us of any changes in ownership, occupancy, or use of the insured property that may affect the loss. We determine coverage based on the facts existing when the loss occurred. We do not cover a loss that is excluded because of those facts.

**W.18** We may pay the claim by repair, replacement, or payment of the amount of covered loss, subject to the policy provisions. You must allow us a reasonable opportunity to inspect and estimate the damage before beginning permanent repairs. We do not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace covered property.

**W.19** We may use comparable materials and methods when repairing covered property. You must allow reasonable access to the property for inspection and repair. We do not pay for improvements or upgrades that are not required to repair covered damage.

**W.20** We do not cover the cost of correcting faulty, inadequate, or defective design, specifications, workmanship, construction, or maintenance. We may cover ensuing covered loss when the policy provides coverage for that loss. You must provide information that allows us to identify the part of the loss that is not covered.

**W.21** We do not cover loss to land, including land on which covered property is located. We may cover damage to covered property resulting from windstorm or hail that also affects the surrounding land. You must not include the value of land in the amount claimed.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by water or moisture that enters through an opening created by wear, deterioration, or defective construction. We may cover damage caused directly by windstorm or hail when the policy provides coverage for that damage. You must protect the property from further water or moisture damage.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We may consider those conditions when determining whether windstorm or hail caused the claimed damage. You must provide evidence of the condition of the property before the loss when reasonably available.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective maintenance. We may cover direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail that occurs independently of a maintenance condition. You must maintain covered property in reasonably good condition.

**W.25** You must notify us when damaged property is repaired or replaced. You must make the property available for inspection when we reasonably request it. We may withhold payment for damage that cannot be evaluated because the property was repaired or replaced without a reasonable opportunity for inspection.

**W.26** We may settle the claim with you or with the person or organization having an interest in the covered property. Payment to an interested person or organization does not increase the amount we owe. You must identify all persons or organizations that may have an interest in the property.

**W.27** You must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of property that has been surrendered to us with our consent. We do not assume ownership of damaged property merely because we make a payment for covered loss.

**W.28** We may recover amounts paid when another person or organization is responsible for the covered loss. You must preserve our rights of recovery and cooperate with us in exercising those rights. You must not impair those rights by releasing or settling with a responsible person or organization without our consent.

**W.29** We determine whether a claim involves windstorm or hail based on the facts and circumstances of the loss. You must provide reasonable access to the property and relevant information for that determination. We do not treat an unsupported description of damage as proof of a covered cause of loss.

**W.30** The deductible applies to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail under the terms of this provision. You must pay the deductible before receiving payment for the covered loss. We do not waive the deductible by making an advance payment, inspecting the property, or discussing settlement.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance, law, or governmental action. This applies whether the action is direct or indirect.

**W.2** We do not cover loss arising from the enforcement of building, zoning, land use, or safety requirements. This includes loss resulting from required demolition, reconstruction, repair, or increased construction cost.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, earth sinking, rising, shifting, or expansion.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water that is excluded from coverage.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building or other structure.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water. We do not cover loss caused by spray from any of these sources.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the pollutants are solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or chemical.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. This exclusion applies whether the nuclear hazard is controlled or uncontrolled.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention of property by governmental authority. This exclusion applies whether the authority acts lawfully or unlawfully.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. An insured must not cause or arrange for loss for the purpose of obtaining insurance benefits.

**W.13** We do not cover loss arising from dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, or intentional acts committed by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the act is committed alone or in concert with another person.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect property from further damage.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. This exclusion applies even when the loss is discovered after windstorm or hail.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical defect. We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, wet rot, dry rot, or decay.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, ceilings, pavements, patios, or similar structures. This exclusion applies whether the condition develops gradually or suddenly.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, or gnawing.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies whether the condition is visible or concealed.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke, vapor, fumes, acids, or chemicals unless the loss is otherwise covered. We do not cover loss caused by contamination of property.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by defective design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies to property containing the defect and to property damaged as a result of the defect.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by faulty materials used in repair, construction, renovation, or maintenance. We do not cover the cost to correct defective work or defective materials.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain property in sound condition. An insured must make reasonable repairs and take reasonable steps to prevent avoidable damage.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by deterioration that existed before the windstorm or hail event. We do not cover the cost to restore property to a better condition than existed immediately before loss.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies when the seepage or leakage occurs from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler systems, or household appliances.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow from a plumbing system that results from wear, deterioration, or defective maintenance. We do not cover the cost to repair the system from which the discharge or overflow occurred.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by power failure or interruption of utility service occurring away from the insured location. We do not cover resulting spoilage, delay, or loss of use unless otherwise covered.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by interruption of communication, internet, satellite, cable, or similar service. This exclusion applies whether the interruption follows windstorm, hail, or any other event.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by failure of a protective device to operate. We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain a protective device in working condition.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by faulty operation, malfunction, or breakdown of electronic equipment. This exclusion applies to data, software, media, and electronic records.

**W.31** We do not cover loss of data, loss of access to data, or loss caused by corruption of data. We do not cover the cost to restore, recreate, replace, or recover data.

**W.32** We do not cover loss arising from business pursuits conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies to property used primarily for business purposes.

**W.61** Failure to perform a duty under this provision may affect coverage only to the extent we are prejudiced by that failure. We retain all rights available under the applicable policy terms.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a covered loss to "covered property" in accordance with this section. We will determine the amount of loss from the property affected by direct physical loss.

**W.2** An insured must retain the damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must permit us to examine the property and relevant records.

**W.3** We will reduce the amount of a covered loss by the applicable "deductible." The deductible applies to the covered loss before we make payment.

**W.4** We will not pay for loss that is not a "direct physical loss." We will not pay for deterioration, wear, marring, or loss that is not caused by a covered peril.

**W.5** We may determine the amount of loss by repair, replacement, valuation, or another reasonable method. We may consider the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

**W.6** We may use estimates, invoices, receipts, photographs, records, and other available information to determine the amount of loss. An insured must provide information reasonably requested to support the claim.

**W.7** The loss settlement basis for "personal property" is replacement cost. We will apply that basis only to covered direct physical loss.

**W.8** We may pay the value of damaged property without requiring repair or replacement. Payment of a claim does not require us to select, hire, or supervise a contractor.

**W.9** We may repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may use material that is reasonably comparable to the damaged property.

**W.10** An insured must cooperate with us in obtaining access to damaged property. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**W.11** We will pay only for damage that is supported by the available evidence. We may exclude from the amount of loss any damage that existed before the reported loss.

**W.12** We will not pay for property that an insured has discarded before we inspect it unless disposal was reasonably necessary to protect persons or property. An insured must preserve samples, records, and photographs when reasonably available.

**W.13** We may require an insured to show us the damaged property. An insured must identify property claimed as damaged with reasonable particularity.

**W.14** We may determine whether damaged property can be repaired. We may limit payment to the reasonable cost to repair property when repair will restore it to like kind and quality.

**W.15** We may determine whether replacement is reasonably necessary. We will not pay for betterment, enlargement, or improvement unless required to restore covered property to like kind and quality.

**W.16** We will not pay for the value of undamaged property merely because matching material is unavailable. We may consider reasonable uniformity when determining the amount of loss.

**W.17** We may settle a loss based on the reasonable cost of materials and labor. We may consider customary charges for work of similar kind and quality in the area where the property is located.

**W.18** We will not pay for costs that are unnecessary to repair or replace damaged property. We will not pay for work that does not relate to covered direct physical loss.

**W.19** An insured must provide receipts, estimates, invoices, and other records that reasonably establish the amount claimed. We may verify the authenticity and relevance of those records.

**W.20** We may inspect repaired or replaced property. An insured must permit reasonable access for that inspection.

**W.21** We may pay an insured, a person legally entitled to receive payment, or another party as permitted by law. Payment to a party does not waive any right available to us.

**W.22** We may make payment jointly when more than one person has an interest in covered property. An insured must provide information concerning known interests in that property.

**W.23** We will not pay more than the applicable "limit." The applicable limit does not increase because a loss affects more than one item of covered property.

**W.24** We will not pay for any portion of a loss that another source is legally obligated to pay. We may pursue recovery rights available to us after making payment.

**W.25** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment when we choose to do so.

**W.26** We may retain salvage from damaged property. The value of salvage may be considered in determining the amount of loss.

**W.27** We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath. An insured must provide truthful answers concerning the claimed loss.

**W.28** We may require an insured to provide a signed statement describing the loss. The statement must identify the property, the cause of loss, and the interest of each known person in the property.

**W.29** We may require a "proof of loss" that states the amount claimed. An insured must provide a proof of loss supported by reasonably available information.

**W.30** We may investigate the cause and extent of the reported loss. Our investigation does not create coverage that is otherwise unavailable.

**W.31** We will not pay for loss resulting from intentional concealment or material misrepresentation by an insured. We may deny the affected claim when an insured knowingly provides false information concerning that claim.

**W.32** We may use experts to inspect, evaluate, estimate, or determine the cause of loss. An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests made in connection with that evaluation.

**W.33** We may obtain records from persons or organizations having information relevant to the claimed loss. An insured must authorize the release of relevant records when reasonably requested.

**W.34** We may consider prior repairs, prior damage, and the preloss condition of covered property. We will not include in the amount of loss costs attributable solely to prior damage.

**W.35** We may separate covered damage from damage that is not covered. We will pay only the portion of loss that is covered.

**W.36** We may allocate shared repair costs between covered damage and damage that is not covered. We will use a reasonable method of allocation based on the available evidence.

**W.37** We will not pay for loss caused by an insured's failure to use reasonable means to protect covered property after a loss. We may pay for reasonable measures taken to protect property from further covered damage.

**W.38** An insured must notify us of any claim against another person arising from the loss. An insured must not impair our recovery rights.

**W.39** We may settle a claim without obtaining an agreement from every person who may assert an interest in covered property. Our payment will not exceed the amount otherwise payable for the covered loss.

**W.40** We may make an advance payment when information reasonably supports an undisputed portion of the claim. An advance payment does not waive our right to investigate the remaining claim.

**W.41** We may withhold payment for an amount that remains reasonably in dispute. We will pay any amount that is undisputed and otherwise payable.

**W.42** We may request additional information when the information already provided does not reasonably establish the amount of loss. An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests for that information.

**W.43** We will not pay for costs incurred solely to prepare, present, or negotiate a claim. We may consider reasonable documentation necessary to establish covered direct physical loss.

**W.44** We will not pay for loss to property for which an insured has no insurable interest. An insured must disclose the nature of the insured's interest in property claimed as damaged.

**W.45** We may determine the value of property based on its age, condition, quality, usefulness, and remaining serviceability. We may consider market information that reasonably reflects the property involved.

**W.46** We will not pay for sentimental value, emotional value, or personal attachment to property. We will determine the amount of loss based on the applicable settlement provisions.

**W.47** We may require an insured to preserve records relating to ownership and value of covered property. An insured must provide those records when reasonably available.

**W.48** We may deny payment for a claimed item when available information does not reasonably establish that the item was owned by an insured or was damaged by a covered loss. We may reconsider the claim if reliable supporting information becomes available.

**W.49** We will not pay for repair work that corrects faulty design, workmanship, or maintenance unless the work is necessary to repair covered direct physical loss. We will pay only the portion of work attributable to covered damage.

**W.50** We may settle loss to property that is part of a set, pair, collection, or system based on the reduction in value caused by covered damage. We will not pay for loss of value to undamaged property unless that loss results directly from covered damage.

**W.51** We may determine the reasonable cost of repair using materials and methods reasonably available for the property involved. We are not required to pay for materials or methods selected solely because they are preferred by an insured.

**W.52** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve evidence of the cause of loss. An insured must not alter the damaged area in a manner that prevents reasonable investigation unless necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.53** We may inspect property before, during, or after repair or replacement. Our inspection does not constitute acceptance of work performed by an insured or another person.

**W.54** We will not pay more than the amount of direct physical loss after application of the deductible and applicable limit. We will not duplicate payment for the same damage.

**W.55** The "special assessment limit" does not change the settlement of covered property under this section. The "fire department service charge limit" does not change the settlement of covered property under this section.

**W.56** The "jewelry theft sublimit" does not change the method used to determine covered direct physical loss. The "business property sublimit" does not change the method used to determine covered direct physical loss.

**W.57** We may require reasonable documentation for property that is subject to a limit or sublimit. An insured must provide information that permits us to apply the applicable limit or sublimit.

**W.58** We may apply the applicable deductible only once to the same covered loss. We will not apply the deductible to amounts that are not covered.

**W.59** We will not pay for costs that an insured did not incur when payment depends on incurred costs. We may require documentation that reasonably establishes incurred costs.

**W.60** Our payment will be subject to the terms and conditions applicable to the claim. Payment of any portion of a claim does not waive any provision applicable to the remaining claim.

**W.61** An insured must comply with duties that apply to the claimed loss. We will determine settlement based on the information reasonably available to us.

## W.8 — Definitions

"This endorsement" means the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible endorsement attached to your policy. We use the terms defined in this endorsement only with respect to the deductible that applies to loss caused by windstorm or hail.

"Windstorm" means wind, gusts of wind, wind-driven objects, or atmospheric disturbance involving wind. Windstorm includes damage caused by wind entering a structure through an opening created by wind.

"Hail" means pellets of ice falling from the atmosphere. Hail includes damage caused by hail striking covered property directly or causing an opening through which precipitation enters.

"Windstorm Or Hail Loss" means direct physical loss to covered property caused by windstorm or hail. Windstorm Or Hail Loss includes resulting direct physical loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, or other precipitation entering through an opening created by windstorm or hail.

"Covered Property" means property to which coverage applies under your policy and to which this endorsement applies. Covered Property includes property only to the extent that it is subject to a limit of liability under your policy.

"Applicable Property Limit" means the limit of liability that applies to the covered property damaged by a Windstorm Or Hail Loss. The Applicable Property Limit is determined without reduction for any deductible.

"Percentage Deductible" means the deductible determined by applying the applicable percentage to the Deductible Base. The Percentage Deductible applies to Windstorm Or Hail Loss as provided by this endorsement.

"Deductible Base" means the property value or limit of liability used to calculate the Percentage Deductible. The Deductible Base depends on the covered property involved in the loss.

"Building" means a dwelling, structure, or part of a structure to which coverage applies. Building includes permanently attached fixtures and materials used to repair or rebuild the structure.

"Dwelling" means the residence building described in your policy. Dwelling includes attached structures to the extent that they are insured as part of the residence building.

"Other Structure" means a structure that is separate from the dwelling and to which coverage applies. Other Structure includes a structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

"Personal Property" means tangible property that is not permanently attached to a building. Personal Property includes property owned or used by an insured to the extent that coverage applies.

"Residence Premises" means the location described in your policy as the residence premises. Residence Premises includes the land on which the dwelling is located and the areas used in connection with that dwelling.

"Loss" means accidental direct physical loss to covered property. Loss does not include a condition that has not caused physical damage to covered property.

"Occurrence" means an accident or a continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence may include windstorm, hail, or both.

"Single Windstorm Or Hail Event" means weather conditions arising from the same atmospheric disturbance. A Single Windstorm Or Hail Event may cause loss at more than one covered location.

"Related Weather Conditions" means weather conditions that arise from, accompany, or follow a windstorm or hail event. Related Weather Conditions include precipitation, airborne debris, and changes in atmospheric pressure.

"Direct Physical Loss" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. Direct Physical Loss must result from a covered cause of loss.

"Opening" means a gap, breach, break, or other physical disruption in a roof, wall, door, window, or other part of a building. Opening does not include a condition resulting solely from wear, deterioration, or defective construction.

"Wind-Driven Debris" means material moved by wind with sufficient force to strike or damage covered property. Wind-Driven Debris includes trees, branches, building materials, and other objects.

"Precipitation Damage" means direct physical loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, or other precipitation. Precipitation Damage is Windstorm Or Hail Loss only when the precipitation enters through an Opening created by windstorm or hail.

"Property Damage" means direct physical injury to tangible covered property. Property Damage includes resulting loss of use of that property.

"Repair Cost" means the reasonable cost to repair damaged covered property with material of like kind and quality. Repair Cost does not include the cost of correcting defects that existed before the loss.

"Replacement Cost" means the reasonable cost to replace damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality. Replacement Cost is determined subject to the terms that apply to the covered property.

"Actual Cash Value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, considering depreciation and physical condition. Actual Cash Value does not include the value of undamaged property.

"Depreciation" means the decrease in value caused by age, wear, use, deterioration, or obsolescence. Depreciation is determined based on the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

"Damaged Property" means covered property that sustains Direct Physical Loss. Damaged Property does not include property that is merely exposed to weather conditions without physical injury.

"Undamaged Property" means covered property that has not sustained Direct Physical Loss. Undamaged Property is not included in the calculation of a loss unless otherwise required by the terms that apply to the property.

"Limit Of Liability" means the maximum amount payable for covered loss to particular property. The Limit Of Liability applies before any deductible is subtracted unless the terms that apply to the property provide otherwise.

"Policy Deductible" means a deductible shown in your policy that applies to covered loss. The Policy Deductible does not apply to Windstorm Or Hail Loss when the Percentage Deductible applies.

"Applicable Deductible" means the deductible that applies to the Windstorm Or Hail Loss. The Applicable Deductible is the Percentage Deductible unless another deductible is required by law.

"Separate Covered Property" means covered property subject to its own Limit Of Liability. Separate Covered Property may include a Building, Other Structure, or Personal Property.

"Combined Loss" means a loss involving Windstorm Or Hail Loss and loss caused by another cause. We determine the Applicable Deductible based on the portion of loss caused by windstorm or hail.

"Concurrent Cause" means a cause of loss that acts together with windstorm or hail to produce damage. A Concurrent Cause may be independent of, or related to, the windstorm or hail event.

"Resulting Loss" means direct physical loss that follows from Windstorm Or Hail Loss. Resulting Loss is subject to the terms that apply to the property and cause of loss involved.

"Claim" means a request by you or an insured for payment under your policy. Claim includes a request for payment for Windstorm Or Hail Loss.

"Insured" means you and any other person qualifying as an insured under your policy. An insured includes a person only to the extent that coverage applies to that person.

"Named Insured" means the person or organization shown in your policy as a named insured. A Named Insured may act for other insureds concerning the presentation of a Claim.
