# HO 23 77 Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible (Edition 2022-07)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement changes the policy. The provisions of this endorsement apply only to loss caused by windstorm or hail.

The term "policy" means the insurance contract to which this endorsement is attached. This endorsement is part of the policy.

The term "we" means the insurer providing coverage under the policy. The terms "us" and "our" refer to the same insurer.

The term "you" means the named insured under the policy. The term "your" refers to the named insured.

The term "insured" means a person qualifying as an insured under the policy. An insured must comply with the duties imposed by the policy and this endorsement.

The term "windstorm or hail" means windstorm, hail, or both. Windstorm or hail includes direct physical loss caused by either peril.

The term "covered property" means property for which coverage is provided under the policy. Covered property is subject to the terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions of the policy.

The term "covered loss" means direct physical loss to covered property for which coverage is provided. A covered loss must result from windstorm or hail.

The term "deductible" means the amount of loss that remains your responsibility. The deductible applies to a covered loss caused by windstorm or hail.

This endorsement applies when the policy provides a windstorm or hail percentage deductible. The deductible applies as provided by this endorsement.

We will determine the amount of covered loss before applying the deductible. We will then reduce the amount payable by the applicable deductible.

The deductible applies to covered loss to property covered under the policy. The deductible does not increase the limit of insurance applicable to the covered property.

If windstorm or hail causes loss to more than one item of covered property, the deductible applies as required by the policy and this endorsement. We will not apply a deductible in a manner that exceeds our obligations under the policy.

If more than one cause of loss contributes to loss or damage, we will apply the deductible only as required by this endorsement. We will determine coverage under the remaining provisions of the policy.

This endorsement does not provide coverage for property, loss, or damage that is not otherwise covered by the policy. We do not cover loss excluded under the policy.

An insured must provide prompt notice of windstorm or hail loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss.

An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must provide records and information reasonably requested by us.

Payment of a claim remains subject to all applicable policy conditions. Our payment will not exceed the amount otherwise payable under the policy after application of the deductible.

If this endorsement conflicts with the policy, this endorsement controls with respect to windstorm or hail loss. All other policy provisions remain unchanged.

The attachment of this endorsement does not alter the identity of an insured. The attachment of this endorsement does not alter coverage except as stated in this endorsement.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the coverage described in this endorsement for direct physical loss to covered property caused by windstorm or hail. This coverage is subject to the applicable "Percentage Deductible."

**W.2** "Windstorm or Hail Loss" means direct physical loss caused by the action of windstorm or hail. Windstorm or Hail Loss includes loss caused by wind-driven objects.

**W.3** Windstorm or Hail Loss includes loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust only when the direct action of windstorm or hail first damages the building or covered property and creates an opening. The opening must permit the rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust to enter.

**W.4** "Percentage Deductible" means the deductible applicable to Windstorm or Hail Loss that is determined by applying the applicable percentage to the applicable limit of liability. We will determine the Percentage Deductible separately for each covered property when required by the applicable basis of calculation.

**W.5** The Percentage Deductible applies to each Windstorm or Hail Loss. We will subtract the applicable Percentage Deductible from the amount otherwise payable for that loss.

**W.6** The Percentage Deductible applies whether the loss affects the dwelling, other structures, personal property, loss of use, or any combination of covered property. The applicable Percentage Deductible will not be reduced because more than one type of covered property is damaged.

**W.7** We will apply the Percentage Deductible before we determine the amount payable for a Windstorm or Hail Loss. We will not pay for any amount of loss that is within the applicable Percentage Deductible.

**W.8** The Percentage Deductible does not increase any limit of liability. The Percentage Deductible does not create coverage for property or loss that is not otherwise covered.

**W.9** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to direct physical loss caused by windstorm. We will also apply the Percentage Deductible to direct physical loss caused by hail.

**W.10** We will apply the Percentage Deductible when windstorm or hail is a cause of loss, whether acting alone or with another cause. This provision applies regardless of the sequence in which the causes contribute to the loss.

**W.11** We will apply the Percentage Deductible when a covered Windstorm or Hail Loss results in damage to a building. The deductible applies to damage to attached structures that are part of the covered dwelling.

**W.12** We will apply the Percentage Deductible when a covered Windstorm or Hail Loss results in damage to structures separated from the dwelling. The deductible applies to covered structures regardless of their use.

**W.13** We will apply the Percentage Deductible when a covered Windstorm or Hail Loss results in damage to personal property. The deductible applies to personal property while it is at an insured location.

**W.14** We will apply the Percentage Deductible when a covered Windstorm or Hail Loss results in loss of use. We will determine the loss of use payment after applying the applicable Percentage Deductible.

**W.15** We will apply the Percentage Deductible when a covered Windstorm or Hail Loss damages property that is permanently installed. The deductible also applies to covered property that is temporarily attached or connected.

**W.16** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by windborne debris. The debris must cause direct physical damage to covered property.

**W.17** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind-driven rain when the required opening is created by windstorm or hail. We will not apply this coverage to water that enters through an opening that was not created by windstorm or hail.

**W.18** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by hail striking covered property. The deductible applies to resulting damage caused by the impact of hail.

**W.19** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind pressure. The deductible applies to resulting damage caused by the movement, displacement, or collapse of covered property.

**W.20** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind-driven collapse of a covered structure. Coverage for collapse applies only when the collapse is otherwise covered.

**W.21** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind damage to exterior building materials. The deductible applies to resulting interior damage only when that interior damage is otherwise covered.

**W.22** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind or hail damage to a roof. The deductible applies to all covered damage arising from that Windstorm or Hail Loss.

**W.23** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind or hail damage to exterior walls. The deductible applies to resulting damage to the interior of the dwelling when that damage is otherwise covered.

**W.24** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind or hail damage to windows, doors, skylights, or similar exterior openings. The deductible applies to covered damage resulting from the failure of those items.

**W.25** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind or hail damage to awnings, shutters, gutters, downspouts, and similar exterior appurtenances. Coverage applies only to property that is otherwise covered.

**W.26** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind or hail damage to fencing, landscaping, or other outdoor property only when that property is otherwise covered. The deductible does not change the coverage applicable to that property.

**W.27** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind or hail damage to detached structures. The deductible applies even if the detached structure is used for storage, shelter, or another covered purpose.

**W.28** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind or hail damage to property under construction. Coverage applies only while the property is otherwise covered.

**W.29** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind or hail damage during repair, renovation, alteration, or maintenance. The deductible applies to covered property regardless of its condition at the time of loss.

**W.30** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by windstorm or hail that occurs while covered property is vacant or unoccupied. Any condition applicable to vacancy or unoccupancy continues to apply.

**W.31** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by windstorm or hail that occurs while the dwelling is occupied. The occupancy of the dwelling does not change the deductible applicable to the loss.

**W.32** We will apply the Percentage Deductible to loss caused by windstorm or hail affecting property at an insured location. The deductible applies to covered property wherever coverage is otherwise provided.

**W.33** We will determine the Percentage Deductible using the limit of liability applicable to the damaged covered property. We will not use a limit that does not apply to that property.

**W.34** When more than one limit of liability applies to damaged covered property, we will determine the applicable Percentage Deductible using the limit required by the applicable basis of calculation. We will not combine limits unless the applicable basis of calculation requires combination.

**W.35** When Windstorm or Hail Loss affects the dwelling and other covered property, we will apply the Percentage Deductible as required by the applicable basis of calculation. The deductible may apply to the combined covered loss when the applicable basis of calculation requires that result.

**W.36** When Windstorm or Hail Loss affects separate covered structures, we will determine the applicable Percentage Deductible for each structure as required by the applicable basis of calculation. A deductible applicable to one structure does not create coverage for another structure.

**W.37** When Windstorm or Hail Loss affects covered personal property and a covered building, we will determine the applicable Percentage Deductible as required by the applicable basis of calculation. The deductible applies before payment for the covered loss.

**W.38** We will not apply a deductible other than the applicable Percentage Deductible to the same covered Windstorm or Hail Loss unless another deductible applies by its terms. We will not duplicate deduction of the same Percentage Deductible.

**W.39** If another deductible applies to a loss that includes Windstorm or Hail Loss, we will apply the deductible that is required by its applicable terms. The Percentage Deductible remains applicable to the Windstorm or Hail Loss.

**W.40** We will determine whether the Percentage Deductible applies based on the facts of the loss. We may consider weather conditions, physical evidence, reports, observations, and other relevant information.

**W.41** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a Windstorm or Hail Loss. Reasonable measures to protect the property do not eliminate the applicable Percentage Deductible.

**W.42** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. Disposal of damaged property before inspection does not change the applicable Percentage Deductible.

**W.43** An insured must provide information we reasonably require to evaluate a Windstorm or Hail Loss. The information must identify the damaged property and describe the damage.

**W.44** An insured must cooperate with our investigation of a Windstorm or Hail Loss. Cooperation includes providing access to damaged covered property when reasonably requested.

**W.45** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve evidence of windstorm or hail damage. Preservation of evidence may include photographs, records, receipts, estimates, and damaged materials.

**W.46** We may inspect damaged property to determine the cause and extent of a Windstorm or Hail Loss. Our inspection does not waive any condition or limitation applicable to the loss.

**W.47** We may obtain information concerning weather conditions near the insured location. We may use that information in determining whether a Windstorm or Hail Loss occurred.

**W.48** We may require examination of damaged materials to determine whether windstorm or hail caused the damage. We may also require information concerning prior damage, repairs, maintenance, or alterations.

**W.49** We will determine the amount of covered Windstorm or Hail Loss after considering the applicable terms of coverage. We will then subtract the applicable Percentage Deductible.

**W.50** We will not pay more than the amount of covered Windstorm or Hail Loss remaining after application of the Percentage Deductible. Our payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.51** Payment for emergency measures following a Windstorm or Hail Loss is subject to the applicable Percentage Deductible. Emergency measures must be reasonable and necessary to protect covered property from further damage.

**W.52** Payment for temporary repairs following a Windstorm or Hail Loss is subject to the applicable Percentage Deductible. Temporary repairs do not waive our right to inspect the damaged property.

**W.53** Payment for permanent repairs following a Windstorm or Hail Loss is subject to the applicable Percentage Deductible. Repair work must relate to covered direct physical loss.

**W.54** Payment for replacement of covered property damaged by windstorm or hail is subject to the applicable Percentage Deductible. Replacement cost coverage applies only when otherwise available.

**W.55** Payment based on actual cash value for covered property damaged by windstorm or hail is subject to the applicable Percentage Deductible. Actual cash value will be determined in accordance with the applicable terms of coverage.

**W.56** We do not cover loss that is not caused by windstorm or hail merely because windstorm or hail occurs near the insured location. The applicable Percentage Deductible does not create coverage for such loss.

**W.57** We do not cover loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust entering through an opening that was not created by windstorm or hail. The applicable Percentage Deductible does not apply to loss that is not covered.

**W.58** We do not cover wear, tear, deterioration, decay, settling, or defective maintenance merely because windstorm or hail reveals the condition. The applicable Percentage Deductible does not convert an uncovered condition into covered loss.

**W.59** We do not cover faulty, inadequate, or defective design, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, grading, compaction, or maintenance merely because windstorm or hail contributes to the discovery of the condition. Coverage for resulting direct physical loss applies only when otherwise covered.

**W.60** We do not cover loss caused solely by seepage, leakage, moisture, condensation, or repeated exposure to weather conditions. Coverage for direct physical damage caused by windstorm or hail remains subject to the applicable Percentage Deductible.

**W.61** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, flood, surface water, overflow, or water below the surface of the ground merely because windstorm or hail also occurs. The applicable Percentage Deductible does not apply to an uncovered cause of loss.

**W.62** We do not cover loss caused by neglect after a Windstorm or Hail Loss. An insured must use reasonable means to save and preserve covered property from further damage.

**W.63** We do not cover the cost to repair an undamaged part of covered property solely to achieve a uniform appearance. The applicable Percentage Deductible does not alter this limitation.

**W.64** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, or improve property for a condition that existed before the Windstorm or Hail Loss. We will pay only for covered direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail.

**W.65** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts of an insured. This exclusion applies even when windstorm or hail occurs at or near the insured location.

**W.66** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to comply with a duty that materially affects our ability to determine the cause or amount of loss. The applicable Percentage Deductible remains subject to all applicable conditions.

**W.67** Windstorm or Hail Loss may include damage caused by a covered falling object that is moved by wind. The Percentage Deductible applies to the covered damage caused by that object.

**W.68** Windstorm or Hail Loss may include damage caused by airborne debris striking covered property. The Percentage Deductible applies whether the debris originates on or away from the insured location.

**W.69** Windstorm or Hail Loss may include damage caused by the collapse of covered property when the collapse is directly caused by windstorm or hail. Coverage for collapse remains subject to all applicable limitations.

**W.70** Windstorm or Hail Loss may include damage caused by wind-driven precipitation after a covered opening is created. The Percentage Deductible applies to all covered damage resulting from that entry.

**W.71** The applicable Percentage Deductible applies to the entire covered Windstorm or Hail Loss. We will not allocate the deductible among damaged items unless the applicable basis of calculation requires allocation.

**W.72** The applicable Percentage Deductible applies regardless of the amount of covered loss. We will not make payment when the covered loss does not exceed the deductible.

**W.73** The applicable Percentage Deductible applies regardless of the manner in which you elect to repair or replace damaged property. Your repair or replacement decision does not change the deductible.

**W.74** The applicable Percentage Deductible applies regardless of whether damaged property is repaired, replaced, or not repaired. We will determine payment based on the applicable terms of coverage.

**W.75** The applicable Percentage Deductible applies to covered loss discovered after the windstorm or hail has ended. Discovery of damage does not change the cause of loss determination.

**W.76** The applicable Percentage Deductible applies to covered loss that becomes apparent after repairs begin. An insured must promptly report additional damage that may be related to the Windstorm or Hail Loss.

**W.77** This endorsement applies to Windstorm or Hail Loss occurring while the coverage provided by this endorsement is in effect. We will determine coverage based on the facts existing at the time of loss.

**W.78** We provide no coverage beyond that stated in this endorsement. All coverage under this endorsement is subject to the applicable Percentage Deductible.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The most we will pay for a "covered loss" caused by windstorm or hail is the applicable limit of liability after application of the "Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible." A covered loss is direct physical loss to covered property for which payment is owed.

**W.2** The Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible is the deductible applicable to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. It applies whether windstorm or hail is the sole cause of loss or acts with another cause of loss.

**W.3** We will apply the Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible before determining the amount payable for covered loss. Application of that deductible does not increase any applicable limit of liability.

**W.4** We will not pay any portion of covered loss that is within the Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible. You are responsible for that portion of the loss.

**W.5** All covered loss arising from the same windstorm or hail event will be treated as arising from the same loss event. Damage occurring at different locations may be treated as arising from the same loss event when caused by the same windstorm or hail.

**W.6** When a loss event causes damage to property subject to different applicable limits of liability, we will apply each applicable limit to the property to which it applies. Payment for damage to one category of property does not increase the limit applicable to another category of property.

**W.7** When windstorm or hail damages property that is subject to a separate limit of liability, our payment for that property will not exceed its separate limit. A separate limit remains subject to the Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible when that deductible applies.

**W.8** We will determine the amount of covered loss from the direct physical damage caused by windstorm or hail. We will not include loss that is not covered merely because it occurs at the same time as covered loss.

**W.9** We will not pay more than the amount necessary to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, subject to the applicable limit of liability. Payment will be based on the loss valuation that applies to the damaged property.

**W.10** The applicable limit of liability is not increased because damaged property cannot be repaired, replaced, or restored. The applicable limit of liability is not increased because replacement property costs more than the damaged property.

**W.11** We will not pay for any improvement, enlargement, or added value that results from repair or replacement. You may make improvements at your expense, but our payment remains limited as provided in this section.

**W.12** We will not pay for loss to undamaged property merely to achieve uniform appearance with damaged property. We will pay for necessary repair or replacement of damaged property to the extent covered and subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.13** When damaged property has value remaining after a covered loss, we may consider that remaining value in determining the amount payable. Our payment will not exceed the amount of covered loss after application of the applicable deductible and limit of liability.

**W.14** Any amount payable for debris removal, preservation of property, or other loss-related expense is subject to the applicable limit of liability when it is part of covered loss. Such payment does not create additional insurance beyond that limit.

**W.15** Expenses that you incur without our agreement are not payable unless they are covered as part of the loss. Our payment for covered expenses is subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.16** Payment for emergency measures does not increase the applicable limit of liability. We will pay only for emergency measures that are necessary to protect covered property from further covered damage.

**W.17** We may pay for covered loss by repairing, replacing, or paying the value of damaged property. Our choice of method does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.18** If we elect to repair or replace damaged property, our obligation is limited to the covered damage caused by windstorm or hail. We are not obligated to repair or replace property that was not damaged by covered loss.

**W.19** An estimate of loss, repair cost, or replacement cost does not establish our liability. Our liability is determined by the covered loss, the applicable deductible, and the applicable limit of liability.

**W.20** A payment by us does not waive any condition, limitation, or exclusion that applies to the loss. A payment by us does not establish coverage for any later loss.

**W.21** We may inspect damaged property and require reasonable documentation of the loss before making payment. An insured must permit us to inspect the property as often as reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim.

**W.22** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.23** An insured must provide records, photographs, receipts, estimates, and other information reasonably needed to establish the amount of covered loss. Failure to provide requested information may affect our ability to determine the amount payable.

**W.24** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a windstorm or hail loss. We will not pay for avoidable additional damage that results from failure to protect the property.

**W.25** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment, but we are not required to do so.

**W.26** If we pay for damaged property and obtain possession of that property, any salvage value belongs to us. Salvage value may be considered in determining the amount payable for covered loss.

**W.27** Any recovery that you receive from a responsible party for covered loss must be applied to the loss. We will not make duplicate payment for the same covered loss.

**W.28** If another source provides payment for the same covered loss, we will not pay more than our share of the covered loss. Our payment remains subject to the applicable deductible and limit of liability.

**W.29** Payment to a mortgagee, lienholder, trustee, or other person with an interest in covered property does not increase the applicable limit of liability. Any such payment is part of, and not in addition to, the amount otherwise payable for covered loss.

**W.30** Payment to an insured does not increase the amount payable to any other person claiming an interest in the same property. The applicable limit of liability is shared by all persons seeking payment for the same covered loss.

**W.31** We may make payment jointly to you and another person who has a legal interest in the damaged property. Joint payment does not increase our total liability for the loss.

**W.32** A dispute concerning the amount of loss does not increase the applicable limit of liability. We remain liable only for covered loss, subject to the applicable deductible and limit of liability.

**W.33** Repair or replacement undertaken before our inspection does not bind us as to the amount of covered loss. An insured must provide reasonable evidence of the damaged condition and the work performed.

**W.34** We will not pay for loss caused by delay in repair or replacement unless that loss is covered. We will not pay for increased costs caused by an insured's failure to act reasonably after the loss.

**W.35** We will not pay for the value of property that was not present at the location of the loss when windstorm or hail caused the damage. An insured must establish that claimed property was damaged by the covered loss.

**W.36** We will not pay for property that has not sustained direct physical damage merely because related property was damaged. Our payment is limited to covered direct physical loss.

**W.37** We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability regardless of the number of claims arising from the same loss event. The applicable deductible applies as provided in this section.

**W.38** The applicable limit of liability is not increased by the cost of investigating, estimating, adjusting, or settling a claim. Our claim handling activities do not create additional coverage.

**W.39** We may settle a covered loss with you, with a person legally entitled to receive payment, or with both. A settlement does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**W.40** When we have paid the applicable limit of liability for covered loss, no further payment is available for that loss. This applies even if additional damage or expense is later discovered.

**W.41** The Windstorm or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to the covered loss before we determine the amount remaining within the applicable limit of liability. The deductible is not available to satisfy any other obligation owed by us.

**W.42** Our liability for covered loss caused by windstorm or hail is limited by the applicable deductible and applicable limit of liability. No payment under this section may exceed the amount we are obligated to pay for covered loss.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible" is the amount you must bear for a covered windstorm or hail loss. We subtract this deductible from the amount otherwise payable for that loss.

**W.2** "Covered windstorm or hail loss" means direct physical loss to covered property caused by windstorm or hail for which coverage applies. The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to each covered windstorm or hail loss.

**W.3** The percentage used to determine the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible must not be less than two percent. The percentage used to determine the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible must not be greater than ten percent.

**W.4** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible is determined by applying the applicable percentage to the applicable limit. The applicable percentage is the percentage selected for this deductible.

**W.5** "Applicable limit" means the limit of liability that applies to the damaged covered property immediately before the covered windstorm or hail loss. We determine the applicable limit without regard to any reduction caused by the loss.

**W.6** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible before determining the amount we will pay for covered windstorm or hail loss. We will not pay the part of covered windstorm or hail loss that is within this deductible.

**W.7** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies whether windstorm or hail acts alone or in combination with another cause of loss. This deductible applies only to the extent that windstorm or hail causes covered direct physical loss.

**W.8** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to loss caused by wind-driven rain only when the requirements for coverage of that loss are met. The presence of rain does not prevent application of this deductible when windstorm caused covered direct physical loss.

**W.9** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to loss caused by hail, including loss caused by the impact of hail. We also apply this deductible to resulting covered direct physical loss caused by hail.

**W.10** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to covered property damaged during the same occurrence. We determine the deductible using the applicable limit for the covered property to which the loss applies.

**W.11** "Occurrence" means an event or a series of related events that results in covered windstorm or hail loss. Related windstorm or hail conditions are treated as part of the same occurrence when they arise from the same weather event.

**W.12** We do not combine applicable limits to reduce the effect of the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible. We apply the deductible to each applicable limit when separate limits apply to damaged covered property.

**W.13** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible does not increase any applicable limit. It does not create coverage for property or loss that is otherwise not covered.

**W.14** We do not cover any expense incurred solely to avoid payment of the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible. We may pay covered expenses subject to this deductible when those expenses are otherwise covered.

**W.15** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the cause, extent, and amount of covered windstorm or hail loss. An insured must provide information we reasonably request to apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible.

**W.16** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a covered windstorm or hail loss. Reasonable protective measures do not eliminate or reduce the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible.

**W.17** We determine the amount of covered windstorm or hail loss before applying the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible. We do not pay for loss that exceeds an applicable limit after application of this deductible.

**W.18** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to covered loss to structures and to covered loss to personal property when those losses are caused by windstorm or hail. We apply the deductible according to the applicable limit for the damaged covered property.

**W.19** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to debris removal only to the extent that debris removal results from covered windstorm or hail loss. We do not apply this deductible to an expense for which coverage does not apply.

**W.20** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to reasonable emergency measures that result from covered windstorm or hail loss. We determine coverage for emergency measures before applying this deductible.

**W.21** We do not cover diminution in value within the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible. We do not cover any part of a covered windstorm or hail loss that you are required to bear under this deductible.

**W.22** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies regardless of whether another person may be responsible for the loss. Our rights against a responsible person are not reduced by your payment of this deductible.

**W.23** Any amount recovered from a responsible person for covered windstorm or hail loss will be handled in accordance with our rights of recovery. Such recovery does not change the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applicable to the loss.

**W.24** We do not cover a loss adjustment expense as part of the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible. We may investigate, adjust, settle, or defend a claim without waiving any right under this deductible.

**W.25** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies even if covered windstorm or hail loss affects property at more than one location. We determine the deductible based on the applicable limit for the covered property at each affected location.

**W.26** We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible without regard to the time required to repair or replace damaged covered property. Delay in repair or replacement does not change this deductible.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail that is excluded from coverage. The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible does not restore coverage removed by an exclusion.

**W.28** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail during construction, repair, or alteration of covered property. Coverage remains subject to all applicable conditions and limitations.

**W.29** We determine the cause of loss from the facts available to us at the time of adjustment. Our application of the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible is based on the covered cause of direct physical loss.

**W.30** An insured must not abandon covered property to us after a covered windstorm or hail loss. We may inspect damaged covered property before payment is made.

**W.31** We do not cover an amount exceeding the applicable limit after deduction of the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible. Payment of a covered windstorm or hail loss does not waive application of this deductible to later covered loss.

**W.32** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies to loss caused by a windstorm or hail event even when the event also causes loss that is not covered. We pay only for the covered portion of direct physical loss after application of this deductible.

**W.33** We may require proof of loss that identifies property damaged by windstorm or hail. An insured must provide such proof when we reasonably request it.

**W.34** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies before we make payment for covered windstorm or hail loss. We will show the deductible applied when we determine the amount payable.

**W.35** We do not cover duplicate payment for the same covered windstorm or hail loss. The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies without duplication of payment.

**W.36** The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible remains applicable unless we agree otherwise in writing. No action by us in investigating or paying a claim changes this deductible.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover "loss" caused by earth movement. We do not cover loss caused by earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by landslide, mudslide, or earth expansion. We do not cover loss caused by volcanic activity.

**W.48** We may require a repair estimate prepared by a qualified contractor or other qualified person. An insured remains responsible for complying with all policy conditions.

**W.49** We may pay for covered loss by repair, replacement, or monetary payment, as permitted by this policy. Our choice does not eliminate the applicable deductible.

**W.50** If we elect to repair or replace damaged covered property, an insured must cooperate with reasonable arrangements for the work. An insured must provide access to the property as reasonably required.

**W.51** An insured must notify us if the property is sold, transferred, or otherwise disposed of after the loss. An insured must also notify us if possession of the property changes.

**W.52** An insured must notify us if another person claims an interest in damaged covered property. We may require information concerning that person's interest.

**W.53** We may deny coverage for prejudice caused by an insured's failure to comply with a duty under this section. We will not deny coverage unless the failure materially affects our ability to investigate, evaluate, or resolve the claim.

**W.54** No insured may abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property only as permitted by this policy or agreed by us.

**W.55** Our payment for a windstorm or hail loss does not establish coverage for any later loss. Each reported loss is evaluated under the policy terms applicable to that loss.

**W.56** A windstorm or hail loss does not change the applicable deductible for unrelated loss. We determine the applicable deductible from the facts of each claim.

**W.57** All policy exclusions, limitations, and conditions remain applicable to a windstorm or hail loss. This endorsement changes only the deductible treatment described in this section.

**W.58** If a provision of this section conflicts with another policy provision concerning the applicable deductible, this section controls. All other policy provisions remain unchanged.

**W.59** An insured must comply with the duties in this section before seeking payment for a windstorm or hail loss. Compliance with those duties does not create coverage where coverage does not otherwise apply.

**W.60** We will adjust each windstorm or hail loss in accordance with the policy terms. We will pay covered loss after application of the applicable deductible.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" to "covered property" under this endorsement in accordance with these provisions. We will not pay more than the amount of the covered loss after application of the applicable "deductible".

**W.2** We determine the amount of loss from the evidence available to us. An insured must provide information that reasonably establishes the cause, extent, and value of the loss.

**W.3** Loss settlement does not create coverage for property or causes of loss that are not covered. We do not cover loss that is excluded or otherwise limited.

**W.4** We apply the deductible applicable to the covered loss before determining the amount payable. An insured must bear the portion of loss represented by the deductible.

**W.5** When windstorm or hail causes a covered loss, we will apply the deductible applicable to that loss. We may require information concerning the event that caused the damage.

**W.6** We may inspect damaged property before making payment. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.7** The "loss settlement basis" for "personal property" is replacement cost. We will determine payment for personal property in accordance with that basis.

**W.8** An insured must identify personal property for which payment is sought. An insured must provide records, photographs, receipts, or other evidence reasonably available to support the claimed loss.

**W.9** We will determine the amount of loss to a "building" from the damaged condition of the property. We may consider the cost to repair or replace damaged materials with materials of like kind and quality.

**W.10** When depreciation is relevant to the applicable method of settlement, we will determine depreciation from the condition, age, expected useful life, and remaining utility of the property. We may consider evidence supplied by an insured or obtained through inspection.

**W.11** We may pay the value of damaged property, the cost to repair damaged property, or the cost to replace damaged property, subject to the applicable basis of settlement. Our payment will not exceed the amount we are obligated to pay for the covered loss.

**W.12** We may elect to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. An insured must cooperate with us and with persons acting on our behalf in connection with that election.

**W.13** If we elect to repair or replace damaged property, we may select the manner, materials, and persons used to perform the work. We will make reasonable efforts to restore the damaged property to its pre-loss condition, subject to the applicable basis of settlement.

**W.14** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We do not cover avoidable damage that results from an insured's failure to take reasonable protective measures.

**W.15** An insured must retain receipts and other records for reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property. We may require documentation before paying those expenses.

**W.16** We may require an insured to exhibit damaged property and undamaged property of the same type. An insured must permit us to examine the property as often as reasonably necessary.

**W.17** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably requested by us. An insured must produce requested records that are material to the adjustment of the loss.

**W.18** We may require a signed statement describing the loss, the property involved, and the ownership interest in that property. An insured must provide that statement when reasonably requested.

**W.19** We may require evidence that damaged property was present at the location of loss. An insured must provide available evidence of ownership, possession, and value.

**W.20** We will not include the value of undamaged property in our settlement of a covered loss. We may include reasonable costs necessary to repair physical damage caused by the covered loss.

**W.21** We will not pay for improvements, betterments, or additions that exceed restoration of the damaged property. We may pay for reasonably necessary work that results from repair methods required by applicable law.

**W.22** We do not cover increased costs caused solely by an insured's preference for materials, construction, or design that differ from the damaged property. An insured may pay any additional cost for such preference.

**W.23** We may account for prior damage, deterioration, wear, corrosion, decay, or defect when determining the amount of a covered loss. We do not pay for conditions that existed before the covered loss unless the conditions were directly damaged by that loss.

**W.24** We may determine the amount of loss from estimates prepared by qualified persons. An insured may provide an estimate for our consideration.

**W.25** We may obtain bids, invoices, reports, photographs, measurements, and other information relevant to the loss. An insured must cooperate in obtaining information reasonably within the insured's control.

**W.26** We will not pay for costs that are not reasonably related to the covered loss. We may exclude charges that are excessive, unsupported, or unrelated to repair or replacement of damaged property.

**W.27** We may make payment to an insured, to a person with a legal interest in the property, or to persons jointly. Payment to a person entitled to receive payment discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.28** We may issue payment jointly to an insured and a mortgagee, lienholder, or other person with a legal interest in covered property. An insured must provide information concerning known legal interests when requested.

**W.29** We will not pay an insured more than the insured's financial interest in covered property. An insured must establish that interest when it is material to settlement.

**W.30** If property is subject to a lease, financing agreement, or other contract, we may consider that agreement in determining the proper payee. We do not assume obligations owed by an insured under that agreement.

**W.31** We may take possession of damaged property for which we make payment when permitted by law. An insured must not abandon property to us unless we agree in writing.

**W.32** We may retain salvage from damaged property after payment for that property. An insured must cooperate in transferring salvage when we request it.

**W.33** We may reduce payment by the value of salvage retained by an insured. An insured must disclose the disposition of damaged property when requested.

**W.34** We may require an insured to assign recovery rights against a person responsible for the loss to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.35** If an insured recovers money from another source for damage for which we have paid, an insured must hold that recovery for us to the extent of our payment. We will not recover more than our payment and reasonable recovery expenses.

**W.36** We will not duplicate payment for the same loss under this endorsement and any other insurance or source of recovery. An insured must disclose other insurance or recovery rights that may apply.

**W.37** We may defer payment while material information concerning the loss remains unavailable. An insured must promptly provide requested information that is reasonably available.

**W.38** We may reopen adjustment of a loss if material information was unavailable when payment was made. We may correct a payment that resulted from fraud, concealment, or material misrepresentation.

**W.39** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts of an insured when coverage is precluded by law. We may deny payment to an insured who conceals or misrepresents a material fact concerning the loss.

**W.40** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve records concerning repair or replacement expenses. We may require copies of contracts, invoices, permits, and proof of payment.

**W.41** The "special assessment limit" applies only as provided by applicable coverage terms. The "fire department service charge limit" applies only as provided by applicable coverage terms.

**W.42** The "jewelry theft sublimit" applies to loss involving jewelry only as provided by applicable coverage terms. The "business property sublimit" applies to property used for business only as provided by applicable coverage terms.

**W.43** We may determine whether property is personal property, building property, or another type of property from its nature, use, and location at the time of loss. An insured must provide information relevant to that determination.

**W.44** We may determine whether property is attached, permanently installed, or readily movable from the facts surrounding the loss. That determination does not alter the ownership interest of an insured.

**W.45** We may settle damage to matching materials based on the damaged area and the availability of reasonably comparable materials. We do not cover replacement of undamaged materials solely because matching materials are unavailable.

**W.46** We may consider reasonable repair methods that return damaged property to functional condition. We do not pay for replacement when repair will reasonably restore the damaged property, unless replacement is required by the applicable basis of settlement.

**W.47** An insured must notify us before permanently repairing or replacing damaged property when inspection is reasonably possible. We may deny payment for damage that cannot be verified because an insured failed to preserve reasonable evidence.

**W.48** Emergency repairs may be made when necessary to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must retain reasonable evidence of the condition of property before emergency repairs when reasonably possible.

**W.49** We may require that repair or replacement work comply with applicable law. We do not cover costs arising from an insured's failure to comply with applicable law before the covered loss.

**W.50** We may consider reasonable market information in determining the value of damaged property. We may also consider the condition and utility of that property immediately before the loss.

**W.51** We may consider the cost of labor, materials, transportation, and disposal that is reasonably necessary to settle a covered loss. We do not pay charges that are unrelated to the covered loss.

**W.52** An insured must notify us of any change in the scope or cost of repair work. We may reevaluate the amount of loss when material conditions are discovered during repair.

**W.53** We may make an advance payment when information available to us supports payment for part of a covered loss. An advance payment does not establish the final amount payable.

**W.54** We may request repayment of an overpayment. An insured must return an overpayment received because of fraud, concealment, material misrepresentation, or an error in settlement.

**W.55** We will determine our obligation from the facts known to us and the terms applicable to the covered loss. Our investigation or payment does not waive any right available to us.

**W.56** An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests made during adjustment of the loss. Failure to cooperate may affect payment when the failure materially prejudices our ability to determine the loss.

**W.57** We may settle any portion of a covered loss separately when the available information permits separate settlement. Separate settlement does not increase the amount payable for the covered loss.

**W.58** We will pay no more than our obligation for covered property damaged by the covered loss. All payments remain subject to the applicable deductible, limitations, exclusions, and conditions.

## W.8 — Definitions

"Windstorm" means wind, including gusts and wind-driven air movement. Windstorm includes damage caused directly by the force of wind.

"Hail" means frozen precipitation that falls from the atmosphere. Hail includes damage caused directly by the impact of hailstones.

"Windstorm Or Hail" means windstorm, hail, or both acting together. Windstorm Or Hail includes conditions arising from the same weather system.

"Windstorm Or Hail Event" means atmospheric conditions involving Windstorm Or Hail. A Windstorm Or Hail Event begins when those conditions first cause direct physical loss.

A Windstorm Or Hail Event continues while related atmospheric conditions remain active. Temporary changes in the intensity of those conditions do not create a separate Windstorm Or Hail Event.

"Direct Physical Loss" means accidental physical injury to tangible property. Direct Physical Loss includes resulting damage that is observable or measurable.

Direct Physical Loss does not include a reduction in market value without physical injury to property. Direct Physical Loss does not include loss of use without physical injury to property.

"Covered Property" means property for which coverage applies. Covered Property includes property only to the extent that the property is subject to a property limit.

"Property Limit" means the limit applicable to Covered Property that sustains Direct Physical Loss. The Property Limit is determined without reduction for any deductible.

"Applicable Limit" means the Property Limit used to determine the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible. The Applicable Limit is the limit that applies to the damaged Covered Property.

When more than one Property Limit applies to the same damaged Covered Property, the Applicable Limit is the limit that most directly applies to that property. We do not combine Property Limits unless the terms applicable to the loss require combination.

"Loss Amount" means the amount of Direct Physical Loss before application of the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible. Loss Amount includes only loss that we cover.

Loss Amount is determined after application of terms that affect valuation. Loss Amount is determined before we subtract any deductible.

"Covered Loss" means Direct Physical Loss for which payment would be due if no deductible applied. Covered Loss does not include loss that is excluded or otherwise not covered.

A Covered Loss may arise from Windstorm Or Hail. A Covered Loss may also include damage caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or debris when that damage results directly from Windstorm Or Hail.

"Wind-Driven Debris" means material moved by Windstorm. Wind-Driven Debris includes objects carried, propelled, or deposited by wind.

Damage caused by Wind-Driven Debris is Windstorm damage when the debris directly causes physical injury to Covered Property. The source of the debris does not change the character of the damage.

"Wind-Driven Precipitation" means rain, snow, sleet, or other precipitation moved by Windstorm. Wind-Driven Precipitation includes precipitation that enters through an opening created directly by Windstorm.

An opening is created directly by Windstorm when wind causes physical damage that permits the entry of precipitation. An opening is not created directly by Windstorm merely because wind is present when water enters.

"Windstorm Or Hail Damage" means Direct Physical Loss caused by Windstorm Or Hail. Windstorm Or Hail Damage includes direct impact damage and resulting damage when the result follows without interruption by an independent cause.

"Resulting Damage" means physical injury that follows from Windstorm Or Hail Damage. Resulting Damage must be a Covered Loss to be included in the Loss Amount.

"Concurrent Cause" means a cause that acts at the same time as Windstorm Or Hail. A Concurrent Cause may contribute to Direct Physical Loss.

When a Concurrent Cause contributes to loss, we determine whether the loss is Windstorm Or Hail Damage from the facts of the loss. The presence of a Concurrent Cause does not by itself prevent application of the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible.

"Separate Cause" means a cause that is independent of Windstorm Or Hail. A Separate Cause may produce damage before, during, or after a Windstorm Or Hail Event.

Damage caused solely by a Separate Cause is not Windstorm Or Hail Damage. Damage caused by Windstorm Or Hail remains Windstorm Or Hail Damage when a Separate Cause does not cause that damage.

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An Occurrence may include a Windstorm Or Hail Event.

Losses arising from the same Windstorm Or Hail Event are treated as arising from the same Occurrence. This treatment applies even when damage appears at different times during that event.

"Damaged Property" means Covered Property that sustains Direct Physical Loss. Damaged Property includes property that requires repair, replacement, or restoration because of that loss.

"Undamaged Property" means Covered Property that has not sustained Direct Physical Loss. Undamaged Property does not become Damaged Property merely because it is adjacent to Damaged Property.

"Repair" means work necessary to restore Damaged Property to its condition immediately before Direct Physical Loss. Repair includes work that is reasonably necessary to correct physical injury.

"Replacement" means substitution of property for Damaged Property. Replacement may involve property of like kind and quality where required by the applicable valuation terms.

"Restoration" means repair, replacement, or other work that returns Damaged Property to a usable condition. Restoration does not include improvement unrelated to Direct Physical Loss.

"Debris Removal" means removal of debris resulting from Direct Physical Loss. Debris Removal is part of a Covered Loss only when the removal is covered.

"Emergency Measures" means reasonable actions taken to protect Covered Property from further Direct Physical Loss. Emergency Measures do not include permanent repair unless permanent repair is reasonably necessary to protect the property.

"Temporary Protection" means materials or work used to reduce the risk of further damage after a Windstorm Or Hail Event. Temporary Protection includes covering an opening created directly by Windstorm Or Hail.

"Physical Injury" means alteration, breakage, deformation, or other material damage to property. Physical Injury does not include expected wear, deterioration, or a condition that existed before the Windstorm Or Hail Event.

"Preexisting Damage" means Physical Injury that existed before the Windstorm Or Hail Event. Preexisting Damage is not Windstorm Or Hail Damage unless Windstorm Or Hail causes additional Direct Physical Loss.

"Additional Damage" means Physical Injury caused by Windstorm Or Hail to property that has Preexisting Damage. Additional Damage is limited to the change in condition caused by Windstorm Or Hail.

"Weather System" means atmospheric conditions associated with Windstorm Or Hail. A Weather System may produce changing wind direction, varying wind speed, or intermittent precipitation.

"Storm Track" means the path or area affected by a Weather System. Storm Track may be established by available weather information and evidence of damage.

"Evidence Of Damage" means information that reasonably supports a determination of Direct Physical Loss. Evidence Of Damage may include physical observations, photographs, reports, and repair information.

"Date Of Loss" means the date on which a Windstorm Or Hail Event causes Direct Physical Loss. When damage occurs over a continuing Windstorm Or Hail Event, the Date Of Loss is the date the event begins.

"Location" means the premises where Damaged Property is situated when Direct Physical Loss occurs. Location includes land and structures only to the extent that they are Covered Property.

"Premises" means the place identified for the Covered Property. Premises includes appurtenant structures and areas used in connection with the Covered Property when coverage applies.

"Structure" means a building or other constructed improvement. Structure includes permanently attached equipment that is part of the building or improvement.

"Personal Property" means movable property that is Covered Property. Personal Property does not include property that is permanently attached to a Structure unless the property is treated as part of that Structure.

"Building Property" means Covered Property that is part of a Structure. Building Property includes materials and fixtures permanently attached to the Structure.

"Appurtenant Structure" means a Structure associated with the Premises. An Appurtenant Structure is separate from the principal Structure.

"Percentage Deductible" means a deductible expressed as a percentage of an Applicable Limit. A Percentage Deductible is determined from the Applicable Limit without regard to the Loss Amount.

"Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible" means the Percentage Deductible that applies to a Covered Loss caused by Windstorm Or Hail. We subtract the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible from the Loss Amount.

The Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible applies separately to each Windstorm Or Hail Event. We do not apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to loss that is not Windstorm Or Hail Damage.

"Deductible Amount" means the amount produced by applying the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to the Applicable Limit. The Deductible Amount is not increased because the Loss Amount exceeds the Applicable Limit.

The Deductible Amount is not reduced because the Loss Amount is less than the Applicable Limit. We subtract no more than the Loss Amount otherwise payable for a Covered Loss.

"Net Loss" means the Loss Amount remaining after subtraction of the applicable deductible. Net Loss is subject to all applicable limits and other terms.

When the Deductible Amount equals or exceeds the Loss Amount, no payment is due for that Covered Loss. When the Loss Amount exceeds the Deductible Amount, we determine payment from the Net Loss.

"Aggregate Loss" means the total Loss Amount arising from the same Windstorm Or Hail Event. Aggregate Loss includes covered damage to all Damaged Property affected by that event.

"Allocation" means the process of assigning Loss Amount to the Applicable Limit for the Damaged Property. Allocation is based on the property that sustained Direct Physical Loss.

When a Covered Loss affects property subject to different Property Limits, we allocate the Loss Amount among those limits. We apply the Windstorm Or Hail Percentage Deductible to the Applicable Limit determined for each allocated loss.

"Claim" means a request for payment for a Covered Loss. A Claim includes information provided to us concerning the claimed Direct Physical Loss.

"Proof Of Loss" means information that supports the amount and cause of a claimed loss. Proof Of Loss includes information reasonably needed to determine whether Windstorm Or Hail caused Direct Physical Loss.

"Insured" means a person or organization entitled to seek payment for a Covered Loss. An Insured includes only a person or organization to whom coverage applies.

"We," "us," and "our" mean the insurer providing this coverage. "You" and "your" mean an Insured.
