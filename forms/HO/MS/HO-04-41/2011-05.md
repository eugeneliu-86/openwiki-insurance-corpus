# HO 04 41 Additional Insured — Residence Premises (Edition 2011-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This attachment applies to the liability insurance provided by us. It extends insured status only as stated in this attachment.

The “additional insured” is the person or organization to whom this attachment applies. The additional insured is an “insured” only with respect to the “residence premises.”

The “residence premises” means the premises to which this attachment applies. The residence premises includes only the part of the premises rented to or occupied by you.

We provide liability coverage to the additional insured for “bodily injury” or “property damage” arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of the residence premises. This coverage applies only to liability otherwise covered by us.

The additional insured has no greater rights than you have under the applicable insurance. The additional insured is subject to all terms that apply to an insured.

An insured must comply with all duties applicable to a claim or suit. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit.

An insured must promptly provide us with information we request concerning a claim or suit. An insured must promptly send us any notice, demand, summons, or legal paper received in connection with a claim or suit.

The additional insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. This duty does not apply to reasonable expenses incurred at our request.

We do not cover bodily injury or property damage that does not arise out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of the residence premises. We do not cover liability arising from any part of the premises not rented to or occupied by you.

We do not cover liability arising from the additional insured’s ownership, maintenance, or use of property other than the residence premises. We do not cover liability arising from an act or omission unrelated to the residence premises.

This attachment does not provide property insurance for the additional insured. This attachment does not create coverage for damage to property owned by, rented to, occupied by, or in the care of the additional insured.

This attachment does not provide coverage for personal injury, advertising injury, or other injury not included within bodily injury. This attachment does not provide coverage for any loss not otherwise covered by us.

The additional insured’s status applies separately from the status of any other insured. The conduct of an insured does not increase the coverage available to another insured.

An insured must not prejudice our rights of recovery. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right to recover from a person or organization responsible for that loss.

Our rights and duties under this attachment apply only to the additional insured in connection with the residence premises. No person or organization becomes an additional insured merely by having an interest in the residence premises.

This attachment does not change the obligations of you or any other insured. An insured remains responsible for complying with all applicable conditions.

We may investigate and settle a claim as we consider appropriate. Our action in investigating or settling a claim does not create a duty to provide coverage where coverage does not otherwise apply.

The additional insured must provide truthful and complete information to us. An insured must correct information previously provided to us when that information is discovered to be incomplete or inaccurate.

No act of an additional insured may expand the insurance provided by this attachment. No statement by an additional insured changes the terms of this attachment unless we agree in writing.

This attachment applies only while the residence premises is rented to or occupied by you. The additional insured’s status ends when this attachment no longer applies to the residence premises.

All provisions of this attachment are subject to the terms applicable to the insurance provided by us. Where this attachment changes those terms, this attachment applies only to the extent of that change.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the coverage described in this endorsement to an "additional insured" with respect to the "residence premises." The additional insured is an insured only as provided by this endorsement.

**W.2** The additional insured has the status of an "insured" for liability arising out of ownership, maintenance, or use of the residence premises. This status applies only to the interest of the additional insured in the residence premises.

**W.3** We cover damages for "bodily injury" or "property damage" for which an insured is legally liable. The bodily injury or property damage must result from an "occurrence."

**W.4** An occurrence must arise from the residence premises. We provide coverage only when the occurrence is within the scope of this endorsement.

**W.5** We will pay damages for bodily injury or property damage for which the additional insured is legally liable. We will not pay damages that an insured is not legally obligated to pay.

**W.6** We will provide a defense for the additional insured against a "suit" seeking covered damages. We may investigate and settle any claim or suit as we consider appropriate.

**W.7** Our duty to defend ends when we have paid the applicable limit for damages arising from the claim or suit. Our duty to defend does not apply to a suit seeking damages that are not covered.

**W.8** Coverage for the additional insured applies only to liability connected with the residence premises. Coverage does not apply to liability arising solely from activities unrelated to the residence premises.

**W.9** We cover liability arising from the condition of the residence premises. We also cover liability arising from maintenance of the residence premises by or for an insured.

**W.10** We cover liability arising from the use of the residence premises by an insured. Such use must be lawful and consistent with the character of the residence premises.

**W.11** We cover liability arising from acts or omissions of an insured in connection with the residence premises. The acts or omissions must not be otherwise excluded.

**W.12** We cover liability arising from the acts or omissions of persons acting on behalf of an insured in connection with the residence premises. The person acting on behalf of an insured must be acting within the scope of that authority.

**W.13** We cover liability arising from the ownership interest of the additional insured in the residence premises. This coverage does not create ownership rights or responsibilities for any person.

**W.14** We cover liability arising from a condition that exists at the residence premises. The condition must have a connection to the additional insured's interest in the residence premises.

**W.15** We cover liability arising from maintenance performed at the residence premises. Maintenance includes ordinary care and upkeep of the residence premises.

**W.16** We cover liability arising from repairs to the residence premises. Repairs must be performed for the benefit of an insured's interest in the residence premises.

**W.17** We cover liability arising from the use of grounds associated with the residence premises. The grounds must be part of the residence premises.

**W.18** We cover liability arising from structures associated with the residence premises. The structures must be located at the residence premises.

**W.19** We cover liability arising from fixtures attached to the residence premises. The fixtures must be used in connection with the residence premises.

**W.20** We cover liability arising from personal property used to maintain the residence premises. The personal property must be under the care or control of an insured.

**W.21** We do not cover liability arising from ownership, maintenance, or use of property other than the residence premises. This exclusion applies even if the liability is asserted against the additional insured.

**W.22** We do not cover liability arising from business activities of the additional insured. This exclusion applies whether the business activity occurs at or away from the residence premises.

**W.23** We do not cover liability arising from professional services performed by or for the additional insured. This exclusion applies to any duty connected with professional services.

**W.24** We do not cover liability arising from the rendering or failure to render medical, dental, nursing, therapeutic, or similar services. This exclusion applies regardless of the capacity in which the services are provided.

**W.25** We do not cover liability arising from an intentional act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies when the bodily injury or property damage is expected or intended by an insured.

**W.26** We do not cover liability arising from a criminal act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not an insured is charged with or convicted of an offense.

**W.27** We do not cover liability arising from a violation of law by an insured. This exclusion applies only to the extent permitted by law.

**W.28** We do not cover liability arising from war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or military action. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing to the loss.

**W.29** We do not cover liability arising from the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies to actual, alleged, or threatened pollutants.

**W.30** We do not cover liability arising from an insured's ownership, operation, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle. This exclusion applies except to the extent coverage is otherwise provided by this policy.

**W.31** We do not cover liability arising from an insured's ownership, operation, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of watercraft. This exclusion applies except to the extent coverage is otherwise provided by this policy.

**W.32** We do not cover liability arising from an insured's ownership, operation, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of aircraft. This exclusion applies to aircraft whether owned, rented, borrowed, or otherwise available to an insured.

**W.33** We do not cover liability arising from the ownership, possession, maintenance, use, or transfer of a firearm. This exclusion applies only to the extent permitted by law.

**W.34** We do not cover liability arising from abuse, molestation, harassment, or similar conduct. This exclusion applies to conduct by an insured or by a person for whom an insured may be legally responsible.

**W.35** We do not cover liability arising from an insured's contractual assumption of liability. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract.

**W.36** We do not cover liability arising from damage to property owned by the additional insured. This exclusion applies whether the property is owned solely or jointly by the additional insured.

**W.37** We do not cover liability arising from damage to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care of an insured. This exclusion applies while the property is in the care of an insured.

**W.38** We do not cover liability arising from damage to property in the custody or control of the additional insured. This exclusion applies to personal property and real property.

**W.39** We do not cover liability arising from an insured's failure to perform a contract. This exclusion applies unless the failure results in bodily injury or property damage otherwise covered by this endorsement.

**W.40** We do not cover punitive, exemplary, multiplied, or similar damages to the extent they are not insurable under applicable law. We do not cover fines, penalties, or assessments imposed by law.

**W.41** The additional insured must promptly notify us of an occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must include information reasonably available about the occurrence.

**W.42** The additional insured must promptly forward to us every demand, notice, summons, or legal paper received in connection with a claim or suit. The additional insured must not withhold information material to our handling of the claim or suit.

**W.43** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of a claim or suit. An insured must assist us in obtaining witnesses, records, and other evidence.

**W.44** An insured must permit us to inspect the residence premises when reasonably necessary to investigate a claim. An insured must preserve evidence relating to a covered occurrence when reasonably possible.

**W.45** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent. This condition does not apply to reasonable expenses incurred to protect persons or property from further damage.

**W.46** An insured must not interfere with our right to investigate, settle, or defend a claim or suit. An insured must not admit liability without our consent.

**W.47** The additional insured must notify us of a change in ownership interest in the residence premises. Coverage for the additional insured applies only while the additional insured has the interest described by this endorsement.

**W.48** The additional insured must notify us if the residence premises is transferred, conveyed, abandoned, or otherwise disposed of. We may determine whether coverage continues after such change.

**W.49** The additional insured must notify us of a material change in the use or occupancy of the residence premises. A material change includes a change that increases the likelihood of liability.

**W.50** The additional insured must use reasonable care to maintain the residence premises in a safe condition. This duty includes taking reasonable action to correct a known hazardous condition.

**W.51** The additional insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further bodily injury or property damage after an occurrence. This duty does not require the additional insured to endanger any person.

**W.52** We may examine an insured under oath while a claim or suit is pending. An insured must provide records and documents reasonably requested by us.

**W.53** We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath concerning a claim or suit. An insured must answer questions truthfully and completely.

**W.54** We may obtain statements from an insured concerning an occurrence, claim, or suit. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for us to evaluate coverage.

**W.55** We have the right to recover from a person responsible for bodily injury or property damage after we make payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.56** If we recover money from a person responsible for bodily injury or property damage, we will apply the recovery as required by law. An insured must cooperate with our recovery efforts.

**W.57** Coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of this policy. If this endorsement conflicts with another provision of this policy, this endorsement applies to the extent of the conflict.

**W.58** The additional insured does not have coverage for loss to the residence premises merely because the additional insured is an insured under this endorsement. Property coverage applies only as otherwise provided by this policy.

**W.59** The additional insured does not have authority to change, cancel, renew, or reinstate this policy unless we agree in writing. The additional insured does not have authority to direct payment under this policy unless we agree in writing.

**W.60** The additional insured may report a claim to us. Reporting a claim does not establish coverage.

**W.61** We may communicate with the additional insured concerning a claim or suit. We may also communicate with any other insured having an interest in the residence premises.

**W.62** Information provided by the additional insured must be accurate and complete. We may rely on information provided by an insured in administering a claim or suit.

**W.63** We do not cover a claim based on concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud by an insured concerning a material fact. This exclusion applies to information provided before or after an occurrence.

**W.64** We do not cover liability arising from a loss that is not accidental. This exclusion applies even if the resulting bodily injury or property damage is unintended.

**W.65** We do not cover liability arising from deterioration, wear, tear, rust, corrosion, rot, or latent defect. This exclusion applies unless bodily injury or property damage results from a covered occurrence.

**W.66** We do not cover liability arising from faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, or compaction. This exclusion applies whether performed by an insured or by another person.

**W.67** We do not cover liability arising from earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudflow, settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of earth. This exclusion applies whether naturally occurring or caused by human activity.

**W.68** We do not cover liability arising from flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of water, or water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether driven by wind or otherwise.

**W.69** We do not cover liability arising from the failure of an insured to comply with a law, ordinance, regulation, or code concerning the residence premises. This exclusion applies only to the extent permitted by law.

**W.70** We do not cover liability arising from the ownership or operation of a farm or agricultural enterprise by the additional insured. This exclusion applies even if farming activity occurs at the residence premises.

**W.71** We do not cover liability arising from an insured's use of the residence premises for commercial lodging, rental, or similar occupancy. This exclusion applies except to the extent coverage is otherwise provided by this policy.

**W.72** We do not cover liability arising from the sale, distribution, furnishing, or serving of alcoholic beverages by the additional insured. This exclusion applies when the additional insured is in the business of such activity.

**W.73** We do not cover liability arising from liability imposed by workers compensation, unemployment compensation, disability benefits, or similar law. This exclusion applies to obligations owed by an insured as an employer.

**W.74** We do not cover liability arising from injury to an employee of an insured that occurs in the course of employment. This exclusion applies whether the insured is liable as an employer or in any other capacity.

**W.75** We do not cover liability arising from communicable disease transmitted by an insured. This exclusion applies to actual, alleged, or threatened transmission.

**W.76** The coverage provided by this endorsement applies separately to the additional insured. This provision does not increase the limits applicable to this policy.

**W.77** The inclusion of the additional insured does not broaden coverage for any other insured. The inclusion of the additional insured does not reduce duties imposed on any insured.

**W.78** We provide coverage to the additional insured only to the extent permitted by law. Any provision that is unenforceable will not affect the enforceability of the remaining provisions.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The "Limit of Liability" applicable to an "additional insured" is the most we will pay for covered damages arising from the additional insured's liability. That limit applies regardless of the number of persons seeking coverage or making claims.

**W.2** The applicable Limit of Liability is the most we will pay for all damages arising from an "occurrence" that results in "bodily injury" or "property damage". All such damages are subject to the same applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.3** The applicable Limit of Liability includes damages awarded by a court and damages agreed to in a settlement approved by us. It also includes damages that an insured is legally obligated to pay because of covered bodily injury or property damage.

**W.4** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from acts, errors, or omissions of an additional insured. It also applies to damages arising from acts, errors, or omissions committed by another person for whom the additional insured is legally responsible.

**W.5** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to all damages arising from a "claim" or "suit" against an additional insured. The manner in which a claim or suit is asserted does not increase that limit.

**W.6** The applicable Limit of Liability applies whether liability is alleged in contract, tort, statute, or otherwise. The legal theory asserted against an additional insured does not increase that limit.

**W.7** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to all damages arising from a covered "loss". Related acts, errors, omissions, conditions, or events do not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.8** The applicable Limit of Liability applies when bodily injury affects more than a single person. The number of persons injured does not increase that limit.

**W.9** The applicable Limit of Liability applies when property damage affects more than a single item of property. The number of items of property damaged does not increase that limit.

**W.10** The applicable Limit of Liability applies when bodily injury and property damage arise from the same occurrence. The presence of both bodily injury and property damage does not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.11** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages sought by an injured person and by persons claiming through that injured person. Claims for care, loss of services, loss of support, or similar damages do not increase that limit.

**W.12** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages sought by owners, occupants, lenders, tenants, guests, or other persons. The status or relationship of a person seeking damages does not increase that limit.

**W.13** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from the ownership, maintenance, or use of the residence premises. Different allegations concerning the residence premises do not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.14** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from a condition of the residence premises. The duration of a condition does not increase that limit.

**W.15** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from a failure to act. A continuing failure to act does not create an additional Limit of Liability.

**W.16** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from related occurrences. Related occurrences are treated as arising from the same occurrence when they result from substantially the same general conditions.

**W.17** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from an occurrence that gives rise to claims at different times. The timing of a claim does not increase that limit.

**W.18** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from an occurrence that gives rise to suits in different forums. The forum in which a suit is brought does not increase that limit.

**W.19** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from an occurrence involving more than a single additional insured. The involvement of other additional insureds does not increase that limit.

**W.20** The applicable Limit of Liability applies separately to each additional insured only as to that additional insured's liability. Separate application does not increase the most we will pay for damages arising from the same occurrence.

**W.21** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased because an additional insured has an interest in the residence premises. The nature or extent of that interest does not create additional coverage.

**W.22** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased because an additional insured is named in more than a single claim or suit. Claims or suits arising from the same occurrence remain subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.23** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased because damages are sought by more than a single claimant. All damages arising from the same occurrence remain subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.24** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased because damages are alleged to have occurred at more than a single location on the residence premises. All damages arising from the same occurrence remain subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.25** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by the addition of an additional insured. The addition of an additional insured does not create separate liability coverage for the residence premises.

**W.26** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by any transfer of an interest in the residence premises. A transfer does not create additional coverage for a prior or subsequent interest holder.

**W.27** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by a change in occupancy, possession, or use of the residence premises. Such a change does not create additional coverage.

**W.28** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by the number of allegations contained in a claim or suit. Multiple allegations arising from the same occurrence remain subject to that limit.

**W.29** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by the number of causes of action asserted against an additional insured. Multiple causes of action arising from the same occurrence remain subject to that limit.

**W.30** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by the number of persons alleged to be responsible for damages. Liability alleged against more than a single person remains subject to that limit.

**W.31** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by the number of insured interests involved in the residence premises. Separate interests in the residence premises do not create separate limits.

**W.32** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by the amount of damages alleged or demanded. A demand in excess of that limit does not increase our obligation to pay.

**W.33** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by a judgment entered against an additional insured. A judgment in excess of that limit does not increase our obligation to pay.

**W.34** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by a settlement amount proposed by a claimant. A proposed settlement in excess of that limit does not increase our obligation to pay.

**W.35** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to covered damages for which an additional insured is legally responsible. We have no obligation to pay damages that exceed the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.36** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to covered damages for which an additional insured and another person are jointly responsible. Joint responsibility does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.37** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to covered damages for which an additional insured is severally responsible. Several responsibility does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.38** The applicable Limit of Liability applies after we have made payment for covered damages arising from an occurrence. Payments made for such damages reduce the amount available for further covered damages arising from that occurrence.

**W.39** The applicable Limit of Liability may be exhausted by payment of covered damages. After that limit is exhausted, we have no further obligation to pay covered damages arising from that occurrence.

**W.40** Payment by us on behalf of an additional insured reduces the amount available under the applicable Limit of Liability. Payment by us on behalf of any other insured also reduces the amount available for the same occurrence.

**W.41** Payment by an additional insured does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability. Payment by another person does not increase our obligation under that limit.

**W.42** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to whether an additional insured has other insurance. The availability of other insurance does not increase our applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.43** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to whether another person may be liable for the same damages. The availability of contribution or indemnity does not increase our applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.44** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to whether a claimant seeks damages directly from us. A direct claim does not increase our obligation under that limit.

**W.45** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to whether an additional insured disputes liability. A dispute concerning liability does not increase our obligation under that limit.

**W.46** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to whether damages are liquidated or unliquidated. The character of the damages sought does not increase that limit.

**W.47** The applicable Limit of Liability applies without regard to whether damages are compensatory, consequential, or derivative. The characterization of damages does not increase that limit.

**W.48** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to damages arising from covered liability of an additional insured only. It does not provide coverage for liability unrelated to the residence premises.

**W.49** The applicable Limit of Liability applies only while coverage is available to the additional insured. It does not extend coverage beyond the liability for which the additional insured is covered.

**W.50** The applicable Limit of Liability does not create a duty to pay any claim or suit that is not covered. It does not alter any condition applicable to coverage.

**W.51** The applicable Limit of Liability does not create a duty to defend after our payment obligation for an occurrence has been exhausted. We may investigate or settle a claim or suit as we consider appropriate before that obligation is exhausted.

**W.52** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to our payment of covered damages only. It does not increase any duty or obligation that we may have concerning a claim or suit.

**W.53** All rights of an additional insured are subject to the applicable Limit of Liability. No act by us, an additional insured, or another person increases that limit.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the amount of a "covered loss" that remains your responsibility. We will pay only the part of a covered loss that exceeds the applicable deductible.

**W.2** The deductible applies to covered loss for which we provide coverage. An "insured" must bear the deductible without contribution from us.

**W.3** We will subtract the applicable deductible from the amount otherwise payable for covered loss. The deductible does not reduce the amount of loss used to determine whether coverage applies.

**W.4** The deductible applies to "property damage" caused by a covered occurrence. The deductible applies whether the property damage is claimed by you or by another person.

**W.5** A deductible applies to a "claim" involving covered loss. The presentation, withdrawal, amendment, or settlement of a claim does not eliminate the applicable deductible.

**W.6** We will apply the deductible to covered loss arising from an "occurrence" covered by us. We may determine whether damage arises from the same occurrence based upon the facts known to us.

**W.7** The deductible applies to covered loss sustained by an "additional insured" to the extent that such person is entitled to coverage. An additional insured has no greater right to payment than you have under this insurance.

**W.8** The deductible applies to covered loss involving the "residence premises." The deductible also applies to covered loss involving property for which coverage is available because of its relationship to the residence premises.

**W.9** We do not cover the deductible portion of a covered loss. An insured must pay that portion even if the covered loss results in liability to another person.

**W.10** The deductible applies before we make payment for covered loss. We may withhold payment otherwise due until the deductible has been satisfied.

**W.11** An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine the applicable deductible. An insured must also provide information concerning the cause, timing, and extent of the covered loss.

**W.12** We may apply the deductible against any amount payable for covered loss. Our application of the deductible does not waive any coverage condition, exclusion, or defense available to us.

**W.13** Payment by us of an amount that includes a deductible adjustment does not establish that the adjustment applies to any other claim. We may correct an error in applying a deductible when the error is discovered.

**W.14** An insured must reimburse us for any deductible amount that we pay on your behalf. We may seek reimbursement from any amount otherwise payable to an insured.

**W.15** We may pay a deductible amount to resolve a covered claim when we consider that payment appropriate. Our payment does not relieve an insured of responsibility for that deductible amount.

**W.16** The deductible applies to covered loss whether repair, replacement, restoration, or payment is selected to resolve the loss. The method used to settle a covered loss does not change the applicable deductible.

**W.17** The deductible applies to the amount of covered loss after we determine the value of damaged property. A disagreement concerning value does not suspend the deductible.

**W.18** We do not cover costs incurred solely to satisfy a deductible. Such costs remain the responsibility of an insured.

**W.19** The deductible applies to covered loss involving damage that develops over time when coverage is otherwise available. We will determine the applicable deductible from the facts concerning the covered loss.

**W.20** The deductible applies to covered loss resulting from related acts, omissions, conditions, or events when we determine that the loss arises from the same occurrence. Our determination will be based on reasonable investigation.

**W.21** An insured must not divide, combine, delay, or present a claim in a manner intended to avoid a deductible. We may apply the deductible as though the claim had been presented in a proper manner.

**W.22** The deductible applies regardless of whether another person may be responsible for the covered loss. We may pursue our rights against a responsible person after making payment.

**W.23** An insured must preserve any right of "recovery" against a person responsible for covered loss. An insured must cooperate with us in obtaining recovery for amounts paid by us.

**W.24** Money recovered from another person may be applied to amounts we paid, including a deductible amount paid by us on your behalf. We will account for recoveries in a manner consistent with the respective interests of us and an insured.

**W.25** The deductible applies before we determine the amount available under any applicable "limit of liability." Payment of the deductible by an insured does not increase that limit of liability.

**W.26** We do not cover any amount exceeding the applicable limit of liability, whether or not an insured has paid the deductible. The deductible does not operate as additional coverage.

**W.27** The deductible applies to covered loss involving "salvage." The value of salvage may be considered in determining the amount of covered loss.

**W.28** An insured must protect damaged property from further loss after a covered occurrence. Reasonable protective measures do not eliminate the deductible.

**W.29** The deductible applies to covered loss even if an insured disputes responsibility for the loss. We may investigate the loss without assuming any obligation to make payment.

**W.30** The deductible applies to a covered loss resolved by "settlement." A settlement does not alter an insured's responsibility for the deductible unless we expressly agree otherwise.

**W.31** We do not cover legal fees, costs, or expenses incurred by an insured to contest the application of a deductible. Such expenses remain the responsibility of the insured who incurs them.

**W.32** An insured must promptly notify us of a covered loss for which payment may be sought. Delay in notice does not remove the deductible.

**W.33** An insured must cooperate in the examination of damaged property and records relating to a covered loss. Failure to cooperate may affect our ability to determine the applicable deductible.

**W.34** The deductible applies to covered loss even if payment is made directly to a person other than an insured. Direct payment does not waive our right to recover a deductible amount from an insured.

**W.35** Our determination of the deductible is subject to correction if material information becomes available. An insured must repay any amount received because a deductible was not properly applied.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss arising from an act or omission of an “additional insured” that is not connected with the “residence premises”. We do not cover liability assumed solely by an additional insured without an obligation otherwise imposed by law.

**W.2** We do not cover “property damage” to property owned by an additional insured. We do not cover loss to property rented to, occupied by, or controlled by an additional insured.

**W.3** We do not cover “bodily injury” to an additional insured. We do not cover consequential loss claimed by an additional insured because of bodily injury to that additional insured.

**W.4** We do not cover loss arising out of business activities conducted by an additional insured. This exclusion applies whether the activity is conducted for profit or not for profit.

**W.5** We do not cover loss arising out of professional services provided by an additional insured. This exclusion applies to advice, design, supervision, inspection, or failure to provide professional services.

**W.6** We do not cover loss arising out of construction, alteration, demolition, or repair performed by an additional insured. This exclusion applies unless the work is solely ordinary maintenance of the residence premises.

**W.7** We do not cover loss arising out of structural work undertaken by an additional insured. We do not cover loss arising from a defect in plans, specifications, materials, or workmanship.

**W.8** We do not cover loss arising out of excavation, grading, filling, or earth moving by an additional insured. We do not cover loss arising from subsidence, settling, slipping, falling away, or collapse of land.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** The "Additional Insured" must comply with the conditions applicable to an insured under this insurance. The duties of the Additional Insured apply only to the extent of the interest for which coverage is provided.

**W.2** An insured must provide information requested by us concerning the Additional Insured's interest in the "residence premises". The information must be complete and accurate.

**W.3** An insured must notify us of a change in the ownership, occupancy, or use of the residence premises that may affect the Additional Insured's interest. Notice must be given without delay after the change becomes known.

**W.4** An insured must notify us of a transfer, assignment, or termination of the Additional Insured's interest in the residence premises. We may rely on information supplied by an insured unless we receive contrary information.

**W.5** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must keep a record of expenses incurred to protect covered property.

**W.6** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss that may involve the Additional Insured. Notice must identify the property involved and the known circumstances of the loss.

**W.7** An insured must notify us without delay if a claim, demand, suit, or proceeding is made against the Additional Insured. An insured must send us copies of legal papers received in connection with the matter.

**W.8** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must assist us in securing evidence and obtaining witnesses when requested.

**W.9** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property as often as reasonably required. An insured must preserve damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.10** An insured must submit to examination under oath when requested by us. An insured must sign the examination transcript if it is accurately recorded.

**W.11** An insured must provide records, documents, photographs, receipts, invoices, and other information reasonably requested by us. An insured must permit us to make copies of the materials provided.

**W.12** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when requested by us. The inventory must describe the property and state the known value, cost, and amount of loss.

**W.13** An insured must show us the damaged property when reasonably requested. An insured must permit us to inspect the property before repair, replacement, disposal, or removal when practicable.

**W.14** An insured must retain salvage when reasonably possible. An insured must not abandon property to us unless we agree in writing.

**W.15** An insured must protect our rights of recovery against any person responsible for a loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.16** An insured must execute documents and take actions reasonably required to transfer recovery rights to us. Our recovery rights apply only to the extent of payment made by us.

**W.17** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses necessary to protect covered property. This condition does not prevent an insured from obtaining emergency services reasonably necessary to prevent further damage.

**W.18** An insured must not admit liability or settle a claim without our consent. An insured may provide truthful information concerning an occurrence or loss.

**W.19** An insured must notify us if other insurance may apply to a loss involving the Additional Insured. An insured must provide available information concerning the other insurance.

**W.20** An insured must preserve evidence relating to the cause and extent of a loss. An insured must not alter, destroy, conceal, or dispose of evidence without our consent when the evidence is material to the claim.

**W.21** An insured must reasonably mitigate loss. Expenses incurred solely to improve property or increase its value are not mitigation expenses.

**W.22** An insured must provide access to the residence premises when reasonably requested by us. Access may be requested for inspection, investigation, appraisal, repair evaluation, or adjustment.

**W.23** An insured must identify persons having knowledge of facts relevant to a claim when requested by us. An insured must provide available contact information for those persons.

**W.27** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to this insurance, a claim, or the Additional Insured's interest. This condition applies before and after a loss.

**W.28** An insured must provide truthful statements to us. An insured must correct information previously provided if the insured learns that the information was materially inaccurate.

**W.52** An action against us may not be maintained unless an insured or the additional insured has complied with these provisions. An action against us may not be maintained unless our obligation has been determined by agreement or final judgment.

**W.53** An insured must not impair our right to recover from a person or organization responsible for a loss. We may require an insured or the additional insured to cooperate in enforcing that right.

**W.54** Our inspection of the residence premises does not constitute an undertaking to determine that the residence premises is safe or complies with applicable law. Our inspection does not create a duty to an insured, the additional insured, or any other person.

**W.55** The additional insured must keep its financial interest in the residence premises separate from any unrelated interest or activity. The additional insured must provide information reasonably requested by us to establish that separation.

**W.56** The additional insured must promptly notify us if it becomes aware of a dispute concerning ownership, possession, occupancy, or control of the residence premises. The additional insured must provide documents relating to that dispute when requested by us.

**W.57** The additional insured must not use our name, logo, or communications in a manner that suggests approval of the residence premises or its condition. The additional insured must correct any such suggestion upon our request.

**W.58** A dispute between an insured and the additional insured does not create coverage or increase our obligations. We may investigate the dispute to the extent reasonably necessary to determine our obligations.

**W.59** If a provision of this section is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by applicable law. An invalid or unenforceable provision does not expand coverage.

**W.60** The additional insured's status ends when its financial interest in the residence premises ends. Ending that status does not affect our duties concerning a claim or suit arising from an occurrence that took place while the status existed.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle covered property losses according to the terms of this policy. The amount of loss will be determined after application of all applicable exclusions, conditions, deductibles, and limits.

**W.2** We will not pay more than your financial interest in covered property at the time of loss. An insured must establish that financial interest when we request reasonable documentation.

**W.3** We will determine the value of damaged property at the time of loss. We may consider age, condition, quality, use, obsolescence, and other relevant characteristics of the property.

**W.4** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may make payment for the loss instead of repairing, rebuilding, or replacing the property.

**W.5** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred solely to protect covered property from further damage when those expenses are covered by this policy.

**W.6** We will not pay for any increase in loss caused by an insured's failure to protect covered property. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.7** We may inspect damaged property before determining the amount we will pay. An insured must provide us reasonable access to the residence premises and to the damaged property.

**W.8** We may require an insured to submit records, receipts, photographs, estimates, invoices, and other information that reasonably supports a claim. An insured must cooperate with our investigation and adjustment of the loss.

**W.9** We may obtain estimates for repair, rebuilding, or replacement of covered property. We may use those estimates in determining the reasonable cost of repair, rebuilding, or replacement.

**W.10** We will not pay for repair or replacement that improves property beyond its condition immediately before the loss. We may pay for reasonable repair methods that are necessary to restore damaged property.

**W.11** We may elect to repair or replace damaged property through persons selected by us. An insured must cooperate with reasonable arrangements made for that repair or replacement.

**W.12** If we elect to repair or replace damaged property, we may select the materials, methods, and contractors used for that work. We will use materials and workmanship that are reasonably comparable to the damaged property.

**W.13** We will settle loss to the dwelling at replacement cost without deduction for depreciation when the loss is covered and the property is repaired or replaced. We will not pay more than the reasonable cost to repair or replace the damaged property.

**W.14** If the dwelling is not repaired or replaced, we will settle the loss at actual cash value. Actual cash value means replacement cost less a deduction for depreciation and physical deterioration.

**W.15** We will settle loss to other structures according to the method applicable to the dwelling. An insured must establish that the damaged structure was covered property at the time of loss.

**W.16** We will settle loss to personal property at actual cash value unless this policy provides replacement cost coverage for that property. We may require an insured to replace the property before we pay replacement cost.

**W.17** When replacement cost coverage applies to personal property, we will not pay more than the reasonable cost to replace the property with property of like kind and quality. We may consider function, capacity, condition, and available comparable property.

**W.18** We will not pay replacement cost for property that an insured does not intend to repair or replace. We will settle that loss at actual cash value.

**W.19** An insured must provide reasonable evidence of repair or replacement when we request it. Evidence may include contracts, invoices, receipts, photographs, payment records, or other documentation reasonably acceptable to us.

**W.20** We will not pay for loss to property that is not covered property merely because it is located at the residence premises. An insured must identify the damaged property and describe the nature and extent of the damage.

**W.21** We will settle loss to property subject to a special limit according to that special limit. This includes property subject to the "special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones".

**W.22** We will apply the applicable limitation before determining any payment for covered loss. We will not combine separate limitations to increase the amount payable for the same property or loss.

**W.23** We will settle a loss involving property used in connection with a business according to the coverage provided for that use. The "home business contents limit" applies to property within its scope.

**W.24** We will settle a loss involving an assessment according to the coverage provided for that assessment. The "assessment coverage limit" applies to covered assessments.

**W.25** We will settle a covered fire department or emergency service charge according to the applicable policy terms. The "fire response charge" applies only when the charge is covered by this policy.

**W.26** We will not pay for undamaged property unless its repair or replacement is necessary because of direct physical loss to covered property. We may pay for work affecting undamaged property when that work is reasonably necessary to complete covered repairs.

**W.27** We will not pay for matching of undamaged property unless matching is necessary to produce a reasonably uniform appearance after covered repairs. Our payment for matching will not exceed the reasonable cost of the necessary matching work.

**W.28** We may settle any covered loss with you, a mortgagee, another person having an insurable interest, or any combination of those parties. Payment to a person with an insurable interest will not exceed that person's interest in the damaged property.

**W.29** We may issue payment jointly when more than one person has an interest in the damaged property. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning any such interest.

**W.30** We may pay for covered repairs directly to a contractor or service provider when an insured authorizes that payment or when payment is otherwise appropriate. Payment to a contractor or service provider does not create a contractual relationship between us and that person.

**W.31** We will not be responsible for the quality, timing, or completion of work performed by a contractor chosen by an insured. An insured must resolve disputes concerning that contractor's work directly with the contractor.

**W.32** We may recover any payment that exceeds the amount properly payable under this policy. An insured must return an overpayment when we request its return.

**W.33** If covered property is recovered after we make payment for its loss, we may require an insured to return the payment or transfer the recovered property to us. We will determine the appropriate adjustment based on the condition and value of the recovered property.

**W.34** We may take possession of damaged property for which we make payment when we are entitled to do so. An insured must transfer any interest in that property that is necessary for us to obtain possession.

**W.35** We will not pay for loss caused by an insured's intentional concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud concerning the claim. An insured must provide truthful information in connection with a claim.

**W.36** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath when reasonably necessary to investigate a claim. An insured must sign and provide any recorded statement or other claim documentation that we reasonably request.

**W.37** We may require an insured to prepare an inventory of damaged personal property. The inventory must describe the property and provide available information concerning its value, age, and condition.

**W.38** We may require an insured to preserve receipts, records, and other evidence supporting a claimed loss. An insured must provide copies of that evidence when reasonably available.

**W.39** We will determine the amount of covered loss after considering all relevant information available to us. A disagreement concerning the amount of loss does not require us to pay an amount not otherwise covered by this policy.

**W.40** Our payment for a covered loss will not exceed the amount payable under the applicable coverage. Payment of a claim does not waive any policy condition, exclusion, limitation, or right available to us.

## W.8 — Definitions

"Additional insured" means a person or organization to whom insured status applies with respect to the "residence premises." An additional insured has only the rights and duties stated for an insured.

"Insured" means you and any additional insured when the applicable requirements are met. Insured does not include a person or organization solely because that person or organization has an interest in property.

"You" means the person or organization identified as the insured. Your duties apply to each insured who seeks coverage.

"We," "us," and "our" mean the insurer providing this coverage. Our obligations are limited to those assumed under this coverage.

"Residence premises" means the premises used as a residence by you. Residence premises includes land and structures that are part of the residential property.

"Premises" means the location where the residence premises is situated. Premises includes areas used in connection with the residence premises.

"Residence" means a dwelling used as a home. A residence does not include property used solely for business purposes.

"Dwelling" means a building or portion of a building used principally for residential purposes. Dwelling includes structures attached to the building.

"Structure" means a building or other constructed property. Structure includes a fixture permanently attached to land or a building.

"Attached structure" means a structure joined to the dwelling by a permanent connection. An attached structure is considered part of the dwelling.

"Detached structure" means a structure that is not attached to the dwelling. A detached structure remains separate from the dwelling even if it is used in connection with the residence premises.

"Land" means the ground forming part of the residence premises. Land includes areas customarily used with the dwelling.

"Property" means tangible property located at or connected with the residence premises. Property does not include an interest that exists only by contract.

"Personal property" means movable property owned or used by an insured. Personal property does not include land or a building.

"Real property" means land and structures affixed to land. Real property includes fixtures that cannot be removed without material damage.

"Fixture" means property permanently attached to real property. A fixture is treated as part of the real property to which it is attached.

"Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain. Business includes activities conducted from the residence premises.

"Business property" means property used principally for business. Business property does not cease to be business property because it is also used personally.

"Loss" means accidental physical loss or accidental physical damage. Loss includes resulting injury to property when caused by an accidental event.

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. Occurrence does not include an expected or intended injury from the standpoint of an insured.

"Accident" means an unexpected event resulting in injury or damage. Accident includes an unintended result of an intentional act only when the injury or damage was not expected or intended.

"Bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. Bodily injury includes resulting death.

"Property damage" means physical injury to tangible property. Property damage includes loss of use of tangible property that is not physically injured.

"Personal injury" means injury arising from conduct that affects a person's rights, reputation, or liberty. Personal injury does not include bodily injury.

"Damages" means sums that an insured is legally obligated to pay because of injury or damage. Damages includes prejudgment interest when imposed as part of that legal obligation.

"Suit" means a civil proceeding seeking damages because of injury or damage. Suit includes an arbitration proceeding when damages are sought and the insured must participate.

"Claim" means a demand for damages or other relief. Claim includes a demand made directly against an insured.

"Insured location" means the residence premises and any location used by an insured in connection with the residence premises. Insured location does not include property that an insured owns solely for business purposes.

"Insured status" means the capacity in which a person or organization is treated as an insured. Insured status applies only with respect to liability arising from the residence premises.

"Liability" means legal responsibility for damages because of injury or damage. Liability includes responsibility imposed by law or assumed by contract when coverage applies.

"Contract" means an agreement creating legal rights or duties. Contract includes a written agreement and an enforceable oral agreement.

"Written agreement" means an agreement reduced to writing and executed by the parties. A written agreement includes a signed amendment.

"Lease" means an agreement granting possession or use of property. Lease includes a rental agreement for residential property.

"Owner" means a person or organization holding legal title to property. Owner includes a person or organization with a beneficial ownership interest.

"Tenant" means a person or organization entitled to occupy property under a lease. Tenant includes a subtenant.

"Occupant" means a person residing at or using the residence premises with permission. Occupant does not include a trespasser.

"Permission" means consent given by a person authorized to grant it. Permission may be express or implied from conduct.

"Family member" means a person related to you by blood, marriage, or adoption who resides with you. Family member includes a ward or foster person who resides with you.

"Resident" means a person who lives at the residence premises with an intent to remain there. Resident status depends on the person's actual use of the residence premises.

"Employee" means a person performing services for an insured in exchange for compensation. Employee does not include an independent contractor.

"Independent contractor" means a person or organization performing work while retaining control over the means and methods of that work. Independent contractor is not an employee solely because an insured directs the desired result.

"Agent" means a person or organization authorized to act for another person or organization. Agent authority may be limited by the terms of the authorization.

"Representative" means a person or organization acting for an insured in a permitted capacity. Representative does not become an insured solely by acting for an insured.

"Legal representative" means a person or organization authorized by law to act for another person or organization. Legal representative has only the authority granted by law or a valid appointment.

"Notice" means information delivered in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the recipient. Notice includes written communication and electronic communication when permitted by law.

"Knowledge" means actual awareness of facts or circumstances. Knowledge of an insured is not automatically knowledge of another insured.

"Concealment" means withholding a material fact. Concealment includes failing to disclose information when an insured has a duty to disclose it.

"Misrepresentation" means a false statement of material fact. Misrepresentation includes an incomplete statement that creates a false impression.

"Material" means having a natural tendency to influence a decision concerning coverage, liability, or payment. Material does not require proof that a decision was actually changed.

"Vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary for customary residential use. Vacant does not mean temporarily unoccupied.

"Unoccupied" means not being lived in or used as a residence. Unoccupied status does not by itself establish vacancy.

"Intentional act" means an act performed deliberately. Intentional act does not include an accidental result that was neither expected nor intended by the insured.
