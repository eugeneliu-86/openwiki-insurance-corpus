# HO 04 55 Identity Fraud Expense Coverage (Edition 2017-01)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement modifies insurance provided under your "policy." The provisions of this endorsement apply only to the extent that they are included in your policy.

The insurance provided by this endorsement applies to "identity fraud expense coverage." We provide that coverage subject to all terms of this endorsement and the policy.

"Identity fraud" means the wrongful use of an insured's identifying information without that insured's knowledge or authorization. Identity fraud includes the use of such information to obtain credit, property, services, or other benefits.

"Identifying information" means information that may be used to identify an individual. Identifying information includes personal, financial, medical, governmental, electronic, and biometric information.

An "insured" means a person who qualifies as an insured under the policy. An insured seeking coverage under this endorsement must comply with the duties imposed by the policy and this endorsement.

A "covered expense" means an expense that results directly from identity fraud and is otherwise payable under this endorsement. A covered expense must be reasonable, necessary, and actually incurred by an insured.

We will pay covered expenses only when identity fraud occurs during the policy period. We will not pay an expense unless the insured discovers the identity fraud during the policy period or after its expiration.

Coverage under this endorsement applies only to identity fraud directed against an insured. We do not cover expenses arising from identity fraud directed solely against a business, organization, trust, estate, or other entity.

This endorsement does not create coverage for loss of money, securities, property, or income. It provides coverage only for covered expenses resulting from identity fraud.

An insured must take reasonable steps to protect identifying information from unauthorized use. An insured must also take reasonable steps to limit further expenses after discovering identity fraud.

An insured must notify us or our representative promptly after discovering identity fraud. Notice must include information sufficient to identify the insured and describe the identity fraud.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of any claim. An insured must provide records, receipts, correspondence, and other information we reasonably request.

An insured must report identity fraud to an appropriate law enforcement authority when reasonably necessary to investigate the claim. An insured must provide us with information concerning that report when we request it.

We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath. We may also require an insured to provide a signed statement describing the identity fraud and the covered expenses claimed.

We do not cover expenses caused by an insured's dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, or intentional act. We do not cover expenses caused by any person acting with an insured's knowledge or consent.

We do not cover expenses arising from identity fraud involving information voluntarily provided by an insured to a person known by the insured to be engaged in an unlawful activity. We do not cover expenses arising from a scheme in which an insured participates.

We do not cover expenses arising from a business pursuit, employment activity, professional service, or commercial transaction. We do not cover expenses that are payable under a contract, agreement, warranty, or other insurance.

We do not cover expenses for which an insured has no legal obligation. We do not cover expenses that are speculative, punitive, consequential, or unrelated to identity fraud.

Payment under this endorsement does not waive any policy condition or exclusion. All policy terms that do not conflict with this endorsement remain applicable.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of the policy, this endorsement controls with respect to identity fraud expense coverage. The policy controls with respect to all other coverage.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the coverage described in this section for "identity fraud expense" resulting from an "identity fraud event." This coverage applies only to an "insured" who sustains covered identity fraud expense.

**W.2** "Identity fraud event" means the unauthorized use, or attempted unauthorized use, of a covered identity. The use or attempted use must be for an unlawful purpose.

**W.3** "Covered identity" means the identity of an insured. A covered identity also includes the identity of an insured's dependent relative residing with you.

**W.4** "Identity fraud expense" means reasonable and necessary expense incurred by an insured as a direct result of an identity fraud event. The expense must be incurred after the insured discovers the identity fraud event.

**W.5** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to prepare affidavits and other documents required by financial institutions, governmental agencies, merchants, or similar entities. The documents must relate directly to an identity fraud event.

**W.6** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to send documents by certified mail, registered mail, courier service, or another delivery method requiring proof of delivery. The delivery must be reasonably necessary to respond to an identity fraud event.

**W.7** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to obtain copies of credit reports, account statements, transaction records, or similar documents. The documents must be reasonably necessary to establish or resolve an identity fraud event.

**W.8** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to reproduce documents required to report, investigate, dispute, or correct an identity fraud event. The reproduction expense must be reasonable.

**W.9** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for charges assessed to replace an identification document, financial instrument, access device, or similar item. The replacement must be required because of an identity fraud event.

**W.10** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for charges assessed to restore the use of a financial account. The account restoration must be necessary because of an identity fraud event.

**W.11** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for charges assessed by a financial institution to stop payment, close an account, open a replacement account, or transfer funds. The charge must result directly from an identity fraud event.

**W.12** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for fees charged to obtain records from a court, governmental agency, financial institution, credit reporting agency, merchant, or similar entity. The records must relate directly to an identity fraud event.

**W.13** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for legal fees and court costs incurred with our prior consent. The legal services must be necessary to correct records or defend against an action arising from an identity fraud event.

**W.14** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for notary charges. The notarized document must be reasonably necessary to address an identity fraud event.

**W.15** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for telephone charges incurred to contact financial institutions, governmental agencies, credit reporting agencies, merchants, law enforcement authorities, or similar entities. The contacts must relate directly to an identity fraud event.

**W.16** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for travel charges reasonably necessary to meet with law enforcement authorities, financial institutions, governmental agencies, or legal counsel. The meeting must concern an identity fraud event.

**W.17** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for childcare or eldercare reasonably necessary while an insured attends a meeting concerning an identity fraud event. The meeting must be required by a governmental agency, financial institution, law enforcement authority, or legal counsel.

**W.18** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred for lost wages. The lost wages must result from time away from work reasonably necessary to address an identity fraud event.

**W.19** We will reimburse lost wages only when the insured is employed for compensation. The insured must provide records establishing the wages lost because of the identity fraud event.

**W.20** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to obtain a replacement driver's license, passport, identification card, or other government-issued identification document. The replacement must be necessary because the document was lost, stolen, altered, or misused in connection with an identity fraud event.

**W.21** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to correct information contained in consumer reports, financial records, public records, medical records, employment records, or similar records. The inaccurate information must result from an identity fraud event.

**W.22** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to place, remove, or maintain a fraud alert, security freeze, or similar protective measure. The measure must be reasonably necessary because of an identity fraud event.

**W.23** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to obtain assistance from an identity restoration service when we approve that service. The assistance must relate directly to the resolution of an identity fraud event.

**W.24** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to prepare and submit a police report, governmental report, or financial institution report. The report must concern an identity fraud event.

**W.25** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to respond to collection activity arising from an identity fraud event. The collection activity must be based on an obligation that the insured did not authorize.

**W.26** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to dispute a transaction, account, loan, service agreement, or other obligation created through an identity fraud event. The dispute must be made in good faith.

**W.27** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred to obtain professional assistance in restoring an insured's identity records. The professional assistance must be reasonable and necessary.

**W.28** We will reimburse identity fraud expense only when it is incurred by an insured. We will not reimburse expense incurred by a business, organization, estate, trust, or other person that is not an insured.

**W.29** We will reimburse identity fraud expense only when the identity fraud event occurs during the policy period. An identity fraud event is considered to occur when an insured first discovers facts that would cause a reasonable person to suspect unauthorized use of a covered identity.

**W.30** We will reimburse identity fraud expense incurred after the policy period if the expense results from an identity fraud event that occurred during the policy period. The insured must comply with the duties stated in this section.

**W.31** We will reimburse identity fraud expense only to the extent it is not paid or payable by a financial institution, merchant, governmental agency, credit reporting agency, employer, service provider, or another source. We will not pay more than the amount actually incurred by the insured.

**W.32** We will reimburse identity fraud expense that is reasonable under the circumstances. We may require documentation supporting the amount, purpose, and necessity of the expense.

**W.33** We do not cover loss of money, securities, property, funds, wages, income, profits, or benefits. We do not cover any obligation incurred through an identity fraud event.

**W.34** We do not cover unauthorized charges, withdrawals, transfers, loans, purchases, or other transactions. This exclusion applies whether or not an insured is legally responsible for the transaction.

**W.35** We do not cover damage to credit standing, credit history, credit capacity, credit rating, reputation, or character. We do not cover any alleged loss resulting from diminished access to credit.

**W.36** We do not cover emotional distress, mental anguish, pain, suffering, humiliation, embarrassment, or similar nonfinancial harm. We do not cover expense incurred to treat or evaluate such harm.

**W.37** We do not cover bodily injury, sickness, disease, disability, or death. We do not cover expense arising from medical treatment or healthcare services.

**W.38** We do not cover fines, penalties, punitive damages, exemplary damages, sanctions, or assessments. We do not cover expense imposed because an insured violated a law, regulation, contract, or court order.

**W.39** We do not cover expense arising from a dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, malicious, or intentional act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if another person also committed an act contributing to the identity fraud event.

**W.40** We do not cover expense arising from an insured's knowing disclosure of account information, access information, authentication information, or personal information to a person not authorized to receive it. This exclusion does not apply when the disclosure results from fraud or deception directed at the insured.

**W.41** We do not cover expense arising from an identity fraud event involving business, professional, commercial, or employment activities. We do not cover expense associated with an account or obligation maintained primarily for such activities.

**W.42** We do not cover expense arising from a dispute over a contract, debt, account, service, ownership interest, or financial obligation when no identity fraud event occurred. We do not cover expense incurred solely to contest a lawful obligation.

**W.43** We do not cover expense arising from an insured's failure to safeguard personal information when that failure was intentional. We do not cover expense arising from an insured's participation in a scheme intended to obtain an improper benefit.

**W.44** We do not cover expense arising from war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, civil commotion, or military action. We do not cover expense arising from seizure or destruction of property by governmental authority.

**W.45** We do not cover expense arising from the release, disclosure, or loss of information caused by a service provider when no unauthorized use of a covered identity occurs. We do not cover preventive measures undertaken solely because information may have been exposed.

**W.46** We do not cover expense incurred before discovery of an identity fraud event. We do not cover expense incurred to investigate a suspected event when no reasonable basis exists to believe unauthorized use occurred.

**W.47** We do not cover expense that is excessive, unnecessary, duplicative, or unrelated to an identity fraud event. We do not cover expense for services that we determine were not reasonably necessary.

**W.48** We do not cover fees charged by an insured, a relative of an insured, or a business controlled by an insured or relative of an insured. We do not cover expense for services that are ordinarily performed without charge.

**W.49** An insured must give us prompt notice of an identity fraud event. The notice must describe the known facts and identify the covered identity involved.

**W.50** An insured must notify appropriate law enforcement authorities when reasonably necessary to investigate or resolve an identity fraud event. We may require evidence that the report was made.

**W.51** An insured must notify financial institutions, merchants, credit reporting agencies, governmental agencies, and other affected entities when reasonably necessary to prevent further unauthorized use. The insured must take reasonable steps to protect the covered identity from additional misuse.

**W.52** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. The insured must provide information, records, authorizations, and documents that we reasonably request.

**W.53** An insured must send us copies of notices, demands, collection letters, account statements, reports, correspondence, legal papers, and other documents relating to an identity fraud event. The insured must send the materials promptly after receiving them.

**W.54** An insured must provide records supporting each claimed identity fraud expense. The records must show that the expense was incurred and that it resulted directly from an identity fraud event.

**W.55** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request. The insured must answer questions truthfully and provide requested documentation.

**W.56** An insured must assist us in obtaining reimbursement from a person or organization that may be responsible for identity fraud expense. The insured must not prejudice our rights to recover amounts that we pay.

**W.57** An insured must take reasonable steps to mitigate identity fraud expense. The insured must not incur expense that could reasonably be avoided.

**W.58** An insured must retain documents and records relating to an identity fraud event until we complete our handling of the claim. The insured must preserve evidence that may assist in establishing the occurrence or scope of the event.

**W.59** An insured must promptly inform us if another person or organization pays, offers to pay, or becomes obligated to pay identity fraud expense. We will reduce payment by amounts paid or payable from another source.

**W.60** An insured must obtain our consent before incurring legal fees or court costs for which reimbursement is sought. We may withhold consent when the proposed services are not reasonably necessary.

**W.61** An insured must not make a voluntary payment, assume an obligation, or incur expense for which reimbursement is sought without our consent when consent is reasonably requested. This duty does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable emergency action to protect a covered identity.

**W.62** An insured must provide a signed statement describing the identity fraud event when we reasonably request it. The statement must identify known unauthorized accounts, transactions, obligations, and affected records.

**W.63** An insured must authorize us to obtain records relevant to the claim when we reasonably request authorization. The authorization must be limited to information reasonably necessary to determine coverage or the amount of identity fraud expense.

**W.64** We may investigate an identity fraud event and determine the amount of covered identity fraud expense. Our investigation does not waive any provision of this coverage.

**W.65** We may communicate with financial institutions, merchants, credit reporting agencies, governmental agencies, law enforcement authorities, legal counsel, and other persons having information relevant to a claim. We may do so to verify information, assist in resolving an identity fraud event, or protect our rights.

**W.66** We may require an insured to use a service provider selected or approved by us for identity restoration services. We will not be responsible for expense incurred through an unapproved service provider when approval was reasonably available.

**W.67** We will pay covered identity fraud expense to the insured who incurred the expense unless another payee is authorized by us. Payment to a service provider or other payee does not increase the amount we owe.

**W.68** We will not duplicate payment for the same identity fraud expense. We may pay a covered expense directly to a person or organization providing goods or services to an insured.

**W.69** Our payment of identity fraud expense does not constitute an admission that an insured is responsible for an account, transaction, debt, or obligation. Our payment does not require us to contest, settle, or assume an obligation in the insured's name.

**W.70** We may deny payment for identity fraud expense when an insured materially misrepresents or conceals information relating to the claim. We may recover amounts paid because of such misrepresentation or concealment.

**W.71** We may require repayment of amounts paid for identity fraud expense that is later paid by another source. The insured must hold such recovered amounts for us to the extent of our payment.

**W.72** We are subrogated to the rights of an insured against a person or organization responsible for identity fraud expense that we pay. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.73** We may pursue recovery in the name of an insured when necessary to enforce our rights. An insured must execute documents and take reasonable action to assist with that recovery.

**W.74** We will determine coverage based on the facts known to us and the terms of this section. We may reconsider a payment determination if we receive material information not previously available.

**W.75** Coverage applies only to identity fraud expense arising from an identity fraud event affecting a covered identity. Coverage does not apply merely because an insured receives a notice of a data incident or possible disclosure of information.

**W.76** Coverage applies only when the identity fraud event causes identity fraud expense. The existence of unauthorized use alone does not establish that an expense is covered.

**W.77** The duties of an insured apply to every person seeking payment under this coverage. Failure to comply with a duty may affect coverage when the failure prejudices us.

**W.78** We will provide coverage in accordance with the provisions of this section. All payments under this coverage are subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions stated in this section.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** We will pay covered "identity fraud expenses" subject to this provision. The most we will pay for covered identity fraud expenses is fifteen thousand dollars.

**W.2** Coverage applies only to expenses that result directly from an act of "identity fraud". An "insured" must establish that direct connection.

**W.3** An insured must incur an expense in good faith. An insured must not seek payment for an expense that is not reasonably necessary to address identity fraud.

**W.4** An insured must provide records supporting the expense for which payment is requested. The records must reasonably identify the service or item obtained.

**W.5** An insured must provide records showing that the expense was paid or that the insured is legally obligated to pay it. We may defer payment while the insured's obligation remains uncertain.

**W.6** An insured must cooperate with us in reviewing a request for payment. This cooperation includes providing information that we reasonably request.

**W.7** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent additional identity fraud after becoming aware of it. An insured must not increase an expense through delay or inaction.

**W.8** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting a person or organization that provides services relating to identity fraud. We may require information concerning the services provided.

**W.9** We will determine whether an expense is covered based on the facts available to us. An insured must provide additional information when the available facts do not reasonably support payment.

**W.10** We do not cover an expense that is speculative or contingent. We do not cover an expense that has not been incurred or legally owed.

**W.11** We do not cover an expense that is not directly related to identity fraud. We do not cover an expense arising from an insured's voluntary assumption of another person's obligation.

**W.12** We do not cover an expense that is unreasonable in amount or unnecessary in nature. We may determine the reasonable value of a covered service or item.

**W.13** An insured must make reasonable efforts to obtain available reimbursement from a responsible person or organization. An insured must not impair our rights of recovery.

**W.14** Payment by us does not relieve an insured of any duty owed to a person or organization. Payment by us does not establish liability for identity fraud.

**W.15** An insured must preserve documents, communications, and other information relating to identity fraud. An insured must make those materials available to us upon request.

**W.16** An insured must promptly notify us of a request for payment. Notice must include sufficient information for us to evaluate the request.

**W.17** An insured must notify us of material changes affecting a request for payment. A material change includes information showing that an expense is no longer owed or has been reimbursed.

**W.18** We may request that an insured submit to an examination under oath regarding identity fraud or a requested payment. An insured must cooperate with that examination.

**W.19** We may request authorization to obtain records relevant to a requested payment. An insured must provide authorization when it is reasonably necessary for our investigation.

**W.20** An insured must provide truthful and complete information concerning identity fraud. We may deny payment for an expense affected by concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud.

**W.21** An insured must not make a payment arrangement that unreasonably increases a covered expense. We are not responsible for charges resulting from an unreasonable arrangement.

**W.22** We may pay a covered expense to an insured or directly to a person or organization providing the related service. Payment to a person or organization does not create a contractual relationship between us and that person or organization.

**W.23** We may require an insured to repay an amount that we paid for an expense later shown not to be covered. An insured must reimburse us for that amount upon our request.

**W.24** An insured must notify us if an expense is paid or reimbursed by another source. We will not pay an expense to the extent it has been paid or reimbursed by another source.

**W.25** An insured must not abandon property, documents, or rights relating to identity fraud without our consent. We may require reasonable measures to protect our interests.

**W.26** We may investigate identity fraud and any requested payment. Our investigation does not waive any policy condition, exclusion, or defense.

**W.27** We may settle a request for payment when we determine that settlement is appropriate. An insured must cooperate in carrying out a settlement that we make.

**W.28** An insured must not incur an expense for services that duplicate services already available without charge from another source. We do not cover duplicated services unless they are reasonably necessary.

**W.29** An insured must use reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects of identity fraud. We do not cover expenses resulting from an insured's failure to take reasonable mitigation measures.

**W.30** We do not cover a penalty, fine, or assessment imposed on an insured. We do not cover an expense arising from a criminal, dishonest, or fraudulent act committed by an insured.

**W.31** We do not cover an expense arising from a business, trade, profession, or occupation of an insured. We do not cover an expense arising from an obligation of an organization in which an insured has an interest.

**W.32** We do not cover an expense arising from bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury. We do not cover an expense for which coverage is prohibited by law.

**W.33** An insured must comply with lawful requests from a government agency or financial institution relating to identity fraud. An insured must provide us with information concerning those requests when relevant to a requested payment.

**W.34** We may require proof that an insured has taken reasonable action to correct inaccurate information resulting from identity fraud. An insured must provide that proof upon our request.

**W.35** An insured must retain copies of records submitted to us when reasonably possible. We may rely on copies unless original records are reasonably necessary.

**W.36** Our payment of an expense is not an admission of coverage for any other expense. Each request for payment will be evaluated under the facts applicable to that request.

**W.37** An insured must comply with all duties stated in this provision. Failure to comply may affect coverage when the failure prejudices us.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the portion of a "covered loss" for which you remain responsible. We apply the deductible as provided in this section.

**W.2** A covered loss is subject to the deductible when the loss is otherwise covered by us. The deductible does not make an uncovered loss covered.

**W.3** We will determine the applicable deductible from the facts of the covered loss. You must provide information we reasonably require to make that determination.

**W.4** We will subtract the deductible from the amount otherwise payable for a covered loss. We will not pay the portion of the loss that is subject to the deductible.

**W.5** If the amount otherwise payable does not exceed the deductible, we will make no payment. You remain responsible for that amount.

**W.6** If the amount otherwise payable exceeds the deductible, we will pay the amount remaining after application of the deductible. Our payment remains subject to all terms that apply to the covered loss.

**W.7** The deductible applies before we make payment for a covered loss. The deductible does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.8** The deductible does not increase any limit that applies to a covered loss. A limit does not reduce your responsibility for the deductible.

**W.9** We apply the deductible only to a covered loss. We do not apply the deductible to an amount for which no payment is otherwise available.

**W.10** You must bear the deductible without reimbursement from us. We will not advance the deductible on your behalf.

**W.11** The deductible is not a premium, fee, or charge payable to us. It is the portion of the covered loss that you must pay or absorb.

**W.12** You must provide records supporting the amount of the covered loss. We may require information showing the expenses for which payment is requested.

**W.13** We may investigate a claimed covered loss before applying the deductible. Our investigation does not waive the deductible.

**W.14** We may request documentation from you before making payment. Your failure to provide requested documentation may prevent us from determining the amount otherwise payable.

**W.15** We will apply the deductible after determining the amount of covered loss. We will not apply the deductible to an amount that we do not owe.

**W.16** A disagreement concerning coverage does not eliminate the deductible. You must continue to cooperate with our investigation of the claimed loss.

**W.17** Payment by us does not waive our right to apply the deductible. Payment of part of a claimed loss does not establish that other amounts are covered.

**W.18** We do not cover the portion of a covered loss that is subject to the deductible. This exclusion applies whether you have paid that portion or remain obligated to pay it.

**W.19** The deductible applies to the amount of covered loss after we determine which expenses are covered. Expenses that are not covered do not satisfy the deductible.

**W.20** You must not include the deductible portion of a covered loss in an amount requested from us. If you do, we will reduce any payment by the applicable deductible.

**W.21** We may state the deductible in a payment explanation. An omission from a payment explanation does not waive the deductible.

**W.22** We may correct a payment if the deductible was not properly applied. You must return any amount that exceeds the amount payable after application of the deductible.

**W.23** We may apply the deductible when resolving a claim by agreement. An agreement concerning a claim does not change the deductible unless we agree in writing.

**W.24** You must notify us if information affecting the deductible changes. We may revise our payment determination when material information becomes available.

**W.25** We will not apply the deductible more broadly than the covered loss requires. We will apply it only to the extent necessary to determine the amount payable.

**W.26** The deductible remains applicable when we pay a covered loss directly to a person or organization entitled to payment. Direct payment does not relieve you of responsibility for the deductible.

**W.27** The deductible remains applicable if you assign a right to payment to another person or organization. An assignment does not transfer our responsibility for the deductible to us.

**W.28** We may withhold payment while the amount of the deductible cannot be determined. We will make payment when we can determine the amount otherwise payable.

**W.29** The deductible applies regardless of the manner in which a covered loss is presented to us. Submission of separate expense records does not avoid application of the deductible.

**W.30** We may combine information relating to the same covered loss when applying the deductible. We will base our determination on the facts known to us.

**W.31** You must cooperate in identifying amounts paid or payable for the covered loss. Such information may affect the amount otherwise payable after application of the deductible.

**W.32** The deductible does not affect your duty to protect your interests following a covered loss. You must take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary expense.

**W.33** We will not pay a deductible portion merely because the loss causes inconvenience or hardship. The deductible applies according to its terms.

**W.34** The deductible applies whether or not you seek payment for all covered expenses. A decision not to seek payment for an expense does not change our application of the deductible.

**W.35** We may rely on reasonable documentation in calculating the deductible. We may revise that calculation if later information shows that it was incorrect.

**W.36** The deductible applies to each covered loss as determined by us from the facts presented. Our determination is subject to the rights and duties that apply to the claim.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover any "identity fraud expense" unless it results directly from "identity fraud". An "insured" must establish that the claimed expense arises from a covered identity fraud event.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by any dishonest, criminal, fraudulent, or intentional act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the act is committed alone or with another person.

**W.3** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's knowing disclosure, transfer, sale, or use of personal identifying information for an unlawful purpose. We also do not cover loss arising from an insured's participation in a scheme to obtain money, property, services, or credit by deception.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover any identity fraud expense arising from such loss.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or usurped power. We also do not cover loss caused by any consequence of war.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by seizure or destruction of property by order of governmental authority. This exclusion does not apply to property destroyed to prevent the spread of fire.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, and any consequence of those conditions.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by the release, discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, escape, or application of pollutants. Pollutants include any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or other irritant or contaminant.

**W.9** We do not cover loss arising from a business conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the business is operated for profit or not for profit.

**W.10** We do not cover loss arising from professional services rendered by an insured or on an insured's behalf. Professional services include services requiring specialized knowledge, judgment, training, or licensing.

**W.11** We do not cover loss arising from any commercial transaction entered into by an insured. This exclusion applies to a transaction involving goods, services, investments, financing, or credit.

**W.12** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's ownership, operation, maintenance, use, entrustment, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle. This exclusion applies whether the vehicle is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.13** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's ownership, operation, maintenance, use, entrustment, loading, or unloading of an aircraft. This exclusion applies whether the aircraft is operated for private or commercial purposes.

**W.14** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's ownership, operation, maintenance, use, or entrustment of a watercraft. This exclusion applies whether the watercraft is owned by an insured or another person.

**W.15** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's ownership, operation, maintenance, use, or entrustment of an animal. This exclusion applies to injury, damage, or fraud involving an animal.

**W.16** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's employment of another person. This exclusion includes loss arising from payroll, employee benefits, personnel records, or workplace access.

**W.17** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's capacity as an officer, director, trustee, partner, manager, or representative of an organization. This exclusion applies whether the organization is for profit or not for profit.

**W.18** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's membership in an organization, association, partnership, or joint venture. This exclusion applies when the claimed expense relates to the affairs or obligations of that organization.

**W.19** We do not cover loss arising from any investment, securities transaction, commodity transaction, or financial market transaction. This exclusion applies whether the transaction is authorized, unauthorized, lawful, or unlawful.

**W.20** We do not cover loss arising from the insolvency, bankruptcy, receivership, liquidation, or financial failure of any person or organization. This exclusion applies even when identity fraud is alleged in connection with that failure.

**W.21** We do not cover loss arising from a dispute concerning title, ownership, possession, or control of property. This exclusion applies to real property, personal property, intangible property, and electronic property.

**W.22** We do not cover loss arising from contractual liability assumed by an insured. This exclusion applies unless the liability would exist without the contract.

**W.23** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to perform a contract. This exclusion applies to any claimed expense associated with cancellation, nonperformance, delay, or breach.

**W.24** We do not cover loss arising from any claim for damages by another person. This exclusion includes damages for bodily injury, property damage, personal injury, emotional distress, or economic loss.

**W.25** We do not cover fines, penalties, punitive damages, exemplary damages, or sanctions imposed upon an insured. We also do not cover any expense incurred to contest or satisfy such amounts.

**W.26** We do not cover taxes, assessments, interest, or governmental charges owed by an insured. This exclusion applies even when the obligation results from identity fraud.

**W.27** We do not cover loss arising from the failure of an insured to file a tax return, report income, maintain records, or comply with a legal duty. This exclusion applies whether the failure is intentional, negligent, or inadvertent.

**W.28** We do not cover loss arising from any act or omission occurring before the inception of this coverage. This exclusion applies even when the resulting identity fraud expense is discovered after inception.

**W.29** We do not cover loss arising after this coverage ends. This exclusion applies even when identity fraud began while this coverage was in effect.

**W.30** We do not cover loss arising from any event that an insured knew of before this coverage began. An insured must disclose known circumstances that could reasonably result in a claim.

**W.31** We do not cover loss arising from a claim made by an insured against another insured. This exclusion applies whether the claim is asserted directly or indirectly.

**W.32** We do not cover loss arising from a claim made by an insured against us. This exclusion includes expenses incurred to establish, pursue, or enforce a claim against us.

**W.33** We do not cover loss arising from identity fraud committed by a person residing in the insured's household. This exclusion applies whether that person is related to an insured or not.

**W.34** We do not cover loss arising from identity fraud committed by a person entrusted with an insured's personal identifying information. This exclusion applies even when the entrustment was limited or informal.

**W.35** We do not cover loss arising from identity fraud committed by an insured's employee, agent, representative, or contractor. This exclusion applies whether the person acted within the scope of assigned duties or otherwise.

**W.36** We do not cover loss arising from the voluntary provision of personal identifying information by an insured to a person known by the insured to be engaged in unlawful conduct. An insured must act with reasonable care in responding to requests for personal identifying information.

**W.37** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to protect access credentials, account information, devices, records, or mail. This exclusion applies when the failure materially contributes to the claimed loss.

**W.38** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to cooperate with law enforcement, financial institutions, credit reporting agencies, or us. An insured must provide information and authorization reasonably required to investigate a claim.

**W.39** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to mitigate an identity fraud expense. An insured must take reasonable steps to reduce further loss after discovering identity fraud.

**W.40** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's voluntary payment of a disputed obligation without our consent. This exclusion applies unless the payment was reasonably necessary to prevent immediate further loss.

**W.41** We do not cover loss arising from the replacement, repair, restoration, or enhancement of property. This exclusion includes computer equipment, software, data, documents, and communications devices.

**W.42** We do not cover loss arising from the loss of use, loss of value, loss of income, loss of profits, or loss of opportunity. This exclusion applies even when such loss results from identity fraud.

**W.43** We do not cover loss arising from emotional distress, humiliation, embarrassment, inconvenience, or loss of reputation. This exclusion applies whether the claimed harm is direct or consequential.

**W.44** We do not cover expenses for legal services unless those services are directly related to resolving a covered identity fraud claim. We do not cover legal services rendered in connection with criminal charges against an insured.

**W.45** We do not cover expenses incurred to defend an insured in any civil, criminal, administrative, disciplinary, or regulatory proceeding. This exclusion applies even when identity fraud is alleged in that proceeding.

**W.46** We do not cover expenses arising from a dispute with a merchant, lender, creditor, service provider, employer, landlord, tenant, or insurer unless the dispute directly results from covered identity fraud. An insured must provide documentation supporting that direct relationship.

**W.47** We do not cover loss arising from a disputed charge when the dispute does not involve the unauthorized use of an insured's identity. This exclusion applies to billing errors, service disputes, and dissatisfaction with goods or services.

**W.48** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's unauthorized use of another person's identity. This exclusion applies even when an insured claims to have acted for the benefit of that person.

**W.49** We do not cover loss arising from the use of a fictitious identity created or maintained by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the fictitious identity was used for personal, business, or unlawful purposes.

**W.50** We do not cover loss arising from a fraudulent application made by an insured for credit, benefits, insurance, employment, housing, or services. This exclusion applies whether the application is made directly or through another person.

**W.51** We do not cover loss arising from a fraudulent claim submitted by an insured. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or omit any material fact relating to a claim.

**W.52** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's fabrication, alteration, destruction, concealment, or falsification of records. This exclusion applies to paper records, electronic records, and any other evidence.

**W.53** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to preserve evidence reasonably available to support a claim. An insured must provide records, correspondence, notices, and other information reasonably requested by us.

**W.54** We do not cover loss arising from a data breach affecting an organization unless an insured incurs a covered identity fraud expense. This exclusion applies to costs incurred by the organization itself.

**W.55** We do not cover loss arising from unauthorized access to an insured's computer, network, device, or account unless the access results in covered identity fraud. This exclusion applies to expenses for restoring data, software, or electronic systems.

**W.56** We do not cover loss arising from extortion, ransom, blackmail, or threats. This exclusion applies whether the threat involves personal identifying information, electronic data, property, or bodily injury.

**W.57** We do not cover loss arising from forgery, alteration, counterfeit currency, or fraudulent instruments unless the loss results in covered identity fraud. This exclusion does not create coverage for the face value of any instrument.

**W.58** We do not cover loss arising from theft, disappearance, or destruction of cash, securities, property, or documents. This exclusion applies even when the property contains personal identifying information.

**W.59** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's failure to maintain insurance, obtain insurance, or recover under another insurance policy. This exclusion applies to any deductible, retention, self-insured amount, or uninsured loss.

**W.60** We do not cover any "named storm deductible minimum" under this coverage. That term does not create or expand coverage for identity fraud expense.

**W.61** We do not cover any "appurtenant structures percentage" under this coverage. That term does not create or expand coverage for identity fraud expense.

**W.62** We do not cover any "Coverage C percentage" under this coverage. That term does not create or expand coverage for identity fraud expense.

**W.63** We do not cover any "Coverage D limit as a percentage of Coverage A" under this coverage. That term does not create or expand coverage for identity fraud expense.

**W.64** We do not cover any "rubble removal percentage" under this coverage. That term does not create or expand coverage for identity fraud expense.

**W.65** We do not cover any expense for monitoring, restoration, or protection that is not directly related to covered identity fraud. This exclusion applies to services obtained for general personal security or credit maintenance.

**W.66** We do not cover any expense that is recoverable from another person, organization, financial institution, governmental authority, or source. An insured must pursue available remedies before seeking payment from us when reasonably practicable.

**W.67** We do not cover any expense incurred without an insured's reasonable belief that identity fraud has occurred. An insured must promptly notify us after discovering facts that may result in a claim.

**W.68** We do not cover any expense that is not supported by reasonable documentation. An insured must submit proof of loss and other information reasonably required by us.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties in this coverage before we provide payment for a "covered loss". Compliance by an insured includes compliance by any person seeking payment under this coverage.

**W.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of an "identity fraud" that may result in a claim. The notice must describe the known circumstances of the identity fraud.

**W.3** An insured must report a loss to us within thirty days after discovery of the loss. Notice must be given in a manner that allows us to investigate the loss.

**W.4** An insured must provide the name and contact information of the person reporting the loss. An insured must also provide information concerning the affected identity.

**W.5** An insured must protect available evidence relating to the identity fraud. An insured must not knowingly destroy, alter, conceal, or discard relevant evidence.

**W.6** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further identity fraud after discovery of the loss. Those steps must be appropriate to the circumstances known to the insured.

**W.7** An insured must notify appropriate financial institutions when the identity fraud involves an account, payment instrument, or transaction. An insured must follow reasonable instructions given by those institutions to protect affected accounts.

**W.8** An insured must report criminal activity to an appropriate governmental authority when reasonably requested by us. An insured must provide us with information concerning that report.

**W.9** An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. Cooperation includes providing requested information that is relevant to the claim.

**W.10** An insured must permit us to inspect records relating to the identity fraud. Inspection may include records in the possession or control of the insured.

**W.11** An insured must provide copies of "records" that support the claimed loss. Records include correspondence, account statements, notices, invoices, and receipts.

**W.12** An insured must provide information concerning any person believed to have caused or contributed to the identity fraud. The information must be provided when known to the insured.

**W.13** An insured must identify all affected accounts, transactions, and obligations known to the insured. An insured must update that information if material information becomes available.

**W.14** An insured must provide a completed "proof of loss" when we request it. The proof of loss must state the facts supporting the claimed covered loss.

**W.15** An insured must sign a proof of loss when we request a signed proof. The signed proof must be true, complete, and based on information reasonably available to the insured.

**W.16** The "sworn proof deadline" applies to an insured's duty to provide a sworn proof of loss. An insured must comply with that requirement when it applies.

**W.17** An insured must provide documents supporting claimed "reasonable expenses". Reasonable expenses must be related to the identity fraud and supported by available records.

**W.18** An insured must identify expenses that have been paid, remain unpaid, or are expected to be incurred. An insured must not present the same expense as more than a single covered loss.

**W.19** An insured must submit claims for reimbursement in good faith. An insured must not seek reimbursement for an expense that was not actually incurred.

**W.20** An insured must notify us of any payment, reimbursement, recovery, or credit received in connection with the loss. That notice must identify the source and nature of the recovery.

**W.21** An insured must preserve rights against any person or organization that may be responsible for the loss. An insured must not impair those rights after a loss.

**W.22** We may require an insured to assign to us rights of recovery to the extent of our payment. An insured must execute documents reasonably necessary to preserve those rights.

**W.23** The "subrogation" rights of us do not relieve an insured of any duty under this coverage. An insured must cooperate with us in pursuing a recovery.

**W.24** An insured must not settle a claim against a responsible person without our consent when that settlement may impair our rights. Consent by us must be in writing.

**W.25** An insured must not make a "voluntary payment" for which reimbursement is sought without our consent when consent is reasonably practicable. This duty does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable protective action.

**W.26** An insured must promptly notify us if a person or organization offers to resolve the loss. An insured must provide us with the terms of any proposed resolution.

**W.27** An insured must provide us with copies of communications received from creditors, collection agencies, financial institutions, or governmental authorities concerning the identity fraud. An insured must provide those communications without material alteration.

**W.28** An insured must notify us of any lawsuit, demand, administrative proceeding, or collection activity related to the identity fraud. Notice must include available documents concerning the matter.

**W.29** An insured must cooperate with any reasonable request for information made by us concerning a lawsuit or proceeding related to the claim. An insured must not admit liability on our behalf.

**W.30** An insured must submit to an examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide requested records in connection with that examination.

**W.31** An insured must answer questions truthfully during an examination under oath. An insured must not knowingly withhold a material fact.

**W.32** A "material fact" is a fact that could affect our investigation, evaluation, payment, or recovery concerning a claim. An insured must promptly correct a material statement that the insured learns is inaccurate.

**W.33** We do not cover loss arising from "concealment", "misrepresentation", or "fraud" by an insured in connection with a claim. This exclusion applies whether the conduct occurs before or after notice of loss.

**W.34** An insured must not knowingly present altered, fabricated, or misleading records to us. An insured must not direct another person to provide altered, fabricated, or misleading records.

**W.35** An insured must provide identifying information reasonably necessary for us to verify the claim. We will use that information only as reasonably necessary to administer the claim.

**W.36** An insured must authorize us to obtain relevant records from persons or organizations that possess information concerning the claim. An authorization must be in a form reasonably acceptable to us.

**W.37** An insured must provide access to "electronic records" relevant to the claimed loss when reasonably requested by us. Electronic records include available account information and communications.

**W.38** An insured must retain copies of documents submitted to us when reasonably practicable. An insured must identify any document that cannot be provided.

**W.39** An insured must notify us if relevant records are lost, unavailable, or held by another person. An insured must explain the circumstances affecting availability of those records.

**W.40** An insured must use reasonable care in safeguarding "personal information" after discovery of identity fraud. Personal information includes information that may be used to access an account or establish an identity.

**W.41** An insured must follow reasonable directions from us that are intended to assist in documenting the loss. Such directions do not require an insured to incur an unreasonable expense.

**W.42** An insured must notify us if the identity fraud involves a deceased person whose identity is connected to the claim. An insured must provide authority to act for that person when applicable.

**W.43** An insured must notify us if the identity fraud involves a minor or another person for whom the insured acts. An insured must provide information establishing the insured's authority to act.

**W.44** An insured must identify any "representative" acting for the insured concerning the claim. A representative must cooperate with us to the same extent required of the insured.

**W.45** An insured must notify us if an attorney, public adjuster, or other representative seeks payment from claim proceeds. An insured must provide available agreements concerning that request.

**W.46** An "assignment" of rights under this coverage is not valid without our written consent. An insured may assign a claim payment after we agree in writing to the amount due.

**W.47** An insured must not abandon property, records, or obligations to us. We are not required to take possession of any item connected with the identity fraud.

**W.48** An insured must notify us if "recovered property" or recovered funds relate to a claimed loss. Recovered property includes documents, account funds, or other items returned after the loss.

**W.49** An insured must hold recovered property for our inspection when reasonably requested. An insured must protect the property from further loss or damage while it is in the insured's control.

**W.50** An insured must repay us for any payment that exceeds the covered loss after a recovery is received. Repayment is required only to the extent the insured receives a recovery for the same loss.

**W.51** An insured must disclose all "other insurance" that may apply to the claimed loss. Other insurance includes any insurance, service contract, indemnity agreement, or similar protection.

**W.52** An insured must seek available benefits from another source when required by applicable law or agreement. An insured must provide us with information concerning benefits sought or received.

**W.53** We may require an insured to transfer to us any recovery rights available under other insurance. An insured must not prejudice our right to contribution or recovery.

**W.54** The "all-other-perils deductible minimum" does not change an insured's duty to comply with the conditions of this coverage. An insured must provide information needed to determine whether a deductible applies.

**W.55** The "coinsurance threshold" does not relieve an insured from providing records supporting the claimed loss. An insured must cooperate with our evaluation of any condition affecting payment.

**W.56** An insured must permit us to communicate with financial institutions, governmental authorities, and service providers concerning the claim. An insured may limit that permission only when the limitation does not impair our investigation.

**W.57** An insured must provide written consent when consent is necessary to obtain protected information relevant to the claim. An insured must not unreasonably withhold that consent.

**W.58** An insured must notify us of a change in contact information while a claim remains open. Notice must include a reliable method for us to communicate with the insured.

**W.59** An insured must review communications from us concerning the claim. An insured must respond to reasonable requests for information or documentation.

**W.60** An insured must notify us if any information previously provided becomes materially inaccurate or incomplete. The notification must describe the corrected information.

**W.61** An insured must make reasonable efforts to mitigate damages caused by the identity fraud. We do not cover avoidable loss caused by an insured's failure to take reasonable protective action.

**W.62** An insured must provide evidence of mitigation efforts when reasonably requested by us. Evidence may include correspondence, confirmation notices, or account records.

**W.63** An insured must retain receipts for expenses for which reimbursement is sought when receipts are available. An insured must explain the absence of a receipt when a receipt cannot be obtained.

**W.64** An insured must identify the purpose of each claimed expense. The stated purpose must show a direct connection between the expense and the identity fraud.

**W.65** An insured must not seek reimbursement for an expense paid by another person or organization unless the insured has a legal obligation to repay that amount. An insured must disclose any obligation to repay another person.

**W.66** An insured must provide documentation concerning a dispute with a creditor or financial institution when that dispute relates to the claim. An insured must preserve notices showing the status of the dispute.

**W.67** An insured must notify us if a creditor withdraws, reduces, or confirms a disputed obligation. An insured must provide documentation supporting that action.

**W.68** An insured must notify us if a governmental authority closes or declines an investigation related to the identity fraud. An insured must provide available documentation of that decision.

**W.69** An insured must not interfere with our investigation of the claim. Interference includes refusing reasonable access to relevant information or directing another person to withhold it.

**W.70** An insured must keep claim information confidential when disclosure could prejudice our investigation or recovery rights. This duty does not prevent disclosure required by law.

**W.71** No action may be brought against us unless an insured has complied with the conditions of this coverage. The "years within which an action against the insurer must be brought" applies to any action against us.

**W.72** An insured must provide notice of a legal action against us in a manner permitted by applicable law. Notice must include available documents concerning the action.

**W.73** A "waiver" of any condition is valid only when made by us in writing. Our investigation of a claim does not constitute a waiver.

**W.74** Our request for information, records, or examinations does not waive any right available to us. Our payment of part of a claim does not waive any condition applicable to the remaining claim.

**W.75** If more than one insured seeks payment for the same loss, each insured must cooperate with us. We may rely on information provided by an insured unless we know that information is materially inaccurate.

**W.76** An insured must act in good faith in all dealings with us concerning a claim. We may deny coverage to the extent an insured's failure to comply with a condition prejudices our ability to investigate, evaluate, settle, defend, or recover concerning the claim.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** An "insured" must give us prompt notice of an "identity fraud event." Notice must include the information then known about the event and the persons involved.

**W.2** An insured must notify us as soon as practicable after discovering an identity fraud event. Delay in giving notice must not prejudice our ability to investigate, evaluate, or protect our rights.

**W.3** An insured must provide a written description of the identity fraud event. The description must state how the event was discovered and identify the accounts, records, or transactions believed to be affected.

**W.4** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further unauthorized use of the insured's identifying information. Such steps must be taken promptly after the insured discovers an identity fraud event.

**W.5** An insured must preserve all "supporting records" relating to an identity fraud event. Supporting records include correspondence, account statements, reports, notices, receipts, and other materials that substantiate a claimed loss or expense.

**W.6** An insured must provide us with supporting records upon our request. We may require copies, originals, or access to records maintained electronically.

**W.7** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of a claim. Cooperation includes responding truthfully to our reasonable requests for information and documentation.

**W.8** An insured must submit to an examination under oath if we request it. The insured must sign the examination transcript if it is accurate.

**W.9** An insured must provide any authorizations reasonably necessary for us to obtain information relevant to a claim. An authorization must be valid and sufficient for the person or organization holding the information to release it.

**W.10** An insured must promptly notify affected financial institutions, merchants, creditors, governmental agencies, and other appropriate parties of an identity fraud event. An insured must make reasonable efforts to limit further unauthorized transactions.

**W.11** An insured must file a report with an appropriate law enforcement agency when reasonably necessary to investigate or resolve an identity fraud event. An insured must provide us with a copy of the report upon our request.

**W.12** An insured must complete and submit affidavits, declarations, dispute forms, and similar documents reasonably required to correct records affected by identity fraud. An insured must not knowingly make a false statement in such a document.

**W.13** An insured must promptly dispute unauthorized charges, accounts, transfers, or other transactions. The dispute must be made to the person or organization responsible for maintaining the affected record.

**W.14** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting any "service provider" retained in connection with an identity fraud event. A service provider includes a person or organization that provides restoration, legal, accounting, credit, or similar services.

**W.15** An insured must obtain our consent before retaining a service provider if the insured seeks reimbursement for that provider's charges. Our consent does not make us responsible for the provider's acts, errors, omissions, or results.

**W.16** An insured must not incur an expense that is unreasonable or unnecessary under the circumstances. We may require information showing that an expense was directly related to an identity fraud event.

**W.17** An insured must not voluntarily assume an obligation or make a payment that could prejudice our rights. This requirement does not prevent an insured from taking reasonable steps to protect identifying information or to stop unauthorized activity.

**W.18** An insured must notify us before entering into a settlement relating to an identity fraud event if the insured seeks coverage for any resulting expense. We are not bound by a settlement to which we did not consent.

**W.19** An insured must provide us with notice of any demand, suit, proceeding, or administrative matter arising from an identity fraud event. Notice must be given promptly after the insured receives the demand, suit, proceeding, or administrative matter.

**W.20** An insured must send us copies of notices, pleadings, correspondence, and other documents received in connection with a "legal proceeding." A legal proceeding includes a civil, criminal, regulatory, or administrative matter.

**W.21** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or omit a material fact relating to an identity fraud event or claim. This duty applies before and after a claim is made.

**W.22** An insured must promptly correct information provided to us if the insured learns that the information is inaccurate or incomplete. We may rely upon information provided by an insured unless we receive corrected information.

**W.23** An insured must identify all persons known to have access to information relevant to an identity fraud event. An insured must provide available contact information for those persons upon our request.

**W.24** An insured must identify all accounts, devices, records, credentials, and documents reasonably believed to be affected by an identity fraud event. An insured must update that information as additional facts become known.

**W.25** An insured must maintain reasonable records of time, travel, communication, and other claimed identity fraud expenses. The records must show the purpose of the expense and its relationship to the identity fraud event.

**W.26** An insured must provide receipts, invoices, canceled payments, or other evidence supporting each claimed identity fraud expense. We may deny reimbursement for an expense that cannot be reasonably substantiated.

**W.27** An insured must permit us to inspect property, records, and documents relevant to a claim when reasonably necessary. Inspection may include electronic records and information stored by a service provider.

**W.28** An insured must assist us in obtaining documents from banks, creditors, consumer reporting agencies, merchants, employers, and governmental agencies. An insured must sign reasonable requests necessary to obtain those documents.

**W.29** An insured must use reasonable efforts to obtain correction of inaccurate information caused by identity fraud. An insured must notify us if a requested correction is denied or delayed.

**W.30** An insured must promptly notify us of any payment, refund, reimbursement, waiver, credit, or other benefit received in connection with an identity fraud event. An insured must preserve our rights concerning any such benefit.

**W.31** An insured must hold any "recovery" received in connection with a covered claim for us to the extent of our payment. A recovery includes money, property, services, credits, or other benefits obtained from a responsible party.

**W.32** An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery. At our request, an insured must transfer to us rights against a person or organization responsible for the loss.

**W.33** An insured must cooperate in any action we take to recover amounts paid under this coverage. We will pay the reasonable expenses we incur in obtaining that cooperation.

**W.34** An insured must notify us if another person or organization may be responsible for an identity fraud expense. Notice must include information known to the insured about that person or organization.

**W.35** An insured must seek available reimbursement from a financial institution, merchant, creditor, service provider, or other responsible party. This duty does not require an insured to pursue a remedy that is clearly unavailable or unreasonable.

**W.36** An insured must notify us of any other insurance, benefit plan, reimbursement arrangement, or assistance available for an identity fraud expense. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning that available source.

**W.37** An insured must keep us informed of material developments relating to an identity fraud event. Material developments include new unauthorized activity, changes in disputed amounts, and communications from responsible parties.

**W.38** An insured must promptly notify us if an identity fraud event results from a "security breach." A security breach means unauthorized access to, acquisition of, or disclosure of identifying information.

**W.39** An insured must preserve evidence of a security breach when reasonably possible. An insured must not alter, destroy, or dispose of evidence relevant to our investigation without our consent.

**W.40** An insured must use reasonable care to safeguard passwords, access codes, account credentials, devices, and identifying documents after discovering an identity fraud event. An insured must change or disable compromised credentials when reasonably appropriate.

**W.41** An insured must notify us before destroying records related to a pending claim. We may require the records to be retained while our investigation remains incomplete.

**W.42** An insured must provide us with a signed proof of loss if we request it. The proof of loss must state the facts known to the insured and identify the identity fraud expenses for which payment is sought.

**W.43** An insured must separate claimed identity fraud expenses from personal, business, or other expenses not caused by an identity fraud event. If an expense has more than one purpose, an insured must provide information allowing us to determine the portion related to identity fraud.

**W.44** An insured must not submit the same identity fraud expense for payment more than once. An insured must notify us if any person or organization later pays an expense for which we made payment.

**W.45** We do not cover an identity fraud expense that is based on false, incomplete, altered, or fabricated supporting records. We do not cover an expense claimed by an insured who knowingly participates in the identity fraud event.

**W.46** We do not cover an identity fraud expense incurred without a reasonable connection to an identity fraud event. We do not cover an expense that is personal, elective, speculative, or unrelated to restoring the insured's identity records.

**W.47** We do not cover an identity fraud expense for a service provider retained without our required consent. We do not cover charges that exceed the reasonable value of services actually provided.

**W.48** We do not cover an identity fraud expense that has been paid, reimbursed, waived, forgiven, or otherwise satisfied by another source. We do not cover the portion of an expense for which an insured has received a recovery.

**W.49** We do not cover an identity fraud expense arising from an insured's intentional, dishonest, fraudulent, or criminal act. This exclusion applies even if another person also committed an act contributing to the loss.

**W.50** We do not cover an identity fraud expense resulting from an insured's voluntary disclosure of identifying information to a person known by the insured to be unauthorized. This exclusion does not apply if the disclosure was induced by deception that the insured could not reasonably detect.

**W.51** We do not cover an identity fraud expense arising from a business, trade, profession, occupation, or employment of an insured. We do not cover expenses related to business accounts, business records, or business transactions.

**W.52** We do not cover an identity fraud expense arising from a dispute over the quality, value, delivery, or performance of goods or services. We do not cover an expense arising solely from a contractual disagreement.

**W.53** We do not cover an identity fraud expense arising from a "dispute" that does not involve unauthorized use of identifying information. A dispute includes a disagreement concerning a debt, account balance, charge, contract, or transaction.

**W.54** We do not cover fines, penalties, punitive damages, sanctions, or amounts imposed because an insured failed to comply with a law, regulation, order, or agreement. We do not cover expenses incurred to contest a lawful obligation of an insured.

**W.55** We do not cover an identity fraud expense arising from loss of income, loss of profits, loss of use, diminished value, or emotional distress. We do not cover indirect or consequential loss.

**W.56** We do not cover an identity fraud expense arising from bodily injury, sickness, disease, death, or property damage. We do not cover expenses for physical security measures unless directly required to address an identity fraud event.

**W.57** We do not cover an identity fraud expense arising from war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or governmental seizure. We do not cover an expense arising from a failure of governmental action to prevent identity fraud.

**W.58** An insured must not assign a claim or any right to payment under this coverage without our written consent. An assignment made without our consent is not binding on us.

**W.59** An insured must comply with all duties that apply to a claim under this coverage. Failure to comply with a duty may result in denial of a claim if the failure materially prejudices us.

**W.60** Our payment of an identity fraud expense does not waive any right, defense, or condition available to us. We may investigate a claim and make a payment without admitting coverage.

**W.61** An insured must repay us for any amount we pay that is not owed under this coverage. We may recover that amount from an insured to the extent permitted by law.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" only after you establish that the loss results directly from identity fraud. We will determine the amount payable from the information available to us.

**W.2** "Identity fraud" includes the unauthorized use of your identifying information that causes a covered loss. An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation and settlement of a claimed loss.

**W.3** A "covered expense" must be reasonably incurred because of identity fraud. We will not pay an expense that is not supported by records showing that it was incurred.

**W.4** We will apply "loss settlement" provisions only to expenses that satisfy all applicable coverage requirements. We may require additional information before we determine whether an expense is covered.

**W.5** "Personal property" means property that is owned or used by an insured and is subject to a claimed loss. We will determine whether personal property is involved in the claimed loss from the available evidence.

**W.6** We will not pay for any expense that is recovered from another source. An insured must take reasonable steps to seek available reimbursement when reimbursement is available.

**W.7** We settle a covered loss to personal property on this basis: replacement cost. We will apply that basis to the facts of the claimed loss.

**W.8** An insured must provide a "proof of loss" when we request it. The proof of loss must describe the claimed loss and identify the expenses for which payment is requested.

**W.9** We may require records, receipts, invoices, correspondence, or other documents that support a claimed expense. An insured must provide available supporting material promptly after our request.

**W.10** We may examine the documents and records relating to a claimed loss. An insured must authorize the release of records that are reasonably necessary for us to determine coverage or payment.

**W.11** We will determine whether an expense was actually incurred by an insured. We will not pay an estimated, anticipated, or speculative expense.

**W.12** We will not pay for an expense that is unrelated to identity fraud. An insured must separate covered expenses from expenses arising from unrelated matters.

**W.13** We will pay only the portion of a covered expense that is reasonable and necessary. We may reduce payment for charges that exceed customary charges for similar services.

**W.14** We will not pay for the value of an insured's time. We will not pay for inconvenience, emotional distress, loss of reputation, or similar harm.

**W.15** We will not pay for lost income, lost business opportunity, or loss of use unless the expense is expressly covered. An insured must provide records supporting any expense that is claimed as a direct result of identity fraud.

**W.16** We will not pay for a penalty, fine, or punitive assessment imposed upon an insured. We will not pay for an expense arising from an insured's dishonest, fraudulent, or criminal act.

**W.17** We will not pay for an expense that another person is legally responsible to pay. An insured must assign to us the right to recover payment from that person to the extent of our payment.

**W.18** Our payment will reduce the amount of a covered loss by amounts recovered from another source. An insured must hold in trust any duplicate recovery received after we make payment.

**W.19** We may settle a covered loss with you or with a person authorized to act for you. We may require evidence of that person's authority before making payment.

**W.20** We may make payment to an insured who incurred the covered expense. We may make payment jointly to an insured and a provider when the available records support joint payment.

**W.21** We will not be obligated to pay a provider directly. A payment made directly to a provider does not create a duty to make later payments to that provider.

**W.22** An insured must protect records, documents, and other evidence relating to the claimed loss. We may deny payment for prejudice caused by an insured's failure to preserve material evidence.

**W.23** An insured must notify us if the facts of the claimed loss change. We may revise our settlement determination when new information affects coverage or the amount payable.

**W.24** We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath concerning a claimed loss. An insured must provide truthful answers and must produce reasonably requested records.

**W.25** We may investigate the circumstances surrounding a claimed loss. Our investigation does not waive any condition, exclusion, or other coverage requirement.

**W.26** We may request that an insured identify persons with knowledge of the claimed loss. An insured must cooperate in obtaining information reasonably needed to evaluate the claim.

**W.27** We will not pay for expenses based on altered, fabricated, or materially inaccurate records. An insured must correct any material error in information provided to us.

**W.28** We may defer settlement while information material to the claimed loss remains unavailable. We will reconsider the claimed loss when the required information becomes available.

**W.29** We will settle only the portion of a claimed loss that is supported by sufficient evidence. The absence of complete records does not relieve an insured of the duty to establish the claimed loss.

**W.30** We will not pay a covered expense more than once. An insured must disclose prior payments, reimbursements, credits, and recoveries relating to the same expense.

**W.31** The "assessment coverage limit" does not establish the amount of a covered loss. We will determine a covered loss independently from any assessment coverage limit.

**W.32** A "fire response charge" is not a covered expense unless it results directly from identity fraud. We will evaluate any claimed fire response charge from the facts presented.

**W.33** The "special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones" does not determine the settlement of a covered expense. We will evaluate a claimed expense under the applicable facts.

**W.34** The "home business contents limit" does not determine whether an expense is covered. We will determine coverage from the nature and cause of the claimed expense.

**W.35** We will not pay for an expense incurred voluntarily without a reasonable connection to the claimed loss. An insured must explain why the expense was necessary.

**W.36** We will not pay for a service that was not performed or a charge that was not incurred. An insured must provide available evidence that a claimed service was obtained.

**W.37** We may use information from public records, financial institutions, service providers, and other sources to evaluate a claimed loss. An insured must cooperate with reasonable requests for authorization to obtain that information.

**W.38** We may deny payment for a claimed expense when an insured intentionally conceals or misrepresents a material fact. This provision applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after a loss.

**W.39** We may reconsider a settlement if later information shows that payment was not owed. An insured must repay any amount received that exceeds the amount properly payable.

**W.40** We will not pay for an expense caused by an insured's failure to take reasonable protective action after discovering identity fraud. An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent additional covered expense.

**W.41** An insured must keep records of communications relating to the claimed loss when those records are available. We may consider those records in determining whether an expense is covered.

**W.42** We may rely on information supplied by an insured unless we have reason to question its accuracy. Our reliance does not prevent us from requesting additional verification.

**W.43** We will settle a covered loss in accordance with the information reasonably available to us. We may adjust the settlement if material information later becomes available.

**W.44** Payment by us does not admit liability for any expense beyond the covered loss. Acceptance of payment does not prevent us from enforcing rights arising from an overpayment.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** "Identity Fraud" means the unauthorized use or attempted use of an insured's Personal Identifying Information. Such use must be committed by a person other than an insured.

**W.2** "Identity Fraud Event" means an act or series of related acts of Identity Fraud. An Identity Fraud Event includes the unauthorized use of Personal Identifying Information to obtain money, goods, services, credit, property, or benefits.

**W.3** "Identity Fraud Claim" means a request made by an insured for payment of Identity Fraud Expenses. The request must arise from an Identity Fraud Event.

**W.4** "Identity Fraud Expense" means a reasonable and necessary expense incurred by an insured because of an Identity Fraud Event. The expense must be directly related to restoring the insured's identity or credit record.

**W.5** "Personal Identifying Information" means information that identifies or reasonably can be used to identify a particular person. This information includes a name, address, birth information, government-issued identification information, account information, access information, or signature.

**W.6** Personal Identifying Information includes information in written, electronic, oral, photographic, recorded, or other form. Personal Identifying Information does not cease to be such information because it has been altered, combined, or reproduced.

**W.7** "Financial Account" means an account maintained by a Financial Institution or other organization for the deposit, transfer, payment, investment, or custody of money or property. A Financial Account includes an account accessible by a card, check, electronic device, or access code.

**W.8** "Financial Institution" means a bank, savings institution, credit union, trust company, investment company, payment service, or similar organization. Financial Institution includes an organization that issues, maintains, or services a Financial Account.

**W.9** "Unauthorized Transaction" means a transaction made without the knowledge, authority, or consent of an insured. An Unauthorized Transaction includes a withdrawal, transfer, purchase, charge, payment, or application for credit.

**W.10** "Access Device" means a card, code, account number, password, personal identification number, token, key, or other means used to obtain money, goods, services, credit, property, or benefits. An Access Device includes information used to initiate or authenticate an electronic transaction.

**W.11** "Credit Card" means a card or other Access Device issued for the purpose of obtaining credit. Credit Card includes information associated with the use of that card or device.

**W.12** "Debit Card" means a card or other Access Device used to obtain funds from a Financial Account. Debit Card includes information associated with the use of that card or device.

**W.13** "Check" means a written or electronically created order directing a Financial Institution to pay money. Check includes a draft, money order, cashier's check, or similar instrument.

**W.14** "Electronic Fund Transfer" means a transfer of money initiated through an electronic terminal, computer, telephone, mobile device, or other electronic means. Electronic Fund Transfer includes a transfer initiated through the use of an Access Device.

**W.15** "Forgery" means the unauthorized signing, alteration, endorsement, or creation of a document with intent to deceive. Forgery includes the unauthorized use of an insured's signature.

**W.16** "Counterfeit" means an imitation of a genuine document, instrument, card, record, or Access Device. A Counterfeit is made or used with intent to deceive.

**W.17** "Loan" means money, property, services, or credit advanced with an obligation to repay. Loan includes a line of credit, installment obligation, lease obligation, or similar extension of credit.

**W.18** "Application" means a request, submission, enrollment, registration, or agreement made to obtain money, goods, services, credit, property, or benefits. Application includes a request made in written, electronic, oral, or other form.

**W.19** "False Statement" means a statement that is untrue, misleading, incomplete, or fraudulently made. False Statement includes information attributed to an insured without that insured's knowledge or authorization.

**W.20** "Creditor" means a person or organization that claims a right to payment from an insured. Creditor includes an assignee, successor, servicer, or collection representative of that person or organization.

**W.21** "Debt Collector" means a person or organization seeking to collect money alleged to be owed by an insured. Debt Collector includes a person or organization acting for a Creditor.

**W.22** "Collection Activity" means an effort to collect, demand, recover, or enforce payment of an alleged debt. Collection Activity includes a demand, communication, report, legal action, or seizure effort.

**W.23** "Credit Report" means a report, record, file, score, history, or other information concerning an insured's creditworthiness or credit activity. Credit Report includes information maintained by a Credit Reporting Agency.

**W.24** "Credit Reporting Agency" means a person or organization that assembles or evaluates consumer credit information for the purpose of furnishing reports to others. Credit Reporting Agency includes an organization that maintains a consumer credit file.

**W.25** "Credit Record" means information concerning an insured's credit history, credit activity, Financial Accounts, debts, payment history, or applications for credit. Credit Record includes information contained in a Credit Report.

**W.26** "Public Record" means information maintained by a governmental body, court, agency, office, or registry. Public Record includes a record concerning an insured's name, address, identification, property, license, benefit, or legal status.

**W.27** "Governmental Body" means a governmental authority, agency, department, bureau, office, board, commission, court, or similar public entity. Governmental Body includes an entity exercising governmental or regulatory authority.

**W.28** "Legal Proceeding" means a civil, criminal, administrative, regulatory, or other formal proceeding. Legal Proceeding includes a hearing, investigation, arbitration, mediation, or action before a Governmental Body.

**W.29** "Legal Costs" means reasonable fees and expenses charged by an attorney for services directly related to an Identity Fraud Event. Legal Costs include expenses necessary to prepare, file, respond to, or defend a Legal Proceeding.

**W.30** "Attorney" means a person licensed to practice law in the jurisdiction where legal services are rendered. Attorney includes a law firm employing or associated with that person.

**W.31** "Court Costs" means charges assessed by a court or other tribunal in connection with a Legal Proceeding. Court Costs include filing charges, service charges, and charges for required records.

**W.32** "Lost Wages" means income an insured would have earned from employment but did not earn because of necessary time away from work. Lost Wages must result directly from efforts to resolve an Identity Fraud Event.

**W.33** "Employment" means work performed by an insured for compensation. Employment includes self-employment and work performed in the insured's usual occupation.

**W.34** "Travel Expense" means a reasonable charge for transportation, lodging, parking, or similar travel necessary to resolve an Identity Fraud Event. Travel Expense must be incurred by an insured.

**W.35** "Mailing Expense" means a reasonable charge for postage, delivery, copying, printing, or transmission of documents necessary to resolve an Identity Fraud Event. Mailing Expense includes charges for certified or tracked delivery.

**W.36** "Document Replacement Expense" means a reasonable charge to replace, correct, obtain, or amend a document affected by an Identity Fraud Event. Document Replacement Expense includes charges imposed by a Governmental Body or other issuing organization.

**W.37** "Document" means a written, printed, electronic, photographic, recorded, or other record of information. Document includes an identification record, account record, certificate, license, title, or application.

**W.38** "Insured" means a person to whom this coverage applies. Insured includes a person whose Personal Identifying Information is the subject of an Identity Fraud Event.

**W.39** "Household" means persons who reside together at the residence premises. Household includes persons who reside together with the intent to maintain a common living arrangement.

**W.40** "Residence Premises" means the dwelling used by an insured as a residence. Residence Premises includes that part of the premises used by an insured for residential purposes.

**W.41** "Discovery" means the time when an insured first becomes aware of facts that would cause a reasonable person to suspect an Identity Fraud Event. Discovery occurs even if the full extent of the Identity Fraud Event is not then known.

**W.42** "Notice" means information communicated to us by an insured or on an insured's behalf. Notice must reasonably identify the Identity Fraud Event and the insured involved.

**W.43** "Proof of Loss" means information, records, documents, statements, or other evidence supporting an Identity Fraud Claim. Proof of Loss includes information reasonably requested by us to evaluate the claim.

**W.44** "Reasonable and Necessary" means appropriate and useful under the circumstances of an Identity Fraud Event. An expense is not Reasonable and Necessary if it is excessive, unrelated, avoidable, or incurred for personal convenience.

**W.45** "Restoration Services" means services intended to assist an insured in correcting records, disputing unauthorized activity, contacting affected organizations, or restoring the insured's identity or credit record. Restoration Services must relate directly to an Identity Fraud Event.

**W.46** "Dispute" means a challenge, objection, correction request, or denial concerning information, a charge, a debt, an account, or an Application. Dispute includes a communication to a Financial Institution, Creditor, Credit Reporting Agency, Governmental Body, or other organization.

**W.47** "Record Correction" means an amendment, removal, revision, notation, or other change to a record affected by an Identity Fraud Event. Record Correction includes the correction of a Credit Record or Public Record.

**W.48** "Unauthorized Account" means a Financial Account, Loan, Credit Card, Debit Card, or other obligation opened, obtained, used, or maintained without an insured's knowledge, authority, or consent. Unauthorized Account includes an account established through an Application containing a False Statement.

**W.49** "Unauthorized Debt" means a debt, obligation, charge, or payment demand resulting from an Identity Fraud Event. Unauthorized Debt includes a debt associated with an Unauthorized Account or Unauthorized Transaction.

**W.50** "Property" means tangible or intangible property, money, goods, services, benefits, rights, records, or interests. Property includes property obtained or sought through an Identity Fraud Event.

**W.51** "Person" means an individual, organization, partnership, association, trust, estate, governmental entity, or other legal entity. Person includes an entity acting through an employee, agent, representative, or contractor.

**W.52** "Organization" means a business, corporation, partnership, association, institution, governmental entity, or other legal entity. Organization includes a Financial Institution, Creditor, Credit Reporting Agency, or Governmental Body.
