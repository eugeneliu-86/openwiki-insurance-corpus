# HO 04 91 Water Backup — Unit-Owners (Edition 2019-03)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" forms part of the agreement between you and us. Its provisions apply only as stated in this endorsement.

We provide the coverage described in this endorsement subject to its terms. An insured must comply with all duties imposed by this endorsement.

Words and phrases that are defined in this endorsement have the meanings assigned to them. Words and phrases not defined in this endorsement retain their ordinary meaning.

The provisions of this endorsement apply to loss that is covered under its terms. We do not cover loss except as provided by this endorsement.

The coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to all applicable conditions. An insured must satisfy those conditions before we will pay for covered loss.

This endorsement does not create coverage where coverage is not otherwise provided by its terms. It does not enlarge any duty beyond that stated in this endorsement.

We will determine covered loss in accordance with the provisions of this endorsement. We will apply its exclusions and limitations as stated.

An insured must provide truthful information concerning a claim under this endorsement. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

We may inspect damaged property and request information that is material to a claim. An insured must preserve damaged property when reasonably possible.

We may adjust a covered loss with any insured. Payment to an insured does not affect the rights and duties of any other insured.

The terms of this endorsement apply separately to each insured. This provision does not increase the amount we will pay for any covered loss.

If any provision of this endorsement is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. We will apply the remaining provisions to the extent permitted by law.

This endorsement applies only while it is in effect. Its provisions govern coverage for loss to which this endorsement applies.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide coverage for direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by "Water Backup", subject to the limit insubject to the limit in (see W.2, Limit of Liability). This coverage applies only when the loss is caused by an accidental event.

**W.2** "Water Backup" means water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer or drain. It also means water or waterborne material that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**W.3** We cover direct physical loss caused by water that enters covered property through a sewer or drain because of a backup. The backup must be the efficient cause of the loss.

**W.4** We cover direct physical loss caused by an overflow from a sump or sump pump. The overflow must occur at the described location.

**W.5** We cover direct physical loss caused by a discharge from related equipment that serves a sump or sump pump. The discharge must result from the operation or failure of that equipment.

**W.6** Coverage applies to covered property owned by you. Coverage also applies to covered property for which you are legally responsible.

**W.7** Coverage applies to building property for which you have an insurable interest. The property must be included within the property covered by this policy.

**W.8** Coverage applies to personal property that is covered under this policy. The personal property must be damaged by Water Backup.

**W.9** We cover resulting damage caused by Water Backup to covered property. The resulting damage must be direct physical loss.

**W.10** We cover reasonable expenses to remove water or waterborne material from covered property when the removal is necessary because of Water Backup. The expenses must result directly from a covered loss.

**W.11** We cover reasonable expenses to clean covered property damaged by Water Backup. Cleaning expenses must be necessary to restore the damaged property.

**W.12** We cover reasonable expenses to dry covered property damaged by Water Backup. Drying expenses must be necessary to prevent further direct physical loss.

**W.13** We cover reasonable expenses to remove debris from covered property damaged by Water Backup. Debris removal must be necessary because of the covered loss.

**W.14** We cover damage to permanently installed fixtures when Water Backup damages those fixtures. The fixtures must be included within covered property.

**W.15** We cover damage to appliances when Water Backup damages those appliances. The appliances must be covered property at the time of loss.

**W.16** We cover damage to floor coverings when Water Backup damages those floor coverings. The floor coverings must be covered property at the time of loss.

**W.17** We cover damage to interior finishes when Water Backup damages those finishes. The interior finishes must be covered property at the time of loss.

**W.18** We cover damage to improvements and betterments made by you when Water Backup damages those improvements and betterments. You must have an insurable interest in the damaged property.

**W.19** We cover damage to property used in connection with the residence premises when that property is covered under this policy. The damage must be caused by Water Backup.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by flood. This exclusion applies whether flood acts alone or with Water Backup.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by surface water. This exclusion applies whether surface water enters the residence premises before, during, or after Water Backup.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps into, or leaks through covered property.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a body of water. This exclusion applies whether the overflow is driven by wind or another force.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters through an opening in a roof, wall, window, door, or foundation. This exclusion does not apply when Water Backup directly causes the opening.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by leakage or seepage that occurs continuously or repeatedly. This exclusion applies even if the leakage or seepage results in water damage.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion applies only to the additional loss caused by that neglect.

**W.27** We do not cover loss to property that is not covered property. This exclusion applies even if Water Backup damages property near the residence premises.

**W.28** We do not cover loss to property owned by another person unless you are legally responsible for that property. Your legal responsibility must exist at the time of loss.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, or related equipment to operate because of a condition that existed before the loss. This exclusion applies when the condition was known to an insured and not corrected.

**W.30** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, or improve a sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, or related equipment solely because it failed to operate. We cover resulting direct physical loss to covered property when caused by Water Backup.

**W.31** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage after Water Backup occurs. An insured must keep a record of expenses incurred to protect the property.

**W.32** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property as often as reasonably required. An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.33** An insured must provide records and documents that support the amount of loss when we request them. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and adjustment of the loss.

**W.34** An insured must notify us promptly of a loss caused by Water Backup. Notice must include information sufficient to identify the damaged property and the apparent cause of loss.

**W.35** Payment for covered loss caused by Water Backup does not increase the coverage provided by this endorsement. All covered loss arising from Water Backup is subject to the applicable limit.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** This endorsement provides a separate limit of liability for covered loss caused by "water backup or sump overflow". This limit applies to property covered under this policy.

**W.2** The most we will pay for the sum of all covered losses caused by water backup or sump overflow is seven thousand five hundred dollars.

**W.3** This limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claims, locations, or items of covered property involved in the loss.

**W.4** This limit applies to all loss resulting from the same water backup or sump overflow. It also applies to loss resulting from related water backup or sump overflow conditions.

**W.5** We will not pay more than this limit for loss to property owned by you. We will not pay more than this limit for loss to property for which you are legally responsible.

**W.6** This limit includes all amounts payable for direct physical loss to covered property. This limit also includes amounts payable for reasonable repairs made solely to protect covered property from further damage.

**W.7** This limit applies before consideration of any deductible that applies to the loss. The applicable deductible will be subtracted from the amount otherwise payable.

**W.8** The deductible will not increase this limit. The deductible will not apply to property that is not covered.

**W.9** This limit is not added to any other limit of liability that applies to covered property. No other limit of liability increases this limit.

**W.10** Payment under this endorsement reduces the amount available under this limit. Payment for any covered loss does not create additional coverage.

**W.11** This limit applies whether the water backup or sump overflow affects all or part of the covered property. The extent of damage does not increase this limit.

**W.12** This limit applies whether the loss arises from water entering through a drain, sewer, sump, or related system. The source of the water does not increase this limit.

**W.13** This limit applies to loss caused by overflow from a sump. This limit also applies to loss caused by discharge from a sump.

**W.14** This limit applies to loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. It applies whether the backup occurs on or away from the residence premises.

**W.15** We will pay only for loss that is covered under this endorsement and the policy. We do not pay for loss that is excluded.

**W.16** An insured must comply with all duties that apply after a loss. Failure to comply with those duties may affect the amount we pay.

**W.17** An insured must protect covered property from further damage when reasonably possible. Reasonable protective measures do not increase this limit.

**W.18** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. Disposal of damaged property without our consent may affect coverage.

**W.19** An insured must provide records and information that we reasonably request to determine the amount of loss. We may inspect the property and examine supporting information.

**W.20** We may repair, replace, or pay for covered property, subject to this limit. Our election does not increase the amount available under this endorsement.

**W.21** We may settle a claim with you or with a person having a legal interest in the covered property. A settlement does not waive any policy condition or exclusion.

**W.22** We do not cover any amount that exceeds this limit. We do not cover any loss for which coverage is otherwise unavailable.

**W.23** This limit applies without regard to the value of the covered property. This limit applies without regard to the cost to repair or replace the covered property.

**W.24** All coverage under this endorsement is subject to this limit of liability. No action by us will increase the amount payable beyond this limit.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** This provision applies to a "covered loss" caused by "water backup." We apply the deductible to the amount otherwise payable for that covered loss.

**W.2** The deductible applies to loss to covered property and to covered expense arising from water backup. The deductible applies whether the loss involves damage, expense, or a combination of damage and expense.

**W.3** The "water backup endorsement deductible" is seven hundred fifty dollars. We apply that deductible to each covered loss caused by water backup.

**W.4** We will subtract the deductible from the amount otherwise payable for covered loss. We will pay only the amount that remains after application of the deductible.

**W.5** We do not pay any part of a covered loss that is within the deductible. Your responsibility for that part of the loss remains unchanged.

**W.6** The deductible applies to the total covered loss arising from water backup. We do not apply the deductible separately to portions of the same covered loss.

**W.7** We apply exclusions before determining the amount of covered loss. The deductible applies only to the amount that remains covered.

**W.8** We determine the amount of covered loss under the valuation provisions that apply to the damaged property. We then apply the deductible to the amount otherwise payable.

**W.9** The deductible does not create coverage for property, damage, expense, or loss that is not covered. The deductible does not affect an exclusion that applies to the loss.

**W.10** The deductible does not increase any applicable limit of liability. Our payment remains subject to the applicable limit of liability.

**W.11** We do not reimburse you for the deductible from any payment we make for covered loss. The deductible remains your responsibility.

**W.12** We apply the deductible without regard to the source of payment for repair, restoration, or replacement. A payment made by another party does not change our application of the deductible.

**W.13** We apply the deductible to the covered loss before determining the amount we will pay. Our payment does not include the portion of loss represented by the deductible.

**W.14** A claim for water backup does not eliminate the deductible. We apply the deductible whenever a covered loss caused by water backup is payable.

**W.15** An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine the amount of covered loss. An insured must support the claimed amount with records or other evidence reasonably available.

**W.16** An insured must allow us to inspect damaged property when we reasonably request. Inspection does not waive our right to apply the deductible.

**W.17** An insured must not incur expense beyond that reasonably necessary to protect covered property from further damage. The deductible applies to covered expense that is otherwise payable.

**W.18** Our investigation of a claim does not waive the deductible. Our payment of any amount does not waive the deductible as to any remaining covered loss.

**W.19** The deductible applies regardless of whether you repair, replace, or do not restore damaged property. The amount of payment remains subject to the deductible.

**W.20** We apply the deductible only to covered loss caused by water backup. We do not pay the portion of that covered loss represented by the deductible.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss caused by "flood" or "surface water." Flood and surface water remains excluded.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by waves, tidal water, tsunami, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies whether driven by wind or not.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a body of water. This exclusion applies whether the water is natural or artificial.

**W.4** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This includes water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, pavement, or other structure.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters a building through an opening caused by flood or surface water. This exclusion applies even if water also backs up through a sewer, drain, or sump.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by erosion, earth movement, or subsidence. This exclusion applies whether water contributes to the loss or not.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies even if pollutants are carried by water.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by the presence, growth, proliferation, spread, or any activity of fungi, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies whether the condition results from water backup or another cause.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must protect property from further damage.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. This exclusion applies if an insured commits or directs an act with the intent to cause a loss.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by fraudulent conduct by an insured. We do not cover any claim that is supported by material misrepresentation or concealment.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by war. This includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or action taken to hinder or defend against any of these.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. This exclusion applies whether the nuclear hazard is controlled or uncontrolled.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action. This includes seizure, confiscation, destruction, or disposal of property by order of a governmental authority.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance, law, or regulation that requires testing, monitoring, cleaning up, removing, containing, treating, detoxifying, neutralizing, or responding to pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the action is required by a governmental authority or another person.

**W.16** We do not cover loss to property that is not "covered property." We do not cover loss to property owned by another person unless the property is covered under this policy.

**W.17** We do not cover loss to land. This exclusion includes land on which a residence premises is located.

**W.18** We do not cover loss to lawns, trees, shrubs, plants, or growing crops. This exclusion applies even if water backup damages soil or landscaping materials.

**W.19** We do not cover loss to property used for business purposes except as otherwise provided by this policy. We do not cover loss arising from business operations conducted by an insured.

**W.20** We do not cover loss to property held as stock, merchandise, or inventory. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the property is intended for sale.

**W.21** We do not cover loss to property of roomers, boarders, tenants, or other occupants. We do not cover loss to property for which an insured has no insurable interest.

**W.22** We do not cover loss to property located away from the residence premises unless the property is otherwise covered by this policy. This exclusion applies even if the loss results from water backup at another location.

**W.23** We do not cover loss to property that is vacant. We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain heat, utilities, or protective safeguards where such failure contributes to the loss.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. This exclusion applies even if such condition allows water to enter or remain within property.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, smog, or mechanical breakdown. This exclusion applies to a sewer, drain, sump, pump, valve, appliance, or plumbing system.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover loss caused by defective design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow from a plumbing system that is not located on the residence premises. This exclusion applies even if the discharge reaches covered property.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by seepage or leakage of water occurring over time. This exclusion applies whether the seepage or leakage is hidden or visible.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by condensation, humidity, or atmospheric moisture. This exclusion applies even if the condition affects property following water backup.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of water in a plumbing system, heating system, air conditioning system, automatic fire protective sprinkler system, household appliance, or other device. This exclusion applies unless the loss results directly from covered water backup.

**W.31** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, maintain, or improve a sewer, drain, sump, pump, valve, plumbing system, or other device that caused or contributed to water backup. We do not cover the cost to correct a defect, blockage, obstruction, or inadequate capacity.

**W.32** We do not cover the cost to install, replace, upgrade, or maintain a backwater valve. A backwater valve is not covered merely because its absence contributes to water backup.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of power, communication, water, or other utility service. This exclusion applies whether the failure occurs on or away from the residence premises.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a sump pump, drain pump, sewage ejector, or similar equipment unless the resulting loss is otherwise covered. We do not cover the equipment itself for mechanical or electrical failure.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by acts or decisions of any person, group, organization, or governmental body. This exclusion applies if the act or decision causes or contributes to the loss.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by an interruption of utility service, business, or occupancy. We do not cover loss consisting of diminished value, loss of use, or loss of market.

**W.37** We do not cover fines, penalties, assessments, or charges imposed by any person or governmental authority. We do not cover expenses incurred to comply with an order unless expressly covered.

**W.38** We do not cover loss consisting of consequential, indirect, incidental, or remote damages. We do not cover damages for delay, inconvenience, loss of enjoyment, or emotional distress.

**W.39** We do not cover any amount that exceeds the applicable limit of liability. The "other structures limit" remains subject to the terms that apply to that limit.

**W.40** We do not cover any amount that exceeds the applicable limit for personal property. The "contents limit percentage" remains subject to the terms that apply to that percentage.

**W.41** We do not cover any amount that exceeds the applicable limit for additional living expense. The "ALE percentage" remains subject to the terms that apply to that percentage.

**W.42** We do not cover debris removal except to the extent otherwise provided by this policy. The "debris removal additional percentage" remains subject to the terms that apply to that percentage.

**W.43** We do not cover any amount that is subject to an applicable deductible. The "minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage" remains subject to the terms that apply to that percentage.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties in this section when there is a "loss" involving "water backup". Compliance by any insured satisfies the duties of all insureds only to the extent that the compliance protects our rights.

**W.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. Notice must include information reasonably available to the insured concerning the location, cause, and apparent extent of the loss.

**W.3** An insured must notify us promptly when an insured becomes aware of circumstances that may result in a claim. An insured must forward to us any notice, demand, summons, or legal paper relating to the loss.

**W.4** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect "covered property" from further damage. An insured must keep a record of expenses incurred to protect covered property and must provide that record to us upon request.

**W.5** An insured must report a loss to us within sixty days after the insured discovers the loss. Delay in reporting does not affect coverage unless the delay prejudices our ability to investigate, adjust, or defend the claim.

**W.6** An insured must identify the source and path of water that caused or contributed to the loss, if reasonably possible. An insured must preserve evidence relating to the source and path of water until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.7** An insured must make damaged property available for our inspection. An insured must permit us to inspect the "residence premises" and any area from which water may have entered or backed up.

**W.8** An insured must not discard damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property from further damage. If disposal is necessary, an insured must preserve photographs, records, and other available evidence of the condition of the property.

**W.9** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer questions truthfully and must provide records and documents reasonably related to the loss.

**W.10** An insured must produce for examination books, receipts, invoices, photographs, estimates, and other records that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records.

**W.11** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining information from persons who may have knowledge of the loss.

**W.12** An insured must provide a "proof of loss" when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary to determine the amount and cause of the loss.

**W.13** A proof of loss must identify the damaged property and describe the damage. A proof of loss must state the amount claimed and the basis on which that amount is calculated.

**W.14** An insured must provide supporting documents for amounts claimed. Supporting documents may include estimates, invoices, receipts, contracts, photographs, and records of repair or replacement.

**W.15** An insured must submit a proof of loss signed by an insured when we reasonably request a signed proof of loss. The "sworn statement deadline" applies when a sworn statement is requested.

**W.16** An insured must not make repairs that materially alter evidence of the cause or extent of the loss before we inspect the property, unless immediate repairs are necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must preserve records of any emergency repair.

**W.17** An insured must promptly notify us before beginning permanent repairs for which reimbursement is sought. Notice is not required when delay would reasonably increase damage or create an unsafe condition.

**W.18** An insured must allow us to inspect repaired or replaced property when we reasonably request inspection. An insured must provide reasonable access to records concerning the repair or replacement.

**W.19** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property from further damage. This condition does not prevent an insured from obtaining emergency services reasonably necessary to protect persons or property.

**W.20** An insured must not abandon property to us. We may take all or any part of damaged property at the agreed or appraised value.

**W.21** An insured must preserve any rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.22** If we pay a loss, an insured's rights of recovery against another person or organization are transferred to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must cooperate with us in enforcing those rights.

**W.23** An insured must execute and deliver papers and take actions reasonably required to secure our recovery rights. We will not require an insured to incur unreimbursed expense in carrying out this duty.

**W.24** If other insurance applies to the same loss, an insured must notify us of that insurance. An insured must provide information reasonably requested concerning the terms, limits, and insurer of the "other insurance".

**W.25** We will pay our share of a covered loss according to the applicable terms of the other insurance. An insured must cooperate with us in determining the availability of other insurance.

**W.26** The "deductible" applies to each covered loss unless the applicable coverage terms provide otherwise. The "base deductible minimum" applies in determining the deductible applicable to this coverage.

**W.27** We will reduce the amount otherwise payable by the applicable deductible. An insured may not apply a deductible paid under another coverage to reduce the deductible applicable to this coverage unless we agree in writing.

**W.28** We may require an appraisal when an insured and we fail to agree on the amount of loss. Appraisal does not determine whether coverage applies.

**W.29** If appraisal is required, each party must select a competent and impartial appraiser. The appraisers must select a competent and impartial "umpire".

**W.30** If the appraisers cannot agree on an umpire, either party may request that an umpire be selected by a court having jurisdiction. Each party will pay the expenses of its appraiser and will share the expenses of the umpire and appraisal equally.

**W.31** A written agreement by the appraisers on the amount of loss is binding. A written agreement by an appraiser and the umpire on the amount of loss is binding.

**W.32** We may investigate a claim and adjust any loss without waiving any policy provision or defense. Our investigation or payment does not constitute an admission of coverage.

**W.33** We may inspect property as often as reasonably necessary to investigate a claim. Our inspection does not constitute a warranty that property is safe or free from damage.

**W.34** We may obtain statements from an insured or other persons with knowledge relevant to the loss. An insured must cooperate in arranging access to such persons when reasonably requested.

**W.35** An insured must provide authorization for us to obtain records reasonably related to the loss. The authorization must be limited to records reasonably necessary for our investigation, adjustment, or defense.

**W.36** An insured must promptly notify us if the ownership, possession, or use of damaged property changes. An insured must also notify us if the property is sold, transferred, repaired, or replaced.

**W.37** An insured must notify us of any demand for payment received from another person or organization arising out of the loss. An insured must not settle such demand without our written consent if the settlement could impair our rights.

**W.38** An insured must notify us promptly of any recovered property. We may adjust the loss payment to reflect the value of "recovered property".

**W.39** An insured must protect "salvage" that may reduce the amount of loss. We may take possession of salvage after payment to the extent permitted by law.

**W.40** An insured must provide access to the unit and related areas when access is reasonably necessary to investigate the loss. An insured must make reasonable efforts to obtain permission for access to areas not under the insured's control.

**W.41** An insured must cooperate with efforts to determine whether damage resulted from water that backed up through sewers, drains, or sumps. An insured must provide available maintenance records relating to the affected system.

**W.42** An insured must promptly report any condition that may increase the extent of water damage. An insured must take reasonable measures to stop or limit continued entry, overflow, discharge, or backup of water.

**W.43** An insured must retain records concerning prior damage to the same property when those records are reasonably available. An insured must disclose prior repairs, prior claims, and known conditions that may relate to the claimed loss.

**W.44** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent any material fact or circumstance relating to this insurance, the loss, or a claim. We do not provide coverage for a loss if an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance.

**W.45** An insured must provide complete and accurate information concerning ownership of damaged property. An insured must identify any person or organization having an interest in the property.

**W.46** An insured must promptly notify us if a lienholder, unit owners association, mortgagee, or other interested party asserts a right relating to the loss. An insured must provide information reasonably requested concerning that asserted right.

**W.47** Payment to an insured does not affect the rights of any person or organization having a legal interest in covered property. We may include any such person or organization in payment when required by law or when necessary to protect our interests.

**W.48** An insured must not bring a legal action against us unless the insured has complied with all applicable policy conditions. The "suit against us period" applies to any legal action against us.

**W.49** An insured must not assign rights under this coverage without our written consent. An assignment after a loss is subject to our rights and defenses existing at the time of the assignment.

**W.50** A waiver or change of any provision is effective only if made in writing by us. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision later.

**W.51** If any provision of this section is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. An unenforceable provision will be applied to the fullest extent permitted by law.

**W.52** An insured must provide a current mailing address, electronic address, and telephone contact when reasonably requested. An insured must notify us promptly of a change in contact information relevant to a claim.

**W.53** Notices and documents provided by an insured must be delivered by a method reasonably calculated to ensure receipt. We may accept notice or documents by electronic means when permitted by law.

**W.54** An insured must promptly notify us if the insured learns that information previously provided was incomplete or inaccurate. An insured must provide corrected information as soon as reasonably possible.

**W.55** An insured must cooperate with reasonable efforts to determine the "actual cash value" of damaged property. An insured must provide information concerning the age, condition, purchase, repair, and replacement of that property when reasonably available.

**W.56** An insured seeking replacement cost settlement must provide records reasonably necessary to establish that replacement has occurred. The "insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement" applies to any replacement cost settlement.

**W.57** An insured must retain invoices and proof of payment for repair or replacement when reimbursement is sought. We may require reasonable evidence that the repair or replacement relates to the covered loss.

**W.58** An insured must notify us before disposing of property for which replacement cost payment is sought. An insured must allow us a reasonable opportunity to inspect that property before disposal.

**W.59** We may deny a claim to the extent an insured's failure to perform a duty under this section prejudices our rights. We will not deny a claim solely because an insured failed to perform a duty that was impossible under the circumstances.

**W.60** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting persons or organizations performing emergency services or repairs. An insured must provide available contracts, invoices, and communications relating to those services or repairs.

**W.61** An insured must promptly notify us if any governmental authority issues an order relating to the loss. An insured must provide a copy of the order when reasonably available.

**W.62** An insured must cooperate with reasonable efforts to identify whether damaged property can be repaired, restored, or replaced. An insured must not prevent us from evaluating reasonable repair alternatives.

**W.63** An insured must provide information reasonably requested concerning the use of the unit at the time of loss. An insured must also provide information reasonably requested concerning occupancy, maintenance, and access conditions relevant to the loss.

**W.64** Our payment of a covered loss is subject to all applicable terms and conditions of this coverage. An insured's compliance with these conditions does not create coverage for a loss that is otherwise not covered.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** This endorsement applies only to direct physical loss caused by a "water backup event". A water backup event means water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer, drain, sump, or related equipment.

**W.2** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from further damage after a water backup event. An insured must allow us to inspect the property and verify the cause and extent of loss.

**W.3** An insured must retain damaged property, when practical, until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must not discard, alter, or repair damaged property in a manner that prevents us from determining the cause of loss.

**W.4** An insured must give us prompt notice of a water backup event. The notice must identify the location of the loss, the apparent source of the water or waterborne material, and the property affected.

**W.5** An insured must make reasonable efforts to stop the entry, backup, overflow, or discharge of water or waterborne material. An insured must protect property from additional damage caused by conditions arising from the water backup event.

**W.6** For purposes of this provision, a "backwater valve" means a device designed to prevent reverse flow through plumbing. A backwater valve is not required where finished areas below grade are served by plumbing that can receive a sewer or drain backup.

**W.7** When a backwater valve is present, an insured must maintain it in operable condition. An insured must promptly correct a known condition that prevents the valve from operating as intended.

**W.8** An insured must cooperate with a property manager, condominium association, or other person responsible for common plumbing or drainage systems. An insured must permit reasonable access to the unit when access is needed to investigate or correct the source of a water backup event.

**W.9** An insured must promptly report a suspected defect in a common plumbing, drainage, sewer, or sump system to the person responsible for that system. Reporting a condition does not relieve an insured of the duty to protect property from further damage.

**W.10** An insured must preserve available evidence showing the source of the water or waterborne material. Such evidence may include photographs, recordings, reports, damaged components, and communications concerning the event.

**W.11** An insured must identify any person known to have information concerning the water backup event. An insured must provide available contact information for that person when we request it.

**W.12** An insured must provide records showing repairs, maintenance, alterations, or service affecting the plumbing, drainage, sewer, sump, or related equipment involved in the loss. An insured must provide those records when they are available to you.

**W.13** An insured must promptly notify us if a governmental authority, property manager, condominium association, or other person directs repairs or mitigation. An insured must provide us with available written directions concerning that work.

**W.14** An insured may take emergency measures that are necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must retain records and receipts for emergency measures when practical.

**W.15** An insured must not authorize permanent repairs until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect the damaged property, unless delay would cause further damage. An insured must notify us when permanent repairs are necessary before our inspection.

**W.16** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting persons who perform mitigation, cleanup, plumbing, drainage, or repair work. An insured must permit us to inspect work performed in connection with the water backup event.

**W.17** An insured must keep damaged property separate from undamaged property when practical. An insured must identify property removed from the unit because of the water backup event.

**W.18** An insured must provide us with available estimates, invoices, work orders, and receipts relating to the claimed loss. An insured must identify work that was performed before the water backup event and work performed because of it.

**W.19** An insured must provide a complete description of the damaged property and the nature of the damage. An insured must state whether the property was repaired, replaced, disposed of, or retained.

**W.20** An insured must provide information concerning prior water intrusion, backups, overflows, drainage problems, plumbing leaks, or sump conditions affecting the unit when we request it. An insured must disclose known repairs or alterations related to those conditions.

**W.21** An insured must provide information concerning any claim, complaint, demand, or request for payment made to another person in connection with the water backup event. An insured must promptly notify us of any recovery received from another person for the same loss.

**W.22** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or omit a material fact concerning the water backup event or the claimed loss. An insured must provide information that is true and complete to the best of the insured's knowledge.

**W.23** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must provide documents and records that we reasonably request for the examination.

**W.24** An insured must provide a signed statement describing the water backup event when we request it. The statement must include information available to you concerning the cause, location, discovery, and resulting damage.

**W.25** An insured must notify us if access to the unit is restricted by a property manager, condominium association, tenant, occupant, or other person. An insured must use reasonable efforts to obtain access needed for our inspection.

**W.26** An insured must notify us of repairs or alterations to shared plumbing, drainage, sewer, sump, or related equipment that may affect the claimed loss. An insured must provide available records concerning those repairs or alterations.

**W.27** An insured must follow reasonable safety directions issued by public authorities or qualified contractors concerning waterborne contaminants. An insured must not expose property to further damage by failing to take reasonable protective measures.

**W.28** An insured must permit us to inspect the source of the water backup event, including accessible plumbing, drainage, sewer, sump, and related equipment. Our inspection does not require us to identify the source of the event.

**W.29** An insured must preserve any removed plumbing, drainage, sewer, sump, or related component that may help establish the cause of loss, when practical. An insured must notify us before disposing of such a component.

**W.30** An insured must provide us with available reports from plumbers, drain specialists, restoration contractors, engineers, or other persons who inspected the condition. An insured must identify any opinion concerning the cause of the water backup event.

**W.31** An insured must promptly notify us if another insurance policy, service contract, warranty, maintenance agreement, or responsible person may provide payment for the claimed loss. An insured must cooperate with us in pursuing available recovery rights.

**W.32** An insured must do nothing after a water backup event to impair our right to recover from a person responsible for the loss. An insured must execute documents that are reasonably necessary to protect that right.

**W.33** An insured must provide a signed, sworn proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the facts of the loss, the property involved, and the amount claimed.

**W.34** An insured must make available for inspection any property for which payment is claimed. An insured must permit us to take samples, photographs, measurements, or recordings that are reasonably necessary to evaluate the loss.

**W.35** An insured must cooperate in determining whether damage resulted from a water backup event or from another cause. An insured must provide available information that distinguishes damage caused by the event from preexisting damage.

**W.36** An insured must promptly correct a condition that continues to cause water backup, overflow, seepage, or discharge after discovery of that condition. This duty applies whether the condition is within the unit or affects the unit from a common system.

**W.37** An insured must comply with these requirements before seeking payment for a claimed loss, unless compliance is not reasonably possible. Failure to comply with a requirement may affect coverage when the failure prejudices us.

**W.38** Our investigation, inspection, payment, or communication concerning a water backup event does not waive any right under this endorsement. We may request additional information that is reasonably necessary to evaluate the claimed loss.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" involving "water backup" to "covered property" only when coverage applies. We will determine the amount payable from the facts of the loss.

**W.2** "Covered property" means property for which coverage applies. Property that is not covered property is not included in loss settlement.

**W.3** We will determine the amount of a covered loss after application of the applicable deductible. Payment of a deductible does not create coverage for property or loss that is not covered.

**W.4** We will settle a covered loss to personal property on a actual cash value basis. The amount payable remains subject to all applicable limitations.

**W.5** We will not pay more than the amount of covered loss sustained by an insured. We will not pay more than an insured's financial interest in covered property.

**W.6** We may pay for the repair of damaged covered property when repair will restore the property to its condition immediately before the covered loss. We may pay the amount necessary to replace damaged covered property when replacement is appropriate.

**W.7** We will not pay for repair or replacement that improves property beyond its condition immediately before the covered loss. We may pay for reasonable work necessary to restore damaged property.

**W.8** We will not pay for loss to undamaged property solely because damaged property cannot be matched in appearance. We will pay for necessary repair or replacement of damaged covered property.

**W.9** We will not pay for any increase in loss caused by delay in protecting damaged property after an insured has a reasonable opportunity to do so. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further loss.

**W.10** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged covered property as often as reasonably required. An insured must retain damaged property for inspection when practicable.

**W.11** An insured must provide records and documents that reasonably support the claimed loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**W.12** An insured must submit a signed statement of loss when we request it. The statement must identify the damaged property and state the amount claimed.

**W.13** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a covered loss. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary to determine the amount payable.

**W.14** We may require an insured to show us the damaged property. We may require an insured to provide access to the location where the loss occurred.

**W.15** We may pay a covered loss to an insured entitled to receive payment. Payment to an insured does not determine ownership of covered property.

**W.16** We may make payment to more than one insured when their interests require it. Payment to one insured does not increase the amount otherwise payable.

**W.17** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged covered property after payment upon terms agreed with an insured.

**W.18** We may retain damaged covered property for salvage after payment. Any salvage value will be considered in determining the amount payable when applicable.

**W.19** An insured must preserve rights of recovery against a person responsible for a covered loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**W.20** If we make payment for a covered loss, we may recover that payment from a person responsible for the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in that recovery.

**W.21** We will not pay for a covered loss to the extent another source has paid the same loss. We will not make duplicate payment for the same property damage.

**W.22** If other insurance applies to a covered loss, we will pay no more than our share of the loss. An insured must provide information about other insurance that may apply.

**W.23** We will determine the amount payable without regard to an insured's personal preference for a particular contractor or method of repair. An insured may select a contractor, subject to the amount we determine is payable.

**W.24** We will not pay for costs attributable solely to a change in design, construction, or materials that is not necessary to repair covered damage. We will pay reasonable costs necessary to repair covered property.

**W.25** We do not cover any increase in loss caused by an insured's intentional concealment of damaged property. We do not cover any loss when an insured intentionally misrepresents a material fact relating to that loss.

**W.26** We may request information concerning the ownership, use, and condition of damaged covered property. An insured must provide that information when it is reasonably available.

**W.27** We may defer payment until we have sufficient information to determine the amount payable. We will not defer payment solely because an insured has chosen not to complete repairs.

**W.28** We will not require an insured to repair or replace damaged covered property as a condition of receiving payment unless required by applicable law. We may consider completed repairs in resolving a dispute about the amount of loss.

**W.29** We will settle a covered loss based on the condition of covered property immediately before the loss. We will not include loss that existed before the water backup.

**W.30** We will not pay for loss caused by wear, deterioration, neglect, or maintenance conditions unless that loss is part of a covered loss. We will pay only for direct physical loss caused by water backup.

**W.31** A disagreement about the amount of loss does not alter an insured's duty to protect covered property. A disagreement does not relieve us of our duty to pay an undisputed amount.

**W.32** Payment of an undisputed amount does not waive our right to investigate the remaining claimed loss. Payment does not waive an insured's right to seek additional amounts that are properly payable.

**W.33** A covered loss does not alter the applicability of the "special assessment limit" or the "fire department service charge limit". Those limits remain separate from the settlement of covered property.

**W.34** A covered loss does not alter the applicability of the "jewelry theft sublimit" or the "business property sublimit". Those sublimits remain separate from the settlement of covered property.

**W.35** We will apply all applicable limitations before making payment. Payment for a covered loss does not increase any applicable limit or sublimit.

**W.36** We may settle a covered loss with an insured or an authorized representative of an insured. An insured must provide evidence of authority when another person acts for that insured.

**W.37** Our payment for a covered loss does not transfer ownership of covered property unless we agree otherwise. An insured retains all duties concerning property not taken by us.

**W.38** We will pay the amount we determine is payable after an insured has complied with the applicable duties. Our payment is subject to any rights of recovery and any applicable limitation.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** We use the following words and phrases with the meanings stated in this section. A word or phrase has the same meaning whether used in singular or plural form.

**W.2** "Water Backup" means water, "waterborne material", or "sewage" that enters through a "sewer" or "drain" because of a "backup". Water Backup also means water, waterborne material, or sewage that enters because of an "overflow" or "discharge" from a "sump", "sump pump", or "related equipment".

**W.3** "Waterborne material" means material carried, suspended, or deposited by water. Waterborne material includes sediment, debris, and other substances transported with water.

**W.4** "Sewage" means waste matter carried or intended to be carried through a sewer. Sewage includes water containing human, animal, or household waste.

**W.5** "Sewer" means a pipe, conduit, channel, or similar facility designed to carry sewage or water away from a building or area. A sewer may be public, private, shared, or maintained for the benefit of property owners.

**W.6** "Drain" means a pipe, opening, fixture, or similar means through which water is intended to leave a building, room, surface, or system. A drain includes a floor drain and a drainage opening serving plumbing equipment.

**W.7** "Building drain" means the portion of a drainage system that receives discharge from plumbing fixtures or equipment within a building. A building drain may connect to a sewer, lateral line, or other drainage facility.

**W.8** "Lateral line" means a pipe or conduit that carries water or sewage between a building drain and a sewer or similar collection facility. A lateral line may be located on or beyond the premises.

**W.9** "Plumbing system" means pipes, fixtures, drains, valves, and related components installed to supply, distribute, collect, or remove water or sewage. A plumbing system includes components located within walls, floors, ceilings, or other concealed spaces.

**W.10** "Sump" means a pit, basin, reservoir, or similar receptacle designed to collect water. A sump may receive water from drains, foundation areas, mechanical equipment, or other collection points.

**W.11** "Sump pump" means a pump designed to remove water from a sump. A sump pump includes the components necessary to move collected water away from the sump.

**W.12** "Related equipment" means equipment used with a sump or sump pump to collect, control, direct, or remove water. Related equipment includes piping, hoses, valves, controls, alarms, and power connections.

**W.13** "Backwater valve" means a valve designed to prevent water or sewage from flowing in a reverse direction through a sewer, drain, or connected pipe. A backwater valve may operate automatically or manually.

**W.14** "Check valve" means a valve designed to allow flow in an intended direction and restrict flow in the opposite direction. A check valve may be part of related equipment or a plumbing system.

**W.15** "Overflow" means the escape of water, waterborne material, or sewage from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment because the material exceeds the capacity of that equipment to contain it. An overflow includes material passing over or out of an intended containment area.

**W.16** "Discharge" means the release of water, waterborne material, or sewage from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. A discharge includes a release that occurs through a connected pipe, hose, opening, or outlet.

**W.17** "Backup" means reverse or impeded flow of water, waterborne material, or sewage through a sewer, drain, pipe, or connected system. A backup may result from an obstruction, limited capacity, or other condition that prevents intended flow.

**W.18** "Obstruction" means material, damage, or a condition that blocks, restricts, or impairs the intended flow through a sewer, drain, pipe, valve, or related equipment. An obstruction may be partial or complete.

**W.19** "Malfunction" means a failure of equipment to operate as intended. A malfunction includes improper operation of a pump, valve, switch, control, alarm, or connected component.

**W.20** "Power interruption" means a loss, reduction, or disruption of electrical power supplied to a sump pump or related equipment. A power interruption includes a condition that prevents the equipment from operating as intended.

**W.21** "Premises" means the location where the unit is situated and the areas used in connection with the unit. The premises include areas under your control that are used for access, maintenance, or operation of covered property.

**W.22** "Unit" means the portion of a residential building that you own or occupy as your residence. A unit includes attached portions of that residential building that are reserved for your exclusive use.

**W.23** "Common area" means an area of the premises available for shared use by occupants or owners. A common area may include passages, utility areas, building systems, and other areas not reserved for your exclusive use.

**W.24** "Direct physical loss" means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property. Direct physical loss does not include a condition that causes no physical injury to property.
