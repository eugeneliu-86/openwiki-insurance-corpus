# HO 24 73 Farmers Personal Liability (Edition 2011-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" modifies the insurance provided by your "policy." The provisions of this endorsement apply only to the extent they modify that insurance.

The term "you" refers to the "Named Insured" shown in the "Declarations." The terms "we," "us," and "our" refer to the insurer providing this policy.

The term "insured" means a person or organization qualifying as an insured under this endorsement. An insured must comply with the applicable duties and conditions of this endorsement.

Words and phrases appearing in quotation marks have the special meaning stated in this endorsement or in your policy. Words and phrases not defined in this endorsement have the meaning assigned in your policy.

The provisions of this endorsement are part of your policy. This endorsement must be read together with the remaining provisions of your policy.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy, this endorsement applies. All provisions of your policy that are not modified by this endorsement remain applicable.

The "Declarations" form part of this endorsement. Information shown in the Declarations applies as stated.

Coverage under this endorsement is subject to all applicable policy conditions. An insured must satisfy those conditions before we have any duty under this endorsement.

We do not cover any loss or liability except as provided by this endorsement and your policy. The applicable exclusions, limitations, and conditions remain in effect.

An insured must give us truthful and complete information concerning matters relevant to this endorsement. Misrepresentation or concealment may affect coverage as permitted by law.

We may enforce the provisions of this endorsement against any insured. An insured may not assign rights or duties under this endorsement without our written consent.

The terms of this endorsement may be changed only by our written agreement. A waiver by us applies only to the matter for which it is given.

This endorsement takes effect as part of your policy when properly attached. Its provisions apply according to their terms.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the insurance described in this section subject to all applicable terms, conditions, and exclusions. Coverage applies only to an "insured" for a covered loss.

**W.2** "Insured" means you and any resident of your household who is related to you or is in your care. An insured also includes a person or organization acting within the scope of duties performed for you in connection with covered farming.

**W.3** "Bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death. "Property damage" means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property.

**W.4** "Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence must result in bodily injury or property damage during the policy period.

**W.5** "Farm premises" means land, structures, and appurtenant grounds used by an insured in "farming". Farming includes the care, cultivation, raising, harvesting, or management of crops, livestock, or other agricultural products.

**W.6** "Claim" means a demand for damages because of bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury. "Suit" means a civil proceeding in which damages because of bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury are alleged.

**W.7** Under "Personal Liability Coverage", we will pay damages for which an insured is legally liable because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence. The occurrence must arise out of covered personal activities, farming, or the ownership, maintenance, or use of farm premises.

**W.8** We will pay covered damages only up to the applicable "Limit of Liability". Our payment obligation applies regardless of the number of insureds, claimants, claims, or suits arising from the occurrence.

**W.9** We will provide a defense at our expense for an insured against a suit seeking covered damages. We may investigate any occurrence and settle any claim or suit when we consider settlement appropriate.

**W.10** We may investigate, negotiate, or settle a claim without admitting liability. Our investigation, negotiation, or settlement does not waive any policy provision or defense available to us.

**W.11** We are not obligated to defend an insured after the applicable Limit of Liability has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements. We may withdraw from a suit after giving notice to the insured.

**W.12** Under "Medical Payments Coverage", we will pay necessary medical expenses incurred because of bodily injury caused by an accident. The accident must arise from covered personal activities, farming, or the ownership, maintenance, or use of farm premises.

**W.13** "Medical expenses" include reasonable charges for medical, surgical, dental, hospital, nursing, ambulance, professional nursing, funeral, and related services. Medical expenses must be incurred for care of bodily injury caused by the accident.

**W.14** We will pay medical expenses for a person who is on an "insured location" with permission and is injured by an accident arising out of that location. An insured location includes farm premises and premises used by an insured as a residence.

**W.15** We will pay medical expenses for a person who is away from an insured location if the bodily injury arises out of an insured's personal activities. We will also pay medical expenses if the bodily injury arises out of farming conducted by an insured.

**W.16** We will pay medical expenses for a person injured by an animal owned by or in the care of an insured. Coverage applies only if the animal is not being used in connection with an excluded activity.

**W.17** We do not cover medical expenses for you, a resident of your household, or a person regularly residing on an insured location. We do not cover medical expenses for a person eligible to receive benefits required by workers compensation, occupational disease, or similar law.

**W.18** An insured must promptly report an accident that may result in medical expenses. An insured must provide information and records we reasonably request to determine whether medical expenses are covered.

**W.19** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage that is expected or intended by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the actual injury or damage differs in kind or degree from that expected or intended.

**W.20** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of controlled substances by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of medication by an insured as directed by a licensed medical provider.

**W.21** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a "business" conducted by an insured. Business means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain.

**W.22** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of "professional services" performed by an insured. Professional services include services requiring specialized knowledge, training, licensing, certification, or professional judgment.

**W.23** We do not cover liability assumed by an insured under a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**W.24** We do not cover an obligation for which an insured may be held liable under workers compensation, occupational disease, disability benefits, unemployment compensation, or similar law. We do not cover a claim seeking benefits available under such law.

**W.25** We do not cover bodily injury to an "employee" of an insured arising out of or in the course of employment by an insured. Employee includes a leased worker, temporary worker, volunteer worker, or person performing services for an insured.

**W.26** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. We do not cover loss of use of property owned by an insured.

**W.27** We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care of an insured. This exclusion applies to property held by an insured for safekeeping, storage, repair, servicing, processing, or sale.

**W.28** We do not cover property damage to property over which an insured exercises care, custody, or control. This exclusion applies whether the property is owned by another person or organization.

**W.29** We do not cover property damage arising out of faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, or maintenance by an insured. This exclusion applies to property damage arising from work performed by or for an insured.

**W.30** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, operation, use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle" by an insured. Motor vehicle means a land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration.

**W.31** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, operation, use, loading, or unloading of an "aircraft" by an insured. Aircraft includes a device used or designed for flight or aerial navigation.

**W.32** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, operation, use, loading, or unloading of a "watercraft" by an insured. Watercraft includes a vessel, craft, or device used or designed for travel on water.

**W.33** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the actual, alleged, or threatened transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the disease is transmitted directly or indirectly.

**W.34** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of abuse, molestation, harassment, assault, or battery committed by an insured. We do not cover a claim alleging failure to supervise, report, prevent, investigate, or respond to such conduct.

**W.35** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the actual, alleged, or threatened discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of "pollutants". Pollutants means solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, chemical, or other irritants or contaminants.

**W.36** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, military action, or seizure by governmental authority. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing to the loss.

**W.37** We do not cover damages arising out of the loss, loss of use, damage, corruption, inability to access, or inability to manipulate "electronic data". Electronic data means information, facts, concepts, or programs stored, transmitted, or processed by electronic means.

**W.38** We do not cover punitive damages, exemplary damages, fines, penalties, or amounts that are not insurable under applicable law. We do not cover defense costs associated solely with damages that are not covered.

**W.39** We do not cover an assessment charged against an insured by an association, corporation, cooperative, or similar organization because of bodily injury or property damage. This exclusion applies whether the assessment is imposed directly or indirectly.

**W.40** The applicable Limit of Liability is the most we will pay for all covered damages arising from an occurrence. Payment under Medical Payments Coverage reduces the amount available under the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.41** The applicable Limit of Liability applies collectively to all insureds. A claim made against more than one insured does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.42** This insurance applies separately to each insured against whom a claim or suit is brought. The separate application of insurance does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.43** An insured must promptly notify us of an occurrence that may result in a claim. Notice must include information sufficient to identify the insured, the persons involved, the location of the occurrence, and the nature of the claimed injury or damage.

**W.44** An insured must promptly send us copies of notices, demands, legal papers, and other documents relating to a claim or suit. An insured must not assume that another person or organization has provided notice to us.

**W.45** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of a claim or suit. An insured must attend hearings, trials, examinations, and other proceedings when reasonably requested by us.

**W.46** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, incur an expense, or settle a claim without our consent. This duty does not prevent an insured from obtaining reasonable emergency medical care or taking reasonable action to protect property from further damage.

**W.47** If other insurance applies to a covered loss, an insured must promptly provide us information concerning that insurance. We will determine our obligations in relation to other insurance according to the applicable policy terms.

**W.48** If we make a payment under this coverage, we are subrogated to the rights of recovery held by the insured against a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights and must cooperate with us in enforcing them.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The applicable "Limit of Liability" is the most we will pay for "damages" covered by this insurance. It applies to damages arising out of "bodily injury" or "property damage" caused by an "occurrence".

**W.2** The applicable Limit of Liability applies regardless of the number of "insureds", claims made, persons injured, or persons whose property is damaged. It also applies regardless of the number of persons or organizations making claims or bringing a "suit".

**W.3** All damages arising out of the same occurrence are subject to the same applicable Limit of Liability. The limit is not increased because damages result from more than one injury, loss, or cause.

**W.4** Damages arising out of continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions are treated as arising out of the same occurrence. This applies whether the resulting injury or damage is suffered by one or more persons.

**W.5** A claim or suit does not create an additional Limit of Liability. The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by the filing, amendment, consolidation, transfer, or dismissal of a claim or suit.

**W.6** The applicable Limit of Liability is shared by all insureds against whom damages are claimed. No insured is entitled to a separate limit merely because that insured is named in a claim or suit.

**W.7** The applicable Limit of Liability applies collectively to damages for which one or more insureds are legally responsible. It is not multiplied by the number of insureds, residences, locations, vehicles, or activities involved.

**W.8** The applicable Limit of Liability applies to all damages claimed by persons arising out of the same occurrence. It does not increase because claims are asserted by or on behalf of different persons.

**W.9** Amounts paid by us as a "judgment" or "settlement" reduce the available Limit of Liability. A payment made on behalf of an insured is treated as a payment made under the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.10** Our duty to settle or defend ends when the applicable Limit of Liability has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements. We have no duty to continue a defense after that exhaustion.

**W.11** We do not pay damages in excess of the applicable Limit of Liability. This applies even if an insured becomes legally obligated to pay a greater amount.

**W.12** An insured must not make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except at that insured's own cost. Consent by an insured does not increase the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.13** No insured may divide a claim or suit in an effort to obtain more than the applicable Limit of Liability. No claimant may recover more than the applicable Limit of Liability for damages arising out of the same occurrence.

**W.14** The applicable limit for "medical expenses" is the most we will pay for medical expenses covered by this insurance. It applies to medical expenses resulting from "bodily injury" caused by an "accident".

**W.15** The applicable limit for medical expenses applies regardless of the number of insureds, claimants, medical providers, or claims. It is not increased because medical expenses are incurred for treatment, care, services, or supplies from different providers.

**W.16** The applicable limit for medical expenses is the most we will pay for medical expenses incurred by any one person as a result of bodily injury sustained in any one accident. Medical expenses arising out of the same accident are subject to that applicable limit.

**W.17** Payment of medical expenses does not increase the Limit of Liability applicable to damages. The Limit of Liability applicable to damages does not increase the applicable limit for medical expenses.

**W.18** We do not pay medical expenses in excess of the applicable limit for medical expenses. This applies even if the injured person incurs additional medical expenses after that limit has been exhausted.

**W.19** We will not pay the same damages more than once under this insurance. Payment made to resolve a claim or suit may be applied to any damages for which we are legally obligated to pay.

**W.20** A judgment or settlement does not increase any applicable Limit of Liability. An agreement by an insured does not bind us beyond the applicable Limit of Liability.

**W.21** An insured remains responsible for amounts that exceed the applicable Limit of Liability. We are not obligated to contribute to an amount for which coverage is not available under this insurance.

**W.22** The applicable Limit of Liability is not increased by the availability of defense, investigation, settlement, or other services. Our payment obligations are subject to the applicable Limit of Liability.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the amount that you are responsible for with respect to covered damages. We apply the deductible as provided by this policy.

**W.2** An "insured" must pay the deductible that applies to a "covered claim". Payment of the deductible does not increase the coverage available to you.

**W.3** We will reduce the amount we pay for covered "damages" by the deductible. We do not cover the deductible.

**W.4** The deductible applies to damages arising from a covered "occurrence". The deductible applies regardless of the manner in which a claim is presented.

**W.5** We will determine whether the deductible applies from the facts of the claim. Our determination will be based upon the available information concerning the occurrence and damages.

**W.6** The deductible applies to amounts that we pay by settlement, judgment, or other resolution of a covered claim. The deductible also applies when we make payment directly to a claimant.

**W.7** We may pay a claimant an amount that includes the deductible. An insured must reimburse us for the deductible that we pay on your behalf.

**W.8** An insured must promptly reimburse us after we request payment of an applicable deductible. Your reimbursement duty continues after settlement or payment of the claim.

**W.9** We may seek reimbursement from any insured responsible for payment of the deductible. Our right to reimbursement does not limit any other right that we may have.

**W.10** Payment of the deductible by us does not waive your responsibility for that amount. Our payment does not prevent us from enforcing your reimbursement duty.

**W.11** The deductible does not create coverage for a loss that is not covered. We do not cover any loss merely because an insured pays the deductible.

**W.12** The deductible does not apply to amounts that we do not owe under this policy. We will not apply the deductible to an amount that is excluded from coverage.

**W.13** If a claim includes covered damages and damages that are not covered, we will apply the deductible only to covered damages. We may allocate the deductible based upon the facts of the claim.

**W.14** We may allocate the deductible among covered damages arising from the occurrence. Our allocation will not create coverage for an otherwise uncovered loss.

**W.15** An insured must provide information that we reasonably request to determine the applicable deductible. An insured must cooperate with us in resolving any question concerning the deductible.

**W.16** An insured must provide records, receipts, and other available evidence relating to damages for which payment is sought. Failure to provide requested information may affect our ability to determine the deductible.

**W.17** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold material information concerning the deductible. We may enforce all rights available to us when material information is concealed, misrepresented, or withheld.

**W.18** We may settle a covered claim without obtaining agreement concerning the deductible. A settlement does not eliminate an insured's duty to pay the applicable deductible.

**W.19** The deductible does not reduce an insured's duty to cooperate with us. The deductible does not alter an insured's duties after an occurrence or claim.

**W.20** The deductible does not create a duty for us to pay damages that exceed the coverage available under this policy. We do not cover amounts beyond the coverage available to you.

**W.21** We do not cover interest, costs, or expenses that arise solely from an insured's failure to pay the deductible when due. An insured remains responsible for such amounts to the extent permitted by law.

**W.22** The deductible does not affect our right to investigate, negotiate, or settle a claim. Our claim activities do not waive the deductible.

**W.23** No act by us in handling a claim constitutes a waiver of the deductible. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

**W.24** An insured may not transfer responsibility for the deductible without our written consent. Any transfer does not relieve an insured of responsibility for the deductible.

**W.25** If application of the deductible is prohibited by law, we will apply the deductible only to the extent permitted by law. The remaining terms of this policy remain applicable.

**W.26** The deductible applies separately from any other amount that an insured may owe in connection with a claim. Payment of another amount does not satisfy the deductible unless we agree in writing.

**W.27** Responsibility among insureds for payment of the deductible does not limit our right to reimbursement. We may seek reimbursement from any insured having responsibility for the applicable deductible.

**W.28** The deductible remains applicable after payment, settlement, or closure of a covered claim. An insured's duty concerning the deductible survives to the extent permitted by law.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" that is expected or intended by an "insured," even if the resulting injury or damage differs in kind or degree from that expected or intended. We do not cover injury or damage arising from an act committed by an insured with the intent to cause harm.

**W.2** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a criminal act or omission by an insured. This exclusion applies whether or not an insured is charged with or convicted of an offense.

**W.3** We do not cover "personal injury" or "advertising injury" arising out of an insured's knowing violation of another person's rights. We do not cover personal injury or advertising injury arising from material published by or at the direction of an insured who knows the material is false.

**W.4** We do not cover bodily injury, property damage, personal injury, or advertising injury arising out of a "business" conducted by an insured. This exclusion applies to an act or omission connected with the business, whether the act or omission occurs on premises used for the business or elsewhere.

**W.5** We do not cover liability arising out of "professional services" performed by or for an insured. This exclusion applies to a failure to render professional services as well as to services that are rendered.

**W.6** We do not cover liability assumed by an insured under a "contract" or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an insured would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**W.7** We do not cover an obligation imposed under a "workers compensation" law, disability benefits law, unemployment compensation law, or similar law. We do not cover an obligation imposed because of bodily injury to a person eligible for benefits under such a law.

**W.8** We do not cover bodily injury arising out of "employment-related practices," policies, acts, or omissions. This exclusion applies to a claim by a current, former, or prospective employee of an insured.

**W.9** We do not cover bodily injury, property damage, personal injury, or advertising injury arising out of a "communicable disease." This exclusion applies to an actual, alleged, or threatened transmission, exposure, or failure to prevent transmission or exposure.

**W.10** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the manufacture, distribution, sale, furnishing, possession, or use of a controlled substance. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of prescription medication as directed by a licensed health care provider.

**W.11** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of a "motor vehicle" owned or operated by an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of who asserts the claim.

**W.12** We do not cover liability arising out of the use of a motor vehicle by a person who lacks permission to use that vehicle. An insured must not permit a person to use a motor vehicle when the insured knows that person is not legally authorized to operate it.

**W.13** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of a motor vehicle while it is being used in a race, speed contest, demolition event, stunt activity, or similar event. This exclusion applies to practice or preparation for such an event.

**W.14** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of a hovercraft. This exclusion applies whether the hovercraft is operated on land or water.

**W.15** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of an aircraft. This exclusion applies to an aircraft that is owned, hired, borrowed, chartered, or operated by an insured.

**W.16** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, unloading, or entrustment of a "watercraft." This exclusion applies while the watercraft is used to carry persons or property for a charge.

**W.17** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the operation or use of an unmanned aircraft system by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the system is operated for personal, recreational, or business purposes.

**W.18** We do not cover bodily injury, property damage, personal injury, or advertising injury arising out of war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, and military action.

**W.19** We do not cover bodily injury, property damage, personal injury, or advertising injury arising out of a nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, or radioactive waste. This exclusion applies regardless of the source of the radioactive material.

**W.20** We do not cover a loss arising from an insured's failure to comply with an ordinance, law, regulation, or governmental order. This exclusion applies to a requirement concerning construction, repair, occupancy, maintenance, or use of property.

**W.21** We do not cover bodily injury, property damage, personal injury, or advertising injury arising out of the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. Pollutants include irritants, contaminants, smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

**W.22** We do not cover liability arising out of asbestos, asbestos-containing material, or the removal, disposal, or abatement of asbestos. This exclusion applies to an actual, alleged, or threatened exposure to asbestos.

**W.23** We do not cover liability arising out of mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies to testing for, monitoring, cleaning up, removing, containing, treating, detoxifying, or neutralizing such material.

**W.24** We do not cover liability arising out of lead, lead-based material, or the removal or abatement of lead. This exclusion applies to an actual, alleged, or threatened exposure to lead.

**W.25** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the discharge of a firearm by an insured in violation of law. An insured must use reasonable care in the storage, handling, and transfer of a firearm.

**W.26** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of an animal owned, kept, harbored, boarded, bred, trained, or controlled by an insured when the animal is used in a business. This exclusion applies to an animal kept for sale, exhibition, racing, guarding, or commercial breeding.

**W.27** We do not cover bodily injury, personal injury, or advertising injury arising out of sexual molestation, sexual misconduct, or sexual harassment. This exclusion applies to an actual, alleged, or threatened act, omission, or failure to prevent such conduct.

**W.28** We do not cover bodily injury or personal injury arising out of abuse, neglect, corporal punishment, or physical discipline by an insured. This exclusion applies whether the conduct is intentional, reckless, negligent, or alleged to be negligent.

**W.29** We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of assault or battery committed by or at the direction of an insured. This exclusion applies to a claim alleging negligent hiring, supervision, retention, training, or failure to protect.

**W.30** We do not cover personal injury or advertising injury arising out of discrimination by an insured. This exclusion applies to an actual, alleged, or threatened refusal to employ, rent to, sell to, serve, or deal with a person.

**W.31** We do not cover personal injury arising out of an invasion of privacy, wrongful eviction, wrongful entry, or interference with another person's occupancy rights. This exclusion applies when the act is committed by or at the direction of an insured.

**W.32** We do not cover liability arising out of the loss, theft, disclosure, alteration, corruption, or destruction of electronic data. This exclusion applies to a failure to protect confidential, personal, financial, medical, or proprietary information.

**W.33** We do not cover liability arising out of unauthorized access to a computer system, network, device, or electronic account. This exclusion applies to the transmission of malicious code, denial of service, or a similar electronic intrusion.

**W.34** We do not cover personal injury or advertising injury arising out of infringement of copyright, patent, trademark, trade secret, trade dress, or other intellectual property rights. This exclusion applies to an alleged misuse of another person's name, likeness, product, or creative work.

**W.35** We do not cover property damage arising out of faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, renovation, or construction. This exclusion applies to work performed by or for an insured.

**W.36** We do not cover property damage arising out of earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, subsidence, sinkhole, settling, cracking, bulging, or expansion of earth. This exclusion applies whether the earth movement is naturally occurring or caused by human activity.

**W.37** We do not cover property damage arising out of flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies regardless of the cause of the water event.

**W.38** We do not cover property damage to property owned by an insured. This exclusion applies to property owned jointly by an insured and another person.

**W.39** We do not cover property damage to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care of an insured. This exclusion applies to damage caused by an insured's intentional act or omission.

**W.40** We do not cover property damage to property in the custody, care, or control of an insured. This exclusion applies to property borrowed, leased, stored, repaired, transported, or held for another person.

**W.41** We do not cover a loss assessment charged against an insured by an association, corporation, or similar organization when the assessment arises from an excluded cause of loss. This exclusion applies even if the assessment is described as a shared obligation.

**W.42** We do not cover any claim seeking payment of an amount identified as the "hurricane deductible floor." This exclusion applies regardless of the cause of the claimed loss.

**W.43** We do not cover any claimed adjustment based upon the "Coverage B percentage." This exclusion applies whether the adjustment is asserted as damages, indemnity, reimbursement, or expense.

**W.44** We do not cover any claimed adjustment based upon the "Coverage C limit as a percentage of Coverage A." This exclusion applies to a claim seeking an increase in available insurance.

**W.45** We do not cover any claim seeking payment based upon the "loss of use limit." This exclusion applies to an amount that is not otherwise covered under this policy.

**W.46** We do not cover any claim seeking payment based upon the "debris removal additional amount." This exclusion applies to an expense arising from an excluded loss.

**W.47** We do not cover punitive damages, exemplary damages, multiplied damages, penalties, or sanctions. This exclusion applies whether the damages are imposed by statute, common law, contract, or judgment.

**W.48** We do not cover fines, fees, assessments, taxes, restitution, disgorgement, or amounts paid as a penalty. This exclusion applies even if an insured is legally obligated to pay the amount.

**W.49** We do not cover liability arising out of the insolvency, receivership, liquidation, or inability to pay of an insured or another person. This exclusion applies to a claim alleging that an insured failed to maintain insurance, funds, credit, or financial security.

**W.50** We do not cover expenses, costs, or damages arising solely from a claim for which coverage is excluded. We have no duty to provide a defense for a claim that is not covered.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** An "insured" must comply with the duties stated in these Conditions. Compliance by an insured is a condition precedent to coverage where permitted by law.

**W.2** An insured must give us prompt notice of an "occurrence" that may result in a claim. Notice must include information reasonably available to the insured.

**W.3** An insured must give us prompt notice of a "claim" or "suit." Notice must include the identity of the claimant and the nature of the allegation.

**W.4** An insured must immediately send us copies of any demand, notice, summons, complaint, petition, or legal paper received in connection with a claim or suit. An insured must not delay delivery of those materials.

**W.5** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit. An insured must assist us in securing evidence and obtaining witnesses.

**W.6** An insured must provide a written statement when we reasonably request it. The statement must contain information material to the claim or suit.

**W.7** An insured must submit to an examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must answer material questions truthfully.

**W.8** An insured must produce "records" that we reasonably request. Records include documents, communications, photographs, recordings, and electronically stored information.

**W.9** An insured must preserve evidence relating to an occurrence, claim, or suit. An insured must not alter, conceal, destroy, discard, or dispose of material evidence.

**W.10** An insured must permit us to inspect property involved in an occurrence when reasonably necessary. An insured must provide access to the property within the insured's control.

**W.11** An insured must identify persons who may have knowledge of an occurrence. An insured must provide contact information reasonably available to the insured.

**W.12** An insured must identify all insurance that may apply to an occurrence, claim, or suit. An insured must provide copies of applicable policies when reasonably requested.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a covered "loss" according to the applicable coverage, exclusions, conditions, and limits of liability. We may investigate and adjust the loss before making payment.

**W.2** We will determine the amount of loss after applying any deductible that applies. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of liability.

**W.3** We may pay the "actual cash value" of damaged property. Actual cash value includes appropriate deductions for depreciation, wear, condition, and obsolescence.

**W.4** We may repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may use material, parts, and workmanship that are reasonably available.

**W.5** We may make payment to you, to a mortgagee, or to another person having a lawful interest in the property. Payment to any such person does not increase our limit of liability.

**W.6** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property.

**W.7** We will pay reasonable costs incurred to protect covered property from further damage when those costs result from a covered loss. We do not cover costs that improve, remodel, or increase the value of property.

**W.8** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.9** An insured must provide us with records, receipts, estimates, photographs, and other information we reasonably request. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of the loss.

**W.10** We may require an insured to submit to an examination under oath. An insured must sign and provide a recorded statement when we reasonably request it.

**W.11** We may require an insured to show us the damaged property and the location where the loss occurred. An insured must permit us to inspect the property as often as reasonably required.

**W.12** An insured must provide a signed proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information we reasonably require concerning the loss and the claimed amount.

**W.13** We may obtain an appraisal of damaged property when the amount of loss is disputed. An insured must cooperate in any appraisal we require.

**W.14** If we and an insured disagree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal. Each party must select a competent and impartial appraiser.

**W.15** The appraisers must select an umpire if they cannot agree on the amount of loss. A written agreement by the appraisers or by an appraiser and the umpire will establish the amount of loss.

**W.16** Each party must pay its chosen appraiser. Each party must share the other expenses of appraisal and the umpire.

**W.17** An appraisal determines only the amount of loss. An appraisal does not determine coverage, cause of loss, or our obligation to pay.

**W.18** We may elect to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property instead of making payment. If we make that election, we will notify you and may take possession of damaged property.

**W.19** We will not be liable for loss to property after we have offered to repair, rebuild, or replace it and you refuse to permit us to do so. We may limit payment to the amount we would have paid had you permitted the repair, rebuilding, or replacement.

**W.20** We will settle loss to personal property at actual cash value unless replacement cost coverage applies. We may require you to replace property before we pay replacement cost.

**W.21** We will not pay for any loss that exceeds the applicable special limit. The special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones applies to loss subject to that limitation.

**W.22** The home business contents limit applies to covered property used in connection with a home business. We will settle that loss subject to the applicable limit and all other policy provisions.

**W.23** The assessment coverage limit applies to a covered assessment for which coverage is provided. We will settle an assessment only after an insured establishes that the assessment is legally enforceable.

**W.24** We will pay a covered fire response charge when it is imposed for responding to a covered loss. We do not cover a fire response charge arising from an excluded loss.

**W.25** We may make payment directly to a person or organization that provided covered emergency services. Payment made on your behalf is part of the amount payable for the loss.

**W.26** We may recover from a responsible person or organization any payment we make. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.27** If we recover more than we paid, we will distribute the recovery as required by applicable law and the respective interests of the parties. An insured must reimburse us from any recovery to the extent permitted by law.

**W.28** We will not settle a loss by paying for property that is not covered property. We do not cover any amount attributable to fraud, concealment, misrepresentation, or intentional loss by an insured.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** “Aircraft” means a device designed for flight through the air. It includes any part or equipment attached to or used with that device.

**W.2** “Animal” means a living creature other than a human being. It includes animals owned by, kept by, or in the care of an insured.

**W.3** “Bodily injury” means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. It includes resulting death.

**W.4** “Business” means a trade, profession, occupation, or activity engaged in for economic gain. It includes activities ordinarily incident to that trade, profession, occupation, or activity.

**W.5** “Claim” means a demand for damages made by or on behalf of a person or organization. A claim may be made in writing or by another form of communication.

**W.6** “Damages” means sums an insured is legally obligated to pay because of a covered injury, loss, or damage. Damages include costs taxed against an insured in a suit.

**W.7** “Family member” means a person related to you by blood, marriage, or adoption who is a resident of your household. It includes a ward or foster person who is a resident of your household.

**W.8** “Farm” means land used for farming. It includes structures, equipment, and animals used in connection with farming.

**W.9** “Farming” means cultivating land, raising or caring for animals, or producing agricultural products. It includes activities ordinarily incident to those operations.

**W.10** “Insured” means you and your family members. It also means a person in the care of you or a family member who resides in your household.

**W.11** “Insured location” means a residence premises and premises used by an insured in connection with a residence premises. It also includes a temporary residence occupied by an insured.

**W.12** “Land” means the ground and natural materials forming part of the ground. It includes private ways and areas used in connection with the ground.

**W.13** “Leasehold” means an insured’s interest in premises arising from a lease. It includes improvements made by an insured to those premises.

**W.14** “Loss” means an accidental event resulting in injury, damage, or financial detriment. Loss includes resulting damages for which an insured may be legally liable.

**W.15** “Motor vehicle” means a land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or other land surfaces. It includes attached equipment and a trailer designed for use with that vehicle.

**W.16** “Loading or unloading” means handling property after it is moved from its place of acceptance for transportation. It also means handling property before it is moved to its place of final delivery.

**W.17** “Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An occurrence may result in bodily injury or property damage.

**W.18** “Personal injury” means injury arising out of an offense other than bodily injury. It includes false arrest, wrongful detention, malicious prosecution, wrongful eviction, and invasion of privacy.

**W.19** “Pollutant” means an irritant or contaminant, including smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, chemicals, and waste. Waste includes material to be recycled, reconditioned, or reclaimed.

**W.20** “Premises” means a location, building, structure, or portion of a building or structure. It includes the grounds used in connection with that location.

**W.21** “Property damage” means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property. It also means loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.

**W.22** “Rental” means the use of premises by another person or organization in exchange for money or other consideration. It includes the granting of possession or occupancy rights.

**W.23** “Residence” means a building or part of a building used by an insured as a home. It includes private structures and grounds used in connection with that home.

**W.24** “Residence employee” means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of a residence premises. It includes an employee performing similar duties at another location for an insured.

**W.25** “Residence premises” means the residence where you reside. It includes the premises, structures, and grounds used in connection with that residence.

**W.26** “Suit” means a civil proceeding alleging damages because of injury, loss, or damage. It includes an arbitration proceeding to which we consent.

**W.27** “Temporary residence” means premises occupied by an insured that are not a residence premises. It remains a temporary residence while it is used by an insured as a residence.

**W.28** “Vacant land” means land that is not occupied or actively used by an insured. It does not include land used for business or farming.

**W.29** “Watercraft” means a craft designed for travel or use on water. It includes attached equipment and a trailer designed for use with that craft.

**W.30** “We,” “us,” and “our” mean the insurer providing insurance under this contract. These terms include our authorized representatives when acting within their authority.

**W.31** “You” and “your” mean the person named as the insured in this contract. These terms include that person’s spouse if the spouse resides in the same household.
