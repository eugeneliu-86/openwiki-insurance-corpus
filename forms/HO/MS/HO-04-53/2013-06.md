# HO 04 53 Credit Card, Fund Transfer Card, Forgery — Increased Limit (Edition 2013-06)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" modifies insurance provided under your "policy". For purposes of this endorsement, "policy" means the contract to which this endorsement is attached.

We provide the coverage described in this endorsement subject to all applicable policy provisions. This endorsement applies only when it is attached to your policy.

This endorsement modifies the coverage for loss involving credit cards, fund transfer cards, and forgery. We apply the modified coverage in accordance with its terms.

We change only those policy provisions that this endorsement expressly changes. All other policy provisions remain unchanged.

If this endorsement conflicts with a policy provision, this endorsement controls. We apply the policy provision when it does not conflict with this endorsement.

Any "limit of liability" changed by this endorsement applies to covered loss under the applicable coverage. We do not increase coverage that this endorsement does not expressly modify.

We apply any applicable deductible in accordance with the policy. This endorsement does not eliminate a deductible unless it expressly provides otherwise.

An "insured" must comply with all duties that apply to a loss. We may require reasonable information to determine whether coverage applies.

An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. The notice must include available information concerning the circumstances of the loss.

An insured must protect available evidence concerning a loss. We may examine records that are reasonably related to the loss.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a claim. We may request a statement from an insured concerning the loss.

An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request. We may retain copies of records and documents we examine.

We do not cover loss caused by an insured's fraudulent act. We do not cover loss for which an insured intentionally provides false information.

We determine coverage based on the facts known to us and the policy terms. We may investigate any claim or loss.

This endorsement does not create coverage for property or loss that is otherwise excluded. We apply all applicable exclusions to coverage provided by this endorsement.

This endorsement does not change who is an insured under the policy. We apply the policy provisions concerning insured status.

This endorsement does not transfer your rights or duties without our consent. We recognize an assignment only as permitted by the policy.

We may enforce any right available to us under the policy. Our failure to enforce a policy provision does not waive that provision.

This endorsement applies only to loss that is covered under the policy as modified by this endorsement. We do not cover loss that occurs outside the policy's applicable coverage territory.

This endorsement becomes part of your policy when we attach it to the policy. We rely on the policy terms as modified by this endorsement.

The policy and this endorsement constitute the agreement between you and us concerning the coverage changed by this endorsement. We apply that agreement according to its terms.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide the coverage described in this section for a "Covered Loss". A Covered Loss is direct financial loss sustained by an insured and caused by a peril described in this section.

**W.2** "Credit Card" means a card or other access device issued to an insured for obtaining goods, services, money, or credit. Credit Card includes information used to obtain access to a credit account.

**W.3** "Fund Transfer Card" means a card or other access device used to initiate, authorize, or complete a transfer of money from an account. Fund Transfer Card includes information used to access an account for that purpose.

**W.4** "Forgery" means the making, alteration, or endorsement of a written instrument with intent to deceive. Forgery includes a signature made without authority.

**W.5** "Unauthorized Use" means use of a Credit Card, Fund Transfer Card, account information, or access device without permission from the person authorized to grant that permission. Unauthorized Use does not require physical possession of a card or device by the person using it.

**W.6** We will pay for loss to an insured resulting from theft of a Credit Card. We will also pay for loss resulting from Unauthorized Use of that Credit Card.

**W.7** The Credit Card must have been issued to, registered to, or lawfully used by an insured. Coverage applies when the loss results directly from the theft or Unauthorized Use.

**W.8** We will pay for loss resulting from theft of information used to access a Credit Card account. The information must be used without authorization to cause a financial loss to an insured.

**W.9** We will pay for loss resulting from an unauthorized charge made through a Credit Card account. The charge must create a legal obligation of an insured or reduce funds available to an insured.

**W.10** We will pay for loss resulting from unauthorized withdrawal of money through use of a Credit Card. The withdrawal must be made from an account available to an insured.

**W.11** We will pay for loss resulting from unauthorized purchase of goods or services through use of a Credit Card. The purchase must be made by a person without authority to use the Credit Card.

**W.12** We will pay for loss resulting from theft of a Fund Transfer Card. We will also pay for loss resulting from Unauthorized Use of that Fund Transfer Card.

**W.13** The Fund Transfer Card must have been issued to, registered to, or lawfully used by an insured. Coverage applies when the loss results directly from the theft or Unauthorized Use.

**W.14** We will pay for loss resulting from theft of information used to access an account through a Fund Transfer Card. The information must be used without authorization to transfer money from the account.

**W.15** We will pay for loss resulting from an unauthorized electronic transfer of money from an account of an insured. The transfer must be initiated through use of a Fund Transfer Card or related access information.

**W.16** We will pay for loss resulting from an unauthorized transfer directed through an automated teller machine, point of sale terminal, or similar electronic device. The transfer must reduce funds available to an insured.

**W.17** We will pay for loss resulting from an unauthorized transfer made through remote access to an account. The access must be obtained through stolen, misappropriated, or improperly used account information.

**W.18** We will pay for loss to an insured caused by Forgery of a check. The check must purport to create, transfer, alter, or discharge an obligation of an insured.

**W.19** We will pay for loss to an insured caused by alteration of a check. The alteration must cause the insured to sustain a direct financial loss.

**W.20** We will pay for loss to an insured caused by Forgery of a negotiable instrument. The negotiable instrument must be one for which an insured has legal responsibility.

**W.21** We will pay for loss to an insured caused by alteration of a negotiable instrument. The alteration must be made without authority of the insured responsible for the instrument.

**W.22** We will pay for loss caused by a forged signature on a check or negotiable instrument. The forged signature must be used to obtain money, property, or credit from an insured.

**W.23** We will pay for loss caused by an unauthorized endorsement on a check or negotiable instrument. The endorsement must result in payment, transfer, or disposition of money or property.

**W.24** We will pay for loss caused by a written instrument altered after execution by an insured. The alteration must materially affect the obligation created by the instrument.

**W.25** We will pay for loss through acceptance in good faith of counterfeit paper currency. The counterfeit paper currency must be represented as lawful money of the United States or Canada.

**W.26** Counterfeit paper currency must be accepted by an insured in exchange for goods, services, money, or property. The acceptance must result in direct financial loss to the insured.

**W.27** We will pay for loss resulting from a counterfeit bill accepted in the ordinary course of a transaction by an insured. The insured must not know that the bill is counterfeit when it is accepted.

**W.28** We will pay only for direct financial loss resulting from a Covered Loss. Direct financial loss includes money that an insured is legally obligated to pay because of the Covered Loss.

**W.29** We will pay for reasonable charges incurred by an insured to prevent further loss after discovery of theft, Forgery, counterfeiting, or Unauthorized Use. The charges must be directly related to protecting the affected card, account, or financial instrument.

**W.30** We will pay for reasonable charges incurred to notify an issuer, financial institution, merchant, or other responsible party of a Covered Loss. The notice must concern theft, Forgery, counterfeiting, or Unauthorized Use covered by this section.

**W.31** We will pay for reasonable charges incurred to replace a stolen Credit Card or Fund Transfer Card. The replacement must be necessary because of a Covered Loss.

**W.32** We will pay for reasonable charges incurred to replace checks or similar financial instruments affected by Forgery or alteration. The replacement must be necessary to prevent further covered loss.

**W.33** We will pay for loss resulting from Unauthorized Use occurring before an insured discovers the theft or misuse. Coverage also applies after discovery when the insured has taken reasonable steps to prevent further Unauthorized Use.

**W.34** An insured must notify the issuer, financial institution, or other responsible party promptly after discovering theft, loss, Forgery, counterfeiting, or Unauthorized Use. The notice must identify the affected card, account, or financial instrument when known.

**W.35** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect against further loss after discovering a Covered Loss. Those steps include canceling access, securing accounts, and preserving available evidence.

**W.36** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide records, statements, and other information reasonably requested by us.

**W.37** An insured must report theft, Forgery, counterfeiting, or Unauthorized Use to law enforcement when reasonably requested by us. An insured must provide information needed to assist law enforcement in investigating the matter.

**W.38** An insured must preserve the original check, negotiable instrument, counterfeit paper currency, card, receipt, statement, or other evidence when available. An insured must provide the preserved evidence to us upon request.

**W.39** An insured must submit a claim for reimbursement or correction to the issuer, financial institution, merchant, or other party responsible for the transaction. We may require the insured to pursue available rights of recovery.

**W.40** We will not pay for loss that has been reimbursed by an issuer, financial institution, merchant, governmental authority, or other source. We will pay only the portion of a Covered Loss that remains unpaid after available reimbursement.

**W.41** We will not pay for loss arising from an act committed by an insured. We will not pay for loss arising from an act committed at the direction of an insured.

**W.42** We will not pay for loss arising from an act committed by a person entrusted by an insured with possession of a Credit Card, Fund Transfer Card, or access information. This exclusion applies when the entrustment includes authority to use the card, device, or information.

**W.43** We will not pay for loss arising from an act committed by a person to whom an insured voluntarily gave a Credit Card, Fund Transfer Card, or access information. This exclusion applies whether or not the person later exceeds granted authority.

**W.44** We will not pay for loss arising from a business activity of an insured. This exclusion does not apply when the loss arises solely from personal use of a Credit Card, Fund Transfer Card, account, or financial instrument.

**W.45** We will not pay for loss consisting of the value of goods or services received by an insured. We will not pay for loss when an insured received the benefit of the transaction.

**W.46** We will not pay for indirect loss, consequential loss, loss of use, or loss of income. We will not pay for damage to reputation, credit standing, or business opportunity.

**W.47** We will not pay for loss caused by voluntary transfer of money, property, or access information in response to deceit, fraud, or misrepresentation. This exclusion applies even when the transfer is induced by impersonation.

**W.48** We will not pay for loss caused by an insured's failure to safeguard a password, personal identification information, access code, or similar credential after the insured knows it has been compromised. This exclusion applies only to loss occurring after that knowledge.

**W.49** We will not pay for loss arising from a financial institution's error unless the error directly results from theft, Forgery, counterfeiting, or Unauthorized Use covered by this section. A dispute concerning an account balance, service charge, or transaction processing error is not a Covered Loss.

**W.50** We will not pay for loss arising from a dispute concerning the quality, condition, delivery, or value of goods or services. A charge made in connection with such a dispute is not Unauthorized Use merely because an insured seeks a refund.

**W.51** We will not pay for loss resulting from a check, negotiable instrument, Credit Card, Fund Transfer Card, or access device used with the knowledge and consent of an insured. Consent may be express or implied from the circumstances.

**W.52** We will not pay for loss resulting from an obligation assumed by an insured under a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to an obligation directly caused by Forgery or Unauthorized Use covered by this section.

**W.53** Our payment for Covered Loss is subject to the applicable limit of liability for this coverage. The limit is the most we will pay for all Covered Loss arising from the same occurrence.

**W.54** All loss arising from related theft, related Unauthorized Use, related Forgery, related alteration, or related acceptance of counterfeit paper currency will be treated as arising from the same occurrence. Related acts include acts connected by a common scheme, method, person, account, card, or financial instrument.

**W.55** Payment under this coverage will not reduce the amount available for property loss not covered by this section. Payment under this coverage is subject to all terms that apply to Covered Loss.

**W.56** If we make payment for a Covered Loss, we may recover that payment from a person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our right of recovery.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** The limit of liability for this coverage is the most we will pay for covered loss. This limit applies regardless of the number of persons who sustain loss. We will not pay more than the applicable limit for the same loss.

**W.2** The most we will pay is ten thousand dollars. Except that this limit applies to the total amount we pay for covered loss under this coverage, regardless of the number of claims or persons making claims.

**W.3** The limit of liability applies to loss sustained by you or by any person who has an insurable interest in the property or account involved. The limit is not increased because more than one person has an interest in the loss. We will pay no more than the applicable limit for all interests combined.

**W.4** The limit of liability applies to loss arising from the use of a "credit card", "fund transfer card", or other access device. Subject to the terms of this coverage, the limit also applies to loss arising from the unauthorized use of an account or access device.

**W.5** The limit of liability applies to loss resulting from the signing, altering, or uttering of a written instrument. We will apply the limit to loss caused by a "forgery" or alteration that is covered under this coverage. We will not apply a separate limit to each instrument involved in the loss.

**W.6** The limit of liability applies to covered loss arising from related acts or transactions. Acts or transactions are related when they arise from the same plan, scheme, or course of conduct. We will consider related acts or transactions together in applying the limit.

**W.7** The limit of liability applies to covered loss discovered during the policy period. We will determine the amount payable based on the covered loss that is sustained or discovered as required by this coverage. Payment does not increase the limit available for another covered loss.

**W.8** We will not pay more than the applicable limit for loss resulting from a single plan or scheme. This applies even when the plan or scheme involves separate persons, accounts, instruments, or transactions. The identity of the person responsible for the loss does not increase the limit.

**W.9** We will apply the limit to the total covered loss after consideration of recoveries. Recoveries include amounts received from any source because of the loss. We will not pay amounts that, when combined with recoveries, exceed the covered loss subject to the limit.

**W.10** We will apply the limit before making payment under this coverage. Payment by us reduces the amount available for any remaining covered loss. We will not restore the amount paid because additional loss is discovered.

**W.11** The limit applies without regard to the number of covered causes contributing to the loss. A covered loss involving more than one cause remains subject to the applicable limit. We will not pay separate amounts for separate causes contributing to the same loss.

**W.12** The limit applies without regard to the number of persons who use, possess, issue, accept, or receive a card or instrument involved in the loss. The conduct of each person is considered in determining the covered loss. The combined loss remains subject to the applicable limit.

**W.13** The limit applies without regard to the number of accounts affected by the loss. Accounts include accounts maintained with financial institutions or other providers of financial services. We will combine covered loss involving related accounts when the loss arises from related acts or transactions.

**W.14** The limit applies to loss involving an account maintained in your name or for your benefit. We will consider the interest of any person entitled to use or control the account. The existence of a joint, representative, or fiduciary interest does not increase the limit.

**W.15** The limit applies to loss involving a card or instrument issued to you or issued for your benefit. A card or instrument remains subject to the limit after it is reported lost, stolen, canceled, or revoked. We will not treat a replacement card or instrument as creating a separate limit for related loss.

**W.16** The limit applies to loss involving a signature, electronic authorization, personal identification number, password, or other means of access. We will consider the means of access part of the transaction when it is used to cause or facilitate covered loss. We will not apply a separate limit based on the form of authorization.

**W.17** The limit applies to loss caused by a person who is not an insured under this coverage. We will not treat the number of persons causing the loss as increasing the amount available for payment. The applicable limit remains available for the covered loss caused by all such persons.

**W.18** The limit applies to covered loss for which you are legally responsible. Legal responsibility does not create a separate limit for each person, account, or obligation. We will apply the limit to the total amount for which coverage is provided.

**W.19** We will determine the amount of covered loss without including expenses that are not covered under this coverage. Expenses incurred to establish the amount of loss are subject to the terms that apply to those expenses. We will not add uncovered expenses to the amount available under the limit.

**W.20** We will apply the limit to the amount of covered loss before subtracting any deductible or other amount that you must bear. The deductible or other amount is not added to the limit. We will not pay the part of the loss that is your responsibility.

**W.21** We will apply the limit to the amount payable after all applicable conditions have been satisfied. Compliance with a condition does not increase the limit. We will pay only the amount otherwise payable under this coverage, subject to the limit.

**W.22** The limit applies to loss sustained by you and to loss sustained by any person claiming through you. A person claiming through you does not receive a separate limit. We will make payment only once for the same covered loss.

**W.23** The limit applies to loss for which more than one person may claim an interest. We may make payment to the person or persons entitled to payment under this coverage. Payment to an entitled person discharges our obligation to the extent of the payment.

**W.24** The limit applies to loss that is assigned, transferred, or otherwise claimed by another person. An assignment or transfer does not increase the amount we may pay. We will recognize rights to payment only to the extent provided by this coverage and applicable law.

**W.25** We will apply the limit to covered loss arising from a continuing course of conduct. A continuing course of conduct is treated as related when the acts or transactions have a common purpose or connection. We will not provide a separate limit for each act in that course of conduct.

**W.26** We will apply the limit to covered loss arising from an act that causes additional loss after the act occurs. Additional loss arising from that act remains subject to the applicable limit. We will not treat the later discovery of additional loss as creating a new limit.

**W.27** The limit applies to covered loss discovered by you, your representative, or a financial institution. Discovery by a representative or financial institution is treated as discovery for purposes of applying this coverage. The manner of discovery does not increase the limit.

**W.28** The limit applies to covered loss whether the loss is discovered in a single amount or in separate amounts. Separate demands, notices, or claims do not create separate limits for related loss. We will consider the nature and relationship of the acts or transactions causing the loss.

**W.29** The limit applies to covered loss involving a payment, withdrawal, transfer, purchase, advance, or other disposition of funds. We will consider related dispositions of funds together when they arise from related acts or transactions. The form of disposition does not increase the limit.

**W.30** The limit applies to covered loss involving a written instrument, electronic record, or other evidence of an obligation. The form of the evidence does not determine whether a separate limit applies. We will apply the limit to the covered loss arising from related evidence or transactions.

**W.31** The limit applies to covered loss even when the loss includes amounts owed to different persons. We will determine the amount payable for the combined covered loss. We will not pay more than the applicable limit because different persons are entitled to different amounts.

**W.32** The limit applies to covered loss involving property, funds, or credit belonging to you or held for your benefit. The character of the property, funds, or credit does not increase the limit. We will pay only for the covered loss otherwise provided by this coverage.

**W.33** The limit applies to covered loss caused by the use of information obtained from a card, account, instrument, or record. We will consider the use of that information in determining whether the loss is related. The source of the information does not create a separate limit.

**W.34** The limit applies to covered loss caused by an unauthorized signature, endorsement, alteration, or electronic instruction. We will apply the limit to the loss resulting from all related unauthorized acts. We will not apply separate limits based on the particular form of unauthorized act.

**W.35** We will apply the limit to covered loss after considering the amount that has been paid, credited, replaced, or restored by another person or entity. Such amounts reduce the covered loss to the extent they are recoveries for the same loss. We will not pay duplicate amounts for the same covered loss.

**W.36** The limit applies to all covered loss arising out of a common source or connected series of events. We will determine whether loss is connected from the facts and circumstances of the loss. We will apply the limit to connected loss as a whole.

**W.37** The limit applies to payment made in settlement of a covered claim. Settlement of a claim does not create an additional amount available for other loss arising from the same acts or transactions. We will not pay a settlement that exceeds the amount otherwise payable under this coverage.

**W.38** The limit applies to any payment we make under this coverage for covered loss. Our payment does not constitute an admission that other loss is covered. We will have no further liability for covered loss after the applicable limit has been paid.

**W.39** The limit applies regardless of whether the covered loss is asserted in a single claim or in separate claims. We may apply amounts paid on related claims against the same available limit. We will not pay more than the applicable limit for related covered loss.

**W.40** The limit applies to covered loss arising from conduct occurring before or after you discover a prior loss when the conduct is related. Discovery of a prior loss does not create a new limit for related conduct. We will apply the limit according to the relationship of the acts and transactions causing the loss.

**W.41** We will apply the limit to covered loss that is established by records, testimony, or other evidence. The method used to establish the loss does not affect the amount of insurance available. We will pay only the amount of covered loss that is established and otherwise payable.

**W.42** The limit applies to covered loss after we determine that the loss falls within this coverage. A determination that part of a loss is covered does not make the remaining loss covered. We will apply the limit only to the covered portion of the loss.

**W.43** The limit applies to all covered loss arising under this coverage from related conduct. We may allocate payment among persons entitled to payment when the total covered loss exceeds the amount available. Our allocation does not increase the limit or create additional coverage.

**W.44** The limit applies to covered loss for which payment is made directly to you or to another person entitled to receive payment. The recipient of payment does not affect the amount of insurance available. We will not pay the same covered loss to more than one claimant.

**W.45** The limit applies to covered loss that is reduced by a recovery after payment by us. We may seek recovery or adjust any remaining amount payable as permitted by this coverage. A recovery does not create an amount in addition to the applicable limit.

**W.46** The limit applies to covered loss whether the responsible person is known or unknown. Identification of the responsible person does not change the amount available for payment. We will apply the limit to the covered loss established under this coverage.

**W.47** The limit applies to covered loss arising from a card, account, instrument, or access method that is used more than once. Repeated use does not create a separate limit for each use when the uses are related. We will consider the related uses together in determining the amount payable.

**W.48** The limit applies to covered loss after any applicable recovery rights have been considered. We may require that rights of recovery be preserved to the extent provided by this coverage. Preserving or pursuing recovery rights does not increase the limit.

**W.49** The limit applies to the total covered loss for which we are responsible under this coverage. We will not pay amounts that exceed the limit, even when the total loss is established by separate evidence or claims. Payment under this coverage is subject to all other applicable terms and conditions.

**W.50** The limit applies to covered loss arising from related conduct, regardless of when the separate acts or transactions are discovered. We will combine related covered loss for purposes of applying the limit. We will not apply the limit separately because the loss is discovered at different times.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "deductible" is the amount that applies to a covered loss before we pay. You must bear the deductible that applies to the covered loss. The deductible does not create coverage for property, conduct, or loss that is not otherwise covered.

**W.2** We apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss that remains after all applicable conditions have been satisfied. You must establish the amount of covered loss before we determine the amount payable. We do not apply the deductible to an amount that is not covered.

**W.3** The deductible applies to each covered loss. A covered loss includes loss arising from covered use, transfer, alteration, or acceptance of a payment instrument. The deductible applies regardless of the identity of the person who caused the covered loss.

**W.4** We determine whether losses arise from the same occurrence based on the facts and circumstances of the loss. Losses arising from the same occurrence are subject to the deductible applicable to that occurrence. Separate occurrences are evaluated separately.

**W.5** You must promptly notify us of any loss that may be covered. Your notice must describe the circumstances giving rise to the loss and the property or funds affected. The deductible does not relieve you of any duty to provide notice.

**W.6** You must protect the property, accounts, cards, and access devices involved in a covered loss from further loss. You must take reasonable steps to prevent additional transactions, transfers, or use after discovering the loss. We may reduce the amount payable to the extent your failure increases the loss.

**W.7** We determine the amount of covered loss from the records and information reasonably available to us. You must provide records supporting the amount claimed and the circumstances of the loss. The deductible applies after we determine the covered amount.

**W.8** You must cooperate with our investigation of the loss. Your cooperation includes providing requested records, statements, authorizations, and other information. The deductible does not limit our right to investigate the cause or amount of loss.

**W.9** You must assist us in securing and preserving rights of recovery against persons responsible for the loss. You must not impair those rights after the loss. We may reduce the amount payable to the extent your conduct impairs our rights of recovery.

**W.10** We apply recoveries received from responsible persons to the covered loss in accordance with our rights of recovery. We do not treat a recovery as a reduction of the deductible until the recovery is actually received. You must promptly report any recovery or potential recovery to us.

**W.11** We apply the deductible before subtracting recoveries from the amount of covered loss. Subject to our rights of recovery, you remain responsible for the deductible. A recovery does not increase the amount we otherwise owe.

**W.12** We determine the amount payable without regard to expenses that are not covered. You must bear the deductible and all amounts that are excluded or otherwise not covered. We do not pay amounts merely because they are included in a claim submitted to us.

**W.13** We do not cover loss that results from conduct or circumstances excluded under the policy. The deductible does not convert excluded loss into covered loss. You must establish that the claimed loss is covered before the deductible is applied.

**W.14** We apply the deductible to covered loss involving money, securities, or other financial interests only to the extent the loss is covered. You must provide evidence of the value and ownership of the financial interest claimed. We do not apply the deductible to an interest that is not covered.

**W.15** We apply the deductible to covered loss involving an account or payment instrument based on the amount of loss for which you are legally responsible. You must provide evidence of that legal responsibility. We do not pay amounts that another person or entity is obligated to pay.

**W.16** We apply the deductible after considering amounts credited, refunded, reversed, or otherwise returned to you. You must notify us of any such amount. We do not pay the returned amount as part of the covered loss.

**W.17** You must use reasonable care to safeguard cards, account information, personal identification information, and access devices. We may reduce the amount payable when your failure to use reasonable care contributes to the loss. The deductible remains applicable to the covered portion of the loss.

**W.18** We determine the covered loss from the transaction history and other reliable evidence available to us. You must identify transactions that you did not authorize or approve. We do not treat an unverified allegation as proof of covered loss.

**W.19** You must provide us with information concerning notices given to a financial institution, card issuer, merchant, or other interested party. You must provide copies of written notices and responses when reasonably available. The deductible does not excuse compliance with duties imposed by the policy or by law.

**W.20** We may require you to sign a sworn statement concerning the loss. The statement must contain information reasonably necessary to determine coverage and the amount of loss. You must correct any material error or omission that you discover.

**W.21** We may require an examination under oath concerning the loss. You must answer questions and produce requested records during the examination. The deductible does not limit our right to obtain information concerning any covered or claimed loss.

**W.22** We determine the amount of loss using the value applicable under the policy. You must support the claimed value with records or other evidence. We do not increase the amount payable because the claimed value exceeds the amount supported by the evidence.

**W.23** We apply the deductible to the covered loss after adjustments for duplicate charges, credits, refunds, and other offsets. You must identify each adjustment known to you. We do not pay the same loss more than once.

**W.24** We may make payment to you or to another person entitled to receive payment. Payment to an entitled person satisfies our obligation to the extent of that payment. The deductible continues to apply even when payment is made to another person.

**W.25** You must assign to us any rights of recovery that arise from payment of the covered loss when we request the assignment. You must execute documents and take action reasonably necessary to protect those rights. We may withhold payment to the extent your failure prejudices our rights.

**W.26** We may settle the covered loss with you or with a person asserting a valid interest in the property or funds. Settlement does not waive the deductible or any applicable condition. You must cooperate with reasonable settlement efforts.

**W.27** We do not cover amounts that arise solely from fees, penalties, interest, or charges that are not part of a covered loss. The deductible does not apply to those amounts because they are not covered. You must identify covered loss separately from other amounts claimed.

**W.28** We apply the deductible to the covered loss remaining after all applicable limitations are applied. You must bear the amount that exceeds an applicable limitation. The deductible does not reduce an amount that is already outside the applicable limitation.

**W.29** We may require proof that a transaction, transfer, alteration, or use was unauthorized or fraudulent. You must provide the proof reasonably necessary to support the claim. The deductible does not apply until we determine that the resulting loss is covered.

**W.30** You must promptly report a lost, stolen, altered, or misused card, account credential, or access device to the person or entity responsible for it. You must follow reasonable instructions intended to prevent further loss. We may reduce the amount payable if your failure causes additional covered loss.

**W.31** We determine the amount we owe after applying the deductible and all other applicable provisions. You must bear the deductible even when the amount of covered loss is less than the amount claimed. We do not pay more than the amount of covered loss that remains after those applications.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an insured. We do not cover loss resulting from an insured's knowing participation in a dishonest act.

**W.2** We do not cover loss arising from a voluntary parting with property by you or an insured. This exclusion applies whether the voluntary parting results from deceit, trick, false pretense, or fraud.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's acceptance of counterfeit money. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's acceptance of altered money.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water that is excluded from coverage.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by war. War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, or revolution.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action. This exclusion applies to seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention by a governmental authority.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes radioactive contamination and the hazardous properties of nuclear material.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by seizure or destruction of property under legal process. We do not cover loss caused by lawful repossession of property.

**W.37** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting and supervising any person authorized to possess or use a card, account, access device, check, draft, or credential. An insured must promptly withdraw authority when misuse is discovered.

**W.38** An insured must retain information reasonably necessary to distinguish authorized transactions from unauthorized transactions. This information may include purchase records, travel records, communications, and account access history.

**W.39** An insured must notify us if any transaction related to the covered loss is reversed, disputed, credited, refunded, canceled, or otherwise adjusted. An insured must provide documentation of the adjustment when available.

**W.40** An insured must not accept a settlement, release, compromise, or waiver from another person or organization without our consent if it affects our right of recovery. This duty applies after we make payment and while a claim is pending.

**W.41** An insured must assist us in identifying the proper recipient of any payment for a covered loss. We may require payment to be made jointly with another person or organization having an interest in the loss.

**W.42** An insured must provide evidence of ownership or responsibility for the card, account, check, draft, access device, or other instrument involved in the covered loss. Evidence may include an agreement, statement, registration, issuance record, or similar record.

**W.43** An insured must promptly notify us if the insured learns that a reported loss was caused by an authorized transaction, an error, a misunderstanding, or another event not involving unauthorized use. An insured must return any payment we made for a loss not covered under this endorsement.

**W.44** An insured must take reasonable steps to secure mail, deliveries, electronic communications, and other materials containing account information or access credentials. An insured must notify the appropriate sender or issuer if such materials are lost, stolen, intercepted, or misdirected.

**W.45** An insured must cooperate in verifying the identity of the insured and any person claiming an interest in the covered loss. We may require documentation reasonably necessary to complete that verification.

**W.46** An insured must provide us with information concerning any prior report of loss involving the same card, account, access device, check, draft, or credential when we request it. An insured must provide information concerning related claims made to another insurer or responsible party.

**W.47** An insured must not incur expense for investigation, recovery, repair, replacement, or protection of property without our consent, except for reasonable expenses incurred to protect against further loss. We do not cover expenses voluntarily incurred without our consent unless they are otherwise covered.

**W.48** An insured must promptly notify us if any person admits responsibility for the covered loss. An insured must preserve any written, recorded, or electronic communication containing that admission.

**W.49** An insured must provide access to originals of relevant records when reasonably requested. If originals are unavailable, an insured must explain their unavailability and provide available copies.

**W.50** An insured must cooperate with us in obtaining assignment, transfer, or preservation of recovery rights to the extent of our payment. Our rights do not exceed the rights of the insured.

**W.51** An insured must repay us to the extent the insured receives a recovery for a covered loss after we have made payment. This applies whether the recovery is obtained by settlement, judgment, refund, credit, restitution, or other means.

**W.52** An insured must not dispose of a card, access device, check, draft, or other instrument involved in the covered loss until the responsible issuer, financial institution, law enforcement authority, or we direct otherwise. An insured must follow reasonable instructions concerning its return or destruction.

**W.53** An insured must provide us with current contact information while a claim is pending. An insured must promptly notify us of a change in mailing address, electronic address, or telephone contact information.

**W.54** An insured must make reasonable efforts to attend interviews, examinations, conferences, hearings, or proceedings relating to the covered loss when we request attendance. We will reimburse reasonable expenses incurred at our request.

**W.55** An insured must cooperate with any reasonable measure we take to prevent additional loss arising from a compromised account, credential, or access device. This may include changing credentials, closing an account, or monitoring account activity.

**W.56** An insured must disclose whether the covered loss involves business, professional, employment, fiduciary, or representative activity. An insured must provide records reasonably necessary to determine the nature of the activity.

**W.57** An insured must promptly provide any information received from a card issuer, financial institution, merchant, processor, payee, or law enforcement authority concerning the covered loss. This duty continues while the claim remains unresolved.

**W.58** An insured must cooperate with our efforts to determine whether another person or organization has primary responsibility for the covered loss. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary to identify that person or organization.

**W.59** An insured must not make any admission, statement, or agreement that creates or increases liability for us. An insured may provide truthful information to a card issuer, financial institution, merchant, payee, law enforcement authority, or governmental authority.

**W.60** We do not cover a covered loss if an insured intentionally fails to perform a duty under these provisions and the failure prejudices our ability to investigate the loss, determine coverage, or recover payment. We may enforce all rights available to us under applicable law.

**W.61** Compliance with these provisions is a condition to coverage for a covered loss. An insured must perform these duties personally or through a person acting with the insured's authority.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a "covered loss" under this endorsement in accordance with the applicable policy terms. We will determine the amount of loss from the information available to us.

**W.2** An insured must give us information necessary to evaluate the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation and settlement of the claim.

**W.3** We may inspect damaged property before it is repaired, discarded, or otherwise disposed of. An insured must preserve the property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.4** An insured must provide records, receipts, statements, and other evidence that support the claimed loss. We may require an insured to identify the property involved in the loss.

**W.5** We will consider the condition, age, quality, and usefulness of property at the time of loss when those matters are relevant to settlement. We may consider any available evidence of ownership and value.

**W.6** We will apply any applicable limitation, exclusion, deductible, and condition in determining the amount payable. We will not pay more than the amount for which you are legally responsible or that is otherwise payable under this endorsement.

**W.7** The loss settlement basis for "contents" subject to this endorsement is replacement cost. We will apply that basis to the covered loss.

**W.8** We may request that an insured provide a signed statement describing the circumstances of the loss. The statement must be truthful and complete to the best of the insured's knowledge.

**W.9** We may require proof that a loss was caused by a covered occurrence. An insured must provide information reasonably available concerning the cause of loss.

**W.10** We may settle a claim with you, with another insured, or with a person legally entitled to payment. Payment to a person entitled to receive payment discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.11** We may pay for loss in money, repair, restoration, or replacement, as permitted by the applicable policy terms. Our election of a method of settlement does not increase the amount payable.

**W.12** If we elect to repair or replace property, an insured must make the property available to us when reasonably necessary. An insured must cooperate in arranging access to the property.

**W.13** We may use reasonably equivalent property, materials, services, or methods in settling a covered loss. We are not required to provide property with characteristics exceeding those of the property lost or damaged.

**W.14** We may consider depreciation, deterioration, obsolescence, and prior damage when those matters affect the amount payable. We may also consider the remaining useful life of property.

**W.15** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from further loss after an occurrence. We will consider reasonable protective measures in determining the covered loss.

**W.16** We will not pay for loss that an insured intentionally causes. We will not pay for any portion of loss that results from an insured's material misrepresentation or concealment.

**W.17** An insured must notify us promptly of a loss that may involve coverage under this endorsement. Delay in notice may affect our ability to determine the loss.

**W.18** We may require an insured to provide documents showing the amount paid or owed in connection with the loss. We may also require documents showing any reimbursement received from another source.

**W.19** We will reduce the amount payable by amounts recoverable from persons or organizations responsible for the loss. Our rights of recovery apply after we make payment.

**W.20** An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our rights of recovery. An insured must cooperate with us in preserving those rights.

**W.21** We may require examination of an insured under oath when reasonably necessary to investigate or settle a claim. An insured must sign the examination record when it accurately states the insured's testimony.

**W.22** We may require an insured to submit a "proof of loss" stating the information reasonably necessary to determine payment. The proof of loss must be signed and must identify the property and amount claimed.

**W.23** We may request additional information if the information previously provided is incomplete, inconsistent, or insufficient. An insured must respond to reasonable requests for clarification.

**W.24** We will determine whether claimed property was owned, possessed, or legally used by an insured at the time of loss. We may require evidence establishing the insured's interest in that property.

**W.25** We may determine the amount of loss separately for each item of property. Damage to one item does not require us to treat unrelated property as damaged.

**W.26** We will not include sentimental, personal, or emotional value in determining the amount payable. We will determine payment from the value recognized under the applicable settlement basis.

**W.27** We may rely on photographs, inventories, appraisals, estimates, records, and expert opinions in evaluating a covered loss. We are not required to accept an estimate submitted by an insured.

**W.28** An insured must allow us reasonable access to records relating to the claimed loss. Access may include records maintained in electronic form.

**W.29** We may inspect records that are relevant to ownership, value, cause of loss, or amounts claimed. We will limit our requests to information reasonably related to the claim.

**W.30** We may request that an insured identify any person with knowledge of the loss. An insured must provide available contact information for that person.

**W.31** We will apply the "association assessment limit" when it applies to a covered loss. We will determine whether an assessment is covered from the facts and applicable policy terms.

**W.32** We will consider a claim for a "fire department call fee" under the applicable policy terms. An insured must provide any available record of the charge.

**W.33** We will apply the "precious stones theft limit" when it applies to property involved in a theft loss. An insured must provide available information identifying the property claimed.

**W.34** We will apply the "special limit on business property on the residence premises" when it applies to the claimed property. An insured must identify the use of property for which payment is sought.

**W.35** We may require an insured to separate damaged property from undamaged property when reasonably possible. An insured must not dispose of damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.36** We may settle only the portion of a claim supported by available information. We may reconsider an unpaid portion if additional supporting information becomes available.

**W.37** We may issue payment jointly to persons having an interest in the covered loss. An insured must provide information necessary to identify those persons.

**W.38** We may make payment to an insured who has authority to receive payment for the loss. We may require evidence of that authority before making payment.

**W.39** We may deduct amounts previously paid for the same covered loss. We will not make duplicate payment for the same property or expense.

**W.40** We may require an insured to return property for which we have made payment if that property is later recovered. We may reduce payment by the value of recovered property retained by an insured.

**W.41** We may take title to property for which we make payment when permitted by law. An insured must transfer any interest necessary to protect our rights in that property.

**W.42** We will not pay for costs incurred solely to improve property beyond its condition immediately before loss. We may pay reasonable costs necessary to restore property as permitted by the applicable policy terms.

**W.43** We may consider prior repairs, maintenance, alterations, and existing defects in determining the amount of loss. An insured must disclose known prior damage that affects the claim.

**W.44** We may use information obtained from public records, merchants, service providers, or other reliable sources to evaluate a claim. An insured must authorize release of relevant information when legally permitted.

**W.45** We may request information concerning any warranty, service agreement, or similar protection applicable to the claimed property. We will consider payments available under those arrangements as permitted by law.

**W.46** An insured must notify us if recovered property is found after payment is made. An insured must preserve the recovered property until we determine the effect on the claim.

**W.47** We may reopen a settled claim if material information was unavailable when settlement was made. We may adjust payment to reflect information that materially affects the covered loss.

**W.48** We may correct a payment made because of clerical error, calculation error, or material misinformation. An insured must return any amount not properly payable.

**W.49** We will pay no more than the amount of the covered loss after application of all applicable policy terms. Payment under this endorsement does not create coverage for an otherwise excluded loss.

**W.50** An insured must retain records supporting a claimed loss until the claim is resolved. An insured must provide those records to us upon reasonable request.

**W.51** We may require an insured to provide an inventory describing claimed property. The inventory must include information reasonably available concerning the property and its value.

**W.52** We may use a qualified appraiser, adjuster, contractor, merchant, or other expert to assist in evaluating the loss. We are not bound by an opinion obtained by an insured.

**W.53** We may settle a claim based upon information obtained during our investigation. We may deny payment for any portion of a claim that is not established as a covered loss.

**W.54** An insured must promptly notify us of any demand, payment, recovery, or settlement involving the claimed loss. An insured must not accept payment that impairs our rights without our consent.

**W.55** We may require repayment of amounts received by an insured from another source for the same covered loss. We will not seek repayment of amounts that we are not entitled to recover.

**W.56** We will determine the amount payable without regard to inconvenience resulting from the loss. We will not pay for indirect loss unless that loss is expressly covered.

**W.57** We may require reasonable documentation of charges incurred because of a covered loss. An insured must provide documentation before we make payment for those charges.

**W.58** We may settle a covered loss despite disagreement about matters that do not affect the amount payable. Settlement of one matter does not waive our rights concerning any other matter.

**W.59** No insured may assign a claim payment right without our written consent when consent is required by law. We may recognize an assignment that is valid and enforceable.

**W.60** Our payment will be made after we receive the information reasonably necessary to determine the amount payable. Payment of a claim does not waive any condition, limitation, or exclusion applicable to another claim.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** For purposes of this endorsement, the terms defined below have the meanings set out in this section. A defined term applies whether used in singular or plural form.

**W.2** "Access device" means a card, code, account number, personal identification number, password, or other means used to obtain access to an account or to initiate a transaction. Access device includes information that permits access without physical possession of a card.

**W.3** "Account" means an arrangement with a "financial institution" or other provider through which funds, credit, goods, services, or other value may be obtained. Account includes an account accessed by a "credit card" or "fund transfer card".

**W.4** "Card" means a tangible or electronic access device issued to a person for use in obtaining value or initiating a transaction. Card includes a credit card, debit card, charge card, or similar device.

**W.5** "Credit card" means a card issued by a "card issuer" that permits the holder to obtain credit for the purchase of goods, services, cash, or other value. Credit card includes a device or account credential used in place of a physical card.

**W.6** "Fund transfer card" means a card or other access device used to direct, authorize, or initiate an electronic transfer of funds from an account. Fund transfer card includes a debit card and a device used to access deposited funds.

**W.7** "Card issuer" means a person, organization, financial institution, or other entity that issues, administers, or honors a card. Card issuer includes an entity acting through an authorized representative.

**W.8** "Authorized user" means a person permitted by the cardholder or account holder to use a card, access device, or account. Authorized user does not include a person whose authority has been withdrawn before the transaction at issue.

**W.9** "Unauthorized use" means use of a card, access device, account, or account information without the permission of the person entitled to grant that permission. Unauthorized use includes use obtained through theft, loss, fraud, deception, or impersonation.

**W.10** "Loss" means direct financial harm resulting from a covered act or occurrence. Loss does not include anticipated income, loss of use, loss of market value, inconvenience, or emotional distress.

**W.11** "Direct loss" means loss that results immediately from an act, transaction, or event without the intervention of an independent cause. Direct loss does not include an indirect, consequential, or remote loss.

**W.12** "Funds" means money, credit balances, deposits, or other monetary value held in or available through an account. Funds include value available for transfer, withdrawal, payment, or purchase.

**W.13** "Financial institution" means a bank, savings institution, credit union, payment service provider, brokerage firm, or similar entity that holds, transfers, or manages funds. Financial institution includes an entity acting through an authorized agent.

**W.14** "Merchant" means a person or organization that offers goods, services, or other value in exchange for payment. Merchant includes a seller acting through an employee, contractor, or representative.

**W.15** "Transaction" means an act by which funds, credit, goods, services, or other value are obtained, transferred, withdrawn, charged, or paid. Transaction includes an attempted transaction when it results in a loss.

**W.16** "Electronic funds transfer" means a transfer of funds initiated through electronic, telephonic, computerized, or similar means. Electronic funds transfer includes a transfer initiated by use of a fund transfer card or access device.

**W.17** "Payment order" means an instruction to pay, transfer, or cause the transfer of funds to a person or account. Payment order includes an instruction communicated electronically or in writing.

**W.18** "Instruction" means a direction, request, authorization, or command intended to cause a transaction or payment. Instruction may be communicated by spoken, written, electronic, or other means.

**W.19** "Signature" means a name, mark, symbol, electronic identifier, or other authentication adopted or used to indicate approval, authorization, or execution. Signature includes a handwritten, electronic, or reproduced signature.

**W.20** "Forgery" means the making, alteration, or use of a signature or written instrument with intent to deceive. Forgery includes signing the name of another person without authority.

**W.21** "Alteration" means an unauthorized change to a written instrument, account record, payment order, or other document. Alteration includes a change to the amount, payee, date, terms, or signature of the document.

**W.22** "Counterfeit" means an imitation made to appear genuine with intent to deceive. Counterfeit includes an imitation card, access device, written instrument, or signature.

**W.23** "Written instrument" means a document, record, or other writing that creates, evidences, transfers, or affects a right to payment or property. Written instrument includes a document maintained in electronic form when it is capable of being authenticated.

**W.24** "Negotiable instrument" means a written instrument that is payable to a person or bearer and is transferable by delivery or endorsement. Negotiable instrument includes a check, draft, or promissory note.

**W.25** "Check" means a written order directing a financial institution to pay funds from an account. Check includes an item presented in electronic form as permitted by the financial institution.

**W.26** "Draft" means a written order by which a person directs another person to pay funds. Draft includes a demand for payment that is payable upon presentation or acceptance.

**W.27** "Promissory note" means a written promise to pay money to another person. Promissory note includes an instrument that may be transferred to a holder.

**W.28** "Account statement" means a record provided by a financial institution, card issuer, or other account provider showing transactions, balances, charges, payments, or account activity. Account statement includes an electronic record made available to the account holder.

**W.29** "Discovery" means the time when you first become aware of facts that would cause a reasonable person to believe that a loss may have occurred. Discovery includes awareness by a person authorized to act for you in connection with the account or property involved.

**W.30** "Notice" means information given to us or another person in a manner reasonably calculated to communicate the information. Notice includes written, electronic, telephonic, or other communication accepted by the recipient.

**W.31** "Investigation" means an inquiry, review, examination, or other effort to determine facts relating to a claimed loss. Investigation includes efforts undertaken by us, you, a financial institution, or a law enforcement authority.

**W.32** "Reasonable care" means the degree of care that a prudent person would use under similar circumstances to protect cards, access devices, account information, and property. Reasonable care includes safeguarding information used to access an account.

**W.33** "Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or profit. Business includes an activity conducted by or on behalf of a person or organization.

**W.34** "Employee" means a person employed by another person or organization. Employee includes a leased worker acting within the scope of assigned duties.

**W.35** "Household member" means a person who resides with you and shares the customary activities of your household. Household member includes a person who is temporarily absent from the household.

**W.36** "Residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside and the part of other premises used by you in connection with that dwelling. Residence premises includes structures and grounds used principally for residential purposes.

**W.37** "Recovery" means money, property, or other value received by you or us from a person or organization responsible for a loss. Recovery includes reimbursement, restitution, repayment, settlement, or salvage.
