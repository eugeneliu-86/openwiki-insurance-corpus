# HO 24 71 Business Pursuits (Edition 2011-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

"This endorsement" modifies insurance provided under your "policy." The provisions of this endorsement apply only to the extent stated in this endorsement.

The terms of your policy apply to this endorsement unless they are modified by this endorsement. Terms that are defined in your policy retain their meaning when used in this endorsement.

"We," "us," and "our" refer to the insurer providing this insurance. "You" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the policy.

This endorsement is attached to and made part of your policy. It applies only while your policy is in force.

This endorsement changes the insurance provided by your policy. Coverage under this endorsement is subject to all applicable policy conditions.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy, this endorsement controls. No provision is changed except as stated in this endorsement.

The insurance provided by this endorsement is limited by its terms. We have no duty to provide coverage that is not afforded by this endorsement.

An insured must comply with the duties imposed by the policy and this endorsement. Failure to comply with those duties may affect coverage.

Statements made by an insured concerning a claim must be truthful. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

We may investigate any occurrence or claim. Our investigation does not waive any right under the policy or this endorsement.

We may assert any condition, exclusion, limitation, or defense available to us. Our decision to investigate, defend, or settle does not create coverage.

We do not cover loss that is excluded by the policy or this endorsement. We do not cover loss beyond the coverage afforded by the policy as modified by this endorsement.

An insured must promptly notify us of an occurrence that may result in a claim. An insured must provide information we reasonably request.

An insured must preserve property when reasonably necessary to protect it from further loss. An insured must permit us to inspect property related to a claim.

No person may bring an action against us unless the applicable policy duties have been met. No person may recover under this endorsement unless coverage applies.

The provisions of this endorsement apply to each insured seeking coverage. An act or omission of an insured may affect coverage as provided by the policy.

Coverage under this endorsement is personal to an insured. Assignment of rights or duties is subject to the policy conditions.

The words and phrases used in this endorsement must be read in their ordinary meaning unless defined by the policy. The singular includes the plural when the context requires.

The captions and arrangement of policy provisions do not alter their meaning. The terms of this endorsement must be read as a whole.

This endorsement does not broaden coverage except as expressly stated. It does not create coverage for a loss that is otherwise not covered.

The obligations of an insured and our rights under the policy remain applicable. This endorsement is subject to all lawful terms of your policy.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide personal liability coverage for damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage" for which an "insured" is legally liable. This coverage applies when the "bodily injury" or "property damage" arises out of a "business pursuit" of an "insured".

**W.2** We provide medical payments coverage for necessary medical expenses resulting from "bodily injury" arising out of a "business pursuit" of an "insured". The "bodily injury" must be caused by an "occurrence" to which this coverage applies.

**W.3** Coverage applies only to a "business pursuit" conducted by an "insured". Coverage does not make any person an "insured".

**W.4** Coverage applies to liability arising from the ownership, maintenance, or use of premises used in connection with a "business pursuit". The premises must be used by an "insured" in the conduct of that "business pursuit".

**W.5** Coverage applies to liability arising from the acts or omissions of an "insured" while conducting a "business pursuit". The acts or omissions must be within the scope of the "business pursuit".

**W.6** Coverage applies to liability arising from the acts or omissions of a person for whom an "insured" is legally responsible. The liability must arise from a "business pursuit" conducted by or for the "insured".

**W.7** Coverage applies to "bodily injury" caused by an accident arising out of a "business pursuit". The accident must occur during the policy period.

**W.8** Coverage applies to "property damage" caused by an accident arising out of a "business pursuit". The accident must occur during the policy period.

**W.9** Coverage applies to "bodily injury" caused by continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions arising out of a "business pursuit". The exposure must begin during the policy period.

**W.10** Coverage applies to "property damage" caused by continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions arising out of a "business pursuit". The exposure must begin during the policy period.

**W.11** We will defend an "insured" against a claim or suit seeking damages covered by this endorsement. We may investigate and settle a claim or suit as we consider appropriate.

**W.12** Our duty to defend ends when the amount we pay for damages equals the limit of liability that applies. Our duty to defend does not create a duty to defend a claim or suit not covered by this endorsement.

**W.13** We will pay reasonable expenses incurred by an "insured" at our request while assisting us in the investigation or defense of a claim or suit. An "insured" must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim or suit.

**W.14** We will pay costs taxed against an "insured" in a suit we defend. We will not pay costs that are not imposed because of a covered claim or suit.

**W.15** We will pay interest accruing after entry of a judgment against an "insured" that we defend. Our obligation to pay interest ends when we pay, offer to pay, or deposit in court the part of the judgment within the applicable limit of liability.

**W.16** We will pay expenses incurred by an "insured" for immediate care of a person sustaining "bodily injury" arising out of a "business pursuit". The expenses must be necessary and reasonable.

**W.17** We will pay medical expenses resulting from "bodily injury" only when the expenses are incurred for necessary medical services. The services may include medical, surgical, dental, hospital, nursing, ambulance, professional nursing, funeral, and prosthetic services.

**W.18** Medical payments coverage applies regardless of fault when the "bodily injury" arises out of a covered "business pursuit". This coverage does not apply to an "insured".

**W.19** An injured person must submit to examination by physicians selected by us when reasonably required. We will pay for an examination we require.

**W.20** An injured person must authorize us to obtain medical reports and other records relating to the claimed "bodily injury". The authorization must allow us to evaluate the claim.

**W.21** An "insured" must promptly notify us of an "occurrence" that may result in a claim. Notice must state the known facts concerning the "occurrence".

**W.22** An "insured" must promptly forward to us every notice, demand, summons, or other legal paper received in connection with a claim or suit. An "insured" must not assume an obligation or incur an expense without our consent, except for reasonable emergency care.

**W.23** An "insured" must cooperate with us in securing and giving evidence. An "insured" must assist us in obtaining the attendance of witnesses and in conducting suits.

**W.24** An "insured" must preserve evidence relating to an "occurrence" when reasonably possible. An "insured" must permit us to inspect property involved in a claim.

**W.25** An "insured" must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense other than for reasonable emergency care. A voluntary payment or obligation does not bind us.

**W.26** Coverage applies only to damages because of "bodily injury" or "property damage". Coverage does not apply to damages that are not caused by an "occurrence".

**W.27** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" expected or intended by an "insured". This exclusion does not apply to "bodily injury" resulting from the use of reasonable force to protect persons or property.

**W.28** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of the rendering or failure to render professional services. This exclusion applies whether the professional services are performed by an "insured" or by another person for whom an "insured" is legally responsible.

**W.29** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of a criminal act or omission committed by or at the direction of an "insured". This exclusion applies even if the resulting injury or damage differs in kind or degree from that expected or intended.

**W.30** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an aircraft. This exclusion applies whether the aircraft is owned, operated, rented, borrowed, or entrusted to an "insured".

**W.31** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a watercraft when coverage is excluded by the policy. This endorsement does not broaden coverage for a watercraft otherwise excluded.

**W.32** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a motor vehicle when coverage is excluded by the policy. This endorsement does not broaden coverage for a motor vehicle otherwise excluded.

**W.33** We do not cover "property damage" to property owned by an "insured". This exclusion applies whether the property is owned solely by an "insured" or jointly with another person.

**W.34** We do not cover "property damage" to property rented to, occupied by, used by, or in the care of an "insured". This exclusion does not apply to "property damage" caused by fire, smoke, or explosion.

**W.35** We do not cover "property damage" to property loaned to an "insured". This exclusion applies while the property is in the possession or control of an "insured".

**W.36** We do not cover "property damage" to personal property in the care, custody, or control of an "insured". This exclusion applies to property held for sale, repair, servicing, storage, or delivery.

**W.37** We do not cover "property damage" to property arising out of work performed by an "insured". This exclusion applies to the part of property on which the work was performed.

**W.38** We do not cover "property damage" arising out of a defect, deficiency, inadequacy, or dangerous condition in work performed by or for an "insured". This exclusion applies after the work has been completed or abandoned.

**W.39** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" for which an "insured" is obligated to pay because of the assumption of liability in a contract or agreement. This exclusion does not apply to liability that an "insured" would have in the absence of the contract or agreement.

**W.40** We do not cover "bodily injury" to an employee of an "insured" arising out of or in the course of employment by an "insured". This exclusion applies whether the "insured" may be liable as an employer or in any other capacity.

**W.41** We do not cover an obligation of an "insured" under a workers compensation, disability benefits, unemployment compensation, or similar law. This exclusion applies to any obligation imposed by law.

**W.42** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. This exclusion applies whether the pollutants are introduced gradually or suddenly.

**W.43** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurpation of power, or action taken by a governmental authority to prevent or defend against any such act. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the injury or damage.

**W.44** We do not cover punitive, exemplary, multiplied, or other damages that are not compensatory damages. This exclusion applies to the extent such damages are not insurable under applicable law.

**W.45** We do not cover a claim for damages arising solely from a dispute concerning fees, charges, commissions, or compensation for services. This exclusion does not apply to "bodily injury" or "property damage" otherwise covered by this endorsement.

**W.46** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's insolvency, bankruptcy, or inability to pay. This exclusion does not affect our duty to pay covered damages for which an "insured" is legally liable.

**W.47** Coverage applies separately to each "insured" against whom a claim is made or a suit is brought. This provision does not increase the limit of liability that applies.

**W.48** The coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to the limits of liability that apply to personal liability coverage and medical payments coverage. The limits of liability are not increased by this endorsement.

**W.49** The coverage provided by this endorsement applies only to a claim arising from a "business pursuit" conducted by an "insured". Coverage does not apply to a "business pursuit" conducted by a person who is not an "insured".

**W.50** An "insured" must maintain records reasonably necessary to identify the activities giving rise to a claim under this endorsement. An "insured" must make those records available to us upon request.

**W.51** An "insured" must notify us promptly if a claim involves a "business pursuit" conducted at a location other than an "insured location". Notice must identify the location and the nature of the "business pursuit".

**W.52** The coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to all policy provisions that are not inconsistent with this endorsement. When this endorsement conflicts with another policy provision, this endorsement controls.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** This limit applies to liability coverage for "business pursuits". It applies subject to all applicable terms of this policy.

**W.2** The most we will pay for the sum of all "damages" arising out of business pursuits is one hundred thousand dollars. This limit is the most available under this endorsement.

**W.3** This limit applies regardless of the number of insureds, claimants, claims, suits, or occurrences. All payments made by us that are subject to this limit reduce the amount available.

**W.4** The limit applies to damages claimed by any person or organization. It applies whether damages are sought directly or indirectly.

**W.5** The limit applies to damages arising from related acts, errors, omissions, conditions, or events. Such damages are subject to the same available limit.

**W.6** The limit applies whether a claim is asserted in a suit or without a suit. A demand, allegation, or request for payment does not increase the limit.

**W.7** The limit applies to amounts payable by judgment or settlement. It also applies to amounts paid by us on your behalf.

**W.8** Payments for "defense costs" do not reduce this limit. Our payment of defense costs does not increase this limit.

**W.9** We may investigate or settle any claim or suit as we consider appropriate. Our authority to investigate or settle ends when the applicable limit has been exhausted by payment.

**W.10** We have no duty to defend any suit after the applicable limit has been exhausted by payment of damages. We have no duty to continue a defense after that exhaustion.

**W.11** Payment by us of damages under this endorsement reduces the limit available for later damages. The available limit is not restored by dismissal, withdrawal, or abandonment of a claim or suit.

**W.12** The limit is not increased by the existence of more than one insured. Each insured shares the same available limit.

**W.13** The limit is not increased because more than one claimant seeks damages. Claims by related persons or organizations share the same available limit.

**W.14** The limit is not increased because damages arise from more than one cause. The manner in which damages are alleged or characterized does not increase the limit.

**W.15** The limit is not increased because damages are sought under different legal theories. Contractual, statutory, or common law allegations do not create an additional limit.

**W.16** The limit applies to damages sought for injury, loss, harm, or expense arising out of business pursuits. The type of damages alleged does not increase the limit.

**W.17** The limit is not increased by the insolvency or bankruptcy of an insured. Such insolvency or bankruptcy does not relieve us of our obligations under this endorsement.

**W.18** The limit is not increased by the insolvency or bankruptcy of any other person or organization. Our obligations remain subject to the applicable limit.

**W.19** The limit is not increased by the insolvency of an insurer or other source of recovery. The unavailability of other insurance does not increase our payment obligation.

**W.20** The limit applies before considering payments available from other sources. Payments by others do not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.21** The limit is not increased by indemnity, contribution, or reimbursement owed to you by another person or organization. Any right of recovery does not increase our liability.

**W.22** The limit applies even if you are entitled to recover from another person or organization. Our payment remains subject to the available limit.

**W.23** The limit applies to liability assumed by an insured only to the extent coverage is otherwise provided. The assumption of liability does not increase the limit.

**W.24** The limit applies to damages for which an insured is legally liable. A request for payment that is not covered does not affect the available limit.

**W.25** The limit is not increased by our investigation of a claim. An investigation does not constitute a payment of damages.

**W.26** The limit is not increased by our defense of a suit. A defense provided by us does not waive any term, condition, or exclusion.

**W.27** The limit is not increased by our reservation of rights. A reservation of rights does not create coverage or expand coverage.

**W.28** The limit is not increased by an allegation that damages exceed the available limit. Our liability remains subject to the applicable limit.

**W.29** The limit applies to settlements made with the consent of an insured or without that consent when permitted by this policy. A settlement does not increase the available limit.

**W.30** The limit applies to judgments entered against an insured. The amount of a judgment does not increase the applicable limit.

**W.31** The limit applies to damages allocated among covered and uncovered allegations. We will pay only the amount that is covered, subject to the applicable limit.

**W.32** The limit is not increased by the inclusion of uncovered allegations in a claim or suit. Coverage for covered allegations remains subject to the applicable limit.

**W.33** The limit applies to obligations of an insured arising from the conduct of employees, agents, or representatives. Such conduct does not create an additional limit.

**W.34** The limit applies to liability arising from the conduct of a business operated by an insured. The number of business activities involved does not increase the limit.

**W.35** The limit is not increased by assignment of a claim, judgment, or right of recovery. An assignee has no greater right than the person making the assignment.

**W.36** The limit applies to a claim made by or on behalf of an estate, trust, successor, or representative. Such status does not increase the amount available.

**W.37** No act, omission, statement, or payment by us increases this limit unless we agree in writing. Any such agreement must be made by us.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "Deductible" is the amount you must bear for a "covered loss" before we make payment. We apply the Deductible as stated in this provision.

**W.2** We will reduce the amount otherwise payable for a covered loss by the applicable Deductible. We will not pay the portion of a covered loss that is within the Deductible.

**W.3** The Deductible applies to "property damage" for which coverage is provided. The Deductible also applies to damages for which an insured is legally responsible, when coverage applies.

**W.4** We apply the Deductible separately to a loss arising from an "occurrence." All damage arising from the same occurrence is subject to the same applicable Deductible.

**W.5** Damage that arises from related acts, conditions, or events may be treated as arising from the same occurrence. We will determine whether damage arises from the same occurrence based on the facts of the loss.

**W.6** The Deductible applies regardless of the number of persons who sustain damage or make a "claim." The Deductible also applies regardless of the number of insureds against whom a claim is made.

**W.7** The Deductible applies before we apply any applicable "limit of liability." Payment of a Deductible does not increase any limit of liability.

**W.8** The Deductible does not reduce the amount of damages for which an insured may be legally liable. It reduces only the amount we will pay under this coverage.

**W.9** An insured must pay the Deductible when we request payment. We may withhold payment until the applicable Deductible has been paid or otherwise satisfied.

**W.10** If we pay an amount that includes the Deductible, an insured must reimburse us for that amount. This duty applies whether payment is made to you or directly to another person or organization.

**W.11** We may settle a claim without obtaining payment of the Deductible from an insured. An insured remains responsible for reimbursing us for the Deductible.

**W.12** The Deductible applies to amounts paid in settlement of a covered claim. It also applies to amounts paid to satisfy a covered "judgment."

**W.13** The Deductible does not apply to amounts we pay for "defense costs" unless those costs are included in damages. Defense costs that are included in damages are subject to the Deductible.

**W.14** The Deductible applies to "loss settlement" amounts that we owe for covered property damage. It applies whether loss settlement is based on "actual cash value," "replacement cost," or another applicable valuation method.

**W.15** We will apply the Deductible after determining the amount of covered loss. We will not apply the Deductible to damage that is not covered.

**W.16** If a covered loss involves property subject to different deductibles, we will apply the deductible that applies to the damaged property. We will not combine deductibles unless the applicable coverage requires that result.

**W.17** If a covered loss involves more than one coverage, we will apply the Deductible to each coverage to which it applies. We will not duplicate the Deductible for the same covered damage.

**W.18** We do not cover any amount within the applicable Deductible. This applies even if an insured has incurred expenses or made payments relating to the covered loss.

**W.19** Payments made by another person or organization do not satisfy the Deductible unless we agree that they do. An insured remains responsible for the Deductible unless we receive payment that satisfies it.

**W.20** Any "recovery" obtained from another person or organization will be applied as permitted by law. A recovery does not eliminate an insured's responsibility for the Deductible.

**W.21** An insured must cooperate with us in obtaining the Deductible from a responsible person or organization. An insured must not impair our right to recover an amount we have paid.

**W.22** The Deductible applies whether the covered loss is discovered immediately or later. The time at which damage is discovered does not change the applicable Deductible.

**W.23** The Deductible applies to covered loss caused by an insured's acts or omissions, subject to the terms of this coverage. It also applies to covered loss caused by the acts or omissions of another person or organization.

**W.24** The Deductible applies to covered loss arising from a "business pursuit" when coverage is provided for that loss. It does not create coverage for a business pursuit or any other activity.

**W.25** We will determine the applicable Deductible based on the circumstances existing when the occurrence takes place. Changes made after the occurrence do not change the applicable Deductible.

**W.26** An insured must provide information we reasonably request to determine the applicable Deductible. This includes information concerning the cause, extent, and timing of the covered loss.

**W.27** If there is a dispute concerning the amount of covered loss, the Deductible will apply to the amount ultimately determined to be covered. Payment of the Deductible does not waive any right of either party concerning that dispute.

**W.28** Participation in "appraisal" does not waive application of the Deductible. Any amount determined through appraisal remains subject to the applicable Deductible.

**W.29** The Deductible applies to loss paid on behalf of an insured. It applies whether we make payment to an insured, a claimant, or another person or organization.

**W.30** No action by us to investigate, defend, settle, or pay a claim waives the Deductible. A waiver is effective only if made by us in writing.

**W.31** An insured may not transfer responsibility for the Deductible to us. Any agreement by another person or organization to pay the Deductible does not alter our rights under this provision.

**W.32** The Deductible remains applicable after a covered loss has occurred. It applies to all payments arising from that covered loss, subject to the terms of this provision.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of a "business pursuit" of an "insured". This exclusion applies whether the activity is conducted for profit or not.

**W.2** We do not cover loss arising out of a business conducted from the "residence premises". This exclusion applies to business property, business operations, and business customers.

**W.3** We do not cover liability arising from the ownership, maintenance, or use of premises used for business purposes. This exclusion applies even when the premises are also used as a residence.

**W.4** We do not cover liability arising from the rendering or failure to render professional services. Professional services include services requiring specialized knowledge, training, judgment, or licensure.

**W.5** We do not cover liability arising from advice, instruction, consultation, or design provided in connection with a business pursuit. This exclusion applies whether the advice is oral, written, electronic, or otherwise communicated.

**W.6** We do not cover liability arising from the sale, distribution, manufacture, or furnishing of goods or products. This exclusion applies to a product that is defective, altered, mislabeled, contaminated, or improperly used.

**W.7** We do not cover liability arising from a product recall, withdrawal, inspection, repair, replacement, adjustment, removal, or disposal. This exclusion applies to costs incurred because a product is suspected of causing injury or damage.

**W.8** We do not cover liability arising from the handling, storage, transportation, or disposal of "pollutants". This exclusion applies to actual, alleged, threatened, or suspected discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by contamination of property. This exclusion applies whether the contamination results from a business operation or from property used in connection with a business operation.

**W.10** We do not cover liability arising from the presence, growth, spread, or exposure to fungi, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or viruses. This exclusion applies to remediation, testing, monitoring, cleaning, removal, and related expenses.

**W.11** We do not cover liability arising from the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "motor vehicle" used in a business pursuit. This exclusion applies while the motor vehicle is used to transport persons, property, equipment, or materials for business purposes.

**W.12** We do not cover liability arising from a motor vehicle held for sale, leased to others, rented to others, or used to carry persons or property for a charge. This exclusion applies regardless of who owns the motor vehicle.

**W.13** We do not cover liability arising from the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of an "aircraft". This exclusion applies to aircraft used in connection with a business pursuit.

**W.14** We do not cover liability arising from the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of a "watercraft" used in a business pursuit. This exclusion applies to watercraft used to transport persons, property, equipment, or materials.

**W.15** We do not cover liability arising from an animal used in a business pursuit. This exclusion applies to breeding, boarding, training, grooming, showing, selling, transporting, or caring for animals.

**W.16** We do not cover liability arising from an animal kept or used for a commercial purpose. This exclusion applies even if the animal is also kept for personal use.

**W.17** We do not cover liability arising from the sale, furnishing, serving, or distribution of alcoholic beverages. This exclusion applies when an insured is engaged in the business of manufacturing, distributing, selling, serving, or furnishing alcoholic beverages.

**W.18** We do not cover liability arising from a criminal act or omission committed by an insured. This exclusion applies whether an insured is charged with, convicted of, or not prosecuted for the act or omission.

**W.19** We do not cover loss arising from an intentional act of an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting injury or damage differs in kind or degree from that intended or expected.

**W.20** We do not cover liability arising from physical or mental abuse, molestation, harassment, or exploitation. This exclusion applies to an insured who commits, directs, authorizes, fails to prevent, or fails to report the conduct.

**W.21** We do not cover liability arising from an employment-related practice, policy, act, or omission. This exclusion applies to hiring, firing, discipline, evaluation, demotion, promotion, compensation, or workplace conduct.

**W.22** We do not cover liability arising from a dispute concerning a contract, agreement, warranty, representation, or guarantee. This exclusion applies to a claimed failure to perform contractual duties.

**W.23** We do not cover liability arising from a breach of fiduciary duty, trust duty, or similar obligation. This exclusion applies to duties undertaken in connection with a business pursuit.

**W.24** We do not cover liability arising from an infringement, misappropriation, unauthorized use, or violation of intellectual property rights. This exclusion applies to trade names, trade secrets, copyrights, patents, service marks, and similar rights.

**W.25** We do not cover liability arising from the collection, storage, disclosure, transmission, loss, theft, corruption, or misuse of data. This exclusion applies to personal information, confidential information, and electronic information.

**W.26** We do not cover liability arising from unauthorized access to an electronic system or network. This exclusion applies to a malicious code, denial of service, ransomware event, or other electronic attack.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudslide, subsidence, sinkhole, or earth sinking. This exclusion applies whether the movement is natural or caused by human activity.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies to water that exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, pavement, or other structure.

**W.29** "Flood" and "surface water" remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by overflow of a body of water or by water carried by flood conditions.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by backup, overflow, or discharge of water or sewage from a sewer, drain, sump, or related system. This exclusion applies whether the condition originates on or away from the residence premises.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a plumbing system because of freezing, blockage, deterioration, or failure. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain plumbing systems and related equipment.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or mechanical failure. This exclusion applies even if the condition results in damage to business property.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, smog, smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations, or discharge of pollutants. This exclusion applies to gradual damage and repeated exposure.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, ceilings, pavements, patios, or similar structures. This exclusion applies to damage arising from faulty construction or maintenance.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. This exclusion applies to damage caused by nesting, chewing, scratching, infestation, or accumulation of waste.

**W.37** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the residence premises. This exclusion applies to resulting loss from interruption of heating, cooling, refrigeration, communications, or security systems.

**W.38** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. This exclusion applies to property used in connection with a business pursuit.

**W.39** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance, law, regulation, or governmental action controlling the construction, repair, replacement, occupancy, or demolition of property. This exclusion applies to the increased cost of compliance.

**W.40** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, confiscation, destruction, or detention by governmental authority. This exclusion applies whether the authority acts lawfully or unlawfully.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. This exclusion applies to any consequence of those events.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies whether the event is controlled, uncontrolled, accidental, or intentional.

**W.43** We do not cover liability arising from the ownership, possession, use, operation, maintenance, or entrustment of property rented to others. This exclusion applies to property held out for rental or commercial occupancy.

**W.44** We do not cover liability arising from construction, demolition, renovation, alteration, or repair performed for a business purpose. This exclusion applies to injury or damage arising from the work, materials, equipment, or site conditions.

**W.45** We do not cover liability arising from excavation, blasting, drilling, tunneling, pile driving, or similar operations. This exclusion applies whether the operations are performed by an insured or by another person acting for an insured.

**W.46** We do not cover liability arising from the collapse of a building, structure, retaining wall, scaffold, platform, or excavation. This exclusion applies to collapse associated with a business operation.

**W.59** An insured must promptly notify us if the insured receives a governmental notice, citation, order, or investigation relating to an occurrence, claim, or condition that may result in a claim. An insured must provide a copy of that notice, citation, order, or investigation request upon our request.

**W.60** An insured must take reasonable steps to comply with a lawful order intended to prevent bodily injury or property damage arising out of the business pursuit. An insured must notify us if compliance with that order may materially affect a pending claim.

**W.61** An insured must perform the duties stated in this section when those duties apply to the business pursuit. Failure to perform a duty does not invalidate coverage unless the failure materially prejudices our ability to investigate, settle, defend, or recover for a claim.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We settle covered loss to "personal property" in accordance with this provision. The applicable settlement basis is determined after we evaluate the property and the loss.

**W.2** We require reasonable information concerning the property that was damaged or destroyed. An insured must provide that information when requested.

**W.3** We may inspect damaged property before settlement. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.4** We may require evidence of ownership, possession, value, and condition. An insured must cooperate with a reasonable request for that evidence.

**W.5** We determine the extent of direct physical loss from the information available to us. We may consider the condition of the property immediately before the loss.

**W.6** We may consider age, wear, depreciation, obsolescence, and market conditions when they are relevant to settlement. We may also consider the property's remaining useful condition.

**W.7** We settle loss to your "personal property" on this basis: replacement cost. This basis applies only to property for which coverage is provided.

**W.8** We do not cover any increase in loss caused by an insured's failure to protect property after a loss. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage.

**W.9** We may repair, replace, or pay for damaged property. Our choice of settlement method does not create an obligation to replace property with identical property.

**W.10** We may use property of like kind and quality when replacement is appropriate. Replacement property may differ in manufacturer, model, design, or appearance.

**W.11** We may settle a loss by payment to you, by payment to a party with an interest in the property, or by arranging repair or replacement. We may require proof of that party's interest.

**W.12** An insured must not voluntarily dispose of damaged property before we complete our inspection unless disposal is necessary to protect health or safety. An insured must retain available evidence concerning the loss.

**W.13** We may take all or part of damaged property at the value we determine under this provision. If we take property, we acquire the interest in that property that you have.

**W.14** We do not cover loss to property after title has passed from you to another person, unless you remain legally responsible for the property. We may require documentation concerning ownership or responsibility.

**W.15** We settle only the portion of a loss that is covered by this policy. We do not pay for damage caused by an excluded cause of loss.

**W.16** If covered and excluded causes of loss contribute to damage, we determine the covered portion of the loss from the available evidence. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for that determination.

**W.17** We may require an insured to submit a signed statement describing the loss. The statement must identify the damaged property and the circumstances of the loss.

**W.18** We may require receipts, invoices, photographs, appraisals, or other records that support a claimed value. An insured must provide records that are reasonably available.

**W.19** We may consider prior repairs, prior damage, and prior claims when they are relevant to the present loss. We do not pay for damage that existed before the reported occurrence.

**W.20** We do not cover the cost to improve property beyond its condition immediately before the loss. We do not pay for betterment unless required by the applicable settlement basis.

**W.21** We do not cover any reduction in value caused solely by style, fashion, or personal preference. We may consider those matters when they affect the value of the property before the loss.

**W.22** We may settle loss to property that is part of a set, pair, or collection by considering the value of the damaged item and the effect of the loss on the remaining property. We do not pay for loss to undamaged property solely because it is part of the same set, pair, or collection.

**W.23** We may settle loss to property with a unique or limited market based on available evidence of value. An insured must provide documentation supporting any claimed special value.

**W.24** We may require an appraisal when we and you disagree on the value of covered property or the amount of loss. Each party must select a competent and impartial appraiser.

**W.25** The appraisers must select an impartial umpire if they do not agree on a valuation. If the appraisers cannot agree on an umpire, either party may request that a court having jurisdiction appoint one.

**W.26** The appraisers must state separately the value of the property and the amount of loss. A written agreement by any two of the appraisers and the umpire will be binding as to those matters.

**W.27** Each party will pay its chosen appraiser. Each party will bear equally the other expenses of the appraisal and umpire.

**W.28** Appraisal does not determine coverage, policy interpretation, or the application of an exclusion. We retain all rights concerning those matters.

**W.29** Payment of a claim does not waive any condition, exclusion, or defense available to us. Our investigation of a loss does not waive any right under this policy.

**W.30** We may request examination under oath of an insured when permitted by this policy. An insured must sign the transcript of that examination if it is accurately recorded.

**W.31** We may require an insured to produce records concerning the loss. An insured must permit us to make copies of records that are reasonably relevant.

**W.32** We do not cover any amount that an insured intentionally conceals or misrepresents in connection with a loss. This includes information concerning ownership, value, condition, or cause of loss.

**W.33** We may reduce any payment by amounts previously paid for the same covered loss. We do not make duplicate payment for the same property damage.

**W.34** We may make payment jointly to insureds or other persons having a legal interest in the property. We may require endorsements, releases, or other documentation before making payment.

**W.35** We do not cover loss that has been restored or compensated by another source to the extent of that restoration or compensation. An insured must disclose other available recovery relating to the loss.

**W.36** If we make payment, we may pursue recovery from a person responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our recovery rights.

**W.37** An insured must transfer recovery rights to us to the extent of our payment when we request it. An insured must cooperate in preserving and enforcing those rights.

**W.38** We may settle a claim without admitting liability. A settlement does not establish coverage for any other claim or occurrence.

**W.39** We may pay the undisputed portion of a covered loss while evaluating the remaining portion. Payment of an undisputed portion does not resolve a disputed portion.

**W.40** We do not cover costs incurred solely to prepare, document, or present a claim, unless those costs are otherwise covered. An insured remains responsible for obtaining any valuation or estimate the insured chooses to obtain.

**W.41** We may consider estimates prepared by qualified persons. We are not required to accept an estimate that does not reasonably reflect the covered loss.

**W.42** We may inspect repairs or replacement property before making any additional payment. An insured must provide reasonable access for that inspection.

**W.43** We do not cover defective, inadequate, or incomplete repair work. We do not guarantee the work of a person selected by an insured.

**W.44** If we arrange repair or replacement, we may select qualified persons to perform the work. An insured must cooperate with reasonable arrangements for access to the property.

**W.45** We do not cover loss arising from an insured's refusal to permit reasonable inspection, repair, or replacement arrangements. We may settle the loss based on the information otherwise available.

**W.46** The "association assessment limit" does not change the settlement basis for property loss. We apply that limit only when coverage for an assessment otherwise applies.

**W.47** A "fire department call fee" does not establish the value of damaged property. We evaluate that charge only if it is otherwise covered.

**W.48** The "precious stones theft limit" applies separately from the valuation of other covered property. We do not use that limit to increase the value of a theft loss.

**W.49** The "special limit on business property on the residence premises" applies separately from the settlement basis for covered property. We do not pay more than the applicable limit.

**W.50** We may require proof that property was at the location described in the claim when the loss occurred. An insured must provide reasonably available information supporting that proof.

**W.51** We may consider whether property was used for business, personal, rental, or other purposes when coverage depends on that use. An insured must accurately describe the use of the property.

**W.52** We do not cover loss to property that is not owned by an insured unless coverage otherwise applies. We may require evidence of an insured's insurable interest.

**W.53** We may settle loss to property owned jointly according to the interest of each owner. We may require all owners to participate in the settlement process.

**W.54** We do not cover loss resulting from an insured's abandonment of property to us. We may accept damaged property without accepting responsibility for property that is not covered.

**W.55** An insured must promptly notify us of a loss and provide information necessary to evaluate it. Delay that materially affects our ability to investigate may affect settlement.

**W.56** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged or destroyed property when reasonably requested. The inventory must describe the property and include available supporting information.

**W.57** We may request that an insured separate damaged property from undamaged property when reasonably practical. An insured must not commingle damaged property in a manner that prevents evaluation.

**W.58** We determine settlement based on the facts known when we evaluate the claim. We may revise a settlement if material information later becomes available.

**W.59** We do not cover loss exceeding the applicable limit of liability. The applicable limit does not increase because property is repaired, replaced, or valued by a particular method.

**W.60** Our payment for covered loss is subject to all applicable policy terms. An insured must comply with the duties that apply to loss settlement.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** “Business” means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or other economic gain. “Business” includes an activity conducted by you or by another person on your behalf.

**W.2** “Business pursuit” means a “business” activity that is continuous, regular, or undertaken with a profit motive. A “business pursuit” may be conducted from your residence or from another location.

**W.3** “Business activity” means an act, service, operation, or undertaking connected with a “business.” A “business activity” includes an act performed in preparation for, during, or after a “business” operation.

**W.4** “Business operations” means the activities, practices, and procedures used to conduct a “business.” “Business operations” include activities performed by you, your employees, or persons acting on your behalf.

**W.5** “Business property” means property owned, rented, used, or intended for use in connection with a “business.” “Business property” includes property held for sale, lease, rental, repair, display, or delivery.

**W.6** “Business premises” means premises used in whole or in part for a “business.” “Business premises” includes an area of your residence used for a “business” activity.

**W.7** “Business equipment” means equipment, machinery, devices, or apparatus used in connection with a “business.” “Business equipment” includes property used to produce, process, store, transport, or provide goods or services.

**W.8** “Business records” means written, printed, or electronically stored information maintained in connection with a “business.” “Business records” include customer information, financial information, contracts, invoices, and correspondence.

**W.9** “Business income” means income arising from a “business” activity. “Business income” includes compensation, fees, commissions, rents, royalties, and proceeds from sales or services.

**W.10** “Business liability” means legal responsibility arising from a “business” or “business activity.” “Business liability” includes responsibility imposed by contract, statute, or common law.

**W.11** “Business invitee” means a person who enters premises in connection with a “business.” A “business invitee” includes a customer, client, supplier, delivery person, or prospective customer.

**W.12** “Customer” means a person who seeks, receives, purchases, or is expected to purchase goods or services from a “business.” A “customer” includes a person acting for another person or organization.

**W.13** “Client” means a person or organization that receives or seeks professional, advisory, personal, or similar services from a “business.” A “client” includes a prospective recipient of those services.

**W.14** “Employee” means a person engaged by you to perform duties connected with a “business.” “Employee” includes a leased worker but does not include a temporary worker.

**W.15** “Volunteer” means a person who performs duties for a “business” without compensation. A “volunteer” may act under your direction or the direction of another person acting on your behalf.

**W.16** “Independent contractor” means a person or organization that performs work or provides services for a “business” under an agreement with you. An “independent contractor” is not an “employee” solely because you may inspect, approve, or coordinate the work.

**W.17** “Partner” means a person who shares ownership, management, profits, or losses of a “business” conducted as a partnership. “Partner” includes a general partner and a limited partner.

**W.18** “Joint venture” means an undertaking in which you and another person or organization combine resources, services, or interests for a “business” purpose. A “joint venture” includes an informal arrangement having the characteristics of a shared “business” enterprise.

**W.19** “Professional service” means a service requiring specialized knowledge, training, judgment, or skill. “Professional service” includes advice, design, consultation, instruction, diagnosis, treatment, or representation.

**W.20** “Personal service” means a service performed directly for another person. “Personal service” includes care, grooming, instruction, assistance, maintenance, or similar service.

**W.21** “Product” means goods, merchandise, food, materials, or other tangible property manufactured, sold, distributed, supplied, or handled by a “business.” A “product” includes containers, packaging, labels, and warnings provided with the property.

**W.22** “Completed work” means work that has been finished or abandoned by you or by another person acting on your behalf. “Completed work” includes work that has been put to its intended use by a person or organization other than a contractor working on the same project.

**W.23** “Sale” means a transfer of ownership or possession of property for compensation or other consideration. A “sale” includes a transaction completed through direct contact, delivery, shipment, or electronic communication.

**W.24** “Lease” means an agreement granting another person or organization the right to possess or use property for consideration. A “lease” includes an agreement described as a rental arrangement.

**W.25** “Rental” means the furnishing of property, premises, or equipment to another person or organization for consideration. “Rental” includes a temporary or continuing arrangement.

**W.26** “Advertising” means communication intended to attract customers or promote a “business,” product, service, or operation. “Advertising” includes communication by print, broadcast, display, mail, electronic media, or personal contact.

**W.27** “Solicitation” means an effort to obtain customers, clients, orders, contracts, or other “business” opportunities. “Solicitation” includes an offer, invitation, proposal, or request for “business.”

**W.28** “Delivery” means the transfer, transportation, or attempted transfer of property to another person or organization. “Delivery” includes loading, unloading, packaging, and handling related to the transfer.

**W.29** “Demonstration” means the display, use, testing, or presentation of a product, service, or method in connection with a “business.” A “demonstration” includes an activity intended to promote or explain a “business” offering.

**W.30** “Instruction” means teaching, training, coaching, advising, or directing another person. “Instruction” includes instruction provided in connection with a “business” activity.

**W.31** “Supervision” means directing, overseeing, monitoring, or controlling the work or conduct of another person. “Supervision” includes responsibility for assigning duties or reviewing performance in connection with a “business.”

**W.32** “Management” means directing or controlling the affairs, operations, assets, or personnel of a “business.” “Management” includes authority to make operational, financial, or personnel decisions.

**W.33** “Ownership interest” means a legal or equitable interest in a “business” or property used by a “business.” An “ownership interest” includes an interest held directly or through another person or organization.

**W.34** “Office” means an area used for administrative, professional, clerical, or management activities connected with a “business.” An “office” includes an area used to meet customers or maintain “business records.”

**W.35** “Workspace” means an area used to perform “business” activities. A “workspace” includes an area used for production, repair, assembly, design, instruction, or administration.

**W.36** “Storage area” means an area used to keep “business property,” inventory, equipment, supplies, or records. A “storage area” includes an area used for property awaiting sale, delivery, repair, or use.

**W.37** “Inventory” means property held for sale, lease, rental, distribution, or use in a “business.” “Inventory” includes raw materials, work in process, finished goods, and packaging materials.

**W.38** “Tools” means implements, instruments, or devices used to perform work or provide services. “Tools” include manual, mechanical, electrical, and electronic implements used in a “business.”

**W.39** “Supplies” means consumable property used in the conduct of a “business.” “Supplies” include materials, stationery, cleaning materials, packaging, and similar property.

**W.40** “Records” means information maintained in written, printed, recorded, or electronic form. “Records” include “business records” and information concerning a “business” transaction or activity.

**W.41** “Electronic data” means information, facts, concepts, or instructions stored, created, transmitted, or used by electronic or computerized equipment. “Electronic data” includes software and electronically stored “business records.”

**W.42** “Electronic media” means devices, systems, networks, or methods used to create, store, transmit, display, or receive “electronic data.” “Electronic media” includes communication platforms and data storage devices.

**W.43** “Tangible property” means property that can be seen, touched, or physically possessed. “Tangible property” does not include “electronic data.”

**W.44** “Property damage” means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of “tangible property.” “Property damage” includes resulting loss of use of property that has not been physically injured.

**W.45** “Bodily injury” means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. “Bodily injury” includes resulting care, loss of services, and death.

**W.46** “Personal injury” means injury arising from conduct that affects a person's rights, reputation, privacy, or liberty. “Personal injury” includes false arrest, detention, malicious prosecution, wrongful eviction, defamation, and invasion of privacy.

**W.47** “Advertising injury” means injury arising from “advertising” that affects another person's rights, reputation, privacy, or intellectual property interests. “Advertising injury” includes the publication or use of material in a manner that causes covered injury.

**W.48** “Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An “occurrence” includes an accident arising from a “business” activity.

**W.49** “Accident” means an unforeseen and unintended event resulting in “bodily injury” or “property damage.” An “accident” includes an unintended result of an act or omission.

**W.50** “Loss” means accidental physical loss of or physical damage to property. “Loss” includes loss resulting from an “occurrence” connected with a “business.”

**W.51** “Claim” means a demand for money, services, property, or other relief because of alleged injury, damage, or loss. A “claim” includes a demand made directly to you or to us on your behalf.

**W.52** “Suit” means a civil proceeding in which damages because of “bodily injury,” “property damage,” “personal injury,” or “advertising injury” are alleged. “Suit” includes arbitration or another dispute resolution proceeding in which such damages are claimed.
