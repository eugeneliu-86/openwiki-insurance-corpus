# HO 04 12 Increased Limits on Business Property (Edition 2018-09)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This "endorsement" modifies the insurance provided by your "policy".

This endorsement is attached to and forms part of your policy.

The provisions of this endorsement apply only to the extent that they modify your policy.

We provide the insurance described in this endorsement subject to all applicable policy provisions.

Any provision of your policy that is not modified by this endorsement remains unchanged.

This endorsement applies to "business property" only as provided in its terms.

Business property includes property used in connection with a business conducted by an insured.

The coverage provided by this endorsement is subject to the terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions that apply to your policy.

An insured must comply with all duties that apply to a loss involving business property.

We will determine coverage for business property under the terms of your policy as modified by this endorsement.

This endorsement does not create coverage for a cause of loss that is not otherwise covered.

This endorsement does not apply to property that is excluded from coverage.

We do not cover loss that is subject to an exclusion applicable to your policy.

We do not cover loss for which an insured has failed to satisfy a condition required by your policy.

The terms of this endorsement control if they conflict with other policy provisions.

The provisions of your policy control when this endorsement does not address a matter.

An insured must provide information that we reasonably request to determine coverage.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim.

We may inspect business property after a loss to the extent permitted by your policy.

We may require documentation supporting the ownership, use, value, or loss of business property.

This endorsement applies only while it is part of your policy.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We provide coverage for "business property" that is covered property under this endorsement. This coverage applies only to business property that is owned or used by an insured.

**W.2** "Business property" means property used in a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity conducted for economic gain. Business property includes property held for use in that activity.

**W.3** We cover business property while it is at the residence premises. Coverage applies while the property is in a building, structure, or other location that is part of the residence premises.

**W.4** We cover business property while it is away from the residence premises when the property is temporarily removed from the residence premises. Coverage away from the residence premises remains subject to the terms of this endorsement.

**W.5** We cover business personal property that is owned by an insured. We also cover business personal property for which an insured is legally responsible.

**W.6** We cover business property that is usual to the business activity conducted by an insured. The property must be used principally in connection with that activity.

**W.7** We cover furniture, fixtures, equipment, supplies, and materials used in the business activity. The property must be located where an insured has lawful access to it.

**W.8** We cover tools, instruments, and portable equipment used in the business activity. The property must be under the care, custody, or control of an insured.

**W.9** We cover stock that is held for sale, lease, delivery, or use in the business activity. Stock includes materials and supplies intended to become part of property held for sale.

**W.10** We cover property of others only while it is in the care, custody, or control of an insured. An insured must be legally responsible for the property at the time of loss.

**W.11** We cover improvements and betterments made by an insured to property that is not owned by an insured. The improvements and betterments must be made at the expense of an insured.

**W.12** We cover business property only for direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss. The loss must occur during the time this endorsement applies.

**W.13** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by fire or lightning. We cover resulting smoke damage caused by accidental fire or lightning.

**W.14** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by windstorm or hail. We do not cover loss caused by wind or hail to property left in the open unless the property is normally kept there.

**W.15** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by explosion. We cover loss caused by the explosion of equipment used in the business activity.

**W.16** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by riot or civil commotion. We cover loss resulting from acts committed during a riot or civil commotion.

**W.17** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by aircraft. This includes loss caused by objects falling from aircraft.

**W.18** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by vehicles. We do not cover loss caused by a vehicle owned or operated by an insured.

**W.19** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by smoke. The smoke must be sudden and accidental.

**W.20** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by vandalism or malicious mischief. We do not cover loss caused by theft or attempted theft in connection with vandalism or malicious mischief.

**W.21** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by falling objects. Damage to property caused by rain, snow, sleet, or sand is covered only if the falling object first damages the building or structure containing the property.

**W.22** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet. The weight must cause direct physical damage to the property.

**W.23** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam. The water or steam must escape from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system.

**W.24** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a steam, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We cover resulting damage to business property.

**W.25** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. An insured must maintain heat in the building or shut off the water supply and drain the system when the building is vacant.

**W.26** We cover direct physical loss to business property caused by sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current. We do not cover loss to tubes, transistors, electronic components, or circuitry caused by electrical current.

**W.27** We cover business property for loss caused by theft. Theft includes attempted theft and loss of property from a known place under circumstances indicating theft.

**W.28** We do not cover theft of business property from a part of the residence premises that is rented to others. We also do not cover theft by a tenant of that part of the residence premises.

**W.29** We do not cover theft of business property while it is at a location occupied by an insured and not part of the residence premises. This exclusion does not apply while an insured is temporarily residing at that location.

**W.30** We do not cover theft of property held for sale to customers. We do not cover loss caused by voluntary parting with property because of trick, scheme, or false pretense.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve business property at and after the time of loss. This duty applies when the property is threatened by a covered cause of loss.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We cover resulting loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.33** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or decay. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss that results from those conditions.

**W.34** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from agricultural smudging, or discharge of pollutants. We cover loss caused by smoke that is sudden and accidental.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We cover resulting loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.36** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We cover resulting loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.37** We do not cover loss caused by inherent vice, latent defect, or any quality in property that causes it to damage or destroy itself. We cover resulting loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.38** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We cover resulting loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**W.39** We do not cover loss caused by power failure or interruption of utility service if the failure or interruption occurs away from the residence premises. We cover loss caused by a covered cause of loss that results from the power failure or interruption.

**W.40** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, including seizure, confiscation, destruction, or taking of property. We cover loss caused by a covered cause of loss that results from governmental action taken to prevent the spread of fire.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an insured. This exclusion applies even if the property is also owned by an insured who did not intend the loss.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military force. We do not cover loss caused by any consequence of war.

**W.43** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination.

**W.44** We do not cover loss to electronic data. "Electronic data" means information, facts, or computer programs stored, created, used, or transmitted by electronic or computerized equipment.

**W.45** We cover the cost to repair or replace covered business property with property of like kind and quality. We will not pay for more than the amount necessary to restore the property to its condition immediately before the loss.

**W.46** We may pay the value of lost or damaged business property in money, repair the property, replace the property, or provide property of like kind and quality. We may choose the manner of payment.

**W.47** An insured must give us prompt notice of loss. The notice must identify the business property involved and describe the known circumstances of loss.

**W.48** An insured must protect business property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property.

**W.49** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and other expenses incurred because of a loss. An insured must provide those records to us when requested.

**W.50** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged and undamaged business property when requested by us. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for the property.

**W.51** An insured must show us the damaged business property when requested. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine records relating to the loss.

**W.52** An insured must provide records, documents, receipts, invoices, and other information reasonably requested by us. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of the loss.

**W.53** An insured must submit to examination under oath when requested by us. An insured must answer our questions truthfully and may be examined separately from another insured.

**W.54** An insured must provide a signed, sworn proof of loss when requested by us. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably necessary for us to determine the amount and cause of loss.

**W.55** We will pay only for loss to business property that is covered under this endorsement. Payment under this endorsement does not create coverage for property otherwise excluded.

**W.56** The coverage provided by this endorsement applies subject to all applicable policy conditions and exclusions. This endorsement changes only the coverage for business property described in this section.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** For this provision, "Business Property" means covered personal property used in connection with a trade, profession, occupation, or other business activity. Business Property includes property held for sale, property used to provide services, and property used to support business operations.

**W.2** The most we will pay for Business Property on the "Residence Premises" is ten thousand dollars. This limit applies to all Business Property located on the Residence Premises.

**W.3** This limit applies regardless of the ownership of Business Property. It also applies to Business Property in your care, custody, or control.

**W.4** This limit applies to a "Covered Loss" involving Business Property. A Covered Loss includes direct physical loss to Business Property when coverage applies.

**W.5** This limit is the most we will pay for all covered damage to Business Property arising from a loss. The limit is not increased because the loss affects separate items of Business Property.

**W.6** This limit applies to Business Property kept inside a dwelling, garage, storage building, or other structure on the Residence Premises. It also applies to Business Property kept in the open on the Residence Premises.

**W.7** This limit applies while Business Property is being used for business purposes on the Residence Premises. It also applies while Business Property is not actively being used.

**W.8** This limit applies to Business Property used by you. It also applies to Business Property used by another person with your permission.

**W.9** This limit applies to Business Property owned by an insured. It also applies to Business Property owned by a business entity when the property is covered property.

**W.10** This limit applies to Business Property held for sale. It also applies to Business Property held for repair, service, demonstration, display, or delivery.

**W.11** This limit applies to Business Property used in producing goods. It also applies to Business Property used in providing services.

**W.12** This limit applies to Business Property used for administrative, clerical, or management activities. It also applies to Business Property used for communications, records, or data processing.

**W.13** This limit applies to Business Property used in connection with a home-based business. It also applies to Business Property used in connection with a business conducted away from the Residence Premises when the property is located on the Residence Premises at the time of loss.

**W.14** This limit applies to Business Property that is temporarily stored on the Residence Premises. It also applies to Business Property awaiting use, sale, repair, delivery, or disposal.

**W.15** This limit applies to Business Property that has been purchased but not yet placed into service. It also applies to Business Property that has been removed from active use but remains on the Residence Premises.

**W.16** This limit applies to Business Property that is leased, rented, borrowed, or entrusted to you. Coverage applies only when the property otherwise qualifies as covered property.

**W.17** This limit applies to Business Property that is subject to a security interest, lien, or other financial interest. Such interest does not increase the amount we will pay.

**W.18** This limit applies without regard to the value assigned to an individual item of Business Property. We will not pay more than the applicable limit for the total covered loss.

**W.19** This limit applies after any applicable deductible. An applicable deductible does not increase this limit.

**W.20** This limit applies whether Business Property is damaged, destroyed, lost, or stolen. The cause of loss does not increase this limit.

**W.21** This limit applies whether the loss involves property at a single location on the Residence Premises or property at separate locations on the Residence Premises. We will not apply the limit separately by building, room, or storage area.

**W.22** This limit applies whether Business Property is insured under replacement cost coverage or actual cash value coverage. The valuation method does not increase this limit.

**W.23** This limit applies to the cost to repair covered Business Property. It also applies to the cost to replace covered Business Property.

**W.24** This limit applies to the cost to restore covered Business Property. It also applies to the cost to replace damaged components, materials, and accessories.

**W.25** This limit applies to property that is attached to Business Property. It also applies to property customarily used with Business Property.

**W.26** This limit applies to business records, manuals, documents, and similar property when they are covered. We will pay only for covered loss and subject to the applicable valuation provisions.

**W.27** This limit applies to equipment, tools, supplies, materials, and stock when they are covered. The presence of business-related contents does not create a separate limit.

**W.28** This limit applies to electronic equipment used for business purposes when the equipment is covered. Software, data, and media are subject to the applicable coverage provisions.

**W.29** This limit applies to Business Property damaged by a covered peril. We do not cover loss caused by a peril that is excluded.

**W.30** This limit does not create coverage for property that is not covered property. It does not change any condition applicable to a covered loss.

**W.31** This limit does not eliminate any exclusion applicable to Business Property. We do not cover loss that remains excluded.

**W.32** This limit does not extend coverage to liability arising out of business activities. Liability coverage is not provided by this provision.

**W.33** This limit does not provide coverage for loss of income, loss of use, delay, interruption, or consequential loss. We do not cover such loss unless coverage is otherwise provided.

**W.34** This limit does not provide coverage for damage to property while it is away from the Residence Premises. Coverage for property away from the Residence Premises is determined under the applicable policy provisions.

**W.35** This limit applies only while the Residence Premises is insured under this policy. Coverage does not apply after coverage for the Residence Premises has ended.

**W.36** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a covered loss involving Business Property. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged Business Property when we reasonably request.

**W.37** An insured must retain damaged Business Property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless we agree otherwise. An insured must protect covered Business Property from further damage after a loss.

**W.38** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, inventories, or other information that we reasonably request to establish ownership, use, and value. An insured must identify Business Property that was used for business purposes.

**W.39** An insured must provide information concerning the business activity connected with the Business Property when we reasonably request. An insured must provide information concerning any ownership interest in the Business Property.

**W.40** We may examine Business Property, records, and related information as permitted by this policy. Our examination does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.41** We may pay you, an insured, or another person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment to any person does not increase this limit.

**W.42** We may repair or replace covered Business Property with like kind and quality when permitted by this policy. Our election to repair or replace does not increase this limit.

**W.43** We may take all or part of damaged Business Property at the value we have agreed upon. Our exercise of that right does not increase this limit.

**W.44** We may require assignment of rights of recovery relating to a covered loss. Amounts recovered after payment will be applied as permitted by this policy.

**W.45** This limit applies to the interest of an insured in Business Property. We will not pay more than the insured's financial interest in the property.

**W.46** This limit applies when Business Property is covered under more than a single policy issued by us. We will not pay more than the amount of covered loss.

**W.47** This limit is not cumulative with any other coverage available for the same Business Property. We will apply the coverage that most specifically applies to the covered loss.

**W.48** All other terms and conditions of this policy remain applicable. This provision changes only the limit applicable to Business Property on the Residence Premises.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "Deductible" is the amount that you must bear before we pay for covered loss to "Business Property." We will apply the Deductible to covered loss in accordance with these provisions.

**W.2** The Deductible applies to the total covered loss arising from an occurrence. We will subtract the Deductible from the amount of covered loss after applying all applicable policy terms.

**W.3** We will not pay any portion of covered loss that is within the Deductible. Your responsibility for the Deductible applies whether you retain damaged property or transfer it to another party.

**W.4** The Deductible applies to direct physical loss of or damage to Business Property. The Deductible also applies to resulting covered expense that is included in the amount of loss.

**W.5** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of covered loss. An insured must provide information we reasonably request concerning the cause, extent, and value of loss.

**W.6** We may investigate any loss before determining the application of the Deductible. Our investigation does not waive any policy condition, exclusion, limitation, or defense.

**W.7** When property is damaged by more than a single cause, we will apply the Deductible to the covered portion of the loss. We will not apply the Deductible to an amount that is not covered.

**W.8** When an occurrence causes loss to Business Property at separate locations, we will determine whether the losses arose from the same occurrence. We may require information from you to make that determination.

**W.9** Losses that arise from separate occurrences are subject to separate application of the Deductible. The existence of similar damage does not establish that losses arose from the same occurrence.

**W.10** The Deductible applies before we determine any payment for loss. We will not increase a payment to reimburse you for the Deductible.

**W.11** The Deductible applies regardless of whether you repair, replace, or do not repair damaged Business Property. The Deductible applies regardless of the disposition of salvage.

**W.12** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect Business Property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable protective measures do not eliminate the application of the Deductible.

**W.13** We may pay for covered emergency measures when required by the policy. The Deductible applies to covered loss that includes those measures.

**W.14** We may elect to repair or replace damaged Business Property, subject to the Deductible. Our election does not require us to pay the portion of loss that you must bear.

**W.15** If we repair or replace damaged Business Property, you must pay the Deductible when requested by us. We may require payment before beginning repairs or replacement.

**W.16** If another party is legally responsible for a covered loss, the Deductible remains applicable to our payment. Your recovery from that party may be subject to our rights of recovery.

**W.17** An insured must not impair our rights to recover amounts paid for covered loss. An insured must preserve available evidence and cooperate in any recovery effort.

**W.18** Amounts recovered by us from another party may be allocated as permitted by applicable law. Such recovery does not change the Deductible applied to our payment unless we agree otherwise in writing.

**W.19** The Deductible applies to loss settled on an actual cash value basis. The Deductible also applies to loss settled on a replacement cost basis.

**W.20** We will determine the amount of covered loss before applying the Deductible. We will not apply the Deductible to depreciation, wear, deterioration, or other amounts that are not covered.

**W.21** An insured must provide records supporting the value of damaged Business Property when reasonably available. We may examine records, property, and supporting documentation related to the loss.

**W.22** If damaged Business Property is part of a larger set, collection, or group, we will determine covered loss under the applicable valuation provisions. The Deductible applies to the covered amount determined by us.

**W.23** The Deductible applies even if the covered loss exceeds the value of the damaged Business Property. We will not pay more than the amount of covered loss after application of the Deductible.

**W.24** The Deductible does not create coverage for property, causes of loss, expense, or damage that is otherwise excluded or limited. We do not cover any loss that is excluded by the policy.

**W.25** The Deductible does not reduce any duty that you have after a loss. An insured must comply with all applicable duties before we are required to make payment.

**W.26** We may request that you submit a signed statement concerning the loss. A statement concerning the loss does not alter the Deductible or expand coverage.

**W.27** If you and we disagree about the amount of loss, the Deductible will apply to the amount ultimately determined to be covered. Our payment obligation remains subject to all policy terms.

**W.28** Payment of any amount for covered loss does not waive our right to apply the Deductible to that loss. We may correct an error in the application of the Deductible to the extent permitted by law.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover direct physical loss to "business property" caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law. We do not cover the cost to comply with any ordinance or law after a loss.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. This exclusion applies whether the earth movement is natural, man-made, or results from another cause.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by earthquake, landslide, mudslide, mine subsidence, or earth sinking. We do not cover loss caused by the settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of earth.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by water that seeps or leaks through a building, foundation, wall, floor, or paved surface. We do not cover loss caused by water pressure beneath the ground.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by neglect. An insured must use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of a loss.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by war. This exclusion applies to declared war, undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or usurped power.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by military force or military action. We do not cover loss caused by destruction, seizure, or use of property for a military purpose.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. We do not cover loss caused by radioactive contamination.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts of an insured. We do not cover loss caused by an insured acting with the intent to cause a loss.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by dishonest acts. This exclusion applies to dishonest acts committed by an insured or by a person entrusted with the property.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by fraudulent acts. We do not cover loss resulting from concealment or misrepresentation of a material fact.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action. This exclusion applies to seizure, confiscation, destruction, or ordered removal of property by a governmental authority.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by delay, loss of use, loss of market, or loss of value. We do not cover consequential economic loss arising from physical loss to property.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear. We do not cover deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical breakdown.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, decay, or deterioration. We do not cover loss caused by smog or dry rot.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, or bacteria. We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by nesting or infestation of birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover loss caused by discharge or release of waste products or secretions by such animals.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by scratching, marring, chipping, or denting. We do not cover loss caused by defective materials, faulty workmanship, or faulty maintenance.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical, electrical, or electronic failure. We do not cover loss caused by power surge, interruption of power, or failure of utility service.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by pressure or weight of water, ice, or snow. We do not cover loss caused by the freezing, thawing, expansion, or contraction of water or ice.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems. This exclusion applies if an insured fails to use reasonable care to maintain heat or to shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems. We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from agricultural smudging, or industrial operations. We do not cover loss caused by vapor, fumes, or gas emitted from any source.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover loss caused by collapse unless collapse is otherwise covered.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by faulty planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, or design. We do not cover loss caused by faulty specifications, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, or compaction.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by defective construction materials. We do not cover loss caused by inadequate repair, maintenance, or protection of property.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by an insured. We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by a person residing in the residence premises.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by theft from a part of the residence premises rented to a person who is not an insured. We do not cover loss caused by theft from property held for rental to others.

**W.31** We do not cover loss caused by theft from an unattended vehicle. We do not cover loss caused by theft of property while it is away from the residence premises unless coverage applies under the policy.

**W.32** We do not cover loss caused by mysterious disappearance. We do not cover loss for property that cannot be located without evidence of a covered cause of loss.

**W.33** We do not cover loss to property used for a business purpose if the property is not covered under the policy. We do not cover income loss, business interruption, or extra expense.

**W.34** We do not cover loss to accounts, bills, currency, deeds, evidences of debt, letters of credit, manuscripts, passports, securities, or tickets. We do not cover loss to data, records, or electronic information except as otherwise covered.

**W.35** We do not cover loss to credit cards, fund transfer cards, access devices, or similar instruments. We do not cover loss arising from unauthorized use of such property.

**W.36** We do not cover loss to animals, birds, fish, or other living creatures. We do not cover loss to plants, shrubs, trees, lawns, or growing crops.

**W.37** We do not cover loss to watercraft, aircraft, hovercraft, or their equipment. We do not cover loss to property while it is in or upon such conveyances unless coverage applies under the policy.

**W.38** We do not cover loss to motor vehicles subject to registration for use on public roads. We do not cover loss to equipment designed for use with motor vehicles unless coverage applies under the policy.

**W.39** We do not cover loss to property of tenants, roomers, boarders, or other persons who are not insureds. We do not cover loss to property owned by another person and held for sale, storage, service, repair, or rental.

**W.40** We do not cover loss to property intended for use in illegal trade, transportation, manufacture, distribution, or sale. We do not cover loss arising from an unlawful act by an insured.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an insured to preserve damaged property for our inspection. An insured must permit us to inspect property and records relating to a claimed loss.

**W.42** We do not cover loss for property that an insured voluntarily parts with because of trick, scheme, or false pretense. We do not cover loss resulting from a fraudulent transfer of property.

**W.43** We do not cover loss caused by computer virus, malicious code, denial of service, or unauthorized access to an electronic system. We do not cover loss caused by corruption, alteration, or destruction of electronic data.

**W.44** We do not cover loss arising from breach of contract, warranty, guarantee, or representation. We do not cover loss arising from delay in performance or failure to perform a contractual obligation.

**W.45** We do not cover loss caused by contamination. We do not cover loss caused by the presence, release, or removal of pollutants, contaminants, or hazardous substances.

**W.46** We do not cover loss caused by asbestos. We do not cover loss arising from testing, monitoring, cleaning, removing, containing, treating, detoxifying, or neutralizing asbestos.

**W.47** We do not cover loss caused by lead. We do not cover loss arising from testing, monitoring, cleaning, removing, containing, treating, detoxifying, or neutralizing lead.

**W.48** We do not cover loss caused by communicable disease. We do not cover loss arising from fear of, exposure to, or transmission of a communicable disease.

**W.49** We do not cover loss caused by sanctions, embargoes, or trade restrictions. We do not cover loss to the extent payment would violate an applicable law or regulation.

**W.50** We do not cover loss caused by civil authority when the authority acts to prevent the spread of a covered peril. We do not cover loss caused by the lawful exercise of police power.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We will settle a covered loss to "business property" under the terms of this endorsement. The applicable limit is not increased by the amount of any loss.

**W.2** The loss settlement basis for personal property under this endorsement is replacement cost. We will apply that basis only to property for which coverage is provided.

**W.3** We will determine the amount of loss after considering the property’s condition immediately before the loss. We may consider age, use, wear, deterioration, and other relevant conditions.

**W.4** We will not pay for any part of a loss that is not covered. We will not pay more than the amount required to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality.

**W.5** "Actual cash value" means the value of property at the time of loss after deduction for depreciation. We may determine actual cash value by using available market information and other relevant evidence.

**W.6** "Depreciation" means a reduction in value resulting from age, wear, condition, obsolescence, or other factors affecting value. We may apply depreciation separately to labor, materials, or other components of damaged property where applicable.

**W.7** You must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. You must make reasonable and necessary repairs solely to protect the property from further damage.

**W.8** We will consider reasonable expenses incurred to protect covered property from further damage when determining a covered loss. Such expenses do not increase the applicable limit.

**W.9** You must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. You must not voluntarily dispose of damaged property unless its disposal is necessary to protect persons or property from immediate danger.

**W.10** We may inspect damaged property and take samples for testing, analysis, or examination. Our inspection does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.11** You must provide records, receipts, inventories, photographs, and other information we reasonably request to determine the amount of loss. You must cooperate with us in obtaining information relevant to the loss.

**W.12** You must prepare an inventory of damaged and undamaged property when we request it. The inventory must identify the property, its condition, and the claimed amount of loss.

**W.13** We may require you to show us the damaged property. We may require you to submit to an examination under oath regarding any matter relating to the loss.

**W.14** You must produce records and documents in your possession or control that relate to the ownership, value, condition, repair, replacement, or disposition of the property. We may make copies of those records and documents.

**W.15** We will determine loss to property that can be repaired by the reasonable cost to repair the property. The repair must restore the property to like kind and quality, subject to applicable depreciation and policy conditions.

**W.16** We will determine loss to property that cannot reasonably be repaired by the value of the property immediately before the loss. We may consider the availability of comparable property when determining that value.

**W.17** We may elect to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. If we make that election, we may use contractors, vendors, materials, and methods reasonably available to us.

**W.18** Our election to repair, rebuild, or replace does not require us to provide property identical in every respect to the damaged property. We will use reasonably comparable materials and methods when identical materials or methods are unavailable.

**W.19** You must permit us to inspect repairs or replacement property when we reasonably request it. You must preserve invoices, contracts, receipts, and other records supporting the claimed loss.

**W.20** We will not pay for repair or replacement that results in betterment beyond the condition of the property immediately before the loss. We may pay for reasonable changes necessary to complete covered repair or replacement.

**W.21** We will not pay for increased cost resulting from your delay in repairing or replacing damaged property. We will not pay for increased cost caused by your voluntary selection of materials or methods exceeding like kind and quality.

**W.22** If damaged property is part of a "pair or set," we will determine the loss based on the reduction in value of the pair or set. We may repair or replace the damaged item if doing so restores the pair or set to like kind and quality.

**W.23** We may pay for the loss of the pair or set if repair or replacement of the damaged item does not reasonably restore its value. We may take possession of the remaining property after payment for the loss.

**W.24** If property consists of articles customarily matched together, we may settle the loss based on the value of the articles as a group. We will not pay for undamaged articles unless their replacement is necessary to restore the group to like kind and quality.

**W.25** We may settle a loss by paying you, paying a repairer or supplier, or providing repair or replacement. Payment to a repairer or supplier does not relieve you of responsibility for charges that exceed the amount we owe.

**W.26** We may require you to assign to us any recoverable interest in damaged property after we make payment for that property. We may retain salvage to the extent permitted by law.

**W.27** "Salvage" means damaged property that retains value after a loss. The value of salvage may be considered in determining the amount we will pay.

**W.28** You must not abandon covered property to us. We may accept abandoned property, but our acceptance does not create a duty to accept other property.

**W.29** We may require appraisal if you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss. Each party will select a competent and impartial appraiser.

**W.30** The appraisers will select an umpire. If the appraisers cannot agree on an umpire, either party may request that an umpire be selected by a court having jurisdiction.

**W.31** The appraisers will state separately the amount of loss and the value of the property. An agreement by any appraiser and the umpire will determine the amount of loss.

**W.32** Each party will pay the expense of its chosen appraiser. Each party will share the expense of the umpire and other appraisal expenses equally.

**W.33** Appraisal determines only the amount of loss and value of property. Appraisal does not determine coverage, policy interpretation, or the application of any exclusion or condition.

**W.34** Payment of a covered loss does not transfer ownership of the damaged property to us unless we take possession of it. We may require a transfer of ownership when necessary to obtain salvage value.

**W.35** We may reduce the amount we pay by the value of any recovered property. You must promptly notify us if property for which we have paid is recovered.

**W.36** If you recover property after we have paid for its loss, you must return to us the amount we paid for that property or transfer the recovered property to us. We may permit you to retain recovered property upon payment of its salvage value.

**W.37** The assessment coverage limit does not determine the amount of loss to business property. We will apply that coverage only as provided by its applicable terms.

**W.38** A fire response charge does not increase the amount payable for loss to business property. We will determine any payment for that charge separately from the settlement of property loss.

**W.39** The special limit on theft of jewelry, watches and precious stones applies separately from the settlement of business property loss. That special limit does not increase the amount payable for other covered property.

**W.40** We will not pay more than the interest you have in the property at the time of loss. We will not pay for loss to property in which you have no insurable interest.

**W.41** If more than one person has an insurable interest in covered property, we will adjust the loss according to the interests shown by the available evidence. Payment to a person with an established interest will reduce the amount otherwise payable for that interest.

**W.42** You must notify us promptly of any claim, payment, settlement, or recovery involving the damaged property. You must not impair our rights to recover from any person responsible for the loss.

**W.43** If we make payment for a covered loss, we are subrogated to your rights of recovery against any person responsible for that loss. You must do nothing after loss to prejudice those rights.

**W.44** We will pay no more than the applicable limit after applying all terms, conditions, and limitations of this endorsement. Our payment for a covered loss will be made after you have complied with your duties relating to that loss.

## W.8 — Definitions

As used in this endorsement, the following words have the meanings stated below. These meanings apply whether a word appears in singular, plural, possessive, or other grammatical form.

“Business” means a trade, profession, occupation, or activity engaged in for compensation or other financial gain. Business includes an activity conducted by you or on your behalf.

“Business Property” means tangible personal property used in a Business. Business Property includes property that you own, lease, rent, borrow, or hold for use in a Business.

“Covered Business Property” means Business Property to which the increased limit provided by this endorsement applies. Covered Business Property must be within the scope of property described by this endorsement.

“Personal Property” means movable property that is not land or permanently attached to land. Personal Property includes property kept for use, sale, storage, repair, display, or delivery.

“Business Personal Property” means Personal Property associated with a Business. Business Personal Property includes property used to conduct, support, maintain, or administer a Business.

“Property of Others” means Personal Property owned by a person or organization other than you. Property of Others includes property in your care, custody, or control.

“Owned” means having legal title to property. Property may be Owned jointly with another person or organization.

“Leased” means held under an agreement that gives you the right to possess or use property. Leased property remains property of the person or organization that retains legal title unless ownership transfers to you.

“Rented” means held under an agreement that gives you temporary possession or use of property. Rented property does not become Owned by you solely because you possess or use it.

“Borrowed” means received from another person or organization for your use with an expectation of return. Borrowed property remains property of the person or organization that provided it to you.

“Care” means responsibility for safeguarding or maintaining property. Care may exist whether or not you have the right to use the property.

“Custody” means physical possession of property. Custody exists when property has been delivered to you or is otherwise held by you.

“Control” means the authority or practical ability to direct the use, handling, storage, movement, or disposition of property. Control may exist without ownership or physical possession.

“Used in a Business” means employed, intended for employment, or held for employment in connection with a Business. Property may be Used in a Business even if it is not in active use at the time of loss.

“Held for Sale” means kept for transfer to another person or organization in exchange for compensation or other consideration. Property Held for Sale includes property displayed, stored, delivered, or prepared for sale.

“Stock” means merchandise held for sale, materials awaiting use, and property in the process of being manufactured or processed. Stock includes packaging and containers used with that property.

“Inventory” means property held for sale or used in producing property for sale. Inventory includes Stock and supplies maintained for Business operations.

“Supplies” means consumable property used in the conduct of a Business. Supplies include materials kept for cleaning, maintenance, packing, office work, or similar Business purposes.

“Materials” means tangible property intended to be incorporated into, processed with, or consumed in Business operations. Materials include unfinished goods and components.

“Tools” means hand-held or portable implements used to perform work. Tools include specialized implements used in a trade, profession, or occupation.

“Equipment” means machinery, devices, instruments, and other tangible property used to conduct a Business. Equipment includes property used to produce, process, repair, maintain, or transport other property.

“Furniture” means movable items used to furnish, equip, or support a Business location. Furniture includes desks, chairs, cabinets, shelving, and similar items.

“Fixtures” means property attached to a building or structure for Business use. Fixtures remain Fixtures while attached, whether or not they can be removed without damage.

“Improvements” means additions or changes made to property for Business use. Improvements include alterations that increase the usefulness, convenience, or value of the property.

“Betterments” means Improvements made by you to property that you do not own. Betterments include alterations that you are permitted to make under an agreement.

“Records” means written, printed, photographed, or otherwise recorded information maintained for Business purposes. Records include books, files, drawings, plans, manuscripts, and similar property.

“Electronic Data” means information, facts, concepts, or instructions stored, created, used, transmitted, or processed by electronic or computerized means. Electronic Data includes information recorded on media or stored within a computer system.

“Computer” means electronic equipment capable of receiving, processing, storing, retrieving, or transmitting information. Computer includes related input, output, storage, and communication devices.

“Money” means currency, coins, bank notes, and similar circulating media of exchange. Money includes property used as a medium of exchange whether or not issued by a governmental authority.

“Securities” means negotiable or nonnegotiable instruments representing money, property, or financial rights. Securities include evidences of debt, ownership interests, and similar financial instruments.

“Premises” means the location where property is situated. Premises includes land, buildings, structures, and areas used in connection with that location.

“Residence Premises” means the premises where you reside. Residence Premises includes areas used by you in connection with your residence.

“Business Premises” means premises used in connection with a Business. Business Premises includes locations where Business Property is stored, displayed, repaired, produced, sold, or otherwise used.

“Temporary Location” means a location where Business Property is situated for a limited Business purpose. Temporary Location includes a location used for repair, demonstration, delivery, installation, storage, or similar activity.

“Located At” means physically situated on or within premises. Property is Located At premises when it is in a building, structure, vehicle, storage area, or other place associated with those premises.

“Away From” means not Located At the Residence Premises. Property remains Away From the Residence Premises while it is being transported or temporarily kept elsewhere.

“Loss” means accidental physical loss of or physical damage to tangible property. Loss includes Damage, Destruction, and Theft when caused by a covered cause of loss.

“Direct Physical Loss” means a distinct and demonstrable physical alteration, destruction, or disappearance of tangible property. Direct Physical Loss does not include a reduction in value that is not caused by physical alteration, destruction, or disappearance.

“Damage” means physical injury to property that impairs its condition, usefulness, or value. Damage includes breakage, deterioration, contamination, or other physical harm.

“Destruction” means physical loss of property that prevents its repair, restoration, or continued use. Destruction includes property that no longer exists in a usable physical form.

“Theft” means the unlawful taking of property by a person or organization without your consent. Theft includes an attempt to unlawfully take property when that attempt causes physical loss or physical damage.

“Occurrence” means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An Occurrence may result in Loss affecting Business Property.

“Employee” means a person performing services for you in connection with a Business. Employee includes a person whose services are directed or controlled by you.

“Customer” means a person or organization that obtains or seeks goods, services, or other Business benefits from you. Customer includes a person or organization acting through an authorized representative.
