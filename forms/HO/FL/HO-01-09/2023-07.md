# HO 01 09 Florida Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2023-07)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

**T.1** This "endorsement" is attached to and forms part of your "policy". It applies to property and interests insured under your policy in Florida.

**T.2** This endorsement changes your policy only to the extent that its terms differ from the terms of your policy. All terms of your policy that are not changed by this endorsement remain applicable.

**T.3** If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy, this endorsement controls. If a provision can be read consistently with your policy, both provisions apply.

**T.4** We will apply this endorsement according to its plain meaning. We will read the provisions of this endorsement together with the applicable provisions of your policy.

**T.5** This endorsement does not provide coverage unless coverage is provided by its terms or by your policy as changed by this endorsement. This endorsement does not broaden coverage beyond its express terms.

**T.6** A limitation, condition, exclusion, or duty in your policy remains applicable unless this endorsement expressly changes that provision. An insured must comply with each applicable duty imposed by this endorsement and your policy.

**T.7** Terms used in this endorsement have the meanings assigned in your policy unless this endorsement assigns a different meaning. A term assigned a different meaning by this endorsement applies only as necessary to give effect to this endorsement.

**T.8** We may enforce any applicable condition of your policy that is not inconsistent with this endorsement. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision.

**T.9** No action by us changes this endorsement unless we agree to the change in writing. No action by an insured changes a duty or condition imposed by this endorsement.

**T.10** This endorsement applies subject to applicable Florida law. A provision of this endorsement that is inconsistent with applicable law is amended only to the extent required by that law.

**T.11** If a provision of this endorsement is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. We will apply the enforceable provisions to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

**T.12** The "AOP deductible floor" applies where it is applicable under this endorsement and your policy. We will determine the applicable deductible in accordance with the terms that control under this endorsement.

**T.13** The "sworn proof deadline" applies to an insured's duty to provide a sworn proof of loss. An insured must satisfy that duty as required by this endorsement and your policy.

**T.14** The "insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement" applies where replacement cost settlement is available. We will determine settlement in accordance with the applicable terms of this endorsement and your policy.

**T.15** We will adjust a claim under the coverage, limitations, conditions, and exclusions that apply to the loss. An insured must provide information and cooperate with us as required by the applicable policy terms.

**T.16** We do not cover a loss that is excluded by this endorsement or by your policy, unless this endorsement expressly restores coverage. A coverage grant remains subject to all applicable limitations and conditions.

**T.17** A duty imposed on an insured applies to each insured seeking coverage under your policy. Compliance by an insured does not excuse noncompliance by another insured where the duty is separately applicable.

**T.18** We may rely on information provided by an insured in administering this endorsement. An insured must promptly provide corrected information when the insured becomes aware that previously provided information is inaccurate or incomplete.

**T.19** The use of a term in the singular includes the plural when the context requires. The use of a term in the plural includes the singular when the context requires.

**T.20** The captions, arrangement, and formatting of this endorsement do not alter its meaning. The provisions of this endorsement control according to their terms.

**T.21** This endorsement becomes part of your policy when it is made effective. Its provisions apply for as long as they remain part of your policy.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** The minimum "Windstorm and Hail Deductible" is two percent. The deductible applies as stated in this endorsement.

**T.2** The maximum "Windstorm and Hail Deductible" is fifteen percent. The deductible may not exceed that amount.

**T.3** We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. This deductible applies whether windstorm, hail, or both cause the loss.

**T.4** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to property covered under this policy. It applies only to loss for which coverage is otherwise provided.

**T.5** We will subtract the Windstorm and Hail Deductible from the amount of covered loss. We will not pay for loss within the deductible.

**T.6** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies separately from any other deductible that may apply. We will apply the deductible that is applicable to the covered loss.

**T.7** A "windstorm" means wind that causes direct physical loss to covered property. Windstorm includes wind-driven rain only when the rain enters through an opening caused by wind.

**T.8** "Hail" means frozen precipitation that causes direct physical loss to covered property. Hail does not include loss caused only by rain or surface water.

**T.9** A "covered windstorm or hail loss" means direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail for which coverage is provided. The term includes resulting loss when the resulting loss is covered.

**T.10** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to all covered property damaged by a covered windstorm or hail loss. We will determine the deductible from the policy limits applicable to the damaged property.

**T.11** If a covered windstorm or hail loss affects a dwelling, we will apply the deductible to the dwelling loss. We will also apply the deductible to covered property associated with that dwelling loss.

**T.12** If a covered windstorm or hail loss affects personal property, we will apply the deductible to the covered personal property loss. This applies whether the personal property is located at the residence premises or elsewhere.

**T.13** If a covered windstorm or hail loss affects structures, we will apply the deductible to covered structures loss. The deductible applies without regard to the location of the damaged structure.

**T.14** We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible before determining the amount payable for covered loss. The deductible does not increase any applicable limit of liability.

**T.15** The deductible applies to the amount of covered loss before any payment is made. The deductible applies even when the covered loss includes debris removal or reasonable repairs.

**T.16** We will not apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to loss that is not caused by windstorm or hail. Another applicable deductible may apply to that loss.

**T.17** We will determine the cause of loss from the facts of the claim. An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation of the cause of loss.

**T.18** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a covered windstorm or hail loss. Reasonable measures taken to protect the property do not alter the applicable deductible.

**T.19** An insured must retain damaged property when reasonably possible. We may inspect damaged property before it is repaired, removed, or discarded.

**T.20** An insured must provide records, estimates, invoices, photographs, and other information reasonably requested by us. The information must relate to the claimed windstorm or hail loss.

**T.21** An insured must show the damaged property to us as often as reasonably required. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and verify the loss.

**T.22** An insured must submit to examination under oath when reasonably requested by us. An insured must answer questions concerning the claimed loss truthfully.

**T.23** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when reasonably requested by us. The statement must identify the property claimed to be damaged.

**T.24** We may investigate a claim before determining whether the Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies. Our investigation does not waive any policy condition, exclusion, or limitation.

**T.25** We may request information concerning weather conditions affecting the residence premises. We may consider available evidence concerning the reported event.

**T.26** We may inspect repairs, replacements, and materials associated with the claimed loss. An insured must preserve access to the property for that purpose.

**T.27** We will determine the amount of covered loss subject to all applicable policy terms. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible will then be applied to that amount.

**T.28** We do not cover any amount of a covered windstorm or hail loss that does not exceed the deductible. This provision applies regardless of the type of covered property damaged.

**T.29** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail when the loss is excluded under this policy. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not create coverage.

**T.30** We do not cover loss caused by wear, tear, deterioration, faulty maintenance, or defective materials. This exclusion applies even if windstorm or hail contributes to the claimed damage only as provided by this policy.

**T.31** We do not cover loss caused by seepage, leakage, or repeated exposure to moisture. Coverage may apply when direct physical loss is caused by a covered windstorm or hail loss.

**T.32** We do not cover loss caused solely by rain entering property without an opening caused by wind. An insured must establish that an opening was caused by wind when coverage depends upon that fact.

**T.33** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, storm surge, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water. This exclusion applies whether or not windstorm or hail occurs at the same time.

**T.34** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. This exclusion applies even when earth movement follows windstorm or hail.

**T.35** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance or law except as otherwise provided by this policy. The deductible applies to covered ordinance or law loss arising from windstorm or hail.

**T.36** We will not reduce the deductible because repairs are delayed. Delay in repair does not change the cause of loss or the amount of the deductible.

**T.37** We will not reduce the deductible because damaged property is repaired, replaced, or not repaired. The deductible applies to the covered loss determined by us.

**T.38** We will not reduce the deductible because an insured obtains estimates that differ from our estimate. We may evaluate all available information in determining the covered loss.

**T.39** We will not reduce the deductible because another person may be responsible for the loss. Our rights of recovery, if any, remain unchanged.

**T.40** Payment by us for a covered windstorm or hail loss does not waive the deductible for later loss. We will apply the deductible to loss as required by this endorsement.

**T.41** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to loss occurring during the policy period. Loss outside the policy period is not covered unless coverage is otherwise provided.

**T.42** The deductible applicable to a claim is determined from the policy in effect when the covered loss occurs. Changes made after the loss do not alter that deductible.

**T.43** An insured must give prompt notice of a windstorm or hail loss. Notice must include information reasonably sufficient to identify the property and claimed damage.

**T.44** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage to covered property. We do not cover avoidable increase in loss resulting from failure to protect the property.

**T.45** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair and mitigation expenses. An insured must provide that record to us upon request.

**T.46** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent any material fact relating to a claim. Concealment or misrepresentation may affect coverage as provided by this policy.

**T.47** An insured must not dispose of damaged property before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. This duty applies unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property.

**T.48** We may make payment for covered loss subject to the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. Payment does not constitute admission of coverage for any other claimed damage.

**T.49** We may deny payment for loss that is not covered under this policy. Application of the deductible does not limit our right to deny uncovered loss.

**T.50** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to direct physical loss only. It does not apply to amounts that are not covered by this policy.

**T.51** The deductible applies whether the claimed damage is visible or discovered after the windstorm or hail event. An insured must establish that the damage resulted from a covered windstorm or hail loss.

**T.52** The deductible applies to covered loss resulting from wind-driven objects. Coverage applies only when the loss is otherwise covered under this policy.

**T.53** The deductible applies to covered loss resulting from collapse caused by windstorm or hail. Coverage applies only when collapse is covered under this policy.

**T.54** All policy terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations remain applicable. This endorsement changes the policy only as stated in this section.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** "Named Storm" means a storm to which a governmental weather authority assigns a name. A Named Storm includes the weather conditions associated with that storm.

**T.2** "Named Storm Designation" means the designation issued by a governmental weather authority identifying a Named Storm. A Named Storm Designation is effective when issued by that authority.

**T.3** "Named Storm Period" means the period during which a Named Storm Designation is in effect. The Named Storm Period begins when the Named Storm Designation is issued.

**T.4** The Named Storm Period continues for seventy-two hours after a Named Storm Designation ends. The end of a Named Storm Designation is determined by the governmental weather authority that issued it.

**T.5** A Named Storm Designation applies only to the Named Storm identified by the governmental weather authority. A designation for a different Named Storm does not alter the Named Storm Period for another Named Storm.

**T.6** We may rely upon public records issued by a governmental weather authority to determine whether a Named Storm Designation was in effect. Such records may include notices, advisories, bulletins, reports, and similar communications.

**T.7** The existence of a Named Storm Period does not depend upon whether you receive notice of the Named Storm Designation. Your lack of knowledge of a Named Storm Designation does not change the Named Storm Period.

**T.8** We determine whether a loss occurred during a Named Storm Period from the available facts. The date you report a loss does not determine whether the loss occurred during a Named Storm Period.

**T.9** We may consider the time at which direct physical loss began in determining whether the loss occurred during a Named Storm Period. We may also consider weather records and other reliable information.

**T.10** Damage discovered after a Named Storm Period does not establish that the damage occurred after that Named Storm Period. Damage discovered during a Named Storm Period does not establish that the damage occurred during that Named Storm Period.

**T.11** A loss may result from conditions that existed before a Named Storm Period. We may determine the cause and timing of loss from the evidence available to us.

**T.12** A later Named Storm Designation does not change the timing of loss caused by an earlier event. Each loss is evaluated according to its own facts.

**T.13** The issuance of a Named Storm Designation does not establish that covered loss has occurred. You must establish covered loss in accordance with the terms of this policy.

**T.14** The end of a Named Storm Designation does not establish that all weather conditions associated with the Named Storm have ended. We determine coverage based upon the applicable policy terms and the facts of the loss.

**T.15** A correction, revision, or withdrawal issued by a governmental weather authority may affect our determination of the Named Storm Period. We may use the corrected, revised, or withdrawn information in making that determination.

**T.16** We may obtain information from governmental weather authorities, public records, and other reliable sources. We may use that information to evaluate a claim.

**T.17** You must cooperate with us in providing information relevant to the timing and cause of loss. You must provide information that you have concerning weather conditions and property damage.

**T.18** You must protect property from further damage after a loss. Your duty to protect property applies whether or not a Named Storm Period is in effect.

**T.19** You must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. You must not discard property that may assist us in determining the cause or timing of loss.

**T.20** You must permit us to inspect property and records relevant to a claim. You must provide access that is reasonably necessary for our investigation.

**T.21** You must provide truthful information concerning a claim. Information concerning the time, cause, and extent of damage must be accurate to the best of your knowledge.

**T.22** A Named Storm Period does not create coverage where coverage does not otherwise apply. A Named Storm Period does not increase any coverage provided by this policy.

**T.23** A Named Storm Period does not alter any exclusion, limitation, condition, or deductible that otherwise applies. All applicable policy terms remain in effect.

**T.24** We do not cover loss merely because a Named Storm Designation was in effect. Coverage depends upon the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations of this policy.

**T.25** Our investigation of a claim during a Named Storm Period does not waive any policy provision. Our payment of any amount does not waive any policy provision.

**T.26** Our request for information does not admit coverage or liability. Our inspection of property does not admit coverage or liability.

**T.27** We may reserve our rights while investigating a claim. A reservation of rights does not affect your duties under this policy.

**T.28** Any dispute concerning the existence or timing of a Named Storm Period will be determined from reliable evidence. We may consider evidence submitted by you in making that determination.

**T.29** You may submit information that you believe is relevant to our determination. We will consider relevant information received during our claim investigation.

**T.30** We may request records concerning repairs, inspections, maintenance, and weather-related damage. You must provide requested records that are available to you.

**T.31** A dispute concerning a Named Storm Period does not relieve you of your duties after loss. You must continue to comply with all applicable policy conditions.

**T.32** If any provision of this section is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect. The invalid provision will be enforced to the extent permitted by law.

**T.33** This section applies to all claims involving loss that may have occurred during a Named Storm Period. This section applies whether the claim is reported during or after the Named Storm Period.

## T.4 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal

**T.1** This provision applies to the “policy” and to each “insured”. We may cancel the policy only as permitted by applicable law and this provision.

**T.2** A “cancellation” ends the policy before its stated expiration. Cancellation does not affect our rights concerning any loss that occurred before cancellation becomes effective.

**T.3** A “nonrenewal” means that we elect not to continue the policy at the end of its current policy period. Nonrenewal does not constitute cancellation.

**T.4** If we cancel the policy for nonpayment of “premium”, we will mail or deliver notice of cancellation at least ten days before the cancellation becomes effective. The notice will state the effective date of cancellation.

**T.5** If we cancel the policy for a reason other than nonpayment of premium, we will mail or deliver notice of cancellation at least forty-five days before the cancellation becomes effective. The notice will state the reason for cancellation.

**T.6** If we elect not to renew the policy, we will mail or deliver notice of nonrenewal at least one hundred thirty-five days before the end of the current policy period. The notice will state the date upon which coverage ends.

**T.7** We may cancel the policy for nonpayment of premium. Nonpayment includes failure to pay any premium due under the policy or under an applicable payment arrangement.

**T.8** We may cancel the policy if you obtained the policy through material misrepresentation, omission, concealment, or fraud. We may exercise this right only as permitted by applicable law.

**T.9** We may cancel the policy if there has been a material change in the risk covered by the policy. A change in risk includes a condition that materially increases the likelihood or severity of loss.

**T.10** We may cancel the policy if you fail to comply with a material policy condition. We will state the applicable reason in the notice of cancellation.

**T.11** We may cancel the policy if the property insured under the policy no longer meets our underwriting requirements. Our underwriting requirements may concern occupancy, maintenance, condition, use, or exposure to loss.

**T.12** We may cancel the policy if the property becomes vacant or unoccupied in a manner that materially affects the risk. We may also cancel if the property is used for a purpose not disclosed to us.

**T.13** We may cancel the policy if an insured intentionally causes or attempts to cause a loss. This right applies whether the conduct is directed toward insured property or another person.

**T.14** We may cancel the policy if an insured commits fraud relating to the policy, a claim, or the property insured. Fraud includes a material false statement made to obtain coverage or benefits.

**T.15** We may cancel the policy if an insured refuses to permit a reasonable inspection of the property. An insured must cooperate with an inspection requested by us in connection with underwriting or claim handling.

**T.16** We may cancel the policy if an insured fails to provide information reasonably requested for underwriting. An insured must provide information that is complete and accurate to the best of that insured's knowledge.

**T.17** We may cancel the policy if the property is transferred without our consent when consent is required by the policy. A transfer includes a change in ownership or a change in the right to possess the property.

**T.18** We may cancel the policy if the property is substantially altered in a manner that materially affects the risk. An insured must promptly notify us of a material alteration of the property.

**T.19** We may cancel the policy if the property is damaged and is not repaired or protected in a manner reasonably necessary to prevent further loss. An insured must use reasonable means to preserve insured property after a loss.

**T.20** We may cancel the policy if required by applicable law, regulation, court order, or governmental action. Our notice will identify the basis for cancellation when disclosure is required.

**T.21** A notice of cancellation or nonrenewal may be mailed to you at your last known mailing address. Mailing to that address constitutes delivery when permitted by applicable law.

**T.22** We may deliver a notice of cancellation or nonrenewal by electronic means if you have consented to electronic delivery as permitted by applicable law. Electronic delivery does not limit any delivery method otherwise available to us.

**T.23** A notice may be delivered to the “named insured” shown in the policy. Notice to a named insured is notice to all insureds to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**T.24** A notice of cancellation will identify the policy and the effective date of cancellation. The notice will also state any premium action that applies following cancellation.

**T.25** A notice of nonrenewal will identify the policy and the end of the current policy period. The notice may state available options for replacement coverage.

**T.26** Cancellation becomes effective on the date stated in our notice. Coverage will not apply to loss occurring after cancellation becomes effective.

**T.27** Nonrenewal becomes effective when the current policy period ends. Coverage will not continue beyond that time unless we issue replacement coverage or otherwise agree in writing.

**T.28** If we cancel the policy, we will return any unearned premium due to you. Return of unearned premium does not delay or invalidate cancellation.

**T.29** If the policy is canceled because of nonpayment of premium, any return premium will be calculated in accordance with applicable law. We may apply any return premium to an amount you owe us when permitted by applicable law.

**T.30** If you cancel the policy, any return premium will be calculated in accordance with the policy terms and applicable law. Your cancellation does not affect any premium owed for coverage provided before cancellation becomes effective.

**T.31** You may request cancellation of the policy by giving us notice. Your request must identify the policy and the date upon which you want cancellation to become effective.

**T.32** We may require evidence that a person requesting cancellation has authority to act for an insured. We may decline a cancellation request that is incomplete or not authorized.

**T.33** An insured must notify us promptly of a change in mailing address. We are not responsible for delayed receipt of notice caused by your failure to provide a current mailing address.

**T.34** An insured must notify us promptly of a change in occupancy, ownership, or use of the property. Notice of a change does not obligate us to continue coverage.

**T.35** An insured must pay premium when due. Payment to an agent does not constitute payment to us unless the agent is authorized to receive the payment.

**T.36** We may accept a late premium payment without reinstating coverage. Acceptance of payment does not waive cancellation unless we expressly agree in writing.

**T.37** If we reinstate coverage after cancellation, reinstatement will be subject to the terms we state. A reinstatement may be effective only from the date we specify.

**T.38** Reinstatement does not provide coverage for a loss occurring during a period when the policy was not in force. We may require information concerning loss or damage before we reinstate coverage.

**T.39** We may offer renewal coverage on terms that differ from the current policy terms. An offer of renewal is not a waiver of our right to nonrenew the policy.

**T.40** We may condition renewal on payment of the required premium and compliance with underwriting requirements. An insured must provide requested renewal information promptly.

**T.41** We may decline to renew the policy if the property does not meet our renewal underwriting requirements. Our decision will be made in accordance with applicable law.

**T.42** We may decline to renew the policy if loss experience, claims activity, or exposure information materially affects the risk. We may also consider information obtained through lawful underwriting sources.

**T.43** We may decline to renew the policy if an insured has made material misrepresentations or committed fraud. This provision applies to information provided at application, during the policy period, or in connection with a claim.

**T.44** We may decline to renew the policy if an insured fails to cooperate with a reasonable inspection or underwriting request. An insured must permit access to the property when reasonably requested by us.

**T.45** We may withdraw an offer of renewal if information received before the renewal effective date materially changes the risk. We will provide notice when required by applicable law.

**T.46** A cancellation or nonrenewal notice may be signed by an authorized representative of us. A signature may be printed, reproduced, or electronic when permitted by applicable law.

**T.47** An error in the address shown in a notice does not invalidate the notice if the error resulted from information supplied by you. We will use the address maintained in our records unless we receive a corrected address.

**T.48** Our failure to exercise a right of cancellation or nonrenewal does not waive that right for a later occurrence. Any waiver must be made by us in writing.

**T.49** Our acceptance of premium does not waive a ground for cancellation or nonrenewal unless we expressly agree in writing. We may retain premium for coverage provided before cancellation or nonrenewal becomes effective.

**T.50** A cancellation or nonrenewal decision applies only to the policy identified in the notice. It does not determine coverage under any other policy issued by us.

**T.51** If applicable law requires a different notice method, notice content, or effective date, we will comply with that requirement. This provision will be applied to conform to applicable law.

**T.52** If any part of this provision is held invalid, the remaining part will remain in effect to the extent permitted by applicable law. Any invalid part will be interpreted to provide the closest lawful effect.

**T.53** An insured must review each notice received from us concerning cancellation or nonrenewal. An insured must promptly notify us if a notice appears to contain incorrect policy information.

**T.54** An insured must continue to comply with policy duties until cancellation or nonrenewal becomes effective. Those duties include duties relating to preservation of property and cooperation concerning a claim.

**T.55** Cancellation or nonrenewal does not relieve an insured of duties concerning a loss that occurred while coverage applied. We retain all rights relating to investigation, adjustment, and payment of that loss.

**T.56** Cancellation or nonrenewal does not create coverage for property, persons, causes of loss, or claims not otherwise covered. All policy terms remain applicable to covered loss occurring before coverage ends.

**T.57** We may provide information concerning cancellation or nonrenewal to a person authorized by an insured or by applicable law. We may also provide information to a mortgagee or other interested party when required by the policy or applicable law.

**T.58** This provision applies regardless of any request by an insured to continue coverage after cancellation or nonrenewal becomes effective. Continued occupancy, possession, or use of the property does not continue the policy.

## T.5 — Claims Handling

**T.1** An insured must give us prompt notice of a "claim." Notice must state the known facts concerning the loss.

**T.2** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property.

**T.3** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses. An insured must provide that record to us when requested.

**T.4** An insured must allow us to inspect the damaged property. An insured must preserve the property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**T.5** We will acknowledge receipt of a claim within fourteen days. Our acknowledgement may request information needed to evaluate the claim.

**T.6** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation of a claim. Cooperation includes providing information that is material to the claim.

**T.7** An insured must provide a signed, sworn "proof of loss" when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information known to the insured concerning the loss.

**T.8** An insured must provide records, receipts, invoices, estimates, photographs, and other documents that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to make copies of the materials provided.

**T.9** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require it. An insured must answer material questions truthfully.

**T.10** An insured must produce for examination books, records, and documents that relate to the claim. An insured must authorize us to obtain records that are material to the claim when permitted by law.

**T.11** An insured must identify damaged property. An insured must provide available information concerning the age, condition, ownership, and value of the property.
