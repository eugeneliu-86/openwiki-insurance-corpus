# HO 01 09 Florida Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2019-01)
> SUPERSEDED by HO 01 09 Florida Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2023-07) for policies effective on or after 2023-07-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.


## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

**T.1** This "endorsement" forms part of your "policy." We apply this endorsement only to property and interests to which your policy applies.

**T.2** This endorsement changes your policy only to the extent that its provisions expressly differ from provisions otherwise applicable to your policy. All provisions not changed by this endorsement remain in effect.

**T.3** If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy, this endorsement controls. If provisions can reasonably be read together, we will apply them together.

**T.4** We will construe the terms of this endorsement according to their plain meaning. A term used in this endorsement has the meaning assigned to it by this endorsement when a different meaning would otherwise apply.

**T.5** Words and phrases not defined in this endorsement retain the meaning applicable under your policy. A word used in the singular includes the plural when the context requires.

**T.6** References to an "insured" include only persons qualifying as insureds under your policy. Nothing in this endorsement expands who is an insured unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

**T.7** This endorsement applies only while your policy is in force. Termination, cancellation, nonrenewal, or expiration of your policy also ends the effect of this endorsement.

**T.8** An insured must comply with all duties that remain applicable under your policy. Compliance with a duty stated in this endorsement does not excuse compliance with any other applicable duty.

**T.9** We will determine coverage under this endorsement from the facts existing at the time of loss. Later changes in ownership, occupancy, use, or condition do not alter coverage for that loss unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

**T.10** This endorsement does not create coverage for property not otherwise within the scope of your policy. It does not create coverage for loss caused by a peril excluded by your policy unless this endorsement expressly provides otherwise.

**T.11** We do not cover loss to property unless the property is covered property under your policy or this endorsement expressly makes it covered property. We do not cover an interest that you do not have at the time of loss.

**T.12** Any condition applicable to coverage under this endorsement applies to each insured seeking coverage. An insured must provide information and cooperation reasonably required for us to determine coverage.

**T.13** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to this endorsement, the property involved, or a claimed loss. We may apply the consequences provided by your policy for concealment, fraud, or material misrepresentation.

**T.14** The "AOP deductible floor" applies only as provided by this endorsement and your policy. No other provision changes its application unless that provision expressly states that it controls.

**T.15** The "sworn proof deadline" applies to an insured’s duty to provide a sworn proof of loss. An insured must satisfy that duty in the manner required by the applicable policy provisions.

**T.16** The "insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement" applies when replacement cost settlement is available. We will determine eligibility for that settlement under the applicable terms of your policy and this endorsement.

**T.17** We will not infer a waiver of any policy term from our investigation, inspection, adjustment, payment, or communication. A waiver is effective only when we clearly state it in writing.

**T.18** Our exercise of a right under this endorsement does not prevent us from exercising any other right available under your policy. An insured’s performance of a duty does not waive any right available to us.

**T.19** If any provision of this endorsement is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. We will apply the enforceable provisions in a manner consistent with the scope of this endorsement.

**T.20** This endorsement does not alter any right or remedy available to us unless it expressly states otherwise. It does not impose a duty on us beyond the duties expressly assumed under your policy and this endorsement.

**T.21** We will apply this endorsement consistently with applicable law. If applicable law requires a construction different from the construction stated in this endorsement, we will apply the required construction.

**T.22** The terms of this endorsement apply to all claims presented under it. An insured seeking coverage must establish that the claimed loss falls within the coverage provided by your policy as amended by this endorsement.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** We apply the "Windstorm and Hail Deductible" to direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail. This deductible applies to covered property only when the loss is otherwise covered under this policy.

**T.2** The minimum windstorm and hail deductible percentage is two percent. We will not apply a percentage below that amount when this deductible applies.

**T.3** The maximum windstorm and hail deductible percentage is ten percent. We will not apply a percentage above that amount when this deductible applies.

**T.4** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies separately from any other deductible that may apply to a covered loss. We will subtract only the deductible that applies under the terms of this provision.

**T.5** A "Windstorm or Hail Loss" means direct physical loss caused by wind, wind-driven material, or hail. The loss must result from an event that occurs during the policy period.

**T.6** We determine whether a Windstorm or Hail Loss has occurred from the facts available to us. Our determination may include weather information, inspection findings, statements, and other reliable evidence.

**T.7** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies whether windstorm or hail is the sole cause of loss or contributes with another cause of loss. We apply the deductible when windstorm or hail directly causes covered physical damage.

**T.8** We do not cover loss that is excluded under this policy. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not create coverage for excluded loss.

**T.9** The deductible applies to the amount of covered loss before we make payment. We will not pay the portion of covered loss that is within the applicable deductible.

**T.10** A "Deductible Basis" means the value or limit used to calculate the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. We determine the Deductible Basis under the applicable property coverage.

**T.11** We calculate the deductible from the Deductible Basis that applies to the damaged property. The deductible calculation is subject to the policy terms applicable to that property.

**T.12** When more than one coverage applies to a Windstorm or Hail Loss, we determine the deductible separately for each coverage unless the policy provides otherwise. We do not combine coverage limits solely to calculate a deductible.

**T.13** When covered property is subject to different limits, we apply the deductible using the limit applicable to the damaged property. We do not use an unrelated coverage limit to calculate the deductible.

**T.14** A "Covered Location" means a location described in the policy declarations for covered property. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies only to a Windstorm or Hail Loss at a Covered Location.

**T.15** The deductible applies to covered building property and covered personal property when windstorm or hail causes direct physical loss to that property. The deductible does not change the coverage that applies to the property.

**T.16** We apply the deductible before determining the amount payable for loss settlement. We do not pay for repair, replacement, or other covered loss until the applicable deductible has been satisfied.

**T.17** A "Loss Adjustment" means our evaluation of the cause, extent, and value of a claimed loss. We may complete a Loss Adjustment before determining the deductible that applies.

**T.18** An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation of a claimed Windstorm or Hail Loss. An insured must provide information that we reasonably request to determine the applicable deductible.

**T.19** An insured must protect damaged property from further loss after a Windstorm or Hail Loss. Reasonable measures to protect property do not eliminate the applicable deductible.

**T.20** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. We may use the inspection to determine whether windstorm or hail caused the claimed damage.

**T.21** An insured must provide records, photographs, receipts, estimates, and other information that support a claim when we reasonably request them. The information must be sufficient for us to evaluate the claimed loss.

**T.22** We may inspect property before or after repairs are made. Repairs made before our inspection do not prevent us from applying the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.23** We may request proof of loss for a claimed Windstorm or Hail Loss. The proof of loss must identify the damaged property and describe the claimed damage.

**T.24** A "Covered Loss" means loss for which coverage is provided after all policy conditions, limitations, and exclusions are applied. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies only to a Covered Loss.

**T.25** We do not cover the cost to repair property that was not physically damaged by windstorm or hail. The deductible does not apply to costs that are not covered loss.

**T.26** We do not cover deterioration, wear and tear, settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion unless coverage is otherwise provided by this policy. Evidence of those conditions may be considered during our Loss Adjustment.

**T.27** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective maintenance, repair, construction, renovation, or design unless coverage is otherwise provided by this policy. Windstorm or hail damage must be distinguished from excluded damage.

**T.28** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage, leakage, or water damage unless coverage is otherwise provided by this policy. The presence of windstorm or hail does not make excluded water damage covered.

**T.29** We determine the portion of damage caused by windstorm or hail from the evidence available to us. We may distinguish covered damage from damage caused by excluded causes.

**T.30** A "Claimed Loss" means loss for which an insured seeks payment under this policy. We may adjust a Claimed Loss in whole or in part.

**T.31** The deductible applies to the adjusted amount of a Covered Loss. We do not pay any amount for a Covered Loss that does not exceed the deductible.

**T.32** A "Deductible Amount" means the portion of a Covered Loss that you are responsible for before we make payment. We subtract the Deductible Amount from the covered amount of loss.

**T.33** We do not pay the Deductible Amount under any coverage provided by this policy. Payment by another person or organization does not change our application of the deductible.

**T.34** The deductible applies without regard to the number of damaged items included in a Claimed Loss. We do not apply a separate deductible to each item of damaged property unless the policy requires it.

**T.35** The deductible applies to covered damage from the same windstorm or hail event as a single loss. We determine whether damage resulted from the same event based on the facts available to us.

**T.36** Damage that results from separate windstorm or hail events may be adjusted as separate losses. We apply the deductible to each separate Covered Loss.

**T.37** We may rely on reports from weather services, governmental authorities, contractors, engineers, inspectors, and other qualified sources. Such reports do not prevent us from conducting our own investigation.

**T.38** We may require examination of damaged property by a person we select. An insured must permit reasonable access to the property for that examination.

**T.39** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold material facts relating to a Claimed Loss. A breach of this duty may affect coverage as provided by this policy.

**T.40** We may request that an insured submit to examination under oath when permitted by this policy. An insured must produce requested records that relate to the Claimed Loss.

**T.41** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies before we determine any amount payable for additional living expense, fair rental value, or other indirect loss, if such coverage applies. We do not duplicate payment for the deductible under separate coverages.

**T.42** We determine payment for a Covered Loss after applying all applicable limits, conditions, exclusions, and deductibles. The deductible does not increase any applicable limit of liability.

**T.43** A "Limit of Liability" means the most we will pay for covered loss under an applicable coverage. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible is not part of the Limit of Liability.

**T.44** We may settle a Covered Loss by repair, replacement, payment, or another method authorized by this policy. The deductible applies regardless of the method of settlement.

**T.45** An insured must not abandon property to us. Our payment of a Covered Loss does not transfer ownership of damaged property to us unless we agree otherwise.

**T.46** We may take possession of damaged property for inspection, evaluation, or salvage after providing notice when reasonably practical. Our possession of property does not waive the deductible.

**T.47** If we make payment for a Covered Loss, we may pursue recovery from a person or organization responsible for the loss. Any recovery is subject to the rights and conditions provided by this policy.

**T.48** An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our right of recovery. This duty applies whether or not the Windstorm and Hail Deductible has been satisfied.

**T.49** A dispute concerning the cause or amount of a Claimed Loss does not suspend application of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. We apply the deductible when covered windstorm or hail damage is established.

**T.50** We may pay an undisputed portion of a Covered Loss while other portions remain under investigation. Any payment remains subject to the applicable deductible.

**T.51** We do not waive any policy condition, limitation, exclusion, or deductible by investigating a Claimed Loss. A waiver must be made by us in writing.

**T.52** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to every insured seeking payment for a Covered Loss. An insured may not avoid the deductible by assigning an interest in damaged property or a claim.

**T.53** The terms of this provision apply to a Windstorm or Hail Loss occurring during the policy period. Coverage is determined from the policy terms in effect when the loss occurs.

**T.54** If any part of this provision is determined to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law. We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible consistent with applicable law.

## T.2 — Notice of Deductible Change

**T.1** This provision applies when we increase the "Windstorm Deductible". The Windstorm Deductible is the deductible that applies to a loss caused by windstorm.

**T.2** We will provide you written notice at least forty-five days before an increase in the Windstorm Deductible takes effect. The notice will describe the change in the deductible.

**T.3** The notice will identify the insurance to which the increased Windstorm Deductible applies. The notice will identify the property affected by the change.

**T.4** The notice will state the Windstorm Deductible that will apply after the change. The notice will state the effective date of the change.

**T.5** We may provide the notice separately or with other communications concerning your insurance. The notice will be clear and conspicuous.

**T.6** We will send the notice to the mailing address shown in our records. We may send the notice to another address you provide for that purpose.

**T.7** We may deliver the notice electronically when you have agreed to electronic delivery. Electronic delivery will be made to the electronic address shown in our records.

**T.8** Notice sent by mail will be addressed to the named insured. Notice delivered electronically will be directed to the named insured or to a person authorized to receive it.

**T.9** An insured must provide us with a current mailing address. An insured must provide us with a current electronic address when electronic delivery is requested.

**T.10** An insured must promptly notify us of a change in contact information. We may rely on the contact information shown in our records until we receive notice of a change.

**T.11** The notice may describe the Windstorm Deductible by amount, percentage, or other applicable method. The notice will describe the method used to determine the deductible.

**T.12** The increased Windstorm Deductible applies only to a loss for which the Windstorm Deductible applies. The notice does not change the cause of loss requirements applicable to windstorm.

**T.13** The notice does not increase any deductible other than the Windstorm Deductible. Any other deductible remains subject to its applicable terms.

**T.14** The notice does not change the amount of insurance applicable to covered property. The notice does not change the coverage provided for a covered loss.

**T.15** The notice does not create coverage. The notice does not extend coverage beyond that otherwise provided.

**T.16** We may correct a clerical error in the notice. A correction will not change the Windstorm Deductible unless we provide notice of the change as required by this provision.

**T.17** A failure by an insured to respond to the notice does not prevent the change from taking effect. A response by an insured does not change the effective date stated in the notice.

**T.18** An insured may contact us with a question concerning the notice. We will provide available information concerning the increased Windstorm Deductible.

**T.19** An insured must pay any premium due for the insurance as required by its applicable terms. Payment of premium does not change the Windstorm Deductible stated in the notice.

**T.20** The notice does not waive any right we have under the insurance. Our delivery of the notice does not waive any duty of an insured.

**T.21** The increased Windstorm Deductible will apply according to the terms stated in the notice. We will apply the deductible to a covered windstorm loss as required by the insurance.

**T.22** An insured must cooperate with us in identifying the property affected by a windstorm loss. An insured must provide information we reasonably request to apply the Windstorm Deductible.

**T.23** The notice will remain part of the insurance while the increased Windstorm Deductible applies. We may replace the notice with a later notice concerning a subsequent change.

**T.24** We will apply this provision in accordance with applicable law. Any provision of this endorsement that conflicts with applicable law is amended to conform to that law.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** The "Named Storm Period" is a period determined under these provisions. A "named storm" is a storm for which a "named storm designation" is in effect.

**T.2** A named storm designation is an official designation identifying a storm by name. The effective time of a named storm designation is determined from the records of the authority issuing that designation.

**T.3** The Named Storm Period begins when the named storm designation takes effect. The Named Storm Period ends seventy-two hours hours after the named storm designation ends.

**T.4** The beginning and ending of the Named Storm Period are determined by the applicable date and time of the named storm designation. A later correction to an official record will be used to determine the Named Storm Period.

**T.5** We determine whether a loss occurred during the Named Storm Period from the time the damage happened. The time an insured discovers, reports, or submits information concerning the loss does not determine the Named Storm Period.

**T.6** Damage caused by a named storm is treated as occurring during the Named Storm Period when the damage begins during that period. This applies even if the damage continues after the Named Storm Period ends.

**T.7** Damage that begins before the Named Storm Period and continues during that period is not treated as having begun during the Named Storm Period. We will determine the cause and timing of the damage from the available facts.

**T.8** Separate damage caused by separate events will be evaluated separately. Damage is not combined solely because it affects the same property.

**T.9** The presence of a named storm does not establish that the named storm caused a loss. An insured must establish the facts supporting the claimed cause of loss.

**T.10** A Named Storm Period may apply when more than one named storm affects the same area. We will determine the applicable Named Storm Period from the designation associated with the claimed damage.

**T.11** The Named Storm Period applies regardless of whether an insured was aware of the named storm designation. Lack of notice of a designation does not change the beginning or ending of the Named Storm Period.

**T.12** The Named Storm Period applies regardless of whether access to covered property is restricted. Restricted access does not change the timing of a loss.

**T.13** An insured must give prompt notice of a loss. Notice given during or after a Named Storm Period does not, by itself, establish when the loss occurred.

**T.14** An insured must provide records, photographs, reports, and other information reasonably requested to determine the cause and timing of a loss. An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the loss.

**T.15** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. Reasonable protective measures do not change the time at which a loss occurred.

**T.16** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. Disposal or alteration of damaged property may affect our ability to determine the cause and timing of damage.

**T.17** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact concerning a named storm or a loss. This duty applies to information concerning the date, time, cause, and extent of damage.

**T.18** Statements by an insured, a contractor, or another person do not alone determine whether a Named Storm Period applies. We may consider all available evidence in making that determination.

**T.19** Weather reports, forecasts, and public communications may be considered in determining the timing of a loss. Such information does not alone establish coverage for a loss.

**T.20** A Named Storm Period may apply to damage at any covered location affected by a named storm. The location of the named storm designation does not alone establish the cause of damage at a covered location.

**T.21** We do not cover a loss solely because it occurs during a Named Storm Period. A loss occurring during a Named Storm Period remains subject to all applicable terms and conditions.

**T.22** These provisions do not increase or decrease an applicable limit. These provisions do not alter an applicable deductible except as expressly provided.

**T.23** The applicable deductible is determined from the facts governing the loss. The existence of a Named Storm Period does not, by itself, determine which deductible applies.

**T.24** We may investigate a loss before determining whether a Named Storm Period applies. Our investigation does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**T.25** Payment of an undisputed amount does not establish that a Named Storm Period applies to other damage. Payment does not waive our right to evaluate additional information.

**T.26** An insured must provide access to covered property when reasonably requested for inspection. An insured must provide access to records reasonably needed to evaluate the loss.

**T.27** An insured must not make permanent repairs, except reasonable emergency repairs, before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect the damage. An insured must keep records of emergency repairs and related expenses.

**T.28** The Named Storm Period applies based on the occurrence of damage and not on the timing of repairs. Repairs performed during a Named Storm Period do not establish that the damage occurred during that period.

**T.29** The Named Storm Period applies based on the occurrence of damage and not on the timing of payment. Payment issued during a Named Storm Period does not establish that the loss occurred during that period.

**T.30** The Named Storm Period applies based on the occurrence of damage and not on the timing of a claim decision. A claim decision does not change the beginning or ending of the Named Storm Period.

**T.31** We may rely on information from governmental weather authorities and other reliable sources. We may also rely on physical evidence, witness information, and records concerning the loss.

**T.32** An insured must promptly notify us of information that may affect the determination of a Named Storm Period. This includes information concerning the cause, timing, and progression of damage.

**T.33** These provisions apply to each loss for which a named storm may be relevant. The determination of a Named Storm Period is made from the facts applicable to that loss.

## T.4 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal

**T.1** We may cancel this policy only as provided in these provisions. "Cancellation" means termination of this policy before the end of the applicable policy period.

**T.2** We may elect not to renew this policy as provided in these provisions. "Nonrenewal" means our decision not to continue this policy at the end of the applicable policy period.

**T.3** A notice of cancellation or nonrenewal must be in writing. We will deliver or mail the notice to you at your mailing address shown in our records.

**T.4** "Notice" means a written communication stating our action and its effective date. A notice will state the reason for cancellation or nonrenewal when required by applicable law.

**T.5** We may cancel this policy if you do not pay the required premium when due. A notice of cancellation for nonpayment of premium will be mailed or delivered at least ten days before cancellation takes effect.

**T.6** We may cancel this policy for a material misrepresentation, omission, concealment, or incorrect statement made by you in obtaining or continuing this insurance. The misrepresentation, omission, concealment, or incorrect statement must be material to the risk or to our acceptance of the risk.

**T.7** We may cancel this policy if there has been a substantial change in the risk covered by this policy. The change must occur after we issue this policy and must materially affect the insurability of the property.

**T.8** We may cancel this policy if you violate a material term of this policy. The violation must be related to the risk covered by this policy or to your duties under this policy.

**T.9** We may cancel this policy if the property is used in a manner that is materially different from the use disclosed to us. We may also cancel if the property is vacant, unoccupied, or otherwise exposed to a material increase in hazard.

**T.10** We may cancel this policy if you fail to provide information that we reasonably request for underwriting purposes. The requested information must be material to our evaluation of the risk.

**T.11** We may cancel this policy if you refuse to permit a reasonable inspection of the property. An inspection may include an inspection of the dwelling, other structures, protective safeguards, and conditions affecting the risk.

**T.12** We may cancel this policy if a material condition affecting the risk is not corrected after we notify you of that condition. The notice may state the action required to correct the condition.

**T.13** We may cancel this policy if the property is subject to a foreclosure proceeding, receivership, or similar proceeding that materially affects the risk. We may also cancel if ownership or control of the property changes without our consent when consent is required.

**T.14** Except for cancellation because of nonpayment of premium, we will mail or deliver notice of cancellation at least forty-five days before cancellation takes effect. The notice will identify the effective date of cancellation.

**T.15** Cancellation will take effect on the date and at the time stated in the notice. Coverage under this policy ends on that effective date and time.

**T.16** Mailing a notice to your mailing address shown in our records will be sufficient proof that we gave notice. "Mailing Address" means the address most recently provided to us for delivery of policy communications.

**T.17** We may deliver a notice personally to you instead of mailing it. Delivery to an adult residing at the insured location will be treated as delivery to you when permitted by applicable law.

**T.18** If this policy covers more than one named insured, we may send notice to the named insured shown in our records. We may send separate notices when we determine that separate notice is appropriate.

**T.19** We will send any required notice to a mortgagee or other party identified in our records as having an interest in covered property. Such notice will not increase the coverage or rights of that party beyond those provided by this policy.

**T.20** "Mortgagee" means a person or organization shown in our records as having a mortgage interest in covered property. A mortgagee must keep its mailing address current with us.

**T.21** "Lienholder" means a person or organization shown in our records as having a lien interest in covered property. A lienholder must notify us of any change in its mailing address.

**T.22** A notice to a mortgagee or lienholder may be mailed separately from the notice sent to you. Failure of a mortgagee or lienholder to receive notice does not extend coverage for you.

**T.23** If we cancel this policy, we will refund any unearned premium that is due. "Unearned Premium" means premium paid for coverage that does not remain in effect after cancellation.

**T.24** We may return unearned premium by mail, electronic transfer, or another method acceptable to us. Return of unearned premium is not a condition of cancellation.

**T.25** You may cancel this policy by providing us written notice of cancellation. Your notice must state the date on which you want cancellation to take effect.

**T.26** If you cancel this policy, we will refund any unearned premium due to you, subject to any applicable policy terms and applicable law. We may calculate the refund based on the coverage provided through the effective date of cancellation.

**T.27** You may request cancellation through an authorized representative acting on your behalf. "Authorized Representative" means a person or organization authorized by you to communicate cancellation instructions to us.

**T.28** We may require reasonable evidence of an authorized representative's authority before acting on a cancellation request. We are not required to honor instructions that appear incomplete, conflicting, or unauthorized.

**T.29** We may rescind this policy if it was obtained through fraud or material misrepresentation. Rescission is subject to applicable law and may affect coverage from the inception of this policy.

**T.30** "Material Misrepresentation" means a statement, omission, concealment, or representation that affects our decision to issue, continue, rate, or insure the risk. We may rely on information provided by you or on your behalf in evaluating materiality.

**T.31** We may decline to renew this policy for an underwriting reason permitted by applicable law. We may also decline to renew if the risk no longer meets our underwriting requirements.

**T.32** We may decline to renew this policy if you fail to comply with a material policy condition. We may also decline to renew if you fail to cooperate with a reasonable request relating to the risk.

**T.33** We may decline to renew this policy if loss history, claim activity, or property conditions materially affect our willingness to continue coverage. Our decision may be based on information available to us from reliable sources.

**T.34** We may decline to renew this policy if required repairs, maintenance, or protective measures are not completed. We may identify the condition that must be addressed before we will consider continued coverage.

**T.35** We may decline to renew this policy if the property becomes ineligible for the form of coverage provided by this policy. A change in occupancy, use, ownership, construction, or condition may affect eligibility.

**T.36** We will mail or deliver notice of nonrenewal at least one hundred twenty days before the end of the applicable policy period. The notice will state that coverage will not be renewed.

**T.37** A nonrenewal notice will identify the date and time that this policy ends. Coverage ends at the date and time shown in the notice unless we agree otherwise in writing.

**T.38** We may offer renewal on terms that differ from the terms of the expiring policy. A renewal offer may include changes in premium, deductibles, coverage, conditions, or eligibility requirements.

**T.39** A renewal offer does not require us to continue coverage unless you accept the offer and satisfy its conditions. We may withdraw a renewal offer before it becomes effective if permitted by applicable law.

**T.40** If we offer renewal, you must pay the required premium and comply with the stated conditions for renewal. Failure to do so may result in expiration of this policy without renewal.

**T.41** We may send renewal, cancellation, and nonrenewal communications electronically when you have consented to electronic delivery. Electronic delivery will be treated as delivery when permitted by applicable law.

**T.42** You must notify us promptly of a change in your mailing address. We may rely on the mailing address shown in our records until you provide us notice of a change.

**T.43** You must notify us promptly if the named insured changes, dies, or becomes legally incapable of managing property or insurance matters. We may require documentation supporting any requested change to the policy.

**T.44** You must notify us promptly if ownership of the insured location changes. We may cancel or decline to renew if the change materially affects the risk or eligibility for coverage.

**T.45** You must notify us promptly of a material change in occupancy or use of the insured location. A material change includes use for purposes not disclosed to us when we issued or renewed this policy.

**T.46** You must maintain the property in a condition that does not materially increase the risk of loss. We may inspect the property to determine whether conditions affecting the risk exist.

**T.47** You must cooperate with our reasonable requests for information concerning the property, occupancy, ownership, and use of the insured location. Your cooperation must be sufficient to allow us to evaluate eligibility and underwriting conditions.

**T.48** You must provide truthful and complete information in any application, questionnaire, inspection response, or other communication relating to this policy. We may rely on that information in deciding whether to issue, continue, cancel, or renew coverage.

**T.49** A request for information, an inspection, or a discussion of corrective action does not waive our right to cancel or decline to renew. Our failure to act immediately on information available to us does not waive that right.

**T.50** An inspection by us does not constitute a warranty that the property is safe, sound, or compliant with any law or standard. We do not assume responsibility for identifying every condition affecting the property.

**T.51** We may correct a clerical error in a notice if the correction does not prejudice your rights. A corrected notice may be mailed or delivered in the same manner as the original notice.

**T.52** If a notice is returned as undeliverable, we may rely on the mailing address shown in our records unless we have received a more current address from you. You remain responsible for providing current contact information.

**T.53** A notice sent by us is effective even if you do not read it or do not receive it because you failed to provide a current mailing address. This provision applies only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## T.6 — Seacoast Territories

**T.1** This provision applies to a "residence premises" located within a "Seacoast Territory". "Seacoast Territory" means an area subject to coastal wind, storm surge, tidal water, or similar coastal conditions.

"Residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. It includes an employee performing similar duties elsewhere for an insured.

"Residence employee" does not include a person while performing duties related to an insured's business. It does not include a person employed by an insured's tenant.

"Motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land or amphibious vehicle. It includes a trailer designed for use with such a vehicle.

"Motor vehicle" includes vehicles designed for recreational use away from public roads. It does not include vehicles used to assist the handicapped.
