# DP-3 Dwelling Property 3 — Special Form (Edition 2012-11)
> SUPERSEDED by DP-3 Dwelling Property 3 — Special Form (Edition 2020-08) for policies effective on or after 2020-08-01. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## AGR — Agreement

In return for the “premium” and subject to all terms of this “policy,” we will provide the insurance described in this policy. This insurance applies only to an “insured.”

We will pay for direct physical loss to “covered property” when the loss is caused by a covered peril. Payment is subject to the applicable policy provisions.

Coverage applies only while this policy is in force. Coverage applies only to property that qualifies as covered property.

An insured must pay the premium when due. An insured must comply with all duties and conditions imposed by this policy.

An insured must provide complete and accurate information material to the insurance provided by this policy. An insured must promptly notify us of any material change affecting the risk.

We may inspect the property and the information relating to the risk. An inspection does not constitute a warranty that the property is safe or complies with any law, ordinance, or regulation.

We may adjust a loss with an insured or with another person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment by us will satisfy our obligation to the extent of that payment.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must preserve rights of recovery against any person responsible for a loss.

No person may bring legal action against us unless the requirements of this policy have been met. Any legal action must be brought in accordance with applicable law.

This policy may be changed only by our written agreement. No waiver or change may be made by an insured unless we agree to it in writing.

The terms of this policy are severable. If any provision is determined to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

Our rights and duties under this policy may not be transferred without our written consent. An insured may not assign a claim or right of recovery in a manner that prejudices our rights.

This policy is a contract between you and us. The policy language governs the insurance we provide.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined by the cost to repair or replace the property with like kind and quality, less depreciation. We may consider the property's age, condition, and useful life in determining that value. It includes the depreciated value of a used roof covering and does not include the full cost of new roof covering without deduction for depreciation.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality without deduction for depreciation. It includes the reasonable cost of materials and labor necessary to restore covered property. It does not include the cost to improve property beyond its condition immediately before the loss.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, that results in bodily injury or property damage. It includes accidental injury arising from a slip on damaged premises. It does not include injury or damage that is expected or intended by an insured.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside, together with structures and grounds used in connection with that dwelling. It includes a detached garage used in connection with your residence. It does not include land or a structure that is not used in connection with your residence.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or persons under your care. It includes a relative who resides in your household. It does not include a tenant who does not reside in your household.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and premises used by you in connection with the residence premises. It includes land you own where a dwelling is being built for your use as a residence. It does not include premises used principally for business.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for financial gain. It includes the regular sale of goods or services. It does not include activities ordinarily incidental to nonbusiness pursuits.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or terrain and includes machinery or equipment attached to that vehicle. It includes a car, truck, or trailer designed for transportation. It does not include a child's toy vehicle that is not designed for transportation.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are directly related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises or the performance of household or domestic services. It includes a gardener employed to maintain the grounds of the residence premises. It does not include a person employed solely in connection with business conducted by an insured.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death. It includes required care and loss of services resulting from bodily harm. It does not include emotional distress that does not arise from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to or destruction of tangible property, including resulting loss of use of that property. It also includes loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured. It does not include loss involving property that has no tangible form.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unexpected, and unintended. It includes damage caused when a pipe unexpectedly bursts. It does not include damage resulting from gradual seepage, leakage, or deterioration.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of occupants and personal property so that the customary use of the dwelling is discontinued. It includes a dwelling without customary furnishings or regular habitation. It does not include a dwelling that is temporarily unoccupied while it remains furnished and intended for return.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover direct physical loss to "covered property" under Coverage A. "Covered property" means the "dwelling" described for this coverage.

**A.2** The "dwelling" includes the building used principally as a private residence. It also includes structures attached to that building.

**A.3** We cover materials and supplies located on or next to the "residence premises" for use in construction, alteration, or repair of the "dwelling". The materials and supplies must be intended to become a permanent part of the "dwelling".

**A.4** "Residence premises" means the location where the "dwelling" is situated. The location must be used as a private residence by you.

**A.5** Our payment for a covered loss will not exceed the applicable "limit of liability". The "limit of liability" is the most we will pay for covered property under Coverage A.

**A.6** We cover only direct physical loss to covered property. The loss must result from a cause of loss that is not excluded or limited by these provisions.

**A.7** We cover damage to attached structures when the damage results from direct physical loss to the "dwelling". Attached structures are subject to the same "limit of liability" as the "dwelling".

**A.8** We do not cover land, including land on which the "dwelling" is located. We do not cover the value of land in determining the amount of a loss.

**A.9** We do not cover structures that are not attached to the "dwelling". We do not cover property that is not intended to become a permanent part of the "dwelling".

**A.10** We cover alterations and additions made to the "dwelling". The alterations and additions must be located at the "residence premises".

**A.11** We cover fixtures, appliances, and building equipment that are permanently installed in the "dwelling". Coverage applies only while those items remain part of the covered building.

**A.12** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**A.13** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**A.14** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**A.15** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**A.16** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss differs from the loss expected or intended.

**A.17** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, demolition, or occupancy of property. We do not cover increased costs resulting from compliance with an ordinance or law.

**A.18** We do not cover loss caused by seizure, destruction, or confiscation of property by governmental authority. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from an act of governmental authority.

**A.19** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, or earth sinking, rising, or shifting. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire or explosion resulting from earth movement.

**A.20** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains.

**A.21** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building or structure.

**A.22** We do not cover loss caused by power failure occurring away from the "residence premises". We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from power failure.

**A.23** We do not cover loss caused by war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. We do not cover loss caused by discharge of a nuclear weapon.

**A.24** We do not cover loss to the interior of the "dwelling" caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless a covered cause of loss first damages the building and creates an opening. We cover damage to the interior only when the covered cause of loss permits the weather element to enter.

**A.25** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. An insured must describe the property involved and the circumstances of the loss.

**A.26** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses.

**A.27** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a loss. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property as often as reasonably required.

**A.28** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**A.29** "Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality without deduction for depreciation. We determine replacement cost using the cost at the time of loss.

**A.30** We settle covered loss to the "dwelling" on a replacement cost basis when the applicable conditions are met. Our payment remains subject to the "limit of liability" and the terms of this coverage.

**A.31** Replacement cost settlement applies only when the amount of insurance carried on the "dwelling" is at least eighty percent of its replacement cost at the time of loss. We determine whether this condition is met from the replacement cost of the "dwelling" immediately before the loss.

**A.32** We will not pay more than the least of the applicable "limit of liability", the replacement cost of the damaged part of the property, or the amount actually and necessarily spent to repair or replace the damaged property. We may pay the "actual cash value" of the loss until repair or replacement is completed.

**A.33** "Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss after deduction for depreciation. We determine depreciation from the condition, age, and expected useful life of the property.

**A.34** An insured must notify us before beginning repairs when the repairs may affect our ability to inspect the damage. An insured must retain damaged property when reasonably possible.

**A.35** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may make payment to you instead of performing repair, rebuilding, or replacement.

**A.36** We do not pay for loss to property after an insured abandons the property to us. We do not accept abandoned property unless we agree in writing.

**A.37** Payment for a covered loss does not transfer ownership of damaged property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment to protect our rights.

**A.38** An insured must not impair our right to recover payment from a person responsible for a covered loss. We may exercise that right to the extent of our payment.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover "other structures" on the "residence premises" under "Coverage B". Coverage applies to direct physical loss to covered other structures, subject to these provisions.

**B.2** Other structures are structures that are set apart from the dwelling by clear space. Other structures include structures connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

**B.3** A structure must be located on the residence premises to be covered under Coverage B. We do not cover a structure located away from the residence premises.

**B.4** Coverage B applies to a structure that is owned by you or jointly owned by you and another person. We do not cover another person's interest in a structure unless that interest is insured by this coverage.

**B.5** The limit applicable to Coverage B is ten percent of the limit applicable to "Coverage A". The applicable limit applies to all covered loss under Coverage B.

**B.6** Payment for covered loss under Coverage B does not increase the limit applicable to Coverage A. Payment for covered loss under Coverage A does not increase the limit applicable to Coverage B.

**B.7** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We do not cover the value of excavation, grading, or filling.

**B.8** We cover permanently installed fixtures that are part of a covered other structure. We do not cover personal property merely because it is located in or near an other structure.

**B.9** We do not cover a structure rented or held for rental to a person who is not a tenant of the dwelling. This exclusion applies whether the rental arrangement is written or oral.

**B.10** We cover a structure rented or held for rental when the structure is used solely as a "private garage". The private garage must not be used for a purpose that makes the structure ineligible for coverage.

**B.11** We do not cover a structure used in whole or in part for "business". This exclusion applies even when the business use is occasional or incidental.

**B.12** We cover a structure used solely as a private garage despite business use of the dwelling. The private garage must remain used solely for private garage purposes.

**B.13** We do not cover a structure used to store "business property". This exclusion applies to business property owned by you or by another person.

**B.14** We cover a structure used to store business property owned by you or by a tenant of the dwelling when that property is used primarily for personal use. The structure must otherwise qualify as a covered other structure.

**B.15** We cover only the portion of an other structure that is eligible for coverage. We do not cover a portion of a structure used for an excluded purpose.

**B.16** We cover a covered other structure while it is being repaired, altered, or rebuilt. The structure must remain located on the residence premises.

**B.17** A covered loss must involve "direct physical loss" to a covered other structure. We do not cover loss consisting only of economic loss, loss of use, or diminished value.

**B.18** We do not cover loss caused intentionally by an insured. This exclusion applies regardless of the insured's reason for causing the loss.

**B.19** We do not cover loss to an other structure that you do not own and do not have a legal obligation to insure. We may cover your interest in improvements that become part of a covered other structure.

**B.20** We determine the amount of loss based on the damaged covered property at the time of loss. We may inspect the property and examine the circumstances of the loss.

**B.21** We may elect to repair, rebuild, or replace damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality. Our election does not require us to provide property that is identical in appearance.

**B.22** We do not pay more than your financial interest in covered property at the time of loss. Payment to you does not create an interest in property that you did not own.

**B.23** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. The notice must identify the damaged other structure and provide information reasonably available to the insured.

**B.24** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs solely to protect the property from further damage.

**B.25** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses incurred to protect covered property. An insured must provide those records to us when requested.

**B.26** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and take samples when reasonably necessary.

**B.27** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request to investigate a loss. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim.

**B.28** An insured must submit a signed statement describing the loss when we request it. The statement must identify the damaged property, the cause of loss, and the insured's interest in the property.

**B.29** An insured must not abandon covered property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment for covered loss.

**B.30** We may recover from a person responsible for covered loss to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" while it is anywhere in the world. Coverage applies only to direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.2** At your request, we cover personal property owned by others while the property is at the "residence premises" occupied by an insured. We also cover personal property of guests or residence employees while the property is at the residence premises occupied by an insured.

**C.3** The limit of liability for "Coverage C" is zero percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". This limit is the most we will pay for all covered personal property loss arising from any occurrence.

**C.4** The applicable limit of liability includes the value of covered property and the cost to repair or replace that property. Payment for a covered loss does not increase the applicable limit of liability.

**C.5** We will pay no more than the actual cash value of damaged personal property until repair or replacement is completed. We will not pay more than the amount you actually and necessarily spend to repair or replace the damaged property.

**C.6** You may elect to make a claim for loss to personal property on an actual cash value basis. Any claim settlement is subject to the applicable limit of liability and the terms of this policy.

**C.7** We do not cover loss to property separately described and specifically insured in this or any other insurance. This exclusion applies whether or not the other insurance provides coverage for the same cause of loss.

**C.8** We do not cover loss to animals, birds, or fish. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the animal, bird, or fish is kept for personal use, sale, breeding, or any other purpose.

**C.9** We do not cover loss to aircraft, aircraft parts, or aircraft equipment. This exclusion applies to property designed for flight or used in connection with flight.

**C.10** We do not cover loss to hovercraft or hovercraft parts. This exclusion applies whether the hovercraft is owned, rented, borrowed, or used by an insured.

**C.11** We do not cover loss to motor vehicles or motor vehicle equipment. We cover equipment and accessories not required for use with a motor vehicle while the equipment and accessories are used solely to service the residence premises.

**C.12** We do not cover loss to property of roomers, boarders, or other tenants. We cover property of roomers, boarders, or tenants who are related to an insured.

**C.13** We do not cover loss to property contained in an apartment regularly rented or held for rental to others by an insured. We cover property of an insured who resides in that apartment.

**C.14** We do not cover loss to property rented or held for rental to others away from the residence premises. This exclusion does not apply to property rented on an occasional basis for use as a residence.

**C.15** We do not cover loss to "business property" while it is away from the residence premises. This exclusion does not apply to business property carried or worn by an insured.

**C.16** We do not cover loss to business data, including data stored in books of account, drawings, paper records, or electronic media. We do not cover the cost to research, replace, restore, or reproduce business data.

**C.17** We do not cover loss to credit cards, debit cards, charge plates, access devices, or similar instruments. We do not cover loss arising from unauthorized use of those items.

**C.18** We do not cover loss to tickets, notes, securities, deeds, evidences of debt, letters of credit, or similar instruments. This exclusion applies to the paper, electronic, or other medium in which the information is recorded.

**C.19** We do not cover loss to bullion, precious metals, or property made principally of precious metals. This exclusion applies whether the property is held for personal use, collection, investment, or sale.

**C.20** We do not cover loss to currency, coins, bank notes, or similar property used as a medium of exchange. We do not cover loss to stored value cards or similar instruments.

**C.21** We do not cover loss to manuscripts, stamps, philatelic property, or numismatic property. We do not cover any value attributable to rarity, historical significance, or collectibility.

**C.22** We do not cover loss to water or steam. This exclusion applies while the water or steam is contained in a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or other system.

**C.23** We do not cover loss to gravestones, markers, or mausoleums. This exclusion applies whether the property is located on or away from the residence premises.

**C.24** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.25** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from inherent vice, latent defect, or any quality in property that causes it to damage or destroy itself. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.26** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from rust, corrosion, fungus, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or decay. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.27** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from smog, smoke from agricultural smudging, or industrial operations. We cover direct physical loss caused by smoke from a sudden and accidental event.

**C.28** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.29** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**C.30** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from nesting, infestation, discharge, or release of waste products by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. This exclusion applies whether the condition is sudden or gradual.

**C.31** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from neglect by an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**C.32** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from intentional loss. Intentional loss means any loss arising out of any act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss.

**C.33** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from governmental action. This exclusion does not apply to acts of civil authority taken to prevent the spread of fire.

**C.34** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from ordinance or law requiring the demolition, repair, replacement, reconstruction, remodeling, or remediation of property. We do not cover loss resulting from the enforcement of any ordinance or law.

**C.35** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. "Pollutants" include any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or chemical irritant or contaminant.

**C.36** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from the failure of power, communication, or utility service occurring away from the residence premises. We cover loss caused by a covered peril that results from that failure.

**C.37** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, erosion, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

**C.38** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building or other structure.

**C.39** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or otherwise.

**C.40** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from water that backs up through sewers, drains, or sumps. We do not cover loss caused by water that overflows from a sump or related equipment.

**C.41** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies to seepage or leakage from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or other system.

**C.42** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system while the residence premises is vacant or unoccupied. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat in the building or shut off the water supply and drain the system and appliances.

**C.43** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from vandalism or malicious mischief if the residence premises has been vacant for an extended period. A building under construction is not vacant.

**C.44** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from war. War includes declared or undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action.

**C.45** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from nuclear hazard. "Nuclear hazard" includes nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, and any consequence of these conditions.

**C.46** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from the failure to preserve property after a loss. An insured must make reasonable repairs necessary to protect the property from further damage.

**C.47** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. The notice must identify the damaged property and state the circumstances of the loss to the extent known.

**C.48** An insured must notify the appropriate public authority if the loss involves theft. An insured must cooperate with us in any investigation of the theft.

**C.49** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item to the extent reasonably available.

**C.50** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, and other documents that support the claim when we request them. An insured must permit us to make copies of those materials.

**C.51** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it.

**C.52** An insured must provide a signed and sworn statement of loss when we request it. The statement must include information reasonably necessary for us to investigate and adjust the claim.

**C.53** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of any claim. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold material information concerning the loss.

**C.54** We may inspect the property and examine the cause and extent of loss. Our inspection does not constitute acceptance of liability or a waiver of any policy provision.

**C.55** We may repair, replace, or pay for covered personal property at our option. We may take all or any part of damaged property at the agreed or appraised value.

**C.56** If there is a dispute concerning the amount of loss, either party may demand an appraisal. Each party must select a competent and impartial appraiser and cooperate in the appraisal process.

**C.57** We will adjust each covered loss with you unless another person is legally entitled to receive payment. Payment to an insured does not affect the rights of any other person having a lawful interest in the property.

**C.58** We do not provide coverage for any loss that is excluded by this policy. All coverage under this section is subject to the applicable policy conditions.

## I.D — Coverage D — Fair Rental Value and E — Additional Living Expense

**D.1** We cover "Fair Rental Value" when a "covered loss" makes the part of the "residence premises" rented to others or held for rental unfit for use. Payment is subject to the terms of this Coverage.

**D.2** "Fair Rental Value" means the rental income you would have received from the affected part of the residence premises. The rental income must be reduced by charges and expenses that do not continue while that part is unfit for use.

**D.3** We will pay the actual loss of Fair Rental Value resulting directly from the covered loss. The loss must occur while the affected part of the residence premises is unfit for use.

**D.4** We will determine Fair Rental Value based on the rental value of the affected premises immediately before the covered loss. We may consider written rental agreements, customary rental charges, and other reliable evidence of rental value.

**D.5** The limit for Fair Rental Value is twenty percent of the limit applicable to "Coverage A". This limit is the most we will pay for all Fair Rental Value arising from any covered loss.

**D.6** Payment for Fair Rental Value does not increase the limit applicable to Coverage A. Payment for Fair Rental Value is subject to all other applicable limits of insurance.

**D.7** We will not pay Fair Rental Value for rental income that you could reasonably avoid losing. You must use reasonable means to make the affected part of the residence premises available for rental when it is fit for use.

**D.8** We will not pay Fair Rental Value for loss caused by cancellation, suspension, or nonrenewal of a rental agreement. We will not pay for any loss resulting from a tenant's failure to occupy the premises when the covered loss did not make the premises unfit for use.

**D.9** We will not pay Fair Rental Value for loss arising from your inability to collect rent. We will not pay for rent that was not legally payable before the covered loss.

**D.10** We cover "Additional Living Expense" when a covered loss makes the residence premises where you reside unfit for use. Payment is subject to the terms of this Coverage.

**D.11** Additional Living Expense means the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living. The increase must result directly from the residence premises being unfit for use.

**D.12** We will pay only expenses that are reasonable and necessary under the circumstances. We may consider the ordinary manner in which your household lived immediately before the covered loss.

**D.13** Additional Living Expense may include the reasonable cost of temporary housing, meals, transportation, and other necessary living expenses. The expense must be in excess of the expense you would have incurred if no covered loss had occurred.

**D.14** We will not pay Additional Living Expense for costs that do not increase because of the covered loss. We will not pay for expenses that are personal, discretionary, or unrelated to maintaining your normal standard of living.

**D.15** We will not pay Additional Living Expense after the residence premises are fit for use. We will not pay expenses that continue because you delay returning to the residence premises without reasonable cause.

**D.16** You must make reasonable efforts to minimize Additional Living Expense. You must cooperate with us in determining whether an expense is necessary and reasonable.

**D.17** We may require receipts, invoices, rental agreements, canceled checks, or other records supporting any claimed Additional Living Expense. An insured must provide those records when requested.

**D.18** We may pay Additional Living Expense directly to a provider of temporary housing or other services. Payment to a provider does not relieve you of your duties under this policy.

**D.19** We will not pay Additional Living Expense for any expense incurred after you permanently relocate from the residence premises. A relocation is permanent when you no longer intend to return to the residence premises after repair or replacement.

**D.20** We do not cover Fair Rental Value or Additional Living Expense caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, or use of property. This exclusion applies unless the expense results directly from a covered loss and is otherwise covered by this policy.

**D.21** We do not cover Fair Rental Value or Additional Living Expense caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not apply to reasonable measures taken to protect property from further damage.

**D.22** We do not cover Fair Rental Value for any portion of the residence premises occupied by you when the claimed loss is based on your own use of that portion. We may pay Additional Living Expense for your necessary increased living expenses if the residence premises is unfit for use.

**D.23** We do not cover Additional Living Expense for a residence premises that was vacant when the covered loss occurred. This exclusion does not apply if you were temporarily absent and intended to return.

**D.24** We do not cover either Fair Rental Value or Additional Living Expense for loss arising from a dispute between you and a tenant. This includes a dispute concerning rent, occupancy, possession, maintenance, or the terms of a rental agreement.

**D.25** We will pay no more than the actual financial loss sustained under Fair Rental Value. We will not pay an amount that duplicates payment made under any other coverage or source.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure against "direct physical loss" to "property covered" when the loss is caused by an "open peril" and is not excluded or limited by this section. An open peril is a fortuitous cause of loss that is not specifically excluded or limited by this section.

**P.2** We cover direct physical loss to a building insured under Coverage A or Coverage B that is caused by an open peril. We cover only the part of the loss that is directly caused by the covered peril.

**P.3** We cover direct physical loss to personal property insured under Coverage C only when the loss is caused by a peril expressly described in this section. We do not cover direct physical loss to personal property under Coverage C merely because the loss results from an open peril.

**P.4** A loss must be accidental and must occur during the policy period. An insured must establish that property covered sustained direct physical loss.

**P.5** We cover an ensuing loss caused by a covered peril when the ensuing loss is separate from the excluded cause of loss. We do not cover the excluded cause of loss itself.

**P.6** An insured must use reasonable care to protect property covered from further damage after a loss. We do not cover avoidable additional damage resulting from an insured's failure to protect the property.

**P.7** We do not cover loss caused by "earth movement," whether naturally occurring or otherwise caused. Earth movement includes earthquake, land shock waves, landslide, mine subsidence, earth sinking, rising, shifting, and movement of earth beneath a structure.

**P.8** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement even if the earth movement is combined with water, wind, fire, or another cause of loss. We cover direct loss by fire, explosion, or breakage of glass when that loss follows earth movement and is otherwise covered.

**P.9** We do not cover loss caused by "water" that backs up through or overflows from a sewer, drain, sump, or related equipment. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground, including water that exerts pressure on or seeps through a building.

**P.10** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. We do not cover loss caused by the release of water from a dam, levee, or similar structure.

**P.11** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters through an opening in a roof, wall, window, door, foundation, or other part of a building unless a covered peril first creates the opening. We cover resulting water damage only to the extent directly caused by the covered peril.

**P.12** We do not cover loss caused by "power failure" when the failure occurs away from the residence premises. We cover direct loss caused by a covered peril that results from power failure.

**P.13** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of power, heating, air conditioning, refrigeration, or other utility service equipment when the failure occurs at the residence premises. We cover direct physical loss caused by a covered peril that follows the equipment failure.

**P.14** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's neglect to use reasonable means to save and preserve property covered at and after the time of loss. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect the property when it is safe to do so.

**P.15** We do not cover loss caused by "war," including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or a warlike act by a military force. We do not cover loss caused by seizure, destruction, or use of property for a military purpose.

**P.16** We do not cover loss caused by a nuclear reaction, radiation, radioactive contamination, or a radioactive event. We cover direct loss by fire resulting from a nuclear reaction when the fire is otherwise covered.

**P.17** We do not cover loss that arises out of an intentional act committed by or at the direction of an insured and intended to cause loss. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the property is owned by an insured.

**P.18** We do not cover loss caused by weather conditions when the weather conditions contribute in any way with an excluded cause of loss. We cover direct loss by a covered peril that occurs independently of the excluded cause.

**P.19** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. **P.21** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, latent defect, or faulty manufacture. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**P.22** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, frost, condensation, or humidity. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**P.23** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We cover sudden and accidental loss caused by smoke from a hostile fire.

**P.64** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's voluntary parting with property through trick, scheme, fraud, or false pretense. We do not cover loss caused by a dishonest act of a person entrusted with the property.

**P.65** We cover a loss involving more than one cause only when a covered peril independently causes direct physical loss. We do not cover loss when an excluded cause is the efficient cause of the loss.

**P.66** We do not cover the cost of correcting a condition that existed before the direct physical loss. We cover repair of damage caused by a covered peril without requiring improvement of undamaged property.

**P.67** An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation of a claimed loss. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold a material fact concerning the insurance, property covered, or loss.

**P.68** We may determine the cause of loss through inspection, testing, engineering, or other reasonable investigation. An insured must provide reasonable access to property covered for that investigation.

**P.69** The roof surfacing age threshold does not, by itself, establish that a loss is covered or excluded. We determine coverage based on the cause of direct physical loss and the applicable provisions of this section.

**P.70** We cover direct physical loss only to the extent permitted by this section. All coverage remains subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations stated in this section.

## I.X — Section I — Exclusions

**X.1** We do not cover "loss" caused directly or indirectly by any excluded peril or condition described in this section. Such loss is excluded regardless of any other cause or event that contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**X.2** We do not cover loss caused by "ordinance or law" requiring the testing, monitoring, cleanup, removal, containment, treatment, detoxification, neutralization, or remediation of property. We also do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, use, occupancy, or demolition of property.

**X.3** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, earth sinking, rising, or shifting, and volcanic eruption.

**X.4** We do not cover loss caused by volcanic eruption when the loss results from earthquake, land shock waves, tremors, landslide, mudflow, earth sinking, rising, or shifting. We do cover direct loss caused by fire, explosion, or theft resulting from earth movement where otherwise covered.

**X.5** We do not cover loss caused by water. Water includes flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, spray from any of these, and waterborne material.

**X.6** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. The "sewer backup limit" applies to covered loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains.

**X.7** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, leaks through, or flows through a building, foundation, pavement, driveway, sidewalk, or other structure.

**X.8** We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, escape, or overflow of water from any source. This exclusion does not apply to accidental discharge or overflow from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system located on the residence premises.

**X.9** We do not cover loss caused by power failure or the failure of power, heating, cooling, or utility service. This exclusion applies when the failure occurs away from the residence premises.

**X.10** We do not cover loss caused by neglect. An insured must use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss.

**X.11** We do not cover loss caused by war. War includes declared war, undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, military action, or nuclear conflict.

**X.12** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action. Governmental action includes seizure, destruction, confiscation, or control of property by any governmental or public authority.

**X.13** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an insured to preserve property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect covered property from further damage.

**X.14** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. Intentional loss means any loss arising out of an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss.

**X.15** We do not cover loss caused by the acts or decisions of any person, group, organization, or governmental body. We do cover ensuing loss caused by a peril otherwise covered.

**X.16** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We do cover ensuing loss caused by a peril otherwise covered.

**X.17** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective materials used in repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, or maintenance. We do cover ensuing loss caused by a peril otherwise covered.

**X.18** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown. Mechanical breakdown includes failure of moving parts, motors, pumps, compressors, controls, or similar equipment.

**X.19** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do cover ensuing loss caused by a peril otherwise covered.

**X.20** We do not cover loss caused by smog, rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or decay. We do cover direct loss caused by fire or explosion where otherwise covered.

**X.21** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do cover direct loss caused by collapse where otherwise covered.

**X.22** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do cover direct loss caused by fire or explosion where otherwise covered.

**X.23** We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, discharge, or release by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from recurring damage.

**X.24** We do not cover loss caused by pollution. Pollution means the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants.

**X.25** We do not cover loss caused by pollutants unless the pollutants result from a peril otherwise covered. Pollutants include any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, chemical, or irritant contaminant.

**X.26** We do not cover loss caused by the presence, growth, proliferation, spread, or activity of fungi, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. The "fungi and bacteria aggregate" applies to covered loss involving fungi, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria.

**X.27** We do not cover loss caused by remediation, removal, testing, monitoring, treatment, detoxification, neutralization, or disposal of fungi, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. This exclusion applies whether the property is damaged or undamaged.

**X.28** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance systems while the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied. An insured must maintain heat in the building or shut off the water supply and drain the systems and appliances.

**X.29** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if covered property has been vacant for sixty days consecutive days immediately before the loss. This exclusion applies whether the vacancy is known to an insured.

**X.30** We do not cover loss caused by theft in or from a dwelling under construction. We also do not cover loss caused by theft of materials or supplies intended for use in construction.

**X.31** We do not cover loss caused by theft of property from any part of the residence premises rented to others by an insured. This exclusion applies unless the property is owned by a tenant.

**X.32** We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by an insured. We also do not cover loss caused by theft committed by a person entrusted with covered property.

**X.33** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief committed by an insured. We also do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief committed by a person entrusted with covered property.

**X.34** We do not cover loss caused by collapse unless the collapse results directly from a peril otherwise covered. We do not cover loss involving settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion.

**X.35** We do not cover loss caused by faulty workmanship, defective materials, or inadequate maintenance that results in collapse. We do cover direct loss caused by fire, explosion, or theft where otherwise covered.

**X.36** We do not cover loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust to property inside a building. We cover such loss only when the building first sustains damage by a peril otherwise covered to its roof or walls.

**X.37** We do not cover loss caused by wind, hail, rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust to property contained in the open. We cover property in the open only when specifically provided by this policy.

**X.38** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail to awnings, signs, antennas, outdoor equipment, or similar property unless the property is specifically covered. The "wind percentage deductible minimum" applies when windstorm or hail loss is covered.

**X.39** We do not cover loss caused by snow, ice, or sleet weight to fences, retaining walls, patios, walks, drives, or other outdoor structures. We do cover direct loss to a building caused by collapse where otherwise covered.

**X.40** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. This exclusion applies whether the discharge or overflow is caused by mechanical failure, power interruption, or any other condition.

**X.41** We do not cover loss caused by seepage or leakage of water that occurs over time from a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system. We do cover direct loss caused by sudden and accidental discharge or overflow where otherwise covered.

**X.42** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage, leakage, or overflow of water or steam. This exclusion applies whether the condition is hidden or apparent.

**X.43** We do not cover loss caused by condensation, humidity, moisture, or vapor. We do cover ensuing loss caused by a peril otherwise covered.

**X.44** We do not cover loss caused by freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of ice that damages foundations, pavements, patios, walks, drives, retaining walls, or other outdoor structures. We do cover direct loss caused by fire or explosion where otherwise covered.

**X.45** We do not cover loss caused by excavation, grading, filling, or movement of soil performed by or for an insured. This exclusion applies whether the work is performed on or away from the residence premises.

**X.46** We do not cover loss caused by collapse of land, earth, fill, pavement, or other surface material. We do cover direct loss caused by fire, explosion, or theft where otherwise covered.

**X.47** We do not cover loss caused by smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations. We do cover sudden and accidental damage from smoke resulting from a hostile fire.

**X.48** We do not cover loss caused by pressure waves from aircraft or other aerial devices. We do cover direct loss caused by fire or explosion where otherwise covered.

**X.49** We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain the residence premises in a condition reasonably suited for its intended use. An insured must promptly repair known conditions that could cause additional damage.

**X.50** We do not cover loss caused by any act undertaken to conceal, misrepresent, or fraudulently alter a material fact concerning this insurance. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of a loss.

**X.51** We do not cover loss when an insured has concealed or misrepresented any material fact or circumstance relating to this insurance. This exclusion applies whether the concealment or misrepresentation occurs before or after a loss.

**X.52** We do not cover loss to property used primarily for business purposes unless the property is specifically covered. Business includes trade, profession, occupation, and commercial activity.

**X.53** We do not cover loss arising out of the operation of a business conducted on the residence premises. We do cover loss to property otherwise covered when the business activity did not cause or contribute to the loss.

**X.54** We do not cover loss arising out of a criminal act committed by an insured. This exclusion applies only to the insured who committed or directed the criminal act.

**X.55** We do not cover loss arising out of the manufacture, sale, distribution, possession, delivery, or use of a controlled substance. This exclusion does not apply to the legitimate use of prescription drugs by an insured.

**X.56** We do not cover loss caused by animals owned, kept, or controlled by an insured. We do cover direct loss caused by fire or explosion where otherwise covered.

**X.57** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to protect property after an occurrence. An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property and records relating to the loss.

**X.58** We do not cover loss caused by testing for, monitoring of, cleanup of, removal of, containment of, treatment of, detoxification of, or neutralization of pollutants. We do cover direct loss caused by fire or explosion where otherwise covered.

**X.59** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies whether the loss is direct or indirect.

**X.60** We do not cover loss caused by the accidental or intentional release of radioactive material. We do cover direct loss caused by fire resulting from radioactive material where otherwise covered.

**X.61** We do not cover loss caused by any failure to comply with a duty imposed by this policy when the failure prejudices our rights. An insured must provide truthful information concerning the loss.

**X.62** We do not cover loss caused by abandonment of property to us. An insured must retain possession of damaged property unless we agree otherwise.

**X.63** We do not cover loss caused by repair, alteration, or demolition undertaken without reasonable protection of covered property. An insured must protect property from damage during repair, alteration, or demolition.

**X.64** We do not cover loss caused by intentional exposure of property to rain, snow, wind, water, or other weather conditions. We do cover direct loss caused by a peril otherwise covered when the exposure was necessary to protect life.

**X.65** We do not cover loss excluded by any provision of this section even when the excluded cause results in loss to otherwise covered property. We cover only that part of a loss caused solely by a peril otherwise covered and not otherwise excluded.

## I.S — Section I — Conditions

**S.1** An "insured" must comply with all applicable conditions of this policy. Compliance by an insured does not relieve any other insured of a separate duty imposed by this policy.

**S.2** A "loss" means accidental direct physical loss to "covered property." An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss.

**S.3** An insured must give notice to the appropriate law enforcement authority when a loss may involve theft, vandalism, malicious mischief, or another unlawful act. An insured must cooperate with any investigation concerning that loss.

**S.4** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property, subject to our right to inspect the property.

**S.5** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and other expenses incurred to protect covered property. An insured must provide those records to us upon request.

**S.6** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged covered property as often as reasonably necessary. An insured must not discard, destroy, alter, or remove damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**S.7** An insured must exhibit damaged property to us upon request. An insured must permit us to take samples, photographs, measurements, and other information reasonably required to investigate the loss.

**S.8** An insured must provide us with records and documents that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to make copies of those records and documents.

**S.9** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must sign the transcript of the examination if it is accurately recorded.

**S.10** An insured must provide a complete inventory of damaged personal property when we request it. The inventory must identify the property and describe its condition, value, and amount of loss.

**S.11** An insured must provide available receipts, bills, invoices, photographs, appraisals, and other documents supporting the claimed loss. An insured must cooperate in obtaining records from persons or organizations having relevant information.

**S.12** An insured must notify us of any change in ownership, occupancy, possession, or use of the covered property that may be material to the risk. An insured must provide information concerning the change upon our request.

**S.13** An insured must notify us if covered property becomes vacant. An insured must provide the date vacancy began and the reason for the vacancy upon our request.

**S.14** An insured must notify us of a mortgage, lien, security interest, or other interest in covered property when we request that information. An insured must provide the name and address of each person or organization having such an interest.

**S.15** An insured must notify us promptly of a claim or demand made by another person arising from a loss involving covered property. An insured must forward to us any notice, demand, summons, complaint, or other legal paper received in connection with that matter.

**S.16** An insured must not voluntarily make a payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except for reasonable measures taken to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must not prejudice our rights against another person or organization.

**S.17** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must assist us in enforcing any right of recovery that we may have.

**S.18** An insured must preserve all rights of recovery against a person or organization responsible for a loss. If we make payment for a loss, our rights of recovery are transferred to us to the extent of our payment.

**S.19** An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right to recover from a person or organization responsible for that loss. An insured must execute documents and take actions reasonably required to secure our recovery rights.

**S.20** We may require an insured to assign to us any recovery obtained from another person or organization for a loss we have paid. An insured must hold any such recovery in trust for us to the extent of our payment.

**S.21** An insured must provide a signed, sworn proof of loss that states the information we reasonably require. The proof of loss must be sent to us within sixty days after our request.

**S.22** A proof of loss must identify the time, place, and cause of the loss. A proof of loss must state the interest of each insured and of all other persons having an interest in the covered property.

**S.23** A proof of loss must identify other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must provide copies of applicable policies or other evidence of insurance when we request them.

**S.24** A proof of loss must state the actual cash value of damaged covered property and the amount claimed. An insured must support those statements with available records and information.

**S.25** An insured must identify repairs already made and repairs that remain necessary. An insured must provide estimates, invoices, and contracts relating to those repairs when available.

**S.26** An insured must identify any damaged property that has been sold, transferred, repaired, disposed of, or discarded after the loss. An insured must provide information concerning the disposition and any proceeds received.

**S.27** An insured must notify us if a claimed loss is also the subject of a governmental inquiry, criminal investigation, administrative proceeding, or civil action. An insured must provide copies of related notices and pleadings upon request.

**S.28** An insured must disclose material information concerning the cause, extent, and value of a loss. An insured must correct information previously given to us if the insured later learns that the information was incomplete or inaccurate.

**S.29** We may inspect the covered property before deciding whether to make payment. Our inspection does not waive any policy condition or any defense available to us.

**S.30** We may obtain statements from persons having knowledge of the loss. An insured must reasonably assist us in identifying those persons.

**S.31** We may obtain reports, records, and opinions relevant to the loss. An insured must authorize the release of information when reasonably necessary for us to investigate the claim.

**S.32** We may retain experts, contractors, engineers, accountants, or other professionals to inspect covered property or evaluate the loss. An insured must provide reasonable access to the property for those persons.

**S.33** We may request that an insured submit damaged property for testing or examination. An insured must cooperate if the testing or examination is reasonably necessary to determine the cause or extent of loss.

**S.34** We will adjust a loss with the insured entitled to payment. Payment to an insured does not establish coverage for any other claimed loss.

**S.35** We will pay no more than the applicable limit of liability for a covered loss. The applicable limit of liability is not increased by the existence of more than one insured, claimant, mortgagee, or interested person.

**S.36** A "deductible" is the portion of a covered loss that remains the responsibility of an insured. We will apply the deductible to each covered loss as required by this policy.

**S.37** The minimum deductible applicable to a covered loss is five hundred dollars. An insured remains responsible for the deductible whether or not another person may be liable for the loss.

**S.38** We will apply the deductible before determining the amount payable for a covered loss. We will not pay the deductible on behalf of an insured unless this policy expressly provides otherwise.

**S.39** The "maximum windstorm and hail deductible percentage" applies when a windstorm or hail deductible is applicable to a covered loss. We will determine the applicable deductible from the circumstances of the loss and the terms of this policy.

**S.40** The "deductible increase notice period" applies to notice of a deductible increase when such notice is required. An insured must provide us with a current mailing address for delivery of notices.

**S.41** The "receipt acknowledgement period" applies to our acknowledgement of a claim or other communication when applicable. An insured must promptly provide information needed for us to acknowledge and evaluate the matter.

**S.42** The "loss referral amount" applies when a loss is referred for further handling or review. An insured must cooperate with our representatives and with persons acting on our behalf.

**S.43** If there is other insurance covering the same loss, an insured must notify us of that insurance. We will not pay more than our share of the covered loss.

**S.44** Our share is the proportion that our applicable limit of liability bears to the total applicable limits of all insurance covering the loss. This condition does not increase any limit of liability under this policy.

**S.45** An insured must not seek duplicate recovery for the same loss. We may reduce payment by amounts paid or payable under other insurance for the same covered property and loss.

**S.46** If the insured and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either may demand appraisal in writing. Each party must select a competent and impartial appraiser within twenty days after receiving the written demand.

**S.47** The appraisers must select a competent and impartial umpire. If the appraisers cannot agree upon an umpire, either party may request that a court of competent jurisdiction appoint an umpire.

**S.48** Each party must pay the appraiser selected by that party. Each party must bear equally the other expenses of appraisal and the expenses of the umpire.

**S.49** The appraisers must state separately the amount of loss. An agreement by the appraisers, or by an appraiser and the umpire, will set the amount of loss.

**S.50** Appraisal determines only the amount of loss unless the parties otherwise agree in writing. Appraisal does not determine coverage, policy interpretation, causation, or the application of any exclusion or condition.

**S.51** We may deny a claim when an insured has concealed or misrepresented a material fact or circumstance concerning the insurance, covered property, an insured interest, or a loss. Concealment or misrepresentation may occur before or after a loss.

**S.52** We may deny a claim when an insured has engaged in fraud relating to this policy or a claimed loss. Fraud by an insured may affect the rights of every insured seeking coverage for that loss.

**S.53** An insured must not intentionally conceal information material to our investigation. An insured must not knowingly provide false, incomplete, or misleading information concerning a claim.

**S.54** We may pay a covered loss to an insured, a mortgagee, or another person entitled to payment under this policy. Payment made in good faith discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**S.55** An insured must notify us of any assignment of an interest in this policy or in covered property. An assignment is not effective against us unless we consent in writing, except when the assignment results from the death of an insured.

**S.56** If an insured dies, the legal representative of the deceased insured must perform the duties imposed by this policy. A person having proper temporary custody of covered property must perform duties necessary to protect that property.

**S.57** An insured must provide us with the name and contact information of a legal representative, trustee, receiver, or other person acting for the insured. That person must provide evidence of authority to act when we request it.

**S.58** An insured must notify us promptly if covered property is subject to foreclosure, repossession, receivership, bankruptcy, or another proceeding affecting possession or ownership. An insured must provide related documents upon request.

**S.59** An insured must notify us if the location of covered property changes. An insured must provide information concerning the new location and the circumstances of the move.

**S.60** An insured must notify us of a substantial change in the condition of covered property that increases the possibility of loss. An insured must provide information concerning the change when we request it.

**S.61** An insured must maintain covered property in a condition reasonably intended to prevent avoidable damage. An insured must take reasonable precautions to protect covered property from freezing, leakage, theft, fire, and weather damage.

**S.62** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting persons who perform repairs, maintenance, or alterations to covered property. An insured must retain available records concerning work performed after a loss.

**S.63** An insured must notify us before beginning permanent repairs when we have requested an opportunity to inspect the damage. Emergency measures reasonably necessary to protect covered property may be taken without prior notice.

**S.64** An insured must provide access to the location of a loss for inspection, repair evaluation, and adjustment. An insured must make reasonable efforts to obtain access when another person controls the location.

**S.65** An insured must preserve salvage to the extent reasonably possible. We may take possession of damaged property for which we make payment, subject to the interests of persons entitled to the property.

**S.66** We may require an insured to transfer title to damaged property to us when we make payment for the full value of that property. An insured must execute documents reasonably necessary to complete that transfer.

**S.67** An insured must notify us promptly if the insured receives payment, reimbursement, settlement proceeds, or other compensation for a loss from another source. An insured must preserve those funds to the extent of our interest.

**S.68** We may offset amounts owed to us against amounts otherwise payable under this policy to the extent permitted by law. An insured must repay us for any payment made in error.

**S.69** We will pay for a covered loss after we reach agreement with you, after entry of a final judgment, or after filing of an appraisal award. Payment will be made within sixty days after the applicable event.

**S.70** We may make payment to the person or organization legally entitled to receive it. An insured must provide information reasonably necessary to identify that person or organization.

**S.71** An insured must not bring an action against us unless the insured has complied with all policy conditions. An action against us must be brought within two years after the date of loss.

**S.72** An insured must bring any action against us in a court having jurisdiction over the matter. No action against us may be maintained if prohibited by applicable law.

**S.73** Our right to investigate, inspect, appraise, adjust, defend, or settle a claim does not waive any policy provision. Our payment of part of a claim does not waive our right to deny another part of the claim.

**S.74** No provision of this policy may be waived unless the waiver is in writing and signed by an authorized representative of us. An act or omission by us does not constitute a waiver unless it satisfies this condition.

**S.75** An insured must provide written notice of any disagreement concerning a claim when we request it. The notice must state the basis of the disagreement and identify available supporting information.

**S.77** An insured must promptly notify us if a representative, contractor, public adjuster, attorney, or other person is authorized to act for the insured. An insured must provide written evidence of that authority upon request.

**S.78** An insured must not interfere with our communications with persons having information relevant to a loss. An insured may obtain advice or representation at the insured's own expense.

**S.79** An insured must comply with applicable law relating to the reporting, investigation, repair, and disposition of a loss. Compliance with applicable law does not create coverage where coverage does not otherwise exist.

**S.80** The duties and conditions in this section apply to each insured seeking payment or other benefit under this policy. Failure to comply with a material duty may affect our obligation to provide coverage or make payment.

**G.31** This policy is void if an insured intentionally conceals or misrepresents a material fact relating to this insurance. This policy is also void if an insured engages in fraudulent conduct relating to a claim.

**G.33** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard. Nuclear hazard includes any nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination, however caused.

**G.34** We do not cover loss resulting from the hazardous properties of nuclear material. This exclusion applies whether the nuclear hazard is controlled or uncontrolled.

**G.53** An insured must not transfer ownership of covered property without promptly notifying us. We may revise, cancel, or continue coverage after receiving notice of a transfer.

**G.54** Coverage for property acquired by an insured is subject to the terms of this policy. An insured must provide information about acquired property when we reasonably request it.

**G.58** An insured must identify persons with an interest in covered property when reasonably requested by us. An insured must provide available evidence of those interests.

**G.59** An insured must notify us promptly if other insurance may cover the same loss. We may coordinate payment with other insurers having an obligation for that loss.

**G.60** We will not pay more than our share of a covered loss when other insurance applies. Our share is determined under the terms of this policy and applicable law.

**G.61** An insured must preserve evidence relevant to a claim, including damaged property and records. An insured must not alter evidence in a manner that materially impairs our investigation.

**G.62** An insured must report a theft loss to the appropriate law enforcement authority. An insured must cooperate with law enforcement authorities investigating the theft loss.

**G.67** We may require an insured to execute documents necessary to transfer rights of recovery to us. An insured must provide reasonable assistance in enforcing those rights.

**G.68** An insured must promptly notify us if recovered property is sold, transferred, or disposed of after a loss. An insured must account to us for proceeds that relate to an amount we paid.

**G.69** The duties and conditions in this policy apply whether a claim is made by an insured or by another person seeking coverage. We may enforce those duties and conditions to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**G.70** Our rights and duties under this policy are binding on an insured and an insured's legal representative. This policy is binding on any successor only to the extent provided by this policy and applicable law.
