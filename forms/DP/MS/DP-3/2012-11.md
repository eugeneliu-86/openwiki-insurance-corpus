## AGR — Agreement

In return for the “premium” and subject to all terms of this “policy,” we will provide
the insurance described in this policy. This insurance applies only to an “insured.”

We will pay for direct physical loss to “covered property” when the loss is caused by a
covered peril. Payment is subject to the applicable policy provisions.

Coverage applies only while this policy is in force. Coverage applies only to property
that qualifies as covered property.

An insured must pay the premium when due. An insured must comply with all duties and
conditions imposed by this policy.

An insured must provide complete and accurate information material to the insurance
provided by this policy. An insured must promptly notify us of any material change
affecting the risk.

We may inspect the property and the information relating to the risk. An inspection does
not constitute a warranty that the property is safe or complies with any law, ordinance,
or regulation.

We may adjust a loss with an insured or with another person legally entitled to receive
payment. Payment by us will satisfy our obligation to the extent of that payment.

An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or
defense of a claim. An insured must preserve rights of recovery against any person
responsible for a loss.

No person may bring legal action against us unless the requirements of this policy have
been met. Any legal action must be brought in accordance with applicable law.

This policy may be changed only by our written agreement. No waiver or change may be
made by an insured unless we agree to it in writing.

The terms of this policy are severable. If any provision is determined to be
unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

Our rights and duties under this policy may not be transferred without our written
consent. An insured may not assign a claim or right of recovery in a manner that
prejudices our rights.

This policy is a contract between you and us. The policy language governs the insurance
we provide.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss,
determined by the cost to repair or replace the property with like kind and quality,
less depreciation. We may consider the property's age, condition, and useful life in
determining that value. It includes the depreciated value of a used roof covering and
does not include the full cost of new roof covering without deduction for depreciation.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with
property of like kind and quality without deduction for depreciation. It includes the
reasonable cost of materials and labor necessary to restore covered property. It does
not include the cost to improve property beyond its condition immediately before the
loss.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to
substantially the same general harmful conditions, that results in bodily injury or
property damage. It includes accidental injury arising from a slip on damaged premises.
It does not include injury or damage that is expected or intended by an insured.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling where you reside, together with
structures and grounds used in connection with that dwelling. It includes a detached
garage used in connection with your residence. It does not include land or a structure
that is not used in connection with your residence.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or
persons under your care. It includes a relative who resides in your household. It does
not include a tenant who does not reside in your household.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and premises used by you in
connection with the residence premises. It includes land you own where a dwelling is
being built for your use as a residence. It does not include premises used principally
for business.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in
for financial gain. It includes the regular sale of goods or services. It does not
include activities ordinarily incidental to nonbusiness pursuits.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or
terrain and includes machinery or equipment attached to that vehicle. It includes a car,
truck, or trailer designed for transportation. It does not include a child's toy vehicle
that is not designed for transportation.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are directly
related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises or the performance of
household or domestic services. It includes a gardener employed to maintain the grounds
of the residence premises. It does not include a person employed solely in connection
with business conducted by an insured.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a
person, including resulting death. It includes required care and loss of services
resulting from bodily harm. It does not include emotional distress that does not arise
from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to or destruction of tangible
property, including resulting loss of use of that property. It also includes loss of use
of tangible property that has not been physically injured. It does not include loss
involving property that has no tangible form.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unexpected, and unintended. It includes
damage caused when a pipe unexpectedly bursts. It does not include damage resulting from
gradual seepage, leakage, or deterioration.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We insure the “dwelling” described in the Declarations. The dwelling includes
the building used principally as your private residence. We insure the dwelling while it
is located at the residence premises.

**A.2** The dwelling includes additions that are attached to and form a part of the
building. An addition must be connected to the building by a permanent structural
connection. We insure the addition under the same terms that apply to the dwelling.

**A.3** We insure fixtures that are permanently installed in the dwelling. Permanently
installed fixtures include plumbing, heating, air-conditioning, and electrical
equipment. We insure those fixtures whether they are built into the dwelling or attached
to it.

**A.4** We insure permanently installed appliances located in the dwelling. The
appliances must be intended for use in the dwelling. We do not change the coverage that
applies to the dwelling because an appliance is removable.

**A.5** We insure materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises
when they are intended for use in constructing, altering, or repairing the dwelling. The
materials and supplies must be owned by you or held by you for that purpose. We insure
them only while they remain available for the described work.

**A.6** We insure structures attached to the dwelling. A structure is attached when it
is physically connected to the dwelling or supported by the same foundation. We treat
the attached structure as part of the dwelling for purposes of this Coverage.

**A.7** We insure a garage attached to the dwelling. The garage must be connected to the
dwelling by a permanent structural connection. We insure fixtures and permanently
installed equipment located in the garage as part of the dwelling.

**A.8** We insure a porch, deck, or patio attached to the dwelling. The structure must
be permanently installed and intended for use with the dwelling. We insure materials
used to repair or replace the structure while they are located on the
residence premises.

**A.9** We insure permanently installed floor coverings that are attached to the
dwelling. We insure permanently installed wall, ceiling, and cabinet coverings that form
part of the dwelling. We do not treat ordinary personal property as part of the dwelling
solely because it is located in the building.

**A.10** We insure permanently installed plumbing, wiring, and service equipment that
serve the dwelling. The equipment may be located within the dwelling or outside the
dwelling when it serves the dwelling. We insure the equipment only to the extent that it
is part of the dwelling.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** “Coverage B” applies to “other structures” on the “residence premises.” The
limit of insurance for Coverage B is ten percent of the limit for “Coverage A.” Except
that the Coverage B limit does not increase because the cost to repair or replace
covered property exceeds that limit.

**B.2** We cover other structures on the residence premises that are set apart from the
dwelling by clear space. We cover those structures when they are used in connection with
the residence premises. Subject to the terms of this policy, a structure may be attached
to the dwelling only when it is separated by a clear space.

**B.3** We cover other structures that are used for residential purposes. We cover
structures that are used to store personal property. Unless otherwise excluded, we cover
structures that are used for purposes incidental to the occupancy of the dwelling.

**B.4** We do not cover land, including land on which a covered structure is located. We
do not cover water or any other substance contained in or on the land. This exclusion
applies whether the land is owned by you or occupied by you.

**B.5** We do not cover structures used in whole or in part for business purposes. This
exclusion does not apply to a structure used to store business property. Provided that
the structure is not used for manufacturing, processing, or sales, we cover the
structure when its use is incidental to the residence premises.

**B.6** We do not cover structures rented or held for rental to any person who is not a
tenant of the dwelling. We cover a structure rented to a tenant of the dwelling when the
rental is incidental to the occupancy of the dwelling. Unless the rental use is
incidental, we do not cover the structure.

**G.65** We may require an insured to assist in the defense of a claim or suit. An
insured must attend hearings, trials, examinations, and other proceedings when
reasonably requested.

**G.66** An insured must not make a voluntary payment, assume an obligation, or incur an
expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses necessary to protect covered
property from further damage. We may reimburse reasonable protective expenses when
covered by this policy.

**G.67** We may require an insured to execute documents necessary to transfer rights of
recovery to us. An insured must provide reasonable assistance in enforcing those rights.

**G.68** An insured must promptly notify us if recovered property is sold, transferred,
or disposed of after a loss. An insured must account to us for proceeds that relate to
an amount we paid.

**G.69** The duties and conditions in this policy apply whether a claim is made by an
insured or by another person seeking coverage. We may enforce those duties and
conditions to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**G.70** Our rights and duties under this policy are binding on an insured and an
insured's legal representative. This policy is binding on any successor only to the
extent provided by this policy and applicable law.
