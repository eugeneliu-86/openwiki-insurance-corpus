## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** We will provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the
premium and subject to all applicable terms. This policy applies only to an "insured"
who meets the conditions of this policy.

**AGR.2** We insure against direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a
covered peril. Coverage is subject to the applicable "limit of liability".

**AGR.3** We will pay only for loss that occurs during the "policy period". The loss
must occur at the "residence premises" shown in the declarations, unless this policy
provides otherwise.

**AGR.4** You must have an insurable interest in covered property at the time of loss.
We will not pay an insured more than that insured's financial interest in the covered
property.

**AGR.5** This policy applies only to property lawfully owned, used, or possessed by an
insured. Coverage does not create an ownership interest in property.

**AGR.6** The declarations are part of this policy. The information shown in the
declarations is incorporated into the terms of this policy.

**AGR.7** We will provide coverage only when the premium required for coverage has been
paid or when we have agreed to accept payment. An insured must comply with all payment
duties imposed by this policy.

**AGR.8** The applicable limit of liability is the most we will pay for loss arising
from a single occurrence, subject to the terms of this policy. Payment of a loss does
not increase an applicable limit of liability.

**AGR.9** We may investigate a loss and adjust a claim as we consider appropriate. Our
investigation, payment, or offer to pay does not waive any policy term or defense.

**AGR.10** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or
defense of a claim. An insured must provide requested information and documents that are
material to the claim.

**AGR.11** We do not cover loss caused by an act, condition, or circumstance excluded by
this policy. We do not cover loss for which coverage is prohibited by law.

**AGR.12** If a policy term conflicts with a provision required by applicable law, the
required provision applies. Any remaining policy term continues to apply to the extent
permitted by law.

**AGR.13** This policy contains the agreement between you and us concerning the
insurance provided. Its terms may be changed only by our written endorsement.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the amount required to repair or replace damaged
property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. The
amount is determined by the cost reasonably necessary to restore the property. It
includes the cost to replace a damaged roof with comparable new materials. It does not

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to

property damage. All resulting injury or damage arising from the same harmful conditions

single occurrence, the claims manual's duration test applies the definitionthe claims

unrelated harmful conditions.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling, other structures, and land used
principally as a private residence by you. The residence premises includes the part of a
building in which you reside and the grounds used in connection with that building. It
includes a detached garage used for personal residential purposes. It does not include
land or structures used principally for business.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or
other persons under your care. The term includes a minor in your care who resides in
your household. It includes a spouse who resides in your household. It does not include
a tenant, boarder, or employee who is not otherwise a resident relative or person under
your care.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other premises used by you
in connection with the residence premises. The insured location includes premises you
acquire during the policy term for use as a residence. It includes a temporary residence
occupied by you. It does not include premises used principally for business or premises
not used by you.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in
for economic gain. The term includes the rental of property and the providing of
services for compensation. It includes an office maintained in a dwelling for regular
commercial activity. It does not include an occasional activity that does not involve
economic gain.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on
public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration. The term includes a trailer or
semitrailer designed to be towed by such a vehicle. It includes an automobile,
motorcycle, or recreational vehicle. It does not include a vehicle used solely to
service the residence premises and not designed for travel on public roads.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related
to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. The term includes an employee
performing domestic services for an insured. It includes a gardener, housekeeper, or
caretaker employed by an insured. It does not include a person employed principally in
connection with business conducted by an insured.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a
person, including resulting death. The term includes emotional distress resulting from
bodily harm, sickness, or disease. It includes an injury caused by a fall on the
residence premises. It does not include purely economic loss not resulting from bodily
harm, sickness, or disease.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of
tangible property. The term includes resulting loss of use of property that has not been
physically injured. It includes damage to a fence caused by a fallen tree. It does not
include loss of use of property that has not suffered physical injury unless the loss
results from covered property damage.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unexpected from the
standpoint of an insured. The event must result from an identifiable occurrence rather
than gradual deterioration. It includes an unexpected discharge from a plumbing system.
It does not include repeated seepage, leakage, corrosion, or wear.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary to normal
habitation or occupancy. A dwelling is vacant when it is not being used for its
customary residential purpose. It includes a dwelling that contains only minimal
furnishings and is not occupied. It does not include a dwelling temporarily unoccupied
while furnished and maintained for normal residential use.

**DEF.14** "named storm" means a storm assigned a name by a governmental weather
authority or other recognized meteorological authority. The term includes a tropical
cyclone identified by an assigned storm name. It includes damage caused by wind or rain
directly associated with that storm. It does not include an unnamed thunderstorm or
other weather event not associated with a named storm.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover the "Other Structures" on the "residence premises" that are set apart
from the dwelling by clear space. We also cover a structure connected to the dwelling
only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. We cover the structure only when
you use it for a purpose related to the residence premises.

**B.2** The limit of insurance for "Coverage B" is fifteen percent of the limit of
insurance for "Coverage A". Except that the limit of insurance for Coverage B does not
increase because of the cost of debris removal, reasonable repairs, or other expenses
covered under this policy. We will not pay more than the applicable limit of insurance
for any covered loss to Other Structures.

**B.3** We cover an Other Structure for accidental direct physical loss to property. We
cover the loss only when the loss is caused by a peril insured against under this
policy. We apply all applicable conditions and exclusions to the loss.

**B.4** We cover building materials and supplies located on or next to an Other
Structure. We cover those materials and supplies only when they are intended for use in
constructing, altering, or repairing an Other Structure. We do not cover materials or
supplies intended for use in constructing, altering, or repairing the dwelling.

**B.5** We cover an Other Structure that is in the course of construction when it meets
the requirements for covered property under this coverage. We cover the structure only
to the extent of your interest in it. We do not cover the value of land or any other
property that is not part of the structure.

**B.6** We cover a structure that is temporarily removed from the residence premises for
repair, renovation, or similar work. We cover the structure while it is removed only
when it would otherwise qualify as an Other Structure. We do not cover loss occurring
after the structure is permanently removed from the residence premises.

**B.7** We cover a fence, retaining wall, or similar improvement when it is an Other
Structure. We cover the improvement only for accidental direct physical loss caused by a
peril insured against. We do not cover soil, fill, or land supporting the improvement.

**B.8** We cover a driveway, walkway, or similar paved surface when it is an Other
Structure. We cover the surface only to the extent that it is located on the
residence premises. We do not cover loss caused by ordinary wear, deterioration, or
settling unless a covered peril causes the loss.

**B.9** We cover a detached garage, carport, shed, or similar structure when it is an
Other Structure. We cover the structure whether or not you use it for storage of
vehicles. We do not cover a vehicle or its equipment under this coverage.

**B.10** We cover an Other Structure used for storage of personal property. We cover the
structure only when the use does not materially increase the risk of loss. We do not
cover the personal property stored in the structure under this coverage.

**B.11** We cover an Other Structure used for a private residential purpose. We cover
the structure only when you use it in a manner that is incidental to the use of the

**C.1** We provide Coverage C for direct physical loss to your "Personal Property" at
the "residence premises" caused by a covered peril. The limit for Coverage C is
zero percent of the Coverage A limit. Subject to the terms of this policy, the limit
applies to all covered Personal Property unless a different limit or provision applies.

**C.2** We cover Personal Property owned or used by you while it is located at the
residence premises. We also cover Personal Property owned or used by a resident "family
