# DP-3 Dwelling Property 3 — Special Form (Edition 2026-01)




## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** We will provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the premium and subject to all applicable terms. This policy applies only to an "insured" who meets the conditions of this policy.

**AGR.2** We insure against direct physical loss to "covered property" caused by a covered peril. Coverage is subject to the applicable "limit of liability".

**AGR.3** We will pay only for loss that occurs during the "policy period". The loss must occur at the "residence premises" shown in the declarations, unless this policy provides otherwise.

**AGR.4** You must have an insurable interest in covered property at the time of loss. We will not pay an insured more than that insured's financial interest in the covered property.

**AGR.5** This policy applies only to property lawfully owned, used, or possessed by an insured. Coverage does not create an ownership interest in property.

**AGR.6** The declarations are part of this policy. The information shown in the declarations is incorporated into the terms of this policy.

**AGR.7** We will provide coverage only when the premium required for coverage has been paid or when we have agreed to accept payment. An insured must comply with all payment duties imposed by this policy.

**AGR.8** The applicable limit of liability is the most we will pay for loss arising from a single occurrence, subject to the terms of this policy. Payment of a loss does not increase an applicable limit of liability.

**AGR.9** We may investigate a loss and adjust a claim as we consider appropriate. Our investigation, payment, or offer to pay does not waive any policy term or defense.

**AGR.10** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must provide requested information and documents that are material to the claim.

**AGR.11** We do not cover loss caused by an act, condition, or circumstance excluded by this policy. We do not cover loss for which coverage is prohibited by law.

**AGR.12** If a policy term conflicts with a provision required by applicable law, the required provision applies. Any remaining policy term continues to apply to the extent permitted by law.

**AGR.13** This policy contains the agreement between you and us concerning the insurance provided. Its terms may be changed only by our written endorsement.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the amount required to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, less depreciation and obsolescence. The amount reflects the property's condition immediately before the loss. It includes the value of used materials after adjustment for wear. It does not include the cost of new materials without such adjustment.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the amount required to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. The amount is determined by the cost reasonably necessary to restore the property. It includes the cost to replace a damaged roof with comparable new materials. It does not include the cost to improve, enlarge, or redesign the property.

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, resulting in bodily injury or property damage. All resulting injury or damage arising from the same harmful conditions is treated as a single occurrence. For determining whether related events constitute a single occurrence, the claims manual's duration test applies the definitionthe claims manual's duration test applies the definition (see Property Claims Handling Manual, C3 Chapter 3 — Water Losses). It does not include separate accidents that arise from unrelated harmful conditions.

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling, other structures, and land used principally as a private residence by you. The residence premises includes the part of a building in which you reside and the grounds used in connection with that building. It includes a detached garage used for personal residential purposes. It does not include land or structures used principally for business.

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are your relatives or other persons under your care. The term includes a minor in your care who resides in your household. It includes a spouse who resides in your household. It does not include a tenant, boarder, or employee who is not otherwise a resident relative or person under your care.

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other premises used by you in connection with the residence premises. The insured location includes premises you acquire during the policy term for use as a residence. It includes a temporary residence occupied by you. It does not include premises used principally for business or premises not used by you.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for economic gain. The term includes the rental of property and the providing of services for compensation. It includes an office maintained in a dwelling for regular commercial activity. It does not include an occasional activity that does not involve economic gain.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration. The term includes a trailer or semitrailer designed to be towed by such a vehicle. It includes an automobile, motorcycle, or recreational vehicle. It does not include a vehicle used solely to service the residence premises and not designed for travel on public roads.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. The term includes an employee performing domestic services for an insured. It includes a gardener, housekeeper, or caretaker employed by an insured. It does not include a person employed principally in connection with business conducted by an insured.

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person, including resulting death. The term includes emotional distress resulting from bodily harm, sickness, or disease. It includes an injury caused by a fall on the residence premises. It does not include purely economic loss not resulting from bodily harm, sickness, or disease.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property. The term includes resulting loss of use of property that has not been physically injured. It includes damage to a fence caused by a fallen tree. It does not include loss of use of property that has not suffered physical injury unless the loss results from covered property damage.

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unintended, and unexpected from the standpoint of an insured. The event must result from an identifiable occurrence rather than gradual deterioration. It includes an unexpected discharge from a plumbing system. It does not include repeated seepage, leakage, corrosion, or wear.

**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary to normal habitation or occupancy. A dwelling is vacant when it is not being used for its customary residential purpose. It includes a dwelling that contains only minimal furnishings and is not occupied. It does not include a dwelling temporarily unoccupied while furnished and maintained for normal residential use.

**DEF.14** "named storm" means a storm assigned a name by a governmental weather authority or other recognized meteorological authority. The term includes a tropical cyclone identified by an assigned storm name. It includes damage caused by wind or rain directly associated with that storm. It does not include an unnamed thunderstorm or other weather event not associated with a named storm.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover direct physical loss to the "dwelling" described in your policy when the loss is caused by a covered peril. The dwelling includes structures attached to it.

**A.2** We cover materials and supplies located on or next to the "residence premises" for use in the construction, alteration, or repair of the dwelling. The materials and supplies must be owned by you or be your responsibility.

**A.3** We cover permanently installed fixtures, equipment, and appliances that are part of the dwelling. We also cover wall-to-wall carpeting when it is permanently attached to the dwelling.

**A.4** We cover additions under construction when they are attached to the dwelling. We cover loss to those additions only while they remain on the residence premises.

**A.5** We cover structures connected to the dwelling by a fence, utility line, or similar connection only when they are attached to the dwelling. We do not cover a structure that is separated from the dwelling by clear space.

**A.6** We cover the dwelling while it is used as a private residence. Incidental occupancy does not change the character of the dwelling as a private residence.

**A.7** We cover damage caused by a covered peril to building glass that is part of the dwelling. We also cover damage to safety glazing material that is part of the dwelling.

**A.8** We cover awnings, outdoor equipment, and permanently installed features when they are attached to the dwelling. We do not cover land, including land on which the dwelling is located.

**A.9** We cover direct physical loss to property used to service the dwelling when that property is permanently installed. Such property includes heating, cooling, plumbing, electrical, and security equipment.

**A.10** We cover debris resulting from a covered loss to property insured under this coverage. The debris must result directly from the same covered peril that caused the loss.

**A.11** We do not cover loss to foundations, retaining walls, bulkheads, piers, wharves, or docks unless the loss results directly from a covered peril. We do not cover land stabilization or grading.

**A.12** We do not cover loss to underground pipes, flues, drains, or wiring caused by settling, wear, deterioration, or mechanical breakdown. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril unless otherwise excluded.

**A.13** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We cover ensuing direct physical loss caused by a covered peril unless otherwise excluded.

**A.14** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect the dwelling from further damage.

**A.15** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss. An insured must provide information and records that we reasonably request concerning the loss.

**A.16** An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**A.17** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request it. An insured must produce records and documents relating to the dwelling, the loss, and the value of the property.

**A.18** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss. An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to the insurance or a loss.

**A.19** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We may make payment for a covered loss instead of repairing or replacing damaged property.

**A.20** We will determine the amount of loss to the dwelling based on the cost to repair or replace damaged property with materials of like kind and quality. We will not pay for loss that is not direct physical loss.

**A.21** We will settle a covered loss to the dwelling at "replacement cost" without deduction for depreciation when the conditions of this coverage are met. Replacement cost does not include the increased cost resulting from an insured's decision to repair or replace with materials or methods of greater value.

**A.22** To receive replacement cost settlement, the amount of insurance applying to the dwelling must be maintained at not less than eighty percent of the full replacement cost of the dwelling immediately before the loss. The dwelling must be repaired or replaced as soon as reasonably possible after the loss.

**A.23** If the conditions for replacement cost settlement are not met, we will settle the loss at "actual cash value." Actual cash value may reflect depreciation and the condition of the damaged property immediately before the loss.

**A.24** We will not pay more than the amount actually spent to repair or replace damaged property. We will not pay replacement cost for property that is not repaired or replaced.

**A.25** We will settle loss to roof surfacing at replacement cost unless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attachedunless an actual cash value roof schedule endorsement is attached (see HO 23 74 Actual Cash Value Loss Settlement — Roof Surfacing (Edition 2025-05), W.1 Coverage Provided). We will apply the applicable loss settlement terms when determining payment for roof surfacing.

**A.26** We will not pay for the cost to comply with an ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, demolition, or occupancy of the dwelling. We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of an ordinance or law unless the loss is otherwise covered.

**A.27** We do not cover the cost to demolish undamaged portions of the dwelling. We do not cover the cost to remove or replace undamaged property to gain access to damaged property.

**A.28** We do not cover increased costs caused by delay, loss of use, loss of market, or loss of value. We do not cover expenses incurred to improve, modernize, or expand the dwelling.

**A.29** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's voluntary parting with property by trick, scheme, or false pretense. We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by an insured.

**A.30** We do not cover loss to property while it is vacant if the vacancy increases the hazard of loss. An insured must take reasonable steps to maintain heat, utilities, and protective safeguards appropriate to the dwelling.

**A.31** We may require an insured to assign to us rights of recovery against a person responsible for a covered loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**A.32** If we make payment for a covered loss, we are entitled to recover that payment from a person legally responsible for the loss. Our recovery rights do not apply against an insured under this policy.

**A.33** We may adjust a loss with an insured having custody of the dwelling. Payment to that insured discharges our obligation for the payment made.

**A.34** We may pay a covered loss to you and to a mortgagee or other person having an insurable interest in the dwelling. Payment will be made according to the interests that are established at the time of loss.

**A.35** We do not cover loss occurring after the dwelling has been transferred to another person unless we agree in writing to continue coverage. An insured must notify us of a transfer of ownership that materially changes the risk.

**A.36** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts committed by an insured with the intent to cause a loss. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss differs in kind or degree from the intended loss.

**A.37** We do not cover loss caused by war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or governmental action taken to prevent or defend against any of those events. We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard.

**A.38** This coverage applies only to property described in this section and subject to its terms. All applicable exclusions, conditions, and limitations remain in effect.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover "other structures" on the "residence premises" that are set apart from the dwelling by clear space. We also cover structures connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

**B.2** Covered other structures must be used principally for purposes incidental to the use of the residence premises. We do not cover a structure that is located away from the residence premises.

**B.3** We cover building materials and supplies located on the residence premises for use in the construction, alteration, or repair of a covered other structure. Such materials and supplies must be intended to become a permanent part of that structure.

**B.4** We cover only your financial interest in covered other structures. We do not cover the interest of any person or organization that is not an insured.

**B.5** The limit for "Coverage B" is fifteen percent of the limit for "Coverage A". This limit applies whether loss affects a single covered other structure or more than one covered other structure.

**B.6** The Coverage B limit is the most we will pay for all loss covered under this coverage. Payment for loss will not increase the applicable limit.

**B.7** We cover "direct physical loss" to covered other structures only when caused by a "covered cause of loss". We do not cover loss that is not direct physical loss.

**B.8** We do not cover an other structure that is used in whole or in part for "business" purposes. This exclusion does not apply to a structure used solely as a private garage.

**B.9** We do not cover an other structure that is rented or held for rental to a person who is not a "tenant" of the dwelling. This exclusion does not apply when the structure is used solely as a private garage.

**B.10** We cover an other structure rented to a tenant of the dwelling when that structure is used solely by that tenant. Coverage applies only to the extent of your financial interest in that structure.

**B.11** We cover a "private garage" that otherwise qualifies as an other structure. Use of the garage for storage incidental to its garage use does not by itself make the garage ineligible for coverage.

**B.12** We do not cover "personal property" located in or on an other structure under this coverage. We cover only the structure and building materials and supplies described in this section.

**B.13** We do not cover land, including land on which a covered other structure is located. We also do not cover the value of land resulting from loss to a covered other structure.

**B.14** We do not cover any reduction in value resulting from loss to covered other structures. Payment is limited to covered physical damage.

**B.15** We do not cover increased costs caused by the enforcement of any "ordinance or law". This exclusion applies whether the enforcement occurs before, during, or after repair or replacement.

**B.16** We may pay the cost to repair or replace damaged portions of covered other structures with material of like kind and quality. We may make payment or arrange for repair or replacement.

**B.17** An "insured" must give us prompt notice of loss involving covered other structures. Notice must include information available to the insured concerning the loss and the damaged property.

**B.18** An insured must take reasonable emergency measures to protect covered other structures from further damage. We will not pay for damage caused by an insured's failure to protect the property when reasonable protection was possible.

**B.19** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and other loss-related costs. An insured must provide those records to us when we request them.

**B.20** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as reasonably required. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine available records relating to the loss.

**B.21** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a loss. An insured must provide a signed statement when we reasonably request one.

**B.22** An insured must not abandon covered property to us unless we agree in writing. We may take possession of damaged property after payment of a covered loss.

**B.23** We may require an insured to preserve damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. We may deny payment for damage that cannot be verified because the property was disposed of without our consent.

**B.24** We will pay covered loss subject to the applicable Coverage B limit. Our payment will not exceed the amount of your financial interest in the damaged other structure.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" for direct physical loss caused by a covered peril. Covered property must be usual or incidental to the occupancy of the "residence premises".

**C.2** We cover personal property while it is at the residence premises or at another location. Coverage applies only to property in which an insured has an insurable interest at the time of loss.

**C.3** We cover only "direct physical loss" to covered property. We do not cover diminution in value, loss of use, delay, or any consequential loss.

**C.4** The limit of liability for "Coverage C" is zero percent of the limit of liability for "Coverage A". The applicable limit is the most we will pay for covered loss under this coverage.

**C.5** The Coverage C limit applies to all covered property damaged in any "occurrence". Payment for loss does not increase the applicable limit.

**C.6** We cover property owned by you and property owned by relatives who reside with you. We also cover property owned by persons under your care who reside with you.

**C.7** We cover property owned by guests while the property is at the residence premises, if you request coverage for that property. Our payment does not benefit any person who has caused or concealed the loss.

**C.8** We cover personal property customarily kept at the residence premises while it is temporarily away from that location. The property must remain owned or used by an insured.

**C.9** We cover personal property while it is being transported by an insured. Coverage applies only while the property is in the care, custody, or control of an insured.

**C.10** We cover direct physical loss caused by the following "perils". A peril must be the efficient cause of the loss.

**C.11** We cover loss caused by fire or lightning. We cover smoke damage only as provided in this coverage.

**C.12** We cover loss caused by windstorm or hail. We do not cover loss to property caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless wind or hail first damages the structure containing the property and creates an opening through which the material enters.

**C.13** We cover loss caused by explosion. We do not cover loss caused by the rupture or bursting of water pipes, unless the rupture or bursting results from a covered peril.

**C.14** We cover loss caused by riot or civil commotion. We do not cover loss arising from seizure, confiscation, destruction, or damage by order of a governmental authority.

**C.15** We cover loss caused by aircraft. This includes self-propelled missiles and spacecraft.

**C.16** We cover loss caused by vehicles. We do not cover loss caused by a vehicle owned or operated by an insured while the vehicle is being used by that insured.

**C.17** We cover loss caused by smoke. Smoke must be sudden and accidental and must result from a covered source.

**C.18** We cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief. We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if the residence premises is vacant.

**C.19** We cover loss caused by theft. We do not cover theft committed by an insured or by any person entrusted with the property.

**C.20** We cover loss caused by volcanic eruption. We do not cover loss caused by earthquake, land shock waves, tremors, or aftershocks.

**C.21** We cover loss caused by falling objects. The object must first damage the exterior of the structure containing the property before we cover resulting damage to property inside that structure.

**C.22** We cover loss caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet. We do not cover loss to awnings, fences, patios, pavements, swimming pools, foundations, retaining walls, or bulkheads.

**C.23** We cover loss caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage, leakage, overflow, or discharge of water or steam.

**C.24** We cover loss caused by sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, rust, corrosion, or mechanical breakdown.

**C.25** We cover loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat in the building or to shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**C.26** We cover loss caused by sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current. We do not cover loss to tubes, transistors, electronic components, or circuitry caused by electrical current.

**C.27** We cover loss caused by the accidental discharge of water or steam from a household appliance. We do not cover the appliance from which the water or steam escaped.

**C.28** We do not cover loss caused by "earth movement". Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, earth sinking, rising, shifting, or expansion.

**C.29** We do not cover loss caused by "flood". Flood includes surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of any body of water, or spray from any of these sources.

**C.30** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sumps, or related equipment. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**C.31** We do not cover loss caused by waterborne material, regardless of its source. This exclusion applies whether the waterborne material acts alone or with any other cause of loss.

**C.32** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not relieve us of any duty otherwise imposed by this coverage.

**C.33** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, or smog. We do not cover resulting loss unless the resulting loss is caused by a covered peril.

**C.34** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or decay. We do not cover resulting loss unless the resulting loss is caused by a covered peril.

**C.35** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We do not cover resulting loss unless the resulting loss is caused by a covered peril.

**C.36** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals. We do not cover damage caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, or discharge of waste.

**C.37** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical, electrical, or electronic failure. We do not cover resulting loss unless the resulting loss is caused by a covered peril.

**C.38** We do not cover loss caused by "ordinance or law". This exclusion applies to enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, replacement, use, or occupancy of property.

**C.39** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law requiring demolition, testing, monitoring, cleanup, removal, containment, treatment, detoxification, neutralization, or disposal. We do not cover any increased cost resulting from such enforcement.

**C.40** We do not cover loss caused by "war". War includes undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or action by a military force.

**C.41** We do not cover loss caused by "nuclear hazard". Nuclear hazard includes nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination.

**C.42** We do not cover loss arising from the release, discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, escape, or presence of pollutants. Pollutants include any solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, chemical, or irritant contaminant.

**C.43** We do not cover loss caused intentionally by an insured. This exclusion applies even if another insured did not participate in, know of, or consent to the act.

**C.44** We do not cover loss arising from illegal trade, transportation, possession, or use of property by an insured. We do not cover property that is contraband.

**C.45** We do not cover animals, birds, fish, or other living creatures. We do cover damage to covered property caused by a covered peril involving such property.

**C.46** We do not cover motor vehicles, including their equipment and accessories, while subject to registration for use on public roads. We do cover vehicles used solely to service the residence premises and vehicles designed to assist persons with disabilities.

**C.47** We do not cover aircraft, including their equipment and accessories. We do cover model or hobby aircraft that are not designed or used to carry persons or cargo.

**C.48** We do not cover watercraft, including their equipment and accessories, while used for commercial purposes. We do not cover property held for sale, display, rental, or delivery.

**C.49** We do not cover "business property" that is held for sale or that is primarily used for manufacturing, processing, or commercial activity. We do cover property used by an insured in a home office if the property is not held for sale.

**C.50** We do not cover accounts, bills, currency, deeds, evidences of debt, manuscripts, passports, tickets, stamps, securities, or valuable papers. We do cover the physical value of blank records or blank media.

**C.51** We do not cover "electronic data" or the loss of use, corruption, destruction, alteration, or inability to access electronic data. Electronic data includes information, facts, concepts, software, programs, and instructions stored or transmitted in electronic form.

**C.52** We do not cover credit cards, debit cards, stored-value cards, access devices, or similar instruments. We do not cover unauthorized charges, forgery, or alteration involving such instruments.

**C.53** We do not cover property separately insured under any other coverage available to you. This exclusion applies only to the extent that other coverage applies to the same loss.

**C.54** We do not cover loss to property after title has passed from an insured to another person. We do not cover property abandoned by an insured.

**C.55** An insured must give us prompt notice of loss. The notice must identify the damaged property, the location of the loss, and the circumstances known to the insured.

**C.56** An insured must protect covered property from further damage. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property.

**C.57** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and preserve receipts for those expenses. An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property before permanent repairs are made when practical.

**C.58** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged and undamaged personal property when we request it. The inventory must identify the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item.

**C.59** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, appraisals, and other documents that we reasonably request. An insured must permit us to make copies of those materials.

**C.60** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must preserve the property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**C.61** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. An insured must assist us in obtaining information concerning the loss.

**C.62** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require. An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when we reasonably require.

**C.63** An insured must provide information concerning any other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must provide the names and addresses of persons with knowledge of the loss when reasonably requested.

**C.64** An insured must notify law enforcement authorities if a theft, vandalism, malicious mischief, or other criminal act is involved. An insured must cooperate with any investigation of that act.

**C.65** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense except to protect covered property from further damage. We will not be bound by an unauthorized settlement or admission of liability.

**C.66** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or omit any material fact relating to the insurance, the property, the loss, or a claim. Coverage is void as to any insured who commits fraud relating to a claim.

**C.67** We determine the value of covered property at its "actual cash value" at the time of loss. Actual cash value reflects depreciation, condition, age, and relevant market value.

**C.68** We may repair, restore, replace, or pay for damaged property. We may select the method of settlement that is reasonably appropriate for the covered loss.

**C.69** We may pay the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. We are not required to replace property with identical property if identical property is not reasonably available.

**C.70** We will not pay for undamaged property. We will not pay for any increase in value resulting from repair, restoration, or replacement.

**C.71** We will not pay more than the amount necessary to restore covered property to its condition immediately before the loss. We will deduct the applicable deductible from the amount of covered loss.

**C.72** If a pair or set is damaged, we may repair or replace any part of the pair or set. We may pay the difference between the actual cash value of the property before and after the loss.

**C.73** If property is recovered after we have paid for the loss, an insured must notify us promptly. We may return the property to an insured and adjust the payment to reflect its recovered value.

**C.74** If there is other insurance covering the same loss, we will pay only our share of the covered loss. Our share is determined by the proportion that our applicable limit bears to the total applicable limits.

**C.75** If we pay a covered loss, we are entitled to recover from any person responsible for that loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair our recovery rights.

**C.76** An insured may waive recovery rights against another person before a loss occurs. Any waiver after loss requires our written consent.

**C.77** If you and we disagree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal. Each party will select a competent appraiser and will cooperate in the appraisal process.

**C.78** An insured must retain any damaged property that may support a claim until we authorize disposal. We may require an insured to transfer salvage rights to us after payment.

**C.79** We may inspect covered property and examine records relating to covered property. Our inspection does not constitute a warranty that property is safe, secure, or compliant with any law.

**C.80** We may deny coverage for any loss materially prejudiced by an insured's failure to perform a required duty. We will not deny coverage unless the failure is material to the loss or claim.

**C.81** Payment under this coverage does not transfer ownership of covered property to us unless we agree otherwise. Any transfer of property to us must be made in writing.

## I.D — Coverage D — Fair Rental Value and E — Additional Living Expense

**D.1** The most we will pay under "Coverage D" is thirty percent of the limit under "Coverage A". Payments under this coverage do not increase the limit under Coverage A.

**D.2** We cover loss of "fair rental value" arising from a covered loss to the "residence premises". This coverage applies only to the part of the residence premises that you hold for rental to others or that is rented to others at the time of loss.

## I.P — Perils Insured Against

**P.1** We insure against risk of "direct physical loss" to "covered property" caused by a covered peril. A "loss" means accidental direct physical loss to covered property.

**P.2** Covered property means property to which insurance applies under this policy. We cover a loss only while the property is at the described premises or another location where coverage applies.

**P.3** A loss must result from a distinct physical alteration of covered property. We do not cover loss consisting solely of diminished value, loss of use, delay, or loss of market.

**P.4** A covered peril must be a direct cause of the loss. We do not cover loss when an excluded cause initiates, contributes to, or results in the loss.

**P.5** We may inspect damaged property and examine available records relevant to a loss. An insured must preserve damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless preservation would create an unsafe condition.

**P.6** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, scratching, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by latent defect or mechanical breakdown.

**P.7** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, decay, or contamination. We do not cover loss caused by dampness, dryness, or changes in temperature.

**P.8** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke, soot, vapor, fumes, or industrial operations, unless the smoke results from a sudden and accidental event. We do not cover loss caused by the release, discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, or escape of pollutants.

**P.9** A "pollutant" means a solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, biological, or chemical irritant or contaminant. Pollutants include waste and materials to be recycled, reclaimed, or reconditioned.

**P.10** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, roofs, pavements, or ceilings. We do not cover cracking, unless the cracking results directly from a covered peril.

**P.11** We do not cover loss caused by faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or materials. This exclusion applies whether the condition is in covered property or property elsewhere.

**P.12** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement. Earth movement includes earthquake, landslide, mudslide, subsidence, sinkhole collapse, earth sinking, rising, shifting, or expanding.

**P.13** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers, drains, sump systems, or related equipment.

**P.14** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through any part of a building or structure.

**P.15** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage.

**P.16** We do not cover loss arising out of an intentional act committed by an insured with intent to cause loss. This exclusion applies even if the resulting damage is different in kind or degree from that intended.

**P.17** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of power, heating, cooling, or communication services supplied from outside the described premises. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril.

**P.18** We do not cover loss to the interior of a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand, or dust unless wind, hail, or another covered peril first damages the building and creates an opening. We cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by the resulting entry of those elements.

**P.19** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler systems, or household appliances. An insured must maintain heat in the building or shut off the water supply and drain the system and appliances.

**P.20** We do not cover loss caused by theft committed by an insured. We do not cover loss caused by theft from a portion of the premises rented to others, unless covered property is in a portion occupied solely by an insured.

**P.21** We do not cover loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief if the dwelling has been vacant. Vacancy means the substantial absence of the customary contents necessary for normal habitation.

**P.22** We cover direct physical loss caused by "collapse" of a building or any part of a building. Collapse means an abrupt falling down or caving in of the building or part of the building so that it cannot be occupied for its intended purpose.

**P.23** We do not cover collapse caused by settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion. We do not cover a building or part of a building that is in danger of falling down, unless collapse has begun.

**P.24** We do not cover loss to lawns, trees, shrubs, plants, or growing crops caused by wind, hail, weight of ice, snow, or sleet. We cover loss to these items only when caused by fire, lightning, explosion, aircraft, riot, civil commotion, or vehicles not owned or operated by an insured.

**P.25** We do not cover loss to fences, driveways, walks, patios, retaining walls, or outdoor equipment caused by freezing, thawing, pressure, or weight of water or ice. We cover direct physical loss to these items caused by a covered peril not otherwise excluded.

**P.26** We do not cover loss caused by birds, vermin, rodents, insects, or domestic animals owned or kept by an insured. We do not cover nesting, infestation, or discharge by animals.

**P.27** We do not cover loss caused by fungi, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or microbes. We cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered peril, unless that peril is otherwise excluded.

**P.28** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam. This exclusion applies to seepage or leakage from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler systems, household appliances, or other systems.

**P.29** We cover sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler systems, or household appliances. We do not cover loss to the system or appliance from which the water or steam escaped.

**P.30** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge, leakage, or overflow of water from a household appliance. We do not cover loss caused by seepage or leakage that occurs over time.

**P.31** We cover direct physical loss caused by fire or lightning. We cover resulting smoke damage caused by either peril.

**P.32** We cover direct physical loss caused by explosion. We do not cover loss caused by explosion of steam boilers, steam pipes, steam engines, or steam turbines owned or leased by an insured.

**P.33** We cover direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail. We do not cover loss to personal property outdoors caused by windstorm or hail, unless the property is specifically designed for outdoor use.

**P.34** We cover direct physical loss caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet. We do not cover loss caused by the weight of water.

**P.35** We cover direct physical loss caused by falling objects. We do not cover loss to the object that falls.

**P.36** We cover direct physical loss caused by aircraft or vehicles. We do not cover loss caused by vehicles owned or operated by an insured.

**P.37** We cover direct physical loss caused by riot or civil commotion. We cover direct physical loss caused by vandalism or malicious mischief, subject to the vacancy exclusion.

**P.38** We cover direct physical loss caused by accidental discharge of water from a plumbing system. We do not cover loss caused by water that enters through foundations, walls, floors, windows, doors, or other openings not created by a covered peril.

**P.39** We cover direct physical loss caused by sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a steam, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system. We do not cover loss caused by deterioration, corrosion, rust, or wear and tear of the system.

**P.40** We cover direct physical loss caused by artificially generated electrical current. We do not cover loss to tubes, transistors, electrical appliances, electrical devices, or wiring caused by the electrical current itself.

**P.41** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of an ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, remodeling, renovation, or demolition of property. We do not cover loss caused by the costs associated with compliance with an ordinance or law.

**G.26** We may require an insured to provide a sworn inventory of damaged property. The inventory must describe the property and state its value, cost, and amount of loss.

**G.27** An insured must provide records supporting ownership, value, and condition of damaged property when reasonably available. We may consider the absence of records in evaluating the claim.

**G.28** If there is other insurance covering the same loss, an insured must disclose that insurance to us. We will not pay more than our share of the covered loss.

**G.29** We may settle a claim with an insured or another person having a lawful interest in the covered property. Payment made in good faith discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**G.30** Our payment of a claim does not transfer ownership of damaged property to us unless we agree otherwise. We may require an insured to transfer salvage rights as a condition of payment.

**G.31** An insured must notify us of a claim or suit brought against an insured that may involve coverage. An insured must promptly send us all legal papers received in connection with the claim or suit.

**G.32** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation and defense of a claim or suit. An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense without our consent, except for reasonable expenses to protect property from further damage.

**G.33** We may investigate and settle a claim as we consider appropriate. Our investigation or settlement efforts do not obligate us to provide coverage.

**G.34** An insured must not bring legal action against us unless the insured has complied with all applicable conditions. Any legal action must be commenced within the time permitted by applicable law.

**G.35** We may recover payment made because of fraud, misrepresentation, mistake, or lack of coverage. An insured must reimburse us for amounts that the insured was not entitled to receive.

**G.36** An insured must notify us if covered property becomes "vacant". Vacancy is determined by the actual use and occupancy of the property.

**G.37** An insured must maintain the property in a condition that does not materially increase the hazard. An insured must notify us of a material increase in hazard known to the insured.

**G.38** An insured must comply with applicable laws relating to the insured property. Compliance with law does not create coverage for a loss otherwise excluded.

**G.39** We may cancel this insurance by mailing or delivering written notice to you at your last known mailing address. When cancellation is for nonpayment of premium, notice will be provided at least ten days before cancellation takes effect.

**G.40** We may cancel this insurance for a reason other than nonpayment of premium by mailing or delivering written notice to you at your last known mailing address. Notice will be provided at least sixty days before cancellation takes effect.

**G.41** We may elect not to renew this insurance by mailing or delivering written notice to you at your last known mailing address. Notice of nonrenewal will be provided at least sixty days before the end of the current coverage period.

**G.42** You may cancel this insurance by providing us written notice. Cancellation requested by you takes effect when we receive notice or at a later time stated in the notice.

**G.43** If this insurance is canceled, we will return any unearned premium as required by law. The return of unearned premium does not affect the effectiveness of cancellation.

**G.44** We may terminate coverage for an insured when permitted by applicable law. Termination does not affect our obligations for a covered loss that occurred before termination became effective.

**G.45** Notice sent by us to your last known mailing address is sufficient notice to you. You must notify us of a change in your mailing address.

**G.46** An insured must provide information concerning any mortgagee or other person having an interest in covered property. We may make payment to a person with a lawful interest in the property.

**G.47** A "mortgagee" must notify us of a change in ownership, occupancy, or substantial change in risk known to the mortgagee. A mortgagee must pay any premium due if you fail to do so after we request payment.

**G.48** A mortgagee must submit a claim in accordance with the duties imposed by this insurance when an insured fails to do so. We may protect the mortgagee's interest as required by applicable law.

**G.49** If we pay a mortgagee for a loss and deny payment to an insured, we are subrogated to the mortgagee's rights against the insured. We may require the mortgagee to assign those rights to us.

**G.50** A mortgagee's right to recover is not invalidated by an act or neglect of an insured if the mortgagee complies with its duties. We may cancel a mortgagee's interest as permitted by applicable law.

**G.51** An insured must advise us of any bankruptcy, insolvency, or receivership that may affect a claim. Bankruptcy or insolvency of an insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this insurance.

**G.52** We may pay a claim to a legal representative when an insured is deceased or legally incapable. Payment to a legal representative discharges us to the extent of the payment.

**G.53** If an insured dies, coverage applies to the legal representative of the deceased insured with respect to the insured property. Coverage also applies to a resident relative who has custody of the property until lawful disposition of the property.

**G.54** An insured must notify us of a transfer of ownership of covered property. We may determine whether coverage continues after a transfer, subject to applicable law.

**G.55** No person may recover under this insurance unless that person has complied with applicable conditions. A person's right to recover is subject to all terms that apply to the claim.

**G.56** We may require appraisal if you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss. Each party must select a competent appraiser and cooperate in the appraisal process.

**G.57** If the appraisers fail to agree, they must select a qualified umpire. A written agreement by any appraiser and the umpire will establish the amount of loss.

**G.58** Each party will pay the expense of its selected appraiser. The parties will share the expense of the umpire and other appraisal expenses equally unless otherwise agreed.

**G.59** Appraisal determines only the amount of loss. Appraisal does not determine coverage, cause of loss, deductibles, conditions, or any other legal issue.

**G.60** An insured must preserve our opportunity to inspect damaged property before repair or disposal when reasonably possible. Emergency measures necessary to protect property may be taken without prior inspection.

**G.61** We may require reasonable proof that claimed property existed at the time of loss. We may require reasonable proof of the condition and value of that property.

**G.62** An insured must not abandon covered property to us unless we agree in writing. We may take possession of damaged property after payment without accepting ownership of the property.

**G.63** We may adjust a loss with any insured having an interest in the covered property. Payment to that insured does not affect the rights of another insured except to the extent of the payment.

**G.64** Knowledge possessed by an insured does not automatically bind another insured. An act or omission of an insured may affect coverage for another insured when required by law or the terms of this insurance.

**G.65** If any provision of this insurance is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. The unenforceable provision will be applied to the fullest extent permitted by law.

**G.66** This insurance is governed by the law of the jurisdiction in which the insured property is located, unless another law must apply. We will administer claims in accordance with applicable law.

**G.67** An insured must provide truthful and complete information in connection with an application, claim, or request for coverage. We may rely on information provided by or on behalf of an insured.

**G.68** We may communicate with you through a method permitted by law and authorized by you. You must maintain access to any communication method that you have authorized.

**G.69** An insured must notify us if a dispute arises concerning a claim. We may review additional information submitted by an insured in support of the claim.

**G.70** The duties imposed on an insured apply to a person seeking coverage or payment under this insurance. Compliance by one insured does not excuse noncompliance by another insured.
