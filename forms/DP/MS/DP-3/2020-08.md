## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** This "policy" is a contract between you and us. We provide the insurance
described in this policy in return for payment of the required "premium" and your
compliance with its terms.

**AGR.2** The "Declarations" and any "endorsements" issued with this policy are part of
this policy. This policy includes the application and any information that you provide
to us when required by us.

**AGR.3** We will pay for direct physical "loss" to covered property only as provided by
this policy. Coverage is subject to all terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions
of this policy.

**AGR.4** The insurance that applies to a covered loss is limited by the applicable
"Limit of Liability." We will not pay more than the amount for which we are liable under
this policy.

**AGR.5** You must pay the required premium when due. An "insured" must comply with all
duties imposed by this policy before we have an obligation to provide coverage or make
payment.

**AGR.6** An insured must give us complete and accurate information concerning property,
loss, and claims. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment,
settlement, or defense of a claim.

**AGR.7** We do not cover loss if an insured intentionally conceals or misrepresents a
material fact or circumstance relating to this policy, covered property, or a claim. We
may void this policy as permitted by law if such concealment or misrepresentation
occurs.

**AGR.8** We may revise this policy only by an endorsement issued by us. No change,
waiver, or consent is effective unless it is made in writing by us.

**AGR.9** You must not transfer your rights or duties under this policy without our
written consent. A transfer made after a covered loss does not require our consent.

**AGR.10** If any provision of this policy is determined to be unenforceable, the
remaining provisions remain in effect. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive
our right to enforce that provision later.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss,
determined after deduction for depreciation and physical condition.
It includes the diminished value of used property before the loss.
It does not include the cost to replace property without a deduction for depreciation.
For example, worn roofing material is valued according to its condition immediately
before the loss.

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with
property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.
It includes reasonable material and labor costs necessary to restore the damaged

It does not include the cost of improvements that increase the property's value beyond

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to
substantially the same general harmful conditions.
It includes an unintended event that causes injury or damage.
It does not include an act expected or intended to cause injury or damage.
For example, an accidental discharge of water from a plumbing system may be an

persons in your care.
It includes a resident relative who lives in your household.
It does not include a person who is merely visiting your household.

It includes land where an insured temporarily resides.
It does not include property used principally for business activities.

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in
for compensation or financial gain.
It includes the regular sale of goods or the performance of professional services.
It does not include a hobby conducted without a profit motive.
For example, operating a repair service for compensation is included.

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle or trailer designed for
travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration.
It includes a private passenger automobile.
It does not include a vehicle designed solely for use off public roads while located on
an insured location.
For example, a passenger car operated on a public road is included.

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means a person employed by an insured to perform duties
related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises.
It includes a person performing domestic services or caring for the grounds.
It does not include an employee of an independent contractor.

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the "dwelling" on the "residence premises" described in the
Declarations. The dwelling includes structures attached to it and fixtures installed for
its use. We settle a covered loss to the dwelling on a replacement cost basis when the
amount of insurance applicable to the dwelling is at least ninety percent of the
replacement cost of the dwelling. Subject to the terms of this policy, we apply the
applicable loss settlement provisions when determining the amount payable.

**A.2** We cover additions, alterations, and improvements that are attached to and part
of the dwelling. We cover building equipment and permanently installed appliances that
service the dwelling. We do not cover property that is not part of the dwelling merely
because it is located on the residence premises.

**A.3** We cover materials and supplies located on the residence premises for use in the
construction, alteration, or repair of the dwelling. We cover those materials and
supplies while they remain at the residence premises. We do not cover materials and
supplies held for sale or use at another location.

**A.4** We cover the dwelling while it is occupied as a private residence. We cover
customary residential uses of the dwelling that are incidental to its use as a private
residence. We do not cover a use that materially increases the risk of loss beyond the
use contemplated by this policy.

**A.5** We cover attached structures that are physically connected to the dwelling. We
cover garages, porches, decks, and similar structures when they are attached to the

**A.6** We cover permanently installed fixtures that are owned by you and used in

unless your interest in them is insured under this Coverage A.

drain the system when required.

**A.12** We cover damage to the dwelling caused by weight of ice, snow, or sleet. We
cover ensuing damage to the dwelling that results from that covered cause of loss. We do
not cover damage caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet to a structure that is not
part of the dwelling.

**A.13** We cover damage to the dwelling caused by falling objects. We cover damage to

dwelling when the discharge or overflow is sudden and accidental. We do not cover damage
caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We insure the "Other Structures" described in this Coverage B provision. The
limit of insurance for Coverage B is ten percent of the limit of insurance for Coverage
A. Subject to the terms of this policy, we cover direct physical loss to an Other
Structure caused by a covered peril.

**B.2** An Other Structure must be located on the "Residence Premises". It must be
separated from the dwelling by clear space or connected to the dwelling by only a fence,
utility line, or similar connection. Unless the structure is used in connection with the
dwelling, we do not cover it under this Coverage B provision.

**B.3** A structure connected to the dwelling by a common wall is considered part of the
dwelling. A structure connected to the dwelling only by a roof, breezeway, or similar
attachment may be considered an Other Structure. Provided that the structure otherwise
qualifies, we cover it under this Coverage B provision.

**B.4** We cover a detached garage used for private purposes. We also cover a private
driveway serving an insured location. Except that, we do not cover a structure or
improvement used for a business purpose unless coverage is provided by this policy.

**B.5** We cover a fence, retaining wall, or similar structure located on the Residence
Premises. We cover such a structure whether or not it is attached to a building. Unless
the structure is used primarily for a business, we cover direct physical loss to it
caused by a covered peril.

**B.6** We cover a permanently installed utility building located on the Residence
Premises. The building must be used for storage or another purpose related to the
residence. Subject to the exclusions and conditions of this policy, we cover direct
physical loss to the building.

**B.7** We cover a private swimming pool located on the Residence Premises. We cover the
pool and equipment permanently installed for its operation. Except that, we do not cover
water in the pool or loss caused by freezing unless coverage is otherwise provided.

**B.8** We cover a permanently installed outdoor cooking structure located on the
Residence Premises. The structure must be used for private residential purposes. Unless
it is used in connection with a business, we cover direct physical loss to the structure
caused by a covered peril.

**B.9** We cover a mailbox, lamp post, or similar fixture serving the Residence
Premises. The fixture must be located on the Residence Premises or directly adjacent to
it. Subject to the applicable exclusions, we cover direct physical loss to the fixture.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is at the
residence premises. We also cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it
is temporarily away from the residence premises. The property must be used for personal
purposes. Property used in connection with a business is subject to the provisions that
apply to business property.

**C.2** The limit for "Coverage C" is zero percent. Subject to the terms of this policy,
that limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss to personal property. The limit
applies regardless of the number of items damaged or destroyed. Payment for an item is
also subject to any applicable special limit or other limitation.

**C.3** We cover personal property that is in your possession or under your control. We
cover the property whether it is located at the residence premises or elsewhere.
Coverage applies only while the property is used or maintained for personal purposes. We
do not cover property that is rented to others unless this policy provides coverage for
that property.

**C.4** We cover personal property belonging to you or to a member of your household who
is a relative. We cover such property while the person is a resident of your household.
Coverage also applies to personal property of a guest or residence employee while the
property is at the residence premises. The property must not be covered under another
insurance policy that provides primary coverage.

**C.5** We cover personal property of a student who is a member of your household and
who is temporarily away from the residence premises. The property must be owned or used
by the student. Coverage applies while the student maintains a residence for school
purposes. Coverage does not apply to property kept at a separate residence occupied by
the student unless the student remains a member of your household.

**C.6** We cover personal property while it is in a newly acquired principal residence
