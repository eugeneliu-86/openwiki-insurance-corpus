## AGR — Agreement

**AGR.1** This "policy" is a contract between you and us. We provide the insurance described in this policy in return for payment of the required "premium" and your compliance with its terms.

**AGR.2** The "Declarations" and any "endorsements" issued with this policy are part of this policy. This policy includes the application and any information that you provide to us when required by us.

**AGR.3** We will pay for direct physical "loss" to covered property only as provided by this policy. Coverage is subject to all terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions of this policy.

**AGR.4** The insurance that applies to a covered loss is limited by the applicable "Limit of Liability." We will not pay more than the amount for which we are liable under this policy.

**AGR.5** You must pay the required premium when due. An "insured" must comply with all duties imposed by this policy before we have an obligation to provide coverage or make payment.

**AGR.6** An insured must give us complete and accurate information concerning property, loss, and claims. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim.

**AGR.7** We do not cover loss if an insured intentionally conceals or misrepresents a material fact or circumstance relating to this policy, covered property, or a claim. We may void this policy as permitted by law if such concealment or misrepresentation occurs.

**AGR.8** We may revise this policy only by an endorsement issued by us. No change, waiver, or consent is effective unless it is made in writing by us.

**AGR.9** You must not transfer your rights or duties under this policy without our written consent. A transfer made after a covered loss does not require our consent.

**AGR.10** If any provision of this policy is determined to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. Our failure to enforce a provision does not waive our right to enforce that provision later.

## DEF — Definitions

**DEF.1** "actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, determined after deduction for depreciation and physical condition.  
It includes the diminished value of used property before the loss.  
It does not include the cost to replace property without a deduction for depreciation.  
For example, worn roofing material is valued according to its condition immediately before the loss.  

**DEF.2** "replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.  
It includes reasonable material and labor costs necessary to restore the damaged property.  
It does not include the cost of improvements that increase the property's value beyond its condition before the loss.  
For example, damaged siding may be replaced with comparable siding of like kind and quality.  

**DEF.3** "occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.  
It includes an unintended event that causes injury or damage.  
It does not include an act expected or intended to cause injury or damage.  
For example, an accidental discharge of water from a plumbing system may be an occurrence.  

**DEF.4** "residence premises" means the dwelling and the land used in connection with the dwelling as a private residence.  
It includes a dwelling occupied as a private residence and structures used in connection with that dwelling.  
It does not include land used principally for farming or commercial activity.  
For example, a private yard used with the dwelling is included.  

**DEF.5** "insured" means you and residents of your household who are relatives or other persons in your care.  
It includes a resident relative who lives in your household.  
It does not include a person who is merely visiting your household.  
For example, a relative residing with you is included.  

**DEF.6** "insured location" means the residence premises and other locations used in connection with the residence premises.  
It includes land where an insured temporarily resides.  
It does not include property used principally for business activities.  
For example, a private driveway serving the residence premises is included.  

**DEF.7** "business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or other activity engaged in for compensation or financial gain.  
It includes the regular sale of goods or the performance of professional services.  
It does not include a hobby conducted without a profit motive.  
For example, operating a repair service for compensation is included.  

**DEF.8** "motor vehicle" means a self-propelled land vehicle or trailer designed for travel on public roads or subject to motor vehicle registration.  
It includes a private passenger automobile.  
It does not include a vehicle designed solely for use off public roads while located on an insured location.  
For example, a passenger car operated on a public road is included.  

**DEF.9** "residence employee" means a person employed by an insured to perform duties related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises.  
It includes a person performing domestic services or caring for the grounds.  
It does not include an employee of an independent contractor.  
For example, a person hired to clean the dwelling is included.  

**DEF.10** "bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease, including resulting death.  
It includes physical injury caused by an accident.  
It does not include purely economic loss without bodily harm, sickness, or disease.  
For example, a broken arm is included.  

**DEF.11** "property damage" means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.  
It includes damage that prevents tangible property from being used.  
It does not include loss involving only electronic data.  
For example, damage to a wall caused by water is included.  

**DEF.12** "sudden and accidental" means abrupt, unexpected, and unintended.  
It includes an event occurring without warning and without design.  
It does not include gradual deterioration, wear, corrosion, or seepage.  
For example, an unexpected rupture of a water pipe is included.  

**DEF.13** "vacant" means substantially empty of personal property necessary for normal occupancy.  
It includes a dwelling without customary furnishings or occupants.  
It does not include a furnished dwelling that is temporarily unoccupied.  
For example, an empty dwelling cleared of household belongings is included.  

**DEF.14** "named storm" means a tropical cyclone that has been assigned a name by a governmental meteorological authority.  
It includes a named hurricane or tropical storm.  
It does not include an ordinary thunderstorm that has not been assigned a name.  
For example, wind from a storm bearing an official name is included.

## I.A — Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the "dwelling" at the "residence premises" for direct physical loss to covered property. The dwelling includes structures attached to it.

**A.2** We cover materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises when they are intended for use in the construction, alteration, or repair of the dwelling. Such materials and supplies must be owned by you or by an insured.

**A.3** We cover permanently installed equipment that serves the dwelling. We also cover fixtures that are part of the dwelling.

**A.4** We cover additions and alterations made to the dwelling. Coverage applies while the additions and alterations remain part of the dwelling.

**A.5** We cover attached decks, porches, patios, and similar structures that are permanently connected to the dwelling. Coverage applies only while those structures serve the dwelling.

**A.6** We cover walls, floors, ceilings, roofs, foundations, and other structural components of the dwelling. Coverage includes materials that are integral to those components.

**A.7** We cover building equipment used to heat, cool, ventilate, or provide water to the dwelling. Coverage applies to equipment that is permanently installed.

**A.8** We cover property used to service the dwelling when that property is located at the residence premises. We do not cover property used principally for a business purpose.

**A.9** We cover direct physical loss caused by a peril unless an exclusion applies. Coverage applies only to property described in this section.

**A.10** We do not cover loss to land, including land on which the dwelling is located. We also do not cover the value of land after loss.

**A.11** We do not cover loss consisting of depreciation, deterioration, or loss of market value. We do not cover loss resulting solely from a defect in title.

**A.12** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, scratching, inherent vice, latent defect, or mechanical breakdown. We cover resulting direct physical loss only when that loss is otherwise covered.

**A.13** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or smog. We cover resulting direct physical loss only when that loss is otherwise covered.

**A.14** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings. We cover resulting direct physical loss only when that loss is otherwise covered.

**A.15** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an "insured" to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of a "loss." An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**A.16** "Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss, after deduction for depreciation. "Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality.

**A.17** The amount of insurance applicable to the dwelling must be at least ninety percent of its full replacement cost immediately before the loss. We determine full replacement cost from the cost to repair or replace the dwelling with like kind and quality.

**A.18** We will pay no more than the amount actually and necessarily spent to repair or replace damaged covered property. We will not pay for any increase in value resulting from repair or replacement.

**A.19** We may pay actual cash value for loss until repair or replacement is completed. After repair or replacement is completed, we will settle the additional covered amount in accordance with this section.

**A.20** We will not pay more than the cost to repair or replace damaged covered property at the same premises. We may settle loss by paying money, repairing property, replacing property, or taking all or part of the damaged property at an agreed value.

**A.21** We will not pay for repair or replacement with property of better kind or quality than the damaged property. We will not pay for any unnecessary or excessive cost.

**A.22** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, replacement, demolition, or occupancy of property. This exclusion applies whether the loss is caused directly or indirectly by enforcement action.

**A.23** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, and earth sinking, rising, or shifting. We cover direct physical loss by fire, explosion, or theft resulting from earth movement when that loss is otherwise covered.

**A.24** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies whether wind drives the water or not.

**A.25** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains. We also do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**A.26** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire or explosion resulting from pollutants when that loss is otherwise covered.

**A.27** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if another insured did not participate in or know of the intentional act.

**A.28** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, seizure, confiscation, destruction, or condemnation of covered property. We cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action when that loss is otherwise covered.

**A.29** We do not cover loss caused by power failure away from the residence premises. We cover direct physical loss caused by a power failure when the failure results from damage to covered property.

**A.30** We do not cover loss caused by war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military action. We also do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard.

**A.31** An insured must give us prompt notice of loss. The notice must identify the dwelling, describe the loss, and state the known circumstances of the loss.

**A.32** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must provide records and documents we reasonably request.

**A.33** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the residence premises and the damaged property.

**A.34** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged covered property when reasonably requested. The inventory must describe the property and show its actual cash value, replacement cost, and amount of loss.

**A.35** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably request. An insured must sign and provide a sworn statement containing information we reasonably require about the loss.

**A.36** We will pay only for direct physical loss to covered property. We will not pay for consequential loss, delay, loss of use, or loss of income under this coverage.

**A.37** Payment under this coverage will not reduce our right to recover from a person responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to prejudice that right.

## I.B — Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We provide coverage for "other structures" at the "residence premises." Coverage applies only to structures that are separated from the dwelling by clear space or connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

**B.2** Covered other structures include structures used for purposes related to the residence premises. The structure need not be occupied for coverage to apply.

**B.3** The limit for this coverage is ten percent of the applicable dwelling limit. This limit is the most we will pay for all covered loss to other structures arising from any loss occurrence.

**B.4** The Coverage B limit applies separately from the limit applicable to the dwelling. Payment under this coverage does not increase any other limit of liability.

**B.5** We will pay for direct physical loss to covered other structures caused by a covered cause of loss. Loss must occur at the residence premises.

**B.6** We will pay only for property that is part of a covered other structure. We do not cover personal property located in or on an other structure under this coverage.

**B.7** We do not cover land, including land on which an other structure is located. We do not cover the cost to restore or stabilize land.

**B.8** We do not cover an other structure that is rented or held for rental to a person who is not a tenant of the dwelling. This exclusion does not apply when the structure is used solely as a private garage.

**B.9** We do not cover an other structure used in whole or in part for "business." This exclusion does not apply when the structure is used solely as a private garage.

**B.10** We do not cover an other structure used to manufacture, process, or store property for business purposes. We do not cover loss arising from business operations conducted in or from an other structure.

**B.11** We do not cover a structure used principally for agricultural, farming, or commercial purposes. We do not cover property used in connection with those purposes.

**B.12** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, or compaction. We do cover ensuing direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**B.13** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered other structures from further damage.

**B.14** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss involving other structures. Notice must state the known circumstances of the loss and the location of the damaged property.

**B.15** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect damaged property from further loss. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses incurred to protect the property.

**B.16** An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property as often as reasonably necessary. An insured must preserve damaged property for our examination when reasonably possible.

**B.17** An insured must provide records and documents that we reasonably request to investigate a claim. An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of a claim.

**B.18** We may examine an insured under oath while not in the presence of another insured. An insured must sign the transcript of an examination when it is correctly recorded.

**B.19** We will determine the amount of loss to a covered other structure according to the applicable loss settlement provisions. We will not pay more than the least amount required by those provisions and the Coverage B limit.

**B.20** We may repair, rebuild, or replace damaged property with like kind and quality. We may make payment for the loss instead of repairing or replacing the property.

**B.21** We will not pay for any loss if an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact relating to the insurance, the covered property, or the claim. This condition applies before and after a loss.

**B.22** If we make payment for a covered loss, we obtain the rights of an insured to recover from a person responsible for that loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair those rights.

**B.23** Payment for a covered loss will be made to the persons legally entitled to receive it. We may adjust the loss with an insured having custody of the damaged property.

## I.C — Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover "personal property" owned or used by an "insured" while it is at the "residence premises" or at another location, subject to this Coverage C. The limit of liability for "Coverage C" is five percent of the Coverage A limit.

**C.2** We cover direct physical "loss" to covered personal property caused by a "covered cause of loss." Covered personal property must be owned or used by an insured at the time of loss.

**C.3** We cover property of others while it is at the residence premises if an insured requests the coverage before the loss occurs. We may adjust a loss directly with the owner of that property.

**C.4** We cover property of guests or residence employees while it is at the residence premises. This coverage applies only while the property is in the care of an insured.

**C.5** We cover personal property while it is temporarily removed from the residence premises. The property must remain subject to the ownership, use, or control requirements of this Coverage C.

**C.6** We cover personal property kept at a newly acquired residence that an insured intends to occupy as a residence. Coverage applies only while the property is being moved, stored, or kept for that intended occupancy.

**C.7** We cover personal property used by an insured in the ordinary conduct of household activities. Coverage does not depend on whether the property is used occasionally away from the residence premises.

**C.8** We cover clothing, furniture, appliances, and other household goods that are personal property. We cover those items only when they are not otherwise excluded by this Coverage C.

**C.9** We cover personal property that is borrowed by an insured for personal use. The person who owns the borrowed property retains any rights that person may have against us.

**C.10** We cover personal property in the possession of an insured while it is being repaired, serviced, or cleaned. Coverage applies only to direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**C.11** We do not cover accounts, bills, currency, deeds, evidence of debt, letters of credit, manuscripts, passports, securities, or similar valuable papers. We also do not cover data stored on paper, film, electronic media, or any other medium.

**C.12** We do not cover property used primarily for a business conducted by an insured. This exclusion does not apply to personal property used solely for the personal convenience of an insured.

**C.13** We do not cover merchandise, inventory, samples, supplies, or property held for sale. We also do not cover property held for delivery or transportation for a business purpose.

**C.14** We do not cover property of roomers, boarders, or tenants. We also do not cover property of others that is rented to, loaned to, or entrusted to an insured for a business purpose.

**C.15** We do not cover animals, birds, fish, or other living creatures. We do cover property used to care for an animal when that property is otherwise covered.

**C.16** We do not cover aircraft and parts intended for installation in aircraft. We also do not cover devices designed for aerial navigation or operation.

**C.17** We do not cover watercraft and parts intended for installation in watercraft. This exclusion applies whether the watercraft is owned, rented, borrowed, or entrusted to an insured.

**C.18** We do not cover motor vehicles and parts intended for installation in motor vehicles. We also do not cover property designed for use with motor vehicles when it is subject to registration or licensing.

**C.19** We do not cover property intended for use in the operation of a motor vehicle, aircraft, or watercraft. We do cover portable personal property that is not permanently attached to the excluded conveyance.

**C.20** We do not cover property separately described and specifically insured by another policy. This exclusion applies only to the extent that the other insurance provides coverage for the loss.

**C.21** We do not cover property that is illegal to possess, transport, or sell. We also do not cover property seized or held by a governmental authority.

**C.22** We do not cover property that is contraband. We do not cover property acquired through theft, fraud, or any other unlawful act.

**C.23** We do not cover loss caused by or resulting from wear and tear, marring, deterioration, or inherent vice. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss that results from such condition.

**C.24** We do not cover loss caused by rust, corrosion, mold, wet rot, dry rot, or decay. We do cover resulting direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss.

**C.25** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, electrical failure, or electronic failure. We do cover loss caused by fire, smoke, or another covered cause of loss that follows such failure.

**C.26** We do not cover loss caused by settling, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of personal property. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss that results from such movement.

**C.27** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke from industrial operations, or pollutants. We do cover direct physical loss caused by smoke from a sudden and accidental event.

**C.28** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of "pollutants." Pollutants include irritants, contaminants, fumes, acids, chemicals, and waste.

**C.29** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. An insured must protect covered property from further damage.

**C.30** We do not cover loss caused by intentional acts committed by an insured. This exclusion applies even if the resulting loss differs in kind or degree from that intended or expected.

**C.31** We do not cover loss caused by the intentional concealment or fraudulent transfer of covered property. We do not cover loss caused by an insured who voluntarily parts with property because of a fraudulent scheme.

**C.32** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, seizure, confiscation, destruction, or order. We do cover direct physical loss caused by fire resulting from governmental action.

**C.33** We do not cover loss caused by war, undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or military force. We do not cover loss caused by any consequence of those events.

**C.34** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, nuclear radiation, or radioactive contamination. This exclusion applies whether the event is controlled or uncontrolled.

**C.35** We do not cover loss caused by ordinance, law, regulation, or order requiring testing, monitoring, cleanup, removal, containment, treatment, detoxification, or neutralization of pollutants. We also do not cover the cost of complying with such requirements.

**C.36** We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain heat, water, electricity, or another utility service. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss that follows the failure.

**C.37** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective systems when an insured fails to use reasonable care. Reasonable care includes maintaining heat or shutting off the water supply and draining the system.

**C.38** We do not cover loss caused by discharge or overflow from a sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, or related system. We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through those systems.

**C.39** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of those sources. We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground.

**C.40** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, earth sinking, rising, shifting, or expansion. This exclusion applies whether the earth movement is caused by natural or human forces.

**C.41** We do not cover loss caused by power surge, voltage fluctuation, or interruption of utility service occurring away from the residence premises. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss that follows such event.

**C.42** We do not cover loss caused by fungi, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or virus. We do cover direct physical loss caused by a covered cause of loss that results from those conditions when otherwise covered.

**C.43** We do not cover the cost to inspect, test, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize pollutants. We do not cover the cost to assess the effects of pollutants.

**C.44** We will pay no more than the applicable limit of liability for any loss. The limit of liability is the most we will pay regardless of the number of insureds, items of property, claims, or events involved in the loss.

**C.45** We will not pay more than the amount required to repair or replace covered property with property of like kind and quality. We may pay the actual cash value of the property at the time of loss.

**C.46** We may elect to repair, replace, or take all or part of the damaged property at the agreed or appraised value. An insured must make the property available to us when we request it.

**C.47** We may pay for the loss in money. We may also provide replacement property or arrange for repair by a person of our choice.

**C.48** We will determine the value of covered property at the time of loss. We will consider its condition, age, useful life, and available market information.

**C.49** An insured must give us prompt notice of loss. The notice must identify the property involved and describe the known facts of the loss.

**C.50** An insured must notify the appropriate law enforcement authority when a theft, disappearance, or other criminal act is involved. An insured must cooperate with any investigation of the loss.

**C.51** An insured must protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses and make the damaged property available for inspection.

**C.52** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged, destroyed, or stolen property. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss for each item.

**C.53** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, estimates, and other documents that support the claim when we request them. An insured must permit us to make copies of those materials.

**C.54** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must submit to examination under oath when we request it.

**C.55** An insured must sign a sworn statement of loss when we request it. The statement must provide the information reasonably necessary for us to investigate and adjust the claim.

**C.56** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, and defense of any claim involving covered property. An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or withhold material information.

**C.57** An insured must not abandon covered property to us unless we agree in writing. We may take possession of damaged property after payment of the loss.

**C.58** An insured must preserve any evidence related to the cause of loss. An insured must not alter, dispose of, or repair evidence before we have a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**C.59** We may inspect the residence premises and the covered property at any reasonable time after notice of loss. Our inspection does not constitute acceptance of coverage or waiver of any condition.

**C.60** We may require an appraisal when we and an insured fail to agree on the amount of loss. Each party must select a competent appraiser and cooperate in the appraisal process.

**C.61** We may deny coverage when an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented a material fact relating to this Coverage C or a loss. This condition applies before and after a loss.

**C.62** We will not provide a benefit to an insured that has already been paid by another source for the same loss. We may reduce our payment by amounts recoverable from another source.

**C.63** If other insurance covers the same loss, we will pay only our share of the loss. Our share is determined by the relationship of our applicable limit to the total applicable limits of all insurance covering the loss.

**C.64** If we make a payment under this Coverage C, we may require an assignment of any rights of recovery against a responsible person. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**C.65** Our right of recovery does not apply against an insured. Our right of recovery does not apply against a person whom an insured has waived recovery rights against before loss in writing.

**C.66** We may settle a claim with an owner of property of others that we cover. Payment to that owner discharges our obligation for that property to the extent of the payment.

**C.67** We may request proof of ownership for any claimed item of personal property. Proof may include receipts, records, photographs, warranties, appraisals, or other reliable evidence.

**C.68** We may require an insured to identify property that remains undamaged after a loss. An insured must separate damaged property from undamaged property when reasonably possible.

**C.69** We will pay only for direct physical loss to covered personal property. We will not pay for sentimental value, emotional value, or any value that cannot be supported by reliable evidence.

**C.70** We will not pay for loss that is not accidental from the standpoint of an insured. We will not pay for loss that is expected or intended by an insured.

**C.71** We will not pay for costs incurred solely to improve, upgrade, alter, or redesign covered property. We will pay only the amount necessary to restore the covered property after a covered loss.

**C.72** We will not pay for expenses incurred before a loss to prevent a possible future loss. We may pay reasonable expenses incurred after a covered loss to protect covered property from further damage.

**C.73** We will not pay for property that cannot be identified as covered personal property. An insured must establish that the property existed and was owned or used by an insured before the loss.

**C.74** We will not pay for loss to property while it is in the custody of a carrier, warehouse, or storage facility when the loss is covered by that party. We may pay only the portion of loss that is not collectible from that party.

**C.75** We will not pay for loss to property arising from voluntary transfer of possession when an insured is induced by trick, false pretense, or fraud. This exclusion applies even if the person obtaining the property uses a false identity.

**C.76** We will not pay for loss caused by confiscation, detention, or destruction of property by customs or tax authorities. We will not pay for loss caused by the enforcement of a law or regulation.

**C.77** We will not pay for loss involving property used in connection with unlawful activity by an insured. This exclusion applies only if the unlawful activity contributes to the loss.

**C.78** We may request that an insured provide access to records concerning the acquisition, maintenance, repair, or value of covered property. An insured must provide available records that are reasonably related to the claim.

**C.79** We may require an insured to submit to recorded statements concerning the loss. An insured must answer questions truthfully and provide information within that insured's knowledge.

**C.80** Payment for a covered loss will be made to the insured entitled to receive payment. We may include any person with a legal interest in the covered property on a payment.

**C.81** No action may be brought against us unless an insured has complied with the duties imposed by this Coverage C. Compliance with those duties is a condition precedent to recovery for a covered loss.

## I.D — Coverage D — Fair Rental Value and E — Additional Living Expense

**D.1** We cover loss of "fair rental value" when a "covered loss" makes the "rented part" of the "residence premises" unfit for use.  
We pay only when you rented that part to others or held that part for rental.  
Payment is subject to the applicable limit of liability and all policy terms.  

**D.2** Fair rental value means the rental income you would have received from the rented part.  
We deduct expenses that do not continue while the rented part is unfit for use.  
We do not deduct expenses that you must continue to pay.  

**D.3** We pay fair rental value only for the time reasonably required to repair or replace the rented part.  
That time begins when the covered loss makes the rented part unfit for use.  
That time ends when the rented part is fit for use or available for rental.  

**D.4** We do not cover fair rental value that results from a tenant's failure to occupy the rented part.  
We do not cover loss caused by a tenant's default, cancellation, or failure to pay rent.  
We do not cover loss caused by an inability to obtain a tenant.  

**D.5** We cover "additional living expense" when a covered loss makes the residence premises unfit for use.  
Additional living expense means the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you so that your household may maintain its normal standard of living.  
We pay only expenses that are reasonable and necessary.  

**D.6** We pay additional living expense only while the residence premises are unfit for use.

**G.18** An insured must notify us promptly of a loss. An insured must notify the appropriate authorities when the law requires reporting of a loss.

**G.19** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. We pay reasonable expenses incurred for necessary measures taken solely to protect covered property from further damage.

**G.20** An insured must keep an accurate record of repair expenses incurred after a loss. An insured must make the damaged property available for our inspection before its disposal, repair, or replacement when reasonably possible.

**G.21** An insured must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property when we request it. The inventory must show the quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss.

**G.22** An insured must provide records and documents supporting the inventory when we request them. An insured must permit us to examine those records and documents.

**G.23** An insured must submit a signed, sworn proof of loss when we request it. The proof of loss must state the information we reasonably need to determine our obligations.

**G.24** An insured must show the interest of all insureds and others in property involved in a loss. An insured must show all encumbrances and other interests affecting that property.

**G.25** An insured must state the actual cash value of each item involved in a loss when we request it. An insured must state the amount of loss claimed.

**G.26** An insured must state the cause and circumstances of a loss when we request it. An insured must identify persons believed to have knowledge of relevant facts.

**G.27** An insured must provide information concerning other insurance that may apply to a loss. An insured must provide copies of applicable policies when we request them.

**G.28** We will adjust a loss with an insured named in the Declarations. Payment to that insured satisfies our obligation for the amount paid.

**G.29** If more than one insured has an interest in covered property, we may pay any insured named in the Declarations. Payment does not determine the ownership interests of insureds or others.

**G.30** We do not cover a loss for the benefit of an insured who has caused or arranged the loss. This condition applies to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**G.31** If property involved in a loss is recovered, an insured must notify us promptly. An insured must permit us to inspect recovered property.

**G.32** If we pay for lost or stolen property and the property is recovered, we may take possession of that property. An insured must transfer to us the rights necessary to enable us to take possession.

**G.33** If recovered property is retained by an insured, the amount of loss will be reduced by the value of the recovered property. We may determine that value based on its condition when recovered.

**G.34** An insured may not bring legal action against us unless the insured has complied with all policy conditions. An insured may bring legal action only concerning a dispute arising under this policy.

**G.35** If we make a payment under this policy, we are subrogated to the rights of the person to whom payment is made. That person must do nothing after a loss to impair our rights of recovery.

**G.36** An insured must cooperate with us in exercising our rights of recovery. An insured must execute documents and take actions reasonably necessary to preserve those rights.

**G.37** An insured may waive rights of recovery against another person or organization in writing before a loss. An insured may not waive our rights of recovery after a loss without our written consent.

**G.38** If other insurance applies to a covered loss, we will pay only our share of the loss. Our share is determined by the proportion that our limit bears to the total applicable limits.

**G.39** We may cancel this policy by mailing or delivering written notice to the named insured. We will state the reason for cancellation in the notice when required by applicable law.

**G.40** If we cancel this policy for nonpayment of premium, we will mail or deliver notice at least fifteen days before cancellation takes effect. Notice may be delivered to the named insured or mailed to the mailing address shown in the Declarations.

**G.41** If we cancel this policy for a reason other than nonpayment of premium, we will mail or deliver notice at least forty-five days before cancellation takes effect. We may cancel only as permitted by applicable law.

**G.42** The named insured may cancel this policy by returning it to us or by giving us written notice. The notice must state when cancellation is to take effect.

**G.43** If this policy is cancelled, we will return any unearned premium due to the named insured. The return premium may be calculated on a basis permitted by applicable law.

**G.44** We may elect not to renew this policy. If we elect not to renew, we will mail or deliver written notice at least forty-five days before the expiration of this policy.

**G.45** Notice of cancellation or nonrenewal mailed to the address shown in the Declarations is sufficient proof of notice. Proof of mailing is sufficient proof of notice.

**G.46** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by nuclear hazard. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**G.47** “Nuclear hazard” means a nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. Nuclear hazard includes any consequence of a nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination.

**G.48** We do not cover loss caused by the discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants unless coverage is provided elsewhere in this policy. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**G.49** “Pollutants” means solid, liquid, gaseous, or thermal irritants or contaminants. Pollutants include smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

**G.50** An insured must comply with all duties and conditions of this policy. Compliance by an insured does not create coverage where coverage is otherwise excluded or limited.
