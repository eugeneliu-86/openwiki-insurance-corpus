# DP 04 95 Water Backup — Dwelling Property (Edition 2021-05)




## W.0 — Preamble and Attachment

This endorsement modifies the insurance provided by your policy for water backup. We provide that insurance subject to the terms of this endorsement.

When attached to your policy, this endorsement forms part of your policy. We apply its provisions only while it remains part of your policy.

We read this endorsement together with your policy. The provisions of this endorsement apply only to the insurance that it modifies.

If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of your policy, this endorsement controls. All provisions of your policy that are not changed by this endorsement remain in effect.

This endorsement does not change the identity of an insured. An insured must comply with the duties imposed by your policy and this endorsement.

The words “you” and “your” refer to the insured shown in your policy. The words “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the insurer providing the insurance.

Words and phrases that have a special meaning in your policy retain that meaning unless this endorsement changes their meaning. We apply those meanings when interpreting this endorsement.

This endorsement does not provide insurance for a loss that is not otherwise subject to the terms of your policy. We provide insurance only as stated in this endorsement and your policy.

We do not waive a policy provision by attaching this endorsement. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

An insured must provide information that we reasonably request to determine whether this endorsement applies. We may rely on information provided by an insured in administering this endorsement.

This endorsement applies to property and interests insured under your policy only as stated in its provisions. It does not create an interest in property for a person who is not an insured.

We may enforce the provisions of this endorsement in the same manner as the provisions of your policy. An insured must perform duties under this endorsement when they apply.

This endorsement is effective only when we make it part of your policy. Its attachment does not change the policy period or the named insured.

## W.1 — Coverage Provided

**W.1** We cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer or drain. We also cover direct physical loss caused by water or waterborne material that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment.

**W.2** Subject to the limit insubject to the limit in (see W.2, Limit of Liability), we will pay for covered loss under this endorsement. This coverage is subject to all applicable terms of the policy except as modified by this endorsement.

**W.3** The water or waterborne material must be the direct cause of loss to covered property. Loss that results only from dampness, deterioration, corrosion, settling, or wear and tear is not covered.

**W.4** We cover damage to that part of covered property directly affected by the water or waterborne material. We do not cover the cost to replace undamaged property solely to match property that was damaged.

**W.5** We cover reasonable emergency measures taken to protect covered property from further direct physical loss after a covered water backup. Those measures must be necessary, reasonable, and directly related to the covered loss.

**W.6** An insured must use reasonable means to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**W.7** An insured must provide prompt notice of a loss to us or our authorized representative. Notice must identify the location of the loss and describe the circumstances known to the insured.

**W.8** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. An insured must provide records, receipts, estimates, photographs, and other information we reasonably request.

**W.9** We may inspect the damaged property before repairs are made. An insured must allow us reasonable access to the property and to records relating to the loss.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters the dwelling through an opening in a roof, wall, door, window, or foundation. This exclusion applies whether the opening existed before the loss or resulted from faulty maintenance.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these. This exclusion applies whether the water is driven by wind or otherwise.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies to water that exerts pressure on, seeps into, or leaks through a building, foundation, pavement, sidewalk, driveway, or other structure.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an insured to maintain a sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, or related equipment. This exclusion applies when the failure to maintain contributes to the loss.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by repeated seepage or leakage of water or waterborne material. This exclusion applies when the seepage or leakage occurs over time and is not sudden and accidental.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. We do not cover the cost to test for, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize pollutants.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or virus. We also do not cover loss consisting of remediation, removal, testing, monitoring, or treatment of those conditions.

**W.17** We do not cover the cost to repair, replace, or improve a sewer, drain, sump, sump pump, or related equipment that failed, overflowed, or discharged. We do cover resulting direct physical loss to covered property when caused by a covered water backup.

**W.18** We do not cover loss to property that is not covered property. We do not cover loss to property owned by others unless that property is covered under the policy.

**W.19** We do not cover loss resulting from an act or decision, including the failure to act or decide, of a governmental body. This exclusion applies whether the act or decision is lawful, negligent, or otherwise.

**W.20** We do not cover loss resulting from faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We do cover ensuing direct physical loss caused by a covered water backup if the ensuing loss is not otherwise excluded.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by intentional conduct of an insured. This exclusion applies even if another cause contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve covered property at and after the time of loss. This exclusion does not apply to reasonable emergency measures taken to protect covered property.

**W.23** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath as often as we reasonably require. An insured must sign the examination transcript if it is accurately recorded.

**W.24** We may require an insured to provide a signed statement describing the loss. The statement must identify the property involved, the cause of loss, and the extent of damage known to the insured.

**W.25** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must not dispose of damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it.

**W.26** An insured must submit to us a claim for covered loss when we request it. The claim must include supporting documentation reasonably available to the insured.

**W.27** We will determine the amount of covered loss under the valuation provisions of the policy. We will not pay for any amount that is not supported by the facts of the loss and the applicable policy terms.

**W.28** Payment for covered loss under this endorsement does not increase any other limit applicable to the same loss. No insured may recover more than the amount of the direct physical loss sustained.

**W.29** We may settle a covered loss with an insured or with a person legally entitled to receive payment. Payment by us will discharge our obligation to the extent of that payment.

**W.30** If there is other insurance that applies to the same loss, we will pay only our share of the covered loss. Our share will be determined in accordance with the other insurance provisions of the policy.

**W.31** If we make payment for covered loss, we are subrogated to the rights of an insured against a person responsible for that loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.32** An insured must cooperate with us in the recovery of payments from a person responsible for a covered loss. Any recovery will be apportioned in accordance with the policy and applicable law.

**W.33** This endorsement applies only to loss occurring during the policy period. The date of loss is the date when the covered water backup first causes direct physical loss to covered property.

**W.34** Coverage under this endorsement applies only at a premises shown in the policy. The premises must be used in a manner consistent with the policy.

**W.35** This endorsement does not change any condition, exclusion, or limitation that is not expressly modified by its terms. All other provisions of the policy remain in effect.

## W.2 — Limit of Liability

**W.1** “Water Backup Coverage” is subject to this “Limit of Liability.” This Limit of Liability applies only to a “covered loss” under Water Backup Coverage.

**W.2** The most we will pay is five thousand dollars. This is the Limit of Liability for Water Backup Coverage.

**W.3** The Limit of Liability applies to all covered loss arising from water backup or sump overflow. It applies regardless of the insureds, claims, or damaged property involved.

**W.4** All payments we make for covered loss under Water Backup Coverage are subject to the Limit of Liability. Payment of a covered loss does not increase the Limit of Liability.

**W.5** Payments made for damage to property reduce the amount available for other covered loss under Water Backup Coverage. We have no obligation to pay covered loss after the Limit of Liability has been exhausted.

**W.6** We will not make duplicate payment for the same covered loss. Any payment we make for a covered loss will be applied only to that loss.

**W.7** We will not pay more than the amount of covered loss sustained by an insured. The Limit of Liability does not create coverage for loss that is not otherwise covered.

**W.8** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the amount of covered loss. An insured must provide information we reasonably request to evaluate the loss.

**W.9** An insured must protect damaged property from further loss. We will not pay for avoidable additional damage caused by an insured's failure to protect the property.

**W.10** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine records relating to the loss.

**W.11** An insured must not abandon property to us. We may take possession of damaged property after payment, subject to our rights and the rights of any other person having an interest in the property.

**W.12** Any salvage value applicable to a covered loss will be considered in determining the amount we will pay. We may retain salvage to the extent permitted by law.

**W.13** We may require an insured to assign rights of recovery against a person responsible for a covered loss. An insured must do nothing after loss to impair those rights.

**W.14** Any recovery obtained after we make payment for a covered loss will be shared according to the respective interests of the insured and us. Our right to recovery is limited to the payment we made.

**W.15** An insured must establish the portion of loss caused by water backup or sump overflow. We will not pay for any portion of loss that an insured cannot establish as a covered loss.

**W.16** If covered loss and loss not covered by Water Backup Coverage affect the same property, we will determine the amount payable based on the portion of loss caused by a covered peril. We will not pay for loss attributable to an excluded cause.

**W.17** Our payment for covered loss is not an admission of liability for any other loss. A payment does not waive any right or defense available to us.

**W.18** No representation by us or by an insured changes the Limit of Liability. A change to the Limit of Liability must be made by us in writing.

**W.19** An insured's request for payment does not increase the Limit of Liability. Notice of a loss does not create coverage or expand the amount we will pay.

**W.20** Our investigation of a loss does not waive any condition of Water Backup Coverage. Our inspection of property does not determine that the loss is covered.

**W.21** We may make payment to an insured, a person legally entitled to receive payment, or both. Payment made in good faith discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.22** We will not pay an insured for loss for which that insured has already received payment from us. We will not pay more than the covered loss because more than one insured has an interest in the property.

**W.23** Repair, replacement, restoration, or disposal of damaged property does not increase the Limit of Liability. The method used to resolve a covered loss does not change the Limit of Liability.

**W.24** We may require reasonable documentation of repair, replacement, or restoration costs. We may withhold payment for costs that are not supported by documentation we reasonably request.

**W.25** An insured must promptly notify us of any recovery, settlement, or payment relating to a covered loss. An insured must preserve evidence relating to the cause and amount of the loss.

**W.26** We may adjust a payment if information available after payment shows that the payment was made in error. An insured must return any payment that exceeds the amount properly payable.

**W.27** We will not pay for an expense that does not result from a covered loss. We will not pay for an expense incurred solely to improve property.

**W.28** An insured must submit to examination when reasonably requested by us. An insured must provide records and other evidence needed to determine the amount of covered loss.

**W.29** Any disagreement concerning the amount of covered loss does not increase the Limit of Liability. Our obligation remains subject to the terms applicable to Water Backup Coverage.

**W.30** The Limit of Liability applies whether or not an insured seeks payment for all covered damage. We will not pay any amount in excess of the Limit of Liability.

## W.3 — Deductible

**W.1** The "water backup endorsement deductible" is one thousand dollars. We apply the deductible to each covered loss caused by water backup.

**W.2** We subtract the deductible from the amount otherwise payable for covered water backup loss. We do not make payment for covered water backup loss that does not exceed the deductible.

**W.3** You are responsible for the portion of covered water backup loss equal to the deductible. We do not reimburse you for that portion of the loss.

**W.4** We apply the deductible after determining the amount of covered water backup loss. We apply the deductible before making payment for that loss.

**W.5** The deductible applies whether we pay you or another person entitled to payment. The method by which we settle covered water backup loss does not affect application of the deductible.

**W.6** The deductible applies whether we settle covered water backup loss by payment, repair, replacement, or another authorized method. We do not pay the deductible as a separate item of loss.

**W.7** The deductible does not create coverage for property or damage that we do not otherwise cover. The deductible does not change an exclusion or limitation that applies to the loss.

**W.8** We do not include excluded damage in determining the amount of covered water backup loss. We do not apply the deductible to an amount that is not covered.

**W.9** The deductible does not increase the amount we will pay for covered water backup loss. Our payment remains subject to all applicable terms and limitations.

**W.10** We apply the deductible to the covered water backup loss, rather than to each item of damaged property. Damage to related property arising from the same covered water backup loss is included in that loss.

**W.11** We apply the deductible only once when we make payments for the same covered water backup loss. Payment made in portions does not cause the deductible to apply again.

**W.12** A payment for covered water backup loss does not waive our right to apply the deductible. Our investigation of a loss does not waive the deductible.

**W.13** An estimate, inspection, or discussion of covered water backup loss does not change the deductible. We apply the deductible when determining the payment due.

**W.14** An insured must provide information reasonably necessary for us to determine the amount of covered water backup loss. An insured must cooperate with our investigation of the loss.

**W.15** An insured must identify damaged property and provide available information concerning the water backup loss. We may rely on that information when determining the covered amount.

**W.16** A request for payment does not alter the deductible. A disagreement concerning the amount of loss does not suspend application of the deductible.

**W.17** We determine the amount payable after applying the deductible to covered water backup loss. We do not pay an amount that remains within the deductible.

**W.18** The deductible applies to covered water backup loss regardless of whether the loss is repaired. The deductible applies if damaged property is replaced rather than repaired.

**W.19** The deductible remains applicable if you retain damaged property. The deductible remains applicable if we pay for covered water backup loss without taking possession of damaged property.

**W.20** We apply the deductible without regard to any amount you may recover from another person. Any recovery rights relating to covered water backup loss remain subject to applicable terms.

## W.4 — What Remains Excluded

**W.1** We do not cover loss caused by seepage, leakage, or overflow that occurs gradually or continuously. We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, corrosion, decay, settling, or latent defect.

**W.2** We do not cover loss caused by neglect to use reasonable means to save and preserve property from further damage. An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss.

**W.3** We do not cover loss caused by water that enters the dwelling through an opening not caused by direct physical loss. We do not cover loss caused by rain, snow, sleet, or ice entering through doors, windows, walls, roofs, or foundations.

**W.4** Flood and surface water remains excluded.

**W.5** We do not cover loss caused by water below the surface of the ground. This exclusion applies whether the water exerts pressure on, seeps through, or leaks through a building, foundation, pavement, or other structure.

**W.6** We do not cover loss caused by waterborne material, mud, silt, sediment, or debris. This exclusion applies whether the material is carried or deposited by water.

**W.7** We do not cover loss caused by a tidal wave, tsunami, storm surge, or overflow of a body of water. We do not cover loss caused by spray from any of these conditions.

**W.8** We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up from a drain when the backup results from inadequate maintenance, repair, or replacement. We do not cover loss caused by an obstruction that you knew about and did not correct.

**W.9** We do not cover loss caused by a plumbing system that is not connected to the insured location. We do not cover loss caused by water originating outside the described dwelling.

**W.10** We do not cover loss caused by discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants. Pollutants include substances that contaminate or irritate property.

**W.11** We do not cover loss caused by discharge of waste from an industrial, commercial, agricultural, or manufacturing operation. This exclusion applies whether the waste is liquid, solid, gaseous, thermal, or biological.

**W.12** We do not cover loss caused by water from a swimming pool, spa, fountain, pond, or similar feature that is not part of the dwelling plumbing system. We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain such property.

**W.13** We do not cover loss caused by an underground storage tank, cistern, well, or similar container. We do not cover loss caused by the collapse, rupture, or leakage of such property.

**W.14** We do not cover loss caused by a sump, sump pump, drain pump, or similar equipment that fails because of wear, deterioration, or lack of maintenance. We do not cover loss caused by failure of electrical service to that equipment unless the failure results from a covered cause of loss.

**W.15** We do not cover loss caused by a defective, inadequate, or improperly installed drain, sewer, plumbing fixture, or appliance. We do not cover loss caused by improper design, construction, remodeling, or repair.

**W.16** We do not cover loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, automatic fire protective sprinkler, or household appliance system while the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied. An insured must use reasonable care to maintain heat in the dwelling or shut off the water supply and drain the system.

**W.17** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of an insured to repair a known plumbing leak. We do not cover loss caused by repeated leakage from the same source.

**W.18** We do not cover loss caused by mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria. We do not cover loss caused by remediation, testing, monitoring, containment, removal, or disposal of these conditions.

**W.19** We do not cover loss caused by odor, staining, discoloration, or cosmetic damage. We do not cover loss caused by loss of use resulting solely from such damage.

**W.20** We do not cover loss caused by rust, oxidation, corrosion, or electrolysis. We do not cover loss caused by deterioration of pipes, drains, fixtures, appliances, or connections.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including sinkhole collapse, landslide, subsidence, settling, expansion, contraction, or earth sinking. We do not cover loss caused by cracking, bulging, or expansion of foundations, walls, floors, ceilings, or pavements.

**W.22** We do not cover loss caused by collapse unless the collapse is directly caused by a covered cause of loss. We do not cover loss caused by defective materials, defective methods, or inadequate support.

**W.23** We do not cover loss caused by mechanical breakdown, electrical breakdown, or failure of an appliance or system. We do not cover loss caused by a short circuit, arcing, or power surge unless direct physical loss is otherwise covered.

**W.24** We do not cover loss caused by interruption of power, water, sewer, drainage, heating, or other utility service. This exclusion applies whether the interruption occurs on or away from the insured location.

**W.25** We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of an ordinance, law, regulation, or governmental requirement. We do not cover loss caused by the cost to test, monitor, clean up, remove, contain, treat, detoxify, or neutralize property.

**W.26** We do not cover loss caused by governmental action, seizure, destruction, confiscation, or condemnation of property. We do not cover loss caused by delay resulting from governmental action.

**W.27** We do not cover loss caused by war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, or military action. We do not cover loss caused by discharge of a nuclear weapon.

**W.28** We do not cover loss caused by nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination. We do not cover loss caused by any consequence of these conditions.

**W.29** We do not cover loss caused by intentional loss. An insured must not intentionally cause or arrange for loss.

**W.30** We do not cover loss caused by concealment, misrepresentation, fraud, or false statement by an insured. This exclusion applies to a material fact relating to the insurance, the property, or a loss.

**W.31** We do not cover loss to property owned by a tenant, boarder, roomer, guest, or other person who is not an insured. We do not cover loss to property for which an insured has no insurable interest.

**W.32** We do not cover loss to property used primarily for business purposes. We do not cover loss to merchandise, samples, tools, equipment, or materials held for sale or delivery.

**W.33** We do not cover loss to property used for illegal activity. We do not cover loss arising from an insured's knowing violation of law.

**W.34** We do not cover loss to property while it is in the care, custody, or control of a carrier, warehouse, repair facility, or similar custodian. We do not cover loss caused by the custodian's acts or omissions.

**W.35** We do not cover loss caused by theft, conversion, disappearance, or shortage. We do not cover loss caused by unexplained loss of property.

**W.36** We do not cover loss to landscaping, lawns, trees, shrubs, plants, or growing crops. We do not cover loss caused by water damage to land or land improvements.

**W.37** We do not cover loss to fences, retaining walls, patios, walks, driveways, or paved surfaces caused by settling, cracking, heaving, erosion, or water pressure. We do not cover loss to outdoor property caused by earth movement.

**W.38** We do not cover loss to foundations, footings, piers, bulkheads, or retaining walls caused by water pressure or water seepage. We do not cover loss caused by movement or settling of the ground.

**W.39** We do not cover loss caused by defective drainage away from the dwelling. An insured must maintain gutters, downspouts, drains, grading, and drainage systems in reasonable repair.

**W.40** We do not cover loss caused by failure to maintain the roof, walls, windows, doors, foundation, plumbing, or appliances. We do not cover loss caused by deferred maintenance.

**W.41** We do not cover loss caused by faulty workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, or maintenance. We do not cover loss to the part of property on which the faulty work was performed.

**W.42** We do not cover loss caused by defective planning, zoning, development, surveying, siting, design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation, remodeling, grading, compaction, or maintenance. We do not cover loss to the part of property affected by the defect.

**W.43** We do not cover the cost to replace property because of matching, uniformity, or aesthetic appearance. We do not cover loss caused by the inability to obtain materials of like kind and quality.

**W.44** We do not cover loss caused by delay, loss of market, loss of use, loss of value, or loss of income. We do not cover any consequential or indirect loss.

**W.45** We do not cover expenses incurred to determine whether a loss is covered unless we request those expenses in writing. We do not cover expenses incurred before an insured discovers direct physical loss.

**W.46** We do not cover costs to repair, replace, or improve a drain, sewer, plumbing fixture, appliance, or system solely to prevent future loss. We do not cover costs to install protective devices or backup systems.

**W.47** A backwater valve is not required where it is not otherwise required by law or by a written agreement with us. We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to maintain a backwater valve that is installed.

**W.48** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to comply with a reasonable request for inspection, repair, or preservation of damaged property. An insured must make damaged property available for inspection when reasonably requested.

**W.49** We do not cover loss when an insured knowingly permits a condition that substantially increases the chance of loss. An insured must promptly report a condition that creates an immediate risk of additional damage.

**W.50** We do not cover loss occurring after an insured transfers ownership of the dwelling. We do not cover loss occurring after an insured abandons the property without reasonable protection.

**W.51** We do not cover loss to property not located at the insured location when the loss occurs. We do not cover loss to property removed from the insured location for storage, repair, sale, or disposal.

**W.52** We do not cover loss involving property that is not shown or described as insured property. We do not cover loss involving property acquired by an insured but not subject to this insurance.

**W.53** We do not cover loss caused by animals, birds, insects, rodents, vermin, or domestic pets. We do not cover loss caused by nesting, infestation, chewing, scratching, or discharge from animals.

**W.54** We do not cover loss caused by smog, smoke, vapor, fumes, or emissions from agricultural, industrial, commercial, or manufacturing activity. We do not cover loss caused by contamination of water or property.

**W.55** We do not cover loss caused by the failure of a septic system, leach field, cesspool, or similar waste disposal system. We do not cover loss caused by overflow, leakage, or discharge from such property.

**W.56** The "named storm deductible minimum" does not create coverage for an excluded loss. The "appurtenant structures percentage" does not apply to property for which coverage is excluded.

**W.57** The "Coverage C percentage" does not apply to an excluded loss. The "Coverage D limit as a percentage of Coverage A" does not apply to loss that we do not cover.

**W.58** The "rubble removal percentage" does not apply to debris resulting from an excluded loss. We do not cover costs to remove debris when the underlying loss is excluded.

**W.59** We do not cover any loss that results from a combination of excluded causes of loss. This exclusion applies whether an excluded cause acts alone or in any sequence with another cause.

**W.60** We do not cover loss that is excluded by these provisions regardless of any other event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss. An insured must establish that direct physical loss is not otherwise excluded.

## W.5 — Conditions

**W.1** These conditions apply to a loss caused by "water backup" sustained by an "insured". An insured must comply with these conditions before we are obligated to make payment.

**W.2** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, and defense of a claim. An insured must provide information that we reasonably request.

**W.3** An insured must give us prompt notice of a "loss". Notice may be given by an insured or by a person acting on behalf of an insured.

**W.4** Notice of loss must identify the affected property, the location of the loss, and the known circumstances of the loss. An insured must provide contact information that permits us to communicate concerning the claim.

**W.5** An insured must report a loss within thirty days after the loss. Failure to provide timely notice may prejudice our ability to investigate the loss.

**W.6** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect "covered property" from further damage. An insured must preserve the condition of the property when doing so is reasonably possible.

**W.7** An insured must make reasonable emergency repairs necessary to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep records of the repairs and related expenses.

**W.8** We will not require an insured to leave property exposed to additional damage while awaiting our inspection. An insured must permit us to inspect emergency repairs when we reasonably request inspection.

**W.9** An insured must retain damaged property until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must not discard damaged property unless retention is unsafe or impracticable.

**W.10** An insured must provide photographs, recordings, receipts, invoices, estimates, and other evidence relating to the loss if available. An insured must identify the source of records that are not in the insured's possession.

**W.11** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require it. An insured must answer questions concerning the loss truthfully and completely.

**W.12** We may require separate examinations of insureds who have knowledge of the loss. An insured must not interfere with our examination of another insured.

**W.13** The "days to submit a signed, sworn proof of loss" is a condition applicable to a loss under this coverage. An insured must submit a signed, sworn proof of loss when we require it.

**W.14** A proof of loss must state the facts concerning the loss that are known to the insured. A proof of loss must identify the damaged property and the amount claimed.

**W.15** An insured must provide supporting documents for a proof of loss when we reasonably request them. An insured must correct information that the insured later learns is inaccurate.

**W.16** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must provide reasonable access to the location of the loss.

**W.17** An insured must permit us to inspect, photograph, measure, test, and evaluate damaged property. Our inspection does not constitute acceptance of a claim or agreement concerning the amount of loss.

**W.18** An insured must identify property that is claimed to be damaged by water backup. An insured must separate claimed damaged property from undamaged property when reasonably possible.

**W.19** An insured must preserve records that show ownership, purchase, maintenance, repair, and use of claimed property. An insured must provide copies of those records when we reasonably request them.

**W.20** An insured must provide estimates for repair or replacement when those estimates are available. An insured must identify the person or business that prepared an estimate.

**W.21** An insured must allow us to obtain information from persons having knowledge of the loss. An insured must execute authorizations that are reasonably necessary for us to obtain relevant records.

**W.22** An insured must not make a voluntary payment, assume an obligation, or incur an expense without our consent, except for reasonable measures taken to protect property from further damage. This condition does not prevent an insured from obtaining emergency services that are reasonably necessary.

**W.23** An insured must not admit liability or settle a claim made against an insured without our consent. An insured must promptly send us any demand, notice, summons, or legal paper received concerning the loss.

**W.24** An insured must assist us in enforcing any right of recovery against a person responsible for the loss. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our right of recovery.

**W.25** If we make payment, an insured's rights of recovery are transferred to us to the extent of our payment. An insured must execute documents and take actions reasonably necessary to secure those rights.

**W.26** An insured must not transfer an interest in a claim without our written consent. A transfer without our consent does not bind us.

**W.27** An insured must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to this insurance, the covered property, the loss, or a claim. We may deny coverage if an insured commits fraud in connection with a claim.

**W.28** An insured must provide truthful information in every communication concerning a claim. Information provided by an insured must be complete to the extent reasonably known by that insured.

**W.29** We may investigate a claim without waiving any right under this insurance. Our investigation, inspection, or request for information does not create an obligation to make payment.

**W.30** We may make payment to an insured, a person legally entitled to receive payment, or another person having an interest in covered property. Payment to any such person does not determine the ownership of the property.

**W.31** An insured must protect our interest in covered property after a loss. An insured must not abandon covered property to us.

**W.32** An insured must provide reasonable access to records maintained in electronic form if those records relate to the claim. An insured must identify the source and custodian of electronically stored records.

**W.33** An insured must notify us of other insurance that may apply to the same loss. An insured must provide information concerning the other insurance when we reasonably request it.

**W.34** We will not pay more than our share of a loss when other insurance applies. An insured must cooperate in the allocation of the loss among insurers.

**W.35** The "AOP deductible floor" applies when we determine the deductible applicable to a covered loss. An insured must pay the applicable deductible before we make payment for loss.

**W.36** We will apply the deductible to the loss in accordance with the applicable loss settlement provisions. The deductible does not reduce an insured's duties after a loss.

**W.37** The "replacement cost threshold percentage" applies when we determine whether replacement cost loss settlement is available. An insured must provide information needed to determine the applicable method of loss settlement.

**W.38** We may determine the value of damaged property based on its condition immediately before the loss. An insured must provide information concerning the age, condition, and prior repair of the property.

**W.39** An insured must notify us before beginning nonemergency repair or replacement work when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property before the work is completed if we reasonably request inspection.

**W.40** An insured must retain invoices, contracts, receipts, and proof of payment for repair or replacement work. An insured must provide those records when we reasonably request them.

**W.41** We may request that an insured obtain an estimate from a qualified repair professional. An insured must make reasonable efforts to obtain requested information that is available from such a professional.

**W.42** An insured must notify us if the location of damaged property changes after the loss. An insured must provide the new location of the property when we reasonably request it.

**W.43** An insured must identify any person having a lien, security interest, or other financial interest in claimed property. An insured must provide documents supporting the claimed interest when we reasonably request them.

**W.44** An insured must comply with applicable legal duties concerning the preservation and cleanup of damaged property. Compliance with a legal duty does not establish that we cover the resulting expense.

**W.45** An insured must take reasonable measures to prevent the recurrence of conditions that caused or contributed to water backup. Such measures do not obligate us to pay for improvements or preventive work.

**W.46** An insured must notify us if a governmental authority restricts access to the damaged property. An insured must provide notices or orders issued by that authority when reasonably requested.

**W.47** The "time to bring action" applies to any action against us arising from a claim under this coverage. An insured must satisfy all applicable conditions before bringing such an action.

**W.48** No person may bring an action against us unless there has been full compliance with the terms of this insurance. An action against us must be based on a disputed obligation under this coverage.

**W.49** A failure by us to enforce a condition does not waive our right to enforce that condition later. A waiver is effective only if made by us in writing.

**W.50** An insured must promptly notify us of any material change in information previously provided concerning the claim. An insured must cooperate with us until the claim is resolved.

## W.6 — Special Requirements

**W.1** These special requirements apply to loss caused by "water backup". They apply only to property for which coverage applies under this policy.

**W.2** An insured must use reasonable care to maintain the insured location in a condition that reduces the possibility of water backup. An insured must correct a condition that is known to create a risk of water backup.

**W.3** An insured must maintain each drain, pipe, fixture, and connection that serves the insured location. An insured must keep each drainage opening reasonably clear of materials that may obstruct the flow of water.

**W.4** An insured must maintain a "drainage system" that serves the insured location. This duty includes equipment that removes water from below-grade areas.

**W.5** An insured must maintain any sump, pump, alarm, and related equipment that is installed at the insured location. An insured must use reasonable care to keep such equipment capable of operating as intended.

**W.6** A "backwater valve" is required where a finished area below grade is served by a drainage system. An insured must maintain any required backwater valve in operable condition.

**W.7** An insured must notify us promptly of a "loss" that may involve water backup. Notice must include information reasonably available to you concerning the location, cause, and extent of the loss.

**W.8** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. An insured must make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs to protect the property from further damage.

**W.9** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect health or safety.

**W.10** An insured must permit us to inspect the insured location and damaged property. An insured must provide reasonable access to areas, equipment, and records relating to the loss.

**W.11** An insured must provide records that support the repair, replacement, or restoration of damaged property. Such records may include invoices, estimates, photographs, and receipts.

**W.12** An insured must cooperate with us in our investigation of a loss. An insured must provide information that we reasonably request concerning the cause of the loss and the property involved.

**W.13** An insured must identify property that was damaged by water backup. An insured must distinguish property damaged by water backup from property damaged by another cause.

**W.14** An insured must report any known condition that may have contributed to the loss. This includes a condition involving a drain, sewer, sump, pump, fixture, or plumbing connection.

**W.15** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve evidence concerning the cause of a loss. An insured must not alter the condition of affected equipment before our inspection, unless alteration is necessary to prevent further damage.

**W.16** An insured must submit to examination under oath when we reasonably require. An insured must provide a signed statement concerning the loss when we reasonably request.

**W.17** An insured must authorize us to obtain records that are relevant to the loss. This includes records concerning maintenance, repair, inspection, and prior damage at the insured location.

**W.18** An insured must notify us of any other insurance that may apply to the loss. An insured must cooperate with us in determining the applicability of other insurance.

**W.19** An insured must preserve our rights of recovery against any person or organization responsible for the loss. An insured must not impair those rights after a loss.

**W.20** We do not cover loss when an insured intentionally causes or directs the water backup. We do not cover loss resulting from concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud by an insured relating to a loss.

**W.21** We do not cover loss caused by an insured's failure to comply with a duty stated in these special requirements if the failure prejudices our investigation, adjustment, or defense of the claim. This provision applies only to the extent permitted by law.

**W.22** An insured must notify us of a change in condition that materially increases the possibility of water backup. An insured must provide information concerning the change when we reasonably request it.

**W.23** An insured must use reasonable care to protect the insured location while it is vacant or unoccupied. This duty includes reasonable care to maintain systems that may contribute to water backup.

**W.24** An insured must use reasonable care in selecting and supervising persons who perform maintenance or repair work at the insured location. An insured must retain available records of work that relates to a claimed loss.

**W.25** An insured must comply with applicable laws and ordinances concerning the maintenance and use of the insured location. Compliance with such laws and ordinances does not increase the coverage provided by this policy.

**W.26** No action by us in inspecting property, investigating a loss, or requesting information waives any condition or exclusion of this policy. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

**W.27** An insured must perform the duties stated in these special requirements. Our rights and duties remain subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions of this policy.

## W.7 — Loss Settlement

**W.1** We settle a "covered loss" to property insured by this endorsement in accordance with these provisions. We will pay only for direct physical loss caused by water backup that is covered under this endorsement.

**W.2** The loss settlement basis for personal property under this endorsement is actual cash value. We apply that basis to property that is damaged by a covered loss.

**W.3** We will not pay more than your financial interest in the damaged property. We will not pay for any loss that exceeds the amount otherwise payable under this endorsement.

**W.4** We determine the amount of loss from the condition of the property immediately before the covered loss. We consider the property's age, condition, quality, usefulness, and remaining serviceability.

**W.5** We may pay the cost to repair damaged property when repair will restore the property to substantially the same condition as existed before the covered loss. We may use materials of like kind and quality in making that determination.

**W.6** We may pay the cost to replace damaged property when repair is not practical. Replacement property may be of like kind, quality, and usefulness.

**W.7** We do not cover any increase in loss caused by delay in repairing or replacing damaged property when the delay is within your control. An insured must take reasonable steps to protect damaged property from further loss.

**W.8** We may consider any value remaining in damaged property after the covered loss. We may require you to retain damaged property until we inspect it or otherwise authorize its disposal.

**W.9** We do not cover loss to property that has been discarded before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it. An insured must preserve evidence of the cause and extent of the loss when reasonably possible.

**W.10** When a covered loss affects part of a pair, set, or collection, we may repair or replace the damaged part. We will not pay for any reduction in value to an undamaged part unless the loss makes the pair, set, or collection unusable for its intended purpose.

**W.11** When a covered loss affects property consisting of components, we may repair or replace only the damaged components. We do not cover the cost to replace undamaged components solely to achieve uniformity of appearance.

**W.12** We do not cover any increased cost caused by changes in style, fashion, design, or improvement. We do not cover the cost to replace property with property of superior quality or greater usefulness.

**W.13** We may settle a covered loss by paying for repair, replacement, or restoration of damaged property. We may make payment to you, to a person having a legal interest in the property, or directly to a person performing the work.

**W.14** Our payment does not require us to select a contractor, vendor, or repair facility for you. An insured remains responsible for obligations undertaken with a contractor, vendor, or repair facility.

**W.15** We do not guarantee the work, materials, or services of a contractor, vendor, or repair facility selected by you. We may inspect completed work before making any remaining payment.

**W.16** An insured must provide records, receipts, photographs, estimates, invoices, and other information we reasonably request to determine the amount of loss. An insured must cooperate with our investigation and settlement of the claim.

**W.17** An insured must show us the damaged property as often as we reasonably require. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine records relating to the loss.

**W.18** We may obtain estimates for repair, replacement, or restoration of damaged property. We may rely on information from qualified persons to determine the reasonable cost of the work.

**W.19** Reasonable emergency measures taken to protect damaged property from further covered loss may be considered in determining the amount of loss. We do not cover emergency measures that are unnecessary, excessive, or unrelated to a covered loss.

**W.20** We may take all or part of the damaged property at the value we determine for it. If we do so, an insured must transfer to us all rights of ownership that are necessary for us to take the property.

**W.21** An insured may not abandon damaged property to us without our consent. We do not assume ownership of damaged property solely because we inspect it or make payment for a covered loss.

**W.22** We may pay any amount due under this endorsement to you and any person having a legal interest in the damaged property. Payment made in good faith discharges us to the extent of that payment.

**W.23** If we make payment for a covered loss, we are entitled to recover from a person responsible for that loss to the extent of our payment. An insured must do nothing after a loss to impair our recovery rights.

**W.24** Any recovery obtained by us will be applied in accordance with the respective interests of the parties. An insured must cooperate in securing and preserving our recovery rights.

**W.25** The applicable "loss assessment additional coverage limit" does not increase the amount payable for property damaged by water backup. We apply that limit only to loss for which it applies.

**W.26** The applicable "fire service charge coverage" does not affect settlement of a covered loss under this endorsement. We determine any payment under that coverage separately.

**W.27** The applicable "jewelry and watches limit" applies when damaged property is subject to that limit. The applicable "on-premises business property cap" applies when damaged property is subject to that cap.

**W.28** We may settle a covered loss with an insured who has authority to act for all insureds having an interest in the damaged property. A settlement with that insured is binding on all insureds to the extent of our payment.

## W.8 — Definitions

**W.1** "Water Backup" means water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer or drain, or that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. Water Backup includes resulting waterborne material.

**W.2** "Sewer" means a conduit, pipe, channel, or similar system designed to carry wastewater, storm water, or sewage away from property. Sewer includes a public, private, or shared system.

**W.3** "Drain" means a pipe, channel, fixture, or opening designed to remove water from property. Drain includes a floor drain, plumbing drain, and drainage connection.

**W.4** "Sump" means a pit, basin, reservoir, or similar receptacle that collects water. A sump may be located within or outside a building.

**W.5** "Sump Pump" means a pump designed to remove water from a sump. Sump Pump includes permanently installed equipment and its integral components.

**W.6** "Related Equipment" means equipment that supports the collection, movement, discharge, or removal of water from a sump. Related Equipment includes connected piping, electrical components, and control devices.

**W.7** "Overflow" means the escape of water or waterborne material from a system, fixture, receptacle, or container because that system, fixture, receptacle, or container cannot contain or discharge it. Overflow does not require a break in the system or fixture.

**W.8** "Discharge" means the release of water or waterborne material from equipment, piping, or a receptacle. Discharge includes release caused by mechanical failure, electrical failure, or interruption of operation.

**W.9** "Back Up" means reverse movement of water or waterborne material through a sewer, drain, pipe, or similar passage. Back Up includes movement caused by obstruction, surcharge, or failure of a connected system.

**W.10** "Waterborne Material" means material carried, transported, or deposited by water. Waterborne Material includes sewage, waste, sediment, and other contaminants.

**W.11** "Sewage" means waste material carried through a sewer or drain. Sewage includes material from a sanitary system or wastewater system.

**W.12** "Wastewater" means used water containing waste material. Wastewater includes water from plumbing fixtures, appliances, and drainage systems.

**W.13** "Storm Water" means water resulting from rain, melting snow, or similar precipitation. Storm Water includes surface water entering a sewer or drainage system.

**W.14** "Plumbing System" means pipes, fixtures, valves, drains, and connected equipment used to supply, distribute, remove, or discharge water. Plumbing System includes portions located within a covered building.

**W.15** "Plumbing Fixture" means a sink, toilet, bathtub, shower, faucet, or similar device connected to a Plumbing System. Plumbing Fixture includes its trap and connected drain.

**W.16** "Drainage System" means drains, channels, piping, catch basins, and related components designed to collect or direct water. Drainage System includes components serving a building or the land on which it is located.

**W.17** "Public System" means a sewer, drain, or water removal system owned, operated, or maintained by a governmental authority or public utility. Public System includes a system available for common public use.

**W.18** "Private System" means a sewer, drain, or water removal system not owned or operated by a governmental authority or public utility. Private System includes a system serving your property.

**W.19** "Shared System" means a sewer, drain, or water removal system serving your property and other property. Shared System may be privately owned or publicly maintained.

**W.20** "Covered Building" means a building insured under this policy to which this endorsement applies. Covered Building includes attached structures when they are part of the insured property.

**W.21** "Dwelling" means the residence premises described in this policy. Dwelling includes the portion of a building used as your residence.

**W.22** "Residence Premises" means the location where you reside and that is shown in this policy. Residence Premises includes land associated with the described dwelling.

**W.23** "Insured Property" means property for which coverage is provided under this policy. Insured Property includes property owned by you when it is covered under this policy.

**W.24** "Direct Physical Loss" means physical alteration of, damage to, or destruction of tangible property. Direct Physical Loss includes loss caused by Water Backup when coverage applies.

**W.25** "Resulting Damage" means damage that follows from Water Backup. Resulting Damage must be a Direct Physical Loss to Insured Property.

**W.26** "Mechanical Failure" means a breakdown, defect, or malfunction of equipment that prevents normal operation. Mechanical Failure includes failure of a Sump Pump or Related Equipment.

**W.27** "Electrical Failure" means interruption, loss, fluctuation, or malfunction of electrical power or electrical components. Electrical Failure includes failure affecting operation of a Sump Pump.

**W.28** "Obstruction" means a blockage or restriction that impairs the movement of water or waterborne material. Obstruction includes material within a sewer, drain, pipe, or Plumbing System.

**W.29** "Surcharge" means a condition in which water or waterborne material exceeds the carrying capacity of a sewer, drain, pipe, or drainage system. Surcharge may cause water or waterborne material to Back Up.

**W.30** "Surface Water" means water on the surface of the ground. Surface Water includes water from rain, melting snow, runoff, or overflow from natural or artificial sources.

**W.31** "Groundwater" means water beneath the surface of the ground. Groundwater includes water that enters a sump or drainage system.

**W.32** "Contaminant" means a substance that makes water or property impure, unsafe, or unsuitable for use. Contaminant includes sewage, waste, chemicals, and waterborne material.

**W.33** "Emergency Measures" means reasonable actions taken to protect Insured Property from further Direct Physical Loss caused by Water Backup. Emergency Measures include actions necessary to remove water or waterborne material from Insured Property.
