# DP 01 09 Florida Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2021-03)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

**T.1** This endorsement amends the "policy" only to the extent its terms apply. All other policy terms remain unchanged unless this endorsement states otherwise.

**T.2** If a provision of this endorsement conflicts with a provision of the policy, this endorsement controls. We apply the conflicting provisions as amended by this endorsement.

**T.3** We apply this endorsement in accordance with applicable Florida law. A policy provision that is not affected by this endorsement continues to apply.

**T.4** The terms of this endorsement apply to each insured under the policy. An insured must comply with duties that apply to that insured.

**T.5** A term used in this endorsement has the meaning assigned to it by the policy unless this endorsement provides a different meaning. A term given a different meaning by this endorsement applies only for purposes of this endorsement.

**T.6** The "all-other-perils deductible minimum" applies as provided by the policy as amended by this endorsement. We determine the applicable deductible under the terms that control.

**T.7** The "proof of loss period" applies as provided by the policy as amended by this endorsement. An insured must provide proof of loss when required by the applicable policy terms.

**T.8** The "replacement cost threshold percentage" applies as provided by the policy as amended by this endorsement. We determine eligibility for replacement cost coverage under the terms that control.

**T.9** We apply coverage only when the loss satisfies all applicable policy conditions. An insured must establish that a claimed loss is covered.

**T.10** We apply exclusions, limitations, and conditions that remain applicable after this endorsement is applied. We do not cover loss excluded by applicable policy terms.

**T.11** A change made by this endorsement does not create coverage where coverage does not otherwise exist. A change made by this endorsement does not broaden coverage beyond its express terms.

**T.12** A provision of the policy that can be read consistently with this endorsement remains effective. We interpret the policy and this endorsement to give effect to both when possible.

**T.13** If this endorsement modifies a duty, an insured must comply with the duty as modified. Compliance with an unmodified policy duty does not satisfy a modified duty.

**T.14** If this endorsement modifies a condition, we apply the condition as modified. An insured must satisfy applicable conditions before we provide coverage.

**T.15** If this endorsement modifies a limitation, we apply the limitation as modified. We do not pay for loss beyond an applicable limitation.

**T.16** The rights and duties of an insured remain subject to all applicable policy terms. This endorsement does not waive a right or defense available to us unless it expressly states otherwise.

**T.17** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. We may request information reasonably necessary to determine coverage or the amount of loss.

**T.18** We determine coverage based on the facts, the policy, and this endorsement. We apply the terms in effect for the claimed loss.

**T.19** A claim remains subject to the conditions applicable to that claim. An insured must comply with applicable claim duties.

**T.20** We do not cover loss merely because this endorsement addresses a related subject. Coverage exists only when the policy, as amended by this endorsement, provides coverage.

**T.21** If a policy term is invalid or unenforceable, we apply the remaining terms to the extent permitted by law. The invalidity or unenforceability of a term does not affect the remaining terms.

**T.22** This endorsement is part of the policy. We apply this endorsement according to its plain terms.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** The "Windstorm and Hail Deductible" is the deductible that applies to a covered loss caused by windstorm or hail. The deductible percentage selected for this policy must be at least two percent and must not exceed ten percent.

**T.2** We apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to direct physical loss to property covered under this policy when windstorm or hail causes the loss. We apply this deductible whether windstorm or hail acts alone or with another cause of loss.

**T.3** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies only when the applicable coverage provides coverage for the loss. It does not create coverage for property, loss, or damage that is otherwise excluded or not covered.

**T.4** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies separately from any other deductible that may apply under this policy. We will apply the deductible that is required by the terms governing the covered loss.

**T.5** We will determine the amount of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible from the amount of insurance applicable to the damaged property. The deductible applies before we determine the amount we will pay for covered loss.

**T.6** We will not pay the part of a covered loss that is within the applicable Windstorm and Hail Deductible. You remain responsible for that part of the loss.

**T.7** We will reduce the amount otherwise payable for covered loss by the applicable Windstorm and Hail Deductible. The reduction applies regardless of the number of items of covered property damaged by the same occurrence.

**T.8** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to loss occurring at the residence premises. It also applies to covered property that is away from the residence premises when coverage for that property applies.

**T.9** We apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to the total covered loss arising from the same occurrence. We do not apply a separate deductible to each item of damaged covered property.

**T.10** We determine whether damage arises from the same occurrence based upon the facts of the loss. A continuation of windstorm or hail conditions does not by itself establish a separate occurrence.

**T.11** We may require information reasonably necessary to determine whether the Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies. An insured must provide that information when requested.

**T.12** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. The applicable deductible does not relieve an insured of any duty imposed by this policy after a loss.

**T.13** An insured must give us prompt notice of a loss that may involve windstorm or hail. Notice must include information reasonably available concerning the damaged property and the cause of loss.

**T.14** An insured must show us the damaged property when reasonably requested. An insured must also permit us to inspect the property and examine available records relating to the loss.

**T.15** We may investigate any claimed loss to determine the cause, scope, and amount of damage. Our investigation does not waive any policy condition, limitation, exclusion, or deductible.

**T.16** We may request a signed statement of loss from an insured. The statement must identify the property claimed to be damaged and the information supporting the claim.

**T.17** An insured must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, and settlement of a claim. Cooperation includes providing reasonably requested documents, records, and access to damaged property.

**T.18** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail until the applicable Windstorm and Hail Deductible has been applied. We will then pay only the amount otherwise payable under this policy.

**T.19** We will apply the Windstorm and Hail Deductible without regard to whether a mortgagee, lienholder, or other person has an interest in the covered property. The deductible applies to the covered loss before payment is made to any person.

**T.20** Payment of a claim does not alter the Windstorm and Hail Deductible applicable to a later loss. The deductible remains subject to the terms of this policy.

**T.21** A change in ownership, occupancy, or use of covered property does not change the Windstorm and Hail Deductible unless we agree to the change in writing. An insured must notify us of a material change that may affect coverage.

**T.22** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered loss to the extent that the loss is caused by windstorm or hail. We may allocate loss between covered causes and causes that are not covered when the facts permit allocation.

**T.23** We do not cover the part of a loss attributable to a cause that is excluded by this policy. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not replace an exclusion applicable to the loss.

**T.24** We may rely on reports, inspections, photographs, repair records, and other available evidence in determining the cause of loss. An insured must preserve evidence of damage when reasonably possible.

**T.25** An insured must not voluntarily make payment, assume obligation, or incur expense for a claimed loss without our consent, except as necessary to protect covered property from further damage. We will not pay expenses that are not covered by this policy.

**T.26** Emergency measures taken to protect covered property must be reasonable and necessary. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered loss even when emergency measures are taken.

**T.27** We may inspect repairs made to damaged property. An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably requested, unless disposal is necessary to protect health or safety.

**T.28** An insured must provide records supporting the value of damaged property when reasonably requested. We may use available records to determine the amount of covered loss.

**T.29** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath when permitted by this policy. An insured must answer questions concerning the claimed loss truthfully and completely.

**T.30** We may request that an insured produce records relating to ownership, value, repair, maintenance, and condition of damaged property. An insured must provide records within the insured's possession or control.

**T.31** We may adjust a covered loss with an insured, a mortgagee, or another person having a lawful interest in the property. Our payment does not increase the amount otherwise payable under this policy.

**T.32** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies before any payment for covered loss is issued. We will not reimburse an insured for the amount of the deductible.

**T.33** We may settle a covered loss by repair, replacement, or payment, subject to the terms of this policy. The applicable deductible applies regardless of the method of settlement.

**T.34** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve any right of recovery against a person responsible for the loss. Our rights of recovery are not affected by application of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.35** We may seek recovery from a responsible person after making payment for covered loss. An insured must cooperate with us in protecting those rights.

**T.36** A dispute concerning the amount of loss does not suspend application of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. We will apply the deductible to the amount of covered loss that is determined under this policy.

**T.37** We may require appraisal when appraisal is available under this policy. Appraisal does not determine coverage, cause of loss, or the applicability of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.38** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to a covered loss regardless of whether repairs have been completed. We may require evidence of repair costs when repair costs are relevant to settlement.

**T.39** We do not cover loss resulting from an insured's failure to comply with a policy condition when that failure prejudices our ability to investigate or settle the claim. Application of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not limit our rights under this provision.

**T.40** An insured must provide accurate information concerning prior damage, repairs, and claims when requested. We may consider available information in evaluating a claimed windstorm or hail loss.

**T.41** We may provide a deductible disclosure as required by Florida bulletinas required by Florida bulletin (see Florida Bulletin OIR-2022-01 — Hurricane Deductible Disclosure, B.2 Requirements). An insured must review any disclosure we provide concerning the Windstorm and Hail Deductible.

**T.42** A deductible disclosure does not amend the terms of this policy unless we state otherwise in writing. The Windstorm and Hail Deductible remains subject to this endorsement.

**T.43** We may correct a clerical error in information relating to the applicable deductible. A correction does not change coverage unless the policy terms require a change.

**T.44** If policy language conflicts concerning the application of a deductible to windstorm or hail loss, this endorsement governs. All other policy terms remain unchanged unless this endorsement expressly changes them.

**T.45** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible applies to covered loss occurring during the policy term. We do not cover loss occurring outside the policy term.

**T.46** An insured must report a loss accurately and must not conceal or misrepresent a material fact relating to the loss. We may deny coverage as permitted by this policy when an insured violates this duty.

**T.47** We may request proof that damaged property was covered property at the time of loss. An insured must provide reasonably available proof of ownership, occupancy, and condition.

**T.48** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible does not apply to a payment unless windstorm or hail caused covered direct physical loss. We determine causation from the facts, applicable policy terms, and available evidence.

**T.49** We will not pay more than the amount payable after application of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible and all other applicable policy terms. This limitation applies to any payment made for the same covered loss.

**T.50** An insured may request information concerning the application of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible to a reported loss. We will provide information consistent with our claim investigation and the terms of this policy.

**T.51** Our failure to enforce a provision at any time does not waive our right to enforce that provision later. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

**T.52** The Windstorm and Hail Deductible is part of the terms applicable to covered windstorm or hail loss. An insured must comply with all duties and conditions applicable to a claim for such loss.

**T.53** We do not cover any amount that exceeds the coverage available under this policy after application of the Windstorm and Hail Deductible. This provision applies notwithstanding any contrary understanding not made part of this policy.

**T.54** This endorsement changes the policy only as stated in these provisions. All other policy provisions apply.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** A "Named Storm" means a weather system that has been given a name by a governmental weather authority. A "Named Storm Designation" means the official designation of a Named Storm by that authority.

**T.2** A "Named Storm Period" begins when a Named Storm Designation takes effect. The Named Storm Period ends seventy-two hours after the Named Storm Designation ends.

**T.3** We determine whether a loss occurred during a Named Storm Period based on the facts available to us. The time that damage is discovered does not, by itself, establish when the damage occurred.

**T.4** A Named Storm Period applies only to loss caused directly or indirectly by the Named Storm for which the Named Storm Designation was made. We may consider weather records, reports, inspections, and other reliable evidence in making this determination.

**T.5** Loss that begins before a Named Storm Period and continues during that period will be evaluated based on the cause and timing of the damage. Loss that begins during a Named Storm Period and continues after that period will be evaluated in the same manner.

**T.6** We will not treat damage as having occurred during a Named Storm Period solely because the damage is reported during that period. An insured must provide information reasonably requested by us to establish the cause and timing of the loss.

**T.7** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage during a Named Storm Period. An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible.

**T.8** An insured must notify us promptly of a loss that may involve a Named Storm. Notice must include information reasonably available to identify the damaged property and the circumstances of the loss.

**T.9** An insured must cooperate with our investigation of a loss that may involve a Named Storm Period. This cooperation includes providing records, photographs, repair information, and access to the damaged property when reasonably requested.

**T.10** An insured must not discard damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property. An insured must retain evidence of the condition requiring disposal.

**T.11** An insured may make reasonable emergency repairs necessary to protect covered property from further damage. An insured must keep records of the repairs and related expenses.

**T.12** Emergency repairs do not establish that the damage is covered or that the damage occurred during a Named Storm Period. We may inspect emergency repairs and request documentation supporting the work performed.

**T.13** An insured must permit us to inspect the damaged property as often as reasonably necessary. We may inspect the property before, during, or after repairs are made.

**T.14** We may obtain information from weather services, contractors, public agencies, and other sources to evaluate a claimed loss. Our use of this information does not limit our right to request information from an insured.

**T.15** We do not cover loss that an insured intentionally conceals, misrepresents, or falsely describes in connection with a claim involving a Named Storm Period. This exclusion applies whether the misrepresentation concerns the cause, timing, extent, or value of the loss.

**T.16** We may separate damage caused by a Named Storm from damage caused by other events, conditions, or causes. An insured must cooperate with reasonable efforts to distinguish such damage.

**T.17** If damage results from more than one cause, we will evaluate the damage under the applicable terms governing those causes. The existence of a Named Storm Period does not establish coverage for damage caused by an excluded cause.

**T.18** A change, correction, extension, or withdrawal of a Named Storm Designation will be recognized when officially made by the governmental weather authority. We may revise our evaluation of a loss to reflect that action.

**T.19** The expiration of a Named Storm Period does not relieve an insured of duties relating to a loss that occurred during that period. An insured must continue to protect property from further damage and cooperate with our investigation.

**T.20** Our investigation of a loss involving a Named Storm Period does not waive any condition, exclusion, or other applicable provision. Payment of any portion of a claim does not waive our right to evaluate the remaining claim.

**T.21** We may require an insured to submit to examination under oath concerning a loss involving a Named Storm Period. An insured must produce requested records that are material to the claim.

**T.22** An insured must provide a signed statement of loss when reasonably requested by us. The statement must identify the damaged property, the claimed cause of loss, and the information supporting the claim.

**T.23** These provisions apply whenever a claim involves damage alleged to have been caused by a Named Storm. We will apply these provisions based on the facts of the loss and the information available to us.

## T.5 — Claims Handling

**T.1** We will investigate each reported "claim" in good faith. We may request information reasonably necessary to determine coverage and the amount of "loss".

**T.2** We will acknowledge receipt of a claim within fourteen days. Our acknowledgment may be made by any reasonable means.

**T.3** Our acknowledgment of a claim does not constitute acceptance of coverage or agreement as to the amount of loss. We reserve all rights available under this policy and applicable law.

**T.4** An "insured" must cooperate with us in the investigation, adjustment, settlement, or defense of a claim. Cooperation includes providing truthful information and access to relevant property, records, and persons.

**T.5** An insured must promptly report a loss to us or our authorized representative. Notice must include sufficient information to identify the insured, the insured location, and the nature of the claimed loss.

**T.6** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect "covered property" from further damage. We will not cover damage caused by an insured’s failure to use reasonable means to protect covered property after a loss.

**T.7** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not voluntarily dispose of damaged property unless disposal is necessary to protect persons or property.

**T.8** We may inspect the property and examine the cause and extent of loss. We may take photographs, measurements, samples, or other documentation reasonably necessary for our investigation.

**T.9** An insured must provide records, documents, photographs, estimates, invoices, receipts, and other information reasonably requested by us. The information must be sufficient to permit us to evaluate coverage and the amount of loss.

**T.10** We may request a signed "proof of loss" when reasonably necessary. The proof of loss must state the information reasonably requested by us concerning the claimed loss.

**T.11** An insured must submit a proof of loss that is complete and accurate to the best of the insured’s knowledge. Submission of a proof of loss does not establish coverage or the amount we owe.

**T.12** We may request that an insured submit to an examination under oath. An insured must answer material questions truthfully and must produce records reasonably requested for the examination.

**T.13** We may record an examination under oath. An insured must sign or otherwise verify the accuracy of the recorded statement when reasonably requested by us.

**T.14** We may request a recorded statement from an insured or other person with relevant knowledge. A person providing a recorded statement must answer material questions truthfully.

**T.15** We may communicate directly with an insured, a "claimant", or an authorized representative concerning a claim. We may require reasonable evidence of authority before communicating with a representative.

**T.16** An insured may obtain estimates, reports, or other information concerning repair or replacement of damaged property. We are not bound by an estimate, report, invoice, or recommendation obtained by an insured.

**T.17** We may obtain our own estimates, reports, expert opinions, or evaluations. We may rely upon information that we reasonably determine is relevant to the claim.

**T.18** We may request access to the insured location for inspection, testing, documentation, or evaluation. An insured must provide reasonable access to the property and to relevant portions of the premises.

**T.19** We may request that damaged property be retained for inspection or testing. If we take possession of property, we will do so only as reasonably necessary to investigate or resolve the claim.

**T.20** We may inspect, repair, replace, or preserve property without waiving any coverage defense. Our investigation or adjustment activities do not constitute a representation that coverage applies.

**T.21** We may make payment for an undisputed portion of a covered loss while continuing to investigate other portions of the claim. Such payment does not waive our right to dispute coverage or the amount of any remaining claim.

**T.22** We may issue payment jointly to an insured and a person or entity having a legal interest in the property. We may require evidence of the interest before issuing payment.

**T.23** We may communicate with a "mortgagee" or other person having a legal interest in covered property. Such communication does not create coverage for any person not otherwise entitled to payment under this policy.

**T.24** We may require reasonable documentation of repair, replacement, or other claimed expense before making payment based upon that expense. We may inspect completed work when reasonably necessary to verify the claim.

**T.25** We may pay the "actual cash value" of covered property when payment on that basis is available under this policy. Additional payment, if available, is subject to the applicable policy terms and conditions.

**T.26** We may require reasonable evidence that damaged property has been repaired or replaced before making payment based upon "replacement cost". We will determine any payment in accordance with the valuation provisions of this policy.

**T.27** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or omit a material fact concerning a claim. We do not cover loss for which an insured has engaged in material concealment, misrepresentation, or fraud.

**T.28** We may deny all or part of a claim when the claimed loss is not covered. We will provide notice of our coverage determination in a reasonable manner.

**T.29** After we receive the items and information we request to evaluate a claim, we will accept or reject the claim within ninety business days. Our acceptance or rejection may address all or part of the claim.

**T.30** If we accept a claim, we will pay the covered amount within twenty business days. Payment is subject to any applicable deductible, limitation, condition, or legal interest in the payment.

**T.31** We may request additional information if the information previously received is insufficient to evaluate the claim. An insured must provide the requested information when it is reasonably available.

**T.32** We may reopen our investigation if we receive material information not previously available. Reopening an investigation does not waive any policy condition or coverage defense.

**T.33** An insured must notify us of a "supplemental claim" involving the same loss. We may investigate a supplemental claim and request information reasonably necessary to evaluate it.

**T.34** We may require an insured to separate damaged from undamaged property when reasonably possible. An insured must preserve the property in a manner that allows us to evaluate the claimed damage.

**T.35** We may request that an insured provide access to records maintained by contractors, repairers, or other persons involved in the claimed loss. An insured must authorize release of records when the records are material to the claim and within the insured’s control.

**T.36** We may request documents concerning ownership, occupancy, maintenance, repair, improvement, or prior damage to covered property. An insured must provide such documents when reasonably requested and available.

**T.37** We may request information concerning other insurance that may apply to the same loss. An insured must cooperate in identifying other potentially applicable insurance.

**T.38** We may pursue recovery from a person responsible for a covered loss after we make payment. An insured must not impair our rights of recovery.

**T.39** An insured must execute documents and take reasonable action necessary to preserve our recovery rights. We will not require an insured to incur unreasonable expense in assisting our recovery efforts.

**T.40** We may deduct or offset amounts as permitted by this policy or applicable law. Any deduction or offset will not exceed the amount lawfully available to us.

**T.41** We may request that an insured participate in "appraisal" when appraisal is available under this policy. Participation in appraisal does not determine whether coverage exists.

**T.42** We may resolve a "dispute" concerning the amount of loss by agreement, appraisal, mediation, or another method permitted by applicable law. A method used to resolve a dispute does not waive a coverage defense unless we expressly state otherwise.

**T.43** We may communicate claim information by mail, electronic transmission, telephone, or another reasonable method. An insured must keep us informed of current contact information.

**T.44** We may issue a reservation of rights while we continue our investigation. A reservation of rights preserves our ability to rely upon applicable policy terms and conditions.

**T.45** We may deny a claim in whole or in part if an insured fails to comply with a condition of this policy and the failure prejudices us. We may also enforce any remedy available under applicable law.

**T.46** An insured must promptly notify us of any change affecting the claimed property or the claimed loss. Notice must include information reasonably necessary for us to evaluate the effect of the change.

**T.47** We may request reasonable information concerning prior claims, prior damage, repairs, or maintenance affecting the property. An insured must answer material questions concerning such information truthfully.

**T.48** We may retain experts, contractors, adjusters, engineers, accountants, or other service providers to assist with claim handling. Their involvement does not alter the policy terms or create coverage.

**T.49** We may inspect repair work or replacement property before making any additional payment dependent upon that work or property. An insured must provide reasonable access for that inspection.

**T.50** Our payment, denial, investigation, inspection, or communication concerning a claim does not waive any provision of this policy. Any waiver by us must be in writing and signed by an authorized representative.

## T.6 — Seacoast Territories

**T.1** These provisions apply to "covered property" located in a "Seacoast Territory." For purposes of these provisions, a "Seacoast Territory" is an area we designate as subject to coastal weather conditions.

**T.2** You must provide complete and accurate information concerning the location, construction, occupancy, and condition of covered property. You must promptly notify us of a material change affecting covered property in a Seacoast Territory.

**T.3** An insured must maintain covered property in a condition that reasonably protects it from wind, rain, salt air, and wind-driven debris. An insured must repair damage and deterioration that could increase the likelihood or severity of a loss.

**T.4** An insured must maintain the roof covering, roof attachments, exterior openings, and exterior walls of covered property in serviceable condition. An insured must secure loose materials and objects that could become airborne during severe weather.

**T.5** An insured must maintain storm shutters, impact-resistant openings, protective panels, or other protective devices, if present at covered property. An insured must use those devices when conditions reasonably indicate that their use is necessary to protect covered property.

**T.6** An insured must keep gutters, drains, downspouts, and other water-management features clear of accumulations that interfere with their intended function. An insured must take reasonable steps to direct water away from covered property.

**T.7** An insured must maintain trees, shrubs, and other vegetation so that they do not create an unreasonable hazard to covered property. An insured must remove dead, diseased, damaged, or unsecured vegetation that could cause loss.

**T.8** An insured must protect covered property when it is vacant, unoccupied, or undergoing repair. Such protection must include reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized entry, water damage, and weather-related damage.

**T.9** An insured must promptly report to us any condition that materially increases the hazard of loss at covered property. Such conditions include unrepaired roof damage, broken exterior openings, structural deterioration, or the absence of required protective measures.

**T.10** You must permit us to inspect covered property at reasonable times. Our inspection does not constitute a representation that covered property is safe, properly maintained, or eligible for coverage.

**T.11** We may require information, photographs, records, or other evidence concerning the condition and maintenance of covered property. An insured must cooperate with our reasonable request for that information or evidence.

**T.12** We may require reasonable protective measures as a condition of continuing coverage for covered property in a Seacoast Territory. An insured must complete those measures within the time we specify in our request.

**T.13** We do not cover loss caused by your failure to take reasonable steps to protect covered property after a condition creating a risk of loss becomes known to you. This exclusion applies only to the extent that the failure contributes to the loss.

**T.14** We do not cover loss to personal property left outdoors when that property is exposed to weather conditions and is not reasonably protected from those conditions. This exclusion does not apply to property that is designed for outdoor use and is reasonably secured.

**T.15** We do not cover loss caused by deterioration, wear, corrosion, rot, or rust that results from your failure to maintain covered property as required by these provisions. This exclusion applies regardless of whether coastal weather conditions contribute to the deterioration.

**T.16** An insured must take reasonable steps to preserve and protect damaged property after a loss. An insured must allow us a reasonable opportunity to inspect damaged property before it is removed, repaired, or discarded, unless immediate action is necessary to prevent further damage.

**T.17** Your compliance with these provisions is a condition of coverage for covered property in a Seacoast Territory. We may deny coverage for a loss to the extent permitted by law when an insured fails to comply with a duty imposed by these provisions.

**T.35** "Governmental authority" means a governmental body, agency, board, commission, or other public authority having lawful jurisdiction. Governmental authority includes a person acting under lawful authority granted by such entity.

**T.36** "Ordinance or law" means a law, ordinance, regulation, code, or order of a governmental authority. Ordinance or law includes requirements concerning construction, repair, demolition, remodeling, or occupancy.

**T.37** "Repair" means work performed to restore damaged property to a condition comparable to its condition immediately before the loss. Repair includes replacement of damaged materials when repair is not practicable.

**T.38** "Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged covered property with property of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation. Replacement cost remains subject to the applicable policy provisions.
