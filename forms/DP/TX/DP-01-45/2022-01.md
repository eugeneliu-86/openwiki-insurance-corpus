# DP 01 45 Texas Amendatory Endorsement (Edition 2022-01)



## T.0 — Scope and Precedence

"This endorsement" applies to property located in Texas and to a loss subject to Texas law. We apply this endorsement only as stated in its terms.

The terms of this endorsement amend terms that would otherwise apply. No term is amended unless this endorsement expressly provides for the amendment.

When a term of this endorsement conflicts with another applicable term, this endorsement controls. We will resolve a conflict according to the plain meaning of this endorsement.

When no conflict exists, the applicable terms remain in effect. We will read those terms together with this endorsement.

This endorsement applies to you and to any "insured" seeking coverage. An insured must comply with duties imposed by this endorsement.

We will determine coverage under the terms that apply at the time of the "loss". A change made by this endorsement applies only within its stated scope.

This endorsement does not broaden coverage unless its language expressly does so. We do not provide coverage merely because a term is amended.

This endorsement does not eliminate a duty unless its language expressly does so. An insured must continue to perform any applicable duty not changed by this endorsement.

A term that limits coverage remains effective unless this endorsement expressly changes that limitation. We will apply any unchanged limitation according to its terms.

A term that excludes coverage remains effective unless this endorsement expressly changes that exclusion. We do not cover a loss excluded by an unchanged applicable term.

A term addressing the "minimum Section I deductible" controls the application of that deductible. We will apply the deductible as required by the applicable terms.

A term addressing the "sworn statement deadline" controls the timing of a required sworn statement. An insured must provide a required sworn statement in accordance with that term.

A term addressing the "coinsurance threshold" controls the application of coinsurance. We will determine any coinsurance adjustment under the applicable terms.

The use of a Texas requirement in this endorsement does not create coverage for property otherwise outside the scope of coverage. We will apply that requirement only to the extent it is applicable.

The use of a Texas requirement in this endorsement does not create coverage for a cause of loss otherwise not covered. We will apply all applicable coverage conditions.

If more than one term could apply to the same matter, we will give effect to each term when they can reasonably be read together. We will not disregard a term unless it cannot be reconciled with this endorsement.

Words and phrases used in this endorsement have their ordinary meaning unless they are given a special meaning by applicable terms. We will apply a special meaning when it is applicable.

The singular includes the plural when the context requires. A reference to a person includes any party to whom the applicable term applies.

A reference to property includes "covered property" when the context requires. We will apply property terms only to property within the applicable scope of coverage.

A reference to a claim includes a request for payment or other benefits. An insured must provide information we reasonably require to determine a claim.

No act or omission by us waives a requirement of this endorsement unless we expressly state that we waive it. A waiver applies only to the matter for which it is given.

If a term of this endorsement cannot be enforced, the remaining terms remain effective to the extent permitted by law. We will apply the remaining terms according to their plain meaning.

This endorsement is part of the agreement between you and us. Its terms govern whenever they apply.

## T.1 — Windstorm and Hail Deductible

**T.1** The "Windstorm or Hail Deductible" applies to direct physical loss caused by "windstorm" or "hail". We apply this deductible to covered property for which coverage is provided.

**T.2** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies separately from any deductible applicable to a loss caused by another peril. We determine the applicable deductible from the cause of loss.

**T.3** We apply the Windstorm or Hail Deductible as required by Texas bulletinas required by Texas bulletin (see Texas Bulletin B-2021-08 — Separate Windstorm and Hail Deductibles, B.2 Requirements). This requirement applies to a loss otherwise covered under this coverage.

**T.4** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible must not be less than one percent. We do not reduce the deductible below that amount.

**T.5** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible must not exceed five percent. We do not apply a deductible greater than that amount.

**T.6** We apply the Windstorm or Hail Deductible before determining the amount payable for a covered loss. The deductible reduces the amount otherwise payable.

**T.7** We do not pay the portion of a covered loss that is within the Windstorm or Hail Deductible. We pay only the covered amount that exceeds the applicable deductible.

**T.8** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies whether windstorm, hail, or a combination of windstorm and hail causes the loss. We determine the cause of loss from the available facts.

**T.9** We apply the deductible to direct physical loss, including resulting damage, when windstorm or hail is a cause of that loss. The deductible does not create coverage for loss that is not otherwise covered.

**T.10** We apply the deductible to loss involving "covered property" only. The deductible does not extend coverage to property that is not covered.

**T.11** An insured must cooperate with us in determining the cause and extent of loss. This duty includes providing information reasonably requested by us.

**T.12** An insured must protect covered property from further damage after a loss. Reasonable measures taken to protect covered property do not alter the applicable deductible.

**T.13** We determine the deductible after applying provisions that determine whether a loss is covered. We do not apply the deductible to an amount for which coverage is not available.

**T.14** We determine the amount of direct physical loss before applying the Windstorm or Hail Deductible. We may adjust that amount for applicable limitations and exclusions.

**T.15** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies to loss occurring during the applicable coverage term. We do not apply it to loss outside that coverage term.

**T.16** We apply the deductible to an "occurrence" involving windstorm or hail. We determine whether damage arises from an occurrence based on the facts of the loss.

**T.17** Damage from windstorm and damage from hail may arise from the same occurrence. We apply the Windstorm or Hail Deductible to the covered loss arising from that occurrence.

**T.18** We may inspect covered property to determine the cause and extent of loss. An insured must allow us reasonable access for that purpose.

**T.19** An insured must show us the damaged property when reasonably possible. Removal, repair, or disposal of damaged property must not prevent us from evaluating the loss.

**T.20** We may require documentation supporting a claim for windstorm or hail loss. An insured must provide available records, photographs, receipts, and other relevant information.

**T.21** We determine the deductible from the applicable amount of insurance and the terms governing the covered property. We apply the deductible in accordance with those terms.

**T.22** We do not apply a deductible that is inconsistent with the applicable coverage. We apply the Windstorm or Hail Deductible only when windstorm or hail causes covered direct physical loss.

**T.23** The deductible applies to covered loss to a building when windstorm or hail causes direct physical damage to that building. It also applies to covered loss to property contained in that building when the coverage applies.

**T.24** The deductible applies to covered loss to personal property when windstorm or hail causes direct physical loss to that property. We determine the payable amount after applying the deductible.

**T.25** We do not combine amounts payable under separate coverages to avoid application of the Windstorm or Hail Deductible. We apply the deductible as required by the applicable coverage.

**T.26** We do not increase the amount payable because a loss involves more than one item of covered property. The applicable deductible remains part of our determination of the covered loss.

**T.27** We may rely on weather information, inspection findings, expert opinions, and other relevant evidence to determine whether windstorm or hail caused a loss. Our determination is subject to applicable law.

**T.28** An insured must promptly report a windstorm or hail loss to us or our authorized representative. Delay in reporting must not prejudice our ability to investigate the loss.

**T.29** An insured must preserve evidence of windstorm or hail damage when reasonably possible. We may request that damaged materials be retained until our inspection is complete.

**T.30** We do not waive the Windstorm or Hail Deductible by investigating a claim. We do not waive the deductible by making an inspection or requesting information.

**T.31** We do not waive the Windstorm or Hail Deductible by discussing a claim with an insured. A waiver is effective only when made by us in writing.

**T.32** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies to a covered loss regardless of whether windstorm or hail damage is visible immediately after the occurrence. We determine coverage from the facts and applicable terms.

**T.33** We may distinguish windstorm or hail damage from wear, deterioration, faulty maintenance, or other noncovered causes. The deductible applies only to the covered portion of a windstorm or hail loss.

**T.34** When covered and noncovered causes contribute to damage, we determine the amount payable under the applicable coverage terms. We then apply the Windstorm or Hail Deductible to the covered amount.

**T.35** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm or hail when an exclusion applies. The Windstorm or Hail Deductible does not affect the application of an exclusion.

**T.36** We do not cover the cost to repair or replace property unless the loss is otherwise covered. The deductible does not create an obligation to pay an uncovered cost.

**T.37** We determine whether temporary repairs are reasonable from the circumstances of the loss. The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies to covered temporary repair costs.

**T.38** An insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further loss after windstorm or hail damage. Failure to take those steps may affect the amount payable for resulting loss.

**T.39** We may request a sworn statement concerning the loss when permitted by applicable terms. An insured must provide a truthful statement when we make that request.

**T.40** An insured must submit a claim for windstorm or hail loss in accordance with applicable claim requirements. We may request information needed to evaluate the claim.

**T.41** We may determine that separate damage results from separate occurrences. We apply the Windstorm or Hail Deductible according to that determination.

**T.42** We may determine that damage arising from continuing weather conditions results from the same occurrence. We apply the deductible to the covered loss arising from that occurrence.

**T.43** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies without regard to the location of damage within the covered premises. We apply the deductible to covered loss at any covered location.

**T.44** We determine the amount of covered windstorm or hail loss before considering any payment made by another party. Rights of recovery do not change the applicable deductible.

**T.45** A payment by us does not establish that all claimed damage was caused by windstorm or hail. We may evaluate additional claimed damage based on the available evidence.

**T.46** An estimate prepared by an insured or another person does not determine the applicable deductible. We determine the deductible under the terms governing this coverage.

**T.47** We may use an estimate, inspection, or other information in determining the covered amount of loss. We apply the Windstorm or Hail Deductible to the covered amount we determine.

**T.48** The deductible applies before we calculate any amount payable for covered repair or replacement. We do not reimburse the deductible as part of a covered loss.

**T.49** We do not pay a claim amount that is less than or equal to the Windstorm or Hail Deductible. We may still investigate the reported loss.

**T.50** We apply the deductible to the covered amount after adjustment for applicable valuation terms. The deductible is not included in the amount payable by us.

**T.51** An insured must provide reasonable assistance in identifying property damaged by windstorm or hail. This assistance includes identifying damaged and undamaged property when reasonably possible.

**T.52** An insured must not conceal, misrepresent, or alter material facts concerning a windstorm or hail loss. We may deny coverage when permitted by applicable terms and law.

**T.53** We may request proof of ownership for claimed personal property. An insured must provide available proof supporting the claim.

**T.54** We may request proof of repair costs or replacement costs when relevant to the claim. An insured must provide available documentation supporting those costs.

**T.55** We determine whether claimed damage was present before the reported windstorm or hail occurrence. The deductible applies only to covered direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail.

**T.56** We do not cover preexisting damage merely because windstorm or hail also causes a later loss. We apply the deductible to the covered portion of the later loss.

**T.57** We may inspect repairs made after a windstorm or hail loss. An insured must provide reasonable access to property for that inspection.

**T.58** We may request that an insured refrain from permanent repairs until we inspect the damage when reasonably possible. This request does not require an insured to leave property exposed to further damage.

**T.59** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies to covered loss caused by wind-driven objects when windstorm is a cause of the loss. We determine the cause of loss from the facts.

**T.60** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies to covered loss caused by the impact of hail. We determine whether hail caused the claimed damage from the available evidence.

**T.61** We do not apply the Windstorm or Hail Deductible to a loss not caused by windstorm or hail. Another applicable deductible may apply to that loss.

**T.62** We apply the deductible regardless of whether the insured occupies the covered property at the time of loss. Occupancy does not determine whether windstorm or hail caused the loss.

**T.63** We apply the deductible regardless of whether repairs begin before our evaluation is complete. An insured must preserve reasonable evidence of the damaged condition.

**T.64** We may obtain information from weather services, contractors, engineers, or other sources in evaluating a claim. We may consider that information with other evidence of loss.

**T.65** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies to covered loss for which we make payment. It applies even if payment is made directly to an insured, a mortgagee, or another person entitled to payment.

**T.66** Payment to a mortgagee or another person does not reduce an insured's duty to comply with claim requirements. The applicable deductible remains part of the loss adjustment.

**T.67** We do not pay more than the covered amount of direct physical loss after application of the Windstorm or Hail Deductible. Other applicable limitations may further reduce the amount payable.

**T.68** We determine the deductible without regard to emotional distress, inconvenience, or other indirect consequences of loss. We do not cover those consequences unless coverage otherwise applies.

**T.69** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies to covered loss whether the property is repaired, replaced, or not repaired. Our payment remains subject to applicable valuation terms.

**T.70** An insured must retain records relating to repairs or replacement for a windstorm or hail claim when reasonably possible. We may request those records to verify the claim.

**T.71** We may reopen our evaluation when additional information becomes available. Any additional payment remains subject to the Windstorm or Hail Deductible.

**T.72** We may correct an error in applying the Windstorm or Hail Deductible. A correction does not expand coverage or waive any applicable term.

**T.73** The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies to a covered loss even when another cause contributes to the damage. We determine the covered amount under the applicable coverage terms.

**T.74** We do not cover loss beyond the applicable limit of liability. The Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies before we determine the amount payable within that limit.

**T.75** An insured must comply with duties after loss that apply to a windstorm or hail claim. Compliance with those duties does not eliminate the applicable deductible.

**T.76** This provision governs the application of the Windstorm or Hail Deductible to covered windstorm or hail loss. All other applicable terms continue to apply.

## T.2 — Notice of Deductible Change

**T.1** As used in this provision, "windstorm deductible" means the deductible that applies to a covered loss caused by windstorm. This provision applies when we increase that deductible.

**T.2** We will provide you written notice at least thirty days before an increase in the "windstorm deductible" takes effect. The notice will identify the change.

**T.3** We will send the notice to the mailing address shown in our records. You must keep that address current.

**T.4** We may deliver the notice by mail or by another method permitted by law. Delivery to your authorized representative is delivery to you.

**T.5** The notice will identify the policy to which the change applies. The notice will describe the revised deductible.

**T.6** The notice may be included with another communication concerning your policy. Inclusion with another communication does not change the notice requirement.

**T.7** An increase in the windstorm deductible does not take effect unless we provide the required notice. We will apply the deductible shown in the applicable policy terms until the increase takes effect.

**T.8** This provision applies only to an increase in the windstorm deductible. It does not change any other deductible unless we separately amend that deductible.

**T.9** We may increase the windstorm deductible at renewal or during the policy term when permitted by law. Any such increase remains subject to this provision.

**T.10** You may review the notice and contact us if you believe it does not apply to your policy. Your request for review does not itself change coverage.

**T.11** We will determine whether the notice applies to the insured location shown in the policy. The applicable deductible will be determined under the policy as amended.

**T.12** A notice of deductible change is not a cancellation notice. It does not terminate your policy.

**T.13** A notice of deductible change is not a nonrenewal notice. It does not prevent us from renewing your policy subject to its terms.

**T.14** We may correct a clerical error in a notice. A correction will not reduce any right you have under applicable law.

**T.15** If a notice contains an error concerning the deductible, we will apply the deductible required by the policy and applicable law. We may issue a corrected notice.

**T.16** You must pay any premium due under the policy as required by its terms. A deductible change does not alter your premium obligations unless we state otherwise.

**T.17** The windstorm deductible applies to a covered windstorm loss only as provided by the policy. This provision does not create coverage for a loss otherwise excluded.

**T.18** We do not cover loss merely because you received a notice of deductible change. Coverage is determined by the policy terms applicable to the loss.

**T.19** The notice does not change the description of covered property. It does not change the insured location.

**T.20** The notice does not change the limits of liability applicable under the policy. It changes only the windstorm deductible identified in the notice.

**T.21** You must notify us promptly if you do not receive mail sent to your address of record. Failure to notify us does not extend coverage beyond the policy terms.

**T.22** We may rely on the address provided by you or your authorized representative. You must provide us with any change of address.

**T.23** If more than one named insured is shown in the policy, notice to any named insured is notice to all named insureds. Each named insured remains responsible for compliance with the policy.

**T.24** If the policy is assigned as permitted by its terms, we may provide notice to the person entitled to receive policy communications. An assignment does not alter our notice obligations.

**T.25** The deductible in effect when a covered windstorm loss occurs will apply to that loss. The date a claim is reported does not determine the applicable deductible.

**T.26** We may include information concerning available deductible options in the notice. Such information does not amend the policy unless we agree in writing.

**T.27** Your acceptance of renewal premium does not waive our right to apply a properly noticed deductible change. Our acceptance of premium does not waive any policy condition.

**T.28** This provision does not limit any notice obligation imposed on us by applicable law. If applicable law requires a different result, we will comply with that law.

**T.29** Any waiver of a policy term must be made by us in writing. No oral statement changes the windstorm deductible.

**T.30** This provision applies to the extent it is valid and enforceable. Any invalid provision will not affect the remaining policy terms.

**T.31** The terms of the policy remain in effect except as changed by this provision. All other terms apply to the notice and any deductible change.

**T.32** We will administer a deductible change in accordance with this provision. You must comply with all applicable policy duties after a covered loss.

## T.3 — Named Storm Period

**T.1** "Named Storm" means a tropical weather system that has been assigned a name by an official meteorological authority. A Named Storm remains a Named Storm while that authority maintains its designation.

**T.2** "Named Storm Period" means the time during which the provisions applicable to a Named Storm apply. The Named Storm Period begins when the Named Storm receives its official designation.

**T.3** The Named Storm Period continues until seventy-two hours after the named storm designation ends. The end of the designation occurs when the official meteorological authority terminates the designation.

**T.4** We determine the beginning and ending of a Named Storm Period from information issued by an official meteorological authority. We may rely upon corrected, revised, or subsequently issued information from that authority.

**T.5** A Named Storm Period applies separately to each Named Storm. The designation, termination, or reclassification of another weather system does not change the Named Storm Period applicable to a Named Storm.

**T.6** A loss caused by a Named Storm is subject to the Named Storm Period applicable to that Named Storm. The fact that damage becomes apparent after the Named Storm Period ends does not alone determine when the damage occurred.

**T.7** Damage that begins during a Named Storm Period remains subject to that Named Storm Period when the damage continues after the Named Storm Period ends. We determine when damage began from the available facts.

**T.8** Damage that begins after a Named Storm Period ends is not treated as damage occurring during that Named Storm Period solely because it results from a condition existing during the Named Storm Period. The cause and timing of the damage remain material to our determination.

**T.9** When a Named Storm and another cause contribute to damage, we determine the applicability of the Named Storm Period from the facts surrounding the damage. The presence of another cause does not by itself eliminate the application of the Named Storm Period.

**T.10** A change in the intensity, location, classification, or forecast track of a Named Storm does not by itself end the Named Storm Period. The official designation controls the beginning and ending of the Named Storm Period.

**T.11** A Named Storm Period applies regardless of whether you receive notice of the Named Storm designation. Your knowledge of a designation does not control the application of the Named Storm Period.

**T.12** A delay in reporting a loss does not alter the Named Storm Period applicable to the loss. We may consider the delay in determining the cause, timing, and extent of damage.

**T.13** An insured must take reasonable steps to protect property from further damage during a Named Storm Period. Reasonable temporary measures do not relieve an insured of other duties that apply to a loss.

**T.14** An insured must report damage believed to be caused by a Named Storm promptly after becoming aware of the damage. The report must identify the property involved and describe the damage known to the insured.

**T.15** An insured must preserve damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must not discard or destroy material evidence of the cause or extent of damage unless necessary to protect persons or property.

**T.16** An insured must permit us to inspect damaged property and to examine records relevant to the loss. An insured must provide reasonable access to the property when requested by us.

**T.17** An insured must provide information reasonably requested by us concerning the timing of the damage. Such information may include photographs, communications, repair records, and information concerning weather conditions observed at the property.

**T.18** An insured must make reasonable efforts to identify repairs performed after a Named Storm. Records of temporary repairs and permanent repairs must distinguish the work performed when reasonably possible.

**T.19** An insured may make reasonable emergency repairs necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must retain receipts, invoices, and other available evidence of the repairs.

**T.20** An insured must not voluntarily dispose of damaged property before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect it, unless disposal is necessary for health or safety. If disposal is necessary, an insured must preserve available photographs and records.

**T.21** Our investigation of a loss does not waive any provision applicable to the loss. Our request for information does not constitute a determination that damage occurred during a Named Storm Period.

**T.22** An insured must cooperate with us in determining whether damage occurred during a Named Storm Period. Cooperation includes providing truthful information concerning the condition of the property before and after the Named Storm.

**T.23** The Named Storm Period does not create coverage for loss that is otherwise not covered. The Named Storm Period does not eliminate any condition applicable to a loss.

**T.24** Any determination concerning a Named Storm Period is based upon the information reasonably available to us. We may revise that determination when additional reliable information becomes available.

## T.4 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal

**T.1** "Cancellation" means termination of this policy before its expiration. We may cancel this policy only as permitted by applicable law and this endorsement.

**T.2** "Nonrenewal" means our decision not to continue this policy at its expiration. We may elect nonrenewal only as permitted by applicable law and this endorsement.

**T.3** A "notice" is a written communication that states our action and its effective date. A notice may include any information required by applicable law.

**T.4** We may cancel this policy for "nonpayment of premium." Nonpayment of premium includes failure to pay any premium due under this policy or under an applicable payment arrangement.

**T.5** We will mail or deliver notice of cancellation for nonpayment of premium at least ten days before cancellation is effective. The notice will identify the premium that remains unpaid.

**T.6** We may cancel this policy for a reason other than nonpayment of premium when cancellation is permitted by applicable law. We will state the reason for cancellation in the notice when required by applicable law.

**T.7** We will mail or deliver notice of cancellation for a reason other than nonpayment of premium at least thirty days before cancellation is effective. The notice will state the effective date of cancellation.

**T.8** We may cancel this policy if you obtained coverage through fraud or "material misrepresentation." A material misrepresentation is a statement or omission that is material to our acceptance of the risk or the hazard assumed.

**T.9** We may cancel this policy if there has been a substantial change in the risk that is material to the hazard assumed. We will not rely on a change that is not material to our underwriting of this policy.

**T.10** We may cancel this policy if you violate a policy condition that is material to the risk. We will identify the material condition involved when required by applicable law.

**T.11** We may cancel this policy if an insured commits fraud in connection with this policy. Fraud includes a knowing misrepresentation or concealment concerning a material fact.

**T.12** We may cancel this policy if an insured intentionally causes or permits an "increase in hazard." An increase in hazard is a material increase in the likelihood or severity of covered loss.

**T.13** We may cancel this policy if the "residence premises" becomes vacant or unoccupied in a manner material to the risk. We will apply this provision only as permitted by applicable law.

**T.14** We may cancel this policy if the property insured under this policy is transferred without our consent when consent is required by the policy. A transfer includes a change in ownership or legal control.

**T.15** We may cancel this policy if the property insured under this policy is used for a purpose that differs materially from the use disclosed to us. We will consider the nature and extent of the changed use.

**T.16** We may cancel this policy if the property insured under this policy is altered in a manner that materially changes the hazard. We may require information concerning an alteration before deciding whether to cancel.

**T.17** We may cancel this policy if required information concerning the risk is not provided after our reasonable request. The information requested must be material to underwriting or administration of this policy.

**T.18** We may cancel this policy if an insured refuses to permit a reasonable inspection of the property. We will request access in a manner consistent with applicable law.

**T.19** We may cancel this policy if an inspection reveals a material condition that was not disclosed to us. We may allow correction of the condition when permitted by applicable law.

**T.20** We may cancel this policy if an insured fails to correct a material condition after agreeing to do so. We will describe the condition with sufficient specificity for you to understand the basis for our action.

**T.21** We may cancel this policy if coverage was issued in error because information material to the risk was incomplete or inaccurate. We will comply with applicable law in determining whether cancellation is available.

**T.22** We may cancel this policy if continued coverage would violate applicable law. We will state that basis in the notice when required by applicable law.

**T.23** We may cancel this policy if an insured has made a material misrepresentation in an application, renewal submission, or other information provided to us. We may rely on information provided by you or on your behalf.

**T.24** We may cancel this policy if the property insured under this policy no longer meets our applicable eligibility requirements and cancellation is permitted by law. Our eligibility requirements must be applied in a manner consistent with applicable law.

**T.25** We may cancel this policy if the policy was issued because of an administrative error that materially affected our acceptance of the risk. We will not cancel solely because of an immaterial clerical error.

**T.26** A cancellation notice will be mailed or delivered to the "named insured" shown in the policy records. The named insured must keep us informed of the current mailing address.

**T.27** We may mail a notice to the mailing address shown in our records. Notice mailed to that address will be effective even if an insured has changed address without notifying us.

**T.28** We may deliver a notice personally to the named insured or to an authorized person at the named insured's mailing address. Delivery may be made by any method permitted by applicable law.

**T.29** "Proof of mailing" will be sufficient proof that we gave notice when applicable law permits that method of proof. We are not required to prove actual receipt unless applicable law requires it.

**T.30** A cancellation notice will state the effective date of cancellation. Coverage will end on that date unless cancellation is withdrawn or modified by us in writing.

**T.31** A cancellation notice may state the reason for cancellation. We will include the reason when applicable law requires us to do so.

**T.32** A cancellation notice may identify the policy, the insured property, and the premium account information. An omission that does not mislead you will not invalidate an otherwise effective notice.

**T.33** We may withdraw a cancellation notice before cancellation becomes effective. Withdrawal must be made in writing unless applicable law permits another method.

**T.34** If we withdraw a cancellation notice, this policy will remain in effect subject to its terms. We may require payment of any premium that remains due.

**T.35** Acceptance of premium after we issue a cancellation notice does not automatically withdraw the notice. We will notify you if acceptance of premium reinstates or continues coverage.

**T.36** We may accept less than the amount due without waiving our right to cancel for the unpaid balance. Any waiver by us must be in writing.

**T.37** Payment after cancellation becomes effective does not reinstate this policy unless we agree in writing. If we agree to reinstate coverage, reinstatement will be subject to the terms we state.

**T.38** Cancellation does not affect any claim arising from a loss that occurred before cancellation became effective. Coverage for that loss remains subject to all policy terms.

**T.39** Cancellation does not create coverage for a loss that occurred after cancellation became effective. We do not cover loss occurring after the effective date of cancellation.

**T.40** If this policy is canceled, we will return any unearned premium when required by applicable law. "Unearned premium" means premium applicable to coverage after cancellation becomes effective.

**T.41** We may deduct from a return premium any amount lawfully owed to us. We will provide an accounting when required by applicable law.

**T.42** If you cancel this policy, we will return any unearned premium as required by applicable law. The method used to calculate return premium will be consistent with applicable law and this policy.

**T.43** You may cancel this policy by providing written notice to us or to your authorized representative. Your notice must identify the policy and state the requested effective date of cancellation.

**T.44** We may require surrender of the policy records when you request cancellation. Failure to surrender policy records will not prevent cancellation if you otherwise give effective notice.

**T.45** A "mortgagee" shown in our records may receive notice of cancellation when required by this policy or applicable law. Notice to a mortgagee does not extend coverage beyond the terms applicable to that interest.

**T.46** A "lienholder" shown in our records may receive notice of cancellation when required by this policy or applicable law. Notice to a lienholder does not create an insured status unless the policy provides otherwise.

**T.47** We may provide notice to an additional insured shown in our records when required by applicable law or the policy terms. Such notice does not alter the effective date of cancellation.

**T.48** We may provide notice to an "authorized representative" acting for an insured. An authorized representative is a person or entity authorized by an insured to receive communications concerning this policy.

**T.49** Notice to an authorized representative does not replace notice to the named insured when notice to the named insured is required. We may provide notice to both persons.

## T.6 — Seacoast Territories

**T.1** These provisions apply to property located in a "Seacoast Territory". They apply only to a loss for which coverage is otherwise available.

**T.2** "Seacoast Territory" means an area that we identify as subject to seacoast windstorm conditions. Our identification of a Seacoast Territory applies to the location of the property at the time of loss.

**T.3** A "Windstorm Loss" means direct physical loss caused by windstorm. A Windstorm Loss includes resulting direct physical loss only when that resulting loss is otherwise covered.

**T.4** We will determine whether a loss is a Windstorm Loss from the facts surrounding the loss. Our determination may consider weather conditions, the location of the property, and the nature of the damage.

**T.5** A "Windstorm Deductible" applies to a Windstorm Loss in a Seacoast Territory. The Windstorm Deductible applies before we determine the amount payable for that loss.

**T.6** The Windstorm Deductible applicable to a Windstorm Loss in a Seacoast Territory shall not exceed ten percent.

**T.7** We do not cover the portion of a Windstorm Loss that is within the applicable Windstorm Deductible. You remain responsible for that portion of the loss.

**T.8** The Windstorm Deductible applies separately to each covered location. Damage to property at different locations does not combine to reduce the deductible applicable to either location.

**T.9** Damage arising from the same windstorm at the same covered location will be treated as part of the same Windstorm Loss. Damage from separate windstorms will be treated separately.

**T.10** The Windstorm Deductible applies whether windstorm is the sole cause of loss or acts with another cause of loss. Coverage for any other cause of loss remains subject to all applicable terms and conditions.

**T.11** If a Windstorm Loss affects property subject to more than one applicable coverage, we will apply the Windstorm Deductible in the manner that most closely reflects the damaged property. We will not apply the Windstorm Deductible more than once to the same damaged property.

**T.12** An "insured" must promptly report a Windstorm Loss to us. An insured must provide information that we reasonably request concerning the cause and extent of the loss.

**T.13** An insured must protect damaged property from further loss. Reasonable expenses incurred to protect damaged property are subject to the applicable coverage and deductible.

**T.14** An insured must retain damaged property for our inspection when reasonably possible. An insured must permit us to inspect the property and examine records relating to the loss.

**T.15** An insured must provide records, photographs, estimates, and other documentation supporting the claimed loss. An insured must cooperate with our investigation and adjustment of the claim.

**T.16** An insured must not discard damaged property unless its retention is unsafe or impracticable. An insured must preserve evidence of the cause and extent of the damage when reasonably possible.

**T.17** Repairs may be made when necessary to protect property from further damage. An insured must provide us a reasonable opportunity to inspect the damage before permanent repairs are completed.

**T.18** Payment of a claim does not waive any applicable Windstorm Deductible. Our investigation, inspection, or adjustment of a claim does not waive any coverage condition or exclusion.

**T.19** We may rely on reports from weather authorities, contractors, engineers, and other qualified sources in evaluating a Windstorm Loss. We may require additional information when the cause or extent of damage cannot be determined from the available information.

**T.20** These provisions do not increase any applicable limit of liability. These provisions do not create coverage for property or causes of loss that are not otherwise covered.

**T.21** We do not cover loss caused by windstorm when the property is not located in a Seacoast Territory under these provisions unless coverage otherwise applies without regard to these provisions. Any such coverage remains subject to its applicable deductible.

**T.22** The Windstorm Deductible applies to a Windstorm Loss even if another deductible might otherwise apply to the same loss. We will apply the deductible that is required by the applicable coverage provisions.

**T.23** An insured must not assign rights relating to a Windstorm Loss without our written consent. An assignment made without our written consent does not bind us.

**T.24** If any provision of these provisions is determined to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in effect. We will apply the remaining provisions to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## T.9 — Definitions Amended

For purposes of this insurance, the words and phrases defined in this endorsement have the meanings stated below. These meanings apply wherever the words or phrases appear.

The words "you" and "your" mean the person or organization to whom this insurance is issued. The words include an authorized representative acting within the scope of that representative's authority.

An "insured" means you and a resident relative of your household. An "insured" also includes a person under your care who is a resident of your household.

"Residence premises" means the location where you reside and that is shown as your residence in the declarations. The location includes the land and structures used in connection with that residence.

"Reside" means to maintain a customary home at a location. Temporary absence from that location does not by itself establish that you do not reside there.

"Dwelling" means a building used principally as a private residence. "Dwelling" includes attached structures that are connected to the building by a common wall, roof, or foundation.

"Other structures" means structures on the "residence premises" that are separated from the "dwelling" by clear space. A structure connected to the "dwelling" solely by a fence, utility line, or similar connection is an "other structure."

"Covered property" means property for which this insurance provides coverage. "Covered property" does not include property that is specifically excluded.

"Business" means a trade, profession, occupation, or activity engaged in for economic gain. "Business" includes an activity conducted from the "residence premises."

"Business property" means property used primarily for a "business." Property held for sale, lease, or delivery is "business property."

"Motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle, trailer, or semitrailer designed for travel on public roads. "Motor vehicle" includes attached machinery or equipment.

"Watercraft" means a craft designed for travel on water. "Watercraft" includes related equipment and motors.

"Aircraft" means a device designed for flight through the air. "Aircraft" includes remotely controlled devices designed for aerial operation.

"Bodily injury" means bodily harm, sickness, or disease sustained by a person. "Bodily injury" includes resulting care, loss of services, and death.

"Property damage" means physical injury to tangible property, including resulting loss of use. "Property damage" also means loss of use of tangible property that has not been physically injured.

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions. An "occurrence" may result in "bodily injury," "property damage," or both.

"Loss" means accidental direct physical loss of or damage to property. "Loss" includes loss resulting from an accidental cause unless excluded by this insurance.

"Direct physical loss" means physical alteration of property or physical damage to property. Economic loss without physical alteration or physical damage is not "direct physical loss."

"Actual cash value" means the value of damaged property at the time of loss. "Actual cash value" reflects depreciation, condition, age, and obsolescence.

"Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality. "Replacement cost" does not include the value of land.

"Collapse" means an abrupt falling down or caving in of a building or part of a building. A building or part of a building that is in danger of falling down is not in a state of "collapse."

"Fungi" means any type or form of fungus, including mold or mildew. "Fungi" includes spores, fragments, and byproducts produced or released by fungi.

"Wet rot" means deterioration of property caused by moisture. "Dry rot" means deterioration of property caused by fungal decay without visible moisture.

"Pollutants" means irritants or contaminants in solid, liquid, gaseous, thermal, or biological form. "Pollutants" include smoke, vapor, soot, fumes, acids, alkalis, chemicals, and waste.

"Vacant" means substantially empty of personal property and customary habitation. A "residence premises" is not "vacant" while occupied by you or a tenant.

"Concealment" means failure to disclose a material fact known by an insured. "Concealment" includes withholding information material to our evaluation of a loss.

"Misrepresentation" means a statement that is false or materially incomplete. A "misrepresentation" is material when it affects our evaluation of this insurance or a loss.

"Increase in hazard" means a change in use, condition, or circumstance that materially increases the likelihood or severity of loss. An "increase in hazard" may result from an act or omission of an insured.

"Insured location" means the "residence premises" and property used in connection with the "residence premises." "Insured location" includes a location where an insured is temporarily residing.

"Premises" means land, buildings, and structures at a location. "Premises" includes areas used for access to or maintenance of the location.

"Rental" means the granting of possession or use of property to another person in exchange for consideration. "Rental" includes a lease, license, or similar arrangement.

"Tenant" means a person who occupies property with your permission under a "rental" arrangement. A "tenant" does not include a temporary guest.

"Weather" means atmospheric conditions, including wind, rain, snow, sleet, hail, lightning, and temperature. "Weather" includes conditions resulting directly from those atmospheric events.
