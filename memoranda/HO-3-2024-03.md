# Filing Memorandum — HO-3 Edition 2024-03

## M.2 — Changes to Definitions

**M.2.1** The prior definition of “residence premises” treated certain appurtenant structures as part of the insured location without clarifying their relationship to the dwelling. The revised definition distinguishes the dwelling from appurtenant structures and applies the term consistently to covered locations. This change was made to clarify the scope of the defined term.

**M.2.2** The prior definition of “insured” did not expressly describe the status of persons acting as legal representatives after the named insured’s death. The revised definition identifies legal representatives as insureds only while acting within their representative capacity. This change was made to align the definition with the policy’s intended treatment of estate-related responsibilities.

**M.2.3** The prior definition of “you” relied on general references to named insureds and resident spouses. The revised definition states that the term applies to the named insured and the resident spouse of the named insured. This change was made to improve readability and reduce uncertainty regarding use of the term.

**M.2.4** The prior definition of “insured location” included wording that could be read to encompass locations not associated with the residence premises. The revised definition identifies qualifying locations by their connection to the residence premises or their use by an insured. This change was made to clarify the locations to which the policy applies.

**M.2.5** The prior definition of “business” used terms that could overlap with casual personal activities. The revised definition distinguishes activities conducted for compensation from activities that remain personal in nature. This change was made to clarify the application of business-related limitations.

**M.2.6** The prior definition of “business” did not expressly address activities involving occasional services or sales. The revised definition explains that incidental activities may be evaluated based on their character and purpose. This change was made to provide a clearer basis for applying the definition.

**M.2.7** The prior definition of “motor vehicle” did not clearly separate vehicles intended for road use from equipment designed for other purposes. The revised definition identifies the characteristics relevant to whether property is treated as a motor vehicle. This change was made to support consistent application of vehicle-related coverage provisions.

**M.2.8** The prior definition of “motor vehicle” could be read to include certain mobility equipment without regard to its intended use. The revised definition addresses mobility equipment according to its design and operation. This change was made to clarify how the definition applies to such equipment.

**M.2.9** The prior definition of “watercraft” did not identify whether propulsion method affected the classification of a vessel. The revised definition describes watercraft in terms of their design for use on water and their means of operation. This change was made to clarify the scope of watercraft-related provisions.

**M.2.10** The prior definition of “aircraft” did not expressly address devices capable of flight that are operated remotely. The revised definition includes remotely operated devices when they meet the stated characteristics of aircraft. This change was made to address evolving forms of aerial equipment.

**M.2.11** The prior definition of “occurrence” used wording that could be interpreted differently when related conditions caused property damage. The revised definition describes the relationship between an accident and continuous or repeated exposure to substantially similar conditions. This change was made to clarify when related damage is treated as arising from an occurrence.

**M.2.12** The prior definition of “property damage” did not clearly distinguish physical injury to tangible property from loss of use of tangible property. The revised definition separately describes physical injury and loss of use. This change was made to improve application of liability coverage terms.

**M.2.13** The prior definition of “bodily injury” did not expressly state that sickness and disease may result from an injury. The revised definition includes sickness and disease resulting from bodily injury. This change was made to clarify the types of harm addressed by the definition.

**M.2.14** The prior definition of “insured” did not clearly describe the status of resident relatives who are away from the residence premises. The revised definition applies the term based on residency and relationship rather than temporary absence alone. This change was made to clarify the treatment of resident relatives.

**M.2.15** The prior definition of “vacant” did not distinguish vacancy from temporary absence by an insured. The revised definition describes vacancy based on the absence of customary contents and use of the premises. This change was made to distinguish vacancy from unoccupancy under the policy.

**M.2.16** The prior wording did not identify how the unoccupancy limit applies when the premises remain furnished but are not regularly lived in. The revised definition clarifies that unoccupancy is evaluated separately from vacancy. This change was made to support consistent application of the unoccupancy limit.

**M.2.17** The prior definition of “replacement cost” did not expressly connect the term to the insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement. The revised definition preserves the meaning of replacement cost while clarifying its use in settlement determinations. This change was made to improve consistency between the definition and settlement provisions.

**M.2.18** The prior definition of “actual cash value” did not state whether depreciation may be considered when determining value. The revised definition explains that actual cash value reflects the value of property at the time of loss, subject to applicable policy terms. This change was made to clarify the valuation concept.

**M.2.19** The prior definition of “hurricane occurrence” did not expressly address the hurricane occurrence tail when related conditions continued after the storm’s direct impact. The revised definition clarifies that the defined occurrence includes related conditions as provided by the policy. This change was made to support consistent treatment of hurricane-related losses.

**M.2.20** The prior definition of “fungi” did not clearly identify whether related wet rot or dry rot was included. The revised definition includes fungi, wet rot, and dry rot within the defined term. This change was made to clarify the property conditions addressed by the policy.

**M.2.21** The prior definition of “collapse” did not distinguish an abrupt falling down from conditions indicating a risk of falling down. The revised definition identifies collapse as an abrupt falling down or caving in that meets the stated characteristics. This change was made to clarify the meaning of collapse.

**M.2.22** The prior definition of “insured location” did not clearly address land used in connection with the residence premises. The revised definition describes qualifying land according to its use with the residence premises. This change was made to clarify the application of coverage to connected land.
