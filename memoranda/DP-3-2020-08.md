## M.1 — Summary of Filing

**M.1.1** Prior wording used terminology that could be read differently across related
policy provisions.
Revised wording aligned the terminology so the provisions apply consistently.
The reason was to improve clarity in policy interpretation.

**M.1.2** Prior wording described the coverage grant without expressly connecting
certain terms to their defined meanings.
Revised wording clarified the relationship between the coverage grant and the applicable
definitions.
The reason was to reduce uncertainty regarding the scope of coverage.

**M.1.3** Prior wording could result in differing interpretations of property
eligibility.
Revised wording clarified the characteristics that determine whether property is
eligible for coverage.
The reason was to support consistent underwriting and claim handling.

distinguish all property interests.

The reason was to make the coverage application more readily understood.

covered property language.

The reason was to reduce potential ambiguity in coverage determinations.

interpreted inconsistently.

The reason was to promote uniform interpretation of the residence premises provisions.

applicable conditions.
Revised wording clarified the interaction of the loss settlement provisions and policy
conditions.
The reason was to support consistent adjustment of covered losses.

**M.1.8** Prior wording described deductible application in language that could create
uncertainty when more than one cause of loss is involved.
Revised wording clarified the circumstances under which the applicable deductible
applies.
The reason was to provide clearer administration of deductibles.

**M.1.9** Prior wording stated duties after loss in a format that could obscure the
responsibilities of the insured.
Revised wording reorganized and clarified those responsibilities.
The reason was to make the postloss requirements easier to identify and apply.

## M.2 — Changes to Definitions

**M.2.1** The prior definition of “insured” could have been read to include persons

revised to identify the persons included within that term, and the revision clarifies
who is eligible for coverage.

insured and other insured persons. The definition was revised to apply that term to the
