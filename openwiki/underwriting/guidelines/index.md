- [Binding Authority Guidance](binding-authority.md) - Summarizes the separate binding-authority guideline for Coverage A ceilings, required approvals, referral triggers, evidence, exceptions, and file controls. It distinguishes internal authority constraints from the referral matrix, underwriting manual, policy contract, and state law.
- [California Homeowners Appetite Guidance](california-appetite.md) - Internal California carrier guidance for homeowners eligibility, referral, roof, wind, water, loss-history, claims-handling, earthquake-offer handoffs, and delegated authority. It separates underwriting controls from policy coverage, state transaction requirements, and claim determinations.
- [Florida Homeowners Appetite Guidance](florida-appetite.md) - Internal Florida underwriting controls for roof age and condition, wind and hail, water exposure, prior losses, claim handoffs, renewal actions, and delegated authority. Separates carrier appetite from OIR requirements and the contractual Florida homeowners and dwelling-fire forms.
- [Louisiana Homeowners Appetite Guidance](louisiana-appetite.md) - Internal Louisiana underwriting direction for homeowners risk selection, roof and storm controls, water exposure, prior-loss referral, claims-handling boundaries, and delegated authority. Separates Louisiana appetite rules from the HO 01 17 contract and Louisiana hurricane-deductible bulletins.
- [New York Homeowners Appetite Guidance](new-york-appetite.md) - Internal carrier guidance for New York homeowners risk selection, including baseline appetite, roof, wind and hail, water, prior-loss, renewal, claims, and delegated-authority controls. It separates internal underwriting direction from the New York overlay, DFS notice and reporting requirements, and the HO 01 31 contract.
- [North Carolina Homeowners Appetite Guidance](north-carolina-appetite.md) - Internal North Carolina homeowners guidance for appetite, referral, property and water risk, storm exposure, loss history, claims handling, and delegated authority. It keeps Rule 550 underwriting controls separate from the North Carolina contract overlay, claims bulletin, and fungi-limit disclosure boundary.
- [Underwriting Referral and Authority Guidance](referral-authority.md) - Operational guidance for deciding when a homeowners submission, change, or related handling question must be referred; assembling the facts and authority record; and communicating pending or approved decisions without changing the policy contract.
- [Texas Homeowners Appetite Guidance](texas-appetite.md) - Internal underwriting guidance for Texas homeowners binding, including eligibility, authority, roof-age and condition controls, wind and hail handling, water-backup screening, prior-loss referrals, and pre-bind and claims procedures. It separates carrier guidance from the policy, endorsement, and Texas bulletin provisions that control contractual meaning and administration.
