## Governing edition first

Read Coverage B from the HO-3 edition issued with the policy. **HO-3 2011-05** remains controlling for policies written under that edition, even if the loss is reported later. **HO-3 2018-09** applies to policies written on or after 2018-09-01 and supersedes 2011-05 for new business from that date. Do not carry 2018-09 additions back onto a 2011-05 policy. [HO-3 2011-05, form status](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2011-05.md#L1-L7) · [HO-3 2018-09, form status](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L1-L4)

## Coverage B scope

Under **HO-3 2018-09 Section I B.1**, Coverage B insures other structures on the residence premises that are separated from the dwelling by clear space. It also reaches a structure connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection. The residence premises is the one-family dwelling, other structures, and grounds at the Declarations location where the insured resides. [HO-3 2018-09 Definition 4](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L15-L19) · [HO-3 2018-09 § B.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L35-L41)

For **HO-3 2011-05**, B.1 is narrower in wording: it covers other structures on the residence premises set apart from the dwelling by clear space. It does not add the 2018-09 fence, utility-line, or similar-connection language. [HO-3 2011-05 § B.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2011-05.md#L35-L40)

Coverage B is limited to **10% of the Coverage A limit** and that amount is **additional insurance**. The limit is derived from the applicable Coverage A amount; it is not a fixed dollar figure and it does not consume Coverage A. [HO-3 2011-05 § B.2](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2011-05.md#L35-L40) · [HO-3 2018-09 § B.2](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L35-L41)

## Rental-use limitation in HO-3 2018-09

**HO-3 2018-09 Section I B.3** excludes an other structure that is rented or held for rental to any person who is not a tenant of the dwelling, unless the structure is used solely as a private garage. This is a scope limitation, so the rental facts must be established before reaching the cause-of-loss analysis. [HO-3 2018-09 § B.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L35-L42)

That rental exclusion is not stated in the supplied **HO-3 2011-05** Coverage B language. For 2011-05 the relevant gates are the clear-space requirement and the same 10% additional limit. [HO-3 2011-05 § B.1–B.2](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2011-05.md#L35-L40)

## Direct-physical-loss gate and Section I exclusions

For HO-3 2018-09, Coverage B insures against direct physical loss to covered property except as excluded in Section I — Exclusions. The Coverage B scope and B.3 rental test come first; the 10% limit does not itself create coverage. [HO-3 2018-09 §§ B.1–B.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L35-L42) · [HO-3 2018-09 § P.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L59-L63)

The Section I exclusions apply to qualifying Coverage B property in the same way they apply to Coverage A property. Important scope-relevant exclusions include:

- flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, storm surge, and overflow of a body of water, whether or not wind-driven;
- water below the ground surface that pressures, seeps, or leaks through a building, foundation, swimming pool, or other structure;
- sewer-or-drain backup and sump-related backup, overflow, or discharge, including when mechanical breakdown caused the backup or overflow;
- earth movement, neglect, wear and tear, deterioration, rust, mold, wet or dry rot, and continuous or repeated seepage or leakage over weeks, months, or years;
- increased construction, demolition, or repair cost required by ordinance or law; and
- intentional loss by an insured, which applies to all insureds even if only one participated. [HO-3 2018-09 §§ A.1–A.4, B.1–C.3, D.1–E.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L67-L97)

## Attachment-dependent write-backs

The base form changes coverage only when it says “if attached.” That means the endorsement must be shown on the issued policy before using it. The HO-3 water-backup exception and ordinance-or-law exception are both conditional on attachment, and HO 04 81 is another attachment-dependent write-back for fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. [HO-3 2018-09 §§ A.3, D.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L69-L76) · [HO-3 2018-09 § D.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L91-L94) · [HO 04 81 2018-09 introductory text](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L1-L4)

### HO 04 90 water backup

If HO 04 90 is attached, verify the exact edition before applying its terms. **2010-10** remains in force for policies written under it regardless of loss-report date. **2026-01** replaces 2010-10 for policies written on or after 2026-01-01. A later edition does not retrofit an earlier issued endorsement. [HO 04 90 2010-10, continuing force and applicability](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2010-10.md#L1-L7) · [HO 04 90 2026-01, replacement scope](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L1-L4)

For either verified edition, **W.1 writes back the selected HO-3 A.3 sewer-or-drain and sump-discharge exclusion** for direct physical loss to Coverage B property caused by the stated water or waterborne material events, including mechanical breakdown. The retained HO-3 exclusions remain in force, so W.1 does not reach flood or subsurface water. [HO-3 2011-05 § A.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2011-05.md#L63-L66) · [HO-3 2018-09 § A.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L75-L77) · [HO 04 90 2010-10 § W.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2010-10.md#L9-L12) · [HO 04 90 2026-01 § W.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L6-L11)

Both editions keep **A.1** and **A.2** excluded. They also add edition-specific payment and condition terms: 2010-10 uses a $5,000 policy-period sublimit, a separate $500 deductible, and a Coverage B settlement rule in W.6; 2026-01 uses a $10,000 sublimit, a separate $1,000 deductible, a backflow-prevention condition in W.6, and the settlement rule in W.7. The edition terms are not interchangeable. [HO 04 90 2010-10 §§ W.2–W.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2010-10.md#L13-L35) · [HO 04 90 2026-01 §§ W.2–W.7](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L13-L56)

The 2026-01 W.6 condition applies only when the residence premises has a finished area below grade, and coverage then depends on an installed, operable backwater valve or equivalent backflow-prevention device on the serving sewer line at the time of loss. That is contract language, not an underwriting guide. [HO 04 90 2026-01 § W.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L42-L47)

### HO 04 81 fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria

If HO 04 81 edition 2018-09 is attached, M.1 writes back HO-3 2018-09 Exclusion C.2 only to the stated extent. It provides limited direct physical-loss coverage for Coverage B property damaged by fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria only when the condition results from a Section I peril insured against during the policy period. It does not restore flood, surface-water, subsurface-water, or continuous-seepage losses. [HO 04 81 2018-09 § M.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L5-L10) · [HO-3 2018-09 §§ A.1–A.2, C.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L69-L90)

The HO 04 81 aggregate is $10,000 for one policy period unless a higher limit is shown. It is part of, not additional to, the applicable Coverage B limit and includes removal, tear-out and replacement for access, post-removal testing, and related Coverage D increase. [HO 04 81 2018-09 §§ M.3–M.5](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L19-L31)

### HO 04 16 ordinance or law

If HO 04 16 edition 2018-09 is attached, O.1 writes back HO-3 2018-09 Exclusion D.1 only for the eligible ordinance-or-law cost path. It covers increased cost actually incurred to repair, rebuild, or demolish a damaged other structure because of an ordinance or law in force at the time of loss, and only when the underlying loss is covered under Section I. It does not convert an excluded cause of loss into covered property damage. [HO 04 16 2018-09 § O.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-16/2018-09.md#L5-L9) · [HO-3 2018-09 § D.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L91-L94)

The HO 04 16 limit is 10% of Coverage A unless a higher percentage is shown, and it is additional insurance. Payment requires completion of repair, rebuilding, or demolition as soon as reasonably possible and no later than two years after loss unless extended in writing. [HO 04 16 2018-09 §§ O.2–O.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-16/2018-09.md#L11-L37)

## Claim file review

For a Coverage B loss, retain the issued HO-3 edition, the Coverage A limit, the structure location and connection facts, and the rental facts. For HO-3 2018-09, document the B.3 tenant and private-garage analysis before the cause-of-loss analysis. Then document the direct physical loss, each applicable Section I exclusion, and, if attached, the endorsement form and edition, its trigger, limit treatment, deductible, and conditions. For an attached HO 04 90 claim, record whether 2010-10 or 2026-01 governs; for 2026-01, preserve evidence of the below-grade status and the installed, operable backflow device at the time of loss. [HO 04 90 2026-01 § W.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L42-L47) · [HO-3 2018-09 §§ S.1–S.2](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L101-L106) · [HO-3 2018-09 § S.5](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L109-L112)
