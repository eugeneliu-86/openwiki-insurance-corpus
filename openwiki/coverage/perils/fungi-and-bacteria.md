# Fungi, Wet or Dry Rot, and Bacteria Coverage

## Governing edition and effective-date boundary

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L13-L31] heading anchor "L13-L31" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L629-L637] heading anchor "L629-L637" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
HO 04 81 is an endorsement, not a stand-alone mold policy. Read it with the applicable HO-3 form: the HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form, edition 2024-03, provides the base direct-physical-loss grant and defines covered property, while its X.28 excludes loss caused by fungi, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria and X.29 preserves an exception only “as provided by a limited fungi endorsement” ([HO-3 2024-03](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L13-L31), [HO-3 2024-03](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L629-L637)).

The controlling HO 04 81 edition is determined by the policy’s writing or renewal date:

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L8-L12] heading anchor "L8-L12" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
- **2018-09** applies to policies written under that edition. It is **superseded** by 2026-09 for policies written or renewed on or after **2026-10-01**; it continues to govern a loss under a policy written under 2018-09 even if the loss is reported later ([HO 04 81 2018-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L8-L12)).
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L1-L7] heading anchor "L1-L7" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
- **2026-09** applies to policies written or renewed on or after **2026-10-01** and replaces 2018-09 for those policies ([HO 04 81 2026-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L1-L7)). Do not apply its $25,000 aggregate to an earlier policy merely because the claim is reported after that date.

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L17-L33] heading anchor "L17-L33" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
The endorsement controls where it conflicts with the policy, but otherwise preserves the base form’s terms, exclusions, conditions, and limits. The base form’s exclusion therefore supplies the starting exclusion; HO 04 81 writes back only the coverage expressly stated in the applicable edition ([HO 04 81 2018-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L17-L33)).

## Shared HO 04 81 coverage conditions

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L49-L61] heading anchor "L49-L61" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L9-L18] heading anchor "L9-L18" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
Both editions define “fungi” to include fungi, wet or dry rot, bacteria, and substances produced by them. Both require fungi-related direct physical loss to covered property to result from a covered cause of loss, with the covered cause occurring before the fungi causes the loss ([HO 04 81 2018-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L49-L61); [HO 04 81 2026-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L9-L18)). A covered water event is not enough by itself: the causal sequence and all base-form terms must still be satisfied.

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L63-L67] heading anchor "L63-L67" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L20-L21] heading anchor "L20-L21" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L93-L117] heading anchor "L93-L117" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L23-L33] heading anchor "L23-L33" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
Both editions exclude fungi arising from constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam and from flood ([HO 04 81 2018-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L63-L67); [HO 04 81 2026-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L20-L21)). The 2018-09 text also expressly excludes preexisting fungi, deterioration, decay, inadequate maintenance, defective work, and other listed conditions, and limits payment to covered property and expressly covered work ([HO 04 81 2018-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L93-L117)). The 2026-09 endorsement states that all provisions other than the limit are unchanged, so those shared conditions remain operative under that edition ([HO 04 81 2026-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L23-L33)).

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L69-L81] heading anchor "L69-L81" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L93-L117] heading anchor "L93-L117" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L14-L21] heading anchor "L14-L21" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L30-L33] heading anchor "L30-L33" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
When the trigger is met, the write-back covers only reasonable and necessary covered work: removal, building tear-out and replacement needed to access fungi, and post-remediation testing. Under 2018-09, remediation is covered only when related to covered direct physical loss; preventive monitoring, routine cleaning, testing or treatment unrelated to covered loss, replacement of undamaged property solely because fungi is present, and upgrades or betterments are excluded ([HO 04 81 2018-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L69-L81); [HO 04 81 2018-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L93-L117)). The 2026-09 wording preserves that scope while stating it more briefly ([HO 04 81 2026-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L14-L21), [HO 04 81 2026-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L30-L33)).

The 2026-09 endorsement expressly identifies the limit as its only change from 2018-09: **$25,000 replaces $10,000; the shared coverage conditions do not change** ([HO 04 81 2026-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L30-L33)). Under 2018-09, payments reduce the remaining aggregate and do not restore it; the deductible is applied under the policy and does not enlarge the aggregate ([HO 04 81 2018-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L151-L153), [HO 04 81 2018-09](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L203-L213)). The same single-aggregate relationship follows for 2026-09 because its W.6 preserves the other provisions.

## Contract analysis and handling boundary

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L13-L31] heading anchor "L13-L31" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L629-L637] heading anchor "L629-L637" does not exist in "../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
A sound coverage position should identify the HO-3 edition, the attached HO 04 81 edition, and the policy’s writing or renewal date before evaluating cause, sequence, exclusions, covered property, necessary remediation, deductible, and remaining aggregate. Do not infer coverage from the word “mold,” from a water loss, or from a remediation invoice. The base HO-3 grant and exclusion and the acting HO 04 81 write-back must be read together ([HO-3 2024-03](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L13-L31), [HO-3 2024-03](../../../forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L629-L637)).

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [../../../training/water-losses-101.md#L59-L91] heading anchor "L59-L91" does not exist in "../../../training/water-losses-101.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
Training material can help investigators separate water source, resulting damage, mitigation, and permanent repair, but it is not contract authority ([Water Losses 101](../../../training/water-losses-101.md#L13-L31), [Water Losses 101](../../../training/water-losses-101.md#L59-L91)). Investigation, inspection, testing, mitigation, or payment does not itself expand the endorsement. The contractual result comes from the applicable base form, attached HO 04 81 edition, policy conditions, and applicable law—not from guidance or training.
