# Files

- [Coverage D — Loss of Use](loss-of-use.md) - Coverage D pays the reasonable increase in living expenses necessary to maintain the insured's normal standard of living when a covered loss makes the residence premises not fit to live in. The 2018-09 HO-3 form adds a shortest-reasonably-required repair-or-replacement period, and HO 04 81 can include attributable Coverage D loss of use in its fungi aggregate.
