# HO-4 Contents Broad Form Editions

## Scope and edition status

This page describes the Mississippi HO-4 form files in the corpus. It is contract language, not claims or underwriting guidance. The 2013-07 form states that it is **superseded** by the 2021-10 edition for policies effective on or after October 1, 2021; the 2013-07 terms remain the governing terms for policies written under that edition. The 2013-07 form is effective July 1, 2013, and the 2021-10 form is effective October 1, 2021. ([HO-4 2013-07, header and supersession](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L1-L9); [HO-4 2021-10, header](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L1-L8))

HO-4 is a contents-oriented tenant form, but it is not safe to treat it as a dwelling form with Coverage A merely omitted from a summary. Both editions expressly provide that Coverage A is **not provided**. The 2013-07 form separately says that no building, building part, foundation, building system, fixture, improvement, landlord property, or legally responsible building property is insured under Coverage A; the 2021-10 form similarly excludes the dwelling and building, including attached structures, building systems, fixtures, garage, land, and other real-property interests. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.A A.1–A.36](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L67-L139); [HO-4 2021-10, I.A A.1–A.38](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L71-L147))

**Practical consequence:** do not infer Coverage A limits, dwelling replacement-cost coverage, ordinance-or-law building coverage, or building loss-of-use coverage from a standard HO-3 mental model. The forms nevertheless use the Coverage A limit as a reference point for Coverage D and some additional coverages. That drafting relationship is stated below; the forms do not turn it into Coverage A coverage.

## The B–F structure

| Part | 2013-07 | 2021-10 | Contents-form consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage A | Not provided | Not provided | No dwelling/building insurance under Coverage A. |
| Coverage B | Other Structures | Other Structures | Limited real-property coverage remains, but it is not Coverage A. |
| Coverage C | Personal Property | Personal Property | The principal contents coverage; location, special limits, perils, exclusions, and valuation control. |
| Coverage D | Loss of Use | Loss of Use | Additional living expense, fair rental value, and civil-authority loss of use. |
| Coverage E | Additional Coverages in Section I; Personal Liability in Section II | Same two-level structure | Section I E is property-related additional insurance; Section II E is liability. |
| Coverage F | Medical Payments to Others | Medical Payments to Others | No-fault medical-expense coverage for qualifying third-party injury, subject to exclusions. |

The form's section labels matter: “Coverage E” appears as **Additional Coverages** in Section I and as **Personal Liability** in Section II. Section II Coverage E is the liability coverage; Section II Coverage F is Medical Payments to Others. ([HO-4 2013-07, B–D and Section I E](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L141-L207); [HO-4 2013-07, Section II E–F](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L1005-L1125); [HO-4 2021-10, B–D](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L149-L217); [HO-4 2021-10, Section II E–F](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L1087-L1195))

### Coverage B — Other Structures

Both editions cover direct physical loss to qualifying other structures at the residence premises, rather than to the dwelling itself. The 2013-07 form requires the structure to be owned by or held for the insured's use, excludes the dwelling, land, business or rental use (with its stated storage exception), and covers materials and supplies at the residence premises for a covered other structure. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.B B.1–B.12](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L141-L165)) The 2021-10 form covers other structures on the residence premises in which the insured has an insurable interest, including permanently installed serving equipment and repair/rebuilding materials, but excludes land, rental or business use, farming, unlawful use, fences and similar non-roofed property. ([HO-4 2021-10, I.B B.1–B.21](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L149-L191))

The settlement wording changed materially. Under 2013-07, the form states that it determines value at actual cash value when repair or replacement is not completed and permits repair, rebuilding, or replacement. Under 2021-10, it will not pay more than the lesser of the amount necessary to repair or replace, actual cash value, or the Coverage B limit, and may take paid-for property at the value paid. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.B B.17–B.18 and B.31](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L175-L177); [HO-4 2013-07, I.B B.31–B.33](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L203-L207); [HO-4 2021-10, I.B B.29–B.33](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L207-L215))

### Coverage C — Personal Property

Coverage C is the core HO-4 protection. Both editions cover qualifying personal property owned or used by an insured at the residence premises and anywhere in the world, with requested coverage for guest or residence-employee property at the residence premises. The 2021-10 wording expressly limits property to personal, family, or household use and separately addresses temporarily removed property and property of others requested before loss. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.C C.1–C.5](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L209-L219); [HO-4 2021-10, I.C C.1–C.13](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L217-L243))

The 2021-10 edition changes several Coverage C special limits from 2013-07:

- electronic apparatus in a motor vehicle: $1,500 in 2021-10, compared with $1,000 in 2013-07;
- money and precious metals: $250 in 2021-10, compared with $200 for money, bank notes, bullion, and listed metals in 2013-07;
- theft of jewelry, watches, and precious stones: $2,000 in 2021-10, compared with $1,500 in 2013-07;
- theft of firearms: $2,500 in 2021-10, compared with $2,000 in 2013-07;
- watercraft and related property: $1,500 in 2021-10, compared with $1,000 in 2013-07; and
- business property on the residence premises: $3,000 in 2021-10, compared with $2,500 in 2013-07.

The silverware limit is $2,500 in both editions. These are special limits within Coverage C, not additional limits, unless the applicable provision says otherwise. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.C C.14–C.22](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L237-L253); [HO-4 2021-10, I.C C.14–C.22](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L245-L261))

The peril presentation also changed. In 2013-07 the form has a separate Section I “Perils Insured Against” list and requires direct physical loss caused by a named peril; in 2021-10 the Coverage C text itself enumerates the covered perils and related limitations. Both editions cover ordinary contents perils such as fire, windstorm or hail, explosion, riot or civil commotion, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, vandalism or malicious mischief, theft, falling objects, weight of ice/snow/sleet, accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam, freezing, artificially generated electrical current, and volcanic eruption, subject to the stated conditions and exclusions. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.P P.1–P.20](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L535-L575); [HO-4 2021-10, I.C C.23–C.42](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L263-L301))

### Coverage D — Loss of Use

Coverage D pays only when a covered loss causes loss of use. It includes additional living expense when the residence premises is not fit to live in, fair rental value for a rented or held-for-rental part that is unfit for normal use, and qualifying civil-authority loss of use. The insured must keep supporting records and use reasonable means to avoid unnecessary expense; civil-authority coverage requires a prohibition resulting from direct damage to nearby premises caused by a covered loss, not merely advice or a voluntary evacuation. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.D D.1–D.30](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L325-L385); [HO-4 2021-10, I.D D.1–D.24](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L381-L429))

The percentage changed from **30% of the Coverage A limit** in 2013-07 to **40% of the Coverage A limit** in 2021-10. Because both editions expressly say Coverage A is not provided, the page should report the formula without inventing a Coverage A amount; the declarations and the governing policy documents must be checked for the applicable limit. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.D D.1–D.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L325-L331); [HO-4 2021-10, I.D D.1–D.4](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L381-L389); [HO-4 2013-07, I.A A.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L67-L71); [HO-4 2021-10, I.A A.1–A.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L71-L79))

Section I Additional Coverages are property-side benefits, not Coverage A. They include debris removal, reasonable protective repairs, property removed from an endangered premises, credit-card/fund-transfer-card and forgery protection, loss assessment, collapse, glass, landlord furnishings, grave markers, ordinance-or-law costs, and refrigerated property. The editions differ in details and limits—for example, the 2013-07 fire-department charge is $500 and its credit-card/forgery group is $500, while 2021-10 states $750 and $1,000 respectively; the 2013-07 loss-assessment limit is $1,000, while 2021-10 states $1,500. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.E E.1–E.40](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L387-L467); [HO-4 2021-10, I.E E.1–E.40](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L431-L483))

## Exclusions and water boundaries

The forms require direct physical loss to covered property and do not insure every consequence of a peril. The principal HO-4 claim boundaries include:

- **Flood and surface water:** 2013-07 excludes flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, and spray; 2021-10 states the same exclusion in the Coverage C peril provisions.
- **Sewer, drain, and sump backup:** both editions exclude water backing up through sewers or drains and sump, sump-pump, or related-equipment overflow or discharge in the base form.
- **Water below ground and gradual water:** both editions exclude subsurface water and continuous or repeated seepage or leakage; sudden and accidental discharge is treated differently from gradual leakage.
- **Mold, rot, bacteria and deterioration:** both editions exclude mold/fungi, wet or dry rot, bacteria, wear, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, and similar conditions, subject to any express endorsement terms.
- **Vacancy, theft, and conduct:** the forms restrict vandalism or malicious mischief at a vacant residence and exclude theft by an insured or a person entrusted with the property, along with intentional loss, concealment, or misrepresentation.

These are coverage boundaries, not a conclusion that every water-related loss is excluded: the cause, direct physical damage, location, and applicable endorsement must be determined from the exact edition. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.P P.21–P.26 and P.45–P.60](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L577-L655); [HO-4 2013-07, I.X X.4–X.18 and X.28–X.32](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L665-L717); [HO-4 2021-10, I.C C.38–C.57](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L293-L331); [HO-4 2021-10, I.X X.7–X.32](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L741-L767))

## Settlement and post-loss conditions

For Coverage C, 2013-07 states that value is determined at the time of loss, depreciation may be applied for actual cash value, and the insurer may repair, replace, or pay. It also addresses pairs and sets, recovered property, proof of loss, inspection, and subrogation. The 2021-10 edition likewise permits repair or replacement with like kind and quality or payment of actual cash value, and says that a pair or set may be adjusted by repair/replacement or the difference in pre- and post-loss actual cash value. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.C C.61–C.81](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L339-L379); [HO-4 2021-10, I.C C.61–C.81](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L339-L379))

The Section I conditions make the claim process material to recovery. In 2013-07, the insured must give prompt notice, protect property, retain it for inspection, prepare an inventory, provide supporting records, submit a sworn proof of loss within 60 days after request, cooperate, preserve recovery rights, and comply with appraisal procedures; appraisal determines the amount of loss rather than coverage, and payment is stated to be due within 60 days after agreement, a binding appraisal award, or final judgment. ([HO-4 2013-07, I.S S.1–S.20](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L843-L883); [HO-4 2013-07, I.S S.35–S.46](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L913-L935)) In 2021-10, the form retains prompt notice, protection, inspection, inventory, records, examination under oath, 60-day sworn proof of loss after request, appraisal, and a 60-day payment rule after agreement, while adding detailed conditions for deductible, appraisal, other insurance, recovered property, and payment handling. ([HO-4 2021-10, I.S S.1–S.25](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L857-L907); [HO-4 2021-10, I.S S.60–S.80](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L977-L1003); [HO-4 2021-10, I.S S.73–S.80](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L1003-L1017))

For Section II, Coverage E pays damages for which an insured is legally liable because of covered bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence and provides a defense for a covered suit; the duty to defend ends when the applicable liability limit is exhausted. Coverage F pays necessary medical expenses for qualifying injury to a person other than an insured without regard to fault, generally for expenses incurred within three years of the accident. Both coverages contain separate exclusions for, among other things, expected or intended injury, business and professional services, vehicles or watercraft, and injury to insureds or household residents as stated in the applicable edition. ([HO-4 2013-07, II.E E.1–E.4 and II.F F.1–F.10](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L1005-L1089); [HO-4 2013-07, II.E E.16–E.30 and II.F F.16–F.27](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L1037-L1065); [HO-4 2021-10, II.E E.1–E.7 and II.F F.1–F.7](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L1087-L1169); [HO-4 2021-10, II.E E.8–E.32 and II.F F.8–F.20](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L1103-L1195))

## Material endorsements and document relationships

### Water endorsements that can change the base result

- **HO 04 92 Water Backup — Tenants (2019-03):** HO 04 92 **writes back** the base HO-4 sewer/drain and sump-backup exclusion for direct physical loss to covered personal property caused by “water backup,” but only while attached and only subject to its terms. It defines water backup as water backing up through a sewer or drain, or overflowing or discharging from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment; the limit is $2,500 and the endorsement deductible is $250. It preserves the base form's other exclusions and does not create coverage for property the policy does not cover. ([HO 04 92 W.1–W.13](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-92/2019-03.md#L13-L39); [HO 04 92 W.1–W.36](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-92/2019-03.md#L41-L113); [HO 04 92 W.2–W.9, limit](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-92/2019-03.md#L115-L133); [HO 04 92 W.1–W.5, deductible](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-92/2019-03.md#L165-L175); [HO-4 2013-07, water-backup exclusion](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2013-07.md#L657-L671); [HO-4 2021-10, water-backup exclusion](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-4/2021-10.md#L323-L331))
- **HO 04 27 Limited Water Damage Coverage (2016-05):** HO 04 27 **modifies** the applicable HO-4 water-damage terms by affirmatively covering sudden, accidental discharge or overflow from plumbing, heating, air-conditioning, sprinkler systems, and household appliances, and certain sudden breakage or freezing, including specified system or appliance repair. It preserves the endorsement's exclusions for repeated seepage, backup through sewers/drains/sumps, subsurface water, flood, fungi/rot/bacteria, and failed water-diversion systems. Its stated limits include $2,500 for sewer/drain backup or sump overflow and $5,000 for fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. ([HO 04 27 W.1–W.9](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-27/2016-05.md#L41-L59); [HO 04 27 W.10–W.18](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-27/2016-05.md#L61-L77); [HO 04 27 W.1–W.8, limits](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-27/2016-05.md#L93-L107))

The endorsements do not make the tenant's building a Coverage A risk. HO 04 92 covers “your covered personal property,” and HO 04 27 says that it applies to policy-covered property and does not create eligibility for otherwise ineligible property. ([HO 04 92 W.2 and W.11](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-92/2019-03.md#L41-L45); [HO 04 27 W.7 and W.23–W.25](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-27/2016-05.md#L27-L35))

### Other seeded endorsements: do not misapply the line

The seeded HO 04 61 Scheduled Personal Property editions (2012-02 and 2020-11) and HO 04 65 Coverage C Increased Special Limits edition (2018-09) identify their line as **HO-3**, not HO-4. They therefore should not be treated as HO-4 endorsements without an applicable policy schedule or attachment establishing that use. Their text illustrates the distinction: HO 04 61 requires property to be specifically described in a Schedule and applies its own scheduled-property coverage and limits; HO 04 65 changes only expressly increased Coverage C special limits and does not itself create coverage. ([HO 04 61 2012-02, header and schedule terms](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-61/2012-02.md#L1-L9); [HO 04 61 2020-11, header and schedule terms](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-61/2020-11.md#L1-L9); [HO 04 61 2020-11, W.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-61/2020-11.md#L55-L67); [HO 04 65 2018-09, header and Coverage Provided](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-65/2018-09.md#L1-L8); [HO 04 65 W.1–W.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-65/2018-09.md#L45-L57))

## Decision checklist

For a claim under an HO-4 policy, identify the governing edition first; confirm that the claimed property is contents or otherwise eligible under B–F because Coverage A is not provided; identify the covered peril and any water, vacancy, theft, deterioration, or conduct exclusion; check the declarations and attached endorsements; apply the edition's special limit and settlement wording; and then verify notice, protection, inspection, inventory, proof-of-loss, appraisal, cooperation, and recovery-rights compliance. When an endorsement and the base form conflict, use the endorsement only to the extent it changes the policy and preserve all unchanged terms. The form itself, not a general homeowners assumption or an internal training interpretation, controls.
