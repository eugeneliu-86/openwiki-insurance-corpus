# Roof Surfacing Settlement and Roof Claims

## The controlling sequence

Treat roof claims as three separate questions:

1. **Coverage and cause:** Is there direct physical loss to covered property caused by a covered peril? The HO-3 coverage grant pays only on that basis; the 2024-03 form also says the roof schedule trigger age does not itself establish coverage for a wind or hail loss. [HO-3 2024-03, AGR.3 and P.40–P.41](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L15-L20) [HO-3 2024-03, P.40–P.41](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L637-L639)
2. **Scope:** Identify the physically damaged roof property and separate covered event damage from pre-existing damage, wear, deterioration, defective installation, maintenance, and unrelated work.
3. **Valuation:** Only after the covered scope is established should the adjuster apply the applicable replacement-cost or ACV rule, then the deductible, limits, and other payment restrictions. The claims guidance expressly puts coverage before deductibles, limits, and other restrictions. [Water Loss Claim Handling Guidance, H.0.12–H.0.15](repo://guidelines/claims/water-loss-handling.md#L35-L43)

An HO 23 74 endorsement **modifies** the settlement basis; it does not broaden the underlying covered perils. The 2018-09 endorsement says it does not broaden a cause of loss and leaves other policy provisions applicable, while the 2025-05 endorsement says it does not change exclusions, conditions, limits, or deductibles. [HO 23 74 2018-09, W.0](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L13-L27) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.0](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L15-L47)

## Which edition governs

Use the endorsement attached to the policy and the edition applicable when that policy was written. HO 23 74 2018-09 is effective September 1, 2018; its form expressly says that HO 23 74 2025-05 **supersedes** it for policies effective on or after May 1, 2025, while the 2018-09 rule remains in force for policies written under it. Do not replace an older policy’s rule with the current schedule merely because the claim is adjusted later. [HO 23 74 2018-09, edition marker](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L2-L9)

The HO-3 base form likewise has editions. HO-3 2018-09 remains the governing form for policies written under that edition; HO-3 2024-03 is the later edition for policies effective on or after March 1, 2024. The current base form states that roof surfacing is settled at replacement cost unless an ACV roof schedule endorsement is attached. [HO-3 2018-09, edition marker](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L2-L9) [HO-3 2024-03, A.19–A.22](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L135-L145)

## Replacement cost versus ACV

Without an applicable HO 23 74 endorsement, the 2024-03 HO-3 provision states replacement-cost treatment for covered roof surfacing. Replacement cost is the like-kind-and-quality repair or replacement cost without depreciation, subject to the base form’s conditions and limits. [HO-3 2024-03, DEF.1–DEF.2 and A.22](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L41-L48) [HO-3 2024-03, A.19–A.22](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L135-L145)

When the attached HO 23 74 schedule applies, the roof surfacing payment is ACV rather than replacement cost. ACV starts with the reasonable like-kind-and-quality repair or replacement basis and deducts depreciation supported by the property’s age, condition, wear, deterioration, obsolescence, useful life, or other stated factors. The payment does not become replacement cost because the insured repairs or replaces the roof. [HO 23 74 2018-09, W.1.1–W.1.10](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L45-L67) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.3–W.1.10](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L59-L77)

### 2018-09 schedule

For the 2018-09 HO 23 74 edition, the ACV settlement section applies when the roof surfacing has reached **15 years of age at the time of loss**. Age must be determined from reliable available evidence; if reliable evidence does not establish age, the endorsement permits use of available information about condition, construction, materials, and roof history. [HO 23 74 2018-09, W.1.2 and W.1.17–W.1.18](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L47-L55) [HO 23 74 2018-09, W.1.16–W.1.18](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L75-L81)

For composition shingle roof surfacing subject to the age classification, the 2018-09 endorsement states a **25% payable percentage**, with the deductible applied after the payable amount is determined. Do not describe that percentage as a general rule for every roof material or every state without checking the policy and applicable overlay. [HO 23 74 2018-09, W.3.1–W.3.5](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L233-L247)

The 2018-09 endorsement permits depreciation of labor when labor is necessary to repair or replace the roof surfacing, applied reasonably and consistently with the damaged property’s condition. It does not permit depreciation for damage caused solely by the covered loss. [HO 23 74 2018-09, W.1.3–W.1.8](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L49-L61)

### 2025-05 schedule

For the 2025-05 HO 23 74 edition, ACV treatment applies when **Roof Age is 12 years or greater**. Roof Age is the age of the damaged roof surfacing at loss; the form identifies installation records, permits, invoices, inspections, photographs, statements, and other reliable evidence as possible sources. A limited-area replacement does not establish the age of the surrounding roof unless evidence shows one installation. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.4–W.1.8](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L63-L77)

For composition shingle subject to an age-based adjustment, the 2025-05 deductible section states a **20% payable percentage**, applied before the deductible. Separately, its exclusions section says covered roof-surfacing payment will not be less than **30% of applicable replacement cost**. Those provisions are not safely reducible to one assumed number: the 20% composition-shingle rule and the 30% minimum-floor language create an apparent internal drafting conflict, compounded by duplicated malformed floor text elsewhere in the form. Apply neither provision by assumption; preserve the conflict in the claim analysis and escalate for controlling legal, filing, or policy interpretation. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.3.3–W.3.5](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L327-L337) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.4.1–W.4.4](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L415-L425) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.35–W.1.37](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L123-L135)

The 2025-05 edition does not permit labor depreciation unless the law applicable to the loss permits it. If permitted, labor depreciation must be reasonable and consistent with the damaged property’s condition. This is a change from the 2018-09 wording and must be applied by the governing edition, not by a generic ACV convention. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.38–W.1.39](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L133-L135)

## What is—and is not—roof surfacing

Roof surfacing is the exterior, weather-exposed material applied to shed water, such as shingles, shakes, tiles, slate, metal panels, membrane material, and similar covering. The 2025 form distinguishes it from the roof system: deck, underlayment, flashing, ventilation components, fasteners, and roof accessories are not automatically roof surfacing. A damaged component must be evaluated under the coverage and settlement terms that actually apply to that component. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.8 definitions](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L829-L859)

The 2025 endorsement separately addresses flashing, underlayment, fasteners, vents, and related components when they are damaged by a covered loss and necessary to complete covered repairs. That provision does not convert all such components into scheduled roof surfacing. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.40–W.1.42](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L137-L141)

## Damage, exclusions, and matching

The schedule does not cover a roof merely because it is old, nor does it cover the cost to correct pre-existing damage, deterioration, wear, defective design or installation, faulty maintenance, gradual or repeated leakage, settling, or excluded water causes. The 2025 endorsement preserves those exclusions and says all policy exclusions and limitations remain applicable. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.4.1–W.4.14 and W.4.22–W.4.28](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L417-L467) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.4.49–W.4.53](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L513-L523)

An adjuster should distinguish direct physical damage from pre-existing condition and pay only the covered portion when the evidence permits separation. The 2018 endorsement requires payment only for damage directly caused by a covered peril and excludes pre-existing damage; the 2025 endorsement likewise requires separating covered damage from conditions not caused by the covered loss. [HO 23 74 2018-09, W.1.41–W.1.42](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L123-L129) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.41–W.1.42](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L137-L141)

Matching is a scope question before it is a valuation question. Neither edition makes an appearance difference alone a basis to replace undamaged surfacing. The 2025 wording allows matching only to the extent necessary to repair direct physical damage with like-kind-and-quality materials and does not require identical materials when reasonably comparable materials are available. [HO-3 2024-03, A.24–A.25](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L143-L147) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.24–W.1.26](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L101-L109)

## Deductibles and other endorsements

Apply the applicable deductible to covered loss after determining the covered scope and the schedule adjustment required by the governing edition. The 2018 endorsement applies the deductible after determining the payable amount; the 2025 endorsement applies a roof-surfacing adjustment before the deductible and applies the deductible only to covered loss. A deductible cannot create coverage for excluded or undamaged property. [HO 23 74 2018-09, W.3.1–W.3.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L233-L247) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.3.3–W.3.12](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L327-L351)

If HO 23 77 is attached, its windstorm-or-hail percentage deductible applies to a covered wind or hail loss, including covered roof damage, and the endorsement does not create coverage for otherwise excluded loss. Confirm which deductible the policy and facts require; do not treat the percentage deductible endorsement as a roof valuation schedule. [HO 23 77 2022-07, W.0 and W.1.1–W.1.10](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-77/2022-07.md#L13-L53) [HO 23 77 2022-07, W.1.21–W.1.25](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-77/2022-07.md#L95-L105)

## Evidence and investigation boundaries

Request and preserve evidence that bears on cause, scope, age, condition, and value: inspection findings, photographs, samples, installation and repair records, permits, invoices, estimates, maintenance records, weather information, and relevant expert or contractor opinions. The endorsement requires access and cooperation, but the request must remain tied to the claim. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.15–W.1.18 and W.1.32–W.1.34](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L87-L95) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.44–W.1.45](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L143-L147)

Inspection and expert evidence help determine whether damage resulted from the reported covered event; they do not themselves create coverage. The form permits consideration of location, pattern, character, and extent, and permits separation of covered damage from wear, deterioration, defective installation, mechanical damage, and other conditions not caused by the event. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.41–W.1.42](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L137-L141) [Water Loss Claim Handling Guidance, H.0.3–H.0.10](repo://guidelines/claims/water-loss-handling.md#L19-L33)

Do not require permanent repairs before inspection when reasonable temporary protection will preserve the property. The endorsement permits reasonable temporary protective measures, requires preservation of damaged property when reasonably possible, and permits supplemental consideration when additional covered damage is established. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.15–W.1.18 and W.1.43–W.1.45](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L87-L95) [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.1.43–W.1.45 and W.1.52–W.1.55](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L143-L167)

The claims investigation boundary is supported damage, not every item in a contractor estimate. Review scope, material, quantity, labor, removal, disposal, access, code work, overhead and profit, and matching separately; pay only amounts reasonably necessary and otherwise covered under the policy. [HO 23 74 2025-05, W.2.28–W.2.40](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2025-05.md#L235-L259) [Roof Claims and the ACV Schedule, L.2.16–L.2.27](repo://training/roof-claims-and-the-schedule.md#L121-L167)

## Underwriting and regulatory overlays are not settlement terms

The internal underwriting manual is guidance, not a policy term: it expressly says not to use the manual to alter coverage. Rule 210 requires an inspection before binding at 15 years, declines at or above 25 years, and requires reliable age evidence and condition review. Those are risk-selection controls, not permission to deny a covered claim or substitute an underwriting age rule for the attached HO 23 74 edition. [Personal Lines Underwriting Manual, Rule 100.B and Rule 100.D](repo://manuals/underwriting/manual.md#L21-L35) [Personal Lines Underwriting Manual, Rule 210.A–Rule 210.D](repo://manuals/underwriting/manual.md#L2211-L2235)

Florida’s bulletins likewise address underwriting and communications rather than silently rewriting a policy. OIR-2023-04 supersedes OIR-2019-11 for actions on or after April 11, 2023; the current bulletin distinguishes roof age from condition, requires reliable evidence, and says an ACV roof schedule may be required at 10 years only when the policy form authorizes that method. It also requires a roof inspection before binding coverage for a 15-year-old roof. The bulletin therefore **constrains** underwriting and disclosure practice; the attached policy endorsement still controls claim valuation. [Florida Bulletin OIR-2019-11, supersession marker](repo://bulletins/FL/oir-2019-11-roof-age.md#L2-L9) [Florida Bulletin OIR-2023-04, B.1 and B.2.5–B.2.10](repo://bulletins/FL/oir-2023-04-roof-age-nonrenewal.md#L13-L29) [Florida Bulletin OIR-2023-04, B.2.5–B.2.10 and B.3.1–B.3.3](repo://bulletins/FL/oir-2023-04-roof-age-nonrenewal.md#L69-L79) [Florida Bulletin OIR-2023-04, B.3.1–B.3.3](repo://bulletins/FL/oir-2023-04-roof-age-nonrenewal.md#L161-L167)

Colorado DOI-2022-08 concerns roof settlement disclosures. It requires clear communication of the method, depreciation, materials, labor, deductible, exclusions, and age basis; it states a 15-year schedule trigger and a 25% minimum when its requirements apply. The bulletin **constrains** disclosure and claim administration but does not authorize presenting a disclosure as a replacement for the policy. When its requirements appear to conflict with a filed form or the 2025 schedule’s 12-year/20%-versus-30% language, preserve the conflict and obtain state-specific legal or compliance direction rather than silently changing the contract. [Colorado Bulletin DOI-2022-08, B.1.1–B.1.8](repo://bulletins/CO/doi-2022-08-roof-settlement.md#L13-L31) [Colorado Bulletin DOI-2022-08, B.2.2–B.2.16](repo://bulletins/CO/doi-2022-08-roof-settlement.md#L59-L91) [Colorado Bulletin DOI-2022-08, B.2.19–B.2.34](repo://bulletins/CO/doi-2022-08-roof-settlement.md#L97-L127)

## A practical claim record

A defensible file should identify the governing HO-3 and HO 23 74 editions, the covered peril and physical damage, the roof-surfacing area and other components, age evidence and its reliability, pre-loss condition, supported repair or replacement scope, matching analysis, labor-depreciation rule, schedule calculation, deductible, limits, exclusions, and any unresolved ambiguity. Claim guidance calls for documenting the reported event, observed conditions, evidence, cause assessment, and payment reasoning; it is operational guidance and does not create coverage. [Water Loss Claim Handling Guidance, H.0.1–H.0.10 and H.0.21–H.0.22](repo://guidelines/claims/water-loss-handling.md#L15-L33) [Water Loss Claim Handling Guidance, H.0.21–H.0.22](repo://guidelines/claims/water-loss-handling.md#L53-L57)

When the cause, roof age, material classification, matching necessity, labor depreciation, schedule percentage, or state overlay remains disputed, do not turn an estimate, inspection, bulletin, or training statement into an automatic coverage conclusion. Document the competing evidence, continue the supported investigation, and escalate the unresolved contract or regulatory issue.
