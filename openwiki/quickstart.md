This is a routing map, not a substitute for the issued policy, declarations, attached endorsements, state forms, applicable law, or operational authority. Contract wording controls over memoranda, training, manuals, and internal guidance ([document families](../README.md#L15-L27); [guidance boundary](../training/guidance-versus-contract.md#L15-L23)).

## Start with the governing policy package

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [policy-assembly/editions-and-state-attachments.md#L13-L25] heading anchor "L13-L25" does not exist in "policy-assembly/editions-and-state-attachments.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
Before interpreting a loss, record the line, state, policy or renewal effective date, declarations, coverage part, damaged interest, and complete attachment package. Then read the issued base form, endorsements, and state amendatory forms together. A later edition does not rewrite an earlier policy; if the package is incomplete or inconsistent, obtain the issued copy or escalate ([editions and state attachments](policy-assembly/editions-and-state-attachments.md#L13-L25)).

### HO 04 81 date gate

For fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria questions, select the endorsement edition before applying a limit:

| Policy population | Governing HO 04 81 edition | Aggregate |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Policy written under **2018-09** | **2018-09**, still governing that policy even when the loss is reported later | **$10,000** |
| Policy written or renewed **on or after 2026-10-01** | **2026-09**, if actually attached; it replaces 2018-09 for this population | **$25,000** |

The 2026-09 endorsement identifies the limit increase as its only change: the shared coverage conditions remain unchanged. Neither amount is additional insurance; the applicable aggregate covers the total covered fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria loss, regardless of the number of claims, insureds, or items of property ([2018-09 form](../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L8-L12), [2018-09 limit](../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L161-L169); [2026-09 form](../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L1-L7), [2026-09 limit and change](../forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2026-09.md#L23-L33)). Do not import the $25,000 limit into a policy governed by 2018-09 merely because the claim is reported after 2026-10-01.

## Route by subject

| Question | Open first | Then verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fungi, mold, wet or dry rot, or bacteria | [Fungi and bacteria coverage](coverage/perils/fungi-and-bacteria.md) | Governing HO 04 81 edition, covered cause and sequence, exclusions, covered property, necessary work, deductible, and remaining aggregate |
| Plumbing discharge, seepage, outside water, flood, or resulting damage | [Water damage and microbial loss](coverage/perils/water-damage.md) | Cause, sequence, base-form exclusion, endorsement write-back, and edition-specific limit |
| Policy date, endorsement, or state attachment uncertainty | [Editions and state attachments](policy-assembly/editions-and-state-attachments.md) | Issued wording, attachment, schedules, state form, and precedence |
| Claim intake, investigation, mitigation, payment, or closure | [Mold claim handling](claims/guidelines/mold-claim-handling.md) or [water loss handling](claims/guidelines/water-loss-handling.md) | Contract route first, evidence and causation, scope and valuation, authority, payment, and escalation |

<!-- openwiki: broken internal link [coverage/perils/fungi-and-bacteria.md#L14-L29] heading anchor "L14-L29" does not exist in "coverage/perils/fungi-and-bacteria.md". Fix the href or restore the target, then delete this comment. -->
HO 04 81 is a write-back to the base policy’s fungi exclusion, not a stand-alone mold policy. Both editions require fungi-related direct physical loss to covered property to result from a covered cause of loss occurring before the fungi loss, and exclude fungi arising from constant or repeated seepage or leakage and from flood. The endorsement’s express coverage and the base policy’s remaining terms must be read together ([fungi coverage](coverage/perils/fungi-and-bacteria.md#L14-L29)). A water event, vendor label, remediation invoice, inspection, or partial payment does not by itself establish coverage.

## Separate contract from operations

After the contract route is established, branch to the operational domain:

- **Claims:** use the [mold claim handling](claims/guidelines/mold-claim-handling.md) and [water loss handling](claims/guidelines/water-loss-handling.md) pages for intake, moisture and microbial evidence, mitigation, investigation, reserves, payment, communication, and closure. Keep coverage, causation, scope, valuation, authority, and recovery as separate work products.
- **Claims manual:** use [property perils and loss types](claims/manual/property-perils-and-loss-types.md) for cross-peril handling and escalation. Internal procedures organize the file; they do not create or restrict coverage.
- **Policy assembly:** use [editions and state attachments](policy-assembly/editions-and-state-attachments.md) when form, edition, attachment, declaration, or state applicability is uncertain. A state amendatory form is contract wording when attached; a bulletin or internal rule is a separate constraint.
- **Water context:** use [water damage and microbial loss](coverage/perils/water-damage.md) to distinguish covered water causes from seepage, flood, and endorsement-specific microbial write-backs.
