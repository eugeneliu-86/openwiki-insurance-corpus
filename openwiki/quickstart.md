## Start with the controlling authority

This is a synthetic insurance corpus, not a carrier product, ISO form, or legal advice. Forms and bulletins are frozen when issued; a changed form is issued as a new edition, while internal guidelines are living carrier material. A prior frozen edition continues to govern policies written under it. [Corpus scope and authority lifecycle](repo://README.md#L3-L18) · [Frozen and living authority](repo://README.md#L28-L36)

| Question | Controlling starting point | Must not be substituted for it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does an issued policy cover, exclude, require, or pay? | The issued-policy record: written date, effective date, state, complete Declarations, selected base form, and verified attached forms. | A repository copy, a bulletin, or staff guidance. |
| What may the insurer do about issuance, renewal, notice, deductibles, filing, or reporting? | The applicable state bulletin after its state and effective-date scope test. | Contract wording or a referral approval. |
| How should staff investigate, document, reserve rights, refer, or bind? | The applicable internal claims or underwriting guidance, after identifying contract and regulatory controls. | A coverage determination or an insured obligation. |

Internal Water Loss Claim Handling Guidance directs adjusters to establish and document water source before evaluating damage, but it is not policy language and must not be quoted to an insured or claimant. [Water Loss Claim Handling Guidance C.1](repo://guidelines/claims/water-loss-handling.md#L1-L11)

## Minimum record and assembly controls

Before a coverage conclusion, obtain the policy-written date, policy effective date, state, full Declarations, limits, selected deductibles, selected HO-3 edition, and every actually attached endorsement or amendment with its edition. For a claim, obtain loss date, alleged source, property involved, and facts needed by the potentially applicable provision. If an attachment, edition, or necessary fact is unverified, keep that issue unresolved; repository availability does not establish attachment.

HO-3 2018-09 supersedes HO-3 2011-05 for policies written on or after 2018-09-01. HO-3 2011-05 remains in force for policies written under it and governs adjustment irrespective of the report date. [HO-3 2018-09 applicability](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L1-L4) · [HO-3 2011-05 supersession and legacy force](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2011-05.md#L1-L7)

Use [Governing Form Editions and Policy Assembly](/openwiki/coverage/policy-editions-and-governing-forms.md) for an uncertain form set, attachment, edition compatibility, or Texas amendment. Its safe order is:

1. Select the base HO-3 edition from the policy-written date.
2. Verify the Declarations and actual attachments; an “if attached” clause does not prove attachment.
3. Apply a verified, in-scope state amendment where its conflict rule directs.
4. Apply the property and cause-of-loss grant, exclusions, a narrow verified write-back, settlement, limits, deductible, and conditions in that order.

HO 01 45 attaches to HO-3, applies to policies effective on or after 2022-01-01, and governs conflicts with the attached form; its scope does not select a base edition or prove that a particular policy carries the amendment. [HO 01 45 scope and conflict rule](repo://forms/HO/TX/HO-01-45/2022-01.md#L1-L4)

## HO 04 90: select the verified edition before analysis

**Contract authority.** HO 04 90 attaches to HO-3 and modifies Section I Exclusions A.3; only a verified issued attachment activates that route. HO 04 90 2010-10 remains in force for policies written under it. HO 04 90 2026-01 replaces 2010-10 for policies written on or after 2026-01-01, remains in force for policies written under it, and governs those losses regardless of report date. HO 04 90 2027-01 replaces 2026-01 for policies written on or after 2027-01-01. [HO 04 90 2010-10 status and attachment target](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2010-10.md#L1-L7) · [HO 04 90 2026-01 status, attachment target, and replacement](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L1-L8) · [HO 04 90 2027-01 attachment target and replacement](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2027-01.md#L1-L4)

| Verified attached HO 04 90 edition | Apply after source and property analysis | Do not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2010-10** | W.1 coverage; W.4 retained exclusions; W.5 maintenance condition; W.2 $5,000 policy-period default sublimit unless higher limit appears in endorsement Declarations; W.3 separate $500 deductible; and W.6 settlement. | Import 2026-01 or 2027-01 terms. |
| **2026-01** | W.1 coverage, W.4, W.5, W.2 $10,000 default sublimit, W.3 separate $1,000 deductible, W.6 below-grade backflow condition when triggered, and W.7 settlement. | Apply the later edition merely because it is available. |
| **2027-01** | W.1 coverage, W.4, W.5, W.2 $10,000 default sublimit, W.3 separate $1,000 deductible, W.6 below-grade backflow condition when triggered, and W.7 settlement. | Treat it as proof that it was attached to the policy. |
| **Attachment, edition, or written date unverified** | Obtain the issued endorsement and Declarations. | Choose by report date, current guidance, or repository availability. |

HO 04 90 2010-10 W.2 makes its sublimit part of—not additional to—the A, B, and C limits, and W.3 substitutes its deductible for the Section I Declarations deductible on a covered endorsement loss. HO 04 90 2026-01 W.2-W.3 and HO 04 90 2027-01 W.2-W.3 use the same limit structure and $1,000 separate deductible. [2010-10 W.2-W.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2010-10.md#L13-L19) · [2026-01 W.2-W.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L17-L27) · [2027-01 W.2-W.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2027-01.md#L13-L23)

HO 04 90 2010-10 W.1, 2026-01 W.1, and 2027-01 W.1 each write back the stated direct physical loss to A/B/C property from sewer/drain backup or sump-related overflow or discharge, including mechanical breakdown. Each edition’s W.4 preserves HO-3 A.1 flood/surface-water and A.2 subsurface-water exclusions; W.5 separately excludes a qualifying known, unremedied maintenance failure. The 2026-01 and 2027-01 W.6 require an installed, operable serving-sewer backwater valve or equivalent when the residence has finished area below grade; 2010-10 has no W.6 requirement. [2010-10 W.1 and W.4-W.5](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2010-10.md#L9-L29) · [2026-01 W.1 and W.4-W.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L10-L51) · [2027-01 W.1 and W.4-W.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2027-01.md#L6-L48) · [HO-3 2018-09 A.1-A.3](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L69-L77)

For the source decision, property-specific settlement, and fungi result, use [Water Damage Exclusions and Water Backup Write-Back](/openwiki/coverage/property/water-damage-and-backup.md). For claims fact development, use [Water Loss Claims Handling](/openwiki/operations/claims-water-loss-handling.md); it is internal guidance, not an alternative contract. For proposed endorsement configuration, use the relevant [Florida appetite](/openwiki/underwriting/florida-appetite.md) or [Texas appetite](/openwiki/underwriting/texas-appetite.md) page and [Referral and Binding Authority](/openwiki/underwriting/referral-and-binding-authority.md). The referral matrix makes requests above $25,000—and above $10,000 with finished below-grade area without a battery-backed sump pump—referral conditions; that internal threshold neither proves attachment nor replaces HO 04 90’s contractual terms. [Referral matrix R.3](repo://guidelines/authority/referral-matrix.md#L19-L31)

Florida H.4 and Texas G.3 use a battery-backed sump pump as a pre-bind internal control for a requested HO 04 90 limit above $10,000 with a finished below-grade area. That control is distinct from the 2026-01 and 2027-01 W.6 at-loss requirement for a serving-sewer backflow device, which states no limit threshold. [Florida appetite H.4](repo://guidelines/appetite/fl-homeowners.md#L27-L33) · [Texas appetite G.3](repo://guidelines/appetite/tx-homeowners.md#L23-L29) · [HO 04 90 2026-01 W.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L46-L51) · [HO 04 90 2027-01 W.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2027-01.md#L42-L48)

[HO 04 81 M.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-81/2018-09.md#L1-L10) · [HO 04 16 O.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-16/2018-09.md#L1-L9) · [HO-3 2018-09 C.2 and D.1](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L83-L94)

[HO 23 74 R.1-R.2 and R.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L1-L15) · [HO 23 74 R.6](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-23-74/2018-09.md#L37-L41) · [HO 01 45 T.1](repo://forms/HO/TX/HO-01-45/2022-01.md#L5-L11) · [HO-3 2018-09 A.4, D.1, and S.5](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L31-L34) · [HO-3 2018-09 D.1 and S.5](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L91-L112) · [Texas Bulletin B-2021-08 B.3](repo://bulletins/TX/2021-08-windstorm-deductible.md#L15-L19)
- **Florida Bulletin OIR-2023-04 controls** personal residential policies issued or renewed in Florida with effective dates on or after 2023-07-01 for the stated roof-age, inspection, offer, nonrenewal, deductible-overlap, and reporting rules. It does not select an individual policy’s roof-settlement wording. [OIR-2023-04 scope and controls](repo://bulletins/FL/2023-04-roof-age-nonrenewal.md#L1-L37)
- **Texas Bulletin B-2021-08 controls** residential property policies delivered or issued for delivery in Texas with effective dates on or after 2022-01-01 for separate windstorm/hail deductible configuration, application, disclosure, and filing requirements. [B-2021-08 scope and requirements](repo://bulletins/TX/2021-08-windstorm-deductible.md#L1-L37)

## Final check

Before an insured-facing conclusion, payment calculation, binding decision, or referral disposition, confirm the issued record and selected edition; label the authority layer; apply the contract sequence; test the state overlay independently; and record facts, calculations, notices, referrals, and escalations without turning internal workflow into a policy duty. If a gate cannot be established, do not fill the gap with a later form, assumed endorsement, bulletin, or internal instruction.
