# Homeowners Coverage Wiki: Start Here

## What this wiki can answer

This wiki is a routing and source-selection aid, not a substitute for the policy packet or a coverage decision. Its substantive base-form corpus contains **HO-3** editions **2018-09** and **2024-03**, plus **HO-6** edition **2023-02**. Start with the line and edition actually issued; do not imply a base-form coverage position for a homeowners line that is absent from this corpus. The HO-3 (2018-09) remains in force for policies written under it even though it is superseded for policies effective on or after March 1, 2024. [HO-3 (2018-09), status](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L2-L9) [HO-3 (2024-03), form identity](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L2-L8) [HO-6 (2023-02), form identity](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-6/2023-02.md#L2-L8)

Use the pages below to locate the governing provisions and the appropriate operational framework. They do not replace fact development, applicable law, or authorized legal/compliance review where the packet or sources leave a material issue unresolved.

## Authority hierarchy: keep the control types separate

| Order of use | Source type | What it does | What it does **not** do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Issued policy record** — loss-date or action-date Declarations, exact base-form edition, and every attached endorsement and state amendment | Establishes the contract: insured/location, coverage grant, exclusions, conditions, limits, deductible, and settlement terms. | A library form, quote, producer statement, or later edition is not proof that a term was issued. |
| 2 | **Applicable law and regulator materials** | Constrain insurer conduct, such as filing, notices, recordkeeping, deductible administration, underwriting, and claims handling. | A bulletin is not automatically an endorsement or a new coverage grant. |
| 3 | **Internal underwriting and claims guidance** | Directs fact gathering, documentation, authority, referral, inspection, and workflow. | It cannot create, expand, restrict, waive, or decide policy coverage. |
| 4 | **Memoranda, training, and summaries** | May help identify a question or explain an operational expectation. | They are not policy terms; take contractual terms and numbers from the controlling issued materials. |

The contract starts with the policy packet. In the reviewed HO-3 forms, the Declarations identify the insured, residence premises, limits, and deductible, while the policy consists of the applicable provisions, endorsements, and Declarations. A policy term changes only through authorized written action. [HO-3 (2018-09), AGR.1–AGR.2](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L15-L17) [HO-3 (2024-03), AGR.8–AGR.12](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L29-L37)

An attached endorsement or state amendment has the contractual role stated in its own text: apply it within that scope and retain compatible, unmodified base-form terms. Regulatory and guidance material have a different role. Florida's roof-age bulletin expressly says that it does not amend a policy; the Texas appetite guide calls itself internal operating guidance, directs users to the applicable coverage language for what is insured, and says it cannot change coverage. [Florida Bulletin OIR-2023-04, B.1](repo://bulletins/FL/oir-2023-04-roof-age-nonrenewal.md#L45-L49) [Texas Homeowners Appetite Guide, H.0](repo://guidelines/appetite/tx-homeowners.md#L13-L35)

> **Do not collapse eligibility into coverage.** Roof age, inspection findings, an appetite rule, or a referral can determine whether and how the carrier may underwrite or service a risk. They do not by themselves establish a covered cause of loss or an ACV settlement basis. Florida requires this distinction in its roof-age controls, including the requirement that claim personnel not impose an underwriting eligibility requirement when adjusting a covered loss. [Florida Bulletin OIR-2023-04, B.2.41–B.2.42](repo://bulletins/FL/oir-2023-04-roof-age-nonrenewal.md#L141-L143)

## The defensible-answer sequence

1. **Fix the event and the role.** Record policy number, named insured, risk location/state, line, policy period, and loss date or underwriting-action date. A loss uses the policy snapshot in force when the loss occurred; a proposed issue, renewal, or change uses its own action-date record.

2. **Assemble the governing record.** Obtain the Declarations, exact base-form edition, every attached endorsement and state amendment, effective dates, selection/change records, and applicable state requirements. Use [Assembling the Governing Homeowners Policy](/openwiki/policy-assembly/assembling-the-governing-policy) for the assembly and edition-lifecycle method.
3. **Classify by coverage part first.** Identify whether the question is about building property, other structures, contents or assessments, loss of use, liability, or medical payments. Then move to the narrower subject page.
4. **Establish entitlement before arithmetic.** Apply the coverage grant, definitions, exclusions, exceptions, conditions, and facts. Only then calculate settlement basis, sublimits/limits, deductible, and payment conditions. A deductible or ACV schedule does not create coverage.
5. **Add the state and role overlay.** Apply an attached state amendment as contract text where applicable; separately apply the state bulletin or other regulatory requirement as a conduct constraint. Use claims or underwriting guidance for workflow, file controls, and referrals—not as policy wording.
6. **Record and refer.** Preserve the governing sources, attachment/edition evidence, relevant facts, calculation, communications, and unresolved issues. Refer a material conflict, missing attachment evidence, uncertain causation, or authority/compliance issue rather than selecting a result by assumption.

The current HO-3 illustrates why that order matters: coverage is subject to the terms and applicable limit, while the policy applies to losses during the policy period; its roof-surfacing settlement provision applies only to a **covered** roof-surfacing loss and only yields to an attached ACV schedule. [HO-3 (2024-03), AGR.3 and AGR.8](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L19-L29) [HO-3 (2024-03), I.A A.22](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2024-03.md#L141-L141)

## Stop-and-check controls

Before communicating a material position, test these failure points:

- **Wrong time or edition:** Did the analysis use the policy/action record in force at the relevant time, not the newest repository form? The 2018 HO-3 is expressly retained for policies written under it. [HO-3 (2018-09), status](repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-3/2018-09.md#L8-L9)
- **Attachment assumed:** Is each endorsement or state amendment shown as attached and effective, rather than merely available in the form library?
- **Coverage skipped:** Has the file established property, cause, direct physical loss, exclusions, and conditions before applying a limit, ACV schedule, or deductible?
- **Authority category mixed:** Is a regulator bulletin recorded as a conduct requirement and a guide recorded as operational direction, rather than presented as policy language?
- **State omitted:** Has the risk location been checked for an attached state amendment and applicable state conduct rules? For Texas wind/hail claims, the regulator requires application of the deductible in the policy in force for the loss and documentation of the policy terms and factual basis. [Texas Bulletin B-2021-08, B.2.10 and B.2.17–B.2.21](repo://bulletins/TX/b-2021-08-windstorm-deductibles.md#L67-L89)
- **Material uncertainty hidden:** Are source/pathway, causation, allocation, policy terms, deadline, or authority uncertainties documented and referred instead of resolved from a label or assumption?

A defensible answer identifies the governing document and edition, the factual question it answers, the applicable state/operational constraint, and any unresolved issue. It does not turn a workflow aid into a contract term.
