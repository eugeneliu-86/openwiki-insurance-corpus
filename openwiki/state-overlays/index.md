# Files

- [California State Overlay](california.md) - California’s homeowners overlay separates the regulator-mandated earthquake offer and disclosure process from earthquake coverage supplied by an attached endorsement. It records the effective positions of HO 01 04 and both CDI earthquake-offer bulletins, including deductible, notice, deadline, filing, underwriting, and claims controls.
- [Colorado State Overlay](colorado.md) - Colorado requirements for HO 01 05, windstorm and hail deductibles, roof settlement disclosures, policy assembly, underwriting controls, and claim handling. Covers bulletin supersession, effective periods, deductible thresholds, notice and disclosure duties, roof valuation, deadlines, and evidence requirements.
- [Florida State Overlay](florida.md) - Edition-aware reference for Florida HO 01 09 and DP 01 09 amendatory forms, OIR roof-age and hurricane-deductible bulletins, and the related underwriting, notice, deductible, roof-settlement, and claims obligations. Use the policy-effective date to select the contract edition, then apply the bulletin and internal-guidance boundaries without treating them as interchangeable authority.
- [Illinois State Overlay](illinois.md) - Illinois regulatory and form overlay for producer-licensing controls, water-backup disclosures, and the HO 01 12 Illinois amendatory endorsement. Summarizes effective dates, notice and deadline rules, deductible treatment, contract boundaries, and Illinois claims-handling controls.
- [Louisiana State Overlay](louisiana.md) - Louisiana’s hurricane and windstorm-deductible requirements across Bulletin LDI-2012-05, current Bulletin LDI-2020-07, and the HO 01 17 amendatory form. Covers edition selection, named-storm periods, deductible rules, disclosure and advance-notice periods, claims deadlines, claim duties, and separate Rule 540 underwriting controls.
- [New York State Overlay](new-york.md) - New York’s homeowners overlay joins the HO 01 31 amendatory endorsement with New York DFS notice and data-call requirements. It records the superseded 2010 nonrenewal position, the current 2016 cancellation and nonrenewal position, the 2018 annual homeowners data call, deductible and claims duties, and the boundary between regulatory requirements and internal underwriting procedures.
- [North Carolina State Overlay](north-carolina.md) - North Carolina’s HO 01 32 (2018-05) contract overlay, fungi-limit disclosure bulletin, claims-handling bulletin, and Rule 550 underwriting controls. Covers the $5,000 fungi limit and disclosure, windstorm and seacoast deductibles, notice periods, cancellation and nonrenewal, claim deadlines, proof and investigation duties, and the boundaries between contract, regulation, and internal underwriting.
- [Texas State Overlay](texas.md) - Edition-aware reference for Texas HO 01 45 and DP 01 45 amendatory forms and Texas windstorm-deductible and prompt-payment bulletins. Covers contractual deductible ranges, named-storm periods, advance notice, disclosure, claim deadlines, claims duties, and the boundary between regulatory requirements and internal underwriting guidance.
