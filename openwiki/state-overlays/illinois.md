# Illinois State Overlay

## Scope and document map

This page combines three Illinois sources with different authority and function:

| Source | Effective date | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Illinois Bulletin IDOI-2015-04 — Producer Licensing Renewals | **2015-04-06** | Regulatory controls for producer authority, renewal administration, communications, records, and claims practices. |
| HO 01 12 Illinois Amendatory Endorsement, edition 2015-02 | **2015-02-01** | Contract terms for Illinois HO-3 policies, including deductibles, cancellation and nonrenewal notices, claim deadlines, suit limitation, and water-backup wording. |
| Illinois Bulletin IDOI-2017-10 — Water Backup Coverage Disclosure | **2017-10-02** | Regulatory disclosure, filing, transaction, and water-backup claims-handling requirements for applicable Illinois personal-lines property insurance. |

The bulletins constrain how an admitted insurer operates in Illinois; they do not turn internal underwriting guidance into contract language. HO 01 12 is contract language for the policies to which it is attached. The 2015-04-06 and 2017-10-02 dates are the source effective dates, not dates to be inferred from a policy's issue date. Sources: [IDOI-2015-04](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L1-L7), [HO 01 12](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L1-L7), [IDOI-2017-10](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L1-L7).

> **Contract-versus-disclosure boundary.** The water-backup disclosure requirement is separate from the water-backup contract grant. IDOI-2017-10 requires an accurate disclosure of whatever the policy offers, limits, excludes, or makes available; it does not itself create coverage. HO 01 12 contains both a $5,000 maximum-payment statement and an immediately following exclusion for sewer, drain, and sump backup. That internal tension must be resolved by the policy assembly and applicable attached terms, not by treating the disclosure as a grant. Sources: [IDOI-2017-10 B.1.2-B.1.7](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L15-L31), [HO 01 12 T.8-T.9](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L635-L643).

## Effective-date and applicability controls

- **Producer licensing:** IDOI-2015-04 applies to admitted insurers conducting producer-licensing renewal activity in Illinois. The insurer remains responsible even when an affiliate, administrator, vendor, or other representative performs the work, and it must not treat an expired, inactive, suspended, surrendered, or otherwise unauthorized producer as eligible to conduct insurer business. The bulletin provides no numeric producer-renewal deadline; the operational control is current-authority verification before dependent activity. Sources: [IDOI-2015-04 B.1.1-B.1.7](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L13-L27), [B.2.1-B.2.6](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L59-L71).
- **Water-backup disclosure:** IDOI-2017-10 applies prospectively to applicable policies issued or renewed on or after **2017-10-02**, and to new business and transactions that change water-backup treatment. It applies to personal-lines property insurance issued or renewed by an admitted insurer and to an endorsement that adds, removes, or materially changes the coverage. Sources: [IDOI-2017-10 B.1.3 and B.1.15-B.1.16](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L19-L23), [B.1.43-B.1.45](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L39-L45), [B.2.10-B.2.12](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L67-L73), [B.2.40-B.2.41](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L127-L131).
- **HO 01 12:** The endorsement controls when attached and says that an expressly changed provision controls a conflicting provision, while an unaddressed provision remains applicable. It does not provide coverage beyond an express grant and does not remove an exclusion unless it expressly does so. Source: [HO 01 12 T.0](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L13-L27).

## Producer licensing: authority and renewal operations

IDOI-2015-04 requires the insurer to maintain a controlled producer-authority process, not merely to accept a producer's representation. Before appointment, compensation, renewal business, or other activity that depends on authority, the insurer must verify that the producer's authority is appropriate to the lines being transacted and current. Licensing and appointment records must be reconciled when material changes occur; onboarding and termination processes must not bypass the licensing check. Sources: [IDOI-2015-04 B.2.2-B.2.5](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L63-L69), [B.2.9-B.2.14](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L77-L87), [B.2.21-B.2.30](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L101-L119).

The control follows the delegated activity. The insurer must oversee affiliates, third-party administrators, vendors, and other representatives performing producer functions; prevent system access that would facilitate unauthorized activity; train relevant personnel; provide a route for reporting deficiencies; investigate suspected unauthorized activity; document material corrective action; and preserve records sufficient for Department review. Sources: [IDOI-2015-04 B.2.15-B.2.20](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L89-L99), [B.2.25-B.2.35](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L109-L129), [B.2.55-B.2.60](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L169-L179).

The bulletin expressly keeps licensing administration separate from policyholder rights: a producer-renewal matter does not change a policy nonrenewal notice, cancellation period, proof-of-loss period, or coverage term. This prevents a licensing workflow from being used as a substitute for policy notices or claim handling. Source: [IDOI-2015-04 B.2.40-B.2.43](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L139-L145).

### Producer renewal communications

A renewal communication must be clear, conspicuous, and in plain language; identify the producer and the licensing matter; state that renewal remains the producer's responsibility unless the insurer expressly agreed to perform it; identify whether it is a reminder, information request, or authority-affecting action; state requested information, consequences of nonresponse, and any dependence on active licensing; and provide useful contact and error-correction channels. The insurer must not state or imply licensing, appointment, or authorization that its records or reliable licensing information do not support. Sources: [IDOI-2015-04 B.3.1-B.3.12](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L181-L205).

The communication must protect nonpublic personal information, separate unrelated requests and marketing, be retainable when electronic, and use terminology that does not imply the insurer issues, renews, suspends, or revokes a state license. Before restricting activity based on suspected lack of authority, the insurer must give notice when practicable and a reasonable opportunity to provide corrected information, unless immediate action is necessary to prevent an unauthorized transaction. Notice substance and delivery method must be retained, including when a vendor sends the notice. Sources: [IDOI-2015-04 B.3.13-B.3.20](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L207-L221), [B.3.21-B.3.28](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L223-L237).

### Producer filings and effective use

Material filed for producer-licensing renewals must be submitted before use. Department receipt is not approval: the material becomes effective only as provided by applicable law or on Department approval, and the insurer must stop using material that is disapproved or required to be withdrawn. The insurer must maintain records of the effective material, promptly correct inaccurate filings, and respond to Department requests before relying on a filing as effective. Sources: [IDOI-2015-04 B.5.1-B.5.10](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L281-L301), [B.5.11-B.5.12](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L303-L305).

### Producer-bulletin claims standards

Beginning on the bulletin's effective date, IDOI-2015-04 requires claims under policies issued or delivered in Illinois to be handled fairly, promptly, and in good faith. The insurer must maintain procedures for timely claim notice, identification, investigation, evaluation, and resolution; acknowledge claim communications within a reasonable time; investigate reasonably before denying or limiting payment; request only information reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim; and explain requested information and material deficiencies in plain language. Sources: [IDOI-2015-04 B.4.1-B.4.7](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L239-L253).

A claim evaluation must use the policy, loss facts, and applicable law without misrepresenting pertinent facts or provisions. A whole or partial denial requires a written explanation identifying the factual and policy basis; the insurer must identify material information that could affect the decision, reconsider when such information arrives, communicate whether payment is authorized or further investigation is required, pay when coverage and amount are reasonably established, and avoid coercive or misleading settlement practices. Claim records must preserve material communications, investigation, evaluation, decisions, and payments. The insurer remains responsible for employees, producers, adjusters, and other persons handling claims on its behalf and must cooperate with Department review. Sources: [IDOI-2015-04 B.4.8-B.4.15](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L255-L269), [B.4.16-B.4.20](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2015-04-producer-licensing.md#L271-L279).

## Water-backup disclosure overlay — separate from coverage

### What the disclosure must say

When water-backup coverage is included, limited, excluded, available by endorsement, or unavailable, the insurer must provide a clear, conspicuous, plain-language description consistent with the policy. It must distinguish water backup from flood, surface water, seepage, and other water damage; identify whether sewer, drain, sump, or related-system events are addressed; state material exclusions and conditions; state whether a separate deductible applies; and identify whether a separate limit applies. The bulletin's B.2.6 states that the most the insurer will pay is **$5,000**. That is a required disclosure value in this bulletin, not a universal replacement for the limit in the attached contract. Sources: [IDOI-2017-10 B.1.2-B.1.9](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L15-L31), [B.2.3-B.2.9](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L53-L65).

The disclosure must not imply that optional coverage is automatic, characterize water backup as flood coverage, or state that it includes loss of use or living expenses unless the policy provides that protection. It must explain the election and declination method, and it must be revised when a material change affects availability, scope, limitation, or exclusion. Sources: [IDOI-2017-10 B.1.13-B.1.14](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L39-L43), [B.3.9-B.3.18](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L151-L169).

### Timing, delivery, and records

Provide the disclosure before the applicant completes the purchase decision and before coverage is accepted when reasonably practicable. Provide it with or before an endorsement that adds, removes, or materially changes water-backup coverage, and make it available at renewal when the coverage is absent or the renewal changes its treatment. Paper or electronic delivery is permitted when lawful, but electronic content must be accessible, retainable, legible, and easy to locate. Sources: [IDOI-2017-10 B.2.10-B.2.15](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L67-L77), [B.3.1-B.3.13](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L135-L159).

Maintain the disclosure version delivered, the policy transaction, and the applicant's or policyholder's selection or rejection. Quotation systems, declarations, producer communications, marketing, applications, endorsements, and policy materials must agree with the disclosure. Remove outdated language, correct inaccurate or misleading content, and remain responsible for disclosures generated or delivered by a producer, managing general agent, administrator, or vendor. Sources: [IDOI-2017-10 B.2.20-B.2.27](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L87-L103), [B.2.34-B.2.38](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L115-L123), [B.5.8-B.5.16](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L247-L261).

Before using a water-backup disclosure, file it with the Department and identify the policy forms and endorsements to which it applies. File a revised disclosure with related revised policy or transaction material when required; do not use a materially different disclosure before the required filing or approval action. A disclosure that no longer accurately describes the contract must be withdrawn and replaced. Sources: [IDOI-2017-10 B.5.1-B.5.7](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L231-L245), [B.5.11-B.5.15](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L251-L261).

## HO 01 12 contract changes

### Deductibles and deductible-change notice

The endorsement applies a deductible to each covered windstorm or hail loss occurrence and subtracts it after the covered amount is determined. It applies only to covered direct physical loss, does not create coverage for an excluded cause, and is not multiplied by the number of damaged items. The endorsement also requires prompt notice, property protection, preservation for inspection, and supporting estimates, invoices, photographs, and records for a windstorm or hail loss. Sources: [HO 01 12 T.1-T.10](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L57-L79), [T.12-T.16](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L81-L89).

If a deductible changes, HO 01 12 requires written notice when applicable law requires it. The notice must identify the affected policy and coverage, state the new deductible and effective date, and use a permitted delivery method. Electronic delivery requires the insured's consent as required by law. The change cannot take effect before the stated effective date or before any legally required advance-notice period. A later deductible notice does not alter the deductible for a prior loss or pending claim, and the insurer may retain preparation, transmission, and delivery records. Sources: [HO 01 12 T.2-T.11](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L165-L187), [T.12-T.18](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L189-L201), [T.24-T.32](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L213-L229).

### Named-storm and water-backup boundaries

A named-storm notice may identify the storm and affected area, but the named-storm period does not create coverage for an otherwise excluded cause. During the period the insured must protect property, give prompt claim notice, preserve damaged property, allow inspection, provide records, and cooperate. Failure affects coverage only to the extent the insurer is prejudiced. Sources: [HO 01 12 T.3 Named Storm Period](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L231-L251), [T.11-T.24](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L253-L279).

For water backup, HO 01 12 T.8 states that the most payable for direct physical loss caused by water backup or sump overflow is **$5,000**, regardless of the number of insureds, locations, occurrences, or claims. T.9 then states that loss caused by water backing up through sewers or drains, or overflowing or discharging from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment, is not covered. Read together, these provisions do not safely establish a standalone water-backup grant; verify the attached base form, endorsement, declarations, and applicable filed disclosure before applying the $5,000 statement. The form separately preserves exclusions for flood, surface water, below-ground water, pollutants, deterioration, and other excluded causes. Sources: [HO 01 12 T.8-T.13](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L637-L649), [T.35-T.40](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L691-L701).

### Cancellation and nonrenewal notices

HO 01 12 permits cancellation only as allowed by law and the endorsement. For nonpayment of premium, the insurer must mail or deliver cancellation notice at least **10 days** before the effective date. For another cancellation reason, notice must be mailed or delivered at least **30 days** before the effective date and state the reason. The notice identifies the policy and refund address; mailing to the address in the insurer's records is sufficient under the endorsement. Sources: [HO 01 12 T.4.1-T.4.15](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L281-L311).

For nonrenewal, the insurer must mail or deliver notice at least **60 days** before policy expiration and state the effective date; the reason is included when required by applicable law. A changed-term renewal offer is not itself cancellation or nonrenewal unless stated otherwise in writing. A pre-expiration cancellation or nonrenewal does not eliminate a claim for a loss occurring before coverage ended, but it does not create coverage for a later loss. Sources: [HO 01 12 T.31-T.40](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L343-L361), [T.48-T.57](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L377-L395).

### Claims handling, deadlines, and suit limitation

The endorsement requires prompt loss notice identifying the insured, property, and known circumstances; reasonable mitigation and temporary repairs; preservation of damaged property; cooperation; inspection access; records, estimates, photographs, and inventories when requested; and a signed sworn proof of loss when requested. An inspection, investigation, partial payment, or reservation of rights does not itself admit coverage or waive defenses. Sources: [HO 01 12 T.5.1-T.5.14](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L397-L425), [T.5.15-T.5.32](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L427-L461).

The specific Illinois claim clock in HO 01 12 is: acknowledge receipt within **15 days**; after receiving all requested items, accept or reject within **30 business days**; and pay an accepted claim within **30 business days**. The acknowledgement is not an acceptance of coverage. The insurer may pay an undisputed amount while investigating a disputed portion, must communicate decisions when required by law or reasonably appropriate, and must review material information submitted after the initial decision. Sources: [HO 01 12 T.5.5-T.5.6](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L407-L409), [T.5.16-T.5.22](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L429-L443), [T.5.33-T.5.36](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L463-L469).

An action against the insurer must be commenced within **two years after the date of loss**, and the insured must first comply with the insurance's duties. The insured must establish both that a covered loss occurred and the amount of covered damage; failure to comply with a duty bars an action only when the failure causes prejudice. Sources: [HO 01 12 T.7.1-T.7.10](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L565-L585), [T.7.17-T.7.27](repo://forms/HO/IL/HO-01-12/2015-02.md#L599-L619).

## Illinois water-backup claims handling

For a claim involving alleged water backup, IDOI-2017-10 applies to claims received on or after **2017-10-02**. The insurer must acknowledge the claim promptly, identify the responsible handler, investigate the reported cause and damage before denial or limitation, evaluate the actual policy and endorsements rather than a general water-damage description, and request only information material to coverage, valuation, mitigation, or another claim issue. Sources: [IDOI-2017-10 B.4.1-B.4.5](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L189-L201).

A denial, limitation, exclusion, condition, deductible, or other policy-based reduction requires a written explanation identifying the relied-on policy language and how it applies to known facts. The insurer must pay an undisputed covered portion unless barred by the policy or law, explain valuation differences, state the deductible and its basis, consider reasonable mitigation, and provide a reasonable reconsideration process for material new information. These are claims-handling duties; they do not expand the water-backup contract grant. Sources: [IDOI-2017-10 B.4.6-B.4.17](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L201-L223), [B.4.18-B.4.20](repo://bulletins/IL/idoi-2017-10-water-backup-disclosure.md#L225-L229).

## Underwriting implementation (guidance, not contract)

The Personal Lines Underwriting Manual is internal carrier direction. It must be applied within delegated authority, before binding, and documented; it must not be given to an applicant or used to alter coverage or create a coverage grant. Sources: [Underwriting Manual Rule 100](repo://manuals/underwriting/manual.md#L13-L43).

For Illinois, Rule 570 requires referral when the requested water-backup limit exceeds **$15,000** and requires review of the sewer-backup limit against the described premises. It also directs referral for unrepaired prior water damage, recurring water intrusion, deteriorated or defective plumbing, an unreliable sump system, unclear prior sewer/drain loss, sewage or drainage conditions, and persistent moisture or mold. Document the exposure, supporting information, and underwriting decision. These are carrier acceptance and referral controls, not a change to the policy limit or the bulletin's disclosure requirement. Sources: [Underwriting Manual Rule 570.E-570.I](repo://manuals/underwriting/manual.md#L8233-L8255), [Rule 570.AL-570.AO](repo://manuals/underwriting/manual.md#L8365-L8383).

## Operational checklist

1. **Identify the governing documents.** Confirm the policy line and edition, HO 01 12 attachment and edition, applicable water-backup endorsement, declarations, and the disclosure version used for the transaction.
2. **Separate the three questions.** Determine (a) whether the contract grants water-backup coverage, (b) what IDOI-2017-10 required the applicant or insured to be told, and (c) what claims-handling duties apply. Do not use the disclosure or the underwriting manual as a coverage grant.
3. **For producer activity, verify authority first.** Reconcile current licensing and appointment data before appointment, compensation, renewal business, or other regulated activity; suspend or restrict activity when necessary to prevent an unauthorized transaction; record the decision.
4. **For a transaction, deliver and preserve the disclosure.** Give it before the purchase decision or changed coverage takes effect, state limit, deductible, exclusions, conditions, election status, and water-source distinction, then retain the delivered version and selection or rejection.
5. **For a claim, preserve evidence and apply the exact clock.** Give prompt notice, mitigate, preserve failed components and damaged property when reasonably possible, inspect, request material records, and apply the HO 01 12 15-day/30-business-day deadlines when that endorsement governs. Apply the two-year suit period and any water-backup-specific policy terms.
6. **Explain the decision in writing.** For an Illinois water-backup claim, identify the exact grant, exclusion, deductible, limit, and known facts; pay any undisputed covered amount unless barred; and do not treat inspection, disclosure, or partial payment as an admission of coverage.
