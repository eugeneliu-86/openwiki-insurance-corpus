# HO-3 Homeowners 3 — Special Form (Edition 2022-03)

Multistate. Effective for policies written on or after 2022-03-01. Supersedes edition 2018-09.

## AGREEMENT

We will provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the premium and compliance with all applicable provisions. Throughout this policy, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the Declarations and the spouse if a resident of the same household. "We", "us" and "our" refer to the company providing this insurance.

## DEFINITIONS

**1. "Actual cash value"** means the cost to repair or replace the damaged property with material of like kind and quality, less a deduction for depreciation based on the age, condition, and remaining useful life of the property immediately before the loss.

**2. "Replacement cost"** means the cost to repair or replace the damaged property with material of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation, at the time of loss.

**3. "Occurrence"** means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions, which results in bodily injury or property damage during the policy period.

**4. "Residence premises"** means the one-family dwelling, other structures, and grounds at the location shown in the Declarations, where you reside.

**5. "Sudden and accidental"** means an event that is both abrupt in onset and unintended from the standpoint of the insured. A condition that develops gradually, or that an insured knew of and failed to remedy, is not sudden and accidental regardless of when its effects became apparent.

**6. "Roof surfacing"** means shingles, tiles, shakes, metal panels, membrane, and the underlayment and flashing directly beneath them. Roof surfacing does not include the roof deck, trusses, rafters, sheathing, or any interior finish.

## SECTION I — PROPERTY COVERAGES

### A. Coverage A — Dwelling

**A.1** We cover the dwelling on the residence premises shown in the Declarations, including structures attached to the dwelling.

**A.2** We cover materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises used to construct, alter, or repair the dwelling.

**A.3** Losses to the dwelling are settled at replacement cost, subject to the roof surfacing provisions in A.4 and to the applicable deductible, provided the dwelling is insured to at least eighty percent of its replacement cost at the time of loss.

**A.4** Loss to roof surfacing caused by windstorm or hail is settled at actual cash value where the roof surfacing is ten years of age or older at the date of loss, and at replacement cost where it is under ten years of age. This provision applies whether or not a roof schedule endorsement is attached. Where a roof schedule endorsement is attached, the depreciation schedule in that endorsement determines the actual cash value payable. All other components of the dwelling continue to be settled under A.3.

### B. Coverage B — Other Structures

**B.1** We cover other structures on the residence premises set apart from the dwelling by clear space, or connected to the dwelling only by a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

**B.2** The limit of liability for Coverage B is ten percent of the Coverage A limit, and this amount is additional insurance.

**B.3** We do not cover other structures rented or held for rental to any person who is not a tenant of the dwelling, unless used solely as a private garage.

### C. Coverage C — Personal Property

**C.1** We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world.

**C.2** Personal property is settled at actual cash value at the time of loss, unless a replacement cost personal property endorsement is attached.

**C.3** Special limits of liability apply to the following categories, and these limits do not increase the Coverage C limit: two hundred dollars on money and precious metals; one thousand five hundred dollars on watercraft including trailers and equipment; two thousand five hundred dollars on theft of jewelry, watches, and precious stones; two thousand five hundred dollars on theft of firearms; and two thousand five hundred dollars on property used primarily for business purposes on the residence premises.

### D. Coverage D — Loss of Use

**D.1** If a covered loss makes the residence premises not fit to live in, we cover the reasonable increase in living expenses necessary to maintain your normal standard of living, for the shortest time reasonably required to repair or replace the premises.

**D.2** The limit of liability for Coverage D is twenty percent of the Coverage A limit.

---

## SECTION I — PERILS INSURED AGAINST

**P.1** For Coverage A and Coverage B, we insure against direct physical loss to property described in those coverages, except as excluded in Section I — Exclusions.

**P.2** For Coverage C, we insure against direct physical loss caused by the following perils only: fire or lightning; windstorm or hail; explosion; riot or civil commotion; aircraft; vehicles; smoke; vandalism or malicious mischief; theft; falling objects; weight of ice, snow, or sleet; accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, or air conditioning system; and sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current.

---

## SECTION I — EXCLUSIONS

### A. Water Damage

**A.1** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, storm surge, or overflow of a body of water, whether or not driven by wind.

**A.2** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by water below the surface of the ground, including water that exerts pressure on or seeps or leaks through a building, foundation, swimming pool, or other structure.

**A.3** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by water or waterborne material that backs up through sewers or drains, or that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment, even if the backup or overflow results from mechanical breakdown. This exclusion does not apply if endorsement HO 04 95 Water Backup and Sump Discharge or Overflow is attached to this policy, in which case coverage is provided to the sublimit stated in that endorsement. Endorsement HO 04 90 is withdrawn as of this edition and may not be attached to a policy written on this form.

**A.4** The exclusions in A.1 through A.3 apply regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

### B. Earth Movement

**B.1** We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by earthquake, landslide, mudflow, sinkhole collapse, subsidence, or any other earth movement, whether combined with water or not.

### C. Neglect and Deterioration

**C.1** We do not cover loss caused by neglect, meaning the failure of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss.

**C.2** We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, mechanical breakdown, rust, mold, wet or dry rot, or settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging, or expansion of pavements, patios, foundations, walls, floors, roofs, or ceilings.

**C.3** We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam over a period of weeks, months, or years from within a plumbing, heating, or air conditioning system, or from within a household appliance. Loss that is sudden and accidental as defined in Definitions 5 is not excluded by this provision.

### D. Ordinance or Law

**D.1** We do not cover the increased cost of construction, demolition, or repair required by any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, or demolition, unless an ordinance or law endorsement is attached to this policy.

### E. Intentional Loss

**E.1** We do not cover loss arising out of any act an insured commits or conspires to commit with the intent to cause a loss. This exclusion applies to all insureds under the policy, whether or not a particular insured participated in the act.

## SECTION I — CONDITIONS

**S.1** In case of a covered loss, an insured must give prompt notice to us or our agent, protect the property from further damage, and prepare an inventory of damaged personal property.

**S.2** An insured must submit a signed, sworn proof of loss within sixty days after our request.

**S.3** We will adjust all losses with you and pay you unless another payee is named in the policy. Loss will be payable sixty days after we receive your proof of loss and reach written agreement with you, or an appraisal award or court judgment is entered.

**S.4** No action may be brought against us unless there has been full compliance with all Section I conditions and the action is started within two years after the date of loss.

**S.5** The deductible shown in the Declarations applies to each loss under Section I. A separate windstorm or hail deductible applies where required by a state amendatory endorsement, and where both apply only the larger is deducted.

---

## SECTION II — LIABILITY COVERAGES

### E. Coverage E — Personal Liability

**E.1** If a claim is made or suit is brought against an insured for damages because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence to which this coverage applies, we will pay up to our limit of liability for the damages for which an insured is legally liable, and provide a defense at our expense.

### F. Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

**F.1** We will pay the necessary medical expenses incurred or medically ascertained within three years from the date of an accident causing bodily injury to a person other than an insured, arising out of a condition on the insured location or caused by the activities of an insured.

---

## SECTION II — EXCLUSIONS

**L.1** Coverage E and Coverage F do not apply to bodily injury or property damage arising out of business activities of an insured, other than the occasional rental of the residence premises for fewer than fifteen days in a policy year.

**L.2** Coverage E and Coverage F do not apply to bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of a motor vehicle, watercraft over twenty-five horsepower, or aircraft.

**L.3** Coverage E does not apply to property damage to property owned by or rented to an insured.
