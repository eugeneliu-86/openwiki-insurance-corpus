# Texas Homeowners Appetite Guide

Internal underwriting guidance. This document states which Texas homeowners risks we will write, at what authority level, and under what conditions. It is not part of any policy contract and is not to be quoted to an insured or a claimant.

Last revised 2026-04-14 by Personal Lines Underwriting.

## G.1 In Appetite

We will write owner-occupied one-family dwellings in Texas with a Coverage A limit between one hundred fifty thousand and one million two hundred thousand dollars, where the dwelling is the insured's primary residence and is insured to at least eighty percent of replacement cost as required by HO-3 Section I A.3.

We will write dwellings of frame, masonry, or masonry veneer construction with a protection class of one through eight.

We will write seasonal and secondary residences only in protection classes one through six, and only with a prior-loss record clear for five years.

## G.2 Roof Age and Settlement

Dwellings with a roof surfacing age of fifteen years or more require the actual cash value roof schedule endorsement HO 23 74 at new business and at renewal. HO-3 Section I A.4 provides that the roof schedule endorsement, when attached, governs settlement for roof surfacing while all other dwelling components continue to settle at replacement cost under A.3.

Dwellings with a roof surfacing age of twenty-five years or more are outside appetite regardless of endorsement, unless a full roof replacement is documented within ninety days of binding.

Composition shingle roofs in the coastal counties listed in G.6 require an inspection report dated within twelve months of the effective date.

## G.3 Water Backup

Water backup coverage is optional and is written by attaching HO 04 90. The base sublimit under HO 04 90 W.2 is five thousand dollars, and underwriters may bind up to twenty-five thousand dollars without referral where the dwelling has no prior water backup claim in five years.

Risks with two or more water backup claims in five years are outside appetite for the endorsement, though the underlying policy may still be written without it. Note that HO 04 90 W.4 preserves the flood and surface water exclusions in full, so this endorsement never responds to a flood loss and must not be represented as flood coverage.

Dwellings with a finished basement below grade require a sump pump with battery backup before the endorsement may be bound at any limit above ten thousand dollars.

## G.4 Wind and Hail

The Texas windstorm deductible is governed by the state amendatory endorsement and by Texas Department of Insurance requirements, not by this guide. Underwriters must confirm the applicable deductible from the amendatory form in force for the policy's effective date rather than from prior-year rating output.

In the seacoast territories defined by state law, windstorm and hail coverage may be excluded from the policy where the insured obtains that coverage through the residual market. A signed exclusion acknowledgment is required at binding.

## G.5 Prior Losses and Referral

Two or more paid property claims in three years require referral to a senior underwriter. Any claim involving mold, continuous seepage, or foundation movement requires referral regardless of count, because HO-3 Section I C.2 and C.3 exclude most of these causes and a paid claim in this category may indicate a coverage dispute rather than a covered loss.

Any risk with a prior liability claim arising out of a dog bite, trampoline, or unfenced pool requires referral and a completed liability supplement.

## G.6 Coastal Counties

The following counties are treated as coastal for the purposes of G.2 and G.4: Aransas, Brazoria, Calhoun, Cameron, Chambers, Galveston, Jefferson, Kenedy, Kleberg, Matagorda, Nueces, Refugio, San Patricio, and Willacy.

## G.7 Authority Limits

Line underwriters may bind Coverage A up to eight hundred thousand dollars within appetite. Senior underwriters may bind up to one million two hundred thousand dollars and may approve one referral condition per risk. Anything above one million two hundred thousand dollars, or carrying more than one referral condition, requires management approval.
