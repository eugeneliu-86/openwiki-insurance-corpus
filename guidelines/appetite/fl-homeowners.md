# Florida Homeowners Appetite Guide

Internal underwriting guidance for Florida personal residential property. Not part of any policy contract and not to be quoted to an insured or a claimant.

Last revised 2026-06-30 by Personal Lines Underwriting.

## H.1 In Appetite

We will write owner-occupied one-family dwellings in Florida with a Coverage A limit between two hundred thousand and nine hundred thousand dollars, where the dwelling is the insured's primary residence and is insured to at least eighty percent of replacement cost as required by HO-3 Section I A.3.

We will write masonry and masonry veneer construction in all approved counties. Frame construction is written only in protection classes one through five and only outside the wind-borne debris region.

Seasonal and secondary residences are outside appetite in Florida regardless of construction, limit, or protection class.

## H.2 Roof Age

Roofs fifteen years of age or older require an inspection before binding. Under Florida bulletin OIR-2023-04 F.3 that inspection is at our expense, and where the insured supplies a qualifying inspection performed by a Florida-licensed inspector within the preceding twelve months we must accept it and may not order our own at the insured's cost.

We may not decline a risk for roof age alone. OIR-2023-04 F.2 requires that any declination on roof condition state the specific deficiency identified, and roofs with five or more years of documented remaining useful life may not be declined on age. Underwriters must record the specific deficiency in the declination note, not a generic roof-age reason.

We may not attach HO 23 74 to a roof less than ten years of age at the policy effective date. OIR-2023-04 F.4 prohibits applying an actual cash value roof schedule below that age, and F.4 further requires that the insured be offered a policy without the schedule at a filed rate, with the premium difference disclosed in writing at the time of offer. The comparison quote must be retained in the file.

## H.3 Nonrenewal

Any nonrenewal for roof condition requires one hundred twenty days written notice stating the specific reason, and the notice must enclose any inspection report we relied on, per OIR-2023-04 F.5. Nonrenewal notices in this category route through Compliance before issuance without exception.

## H.4 Water Backup

Water backup coverage is written by attaching HO 04 90. Underwriters may bind the base sublimit under HO 04 90 W.2 without referral. Limits above ten thousand dollars require a sump pump with battery backup where any finished area is below grade.

Florida risks carry elevated water loss frequency. Two or more water claims of any type in five years places the risk outside appetite for this endorsement, and three or more places the entire risk outside appetite.

Underwriters must not represent this endorsement as flood coverage under any circumstance. HO 04 90 W.4 preserves HO-3 Section I A.1 in full, so the endorsement never responds to a flood or storm surge loss. Flood must be placed separately.

## H.5 Hurricane Deductible

The hurricane deductible is set by filed rule and is not discretionary. Underwriters must confirm the applicable deductible from the filing in force for the policy effective date rather than from prior-year rating output.

Where a separate roof deductible would also apply, only the larger is deducted per OIR-2023-04 F.6. Do not quote both as though they stacked.

## H.6 Mold

HO 04 81 is written at the base ten thousand dollar aggregate limit in M.3 without referral. Higher limits require referral and a plumbing age disclosure.

Risks with a prior mold claim in five years are outside appetite for this endorsement. The underlying policy may still be written without it.

## H.7 Authority Limits

Line underwriters may bind Coverage A up to six hundred thousand dollars within appetite. Senior underwriters may bind up to nine hundred thousand dollars and may approve one referral condition per risk. Anything above nine hundred thousand dollars, any wind-borne debris region frame risk, and any nonrenewal for roof condition require management approval.
