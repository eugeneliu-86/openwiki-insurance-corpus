## H.0 — Purpose and Status

**H.0.1** You use this guide to apply Louisiana homeowners appetite consistently during
underwriting review. It supports selection decisions because the insuring agreement and
declarations, rather than internal guidance, establish the insurance offered.

**H.0.2** You may use the guide to identify risks that require closer review, referral,
or declination. This promotes disciplined selection because the application
representations condition the insurer’s evaluation of the risk.

**H.0.3** You must not treat this guide as a policy, endorsement, binder, or coverage
grant. That distinction is necessary because the insuring agreement defines the
insurer’s contractual promise.

**H.0.4** You must not rely on this guide to alter a filed form provision or a
declaration entry. The declarations control the coverage shown for a particular insured
location.

**H.0.5** You should evaluate submitted information for completeness and internal
consistency before making an appetite decision. This protects underwriting accuracy
because the application representations support risk classification.

**H.0.6** You may request clarification when property facts, occupancy information, or
prior-loss information is unclear. Clarification is appropriate because the concealment
or fraud condition addresses material inaccuracies in information provided to the
insurer.

**H.0.7** You should apply the guide to the risk as presented, without creating
assumptions that are unsupported by the submission. This approach is necessary because
the application representations remain the basis for the underwriting decision.

**H.0.8** You must refer unusual, complex, or incomplete submissions through established
underwriting authority. Referral protects consistent decision-making because the
declarations require accurate reflection of the risk accepted.

**H.0.9** You should distinguish eligibility concerns from coverage interpretation
questions. This distinction matters because the definitions provision governs the
meaning of policy terms.

**H.0.10** You must not promise coverage while discussing appetite, eligibility, or
referral status. Coverage communications must remain accurate because the insuring
agreement controls the scope of protection.

**H.0.11** You should confirm that requested coverages can be reflected accurately in
the declarations. This is necessary because the declarations identify the insured
property, applicable coverages, and selected options.

**H.0.12** You should treat the Coverage D limit as a percentage of Coverage A as a
declarations matter rather than an independent underwriting promise. This preserves
proper form administration because the declarations establish the applicable limit.

**H.0.13** You should review the association assessment limit as a coverage-selection
matter when the submission involves an eligible association interest. This is

## H.2 — Roof Age and Condition

condition and shows no unresolved damage. This rule rests on the applicable homeowners
form roof condition provisions because a sound covering is necessary to prevent
avoidable interior loss.

age can increase the likelihood of weather-related loss.

**H.3.3** You must obtain a wind mitigation inspection when Coverage A exceeds $500,000.
