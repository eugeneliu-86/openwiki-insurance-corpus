# Underwriting Referral and Authority Matrix

Internal guidance establishing who may bind what, and which conditions require escalation, across all personal residential property states. State-specific appetite guides may impose tighter limits but may never grant broader authority than this document.

Last revised 2026-07-08 by Personal Lines Underwriting.

## R.1 Authority Tiers

Line underwriters hold base binding authority. Senior underwriters hold expanded authority and may clear one referral condition per risk. Management approval is required for anything exceeding senior authority, for any risk carrying more than one referral condition, and for any exception to a filed rule.

Where a state appetite guide states a lower Coverage A ceiling than this document, the state guide governs. Texas ceilings are in the Texas appetite guide at G.7 and Florida ceilings are in the Florida appetite guide at H.7.

## R.2 Coverage A Limits

Line underwriters may bind Coverage A up to eight hundred thousand dollars. Senior underwriters may bind up to one million five hundred thousand dollars. Above that, management approval is required and a property inspection is mandatory regardless of construction or protection class.

Any risk where the Coverage A limit is below eighty percent of estimated replacement cost is outside appetite entirely and may not be bound at any authority level, because HO-3 Section I A.3 conditions replacement cost settlement on that threshold and binding below it creates a coinsurance dispute at first loss.

## R.3 Mandatory Referral Conditions

The following require referral regardless of Coverage A limit or state.

Two or more paid property claims in three years. Any claim involving mold, continuous seepage, or foundation movement, regardless of count, because HO-3 Section I C.2 and C.3 exclude most causes in this category and a paid claim may indicate a prior coverage dispute rather than a covered loss.

Any prior liability claim arising out of a dog bite, trampoline, or unfenced pool, with a completed liability supplement required before binding.

Any risk where windstorm and hail is to be excluded and placed in a residual market.

Any request to bind a water backup limit above twenty-five thousand dollars, or above ten thousand dollars where a finished area lies below grade without a battery-backed sump pump.

Any dwelling with a roof surfacing age of twenty years or more, and any dwelling of twenty-five years or more roof age regardless of documentation.

## R.4 Conditions That May Not Be Cleared by Referral

Some conditions place a risk outside appetite and cannot be approved at any level.

Three or more paid water claims of any type in five years. Any dwelling with known unrepaired structural damage. Any dwelling under renovation where the structure is unoccupied for more than thirty consecutive days, which requires a builders risk or vacancy form instead.

Any risk where binding would violate a state filing or bulletin requirement. Regulatory requirements are not underwriting discretion, and a referral cannot approve an exception to them.

## R.5 Endorsement Authority

Line underwriters may attach HO 04 90 and HO 04 81 at base limits, and HO 04 16 and HO 23 74 without limit conditions.

Attaching HO 23 74 requires confirming the state permits it at the roof's age. Florida prohibits attachment below ten years of roof age under OIR-2023-04 F.4 and requires the comparison quote be retained, and Florida risks in this category must clear H.2 before binding.

Senior underwriters may bind water backup limits up to fifty thousand dollars and mold limits up to twenty-five thousand dollars.

## R.6 Documentation Standard

Every cleared referral must record the condition, the authority level that cleared it, the specific facts relied on, and the date. A referral cleared without a recorded basis is treated as unbound authority in audit and is charged back to the clearing underwriter's file review.

Declination notes must state a specific deficiency. Where a state bulletin restricts the permissible basis for a declination — as Florida does for roof condition at OIR-2023-04 F.2 — the note must satisfy that standard, not merely this one.
