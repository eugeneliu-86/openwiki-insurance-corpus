# Florida Bulletin OIR-2022-01 — Hurricane Deductible Disclosure




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This section establishes disclosure expectations for hurricane deductibles used by an admitted insurer in Florida. The purpose of these expectations is to promote clear communication to applicants and policyholders.

**B.1.2** This section applies when an insurer offers, includes, modifies, or renews coverage subject to a hurricane deductible. An insurer must apply these expectations to applicable residential property insurance business.

**B.1.3** An insurer must ensure that a hurricane deductible disclosure accurately describes the deductible applicable to the coverage offered. The disclosure must be consistent with the policy terms made available to the applicant or policyholder.

**B.1.4** An insurer must provide the disclosure in a manner reasonably calculated to be seen and understood before the applicant or policyholder accepts coverage subject to a hurricane deductible. An insurer must not obscure the disclosure through presentation, formatting, or unrelated content.

**B.1.5** An insurer must comply with this section beginning on the effective date of this bulletin. An insurer may adopt compliant disclosure practices before that effective date.

**B.1.6** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section when a producer, managing general agent, or other representative provides information on the insurer's behalf. An insurer must maintain reasonable oversight of those communications.

**B.1.7** A disclosure under this section must use plain language appropriate for the intended audience. An insurer must avoid language that could reasonably mislead an applicant or policyholder about the effect of a hurricane deductible.

**B.1.8** An insurer must distinguish a hurricane deductible from other deductibles that may apply under the policy. The disclosure must not imply that a hurricane deductible applies in circumstances not stated by the policy.

**B.1.9** An insurer must ensure that its application materials, policy forms, renewal materials, and electronic communications are materially consistent with the disclosure required by **B.1.4**. An insurer must correct material inconsistencies promptly.

**B.1.10** An insurer may provide the disclosure electronically when electronic delivery is permitted and the method used reasonably presents the information to the applicant or policyholder. Electronic presentation must not reduce the clarity required by **B.1.7**.

**B.1.11** An insurer must make the disclosure available through the same distribution channel used to offer the applicable coverage. An insurer must provide reasonable access to the disclosure upon request.

**B.1.12** This section governs disclosure practices and does not alter the coverage provided by a policy. An insurer must continue to administer policy terms according to the applicable contract.

**B.1.13** This section does not prescribe a minimum Section I deductible, an eighty percent condition, or an additional living expense limit. An insurer must not represent that this bulletin establishes those policy terms.

**B.1.14** An insurer must ensure that a disclosure does not state or suggest that a hurricane deductible is waived unless the policy provides for that result. Any explanation of deductible application must remain consistent with the policy language.

**B.1.15** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section. Those records must reasonably identify the disclosure used for the applicable coverage.

**B.1.16** An insurer must train personnel responsible for offering, issuing, renewing, or servicing applicable coverage regarding the insurer's disclosure practices. An insurer must provide guidance sufficient to support accurate responses to consumer inquiries.

**B.1.17** An insurer must review its disclosure practices when it changes policy language relating to a hurricane deductible. The insurer must revise affected materials as necessary to preserve accuracy.

**B.1.18** An insurer must not use a disclosure required by this section as a substitute for policy language. An insurer must not represent that the disclosure expands, limits, or replaces the policy.

**B.1.19** An insurer must apply this section consistently across its applicable underwriting and distribution practices. An insurer may use different presentation methods if each method satisfies the clarity requirements of this section.

**B.1.20** An insurer must address material consumer confusion concerning its hurricane deductible disclosure. The insurer must take reasonable steps to prevent recurring misleading communications.

**B.1.21** Compliance with this section is evaluated based on the disclosure provided and the circumstances of its presentation. An insurer must be able to explain how its practices satisfy the requirements of this section.

**B.1.22** Nothing in this section relieves an insurer of any obligation otherwise applicable to its insurance business. An insurer must administer hurricane deductible disclosures in good faith and consistent with the purposes stated in **B.1.1**.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** These requirements apply to an admitted insurer that issues or renews a personal residential property policy providing hurricane coverage in this state. An insurer must apply these requirements on and after the effective date of this bulletin.

**B.2.2** An insurer must provide a clear disclosure of any hurricane deductible applicable to covered property. The disclosure must be provided in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the applicant or policyholder before coverage is bound or renewed.

**B.2.3** An insurer must describe the hurricane deductible using terms that are consistent throughout the policy, application, declarations, and related consumer communications. An insurer must not use terminology that could cause a reasonable consumer to confuse a hurricane deductible with another deductible.

**B.2.4** An insurer must identify the event or condition that triggers application of the hurricane deductible. The identification must be consistent with the policy language governing the deductible.

**B.2.5** An insurer must state whether the hurricane deductible applies separately from, replaces, or is combined with another applicable deductible. The disclosure must not suggest that more than one deductible applies unless the policy permits that result.

**B.2.6** An insurer must describe the property value or other basis used to calculate a percentage hurricane deductible. The description must identify the policy provision that controls when the applicable basis changes.

**B.2.7** An insurer must state the named storm deductible in a manner that permits the applicant or policyholder to understand the minimum applicable percentage. The stated percentage must not be less than two (2) percent.

**B.2.8** An insurer must state the hurricane deductible in a manner that permits the applicant or policyholder to understand the maximum applicable percentage. The stated percentage must not exceed ten (10) percent.

**B.2.9** An insurer must not present the hurricane deductible as a premium reduction, savings opportunity, or other marketing benefit without a clear accompanying explanation of the insured's responsibility for the deductible. The explanation must be no less prominent than the related marketing statement.

**B.2.10** An insurer must disclose the hurricane deductible on the declarations page or in a document delivered with the declarations page. The disclosure must be readily identifiable to a reasonable policyholder.

**B.2.11** An insurer must use readable type, plain language, and a format that distinguishes the hurricane deductible from other policy terms. An insurer must not obscure the disclosure through placement, formatting, or unrelated text.

**B.2.12** An insurer must provide the disclosure before accepting an applicant's premium for coverage subject to the hurricane deductible. If coverage is issued through an electronic process, the insurer must make the disclosure available before final acceptance.

**B.2.13** An insurer must obtain an acknowledgment when the insurer's application process requires an applicant to select or accept a hurricane deductible. The acknowledgment process must reasonably demonstrate that the applicant was given the disclosure.

**B.2.14** An insurer may use an electronic acknowledgment if the process creates a record that can be retained and produced. The electronic process must allow the applicant to access the disclosure at the time of acknowledgment.

**B.2.15** An insurer must retain records supporting delivery of the hurricane deductible disclosure. The retained records must identify the disclosure version provided to the applicant or policyholder.

**B.2.16** An insurer must maintain procedures to ensure that underwriting personnel provide the required disclosure when a hurricane deductible is selected, changed, or otherwise becomes applicable. Those procedures must be reflected in carrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exceptioncarrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exception (see Personal Lines Underwriting Manual, R500 Rule 500 — Florida State Exceptions).

**B.2.17** An insurer must train relevant personnel regarding the hurricane deductible disclosure process. Training materials must explain how to respond accurately to consumer questions concerning the deductible.

**B.2.18** An insurer must ensure that producers acting on its behalf have access to the current hurricane deductible disclosure. An insurer remains responsible for the adequacy of disclosures made through its authorized distribution channels.

**B.2.19** An insurer must provide the disclosure in connection with a renewal when the hurricane deductible remains applicable. The renewal disclosure must clearly identify any change affecting the deductible.

**B.2.20** An insurer must provide a revised disclosure before a change to the hurricane deductible takes effect. The revised disclosure must describe the changed term without requiring the policyholder to compare prior documents.

**B.2.21** An insurer must not alter a hurricane deductible through an endorsement without providing notice of the alteration. The notice must state that the policyholder's out-of-pocket responsibility may change.

**B.2.22** An insurer must ensure that the application, policy, declarations, endorsement, and notice materials do not conflict concerning the hurricane deductible. If materials conflict, the insurer must correct the inconsistency promptly.

**B.2.23** An insurer must use the same deductible description in consumer-facing materials unless a different description is necessary to explain a particular coverage feature. Any different description must remain accurate and must not narrow or expand the policy terms.

**B.2.24** An insurer must explain whether the hurricane deductible applies to damage caused by wind, hail, rain, water intrusion, or another covered cause of loss when the policy language makes that explanation necessary. The explanation must not imply coverage where the policy does not provide coverage.

**B.2.25** An insurer must explain the relationship between the hurricane deductible and any named storm deductible when both terms appear in policy materials. The explanation must identify the controlling policy language without creating ambiguity.

**B.2.26** An insurer must not use a label for the hurricane deductible that differs from the label used in the policy unless the disclosure clearly connects the labels. The connection must be understandable without specialized insurance knowledge.

**B.2.27** An insurer must provide reasonable access to the hurricane deductible disclosure for an applicant or policyholder with a communication need. An insurer may use an accessible electronic format or another reasonable method.

**B.2.28** An insurer must provide a consumer who requests clarification with an explanation that is consistent with the policy terms. The insurer must not advise a consumer that a claim outcome is assured before the relevant facts are evaluated.

**B.2.29** An insurer must not condition access to the hurricane deductible disclosure on completion of an application or payment of premium. The disclosure must be available during the consumer's consideration of coverage.

**B.2.30** An insurer must ensure that online materials prominently display the hurricane deductible disclosure before an applicant completes a coverage selection. A hyperlink may be used only if it is clearly labeled and reasonably conspicuous.

**B.2.31** An insurer must ensure that a consumer can retain or print an electronically delivered hurricane deductible disclosure. The insurer must not use a format that prevents reasonable access after delivery.

**B.2.32** An insurer must include sufficient information for a policyholder to identify the deductible applicable to the insured location. The disclosure must not rely solely on generalized descriptions that omit the policyholder's selected deductible.

**B.2.33** An insurer must disclose material limitations associated with the hurricane deductible when those limitations affect the deductible's application. The disclosure must be written so that a reasonable policyholder can distinguish a limitation from a coverage grant.

**B.2.34** An insurer must not represent that the hurricane deductible applies only to a particular type of storm unless the policy language supports that representation. An insurer must describe applicable triggers consistently with the policy.

**B.2.35** An insurer must ensure that claim-related communications accurately identify the hurricane deductible when the deductible may apply to a reported loss. Those communications must not prejudge whether the deductible applies.

**B.2.36** An insurer must provide a policyholder with an explanation of the hurricane deductible upon reasonable request after a loss is reported. The explanation must be consistent with the policy and the facts then known.

**B.2.37** An insurer must not require a policyholder to waive rights or accept an unsupported interpretation of the hurricane deductible as a condition of receiving information. The insurer must preserve the policyholder's ability to seek clarification.

**B.2.38** An insurer must maintain internal controls to review hurricane deductible disclosures for accuracy and consistency. The controls must address policy form changes, underwriting changes, and changes to consumer materials.

**B.2.39** An insurer must correct a materially inaccurate hurricane deductible disclosure when the insurer becomes aware of the inaccuracy. The correction must be delivered in a manner reasonably calculated to reach affected policyholders.

**B.2.40** An insurer must document the basis for any correction to a hurricane deductible disclosure. The documentation must be available for examination by the Office.

**B.2.41** An insurer must ensure that vendors performing application, policy issuance, billing, or claims functions follow these requirements when their work includes hurricane deductible disclosures. Delegation of a function does not relieve the insurer of responsibility.

**B.2.42** An insurer must monitor consumer complaints concerning hurricane deductible disclosures. The insurer must investigate patterns indicating that its disclosures may be unclear, incomplete, or inconsistent.

**B.2.43** An insurer must take corrective action when monitoring identifies a material deficiency in its hurricane deductible disclosure practices. Corrective action must address affected consumers and the process that caused the deficiency.

**B.2.44** An insurer must make its hurricane deductible disclosure procedures available to the Office upon request. The procedures must identify the personnel or functions responsible for implementation and oversight.

**B.2.45** An insurer must preserve records showing the applicable hurricane deductible disclosed for an issued or renewed policy. The records must be sufficient to verify the disclosure provided for that policy.

**B.2.46** An insurer must ensure that cancellation, nonrenewal, and reinstatement communications do not misstate the hurricane deductible applicable to the policy. If a communication addresses the deductible, it must use the current policy terms.

**B.2.47** An insurer must not use a hurricane deductible disclosure that is misleading by omission. A disclosure is misleading by omission if it fails to state material information necessary to understand how the deductible may apply.

**B.2.48** An insurer must not use illustrations or examples that imply a guaranteed claim payment or a guaranteed deductible outcome. Any illustration must be clearly identified as illustrative and must remain consistent with the policy terms.

**B.2.49** An insurer must review hurricane deductible disclosures when it revises related policy language. The review must occur before the revised language is used for new business or renewals.

**B.2.50** An insurer must ensure that its complaint-handling personnel can identify the hurricane deductible disclosure applicable to a policyholder's inquiry. Personnel must provide information that is consistent with the insurer's records.

**B.2.51** An insurer must administer these requirements in good faith and in a manner consistent with the policyholder's reasonable understanding of the disclosure. Nothing in these requirements authorizes an insurer to apply a deductible that is not supported by the policy.

**B.2.52** An insurer must comply with these requirements in addition to any applicable obligations governing policy forms, underwriting, claims handling, and consumer communications. Where a requirement imposes a more protective disclosure obligation, the insurer must apply that obligation.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** An insurer must provide a clear written notice when a policy includes a hurricane deductible. The notice must be reasonably calculated to inform the policyholder of the deductible’s application.

**B.3.2** An insurer must state whether the hurricane deductible applies to the policy. The statement must appear in language that is clear and conspicuous.

**B.3.3** An insurer must identify the deductible as a hurricane deductible when that deductible differs from another deductible under the policy. The notice must avoid language that could cause a policyholder to confuse the hurricane deductible with another policy deductible.

**B.3.4** An insurer must explain the event or conditions that cause the hurricane deductible to apply. The explanation must describe the applicable policy terms without limiting or expanding those terms.

**B.3.5** An insurer must disclose the manner in which the hurricane deductible is calculated. The disclosure must state whether the deductible is expressed as a fixed amount, a percentage, or another method permitted by the policy.

**B.3.6** An insurer must state the property coverage to which the hurricane deductible applies. The notice must identify any coverage to which the deductible does not apply when that distinction is material to the policyholder’s understanding.

**B.3.7** An insurer must provide the notice with the policy, endorsement, renewal offer, or other communication that establishes or changes the hurricane deductible. The notice must be presented so that a policyholder can retain it with policy records.

**B.3.8** An insurer may combine the hurricane deductible notice with other required policy disclosures if the hurricane deductible information remains clear and conspicuous. An insurer must not obscure the notice through format, placement, or unrelated content.

**B.3.9** An insurer must use terms consistent with the policy when describing the hurricane deductible. An insurer must define or explain a term when its meaning is necessary for a reasonable policyholder to understand the deductible.

**B.3.10** An insurer must provide a revised notice when it changes a hurricane deductible. The revised notice must identify the change in a manner that distinguishes it from prior policy terms.

**B.3.11** An insurer must provide notice at least forty-five (45) days before an increase in a windstorm deductible takes effect. The notice must clearly state that the deductible is increasing.

**B.3.12** An insurer must not represent that a hurricane deductible applies unless the policy supports that application. A notice must accurately reflect the policy form and any applicable endorsement.

**B.3.13** An insurer must describe the hurricane deductible without suggesting that all wind-related losses are subject to that deductible. The notice must distinguish between covered events when the policy provides different deductible treatment.

**B.3.14** An insurer must ensure that a notice provided at renewal accurately reflects the terms offered for the renewal policy period. A notice must not rely on a prior disclosure when the deductible terms have changed.

**B.3.15** An insurer must provide the notice in a form that is legible and readily understandable. An insurer may use electronic delivery when electronic delivery is authorized and the notice remains accessible to the policyholder.

**B.3.16** An insurer must make the hurricane deductible disclosure available to an applicant before coverage is bound when the deductible is included in the coverage offered. The disclosure must allow the applicant to understand the deductible before accepting the offered coverage.

**B.3.17** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate that it provided the required notice. The records must identify the policy or proposed policy associated with the notice.

**B.3.18** An insurer must maintain procedures designed to provide accurate hurricane deductible notices. Those procedures must address notices issued by the insurer and notices issued on the insurer’s behalf.

**B.3.19** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this paragraph when an agent, managing general agent, or other representative provides the notice. An insurer must provide reasonable direction concerning the content and delivery of the notice.

**B.3.20** An insurer must correct a materially inaccurate hurricane deductible notice promptly after discovering the inaccuracy. The correction must clearly identify the information being corrected.

**B.3.21** An insurer must not use a hurricane deductible notice to waive a policyholder right or impose a condition not contained in the policy. The notice is informational and must remain consistent with the contract of insurance.

**B.3.22** An insurer must make any explanation of deductible application consistent with applicable claims handling practices. The notice must not misstate the circumstances under which a deductible may be applied to a loss.

**B.3.23** An insurer must disclose a hurricane deductible separately from a general explanation of policy exclusions when necessary to avoid confusion. The disclosure must permit a reasonable policyholder to locate the deductible information readily.

**B.3.24** An insurer must use a presentation that does not diminish the prominence of a hurricane deductible because of surrounding text. An insurer must not place the material disclosure only in a location unlikely to be reviewed by a policyholder.

**B.3.25** An insurer must ensure that translated or alternative-format notices accurately convey the hurricane deductible information. An insurer must not provide a version that materially changes the meaning of the policy terms.

**B.3.26** An insurer must respond accurately to a policyholder inquiry concerning the hurricane deductible. A response must not contradict the written notice or the policy.

**B.3.27** An insurer must train personnel responsible for issuing deductible notices regarding the insurer’s disclosure procedures. The training must support consistent and accurate communications with applicants and policyholders.

**B.3.28** An insurer must review notice templates when it revises policy language affecting the hurricane deductible. An insurer must update the templates as necessary to remain consistent with the revised policy language.

**B.3.29** An insurer must identify any endorsement that changes the hurricane deductible when providing the related notice. The notice must explain the effect of the endorsement on the deductible in plain language.

**B.3.30** An insurer must not characterize a hurricane deductible as optional unless the policyholder may elect not to have the deductible under the insurer’s applicable underwriting terms. Any description of available options must be accurate.

**B.3.31** An insurer must provide the notice in a manner that allows a policyholder to identify the applicable deductible before a loss occurs. The notice must not depend on post-loss communications to satisfy this paragraph.

**B.3.32** An insurer must ensure that a hurricane deductible notice is consistent across materials used for the same policy transaction. An insurer must resolve any material inconsistency before issuing the policy or endorsement.

**B.3.33** An insurer must not use vague language when describing the circumstances that trigger the hurricane deductible. The notice must provide sufficient information for a reasonable policyholder to understand the policy’s stated application.

**B.3.34** An insurer must preserve the content of a hurricane deductible notice in its policy records. The retained record must permit verification of the notice provided to the applicant or policyholder.

**B.3.35** An insurer must make its hurricane deductible notice procedures available for examination upon request by the Department. An insurer must provide records supporting compliance with this paragraph.

**B.3.36** An insurer must apply this paragraph to notices provided for personal and commercial residential property coverage when a hurricane deductible is included. An insurer must apply the requirements without regard to the delivery method used for the policy.

**B.3.37** An insurer must use this paragraph to guide the content and delivery of hurricane deductible notices issued on or after the effective date of this bulletin. An insurer may provide disclosures that offer greater clarity if those disclosures remain accurate and consistent with the policy.

**B.3.38** An insurer must comply with this paragraph in addition to any other applicable notice obligation. Compliance with another notice obligation does not excuse an insurer from providing the hurricane deductible disclosure required by this paragraph.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer handling a claim under a residential property policy issued or delivered in this state. These requirements take effect upon issuance and govern claim handling to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**B.4.2** An insurer must establish claim procedures that permit prompt identification of a claimed hurricane loss. The procedures must support accurate application of the hurricane deductible stated in the policy.

**B.4.3** An insurer must not apply a hurricane deductible unless the policy terms and applicable law permit its application. An insurer must apply the deductible consistently with the coverage provided and the facts of the reported loss.

**B.4.4** An insurer must acknowledge receipt of a hurricane claim promptly and provide the insured with information concerning the claim process. The information must be clear and must identify the materials reasonably needed to evaluate the claim.

**B.4.5** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation of each reported hurricane claim. The investigation must consider available information concerning the cause, scope, and covered nature of the damage.

**B.4.6** An insurer must communicate with the insured in a manner reasonably calculated to avoid confusion concerning the hurricane deductible. An insurer must explain how the deductible affects the amount payable for covered damage.

**B.4.7** An insurer must not represent that a hurricane deductible applies merely because a hurricane or related weather event occurred. The insurer must determine whether the policy conditions for application of the deductible have been satisfied.

**B.4.8** An insurer must evaluate claimed damage under the policy provisions applicable to the loss. The insurer must distinguish damage subject to the hurricane deductible from damage that is not subject to that deductible when the policy requires that distinction.

**B.4.9** An insurer must maintain adequate claim documentation supporting its decision to apply or not apply a hurricane deductible. The documentation must identify the information relied upon and the basis for the decision.

**B.4.10** An insurer must provide a written explanation when it applies a hurricane deductible to a claim payment. The explanation must be sufficiently specific to allow the insured to understand the basis for the deduction.

**B.4.11** An insurer must provide a written explanation when it determines that no payment is owed because of the hurricane deductible. The explanation must identify the covered loss evaluation and the effect of the deductible on that evaluation.

**B.4.12** An insurer must not delay adjustment, payment, or communication while awaiting information that is not reasonably necessary to resolve the claim. An insurer may request additional information when the request is material to coverage, valuation, or application of the deductible.

**B.4.13** An insurer must promptly advise the insured of information that remains necessary to complete its evaluation. The insurer must describe the information requested in plain language.

**B.4.14** An insurer must ensure that its adjusters have authority and training sufficient to evaluate hurricane claims and deductible issues. An insurer remains responsible for claim handling performed by an adjuster or other representative acting on its behalf.

**B.4.15** An insurer must ensure that estimates used in claim adjustment reasonably identify covered damage and applicable deductions. An insurer must not obscure the hurricane deductible within an estimate, payment statement, or other claim communication.

**B.4.16** An insurer must evaluate supplemental information submitted by an insured in support of a hurricane claim. The insurer must document its consideration of information that could affect coverage, valuation, or deductible application.

**B.4.17** An insurer must review a claim decision when it receives credible information indicating that the hurricane deductible may have been applied incorrectly. The review must be conducted by personnel with appropriate authority.

**B.4.18** An insurer must correct a deductible application error without unnecessary delay. The insurer must provide an explanation of the correction to the insured.

**B.4.19** An insurer must not use claim communications that are misleading, incomplete, or inconsistent with the policy. An insurer must not discourage an insured from seeking clarification regarding the hurricane deductible.

**B.4.20** An insurer must preserve claim records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section. The records must include material communications, estimates, coverage determinations, and deductible determinations.

**B.4.21** An insurer must maintain procedures for supervisory review of hurricane claim handling. The procedures must identify and address patterns that indicate inconsistent deductible application.

**B.4.22** An insurer must apply these standards when using a contractor, vendor, managing general agent, or other service provider in the claim process. Delegation of claim functions does not relieve an insurer of its obligations under this section.

**B.4.23** An insurer may investigate suspected fraud or material misrepresentation. Such investigation must not be used to avoid a reasonable claim determination or to delay payment of an undisputed covered amount.

**B.4.24** An insurer must provide claim communications in a form reasonably accessible to the insured. An insurer must respond reasonably to an insured's request for clarification of a deductible determination.

**B.4.25** An insurer must not treat a request for reconsideration as a basis to withdraw, reduce, or delay an otherwise supported payment. An insurer may revise a claim determination when material information supports the revision.

**B.4.26** An insurer must administer hurricane claims fairly and in good faith. Claim handling practices must be consistent with the policy, applicable law, and the requirements of this section.

**B.4.27** An insurer must make its claim procedures available for review by the department upon request. Failure to comply with this section may be considered in evaluating the insurer's market conduct.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that files a policy form, endorsement, or related disclosure concerning a hurricane deductible. An insurer must comply with this section when the filing is submitted for review.

**B.5.2** An insurer must file the hurricane deductible disclosure with the policy form or endorsement to which the disclosure applies. The filing must clearly identify the form or endorsement that will be delivered with the disclosure.

**B.5.3** An insurer must submit the disclosure in a manner that permits the Office to review the disclosure as it will be provided to an insured. The submitted version must be complete and legible.

**B.5.4** An insurer must identify the intended use of the disclosure in the filing materials. The description must state whether the disclosure will accompany a new policy, a renewal policy, or another policy transaction.

**B.5.5** An insurer must describe the circumstances under which the hurricane deductible may apply. The description must be consistent with the applicable policy language.

**B.5.6** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure accurately explains the insured’s responsibility for the hurricane deductible. The disclosure must not conflict with, qualify, or obscure the policy terms.

**B.5.7** An insurer must file any revised disclosure before using the revised disclosure with an insured. A revision includes a change that affects the content, presentation, or applicability of the disclosure.

**B.5.8** An insurer must identify each change made to a previously filed disclosure. The filing must provide a clear explanation of the reason for the change.

**B.5.9** An insurer may use a hurricane deductible disclosure only after the filing has been approved when approval is required. An insurer must not represent that a disclosure has been approved unless the Office has approved the filing.

**B.5.10** An insurer must retain records demonstrating the version of the disclosure provided to an insured. The records must be available to the Office upon request.

**B.5.11** An insurer must provide the disclosure in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the insured of the hurricane deductible. The method of delivery must be consistent with the insurer’s policy issuance and renewal practices.

**B.5.12** An insurer must ensure that personnel responsible for policy issuance and renewal use the filed disclosure. An insurer must maintain procedures to prevent use of an unfiled or superseded disclosure.

**B.5.13** An insurer must promptly correct a disclosure that the insurer determines is inaccurate, incomplete, or inconsistent with the applicable policy language. The insurer must take appropriate action concerning affected policyholders.

**B.5.14** An insurer must respond to an Office request for information concerning a filing or disclosure. The response must be sufficiently detailed to permit the Office to evaluate compliance with this section.

**B.5.15** Compliance with this section does not relieve an insurer of any obligation imposed by applicable law or the policy contract. An insurer remains responsible for the accuracy of its hurricane deductible disclosure.

**B.5.16** This section is effective as provided by law. An insurer must apply this section to filings submitted on or after the effective date.
