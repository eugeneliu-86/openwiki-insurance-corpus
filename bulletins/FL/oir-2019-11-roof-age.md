# Florida Bulletin OIR-2019-11 — Roof Age Underwriting Restrictions
> SUPERSEDED by Florida Bulletin OIR-2023-04 — Roof Age and Nonrenewal for policies effective on or after 2023-04-11. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This section states the Office’s expectations for underwriting practices that consider the age or condition of a residential roof. An admitted insurer must apply those practices fairly and consistently.

**B.1.2** This section applies to property insurance coverage issued or renewed by an admitted insurer for risks located in this state. It applies when roof age is considered in eligibility, underwriting, inspection, renewal, cancellation, or nonrenewal decisions.

**B.1.3** An insurer may consider roof age as an underwriting factor when the consideration is reasonably related to the risk presented. An insurer must not treat roof age as the sole indicator of a roof’s remaining useful condition when other reliable information is available.

**B.1.4** An insurer must maintain underwriting standards describing how it evaluates roof age and roof condition. Those standards must be applied without unfair discrimination among similarly situated applicants and insureds.

**B.1.5** An insurer may request information concerning the roof’s installation, repair, replacement, material, maintenance, and condition. An insurer must give reasonable consideration to credible information supplied by an applicant or insured.

**B.1.6** An insurer may rely on an inspection, report, photograph, certification, or other reliable documentation to evaluate roof condition. An insurer must evaluate such information in accordance with its filed and approved underwriting practices.

**B.1.7** An insurer must not deny, cancel, or nonrenew coverage solely because a roof has reached an age identified in an internal guideline if available evidence shows that the roof remains in serviceable condition. The insurer may take action when the evidence supports the action under its underwriting standards.

**B.1.8** An insurer must provide notice that clearly states the underwriting reason for an adverse action involving roof age or roof condition. The notice must not characterize a roof as uninsurable without identifying the applicable underwriting concern.

**B.1.9** An insurer must use communications concerning roof condition that are understandable to an applicant or insured. The insurer must identify any information that may be submitted for reconsideration of an underwriting decision.

**B.1.10** An insurer may establish eligibility requirements related to roof condition if those requirements are reasonably related to loss exposure. Such requirements must be supported by objective underwriting criteria.

**B.1.11** An insurer must administer roof-related underwriting requirements consistently across new business and renewal business, except where a legitimate underwriting distinction applies. A distinction must be documented in the insurer’s underwriting records.

**B.1.12** This section does not establish a base deductible minimum. An insurer remains responsible for administering deductibles in accordance with applicable law and its policy forms.

**B.1.13** This section does not establish an insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement. An insurer must administer replacement cost settlement according to the applicable policy terms and underwriting requirements.

**B.1.14** This section does not establish a loss of use limit. An insurer must clearly describe applicable loss of use coverage in its policy forms and communications.

**B.1.15** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section. The records must show the basis for roof-related underwriting actions and the information considered.

**B.1.16** This section is effective upon issuance and applies prospectively to underwriting actions taken on or after its effective date. An insurer must bring applicable underwriting practices into compliance with this section.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer must apply roof age underwriting standards consistently to eligible property insurance risks. An insurer may not use roof age in a manner that is unfairly discriminatory.

**B.2.2** An insurer must maintain written underwriting guidelines governing roof age. The guidelines must identify the information used to determine roof age.

**B.2.3** An insurer must apply the actual cash value roof schedule when the roof age is fifteen (15) years or greater. An insurer may apply more favorable settlement terms when permitted by its filed policy form.

**B.2.4** An insurer must determine roof age using reliable information available in the underwriting record. An insurer may request documentation reasonably necessary to verify roof age.

**B.2.5** An insurer may rely on a permit record, inspection report, invoice, contract, or other credible evidence of roof replacement. An insurer must evaluate conflicting evidence in a reasonable and consistent manner.

**B.2.6** An insurer may not treat a repair as a full roof replacement unless the available documentation supports that conclusion. An insurer must distinguish between replacement of a roof covering and repair of a roof covering.

**B.2.7** An insurer must identify the roof covering to which its roof age underwriting guideline applies. An insurer may use different underwriting treatment for different roof covering materials if the treatment is supported by its underwriting standards.

**B.2.8** An insurer must make its roof age requirements available to underwriting personnel. An insurer must train underwriting personnel to apply those requirements consistently.

**B.2.9** An insurer may use an inspection to obtain information about roof condition. An insurer may not substitute a condition finding for a roof age determination without a reasonable basis.

**B.2.10** An insurer must consider information showing that a roof has been replaced or materially improved. An insurer may require sufficient documentation before changing the underwriting record.

**B.2.11** An insurer must record the source of roof age information used for an underwriting decision. The record must be sufficient to explain the decision.

**B.2.12** An insurer may request additional information when the roof age cannot be determined from the information initially provided. An insurer must communicate the request in a clear manner.

**B.2.13** An insurer may decline an application that does not satisfy its filed underwriting guidelines. An insurer must provide any notice required by applicable law.

**B.2.14** An insurer may condition issuance upon verification of roof age. An insurer must apply that condition uniformly to similarly situated applicants.

**B.2.15** An insurer must not represent that roof age is unavailable when reliable documentation is present in the underwriting file. An insurer must review material documentation before making a roof age decision.

**B.2.16** An insurer may use information supplied by an applicant, agent, inspector, or contractor. An insurer must reasonably evaluate information that appears incomplete or inconsistent.

**B.2.17** An insurer must not require an applicant to provide documentation that is not reasonably related to verification of roof age or roof condition. An insurer may request clarification of submitted documentation.

**B.2.18** An insurer must apply roof age underwriting rules to new business in accordance with its filed underwriting guidelines. An insurer must not apply an unfiled roof age rule.

**B.2.19** An insurer must apply roof age underwriting rules to renewal business in accordance with applicable law and its filed underwriting guidelines. An insurer must provide required notice before taking an adverse underwriting action.

**B.2.20** An insurer may revise its roof age underwriting guidelines. An insurer must implement a revision in a manner that permits consistent administration.

**B.2.21** An insurer must retain records supporting a roof age underwriting decision. The records must be available for review by the Office.

**B.2.22** An insurer must retain documentation supporting application of the actual cash value roof schedule. The documentation must identify the roof information relied upon.

**B.2.23** An insurer must ensure that policy issuance systems apply the roof age rule reflected in its underwriting guidelines. An insurer must correct a system rule that does not conform to those guidelines.

**B.2.24** An insurer must ensure that rating, underwriting, and claims functions use compatible roof age information when that information affects coverage settlement. An insurer must investigate material discrepancies between relevant records.

**B.2.25** An insurer may use a roof inspection report prepared by a qualified inspector. An insurer must not disregard material findings in the report without a documented reason.

**B.2.26** An insurer may use aerial imagery or similar technology as underwriting information. An insurer must not rely on such information as the sole basis for a roof age determination when the information does not establish roof age.

**B.2.27** An insurer must identify any assumption used to determine roof age from incomplete records. An insurer must apply the assumption consistently.

**B.2.28** An insurer must not infer roof replacement solely from a change in roof appearance. An insurer may consider appearance together with other reliable information.

**B.2.29** An insurer must communicate a roof age underwriting requirement in language reasonably understandable to an applicant. An insurer must avoid statements that conflict with the policy form.

**B.2.30** An insurer must provide a clear explanation when roof age information affects the coverage settlement applicable to a policy. The explanation must accurately describe the applicable policy terms.

**B.2.31** An insurer may offer coverage subject to a roof-related underwriting condition that is permitted by law. An insurer must state the condition clearly in the applicable notice or policy documentation.

**B.2.32** An insurer must not use roof age as a basis to deny coverage for a loss when the policy does not support that denial. An insurer must adjust claims according to the policy terms.

**B.2.33** An insurer must apply the actual cash value roof schedule as stated in the policy form. An insurer may not apply a settlement method that is inconsistent with the policy form.

**B.2.34** An insurer must ensure that claims personnel can identify when the actual cash value roof schedule applies. An insurer must provide claims personnel with access to relevant underwriting records.

**B.2.35** An insurer must not alter roof age information after a loss without documenting the basis for the alteration. An insurer must preserve the prior information in the claim or underwriting record.

**B.2.36** An insurer must address a dispute concerning roof age in good faith. An insurer must consider documentation submitted by an insured that reasonably bears on the dispute.

**B.2.37** An insurer may request inspection or other verification after receiving information that reasonably calls roof age into question. An insurer must not use a verification request to delay a claim decision improperly.

**B.2.38** An insurer must ensure that its underwriting guidelines distinguish roof age from roof condition. An insurer may consider both factors when permitted by its filed underwriting standards.

**B.2.39** An insurer must not treat ordinary maintenance as evidence of roof replacement. An insurer may consider maintenance records as evidence of roof condition.

**B.2.40** An insurer must establish controls to identify inconsistent roof age determinations. An insurer must take corrective action when a material inconsistency is identified.

**B.2.41** An insurer must periodically review roof age underwriting decisions for conformity with its written guidelines. An insurer must document material corrective measures.

**B.2.42** An insurer must ensure that agents acting on its behalf receive accurate information concerning roof age requirements. An insurer remains responsible for the administration of its underwriting standards.

**B.2.43** An insurer must not instruct an agent to make a representation about roof age that conflicts with the insurer's written guidelines. An insurer must correct a material inaccurate representation when identified.

**B.2.44** An insurer must use roof age information only for purposes permitted by its policy forms, underwriting guidelines, and applicable law. An insurer must safeguard documents containing applicant or insured information.

**B.2.45** An insurer must respond to an Office request for records concerning roof age underwriting. The response must include information sufficient to evaluate compliance with B.2.1 through B.2.44.

**B.2.46** An insurer must make available the version of its underwriting guideline in effect when an underwriting decision was made. An insurer must identify material changes relevant to the decision.

**B.2.47** An insurer must not apply a roof age rule retroactively in a manner inconsistent with the policy terms. An insurer must honor coverage already provided unless a lawful basis exists for a change.

**B.2.48** An insurer must ensure that notices concerning roof age are not misleading. An insurer must use terminology that is consistent with the applicable policy form.

**B.2.49** An insurer must maintain procedures for correcting roof age information shown to be inaccurate. An insurer must communicate a material correction to affected operational functions.

**B.2.50** An insurer must ensure that a correction under B.2.49 is reflected in subsequent underwriting and claims handling, as applicable. An insurer must preserve documentation supporting the correction.

**B.2.51** An insurer may use roof age in evaluating eligibility for coverage when authorized by its filed underwriting guidelines. An insurer must not use roof age as a pretext for an unlawful adverse action.

**B.2.52** An insurer must administer roof age underwriting practices in accordance with B.2.1 through B.2.51. An insurer must implement these requirements upon the effective date of this bulletin.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** Before binding coverage for a risk with a roof that is twenty (20) years years of age or older, an insurer must obtain a roof inspection. The inspection must be reasonably sufficient to support the insurer’s underwriting decision.

**B.3.2** An insurer must disclose the roof inspection requirement to an applicant before requesting or accepting an inspection. The disclosure must state that coverage may depend upon the inspection findings.

**B.3.3** An insurer may request information concerning roof age, roof covering type, visible condition, prior repairs, and replacement history. An insurer must identify the information needed to evaluate eligibility.

**B.3.4** An insurer must apply its roof inspection requirement in a manner that is consistent with its filed underwriting guidelines. The insurer must retain records demonstrating the basis for the underwriting action.

**B.3.5** An insurer may require an inspection by a qualified inspector or other person authorized by the insurer to perform the inspection. The insurer must communicate any qualification requirements to the applicant upon request.

**B.3.6** An insurer must not represent that an inspection guarantees coverage, renewal, payment of a claim, or a particular premium. The insurer must explain that inspection results are subject to underwriting review.

**B.3.7** An insurer must provide an applicant with a reasonable opportunity to submit an inspection report before declining to bind coverage based on unavailable inspection information. The insurer may identify the manner in which the report must be submitted.

**B.3.8** An insurer may use an inspection report obtained for another lawful purpose if the report is current, reliable, and relevant to the risk. The insurer must disclose any material reliance on that report when communicating an adverse underwriting action.

**B.3.9** An insurer must review an inspection report using standards that are applied consistently to similarly situated risks. The insurer must not use roof age alone as a substitute for review of material inspection findings.

**B.3.10** An insurer may decline to bind coverage when an inspection identifies conditions that do not meet the insurer’s underwriting standards. The insurer must provide a clear written explanation of the basis for the decision.

**B.3.11** An insurer must identify material roof conditions that require correction before coverage may be bound. The notice must state that the applicant may provide documentation of completed corrections for further underwriting consideration.

**B.3.12** An insurer may condition binding on repair, replacement, or other corrective action supported by the inspection findings. The insurer must state the condition in writing.

**B.3.13** An insurer must not require an applicant to obtain unnecessary repairs solely to generate a more favorable inspection result. Any requested corrective action must have a reasonable relationship to the insurer’s underwriting standards.

**B.3.14** An insurer must disclose whether it will accept an inspection report prepared for a prior insurer, lender, seller, or other party. The disclosure must identify any conditions for acceptance.

**B.3.15** An insurer must maintain procedures for resolving questions regarding the accuracy or completeness of an inspection report. Those procedures must permit the applicant to submit relevant supporting information.

**B.3.16** An insurer may require a new inspection when available information reasonably indicates that a prior report is no longer reliable. The insurer must communicate the reason for requesting the new inspection.

**B.3.17** An insurer must provide written notice when it determines that inspection findings prevent binding coverage. The notice must be sufficiently specific to permit the applicant to understand the material underwriting concern.

**B.3.18** An insurer may communicate inspection requirements electronically if the applicant has agreed to receive communications by that method. The insurer must maintain evidence of the communication.

**B.3.19** An insurer must protect inspection reports and related applicant information in accordance with applicable privacy and information-security requirements. Access to those records must be limited to persons with a legitimate business need.

**B.3.20** An insurer must ensure that producers acting on its behalf communicate roof inspection requirements accurately. The insurer remains responsible for its underwriting communications.

**B.3.21** An insurer must not state or imply that a producer, inspector, or contractor can assure a particular underwriting outcome. The insurer must address misleading communications promptly.

**B.3.22** An insurer may provide general information concerning the types of roof conditions that may affect eligibility. The information must not be presented as a complete statement of all underwriting standards.

**B.3.23** An insurer must disclose when an inspection is required as a condition of continued coverage. The disclosure must be provided in a manner reasonably calculated to reach the named insured.

**B.3.24** An insurer must provide notice of nonrenewal at least ninety (90) days days before the nonrenewal takes effect. The notice must clearly state the roof-related reason for nonrenewal when roof condition or inspection findings form the basis for the action.

**B.3.25** An insurer must state any available process for submitting information that may affect a pending nonrenewal decision. The insurer may establish reasonable documentation requirements for that process.

**B.3.26** An insurer must not characterize a request for roof information as a cancellation or nonrenewal unless the insurer has made the applicable underwriting determination. Communications must accurately describe the status of coverage.

**B.3.27** An insurer must retain copies of notices, inspection reports, underwriting evaluations, and related communications. The retained records must be sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section.

**B.3.28** An insurer must train personnel responsible for underwriting, policy issuance, renewal, and customer communications on the requirements of this section. Training materials must reflect the insurer’s current underwriting practices.

**B.3.29** An insurer must apply this section to roof-age underwriting actions taken on or after the effective date of this bulletin. An insurer may apply more protective consumer practices if those practices are consistent with applicable law.

**B.3.30** An insurer must administer the notice and disclosure requirements of this section in good faith. An insurer may revise its procedures as necessary to maintain compliance with this section.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** An insurer must apply this section to a residential property claim involving alleged roof damage when claims handling occurs after this section becomes effective. An insurer must apply these standards to an open claim to the extent handling continues after effectiveness, without altering policy rights fixed before that time.

**B.4.2** An insurer must evaluate a roof claim based on the policy terms in force at the time of loss and the facts developed during the investigation. An insurer may not use a roof-age underwriting guideline as an independent basis to deny, limit, or delay a claim.

**B.4.3** A prior underwriting decision to issue, renew, nonrenew, or cancel coverage does not establish that a claimed loss is not covered. Claims personnel must make an independent coverage determination under the applicable policy.

**B.4.4** An insurer must provide the claimant with a clear explanation of the information reasonably needed to evaluate the claim. An insurer may not demand information that is not reasonably available to the claimant when other reliable information can be obtained.

**B.4.5** An insurer must provide a reasonable opportunity for inspection of the damaged property. An insurer may not condition consideration of a claim upon access that the claimant cannot safely or lawfully provide.

**B.4.6** An insurer must ensure that a person assigned to inspect roof damage is qualified to evaluate the issues presented by the claim. The insurer must document the scope and material findings of the inspection.

**B.4.7** An insurer must consider all relevant information submitted by the claimant, including photographs, repair estimates, reports, and information concerning the reported event. An insurer must give the information reasonable consideration based on its reliability and relevance.

**B.4.8** An insurer must distinguish between covered damage and conditions that are excluded or otherwise not covered under the policy. An insurer may not characterize claimed damage as wear, deterioration, maintenance-related damage, or a similar condition without a factual basis supporting that conclusion.

**B.4.9** An insurer must apply policy provisions concerning repair, replacement, valuation, and deductibles as written. An insurer may not reduce a covered payment based solely on the age of the roof unless the policy expressly permits the reduction and the reduction is supported by the claim facts.

**B.4.10** An insurer must communicate its coverage decision in writing when the decision denies or limits requested benefits. The communication must state the material factual basis and the applicable policy basis for the decision.

**B.4.11** An insurer that denies or limits only part of a claim must identify the affected portion of the claim with reasonable clarity. An insurer may not obscure a partial denial by issuing a communication that fails to distinguish covered damage from damage that is not covered.

**B.4.12** An insurer must issue undisputed benefits when due under the policy, even if another portion of the claim remains under review. An insurer may not withhold undisputed benefits solely to obtain leverage concerning a disputed portion of the claim.

**B.4.13** An insurer must give reasonable consideration to a repair estimate or other damage evaluation submitted by the claimant. If the insurer relies on a materially different estimate, the insurer must be able to explain the basis for the difference.

**B.4.14** An insurer may use an adjuster, engineer, contractor, or other vendor to assist with claim handling. An insurer remains responsible under this paragraph for the accuracy, fairness, and timeliness of the claim decision.

**B.4.15** An insurer may investigate suspected fraud or material misrepresentation when the available information provides a reasonable basis for that investigation. An insurer may not use a fraud investigation to delay a claim decision or payment without a reasonable basis.

**B.4.16** An insurer must reasonably consider supplemental information submitted after an initial claim decision. An insurer must revise its decision when the supplemental information materially changes the applicable facts or coverage analysis.

**B.4.17** A catastrophe, weather event, staffing condition, or claim volume does not relieve an insurer of its obligations under this paragraph. An insurer must maintain claim handling practices that permit fair evaluation of roof claims during unusual claim activity.

**B.4.18** An insurer must maintain claim records sufficient to show the information considered, the investigation performed, and the basis for the coverage decision. The records must permit review of the insurer's compliance with this paragraph.

**B.4.19** An insurer must supervise claims personnel and vendors handling roof claims to identify practices inconsistent with this paragraph. When the insurer identifies an improper practice, the insurer must take reasonable corrective action for affected claims.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that files or revises a roof-age underwriting practice for residential property insurance in this state. An insurer must submit the filing to the Department before using the practice.

**B.5.2** A filing must clearly identify the underwriting action affected by the proposed practice. The filing must state whether the practice may result in declination, cancellation, nonrenewal, conditional renewal, inspection, or other underwriting action.

**B.5.3** An insurer must describe the roof-age criteria that will be applied. The description must be sufficiently specific to permit the Department to determine how the insurer will apply the criteria.

**B.5.4** A filing must explain the information the insurer will use to determine roof age. An insurer must identify the sources of information and the manner in which conflicting information will be evaluated.

**B.5.5** An insurer must provide the underwriting guidelines, rules, manuals, notices, and related materials that implement the proposed practice. The materials must be consistent with the filing.

**B.5.6** A filing must identify any exception process available to an applicant or policyholder. An insurer must explain the standards for granting an exception and the personnel authorized to make that determination.

**B.5.7** An insurer must describe any inspection, documentation, or verification requirement associated with the proposed practice. The filing must explain how the insurer will communicate those requirements to affected consumers.

**B.5.8** A filing must include the insurer’s basis for concluding that the proposed practice is actuarially supported, not unfairly discriminatory, and consistent with applicable law. The Department may request additional support where the filing does not provide an adequate basis for review.

**B.5.9** An insurer must identify the policy forms and endorsements to which the proposed practice applies. An insurer must also identify any related rate, rule, or underwriting filing affected by the proposed practice.

**B.5.10** A filing must state the proposed effective date for the practice. The proposed effective date must not precede Department approval where approval is required.

**B.5.11** An insurer may not implement a roof-age underwriting practice that differs from the practice described in its filing. A material change to the filed practice must be submitted to the Department before use.

**B.5.12** An insurer must maintain records supporting each underwriting action taken under a roof-age practice. The records must be sufficient to demonstrate compliance with the insurer’s filed guidelines.

**B.5.13** An insurer must provide clear notice of an adverse underwriting action resulting from a roof-age practice. The notice must accurately state the reason for the action and any available consumer options.

**B.5.14** Department acceptance or approval of a filing does not relieve an insurer of its obligation to comply with applicable law. An insurer remains responsible for the administration of its underwriting practices.

**B.5.15** An insurer must promptly respond to a Department request for information concerning a filing or implemented practice. The response must include complete and accurate materials sufficient for Department review.

**B.5.16** A roof-age underwriting practice becomes effective only as stated in the filing and as permitted by Department action. An insurer must discontinue use of a practice if the Department determines that the practice does not comply with applicable requirements.
