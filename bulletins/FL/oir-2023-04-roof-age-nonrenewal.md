# Florida Bulletin OIR-2023-04 — Roof Age and Nonrenewal




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

This bulletin states the Office’s expectations for admitted insurers that use roof age in underwriting, renewal, or nonrenewal decisions. It is intended to promote clear, fair, and supportable treatment of policyholders.

This bulletin applies to residential property insurance written in Florida by an admitted insurer. It applies whether roof age is considered directly or through an underwriting rule, inspection practice, eligibility standard, or decision process.

An insurer must administer roof-age practices consistently with its filed forms, underwriting rules, and policyholder communications. An insurer may use roof condition information when the information is relevant to the insurer’s assessment of risk.

Roof age alone may not be treated as a substitute for an assessment of the roof’s condition when available information reasonably indicates that the roof remains serviceable. An insurer should evaluate reliable information concerning maintenance, repairs, replacement, materials, and observed condition.

An insurer must not represent that a policy is being nonrenewed for roof age when the insurer’s actual basis includes other underwriting considerations. A nonrenewal notice must accurately state the reason or reasons supporting the insurer’s action.

An insurer must provide policyholders with notices that are clear and understandable. Notice language must not obscure the practical basis for a nonrenewal decision.

An insurer should maintain records supporting its use of roof age in each underwriting decision. Those records should identify the information reviewed and the basis for the resulting action.

An insurer may rely on inspections, reports, photographs, policyholder submissions, contractor information, or other reliable sources. An insurer should establish reasonable procedures to address information that is incomplete, inconsistent, or outdated.

An insurer must apply roof-age standards in a manner that does not unfairly discriminate among similarly situated policyholders. Differences in treatment should be supported by risk-related distinctions.

An insurer should provide a meaningful opportunity for a policyholder to submit information relevant to a roof-age or roof-condition determination. An insurer should reasonably consider timely information received before a final underwriting action is taken.

An insurer must ensure that its producers, underwriters, inspectors, and vendors use roof-age criteria consistently. An insurer remains responsible for underwriting actions taken through a vendor or other representative.

An insurer should train personnel responsible for communicating underwriting decisions. Communications should avoid statements that are misleading, incomplete, or inconsistent with the insurer’s documented basis for action.

An insurer must distinguish between a request for additional information and a final nonrenewal decision. A policyholder should not be led to believe that submission of information will alter an outcome when the insurer has already reached a final determination.

An insurer may establish roof-age guidelines that reflect its underwriting judgment. Those guidelines should permit appropriate consideration of credible evidence of roof condition and remaining useful life.

An insurer should review its roof-age practices periodically to confirm that they remain consistent with current underwriting operations and policyholder notice procedures. A review should include the treatment of exceptions and appeals.

This bulletin does not establish an all-other-perils deductible minimum, a coinsurance threshold, or an ALE percentage. Those concepts are outside the purpose and applicability of this bulletin.

An insurer must not use this bulletin as a basis to alter policy terms without complying with applicable filing and notice requirements. This bulletin does not itself amend an insurance policy.

An insurer must continue to comply with all applicable obligations concerning underwriting, cancellation, nonrenewal, claims handling, and consumer communications. Compliance with this bulletin does not relieve an insurer of any separate obligation.

The Office may request records demonstrating an insurer’s implementation of the expectations described in this bulletin. An insurer should be prepared to explain its roof-age standards and its procedures for reviewing policyholder information.

This bulletin is effective upon issuance. An insurer must apply its requirements to underwriting and nonrenewal actions taken on or after its effective date.

The Office will evaluate insurer conduct based on the facts and circumstances of each matter. An insurer should promptly correct practices that do not conform to this bulletin.

This bulletin applies unless revised or withdrawn by the Office. An insurer should monitor Office communications for updates affecting roof-age underwriting or nonrenewal practices.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer must apply these requirements to any residential property policy subject to a roof-age-based underwriting or nonrenewal decision. An insurer may apply more favorable treatment to an insured if the treatment is consistent with the policy and applicable law.

**B.2.2** An insurer must use roof age information that is reasonably reliable and relevant to the insured property. An insurer must not rely on unsupported assumptions about roof age when taking an adverse underwriting action.

**B.2.3** An insurer must identify the roof covering material and roof configuration used in its underwriting evaluation. An insurer must maintain records supporting the information used for that evaluation.

**B.2.4** An insurer must provide underwriting personnel with written standards for evaluating roof age and roof condition. The standards must be applied consistently to similarly situated risks.

**B.2.5** An insurer must distinguish between roof age and roof condition when evaluating eligibility for coverage. Roof age alone must not be represented as proof that a roof is damaged or unsuitable for continued service.

**B.2.6** An insurer may request documentation supporting the age, repair, replacement, or condition of a roof. An insurer must give reasonable consideration to documentation supplied by an applicant or insured.

**B.2.7** An insurer must evaluate credible evidence of roof replacement, substantial repair, or material improvement before issuing a nonrenewal notice based on roof age. The insurer must document its evaluation in the underwriting file.

**B.2.8** An insurer must apply an actual cash value roof schedule when roof age reaches ten (10) years. An insurer must describe the schedule clearly in the policy form and related coverage materials.

**B.2.9** An insurer must not apply an actual cash value roof schedule to a loss unless the policy form authorizes that method of settlement. The policy language must identify the roof components to which the schedule applies.

**B.2.10** An insurer must explain any roof settlement limitation in language that is clear and conspicuous. An insurer must not describe an actual cash value settlement as replacement cost coverage.

**B.2.11** An insurer must use roof age information in a manner consistent with its filed underwriting rules. An insurer must not use undisclosed roof-age criteria to deny, cancel, or nonrenew coverage.

**B.2.12** An insurer must provide a written explanation when roof age is a material reason for a nonrenewal decision. The explanation must be sufficiently specific to allow the insured to understand the basis for the action.

**B.2.13** An insurer must identify any documentation that could affect a roof-age-based underwriting decision. An insurer must not require documentation that is unnecessary to evaluate the stated reason for the decision.

**B.2.14** An insurer must review information received from an insured before the effective date of a proposed nonrenewal. An insurer must correct or withdraw the action when the information shows that the stated basis is inaccurate.

**B.2.15** An insurer must not treat a roof as having the same age as the residence unless the insurer lacks reliable information showing a different roof installation date. The insurer must document the basis for any age determination made from available records.

**B.2.16** An insurer may use inspection reports, permits, invoices, photographs, affidavits, and other reliable records to determine roof age. An insurer must evaluate the relevance and credibility of the records it receives.

**B.2.17** An insurer must not reject a claim of roof replacement solely because the insured lacks a particular type of record. An insurer must consider alternative evidence that reasonably supports the claimed replacement.

**B.2.18** An insurer must ensure that inspection vendors are instructed to report roof condition accurately and without unsupported conclusions. The insurer remains responsible for underwriting actions based on vendor information.

**B.2.19** An insurer must maintain procedures for resolving conflicts between inspection findings and information supplied by an insured. Those procedures must provide for meaningful review by personnel with authority to reconsider the underwriting decision.

**B.2.20** An insurer must not issue a nonrenewal notice based on roof age when the underwriting file does not support the stated roof age. The insurer must retain the materials supporting its decision.

**B.2.21** An insurer must state the applicable reason for nonrenewal in the notice provided to the insured. A generic statement that the risk no longer meets underwriting guidelines is insufficient when roof age is a material basis for the action.

**B.2.22** An insurer must ensure that a nonrenewal notice does not misstate the roof condition, roof age, or roof material of the insured property. An insurer must promptly correct a notice containing a material error.

**B.2.23** An insurer must provide notice of nonrenewal in the manner required by applicable law and the policy. The notice must not be delayed to prevent the insured from reasonably responding to the stated concern.

**B.2.24** An insurer must provide customer service personnel with access to the reasons supporting a roof-age-based underwriting action. Personnel communicating with an insured must not provide information that conflicts with the underwriting file.

**B.2.25** An insurer must establish a process for an insured to submit additional roof information after receiving a proposed adverse action. The process must identify a reasonable method for submitting the information.

**B.2.26** An insurer must consider timely submitted information before finalizing a roof-age-based nonrenewal. An insurer must record the result of that consideration in the underwriting file.

**B.2.27** An insurer must not require replacement of a roof when available information shows that the roof remains eligible under the insurer's filed underwriting rules. An insurer may request further inspection when the available information is incomplete or conflicting.

**B.2.28** An insurer must not condition renewal on roof replacement without identifying the underwriting basis for that condition. The condition must be communicated clearly to the insured.

**B.2.29** An insurer must apply roof-age-based underwriting criteria without unfair discrimination. An insurer must not vary its treatment of similarly situated insureds without a documented underwriting basis.

**B.2.30** An insurer must train relevant underwriting and claims personnel regarding the distinction between roof eligibility rules and roof loss settlement provisions. The training must address how those provisions are communicated to insureds.

**B.2.31** An insurer must ensure that advertising, policy summaries, and customer communications do not contradict the policy's roof settlement provisions. An insurer must correct material inconsistencies discovered in those materials.

**B.2.32** An insurer must not use a roof-age-based underwriting rule that conflicts with coverage provided by the policy form. Any limitation or exclusion relied upon by the insurer must be stated in the policy.

**B.2.33** An insurer must administer roof-age-based underwriting rules in accordance with applicable filing requirements. An insurer must retain records showing the version of the underwriting rule applied to the insured risk.

**B.2.34** An insurer must review roof-age-based nonrenewal activity for accuracy and consistency. The review must identify patterns suggesting that notices are being issued without adequate supporting information.

**B.2.35** An insurer must maintain a process for correcting underwriting records when reliable information establishes that the recorded roof age is inaccurate. The corrected record must be used in subsequent underwriting actions.

**B.2.36** An insurer must not treat ordinary roof maintenance as evidence that a roof has been replaced. An insurer must distinguish maintenance from replacement or material repair based on the available documentation.

**B.2.37** An insurer must not treat the absence of visible damage as proof that a roof meets all underwriting requirements. An insurer may consider roof condition, roof material, construction, and other lawful underwriting factors.

**B.2.38** An insurer must not treat visible wear as conclusive evidence that a roof is ineligible for renewal without applying its underwriting standards. The insurer must document the facts supporting an adverse action.

**B.2.39** An insurer must use clear procedures when a roof has multiple covering materials or separate roof sections. The procedures must identify how the insurer determines the applicable roof age and settlement treatment.

**B.2.40** An insurer must not apply a roof-age-based rule to a component that is not a covered roof component under the policy. The insurer must explain the component at issue when communicating an adverse action.

**B.2.41** An insurer must ensure that claim personnel apply roof settlement provisions as written. Claim personnel must not impose an underwriting eligibility requirement when adjusting a covered loss.

**B.2.42** An insurer must ensure that underwriting personnel do not use claim settlement language to explain a nonrenewal decision unless that language is relevant to the decision. Communications must distinguish coverage eligibility from loss valuation.

**B.2.43** An insurer must retain nonrenewal notices, supporting roof information, and related communications in accordance with applicable recordkeeping requirements. The records must be available for regulatory review.

**B.2.44** An insurer must monitor complaints concerning roof-age-based nonrenewals and roof settlement practices. The insurer must investigate material allegations of inaccurate roof information or misleading communications.

**B.2.45** An insurer must promptly address identified deficiencies in its roof-age underwriting practices. Corrective measures must be documented and applied consistently.

**B.2.46** An insurer must ensure that third parties acting on its behalf comply with these requirements when conducting inspections, communicating underwriting decisions, or handling related information. The insurer may not avoid responsibility by delegating those functions.

**B.2.47** An insurer must apply these requirements to actions taken on or after the effective date. An insurer must not apply a new roof-age-based adverse standard to an insured without providing any notice required by applicable law.

**B.2.48** An insurer may revise its roof-age underwriting practices if the revised practices comply with applicable law and filed requirements. The insurer must ensure that revised practices are communicated to affected personnel.

**B.2.49** An insurer must administer any exception to a roof-age underwriting rule under written standards. The insurer must document the basis for granting or denying an exception.

**B.2.50** An insurer must be able to demonstrate compliance with this paragraph upon request by the Office. An insurer must provide complete and accurate records supporting its roof-age-based underwriting and nonrenewal practices.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** This section applies to an insurer authorized to transact residential property insurance in this state. An insurer must comply with this section for any binding, renewal, or nonrenewal action taken on or after this section becomes effective.

**B.3.2** Before binding coverage for property with a roof that is fifteen (15) years old, an insurer must obtain a roof inspection consistent with its filed underwriting standards. The insurer must retain the inspection information supporting its underwriting action.

**B.3.3** An insurer must disclose any roof inspection requirement to an applicant in clear and conspicuous language. The disclosure must state that the inspection may affect eligibility, coverage terms, premium, or a decision not to offer coverage.

**B.3.4** An insurer must provide the inspection disclosure before the applicant is required to pay a premium or accept coverage. The disclosure may be provided electronically if the applicant has consented to electronic delivery.

**B.3.5** An insurer must identify the party responsible for arranging and paying for a required inspection. The insurer must not represent that an inspection is free unless no charge will be imposed on the applicant or insured.

**B.3.6** An insurer must provide an applicant a reasonable opportunity to submit an inspection report obtained from a qualified inspector. The insurer may require that the report contain information reasonably necessary for underwriting.

**B.3.7** An insurer must apply roof inspection standards uniformly to similarly situated risks. The insurer must not use a roof inspection requirement as a pretext for an unfairly discriminatory underwriting action.

**B.3.8** An insurer must disclose material inspection criteria that may result in an adverse underwriting decision. The disclosure need not reveal confidential rating, scoring, or proprietary underwriting methods.

**B.3.9** An insurer must communicate an adverse binding decision in writing. The communication must state the principal roof-related reason for the decision with sufficient specificity for an applicant to understand the basis for the action.

**B.3.10** An insurer must not state or imply that a roof inspection guarantees eligibility for coverage. An insurer must explain that underwriting remains subject to all applicable policy and eligibility requirements.

**B.3.11** An insurer must ensure that a roof inspection disclosure is not misleading by omission. Any qualification material to an applicant's understanding of the requirement must accompany the disclosure.

**B.3.12** An insurer must use plain language in communications concerning roof age, roof condition, and inspection findings. An insurer must avoid technical terminology unless it is reasonably explained.

**B.3.13** An insurer must maintain procedures for reviewing inspection information before making an underwriting decision. Those procedures must provide for correction of an apparent clerical or factual error that is material to the decision.

**B.3.14** An insurer must provide an applicant or insured with a meaningful opportunity to dispute material roof information used in an adverse underwriting action. The insurer must identify the information at issue upon request.

**B.3.15** An insurer must document its disposition of information submitted to dispute an inspection finding. The documentation must show whether the information changed the underwriting decision.

**B.3.16** An insurer must provide a notice of nonrenewal in writing. The notice must be delivered in the manner required by applicable law and the policy.

**B.3.17** An insurer must provide notice of nonrenewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the policy expiration date. The insurer must calculate the notice period in a manner that provides the insured the required advance notice.

**B.3.18** An insurer must clearly identify a notice of nonrenewal as a nonrenewal notice. The notice must not be presented in a format that could reasonably be mistaken for a routine policy communication.

**B.3.19** An insurer must state the effective date of nonrenewal in the notice. The stated date must be consistent with the policy expiration date and the insurer's records.

**B.3.20** An insurer must state the principal reason for nonrenewal in clear and specific terms. A general reference to underwriting guidelines, without further explanation, is insufficient.

**B.3.21** When roof condition or roof age is a basis for nonrenewal, an insurer must identify the relevant condition or information relied upon. The notice must not characterize an inspection finding more adversely than the inspection report supports.

**B.3.22** An insurer must disclose whether the insured may submit updated roof information for review before nonrenewal takes effect. If the insurer permits review, the notice must explain how the insured may submit the information.

**B.3.23** An insurer must not represent that repair, replacement, or other action will assure renewal unless the insurer can make that representation accurately. Any statement concerning possible reconsideration must describe applicable limitations.

**B.3.24** An insurer must provide contact information through which an insured may request an explanation of the nonrenewal decision. The contact information must direct the insured to personnel or systems able to address the stated reason.

**B.3.25** An insurer must provide contact information in a nonrenewal notice in a reasonably accessible format. The insurer must offer reasonable assistance to an insured seeking to understand the notice.

**B.3.26** An insurer must preserve a copy of each nonrenewal notice and evidence of its delivery. The insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section.

**B.3.27** An insurer must retain the underwriting materials supporting a nonrenewal decision. The materials must include relevant inspection information, roof information, and internal decision records.

**B.3.28** An insurer must establish controls to prevent a nonrenewal notice from being issued in error. The controls must address policy status, mailing information, and the stated reason for nonrenewal.

**B.3.29** If an insurer withdraws a nonrenewal notice, the insurer must promptly provide written confirmation to the insured. The confirmation must clearly state the policy status after withdrawal.

**B.3.30** An insurer must not use a notice of nonrenewal to impose a midterm reduction, exclusion, or cancellation not otherwise permitted by law or the policy. Any separate underwriting action must be communicated separately and accurately.

**B.3.31** An insurer must ensure that communications issued by its agents, managing general agents, and third-party administrators comply with this section. An insurer remains responsible for notices issued on its behalf.

**B.3.32** An insurer must train personnel who handle roof inspection disclosures and nonrenewal notices. Training must address accurate communication, record handling, and escalation of disputed information.

**B.3.33** An insurer must periodically review its notice and disclosure practices for clarity and consistency. The review must identify and correct recurring errors or misleading communications.

**B.3.34** An insurer must make this section's required disclosures available in a form reasonably calculated to be understood by the applicant or insured. An insurer may provide additional explanatory material that is consistent with the required disclosure.

**B.3.35** An insurer must not condition consideration of an applicant or insured on waiver of a right to receive required notice or disclosure. A waiver that conflicts with this section is ineffective.

**B.3.36** An insurer must respond to a department inquiry concerning compliance with this section by producing relevant records and explanations. The insurer must ensure that its response is complete and accurate.

**B.3.37** An insurer must correct a material defect in a required notice or disclosure promptly after discovery. The corrective communication must plainly identify the information being corrected.

**B.3.38** An insurer must administer this section in good faith and consistently with its filed underwriting practices. Compliance with this section does not limit any obligation otherwise imposed by applicable law.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer handling a claim involving roof damage under a property insurance policy. This section applies as of its effective date.

**B.4.2** An insurer must evaluate a reported loss based on the facts available for that loss. An insurer may not deny, limit, or delay a claim solely because of roof age.

**B.4.3** An insurer must distinguish between a claim determination and an underwriting determination. A nonrenewal decision may not substitute for a proper claim investigation.

**B.4.4** An insurer must acknowledge a claim in a manner that permits the insured to understand the claim process. Communications must identify information reasonably needed to evaluate coverage and damage.

**B.4.5** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation before making a claim decision. The investigation must address the reported cause of loss and the observed condition of the roof.

**B.4.6** An insurer may consider wear, deterioration, prior damage, installation defects, and maintenance conditions when those matters are relevant under the policy. The insurer must document the basis for any reliance on those matters.

**B.4.7** An insurer may request records, photographs, inspections, or other information reasonably related to the claim. The request must be clear and must not impose unnecessary burdens on the insured.

**B.4.8** An insurer must evaluate evidence provided by the insured, the insurer’s representatives, and other persons with relevant knowledge. An insurer may not disregard relevant evidence without a reasonable basis.

**B.4.9** An insurer must ensure that an inspection is sufficient to evaluate the reported damage. An insurer must consider whether conditions affecting the roof require further review.

**B.4.10** An insurer must communicate material claim findings to the insured in plain language. The communication must explain the basis for a denial, limitation, or reservation of rights.

**B.4.11** An insurer may apply policy exclusions, conditions, deductibles, and limitations when supported by the policy and the claim facts. The insurer must identify the policy basis for its position.

**B.4.12** An insurer must not represent that roof age alone establishes that damage is excluded, preexisting, or unrelated to a reported loss. An insurer must evaluate the actual condition and cause of the claimed damage.

**B.4.13** An insurer must assess whether covered damage requires repair or replacement under the policy. The assessment must not rely on generalized assumptions about roofs of a particular age.

**B.4.14** An insurer must provide a claim payment when coverage and the amount owed have been determined. An insurer must not withhold payment to obtain information that is not material to the claim decision.

**B.4.15** An insurer must maintain claim notes and records sufficient to show the investigation, evaluation, and disposition of the claim. The records must support the insurer’s communications with the insured.

**B.4.16** An insurer must provide reasonable assistance to an insured seeking to understand a claim decision. An insurer must respond to material questions concerning coverage and the factual basis for the decision.

**B.4.17** An insurer may obtain the services of an adjuster, engineer, contractor, or other professional. The insurer remains responsible for ensuring that the claim is handled fairly and in accordance with the policy.

**B.4.18** An insurer must review information received after an initial claim decision when the information is material to coverage or damages. An insurer must revise its decision when warranted by the additional information.

**B.4.19** An insurer may issue a reservation of rights when coverage remains uncertain. The reservation must state the policy provisions and claim issues reasonably supporting the insurer’s position.

**B.4.20** An insurer must not use a claim investigation to pressure an insured to accept a nonrenewal decision. Claim communications and underwriting communications must remain clear as to their separate purposes.

**B.4.21** An insurer may elect not to renew a policy in accordance with applicable law and the policy terms. That decision does not relieve the insurer of its obligations concerning a pending or reported claim.

**B.4.22** An insurer must not condition claim handling on the insured’s agreement to repair, replace, or alter a roof except as permitted by the policy and applicable law. An insurer must explain any policy condition on which it relies.

**B.4.23** An insurer must train personnel responsible for claim handling to recognize the distinction between roof condition, roof age, and reported storm or accidental damage. Personnel must apply policy terms to the facts of the individual claim.

**B.4.24** An insurer must maintain procedures for supervisory review of claim denials and material limitations involving roof damage. The procedures must permit correction of errors identified during review.

**B.4.25** An insurer must handle claims consistently with representations made to the insured concerning coverage and claim handling. An insurer may not make misleading statements about the effect of roof age on a claim.

**B.4.26** An insurer must preserve the insured’s opportunity to provide information relevant to the claim. An insurer must consider that information before reaching a final decision when reasonably practicable.

**B.4.27** An insurer must administer this section in good faith and with due regard for the insured’s interests. Nothing in this section limits an insurer’s obligation to comply with applicable law and the policy.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** An insurer must file any form, rule, rate, rating factor, underwriting guideline, or other material that is required to be filed before use. The filing must clearly identify its application to residential property insurance.

**B.5.2** An insurer must describe how roof age is determined for underwriting, renewal, and nonrenewal purposes. The description must identify the records, inspections, or other information on which the insurer relies.

**B.5.3** An insurer must explain any roof-age eligibility standard that may affect an applicant or policyholder. The filing must state how the insurer applies that standard when roof information is unavailable, inconsistent, or disputed.

**B.5.4** An insurer must identify any distinction between new business, renewal business, and policies already in force. The filing must explain how the insurer will administer any change affecting existing policyholders.

**B.5.5** An insurer must provide the policy language, notice language, and underwriting materials that implement a roof-age requirement or nonrenewal practice. The materials must be consistent with the insurer's filed rules and with applicable law.

**B.5.6** An insurer must not use a roof-age criterion that is not supported by the filed material when a filing is required. An insurer must administer filed criteria uniformly, subject to lawful underwriting discretion stated in the filing.

**B.5.7** An insurer must provide a clear explanation of any inspection process used to evaluate roof condition or roof age. The filing must describe how the insurer addresses information supplied by the applicant or policyholder.

**B.5.8** An insurer must describe the circumstances under which a policy may be nonrenewed based on roof age or roof condition. The description must distinguish a nonrenewal decision from a cancellation decision.

**B.5.9** An insurer must ensure that any notice used in connection with a roof-related nonrenewal is clear and not misleading. The notice must state the basis for the action in terms a policyholder can understand.

**B.5.10** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with its filed underwriting practices. Those records must support the insurer's roof-age determination and any resulting nonrenewal action.

**B.5.11** An insurer must not implement a filing until the filing is approved or otherwise effective under applicable law. An insurer must use the approved or effective materials as filed.

**B.5.12** An insurer must promptly file any revision that materially changes its roof-age or nonrenewal practice when a filing is required. The revision must be approved or otherwise effective under applicable law before implementation.
