# Florida Office of Insurance Regulation Bulletin OIR-2023-04 — Roof Age and Nonrenewal

Issued 2023-04-11. Applies to personal residential property policies issued or renewed in Florida with an effective date on or after 2023-07-01.

## F.1 Purpose

This bulletin states the department's requirements governing an insurer's use of roof age as a basis for refusing to write or refusing to renew personal residential property coverage, and the circumstances under which an insurer may require a roof inspection or a roof deductible.

## F.2 Roof Age Alone Is Not a Permitted Basis

An insurer may not refuse to issue or refuse to renew a personal residential property policy solely because of the age of the roof, where the roof has a remaining useful life of five years or more as established by an inspection performed by an authorized inspector within the twelve months preceding the effective date.

An insurer that declines a risk on the basis of roof condition must state the specific deficiency identified, and may not state roof age alone as the reason.

## F.3 Inspection Rights

Where a roof is fifteen years of age or older, an insurer may require an inspection at the insurer's expense as a condition of issuance or renewal. Where the insured obtains and provides an inspection meeting the requirements of F.2, the insurer must accept it and may not require a second inspection at the insured's expense within the same policy period.

An insurer must accept an inspection performed by an inspector licensed in this state and must not restrict acceptance to inspectors of the insurer's own selection.

## F.4 Roof Deductibles and Settlement

An insurer may offer a separate roof deductible or an actual cash value roof settlement schedule, but only where the insured is offered a policy without that provision at a filed and approved rate, and where the difference in premium between the two is disclosed in writing at the time of offer.

An insurer may not apply an actual cash value roof settlement schedule to a roof less than ten years of age at the effective date of the policy.

## F.5 Notice of Nonrenewal

An insurer must give at least one hundred twenty days written notice before nonrenewing a personal residential property policy, and that notice must state the specific reason. Where the stated reason relates to roof condition, the notice must include a copy of any inspection report the insurer relied upon.

## F.6 Hurricane Deductible Unaffected

Nothing in this bulletin limits an insurer's application of a hurricane deductible as otherwise permitted by law. A hurricane deductible and a separate roof deductible may not both be applied to the same loss; where both would apply, only the larger is deducted.

## F.7 Reporting

An insurer must report annually the number of policies nonrenewed for reasons relating to roof condition, disaggregated by county, on the form prescribed by the office.
