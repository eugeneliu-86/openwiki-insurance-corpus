# New York Bulletin DFS-2016-02 — Nonrenewal and Cancellation Notice Requirements




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This section states notice requirements governing cancellation and nonrenewal by an admitted insurer. It applies to insurance coverage issued or delivered for risks located in New York.

**B.1.2** An insurer must administer cancellation and nonrenewal practices in a manner consistent with this section. An insurer may not use a notice practice that impairs a policyholder's ability to understand the insurer's action.

**B.1.3** This section applies to notices issued by an insurer, including notices issued through a managing general agent, producer, administrator, or other person acting for the insurer. The insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section.

**B.1.4** A cancellation notice is a notice that terminates coverage before the expiration of the policy term. A nonrenewal notice is a notice that advises that coverage will not be continued after expiration of the policy term.

**B.1.5** An insurer must distinguish clearly between cancellation and nonrenewal in each notice. The stated reason for the insurer's action must correspond to the action identified in the notice.

**B.1.6** This section applies whether coverage is written on an individual, group, commercial, personal, or other basis. An insurer must apply the requirements according to the coverage at issue and the circumstances of the proposed action.

**B.1.7** An insurer must provide required notices to the policyholder and to any other person entitled to receive notice. The insurer must use an address or delivery method permitted for the applicable policy and notice.

**B.1.8** A notice must be clear, accurate, and sufficiently specific to disclose the insurer's intended action. An insurer may not rely on vague, misleading, or inconsistent language to explain cancellation or nonrenewal.

**B.1.9** An insurer must retain records demonstrating that a required notice was prepared and transmitted. The records must permit the Department to evaluate the content, timing, and method of delivery of the notice.

**B.1.10** This section applies to notices generated by automated systems as well as notices prepared manually. An insurer must maintain controls appropriate to ensure that automated notice processes comply with this section.

**B.1.11** An insurer must ensure that notice language is consistent with the policy provisions and underwriting action involved. A notice may not state a ground for cancellation or nonrenewal that differs from the insurer's actual basis for action.

**B.1.12** An insurer may provide information concerning a policyholder's rights, available options, or means of obtaining further information. Such information may not obscure, contradict, or diminish the required notice.

**B.1.13** An insurer must apply this section to a policyholder whose coverage is being cancelled or nonrenewed, regardless of the channel through which the policy was obtained. Use of a producer does not relieve the insurer of its notice obligations.

**B.1.14** This section does not establish or revise the all-other-perils deductible minimum, coinsurance threshold, or ALE percentage. An insurer must address those matters in accordance with the applicable policy terms and requirements.

**B.1.15** An insurer must use notice procedures that are reasonably designed to identify the correct policy, insured, coverage, and proposed action. A notice containing a material error may not be treated as compliant.

**B.1.16** An insurer must address each policy action separately when separate actions require separate notices. An insurer may combine information only when the resulting notice remains clear and complies with this section.

**B.1.17** This section does not limit an insurer's obligation to comply with any more protective notice requirement applicable to the policy. Where requirements differ, an insurer must provide the notice that affords the policyholder the greater protection.

**B.1.18** An insurer must train personnel responsible for underwriting, policy administration, claims administration, and customer communications regarding the requirements of this section. An insurer must maintain procedures for correcting identified notice errors.

**B.1.19** An insurer must review its forms, systems, and practices to ensure that cancellation and nonrenewal notices remain compliant. A change in policy language, underwriting practice, or delivery method must be evaluated for its effect on notice compliance.

**B.1.20** This section takes effect upon issuance and applies prospectively to cancellation and nonrenewal actions initiated by an insurer. An insurer must implement the requirements before issuing a notice subject to this section.

**B.1.21** An insurer may adopt procedures that provide protections greater than those described in this section. Such procedures may not reduce a policyholder's rights or the insurer's obligations.

**B.1.22** The Department may review an insurer's notices, records, forms, and related procedures for compliance with this section. An insurer must make responsive information available upon request.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer must comply with these requirements when issuing a cancellation notice or a nonrenewal notice for coverage issued in this State. These requirements apply to an admitted insurer and to a producer acting on the insurer's behalf.

**B.2.2** An insurer must apply these requirements prospectively from their effective date. A notice issued before the effective date remains subject to the requirements applicable when that notice was issued.

**B.2.3** An insurer must provide a cancellation notice when it seeks to terminate coverage before the expiration of the current coverage term. An insurer must provide a nonrenewal notice when it elects not to continue coverage at the end of the current coverage term.

**B.2.4** An insurer may not characterize a cancellation as a nonrenewal when the coverage would otherwise remain in effect through the current coverage term. An insurer must identify the action accurately in the notice.

**B.2.5** An insurer must send the notice to the named insured at the address maintained for the coverage. An insurer must also provide the notice to any person entitled to receive it under applicable law or the coverage terms.

**B.2.6** An insurer must use a method of delivery that provides reliable evidence of transmission or mailing. The insurer must retain evidence sufficient to establish that the notice was sent.

**B.2.7** An insurer may use electronic delivery only when electronic delivery is permitted and the insured has provided any consent required for that delivery. The insurer must retain evidence of the applicable consent and delivery.

**B.2.8** An insurer must state the effective date of the cancellation or nonrenewal clearly and prominently. The effective date stated in the notice must be consistent with the insurer's intended action.

**B.2.9** An insurer must state the reason for cancellation or nonrenewal in clear language. The reason may not be so general that the insured cannot understand the basis for the action.

**B.2.10** An insurer must ensure that the stated reason is supported by the information available to the insurer when the notice is issued. An insurer may not use a reason that is inaccurate, misleading, or unsupported.

**B.2.11** An insurer must identify the applicable coverage sufficiently for the insured to determine which coverage is affected. The notice must not create uncertainty concerning whether other coverage remains in force.

**B.2.12** An insurer must use language that is understandable to a reasonable insured. Technical terms may be used when needed, but the notice must explain their meaning when clarification is necessary.

**B.2.13** An insurer must not rely on internal codes, abbreviations, or underwriting labels as the sole explanation for the action. Any internal designation included in the notice must be accompanied by a plain-language explanation.

**B.2.14** An insurer must provide information concerning any action the insured may take to avoid cancellation when the insurer permits cure of the stated condition. The notice must describe the condition to be cured without ambiguity.

**B.2.15** An insurer may not represent that cancellation is unavoidable if the insurer will accept corrective action that would allow coverage to remain in effect. The notice must accurately describe the insurer's position.

**B.2.16** An insurer must provide contact information through which the insured may request an explanation of the action. The contact information must enable the insured to reach personnel able to address the notice.

**B.2.17** An insurer must handle inquiries concerning cancellation and nonrenewal notices promptly and consistently. An insurer may not provide an explanation to the insured that conflicts with the reason stated in the notice.

**B.2.18** An insurer must maintain procedures to confirm that a proposed cancellation or nonrenewal is authorized before the notice is issued. Those procedures must address the review of the reason, the affected coverage, and the effective date.

**B.2.19** An insurer must ensure that personnel responsible for issuing notices are trained to apply these requirements. The insurer must supervise its producers and service providers when they perform notice-related functions.

**B.2.20** An insurer must maintain records supporting its cancellation and nonrenewal decisions. The records must be sufficient to demonstrate the reason for the action and the manner in which notice was provided.

**B.2.21** An insurer must make relevant records available to the Department upon request. The insurer must provide records in a form that permits the Department to evaluate compliance with these requirements.

**B.2.22** An insurer may not issue a notice based on prohibited discrimination. An insurer must ensure that its cancellation and nonrenewal practices are applied in a manner consistent with applicable law.

**B.2.23** An insurer must apply underwriting criteria consistently to similarly situated insureds, subject to lawful distinctions supported by risk-related information. An insurer must be able to explain material differences in treatment.

**B.2.24** An insurer must ensure that carrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exceptioncarrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exception (see Personal Lines Underwriting Manual, R530 Rule 530 — New York State Exceptions) is consistent with these requirements. An insurer may not apply an underwriting exception in a manner that results in an inaccurate or deficient notice.

**B.2.25** An insurer must review information received after issuance of a notice when that information materially affects the stated basis for cancellation or nonrenewal. The insurer must take appropriate corrective action when the notice is no longer accurate.

**B.2.26** An insurer that withdraws a cancellation or nonrenewal must communicate the withdrawal clearly to the insured. The communication must identify the affected coverage and state that the prior action has been withdrawn.

**B.2.27** An insurer must not require an insured to infer the status of coverage from a billing communication, claim communication, or other routine correspondence. The cancellation or nonrenewal notice must stand on its own.

**B.2.28** An insurer must ensure that any premium-related reason is described accurately. The notice must distinguish a failure to satisfy an obligation from an insurer decision based on underwriting.

**B.2.29** An insurer must ensure that any claim-related reason is described accurately and in a manner that does not disclose unnecessary personal information. The insurer must protect information concerning claimants and other affected persons.

**B.2.30** An insurer must ensure that any property-related reason identifies the condition or circumstance supporting the action. The notice may not rely solely on a conclusory statement that the risk is unacceptable.

**B.2.31** An insurer must ensure that any information obtained from a third party is reasonably verified before it is used as the basis for cancellation or nonrenewal. An insurer must correct the action if material information is determined to be unreliable.

**B.2.32** An insurer must use procedures designed to prevent duplicate, conflicting, or premature notices. When conflicting notices are issued, the insurer must provide prompt clarification to the insured.

**B.2.33** An insurer must ensure that a notice is issued by or with the authority of the insurer responsible for the coverage. A producer may not issue a notice that misstates the insurer's decision or authority.

**B.2.34** An insurer must not alter a notice in a manner that changes its meaning without adequate review. Any revised notice must accurately state the insurer's action and the basis for that action.

**B.2.35** An insurer must ensure that translated communications, when provided, are consistent with the controlling notice. The insurer must correct a material inconsistency promptly.

**B.2.36** An insurer must protect the confidentiality of personal information used in the cancellation or nonrenewal process. Access to that information must be limited to persons with a legitimate business need.

**B.2.37** An insurer must maintain controls designed to identify notices sent to an incorrect recipient or address. Upon discovery of an error, the insurer must evaluate whether corrective notice is required.

**B.2.38** An insurer must not use a cancellation or nonrenewal notice to pressure an insured into purchasing unrelated coverage or services. The notice must be limited to information relevant to the affected coverage and the insurer's action.

**B.2.39** An insurer must ensure that notice templates and automated systems produce notices that comply with these requirements. The insurer must test and monitor those systems for material errors.

**B.2.40** An insurer must investigate material complaints concerning cancellation or nonrenewal notices. The insurer must take corrective action when the investigation identifies a deficiency.

**B.2.41** An insurer must correct systemic deficiencies identified through complaint handling, quality review, examination, or internal monitoring. Corrective action must be reasonably designed to prevent recurrence.

**B.2.42** An insurer's compliance with these requirements does not limit any obligation imposed by applicable law. An insurer must comply with the requirement that provides the greater protection to the insured when requirements differ.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** An insurer must apply this section to any notice of nonrenewal or cancellation issued to a policyholder. An insurer must provide the notice in writing and in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the policyholder.

**B.3.2** An insurer must provide notice of nonrenewal at least sixty (60) days before the policy expires. The notice must identify the policy to which the nonrenewal applies.

**B.3.3** An insurer must provide notice of cancellation for a reason other than nonpayment of premium at least twenty (20) days before the cancellation becomes effective. The notice must clearly state the effective date of cancellation.

**B.3.4** An insurer must provide notice of cancellation for nonpayment of premium at least fifteen (15) days before the cancellation becomes effective. The notice must identify the unpaid premium obligation giving rise to the cancellation.

**B.3.5** An insurer must address a notice to the named insured at the address shown in the insurer's records. An insurer must use any additional address required by the policy or applicable law.

**B.3.6** An insurer must state whether the notice is a notice of nonrenewal or a notice of cancellation. The subject of the notice must not be obscured by unrelated information.

**B.3.7** An insurer must state the policy number in the notice. The insurer may also include another identifier reasonably necessary to assist the policyholder in identifying the policy.

**B.3.8** An insurer must state the name of the insurer issuing the notice. The notice must provide a mailing address or other contact information through which the policyholder may communicate with the insurer.

**B.3.9** An insurer must state the effective date of the proposed nonrenewal or cancellation. The date stated in the notice must be consistent with the insurer's policy records.

**B.3.10** An insurer must state the reason for a nonrenewal or cancellation with sufficient specificity to permit the policyholder to understand the basis for the action. A conclusory statement without supporting explanation is not sufficient.

**B.3.11** An insurer may describe the factual circumstances supporting the stated reason. The description must be accurate and must not mislead the policyholder concerning the insurer's action.

**B.3.12** An insurer must identify any action the policyholder may take to avoid cancellation when the insurer permits cure of the stated condition. The notice must describe the action in plain language.

**B.3.13** An insurer must identify the premium amount claimed to be unpaid when cancellation is based on nonpayment of premium. The notice must state how the policyholder may submit payment if payment may prevent cancellation.

**B.3.14** An insurer must not characterize a policy as cancelled before the stated cancellation date. An insurer may explain that coverage will terminate on the stated date if the cancellation takes effect.

**B.3.15** An insurer must not characterize a policy as nonrenewed before expiration of the current policy term. An insurer may explain that the insurer does not intend to offer continuation of coverage after expiration.

**B.3.16** An insurer must use language that is reasonably understandable to the policyholder. Technical terms used in the notice must be explained when explanation is necessary to avoid confusion.

**B.3.17** An insurer must not include a statement that contradicts the policy records or the stated reason for the action. An insurer must correct a material error in a notice without undue delay.

**B.3.18** An insurer may issue a corrected notice when an earlier notice contains an error. A corrected notice must clearly identify the correction and must not reduce any notice protection otherwise available to the policyholder.

**B.3.19** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate the content of each notice issued. The records must identify the policyholder, policy, reason for the action, and method of delivery.

**B.3.20** An insurer must maintain evidence showing when a notice was sent. The evidence must be reasonably capable of verification upon request by the department.

**B.3.21** An insurer must use a delivery method that provides the notice to the policyholder in accordance with applicable requirements. An insurer may use an authorized electronic delivery method when the policyholder has consented to that method.

**B.3.22** An insurer using electronic delivery must ensure that the notice remains accessible in a form capable of being retained by the policyholder. Electronic delivery must not impair any right the policyholder has to receive notice by another required method.

**B.3.23** An insurer must provide the notice in the name of the insurer responsible for the policy action. A producer or other representative may assist with delivery, but the insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section.

**B.3.24** An insurer must ensure that a notice issued by a managing general agent, third-party administrator, or other service provider satisfies this section. Delegation of notice functions does not relieve the insurer of responsibility.

**B.3.25** An insurer must not use a notice format that is likely to cause a policyholder to mistake a cancellation notice for a billing statement or marketing communication. The notice must be reasonably prominent and readily identifiable.

**B.3.26** An insurer may include information concerning replacement coverage or assistance available to the policyholder. Such information must not be presented in a manner that obscures the required notice.

**B.3.27** An insurer must provide contact information for a person or unit able to respond to questions concerning the notice. The contact information must permit the policyholder to seek clarification of the stated reason and effective date.

**B.3.28** An insurer must treat a request for explanation of a notice in good faith. A response by the insurer must be consistent with the notice and the insurer's records.

**B.3.29** An insurer must apply its notice procedures consistently to similarly situated policyholders. An insurer must not use notice practices that result in misleading or discriminatory treatment.

**B.3.30** An insurer must ensure that the reason stated in the notice reflects the actual basis for the action. An insurer must not substitute a different reason after issuance unless the insurer provides an appropriate corrected notice.

**B.3.31** An insurer must review notice templates and automated notice procedures for compliance with this section. The review must address the accuracy of policy information, effective dates, and stated reasons.

**B.3.32** An insurer must train personnel responsible for issuing or reviewing notices regarding the requirements of this section. The insurer must maintain procedures designed to prevent issuance of inaccurate or incomplete notices.

**B.3.33** An insurer must make notice records available to the department upon request. Records provided to the department must be complete and legible.

**B.3.34** An insurer must preserve any record of a policyholder's consent to electronic delivery when electronic delivery is used. The record must identify the scope of the consent.

**B.3.35** An insurer must ensure that a notice sent to a policyholder with a known representative also complies with any applicable direction concerning notice to that representative. Notice to a representative does not replace notice to the policyholder unless authorized.

**B.3.36** An insurer must not rely on an oral communication as a substitute for the written notice required by this section. An insurer may communicate orally with a policyholder in addition to providing the required written notice.

**B.3.37** An insurer must implement these requirements for notices issued on or after the effective date of this bulletin. A notice issued before that date remains subject to the requirements applicable when it was issued.

**B.3.38** An insurer that fails to comply with this section may be subject to review by the department. Compliance with this section does not limit any other obligation imposed on an insurer.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that issues a cancellation or nonrenewal notice based in whole or in part on claims information. An insurer must apply these requirements to the insurer’s underwriting, claims, and notice practices.

**B.4.2** An insurer must maintain written standards governing the use of claims information in cancellation and nonrenewal decisions. The standards must be applied consistently to similarly situated insureds.

**B.4.3** An insurer may consider claims information only when the information is relevant, accurate, and reasonably related to the underwriting decision. An insurer must not rely on claims information that the insurer knows, or should know, is incomplete or inaccurate.

**B.4.4** A cancellation or nonrenewal notice based on claims information must state the specific reason for the action in clear language. A general statement that the action is based on claims experience, loss history, or underwriting standards is not sufficient.

**B.4.5** The reason required by **B.4.4** must identify the claims circumstance on which the insurer relied without disclosing unnecessary personal information. An insurer must describe the basis for the decision with enough detail to permit the insured to understand the insurer’s action.

**B.4.6** An insurer must distinguish an actual claim from an inquiry, report, or request for information that did not result in a claim. An insurer must not treat an inquiry alone as a claim unless the insurer’s records establish that a claim was opened and evaluated.

**B.4.7** An insurer must verify the claims information supporting a cancellation or nonrenewal decision before issuing the notice. Verification must include review of the insurer’s available records and any material information provided by the insured.

**B.4.8** An insurer must not base a cancellation or nonrenewal decision on a claim that was reported in error, withdrawn, or attributed to the wrong insured. An insurer must correct its records when it determines that claims information is inaccurate.

**B.4.9** An insurer must not characterize a claim as adverse solely because the claim was reported. The insurer must consider the nature of the claim and the information reasonably available concerning the loss.

**B.4.10** An insurer must not rely on a claim denial as a basis for cancellation or nonrenewal unless the insurer has separately determined that the facts underlying the claim support the underwriting action. The notice must state the underwriting basis rather than merely stating that a claim was denied.

**B.4.11** An insurer must ensure that personnel authorized to approve cancellation or nonrenewal decisions have access to the information supporting the decision. An insurer must provide reasonable oversight of decisions based on claims information.

**B.4.12** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section. The records must include the claims information reviewed, the underwriting reason for the action, and the notice issued to the insured.

**B.4.13** An insurer must provide a meaningful response when an insured disputes claims information identified in a cancellation or nonrenewal notice. The insurer must investigate the dispute and correct the notice or underwriting record when appropriate.

**B.4.14** When an insurer determines that a notice was based on inaccurate claims information, the insurer must take appropriate corrective action without undue delay. Corrective action may include withdrawal of the notice, reinstatement of coverage, or other action necessary to restore the insured’s position.

**B.4.15** An insurer must not use notice language that is false, misleading, or likely to obscure the actual claims basis for the action. An insurer must ensure that any explanation given by an agent or producer is consistent with the notice.

**B.4.16** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section when claims information is obtained from an affiliate, vendor, producer, or other source. An insurer must use reasonable procedures to assess the reliability of information received from another source.

**B.4.17** An insurer must not apply claims-based underwriting standards in a manner that results in arbitrary or unfair treatment of an insured. An insurer must administer its standards in accordance with the facts of each underwriting file.

**B.4.18** An insurer may use claims information for legitimate underwriting purposes when the insurer complies with this section. Nothing in this section requires an insurer to provide coverage that the insurer is not otherwise required to provide.

**B.4.19** An insurer must ensure that its forms, manuals, and internal procedures do not conflict with the requirements of this section. A practice that is permitted by an internal procedure does not excuse noncompliance with this section.

**B.4.20** These requirements apply to cancellation and nonrenewal notices issued on or after the effective date of this section. An insurer must comply with this section for as long as this section remains in effect.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that issues or delivers a policy in this State and uses a notice of cancellation or nonrenewal. An insurer must comply with this section before using a notice subject to filing.

**B.5.2** An insurer must file each notice form required by this section with the Department before the form is used. A filing must identify the insurer and the policy type for which the notice will be used.

**B.5.3** An insurer must file a notice form in the format prescribed by the Department. An insurer must provide a complete and legible copy of the proposed notice.

**B.5.4** An insurer must include all policyholder-facing language that will appear in the notice submitted for filing. An insurer may not omit variable text, instructions, or explanatory language from a filing.

**B.5.5** An insurer must identify each field that will be completed when the notice is issued. The filing must explain how the insurer will populate each such field.

**B.5.6** An insurer must file a revised notice before using the revised notice. A revision includes any change that affects the content, appearance, or operation of the notice.

**B.5.7** An insurer may use a notice that differs from a filed notice only when the difference is limited to information specific to the policyholder or policy. Such information must be completed in a manner consistent with the filed notice.

**B.5.8** An insurer must ensure that a filed notice remains consistent with the insurer's policy forms and underwriting practices. An insurer must revise a notice when a change makes the filed notice inaccurate or misleading.

**B.5.9** A filing must state whether the notice will be provided by mail, electronic means, or another permitted method. An insurer must ensure that the delivery method used is authorized for the policyholder and the policy.

**B.5.10** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section. The records must include the notice form used and the information completed for the policyholder.

**B.5.11** An insurer must make filing and notice records available to the Department upon request. An insurer must provide the records in a form that permits the Department to review compliance with this section.

**B.5.12** Department review of a filing does not relieve an insurer of responsibility for compliance with this section. An insurer remains responsible for the accuracy and legal sufficiency of each notice issued.

**B.5.13** An insurer must correct a notice practice that the Department determines does not comply with this section. The insurer must take corrective action under the Department's direction.

**B.5.14** An insurer may not use a notice that is false, misleading, incomplete, or inconsistent with this section. A notice must clearly state the action affecting the policy and the basis for that action.

**B.5.15** This section is effective as of the effective date of this bulletin. An insurer must apply this section to notices issued on or after that effective date.

**B.5.16** An insurer must continue to comply with this section unless the Department amends, replaces, or withdraws this bulletin. Compliance with this section is subject to Department examination and enforcement.
