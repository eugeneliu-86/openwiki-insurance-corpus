# New York Bulletin DFS-2018-11 — Annual Homeowners Data Call




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** The purpose of this section is to establish the applicability of the Department’s homeowners data call to admitted insurers. The data call supports the Department’s review of homeowners insurance business written or serviced in New York.

**B.1.2** This section applies to an insurer authorized to write homeowners insurance in New York that writes, renews, services, administers, or otherwise maintains homeowners insurance business subject to Department oversight. An insurer subject to this section must provide information responsive to the Department’s homeowners data call.

**B.1.3** Applicability is determined by the insurer’s homeowners insurance activity and not solely by the insurer’s description of its business. An insurer may not avoid applicability by using an affiliate, administrator, managing agent, or other service provider to perform homeowners insurance functions.

**B.1.4** An insurer remains responsible for the completeness and accuracy of information submitted on its behalf. The use of a third party does not relieve an insurer of its obligations under this section.

**B.1.5** An insurer must report information on a legal-entity basis unless the Department permits another basis for reporting. Information for affiliated insurers must remain identifiable to the insurer responsible for the applicable homeowners insurance business.

**B.1.6** This section applies to homeowners insurance business regardless of the manner in which the insurer markets, distributes, underwrites, issues, renews, or services the coverage. The section also applies where homeowners insurance functions are performed through electronic, direct, agency, or other distribution methods.

**B.1.7** An insurer must use records reasonably available to it to prepare a complete response. An insurer must maintain sufficient supporting records to explain information submitted to the Department.

**B.1.8** Information submitted under this section must be accurate, internally consistent, and reasonably reconciled to the insurer’s relevant business records. An insurer must correct an identified material error promptly.

**B.1.9** The Department may use information submitted under this section to evaluate homeowners insurance availability, underwriting practices, coverage characteristics, claims experience, and consumer impact. Submission of information does not limit the Department’s authority to request clarification or supporting material.

**B.1.10** This section does not establish coverage terms or require an insurer to offer a particular policy feature. This section also does not establish a minimum Section I deductible, an eighty percent condition, or an additional living expense limit.

**B.1.11** An insurer must apply a reasonable and consistent approach when identifying homeowners insurance business for purposes of the data call. An insurer must be able to explain that approach upon Department request.

**B.1.12** An insurer may use information maintained by a service provider if the insurer remains able to verify the information. An insurer must take reasonable steps to protect confidential policyholder and claimant information while preparing its response.

**B.1.13** The requirements of this section apply to information concerning policies, risks, claims, and other homeowners insurance activity within the scope of the Department’s data call. An insurer must not omit responsive information because the information is maintained in a system outside its primary reporting system.

**B.1.14** An insurer must provide information in good faith and must not submit information that is misleading through omission, classification, aggregation, or presentation. An insurer must identify any limitation that materially affects the interpretation of submitted information.

**B.1.15** An insurer may seek Department clarification concerning the applicability of this section where its homeowners insurance operations present an unusual circumstance. Pending clarification, the insurer must preserve information that may be responsive to the data call.

**B.1.16** The obligations in this section apply when an insurer is within the applicability described in **B.1.2** and remain applicable while the insurer is required to respond to the Department’s homeowners data call. An insurer must comply with this section as directed by the Department.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** This section applies to each admitted insurer that writes homeowners insurance in New York. An insurer must comply with the Department’s data call requirements when directed by the Department.

**B.2.2** An insurer must submit complete and accurate data through the method designated by the Department. An insurer may not substitute estimates for required data unless the Department authorizes their use.

**B.2.3** An insurer must identify the legal name under which it is admitted to transact insurance in New York. An insurer must also identify any assumed name used in connection with the reported business.

**B.2.4** An insurer must provide contact information for an individual authorized to respond to Department inquiries concerning the submission. The designated individual must be able to explain the source and preparation of the reported data.

**B.2.5** An insurer must certify that its submission is complete and accurate to the best of its knowledge. The certification must be made by an officer or other person authorized to bind the insurer.

**B.2.6** An insurer must maintain records supporting each item reported in the data call. Supporting records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.2.7** An insurer must use data derived from its books, records, systems, and files. An insurer must reconcile reported data to its internal records before submission.

**B.2.8** An insurer must report business written directly by the insurer. An insurer must separately identify business assumed from or ceded to another insurer when the Department requires that information.

**B.2.9** An insurer must report data for homeowners insurance issued, renewed, or otherwise maintained in New York. An insurer may not include business that is outside the scope of the Department’s data call.

**B.2.10** An insurer must classify each policy using the classifications required by the Department. An insurer must apply those classifications consistently throughout its submission.

**B.2.11** An insurer must report policy data according to the location of the insured risk. An insurer may not allocate policy data based solely on the location of an office, producer, or servicing unit.

**B.2.12** An insurer must report exposure information in the format required by the Department. An insurer must use the same exposure basis for related premium and loss information unless the Department directs otherwise.

**B.2.13** An insurer must report written premium in accordance with its accounting records. An insurer must clearly identify adjustments that affect reported premium.

**B.2.14** An insurer must report earned premium when required by the Department. An insurer must apply its established accounting practices consistently in calculating earned premium.

**B.2.15** An insurer must report claims information using records maintained in the ordinary course of business. An insurer must not exclude claims because payment has not been made.

**B.2.16** An insurer must report paid losses in accordance with its claim records. An insurer must distinguish loss payments from expense payments when the Department requires that distinction.

**B.2.17** An insurer must report outstanding loss reserves using amounts recorded in its claim systems. An insurer must identify reserve information that includes amounts for claims not yet reported.

**B.2.18** An insurer must report loss adjustment expense when required by the Department. An insurer must use a reasonable and consistently applied method to allocate expense information.

**B.2.19** An insurer must report salvage and subrogation recoveries in the manner required by the Department. An insurer must not report recoveries as negative claim counts.

**B.2.20** An insurer must report claim closures using its established claim handling records. An insurer must apply the same closure standard to all reported claim data.

**B.2.21** An insurer must report claim denials when required by the Department. An insurer must retain records supporting the reason for each reported denial.

**B.2.22** An insurer must report policy cancellations in the manner required by the Department. An insurer must maintain records supporting the reason for each reported cancellation.

**B.2.23** An insurer must report whether its policy forms address a cancellation notice period. An insurer must accurately identify the form provisions applicable to the reported business.

**B.2.24** An insurer must report whether its policy forms address a notice of nonrenewal. An insurer must not characterize a cancellation as a nonrenewal or a nonrenewal as a cancellation.

**B.2.25** An insurer must report policy terminations using the terminology required by the Department. An insurer must ensure that policy administration records support the reported termination classification.

**B.2.26** An insurer must report underwriting action information when required by the Department. An insurer must use records that identify the basis for the underwriting action.

**B.2.27** An insurer must report property characteristics only as required by the Department. An insurer must use information available from its underwriting or policy records.

**B.2.28** An insurer must report mitigation information when required by the Department. An insurer must not report a mitigation feature unless it is supported by the insurer’s records.

**B.2.29** An insurer must report deductible information in the format required by the Department. An insurer must distinguish deductible information from policy limits and other coverage terms.

**B.2.30** An insurer must report coverage information using the policy form or endorsement that governs the coverage. An insurer must retain records sufficient to identify the applicable form language.

**B.2.31** An insurer must report whether its policy forms address a proof of loss period. An insurer must accurately identify the policy provision governing that requirement.

**B.2.32** An insurer must report whether its policy forms address a special assessment limit. An insurer must distinguish that provision from other limits that may apply to covered property.

**B.2.33** An insurer must report endorsements that materially affect reported coverage information. An insurer must not omit an endorsement solely because it is issued separately from the base policy.

**B.2.34** An insurer must report exclusions when required by the Department. An insurer must identify exclusions using the insurer’s policy form records.

**B.2.35** An insurer must report claim cause information using its established claim coding practices. An insurer must provide an explanation of any coding practice that materially affects the reported data.

**B.2.36** An insurer must report catastrophe-related information when required by the Department. An insurer must use records that reasonably identify claims associated with the reported event.

**B.2.37** An insurer must report geographic information in the format specified by the Department. An insurer must validate geographic information against the location of the insured risk.

**B.2.38** An insurer must report data separately where the Department requires separate reporting. An insurer may not combine categories that the Department requires to be distinguished.

**B.2.39** An insurer must report data for affiliates separately when the Department requires separate insurer reporting. An insurer may not report group data in place of insurer-specific data unless authorized by the Department.

**B.2.40** An insurer that uses a managing general agent, administrator, or other service provider remains responsible for compliance with this section. An insurer must ensure that data supplied by a service provider is complete and accurate.

**B.2.41** An insurer must establish controls reasonably designed to identify missing, duplicate, or inconsistent data. An insurer must correct identified errors before submitting its data.

**B.2.42** An insurer must review material variances in reported data against its internal records. An insurer must be able to explain a material variance to the Department upon request.

**B.2.43** An insurer must preserve the methodology used to prepare its submission. The methodology must identify significant assumptions, allocations, and data transformations.

**B.2.44** An insurer must document any use of estimated data authorized by the Department. The documentation must explain why complete actual data was not available.

**B.2.45** An insurer must notify the Department promptly if it discovers a material error in a submitted response. An insurer must submit corrected information in the manner directed by the Department.

**B.2.46** An insurer must identify a corrected submission as a correction. An insurer must describe the nature of the correction and the data affected.

**B.2.47** An insurer must respond to a Department request for clarification concerning its submission. An insurer must provide records, explanations, or revised data requested by the Department.

**B.2.48** An insurer must protect nonpublic information used to prepare the data call response. An insurer must provide information to the Department through a secure method when directed by the Department.

**B.2.49** An insurer may use an affiliate or service provider to prepare a submission. The insurer remains responsible for the completeness, accuracy, and timely delivery of the submission.

**B.2.50** An insurer must not alter data to avoid a reporting requirement. An insurer must not omit information that is responsive to a required field.

**B.2.51** An insurer must not report data in a manner that is misleading or materially incomplete. An insurer must ensure that narrative explanations are consistent with reported data.

**B.2.52** An insurer must retain records sufficient to permit the Department to verify each reported item. The retained records must include source data and relevant reconciliation materials.

**B.2.53** An insurer must make personnel knowledgeable about the submission available to the Department upon request. An insurer must ensure that those personnel can explain the insurer’s reporting practices.

**B.2.54** An insurer must comply with Department instructions concerning submission validation. An insurer must address identified validation errors before the Department accepts the submission.

**B.2.55** An insurer must use the Department’s prescribed definitions when definitions are provided. An insurer must not apply an internal definition that conflicts with a Department definition.

**B.2.56** An insurer must report no data for a required category only when no responsive business or activity exists. An insurer must not use a blank field where the Department requires an affirmative response.

**B.2.57** An insurer must explain data that appears unusual, incomplete, or inconsistent when requested by the Department. An explanation must be supported by the insurer’s records.

**B.2.58** An insurer must comply with this section regardless of whether it has delegated policy administration or claim handling functions. Delegation does not relieve the insurer of responsibility for the submission.

**B.2.59** An insurer must treat compliance with this section as a condition of its participation in the Department’s data call. The Department may require additional information necessary to evaluate the submitted data.

**B.2.60** An insurer that fails to comply with this section may be subject to Department action. Compliance with this section does not limit any authority of the Department under applicable law.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** This notice applies to each admitted insurer authorized to write homeowners insurance in this State. An insurer must provide the notices and disclosures required by this paragraph in connection with the annual homeowners data call.

**B.3.2** This notice is effective upon issuance. An insurer must apply this notice to data submitted after the effective date.

**B.3.3** An insurer must identify a responsible officer or employee who may receive communications from the Department concerning the data call. The identified person must have authority to coordinate the insurer’s response.

**B.3.4** An insurer must provide current contact information for the person responsible for the submission. The insurer must promptly notify the Department if that contact information changes.

**B.3.5** An insurer must disclose whether the submission was prepared by the insurer, an affiliate, a managing general agent, a vendor, or another person acting for the insurer. The insurer remains responsible for the completeness and accuracy of all information submitted.

**B.3.6** An insurer must disclose any material limitation affecting the information reported. A disclosure must describe the nature of the limitation in plain language.

**B.3.7** An insurer must disclose any material change in its underwriting, rating, claims, policy administration, or data-management practices that affects reported information. The disclosure must permit the Department to understand the effect of the change on comparability.

**B.3.8** An insurer must disclose when reported information reflects an estimate, allocation, approximation, or other derived value. The insurer must describe the method used to develop the reported value.

**B.3.9** An insurer must disclose when a reported value is unavailable. The insurer must explain the reason the value is unavailable and identify any information used as a substitute.

**B.3.10** An insurer must disclose when information has been restated from a prior submission. The disclosure must describe the reason for the restatement and the affected reporting elements.

**B.3.11** An insurer must not represent that information is final if the insurer knows that the information is subject to material revision. An insurer must notify the Department promptly when it identifies a material revision.

**B.3.12** An insurer must disclose whether reported information includes business written through an affiliate or other entity. The disclosure must identify the relationship of the entity to the insurer.

**B.3.13** An insurer must disclose whether reported information excludes business administered, serviced, or handled by another person. The insurer must explain the basis for the exclusion.

**B.3.14** An insurer must disclose the treatment of policies that are issued, renewed, cancelled, nonrenewed, rescinded, or otherwise terminated. The disclosure must be consistent with the insurer’s ordinary business records.

**B.3.15** An insurer must disclose the treatment of endorsements, amendments, and policy changes that affect reported information. The insurer must describe any method used to assign those changes.

**B.3.16** An insurer must disclose the treatment of claims that are reopened, adjusted, transferred, closed, or otherwise revised. The disclosure must identify any material effect on the reported claim information.

**B.3.17** An insurer must disclose whether losses, expenses, recoveries, or other claim amounts are reported on a paid, incurred, case-reserve, or other basis. The insurer must apply the disclosed basis consistently throughout the submission.

**B.3.18** An insurer must disclose the treatment of salvage, subrogation, reinsurance, deductibles, and other recoveries. The disclosure must permit the Department to evaluate the reported loss information.

**B.3.19** An insurer must disclose the basis used to identify the location of insured property. An insurer must not use a location method that materially obscures geographic information.

**B.3.20** An insurer must disclose the basis used to classify homeowners business for reporting purposes. The classification method must be reasonably consistent with the insurer’s underwriting and policy records.

**B.3.21** An insurer must disclose any use of external data sources, mapping tools, predictive models, or vendor-generated information that materially affects the submission. The disclosure must identify the general purpose of the external information.

**B.3.22** An insurer must disclose any material data conversion, migration, or system change affecting the submission. The disclosure must explain how the insurer evaluated the reliability of the converted information.

**B.3.23** An insurer must disclose any material reconciliation difference between the submission and the insurer’s financial, policy, premium, or claims records. The insurer must describe the reason for the difference.

**B.3.24** An insurer must retain records supporting the information submitted under this paragraph. The records must be sufficient to permit the Department to verify the insurer’s response.

**B.3.25** An insurer must make supporting records available to the Department upon request. The insurer must provide an explanation of the records sufficient to permit review.

**B.3.26** An insurer must maintain controls reasonably designed to prevent material omissions, duplications, and errors in the submission. The insurer must disclose a known control failure that materially affects reported information.

**B.3.27** An insurer must review the submission for internal consistency before filing it with the Department. The review must address material discrepancies identified by the insurer.

**B.3.28** An insurer must certify that the submission is complete and accurate to the best of the certifying person’s knowledge after reasonable inquiry. A certification does not limit the insurer’s duty to correct a material error.

**B.3.29** An insurer must ensure that a certification is made by a person with authority to bind the insurer concerning the submission. The insurer must identify the certifying person and the person’s title.

**B.3.30** An insurer must promptly correct a material error or omission discovered after submission. The correction must identify the affected information and explain the basis for the correction.

**B.3.31** An insurer must promptly respond to a Department inquiry concerning the submission. A response must be complete, accurate, and supported by the insurer’s records.

**B.3.32** An insurer must preserve the confidentiality of nonpublic information used to prepare the submission. An insurer must provide nonpublic information to the Department when required by law or requested for regulatory review.

**B.3.33** An insurer must not omit information on the ground that the information is commercially sensitive. An insurer may identify information it believes should receive confidential treatment under applicable law.

**B.3.34** An insurer must not use a disclaimer that is inconsistent with the certification required by this paragraph. An insurer may provide an explanatory statement that does not qualify, limit, or contradict the submission.

**B.3.35** An insurer must submit information in the manner directed by the Department. An insurer must not alter a required reporting format unless the Department permits the alteration.

**B.3.36** An insurer must use reasonable efforts to ensure that information reported by a third party is accurate and complete. Reliance on a third party does not relieve the insurer of its obligations under this paragraph.

**B.3.37** An insurer must notify the Department if it cannot comply with a reporting requirement. The notice must explain the reason for noncompliance and describe the insurer’s proposed corrective action.

**B.3.38** The Department may review an insurer’s disclosures, supporting records, and corrective actions for compliance with this paragraph. An insurer must cooperate with the Department’s review.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to each admitted insurer required to submit homeowners claim information in the annual data call. An insurer must report claim information in accordance with this section.

**B.4.2** An insurer must report claims arising under homeowners policies written in this State. The report must include claim activity handled by the insurer and by any person acting on its behalf.

**B.4.3** An insurer must use records maintained in the ordinary course of business to prepare the submission. An insurer must retain sufficient supporting records to substantiate each reported item.

**B.4.4** An insurer must identify each claim according to the reporting instructions issued for the data call. An insurer may use an internal claim identifier if the identifier permits the Department to distinguish the claim from other reported claims.

**B.4.5** An insurer must report the date on which it received notice of the claim. An insurer must report the date on which it closed the claim, if applicable.

**B.4.6** An insurer must report the policy status applicable when the loss occurred. An insurer must not report a claim under a policy that was not in force when the reported loss occurred.

**B.4.7** An insurer must report the loss location associated with the claim. The reported location must reflect the insured property or other location to which coverage applies.

**B.4.8** An insurer must classify the cause of loss using the category that most accurately reflects the reported loss. An insurer must apply the same classification methodology throughout its submission.

**B.4.9** An insurer must report whether a claim involves damage to the dwelling, other covered property, additional living expense, personal liability, or another applicable coverage. An insurer must not omit a coverage component solely because another coverage component is also present.

**B.4.10** An insurer must report paid loss amounts attributable to the claim. Paid loss amounts must exclude amounts not paid under the applicable homeowners policy.

**B.4.11** An insurer must report allocated loss adjustment expense attributable to the claim when required by the reporting instructions. The insurer must apply its established expense-allocation methodology consistently.

**B.4.12** An insurer must report outstanding loss reserves for each claim that remains open at the reporting cutoff. The reported reserve must reflect the insurer's good-faith estimate of unpaid covered loss.

**B.4.13** An insurer must report outstanding allocated loss adjustment expense reserves when required by the reporting instructions. The reported reserve must be supported by the insurer's claim file or reserving records.

**B.4.14** An insurer must report claim payments and reserves on a gross basis before reinsurance recoverables. An insurer must not reduce reported claim amounts by anticipated salvage, subrogation, or other recoveries unless the reporting instructions require that treatment.

**B.4.15** An insurer must report a claim that is denied, withdrawn, closed without payment, or otherwise resolved without indemnity payment as required by the reporting instructions. The insurer must accurately identify the claim disposition.

**B.4.16** An insurer must report reopened claim activity when a previously closed claim is reopened. The insurer must maintain records showing the basis for reopening the claim.

**B.4.17** An insurer must include catastrophe-related claims in the submission when the claims arise under homeowners policies subject to this data call. An insurer must use the catastrophe designation required by the reporting instructions.

**B.4.18** An insurer must include claims involving water damage, fire, wind, theft, liability, and other covered causes of loss when applicable. An insurer must not combine unrelated claims solely because they involve the same insured or property.

**B.4.19** An insurer must report claims assigned to a managing general agent, third-party administrator, affiliate, or other service provider. The insurer remains responsible for the completeness and accuracy of information reported under B.4.

**B.4.20** An insurer must reconcile reported claim information to its claim administration records before submission. An insurer must investigate and correct material discrepancies identified through the reconciliation.

**B.4.21** An insurer must ensure that claim status, payment, reserve, and disposition information is internally consistent. An insurer must correct information that conflicts with the underlying claim file.

**B.4.22** An insurer may correct a submitted claim record when it identifies an error or omission. A correction must be made in the manner required for the applicable data call.

**B.4.23** An insurer must not report estimated claim information when actual information is available in its records. An insurer may use a reasonable estimate only when permitted by the reporting instructions and supported by documentation.

**B.4.24** An insurer must preserve claim records used to prepare the submission. The records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.4.25** An insurer must provide an explanation for any material change in claim reporting methodology. The explanation must identify the effect of the change on reported information.

**B.4.26** An insurer must designate an individual authorized to respond to Department inquiries concerning claim information submitted under B.4. The designated individual must be able to obtain supporting claim records promptly.

**B.4.27** An insurer's submission under B.4 must be complete, accurate, and made in good faith. Submission of information that the insurer knows, or reasonably should know, is materially inaccurate is not compliance with this section.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** This section applies to each insurer authorized to write homeowners coverage in this State. An insurer subject to this section must make the required filing with the Department.

**B.5.2** This section is effective upon issuance. An insurer must comply with this section for each filing requested by the Department after the effective date.

**B.5.3** An insurer must submit its filing by the filing date specified by the Department. A filing is not timely unless the Department receives it in the manner prescribed by the Department.

**B.5.4** An insurer must provide complete and accurate information in its filing. An insurer must ensure that the information reflects its applicable homeowners business.

**B.5.5** An insurer must submit information in the format required by the Department. An insurer may not alter a required format in a manner that prevents review or use of the filing.

**B.5.6** An insurer must identify itself in its filing as directed by the Department. An insurer must use information that permits the Department to associate the filing with the reporting insurer.

**B.5.7** An insurer must include all reportable data within the scope of the filing requirement. An insurer may not omit data based on internal reporting practices or recordkeeping limitations.

**B.5.8** An insurer with no reportable data must submit a filing that clearly states that condition. An insurer may not fail to file solely because it has no reportable data.

**B.5.9** An insurer must review its filing before submission. An insurer must take reasonable steps to prevent material errors, inconsistencies, and unsupported entries.

**B.5.10** An insurer must correct a filing when it discovers that submitted information is inaccurate or incomplete. An insurer must provide the correction in the manner directed by the Department.

**B.5.11** An insurer must retain records sufficient to support the information reported in its filing. An insurer must make those records available to the Department upon request.

**B.5.12** An insurer must have its filing certified by an authorized officer or other authorized representative. The certification must state that the filing has been reviewed and is accurate to the best of the certifying person’s knowledge.

**B.5.13** An insurer remains responsible for the completeness and accuracy of a filing prepared by a third party. Use of a third party does not relieve an insurer of any obligation under this section.

**B.5.14** The Department may identify a filing as deficient if the filing does not comply with this section. An insurer must address a deficiency identified by the Department as directed.

**B.5.15** Department acceptance of a filing does not constitute approval of the information reported. Acceptance does not limit the Department’s authority to request clarification, correction, or supporting records.

**B.5.16** An insurer must comply with this section in addition to any applicable legal or regulatory obligation. Nothing in this section limits the Department’s authority to enforce compliance with filing requirements.
