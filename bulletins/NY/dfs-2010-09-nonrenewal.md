# New York Bulletin DFS-2010-09 — Nonrenewal Notice Requirements
> SUPERSEDED by New York Bulletin DFS-2016-02 — Nonrenewal and Cancellation Notice Requirements for policies effective on or after 2016-02-22. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This section states the purpose and applicability of the Department’s nonrenewal notice requirements for admitted insurers. An insurer must administer nonrenewal practices in a manner consistent with applicable law and the policy contract.

**B.1.2** The requirements apply when an insurer elects not to renew a policy issued or delivered in this State. They apply to personal and commercial policies to the extent nonrenewal notice obligations govern the coverage.

**B.1.3** An insurer must provide a nonrenewal notice that is clear, accurate, and capable of being understood by the named insured. The notice must identify the insurer’s decision not to continue coverage.

**B.1.4** A nonrenewal notice must not mischaracterize a nonrenewal as a cancellation, rescission, or policyholder-initiated termination. An insurer must use terminology that accurately describes the action being taken.

**B.1.5** An insurer must apply these requirements to each policy for which the insurer has made a nonrenewal determination. The requirements remain applicable when the determination results from underwriting, risk selection, rating, or portfolio management practices.

**B.1.6** An insurer may use lawful underwriting standards in deciding whether to renew a policy. An insurer must not use those standards in a manner that evades notice obligations or unfairly deprives an insured of meaningful notice.

**B.1.7** A nonrenewal notice must be based on information reasonably available to the insurer when the notice is issued. An insurer must maintain records sufficient to support the stated basis for nonrenewal.

**B.1.8** An insurer must ensure that a nonrenewal notice is sent to the appropriate named insured and any other person entitled to receive notice. An insurer may use a producer to assist with delivery, but remains responsible for compliance.

**B.1.9** An insurer must administer nonrenewal notices consistently with the policy’s terms and conditions. A notice must not alter coverage, impose a new obligation, or create uncertainty regarding the insurer’s action.

**B.1.10** These requirements apply whether an insurer elects not to renew an individual policy or a group of policies. An insurer must provide notice on a policy-specific basis when policy-specific information is necessary to explain the action.

**B.1.11** An insurer must not rely on an internal classification or administrative label to avoid treating an action as a nonrenewal. The substance of the insurer’s action controls.

**B.1.12** An insurer must ensure that personnel responsible for underwriting, policy administration, claims, and customer communications understand the insurer’s nonrenewal procedures. Internal procedures must support accurate and timely notice practices.

**B.1.13** An insurer must use forms and communications that do not mislead an insured about the availability of replacement coverage. An insurer may identify lawful options available to the insured without suggesting that coverage will continue when it will not.

**B.1.14** An insurer must address an insured’s reasonable inquiry concerning a nonrenewal decision through appropriate personnel or procedures. The insurer’s response must be consistent with the information provided in the notice.

**B.1.15** An insurer must preserve records relating to a nonrenewal decision, including the notice and supporting underwriting information. Records must be available for Department review upon request.

**B.1.16** An insurer must apply nonrenewal standards without unlawful discrimination. A nonrenewal decision must be supported by lawful underwriting considerations relevant to the risk.

**B.1.17** Matters concerning the all-other-perils deductible minimum are outside the purpose of this section unless they form part of the stated basis for a nonrenewal decision. An insurer must describe that basis accurately when it is relied upon.

**B.1.18** Matters concerning the coinsurance threshold are outside the purpose of this section unless they form part of the stated basis for a nonrenewal decision. An insurer must not use unclear policy terminology to obscure the reason for nonrenewal.

**B.1.19** Matters concerning the ALE percentage are outside the purpose of this section unless they form part of the stated basis for a nonrenewal decision. An insurer must communicate any applicable coverage-related reason in plain language.

**B.1.20** An insurer may adopt procedures that provide protections greater than those described in this section. Such procedures must not conflict with applicable law or diminish an insured’s notice rights.

**B.1.21** An insurer must review its nonrenewal practices when changes in law, policy forms, underwriting standards, or administrative processes affect notice compliance. The review must be reasonably designed to identify and correct deficiencies.

**B.1.22** This section applies to nonrenewal determinations made by an admitted insurer for policies subject to this State’s insurance requirements. An insurer remains responsible for compliance when a third party performs administrative or underwriting functions on the insurer’s behalf.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that elects not to renew a policy issued or delivered in this State. An insurer must comply with this section when issuing a nonrenewal notice.

**B.2.2** An insurer must provide a nonrenewal notice in writing. The notice must be clear, legible, and reasonably calculated to inform the named insured of the insurer’s action.

**B.2.3** An insurer must deliver or mail the nonrenewal notice in the manner required by applicable law. An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with its delivery or mailing obligations.

**B.2.4** An insurer must issue the notice sufficiently in advance of policy expiration to permit the insured to seek other coverage. An insurer may not delay notice in a manner that unfairly impairs the insured’s ability to obtain replacement coverage.

**B.2.5** A nonrenewal notice must identify the policy to which it applies. The identification must be sufficient for the insured to determine the affected coverage.

**B.2.6** A nonrenewal notice must state that the insurer will not renew the policy. The notice may not describe a nonrenewal in a manner that is misleading or ambiguous.

**B.2.7** An insurer must state the effective date of the nonrenewal. The stated effective date must correspond to the expiration of the affected policy unless otherwise permitted by applicable law.

**B.2.8** An insurer must provide the reason or reasons for nonrenewal. The stated reason or reasons must be specific enough to allow the insured to understand the basis for the action.

**B.2.9** An insurer may not use a vague, generic, or conclusory reason when a more specific explanation is available. A statement that the action is based solely on underwriting judgment is not sufficient unless accompanied by an explanation of the relevant underwriting basis.

**B.2.10** An insurer must ensure that the reason stated in the notice is accurate. The insurer must be able to support the stated reason from its underwriting, claims, inspection, or other relevant records.

**B.2.11** An insurer may state more than one reason for nonrenewal when more than one reason applies. The inclusion of multiple reasons may not obscure the principal basis for the action.

**B.2.12** An insurer may not state a reason that is inconsistent with the insurer’s records. An insurer may not rely on a reason that it knows, or reasonably should know, is erroneous.

**B.2.13** When nonrenewal is based on information obtained from an inspection, report, or other source, the insurer must reasonably identify the condition or information relied upon. The notice must not imply that the insured was responsible for a condition when the insurer has no reasonable basis for that implication.

**B.2.14** When nonrenewal is based on loss history, the notice must reasonably identify the loss-related basis for the action. The insurer may not characterize a claim, report, or inquiry as a loss when its records do not support that characterization.

**B.2.15** When nonrenewal is based on material changes in risk, the notice must reasonably describe the relevant change. The description must enable the insured to understand the underwriting concern.

**B.2.16** When nonrenewal is based on property condition, the notice must reasonably describe the condition requiring action. An insurer may include information concerning available corrective measures when appropriate.

**B.2.17** An insurer may not nonrenew a policy for a reason prohibited by law. An insurer must apply its underwriting standards in a manner consistent with applicable law.

**B.2.18** An insurer must administer nonrenewal practices without unfair discrimination. An insurer may not use protected characteristics, or information that serves as an unlawful proxy for protected characteristics, as a basis for nonrenewal.

**B.2.19** An insurer must apply its filed and approved underwriting rules consistently. A departure from an underwriting rule must be documented and supported by a legitimate business reason.

**B.2.20** An insurer may use underwriting information obtained from an agent, broker, managing general agent, or other representative. The insurer remains responsible for ensuring that the nonrenewal notice complies with this section.

**B.2.21** An insurer must ensure that its agents and representatives do not provide information that contradicts the nonrenewal notice. An insurer must take reasonable steps to correct material misinformation communicated to an insured.

**B.2.22** An insurer may provide explanatory material with a nonrenewal notice. Any explanatory material must be consistent with the notice and may not diminish or obscure the required information.

**B.2.23** An insurer may include contact information for questions concerning the nonrenewal. The contact information must direct the insured to personnel or representatives able to address the stated reason for the action.

**B.2.24** An insurer must provide a reasonable means for an insured to request clarification of the stated reason for nonrenewal. The insurer must respond to a request in a manner consistent with its records and underwriting practices.

**B.2.25** An insurer must retain a copy of each nonrenewal notice. The retained copy must reflect the notice actually issued to the insured.

**B.2.26** An insurer must retain supporting records for the nonrenewal decision. Supporting records must be sufficient to permit review of the insurer’s compliance with this section.

**B.2.27** An insurer must maintain procedures for reviewing nonrenewal notices before issuance. Those procedures must be reasonably designed to identify inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading notices.

**B.2.28** An insurer must train personnel involved in nonrenewal decisions regarding the requirements of this section. Training must address the need for accurate and specific notice content.

**B.2.29** An insurer must implement controls appropriate to its underwriting operations to monitor compliance with this section. The controls must provide for correction of identified deficiencies.

**B.2.30** An insurer must investigate a credible complaint alleging that a nonrenewal notice was inaccurate or deficient. The insurer must document its review and any corrective action taken.

**B.2.31** An insurer that determines that a notice contains a material error must take prompt corrective action. Corrective action must be reasonably designed to protect the insured from prejudice caused by the error.

**B.2.32** An insurer may withdraw a nonrenewal decision when permitted by applicable law and the terms of the policy. An insurer must communicate any withdrawal clearly to the insured.

**B.2.33** An insurer may offer renewal subject to changed terms when permitted by applicable law. An insurer may not characterize such action as a nonrenewal unless the policy will in fact not be renewed.

**B.2.34** An insurer may not use a cancellation notice in place of a nonrenewal notice when the action is a nonrenewal. An insurer must distinguish clearly between cancellation and nonrenewal in its communications.

**B.2.35** An insurer may not use a conditional or contingent notice that leaves the insured unable to determine whether coverage will be renewed. A notice must state the insurer’s actual decision at the time of issuance.

**B.2.36** An insurer may communicate with an insured electronically when permitted by applicable law and with the insured’s consent where consent is required. Electronic delivery must preserve the content and clarity required by this section.

**B.2.37** An insurer must ensure that a notice issued in a language other than English is accurate and complete. A translated notice may not materially alter the meaning of the required notice.

**B.2.38** An insurer must protect nonpublic personal information contained in a nonrenewal notice and related records. The insurer must disclose such information only as permitted by applicable law.

**B.2.39** An insurer must ensure that automated systems used to generate nonrenewal notices produce accurate notice content. The insurer must test and monitor such systems for material errors.

**B.2.40** An insurer may use standardized notice language if the language remains accurate for the particular policy and reason for nonrenewal. Standardized language may not replace policy-specific information required by this section.

**B.2.41** An insurer must ensure that any underwriting guideline used to support nonrenewal is current and applicable to the affected policy. An obsolete or inapplicable guideline may not serve as the stated basis for nonrenewal.

**B.2.42** An insurer must provide information requested by the superintendent concerning its nonrenewal practices. The information must be complete, accurate, and provided in the manner directed by the superintendent.

**B.2.43** An insurer must make relevant nonrenewal records available for examination upon request by the department. The insurer must preserve records in a form that permits meaningful review.

**B.2.44** An insurer may not alter, destroy, or withhold records relevant to a nonrenewal decision. An insurer must preserve such records in accordance with applicable record retention requirements.

**B.2.45** An insurer must treat a request for reconsideration fairly and in good faith. A request for reconsideration does not relieve the insurer of its obligation to provide a compliant notice.

**B.2.46** An insurer must correct a nonrenewal practice that the department determines does not comply with this section. The insurer must take remedial action appropriate to the nature and scope of the deficiency.

**B.2.47** Failure to comply with this section may constitute an unfair or deceptive act or practice where applicable law so provides. The department may take action within its authority to address noncompliance.

**B.2.48** An insurer may adopt procedures that provide greater consumer protection than the minimum required by this section. Such procedures may not conflict with applicable law.

**B.2.49** This section applies to nonrenewal notices issued on or after the effective date of this bulletin. An insurer must apply the requirements of this section to notices in process when doing so is practicable and does not impair compliance with applicable law.

**B.2.50** An insurer must interpret and administer this section in a manner consistent with its purpose of providing meaningful notice of nonrenewal. An insurer may not rely on a technical omission to avoid its obligation to provide fair and accurate notice.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** These provisions apply to an insurer subject to this bulletin when the insurer elects not to renew or cancels a policy. An insurer must comply with these provisions on and after the effective date of this bulletin.

**B.3.2** An insurer must provide notice in writing. The notice must be reasonably designed to inform the named insured of the insurer's action.

**B.3.3** An insurer must state clearly whether the action is a nonrenewal or a cancellation. An insurer must not use language that could cause a reasonable insured to misunderstand the action being taken.

**B.3.4** A notice of nonrenewal must identify the policy to which it applies. The identification must be sufficient to permit the insured to determine the affected coverage.

**B.3.5** An insurer must mail or deliver a notice of nonrenewal at least forty-five (45) days before the policy's stated end date. The insurer must be able to demonstrate the date and manner of mailing or delivery.

**B.3.6** An insurer must mail or deliver a notice of cancellation for reasons other than nonpayment at least twenty (20) days before the stated effective date of cancellation. The notice must identify the date on which coverage will cease.

**B.3.7** A notice must be addressed to the named insured at the address shown in the insurer's records. An insurer must use any additional address required by the policy or applicable law.

**B.3.8** An insurer must state the reason for the nonrenewal or cancellation with sufficient specificity to permit the insured to understand the basis for the action. A conclusory statement that does not identify the underlying basis is insufficient.

**B.3.9** An insurer must ensure that the stated reason is accurate as of the date the notice is issued. An insurer must not state a reason that is inconsistent with its underwriting records.

**B.3.10** An insurer may use plain-language descriptions of its underwriting reasons. The description must not omit material information needed to understand the insurer's action.

**B.3.11** A notice must state the effective date of the nonrenewal or cancellation. The stated effective date must be consistent with the insurer's records and the notice provided under this paragraph.

**B.3.12** An insurer must identify the insurer issuing the notice. The notice must provide information sufficient for the insured to contact the insurer concerning the notice.

**B.3.13** An insurer must include the policy number or other unique policy identifier in the notice. The identifier must correspond to the policy affected by the insurer's action.

**B.3.14** An insurer must state whether any coverage remains in effect before the stated effective date. The notice must not suggest that coverage has ceased before the stated effective date unless coverage has lawfully ceased.

**B.3.15** An insurer must distinguish a nonrenewal from a cancellation in any communication accompanying the notice. An insurer must not characterize a nonrenewal as a cancellation, or a cancellation as a nonrenewal.

**B.3.16** An insurer must provide a notice that is legible and reasonably understandable. An insurer must not obscure material information through formatting, placement, or presentation.

**B.3.17** An insurer may provide the notice electronically only when the method of delivery is permitted by applicable law. An insurer must maintain evidence of electronic transmission when electronic delivery is used.

**B.3.18** An insurer must provide notice by a method that permits the insurer to establish compliance with this paragraph. The insurer's records must identify the date of mailing, delivery, or transmission.

**B.3.19** An insurer must retain a copy of the notice issued to the insured. The copy must reflect the content of the notice as issued.

**B.3.20** An insurer must retain records supporting the reason stated in the notice. Those records must be sufficient to permit review of the insurer's compliance with this paragraph.

**B.3.21** An insurer must ensure that its underwriting, policy administration, and claims personnel apply this paragraph consistently. Internal procedures must identify the personnel responsible for issuing notices.

**B.3.22** An insurer must not issue a notice before determining that the stated reason supports the action taken. A notice must reflect a completed underwriting or policy administration decision.

**B.3.23** An insurer may correct an inaccurate notice by issuing a replacement notice. The replacement notice must clearly identify the correction and must comply with this paragraph.

**B.3.24** An insurer must not rely on oral communication as a substitute for the written notice required by this paragraph. Oral communication may supplement, but must not replace, the written notice.

**B.3.25** An insurer must respond accurately to a reasonable inquiry from an insured concerning a notice. The response must not conflict with the reason or effective date stated in the notice.

**B.3.26** An insurer must ensure that a producer, administrator, or other representative does not issue a notice that misstates the insurer's action. The insurer remains responsible for compliance with this paragraph.

**B.3.27** An insurer must provide any required notice to each person entitled to receive notice under the policy or applicable law. The insurer must maintain records showing the recipients of the notice.

**B.3.28** An insurer must not condition issuance of a required notice on an insured's request. Notice must be provided without requiring action by the insured.

**B.3.29** An insurer must not use a notice to discourage an insured from seeking replacement coverage. Information in the notice must be limited to accurate and material information concerning the insurer's action.

**B.3.30** An insurer must ensure that translations or alternative-format notices accurately convey the material terms of the notice. A translated or alternative-format notice must not alter the insurer's stated reason or effective date.

**B.3.31** An insurer must apply this paragraph to notices generated through automated systems. Use of an automated system does not relieve the insurer of responsibility for the content or timing of a notice.

**B.3.32** An insurer must review notice templates and related procedures for compliance with this paragraph. The insurer must correct any template or procedure that could produce a noncompliant notice.

**B.3.33** An insurer must make relevant notice records available for review upon request by the department. Records provided for review must be complete and accurate.

**B.3.34** An insurer must not alter or destroy notice records in a manner that prevents verification of compliance with this paragraph. The insurer must preserve the information needed to identify the notice issued and the basis for the action.

**B.3.35** An insurer must treat each policy action separately when notices concern more than one policy. A notice must clearly identify each policy to which the action applies.

**B.3.36** An insurer must resolve any inconsistency between the notice and its internal records before issuing the notice. If an inconsistency is discovered after issuance, the insurer must take prompt corrective action.

**B.3.37** Compliance with this paragraph does not limit any other obligation imposed on an insurer by applicable law. An insurer must not rely on this paragraph to reduce any notice or disclosure obligation otherwise required.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that uses claim information in deciding whether to nonrenew a policy. An insurer must apply these standards to any nonrenewal notice issued on or after the effective date applicable to this bulletin.

**B.4.2** An insurer must maintain claim-handling practices that support accurate nonrenewal determinations. An insurer may not rely on claim information that it knows, or reasonably should know, is materially inaccurate.

**B.4.3** An insurer must identify the claim information considered in reaching a nonrenewal decision. The information must be sufficient to permit the insurer to explain the basis for the decision if requested by the department or the insured.

**B.4.4** An insurer may consider a claim only when the claim is relevant to the risk presented by the policy. An insurer may not treat a report of loss, inquiry, or request for information as a claim unless the insurer has handled it as a claim.

**B.4.5** An insurer must distinguish between an allegation of loss and a verified loss. An insurer may not characterize an unverified allegation as an established claim in a nonrenewal notice or related record.

**B.4.6** An insurer must promptly correct claim information shown to be inaccurate, incomplete, or attributed to the wrong insured or property. An insurer must ensure that corrected information is considered before issuing a nonrenewal notice when reasonably practicable.

**B.4.7** An insurer must use claim coding and claim descriptions consistently. Claim records used for nonrenewal purposes must reasonably reflect the nature and disposition of the reported loss.

**B.4.8** An insurer may consider a closed claim in accordance with its filed underwriting standards. An insurer may not rely on a closed claim record that omits a material disposition relevant to the nonrenewal decision.

**B.4.9** An insurer must reasonably investigate a claim before treating the claim as support for nonrenewal. A claim that remains unresolved may be considered only where the insurer can explain its relevance to the risk.

**B.4.10** An insurer may not use claim information in a manner that is unfairly discriminatory. An insurer must apply its claim-related underwriting standards consistently to similarly situated insureds.

**B.4.11** An insurer must maintain records supporting any claim-based nonrenewal determination. Those records must identify the information relied upon and the person or process that authorized the determination.

**B.4.12** An insurer must ensure that personnel responsible for nonrenewal decisions have access to current claim information. An insurer may not issue a notice based on superseded claim data when current data is reasonably available.

**B.4.13** An insurer must provide a nonrenewal notice that states the specific claim-related reason for the action when claim experience is a basis for nonrenewal. General references to loss history, unfavorable experience, or underwriting concerns are insufficient without a meaningful explanation.

**B.4.14** An insurer must describe the relevant claim information in clear and plain language. An insurer may not use terminology that is likely to mislead an insured about the nature of the claim relied upon.

**B.4.15** An insurer must identify the applicable property, coverage, or exposure when necessary to make the claim-related reason understandable. An insurer may not attribute a claim involving unrelated property or coverage to the policy being nonrenewed.

**B.4.16** An insurer must establish a process for reviewing an insured's assertion that claim information used in a nonrenewal decision is inaccurate. The process must permit correction of material errors without unreasonable delay.

**B.4.17** An insurer must consider documentation submitted by an insured that materially contradicts the claim information relied upon. An insurer may not disregard credible documentation solely because it was received after the initial underwriting review.

**B.4.18** An insurer must reevaluate a nonrenewal decision when a material claim record is corrected before the nonrenewal becomes effective. If the correction removes the stated basis for the action, the insurer must take appropriate corrective action.

**B.4.19** An insurer may use information obtained from a claim database only after taking reasonable steps to confirm that the information pertains to the insured or risk at issue. An insurer remains responsible for the accuracy of information used in its decision.

**B.4.20** An insurer must protect claim information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Claim information used for nonrenewal purposes must be handled in accordance with applicable privacy obligations.

**B.4.21** An insurer must ensure that a producer, managing general agent, or other person acting on its behalf follows these standards when supplying claim information for a nonrenewal decision. An insurer may not avoid responsibility by delegating claim review functions.

**B.4.22** An insurer must retain the notice and supporting claim-related records in a manner that permits review by the department. Records must be legible and reasonably organized.

**B.4.23** An insurer must make supporting claim records available to the department upon request. An insurer must also provide an explanation of how the claim information was applied to the nonrenewal decision.

**B.4.24** An insurer may not use a claim-related nonrenewal reason as a pretext for an impermissible underwriting action. The stated reason must correspond to the information actually considered by the insurer.

**B.4.25** An insurer must maintain procedures for quality review of claim-based nonrenewal decisions. The procedures must be reasonably designed to detect inaccurate claim information and unsupported notice reasons.

**B.4.26** An insurer must train personnel whose work affects claim-based nonrenewal decisions. Training must address accurate claim identification, notice content, record retention, and correction of material errors.

**B.4.27** An insurer must administer these standards in good faith and in a manner consistent with the purpose of fair and accurate nonrenewal notices. Failure to comply with this section may be considered in the department's review of an insurer's practices.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** An admitted insurer must file a nonrenewal notice form with the Department before using the form for a policy issued or delivered in this State. The filing must identify the line of insurance to which the form applies.

**B.5.2** An insurer must submit the notice form in a manner that permits Department review. The filing must be complete and must include all language proposed for use.

**B.5.3** An insurer must ensure that the filed notice form states the reason for nonrenewal in clear and specific terms. The reason stated in the notice must be consistent with the insurer’s underwriting action.

**B.5.4** An insurer may use a notice form only after the Department has approved the form or otherwise permitted its use. An insurer must not use a form that differs materially from the form filed with the Department.

**B.5.5** An insurer must file any material revision to an approved notice form before use of the revised form. A revision includes a change that affects the notice’s substance, clarity, or applicability.

**B.5.6** An insurer must maintain records demonstrating the form version used for each nonrenewal. The records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.5.7** An insurer must ensure that its filing accurately describes the policies, insureds, and circumstances for which the notice form will be used. A filing must not represent that a form applies more broadly than the insurer intends to use it.

**B.5.8** An insurer must provide any supporting explanation requested by the Department during review of a filing. The explanation must be sufficient to allow the Department to evaluate the notice form and its proposed use.

**B.5.9** An insurer may withdraw a filed notice form that it no longer intends to use. Withdrawal of a form does not excuse an insurer from retaining records concerning notices previously issued.

**B.5.10** An insurer must discontinue use of a notice form when directed by the Department. The insurer must take reasonable steps to prevent further issuance of the discontinued form.

**B.5.11** An insurer must apply an approved notice form in accordance with the conditions of its approval. An insurer must not rely on approval of a form for a use not described in the filing.

**B.5.12** An insurer must ensure that notices issued after a form revision use the applicable approved language. The insurer must maintain procedures designed to prevent use of superseded language.

**B.5.13** An insurer must treat the effective date of an approved notice form as the date authorized by the Department. A filing does not authorize use before the form becomes effective.

**B.5.14** An insurer must ensure that a nonrenewal notice issued during a transition to a revised form complies with the form requirements applicable to that notice. The insurer must resolve any uncertainty concerning form applicability before issuing the notice.

**B.5.15** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section when a service provider prepares or delivers a nonrenewal notice on the insurer’s behalf. The insurer must maintain oversight sufficient to ensure use of the proper approved form.

**B.5.16** An insurer must promptly correct a filing that contains inaccurate or incomplete information. Correction of a filing does not limit the Department’s authority to require further action under this paragraph.
