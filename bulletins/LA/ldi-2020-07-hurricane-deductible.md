# Louisiana Bulletin LDI-2020-07 — Hurricane Deductible Notice and Named Storm Periods




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This section states the Department’s expectations for notices addressing hurricane deductibles and named storm periods. It applies to an admitted insurer writing property insurance in this state when the policy may impose a hurricane deductible or other storm-related deductible.

**B.1.2** An insurer must provide clear and accurate information concerning the circumstances under which a hurricane deductible may apply. The information must be consistent with the policy language, endorsements, declarations, and applicable law.

**B.1.3** An insurer may use a separate notice, policy materials, electronic delivery, or another lawful method to provide the required information. The method used must reasonably inform the policyholder of the applicable deductible terms.

**B.1.4** A notice must identify the deductible that may apply to a covered loss associated with a hurricane or named storm. The notice must not describe a deductible in a manner that is misleading, incomplete, or inconsistent with the policy.

**B.1.5** An insurer must explain the event or conditions that trigger application of the hurricane deductible. The explanation must state whether the insurer relies upon a governmental designation, weather-related determination, policy definition, or another applicable policy standard.

**B.1.6** An insurer must explain when a named storm period begins and ends under the policy. The explanation must identify the source of any determination that establishes the named storm period.

**B.1.7** A notice must make clear that the application of a hurricane deductible depends upon the facts of the loss and the policy terms in effect. An insurer must not represent that a deductible will apply without regard to those facts and terms.

**B.1.8** An insurer must describe any difference between the hurricane deductible and any other deductible available under the policy. The description must permit a reasonable policyholder to understand which deductible may apply to a particular loss.

**B.1.9** If a policy includes more than one deductible that could apply to storm damage, the insurer must explain how the applicable deductible is determined. The explanation must be consistent with the policy’s order of application and claims handling practices.

**B.1.10** An insurer must ensure that a notice does not obscure material limitations through unclear language or presentation. A notice must be written in plain language appropriate for the intended policyholder.

**B.1.11** An insurer must use terminology consistently throughout its policy materials and notices. If the policy uses a defined term for hurricane, named storm, or related event, the notice must use that term or clearly explain its meaning.

**B.1.12** An insurer may summarize policy terms in the notice, but the summary must not alter coverage or create an ambiguity concerning the policy. The policy and applicable endorsements govern the contractual rights and obligations of the parties.

**B.1.13** An insurer must make the notice available in a manner reasonably calculated to reach the named insured. An insurer that delivers policy materials electronically must use a delivery method permitted by applicable law.

**B.1.14** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate the content and method of notice provided to policyholders. The records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.1.15** An insurer must apply hurricane deductible provisions in accordance with the policy language provided to the policyholder. Claims personnel must not apply a hurricane deductible based solely upon internal guidance that conflicts with the policy.

**B.1.16** An insurer must evaluate each reported loss based on the available facts, the applicable named storm period, and the policy terms. A claims determination must accurately state the basis for application of any hurricane deductible.

**B.1.17** If an insurer denies or limits payment based on a hurricane deductible, the insurer must communicate the basis for that determination to the policyholder. The communication must identify the relevant policy provision in a clear manner.

**B.1.18** An insurer must ensure that its notices and related claims communications are consistent. Material differences between those communications may create confusion and must be avoided.

**B.1.19** An insurer must review its policy forms and notices for consistency with this section before use in this state. An insurer must correct a notice that it determines is inaccurate or materially incomplete.

**B.1.20** This section does not alter the minimum Section I deductible, the eighty percent condition, or the additional living expense limit. An insurer must address those policy terms in accordance with the policy and applicable law.

**B.1.21** This section applies to policies issued, delivered, renewed, or amended after this bulletin becomes effective, unless applicable law requires earlier compliance. An insurer may apply this section to existing policies when doing so does not diminish coverage or conflict with the policy.

**B.1.22** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with all applicable insurance requirements governing policy forms, notices, underwriting, and claims practices. Compliance with this section does not excuse conduct that is otherwise prohibited by applicable law.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer must comply with this section upon the effective date of this bulletin. An insurer must apply these requirements to affected personal residential property insurance business issued, delivered, renewed, or modified on or after that effective date.

**B.2.2** An insurer must administer hurricane deductibles and named storm periods in a manner that is consistent with the policy contract and the disclosures required by this section. An insurer must not apply a deductible that is not authorized by the policy contract.

**B.2.3** An insurer must use clear and conspicuous language when describing a hurricane deductible. The disclosure must identify the circumstances under which the deductible may apply.

**B.2.4** An insurer must provide the required disclosure before the applicant is bound to coverage when the insurer offers coverage subject to a hurricane deductible. An insurer must provide the disclosure in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the applicant.

**B.2.5** An insurer must provide the required disclosure to an insured at renewal when the renewed policy includes, changes, or continues a hurricane deductible. An insurer must make the disclosure sufficiently prominent that an insured can identify the deductible without searching through unrelated policy materials.

**B.2.6** An insurer must describe the deductible using the same terminology used in the policy contract. An insurer must not use a summary, quotation, or notice that conflicts with the policy language.

**B.2.7** An insurer must state whether the hurricane deductible applies separately from any other deductible. An insurer must explain the relationship between deductibles when more than one deductible may apply to the same loss.

**B.2.8** An insurer must identify the property coverage to which a hurricane deductible applies. An insurer must not imply that a deductible applies to coverage for which the policy does not authorize its application.

**B.2.9** An insurer must identify the loss conditions that may trigger application of a hurricane deductible. The description must be consistent with the insurer's claims handling procedures.

**B.2.10** An insurer must explain how an insured may obtain information about whether a named storm period is in effect. An insurer must make that information available through reasonable customer service channels.

**B.2.11** An insurer must not make a hurricane deductible contingent upon an internal determination that is not described in the policy contract. An insurer must maintain written procedures for making determinations concerning deductible applicability.

**B.2.12** An insurer must ensure that its underwriting, policy issuance, billing, claims, and customer service functions use consistent deductible terminology. An insurer must correct material inconsistencies promptly.

**B.2.13** An insurer must ensure that a hurricane deductible does not exceed five (5) percent of the applicable covered loss. An insurer must calculate the deductible in accordance with the policy language and this paragraph.

**B.2.14** An insurer must disclose the basis used to calculate a percentage deductible. The disclosure must identify the applicable insured value or other policy basis used for that calculation.

**B.2.15** An insurer must not describe a percentage deductible in a manner that suggests the deductible is a fixed monetary amount. An insurer must provide a reasonable explanation when an applicant or insured requests clarification.

**B.2.16** An insurer must apply the same deductible calculation methodology reflected in its policy forms and customer disclosures. An insurer must not alter that methodology after a loss except as authorized by the policy contract and applicable law.

**B.2.17** An insurer must maintain records showing the deductible applicable to each affected policy. The records must be sufficient to permit review of the insurer's disclosure and calculation practices.

**B.2.18** An insurer must retain copies of disclosures provided to applicants and insureds. The retained records may be maintained electronically if they are accessible and reproducible.

**B.2.19** An insurer must ensure that electronic disclosures are presented in a format that an applicant or insured can retain. An insurer must provide a paper disclosure upon request where required by applicable law.

**B.2.20** An insurer must not rely solely on a general reference to policy terms as the required hurricane deductible disclosure. An insurer must identify the deductible in language understandable to a reasonable applicant or insured.

**B.2.21** An insurer must provide claims personnel with instructions for determining whether a named storm period affects a reported loss. Those instructions must be consistent with the policy contract and the insurer's public communications.

**B.2.22** An insurer must document the basis for applying a hurricane deductible to a claim. The claim file must identify the policy provision and the facts supporting the determination.

**B.2.23** An insurer must notify an insured when a hurricane deductible is applied to a claim. The notice must explain the basis for the application and the resulting effect on the claim payment.

**B.2.24** An insurer must respond to a reasonable request for an explanation of a hurricane deductible. The response must be accurate, complete, and consistent with the policy contract.

**B.2.25** An insurer must not represent that a named storm period applies unless the insurer has made the determination under its written procedures. An insurer must promptly correct any inaccurate representation concerning a named storm period.

**B.2.26** An insurer must maintain procedures for communicating deductible status to producers and other authorized representatives. Those procedures must require communications that are consistent with the insurer's approved policy language.

**B.2.27** An insurer must ensure that producer materials do not minimize or obscure the existence of a hurricane deductible. An insurer remains responsible for materials it prepares, approves, or directs for use.

**B.2.28** An insurer must train personnel who communicate with applicants and insureds about hurricane deductibles. The training must address the disclosures required by B.2.3 through B.2.6.

**B.2.29** An insurer must train claims personnel concerning the documentation required by B.2.22 and the notice required by B.2.23. The insurer must maintain evidence that the training has been made available to affected personnel.

**B.2.30** An insurer must review its policy forms, endorsements, applications, declarations, and notices for consistency with this section. An insurer must revise materials that do not clearly disclose the hurricane deductible.

**B.2.31** An insurer must ensure that declarations information accurately reflects the deductible applicable to the policy. An insurer must not use an abbreviation or label that is likely to mislead an insured.

**B.2.32** An insurer must ensure that a policy change affecting a hurricane deductible is communicated to the insured. The communication must identify the change and its effective application under the policy contract.

**B.2.33** An insurer must not apply a revised hurricane deductible to an existing policy except as permitted by the policy contract and applicable law. An insurer must provide any required notice before the revised deductible becomes applicable.

**B.2.34** An insurer must use reasonable controls to prevent the application of a hurricane deductible to an ineligible claim. The controls must include review procedures appropriate to the insurer's operations.

**B.2.35** An insurer must correct an improper deductible application and adjust the claim accordingly. An insurer must document the correction in the claim file.

**B.2.36** An insurer must establish a process for handling complaints concerning hurricane deductibles and named storm periods. The process must provide for review by personnel with authority to resolve the matter.

**B.2.37** An insurer must investigate a complaint concerning deductible applicability using the policy contract, claim information, and relevant underwriting records. An insurer must provide a response that addresses the material issues raised by the complainant.

**B.2.38** An insurer must monitor complaint information for patterns suggesting unclear disclosures or inconsistent administration. An insurer must take corrective action when monitoring identifies a material concern.

**B.2.39** An insurer must maintain written procedures governing the start and end of a named storm period for purposes of policy administration. The procedures must identify the source of information used by the insurer.

**B.2.40** An insurer must apply its named storm period procedures consistently across affected policies unless the policy contract provides otherwise. An insurer must document any policy-specific basis for different treatment.

**B.2.41** An insurer must not use a named storm period designation to deny coverage that is otherwise available under the policy contract. A named storm period may affect deductible application only as authorized by the policy contract.

**B.2.42** An insurer must ensure that policyholder communications distinguish between coverage availability and deductible applicability. An insurer must not state or imply that a deductible eliminates coverage.

**B.2.43** An insurer must ensure that any automated system used to calculate or apply a hurricane deductible operates consistently with approved policy terms. An insurer must test and correct system functions that produce inaccurate results.

**B.2.44** An insurer must maintain controls over changes to automated deductible rules. The controls must preserve a record of the change, its authorization, and its implementation.

**B.2.45** An insurer must make relevant records available to the department upon request. The records must include materials sufficient to evaluate compliance with this section.

**B.2.46** An insurer must document carrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exceptioncarrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exception (see Personal Lines Underwriting Manual, R540 Rule 540 — Louisiana State Exceptions) and must make that documentation available to the department upon request. The documentation must be consistent with the insurer's policy forms and operational practices.

**B.2.47** An insurer must ensure that its underwriting rules do not conflict with the hurricane deductible disclosures provided to applicants and insureds. An insurer must correct any conflict identified through review or complaint handling.

**B.2.48** An insurer must provide reasonable oversight of third parties performing underwriting, policy administration, claims, or customer service functions. An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section when such functions are performed by a third party.

**B.2.49** An insurer must require a third party acting on its behalf to use the insurer's approved deductible language. An insurer must take corrective action when a third party uses inaccurate or misleading language.

**B.2.50** An insurer must establish internal escalation procedures for questions concerning hurricane deductible applicability. The procedures must identify personnel authorized to provide final operational guidance.

**B.2.51** An insurer must ensure that final operational guidance is consistent with the policy contract and the written procedures required by this section. An insurer must preserve material guidance for future reference.

**B.2.52** An insurer must periodically review its practices under this section for continued compliance. The review must consider policy language, disclosures, claim outcomes, complaints, and operational controls.

**B.2.53** An insurer must promptly address deficiencies identified through internal review, market conduct review, complaint review, or department inquiry. Corrective action must be documented.

**B.2.54** An insurer must not retaliate against an insured for asking questions or submitting a complaint concerning a hurricane deductible or named storm period. An insurer must handle such inquiries in good faith.

**B.2.55** An insurer must ensure that all communications required by this section are legible and reasonably understandable. An insurer must not use language that defeats the purpose of the required disclosure.

**B.2.56** An insurer must apply this section in a manner that gives effect to the policy contract and applicable law. Where an insurer identifies uncertainty concerning compliance, the insurer must seek appropriate guidance before taking action that may adversely affect an insured.

**B.2.57** An insurer must treat the requirements of this section as minimum standards. An insurer may adopt more protective procedures that do not conflict with the policy contract or applicable law.

**B.2.58** An insurer must ensure that its records demonstrate compliance with each applicable requirement of this section. An insurer must preserve those records in a manner that permits timely review by the department.

**B.2.59** An insurer must implement this section consistently for all affected business. An insurer must not use an exception, practice, or interpretation that undermines the protections required by this section.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** An insurer must provide clear notice of any hurricane deductible that applies to a policy. The notice must be written so that a policyholder can reasonably understand when the deductible may apply.

**B.3.2** An insurer must disclose the basis used to determine the hurricane deductible. The disclosure must state whether the deductible is expressed as a stated amount, a percentage of coverage, or another permitted method.

**B.3.3** An insurer must identify the coverage to which a hurricane deductible applies. The notice must not suggest that the deductible applies to coverage that is not subject to it.

**B.3.4** An insurer must provide the notice at policy issuance, renewal, replacement, or amendment when the hurricane deductible is added, changed, or otherwise affected. An insurer must provide the notice in a manner reasonably calculated to reach the policyholder.

**B.3.5** An insurer must give a policyholder advance written notice before increasing a windstorm deductible. The notice must plainly describe the prior deductible and the deductible that will apply after the change.

**B.3.6** An insurer must provide the notice required by Paragraph B.3.5 at least thirty (30) days before the increased windstorm deductible takes effect. An insurer must not impose the increased deductible before the notice period has expired.

**B.3.7** An insurer must state the effective date of a deductible change in the notice. The stated effective date must be consistent with the policy transaction and the notice provided under this Paragraph B.3.

**B.3.8** An insurer must not obscure a deductible notice within unrelated policy materials. An insurer may include the notice with other policy materials if the deductible notice remains conspicuous.

**B.3.9** An insurer must use headings, type, and presentation that reasonably distinguish deductible information from general policy language. The notice must not use misleading or contradictory language.

**B.3.10** An insurer must explain the event that causes a hurricane deductible to apply. The explanation must identify the applicable trigger without creating uncertainty about the insurer's obligations.

**B.3.11** An insurer must disclose whether a named storm period affects application of the deductible. The disclosure must explain the relationship between the named storm period and the covered loss.

**B.3.12** An insurer must state that the deductible determination depends on the policy terms in effect at the time of loss. The notice must not imply that a later deductible change applies to an earlier loss.

**B.3.13** An insurer must provide deductible information in policy forms, endorsements, declarations, or accompanying notices that are delivered to the policyholder. The information must be consistent across those materials.

**B.3.14** An insurer must ensure that a producer or other representative does not make statements that conflict with the written deductible notice. An insurer remains responsible for communications made on its behalf.

**B.3.15** An insurer must make the deductible notice available in a format that can be retained by the policyholder. Electronic delivery may be used when permitted and when the policyholder can access the notice.

**B.3.16** An insurer must retain records showing the content and delivery of notices required by this Paragraph B.3. The records must be sufficient to demonstrate compliance upon request by the department.

**B.3.17** An insurer must respond accurately to a policyholder inquiry concerning a hurricane deductible. A response must not alter the policy terms or diminish the notice otherwise required by this Paragraph B.3.

**B.3.18** An insurer must apply the same deductible description used in its notice when adjusting a covered loss. If the policy language controls a claim determination, the insurer must explain that determination consistently with the policy.

**B.3.19** An insurer must not characterize a hurricane deductible as optional unless the policyholder has a meaningful option under the applicable policy terms. Any description of an option must state the conditions affecting that option.

**B.3.20** An insurer must disclose the commencement of a named storm period in language consistent with the policy. The disclosure must identify the event or designation that begins the period.

**B.3.21** An insurer must state that the named storm period continues for seventy-two (72) hours after the named storm designation ends. An insurer must apply that period consistently with the policy language and this Paragraph B.3.

**B.3.22** An insurer must not state or imply that a named storm period ends merely because weather conditions improve. The notice must describe the applicable end of the period without misleading qualification.

**B.3.23** An insurer must use a deductible notice that is reasonably understandable to an ordinary policyholder. Technical terms may be used when necessary, but an insurer must explain terms that could materially affect understanding.

**B.3.24** An insurer must describe any deductible that applies differently to distinct property, coverages, or locations. The description must allow the policyholder to determine which deductible may apply to a loss.

**B.3.25** An insurer must disclose a deductible change even if the premium also changes. A premium notice alone does not satisfy the notice requirement of this Paragraph B.3 unless it contains the required deductible information.

**B.3.26** An insurer must provide corrected notice when it discovers that a deductible notice was materially inaccurate or incomplete. The corrected notice must clearly identify the information being corrected.

**B.3.27** An insurer must not use a notice that conflicts with the declarations, policy, endorsement, or other controlling contract language. If materials differ, the insurer must promptly address the inconsistency.

**B.3.28** An insurer must ensure that deductible notices used for renewal accurately reflect the renewal policy terms. A notice prepared for an earlier policy term must not be reused without review for accuracy.

**B.3.29** An insurer must identify a deductible as a hurricane deductible when that description is necessary to distinguish it from another deductible. An insurer must not use terminology that conceals the nature of the deductible.

**B.3.30** An insurer must make required notices available to a policyholder upon reasonable request. The insurer may provide a copy of the applicable policy materials with the notice.

**B.3.31** An insurer must train personnel responsible for policy communications to recognize the notice obligations in this Paragraph B.3. The training must support accurate and consistent disclosure.

**B.3.32** An insurer must maintain procedures to review deductible notices before use. The procedures must reasonably ensure that notices remain consistent with current policy terms.

**B.3.33** An insurer must not deny, delay, or condition a policyholder's right to receive deductible information. The insurer must provide the information without requiring the policyholder to waive any right or remedy.

**B.3.34** An insurer must apply this Paragraph B.3 to policies for which a hurricane or windstorm deductible may apply. An insurer must comply when issuing, renewing, replacing, amending, or administering those policies.

**B.3.35** An insurer must comply with this Paragraph B.3 for notices delivered after the bulletin becomes effective. An insurer must also use reasonable efforts to ensure that continuing policy communications remain consistent with this Paragraph B.3.

**B.3.36** An insurer may use plain-language examples to explain deductible operation if the examples are accurate. An example must not limit or expand coverage beyond the policy terms.

**B.3.37** An insurer must not rely on an oral explanation in place of the written notice required by this Paragraph B.3. Oral communications must remain consistent with the written notice.

**B.3.38** An insurer must administer hurricane deductible notices in good faith. Compliance with this Paragraph B.3 does not alter any policyholder right available under applicable law or the policy.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** An insurer must administer each hurricane-related claim in good faith and with due regard for the interests of the insured. This section applies from its effective date to claims under property insurance policies issued or delivered in this state.

**B.4.2** An insurer must maintain claim-handling procedures that permit timely identification of a reported loss involving a hurricane deductible or a named storm period. Those procedures must provide for review of the policy terms applicable to the reported loss.

**B.4.3** An insurer must acknowledge receipt of a claim communication without unreasonable delay. The acknowledgment must provide contact information for the person or unit responsible for handling the claim.

**B.4.4** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation before denying a claim in whole or in part. The investigation must consider information supplied by the insured and information reasonably available to the insurer.

**B.4.5** An insurer must explain its claim determination in plain language. A denial or limitation of coverage must identify the policy provision on which the insurer relies.

**B.4.6** An insurer may apply a hurricane deductible only when the policy terms and the facts of the loss support its application. The insurer must not apply a hurricane deductible solely because a storm has been named.

**B.4.7** An insurer must determine whether the loss occurred during a named storm period before applying any deductible that depends upon that period. The claim file must contain documentation supporting that determination.

**B.4.8** An insurer must evaluate the cause of loss separately from the applicability of a hurricane deductible. A dispute concerning the deductible must not delay investigation or adjustment of covered damage.

**B.4.9** An insurer must provide the insured with a clear explanation when it applies a hurricane deductible. The explanation must state the basis for the application and identify the relevant policy language.

**B.4.10** An insurer must not represent that a hurricane deductible applies when the insurer has not completed a reasonable review of the available facts. An insurer must correct a prior communication if later information shows that the deductible does not apply.

**B.4.11** An insurer must communicate material claim information in a manner reasonably calculated to be understood by the insured. An insurer must respond to reasonable requests for information concerning the status of the claim.

**B.4.12** An insurer must advise the insured of information or documentation reasonably needed to evaluate the claim. The insurer must not request information that is unnecessary to determine coverage, damages, or the applicability of a deductible.

**B.4.13** An insurer must evaluate covered damage using reasonable methods and competent personnel. The insurer must consider the condition of the property before the reported loss when that condition is relevant to the claim determination.

**B.4.14** An insurer must maintain a claim file sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section. The file must include material communications, investigation records, estimates, and the basis for the claim decision.

**B.4.15** An insurer must provide a reasonable explanation of any difference between the insured's claimed damage and the insurer's evaluation. The explanation must identify material facts or policy terms supporting the difference.

**B.4.16** An insurer must issue payment for covered loss without unreasonable delay after reaching agreement on the amount owed or otherwise determining the amount payable. A payment must not be withheld because the insurer is considering unrelated portions of the claim.

**B.4.17** An insurer must promptly reevaluate a claim when the insured provides material information not previously considered. The insurer must communicate the result of that reevaluation to the insured.

**B.4.18** An insurer must not use the occurrence of a hurricane or named storm period as a basis to reduce, delay, or deny coverage except as permitted by the policy and supported by the claim facts. The insurer bears responsibility for maintaining records that support its coverage determination.

**B.4.19** An insurer must ensure that its adjusters, claim representatives, and service providers are informed of the requirements of this section. The insurer remains responsible for compliance when claim functions are performed by another person or entity.

**B.4.20** An insurer must apply this section consistently with the policy and applicable law. Nothing in this section authorizes an insurer to impose a limitation, exclusion, or deductible not contained in the policy.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** An insurer must file any hurricane deductible notice for Department review before the insurer uses the notice with a policyholder. The filing must clearly identify the policy forms and endorsements to which the notice applies.

**B.5.2** An insurer must file any language that describes a named storm period when that language affects the application of a hurricane deductible. The filing must state how the proposed language operates with the applicable policy provisions.

**B.5.3** An insurer must provide a complete copy of the proposed notice with the filing. An insurer must also provide any related endorsement, declaration language, or policy language needed to evaluate the notice.

**B.5.4** An insurer must identify the proposed effective date for each filing made under B.5.1. The proposed effective date must allow the notice to be provided as required before the hurricane deductible may apply.

**B.5.5** An insurer may use filed notice language only for the policy forms and endorsements identified in the filing. An insurer must file revised notice language before using the revision.

**B.5.6** An insurer must ensure that the filed notice accurately describes the hurricane deductible contained in the applicable policy. A notice may not describe a deductible, trigger, or named storm period in a manner that conflicts with the policy.

**B.5.7** An insurer must identify any variation in notice language that applies to different policy forms, coverages, territories, or classes of insureds. The filing must explain the reason for the variation.

**B.5.8** An insurer must file a notice that is clear and conspicuous to a reasonable policyholder. The notice must not obscure or contradict material conditions governing the hurricane deductible.

**B.5.9** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate the version of the notice provided to an insured. The records must permit the Department to determine whether the notice was provided before the applicable hurricane deductible was imposed.

**B.5.10** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section when a producer, administrator, or other representative provides the notice on the insurer’s behalf. An insurer must maintain procedures to ensure that the representative uses the filed notice language.

**B.5.11** An insurer must promptly provide additional information requested by the Department concerning a filing under B.5.1. The insurer may not treat Department review of a filing as authorization to use language that is incomplete, misleading, or inconsistent with the policy.

**B.5.12** An insurer must file any withdrawal of a notice or related policy language that affects the application of a hurricane deductible. The withdrawal filing must identify the affected policy forms and the intended effective date of the withdrawal.

**B.5.13** An insurer must give policyholders notice of a change to hurricane deductible language in a manner consistent with the policy and this section. A change may not be applied to an insured before the insurer has provided the required notice.

**B.5.14** An insurer must ensure that a filing addressing a named storm period uses terms consistently throughout the notice and related policy materials. Any defined term must have the same meaning when used in the notice and in the policy.

**B.5.15** The Department may request clarification, revision, or supporting material for a filing made under this section. An insurer must respond to such a request before using language that remains under review.

**B.5.16** Compliance with the filing requirements of this section does not relieve an insurer of responsibility for the accuracy of its hurricane deductible notice. An insurer must correct any notice language that the insurer determines is inaccurate or inconsistent with the applicable policy.
