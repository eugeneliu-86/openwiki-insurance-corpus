# Louisiana Bulletin LDI-2012-05 — Hurricane Deductible Notice
> SUPERSEDED by Louisiana Bulletin LDI-2020-07 — Hurricane Deductible Notice and Named Storm Periods for policies effective on or after 2020-07-15. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This bulletin states the Department’s expectations concerning notice of a hurricane deductible in property insurance policies issued by admitted insurers. Its purpose is to promote clear disclosure to policyholders and applicants.

**B.1.2** This bulletin applies to an admitted insurer that delivers or issues for delivery a property insurance policy covering a risk located in Louisiana when the policy includes a hurricane deductible. This bulletin also applies when such coverage is renewed, amended, or replaced.

**B.1.3** An insurer must provide a notice that clearly identifies the existence of the hurricane deductible. The notice must describe the circumstances under which the deductible may apply.

**B.1.4** An insurer must present the notice in language that is understandable to a reasonably informed policyholder or applicant. An insurer must not use wording that obscures the operation or effect of the hurricane deductible.

**B.1.5** An insurer must distinguish a hurricane deductible from deductibles that may apply to other covered causes of loss. The notice must explain that the hurricane deductible may differ from an all-other-perils deductible minimum.

**B.1.6** An insurer must describe the policy terms that govern the application of the hurricane deductible. The notice must not state or imply that coverage is available when the policy terms do not provide coverage.

**B.1.7** An insurer must make the notice available in a manner reasonably likely to inform the policyholder or applicant before the insurer relies upon the hurricane deductible. The insurer must maintain the notice with the applicable policy materials.

**B.1.8** An insurer may combine the required notice with other policy disclosures if the hurricane deductible notice remains conspicuous and understandable. Material unrelated to the hurricane deductible must not distract from the required disclosure.

**B.1.9** An insurer must ensure that its representatives provide information consistent with the notice. An insurer must establish procedures reasonably designed to avoid conflicting explanations of the hurricane deductible.

**B.1.10** An insurer must identify any policy provision that affects the insured’s responsibility for a covered loss subject to the hurricane deductible. The notice must not mischaracterize a coinsurance threshold as a hurricane deductible.

**B.1.11** An insurer must describe the relationship between the hurricane deductible and applicable loss settlement provisions when that relationship is relevant to the policyholder’s recovery. The notice must be consistent with the policy language.

**B.1.12** An insurer must not describe an ALE percentage as part of the hurricane deductible unless the policy expressly makes that description accurate. The notice must clearly distinguish deductible obligations from additional living expense coverage.

**B.1.13** An insurer may use an electronic notice when the insurer provides the notice in a manner that permits the policyholder or applicant to retain it. An electronic notice must be as clear and accessible as a notice provided in paper form.

**B.1.14** An insurer must make reasonable efforts to ensure that a notice remains accurate when the insurer changes policy terms affecting the hurricane deductible. A revised notice must not create uncertainty concerning the policy terms in effect.

**B.1.15** This bulletin takes effect upon issuance and applies to notices provided thereafter for policies within its scope. An insurer must administer its hurricane deductible notice practices consistently with this bulletin.

**B.1.16** This bulletin does not alter the terms of an insurance policy or create coverage that is not provided by the policy. An insurer remains responsible for clear and accurate communication concerning the hurricane deductible.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer that delivers, issues, renews, or continues a policy for delivery in this state with a hurricane deductible must comply with these requirements. These requirements apply upon the effective date of this bulletin.

**B.2.2** An insurer must provide a clear written notice of the hurricane deductible to the applicant or policyholder. The notice must be provided in a manner reasonably calculated to call attention to the deductible.

**B.2.3** An insurer must provide the notice before the applicant becomes obligated under a policy containing a hurricane deductible. An insurer must provide the notice to a policyholder before renewal when the renewed policy contains a hurricane deductible.

**B.2.4** The notice must identify the policy coverage to which the hurricane deductible applies. The notice must describe the loss circumstances that may cause the deductible to apply.

**B.2.5** The notice must state whether the hurricane deductible applies to windstorm loss, hail loss, or both. The notice must use language that is understandable to a reasonable policyholder.

**B.2.6** An insurer must state the hurricane deductible separately from any other deductible shown in the notice. An insurer may not present the hurricane deductible in a manner that obscures its effect on a covered loss.

**B.2.7** The notice must explain whether the hurricane deductible applies only after a hurricane has been declared or designated by an authorized governmental authority. The notice must identify the event or condition used by the insurer to determine when the deductible applies.

**B.2.8** An insurer must explain the geographic area in which the hurricane deductible may apply. The explanation must be consistent with the policy language and the insurer's underwriting rules.

**B.2.9** The notice must identify whether the hurricane deductible applies to the insured location, the policy, or a particular coverage. An insurer may not use a description that creates uncertainty concerning the applicable property.

**B.2.10** An insurer must describe the basis used to calculate the hurricane deductible. The description must state whether the deductible is based on insured value, a stated amount, or another policy basis.

**B.2.11** The deductible applicable to windstorm and hail losses must not exceed five (5) percent of the insured value. An insurer must administer the deductible consistently with this limitation.

**B.2.12** An insurer must ensure that the deductible shown in the notice matches the deductible contained in the policy. An insurer must correct any inconsistency before issuing or renewing the policy.

**B.2.13** An insurer may provide the notice as part of an application, declarations page, endorsement, renewal offer, or separate communication. The notice must remain conspicuous when included with other policy materials.

**B.2.14** An insurer must use type, spacing, and placement that make the notice readily noticeable. An insurer may not place the notice among unrelated terms in a manner likely to mislead an applicant or policyholder.

**B.2.15** An insurer must state the hurricane deductible in the same manner in which it will be applied to a covered loss. The notice may include an illustration if the illustration is accurate and not misleading.

**B.2.16** An illustration included in the notice must identify the assumptions used. An insurer may not use an illustration that suggests a deductible applies differently from the policy terms.

**B.2.17** An insurer must disclose any condition that must occur before the hurricane deductible applies. The disclosure must be consistent with the insurer's claims-handling practices.

**B.2.18** An insurer must explain whether the hurricane deductible applies to direct physical loss, additional living expense, or another coverage affected by the deductible. The explanation must not broaden or narrow coverage beyond the policy terms.

**B.2.19** An insurer must identify any policy provision that controls when the hurricane deductible applies. The notice must direct the applicant or policyholder to review the policy for the complete terms and conditions.

**B.2.20** An insurer may not represent that a hurricane deductible applies when the policy does not contain a deductible authorized by the policy language. An insurer must promptly correct a notice that contains such a representation.

**B.2.21** An insurer must provide a revised notice when it changes the hurricane deductible applicable to an in-force policy. The revised notice must be provided before the change takes effect.

**B.2.22** An insurer may not increase a hurricane deductible through a renewal without giving the policyholder clear advance notice. The notice must identify that the deductible is changing and describe the changed term.

**B.2.23** An insurer must provide a notice of a new hurricane deductible before the policyholder is required to accept the policy containing that deductible. The notice must not characterize the addition as a routine administrative change.

**B.2.24** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with these requirements. The records must show the notice used and the manner in which the notice was provided.

**B.2.25** An insurer must make compliance records available to the department upon request. The records must be maintained in a form that permits review of the notice content.

**B.2.26** An insurer that uses electronic delivery may provide the notice electronically when electronic delivery is permitted by applicable law. The electronic notice must be presented in a format that can be retained by the recipient.

**B.2.27** An insurer providing an electronic notice must ensure that the hurricane deductible disclosure is readily accessible. A link or reference may be used only if it clearly identifies the disclosed information.

**B.2.28** An insurer must provide a paper notice when required by applicable law or requested by the applicant or policyholder. The paper notice must contain the same material information as the electronic notice.

**B.2.29** An insurer must ensure that its producers and representatives use approved hurricane deductible notices. An insurer remains responsible for notices provided on its behalf.

**B.2.30** An insurer must train personnel who communicate deductible information to provide information consistent with the policy and notice. An insurer may not permit personnel to make statements that contradict the required notice.

**B.2.31** An insurer must review its application and renewal materials to ensure that the hurricane deductible is accurately described. An insurer must revise materials that are inconsistent with these requirements.

**B.2.32** An insurer must ensure that a policyholder can determine from the notice whether a hurricane deductible may apply to a reported loss. The notice must not require the policyholder to infer material terms from unrelated policy provisions.

**B.2.33** An insurer must use consistent terminology for the hurricane deductible throughout its application, policy, endorsements, renewal materials, and notices. If different terminology is necessary, the notice must clearly explain that the terms refer to the same deductible.

**B.2.34** An insurer may not describe a hurricane deductible as optional unless the policyholder may decline it without losing the applicable coverage. Any description of an option must accurately state the available choice.

**B.2.35** An insurer must not use language that minimizes the significance of the hurricane deductible. An insurer must not imply that the deductible is unlikely to apply when the policy terms permit its application.

**B.2.36** An insurer must respond accurately to reasonable inquiries concerning the hurricane deductible. A response must be consistent with the notice and the policy language.

**B.2.37** An insurer must provide a corrected notice if it discovers that a notice materially misstates the hurricane deductible. The correction must clearly identify the information being corrected.

**B.2.38** An insurer must administer claims in a manner consistent with the notice provided to the policyholder. An insurer may not apply a hurricane deductible that differs from the deductible authorized by the policy.

**B.2.39** An insurer must ensure that claim communications accurately explain the deductible applied to a loss. The communication must identify the policy basis for the deductible determination.

**B.2.40** An insurer may not use the hurricane deductible notice to alter policy coverage, impose an unfiled term, or waive a policyholder right. The policy and applicable law control the contractual obligations of the parties.

**B.2.41** An insurer must monitor its forms and procedures for continued compliance with these requirements. An insurer must promptly address any deficiency identified through that review.

**B.2.42** An insurer must maintain procedures for handling complaints concerning hurricane deductible notices. The procedures must provide for review of the notice, policy language, and relevant communications.

**B.2.43** An insurer must cooperate with a department inquiry concerning its hurricane deductible notices or practices. An insurer must provide a complete and accurate response to a request for information.

**B.2.44** Compliance with these requirements does not limit an insurer's duty to comply with any other applicable requirement. An insurer must apply these requirements in a manner that protects applicants and policyholders from misleading deductible disclosures.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** An insurer must provide the notice and disclosure required by this provision for a policy issued, renewed, amended, or delivered in this state that includes a windstorm deductible. The notice must be provided in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the policyholder of the deductible that may apply.

**B.3.2** An insurer must state the windstorm deductible clearly and conspicuously. The disclosure must not be obscured by unrelated policy language, formatting, or presentation.

**B.3.3** An insurer must identify the policy coverage to which the windstorm deductible applies. The disclosure must explain whether the deductible applies to covered property, covered loss, or another stated basis of coverage.

**B.3.4** An insurer must describe the event or conditions that cause the windstorm deductible to apply. The description must be consistent with the policy terms and any applicable endorsement.

**B.3.5** An insurer must state whether the windstorm deductible replaces another deductible or applies in addition to another deductible. The disclosure must not leave the policyholder unable to determine which deductible applies to a covered loss.

**B.3.6** An insurer must disclose the method used to calculate the windstorm deductible. If the deductible is based on a value stated in the policy, the disclosure must identify that value.

**B.3.7** An insurer must use language that permits a reasonable policyholder to understand the financial effect of the windstorm deductible. The disclosure must accurately reflect the deductible that applies under the policy.

**B.3.8** An insurer must provide the notice in the policy, an endorsement, the declarations, or another policy document delivered to the policyholder. The document containing the notice must be reasonably accessible to the policyholder.

**B.3.9** An insurer must ensure that the notice provided at issuance is consistent with the deductible shown in the policy documents. An insurer may not rely on a disclosure that conflicts with the policy terms.

**B.3.10** An insurer must provide a revised notice when the windstorm deductible is changed. The revised notice must identify the changed deductible and the effective date of the change.

**B.3.11** An insurer must provide written notice to the policyholder no less than thirty (30) days before an increase in a windstorm deductible takes effect. The notice must clearly state that the deductible is increasing.

**B.3.12** An insurer may not make an increase in a windstorm deductible effective unless the required notice has been provided. The insurer must maintain evidence of the notice and its delivery.

**B.3.13** An insurer must send a notice of deductible increase to the policyholder at the address shown in the insurer's records. An insurer may provide the notice electronically when electronic delivery is permitted and the insurer can document delivery.

**B.3.14** An insurer must state the effective date of a deductible increase in the notice. The stated effective date must be consistent with the policy amendment, renewal, or other transaction implementing the increase.

**B.3.15** An insurer must provide the notice of deductible increase separately from materials that could reasonably distract from the change. An insurer may include the notice with other policy materials if the notice remains clear and conspicuous.

**B.3.16** An insurer must not characterize an increase in a windstorm deductible as a routine administrative change. The notice must describe the change in plain and direct language.

**B.3.17** An insurer must provide the applicable disclosure at renewal when the renewing policy includes a windstorm deductible. The renewal materials must identify any deductible that differs from the deductible applicable under the expiring policy.

**B.3.18** An insurer must provide an updated disclosure when it replaces, removes, or modifies a policy provision affecting the windstorm deductible. The updated disclosure must accompany or precede the policy change.

**B.3.19** An insurer may provide notice of a decrease in a windstorm deductible through the policy documents reflecting the decrease. The insurer must ensure that the documents accurately describe the deductible after the change.

**B.3.20** An insurer must show the windstorm deductible on the declarations when the declarations identify applicable deductibles. The presentation must distinguish the windstorm deductible from deductibles applicable to other covered causes of loss.

**B.3.21** An insurer must ensure that an endorsement changing the windstorm deductible identifies the policy to which it applies. The endorsement must be consistent with the related notice and declarations.

**B.3.22** An insurer must provide accurate deductible information in a binder, quote, or other pre-policy communication when that communication describes the windstorm deductible. Such communication must not misstate the policy deductible that will apply if coverage is issued.

**B.3.23** An insurer is responsible for compliance with this provision when a producer, managing general agent, or other representative provides notice on the insurer's behalf. The insurer must provide procedures sufficient to support accurate delivery of the required disclosure.

**B.3.24** An insurer must use terminology consistently throughout the policy materials when referring to the windstorm deductible. The insurer must not use differing terms in a manner that creates uncertainty about deductible applicability.

**B.3.25** An insurer must explain material limitations associated with application of the windstorm deductible when those limitations are necessary to understand the deductible. The explanation must be consistent with the policy language.

**B.3.26** An insurer must not state or imply that a windstorm deductible applies when the policy does not provide for that deductible. An insurer must not omit a required disclosure in a manner that could mislead a policyholder.

**B.3.27** An insurer must ensure that the format, type size, contrast, and placement of the disclosure permit reasonable review. The disclosure must remain legible when reproduced or transmitted electronically.

**B.3.28** An insurer may use plain-language explanations in addition to policy language describing the windstorm deductible. Any explanation must not alter, limit, expand, or conflict with the policy terms.

**B.3.29** An insurer must not rely on an oral explanation as a substitute for the written notice required by this provision. Oral communications must be consistent with the written disclosure.

**B.3.30** An insurer must retain records showing the content of each notice used and the manner in which the notice was delivered. The records must be sufficient to demonstrate compliance upon request by the Department.

**B.3.31** An insurer must make the notice form and related disclosure materials available for review by the Department. The materials must identify the policy forms and endorsements to which they apply.

**B.3.32** An insurer must investigate a policyholder inquiry concerning the applicability or amount of a windstorm deductible using the policy terms in effect for the relevant loss. The insurer must provide a response that does not conflict with the required disclosure.

**B.3.33** An insurer must ensure that claim communications accurately identify the deductible applied to a covered loss. A claim communication must not state that a windstorm deductible applies unless the policy terms support that application.

**B.3.34** An insurer must not use a catastrophe-related communication to modify a windstorm deductible without providing the notice required by this provision. Emergency communications must remain consistent with the policy and applicable notice requirements.

**B.3.35** An insurer must apply this provision to all policy forms that provide comparable windstorm coverage in this state. The insurer must review its notices and disclosures for consistency across those forms.

**B.3.36** An insurer must ensure that policy administration systems produce notices consistent with the deductible elected or otherwise applicable to the policyholder. System-generated notices must be subject to procedures designed to prevent inaccurate deductible disclosures.

**B.3.37** An insurer must correct a materially inaccurate windstorm deductible notice promptly after discovering the inaccuracy. The corrective notice must clearly identify the prior error and state the accurate deductible information.

**B.3.38** An insurer must maintain procedures to monitor compliance with this provision. The procedures must address issuance, renewal, policy amendment, notice delivery, and record retention.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** An insurer must apply the hurricane deductible only in accordance with the policy terms in effect for the reported loss. An insurer must administer the deductible consistently for similarly situated insureds.

**B.4.2** An insurer must investigate a reported loss promptly after receiving notice of the claim. An insurer must maintain claim records sufficient to show the basis for its coverage and deductible determination.

**B.4.3** An insurer must advise the insured when it concludes that a hurricane deductible may apply to a claim. The advice must be clear and must identify the policy language on which the insurer relies.

**B.4.4** An insurer must not represent that a hurricane deductible applies unless the reported loss falls within the policy conditions governing that deductible. An insurer must explain the facts supporting its determination upon request.

**B.4.5** An insurer must evaluate all available information relevant to the cause and timing of the reported damage. An insurer must not deny or limit coverage based solely on an assumption concerning storm-related damage.

**B.4.6** An insurer must distinguish damage subject to a hurricane deductible from damage subject to another deductible or no deductible. The claim file must document the basis for any allocation of covered loss.

**B.4.7** An insurer must provide a timely written explanation of any claim payment that reflects application of a hurricane deductible. The explanation must state how the deductible affected the payment.

**B.4.8** An insurer must provide a timely written explanation when it denies coverage based in whole or in part on a hurricane deductible. The explanation must identify the relevant policy provisions and material facts.

**B.4.9** An insurer must not delay adjustment of undisputed portions of a claim while evaluating whether a hurricane deductible applies to another portion. An insurer must handle undisputed coverage in accordance with applicable claim-handling obligations.

**B.4.10** An insurer must respond promptly to an insured’s reasonable request for information concerning the hurricane deductible. The response must be accurate and consistent with the policy and claim file.

**B.4.11** An insurer must ensure that adjusters and claim representatives receive guidance concerning the application of hurricane deductibles. The guidance must promote consistent claim determinations and clear communications with insureds.

**B.4.12** An insurer must not require an insured to establish facts that are reasonably available to the insurer through its own investigation. An insurer may request information reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim.

**B.4.13** An insurer must consider documentation provided by the insured concerning the cause, timing, and extent of damage. An insurer must reasonably explain its treatment of material information that it does not accept.

**B.4.14** An insurer must preserve communications and documents material to its hurricane deductible determination. The records must be available for review by the department upon request.

**B.4.15** An insurer must correct a claim determination when it identifies an error in the application of a hurricane deductible. The insurer must promptly provide any additional payment due as a result of the correction.

**B.4.16** An insurer must not use a hurricane deductible as a basis to avoid investigation of covered damage. The insurer must complete a reasonable evaluation of the reported loss.

**B.4.17** An insurer must communicate with an insured in language reasonably calculated to be understood. An insurer must not use misleading or ambiguous statements concerning deductible application.

**B.4.18** An insurer must apply any policy endorsement affecting a hurricane deductible according to its terms. The insurer must retain documentation showing that the endorsement was applicable to the claim.

**B.4.19** An insurer must supervise vendors, adjusters, and other representatives performing claim functions on its behalf. The insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section.

**B.4.20** An insurer must implement procedures reasonably designed to assure compliance with this section. Those procedures must apply to claims arising under policies issued or delivered in this state.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** This paragraph applies to an insurer authorized to transact property insurance in Louisiana that uses a hurricane deductible notice. An insurer must file the notice with the Department before using the notice with a policyholder or applicant.

**B.5.2** An insurer must submit a clear and complete copy of the proposed notice. The filing must identify the policy forms and endorsements to which the notice applies.

**B.5.3** An insurer must ensure that the filed notice accurately describes the applicable hurricane deductible. The notice must be consistent with the policy language and with the insurer’s underwriting and claims practices.

**B.5.4** An insurer may not use a hurricane deductible notice merely because the notice has been submitted for filing. Filing does not authorize use of a notice that has not been approved by the Department.

**B.5.5** A hurricane deductible notice becomes effective only upon approval by the Department and implementation by the insurer. An insurer must not represent that an unapproved notice is effective.

**B.5.6** An insurer must use the approved notice without alteration. An insurer may make only changes that have been filed and approved by the Department.

**B.5.7** An insurer must file any material revision to an approved hurricane deductible notice before using the revision. A revision includes a change that affects the notice’s meaning, applicability, or presentation.

**B.5.8** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate that the notice used with a policyholder or applicant was approved and applicable to the related policy. The records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.5.9** An insurer must provide the hurricane deductible notice in a manner that is clear and reasonably calculated to inform the recipient. The insurer must not obscure or diminish the notice through formatting, placement, or unrelated language.

**B.5.10** An insurer must ensure that personnel and producers acting on its behalf use only the hurricane deductible notice that is applicable to the offered or issued coverage. The insurer remains responsible for compliance with this paragraph.

**B.5.11** An insurer must discontinue use of a hurricane deductible notice when the Department withdraws approval or when the notice no longer accurately reflects the applicable policy terms. The insurer must take appropriate action to prevent further distribution of the notice.

**B.5.12** The Department may require an insurer to provide additional information concerning a filing under this paragraph. An insurer must respond to a Department request in a manner that permits review of the hurricane deductible notice and its proposed use.
