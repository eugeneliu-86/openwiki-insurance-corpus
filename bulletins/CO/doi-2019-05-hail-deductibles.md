# Colorado Bulletin DOI-2019-05 — Hail Deductibles




Colorado Bulletin DOI-2019-05 · Edition 2019-05-20 · Page 1 of 12
## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This bulletin states the Department’s expectations for the use of hail
deductibles by admitted insurers in Colorado. An insurer must administer hail deductible
provisions in a manner that is clear, fair, and consistent with Colorado insurance law.

**B.1.2** This bulletin applies to personal and commercial property insurance policies
that provide coverage for hail loss in Colorado. It applies whether the hail deductible
appears in a policy form, endorsement, declaration, schedule, or other policy document.

**B.1.3** This bulletin is effective upon issuance. An insurer must apply this bulletin
prospectively to policy forms, endorsements, underwriting practices, sales practices,
and claim practices used after its effective date.

**B.1.4** This bulletin does not retroactively change the terms of a policy already
issued. An insurer remains responsible for administering an existing policy according to
its terms, applicable law, and the insurer’s obligations of good faith and fair dealing.

**B.1.5** An insurer may use a hail deductible only when the deductible is plainly
stated in the policy documents provided to the insured. The policy language must
identify the coverage to which the deductible applies and the circumstances that trigger
its application.

**B.1.6** An insurer must not describe a deductible in a manner that is misleading,
incomplete, or likely to cause an insured to misunderstand the deductible’s operation.
The insurer must use language that a reasonable insured can understand.

**B.1.7** An insurer must disclose a hail deductible before the insured is bound to
coverage. The disclosure must allow the insured to understand that a hail loss may be
subject to a deductible different from another property loss.

**B.1.8** An insurer must ensure that its application materials, declarations, policy
forms, endorsements, and claim communications accurately describe the hail deductible.
Material differences among those documents must be resolved in a manner consistent with
applicable law.

**B.1.9** An insurer must not impose a hail deductible that is absent from the policy or
that differs from the deductible shown in the applicable policy documents. An insurer
must maintain procedures to verify the applicable deductible before adjusting a hail
claim.

**B.1.10** An insurer may offer deductible options when the options are clearly
disclosed and fairly administered. The insurer must retain records sufficient to
demonstrate the deductible selected by the insured.

**B.1.11** An insurer must apply the same standards to similarly situated insureds when
determining whether a hail deductible applies. Underwriting or claim decisions must not
be arbitrary or inconsistent with the policy language.

**B.1.12** An insurer must train personnel involved in sales, underwriting, customer
service, and claims concerning the insurer’s hail deductible provisions. Training must
address the need to provide accurate information to insureds and claimants.

**B.1.13** An insurer must provide a reasonable explanation when it applies a hail
deductible to a claim. The explanation must identify the policy provision relied upon
and describe the basis for the deductible determination.

**B.1.14** An insurer must not use a hail deductible to reduce or avoid coverage beyond
the deductible authorized by the policy. The insurer must adjust covered hail damage in
accordance with the remaining policy terms.

**B.1.15** An insurer must ensure that a hail deductible is compatible with the policy’s
valuation provisions. The insurer must not administer the deductible in a manner that
improperly changes the settlement method otherwise available under the policy.

**B.1.16** An insurer must describe the relationship, if any, between a hail deductible
and the base deductible minimum in language that is not misleading. The insurer must not
imply that a deductible applies more broadly than the policy provides.

**B.1.17** An insurer must administer replacement cost provisions separately from any
deductible determination unless the policy clearly provides otherwise. Communications
concerning the insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement must remain
accurate and distinct from explanations of the hail deductible.

**B.1.18** An insurer must not represent that application of a hail deductible changes
coverage for additional living expense or similar benefits unless the policy so
provides. The insurer must communicate the loss of use limit accurately when that
coverage is relevant to a hail claim.

**B.1.19** An insurer must maintain claim files that document the basis for applying a
hail deductible. The documentation must be sufficient for the Department to evaluate
compliance with this bulletin.

**B.1.20** An insurer must respond promptly and accurately to an insured’s inquiry
concerning a hail deductible. The insurer must correct material misinformation provided
by its representatives when the insurer becomes aware of the misinformation.

**B.1.21** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this bulletin when it uses
producers, managing general agents, third party administrators, adjusters, or other
representatives. Delegation of a function does not relieve the insurer of responsibility
for that function.

**B.1.22** The Department may review an insurer’s policy forms, disclosures,
underwriting materials, claim files, and related records to determine compliance with
this bulletin. An insurer must cooperate with a Department inquiry concerning its hail
deductible practices.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** These requirements apply to an insurer that offers or issues property
insurance providing coverage for loss caused by hail. An insurer must administer a hail
deductible in a manner consistent with the policy language and these requirements.

**B.2.2** An insurer must clearly identify any hail deductible in the policy
declarations and in any applicable endorsement. The identification must distinguish the
hail deductible from any other deductible that may apply.

**B.2.3** An insurer must state the circumstances under which a hail deductible applies.
The policy language must permit a policyholder to determine whether the deductible
applies to a reported loss.

**B.2.4** An insurer may use a deductible applicable to windstorm and hail loss only if
the deductible is described in clear and conspicuous policy language. The description
must not mislead a policyholder regarding the coverage affected.

**B.2.5** A windstorm and hail deductible may not exceed five (5) percent of the
applicable covered loss. An insurer must apply the deductible in accordance with the
policy language.

**B.2.6** An insurer must calculate a percentage deductible using the value stated in
the policy. The insurer must clearly disclose the value used for that calculation.

**B.2.7** An insurer must state whether the deductible applies separately to each
covered loss or otherwise applies under the policy. The policy language must not create
uncertainty concerning the insurer's deductible calculation.

**B.2.8** An insurer may not apply a hail deductible to a loss unless the loss results
from a covered cause of loss for which the deductible applies. A deductible may not be
imposed solely because hail occurred in the vicinity of the insured property.

**B.2.9** An insurer must evaluate the reported facts of a loss before determining that
a hail deductible applies. The claim file must support the insurer's determination.

**B.2.10** An insurer must provide a policyholder with an explanation of the deductible
applied to a covered loss upon request. The explanation must identify the policy
provision relied upon by the insurer.

**B.2.11** An insurer must not describe a hail deductible as a coverage limitation
unless the policy language clearly establishes the limitation. Deductible disclosures
must be consistent with the policy terms.

**B.2.12** An insurer must use policy language that is understandable to a reasonable
policyholder. The language must explain the effect of the hail deductible on payment of
a covered loss.

**B.2.13** An insurer may offer different deductible options when permitted by its
underwriting practices. Each option must be accurately described before the policyholder
selects coverage.

**B.2.14** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to show the deductible option
selected for the policy. The records must be available for review by the Division.

**B.2.15** An insurer must not change a hail deductible without providing notice
required by the policy and applicable requirements. The notice must clearly describe the
change and its effect on coverage.

**B.2.16** An insurer must apply a changed deductible only in accordance with the policy
terms and the notice provided to the policyholder. The insurer must retain documentation
supporting the change.

**B.2.18** An insurer must not use a sales presentation that obscures the existence or
operation of a hail deductible. A representation concerning the deductible must be

**B.2.19** An insurer must train personnel responsible for underwriting, policy

**B.2.24** An insurer must handle claims involving hail damage in accordance with the
applicable policy provisions. A claim determination must not rely on an undisclosed
deductible practice.

**B.2.25** An insurer must explain any deduction from a claim payment that results from
application of a hail deductible. The explanation must be provided in a manner
reasonably calculated to be understood by the policyholder.

**B.2.26** An insurer must maintain claim documentation showing the basis for applying a
hail deductible. The documentation must identify the information considered by the
insurer.

**B.2.27** An insurer must not apply more than the deductible permitted by the policy to
the same covered loss. The insurer must correct an overapplication promptly upon
discovery.

**B.2.28** An insurer must review a policyholder inquiry concerning a hail deductible in
good faith. The insurer must provide a response that addresses the inquiry raised.

**B.2.29** An insurer must maintain complaint-handling procedures for concerns regarding
hail deductibles. The procedures must provide for review by personnel with appropriate

**B.2.31** An insurer must retain records relating to hail deductible disclosures,

**B.2.32** An insurer must make relevant records available to the Division upon request.

incorrect deductible determination. A policyholder may dispute the insurer's application

**B.2.37** An insurer must provide claim personnel with access to the current policy

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** An insurer must provide notice and disclosure of a hail deductible in a manner
that is clear and reasonably calculated to be understood by the applicant or
policyholder. The notice must identify the deductible as applicable to hail loss.

**B.3.2** An insurer must provide the required disclosure before an applicant becomes
bound under a policy containing a hail deductible. An insurer must provide the
disclosure to a policyholder when the insurer offers or issues a change that adds a hail
deductible.

**B.3.3** An insurer must provide written notice at least thirty (30) days before an
increase in a windstorm deductible takes effect. The notice must clearly describe the
change and its effect on the policyholder's responsibility for a covered loss.

**B.3.4** An insurer must state the deductible in the same manner in which it will be
applied to a covered hail loss. The disclosure must not use wording that is misleading,
incomplete, or likely to obscure the deductible's operation.

**B.3.5** An insurer must identify whether the hail deductible applies separately from
any other deductible under the policy. The disclosure must explain the circumstances
under which the hail deductible may apply.

**B.3.6** An insurer must use terminology that is consistent throughout the policy,
application, declarations, endorsements, and related notices. An insurer must not
describe a hail deductible by a different term unless the related disclosure clearly
explains that the terms have the same meaning.

**B.3.7** An insurer must present a hail deductible conspicuously within the policy
documents provided to the applicant or policyholder. The deductible must not be placed
in a manner that would reasonably prevent a person from recognizing its application.

**B.3.8** An insurer must disclose any condition that triggers application of a hail
deductible. The disclosure must be sufficient to allow the applicant or policyholder to
understand when the deductible may be imposed.

**B.3.9** An insurer must explain whether the hail deductible applies to damage caused
by hail alone or in combination with other covered causes of loss. The explanation must
be consistent with the coverage terms of the policy.

**B.3.10** An insurer must provide a policyholder with revised disclosure when an
endorsement changes the application of a hail deductible. The revised disclosure must

**B.3.11** An insurer must not represent that a hail deductible is optional when the
deductible is required by the offered policy form. An insurer must not represent that
coverage is unchanged when a hail deductible changes the policyholder's financial
responsibility.

**B.3.12** An insurer may use an application, acknowledgment, electronic record, or
other written method to document delivery of a required disclosure. The method used must
permit the insurer to demonstrate that the disclosure was provided.

**B.3.13** An insurer must make a required disclosure available in a form that can be
retained by the applicant or policyholder. An electronic disclosure must be capable of
being accessed and retained in a reasonably usable form.

**B.3.14** An insurer must ensure that a producer acting on its behalf provides
disclosures consistent with this section. An insurer remains responsible for the
adequacy of disclosures made in connection with its policies.

**B.3.15** An insurer must train personnel who communicate with applicants or
policyholders concerning hail deductibles. The training must address the need to provide
accurate and consistent explanations of deductible terms.

**B.3.16** An insurer must not rely solely on general policy language to satisfy a
disclosure obligation when a separate notice is required by this section. A disclosure
must direct the applicant or policyholder to the applicable policy language when further
detail is necessary.

**B.3.17** An insurer must describe a change in deductible terms using language that
distinguishes the prior terms from the revised terms. The notice must not imply that a
material change is merely an administrative update.

**B.3.18** An insurer must provide notice of a hail deductible change to the named
insured at the address or electronic location maintained in the insurer's records. An
insurer may provide notice through an authorized representative when the policyholder
has authorized that method.

**B.3.19** An insurer must maintain records of notices and disclosures provided under
this section. The records must identify the policy or proposed policy to which the
notice or disclosure relates.

**B.3.20** An insurer must maintain records in a manner that permits review of the
content of the notice or disclosure. The records must also permit verification of the
method and date of delivery.

**B.3.21** An insurer must make records required by this section available to the
Division upon request. An insurer must provide the records in a format that reasonably
preserves the substance of the original notice or disclosure.

**B.3.22** An insurer must use policy declarations and renewal materials that accurately
identify the applicable hail deductible. Any summary of coverage must be consistent with
the policy terms.

**B.3.23** An insurer must not use a disclosure that contradicts, limits, or expands the
policy terms unless the insurer issues an appropriate policy amendment. A disclosure may
explain policy terms but must not create uncertainty regarding the coverage provided.

**B.3.24** An insurer must clearly identify any distinction between a hail deductible
and a deductible applicable to another type of loss. The disclosure must avoid language
suggesting that all deductibles apply in the same manner when the policy provides
otherwise.

**B.3.25** An insurer must disclose the effect of a hail deductible on the amount
payable for a covered hail loss. The disclosure must be stated in plain language
appropriate for an ordinary consumer.

**B.3.26** An insurer must provide a meaningful opportunity for an applicant or
policyholder to review deductible information before accepting a policy change. An
insurer must respond accurately to reasonable questions regarding the disclosed
deductible.

**B.3.27** An insurer must ensure that a renewal notice accurately states any hail
deductible that will apply during the renewal term. A renewal notice must identify a
material change from the deductible applicable during the prior term.

**B.3.28** An insurer must not obscure a hail deductible through formatting, placement,
or unrelated information. An insurer may provide explanatory material if that material
does not diminish the clarity of the required disclosure.

**B.3.29** An insurer must use a disclosure that is consistent with the insurer's
underwriting practices and policy administration. An insurer must correct a disclosure
that the insurer knows, or reasonably should know, is inaccurate.

**B.3.30** An insurer must provide corrected information promptly when it discovers that
a policyholder received an inaccurate deductible disclosure. The corrective notice must
clearly identify the inaccurate information and the applicable policy terms.

**B.3.31** An insurer must ensure that notices concerning hail deductibles are
understandable when read together with the declarations and policy forms. An insurer
must not require a policyholder to infer material deductible terms from unrelated
provisions.

**B.3.32** An insurer must treat a material change to the application of a hail
deductible as requiring clear disclosure. A change is material when it may reasonably
affect the policyholder's responsibility for a covered hail loss.

**B.3.33** An insurer may provide additional explanatory information concerning a hail
deductible. Additional information must be accurate and must not conflict with the
required disclosure or policy terms.

**B.3.34** An insurer must apply the disclosure practices in this section to policies
issued, renewed, or amended for risks located in this state. An insurer must apply the
practices whenever a hail deductible is included in the applicable policy terms.

**B.3.35** An insurer must ensure that a notice under this section is not conditioned on

**B.3.36** An insurer must establish procedures reasonably designed to verify compliance

**B.3.38** An insurer must administer hail deductible notices and disclosures in good

policyholder's understanding of applicable deductible terms.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to an insurer handling a claim for covered hail loss
under a policy issued or delivered in Colorado. An insurer must apply these standards
consistent with the policy terms and applicable law.

**B.4.2** An insurer must acknowledge a hail claim promptly and communicate in a manner
reasonably calculated to assist the claimant. An insurer must provide contact
information for the person or unit responsible for claim handling.

**B.4.3** An insurer must investigate a hail claim fairly and without unreasonable
delay. An insurer must take reasonable steps to identify covered damage, excluded
damage, and damage not caused by hail.

**B.4.4** An insurer must clearly explain the applicable hail deductible before applying
the deductible to a payment determination. The explanation must identify the policy
provision establishing the deductible.

**B.4.5** An insurer must apply only the deductible authorized by the policy for the
reported loss. An insurer must not apply a deductible in a manner that is inconsistent
with the policy language.

**B.4.6** An insurer must not apply a hail deductible more than once to the same covered
loss unless the policy expressly permits that application. An insurer must explain the
basis for any separate deductible application in writing.

**B.4.7** An insurer must provide a written estimate or other written explanation
supporting its valuation of covered hail damage. The written material must reasonably
identify the damaged property and the scope of covered repairs.

**B.4.8** An insurer must distinguish covered hail damage from preexisting damage, wear,
deterioration, and excluded causes of loss when those distinctions affect payment. An
insurer must state the factual basis for the distinction in its claim file.

**B.4.9** An insurer must not deny or limit a hail claim based solely on an unsupported
conclusion regarding causation. An insurer must consider information reasonably
available from the claimant, an inspection, and other relevant sources.

**B.4.10** An insurer must conduct any inspection in a reasonable manner and must

provide reasonable notice when access to the property is required. An insurer must
document material observations relevant to coverage and valuation.

**B.4.11** An insurer must consider photographs, repair proposals, contractor
information, and other materials submitted by a claimant when relevant to the hail
claim. An insurer must explain in writing any material disagreement with such
information.

**B.4.12** An insurer must not require a claimant to use a particular contractor as a
condition of payment. An insurer may identify contractors or repair resources if the
insurer clearly states that the claimant may select another qualified contractor.

**B.4.13** An insurer must communicate the scope of covered repairs in sufficient detail

**B.4.14** An insurer must issue payment for undisputed covered hail damage without
unreasonable delay. An insurer must not withhold an undisputed amount solely because
another portion of the claim remains under review.

**B.4.15** An insurer must provide a written explanation whenever it denies a hail claim
in whole or in part. The explanation must identify the policy language and facts
supporting the denial.

**B.4.16** An insurer must provide a written explanation whenever it limits payment for
covered hail damage. The explanation must identify the basis for the limitation and any
information needed to reconsider the determination.

**B.4.17** An insurer must maintain claim documentation sufficient to show how it
determined coverage, valuation, and the applicable deductible. The documentation must
include material communications with the claimant and material evidence considered.

**B.4.18** An insurer must respond promptly to reasonable claimant inquiries concerning
the status of a hail claim. An insurer must provide a meaningful status update when
additional investigation or information is necessary.

**B.4.19** An insurer must advise a claimant of material policy conditions that may
affect payment for a hail claim. An insurer must not represent that a condition applies
when the policy does not support that representation.

**B.4.20** An insurer must explain any depreciation applied to a covered hail loss when
depreciation affects the amount payable. The explanation must reasonably identify the
property or repair item to which the depreciation applies.

**B.4.21** An insurer must explain any replacement cost condition relevant to payment
for covered hail damage. An insurer must not misstate the documentation or repair
information required under the policy.

**B.4.22** An insurer must consider a reasonable request for supplemental payment when
additional covered hail damage or necessary repair work is identified. An insurer must
communicate its supplemental claim determination in writing.

reasonably necessary to evaluate the claimed additional damage or cost.

**B.4.24** An insurer must communicate in clear language when a hail claim presents a
coverage question, valuation dispute, or deductible dispute. An insurer must identify
available information that may assist in resolving the matter.

**B.4.25** An insurer must ensure that persons acting on its behalf in the adjustment of
hail claims follow the standards in this section. An insurer remains responsible for
claim handling performed by its representatives and vendors.

**B.4.26** An insurer must apply these standards to hail claims subject to the effective

**B.4.27** An insurer must retain claim records in a manner that permits the Division to
evaluate compliance with this section. An insurer must make such records available upon
lawful request.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that files policy forms,
endorsements, rules, rates, or rating plans involving a hail deductible for use in this
state. An insurer must submit the filing in a manner that permits the Division to
determine the filing’s purpose and effect.

**B.5.2** An insurer must clearly identify each policy form, endorsement, application,
notice, rule, rate, or rating plan included in the filing. An insurer must distinguish
new material from material that is revised, replaced, or withdrawn.

**B.5.3** An insurer must describe the property, coverage, and policyholders to which
the proposed hail deductible applies. The description must explain the circumstances
under which the hail deductible may be applied.

**B.5.4** An insurer must provide the complete proposed hail deductible language with
the filing. The language must state how the deductible applies and how it interacts with
any other applicable deductible.

**B.5.5** An insurer must provide all supporting rules necessary to administer the
proposed hail deductible. The rules must be sufficiently clear for the Division to
evaluate consistent application by the insurer.

**B.5.6** An insurer must provide an explanation of the rating and underwriting effect
of the proposed hail deductible. The explanation must identify any change affecting
premium, eligibility, coverage availability, or deductible selection.

**B.5.7** An insurer must demonstrate that policyholders will receive clear notice of
the hail deductible before it becomes applicable to a policy. Any notice or disclosure
used for this purpose must be included with the filing.

**B.5.8** An insurer must ensure that the hail deductible language is not misleading,
ambiguous, or inconsistent with other policy provisions. An insurer must resolve any
conflict between the proposed language and related policy language before use.

**B.5.9** An insurer may not use a hail deductible form, endorsement, rule, rate, or
rating plan unless the filing has been made as required by applicable law. An insurer
must comply with any action taken by the Division concerning the filing.

**B.5.10** An insurer must identify when it proposes the filed material will become
effective. An insurer may not apply the proposed hail deductible to a policy before the
material is effective for that policy.

**B.5.11** An insurer must submit a revised filing when it changes material hail
deductible language, rules, rates, or rating plans. An insurer may not treat a material
change as previously approved or accepted solely because related material was previously
filed.

**B.5.12** An insurer must maintain records supporting its hail deductible filing and
implementation. The records must be available to the Division upon request.
