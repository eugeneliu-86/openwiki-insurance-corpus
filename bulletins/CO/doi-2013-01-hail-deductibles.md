# Colorado Bulletin DOI-2013-01 — Hail Deductibles
> SUPERSEDED by Colorado Bulletin DOI-2019-05 — Hail Deductibles for policies effective on or after 2019-05-20. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This bulletin states the Department’s expectations concerning hail deductibles in property insurance issued by admitted insurers in Colorado. It is intended to promote clear policy terms and fair communication with insureds.

**B.1.2** This bulletin applies to an admitted insurer that offers, issues, delivers, renews, or administers property insurance providing coverage for hail loss in Colorado. It applies regardless of the distribution method used for the coverage.

**B.1.3** An insurer must administer hail deductible provisions in a manner consistent with the policy language provided to the insured. An insurer may not use unclear or misleading language to describe the application of a hail deductible.

**B.1.4** A hail deductible must be distinguishable from any deductible that applies to other covered causes of loss. The policy must identify the circumstances under which the hail deductible applies.

**B.1.5** An insurer must make material hail deductible terms available to an applicant before the applicant is bound to coverage. The disclosure must be reasonably understandable to a person considering the coverage.

**B.1.6** An insurer must communicate whether a hail deductible applies on a per-loss, per-occurrence, or other basis stated in the policy. The insurer must apply that basis consistently during claim handling.

**B.1.7** An insurer may offer different deductible options when those options are lawful and adequately disclosed. The insurer must identify the selected deductible in the policy records and in information provided to the insured.

**B.1.8** An insurer must not describe a hail deductible as a premium reduction, savings feature, or coverage enhancement in a manner that obscures the insured’s financial responsibility following a covered loss. Any explanation of the deductible must remain accurate and complete.

**B.1.9** An insurer must ensure that producer communications concerning hail deductibles are consistent with the policy terms. An insurer remains responsible for communications made on its behalf in connection with the sale or servicing of coverage.

**B.1.10** An insurer must provide claim personnel with guidance sufficient to identify when a hail deductible applies. That guidance must support consistent treatment of similarly situated insureds.

**B.1.11** An insurer must explain the application of a hail deductible when the deductible affects the adjustment or settlement of a claim. The explanation must identify the relevant policy provision without suggesting that coverage is unavailable when coverage remains subject to the deductible.

**B.1.12** An insurer may investigate the cause and extent of claimed damage before applying a hail deductible. The investigation must be conducted fairly and may not be used to delay a coverage determination.

**B.1.13** An insurer must maintain records supporting the application of a hail deductible to a claim. The records must reasonably identify the facts, policy terms, and claim determination relied upon by the insurer.

**B.1.14** An insurer must administer a hail deductible separately from limitations that concern other policy benefits unless the policy clearly provides otherwise. Terms concerning the base deductible minimum must be presented so that an insured can understand their relationship to a hail deductible.

**B.1.15** An insurer must not imply that a hail deductible changes the valuation method for covered property unless the policy expressly provides for that result. The insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement must be described separately from a hail deductible when both concepts are relevant.

**B.1.16** An insurer must not use the application of a hail deductible to alter coverage for additional living expense or similar benefits except as the policy clearly permits. The loss of use limit must be communicated independently of any hail deductible.

**B.1.17** An insurer must review its policy language, underwriting materials, and claim practices to confirm that hail deductible provisions are administered consistently. An insurer should correct identified practices that could mislead insureds or produce inconsistent claim outcomes.

**B.1.18** This bulletin is effective upon issuance. An insurer must apply its requirements to conduct occurring on or after the effective date.

**B.1.19** An insurer should take reasonable steps to align existing procedures with this bulletin as promptly as practicable. The Department may consider an insurer’s implementation efforts when evaluating compliance with the expectations stated here.

**B.1.20** This bulletin does not expand coverage beyond the terms of an applicable policy. It does not relieve an insurer of any obligation imposed by Colorado insurance law.

**B.1.21** An insurer must continue to evaluate each claim based on its own facts and the applicable policy language. Nothing in this bulletin permits an insurer to deny, limit, or delay a claim without a reasonable basis.

**B.1.22** The Department expects an insurer to make hail deductible information accessible to insureds and claimants who request an explanation. Clear communication and consistent administration support fair treatment of Colorado consumers.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** This paragraph applies to an admitted insurer that issues or renews property insurance in this state after this bulletin takes effect. An insurer must administer windstorm and hail deductibles in accordance with this paragraph.

**B.2.2** An insurer must state any windstorm or hail deductible clearly in the policy. The statement must identify the coverage to which the deductible applies.

**B.2.3** An insurer must limit any windstorm or hail deductible to no more than three (3) percent of the applicable property coverage limit. An insurer must apply that limitation consistently.

**B.2.4** An insurer must not describe a windstorm or hail deductible in a manner that is misleading. The policy language must permit a reasonable insured to determine when the deductible applies.

**B.2.5** An insurer must identify the event or conditions that trigger a windstorm or hail deductible. The trigger must be stated in the policy and reflected in the insurer's claim handling practices.

**B.2.6** An insurer must not apply a windstorm or hail deductible unless the claimed loss resulted from a covered cause of loss. The insurer must maintain claim documentation supporting the application of the deductible.

**B.2.7** An insurer must disclose whether a windstorm or hail deductible applies separately from another deductible. The disclosure must be sufficiently clear to avoid an unreasonable expectation of duplicate deductibles.

**B.2.8** An insurer must not impose more than one deductible for the same covered loss unless the policy plainly authorizes the application. Any such policy language must be clear and unambiguous.

**B.2.9** An insurer must calculate a windstorm or hail deductible using the coverage limit identified in the policy. The insurer must not use a limit that differs from the limit stated for the affected property.

**B.2.10** An insurer must explain the basis for its deductible calculation upon request by an insured. The explanation must be consistent with the policy language and claim file.

**B.2.11** An insurer must provide policy language that distinguishes a windstorm or hail deductible from an exclusion. The policy must not characterize a deductible as a limitation that eliminates covered loss.

**B.2.12** An insurer must use deductible language that is readable and reasonably prominent. The language must not be obscured by unrelated provisions or presentation.

**B.2.13** An insurer must ensure that its declarations and endorsements accurately identify the applicable windstorm or hail deductible. Any inconsistency must be corrected promptly.

**B.2.14** An insurer must not apply a windstorm or hail deductible that is absent from the policy in effect when the loss occurred. The insurer must retain records showing the policy terms in effect.

**B.2.15** An insurer must provide an insured with the applicable deductible information before the insured is bound to coverage. The information must be presented in a manner that permits review before acceptance.

**B.2.16** An insurer must provide notice of a change to a windstorm or hail deductible before the change becomes applicable. The notice must describe the changed deductible terms.

**B.2.17** An insurer must not alter a windstorm or hail deductible through claim correspondence. A deductible change must be made through policy language or an applicable endorsement.

**B.2.18** An insurer must apply the deductible selected for the insured's policy. The insurer must not substitute a different deductible based solely on the location or severity of a reported loss.

**B.2.19** An insurer must maintain underwriting records supporting the deductible offered and accepted. Those records must be available for review by the Division.

**B.2.20** An insurer must train personnel responsible for underwriting and claims handling regarding the insurer's windstorm and hail deductible provisions. The training must support consistent administration of those provisions.

**B.2.21** An insurer must establish procedures for reviewing a claim in which a windstorm or hail deductible may apply. The procedures must require consideration of the facts supporting the deductible.

**B.2.22** An insurer must not deny coverage merely because a windstorm or hail deductible applies. The insurer must adjust the covered loss subject to the applicable deductible.

**B.2.23** An insurer must communicate the application of a windstorm or hail deductible in its claim determination. The communication must identify the policy basis for the deductible.

**B.2.24** An insurer must provide sufficient information for an insured to understand the deductible's effect on the claim payment. The information must not omit material calculation details.

**B.2.25** An insurer must apply deductible provisions uniformly to similarly situated insureds. The insurer must not administer those provisions in an unfairly discriminatory manner.

**B.2.26** An insurer must ensure that agents and producers use materials consistent with the policy's deductible terms. An insurer remains responsible for materials used on its behalf.

**B.2.27** An insurer must not use advertising or sales materials that misrepresent the effect of a windstorm or hail deductible. Any description must be consistent with the policy.

**B.2.28** An insurer must make the applicable deductible available to an insured upon reasonable request. The insurer must provide the deductible information without requiring a claim to be reported.

**B.2.29** An insurer must retain records concerning deductible disclosures, selections, and changes. The records must demonstrate compliance with this paragraph.

**B.2.30** An insurer must review complaint information involving windstorm or hail deductibles. The insurer must correct practices that do not comply with this paragraph.

**B.2.31** An insurer must respond accurately to inquiries concerning the application of a windstorm or hail deductible. The response must not conflict with the policy or claim file.

**B.2.32** An insurer must ensure that automated systems calculate windstorm and hail deductibles in accordance with the policy. The insurer must correct system errors that affect deductible application.

**B.2.33** An insurer must maintain controls to prevent unauthorized changes to deductible information. The controls must preserve an accurate record of policy terms.

**B.2.34** An insurer must not condition payment of an undisputed covered amount on acceptance of a disputed deductible calculation. The insurer must handle any dispute in good faith.

**B.2.35** An insurer must investigate whether a reported loss is subject to a windstorm or hail deductible before applying it. The investigation must be reasonable under the circumstances.

**B.2.36** An insurer must not rely on an internal guideline that conflicts with the policy deductible language. Internal guidance must be consistent with this paragraph.

**B.2.37** An insurer must ensure that claim representatives can identify the policy provision supporting a windstorm or hail deductible. The representative must accurately communicate that provision to the insured.

**B.2.38** An insurer must correct a deductible application that is inconsistent with the policy. The correction must include any resulting adjustment to the claim payment.

**B.2.39** An insurer must not apply a windstorm or hail deductible to property or coverage not identified by the policy provision. Any application must be limited to the coverage addressed by the deductible.

**B.2.40** An insurer must preserve claim file documentation concerning the cause of loss and deductible determination. The documentation must support the insurer's decision.

**B.2.41** An insurer must monitor its administration of windstorm and hail deductibles for compliance with this paragraph. The monitoring must address underwriting, policy issuance, and claims handling.

**B.2.42** An insurer must make records relevant to compliance with this paragraph available to the Division upon request. The records must be complete and reasonably organized.

**B.2.43** An insurer must comply with this paragraph for policies subject to its applicability. Compliance with this paragraph does not relieve an insurer of any other obligation imposed by law.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that offers, issues, renews, or amends a policy containing a hail deductible applicable to property located in Colorado. An insurer must provide the notice and disclosure required by this section in a clear and conspicuous manner.

**B.3.2** An insurer must provide the required notice before the applicant is bound to coverage containing a hail deductible. The notice must permit the applicant to understand the deductible before making a coverage selection.

**B.3.3** An insurer must provide the required disclosure with a renewal offer when the renewed policy contains a hail deductible. The disclosure must identify any material change to the application of the hail deductible.

**B.3.4** An insurer must provide a new disclosure when it proposes to add a hail deductible to existing coverage. An insurer may not add the hail deductible unless the policyholder receives the disclosure required by this paragraph.

**B.3.5** The notice must state that a separate deductible may apply to a covered loss caused by hail. The notice must not suggest that the hail deductible applies to losses for which it does not apply.

**B.3.6** The disclosure must identify the policy provision that establishes the hail deductible. The disclosure must describe the circumstances under which the hail deductible applies.

**B.3.7** An insurer must state whether the hail deductible applies on a per occurrence basis, a per location basis, or another stated basis. The stated basis must be consistent with the policy language.

**B.3.8** An insurer must explain how it determines that a loss is caused by hail for purposes of applying the deductible. The explanation must be sufficiently specific to permit a policyholder to understand the insurer's claim decision.

**B.3.9** An insurer must disclose whether the hail deductible applies when hail and another covered cause of loss contribute to the same damage. The disclosure must explain the insurer's treatment of concurrent damage.

**B.3.10** An insurer must identify the property to which the hail deductible applies. If the deductible does not apply uniformly to all covered property, the disclosure must clearly describe the distinction.

**B.3.11** An insurer must disclose the method used to calculate the hail deductible. The disclosure must state the applicable valuation or coverage basis used in the calculation.

**B.3.12** An insurer must explain whether the hail deductible is subject to a minimum or maximum amount. The explanation must be consistent with the deductible provision and the declarations.

**B.3.13** An insurer must state whether the hail deductible applies separately from any other deductible in the policy. If more than one deductible may apply to the same loss, the insurer must explain how the deductibles will be applied.

**B.3.14** An insurer must describe any endorsement or policy condition that changes the application of the hail deductible. The description must be provided with the notice required by B.3.2 or B.3.3, as applicable.

**B.3.15** An insurer must use language reasonably understandable to a policyholder. The insurer may use defined terms, but must explain a defined term when necessary to understand the hail deductible.

**B.3.16** An insurer must not bury the hail deductible disclosure among unrelated policy information. The disclosure must be presented in a manner likely to be seen by the applicant or policyholder.

**B.3.17** An insurer must not characterize a hail deductible as a benefit, discount, or enhanced protection. An insurer may accurately describe premium effects only if the description does not obscure the deductible obligation.

**B.3.18** An insurer must not use a disclosure that is inconsistent with the policy, endorsement, application, or declarations. If a conflict exists, the insurer must correct the disclosure and provide the corrected disclosure to the affected policyholder.

**B.3.19** An insurer must make the disclosure available in the same transaction through which it offers the applicable coverage. An electronic disclosure may be used if it is reasonably accessible and capable of being retained by the recipient.

**B.3.20** An insurer must provide a copy of the disclosure upon request by an applicant, insured, policyholder, claimant, or authorized representative. The copy must reflect the hail deductible applicable to the policy at issue.

**B.3.21** An insurer must maintain records demonstrating its compliance with this section. The records must include the disclosure used and evidence of the manner in which it was provided.

**B.3.22** An insurer must train personnel responsible for sales, underwriting, and claims handling regarding the hail deductible disclosure. The training must support consistent communications with applicants and policyholders.

**B.3.23** An insurer must ensure that producer communications concerning a hail deductible are not misleading. An insurer remains responsible for disclosures made on its behalf by a producer.

**B.3.24** An insurer must ensure that claim communications accurately state the hail deductible applied to a covered loss. The communication must explain the basis for the deductible amount withheld from the claim payment.

**B.3.25** An insurer may provide illustrative examples of how a hail deductible operates. An illustrative example must be clearly identified as an example and must not alter the policy terms.

**B.3.26** An insurer may use a combined notice and disclosure if the combined material satisfies each requirement of this section. The combined material must remain clear and conspicuous.

**B.3.27** An insurer must apply this section upon its effective date to policies within the scope of B.3.1. An insurer must revise its notice and disclosure practices as necessary to maintain compliance with this section.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer handling a claim for loss or damage under a policy containing a hail deductible and subject to Colorado law. It applies prospectively from this section’s effective date.

**B.4.2** An insurer must handle each claim in good faith and in accordance with the policy terms, applicable law, and the facts developed during the investigation. An insurer may not use a hail deductible in a manner that is inconsistent with the policy.

**B.4.3** An insurer must acknowledge communications concerning a claim promptly and must provide a claimant with reasonable information concerning the claim process. Communications must be clear and must not misstate coverage, deductible obligations, or claim status.

**B.4.4** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation before denying coverage or applying a hail deductible. The investigation must be based on information sufficient to support the insurer’s coverage determination.

**B.4.5** An insurer may request information reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim. An insurer must explain the information requested when a claimant reasonably seeks clarification.

**B.4.6** An insurer must arrange an inspection when an inspection is reasonably necessary to evaluate claimed hail damage. The inspection must be conducted in a manner that permits a fair assessment of the claimed property damage.

**B.4.7** An insurer must use personnel with appropriate knowledge and authority to evaluate hail damage and apply the policy deductible. An insurer remains responsible for the acts and communications of persons acting on its behalf.

**B.4.8** An insurer must distinguish, where reasonably possible, damage caused by hail from damage caused by other causes. An insurer may not attribute damage to an excluded cause without a reasonable factual basis.

**B.4.9** An insurer must document the material facts supporting its determination that hail caused, did not cause, or contributed to claimed damage. The documentation must be sufficient to explain the determination to the claimant.

**B.4.10** An insurer may apply a hail deductible only when the policy permits its application to the claimed loss. An insurer must apply the deductible according to the policy language and the facts of the claim.

**B.4.11** An insurer must advise the claimant when it intends to apply a hail deductible. The advice must identify the policy provision relied upon and explain how the deductible affects the claimed payment.

**B.4.12** An insurer must provide a clear calculation of the loss payment when a hail deductible is applied. The calculation must identify the covered loss amount, applicable deductions, and the resulting payment.

**B.4.13** An insurer must evaluate whether claimed damage arose from the same occurrence or from distinct occurrences when that determination affects deductible application. The insurer must base that determination on the policy language and the available facts.

**B.4.14** An insurer may not deduct the same hail deductible more than the policy permits. An insurer must correct a payment calculation when it identifies an improper deductible application.

**B.4.15** An insurer must evaluate repair estimates fairly and may not reject an estimate without a reasonable explanation. An insurer may obtain its own estimate or other relevant information to assess the claimed damage.

**B.4.16** An insurer must explain any adjustment for depreciation, wear, condition, or preexisting damage that affects the claim payment. The explanation must state the factual basis for the adjustment.

**B.4.17** An insurer must determine whether a payment is based on actual cash value, replacement cost, or another valuation method provided by the policy. An insurer must explain any policy condition affecting payment under the applicable valuation method.

**B.4.18** An insurer must consider additional hail damage reported after an initial inspection when the information reasonably indicates that further evaluation is warranted. An insurer must communicate its determination concerning the additional damage.

**B.4.19** An insurer must respond promptly to reasonable claimant inquiries concerning coverage, inspection findings, estimates, and deductible application. An insurer must provide meaningful information rather than a conclusory response.

**B.4.20** An insurer that denies a claim must provide a written explanation identifying the material policy provisions and factual grounds supporting the denial. The explanation must be sufficiently specific to permit the claimant to understand the basis for the determination.

**B.4.21** An insurer that partially denies a claim must identify the portion of the claim that remains covered and the basis for the portion denied. An insurer may not represent that an entire claim is denied when coverage remains available for part of the loss.

**B.4.22** An insurer must reasonably consider a claimant’s request for reinspection or review when the claimant provides information that may affect the claim determination. An insurer must document its response to material information received during that review.

**B.4.23** An insurer may not condition payment of an undisputed covered amount on a claimant’s release of rights unrelated to that payment. An insurer may seek a release only when its terms are fair, clear, and consistent with applicable law.

**B.4.24** An insurer must issue payment of an undisputed covered amount without using a coverage dispute as a basis to withhold that amount. An insurer must explain any amount withheld pending resolution of a reasonable dispute.

**B.4.25** An insurer may recommend contractors, repair facilities, or other service providers, but may not pressure a claimant to use a particular provider. An insurer must not imply that coverage depends on the claimant’s selection of a recommended provider.

**B.4.26** An insurer must maintain claim records showing its investigation, communications, coverage analysis, deductible calculation, and payment determination. The records must permit the Division to evaluate the insurer’s compliance with this section.

**B.4.27** An insurer must establish practices reasonably designed to ensure consistent application of hail deductibles in claims handling. An insurer must correct practices that result in improper claim determinations or deductible applications.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** An insurer must file for approval each policy form, endorsement, deductible provision, and related rule that establishes or changes a hail deductible. An insurer may not deliver, issue, renew, or otherwise use the filing until the Department has approved it.

**B.5.2** A filing must identify the insurance coverage to which the hail deductible applies. The filing must state whether the deductible applies separately from, or together with, any other deductible available under the policy.

**B.5.3** An insurer must provide policy language that clearly describes the event or condition that triggers application of the hail deductible. The language must permit a policyholder to determine when the deductible applies.

**B.5.4** An insurer must provide a complete explanation of the method used to calculate the hail deductible. The explanation must identify the policy value, limit, loss amount, or other basis used in the calculation.

**B.5.5** An insurer must file any declaration page, schedule, application, quotation, or notice that identifies or explains the hail deductible to an applicant or policyholder. Such material must be consistent with the policy form and endorsement language.

**B.5.6** An insurer must include sufficient underwriting and rating information to permit review of the proposed hail deductible. The information must explain how the insurer will apply the deductible within the affected coverage.

**B.5.7** An insurer must identify any eligibility condition, territorial limitation, construction characteristic, occupancy characteristic, or other restriction associated with use of the hail deductible. An insurer may not apply an undisclosed restriction to deny or reduce coverage.

**B.5.8** An insurer must file a clear explanation of any policyholder option concerning a hail deductible. The explanation must state the effect of the option on coverage and the deductible that will apply.

**B.5.9** An insurer must file any revision to an approved hail deductible provision before using the revision. A revision includes a change to policy language, eligibility, calculation, notice, underwriting practice, or rating treatment.

**B.5.10** An insurer must submit filing materials in a manner that allows the Department to identify the proposed hail deductible and evaluate its operation. An insurer must promptly provide additional information requested by the Department.

**B.5.11** Department approval does not relieve an insurer of responsibility for compliance with applicable insurance law. An insurer remains responsible for ensuring that its hail deductible provisions are not misleading, unfairly discriminatory, or inconsistent with the policy.

**B.5.12** An insurer may not represent that a hail deductible has been approved unless the Department has issued written approval for the applicable filing. Department inaction does not constitute approval.

**B.5.13** An approved hail deductible may become effective only as authorized by the Department’s written approval. An insurer must apply the approved provision in accordance with the terms of that approval.

**B.5.14** An insurer may not apply a hail deductible to a policy already in force unless the policy permits the application and the insurer has provided any notice or obtained any consent required by law. An insurer must administer any such change consistently with the policyholder’s contractual rights.

**B.5.15** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate the form, endorsement, rule, and notice used for each hail deductible. The records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.5.16** An insurer must discontinue use of a hail deductible provision when directed by the Department. An insurer must take appropriate action to correct any policyholder communication or claims practice that does not comply with this section.
