# Colorado Bulletin DOI-2022-08 — Roof Settlement Disclosures




Colorado Bulletin DOI-2022-08 · Edition 2022-08-09 · Page 1 of 12
## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This section states the Department’s expectations for roof settlement
disclosures provided by an admitted insurer writing property insurance coverage in
Colorado. An insurer must present disclosures in a manner that is clear, accurate, and
reasonably calculated to inform an insured of the basis for a roof-related settlement.

**B.1.2** This section applies when an insurer communicates a settlement, estimate,
payment, or coverage determination involving roof damage. The obligation applies whether
the communication is made directly to an insured or through an authorized
representative.

**B.1.3** An insurer must not use a disclosure that is misleading, incomplete, or
inconsistent with the applicable coverage terms. An insurer must provide sufficient
explanation for an insured to understand the material settlement basis.

**B.1.4** A roof settlement disclosure must distinguish the coverage determination from
the amount offered or paid for covered damage. An insurer must avoid language that could
reasonably cause an insured to misunderstand the scope of available coverage.

**B.1.5** This section applies to communications concerning repair, replacement, actual
cash value, depreciation, deductible application, and other settlement elements
affecting roof damage. An insurer must describe those elements consistently with the
applicable coverage.

**B.1.6** An insurer must ensure that a disclosure remains accurate when the settlement
position changes. A revised communication must clearly identify the basis for the
revision.

**B.1.7** An insurer may use plain-language explanations to supplement coverage
terminology. Such explanations must not narrow, expand, or replace the applicable
coverage terms.

**B.1.8** This section does not establish an all-other-perils deductible minimum. An
insurer remains responsible for accurately explaining any deductible that applies to a
roof-related loss.

**B.1.9** This section does not establish a coinsurance threshold. An insurer must
nevertheless disclose material coinsurance treatment when it affects the settlement of
roof damage.

**B.1.10** This section does not establish an ALE percentage. An insurer must
communicate any applicable additional living expense coverage consistently with the
applicable coverage terms.

**B.1.11** An insurer must provide disclosures in a format reasonably accessible to the
insured. The format must permit the insured to retain and review the information.

**B.1.12** An insurer must not condition an explanation of a roof settlement upon the
insured’s acceptance of the settlement. An insured must be able to obtain material
settlement information before deciding whether to accept payment.

**B.1.14** An insurer may provide disclosures electronically when the method is

**B.1.15** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this

**B.1.16** This section applies to an insurer’s handling of roof-related claims under

material to a roof settlement. An insurer must continue to satisfy all applicable
claims-handling obligations.

insurer must implement procedures reasonably necessary to comply with the disclosure
expectations stated herein.

**B.1.20** The Department may evaluate an insurer’s compliance with this section through
review of claims practices and records. An insurer must cooperate with a Department
inquiry concerning roof settlement disclosures.

**B.1.21** An insurer must interpret this section in a manner that promotes informed
claim decisions by insureds. Any ambiguity in a disclosure should be resolved through
clear explanation rather than omission.

**B.1.22** This section is intended to improve transparency in roof settlement
communications. An insurer must administer its disclosures consistently with the purpose
and applicability stated in this section.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer must comply with these requirements for a policy that provides
coverage for roof surfacing loss or damage. An insurer must administer the policy
consistently with the disclosures required by this section.

**B.2.2** An insurer must state the roof settlement method in clear policy language. An
insurer must not use language that is misleading, incomplete, or inconsistent with the
settlement method applied to a covered loss.

**B.2.3** An insurer must provide a clear disclosure when roof surfacing coverage is
subject to an actual cash value settlement method. The disclosure must identify that
depreciation may affect the amount payable for covered roof surfacing loss or damage.

**B.2.4** An insurer must distinguish roof surfacing coverage from coverage for other
damaged property when the applicable settlement methods differ. The disclosure must
explain that a different settlement method may apply to related property damage.

**B.2.5** An insurer must identify the roof surfacing materials subject to a scheduled
settlement method. An insurer must not suggest that all roof components are settled
under the same method unless that statement is accurate.

**B.2.6** An insurer may apply an actual cash value roof schedule to roof surfacing only
when the roof age is at least fifteen (15) years. An insurer must disclose the
circumstances under which the schedule applies.

**B.2.7** An insurer must make the applicable roof settlement schedule available to an
applicant or policyholder upon request. An insurer must provide the schedule in a form
that can be reasonably retained and reviewed.

**B.2.8** An insurer must describe how roof age is determined when roof age affects
settlement. The description must identify the information the insurer may use to
determine roof age.

**B.2.9** An insurer must not deny the use of reliable roof age information solely
because the information was not generated by the insurer. An insurer may request
reasonable documentation to evaluate the information.

**B.2.10** An insurer must explain how depreciation is applied to covered roof surfacing
loss or damage. The explanation must be sufficient to allow a policyholder to understand
the basis for the settlement.

**B.2.11** An insurer must not apply depreciation in a manner that conflicts with the
policy disclosure. An insurer must maintain claim practices that are consistent with the
disclosed roof settlement method.

**B.2.12** An insurer must state whether labor, materials, removal, disposal, and
related work are included in the roof surfacing settlement calculation. An insurer must
not omit material limitations affecting the payable amount.

**B.2.13** An insurer must identify any limitation that applies because of roof
surfacing type, condition, age, or expected useful life. The limitation must be stated
in language that a reasonable policyholder can understand.

**B.2.14** An insurer must not characterize a scheduled roof settlement as replacement
cost coverage. An insurer must not characterize actual cash value coverage as providing
payment without depreciation when depreciation may apply.

**B.2.15** An insurer must disclose whether the policy provides replacement cost
coverage for roof surfacing under any circumstances. The disclosure must state the
conditions that must be met for that settlement method to apply.

**B.2.16** An insurer must explain the effect of the policy deductible on a roof
surfacing claim. The explanation must not imply that the deductible is waived unless the
policy expressly provides for a waiver.

**B.2.17** An insurer must disclose material exclusions that may affect a roof surfacing

practical effect.

**B.2.18** An insurer must disclose material conditions that may affect eligibility for
roof surfacing payment. An insurer must not rely on an undisclosed condition to reduce
or limit a settlement.

**B.2.19** An insurer must provide required roof settlement disclosures before an
applicant is bound to coverage. An insurer must provide the disclosures in connection
with a renewal when the applicable roof settlement method changes.

**B.2.20** An insurer must provide a policyholder with the applicable roof settlement
disclosure when the insurer changes the policy terms affecting roof surfacing
settlement. The disclosure must be provided in a manner reasonably calculated to inform
the policyholder of the change.

**B.2.21** An insurer must ensure that an agent acting on its behalf has access to
current roof settlement disclosures. An insurer remains responsible for disclosures made
by its authorized representatives.

**B.2.22** An insurer must not use an application, quotation, estimate, or sales
communication that contradicts the policy roof settlement disclosure. An insurer must
correct material inconsistencies before issuing coverage.

**B.2.23** An insurer must provide claim communications that accurately describe the
settlement method applied to the claimed roof surfacing damage. The communication must
identify the basis for any depreciation or schedule adjustment.

**B.2.24** An insurer must provide a policyholder with a written explanation of a roof
surfacing settlement upon request. The explanation must identify the information used to
calculate the payment.

**B.2.25** An insurer must identify any information needed from a policyholder to
evaluate roof age, condition, or damage. An insurer must request that information in a
manner that does not unnecessarily delay claim handling.

**B.2.26** An insurer must consider information reasonably available from inspections,
records, photographs, receipts, or other reliable sources. An insurer must document the
information relied upon for a roof settlement determination.

**B.2.27** An insurer must not base a roof settlement on unsupported assumptions
regarding roof age or condition. An insurer must reasonably investigate material facts
affecting the settlement method.

**B.2.28** An insurer must apply the disclosed roof settlement method consistently to
similarly situated claims. An insurer may consider claim-specific facts when those facts
are relevant to coverage or valuation.

**B.2.29** An insurer must not use a roof settlement schedule that produces a result
inconsistent with the policy disclosure. An insurer must ensure that claim personnel can
determine when the schedule applies.

**B.2.31** An insurer must communicate any dispute concerning roof age, roof condition,
or roof settlement method to the policyholder. The communication must explain the basis
for the insurer’s position.

**B.2.32** An insurer must provide a reasonable opportunity for a policyholder to submit
additional information relevant to a disputed roof settlement. An insurer must consider

**B.2.33** An insurer must maintain records supporting the roof settlement method used
for a claim. The records must permit review of the disclosure, valuation, depreciation,

**B.2.34** An insurer must maintain records showing the policy language in effect when a

**B.2.35** An insurer must train claim personnel on the roof settlement methods offered

claim communications required by this section.

**B.2.36** An insurer must establish procedures for reviewing roof surfacing claim
determinations for consistency with the policy disclosure. The procedures must address
corrections when an error is identified.

**B.2.37** An insurer must correct a roof surfacing settlement that does not comply with
the applicable policy terms or these requirements. An insurer must provide an
explanation of the correction to the policyholder.

**B.2.38** An insurer must not impose a condition on roof surfacing payment that is not
authorized by the policy. An insurer must not use a claim practice that has the effect
of adding an undisclosed limitation.

**B.2.39** An insurer must not discourage a policyholder from reporting or pursuing a
roof surfacing claim by misstating the available settlement method. An insurer must
provide accurate information when responding to questions about roof coverage.

**B.2.40** An insurer must make roof settlement disclosures reasonably accessible to a
policyholder during the policy period and during claim handling. An insurer may provide
the disclosure electronically when permitted by law and accepted by the policyholder.

**B.2.41** An insurer must use disclosures that are legible and reasonably prominent. An

overlooked.

**B.2.42** An insurer must ensure that translations or alternative formats used for roof

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** An insurer must provide the notice and disclosures required by this section
when a claim includes roof damage. The notice must be clear, conspicuous, and written in
language reasonably calculated to be understood by the named insured.

**B.3.2** An insurer must provide the notice before issuing a settlement offer that
includes a roof valuation. An insurer must provide an updated notice when a material
change affects the roof settlement.

**B.3.3** An insurer must identify the method used to value the damaged roof. The
disclosure must explain whether the settlement is based on replacement cost, actual cash
value, or another valuation method permitted by the policy.

**B.3.4** An insurer must describe how depreciation affects the roof settlement when
depreciation is applied. The description must state whether depreciation is recoverable
and the conditions for recovering withheld depreciation.

**B.3.5** An insurer must identify any policy provision that limits payment for roof
damage based on roof age, roof condition, roof material, or cause of loss. The insurer
must describe the practical effect of the limitation on the settlement.

**B.3.6** An insurer must disclose when the policy provides actual cash value settlement
for a roof. The disclosure must explain that actual cash value may reflect depreciation
or condition.

**B.3.7** An insurer must disclose when the policy provides replacement cost settlement
for a roof. The disclosure must explain any conditions that must be met before
replacement cost benefits are payable.

**B.3.8** An insurer must explain any requirement that repair or replacement be
completed before additional payment is available. The explanation must identify the
information the insurer requires to consider a request for additional payment.

**B.3.9** An insurer must disclose any requirement that the insured incur repair or
replacement costs before payment is made. The disclosure must not suggest that the
insured is required to begin repairs before the insurer completes its claim
investigation.

**B.3.10** An insurer must identify the deductible applied to the roof claim. The
insurer must explain how the deductible affects the payment available under the policy.

**B.3.11** An insurer must state whether a separate deductible applies to the reported
cause of loss. The disclosure must identify the basis for applying that deductible under
the policy.

**B.3.12** An insurer must disclose any limitation applicable to matching materials. The
disclosure must explain whether payment is limited to damaged property or may include
undamaged property when necessary to achieve a reasonably uniform appearance.

**B.3.13** An insurer must identify any limitation applicable to ordinance or law
coverage. The disclosure must explain that coverage for code-related work may be subject
to policy conditions and limits.

**B.3.14** An insurer must disclose any limitation applicable to debris removal,
tear-off, disposal, or related roof work. The disclosure must state whether the claimed
work is included in the settlement estimate.

**B.3.15** An insurer must provide a written estimate when the insurer prepares or
relies upon an estimate for roof repairs. The estimate must identify the scope of work
used to calculate the proposed settlement.

**B.3.16** An insurer must describe material differences between the insurer's estimate
and an estimate submitted by the insured. The explanation must be sufficiently specific
to permit the insured to understand the basis for the difference.

**B.3.17** An insurer must identify roof components included in the insurer's estimate.
The estimate must distinguish repair work from replacement work when that distinction
affects coverage or payment.

**B.3.18** An insurer must identify roof components excluded from the insurer's estimate
when the insured has requested payment for those components. The insurer must state the
coverage basis for the exclusion.

**B.3.19** An insurer must explain any determination that damage is unrelated to the
reported loss. The explanation must identify the information relied upon for that
determination.

**B.3.20** An insurer must explain any determination that roof damage resulted from
wear, deterioration, defective construction, maintenance, or another excluded cause. The
insurer must not characterize a cause as excluded without a reasonable basis in the
claim file.

**B.3.21** An insurer must identify the policy language supporting a denial or partial
denial of roof coverage. The disclosure must connect the policy language to the facts
supporting the coverage decision.

**B.3.22** An insurer must disclose when payment is issued for undisputed roof damage
while other portions of the claim remain under review. The disclosure must state that
payment of an undisputed portion does not resolve a disputed portion unless the parties
agree otherwise.

**B.3.23** An insurer must state whether the proposed payment represents a final
settlement of the roof claim. An insurer must not represent that a payment is final when
the policy permits additional payment upon satisfaction of stated conditions.

**B.3.24** An insurer must provide contact information for a person or unit able to
explain the roof settlement. The insurer must provide a reasonable means for the insured
to request clarification or submit additional information.

**B.3.25** An insurer must inform the insured of the right to submit documents,
photographs, contractor estimates, or other information relevant to the roof claim. The
insurer must consider relevant information received from the insured.

**B.3.26** An insurer must disclose when an inspection has been completed without
finding covered roof damage. The insurer must provide a clear explanation of the basis
for that conclusion.

**B.3.27** An insurer must disclose when an inspection identifies covered roof damage
but does not support full roof replacement. The disclosure must explain the
repairability determination in plain language.

**B.3.28** An insurer must identify any material inspection finding that supports the
settlement decision. An insurer must maintain documentation supporting the finding in
the claim file.

**B.3.29** An insurer must not use a notice or disclosure that is misleading,
incomplete, or inconsistent with the policy. An insurer must correct a material error
promptly after discovering the error.

**B.3.30** An insurer must ensure that a vendor, adjuster, or other representative
acting on its behalf provides disclosures consistent with this section. The insurer
remains responsible for the adequacy of communications made on its behalf.

**B.3.31** An insurer may provide the required notice and disclosures electronically
when permitted by applicable law and the policyholder's consent. The electronic
communication must be capable of being retained and reviewed by the insured.

**B.3.32** An insurer must provide the notice and disclosures in a manner accessible to
an insured who requests reasonable assistance. The insurer must not condition claim
consideration on the insured's use of a particular communication method.

**B.3.33** An insurer must retain the notice and disclosures provided to the insured in
the claim file. The claim file must reflect the date and method by which the information
was provided.

**B.3.34** An insurer must retain records supporting a roof valuation, depreciation
determination, and coverage decision. The records must be sufficient to demonstrate
compliance with this section.

**B.3.35** An insurer must apply this section to communications concerning a roof claim
made after this section becomes effective. An insurer may use revised claim materials
that incorporate the required disclosures.

**B.3.36** An insurer must not delay adjustment or payment of a roof claim solely to
prepare a notice required by this section. The insurer must provide required disclosures
as part of its ordinary claim handling process.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer handling a policyholder property
claim involving roof damage to property located in Colorado. An insurer must apply these
standards regardless of the settlement method available under the policy.

**B.4.2** This section is effective upon issuance of this bulletin. An insurer must
comply with this section for claim activity conducted while this bulletin is effective.

**B.4.3** An insurer must provide a clear written explanation of the proposed roof
settlement before requesting acceptance of that settlement. The explanation must be
sufficient to allow the policyholder to understand the basis for payment.

**B.4.4** An insurer must identify whether the settlement is based on actual cash value,
replacement cost value, repair cost, or another valuation method authorized by the
policy. An insurer may not describe a settlement in a manner that obscures the valuation
method used.

**B.4.5** An insurer must identify the roof components included in the scope of loss.
The disclosure must distinguish between damaged components, undamaged components, and
components excluded from the proposed settlement.

**B.4.6** An insurer must provide the policyholder with the estimate supporting the
proposed settlement. The estimate must be reasonably itemized and must identify
material, labor, and applicable deductions.

**B.4.7** An insurer must explain material estimate entries that are not readily
understandable to a reasonable policyholder. An insurer must provide the explanation in
plain language and may not rely solely on abbreviations or internal estimating
terminology.

**B.4.8** When depreciation is applied, an insurer must disclose the depreciation
applied to the settlement. The disclosure must explain whether depreciation is
recoverable under the policy and the conditions applicable to recovery.

**B.4.9** An insurer must identify any amount withheld pending repair or replacement. An
insurer must explain the policy conditions that must be satisfied before the withheld
amount may be paid.

**B.4.10** An insurer must disclose the applicable deductible and explain how the
deductible affects the settlement. An insurer may not present the deductible as a charge
imposed by a contractor or other person not authorized by the policy.

**B.4.11** An insurer must disclose whether the proposed settlement contemplates repair,
replacement, or another method of resolving the roof damage. If the insurer proposes

repair, the disclosure must describe the repair work included in the scope.

**B.4.12** An insurer must address material differences between the insurer's estimate
and a contractor estimate submitted by the policyholder. An insurer must explain the
basis for any disputed scope, pricing, or damage determination.

**B.4.13** An insurer may request information reasonably needed to evaluate a contractor
estimate or supplemental claim submission. An insurer must communicate the information
requested in a manner that does not improperly delay claim handling.

**B.4.14** An insurer must provide a written response when it denies coverage for
claimed roof damage or declines to include claimed work in the settlement. The response
must identify the policy basis and factual basis for the determination.

**B.4.15** An insurer must explain any determination that damage is attributable to
wear, deterioration, faulty workmanship, or another excluded cause. The explanation must
distinguish the excluded condition from covered damage when both may be present.

**B.4.16** An insurer may not represent that a policyholder must use a particular
contractor, vendor, or repair method unless the policy expressly requires that result.
An insurer must make clear that the policyholder may obtain information from contractors
or other qualified persons.

**B.4.17** An insurer may not characterize a settlement as full and final unless the
statement is accurate under the policy and the facts known to the insurer. An insurer
must identify any claim rights that remain available after payment.

**B.4.18** An insurer must explain the purpose of each payment issued for roof damage.
If a payment relates to a particular portion of the scope, the insurer must identify
that portion with reasonable clarity.

**B.4.19** An insurer must provide updated settlement information when it changes the
scope, valuation, depreciation, deductible treatment, or coverage determination. The
updated information must explain the reason for the change.

**B.4.20** An insurer must respond to a policyholder's reasonable request for
clarification concerning the roof settlement. The response must address the question
presented and may not merely repeat unexplained estimate language.

**B.4.21** An insurer must ensure that claim personnel communicating settlement
information have access to the applicable policy provisions and claim file materials. An
insurer must provide supervision sufficient to promote accurate roof settlement
disclosures.

**B.4.22** An insurer may provide required disclosures electronically when the
policyholder has agreed to receive claim communications electronically or otherwise
requests electronic delivery. An insurer must provide the disclosures in a format
reasonably capable of being retained and reviewed.

**B.4.23** An insurer must maintain claim file documentation showing the disclosures
provided under this section. The documentation must identify the information provided to
the policyholder and the manner of delivery.

**B.4.24** An insurer must not condition consideration of a supplemental roof claim on
acceptance of an earlier settlement. An insurer may require reasonable supporting
information consistent with the policy and the claim circumstances.

**B.4.25** An insurer must not use a disclosure that is misleading, incomplete, or
inconsistent with the policy. A disclosure may not waive, reduce, or alter coverage
without a policy basis.

**B.4.26** An insurer must inform a policyholder of available claim review or complaint
options when the policyholder disputes a roof settlement and requests that information.
The insurer must provide that information without discouraging the policyholder from
seeking review.

**B.4.27** Compliance with this section does not alter the terms of an insurance policy
or limit obligations otherwise imposed on an insurer. An insurer must apply this section
in a manner consistent with the policy and the insurer's claim-handling duties.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** An insurer must file each roof settlement disclosure it intends to use with an
applicable policy. The filing must identify the policy forms and endorsements with which
the disclosure will be used.

**B.5.2** An insurer must submit the disclosure in a form that is complete, legible, and
suitable for review. The submitted version must match the version provided to
policyholders and applicants.

**B.5.3** An insurer may not use a roof settlement disclosure before filing it with the
Department. An insurer must discontinue use of a disclosure that differs from the
disclosure on file.

**B.5.4** A filing must clearly identify the insurer making the filing. An insurer that
files through an authorized representative remains responsible for compliance with this
paragraph.

**B.5.5** A filing must describe the circumstances in which the roof settlement
disclosure will be provided. The description must permit the Department to determine
whether the disclosure is provided when required.

**B.5.6** An insurer must file any revision to a roof settlement disclosure before using
the revised disclosure. A revision includes a change that affects the disclosure’s
content, presentation, or applicability.

**B.5.7** An insurer must withdraw a roof settlement disclosure from use when the
disclosure is no longer applicable to the insurer’s policies. The insurer must maintain
records sufficient to identify the withdrawn disclosure.

**B.5.8** A filing under this paragraph must be made through the Department’s designated
filing process. An insurer must provide supporting information requested by the
Department for review of the filing.

**B.5.9** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure is consistent with the policy
language that governs roof loss settlement. The disclosure may not alter, limit, expand,

or replace the policy terms.

**B.5.10** An insurer must ensure that a filed disclosure is understandable to a
reasonable policyholder. The disclosure must not use wording that is misleading,
deceptive, or likely to cause confusion.

**B.5.11** An insurer may use a disclosure filed by an affiliated insurer only if the
filing identifies the insurer that will use the disclosure. Each insurer using the
disclosure remains responsible for its accuracy and compliance.

**B.5.12** The Department may require an insurer to revise or withdraw a disclosure that
does not comply with this paragraph. An insurer must comply with a Department request
concerning a filed disclosure.

**B.5.13** Filing a disclosure does not relieve an insurer of its obligation to comply
with applicable law. An insurer remains responsible for the administration of roof loss
claims in accordance with the policy and governing requirements.

**B.5.14** An insurer must retain the materials submitted with a filing and the version
of the disclosure placed into use. The retained materials must be available to the
Department upon request.

**B.5.15** An insurer must provide a current roof settlement disclosure when the
disclosure is required for a policyholder or applicant. An insurer may not provide a
superseded disclosure in place of the current disclosure.

**B.5.16** This paragraph applies to an insurer that issues or renews policies providing
coverage for roof losses in this state. An insurer must comply with this paragraph upon
using a roof settlement disclosure.
