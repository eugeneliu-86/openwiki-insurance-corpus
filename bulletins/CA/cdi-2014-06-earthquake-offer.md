# California Bulletin CDI-2014-06 — Mandatory Earthquake Coverage Offer
> SUPERSEDED by California Bulletin CDI-2022-03 — Mandatory Earthquake Coverage Offer for policies effective on or after 2022-03-14. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

This bulletin states the Department’s expectations for an admitted insurer that issues residential property insurance in California. An insurer subject to this bulletin must make the required offer of earthquake coverage in a manner that is clear, timely, and capable of verification.

The requirement applies when an insurer offers or issues residential property insurance for property located in California. An insurer may use its customary underwriting and policy administration processes only if those processes preserve the required earthquake coverage offer.

An insurer must treat the earthquake coverage offer as a required component of the residential property insurance transaction. The offer must not be presented in a manner that obscures its availability or discourages reasonable consideration by the applicant or insured.

An insurer must provide information sufficient for an applicant or insured to understand that earthquake damage may not be covered by the underlying residential property insurance. The insurer must accurately describe the coverage offered and any material limitations that apply.

An insurer may offer earthquake coverage through an affiliated insurer, a participating insurer, or another lawful arrangement. The insurer remains responsible for ensuring that the offer satisfies the requirements applicable to the residential property insurance transaction.

An insurer must make the offer using forms, notices, and procedures that are consistent with California law. An insurer must maintain records demonstrating that the offer was made and that any response was recorded appropriately.

An insurer must apply the requirement without regard to whether an applicant or insured is expected to accept the offered coverage. The obligation to make the offer is distinct from the applicant’s or insured’s decision concerning the coverage.

An insurer may permit an applicant or insured to decline earthquake coverage. A declination process must be reasonably designed to show that the applicant or insured had the opportunity to consider the offer.

An insurer must not represent that earthquake coverage is included in residential property insurance unless the policy terms provide that coverage. An insurer must not use policy language, sales materials, or communications that are misleading concerning earthquake coverage.

An insurer must communicate the relationship between the offered earthquake coverage and the underlying residential property insurance in plain language. The communication must identify material differences in coverage, exclusions, deductibles, and conditions.

An insurer must explain the all-other-perils deductible minimum in a manner that does not confuse it with the earthquake coverage deductible. An insurer must administer deductible disclosures consistently with the terms of the coverage offered.

An insurer must describe the coinsurance threshold accurately when that concept is applicable to the offered earthquake coverage. The description must not imply that coverage applies more broadly than the policy terms allow.

An insurer must address the ALE percentage accurately when additional living expense coverage is part of the offer. The insurer must distinguish that coverage from other policy benefits and limitations.

An insurer may use electronic communications to make the required offer when the communication method is lawful and reasonably calculated to reach the applicant or insured. The insurer must preserve evidence of delivery and any response when electronic records are used.

An insurer must train personnel involved in marketing, underwriting, policy issuance, and customer service on the requirement to make the earthquake coverage offer. Training must support accurate communications and consistent administration.

An insurer must ensure that producers acting on its behalf are provided with procedures sufficient to make the required offer. The insurer remains accountable for the adequacy of its producer oversight.

An insurer must not condition the availability of the required offer on the purchase of unrelated coverage or services. An insurer may apply lawful underwriting criteria to the earthquake coverage offered.

An insurer must process accepted offers in accordance with the coverage terms presented to the applicant or insured. Any change to the offered terms must be communicated clearly before coverage is issued.

An insurer must retain records concerning offers, acceptances, declinations, policy issuance, and related communications. Records must be maintained in a form that can be provided to the Department upon request.

An insurer must review its forms and procedures when changes in operations could affect compliance with this bulletin. The review must address whether the required offer remains clear and consistently available.

An insurer must correct identified deficiencies promptly and must take reasonable measures to prevent recurrence. Corrective action must include appropriate review of affected transactions when warranted.

This bulletin applies to an admitted insurer conducting residential property insurance business in California. An insurer must comply with the requirements described in this section for transactions subject to the mandatory earthquake coverage offer.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** These requirements apply to each admitted insurer required to make an earthquake coverage offer in this state. An insurer must implement these requirements on and after the effective date of this bulletin.

**B.2.2** An insurer must make the earthquake coverage offer in connection with a policy to which the offer requirement applies. The offer must be made in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the applicant or insured of the available coverage.

**B.2.3** An insurer may use an application, endorsement, notice, or other written communication to present the earthquake coverage offer. The communication must be clear, conspicuous, and understandable to a reasonable applicant or insured.

**B.2.4** An insurer must identify the earthquake coverage as coverage separate from the underlying policy. An insurer must not describe the underlying policy as providing earthquake coverage unless that coverage is actually included.

**B.2.5** An insurer must state the principal coverages available under the earthquake policy or endorsement. The description must not omit a material limitation that would reasonably affect an applicant's decision whether to accept the offer.

**B.2.6** An insurer must disclose that earthquake coverage may be subject to exclusions, conditions, and limitations. The disclosure must be consistent with the policy language the insurer will issue.

**B.2.7** An insurer must describe the deductible applicable to earthquake coverage in plain language. The earthquake coverage offered under this paragraph must provide a seismic deductible of ten (10) percent.

**B.2.8** An insurer must explain the basis on which the deductible applies. The explanation must permit an applicant or insured to understand that a loss may be subject to the deductible before payment is made.

**B.2.9** An insurer must not characterize the deductible as insignificant, nominal, or otherwise minimize its effect. Any illustration used by an insurer must fairly reflect the operation of the deductible.

**B.2.10** An insurer must identify whether the earthquake coverage is subject to a separate policy limit or other coverage limitation. The insurer must present that information in a manner that does not obscure a material restriction.

**B.2.11** An insurer must provide sufficient information for an applicant or insured to request the offered earthquake coverage. The insurer must provide a reasonable means to communicate acceptance of the offer.

**B.2.12** An insurer must process an acceptance of the earthquake coverage offer in accordance with its filed underwriting practices. An insurer must not impose an undisclosed condition on acceptance.

**B.2.13** An insurer may require information reasonably necessary to underwrite earthquake coverage. The insurer must inform the applicant or insured when additional information is needed to complete the request.

**B.2.14** An insurer must apply its underwriting standards consistently to applicants and insureds seeking earthquake coverage. An insurer must not use the offer process to discourage a lawful request for coverage.

**B.2.15** An insurer must make the offer available through each distribution channel used for the applicable underlying policy. The insurer must maintain procedures to ensure that its producers understand those requirements.

**B.2.16** An insurer remains responsible for compliance when an offer is presented by an agent, broker, administrator, or other representative. An insurer must provide appropriate instructions and oversight to persons acting on its behalf.

**B.2.17** An insurer must ensure that producer communications concerning earthquake coverage are accurate. An insurer must correct a material misstatement or omission promptly upon discovery.

**B.2.18** An insurer must not condition the availability of the required offer on the purchase of another product or service. An insurer may offer related coverage only if the applicant or insured is able to evaluate the earthquake coverage independently.

**B.2.19** An insurer must not require an applicant or insured to waive the opportunity to consider earthquake coverage through a preselected election. Any declination process must allow an informed and voluntary decision.

**B.2.20** An insurer must preserve evidence that the required offer was made. The evidence must identify the applicable policy, the person to whom the offer was made, and the form of the offer.

**B.2.21** An insurer must preserve evidence of an acceptance, declination, or other response to the offer. The evidence must be sufficient to demonstrate the insurer's compliance with this section.

**B.2.22** An insurer may retain compliance records in paper or electronic form. Electronic records must remain accessible and reproducible for examination by the Department.

**B.2.23** An insurer must maintain procedures for responding to an inquiry concerning the availability of earthquake coverage. The procedures must direct personnel to provide information consistent with the insurer's approved materials.

**B.2.24** An insurer must train personnel responsible for presenting or processing the earthquake coverage offer. The training must address the coverage description, deductible, limitations, and documentation requirements.

**B.2.25** An insurer must maintain internal controls reasonably designed to identify failures to make the required offer. The controls must provide for correction of identified errors.

**B.2.26** An insurer must review complaints concerning earthquake coverage offers for indications of a systemic compliance issue. An insurer must take appropriate corrective action when a review identifies such an issue.

**B.2.27** An insurer must respond accurately to an applicant or insured who asks whether earthquake damage is covered under the underlying policy. The response must distinguish between coverage provided by the underlying policy and coverage available through the earthquake offer.

**B.2.28** An insurer must not use language that suggests earthquake coverage is automatically included when it is not. An insurer must not use language that suggests an applicant or insured is ineligible without applying the insurer's underwriting standards.

**B.2.29** An insurer must provide any material coverage summary in a readable format. The summary must be consistent with the policy, endorsement, or contract proposed for issuance.

**B.2.30** An insurer must identify material exclusions applicable to earthquake coverage when describing the offer. The insurer must not rely on a general statement of exclusions to conceal a significant limitation.

**B.2.31** An insurer must identify material conditions that may affect payment of an earthquake claim. The insurer must state those conditions in language that is not misleading.

**B.2.32** An insurer must ensure that premium information associated with the offer is accurately stated when provided. An insurer must not represent an estimated premium as fixed if the premium remains subject to underwriting or other lawful adjustment.

**B.2.33** An insurer may provide premium estimates before completing underwriting. Any estimate must be clearly identified as an estimate and must not misrepresent the basis for a later premium determination.

**B.2.34** An insurer must provide a reasonable method for an applicant or insured to ask questions about the earthquake offer. The insurer must ensure that responses to those questions are consistent with approved coverage materials.

**B.2.35** An insurer must not delay the required offer for the purpose of avoiding an applicant's or insured's consideration of earthquake coverage. An insurer must present the offer at a time reasonably related to the applicable policy transaction.

**B.2.36** An insurer must not withdraw an accepted offer except as permitted by its underwriting standards and applicable law. Any withdrawal must be communicated clearly to the affected applicant or insured.

**B.2.37** An insurer must issue earthquake coverage that conforms to the accepted offer, subject to disclosed underwriting conditions. If the issued coverage differs materially from the offer, the insurer must provide a clear explanation of the difference.

**B.2.38** An insurer must provide policy documents that identify the earthquake coverage purchased. The documents must identify the applicable deductible and material limitations.

**B.2.39** An insurer must not use an earthquake coverage offer to mislead an applicant or insured about claim payment. An insurer must avoid statements that promise payment without regard to policy terms and applicable facts.

**B.2.40** An insurer must handle communications concerning earthquake coverage in good faith. An insurer must not make a statement that is false, deceptive, or materially incomplete.

**B.2.41** An insurer must apply the same standards of clarity to communications delivered electronically as to communications delivered in paper form. An electronic presentation must permit the applicant or insured to retain the material information.

**B.2.42** An insurer must make reasonable accommodations in its offer process when necessary to communicate effectively with an applicant or insured. The accommodation must preserve the substance of the required offer.

**B.2.43** An insurer must ensure that automated systems used to generate offers present current and approved coverage information. An insurer must correct an automated system error that affects a required offer.

**B.2.44** An insurer must test material changes to systems, forms, scripts, and procedures that affect the earthquake coverage offer. The testing must be reasonably designed to prevent inaccurate or incomplete offers.

**B.2.45** An insurer must maintain version control for materials used to present earthquake coverage. The insurer must be able to identify the material used for a particular offer.

**B.2.46** An insurer must ensure that any translated earthquake coverage material accurately conveys the material terms of the offer. A translation must not omit a limitation, condition, or deductible explanation included in the corresponding material.

**B.2.47** An insurer must not rely solely on oral statements to establish compliance when its process otherwise creates written or electronic evidence. The insurer must preserve available documentation of the offer and response.

**B.2.48** An insurer must provide its compliance records to the Department upon request. The records must be organized in a manner that allows the Department to evaluate the insurer's offer practices.

**B.2.49** An insurer must cooperate with a Department examination concerning earthquake coverage offers. An insurer must provide a complete and accurate explanation of its procedures when requested.

**B.2.50** An insurer must promptly investigate a credible indication that a required offer was omitted or inaccurately presented. The investigation must include review of the affected transaction and relevant business practices.

**B.2.51** An insurer must take remedial action when it determines that an applicant or insured did not receive a required offer. The remedial action must be reasonably designed to provide the affected person a meaningful opportunity to consider coverage.

**B.2.52** An insurer must document corrective action taken in response to an identified compliance failure. The documentation must identify the nature of the failure and the steps taken to address it.

**B.2.53** An insurer must ensure that its complaint-handling procedures recognize complaints involving earthquake coverage offers. The procedures must provide for review by personnel able to evaluate the offer requirements.

**B.2.54** An insurer must not retaliate against an applicant, insured, producer, or employee for raising a good-faith concern regarding compliance with this section. An insurer must address such concerns through appropriate compliance procedures.

**B.2.55** An insurer must ensure that marketing or promotional materials do not contradict the required earthquake coverage offer. Materials that discuss earthquake protection must accurately distinguish available coverage from uninsured risk.

**B.2.56** An insurer must revise its offer materials when a material change affects the accuracy of those materials. An insurer must withdraw obsolete materials from use.

**B.2.57** An insurer must monitor delegated service providers that perform functions related to the earthquake coverage offer. The insurer must require those service providers to follow procedures consistent with this section.

**B.2.58** An insurer must ensure that a transfer of servicing responsibility does not interrupt compliance with the earthquake coverage offer requirement. The insurer must preserve relevant records through the transfer.

**B.2.59** An insurer must apply these requirements to renewals when an earthquake coverage offer is required in connection with renewal. An insurer must not treat a prior declination as a substitute for a required offer.

**B.2.60** An insurer must interpret and administer this section in a manner that furthers an informed opportunity to obtain earthquake coverage. An insurer remains responsible for compliance with each applicable requirement of this section.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** An admitted insurer that offers residential property insurance in this state must make an earthquake coverage offer that is clear, conspicuous, and capable of being accepted or declined by the applicant or insured. The offer must be provided in accordance with the requirements of this paragraph.

**B.3.2** An insurer must provide the notice required by **B.3.1** at the time the insurer offers residential property insurance. An insurer must also provide the notice when presenting a renewal offer.

**B.3.3** An insurer must identify earthquake coverage as coverage that is separate from the residential property insurance otherwise offered. The notice must not suggest that earthquake coverage is included unless it is in fact included.

**B.3.4** An insurer must state plainly that damage caused by earthquake may not be covered unless earthquake coverage is purchased. The notice must describe the consequence of declining the earthquake coverage offer without using misleading language.

**B.3.5** An insurer must present the earthquake coverage offer in a manner reasonably likely to be seen by the applicant or insured. An insurer may not obscure the offer through placement, typography, or unrelated disclosures.

**B.3.6** An insurer must use language that an ordinary applicant or insured can understand. Technical terms may be used only when accompanied by an understandable explanation.

**B.3.7** An insurer must disclose that the terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions of earthquake coverage may differ from those applicable to the residential property insurance. The disclosure must not imply that all causes of loss are covered.

**B.3.8** An insurer must disclose that the premium for earthquake coverage is separate from the premium for the residential property insurance, unless the insurer clearly explains another applicable billing arrangement. The insurer must provide sufficient information for the applicant or insured to understand that accepting the offer may affect the premium charged.

**B.3.9** An insurer must disclose the applicable deductible in a manner that permits the applicant or insured to understand how the deductible may apply to a covered loss. The disclosure must not characterize a deductible as insignificant or nominal.

**B.3.10** An insurer must disclose the applicable coverage limits or explain how those limits will be determined. The disclosure must not imply that coverage limits will necessarily equal the amount of a loss.

**B.3.11** An insurer must disclose material exclusions and limitations that may affect an applicant’s or insured’s decision regarding earthquake coverage. The disclosure must be reasonably specific and may not rely solely on general statements that exclusions may apply.

**B.3.12** An insurer must disclose any material condition affecting when earthquake coverage becomes effective. The notice must clearly distinguish an offer of coverage from coverage that has been accepted and placed in force.

**B.3.13** An insurer must state the action required to accept the earthquake coverage offer. The instructions must identify any information, signature, authorization, or payment required by the insurer.

**B.3.14** An insurer must state how an applicant or insured may decline the earthquake coverage offer. An insurer may not make a declination process more burdensome than is reasonably necessary to document the decision.

**B.3.15** An insurer must not treat silence as an acceptance of earthquake coverage unless the applicant or insured has otherwise provided affirmative authorization. The insurer must retain evidence supporting any asserted authorization.

**B.3.16** An insurer must not represent that an applicant or insured has declined earthquake coverage without a reasonable basis for that representation. A record of declination must accurately reflect the decision made.

**B.3.17** An insurer must provide contact information through which an applicant or insured may obtain additional information about the earthquake coverage offer. The contact information must be reasonably accessible and current.

**B.3.18** An insurer must ensure that personnel involved in presenting the offer can provide accurate general information about the coverage. An insurer must not permit personnel to make statements that conflict with the notice required by **B.3.1**.

**B.3.19** An insurer must ensure that communications by an appointed agent or other representative comply with the disclosure requirements of this paragraph. The insurer remains responsible for the content of an offer made on its behalf.

**B.3.20** An insurer may provide the notice electronically if the applicant or insured has agreed to electronic delivery in a manner permitted by applicable law. Electronic presentation must preserve the clarity and conspicuousness required by **B.3.5**.

**B.3.21** An insurer must provide a printable or retainable version of an electronically delivered notice. The retained version must contain the material disclosures presented to the applicant or insured.

**B.3.22** An insurer must make reasonable accommodations for an applicant or insured who requests an accessible method of receiving the notice. An accommodation must not alter the substance of the earthquake coverage offer.

**B.3.23** An insurer must not use illustrations, examples, or comparisons that are likely to mislead an applicant or insured regarding earthquake coverage. Any illustration used must be consistent with the coverage actually offered.

**B.3.24** An insurer must not state or imply that earthquake coverage is endorsed, guaranteed, or approved by the state merely because the insurer is authorized to transact insurance in this state. The notice must avoid governmental sponsorship implications.

**B.3.25** An insurer must not state or imply that an applicant or insured is required to purchase earthquake coverage as a condition of obtaining residential property insurance unless such a condition is expressly applicable. Any applicable condition must be stated clearly.

**B.3.26** An insurer must not condition the availability of the earthquake coverage offer on the purchase of unrelated insurance products. An insurer may describe other available products if the description does not interfere with the notice required by **B.3.1**.

**B.3.27** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate that it provided the earthquake coverage offer and required disclosures. The records must identify the method used to provide the offer.

**B.3.28** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate the applicant’s or insured’s acceptance or declination of earthquake coverage. The records must be capable of being produced upon request by the Department.

**B.3.29** An insurer must correct a material error in an earthquake coverage notice when the insurer discovers the error. The insurer must take reasonable steps to provide corrected information to affected applicants or insureds.

**B.3.30** An insurer must review its notice and disclosure practices when it changes the earthquake coverage offered. The insurer must ensure that the notice continues to describe the coverage accurately.

**B.3.31** An insurer must ensure that any summary used with the earthquake coverage offer is consistent with the coverage terms. A summary may not replace disclosures required by **B.3.7** through **B.3.12**.

**B.3.32** An insurer must provide the earthquake coverage offer without using coercive, deceptive, or unfair sales practices. An insurer may explain the potential benefits of coverage if the explanation is balanced and accurate.

**B.3.33** An insurer must not discourage an applicant or insured from reviewing the earthquake coverage offer by characterizing the offer as routine or unimportant. The insurer must allow a reasonable opportunity to consider the disclosed information.

**B.3.34** An insurer must ensure that a declination notice does not falsely state that the applicant or insured understands every coverage consequence of declining the offer. A declination notice may confirm that the offer was presented and declined.

**B.3.35** An insurer must ensure that disclosures concerning earthquake coverage remain legible when reproduced, transmitted, or displayed electronically. An insurer may not use formatting that materially interferes with comprehension.

**B.3.36** An insurer must apply the requirements of this paragraph to communications generated through automated systems. Automation does not relieve an insurer of responsibility for the accuracy or completeness of the notice.

**B.3.37** An insurer must adopt procedures reasonably designed to monitor compliance with **B.3.1** through **B.3.36**. The procedures must address notices provided by the insurer and by persons acting on the insurer’s behalf.

**B.3.38** An insurer must make its notice and disclosure practices available for examination by the Department. Failure to provide the required offer or disclosure may constitute a violation of this paragraph.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** These standards apply to claims arising under earthquake coverage issued by an admitted insurer. An insurer must apply these standards to claims received on or after the effective date of this bulletin.

**B.4.2** An insurer must adopt and maintain written procedures for the prompt, fair, and equitable handling of earthquake claims. The procedures must be applied consistently by personnel and representatives acting on the insurer’s behalf.

**B.4.3** An insurer must acknowledge a claim or claim-related communication promptly. The acknowledgment must provide contact information for the person responsible for handling the claim.

**B.4.4** An insurer must provide a claimant with reasonable assistance in understanding the claim process. An insurer must communicate in plain language when explaining requested information, coverage terms, and claim decisions.

**B.4.5** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation before denying a claim or limiting payment. The investigation must address the reported loss, the applicable policy provisions, and information reasonably available to the insurer.

**B.4.6** An insurer must not require a claimant to provide information that is not reasonably related to the claim. An insurer must identify the information needed to evaluate the claim and explain the reason for the request when appropriate.

**B.4.7** An insurer must promptly assign qualified personnel to investigate and evaluate an earthquake claim. Personnel handling the claim must have sufficient knowledge of earthquake coverage and the policy provisions at issue.

**B.4.8** An insurer must evaluate reported damage using competent and objective methods. An insurer must not rely on an estimate, report, or opinion that it knows or reasonably should know is incomplete or unreliable.

**B.4.9** An insurer may inspect damaged property when reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim. An insurer must conduct an inspection in a manner that reasonably accommodates the claimant and the condition of the property.

**B.4.10** An insurer must give reasonable consideration to information submitted by a claimant. An insurer must document material information that supports its claim determination.

**B.4.11** An insurer must explain any request for additional investigation or documentation that may delay a claim determination. The explanation must identify the matter requiring further review.

**B.4.12** An insurer must provide a written claim decision when it approves, denies, or partially denies a claim. The decision must identify the relevant policy provisions and state the basis for the determination.

**B.4.13** An insurer must not deny a claim solely because the claimant cannot produce information that is unavailable through no fault of the claimant. An insurer must consider other reasonably available evidence concerning the loss.

**B.4.14** An insurer must distinguish covered earthquake damage from damage that is not covered under the policy when making a partial claim determination. The insurer must explain the basis for that distinction in its written communication.

**B.4.15** An insurer must not misrepresent pertinent facts or policy provisions relating to earthquake coverage. An insurer must correct a material misstatement promptly after learning of it.

**B.4.16** An insurer must advise a claimant of material policy conditions that may affect payment of the claim. The advice must be provided in a manner that does not discourage the claimant from pursuing a valid claim.

**B.4.17** An insurer must make payment for undisputed covered amounts without conditioning payment on resolution of disputed portions of the claim. Payment of an undisputed amount does not relieve the insurer of its duty to continue evaluating the remaining claim.

**B.4.18** An insurer must provide a reasonable explanation when it offers less than the amount requested by a claimant. The explanation must identify the facts, estimates, or policy provisions supporting the offer.

**B.4.19** An insurer must maintain claim files that document material communications, investigation activities, evaluations, and claim decisions. The file must be sufficient to permit review of the insurer’s compliance with these standards.

**B.4.20** An insurer must supervise adjusters, contractors, and other representatives involved in earthquake claim handling. An insurer remains responsible for claim-handling conduct performed on its behalf.

**B.4.21** An insurer must protect nonpublic personal information obtained during the investigation and adjustment of a claim. An insurer may disclose such information only as permitted by applicable law and as reasonably necessary to handle the claim.

**B.4.22** An insurer must establish procedures to identify and address patterns of claim-handling practices that may result in unfair treatment of claimants. Corrective action must be taken when an insurer identifies a material deficiency.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** An admitted insurer must file the mandatory earthquake coverage offer and all related materials with the Department before using them in this state. The filing must clearly identify the insurer submitting the materials.

**B.5.2** An insurer must submit materials that are complete, accurate, and consistent with the requirements of this bulletin. An insurer may not use incomplete materials as an earthquake coverage offer.

**B.5.3** The filing must identify the policy forms to which the earthquake coverage offer applies. The filing must also describe the manner in which the offer will be presented to eligible policyholders.

**B.5.4** An insurer must include the proposed offer language in the filing. The language must be sufficiently clear to permit the Department to determine whether the offer complies with this section.

**B.5.5** An insurer must identify any application, notice, endorsement, disclosure, or other material used with the offer. Materials submitted for review must be legible and suitable for reproduction.

**B.5.6** An insurer must identify the proposed effective date for each filed material. An insurer may not use a material before the effective date identified in the filing and accepted by the Department.

**B.5.7** An insurer must promptly revise a filing when a material change affects the earthquake coverage offer. The revised filing must identify the changes made and the reason for the changes.

**B.5.8** An insurer may withdraw a pending filing by providing written notice to the Department. A withdrawn filing may not be used unless the insurer submits a new filing in accordance with this section.

**B.5.9** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with the filing requirements of this section. The records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.5.10** An insurer must ensure that filed materials match the materials made available to policyholders. An insurer may not alter approved offer language without filing the altered language as required by this section.

**B.5.11** An insurer must provide a clear explanation of any material that may not be apparent from the face of the filing. The explanation must allow the Department to evaluate the intended use of the material.

**B.5.12** An insurer must identify a contact person authorized to respond to Department inquiries concerning the filing. The contact information must remain current while the filing is pending.

**B.5.13** Department review of a filing does not relieve an insurer of responsibility for compliance with applicable law. An insurer remains responsible for the accuracy and operation of its earthquake coverage offer.

**B.5.14** An insurer must discontinue use of a material if the Department determines that the material does not comply with this section. The insurer must take appropriate action to correct affected materials.

**B.5.15** An insurer may use a filing only for the purpose and policy forms identified in the filing. Use beyond that scope requires a filing that supports the proposed use.

**B.5.16** This section applies to each admitted insurer required to make a mandatory earthquake coverage offer in this state. An insurer must comply with this section on the effective date of this bulletin.
