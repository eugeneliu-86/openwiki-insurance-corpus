# California Bulletin CDI-2022-03 — Mandatory Earthquake Coverage Offer




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This bulletin states the Department’s expectations concerning the mandatory offer of earthquake coverage by admitted insurers. It is intended to promote clear, consistent treatment of eligible policyholders.

**B.1.2** This bulletin applies to an insurer authorized to transact the relevant property insurance business in California. An insurer must administer its earthquake coverage offer in a manner consistent with applicable law.

**B.1.3** The applicability of this bulletin depends on the policy and risk presented, rather than on an insurer’s marketing practices. An insurer may use reasonable administrative procedures to identify policies subject to the offer requirement.

**B.1.4** An insurer must make the required offer in a form that is understandable to the policyholder. The offer must not be presented in a manner that obscures its availability or material terms.

**B.1.5** An insurer may provide earthquake coverage through its own policy form or through another lawful arrangement. The method used must allow the insurer to satisfy its obligations concerning the required offer.

**B.1.6** This bulletin applies to new business and to existing business when an offer obligation arises. An insurer must apply its procedures consistently to similarly situated policyholders.

**B.1.7** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this bulletin. Such records must reasonably identify the offer made and the policyholder’s response, if any.

**B.1.8** An insurer may use electronic communications for an earthquake coverage offer when permitted by applicable law. The insurer must ensure that the communication provides the policyholder a meaningful opportunity to consider the offer.

**B.1.9** An insurer must not condition the availability of the required offer on the purchase of unrelated coverage. An insurer may apply lawful underwriting standards to the earthquake coverage offered.

**B.1.10** The required offer must describe the coverage in a manner that distinguishes it from the underlying property policy. An insurer must avoid statements that could reasonably cause a policyholder to believe that earthquake loss is covered when it is not.

**B.1.11** An insurer must disclose material limitations that apply to the earthquake coverage offered. The disclosure should be presented with the offer so that the policyholder may evaluate the coverage.

**B.1.12** The base deductible minimum is a material feature of earthquake coverage. An insurer must communicate deductible information clearly and consistently with the coverage terms.

**B.1.13** The insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement may affect claim settlement under earthquake coverage. An insurer must not describe replacement cost settlement in a misleading or incomplete manner.

**B.1.14** The loss of use limit is a material term when loss of use coverage is included or offered. An insurer must describe the applicable limitation in language reasonably calculated to inform the policyholder.

**B.1.15** This bulletin does not require an insurer to offer coverage beyond that required by applicable law. An insurer may offer broader coverage or additional options if those offerings are lawful and clearly described.

**B.1.16** An insurer must ensure that personnel responsible for issuing policies understand the insurer’s procedures for making the required offer. The insurer may provide training and written guidance appropriate to its operations.

**B.1.17** An insurer must apply its offer procedures without unfair discrimination. Eligibility determinations and communications must be based on lawful underwriting and rating practices.

**B.1.18** An insurer may revise its forms, systems, or procedures to achieve compliance with this bulletin. Any revised materials must remain consistent with the insurer’s approved forms and applicable requirements.

**B.1.19** The Department may review an insurer’s compliance through examination, inquiry, or other lawful supervisory activity. An insurer must provide responsive information requested by the Department.

**B.1.20** An insurer must correct practices that do not comply with this bulletin promptly upon identification. Corrective action should address affected policyholders when appropriate.

**B.1.21** This bulletin applies from its effective date. An insurer must implement procedures necessary to comply by the time this bulletin becomes effective.

**B.1.22** Nothing in this bulletin limits the Department’s authority to enforce applicable insurance requirements. An insurer remains responsible for compliance with all duties governing earthquake coverage offers.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer must make an earthquake coverage offer when issuing or renewing eligible residential property insurance. The offer must be presented in a manner that permits the applicant or insured to understand that acceptance is optional.

**B.2.2** An insurer must apply these requirements to offers made on or after the applicable effective date. An insurer must administer pending transactions in a manner consistent with the applicable effective date.

**B.2.3** An insurer must use clear and conspicuous language in an earthquake coverage offer. The language must distinguish earthquake coverage from the underlying residential property insurance.

**B.2.4** An insurer must identify the insurer making the earthquake coverage offer. The offer must provide contact information through which an applicant or insured may request assistance.

**B.2.5** An insurer must provide the earthquake coverage offer in writing. An insurer may provide the offer electronically when the applicant or insured has consented to electronic delivery.

**B.2.6** An insurer must ensure that an electronic offer is reasonably accessible to the intended recipient. The electronic presentation must permit the recipient to retain or reproduce the offer.

**B.2.7** An insurer must state the material terms of the offered earthquake coverage. The statement must not omit a material limitation, exclusion, condition, or deductible.

**B.2.8** An insurer must describe the property to which the offered earthquake coverage applies. The description must be sufficient to associate the offer with the underlying residential property insurance.

**B.2.9** An insurer must identify the proposed coverage limits in the earthquake coverage offer. An insurer must explain when a stated limit is subject to a separate limitation or condition.

**B.2.10** An insurer must identify the applicable deductible in the earthquake coverage offer. An insurer must state that the seismic deductible is fifteen (15) percent of the applicable covered loss.

**B.2.11** An insurer must describe how the deductible applies to a covered loss. The description must not suggest that the deductible is waived unless the offered coverage expressly provides otherwise.

**B.2.12** An insurer must state the premium or rating basis applicable to the offered earthquake coverage. An insurer must not represent an estimated premium as a final premium without an appropriate qualification.

**B.2.13** An insurer must disclose material underwriting conditions affecting the availability of the offered earthquake coverage. The disclosure must be consistent with the insurer's underwriting practices.

**B.2.14** An insurer must not condition the earthquake coverage offer on the purchase of unrelated insurance. An insurer may require information reasonably necessary to evaluate eligibility for the offered coverage.

**B.2.15** An insurer must make the earthquake coverage offer available through its authorized distribution channels. An insurer must maintain procedures that promote consistent offer practices across those channels.

**B.2.16** An insurer must train personnel who present or process earthquake coverage offers. The training must address the insurer's offer procedures and required disclosures.

**B.2.17** An insurer must supervise producers and representatives involved in the offer process. The insurer must correct practices that result in incomplete, misleading, or inconsistent offers.

**B.2.18** An insurer must maintain carrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exceptioncarrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exception (see Personal Lines Underwriting Manual, R520 Rule 520 — California State Exceptions). The insurer must apply that implementation consistently when determining eligibility for an earthquake coverage offer.

**B.2.19** An insurer must ensure that underwriting criteria used for earthquake coverage are applied as filed or otherwise authorized. An insurer must not use undisclosed criteria to deny or restrict an offer.

**B.2.20** An insurer must provide an applicant or insured a reasonable means to accept the earthquake coverage offer. The acceptance process must identify the coverage selected and the person providing the acceptance.

**B.2.21** An insurer must provide an applicant or insured a reasonable means to decline the earthquake coverage offer. A declination must not be treated as authorization for any unrelated coverage change.

**B.2.22** An insurer must not infer acceptance of earthquake coverage from silence. An insurer must obtain affirmative authorization before issuing the offered coverage.

**B.2.23** An insurer must not infer a declination from an applicant's failure to complete an unrelated transaction. The insurer must document the basis for any recorded declination.

**B.2.24** An insurer must confirm acceptance of earthquake coverage in writing. The confirmation must identify the coverage, applicable premium, deductible, and material conditions.

**B.2.25** An insurer must provide written confirmation when an applicant or insured declines earthquake coverage. The confirmation may be included in another written communication if the declination is clearly identified.

**B.2.26** An insurer must preserve records demonstrating that an earthquake coverage offer was made. The records must identify the recipient, method of delivery, and disposition of the offer.

**B.2.27** An insurer must preserve records supporting any determination that an applicant or insured was not eligible for an earthquake coverage offer. The records must identify the underwriting basis for that determination.

**B.2.28** An insurer must maintain records in a form that can be produced to the Department upon request. The records must be complete, legible, and reasonably organized.

**B.2.29** An insurer must ensure that records created by producers are incorporated into the insurer's records. An insurer may use electronic records if they reliably reflect the offer transaction.

**B.2.30** An insurer must protect personal information obtained in connection with an earthquake coverage offer. Access to that information must be limited to persons with a legitimate business need.

**B.2.31** An insurer must use forms and notices that accurately reflect the earthquake coverage being offered. An insurer must withdraw or correct a form or notice that contains a material error.

**B.2.32** An insurer must ensure that translated communications do not materially alter the meaning of the earthquake coverage offer. The insurer must maintain controls for translated materials used in the offer process.

**B.2.33** An insurer must not use marketing language that obscures the limitations of earthquake coverage. An insurer must present material limitations with clarity comparable to statements of coverage.

**B.2.34** An insurer must not characterize earthquake coverage as included in residential property insurance unless it is actually included. Any explanation of coverage must accurately describe the applicable policy terms.

**B.2.35** An insurer must not state or imply that an earthquake loss will be covered without regard to exclusions or deductibles. An insurer must provide a balanced description of available protection and material limitations.

**B.2.36** An insurer must respond accurately to questions concerning the earthquake coverage offer. Personnel must not provide advice that conflicts with the insurer's approved materials.

**B.2.37** An insurer must provide updated offer information when a material change affects the offered coverage before acceptance. The insurer must give the applicant or insured an opportunity to review the updated information.

**B.2.38** An insurer must issue earthquake coverage only after receiving the required acceptance and premium arrangement. The insurer must communicate the effective coverage terms to the insured.

**B.2.39** An insurer must provide policy documentation for accepted earthquake coverage. The documentation must be consistent with the terms presented in the offer.

**B.2.40** An insurer must correct a discrepancy between an accepted offer and issued policy documentation promptly. The insurer must notify the affected insured of the correction.

**B.2.41** An insurer must establish procedures for handling disputes concerning an earthquake coverage offer. The procedures must permit review of the offer record and related communications.

**B.2.42** An insurer must investigate a complaint alleging that an earthquake coverage offer was not made or was misleading. The investigation must include review of the applicable offer records.

**B.2.43** An insurer must take appropriate corrective action when an internal review identifies a failure to comply with these requirements. Corrective action must address affected transactions and the cause of the failure.

**B.2.44** An insurer must monitor compliance with its earthquake coverage offer procedures. The monitoring must be designed to identify recurring errors or inconsistent practices.

**B.2.45** An insurer must maintain written controls for changes to earthquake coverage offer materials. The controls must ensure that revised materials are reviewed before use.

**B.2.46** An insurer must ensure that automated systems present required earthquake coverage information accurately. An insurer must test material system changes before the changes are placed into use.

**B.2.47** An insurer must maintain controls for third parties that assist with earthquake coverage offers. The insurer remains responsible for compliance with these requirements when a third party performs offer-related functions.

**B.2.48** An insurer must ensure that its complaint personnel can identify issues involving earthquake coverage offers. The insurer must route those issues for appropriate review.

**B.2.49** An insurer must not retaliate against an applicant or insured for accepting or declining earthquake coverage. An insurer must not use the acceptance or declination in a manner inconsistent with applicable underwriting practices.

**B.2.50** An insurer must make earthquake coverage offer information available to the Department upon request. The information must be provided in a format reasonably requested by the Department.

**B.2.51** An insurer must cooperate with a Department examination concerning earthquake coverage offer practices. The insurer must provide relevant records and explanations requested during the examination.

**B.2.52** An insurer must designate personnel responsible for oversight of earthquake coverage offer compliance. The designated personnel must have authority to address identified deficiencies.

**B.2.53** An insurer must communicate material changes to its earthquake coverage offer procedures to affected personnel. The communication must occur before the changed procedures are used.

**B.2.54** An insurer must ensure that offer procedures remain consistent with its policy forms, rating practices, and underwriting practices. The insurer must resolve any inconsistency before presenting an affected offer.

**B.2.55** An insurer must not impose a condition that prevents an eligible applicant or insured from receiving a required earthquake coverage offer. An insurer may apply lawful eligibility requirements that are disclosed and consistently administered.

**B.2.56** An insurer must review earthquake coverage offer materials for clarity and accuracy. The review must address whether a reasonable applicant or insured can identify the offered coverage and material limitations.

**B.2.57** An insurer must retain evidence of approval for earthquake coverage offer materials. The evidence must identify the materials approved for use.

**B.2.58** An insurer must promptly address a material defect in an earthquake coverage offer process. The insurer must evaluate whether affected applicants or insureds require notice or a corrected offer.

**B.2.59** An insurer must administer earthquake coverage offers in good faith. The insurer must not use procedures intended to discourage informed consideration of the offered coverage.

**B.2.60** An insurer must comply with these requirements throughout the earthquake coverage offer process. Compliance with these requirements does not limit any other obligation applicable to an insurer.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** This paragraph applies to an insurer that issues or renews residential property insurance in this State. An insurer must provide the notice and disclosure required by this paragraph while these provisions are in effect.

**B.3.2** An insurer must make the mandatory earthquake coverage offer in a clear and conspicuous manner. The offer must be reasonably calculated to inform the applicant or insured of the availability of earthquake coverage.

**B.3.3** An insurer must provide the offer in writing. An insurer may provide the offer by electronic means when the applicant or insured has consented to electronic delivery as permitted by applicable law.

**B.3.4** The notice must identify the insurer making the offer. The notice must identify the residential property policy to which the offer relates.

**B.3.5** The notice must state that earthquake damage may not be covered by the residential property policy unless earthquake coverage is purchased. The notice must not suggest that coverage is included when it is not included.

**B.3.6** The notice must describe the manner in which an applicant or insured may request earthquake coverage. The description must include contact information sufficient to permit the applicant or insured to communicate the request to the insurer.

**B.3.7** An insurer must state that an applicant or insured may obtain information concerning available earthquake coverage from the insurer. The notice must not discourage an applicant or insured from seeking that information.

**B.3.8** An insurer must describe material limitations, exclusions, and deductibles applicable to the earthquake coverage offered. The description must use language that is understandable to a reasonable applicant or insured.

**B.3.9** An insurer must disclose that earthquake coverage may be subject to underwriting requirements. The disclosure must not imply that coverage is unavailable unless the insurer has made that determination under its underwriting rules.

**B.3.10** An insurer must disclose that the premium for earthquake coverage is separate from the premium for the residential property policy when that is the case. The disclosure must identify any condition that may affect the premium quoted or offered.

**B.3.11** An insurer must state whether the earthquake coverage offer is subject to a deductible. The notice must explain that a deductible may apply to a covered earthquake loss.

**B.3.12** An insurer must state whether the offered coverage is provided by the insurer or by another admitted insurer. The notice must identify the entity responsible for issuing the offered coverage.

**B.3.13** An insurer must not describe earthquake coverage as mandatory when purchase of that coverage is optional. An insurer must not characterize a declination as a waiver of rights not addressed by the offer.

**B.3.14** An insurer must provide the notice at a time that allows the applicant or insured a meaningful opportunity to consider the offer. The notice must not be withheld until after the insurer has completed the related policy transaction.

**B.3.15** An insurer must provide a notice of nonrenewal at least seventy-five (75) days before the nonrenewal takes effect. The notice must clearly state that the related residential property policy will not be renewed.

**B.3.16** A notice of nonrenewal must not state or imply that declining earthquake coverage is the reason for nonrenewal unless that statement is accurate and permitted by law. An insurer must maintain support for any reason stated in the notice.

**B.3.17** An insurer must present the earthquake coverage offer separately from a notice of cancellation or nonrenewal when presentation in the same communication would be misleading. An insurer may include related information in the same communication if each notice remains clear and conspicuous.

**B.3.18** An insurer must use a format that distinguishes the earthquake coverage offer from general policy advertising or promotional material. The required disclosure must remain readily visible in any format used by the insurer.

**B.3.19** An insurer must ensure that a producer acting on its behalf provides the required notice and disclosure. The insurer remains responsible for compliance with this paragraph.

**B.3.20** An insurer must provide reasonable assistance to an applicant or insured who requests clarification of the offer. The assistance must not misrepresent the scope, cost, or availability of earthquake coverage.

**B.3.21** An insurer must provide the notice in a manner reasonably accessible to the applicant or insured. An insurer must make reasonable efforts to accommodate a communication need known to the insurer.

**B.3.22** An insurer must retain evidence that the required offer and disclosure were provided. The retained evidence must identify the policy or application associated with the offer.

**B.3.23** An insurer must retain any acceptance, declination, or request for additional information received in response to the offer. The record must be sufficient to show the manner in which the response was received.

**B.3.24** An insurer must not treat the absence of a response as an affirmative acceptance of earthquake coverage. An insurer must obtain authorization before adding earthquake coverage to a policy.

**B.3.25** An insurer must not treat an applicant's or insured's request for information as a declination of earthquake coverage. A declination must be based on a clear expression by the applicant or insured or on the absence of acceptance where acceptance is required.

**B.3.26** An insurer must ensure that the notice is consistent with the coverage actually available for the insured property. The insurer must correct a material error in the notice promptly after discovering the error.

**B.3.27** An insurer must provide a corrected notice when a material error could affect an applicant's or insured's decision concerning earthquake coverage. The corrected notice must identify the information that was corrected.

**B.3.28** An insurer must not use undefined technical terms when plain language would reasonably convey the same information. An insurer may include technical terms when necessary to describe coverage accurately.

**B.3.29** An insurer must ensure that any illustration or example used with the notice is not misleading. An illustration must be clearly identified as an illustration and not as a guarantee of coverage or payment.

**B.3.30** An insurer must train personnel responsible for providing the offer and disclosure. The training must address the requirements of this paragraph and the insurer's procedures for documenting compliance.

**B.3.31** An insurer must establish procedures for monitoring compliance with this paragraph. The procedures must provide for correction of identified deficiencies.

**B.3.32** An insurer must make records required by this paragraph available to the Department upon request. The records must be maintained in a form that permits timely review.

**B.3.33** An insurer must not alter, obscure, or omit required disclosure language in a manner that reduces its clarity. An insurer may revise presentation or formatting if the required information remains complete and conspicuous.

**B.3.34** An insurer must apply the same disclosure standards to an offer made through an online system. An online system must permit the applicant or insured to review the disclosure before responding to the offer.

**B.3.35** An insurer must ensure that a telephone offer is followed by written disclosure when the offer is accepted or when the applicant or insured requests written information. The written disclosure must accurately reflect the offer communicated by telephone.

**B.3.36** An insurer must not condition the availability of the residential property policy on acceptance of earthquake coverage unless otherwise permitted by law. The notice must not state or imply such a condition when it does not apply.

**B.3.37** An insurer must review its notice and disclosure practices when material changes are made to its earthquake coverage program. The insurer must update its materials as necessary to remain compliant with this paragraph.

**B.3.38** An insurer must administer this paragraph in good faith. Compliance with this paragraph does not relieve an insurer of any other applicable duty concerning policyholder communications or insurance transactions.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to claims arising under earthquake coverage offered or issued by an admitted insurer in this State. An insurer must administer those claims in good faith and in accordance with the policy terms.

**B.4.2** An insurer must acknowledge a claim promptly and provide the claimant with information reasonably necessary to understand the claims process. The acknowledgment must identify information then known to be needed for the insurer’s evaluation.

**B.4.3** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation before denying a claim or limiting payment. The investigation must be appropriate to the reported earthquake damage and the coverage at issue.

**B.4.4** An insurer must assign claim personnel with sufficient authority and knowledge to evaluate earthquake losses. An insurer remains responsible for the conduct of its employees, adjusters, contractors, and representatives.

**B.4.5** An insurer must communicate with a claimant in a manner that is clear, accurate, and not misleading. An insurer must respond reasonably to communications concerning coverage, valuation, repair, and payment.

**B.4.6** An insurer must explain any request for information or documentation in plain terms. An insurer may request only information reasonably relevant to the claim evaluation.

**B.4.7** An insurer must not require a claimant to provide records that are unavailable through no fault of the claimant without considering other reasonably available evidence. An insurer must give appropriate consideration to evidence provided by the claimant.

**B.4.8** An insurer must inspect reported damage when an inspection is reasonably necessary to determine coverage or the amount of loss. The inspection must be performed by personnel qualified for the condition being evaluated.

**B.4.9** An insurer must consider the effect of earthquake-related ground movement, fire, water damage, and other reported causes of loss as required by the policy. An insurer must not attribute damage to an excluded cause without a reasonable factual basis.

**B.4.10** An insurer must maintain claim notes and records sufficient to show the basis for material claim decisions. The records must identify material information considered in approving, denying, or limiting a claim.

**B.4.11** An insurer must evaluate repair estimates fairly and must consider information supporting necessary work. An insurer must not reject an estimate solely because it was obtained by the claimant.

**B.4.12** An insurer may obtain an independent estimate, report, or expert opinion when reasonably necessary. An insurer must disclose the material basis for relying on that information when it affects a claim determination.

**B.4.13** An insurer must explain the policy provisions on which it relies when denying coverage or limiting payment. The explanation must relate the cited provision to the facts known to the insurer.

**B.4.14** An insurer must not represent that a loss is excluded, limited, or subject to a condition unless the representation is supported by the policy and the claim investigation. An insurer must correct a material misstatement when it becomes aware of the error.

**B.4.15** An insurer must provide a written explanation of any denial of a claim or portion of a claim. The explanation must identify the factual and policy basis for the decision.

**B.4.16** An insurer must issue payment for covered loss without unreasonable delay after the amount due is determined. An insurer must not withhold undisputed payment because another portion of the claim remains under review.

**B.4.17** An insurer must identify any information preventing a final claim determination when the investigation remains incomplete. An insurer must continue the investigation diligently until it can make a determination.

**B.4.18** An insurer must treat communications from a public adjuster, attorney, contractor, or other authorized representative as communications from the claimant when appropriate authorization has been provided. An insurer may verify the scope of that authorization.

**B.4.19** An insurer must not discourage a claimant from seeking advice, obtaining repair estimates, or submitting information relevant to the claim. An insurer must not use claim handling practices that improperly pressure a claimant to accept less than the covered amount.

**B.4.20** An insurer must apply deductibles and other policy conditions consistently with the policy language and the facts of the loss. An insurer must explain how a deductible or condition affects the payment determination.

**B.4.21** An insurer must evaluate additional living expense or loss of use coverage, when provided by the policy, based on the claimant’s reported circumstances and supporting information. An insurer must explain any decision affecting that coverage.

**B.4.22** An insurer must consider reasonable emergency measures taken to protect covered property from further damage. An insurer must not deny otherwise covered emergency expense solely because permanent repairs have not begun.

**B.4.23** An insurer must preserve a claimant’s opportunity to submit supplemental information concerning newly discovered damage. An insurer must evaluate supplemental information under the policy and this paragraph.

**B.4.24** An insurer must provide a claimant with a reasonable opportunity to review material claim information on which an adverse determination is based, subject to applicable confidentiality obligations. An insurer must explain how the claimant may provide responsive information.

**B.4.25** An insurer must supervise catastrophe claim operations to ensure that claim handling remains consistent with this paragraph. An insurer must maintain procedures for identifying and correcting material claim handling errors.

**B.4.26** An insurer must not discriminate in claim handling on the basis of a claimant’s protected characteristic or location within this State. An insurer must apply comparable standards to similarly situated claimants.

**B.4.27** An insurer must make claim handling records available to the Department upon request. An insurer must retain those records in a manner sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this paragraph.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** An insurer must file the forms, endorsements, notices, and related materials used to make the mandatory earthquake coverage offer. The filing must be complete and must clearly identify the policy forms to which the offer applies.

**B.5.2** An insurer must submit the filing to the Department in the manner required for property insurance form filings. The filing must identify the insurer and the applicable line of insurance.

**B.5.3** An insurer must include the proposed earthquake coverage offer with the filing. The offer must be written in clear language and must be suitable for delivery to the insured.

**B.5.4** An insurer must identify any premium, deductible, limit, condition, exclusion, or other term applicable to the offered earthquake coverage. The filing must explain how those terms will be presented to an insured.

**B.5.5** An insurer must file any application, election, rejection, acknowledgement, or other document used in connection with the offer. The documents must be consistent with the forms and terms submitted for review.

**B.5.6** An insurer must identify the manner in which it will provide the earthquake coverage offer to an insured. The filing must describe the insurer’s procedures for documenting the insured’s response.

**B.5.7** An insurer must ensure that filed materials do not misstate the availability, scope, cost, or limitations of earthquake coverage. An insurer must not use language that could reasonably mislead an insured regarding the effect of accepting or declining the offer.

**B.5.8** An insurer must distinguish the earthquake coverage offer from the underlying residential property insurance coverage. The offer must state the material terms of the coverage in a manner that permits an insured to make an informed decision.

**B.5.9** An insurer must include any revised or replacement material in a filing before using that material. A revision must be identified clearly enough to permit the Department to determine the change made.

**B.5.10** An insurer must not use the earthquake coverage offer, or any related form, unless the material has been filed as required by this bulletin. Use of unfiled material is not permitted.

**B.5.11** An insurer may implement filed materials only when the materials are effective under applicable Department requirements. An insurer must comply with any condition imposed in connection with Department approval or acceptance of the filing.

**B.5.12** An insurer must apply an effective filing consistently to policies within the scope identified in the filing. An insurer must maintain procedures reasonably designed to prevent use of superseded materials.

**B.5.13** An insurer must retain records demonstrating its compliance with the filing and implementation requirements of this section. The records must be sufficient to show the form of the offer provided and the insured’s response.

**B.5.14** An insurer must make compliance records available to the Department upon request. The insurer must provide a complete and accurate response to any Department inquiry concerning its earthquake coverage offer.

**B.5.15** An insurer must promptly correct any material that does not comply with this section. The insurer must take appropriate action to ensure that affected insureds receive compliant materials.

**B.5.16** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section when another person prepares, delivers, or administers the earthquake coverage offer on the insurer’s behalf.
