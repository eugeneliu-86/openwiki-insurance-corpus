# Texas Bulletin B-2016-04 — Separate Windstorm and Hail Deductibles
> SUPERSEDED by Texas Bulletin B-2021-08 — Separate Windstorm and Hail Deductibles for policies effective on or after 2021-08-19. This edition remains in force for policies written under it.



## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This section states the Department’s purpose and applicability standards for separate windstorm and hail deductibles. An insurer must apply these standards when offering or using a deductible that applies to windstorm or hail loss separately from another property deductible.

**B.1.2** This section applies to an admitted insurer that delivers, issues for delivery, or renews a property insurance policy in this state. It applies when the policy includes coverage for windstorm, hail, or both.

**B.1.3** An insurer may use a separate windstorm and hail deductible only if the deductible is clearly described in the policy. The description must identify the loss circumstances to which the deductible applies.

**B.1.4** An insurer must present deductible terms in a manner that is not misleading. A policyholder must be able to distinguish a windstorm or hail deductible from any other deductible applicable to property loss.

**B.1.5** An insurer must use policy language that reasonably explains when a windstorm or hail deductible is triggered. The language must not create uncertainty concerning whether the deductible applies to a reported loss.

**B.1.6** An insurer must ensure that declarations, endorsements, and other policy materials are consistent with the applicable deductible provisions. Material inconsistencies may mislead a policyholder regarding the deductible that applies.

**B.1.7** An insurer may not describe a windstorm or hail deductible as applying to losses for which the policy language does not support its application. An insurer must adjust a claim in accordance with the policy as issued.

**B.1.8** An insurer must make deductible information available before a policyholder is bound to coverage. The information must allow the policyholder to understand the material effect of the deductible on covered windstorm or hail loss.

**B.1.9** An insurer must provide policyholders with clear notice of a deductible change. The notice must identify the change in terms that are understandable to a reasonable policyholder.

**B.1.10** An insurer may use different deductible structures for different property insurance programs if each structure complies with this section. An insurer must administer each structure consistently with its filed policy language.

**B.1.11** An insurer must not use a separate windstorm and hail deductible to avoid obligations otherwise owed under the policy. The deductible may be applied only to the extent permitted by the policy terms and applicable law.

**B.1.12** An insurer must maintain claim practices that accurately identify the cause of loss. A claim determination concerning windstorm or hail must be supported by information reasonably relevant to the loss.

**B.1.13** An insurer must explain the application of a separate windstorm and hail deductible when communicating a claim decision to an insured. The explanation must be sufficiently clear to permit the insured to understand the basis for the deductible.

**B.1.14** An insurer must not apply a windstorm or hail deductible based solely on a generalized description of weather conditions. The insurer must evaluate the reported damage and the applicable policy provisions.

**B.1.15** An insurer may coordinate deductible disclosures with other policy disclosures. The coordination must not obscure the separate windstorm and hail deductible or diminish the clarity required by this section.

**B.1.16** This section does not itself establish a base deductible minimum. An insurer remains responsible for compliance with any otherwise applicable requirement governing deductible terms.

**B.1.17** This section does not alter an insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement. An insurer must administer replacement cost settlement according to the applicable policy provisions.

**B.1.18** This section does not alter a loss of use limit. An insurer must disclose and administer loss of use coverage according to the policy terms.

**B.1.19** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section. The records must reasonably support the insurer’s underwriting, disclosure, and claim-handling practices.

**B.1.20** An insurer must make compliance records available to the Department upon request. The records may include policy language, communications, claim materials, and related operational materials.

**B.1.21** An insurer must ensure that personnel responsible for policy issuance and claim handling understand the insurer’s separate windstorm and hail deductible practices. The insurer remains responsible for acts performed on its behalf.

**B.1.22** This section applies prospectively to policy activity subject to its terms. An insurer must take reasonable steps to implement compliant practices for affected policies and claims.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer may offer a separate deductible for loss caused by windstorm or hail only in accordance with this section. The deductible must be stated clearly in the policy and in all related coverage materials.

**B.2.2** An insurer must apply this section to policies issued, delivered, or renewed while this section is in effect. An insurer must administer the deductible consistently with the policy language provided to the policyholder.

**B.2.3** A separate windstorm and hail deductible must be applicable only to loss that results from a covered windstorm or hail event. An insurer must not apply that deductible to loss having no causal connection to windstorm or hail.

**B.2.4** A deductible applicable to windstorm and hail loss must be no less than one (1) percent of the applicable insured value. An insurer must calculate the deductible using the insured value identified in the policy.

**B.2.5** A deductible applicable to hurricane loss must not exceed three (3) percent of the applicable insured value. An insurer must apply the limitation in this paragraph when the loss is subject to a hurricane deductible.

**B.2.6** An insurer must identify the event or conditions that trigger a windstorm and hail deductible. The triggering language must be sufficiently clear for a policyholder to determine when the deductible may apply.

**B.2.7** An insurer must define any term used to determine whether a loss is subject to the separate deductible. A definition must not be misleading, internally inconsistent, or contrary to the coverage grant.

**B.2.8** An insurer must disclose the separate deductible in the declarations, policy jacket, endorsement, or other policy material that identifies deductibles. The disclosure must be conspicuous and understandable to a reasonable policyholder.

**B.2.9** An insurer must state whether the deductible applies separately to building property, personal property, or other covered property. The policy must describe how the deductible applies when more than one category of covered property is damaged.

**B.2.10** An insurer must state whether the deductible applies to an individual occurrence, a related series of occurrences, or another identified basis. The stated basis must be applied consistently during claim handling.

**B.2.11** An insurer must not use policy language that permits the same covered loss to be subject to duplicative windstorm and hail deductibles. A deductible may be applied only as authorized by the policy and this section.

**B.2.12** An insurer must explain the relationship between a separate windstorm and hail deductible and any other deductible in the policy. The explanation must identify which deductible applies when policy provisions could reasonably appear to overlap.

**B.2.13** An insurer must provide deductible information before or at the time coverage is bound. The information must be presented in a manner that permits the applicant to understand the effect of the deductible on a covered loss.

**B.2.14** An insurer must provide the policyholder with the deductible terms when the policy is issued or renewed. An insurer must also provide revised deductible terms when the insurer changes the applicable policy language.

**B.2.15** An insurer must not describe a separate windstorm and hail deductible as optional unless the policyholder may elect coverage without that deductible. Any election process must be accurately described in the policy materials.

**B.2.16** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to show the deductible terms applicable to each policy. The records must identify the policy language in effect at the time of loss.

**B.2.17** An insurer must maintain records supporting the application of a separate deductible to a claim. The records must show the factual basis for determining that the deductible applied.

**B.2.18** An insurer must explain the deductible calculation upon request by a policyholder or claimant entitled to receive claim information. The explanation must be consistent with the policy language and the claim file.

**B.2.19** An insurer must not apply a separate windstorm and hail deductible based solely on a generalized weather condition. The insurer must determine whether the reported damage resulted from an event covered by the deductible provision.

**B.2.20** An insurer must evaluate all available information relevant to the cause of loss before applying the deductible. The insurer must document material information supporting its determination.

**B.2.21** An insurer must not deny or reduce coverage through application of the deductible unless the policy authorizes that application. Any reduction must be reflected accurately in the claim payment explanation.

**B.2.22** An insurer must state the deductible in a form that permits the policyholder to determine the amount retained by the policyholder after a covered loss. The policy must not rely on undisclosed underwriting or claims practices to determine the deductible.

**B.2.23** An insurer must ensure that an endorsement establishing a separate deductible is consistent with the base policy. If an endorsement changes the deductible, the endorsement must clearly state the change.

**B.2.24** An insurer must resolve conflicts between deductible provisions in favor of the policyholder unless the policy clearly provides another result permitted by this section. An insurer must not rely on ambiguous language to impose a separate deductible.

**B.2.25** An insurer must use the same deductible trigger described in the policy when adjusting a claim. An insurer must not apply a broader trigger through claims guidelines or internal procedures.

**B.2.26** An insurer must ensure that underwriting materials accurately describe the deductible available under the policy. Sales, application, and renewal materials must not omit material limitations affecting deductible application.

**B.2.27** An insurer must train personnel responsible for underwriting, policy issuance, and claim handling regarding the separate deductible provisions. The training must address the policy language and the requirements of this section.

**B.2.28** An insurer must establish procedures for reviewing disputes concerning application of a separate windstorm and hail deductible. Those procedures must permit prompt review of the policy language, cause of loss, and deductible calculation.

**B.2.29** An insurer must not condition coverage for a nonwindstorm loss on payment of a separate windstorm and hail deductible. The insurer must apply the deductible only to the extent supported by the covered cause of loss.

**B.2.30** An insurer must distinguish damage caused by windstorm or hail from damage caused by other covered perils when the distinction affects the deductible. The claim file must contain the basis for that allocation.

**B.2.31** An insurer must not apply a separate deductible to excluded loss merely because windstorm or hail occurred near the time of loss. The insurer must first determine whether covered windstorm or hail damage is present.

**B.2.32** An insurer must provide a clear explanation when it concludes that a separate deductible applies to only part of a claimed loss. The explanation must identify the covered damage subject to the deductible.

**B.2.33** An insurer must apply the deductible consistently to similarly situated policyholders under materially similar policy terms. An insurer must not vary deductible treatment based on factors not stated in the policy.

**B.2.34** An insurer must retain policy forms, endorsements, notices, and claim records relevant to compliance with this section. The records must be available for review by the department upon request.

**B.2.35** An insurer must review policy forms and endorsements for compliance before use. An insurer must revise any form language that does not clearly implement the requirements of this section.

**B.2.36** An insurer must not use a separate windstorm and hail deductible in a manner that conflicts with the declarations or other controlling policy terms. Where policy materials conflict, the insurer must resolve the conflict in accordance with applicable law.

**B.2.37** An insurer must communicate deductible changes in a manner reasonably calculated to provide notice to the policyholder. The communication must identify the policy language being changed and the effect of the change.

**B.2.38** An insurer must not impose a separate deductible through a billing notice, claim correspondence, or other material that is not part of the policy. The deductible must be authorized by policy language provided to the policyholder.

**B.2.39** An insurer must ensure that electronic policy materials display the separate deductible as clearly as paper policy materials. Electronic presentation must not obscure the deductible or its triggering conditions.

**B.2.40** An insurer must respond accurately to policyholder inquiries concerning the applicability of the separate deductible. A response must not contradict the policy, the claim file, or the requirements of this section.

**B.2.41** An insurer must correct an error that results in application of an unauthorized deductible. The correction must include appropriate adjustment of the claim payment or other policyholder account affected by the error.

**B.2.42** An insurer must monitor its use of separate windstorm and hail deductibles for compliance with this section. The insurer must address identified deficiencies through corrective action reasonably designed to prevent recurrence.

**B.2.43** An insurer must comply with each requirement of this section when issuing, administering, or adjusting a policy containing a separate windstorm and hail deductible. Compliance with this section does not relieve an insurer of any other applicable duty.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** An insurer that offers or renews a policy with a separate windstorm or hail deductible must provide a clear written notice of that deductible. The notice must be sufficiently conspicuous to be understood by the policyholder.

**B.3.2** An insurer must provide the notice before a separate windstorm or hail deductible becomes applicable to coverage. The notice requirement applies whether the deductible is newly imposed or increased.

**B.3.3** An insurer must provide notice at least thirty (30) days before an increase in a windstorm deductible takes effect. An insurer may not make the increased deductible effective before the required notice period has elapsed.

**B.3.4** An insurer must identify the policy to which the notice applies. The notice must identify the insured property or coverage in a manner that permits the policyholder to recognize the affected policy.

**B.3.5** An insurer must state that the policy includes, or will include, a separate windstorm or hail deductible. The statement must not suggest that the separate deductible applies to losses that are not subject to that deductible.

**B.3.6** An insurer must describe the circumstances under which the separate windstorm or hail deductible applies. The description must use terms that are consistent with the policy.

**B.3.7** An insurer must disclose the amount or method used to determine the separate windstorm or hail deductible. If the deductible is determined by reference to a policy value, the notice must explain the value used in that determination.

**B.3.8** An insurer must distinguish the separate windstorm or hail deductible from any other deductible applicable under the policy. The notice must not combine deductible descriptions in a manner that obscures the deductible applicable to windstorm or hail loss.

**B.3.9** An insurer must disclose whether the separate deductible applies to windstorm loss, hail loss, or both. The notice must accurately describe the covered causes of loss subject to the deductible.

**B.3.10** An insurer must provide the notice in language that is plain and readily understandable. Technical terms may be used only when their meaning is adequately explained or apparent from the notice.

**B.3.11** An insurer must present the notice in a format reasonably calculated to call the policyholder's attention to the separate deductible. The notice must not be concealed within unrelated material or presented in a misleading manner.

**B.3.12** An insurer may provide the notice with an application, policy, renewal offer, endorsement, billing communication, or other policyholder communication. The manner of delivery must permit the insurer to demonstrate that the notice was provided.

**B.3.13** An insurer that provides notice electronically must do so in a manner consistent with the policyholder's election or consent to electronic delivery. The electronic notice must remain clear and accessible to the policyholder.

**B.3.14** An insurer must provide the notice to the named insured or other person authorized to receive policy notices. Delivery to an agent does not relieve an insurer of its obligation under this paragraph unless the delivery constitutes notice to the policyholder.

**B.3.15** An insurer may use a separate notice or include the required disclosure in another policy communication. When included in another communication, the disclosure must remain conspicuous and complete.

**B.3.16** An insurer must ensure that a notice concerning an increased deductible clearly states that the deductible is changing. The notice must identify the deductible that will apply after the change becomes effective.

**B.3.17** An insurer must not represent that a separate windstorm or hail deductible is required when the policyholder has an available coverage option without that deductible. Any description of available options must be accurate and not misleading.

**B.3.18** An insurer must not use a notice that minimizes or misstates the effect of the separate windstorm or hail deductible. A notice must fairly communicate that the deductible may affect the amount payable for a covered loss.

**B.3.19** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this paragraph. The records must identify the notice provided and the manner in which it was delivered.

**B.3.20** An insurer must be able to produce the applicable notice upon request by the department. An insurer must also be able to explain how the notice was associated with the affected policy.

**B.3.21** An insurer must apply this paragraph to notices provided in connection with new business and renewal business. An insurer may also provide the disclosure when a policy is amended during its term.

**B.3.22** An insurer must ensure that notices used by producers or other representatives are consistent with the insurer's policy forms and underwriting practices. An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this paragraph when notice is provided through a representative.

**B.3.23** An insurer must review its notice language when it changes the policy language governing a separate windstorm or hail deductible. The revised notice must accurately reflect the policy language in effect for the affected coverage.

**B.3.24** An insurer must not rely on general policy delivery alone as notice of an increased windstorm deductible unless the delivery satisfies the notice requirements of this paragraph. The insurer bears responsibility for ensuring that the disclosure is timely and clear.

**B.3.25** An insurer must provide a notice that is consistent with the declarations page and all applicable endorsements. If the notice conflicts with policy terms, the insurer must correct the inconsistency promptly.

**B.3.26** An insurer must not condition the required notice on a policyholder request. The notice must be provided by the insurer as part of its implementation of a separate windstorm or hail deductible.

**B.3.27** An insurer must apply the requirements of this paragraph to any separate deductible described as applicable to windstorm or hail loss, regardless of the label used by the insurer. A different label does not avoid the disclosure obligation.

**B.3.28** An insurer must comply with this paragraph before making a separate windstorm or hail deductible effective for the affected policyholder. This paragraph governs the content, timing, and retention of the required notice.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** An insurer must apply the deductible stated in the policy and applicable to the reported cause of loss. An insurer must not apply a deductible that is not authorized by the policy.

**B.4.2** An insurer must identify whether the reported loss involves windstorm, hail, or another covered cause of loss. The claim file must support the insurer's determination.

**B.4.3** An insurer must explain in clear written language the deductible that it intends to apply to a covered claim. The explanation must distinguish the deductible from any limitation, exclusion, or other policy condition affecting payment.

**B.4.4** An insurer must not describe a separate windstorm or hail deductible as a reduction in coverage. An insurer must accurately describe the deductible as the amount of covered loss for which the policyholder remains responsible.

**B.4.5** An insurer must evaluate all information reasonably available concerning the cause of loss. An insurer must not assign damage to windstorm or hail without a reasonable basis.

**B.4.6** An insurer must evaluate damage caused by different perils separately when the policy requires separate treatment. The insurer's estimate must reasonably identify the damage attributed to each applicable cause of loss.

**B.4.7** An insurer must not apply more than one deductible to the same damage unless the policy clearly authorizes that result. The claim file must identify the policy language supporting any application of separate deductibles.

**B.4.8** An insurer must not apply a windstorm or hail deductible to damage that is not caused by windstorm or hail. A coverage decision must be based on the facts of the reported loss and the terms of the policy.

**B.4.9** An insurer must provide a policyholder with a copy of the estimate upon request. The estimate must show the covered damage, applicable deductions, and the basis for the net claim payment.

**B.4.10** An insurer must communicate material claim decisions in writing when requested by the policyholder. The communication must state the principal factual and policy grounds for the decision.

**B.4.11** An insurer must respond to reasonable policyholder questions concerning the application of a separate windstorm or hail deductible. A response must not be misleading, incomplete, or inconsistent with the policy.

**B.4.12** An insurer must train personnel who adjust or supervise claims involving separate windstorm or hail deductibles. The training must address consistent application of policy language and accurate communication with policyholders.

**B.4.13** An insurer must maintain claim records sufficient to show how it determined the applicable deductible. The records must include relevant inspection findings, estimates, communications, and policy provisions considered.

**B.4.14** An insurer must consider information submitted by a policyholder concerning the cause or extent of damage. An insurer must document its consideration of material information that affects coverage or deductible application.

**B.4.15** An insurer must not delay investigation or payment of a covered claim because a dispute exists concerning the application of a deductible. An insurer must handle any undisputed portion of the claim in accordance with the policy and applicable law.

**B.4.16** An insurer must use claim correspondence that accurately identifies the applicable deductible. Correspondence must not imply that the deductible applies to all property damage when the claim determination is more limited.

**B.4.17** An insurer must provide a reasonable explanation when it changes the deductible previously identified for a claim. The explanation must state the information or policy provision supporting the change.

**B.4.18** An insurer must apply claim standards consistently among similarly situated policyholders. An insurer must not vary deductible application based on an impermissible consideration.

**B.4.19** An insurer must make claim decisions based on the policy in effect for the reported loss. An insurer must not rely on later policy language to alter the deductible applicable to that loss.

**B.4.20** An insurer must apply this section to claims under a policy that provides a separate windstorm or hail deductible. This section does not authorize coverage, a deductible, or a claim payment not provided by the policy.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.** An insurer must file any policy form, endorsement, rule, rate, rating factor, or manual provision that establishes or revises a separate windstorm and hail deductible before using the provision. The filing must clearly identify the coverage to which the deductible applies.

An insurer must describe the deductible in language that is clear and consistent throughout the filing. The description must distinguish the windstorm and hail deductible from any other deductible applicable to the policy.

An insurer must include the proposed policy language that will be delivered to policyholders. The language must state the conditions under which the separate deductible applies.

An insurer must file all related declarations, endorsements, notices, and application materials that disclose the deductible. An insurer must ensure that those materials do not conflict with the filed policy language.

An insurer must provide a complete explanation of the proposed deductible structure. The explanation must identify the underwriting purpose and rating treatment of the separate deductible.

An insurer must support the filed rate or rating impact with sufficient information to permit review. The supporting information must show how the separate deductible is reflected in the insurer’s rating methodology.

An insurer must identify any geographic, construction, occupancy, or coverage limitations associated with the separate deductible. The filing must explain how those limitations will be administered.

An insurer must state whether the separate deductible applies to direct physical loss, additional coverage, or both. The filing must explain the treatment of losses involving concurrent causes or multiple covered perils.

An insurer must provide clear claim-handling instructions for determining when the separate deductible applies. Those instructions must be consistent with the policy language and the insurer’s filed rules.

An insurer must not use a separate windstorm and hail deductible unless the applicable filing requirements have been satisfied. An insurer must maintain filed materials in a form that permits the Department to verify the deductible’s use.

An insurer must request an effective date for each filing. The requested effective date must allow for completion of the Department’s review and any required revisions.

An insurer may implement an approved filing only in accordance with its approved effective date. An insurer must not apply the filing to coverage issued or renewed before that effective date unless the policy terms permit the change.

An insurer must provide policyholders with any notice required for the implementation of a separate windstorm and hail deductible. The notice must accurately describe the deductible and its application.

An insurer must administer the deductible uniformly in accordance with the filed policy forms and rules. An insurer must not apply the deductible in a manner that differs from the filed provisions.

An insurer must promptly correct any policy, rating, or claims practice that does not conform to the approved filing. The insurer must maintain records demonstrating the corrective action taken.

An insurer remains responsible for ensuring that its separate windstorm and hail deductible complies with applicable insurance requirements. Approval of a filing does not relieve an insurer of that responsibility.
