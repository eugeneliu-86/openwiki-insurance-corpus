# Texas Department of Insurance Bulletin B-2021-08 — Separate Windstorm and Hail Deductibles

Issued 2021-08-19. Applies to residential property policies delivered or issued for delivery in Texas with an effective date on or after 2022-01-01.

## B.1 Purpose

This bulletin states the department's requirements for the disclosure, application, and minimum and maximum amounts of separate windstorm and hail deductibles on residential property policies, and supersedes Bulletin B-2016-04 in its entirety.

## B.2 Percentage Deductibles Permitted

An insurer may apply a separate windstorm and hail deductible expressed as a percentage of the Coverage A limit of liability. The percentage may not be less than one percent and may not exceed five percent of the Coverage A limit, except in the seacoast territories described in B.6 where the maximum permitted percentage is ten percent.

An insurer may alternatively apply a flat dollar windstorm and hail deductible, which may not be less than the policy's all-other-perils deductible.

## B.3 Application to a Loss

Where a loss is caused by windstorm or hail and a separate windstorm and hail deductible applies, only that deductible is applied to the loss. The all-other-perils deductible must not also be applied to the same loss.

Where a single occurrence causes loss from both a windstorm or hail peril and another covered peril, and the damage attributable to each can be separately determined, each deductible applies to its respective portion of the loss. Where the damage cannot be separately determined, only the larger single deductible applies to the entire loss.

## B.4 Disclosure Requirements

An insurer must disclose the separate windstorm and hail deductible on the declarations page in type no smaller than the type used for the all-other-perils deductible, stated both as a percentage and as the dollar amount that percentage produces at the Coverage A limit in effect at issuance.

An insurer must provide written notice to the policyholder at least thirty days before the renewal effective date of any increase in the windstorm and hail deductible percentage. Failure to provide this notice renders the increase ineffective for that renewal term, and the prior percentage continues to apply.

## B.5 Named Storm Provisions

An insurer may apply a windstorm and hail deductible on a per-named-storm basis rather than a per-occurrence basis, provided the policy states the trigger and duration of the named storm period explicitly. The named storm period may not begin earlier than the time a storm is named by the National Hurricane Center and may not extend beyond seventy-two hours after that storm's designation is discontinued.

## B.6 Seacoast Territories

For the purposes of B.2, the seacoast territories are the first tier coastal counties and those portions of Harris County designated in the department's territory definitions in effect on the date of this bulletin.

## B.7 Filing

An insurer applying a separate windstorm and hail deductible must file the applicable amendatory endorsement and rate rule with the department, and may not apply the deductible before the filing is approved or deemed approved.
