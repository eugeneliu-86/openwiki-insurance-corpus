# Texas Bulletin B-2019-02 — Prompt Payment of Claims




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This bulletin states the Department’s expectations for prompt payment of claims by an admitted insurer. An insurer must administer covered claims in a manner that promotes fair, timely, and accurate claim resolution.

**B.1.2** This bulletin applies to an admitted insurer that delivers, issues for delivery, renews, or administers an insurance policy in Texas. It applies to claim handling activity conducted by an insurer and by a person acting on the insurer’s behalf.

**B.1.3** An insurer remains responsible for compliance when a managing general agent, third-party administrator, adjuster, or other representative performs claim functions. Delegation of a claim function does not relieve an insurer of its obligations under this bulletin.

**B.1.4** This bulletin applies to a claim submitted by an insured, claimant, beneficiary, or other person seeking policy benefits. An insurer must evaluate each claim according to the policy, the available information, and applicable requirements.

**B.1.5** An insurer must maintain claim practices that support prompt acknowledgment, investigation, evaluation, communication, and payment or denial. Claim practices must not create avoidable delay in resolving a claim.

**B.1.6** An insurer may request information reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim. An insurer must make requests clearly and must not seek information that is unnecessary to the claim decision.

**B.1.7** An insurer must communicate with a claimant in a manner that reasonably identifies information needed to complete claim handling. An insurer must provide a meaningful response when a claimant seeks information concerning claim status.

**B.1.8** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation before making a claim decision. An insurer must document material claim activity sufficiently to demonstrate the basis for its decision.

**B.1.9** An insurer must not use a claim investigation, policy interpretation, or internal review process to postpone a decision without a reasonable claim-related basis. An insurer must act diligently when information material to the claim becomes available.

**B.1.10** An insurer must pay benefits when payment is due under the policy and the claim determination. An insurer must not condition payment of an undisputed amount on resolution of a disputed matter.

**B.1.11** An insurer that denies a claim, in whole or in part, must provide an explanation that identifies the basis for the decision. The explanation must be consistent with the policy and the information relied upon by the insurer.

**B.1.12** An insurer must apply deductible provisions as stated in the policy and as supported by the claim record. The base deductible minimum must be administered consistently with applicable policy terms.

**B.1.13** An insurer must evaluate replacement cost settlement in accordance with the policy and the claim record. The insured-to-value threshold for replacement cost settlement must be applied fairly and consistently.

**B.1.14** An insurer must evaluate loss of use benefits under the applicable policy provisions. The loss of use limit must be applied in a manner consistent with the policy and the facts of the claim.

**B.1.15** An insurer must retain records sufficient for the Department to evaluate whether its claim practices comply with this bulletin. Records must reflect material communications, requests for information, claim decisions, and payments.

**B.1.16** An insurer must ensure that personnel involved in claim handling understand the insurer’s procedures for prompt payment of claims. An insurer must provide appropriate oversight of claim handling performed on its behalf.

**B.1.17** This bulletin applies to claim activity occurring after the bulletin takes effect. An insurer must not apply this bulletin in a manner that impairs rights established before the bulletin takes effect.

**B.1.18** The Department may review an insurer’s claim practices for consistency with this bulletin. An insurer must cooperate with a Department inquiry concerning claim handling and prompt payment practices.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer must administer a claim in good faith and with due regard for the interests of the claimant. An insurer must maintain procedures that support prompt claim handling.

**B.2.2** An insurer must treat a communication concerning a claim as a claim-related communication when the communication reasonably identifies the loss, the coverage, or the person seeking benefits. An insurer may request clarification when the communication does not provide sufficient information for claim handling.

**B.2.3** An insurer must establish a claim file upon receipt of notice of a claim. The claim file must contain material information received or developed during claim handling.

**B.2.4** An insurer must document the date on which notice of a claim is received. An insurer must preserve records sufficient to demonstrate its handling of the claim.

**B.2.5** An insurer must acknowledge communications from a claimant or the claimant's authorized representative. An insurer must provide a meaningful response that addresses the communication received.

**B.2.6** An insurer may communicate with a claimant by electronic means when permitted by law and when the method is reasonably calculated to provide notice. An insurer must protect nonpublic personal information used in electronic communications.

**B.2.7** An insurer must identify information reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim. An insurer must not request information that is not relevant to the coverage determination, valuation, or payment decision.

**B.2.8** An insurer must explain a request for claim information in plain language. An insurer must identify the purpose of information requested when the purpose would not otherwise be apparent.

**B.2.9** An insurer must review information submitted in support of a claim. An insurer must not disregard material information without a reasonable basis documented in the claim file.

**B.2.10** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation before denying a claim. An insurer may rely on qualified persons to investigate matters requiring specialized knowledge.

**B.2.11** An insurer must evaluate coverage under the policy terms applicable to the reported loss. An insurer must apply policy provisions consistently with applicable law.

**B.2.12** An insurer must communicate material coverage issues to the claimant when those issues affect claim handling. An insurer must avoid communications that are misleading, incomplete, or likely to cause confusion.

**B.2.13** An insurer must provide a reasonable explanation when it determines that coverage is unavailable. The explanation must identify the material basis for the determination in language understandable to a claimant.

**B.2.14** An insurer must provide a reasonable explanation when it limits payment for a covered loss. The explanation must identify the material facts and policy provisions supporting the limitation.

**B.2.15** An insurer must not deny a claim based on an alleged policy condition without determining whether the condition applies to the facts of the loss. An insurer must document the basis for its determination.

**B.2.16** An insurer must not use a claim investigation as a basis for unnecessary delay. An insurer must pursue available information with reasonable diligence.

**B.2.17** An insurer must communicate with persons involved in claim handling in a manner that supports accurate evaluation. An insurer must provide relevant claim information to personnel responsible for claim decisions.

**B.2.18** An insurer must maintain authority levels that permit claim personnel to make appropriate decisions. An insurer must not impose internal practices that unreasonably delay a claim decision or payment.

**B.2.19** An insurer must evaluate estimates, invoices, photographs, reports, and other claim materials in good faith. An insurer may seek clarification or further support when the materials present a reasonable question.

**B.2.20** An insurer must not misrepresent pertinent facts or policy provisions relating to coverage. An insurer must correct a material misstatement when the insurer becomes aware of it.

**B.2.21** An insurer must communicate a claim decision to the claimant or the claimant's authorized representative. The communication must reasonably identify whether the claim is accepted, denied, or subject to further evaluation.

**B.2.22** An insurer must pay amounts that it determines are owed under the policy without withholding payment for unrelated matters. An insurer must not condition payment on the resolution of a dispute that does not affect the amount owed.

**B.2.23** An insurer may issue payment for an undisputed portion of a claim while evaluating a disputed portion. An insurer must clearly communicate the basis for any amount not paid.

**B.2.24** An insurer must issue claim payments in a manner that reasonably enables the intended recipient to obtain the payment. An insurer must promptly address a payment that is returned, rejected, or otherwise not received.

**B.2.25** An insurer must provide a claimant with information needed to understand a payment. The information must reasonably identify the claim, the payment purpose, and material deductions or limitations.

**B.2.26** An insurer must handle communications from an attorney, public adjuster, or other authorized representative in accordance with the representative's authority. An insurer may request documentation of authority when reasonably necessary.

**B.2.27** An insurer must maintain complaint-handling procedures for concerns about claim administration. An insurer must investigate a complaint and document its response.

**B.2.28** An insurer must ensure that claim personnel receive instruction concerning applicable claim-handling obligations. An insurer must supervise claim personnel in a manner designed to promote compliance with those obligations.

**B.2.29** An insurer must ensure that vendors used in claim handling perform assigned services competently and fairly. An insurer remains responsible for claim-handling conduct performed on its behalf.

**B.2.30** An insurer must not use a vendor arrangement to avoid its claim-handling responsibilities. An insurer must review vendor work that materially affects a coverage, valuation, or payment decision.

**B.2.31** An insurer must preserve claim records in a form that permits meaningful review of its actions. The records must reflect material communications, decisions, and payment activity.

**B.2.32** An insurer must document a material change in its claim position. The documentation must identify the information or analysis supporting the change.

**B.2.33** An insurer must communicate respectfully with a claimant and other persons participating in claim handling. An insurer must not use coercive, abusive, or deceptive claim practices.

**B.2.34** An insurer must make reasonable efforts to accommodate communication needs disclosed by a claimant. An insurer must not use communication practices that unfairly impede a claimant's ability to pursue benefits.

**B.2.35** An insurer must evaluate a claim based on the facts available at the time of the decision. An insurer must reconsider a decision when material new information warrants further evaluation.

**B.2.36** An insurer must not require a claimant to undertake unnecessary acts as a condition of claim consideration. An insurer may require reasonable cooperation that is material to the claim.

**B.2.37** An insurer must explain material requests for cooperation in a manner that allows the claimant to understand what is sought. An insurer must consider information reasonably available from its own records or investigation.

**B.2.38** An insurer must address material claim issues before closing a claim file. An insurer may close a claim file when the claim has been resolved, withdrawn, or otherwise appropriately concluded.

**B.2.39** An insurer must reopen a claim file when it receives information requiring further claim handling. An insurer must document the reason for reopening the claim file.

**B.2.40** An insurer must not retaliate against a claimant for making an inquiry, complaint, or request concerning a claim. An insurer must evaluate the claim independently of such activity.

**B.2.41** An insurer must apply these requirements to claim-handling activity conducted by the insurer and by persons acting on its behalf. An insurer must maintain controls reasonably designed to detect and correct noncompliant practices.

**B.2.42** An insurer must comply with these requirements in connection with claims subject to this bulletin. An insurer must implement procedures necessary to support ongoing compliance.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** This section applies to a notice or disclosure that an insurer provides concerning a claim. An insurer must provide the notice or disclosure in a manner consistent with this section.

**B.3.2** An insurer must comply with this section when this section takes effect. An insurer must maintain compliance while a claim remains pending.

**B.3.3** An insurer must make a notice or disclosure clear and conspicuous. An insurer must use language reasonably understandable to the intended recipient.

**B.3.4** An insurer must identify itself in a notice concerning a claim. An insurer must provide information that permits the claimant to communicate with the insurer about the claim.

**B.3.5** An insurer must accurately describe the status of a claim. An insurer must not characterize a claim as resolved when material claim activity remains pending.

**B.3.6** An insurer must direct a claim notice to the claimant or an authorized representative. An insurer must protect information that is not authorized for disclosure to the recipient.

**B.3.7** An insurer must acknowledge receipt of a claim communication when an acknowledgment is required by this section. The acknowledgment must identify the claim communication received.

**B.3.8** An insurer must notify the claimant when it receives claim materials that affect claim handling. The notice must reasonably identify the materials received.

**B.3.9** An insurer must state what information or material it seeks when requesting claim information. The request must not be vague, misleading, or unnecessarily burdensome.

**B.3.10** An insurer must not request information or material already available to the insurer. An insurer must review available claim information before making a request.

**B.3.11** An insurer must explain the relevance of requested information or material when the relevance is not apparent. The explanation must relate to the claim under review.

**B.3.12** An insurer must not state that a claim is incomplete if available information permits a claim determination. An insurer may seek clarification when clarification is reasonably necessary.

**B.3.13** An insurer must provide a clear disclosure when it accepts a claim in whole or in part. The disclosure must describe the claim decision and the action the insurer will take.

**B.3.14** An insurer must provide a clear explanation when it denies a claim in whole or in part. The explanation must identify the basis for the denial under the applicable policy.

**B.3.15** An insurer must distinguish a payment for an undisputed portion of a claim from a disputed portion. An insurer must not imply that payment of an undisputed portion resolves a disputed portion.

**B.3.16** An insurer may reserve rights under a policy when appropriate. A reservation of rights notice must not misstate the policy or mislead the claimant about claim status.

**B.3.17** An insurer must identify material issues that prevent resolution of a claim. An insurer must describe what claim activity remains necessary to address those issues.

**B.3.18** An insurer must disclose the basis for a payment made on a claim. The disclosure must reasonably identify the claim benefits or damages addressed by the payment.

**B.3.19** An insurer must provide payment information in a manner that permits the claimant to understand the payment. An insurer must identify material conditions associated with the payment.

**B.3.20** An insurer must provide notice of a material change in its claim position. The notice must explain the reason for the change.

**B.3.21** An insurer must correct a prior notice or disclosure that contains a material error. The correction must clearly identify the information being corrected.

**B.3.22** An insurer must not state or imply that a claimant waives rights by failing to respond to a communication, unless the statement is accurate under the applicable policy. An insurer must not use a notice to obtain an improper waiver.

**B.3.23** An insurer may provide a notice through an electronic channel when the channel is appropriate for the recipient. An insurer must preserve the confidentiality of claim information transmitted through that channel.

**B.3.24** An insurer may communicate orally with a claimant about a claim. An insurer must document a material oral communication in the claim record.

**B.3.25** An insurer must provide a notice in an accessible format when reasonably requested by a claimant. An insurer must not use format limitations to impede a claimant's understanding of a claim decision.

**B.3.26** An insurer must ensure that claim notices and disclosures are consistent with information in the claim record. An insurer must resolve material inconsistencies without delay.

**B.3.27** An insurer must maintain records of notices and disclosures provided under this section. The records must permit the insurer to demonstrate compliance with this section.

**B.3.28** An insurer must record when a material notice was sent and the manner in which it was sent. An insurer must retain information sufficient to identify the recipient.

**B.3.29** An insurer must maintain procedures designed to support accurate claim notices and disclosures. The procedures must address review of material claim communications.

**B.3.30** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section when a service provider handles claim communications. An insurer must maintain oversight of the service provider's claim communication practices.

**B.3.31** An insurer may use standardized claim forms or notices. Use of a standardized form or notice does not relieve an insurer of an obligation under this section.

**B.3.32** An insurer must not use policy language, correspondence, or claim forms in a manner that conflicts with a notice required by this section. An insurer must provide clarification when a claimant could reasonably misunderstand the communication.

**B.3.33** An insurer must promptly address a material deficiency in a notice or disclosure after discovering the deficiency. Corrective action does not limit the Department's authority under this section.

**B.3.34** An insurer must not use a claim notice or disclosure to harass, coerce, or mislead a claimant. An insurer must ensure that claim communications are professional and accurate.

**B.3.35** An insurer must limit claim disclosures to information relevant to claim handling. An insurer must not disclose confidential information that is unrelated to the claim.

**B.3.36** An insurer must not condition a required notice or disclosure on a claimant's agreement to an improper term. An insurer must not use a required notice to discourage submission of a claim.

**B.3.37** An insurer must cure a failure to comply with this section upon discovery of the failure. A cure does not excuse a failure to comply with an obligation under this section.

**B.3.38** An insurer must be able to demonstrate compliance with this section upon request by the Department. An insurer must cooperate with the Department's review of claim notices and disclosures.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer handling a claim subject to prompt-payment requirements. An insurer must apply these standards to communications, investigations, decisions, and payments.

**B.4.2** An insurer must establish procedures that allow a claimant to submit a claim and supporting information through reasonable means. An insurer must maintain records showing the date on which the claim was received.

**B.4.3** An insurer must acknowledge receipt of a claim not later than fifteen (15) days days after receipt. The acknowledgment must identify information reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim, if any.

**B.4.4** An insurer may request information relevant to coverage, liability, damages, or the amount of loss. An insurer must limit its requests to information reasonably necessary to resolve the claim.

**B.4.5** An insurer must communicate requests for additional information clearly and in writing when practicable. A request must describe the information sought with sufficient specificity for the claimant to respond.

**B.4.6** An insurer must promptly review information received in support of a claim. An insurer may seek clarification when submitted information is incomplete, inconsistent, or insufficient for a coverage determination.

**B.4.7** An insurer must not delay a claim decision by repeatedly requesting information already provided. An insurer must reasonably evaluate available information when a claimant cannot provide information outside the claimant's control.

**B.4.8** An insurer must accept or reject a claim within fifteen (15) business days business days after receiving all items reasonably requested for the claim. The decision must be based on the policy terms and the information available to the insurer.

**B.4.9** An insurer that accepts a claim must provide notice of the acceptance to the claimant. The notice must state the basis for the payment and identify any information needed to complete payment.

**B.4.10** An insurer that rejects a claim must provide written notice to the claimant. The notice must explain the factual and policy basis for the rejection in plain language.

**B.4.11** An insurer may accept part of a claim and reject another part of the claim when the policy and available information support that result. The insurer must clearly distinguish the accepted portion from the rejected portion.

**B.4.12** An insurer must not condition payment of an undisputed amount on resolution of a disputed amount. An insurer must process an undisputed amount in accordance with this section.

**B.4.13** An insurer must pay an accepted claim within five (5) business days business days after accepting the claim. Payment must be made to the person entitled to payment under the policy.

**B.4.14** An insurer may issue payment subject to a valid reservation of rights when appropriate under the policy. A reservation of rights must not be used to avoid payment of an amount that is otherwise due.

**B.4.15** An insurer must promptly notify a claimant when payment cannot be issued because required payment information is unavailable or conflicting. The notice must identify the information needed to issue payment.

**B.4.16** An insurer must maintain claim files that document material communications, requests, decisions, and payments. The file must permit the department to determine whether the insurer complied with this section.

**B.4.17** An insurer must train personnel responsible for claim handling on the insurer's prompt-payment procedures. An insurer must provide reasonable supervision to ensure that claim handling is consistent with those procedures.

**B.4.18** An insurer must apply these standards without unfair discrimination among similarly situated claimants. An insurer must not use claim-handling practices that unreasonably hinder a claimant's ability to obtain benefits.

**B.4.19** An insurer may use a vendor, administrator, or other representative to perform claim-handling functions. The insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section when claim-handling functions are delegated.

**B.4.20** An insurer must apply this section to claims received on or after the bulletin's effective date. An insurer must continue to comply with any applicable obligation that survives claim payment or closure.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

An insurer must file its prompt-payment claims procedures with the department before use. The filing must identify the lines of insurance to which the procedures apply.

An insurer must describe the internal process used to receive, evaluate, and resolve claims subject to prompt-payment requirements. The description must be sufficiently clear to permit departmental review.

An insurer must include the claim communications used with claimants and representatives when those communications concern claim acknowledgment, information requests, investigation, acceptance, rejection, or payment. The communications must be consistent with the insurer’s filed procedures.

An insurer must identify the personnel or functions responsible for monitoring compliance with prompt-payment requirements. The insurer must maintain adequate oversight of claim handling performed by employees, contractors, and managing agents.

An insurer must file any material revision to its prompt-payment claims procedures before implementing the revision. A revision is material when it may affect the timeliness or manner of claim handling.

An insurer may make a temporary operational adjustment necessary to address an emergency or systems disruption. The insurer must notify the department promptly when the adjustment materially affects compliance with prompt-payment requirements.

An insurer must ensure that a filing accurately reflects its current claim practices. The department may request clarification or additional information needed to evaluate a filing.

An insurer must respond to a departmental request for additional information in a timely manner. An incomplete response may delay departmental review.

An insurer must not use a procedure that conflicts with applicable prompt-payment requirements. Departmental acceptance of a filing does not excuse noncompliance with those requirements.

An insurer must retain records supporting the filed procedures and any subsequent revisions. The records must be available for departmental examination.

An insurer must apply filed procedures consistently to claims within the stated scope of the filing. An insurer must promptly correct a practice that differs materially from its filed procedures.

An insurer may use a vendor or other service provider in the claim process. The insurer remains responsible for compliance with prompt-payment requirements and with the procedures filed under this section.

An insurer must provide notice to the department when it discontinues use of a filed procedure. The notice must identify the procedure being discontinued and the procedure that will replace it, if any.

A filing becomes effective upon departmental approval unless the department specifies a different effective date. An insurer must not implement a filing before its effective date.

An insurer must treat a departmental request for revision as a condition of approval when the request concerns compliance with prompt-payment requirements. The insurer may submit a revised filing for further review.

This section applies to each admitted insurer writing business in this state that is subject to prompt-payment claim requirements. An insurer must comply with this section for claims handled after the applicable filing becomes effective.
