# Texas Bulletin B-2021-08 — Separate Windstorm and Hail Deductibles




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This bulletin states the Department’s expectations for an admitted insurer that offers residential property insurance with separate windstorm and hail deductibles. An insurer must administer those deductibles in a manner that is clear, consistent, and not misleading.

**B.1.2** This bulletin applies to an insurer that delivers, issues for delivery, or renews a residential property policy in this state. It applies when the policy provides a deductible that may be applied to windstorm loss, hail loss, or both.

**B.1.3** An insurer must identify the deductible applicable to windstorm and hail losses in the policy and in any related coverage communication. The identification must enable an insured to distinguish that deductible from deductibles applicable to other covered perils.

**B.1.4** An insurer may provide separate windstorm and hail deductibles if the policy terms state when each deductible applies. An insurer must not use policy language that creates uncertainty about whether a loss is subject to a separate deductible.

**B.1.5** An insurer must apply a separate windstorm and hail deductible in accordance with the policy language provided to the insured. An insurer must not apply a deductible based on an undisclosed interpretation of a policy term.

**B.1.6** An insurer must ensure that declarations, endorsements, applications, and other policy materials are reasonably consistent concerning the applicable deductible. If materials conflict, an insurer must resolve the conflict in a manner that does not unfairly disadvantage the insured.

**B.1.7** An insurer may use an all-other-perils deductible minimum only if its policy materials clearly distinguish that concept from a separate windstorm and hail deductible. An insurer must not suggest that the all-other-perils deductible minimum governs a windstorm or hail loss unless the policy so provides.

**B.1.8** An insurer must describe any coinsurance threshold accurately and separately from deductible provisions. An insurer must not present a coinsurance threshold as though it were a deductible applicable to windstorm or hail damage.

**B.1.9** An insurer must describe an ALE percentage accurately when communicating coverage for additional living expense. An insurer must not represent an ALE percentage as changing the deductible applicable to a covered windstorm or hail loss.

**B.1.10** An insurer must provide producers and personnel responsible for policy issuance and claims handling with guidance sufficient to administer separate windstorm and hail deductibles consistently. That guidance must address the policy terms that determine whether the deductible applies.

**B.1.11** An insurer must handle a claim involving windstorm or hail damage using the deductible stated in the applicable policy. An insurer must explain the basis for the deductible applied when an insured reasonably requests that explanation.

**B.1.12** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate its compliance with this bulletin. The records must show the policy terms in effect and the basis for the deductible applied to a claim.

**B.1.13** An insurer must make its deductible disclosures available before an insured is bound to coverage and when the policy is issued or renewed. The disclosures must be written so that a reasonable insured can understand the separate treatment of windstorm and hail losses.

**B.1.14** This bulletin applies from its effective date. An insurer must take reasonable steps to bring applicable policy forms, disclosures, underwriting practices, and claim practices into compliance from that date.

**B.1.15** Nothing in this bulletin authorizes an insurer to apply a deductible that is not permitted by the policy or applicable law. An insurer remains responsible for fair claim handling and for compliance with all requirements governing residential property insurance.

**B.1.16** The Department may review an insurer’s policy language, communications, and claim practices for consistency with this bulletin. An insurer must cooperate with a Department request for information concerning its administration of separate windstorm and hail deductibles.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** These requirements apply to an admitted insurer that issues or renews property insurance with a separate windstorm or hail deductible. An insurer must administer the deductible in accordance with B.2.

**B.2.2** An insurer may offer a separate windstorm or hail deductible only when the deductible is clearly described in the policy. The description must identify the loss conditions that cause the deductible to apply.

**B.2.3** An insurer must not establish a named storm windstorm and hail deductible that is less than one (1) percent. The insurer must apply the deductible consistently with the policy language.

**B.2.4** An insurer must not establish a hurricane deductible that exceeds five (5) percent. An insurer must not use another deductible label to avoid that limitation.

**B.2.5** For property located in a seacoast territory, an insurer must not establish a windstorm deductible that exceeds ten (10) percent. The insurer must identify the applicable territory before applying the deductible.

**B.2.6** An insurer must use territory definitions that are reasonably capable of consistent application. The insurer must retain sufficient information to show the basis for the territory assignment.

**B.2.7** An insurer must state whether a separate deductible applies to windstorm, hail, hurricane, named storm, or another identified peril. The policy must not leave the applicable deductible to discretionary claim handling.

**B.2.8** An insurer must describe the event that triggers a named storm deductible. The description must permit a policyholder to determine whether the deductible applies to a reported loss.

**B.2.9** An insurer must not apply a separate deductible unless the loss falls within the policy language establishing that deductible. A claim determination must be supported by the facts of the reported event.

**B.2.10** An insurer must apply the deductible shown in the policy in force for the loss. An insurer must not substitute a different deductible after a loss is reported.

**B.2.11** An insurer must disclose a separate windstorm or hail deductible in a manner reasonably likely to be noticed by an applicant or policyholder. The disclosure must not be misleading as to the effect of the deductible.

**B.2.12** An insurer must use clear language when describing how the deductible is calculated. The policy language must identify the value or limit to which the deductible applies when that information is necessary to calculate the deductible.

**B.2.13** An insurer must not represent that a separate deductible applies to all storm losses unless the policy language supports that representation. Communications concerning coverage must be consistent with the issued policy.

**B.2.14** An insurer must maintain underwriting and rating practices that support the separate deductible offered. Those practices must be applied without unfair discrimination.

**B.2.15** An insurer must maintain carrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exceptioncarrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exception (see Personal Lines Underwriting Manual, R510 Rule 510 — Texas State Exceptions) as necessary to support the use of a separate deductible. The implementation must be consistent with the policy language and rating practice.

**B.2.16** An insurer must ensure that its underwriting personnel can identify when a separate deductible may be offered. An insurer must provide controls for resolving questions concerning applicability.

**B.2.17** An insurer must ensure that its claim personnel can identify the deductible applicable to a reported loss. Claim personnel must document the basis for applying a separate deductible.

**B.2.18** An insurer must provide a policyholder with an explanation of the deductible application upon request. The explanation must identify the policy provision relied upon for the determination.

**B.2.19** An insurer must not impose more than one deductible for the same covered loss unless the policy clearly permits that result. The claim file must support any application of multiple deductibles.

**B.2.20** An insurer must not apply a windstorm or hail deductible to damage that is not subject to that deductible under the policy. The insurer must evaluate mixed-cause losses in accordance with the applicable policy provisions.

**B.2.21** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with B.2. The records must show the deductible selected, the policy language used, and the basis for claim application.

**B.2.22** An insurer must retain records in a form that permits review by the department. Records must be reasonably organized and available upon request.

**B.2.23** An insurer must establish controls to prevent the issuance of policy language that conflicts with its underwriting practices. The controls must address policy issuance, renewal, and endorsement activity.

**B.2.24** An insurer must review forms, notices, and other policyholder communications for consistency with B.2. An insurer must correct material inconsistencies before using the communication.

**B.2.25** An insurer must not characterize a deductible as optional when the policy requires it. An insurer must clearly distinguish an available option from a mandatory policy term.

**B.2.26** An insurer must not characterize a deductible as mandatory when the policy provides a choice. The insurer must accurately describe the available deductible selections.

**B.2.27** An insurer must ensure that a deductible selection made by an applicant or policyholder is reflected in the issued policy. The insurer must maintain evidence supporting the selection.

**B.2.28** An insurer must provide an applicant or policyholder with information sufficient to understand the separate deductible before the policy is issued or changed. The information must be consistent with the coverage offered.

**B.2.29** An insurer must not alter a deductible through an endorsement unless the endorsement clearly states the change. The insurer must provide the endorsement in a manner consistent with applicable policy administration requirements.

**B.2.30** An insurer must apply a changed deductible only after the change is properly reflected in the policy record. An insurer must not apply the changed deductible to a loss outside its effective use.

**B.2.31** An insurer must establish procedures for correcting a policy record that does not accurately reflect the deductible selected. The correction procedure must preserve the basis for the correction.

**B.2.32** An insurer must investigate a dispute concerning deductible applicability promptly and fairly. The insurer must communicate its determination to the policyholder in clear language.

**B.2.33** An insurer must not deny or reduce a claim solely because a separate deductible may apply without determining whether the policy conditions are satisfied. The claim file must reflect that determination.

**B.2.34** An insurer must apply the same deductible interpretation to similarly situated policyholders. Any variation in application must be supported by a material difference in policy language or loss facts.

**B.2.35** An insurer must monitor complaints and claim disputes involving separate windstorm and hail deductibles. The insurer must address recurring operational errors identified through that monitoring.

**B.2.36** An insurer must maintain training appropriate to the personnel responsible for underwriting, servicing, and claims handling. The training must address the requirements of B.2.

**B.2.37** An insurer must ensure that automated systems apply separate deductibles in accordance with the issued policy. The insurer must test system changes that may affect deductible application.

**B.2.38** An insurer must correct a system or operational error that causes an improper deductible to be applied. The insurer must take appropriate action to address affected policyholders and claims.

**B.2.39** An insurer must not use a separate deductible in a manner that is unfair, misleading, or inconsistent with the policy. The insurer remains responsible for the acts of personnel and representatives involved in the transaction.

**B.2.40** An insurer must make its deductible practices available for examination under B.2. The insurer must provide complete and accurate information concerning those practices upon request.

**B.2.41** An insurer must implement these requirements before offering, issuing, renewing, changing, or administering a policy subject to B.2. An insurer must not delay compliance until a claim occurs.

**B.2.42** An insurer must apply these requirements to policy activity occurring after the requirements become effective for that activity. An insurer must not apply a requirement retroactively unless the policyholder receives the benefit of the change.

**B.2.43** An insurer must continue to comply with B.2 unless and until the department provides otherwise through an authorized action. Compliance with B.2 does not excuse compliance with any other applicable requirement.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** The requirements of this section apply to an admitted insurer that issues, renews, or amends a policy containing a windstorm deductible, a hail deductible, or both. An insurer must provide the notice and disclosure required by this section to the policyholder.

**B.3.2** An insurer must state whether the policy includes a windstorm deductible that is separate from any other deductible. The statement must be clear and conspicuous.

**B.3.3** An insurer must state whether the policy includes a hail deductible that is separate from any other deductible. The statement must be clear and conspicuous.

**B.3.4** An insurer must identify the coverage to which a windstorm deductible applies. The disclosure must describe the circumstances under which the deductible may be applied.

**B.3.5** An insurer must identify the coverage to which a hail deductible applies. The disclosure must describe the circumstances under which the deductible may be applied.

**B.3.6** An insurer must disclose when a separate windstorm deductible may apply instead of another deductible shown in the policy. The disclosure must not suggest that a deductible applies when the policy does not support its application.

**B.3.7** An insurer must disclose when a separate hail deductible may apply instead of another deductible shown in the policy. The disclosure must not suggest that a deductible applies when the policy does not support its application.

**B.3.8** An insurer must use language that enables a reasonable policyholder to distinguish a windstorm deductible from a hail deductible. An insurer must not combine the disclosures in a manner that obscures the separate application of either deductible.

**B.3.9** An insurer must provide the required disclosure at the time of application when the applicant is offered coverage containing a separate windstorm or hail deductible. The disclosure must be provided in a manner reasonably calculated to come to the applicant's attention.

**B.3.10** An insurer must provide the required disclosure with the policy when coverage is issued. The policyholder must be able to identify the deductible terms from the policy materials.

**B.3.11** An insurer must provide the required disclosure with a renewal offer when the renewal includes a separate windstorm or hail deductible. The insurer must clearly identify any change from the deductible terms previously offered.

**B.3.12** An insurer must provide written notice before increasing a windstorm deductible. The insurer must provide the notice at least thirty (30) days before the increase takes effect.

**B.3.13** A notice of a windstorm deductible increase must identify the deductible that will apply after the change takes effect. The notice must describe the policyholder's opportunity to review the proposed change.

**B.3.14** An insurer must not characterize a windstorm deductible increase as a routine renewal matter if the notice does not plainly disclose the increase. The notice must be separate from material that could reasonably distract from the change.

**B.3.15** An insurer must explain whether a hail deductible is changing at renewal. The insurer must distinguish that change from a change affecting a windstorm deductible.

**B.3.16** An insurer must state the basis used to express each separate deductible. The statement must allow the policyholder to understand how the deductible is determined under the policy.

**B.3.17** An insurer must disclose whether the deductible applies to covered physical loss, covered loss adjustment, or another policy obligation. The disclosure must be consistent with the policy language.

**B.3.18** An insurer must not use a disclosure that conflicts with the insuring agreement, exclusions, conditions, endorsements, or declarations. If a conflict exists, the insurer must correct the disclosure before using it.

**B.3.19** An insurer must identify any endorsement that establishes or changes a separate windstorm deductible or hail deductible. The endorsement description must be sufficiently specific for the policyholder to locate the applicable terms.

**B.3.20** An insurer must explain the relationship between a named storm designation and the application of a deductible when the policy makes that relationship relevant. The explanation must identify the event or authority used to determine the designation.

**B.3.21** If a policy uses a named storm period, the insurer must describe when that period begins. The description must be consistent with the policy terms governing the named storm designation.

**B.3.22** A named storm period must continue for seventy-two (72) hours after the named storm designation ends. An insurer must disclose that continuation in language that is clear to a reasonable policyholder.

**B.3.23** An insurer must not apply a named storm deductible outside the named storm period stated in the policy. The insurer must administer the deductible consistently with the disclosure provided under this section.

**B.3.24** An insurer must state whether damage from wind and damage from hail may be subject to different deductibles. The statement must not imply that a claim will be subject to more than the deductible authorized by the policy.

**B.3.25** An insurer must describe the process used to determine which deductible applies when a loss involves wind, hail, or a named storm. The description must not alter the insurer's obligations to investigate and adjust a claim fairly.

**B.3.26** An insurer must provide the disclosure in the same language as the policyholder communications used to offer or renew the coverage, when practicable. An insurer must ensure that the translated disclosure accurately reflects the deductible terms.

**B.3.27** An insurer may provide the disclosure electronically if the policyholder has agreed to receive policy communications electronically. The electronic disclosure must be capable of being retained and reproduced.

**B.3.28** An insurer must make the disclosure reasonably accessible to a policyholder who requests it. The insurer must not condition access to the disclosure on the submission of a claim.

**B.3.29** An insurer must maintain records demonstrating the form and content of each disclosure used for policies subject to this section. The records must be sufficient to show when the disclosure was provided.

**B.3.30** An insurer must maintain records of a windstorm deductible increase notice provided under B.3.12. The records must identify the policy form or endorsement affected by the notice.

**B.3.31** An insurer must ensure that its producers and representatives use disclosures consistent with this section. An insurer remains responsible for disclosures made on its behalf.

**B.3.32** An insurer must train personnel responsible for issuing, renewing, or servicing affected policies on the separate application of windstorm and hail deductibles. The training must address the disclosure obligations imposed by this section.

**B.3.33** An insurer must review its forms, notices, and electronic communications for consistency with B.3.2 through B.3.25. An insurer must correct material inconsistencies before using those materials.

**B.3.34** An insurer must not use ambiguous labels for a deductible that may apply to windstorm, hail, or a named storm. The label must correspond to the deductible description in the policy.

**B.3.35** An insurer must not state or imply that a separate deductible is mandatory unless the policy terms make it applicable. An insurer must not omit material conditions affecting the deductible's application.

**B.3.36** An insurer must provide a policyholder with a reasonable opportunity to obtain clarification regarding a disclosed windstorm or hail deductible. The insurer's response must be consistent with the policy and the disclosure.

**B.3.37** An insurer must apply this section to policy forms and endorsements used for risks in this state. An insurer must not avoid the requirements of this section by placing the required information only in marketing material.

**B.3.38** An insurer must administer the notice and disclosure requirements of this section in good faith. Compliance with B.3 does not limit any obligation imposed by applicable law or the policy contract.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** An insurer must apply this paragraph to a claim under a policy that contains a separate windstorm or hail deductible. This paragraph applies while this bulletin is in effect.

**B.4.2** An insurer must investigate a reported windstorm or hail loss promptly and in good faith. The investigation must be sufficient to identify the claimed damage and the cause of loss.

**B.4.3** An insurer must provide the claimant with a clear explanation of the information needed to evaluate the claim. The insurer must not request information that is not reasonably related to the investigation or adjustment of the claim.

**B.4.4** An insurer must identify the policy provision relied upon when applying a separate windstorm or hail deductible. The explanation must describe how the deductible affects the payment determination.

**B.4.5** An insurer must not apply a separate windstorm or hail deductible unless the insurer reasonably determines that the claimed damage resulted from the applicable covered peril. The insurer must document the basis for that determination in the claim file.

**B.4.6** An insurer must evaluate damage caused by windstorm or hail separately from damage caused by another peril when the available information permits a reasonable allocation. The insurer must explain any allocation that affects the deductible applied to the claim.

**B.4.7** An insurer must not allocate damage to windstorm or hail without a reasonable factual basis. A conclusion concerning causation must be supported by the inspection, records, expert analysis, or other relevant claim information.

**B.4.8** An insurer must communicate material claim decisions in a manner reasonably calculated to be understood by the claimant. The communication must state whether the separate windstorm or hail deductible was applied.

**B.4.9** An insurer must provide a claimant with an explanation of any denial based in whole or in part on the separate windstorm or hail deductible. The explanation must identify the relevant policy language and the facts supporting the decision.

**B.4.10** An insurer must not represent that damage is subject to a separate windstorm or hail deductible when the policy does not support that application. An insurer must correct a material misstatement concerning deductible applicability when the insurer becomes aware of it.

**B.4.11** An insurer must maintain claim records showing the basis for the deductible determination. The records must include material communications, inspection information, estimates, and payment calculations relevant to that determination.

**B.4.12** An insurer must ensure that personnel handling windstorm or hail claims are instructed concerning the policy terms governing separate deductibles. The insurer must provide reasonable oversight of claim handling practices involving those deductibles.

**B.4.13** An insurer may use an adjuster, engineer, contractor, or other qualified professional to assist with a claim investigation. The insurer remains responsible for the accuracy and fairness of the claim decision.

**B.4.14** An insurer must evaluate all information reasonably available when determining whether windstorm or hail caused claimed damage. The insurer must not rely solely on a general weather event report when claim-specific information indicates a different result.

**B.4.15** An insurer must explain the basis for any conclusion that claimed damage existed before the reported loss. The explanation must distinguish prior damage from damage attributed to the reported event when that distinction affects coverage or the deductible.

**B.4.16** An insurer must consider repair estimates and other information submitted by the claimant. The insurer must address material differences between its evaluation and information that reasonably supports a different claim outcome.

**B.4.17** An insurer must not delay a claim decision solely because the claim involves a separate windstorm or hail deductible. The insurer may seek additional information when it is reasonably necessary to resolve a material issue.

**B.4.18** An insurer must provide a clear payment explanation when a covered loss exceeds the applicable deductible. The explanation must show the relationship between the covered damage evaluation, the deductible, and the payment.

**B.4.19** An insurer must provide a clear explanation when no payment is issued because the covered loss does not exceed the applicable deductible. The explanation must not characterize the claim as denied if coverage exists but no payment is due after application of the deductible.

**B.4.20** An insurer must not apply a deductible more than permitted by the policy terms. The insurer must document the policy basis for any deductible application affecting the claim.

**B.4.21** An insurer must preserve material evidence obtained during the claim investigation. The insurer must retain sufficient information to permit review of the cause of loss determination and deductible application.

**B.4.22** An insurer must respond reasonably to a claimant’s request for an explanation of the deductible determination. The response must address the claimant’s material questions concerning the policy provision and the claim evaluation.

**B.4.23** An insurer must not condition consideration of a claim on the claimant’s acceptance of the insurer’s causation determination. The insurer may request reasonable cooperation needed to investigate the reported loss.

**B.4.24** An insurer must review a claim decision when material information not previously considered may affect the application of the separate windstorm or hail deductible. The insurer must communicate the result of that review to the claimant.

**B.4.25** An insurer must apply claim handling practices consistently for similarly situated claimants, subject to differences in policy terms and claim facts. The insurer must not use inconsistent deductible practices to discourage the reporting or pursuit of a claim.

**B.4.26** An insurer must ensure that claim communications do not misstate the scope of windstorm or hail coverage. Any explanation of a separate deductible must be consistent with the policy and the claim facts.

**B.4.27** An insurer must administer claims subject to this paragraph fairly and in good faith. Nothing in this paragraph permits an insurer to limit coverage or impose a deductible beyond the applicable policy terms.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** An insurer must file policy forms, endorsements, and related materials that provide a separate windstorm and hail deductible when filing is required by applicable law. The filing must identify the coverage to which the deductible applies.

**B.5.2** An insurer must present the deductible provision in clear policy language. The provision must state the circumstances under which the separate deductible applies.

**B.5.3** An insurer must file any rule used to determine the application of a separate windstorm and hail deductible. The rule must be consistent with the filed policy language.

**B.5.4** An insurer must file rating information associated with the deductible when filing is required by applicable law. The rating information must correspond to the coverage and deductible shown in the policy materials.

**B.5.5** An insurer must ensure that all submitted materials are internally consistent. Policy forms, endorsements, rules, and rating information must not create conflicting descriptions of the deductible.

**B.5.6** An insurer may use a filed deductible provision only after the filing has received any approval required by applicable law. Submission of a filing does not by itself authorize use of the provision.

**B.5.7** An insurer must not issue, deliver, or renew coverage using an unapproved deductible provision when approval is required by applicable law. An insurer must maintain controls to prevent unauthorized use of filed materials.

**B.5.8** An insurer must provide policyholders with any notice required by applicable law before applying a separate windstorm and hail deductible. The notice must be consistent with the policy form and related materials.

**B.5.9** An insurer must not alter the deductible applicable to an existing policy except as permitted by applicable law and the policy terms. Any change must be reflected in the insurer's records.

**B.5.10** An insurer must retain records supporting the filing and use of the deductible provision. The records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.5.11** An insurer must respond promptly to a Department request for information concerning a filing. The response must include materials necessary to explain the insurer's implementation of the deductible.

**B.5.12** An insurer remains responsible for compliance with applicable law after a filing becomes effective. Use of a separate windstorm and hail deductible must remain consistent with the filed materials.
