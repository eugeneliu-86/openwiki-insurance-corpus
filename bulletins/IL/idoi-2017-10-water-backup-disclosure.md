# Illinois Bulletin IDOI-2017-10 — Water Backup Coverage Disclosure




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** These requirements establish disclosure expectations for water backup coverage offered by an admitted insurer in Illinois. The disclosure is intended to assist applicants and insureds in understanding the scope of available protection.

**B.1.2** An insurer must provide a clear description of water backup coverage when that coverage is offered, limited, excluded, or subject to separate terms. The description must be consistent with the policy language and applicable underwriting practices.

**B.1.3** These requirements apply to personal lines property insurance issued or renewed by an admitted insurer in Illinois. An insurer may also use substantially similar disclosures for other property insurance where appropriate.

**B.1.4** An insurer must not describe water backup coverage in a manner that is misleading, incomplete, or likely to create an unreasonable expectation of coverage. The disclosure must distinguish water backup from other sources of water damage when the policy makes that distinction.

**B.1.5** An insurer must identify whether water backup coverage is included within the policy, available by endorsement, or unavailable. An insurer must present any material limitation in language reasonably calculated to be understood by the applicant or insured.

**B.1.6** An insurer must explain the relationship between water backup coverage and any applicable deductible. The disclosure may identify the minimum Section I deductible when that deductible affects the coverage described.

**B.1.7** An insurer must disclose when water backup coverage is subject to a separate coverage limit or other material condition. The disclosure must not imply that the policy provides broader protection than the applicable terms provide.

**B.1.8** An insurer may describe underwriting measures that affect eligibility for water backup coverage. An insurer must ensure that any such description accurately reflects the insurer's underwriting requirements.

**B.1.9** An insurer must clearly distinguish coverage for water backup from coverage for flood, surface water, seepage, or other excluded causes of loss. The disclosure must not substitute a general water-damage description for the policy's applicable terms.

**B.1.10** An insurer must provide the disclosure in connection with the sale, issuance, or renewal of applicable coverage. The insurer may provide the disclosure electronically when electronic delivery is permitted.

**B.1.11** An insurer must make the disclosure available in a form that can be retained by the applicant or insured. The insurer must maintain procedures reasonably designed to demonstrate that the disclosure was provided.

**B.1.12** An insurer must ensure that producer communications concerning water backup coverage are consistent with the required disclosure. An insurer remains responsible for the content of disclosures used in its marketing and sales process.

**B.1.13** An insurer may explain how policy conditions affect payment for a covered water backup loss. The disclosure may address the eighty percent condition when that condition is relevant to the policy's coverage.

**B.1.14** An insurer must not state or suggest that water backup coverage includes loss of use or living expenses unless the policy provides that protection. The disclosure may address the additional living expense limit when relevant to the coverage offered.

**B.1.15** These requirements apply on the effective date established by the Department. An insurer must apply the requirements prospectively to applicable policies issued or renewed on or after that effective date.

**B.1.16** An insurer must review its water backup coverage disclosures for consistency with these requirements. An insurer may revise its disclosures as necessary to maintain clarity and accuracy.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that offers, issues, renews, or amends personal lines property insurance in this State. An insurer must administer water backup coverage in a manner consistent with this section.

**B.2.2** An insurer must provide a clear disclosure when water backup coverage is not included in the base policy. The disclosure must identify the coverage as an available option when the insurer offers the option.

**B.2.3** An insurer must use plain language that a reasonable applicant or policyholder can understand. The disclosure must not obscure material limitations through formatting, placement, or terminology.

**B.2.4** An insurer must describe the types of water backup events addressed by the offered coverage. The description must distinguish covered events from excluded events.

**B.2.5** An insurer must identify whether coverage applies to water that enters through a sewer, drain, sump, or related system. An insurer must explain any condition that affects whether coverage applies.

**B.2.6** An insurer must disclose the most an insurer will pay under water backup coverage. The most an insurer will pay is $5,000 (five thousand dollars).

**B.2.7** An insurer must disclose any deductible that applies to water backup coverage. The disclosure must state whether the deductible differs from a deductible that applies to other property damage.

**B.2.8** An insurer must disclose material exclusions applicable to water backup coverage. An insurer must present exclusions in a manner that does not conflict with the coverage description.

**B.2.9** An insurer must disclose material conditions applicable to water backup coverage. The disclosure must explain conditions that may affect payment of a claim.

**B.2.10** An insurer must provide the disclosure before an applicant completes a purchase decision. An insurer must provide the disclosure in a manner that allows the applicant to retain it.

**B.2.11** An insurer must make the disclosure available at renewal when water backup coverage is not included. An insurer must make the disclosure available when a renewal offer changes the treatment of water backup coverage.

**B.2.12** An insurer must provide the disclosure when an endorsement adds, removes, or materially changes water backup coverage. An insurer must ensure that the policyholder receives the disclosure with or before the endorsement.

**B.2.13** An insurer may provide the disclosure in paper or electronic form when permitted by applicable law. An insurer must use a delivery method reasonably calculated to provide notice to the applicant or policyholder.

**B.2.14** An insurer must ensure that an electronic disclosure is accessible in a format that can be viewed and retained. An insurer must not require an applicant or policyholder to navigate unrelated material to locate the disclosure.

**B.2.15** An insurer must make the disclosure conspicuous in the application, quotation, policy materials, or related communications. An insurer must not present the disclosure in a manner likely to be overlooked.

**B.2.16** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure is consistent with the policy language. If a conflict exists, an insurer must correct the disclosure or policy materials promptly.

**B.2.17** An insurer must ensure that producer communications regarding water backup coverage are accurate. An insurer must provide producers with instructions sufficient to support compliant disclosures.

**B.2.18** An insurer must not represent that water backup coverage is included when the policy excludes or limits the coverage. An insurer must not describe an optional coverage as automatic coverage.

**B.2.19** An insurer must not characterize water backup coverage as flood coverage. An insurer must distinguish water backup coverage from coverage for other sources of water damage.

**B.2.20** An insurer must maintain records demonstrating that required disclosures were provided. The records must identify the policy transaction and the disclosure version used.

**B.2.21** An insurer must maintain records of an applicant's or policyholder's selection or rejection of offered water backup coverage. The record must be sufficient to show the action taken.

**B.2.22** An insurer must retain disclosure records in a form that can be produced upon request by the Department. An insurer must ensure that retained records are complete and legible.

**B.2.23** An insurer must establish procedures to review water backup disclosures for accuracy. The procedures must address changes to policy language, underwriting practices, and claims handling practices.

**B.2.24** An insurer must correct a disclosure that is inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading. An insurer must take appropriate steps to address affected applicants or policyholders.

**B.2.25** An insurer must ensure that advertising and marketing materials do not contradict required disclosures. An insurer must review material statements concerning water damage coverage for consistency.

**B.2.26** An insurer must ensure that quotation systems accurately identify whether water backup coverage is included, excluded, or offered as an option. An insurer must correct system output that does not accurately reflect the applicable coverage.

**B.2.27** An insurer must ensure that policy declarations accurately reflect the water backup coverage selected. An insurer must provide a corrected declaration when a declaration does not accurately reflect the policy.

**B.2.28** An insurer must apply underwriting rules consistently with the disclosure provided to applicants and policyholders. An insurer must document carrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exceptioncarrier implementation in the underwriting manual state exception (see Personal Lines Underwriting Manual, R570 Rule 570 — Illinois State Exceptions) when an underwriting practice affects the availability of water backup coverage.

**B.2.29** An insurer must ensure that claims personnel can identify the applicable water backup coverage and relevant policy provisions. An insurer must not deny or limit a claim based on a disclosure that conflicts with the policy.

**B.2.30** An insurer must provide reasonable training to personnel responsible for selling, underwriting, servicing, or handling claims involving water backup coverage. The training must address the disclosure obligations in this section.

**B.2.31** An insurer must make procedures available to address policyholder questions about water backup coverage. An insurer must ensure that responses are consistent with the applicable policy and disclosure.

**B.2.32** An insurer must not condition the availability of required disclosure on a request by an applicant or policyholder. An insurer must provide the disclosure as part of the applicable transaction.

**B.2.33** An insurer must ensure that translated or alternate-format materials do not omit or materially alter the required disclosure. An insurer must review such materials for consistency with the English-language disclosure.

**B.2.34** An insurer must ensure that a managing general agent, producer, administrator, or other representative acting on its behalf follows this section. An insurer remains responsible for compliance with this section.

**B.2.35** An insurer must monitor vendor systems used to generate applications, quotations, declarations, endorsements, or notices for compliance with this section. An insurer must address identified deficiencies without undue delay.

**B.2.36** An insurer must establish controls to prevent use of outdated disclosure language. An insurer must remove superseded disclosure materials from active use.

**B.2.37** An insurer must review complaints concerning water backup coverage for indications that disclosures are unclear or misleading. An insurer must use complaint information to identify needed corrective action.

**B.2.38** An insurer must cooperate with Department inquiries concerning compliance with this section. An insurer must provide requested disclosure materials and related records in a timely manner.

**B.2.39** An insurer may use policy-specific language when necessary to accurately describe its coverage. An insurer must not use policy-specific language to reduce the clarity required by this section.

**B.2.40** An insurer must apply this section to new business and to policy transactions affected by a change in water backup coverage. An insurer must not delay required disclosure pending a later policy transaction.

**B.2.41** An insurer must comply with this section when these requirements are effective. An insurer must implement procedures necessary to support ongoing compliance.

**B.2.42** An insurer must administer water backup coverage disclosures in good faith and in a manner that promotes informed insurance decisions. An insurer must treat compliance with this section as part of its regular market conduct obligations.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** An insurer that offers water backup coverage must provide a clear and conspicuous disclosure describing the coverage. The disclosure must be provided in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the applicant or insured before coverage is accepted.

**B.3.2** An insurer must state whether water backup coverage is included within the coverage being offered. An insurer must not imply that water backup coverage is included when it is available only by separate election.

**B.3.3** An insurer must describe water backup coverage in plain language. The description must reasonably distinguish water backup coverage from coverage for other sources of water damage.

**B.3.4** An insurer must identify material limitations that apply to water backup coverage. The disclosure must not obscure a limitation through placement, format, or wording.

**B.3.5** An insurer must state whether water backup coverage is subject to a separate deductible. An insurer must describe the manner in which the deductible applies when the disclosure identifies a separate deductible.

**B.3.6** An insurer must state whether water backup coverage is subject to a separate limit. The disclosure must reasonably explain that payment may be limited even when other property coverage is available.

**B.3.7** An insurer must describe the types of water backup events for which coverage may be available. An insurer must not use terminology that a reasonable applicant or insured would likely misunderstand.

**B.3.8** An insurer must identify material exclusions applicable to water backup coverage. The disclosure must distinguish an exclusion from a condition or limitation when that distinction is material.

**B.3.9** An insurer must disclose when an applicant or insured may elect water backup coverage. The disclosure must state the manner in which an election may be made.

**B.3.10** An insurer must disclose when an applicant or insured may decline water backup coverage. An insurer must not treat the absence of an election as an affirmative selection unless the applicable coverage materials clearly provide for that treatment.

**B.3.11** An insurer must make the disclosure available in connection with the offer of coverage. An insurer may provide the disclosure through electronic means when the method is reasonably accessible to the applicant or insured.

**B.3.12** An insurer that provides the disclosure electronically must present it in a format capable of being retained. The electronic presentation must not require the applicant or insured to search unrelated material to locate the disclosure.

**B.3.13** An insurer must provide the disclosure in a form that is legible and understandable. An insurer may use headings, emphasis, or spacing that improves the prominence of material information.

**B.3.14** An insurer must use terminology consistently throughout the disclosure. If an insurer uses a defined term, the disclosure must provide a reasonably understandable explanation of that term.

**B.3.15** An insurer must not describe water backup coverage as comprehensive or unrestricted unless that description is accurate in light of the applicable terms. General statements of coverage must be qualified when material limitations apply.

**B.3.16** An insurer must not characterize water backup coverage as flood coverage. An insurer must make clear that water backup coverage may differ from coverage for flooding or surface water.

**B.3.17** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure is consistent with the coverage offered. An insurer must correct a disclosure that is inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading.

**B.3.18** An insurer must provide a revised disclosure when a material change affects the availability, scope, limitation, or exclusion of water backup coverage. The revised disclosure must be provided in connection with the changed offer of coverage.

**B.3.19** An insurer must provide the disclosure to an applicant or insured in a manner that permits a reasonable opportunity for review. An insurer must not rely on a disclosure delivered after the coverage decision when earlier disclosure was reasonably practicable.

**B.3.20** An insurer may combine the disclosure with other coverage information if the water backup coverage disclosure remains clear and conspicuous. Combined materials must not diminish the prominence of material information concerning water backup coverage.

**B.3.21** An insurer must ensure that producer communications concerning water backup coverage are consistent with the disclosure. An insurer remains responsible for the accuracy of disclosure materials used on its behalf.

**B.3.22** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section. The records must identify the disclosure form or content provided to the applicant or insured.

**B.3.23** An insurer must make compliance records available to the Department upon request. Records must be maintained in a manner that permits the Department to evaluate the insurer’s disclosure practices.

**B.3.24** An insurer must apply this section to water backup coverage offered with property insurance issued or delivered in this State. An insurer must also apply this section when water backup coverage is added to existing property insurance.

**B.3.25** An insurer may provide additional information concerning loss prevention measures. Additional information must not be presented in a manner that suggests loss prevention measures alter coverage unless the applicable coverage terms so provide.

**B.3.26** An insurer must not condition the disclosure required by this section on a request from the applicant or insured. The disclosure obligation applies whether water backup coverage is accepted, declined, or unavailable.

**B.3.27** An insurer must comply with this section for coverage subject to this bulletin. An insurer must administer its disclosure practices in good faith and in a manner consistent with the purpose of informing applicants and insureds.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This Section applies to an insurer handling a claim involving alleged water backup loss under a policy issued or delivered in this State. An insurer must apply these standards to claims received on or after this bulletin becomes effective.

**B.4.2** An insurer must acknowledge a claim communication promptly and must provide the claimant with information reasonably necessary to understand the claim process. An insurer must identify the person or unit responsible for handling the claim.

**B.4.3** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation before denying, limiting, or otherwise resolving a claim. The investigation must address the reported cause of loss, the claimed damage, and the relevant policy provisions.

**B.4.4** An insurer may request information reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim. An insurer must not request information that is not material to coverage, valuation, mitigation, or another claim issue.

**B.4.5** An insurer must evaluate whether water backup is covered under the applicable policy terms and endorsements. An insurer must not base a coverage determination solely on a general description of water damage without considering the reported facts.

**B.4.6** An insurer must provide a written explanation when it denies coverage, limits payment, or applies an exclusion, condition, deductible, or other policy provision. The explanation must identify the policy language on which the insurer relied and describe how that language applies to the known facts.

**B.4.7** An insurer must pay any undisputed covered portion of a claim without awaiting resolution of a disputed portion, unless payment is barred by applicable policy terms or law. Payment of an undisputed amount does not waive the insurer's right to investigate a remaining claim issue.

**B.4.8** An insurer must evaluate repair estimates, invoices, photographs, and other relevant evidence in good faith. An insurer must explain material differences between its valuation and the claimant's submitted valuation.

**B.4.9** An insurer may retain an adjuster, engineer, plumber, contractor, or other qualified professional when reasonably necessary to investigate or value a claim. An insurer remains responsible for the fairness and accuracy of its claim determination.

**B.4.10** An insurer must consider reasonable mitigation efforts undertaken to protect covered property from further damage. An insurer must not require a claimant to complete repairs before the insurer has had a reasonable opportunity to inspect the reported damage, unless immediate repair is reasonably necessary to prevent further loss.

**B.4.11** An insurer must not require a claimant to use a particular contractor, vendor, or repair service as a condition of claim payment. An insurer may provide information concerning available repair services if the communication clearly states that the claimant may select another qualified provider.

**B.4.12** An insurer must apply valuation provisions consistently with the applicable policy. If replacement cost, actual cash value, depreciation, or another valuation method affects payment, the insurer must explain the method used.

**B.4.13** An insurer must clearly identify any policy condition affecting payment of recoverable depreciation or replacement cost benefits. An insurer must not withhold payment based on an uncommunicated repair, replacement, or documentation requirement.

**B.4.14** An insurer must state the deductible applied to a claim and the basis for its application. An insurer must not apply a deductible that is inconsistent with the applicable policy terms.

**B.4.15** An insurer must not delay a coverage determination or payment solely because it may pursue recovery from another person or entity. An insurer may preserve its subrogation rights in a manner consistent with the claimant's right to timely claim handling.

**B.4.16** An insurer must communicate material claim information in a manner reasonably understandable to the claimant. An insurer must provide reasonable assistance when a claimant requests clarification of a coverage determination or payment calculation.

**B.4.17** An insurer must provide a reasonable process for reconsidering a claim determination when the claimant submits material information not previously considered. The reconsideration process must be administered consistently with B.4.3 through B.4.6.

**B.4.18** An insurer must maintain claim records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this Section. The records must include material communications, investigation information, coverage determinations, and payment information.

**B.4.19** An insurer must provide appropriate oversight of persons handling water backup claims on its behalf. An insurer is responsible for the acts and omissions of an authorized representative to the extent provided by applicable law.

**B.4.20** An insurer must not misrepresent applicable coverage, claim procedures, or the effect of a policy exclusion, condition, deductible, or endorsement. An insurer must administer claims under this Section in good faith and in accordance with the policy and applicable law.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** This section applies to an admitted insurer that delivers, issues for delivery, renews, or amends a policy providing water backup coverage in Illinois. An insurer must comply with the filing and disclosure requirements stated in this section.

**B.5.2** An insurer must file the disclosure proposed for use with water backup coverage with the Department before using the disclosure. The filing must identify the policy forms and endorsements to which the disclosure applies.

**B.5.3** An insurer must submit the disclosure in a form that permits the Department to evaluate its content, readability, and consistency with the applicable policy language. The disclosure must not omit information necessary for a policyholder to understand the availability and scope of water backup coverage.

**B.5.4** An insurer must file the disclosure together with any revised policy form, endorsement, application, notice, or other material that affects the disclosure. The filing must explain the relationship between the disclosure and the related insurance contract language.

**B.5.5** An insurer must identify material differences between a previously filed disclosure and a proposed disclosure. The explanation must describe the reason for the change and the policyholder communication affected by the change.

**B.5.6** An insurer must not use a disclosure that differs materially from the disclosure filed with the Department unless the insurer files the revised disclosure before use. A filing must be complete when submitted and must include all materials needed for Department review.

**B.5.7** Filing a disclosure does not authorize an insurer to use the disclosure before any required approval or other Department action. An insurer may use the disclosure only when use is permitted under applicable filing requirements.

**B.5.8** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure used with a policy accurately reflects the water backup coverage available under that policy. The disclosure must be consistent with applicable exclusions, limitations, conditions, and endorsements.

**B.5.9** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure is provided in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the applicant or policyholder. An insurer must not present the disclosure in a manner that obscures or contradicts its material terms.

**B.5.10** An insurer must maintain records demonstrating the version of the disclosure used and the policy forms associated with that version. The records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.5.11** An insurer must maintain procedures to ensure that filed disclosures are used by its producers, employees, and other authorized representatives. Those procedures must address changes to policy forms or water backup coverage that require a revised disclosure.

**B.5.12** An insurer must discontinue use of a disclosure when the disclosure no longer accurately describes the related policy coverage. The insurer must file a replacement disclosure before using revised disclosure language.

**B.5.13** An insurer may provide additional explanatory material concerning water backup coverage if that material is accurate and is not misleading. Additional material must not replace the required disclosure.

**B.5.14** An insurer must ensure that any disclosure provided electronically is accessible to the applicant or policyholder and capable of being retained. Electronic delivery must not alter the substance of the filed disclosure.

**B.5.15** These requirements apply upon the effective date of this bulletin. An insurer must bring disclosures used after that effective date into compliance with this section.

**B.5.16** The Department may review an insurer’s filing practices and disclosures for compliance with this section. An insurer must respond to Department requests concerning a filing or disclosure in a timely and complete manner.
