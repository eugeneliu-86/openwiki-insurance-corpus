# Illinois Bulletin IDOI-2015-04 — Producer Licensing Renewals




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

**B.1.1** This section states the purpose and applicability of this bulletin. It applies to an admitted insurer conducting producer licensing renewal activity in Illinois.

**B.1.2** An insurer must administer producer licensing renewals in a manner consistent with applicable Illinois insurance requirements. An insurer may use operational procedures that support accurate and timely renewal administration.

**B.1.3** This bulletin concerns insurer practices affecting the renewal status of an insurance producer. It does not alter the authority of the Department to oversee insurer compliance.

**B.1.4** An insurer must ensure that its producer licensing renewal practices are applied consistently. An insurer may assign administrative functions to personnel or service providers, but remains responsible for those functions.

**B.1.5** An insurer must maintain practices that allow it to identify producers authorized to act on its behalf. An insurer must not treat an expired, inactive, or otherwise unauthorized producer as eligible to conduct insurer business.

**B.1.6** This section applies to producer renewal activity performed directly by an insurer. It also applies when an insurer relies upon an affiliate, administrator, vendor, or other representative.

**B.1.7** An insurer may establish internal controls for monitoring producer licensing renewal information. Those controls must be reasonably designed to detect and address inaccurate or incomplete information.

**B.1.8** An insurer must communicate licensing renewal expectations to producers acting on its behalf. Communications should be clear enough to support compliance with applicable requirements.

**B.1.9** An insurer may discontinue or limit a producer appointment or business relationship when licensing renewal requirements are not satisfied. Any such action must be administered in accordance with applicable law and the insurer's governing procedures.

**B.1.10** An insurer must retain records sufficient to demonstrate its administration of producer licensing renewals. Records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.1.11** This section does not establish an AOP deductible floor. An insurer must continue to administer deductibles in accordance with applicable requirements.

**B.1.12** This section does not establish a replacement cost threshold percentage. An insurer must not treat producer licensing renewal requirements as a basis for changing coverage terms.

**B.1.13** This section does not establish a Coverage D limit as a percentage of Coverage A. An insurer must separately administer producer licensing and policy coverage requirements.

**B.1.14** An insurer may use electronic systems to support producer licensing renewal administration. The insurer must ensure that such systems produce reliable information and preserve appropriate records.

**B.1.15** An insurer must address identified errors affecting producer licensing renewal activity promptly. Corrective action should include reasonable review of related insurer records and transactions.

**B.1.16** An insurer may provide training or guidance concerning producer licensing renewal practices. Training or guidance must not misstate applicable Illinois requirements.

**B.1.17** An insurer must use reasonable care when relying on information supplied by a producer or another party. Reliance does not relieve the insurer of responsibility for its own licensing renewal administration.

**B.1.18** This section applies regardless of the line of insurance for which a producer acts on behalf of an insurer. An insurer must apply relevant licensing renewal controls to each affected producer relationship.

**B.1.19** An insurer may adopt procedures more protective than those described in this section. Such procedures must not conflict with applicable Illinois requirements.

**B.1.20** An insurer must make its producer licensing renewal practices available for internal review. Internal review should be sufficient to identify material compliance concerns.

**B.1.21** The Department may review an insurer's compliance with this section in the course of its supervisory activities. An insurer must cooperate with a Department request concerning producer licensing renewal practices.

**B.1.22** This section is intended to promote orderly administration of producer licensing renewals by admitted insurers. An insurer remains responsible for complying with all applicable Illinois requirements.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer must apply these requirements to producer licensing renewals handled by the insurer for business written in this State. An insurer must maintain procedures that support lawful renewal activity.

**B.2.2** An insurer must verify that a producer holds authority appropriate to the insurance business the producer will transact. An insurer may not treat an expired, suspended, surrendered, or otherwise inactive authority as valid.

**B.2.3** An insurer must use current licensing information when determining whether a producer may continue to act on the insurer's behalf. An insurer must address information indicating that licensing authority has changed.

**B.2.4** An insurer must not appoint, compensate, or permit a producer to transact insurance beyond the producer's authorized lines of authority. An insurer must apply this restriction to renewal business as well as new business.

**B.2.5** An insurer must maintain controls for identifying producers whose authority is subject to a renewal requirement. An insurer must use those controls before accepting producer activity that depends on active authority.

**B.2.6** An insurer may rely on information from a licensing record, provided the insurer reasonably determines that the information is current and complete. An insurer must resolve material discrepancies before relying on the record.

**B.2.7** An insurer must establish responsibility within its compliance operations for producer licensing renewal oversight. An insurer must ensure that responsible personnel have access to relevant licensing information.

**B.2.8** An insurer must communicate applicable renewal expectations to producers acting for the insurer. An insurer must communicate those expectations in a manner reasonably designed to avoid misunderstanding.

**B.2.9** An insurer must not represent that a producer is authorized when the insurer knows, or reasonably should know, that the producer lacks authority. An insurer must promptly correct inaccurate authorization representations.

**B.2.10** An insurer must maintain records supporting its determination that a producer was authorized to act. An insurer must make those records available to the Department upon request.

**B.2.11** An insurer must apply consistent licensing renewal controls to producers performing substantially similar functions. An insurer may apply enhanced controls where the insurer identifies elevated compliance risk.

**B.2.12** An insurer must ensure that producer onboarding procedures do not override licensing renewal controls. An insurer must reconcile onboarding information with current licensing information.

**B.2.13** An insurer must ensure that producer termination procedures address the producer's authority to act for the insurer. An insurer must update internal systems to reflect termination information.

**B.2.14** An insurer must prevent continued producer access to insurer systems when such access would facilitate unauthorized insurance activity. An insurer may retain access needed solely for lawful recordkeeping or compliance review.

**B.2.15** An insurer must train relevant personnel regarding the insurer's producer licensing renewal procedures. An insurer must ensure that training addresses escalation of licensing concerns.

**B.2.16** An insurer must provide a method for personnel to report suspected producer licensing deficiencies. An insurer must review reports received through that method.

**B.2.17** An insurer must investigate information suggesting that a producer has acted without required authority. An insurer must take appropriate corrective action based on the investigation.

**B.2.18** An insurer must document material actions taken in response to a producer licensing concern. An insurer must retain documentation sufficient to explain its compliance decision.

**B.2.19** An insurer must ensure that compensation practices do not encourage unlicensed or unauthorized producer activity. An insurer must review compensation controls when licensing concerns arise.

**B.2.20** An insurer may withhold or adjust compensation when necessary to prevent payment for unauthorized activity. An insurer must administer any such action consistently with applicable contractual obligations.

**B.2.21** An insurer must ensure that its producer agreements accurately describe licensing-related responsibilities. An insurer must not use agreement language that suggests authorization beyond what is permitted.

**B.2.22** An insurer must identify the insurer personnel authorized to approve producer appointments and related changes. An insurer must limit approval authority to personnel with appropriate compliance responsibility.

**B.2.23** An insurer must ensure that appointment records and licensing records are reconciled when material changes occur. An insurer must investigate unresolved differences between those records.

**B.2.24** An insurer must not rely solely on producer representations when available information indicates a licensing issue. An insurer must seek sufficient confirmation before permitting further regulated activity.

**B.2.25** An insurer must ensure that affiliated entities do not perform producer functions for the insurer without appropriate authority. An insurer must apply oversight to delegated arrangements that involve producer activity.

**B.2.26** An insurer must address producer licensing renewal requirements in its oversight of third-party administrators and similar service providers. An insurer must require prompt escalation of identified licensing concerns.

**B.2.27** An insurer must maintain safeguards for information used to evaluate producer licensing status. An insurer must limit access to that information to persons with a legitimate business need.

**B.2.28** An insurer must correct internal records that inaccurately identify a producer's licensing status. An insurer must communicate corrections to affected business functions when necessary.

**B.2.29** An insurer must ensure that producer directories, consumer communications, and internal references do not misstate producer authority. An insurer must remove or correct inaccurate information promptly.

**B.2.30** An insurer must review automated controls that affect producer authorization decisions. An insurer must ensure that automated controls produce results consistent with current licensing information.

**B.2.31** An insurer must establish an escalation process for licensing matters that cannot be resolved through routine review. An insurer must ensure that escalated matters receive appropriate compliance consideration.

**B.2.32** An insurer must preserve evidence relevant to a licensing review, inquiry, or examination. An insurer must not alter records in a manner that obscures the basis for an authorization decision.

**B.2.33** An insurer must cooperate with Department requests concerning producer licensing renewal practices. An insurer must provide responsive information that is accurate and complete.

**B.2.34** An insurer must identify and correct systemic deficiencies found through internal review or Department inquiry. An insurer must document corrective measures taken to address material deficiencies.

**B.2.35** An insurer must monitor compliance with its producer licensing renewal procedures. An insurer may use auditing, quality assurance, or other reasonable compliance methods for that purpose.

**B.2.36** An insurer must ensure that compliance findings are communicated to appropriate management personnel. An insurer must provide management with information necessary to oversee remediation.

**B.2.37** An insurer must not condition producer licensing renewal compliance on production volume, premium volume, or other sales performance. An insurer must treat licensing compliance as independent from sales objectives.

**B.2.38** An insurer must ensure that marketing materials do not imply that an unapproved or unauthorized person may act as a producer. An insurer must review material concerns before distribution.

**B.2.39** An insurer must ensure that consumer communications accurately identify the role of persons involved in the insurance transaction. An insurer must not use titles or descriptions that conceal a licensing deficiency.

**B.2.40** An insurer must distinguish producer licensing renewal matters from policyholder communications concerning a notice of nonrenewal. An insurer must not use producer licensing procedures to alter policyholder rights.

**B.2.41** An insurer must distinguish producer licensing renewal matters from any cancellation notice period applicable to a policy. An insurer must administer policy communications under applicable policy requirements.

**B.2.42** An insurer must not treat a producer licensing renewal matter as affecting a policyholder's proof of loss period. An insurer must maintain appropriate separation between producer compliance records and claim handling obligations.

**B.2.43** An insurer must not represent that producer licensing renewal requirements modify a special assessment limit. An insurer must ensure that compliance communications remain limited to their proper subject.

**B.2.44** An insurer must ensure that producer licensing controls apply regardless of the method used to solicit, negotiate, or effect insurance. An insurer must address electronic and non-electronic activity through appropriate controls.

**B.2.45** An insurer must ensure that producers acting through remote means remain subject to the insurer's licensing verification procedures. An insurer must not treat remote activity as exempt from authorization requirements.

**B.2.46** An insurer must maintain procedures for responding to a producer's reported change in legal name, business name, or contact information. An insurer must determine whether the reported change affects licensing or appointment records.

**B.2.47** An insurer must review changes in a producer's business organization that may affect authority to act for the insurer. An insurer must update its records based on verified information.

**B.2.48** An insurer must ensure that a producer's use of assistants, employees, or contractors does not result in unauthorized producer activity. An insurer must address known concerns through its oversight procedures.

**B.2.49** An insurer must maintain a process for handling producer questions concerning licensing renewal status. An insurer must provide responses that do not misstate applicable requirements.

**B.2.50** An insurer may direct a producer to appropriate licensing resources when the producer seeks information outside the insurer's authority to determine. An insurer must not provide guidance that conflicts with known licensing information.

**B.2.51** An insurer must ensure that its complaint-handling procedures identify complaints alleging unauthorized producer conduct. An insurer must route such complaints for appropriate licensing review.

**B.2.52** An insurer must evaluate whether substantiated complaints indicate a need for broader review of producer licensing controls. An insurer must take corrective action when the review identifies deficiencies.

**B.2.53** An insurer must ensure that acquisitions, mergers, and similar business changes include review of producer licensing information. An insurer must address licensing discrepancies arising from such changes.

**B.2.54** An insurer must ensure that transfers of business do not result in unauthorized producer activity. An insurer must verify applicable producer authority before permitting continued activity related to transferred business.

**B.2.55** An insurer must maintain governance practices that assign accountability for producer licensing renewal compliance. An insurer must ensure that accountability is meaningful and documented.

**B.2.56** An insurer must periodically evaluate whether its producer licensing renewal procedures remain effective. An insurer must revise procedures when operational changes create material compliance risk.

**B.2.57** An insurer must ensure that records supporting producer licensing decisions are legible, organized, and retrievable. An insurer must preserve records in a manner that supports Department examination.

**B.2.58** An insurer must not retaliate against personnel who raise good-faith concerns regarding producer licensing renewal compliance. An insurer must investigate credible reports of retaliation.

**B.2.59** An insurer must ensure that corrective action for licensing deficiencies is proportionate to the identified concern and sufficient to prevent recurrence. An insurer must monitor implementation of material corrective action.

**B.2.60** An insurer must comply with these requirements upon their applicability to the insurer's producer licensing renewal operations. An insurer must continue compliance unless and until the Department changes the applicable requirements.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** This Section applies to an insurer authorized to transact insurance in this State that communicates with a producer concerning producer licensing renewal. An insurer must comply with this Section beginning on the effective date of this Bulletin.

**B.3.2** An insurer must provide notice in a manner reasonably calculated to reach the producer. The notice must be clear, conspicuous, and written in plain language.

**B.3.3** An insurer must identify the producer to whom a renewal communication relates. An insurer must use identifying information sufficient to permit the producer to recognize the license matter addressed by the communication.

**B.3.4** An insurer must state that producer licensing renewal remains the responsibility of the producer. An insurer must not state or imply that the insurer will complete a renewal obligation unless the insurer has expressly agreed to perform the stated activity.

**B.3.5** An insurer must distinguish a producer licensing renewal notice from a notice concerning appointment, contracting, compensation, or policy servicing. The notice must not obscure the licensing matter through unrelated information.

**B.3.6** An insurer must disclose whether the communication is a reminder, a request for information, or a notice of an action affecting the producer's authority to act for the insurer. An insurer must describe the purpose of the communication accurately.

**B.3.7** An insurer must identify any information the producer is expected to provide to the insurer. An insurer must describe how the producer may provide that information.

**B.3.8** An insurer must state any consequence that may result from a producer's failure to respond to a request made by the insurer. An insurer must not describe a consequence more broadly than the insurer may lawfully impose.

**B.3.9** An insurer must disclose when an insurer action depends on the producer maintaining an active license. An insurer must make that disclosure without suggesting that the insurer controls the licensing determination.

**B.3.10** An insurer must not represent that a producer is licensed, renewed, appointed, or authorized unless the representation is supported by the insurer's records or reliable licensing information. An insurer must correct a material inaccuracy when identified.

**B.3.11** An insurer must provide contact information through which a producer may seek assistance regarding the notice. The contact information must direct the producer to personnel or resources able to address the stated subject.

**B.3.12** An insurer must provide a reasonable method for a producer to report an apparent error in a renewal communication. An insurer must review a reported error promptly and update its records when warranted.

**B.3.13** An insurer must protect nonpublic personal information included in a producer licensing renewal communication. An insurer must limit the information displayed to that reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.

**B.3.14** An insurer must not request information that is unrelated to the licensing renewal matter unless the communication clearly identifies the separate purpose for the request. An insurer must separate unrelated requests in a manner that does not mislead the producer.

**B.3.15** An insurer must ensure that an electronic notice is capable of being retained by the producer. An insurer must provide a practicable alternative communication method when an electronic delivery failure is known to the insurer.

**B.3.16** An insurer must not condition access to licensing renewal information on acceptance of marketing, solicitation, or unrelated contractual terms. An insurer may provide related business information if it is presented separately from the required notice.

**B.3.17** An insurer must use terminology that accurately describes the insurer's role in the producer licensing renewal process. An insurer must not use terminology that suggests the insurer issues, renews, suspends, or revokes a producer license.

**B.3.18** An insurer must disclose any insurer requirement for documentation related to continued appointment or affiliation. The disclosure must state that the insurer requirement is separate from any licensing requirement administered by the State.

**B.3.19** An insurer must provide notice before taking an action based on information indicating that a producer may lack required licensing authority, when notice is practicable. The notice must describe the information on which the contemplated action is based.

**B.3.20** An insurer must give a producer a reasonable opportunity to provide corrected licensing information before restricting the producer's activities, unless immediate action is necessary to prevent an unauthorized insurance transaction. An insurer must document the basis for any immediate action.

**B.3.21** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this Section. The records must include the substance of the notice and the manner in which the insurer provided it.

**B.3.22** An insurer must maintain procedures designed to promote consistent renewal communications across its producer population. The procedures must address review of notice content for accuracy and clarity.

**B.3.23** An insurer must train personnel who prepare or issue producer licensing renewal communications regarding the requirements of this Section. An insurer must ensure that personnel understand the distinction between licensing status and insurer appointment status.

**B.3.24** An insurer must review templates used for producer licensing renewal communications before use. An insurer must revise a template when the insurer identifies language that may mislead a producer.

**B.3.25** An insurer must ensure that a notice issued by a vendor or other service provider on the insurer's behalf complies with this Section. The insurer remains responsible for the content and delivery of the notice.

**B.3.26** An insurer must not retaliate against a producer for seeking clarification, reporting an error, or providing corrected licensing information. An insurer must evaluate such communications in good faith.

**B.3.27** An insurer may provide supplemental educational material concerning producer licensing renewal. Supplemental material must not contradict, replace, or diminish the notice required by this Section.

**B.3.28** An insurer must make its producer licensing renewal communications available for review upon request by the Department. An insurer must cooperate with an inquiry concerning compliance with this Section.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** These provisions apply upon the effective date of this bulletin to an insurer handling claims under policies issued or delivered in this State. An insurer must administer claims fairly, promptly, and in good faith.

**B.4.2** An insurer must maintain claim practices that permit timely identification, investigation, evaluation, and resolution of each claim. An insurer may not use claim procedures that unreasonably delay a claimant’s access to benefits that are due.

**B.4.3** An insurer must acknowledge communications concerning a claim within a reasonable time. An insurer must provide a meaningful response that addresses the communication received.

**B.4.4** An insurer must establish reasonable procedures for receiving and recording claim notices. Those procedures must permit the insurer to identify the policy, claimant, loss, and requested benefits.

**B.4.5** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation before denying a claim or limiting payment. The investigation must be appropriate to the facts known or reasonably available to the insurer.

**B.4.6** An insurer may request information reasonably necessary to evaluate a claim. An insurer may not request information that is irrelevant, duplicative, or intended to discourage pursuit of a claim.

**B.4.7** An insurer must explain the information needed from a claimant in plain language. An insurer must advise the claimant of any material deficiency in the information provided.

**B.4.8** An insurer must evaluate a claim based upon the policy provisions, the facts of the loss, and applicable law. An insurer may not misrepresent pertinent facts or policy provisions relating to coverage.

**B.4.9** An insurer must provide a written explanation when it denies a claim in whole or in part. The explanation must identify the factual and policy basis for the insurer’s decision.

**B.4.10** An insurer must identify any additional information that could affect a denial decision when such information is reasonably known to the insurer. An insurer must reconsider the claim when material information is later received.

**B.4.11** An insurer may not compel a claimant to institute litigation by offering substantially less than the amount reasonably supported by the available information. An insurer must make claim payment when coverage and the amount due are reasonably established.

**B.4.12** An insurer must not delay payment by failing to communicate material claim decisions. An insurer must promptly notify a claimant when payment is authorized or when further investigation is required.

**B.4.13** An insurer must issue payment in a manner that reasonably identifies the claim and the benefits paid. An insurer must provide an explanation for any payment that does not resolve all requested benefits.

**B.4.14** An insurer may settle a claim only through practices that are fair to the claimant and consistent with the policy. An insurer may not use misleading statements or coercive conduct to obtain a settlement.

**B.4.15** An insurer must preserve claim records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this paragraph. The records must reflect material communications, investigative activity, evaluations, decisions, and payments.

**B.4.16** An insurer must supervise employees, producers, adjusters, and other persons acting on its behalf in claim handling. An insurer remains responsible for claim practices performed by those persons.

**B.4.17** An insurer must provide claim personnel with instruction sufficient to carry out the requirements of this paragraph. An insurer must maintain procedures for correcting claim practices that do not comply with this paragraph.

**B.4.18** An insurer must apply claim standards consistently to similarly situated claimants. An insurer may consider material differences in policy language, facts, coverage, or applicable law.

**B.4.19** An insurer must cooperate with the Department’s review of claim practices under this paragraph. An insurer must make responsive claim records available upon request by the Department.

**B.4.20** A failure to comply with this paragraph may be considered in determining whether an insurer has engaged in an unfair claim practice. Nothing in this paragraph limits any authority of the Department to enforce applicable insurance law.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** This section applies to an insurer that files material concerning producer licensing renewals with the Department. An insurer must ensure that a filing is complete and suitable for Department review.

**B.5.2** An insurer must submit a filing before using the filed material for producer licensing renewals. An insurer may not implement material that is inconsistent with an approved filing.

**B.5.3** A filing must clearly identify the insurer submitting it and the material proposed for use. An insurer must provide sufficient information for the Department to determine whether the filing complies with applicable law.

**B.5.4** An insurer must identify any change to previously filed material. The filing must explain the purpose and effect of the change.

**B.5.5** An insurer must provide supporting documentation upon request by the Department. Supporting documentation must be accurate, current, and responsive to the filing.

**B.5.6** The Department may request clarification, correction, or additional information concerning a filing. An insurer must respond to a Department request before relying on the filing as effective.

**B.5.7** A filing becomes effective only as provided by applicable law or upon Department approval. An insurer may not treat Department receipt of a filing as approval.

**B.5.8** An insurer must use filed material only after the material becomes effective under this section. An insurer must discontinue use of material that the Department disapproves or requires to be withdrawn.

**B.5.9** An insurer must maintain records demonstrating the material used for producer licensing renewals. The records must permit the Department to verify that the insurer used effective material.

**B.5.10** An insurer must promptly notify the Department if information in an effective filing is no longer accurate. The insurer must file corrected material before continuing to rely on inaccurate information.

**B.5.11** Department approval of a filing does not excuse an insurer from compliance with applicable law. An insurer remains responsible for the accuracy and lawful use of its producer licensing renewal material.

**B.5.12** An insurer must comply with this section on and after the effective date of this bulletin. The Department may take appropriate action when an insurer fails to comply with this section.
