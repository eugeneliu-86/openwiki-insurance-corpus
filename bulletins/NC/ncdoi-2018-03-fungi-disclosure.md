# North Carolina Bulletin NCDOI-2018-03 — Fungi Coverage Limit Disclosure




## B.1 — Purpose and Applicability

This section states the Department’s expectations for clear disclosure of a fungi coverage limit. Its purpose is to promote understandable communication of coverage restrictions that may affect a policyholder’s recovery for fungi-related loss.

This section applies to an admitted insurer writing property insurance in this State. It applies when the insurer offers coverage containing a fungi coverage limit or another restriction applicable to fungi-related loss.

An insurer must present the disclosure in a manner reasonably calculated to draw the applicant’s or policyholder’s attention to the applicable restriction. The disclosure must not be obscured by unrelated language or presented in a misleading manner.

An insurer may use policy language, application language, notices, declarations, or other materials to provide the required disclosure. The manner of disclosure must accurately describe the coverage provided.

An insurer must ensure that a description of fungi-related loss is consistent with the coverage afforded under the insurance contract. A disclosure must not suggest broader coverage than the insurer provides.

An insurer must identify the existence of a fungi coverage limit when that limit may apply to a covered loss. The disclosure must reasonably distinguish the fungi coverage limit from other limitations that may affect payment.

An insurer must describe material conditions affecting the availability of coverage for fungi-related loss. An insurer may not characterize a limitation as a routine exclusion if the limitation operates as a separate coverage restriction.

This section applies to disclosures provided to applicants before or at the time coverage is made available. It also applies to disclosures provided to policyholders when coverage is issued, renewed, amended, or replaced.

An insurer must provide disclosure that is consistent across materials used for the same coverage. Differences in wording are permitted only when they do not create uncertainty concerning the applicable fungi coverage limit.

An insurer must take reasonable steps to ensure that personnel communicating with applicants or policyholders do not describe fungi coverage in a manner inconsistent with the disclosure. The insurer remains responsible for the accuracy of communications made on its behalf.

A disclosure may explain the relationship between fungi coverage and water-related loss when that explanation is accurate. The explanation must not imply that coverage exists for loss excluded or limited by the insurance contract.

An insurer must not use the disclosure to alter coverage by implication. Coverage obligations remain governed by the terms of the applicable insurance contract.

An insurer must ensure that the disclosure does not misstate the all-other-perils deductible minimum. The insurer may provide additional explanatory language when that language remains accurate and clear.

An insurer must ensure that any discussion of the coinsurance threshold is consistent with the insurance contract. The discussion must not imply that fungi-related loss is subject to treatment not provided by the contract.

An insurer must ensure that any statement concerning the ALE percentage is accurate. The statement must not obscure any limitation applicable to fungi-related loss.

This section does not require an insurer to provide coverage for fungi-related loss. It requires clear disclosure when the insurer offers coverage subject to a fungi coverage limit or related restriction.

This section does not prohibit an insurer from using different disclosures for different coverages. Each disclosure must accurately reflect the coverage to which it applies.

An insurer must maintain sufficient information to demonstrate compliance with this section upon request by the Department. Such information may include materials used to communicate the fungi coverage limit.

This section takes effect upon issuance by the Department. It applies to coverage issued, renewed, amended, or replaced on or after that effective date.

This section does not require retroactive modification of coverage already in force before its effective date. An insurer may provide updated disclosure for existing coverage when appropriate.

An insurer must comply with this section in addition to any applicable requirement of North Carolina law. Nothing in this section limits the Department’s authority to review insurer communications for compliance with applicable law.

The Department will evaluate compliance based upon the clarity, accuracy, and prominence of the insurer’s disclosure. An insurer should resolve ambiguity in a manner that does not mislead an applicant or policyholder.

## B.2 — Requirements

**B.2.1** An insurer must provide a clear disclosure when a policy contains a limitation applicable to fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. The disclosure must be provided in a manner reasonably calculated to inform the insured of the limitation.

**B.2.2** This requirement applies to a policy issued or delivered for risks located in this State when the policy includes the applicable limitation. An insurer must apply the requirement on the effective date applicable to the policy.

**B.2.3** An insurer must use language that an ordinary insured can reasonably understand. The disclosure must not obscure the limitation through technical wording or unclear presentation.

**B.2.4** An insurer must identify that the limitation applies to fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. An insurer may use defined policy terms if the disclosure makes their meaning reasonably clear.

**B.2.5** An insurer must present the disclosure separately from unrelated coverage descriptions when necessary to avoid confusion. The presentation must reasonably direct the insured's attention to the limitation.

**B.2.6** An insurer must not describe the applicable coverage in a manner that is misleading when read with the disclosure. The disclosure must be consistent with the policy language.

**B.2.7** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure accurately reflects the coverage afforded by the policy. An insurer must revise the disclosure when a policy revision changes the applicable limitation.

**B.2.8** An insurer may include the disclosure in the policy, an endorsement, or accompanying materials. The insurer must ensure that the insured receives the disclosure with the applicable coverage information.

**B.2.9** An insurer must make the disclosure conspicuous. An insurer must not place the disclosure where its significance is likely to be overlooked.

**B.2.10** An insurer must state in the disclosure: The most we will pay is $5,000 (five thousand dollars). The insurer must present that statement without qualification that conflicts with the policy.

**B.2.11** An insurer must not state or imply that coverage is broader than the coverage actually provided. Any explanatory language must remain consistent with the limitation disclosed under **B.2.10**.

**B.2.12** An insurer must identify whether the limitation applies to covered loss involving fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. The disclosure must not suggest that the limitation applies only to a condition not addressed by the policy.

**B.2.13** An insurer must use the same general terminology in the disclosure that is used in the policy. An insurer may provide a plain-language explanation when it does not alter the policy meaning.

**B.2.14** An insurer must ensure that any explanation of the limitation is accurate. The explanation must not create coverage that is unavailable under the policy.

**B.2.15** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure does not reduce coverage beyond the policy terms. The policy controls if a conflict exists between the policy and explanatory material.

**B.2.16** An insurer must provide the disclosure in connection with new business subject to this requirement. An insurer must also provide the disclosure in connection with renewal coverage subject to this requirement.

**B.2.17** An insurer must provide the disclosure when an endorsement adds or changes the applicable limitation. The disclosure must reflect the coverage as changed by the endorsement.

**B.2.18** An insurer must provide the disclosure when replacement coverage contains the applicable limitation. The insurer must not rely solely on disclosure provided for prior coverage.

**B.2.19** An insurer may provide the disclosure through electronic delivery when electronic delivery is permitted. The electronic presentation must be reasonably accessible to the insured.

**B.2.20** An insurer must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this requirement. The records must show that the disclosure used was applicable to the policy.

**B.2.21** An insurer must retain the version of the disclosure provided to the insured. The retained version must correspond to the policy language in effect for the coverage.

**B.2.22** An insurer must ensure that its producers and other authorized representatives have access to the applicable disclosure. An insurer remains responsible for compliance when a disclosure is provided through a representative.

**B.2.23** An insurer must instruct personnel responsible for policy issuance regarding the disclosure requirement. The instruction must address the need to use the disclosure applicable to the policy.

**B.2.24** An insurer must use procedures reasonably designed to prevent omission of the disclosure. Those procedures must account for policy issuance, renewal, replacement, and endorsement activity.

**B.2.25** An insurer must review its forms and procedures when policy language affecting fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria coverage changes. The review must determine whether the disclosure remains accurate.

**B.2.26** An insurer must not use a disclosure that is inconsistent with an endorsement. An insurer must update the disclosure when an endorsement changes the applicable coverage.

**B.2.27** An insurer must not use language that characterizes the limitation as unlimited or unrestricted. An insurer must not otherwise minimize the effect of the limitation.

**B.2.28** An insurer may explain that coverage remains subject to policy terms and conditions. That explanation must not distract from or contradict the required disclosure.

**B.2.29** An insurer must ensure that a summary of coverage does not omit the applicable limitation when the summary addresses fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. A summary must be consistent with the disclosure.

**B.2.30** An insurer must ensure that marketing or sales materials do not conflict with the disclosure. An insurer must correct materials that could reasonably mislead an insured about the limitation.

**B.2.31** An insurer must make the disclosure available in a format that can be retained by the insured. The format must permit the insured to review the disclosure with the policy.

**B.2.32** An insurer must provide the disclosure in a legible form. An insurer must not use type, spacing, contrast, or placement that makes the disclosure difficult to read.

**B.2.33** An insurer may combine the disclosure with other required policy notices if the combination remains clear. The required disclosure must retain its conspicuous character.

**B.2.34** An insurer must not condition delivery of the disclosure on a request from the insured. The disclosure must be provided as part of the applicable policy transaction.

**B.2.35** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure is available to an insured who receives policy materials through an online account. The insurer must use a method reasonably designed to notify the insured that the disclosure is available.

**B.2.36** An insurer must respond accurately to an insured's inquiry concerning the limitation. An insurer must not provide an explanation that is inconsistent with the disclosure or policy.

**B.2.37** An insurer must ensure that claim personnel understand the disclosure requirement when handling claims involving fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria. Claim communications must remain consistent with the policy and disclosure.

**B.2.38** An insurer must not use the disclosure to deny coverage that the policy otherwise provides. The disclosure must describe the limitation without altering the policy contract.

**B.2.39** An insurer must apply the disclosure requirement without regard to the distribution method used for the policy. The requirement applies whether the policy is issued directly or through a representative.

**B.2.40** An insurer must ensure that any translated disclosure accurately conveys the applicable limitation. The insurer must preserve the meaning of the required disclosure in each language used.

**B.2.41** An insurer may provide additional explanatory material concerning loss prevention or property maintenance. Additional material must not be presented as a substitute for the required disclosure.

**B.2.42** An insurer must ensure that any additional explanatory material does not imply additional coverage. The material must remain consistent with the policy's treatment of fungi, wet or dry rot, or bacteria.

**B.2.43** An insurer must establish controls for selecting the disclosure applicable to each policy form. The controls must reasonably prevent use of a disclosure intended for different coverage.

**B.2.44** An insurer must correct a disclosure error promptly after identifying the error. The insurer must take reasonable steps to provide corrected information to affected insureds.

**B.2.45** An insurer must not rely on oral disclosure alone. Any oral explanation must be accompanied by the written or electronically retained disclosure required by this section.

**B.2.46** An insurer must ensure that the disclosure is not contradicted by declarations, endorsements, summaries, or other policy materials. An insurer must resolve inconsistencies before issuing the policy materials when practicable.

**B.2.47** An insurer must maintain internal review procedures for disclosures used under this section. The procedures must reasonably confirm that current policy language is reflected.

**B.2.48** An insurer must make compliance records available upon request by the Department. The records must be sufficient to demonstrate the insurer's implementation of this section.

**B.2.49** An insurer must not treat the disclosure as a waiver by an insured of rights provided by the policy. The disclosure serves to inform the insured of the applicable limitation.

**B.2.50** An insurer must not require an insured to acknowledge the disclosure as a condition of coverage. An insurer may obtain acknowledgment if the acknowledgment is not misleading and does not alter coverage.

**B.2.51** An insurer must ensure that acknowledgment language, if used, does not imply that the insured agreed to a limitation not contained in the policy. The acknowledgment must not replace the required disclosure.

**B.2.52** An insurer must apply this section to policy forms containing the applicable limitation, regardless of the policy's general coverage classification. The insurer must evaluate the actual policy language to determine applicability.

**B.2.53** An insurer must use reasonable quality-control measures to confirm delivery of the disclosure. A delivery failure identified through those measures must be addressed promptly.

**B.2.54** An insurer must ensure that third-party administrators acting on its behalf follow this section. The insurer remains accountable for disclosures provided through such administrators.

**B.2.55** An insurer must ensure that policy system changes do not remove, alter, or obscure the disclosure. The insurer must test revised processes to the extent reasonably necessary to support compliance.

**B.2.56** An insurer must not use a disclaimer that defeats the purpose of the disclosure. A disclaimer may clarify policy administration only if it does not conflict with the required notice.

**B.2.57** An insurer must interpret this section in a manner that promotes clear communication of the applicable limitation. An insurer must not adopt a practice designed to avoid meaningful disclosure.

**B.2.58** An insurer must continue to provide the disclosure while the policy contains the applicable limitation. The disclosure obligation ends only when the policy no longer includes that limitation.

**B.2.59** An insurer must ensure that its compliance practices are reasonably designed for the insurer's methods of issuance and delivery. Those practices must produce a disclosure that is timely, clear, and consistent with the policy.

**B.2.60** An insurer must comply with this section for coverage subject to its effective date. An insurer may provide disclosures exceeding these requirements if the additional disclosure is accurate and not misleading.

## B.3 — Notice and Disclosure

**B.3.1** This section takes effect upon issuance. An admitted insurer must apply this section to coverage issued or renewed after the effective date.

**B.3.2** An insurer must provide a clear written notice when a policy contains a fungi coverage limit. The notice must be provided in a manner that allows the applicant or insured to review it before accepting the coverage.

**B.3.3** The notice required by **B.3.2** must identify the fungi coverage limit as a limitation on coverage. An insurer must not present the limitation in a manner that is misleading or likely to be overlooked.

**B.3.4** An insurer must use language that is reasonably understandable to an applicant or insured. The notice must state that coverage for loss involving fungi may be limited.

**B.3.5** An insurer must identify the coverage to which the fungi coverage limit applies. The notice must describe the affected coverage consistently with the policy language.

**B.3.6** An insurer must identify material conditions that apply to coverage subject to the fungi coverage limit. The notice must not imply that coverage applies when policy conditions have not been satisfied.

**B.3.7** An insurer must identify material exclusions associated with the fungi coverage limit. The notice may summarize an exclusion if the summary is accurate and does not broaden coverage.

**B.3.8** An insurer must make the notice conspicuous. An insurer may provide the notice within an application, policy, endorsement, renewal material, or separate disclosure.

**B.3.9** An insurer must distinguish the fungi coverage limit from other policy limitations. The notice must avoid language that could cause an applicant or insured to confuse separate coverage restrictions.

**B.3.10** An insurer must ensure that the notice corresponds to the policy form offered. A notice must not describe coverage that differs from the coverage provided by the policy.

**B.3.11** An insurer must ensure that the notice corresponds to any endorsement that modifies the fungi coverage limit. The notice must describe the effect of the endorsement accurately.

**B.3.12** An insurer may use plain-language explanations in addition to policy language. Any explanation must remain consistent with the policy and must not alter the contract.

**B.3.13** An insurer must not use a notice to create coverage. A notice must not waive a policy condition, exclusion, or limitation.

**B.3.14** An insurer must not state or imply that a fungi coverage limit is unrelated to the insured’s selected coverage. The notice must accurately describe the relationship between the limitation and the policy.

**B.3.15** An insurer must provide a notice for new coverage when the offered policy includes a fungi coverage limit. The insurer must make the notice available before the applicant is bound to the policy.

**B.3.16** An insurer must provide a notice at renewal when the renewing policy includes a fungi coverage limit that differs materially from the prior policy. The notice must identify the material change in clear language.

**B.3.17** An insurer must provide a notice when a policy change adds a fungi coverage limit. The notice must be provided before the change becomes effective, unless the change is requested by the insured.

**B.3.18** When an insured requests a policy change, an insurer must provide notice of any resulting fungi coverage limit. The notice may accompany the material confirming the requested change.

**B.3.19** An insurer must retain records demonstrating compliance with this section. The records must show the notice used and the manner in which it was provided.

**B.3.20** An insurer must make compliance records available to the Department upon request. The records must be sufficient to permit review of the insurer’s notice practices.

**B.3.21** An insurer must maintain controls designed to ensure that the notice matches the applicable policy language. The controls must address changes to forms, endorsements, and related communications.

**B.3.22** An insurer must review notice content when it revises a policy provision affecting fungi coverage. The insurer must revise the notice when necessary to remain accurate.

**B.3.23** An insurer may use electronic delivery for a notice required by this section when electronic delivery is permitted by applicable law. The insurer must preserve evidence of electronic delivery.

**B.3.24** An insurer that uses an agent or producer to provide the notice remains responsible for compliance with this section. The insurer must establish procedures reasonably designed to support accurate delivery.

**B.3.25** An insurer must ensure that agent or producer materials do not contradict the required notice. The insurer must correct material inconsistencies when identified.

**B.3.26** An insurer must respond accurately to an applicant’s or insured’s reasonable inquiry regarding the fungi coverage limit. A response must not conflict with the policy or notice.

**B.3.27** An insurer may direct an applicant or insured to the applicable policy language for complete terms. The insurer must not use that direction as a substitute for the notice required by this section.

**B.3.28** An insurer must not obscure the notice through formatting, placement, or unrelated content. The notice must be reasonably prominent within the communication in which it appears.

**B.3.29** An insurer must use type, headings, and spacing that reasonably distinguish the notice from surrounding material. An insurer may use emphasis to draw attention to the fungi coverage limit.

**B.3.30** An insurer must ensure that a translated notice, if provided, accurately conveys the fungi coverage limit. The controlling policy language remains subject to applicable law.

**B.3.31** An insurer must apply this section to personal and commercial coverage when the policy includes a fungi coverage limit. The notice must be suitable for the coverage being offered.

**B.3.32** An insurer must ensure that the notice does not mischaracterize coverage available under another policy provision. The notice must describe only the limitation applicable to the offered coverage.

**B.3.33** An insurer must not condition delivery of the notice on a request by the applicant or insured. The notice is required whenever this section applies.

**B.3.34** An insurer must address a material discrepancy between the notice and policy language without undue delay. The insurer must provide corrected information to affected applicants or insureds when appropriate.

**B.3.35** An insurer must train personnel responsible for policy issuance, renewal, and customer communications regarding the notice requirements of this section. The training must address accurate description of the fungi coverage limit.

**B.3.36** An insurer must monitor its notice practices for compliance with this section. The insurer must take corrective action when its monitoring identifies a material deficiency.

**B.3.37** An insurer may use a notice that includes additional information about fungi coverage if the additional information is accurate. Additional information must not diminish the clarity or prominence of the required disclosure.

**B.3.38** Compliance with this section does not relieve an insurer of any obligation imposed by applicable law. An insurer must administer the fungi coverage limit consistently with the policy and the notice provided.

## B.4 — Claims Standards

**B.4.1** This section applies to an insurer handling a property insurance claim involving alleged loss caused by fungi. An insurer must administer the claim in a manner consistent with the policy terms and the disclosure provided to the insured.

**B.4.2** An insurer must identify the policy provisions that may affect coverage for fungi-related loss. The insurer must not describe a limitation in a manner that is inconsistent with the policy.

**B.4.3** An insurer must review the reported facts before determining whether a fungi limitation applies. The insurer must not deny or limit a claim solely because fungi are alleged or observed.

**B.4.4** An insurer must evaluate whether a covered cause of loss resulted in the condition involving fungi. The insurer must consider all material information reasonably available concerning the reported loss.

**B.4.5** An insurer must distinguish between damage caused by fungi and damage caused by another covered cause of loss. The insurer must explain the basis for any distinction made in the claim determination.

**B.4.6** An insurer must provide a clear written explanation when applying a fungi limitation. The explanation must identify the relevant policy language and its effect on the claim.

**B.4.7** An insurer must not rely on an exclusion, limitation, or condition that was not made part of the applicable policy. The insurer must retain information supporting its determination that the provision applies.

**B.4.8** An insurer must communicate with the insured in plain language concerning the status of a fungi-related claim. The insurer must respond to reasonable requests for information relevant to the claim determination.

**B.4.9** An insurer must advise the insured when additional information is needed to evaluate a reported fungi-related loss. The request must describe the information sought and its relevance to the claim.

**B.4.10** An insurer must conduct a reasonable investigation of a reported loss before denying coverage based on a fungi limitation. The investigation must be appropriate to the facts presented.

**B.4.11** An insurer may use an inspection, expert opinion, laboratory result, or other reliable information when evaluating a fungi-related claim. An insurer must consider such information fairly and in context.

**B.4.12** An insurer must not require the insured to establish facts that are not reasonably necessary to determine coverage. An insurer must not impose unreasonable conditions on the adjustment of a fungi-related claim.

**B.4.13** An insurer must document material communications with the insured concerning the fungi limitation. The claim file must reflect the information relied upon in reaching the coverage determination.

**B.4.14** An insurer must provide the insured with a written notice of any partial denial involving fungi-related loss. The notice must state the factual and policy basis for the portion of the claim not paid.

**B.4.15** An insurer must not characterize a claim as excluded when the policy instead provides limited coverage. An insurer must accurately describe the coverage available under the applicable policy terms.

**B.4.16** An insurer must evaluate reasonable expenses claimed in connection with a covered fungi-related loss under the applicable policy provisions. The insurer must explain any decision to deny or limit payment for such expenses.

**B.4.17** An insurer must not delay a coverage determination while awaiting information that is not material to the claim. An insurer may request material information needed to resolve a genuine coverage issue.

**B.4.18** An insurer must preserve claim records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this section. The records must include the policy provisions considered and the basis for the final determination.

**B.4.19** An insurer must ensure that personnel responsible for claim handling understand the applicable fungi limitations and related policy language. An insurer must administer claims consistently with those provisions.

**B.4.20** An insurer must not use a fungi limitation as a basis to avoid investigating a potentially covered loss. The insurer must evaluate the reported cause of loss before applying the limitation.

**B.4.21** An insurer must consider information submitted by the insured after an initial claim determination. An insurer must revise its determination when the additional information supports a different result under the policy.

**B.4.22** An insurer must provide a reasonable explanation when an insured requests clarification of a fungi-related coverage determination. The explanation must remain consistent with the policy and the claim file.

**B.4.23** An insurer must not state or imply that a fungi limitation eliminates all coverage unless the applicable policy language so provides. Any coverage communication must accurately state the scope of the limitation.

**B.4.24** An insurer must treat a dispute concerning a fungi limitation as a claim-handling matter requiring a good-faith review. The insurer must not discourage an insured from submitting information relevant to coverage.

**B.4.25** An insurer must apply the policy language in effect for the claim at issue. The insurer must not apply a revised limitation unless that limitation is part of the applicable policy.

**B.4.26** An insurer must make claim determinations without unfair discrimination among insureds presenting substantially similar facts and policy provisions. The insurer must maintain procedures supporting consistent claim handling.

**B.4.27** An insurer must comply with this section in addition to all other applicable claim-handling obligations. Compliance with this section does not alter the coverage provided by the applicable policy.

## B.5 — Filing and Effective Date

**B.5.1** The requirements in this section take effect upon issuance by the Department. An admitted insurer must comply with these requirements before using a fungi coverage limit disclosure.

**B.5.2** An insurer must submit the applicable policy language, endorsement language, notice language, and supporting materials to the Department for review. An insurer must not deliver or issue material subject to this section before Department approval.

**B.5.3** A filing must clearly identify the fungi coverage limit disclosure proposed for use. The filing must identify the lines of insurance and policy forms to which the disclosure applies.

**B.5.4** An insurer must provide the proposed disclosure in the form in which it will be presented to the insured. The disclosure must be clear, conspicuous, and consistent with the applicable policy language.

**B.5.5** An insurer must explain the placement of the disclosure within the policy materials. The explanation must describe how the insured will receive the disclosure.

**B.5.6** An insurer must ensure that the filed disclosure accurately reflects the coverage provided under the applicable policy. An insurer must not use language that is inconsistent with the policy, endorsement, or other applicable contract provision.

**B.5.7** A filing must include a transmittal letter describing the purpose of the submission. The transmittal letter must identify whether the submission is a new filing, a revision, or a replacement.

**B.5.8** An insurer must include a certification that the filing complies with this section. The certification must be executed by an authorized representative of the insurer.

**B.5.9** An insurer must file revisions to an approved fungi coverage limit disclosure before use. A revision must be submitted with a clear explanation of each change.

**B.5.10** An insurer must submit replacement policy or endorsement language when a change affects the accuracy of the disclosure. The replacement materials must be suitable for review by the Department.

**B.5.11** The Department may request additional information necessary to evaluate a filing. An insurer must provide the requested information before the Department completes its review.

**B.5.12** Department approval applies only to the materials submitted and approved. An insurer must obtain Department approval before using materially different disclosure language.

**B.5.13** An insurer must withdraw superseded disclosure materials from use when replacement materials are approved. An insurer must ensure that its distribution practices reflect the approved materials.

**B.5.14** An insurer must retain records supporting its filing and use of the approved disclosure. The records must be available to the Department upon request.

**B.5.15** An insurer must promptly identify any use of disclosure language that does not comply with this section. The insurer must take appropriate corrective action when noncompliant language is identified.

**B.5.16** An insurer must apply an approved fungi coverage limit disclosure to policy materials delivered or issued after Department approval. An insurer must not represent that a disclosure is approved unless the Department has approved the submitted materials.
